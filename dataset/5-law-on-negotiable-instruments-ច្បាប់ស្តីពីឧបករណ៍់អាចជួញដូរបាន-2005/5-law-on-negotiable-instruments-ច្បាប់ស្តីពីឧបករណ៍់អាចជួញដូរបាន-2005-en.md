### PROMULGATE

The Law on Negotiable Instruments and Payment Transactions, which was adopted by the National Assembly on September 19, 2005, during the 3rd session of the 3rd legislature, and approved in its entirety by the Senate regarding its form and legal aspects on September 24, 2005, during the 10th plenary session of the 1st legislature, the full text of which is as follows:

## Chapter 1: General Provisions

### Article 1

This Law governs negotiable instruments and payment transactions, including bank accounts and operations of payment systems.

1- "Negotiable instrument" refers to a written order or promise to pay a specific amount of money, which is transferable by delivery and, when necessary, with endorsement. Negotiable instruments governed by this Law include cheques, bills of exchange, and promissory notes.

2- "Payment transaction" governed and defined by this Law is a transfer of funds between or from bank accounts. A payment transaction may be a credit transfer or a debit transfer. This transaction is initiated by a payment order, which may be in writing, electronic, or oral under certain conditions.

3- "Payment system" includes institutions or mechanisms that facilitate the payment of money and the transfer of monetary value through payment transactions.

### Article 2

Technical terms used in this Law shall have the following definitions:

1- "Force majeure" refers to an event which is an unavoidable obstacle, uncontrollable, and beyond the control of the person who is to perform the obligation.

2- "Issue" refers to the writing, signing, and delivery of a cheque, bill of exchange, or promissory note to the first holder.

3- "Drawing of a cheque or bill of exchange" refers to the writing and signing of a cheque or bill of exchange.

4- "Settlement" refers to the payment of money to discharge obligations under an interbank payment order.

5- "Presentation" refers to the delivery of a cheque, bill of exchange, or promissory note to request acceptance of the bill of exchange or payment from the drawee (or co-drawees) or maker of the note.

6- "Execution", in Chapter 5 of this Law, refers to the implementation of the instruction contained in a payment order by issuing a related payment order to the receiving bank.

7- "Clearing" refers to the offsetting and calculation of net amounts on a bilateral or multilateral basis that banks owe to one another for the settlement of all payment orders entered into each clearing system.

8- "In-house transfer" refers to a transfer of funds from the sender to the receiver within a single bank by a single payment order. Participants in an in-house transfer include the sender, the bank acting as both the sending bank and the destination bank, and the receiver.

9- "Interbank transfer" refers to a transfer of funds from the sender to the receiver at two different banks by at least two payment orders. In an interbank transfer:

a- Each receiving bank that is not the destination bank executes the payment order of its sender by issuing a related payment order to a receiving bank, and the final payment order is sent to the destination bank.

b- Participants in an interbank transfer include the sender, the sending bank, the destination bank, and the receiver, and may also include

one or more intermediary banks.

c- When the sending bank does not settle directly with the destination bank, there must be at least one or more intermediary banks.

d- For each interbank payment order, the sending bank settles with the receiving bank as provided in Article 202 of this Law.

10- "Debit transfer" refers to a payment transaction initiated by the receiver through its bank, based on an authorization from the sender to collect funds from the sender's account.

11- "Credit transfer" refers to a payment transaction initiated by the sender who issues a payment order to the bank to transfer funds from its account to the receiver or to the receiver's account.

12- "Agreement", in Section 7 of Chapter 5 of this Law, refers to a contract between a bank and a customer applicable to account transactions, including various orders, such as the timing of the transaction, modifications, and payment orders.

13- "Account" refers to any account opened under a contractual relationship between a customer and a bank, whether a current account, a deposit account, or any other account, including accounts under a special relationship established for the sole purpose of executing a single payment transaction.

14- "Settlement account" refers to an account opened by a bank on the books of the National Bank of Cambodia for incoming and outgoing payment transactions.

15- "Passbook deposit account" refers to an account which is debited and credited by the bank to record transactions in a deposit passbook originally issued by the bank to the customer, and which is presented to the bank to record all transactions.

16- "By endorsement" refers to an endorsement with limitations

to collect payment on behalf of the endorser.

17- "Electronic" refers to a telecommunications network connected by computer systems or wired or wireless transmissions, such as computer system connections, fiber optics, radio frequencies, or similar devices.

18- "Value for collection" refers to the value received by the transferor upon endorsement, or the value at which the bank repurchases from the transferee, on the negotiable instrument.

19- "Value for security" or "value for pledge" refers to a transfer with a guarantee or a transfer for the purpose of creating a security interest, and which is not a transfer of ownership.

20- "Banking day" refers to the part of a day during which a bank is open to the public to receive and process transmissions of payment orders and other communications relating to payment transactions.

21- "Effective date" refers to the banking day on which a payment order is executed in accordance with the instruction of the sender.

22- "Bank" refers to the National Bank of Cambodia and banking and financial institutions licensed to carry out banking operations under the Law on Banking and Financial Institutions, and any institution or entity authorized by law to accept deposits or participate in payment transactions on customer accounts.

A branch or separate office of a bank is considered a separate bank for the purposes of (a) the duties in executing payment transactions and (b) calculating the time within which, or determining the place at which, action is to be taken or communications or orders are to be made.

23- "Destination bank" refers to the bank specified in a payment order in which the payment to the receiver in a credit transfer is to be

was made, or who holds the sender's account in a credit transfer. "Destination bank" means the bank which receives the payment order last in a payment transaction. In a credit transfer, the destination bank is the beneficiary's bank. In a debit transfer, the destination bank is the sender's bank.

24- "Originating bank" means the bank receiving the payment order that initiates the payment transaction. In a credit transfer, the originating bank is the sender's bank. In a debit transfer, the originating bank is the beneficiary's bank.

25- "Receiving bank" means a bank to which the sender's payment order is sent.

26- "Intermediary bank" means a receiving bank other than the originating bank and the destination bank.

27- "Security procedure" means a procedure established by agreement between a customer and a receiving bank for the purpose of:

- verifying that a payment order or a cancellation is indeed the order of the customer, or

- detecting errors or alterations in the transmission or content of a payment order or a cancellation.

A security procedure may require the use of algorithms, codes or passwords, identifying words or numbers, encryption, callback procedures or similar security devices. Comparison of a signature on a payment order or communication with the customer with a specimen signature is not of itself a security procedure.

28- "Recourse without expense" means a promise that allows recourse against relevant parties without requiring the holder to make a protest.

29- "Payment order" means an instruction to a bank to pay or collect a sum of money from an account indicated, or for the beneficiary, or to or for the account of the beneficiary, and includes any amendment to the payment order. The order may specify an effective date but shall not be subject to any condition other than those agreed upon by the originating bank. The parties to a payment order include the sender and the receiving bank. When the sender is a bank, the payment order is an interbank payment order.

30- "Drawer" means a person who issues an order to pay a check or a bill of exchange.

31- "Drawee" means a person who receives an order to pay a check or a bill of exchange.

32- "Payment transaction" means the transfer of funds from the account of the payer/sender to the beneficiary or to the account of the beneficiary. A payment transaction:

(ក) may be a credit transfer or a debit transfer;

(ខ) is initiated by a payment order of the originator to the originating bank to perform the payment transaction;

(គ) is executed by the sender's bank by debiting the account of the sender, and for the beneficiary's bank, crediting the account of the beneficiary or placing the funds at the disposal and for the use of the beneficiary as ordered by the originator;

(ឃ) may be a transfer of funds from the sender to the beneficiary who may be one or two different natural persons or legal entities; and

(e) may be a transfer of funds in which the sender must have an account, and the payment to the beneficiary may be made within the same bank or between two different banks.

33- "Endorsement" means a signature on the back of a negotiable instrument, with or without certifying words. To be effective, an endorsement must be completed by delivery.

34- "Person" means a legal entity and a natural person.

35- "Sufficiency of balance" with respect to a payment order means the existence of a sufficient balance of funds in the account, excluding the amount of the payment order, including the balance on the bank's books on the preceding business day, credit of transfers into the sender's account on the bank's business day of today, other deposits and incoming transfers, payments out, withdrawals already made on the bank's business day of today, and any bank fees that may apply. Funds listed in the temporary payment section of the sender's account shall not be included as part of the funds required for that payment.

36- "Funds" means cash and deposits held in a bank account.

37- "Aval" means a guarantee of payment on a check, bill of exchange or promissory note.

38- "Foreign currency" means any currency other than domestic currency.

39- "Domestic currency" means the Riel, which is the official currency of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

40- "Protest" means a written and official declaration in all circumstances such as a protest or a refusal to certify, non-payment or non-acceptance, which gives the holder the right to protest against

negotiable instruments. A protest must be made in such manner and at such time as required by specific provisions of this Law.

41- "Acceptance" means the agreement of the drawee of a bill of exchange to comply with the order of the drawer. To be effective, the acceptance must be completed by delivery and notification.

42- "For collection" means an endorsement which shows that the endorsement is not a transfer of ownership, but is for the purpose of facilitating collection or recovering the amount of money on the negotiable instrument by the endorsee on behalf of the endorser.

43- "Payee" in Chapters 2, 3 and 4 of this Law, means a person who benefits from the payment of a check, a bill of exchange or a promissory note at the time it is issued.

44- "Customer" means any individual, whether engaged in business or not, or a legal entity including a bank, who has an account at a bank.

45- "Intervention for payment" means a procedure in which a party not involved in the negotiable instrument intervenes to accept or pay on behalf of a party who is liable.

46- "Maker of a promissory note" or "Maker" means a person who promises to pay on a promissory note.

47- "Sender" means a person who sends a payment order to a receiving bank.

48- "Originator" means the first sender of a payment order who initiates a payment transaction. In a credit transfer, the originator is the payer/sender. In a debit transfer, the originator is the beneficiary.

49- "Beneficiary" means a participant whose bank is the last to receive the payment order in a payment transaction. In a credit transfer, the beneficiary is the payee. In a debit transfer, the beneficiary is the

payment.

50- "Beneficiary" in Chapter 5 of this Law, means a participant who is to receive money in a payment transaction, and in a debit transfer, "beneficiary" includes a person who is to receive money by a transfer with authorized assignment, if any. In a credit transfer, the beneficiary is the beneficiary. In a debit transfer, the beneficiary is the originator.

51- "Payer" means a participant who is liable to pay in a payment transaction. In a credit transfer, the payer is the originator. In a debit transfer, the payer is the beneficiary.

52- "Checkable credit line" means a credit scheme which allows the borrowing of funds, and the withdrawal or transfer of such funds must be made by check or payment order.

53- "Maturity" means the date on which a bill of exchange or a promissory note is due for payment.

54- "Recourse" means the right to sue for compensation or recovery of damages from liable persons on a negotiable instrument.

#### Chapter 2
#### Checks

#### Section 1
#### Issue and Form of Checks

##### Article 3.-
A check is an unconditional order in writing addressed to a bank and signed by the drawer, requiring the bank to pay a specified sum of money to, or to the order of, a specified person, or to the bearer. A check

must be paid on demand, and in accordance with all obligations set forth in this Chapter.

##### Article 4.-
A check may be written in Khmer or in a foreign language and must contain the following particulars:

- the term "check" inserted in the body of the instrument and expressed in the language employed in drawing the instrument;

- an unconditional order to pay a determinate sum of money;

- the name of the drawee, who must be a bank, who is to pay;

- the place where payment is to be made;

- the date and the place where the check is issued;

- the signature of the drawer, who is the person issuing the order on the check.

##### Article 5.-
An instrument in which any of the requirements mentioned in Article 4 is wanting is invalid as a check, except in the cases specified in the following paragraphs:

1- In the absence of special mention, the place specified beside the name of the drawee is deemed to be the place of payment. If several places are specified beside the name of the drawee, the check is payable at the first place specified.

2- In the absence of these mentions and any other mention, the check is payable at the place where the drawee has its principal place of business.

3- A check which does not specify the place of issue is deemed to have been issued at the place specified beside the name of the drawer.

##### Article 6.-
A check must be drawn on a bank holding funds at the disposal of the drawer and in conformity with an agreement, express or implied, according to which the drawer has the right to dispose of those funds by check. Nevertheless, even if these provisions are not complied with, the instrument is still valid as a check.

##### Article 7.-
A check cannot be accepted. A statement of acceptance on a check is disregarded.

A check does not operate as an assignment of any funds in the hands of the drawee available for the payment thereof, and the drawee of a check is not liable to the holder.

##### Article 8.-
A check may be issued payable to:

- a specified person with or without the words "to order";

- a specified person with the words "not to order" or equivalent words; or

- the bearer.

A check payable to a specified person with the words "pay to bearer" or equivalent words is deemed to be a check payable to bearer.

A check which does not specify the name of the payee is deemed to be a check payable to bearer.

##### Article 9.-
A check may be payable to the drawer's own order.

A check may be drawn for the account of a third person.

A check may not be drawn on the drawer himself, unless the check is drawn between different establishments of the same drawer.

##### Article 10.-
Any stipulation of interest on a check shall be deemed unwritten.

##### Article 11.-
A check may be payable at the domicile of a third person, either in the locality where the drawee has its domicile or in another locality, provided always that the third person is a bank.

##### Article 12.-
Where the sum payable by a check is expressed in words and also in figures, and there is a discrepancy between the two, the sum expressed in words is the amount payable.

Where the sum payable by a check is expressed more than once in words or more than once in figures, and there is a discrepancy, the smaller sum is the amount payable.

##### Article 13.-
Every obligation of other persons who signed the check remains valid, even if the check bears the signature of a person who is incapable of

incapacity, or forged signatures, or signatures of fictitious persons, or signatures which for any other reason cannot bind the persons who signed them or on whose behalf they were signed.

##### Article 14.-
Whosoever puts his signature on a check as representing a person for whom he had no power to act is bound himself as a party to the check and, if he pays, has the same rights as the person whom he purported to represent. The same rule applies to an agent who has exceeded his powers.

##### Article 15.-
The drawer guarantees payment. Any stipulation by which the drawer releases himself from this guarantee is deemed unwritten.

##### Article 16.-
If a check which was incomplete when issued has been completed otherwise than in accordance with the agreements entered into, the non-observance of such agreements cannot be set up against the holder, unless he has acquired the check in bad faith or, in acquiring it, has been guilty of gross negligence.

#### Section 2
#### Endorsement of Checks

##### Article 17.-
A check payable to a specified person, with or without the words "to order", may be transferred by endorsement.

A check payable to a specified person, containing the words "not to order" or an equivalent expression, can only be transferred according to the form and with the effects of an ordinary assignment.

An endorsement may be made even to the drawer or to any other party to the check. These persons may endorse the check again.

##### Article 18.-
An endorsement must be unconditional. Any condition to which it is subject is deemed unwritten.

A partial endorsement is void.

An endorsement by the drawee is void.

An endorsement to bearer is equivalent to a blank endorsement.

An endorsement to the drawee only has the value of a receipt, except where the drawee has several establishments and the endorsement is made in favor of an establishment other than that on which the check was drawn.

##### Article 19.-
An endorsement must be written on the check itself or on a slip affixed thereto called an allonge, and must be signed by the endorser.

An endorsement may not specify the beneficiary or may consist simply of the signature of the endorser, in which case it is a blank endorsement.

**Article 20.-**

An endorsement transfers all the rights arising out of a cheque.

If the endorsement is in blank, the holder may:

- fill up the blank either with his own name or with the name of some other person;

- re-endorse the cheque in blank, or to some other person;

- transfer the cheque to a third person without filling up the blank, and without endorsing it.

**Article 21.-**

In the absence of any stipulation to the contrary, the endorser guarantees payment.

He may prohibit any further endorsement; in this case, he is not liable to guarantee payment to the persons to whom the cheque is subsequently endorsed.

**Article 22.-**

The possessor of an endorsable cheque is deemed to be the lawful holder if he establishes his title to the cheque through an uninterrupted series of endorsements, even if the last endorsement is in blank. In this connection, cancelled endorsements are deemed not to be written. Where an endorsement in blank is followed by another endorsement, the person who signed this last endorsement is deemed to have acquired the cheque by the endorsement in blank.

**Article 23.-**

An endorsement on a cheque payable to bearer makes the endorser liable in accordance with the provisions governing the right of recourse, but the endorsement

does not convert the instrument into a cheque payable to order.

**Article 24.-**

Where a person has been dispossessed of a cheque by any event whatsoever, whether it is a cheque payable to bearer or an endorsable cheque in respect of which the holder establishes his right in the manner mentioned in Article 22, the holder who has acquired the cheque is not bound to give up the cheque, unless he has acquired it in bad faith or in obtaining it he has been guilty of gross negligence.

**Article 25.-**

Persons sued on a cheque cannot set up against the holder defences founded on their personal relations with the drawer or with previous holders, unless the holder, in acquiring the cheque, has knowingly acted to the detriment of the debtor.

The above paragraph of this Article does not apply to a cheque issued for payment to a specific person which contains the words "not to order" or an equivalent expression.

**Article 26.-**

When an endorsement contains the statement "value in security", "value in pledge" or any other statement implying a pledge, the holder may exercise all rights arising out of the cheque, but he can only endorse it in his capacity as an agent.

In this case, all parties liable can sue the holder, and the holder can also sue the endorser.

The authority contained in an endorsement is not terminated by the death of the party giving the authorization or by his legal incapacity.

**Article 27.-**

An endorsement made after protest or after an equivalent declaration, or after the expiration of the limit of time for presentment, can operate only as:

- an ordinary assignment;

- it does not operate to grant rights under Article 22, Article 24 and Article 25 of this Law.

In the absence of proof to the contrary, an undated endorsement is deemed to have been placed on the cheque prior to the protest or equivalent declaration, or prior to the expiration of the limit of time referred to in the preceding paragraph.

#### Part 3
#### Aval of Cheques

**Article 28.-**

Payment of a cheque may be guaranteed by an aval as to the whole or part of its amount.

This guarantee may be given by a third person other than the drawee, or even by a person who has signed the cheque.

**Article 29.-**

An aval may be written on the cheque itself or on an allonge.

It must be expressed by the words "good as aval" or by any other equivalent formula and must be signed by the provider of the

aval, called the avalist.

The signature of the aval must be placed on the face of the cheque.

The aval must specify the person for whose benefit it is given. In the absence of such specification, the aval is deemed to be given for the drawer.

**Article 30.-**

An avalist is bound in the same manner as the person for whom he has become guarantor.

His undertaking is valid even when the liability which he has guaranteed is inoperative for any reason other than defect of form.

He has, when he pays the cheque, the rights arising out of the cheque against the person guaranteed and the persons liable to the latter on the cheque.

#### Part 4
#### Presentment and Payment

**Article 31.-**

A cheque is payable at sight. Any contrary stipulation is deemed to be unwritten.

A cheque presented for payment before the date stated as the date of issue is payable on the day of presentment.

**Article 32.-**

A cheque must be presented for payment within a period of six (6) months from the date of issue.

**Article 33.-**

Where a cheque is drawn between two places having different calendars, the date of issue shall be referred to the corresponding day of the calendar of the place of payment.

**Article 34.-**

Presentment of a cheque at or through a clearing house, or through an electronic system as provided in an agreement or regulation issued by the National Bank of Cambodia, constitutes a presentment for payment.

**Article 35.-**

Payment of a cheque may be countermanded without prejudice to the rights of the holder.

If the payment of a cheque has not been countermanded, the drawee may pay it even after the expiration of the limit of time for presentment.

**Article 36.-**

The death or incapacity of the drawer, occurring after the issue of the cheque, has no effect on the cheque.

**Article 37.-**

The drawee who pays a cheque may require that it shall be given up to him with a receipted statement from the holder.

If there are insufficient funds in the account of the drawee, the holder may not refuse a partial payment.

In case of a partial payment, the drawee may require that mention of such payment be made on the cheque and that a receipt be given to him.

**Article 38.-**

The drawee who pays an endorsable cheque is bound to verify the regularity of the series of endorsements, but not the signature of the endorsers.

**Article 39.-**

The currency to be used for the payment of a cheque is determined as follows:

1- A cheque is deemed to authorize the drawee to pay in the currency of the account on which the cheque is drawn.

2- Where a cheque is drawn in a currency other than that of the account, the amount may be paid in the currency of the account by the drawee at the value of that currency on the day of payment.

3- If the cheque is drawn in a currency other than that of the place of payment, and the cheque has not been paid upon presentment, the holder may require the parties liable to pay the amount of the cheque in the currency of the place of payment at the rate of exchange on the day of presentment or on the day of payment, at his option.

4- The usages of the place of payment shall be applied in determining the value of the currency. However, the drawee may determine that the amount to be paid shall be calculated according to the rate of exchange specified on the cheque.

5- Notwithstanding the third paragraph of this Article, where a cheque is drawn for payment in a currency used in the Kingdom of Cambodia, or if the drawer has specified on the cheque that the payment must be made in a specific currency, the holder may demand from any party liable that payment be made in that specified currency.

6- If the amount of the cheque is specified in a currency having

the same name but a different value in the country of issue and the country of payment, and unless otherwise specified, where the currency specified is not the currency of the account on which the cheque is drawn, the payment shall be made in the currency of the country of payment. Where the country of payment has no currency of that name, the payment of the cheque shall be made in the currency commonly used at the place of payment.

#### Part 5
#### Crossed Cheques and Cheques Payable in Account

**Article 40.-**

The drawer or the holder may cross a cheque.

The crossing consists of two parallel lines drawn on the face of the cheque. The crossing may be general or special.

The crossing is general if it consists of two lines only or if between the two lines there is the word "bank" or some equivalent term. The crossing is special if the name of a banker is written between the two lines.

A general crossing may be converted into a special crossing, but a special crossing cannot be converted into a general crossing.

The obliteration or alteration of a crossing or of the name of the banker is not permitted.

**Article 41.-**

A cheque with a general crossing can be paid by the drawee only to a banker or to a customer of the drawee.

A cheque with a special crossing can be paid by the drawee only to the banker named on the cheque, or if the latter is the drawee

he may pay it to his customer. However, the banker named on the cheque may employ another banker to collect the cheque.

A banker may not acquire a crossed cheque except from one of his customers or from another banker. He may not collect it for the account of other persons than the foregoing.

A drawee shall not pay a cheque with more than one special crossing, except in the case of two crossings, of which one is for collection through a clearing house.

A drawee or banker who fails to observe the above provisions is liable for resulting damage up to the amount of the cheque.

**Article 42.-**

The drawer or the holder of a cheque may forbid its payment in cash by writing across the face of the cheque the words "payable in account" or an equivalent expression.

In this case, the cheque can only be settled by the drawee by means of book entries, such as crediting an account, transfer from one account to another, set-off or payment through a clearing house. Settlement by book entry is equivalent to payment.

The obliteration or alteration of the words "payable in account" is not permitted.

A drawee who fails to observe the above provisions is liable for resulting damage up to the amount of the cheque.

#### Part 6
#### Recourse for Non-Payment

**Article 43.-**

The holder may exercise his right of recourse against the endorsers, the drawer and the other parties liable, if the cheque on being presented in due time is not paid, and if the refusal to pay is evidenced:

- by a protest or other equivalent formal instrument; or

- by a dated declaration written by the drawee showing the day of presentment; or

- by a dated declaration made by a clearing house stating that the cheque has been delivered in due time and has not been paid.

**Article 44.-**

A protest must be authenticated by a legally authorized officer. Until such legally authorized officer is appointed, the officer must be appointed by the National Bank of Cambodia. The protest must contain a copy or the original of the cheque, and must specify the person who makes the protest, the place and date, the grounds for the protest, the demand made and the answer given, if any, or the fact that the drawee could not be found. The protest must be made at the place of the refusal to pay or to accept, or at another place in the Kingdom of Cambodia within a distance not exceeding ten (10) kilometers from the place of presentment and the place of refusal to pay or to accept.

A protest or equivalent declaration must be made before the expiration of the limit of time for presentment.

If presentment takes place on the last day of the limit of time, the protest or equivalent declaration may be made on the first business day following.

After the expiration of the limit of time for protest of non-payment, the holder loses his right of recourse against the parties liable on the cheque, but he may sue the drawee under the extra-contractual provisions, and any recovery shall be set-off against damages caused by the holder to the drawee by reason of the failure to observe the limit of time.

**Article 45.-**

The holder must give notice of non-payment to his endorser and to the drawer within the four (4) business days which follow the day on which the protest or equivalent declaration was made, or in case of a stipulation "return without cost" or "without protest", or any equivalent expression, the day of presentment. Within two (2) business days following the day on which he receives notice, each endorser must give notice to his endorser of the notice he has received, specifying the names and addresses of those who have given the previous notices, and so on in order until the drawer receives it. The limit of time specified above runs from the receipt of the preceding notice.

In accordance with the preceding paragraph, when notice is given to a person who has signed a cheque, the same notice must be given to his avalist within the same limit of time.

Where the person making the protest has not specified his address, or has specified it unclearly, the notice of protest given to the next preceding endorser is sufficient.

A person who is required to give notice of protest may do so in any form, even by simply returning the cheque.

The person who is required to give notice of protest must prove that he has given the notice within the specified period. This period shall be deemed to have been observed if the letter of notice has been posted within that period.

A person who fails to give notice of protest within the specified period above shall be liable for any damage resulting from his negligence, if any, but the amount of liability shall not exceed the amount of the cheque.

**Article 46 (មាត្រា ៤៦).-**
By a stipulation "return without costs" (បង្គិលវិញដោយគ្មានសោហ៊ុយ) or "without protest" (បង្គិលវិញដោយគ្មានទំនាស់ទារ) or any other equivalent expression, written on the cheque and signed, the drawer (បហាវីក), the endorser (អ្នកធ្វើបដិសេធ) or the guarantor (រូបិយបដិភោគ) may release the holder from making a protest or an equivalent reservation in order to exercise his right of recourse.

This stipulation does not release the holder from presenting the cheque within the specified period, or from any requirement to give notice. The costs of proving non-observance of the specified period by the holder shall be borne by the person who has stipulated this clause.

If the stipulation is written by the drawer (បហាវីក), it shall apply to all parties who have signed the cheque. If the stipulation is written by the endorser (អ្នកធ្វើបដិសេធ) or the guarantor (រូបិយបដិភោគ), it shall apply only to

the endorser (អ្នកធ្វើបដិសេធ) or the guarantor (រូបិយបដិភោគ) only. If, in spite of the stipulation written by the drawer (បហាវីក), the holder makes a protest or an equivalent reservation, he must bear all the costs. When the stipulation emanates from the endorser (អ្នកធ្វើបដិសេធ) or the guarantor (រូបិយបដិភោគ), the costs of the protest or of the equivalent reservation, if any, may be recovered from all the persons who have signed the cheque.

**Article 47 (មាត្រា ៤៧).-**
All persons liable on a cheque are jointly and severally liable to the holder.

The holder has the right of recourse against all these persons individually or collectively, without being required to observe the order in which they have become liable.

The same right shall possess any person who has signed the cheque and has taken it up and paid it.

An action brought against one of the parties liable does not prevent actions against the others, even though they may be subsequent to the party first proceeded against.

**Article 48 (មាត្រា ៤៨).-**
The holder may claim from the party against whom he exercises his right of recourse the unpaid amount of the cheque, interest calculated at the rate set by the National Bank of Cambodia (ធនាគារជាតិនៃកម្ពុជា) from the day of presentment for payment, and any expenses incurred.

**Article 49 (មាត្រា ៤៩).-**
A party who has taken up and paid a cheque may claim from the parties liable to him the entire sum he has paid, interest on

the sum of money paid, interest calculated at the rate set by the National Bank of Cambodia (ធនាគារជាតិនៃកម្ពុជា) from the day he paid, and any expenses incurred.

**Article 50 (មាត្រា ៥០).-**
Any party liable against whom recourse is exercised or who is subject to recourse may require, against payment, the surrender of the cheque, together with the protest (វិរុទ្ធភាន) or an equivalent reservation, and a receipt.

Any endorser (អ្នកធ្វើបដិសេធ) who has taken up and paid a cheque may cancel his own endorsement and those of subsequent endorsers.

**Article 51 (មាត្រា ៥១).-**
When the presentment of the cheque or the making of a protest (វិរុទ្ធទាន) or an equivalent reservation within the specified period is prevented by an insurmountable obstacle (force majeure / ប្រធានសក្តិ), these periods shall be extended.

The holder is obliged to give notice without delay of the case of force majeure (ប្រធានសក្តិ) to his endorser (អ្នកធ្វើបដិសេធ) and to make a reservation regarding this notice, dated and signed on the cheque or on an allonge (អនុប័ត្រ). In other respects, the provisions of Article 45 of this Law shall apply.

When the force majeure (ប្រធានសក្តិ) has ceased, the holder must present the cheque without delay for payment and, if necessary, make a protest or an equivalent reservation.

If the force majeure (ប្រធានសក្តិ) continues to exist for more than fifteen (15) days after the date on which the holder has given notice of force majeure to his endorser, even if this date is before the expiration of the period for presentment of the cheque, recourse may be exercised, and neither presentment for payment nor the making of a protest (វិរុទ្ធភាន) or an equivalent reservation shall be necessary.

Facts which are purely personal to the holder or to the person who has been commissioned to present the cheque or to make the protest or an equivalent reservation shall not be deemed to be cases of force majeure (ប្រធានសក្តិ).

Liability on a cheque which has been lost or destroyed may be enforced only upon the provision of security by the party enforcing the cheque to indemnify the party liable against any other claims on the cheque, pursuant to a court decision, provided that the holder acquired the cheque in good faith.

## Section 7 (ផ្នែកទី ៧)
## Cheque Collection (បេឡានិធិយមូលប្បទានប័ត្រ)

**Article 52 (មាត្រា ៥២).-**
Cheque collection (បេឡានិធិយមូលប្បទានប័ត្រ) is established as follows:

1- When a cheque is delivered to a bank for credit to a customer's account for the amount of the cheque, the bank acquires all the rights and powers of a holder of the cheque.

2- Unless otherwise agreed, such credit to the account is provisional, and the bank may reverse the credit (charge back the account) or otherwise recover the amount from the customer if the cheque is not paid. Unless otherwise agreed, withdrawal of funds from the provisional credit account is permitted only upon the decision of the bank, and is subject to the bank's right of reimbursement or recourse against the customer if the cheque is not paid.

3- Without regard to any lack of capacity, irregularity, restriction, or limitation of form or type of endorsement of the customer, the bank's rights under paragraph 1 of this Article are those of a holder of the cheque who may endorse it in accordance with Article 22, notwithstanding Article 26 of this Law to the contrary.

**Article 53 (មាត្រា ៥៣).-**
When a cheque is presented to a bank for collection (បេឡានិធិយ), the cheque is a payment order governed by Chapter 5 of this Law on Payment Transactions. In the event of any inconsistency regarding cheques between the provisions of this Chapter and Chapter 5 of this Law, the provisions of this Chapter shall prevail.

## Section 8 (ផ្នែកទី ៨)
## Alteration of Cheques (ការកែលើមូលប្បទានប័ត្រ)

**Article 54 (មាត្រា ៥៤).-**
Where a material alteration is made to the text of a cheque, parties who have signed after the alteration are bound by the altered text. Parties who signed before the alteration are bound by the original text of the cheque.

## Section 9 (ផ្នែកទី ៩)
## Limitation of Recourse Actions (រយៈពេលកំណត់នៃការធ្វើឧបាស្រ័យ)

**Article 55 (មាត្រា ៥៥).-**
Actions of recourse by the holder against the endorsers (អ្នកធ្វើបដិសេធ), the drawer (បហាវីក) and the other parties liable are barred after six (06) months from the expiration of the period for presentment for payment.

Actions of recourse by the other parties liable for payment of the cheque against each other are barred after six (06) months from the day on which the party has taken up and paid the cheque or has been sued.

**Article 56 (មាត្រា ៥៦).-**
The limitation of recourse actions specified in Article 55 of this Law is effective only against the person for whom the time limit has run.

## Section 10 (ផ្នែកទី ១០)
## Calculation of Limits of Time (ការគណនារយៈពេលកំណត់)

**Article 57 (មាត្រា ៥៧).-**
Presentment of a cheque for payment or the making of a protest (វិរុទ្ធទាន) may only be done on a business day.

When the last day of the period specified for performing any act relating to a cheque, and in particular for presentment for payment or for making a protest (វិរុទ្ធទាន) or an equivalent reservation, is a legal holiday (ថ្ងៃឈប់សម្រាកជាផ្លូវការ), the period is extended until the first business day following the expiration of the period. Legal holidays (ថ្ងៃឈប់សម្រាក) falling within the period shall be included in the calculation.

**Article 58 (មាត្រា ៥៨).-**
The periods of time specified in this Chapter shall not include the day on which the period begins.

**Article 59 (មាត្រា ៥៩).-**
No days of grace (រយៈពេលអនុគ្រោះ), whether legal or judicial, are permitted.

# Chapter 3 (ជំពូកទី ៣)
# Bill of Exchange (រូបិយាណត្តិ)

## Section 1 (ផ្នែកទី ១)
## Issue and Form of a Bill of Exchange (ការចេញផ្សាយនិងទម្រង់នៃរូបិយាណត្តិ)

**Article 60 (មាត្រា ៦០).-**
A bill of exchange (រូបិយាណត្តិ) is an unconditional order in writing, issued by one person to another, signed by the person issuing it (drawer / បហាវីក), requiring the person to whom it is addressed (drawee / បហាវិត) to pay a certain sum of money to, or to the order of, a specified person or to bearer. A bill of exchange must be paid when due, in accordance with the obligations set forth in this Chapter.

**Article 61 (មាត្រា ៦១).-**
A bill of exchange (រូបិយាណត្តិ) is a credit instrument. A bill of exchange may be written in Khmer or in a foreign language and must contain the following particulars:

- The term "bill of exchange" (រូបិយាណត្តិ) inserted in the body of the instrument and expressed in the language employed in drawing the instrument;

- An unconditional order to pay a determinate sum of money;

- The name of the person who is to pay (drawee / បហាវិត);

- The maturity date (time of payment);

- The place where payment is to be made;

- The name of the person to whom or to whose order payment is to be made;

- The date and place where the bill is issued;

- The signature of the person who issues the bill (drawer / បហាវីក).

**Article 62 (មាត្រា ៦២).-**
An instrument in which any of the requirements mentioned in Article 61 of this Law is wanting is invalid as a bill of exchange, except in the cases specified in the following paragraphs:

1- A bill of exchange which does not specify the time of payment is deemed to be payable at sight.

2- In the absence of special mention, the place specified beside the name of the drawee (បហាវិត) is deemed to be the place of payment, and at the same time, the domicile of the drawee (បហាវិត).

3- A bill of exchange which does not specify the place of its issue is deemed to have been drawn in the place designated beside the name of the drawer (បហាវីក).

4- A bill of exchange may be issued payable to bearer. A bill of exchange which does not specify the name of the payee or the person to whose order it is payable is deemed to be a bill of exchange payable to bearer.

**Article 63 (មាត្រា ៦៣).-**
A bill of exchange may be drawn to the drawer's (បហាវីក) own order. A bill of exchange may be drawn on the drawer (បហាវីក) himself, or for the account of a third person.

**Article 64 (មាត្រា ៦៤).-**
A bill of exchange may be payable at the domicile of a third person, either in the locality where the drawee (បហាវិត) has his domicile or in another locality.

**Article 65 (មាត្រា ៦៥).-**
In a bill of exchange which is payable at sight or at a fixed period after sight, the drawer (បហាវីក) may stipulate

that the sum payable shall bear interest. In the case of any other bill of exchange, this stipulation is deemed to be unwritten.

The rate of interest must be specified in the bill. In default of such specification, the stipulation shall be deemed to be unwritten.

Interest runs from the date of issue of the bill of exchange, unless some other date is specified.

**Article 66 (មាត្រា ៦៦).-**
Where the sum payable by a bill of exchange is expressed in words and also in figures, and there is a discrepancy between the two, the sum expressed in words is the amount payable.

Where the sum payable by a bill of exchange is expressed more than once in words or more than once in figures, and there is a discrepancy between them, the smaller sum is the amount payable.

**Article 67 (មាត្រា ៦៧).-**
The obligations of other signers of a bill of exchange are nevertheless valid, even if the bill of exchange bears the signatures of persons incapable of binding themselves, or forged signatures, or signatures of fictitious persons, or signatures which for any other reason cannot bind the persons who signed the bill or on whose behalf it was signed.

**Article 68 (មាត្រា ៦៨).-**
Anyone who puts his signature on a bill of exchange on behalf of a person for whom he had no power to act is bound himself as a party to the bill and, if he pays, has the same rights as the person on whose behalf he purported to act. The same rule applies to an agent (អាណត្តិជន) who has exceeded his authority.

**Article 72.-**

An endorsement must be unconditional. Any condition to which it is made subject shall be deemed not to be written.

A partial endorsement is null and void.

An endorsement to bearer is equivalent to an endorsement in blank.

**Article 73.-**

An endorsement must be written on the bill of exchange or on a slip affixed thereto (allonge). It must be signed by the endorser.

An endorsement may leave the beneficiary unspecified or may consist simply of the signature of the endorser (endorsement in blank).

**Article 74.-**

An endorsement transfers all the rights arising out of a bill of exchange.

If the endorsement is in blank, the holder may:

- fill up the blank either with his own name or with the name of some other person;

- re-endorse the bill of exchange in blank, or to some other person;

- transfer the bill of exchange to a third person without filling up the blank, and without endorsing it.

**Article 75.-**

In the absence of any stipulation to the contrary, the endorser guarantees payment.

He may prohibit any further endorsement; in this case, he is not liable to guarantee payment to the persons to whom the bill of exchange is subsequently endorsed.

An endorsement on a bill of exchange payable to bearer makes the endorser liable in accordance with the provisions governing the right of recourse, but the bill of exchange payable to bearer cannot be converted into a bill of exchange payable to order.

**Article 76.-**

The possessor of an endorsable bill of exchange is deemed to be the lawful holder if he establishes his title to the bill of exchange through an uninterrupted series of endorsements, even if the last endorsement is in blank. In this connection, cancelled endorsements are deemed not to be written. Where an endorsement in blank is followed by another endorsement, the person who signed this last endorsement is deemed to have acquired the bill of exchange by the endorsement in blank.

Where a person has been dispossessed of a bill of exchange, by any event whatsoever, the holder who establishes his/her right to the bill of exchange in the manner mentioned in the first paragraph of this Article is not bound to give up the bill of exchange, unless he/she acquired it in bad faith or, in acquiring it, was guilty of gross negligence.

**Article 77.-**

Persons sued on a bill of exchange cannot set up against the holder defenses founded on their personal relations with the co-signatories or with previous holders, unless the holder, in acquiring the bill, has acted knowingly to the detriment of the debtor.

The first paragraph of this Article does not apply to a bill of exchange issued to pay a specified person, containing the words "not to order" or an equivalent expression.

**Article 78.-**

When an endorsement contains the statements "value in collection", "for collection", "by procuration" or any other phrase implying a simple mandate, the holder may exercise all rights arising out of the bill of exchange, but he/she can only endorse it in his/her capacity as an agent.

In this case, the parties liable can only set up against the holder defenses which could be set up against the endorser.

The mandate contained in an endorsement does not terminate by reason of the death of the mandator or his/her becoming legally incapable.

When a bill of exchange is presented to a bank for clearing, the bill of exchange is a payment order governed by Chapter 5 on Payment Transactions of this Law. In the event of inconsistency between the provisions of this Chapter and Chapter 5 of this Law, the provisions of this Chapter shall prevail.

**Article 79.-**

When an endorsement contains the statements "value in security", "value in pledge" or any other statement implying a pledge, the holder may exercise all rights arising out of the bill of exchange, but an endorsement made by him/her has the effects only of an endorsement by an agent.

The parties liable cannot set up against the holder defenses founded on their personal relations with the endorser, unless the holder, in acquiring the bill, has acted knowingly to the detriment of the debtor.

**Article 80.-**

An endorsement made after protest, or after the expiration of the limit of time fixed for presentment for payment, operates only as an ordinary transfer:

- it only has the effects of an ordinary transfer;

- it does not apply to the rights granted under Article 76 and Article 77 of this Law.

In the absence of proof to the contrary, an undated endorsement is deemed to have been made on the bill of exchange before the protest, or before the expiration of the limit of time mentioned in the first paragraph of this Article.

# Section 3
# Acceptance of a Bill of Exchange

**Article 81.-**

Until maturity, a bill of exchange may be presented to the drawee for acceptance at his domicile, either by the holder or by a person who is merely in possession of it.

**Article 82.-**

In any bill of exchange, the drawer may stipulate that it shall be presented for acceptance, with or without a limit of time.

The drawer may prohibit presentment for acceptance, except in the case of a bill of exchange payable at the address of a third party, or in a locality other than the domicile of the drawee, or except in the case of a bill of exchange payable at a certain time after sight.

The drawer may also stipulate that presentment for acceptance shall not take place before a specified date.

Every endorser may stipulate that the bill of exchange shall be presented for acceptance, with or without a limit of time, unless the drawer has prohibited acceptance.

**Article 83.-**

Bills of exchange payable at a certain time after sight must be presented for acceptance within one year of their date.

The drawer may abridge or extend this period.

These periods may be abridged by the endorsers.

**Article 84.-**

The drawee may demand that a bill shall be presented to him a second time on the day after the first presentment. Parties interested are not allowed to set up that this demand has not been complied with, unless this request is mentioned in the protest.

The holder is not bound to surrender to the drawee the bill presented for acceptance.

**Article 85.-**

An acceptance is written on the bill of exchange. It is expressed by the word "accepted" or any other equivalent term; it is signed by the drawee. The simple signature of the drawee on the face of the bill constitutes an acceptance.

When a bill is payable at a certain time after sight, or when it must be presented for acceptance within a certain limit of time in accordance with a special stipulation, the acceptance must be dated as of the day when the acceptance is given, unless the holder requires that it shall be dated as of the day of presentment. If the acceptance is undated,

undated, the holder, in order to preserve his right of recourse against the endorsers and the drawer, must certify this omission by a protest drawn up within the proper time.

**Article 86.-**

An acceptance is unconditional; however, the drawee may restrict it to a part of the sum payable.

Every other modification introduced by an acceptance into the tenor of the bill of exchange operates as a refusal to accept. Nevertheless, the acceptor is bound according to the terms of his acceptance.

**Article 87.-**

When the drawer of a bill of exchange has indicated a place of payment other than the domicile of the drawee, without specifying a third party at whose address payment must be made, the drawee may indicate such third party at the time of acceptance. In the absence of this indication, the acceptor is deemed to have undertaken to pay the bill of exchange himself at the place of payment.

If the bill of exchange is payable at the domicile of the drawee, the address of the place where payment is to be made may be specified in the acceptance.

**Article 88.-**

A bill of exchange does not of itself operate as an assignment of funds in the hands of the drawee available for the payment thereof, and a drawee who does not accept is not liable on the bill of exchange.

By accepting, the drawee undertakes to pay the bill of exchange at its maturity

maturity. In default of payment, the holder, even if he/she is the drawer, has a direct action on the bill of exchange against the acceptor for all that can be demanded in accordance with Article 106 and Article 107 of this Law.

**Article 89.-**

Where the drawee who has put his/her acceptance on a bill of exchange has cancelled it before restoring the bill, the acceptance is deemed to be refused. In the absence of proof to the contrary, the cancellation is deemed to have taken place before the bill was restored.

If the drawee has given notice of his/her acceptance in writing to the holder or to any party who has signed the bill, he/she is liable to them according to the terms of his/her acceptance.

# Section 4
# Guarantee of a Bill of Exchange (Aval)

**Article 90.-**

Payment of a bill of exchange may be guaranteed by an aval as to the whole or part of its amount.

This guarantee may be given by a third person or by a person who has signed the bill.

**Article 91.-**

An aval is given on the bill of exchange or on an allonge (slip).

It is expressed by the words "good as aval" or by any other equivalent formula. It is signed by the giver of the aval.

It is deemed to be constituted by the mere signature of the giver of the aval placed on the face of the bill of exchange, except in the case of the signature of the drawee or of the drawer.

An aval must specify for whose account it is given. In default of this, it is deemed to be given for the drawer.

**Article 92.-**

The giver of an aval is bound in the same manner as the person for whom he/she has become guarantor.

His/her undertaking is valid even when the liability which he/she has guaranteed is inoperative for any reason other than defect of form.

When he/she pays the bill of exchange, the giver of an aval has the rights arising out of the bill of exchange against the person guaranteed and against those who are liable to the latter on the bill of exchange.

# Section 5
# Maturity of a Bill of Exchange

**Article 93.-**

A bill of exchange may be drawn:

- at sight;

- at a fixed period after sight;

- at a fixed period after date; or

- at a fixed date.

A bill of exchange may be drawn payable by installments at fixed dates, provided that in case of default of payment of any installment, the balance of the sum payable shall become immediately due and payable.

Bills of exchange containing other maturities than those specified above shall be null and void.

**Article 94.-**

A bill of exchange at sight is payable on presentment. It must be presented for payment within a year of its date. The drawer may abridge or extend this period. These periods may be abridged by the endorsers.

The drawer may prescribe that a bill of exchange payable at sight shall not be presented for payment before a fixed date. In this case, the period for presentment begins from the said date.

**Article 95.-**

The maturity of a bill of exchange payable at a fixed period after sight is determined either by the date of the acceptance or by the date of the protest.

In the absence of protest, an undated acceptance is deemed to have been given to the holder on the last day of the limit of time available for presentment for acceptance.

**Article 96.-**

Where a bill of exchange is drawn at one or more months after date or after sight, the bill matures

at the corresponding date of the month when payment must be made. If there is no corresponding date, the bill matures on the last day of this month.

When a bill of exchange is drawn at one or more months and a-half after date or after sight, the entire months must first be calculated.

If the maturity is fixed at the commencement, in the middle (mid-month) or at the end of the month, the first, fifteenth or last day of the month is to be understood.

The expressions "eight days" or "fifteen days" indicate not one or two weeks, but a period of eight or fifteen actual days.

The expression "half-month" means a period of fifteen days.

**Article 97.-**

When a bill of exchange is payable on a fixed date in a place where the calendar is different from that in the place of issue, the date of maturity is determined according to the calendar of the place of payment.

When a bill of exchange drawn between two places having different calendars is payable at a fixed period after date, the day of issue is referred to the corresponding day of the calendar of the place of payment, and the maturity is fixed accordingly. The time for presenting the bill of exchange shall be calculated in accordance with this rule.

These rules do not apply if a stipulation or the terms of the bill indicate that a different rule is to be followed.

# Section 6 (ផ្នែកទី ៦)
# Payment (ការទូទាត់សងប្រាក់)

**Article 98.- (មាត្រា ៩៨.-)**
The holder of a negotiable instrument that is to be paid on a specified date, or within a specified period after date or after presentation, must present it for payment on the due date or on one of the two (02) business days immediately following.

Presentment of a negotiable instrument through a clearing house or through an electronic system, as provided in an agreement or regulation issued by the National Bank of Cambodia, constitutes formal presentment for payment.

**Article 99.- (មាត្រា ៩៩.-)**
A drawee who pays the value of a negotiable instrument may require that it be surrendered to him after the receipt of payment has been certified by the holder.

The holder may not refuse a partial payment of the amount of the negotiable instrument.

In case of a partial payment, the drawee may require that the partial payment be noted on the negotiable instrument and that a receipt for the payment be also given.

**Article 100.- (មាត្រា ១០០.-)**
When a negotiable instrument is not presented for payment within the period specified by Article 98 of this Law, the debtor is permitted to deposit the amount of money with the competent authority, in accordance with the regulations of the National Bank of Cambodia, at the expense and risk of the holder.

**Article 101.- (មាត្រា ១០១.-)**
The holder of a negotiable instrument cannot be compelled to accept payment before maturity.

A drawee who pays a negotiable instrument before maturity does so at his own risk.

A drawee who pays a negotiable instrument at maturity is validly discharged from his obligations, unless he has been guilty of fraud or gross negligence. The drawee is bound to verify the regularity of the series of endorsements, but not the signature of the endorsers.

**Article 102.- (មាត្រា ១០២.-)**
When a negotiable instrument is drawn payable in a currency which is not that of the place of payment, the amount of money may be paid in the currency of the country of the place of payment according to the exchange rate on the maturity date (ថ្ងៃណលប្រណិទាន). If the payment has not been made upon presentment, the holder may require that the payment of the amount of the negotiable instrument be made in the currency of the country of the place of payment according to the exchange rate on the maturity date (ថ្ងៃណលប្រណិទាន) or on the payment date at his own option.

The usages of the place of payment shall be applied to determine the value of the currency. However, the drawee may stipulate that the amount to be paid shall be calculated according to the exchange rate specified in the negotiable instrument.

The above rules shall not apply to negotiable instruments:
- which are issued in a currency that is accepted in the Kingdom of Cambodia
- in which the drawee has specified that payment must be made in a specific currency. In this case, the negotiable instrument must be paid in the specified currency.

If the amount of the negotiable instrument is specified in a currency having the same name but a different value in the country of payment and the country of issue or any other country, unless otherwise specified, the amount of money in

the negotiable instrument shall be deemed to be payable in the currency of the country of payment. When the currency of the country of payment does not have the same denomination, the payment shall be made in the currency which is widely used at the place of payment.

When a negotiable instrument is paid at a bank, the debiting or crediting of the account to be charged with the amount to be paid shall be deemed to be an authorization to pay in the currency of the account.

# Section 7 (ផ្នែកទី ៧)
# Recourse for Non-Acceptance or Non-Payment (ឧទាស័យចំពោះការមិនធ្វើសេចក្តីស័្មគ្រឬការមិនទូទាត់សងប្រាក់)

**Article 103.- (មាត្រា ១០៣.-)**
The holder of a negotiable instrument may exercise his right of recourse against the endorsers (អ្នកធ្វើបដិសរសេរទាំងឡាយ), the drawee/payor, and other parties liable:
1- At maturity, if payment has not been made, or
2- Even before maturity:
- If there has been a refusal of acceptance (សេចក្តីស័្មគ្រ) for the total or partial amount of the negotiable instrument.
- In case of the bankruptcy of the drawee (សហវិក), whether he has accepted or not, or in the event that the drawee is prohibited from making payments by a decision/order of the court, or when the implementation of measures to manage the assets of the drawee is unsuccessful.
- In case of the bankruptcy of the drawer (អ្នកសរសេរ) of a negotiable instrument which has not been accepted (ដែលមិនធ្វើសេចក្តីស័្មគ្រ).

**Article 104.- (មាត្រា ១០៤.-)**
Protest (វិរោធ) may be made under the following conditions:
1- A default of acceptance (សេចក្តីស័្មគ្រ) or of payment must be evidenced by a formal protest in accordance with the law.

2- Protest for non-acceptance (ការមិនធ្វើសេចក្តីស័្មគ្រ) must be made within the limit of time fixed for presentment for acceptance. If, in the case contemplated by Article 84 of this Law, the first presentment takes place on the last day of that period, the protest may nevertheless be made on the next day.

3- Protest for non-payment of a negotiable instrument (វិរោធចំពោះការមិនទូទាត់សងរូបិយណត្តិ) payable on a specified date or within a specified period after date or after presentation must be made on one of the two (02) business days immediately following the day on which the negotiable instrument is payable. In the case of a negotiable instrument payable on presentment, the protest must be made under the conditions specified in paragraph 2 above regarding protest for non-acceptance.

4- Protest for non-acceptance (វិរោធចំពោះការមិនធ្វើសេចក្តីស័្មគ្រ) dispenses with presentment for payment and protest for non-payment.

5- If the drawee (បហរិក/សហវិក) is prohibited from making payments, whether he has accepted (សេចក្តីស័្មគ្រ) or not, or if the implementation of measures to manage his assets is unsuccessful, the holder may exercise his right of recourse only after presentment of the negotiable instrument to the drawee for payment and after protest has been made.

6- If the drawee (បហរិក/សហវិក) is declared bankrupt, whether he has accepted (សេចក្តីការ) or not, or if the drawee of a negotiable instrument which has not been accepted is declared bankrupt, the production of the court decision/judgment declaring the bankruptcy is sufficient to enable the holder to exercise his right of recourse.

7- A protest (វិរោធ) must be signed and authenticated as an authentic document by a legally authorized officer. Until such a legally authorized officer is available, that officer must be appointed by the National Bank of Cambodia. The protest must be accompanied by a copy or the original of the negotiable instrument, and must clearly specify the person making the protest, the place and date, and the reasons for the protest.

the questions asked and the answers given, if any, or the fact that the drawee (សហវិក) or employee could not be found. The protest must be made at the place of refusal of payment or of non-acceptance (មិនធ្វើសេចក្តីការ), or at any other place in the Kingdom of Cambodia within a distance of not more than ten (10) kilometers from the place of presentment and of refusal of payment or of non-acceptance.

8- The protest may be made by a written declaration (ប្រកាសជាលាយលក្ខណ៍អក្សរ) signed by the drawee (សហវិក) clearly stating all information specified in paragraph 7 of this Article.

**Article 105.- (មាត្រា ១០៥.-)**
The holder must give notice of non-acceptance (ការមិនធ្វើសេចក្តីការ) or non-payment to his endorser (អ្នកធ្វើបដិសរសេរ), the drawer (បហរិក), and any other obligor (រូបិយបតិភោគី) liable on the instrument within four (04) business days following the day on which the protest is made, or, if there is a stipulation 'return without costs' (បង្វិលវិញដោយគ្មានសោហ៊ុយ) or 'without protest' (បង្វិលវិញដោយគ្មានវិរោធ) or other words of similar import, within the same period following the day of presentment for payment. Every endorser must within two (02) business days following the day on which he receives notice, inform his endorser of the notice he has received, specifying the names and addresses of those who have given the previous notices, in order, until the drawee (សហវិក) has received it. The periods specified above shall run from the receipt of the preceding notice.

In accordance with the above paragraph of this Article, when the notice is given to a person who has signed a negotiable instrument, the same notice must also be given to the obligor/guarantor (រូបិយបតិភោគី) within the same period.

Where an endorser has not clearly specified his address or has specified it unclearly, the notice given to the immediately preceding endorser (អ្នកធ្វើបដិសរសេរមុនបន្ទាប់) is sufficient.

A person who is required to give notice (បុគ្គលដែលត្រូវផ្តល់សេចក្តីជូនដំណឹង) may do so in any form, even by a simple return of the negotiable instrument.

The person who is required to give notice must prove that he has given the notice within the specified period. This period shall be deemed to have been observed if the letter containing the notice has been posted within the specified period.

A person who fails to give notice within the specified period is responsible for the damage, if any, caused by his negligence, but the amount of liability shall not exceed the amount of the negotiable instrument.

**Article 106.- (មាត្រា ១០៦.-)**
By a stipulation 'return without costs' (បង្វិលវិញដោយគ្មានសោហ៊ុយ) or 'without protest' (បង្វិលវិញដោយគ្មានវិរោធទាន) or any other equivalent expression, written and signed on the negotiable instrument, the drawer (បហរិក), an endorser (អ្នកធ្វើបដិសរសេរ), or a guarantor/obligor (រូបិយបដិភោគី) may release the holder from making a protest (ធ្វើវិរោធ) in order to exercise his right of recourse.

This stipulation does not release the holder from presenting the negotiable instrument within the specified period, or from the requirements of giving notices. The expenses incurred in proving non-compliance with the time limit by the holder shall be borne by the person who made the stipulation against the holder (បដិញ្ញាទាស់នឹងអ្នកកាន់).

If the stipulation is written by the drawer (បហរិក), it is applicable to all persons who have signed the negotiable instrument. If the stipulation is written by an endorser (អ្នកធ្វើបដិសរសេរ) or a guarantor/obligor (រូបិយបដិភោគី), it is applicable only to that endorser or guarantor/obligor. Even if the stipulation is written by the drawer, if the holder makes a protest, the holder must bear all expenses incurred. When the stipulation comes from an endorser or guarantor/obligor, the expenses of

The expenses of protest, if any, may be recovered from all persons who have signed the negotiable instrument.

**Article 107.- (មាត្រា ១០៧.-)**
All drawers (រាល់បហរិក), acceptors (អ្នកធ្វើសេចក្តីការ), endorsers (អ្នកធ្វើបដិសរសេរ), or guarantors (បុគ្គលធានា) of a negotiable instrument are jointly and severally liable to the holder.

The holder has the right of recourse (សិទ្ធិប្តឹងទាស់) against these persons, individually or collectively, without being required to observe the order in which they have become bound.

The same right belongs to any person who has signed the negotiable instrument and has taken it up (ទទួលយកនូវរូបិយណត្តិរក្សាទុក) and paid it.

Proceedings against one of the parties liable do not prevent proceedings against the others, even though they may be subsequent to the party first proceeded against.

**Article 108.- (មាត្រា ១០៨.-)**
The holder may recover from the person against whom he exercises his right of recourse (អនុវត្តសិទ្ធិឧទាស័យ): the amount of the negotiable instrument which has not been accepted (មិនបានធ្វើសេចក្តីការ) or paid, with interest if interest was specified in the negotiable instrument; interest calculated at the rate set by the National Bank of Cambodia from the date of maturity (ថ្ងៃដល់កំណត់ប្រតិទាន); and all expenses incurred.

If the right of recourse is exercised before maturity, the amount of the negotiable instrument shall be subject to a discount (អប្បហារ). This discount shall be calculated according to the official bank discount rate on the date when recourse is exercised at the place of residence of the holder.

**Article 109.- (មាត្រា ១០៩.-)**
A party who has taken up and paid a negotiable instrument may recover from the parties liable to him: the total amount he has paid; interest

on the amount paid, calculated at the rate set by the National Bank of Cambodia from the day on which the payment was made, and all expenses incurred.

**Article 110.- (មាត្រា ១១០.-)**
Any party liable against whom the right of recourse is exercised (ទទួលរងការអនុវត្តសិទ្ធិឧទាស័យ) may require, against payment, the surrender of the negotiable instrument with the protest and a receipt.

Every endorser (អ្នកធ្វើបដិសរសេរ) who has taken up and paid a negotiable instrument may cancel his endorsement and those of subsequent endorsers.

**Article 111.- (មាត្រា ១១១.-)**
In the case of the exercise of the right of recourse after a partial acceptance (ការធ្វើសេចក្តីការតែលើមួយផ្នែក), the party who pays the amount for which the negotiable instrument has not been accepted may require that this payment be specified on the negotiable instrument and that a receipt be given to him. The holder must also give him a certified copy of the negotiable instrument and the protest, in order to enable him to exercise subsequent recourse.

**Article 112.- (មាត្រា ១១២.-)**
Unless there is an agreement to the contrary, any person who has the right of recourse may pay himself by means of a new negotiable instrument (a redraft) drawn at sight on one of the parties liable to him and payable at the place of residence of that party.

In addition to the amounts specified in Article 108 and Article 109 of this Law, the redraft includes brokerage (កម្រៃជើងសា) and stamp duty (ថ្លៃតែមប្រី).

If the redraft is drawn by the holder, the amount payable is determined according to the rate for a sight negotiable instrument at the place where the original negotiable instrument was payable, drawn at the place of residence of the party liable

liable. If the redraft is drawn by an endorser (អ្នកធ្វើបដិសរសេរ), the amount payable is determined according to the rate for a sight negotiable instrument at the place where the drawer (បហរិក) of the redraft has his residence, at the place of residence of the party liable.

**Article 113.- (មាត្រា ១១៣.-)**
After the expiration of the limits of time fixed:
- for the presentment of a negotiable instrument payable at sight or at a fixed period after sight;
- for the drawing up of the protest for non-acceptance (មិនធ្វើសេចក្តីការ) or non-payment;
- for presentment for payment in the case of a stipulation 'return without costs' (បង្វិលវិញដោយគ្មានសោហ៊ុយ) or 'without protest' (បង្វិលវិញដោយគ្មានវិរោធ) or other words of similar import;
the holder loses his right of recourse (សិទ្ធិធ្វើឧទាស័យ) against the endorsers (អ្នកធ្វើបដិសរសេរទាំងឡាយ), the drawer (បហរិក), and the other parties liable, with the exception of the right of recourse against the acceptor (អ្នកធ្វើសេចក្តីការ).

In default of presentment for acceptance (សេចក្តីការ) within the limit of time specified by the drawer (បហរិក), the holder loses his right of recourse for non-payment as well as for non-acceptance, unless it appears from the terms of the stipulation that the drawer intended to release himself only from the guarantee of acceptance.

If the stipulation as to the limit of time for presentment is contained in an endorsement, its effects are applicable only to the endorser who made it.

A holder who has lost his right of recourse under this Article may sue the drawer (បហរិក) under the specific provisions governing contracts. Any recovery obtained under this paragraph shall be set off against the damages caused by the holder to the drawer as a result of the failure to observe the limits of time.

**Article 114.- (មាត្រា ១១៤.-)**
When the presentment of the negotiable instrument or the drawing up of the protest within the specified time limits is prevented by an insurmountable obstacle (force majeure - ករណីប្រធានស័ក្តិ), these time limits shall be extended.

The holder is bound to give notice without delay of the case of force majeure to his endorser and to specify this notice, dated and signed, on the negotiable instrument or on an allonge (អនុប័ត្រ). For other matters, the provisions of Article 105 of this Law shall apply.

When the force majeure has terminated, the holder must present the negotiable instrument for acceptance or payment without delay, and, if necessary, draw up the protest.

If the force majeure continues to exist for more than thirty (30) days after maturity, recourse may be exercised, and neither presentment for payment nor the drawing up of a protest shall be necessary.

For negotiable instruments payable at sight or at a fixed period after sight, the thirty (30) days limit shall run from the date on which the holder has given notice of the force majeure to his endorser, even if this is done before the expiration of the time limit for presentment. For negotiable instruments payable at a fixed period after sight, the thirty (30) days limit shall be added to the period after sight specified in the negotiable instrument.

Facts which are purely personal to the holder or to the person whom he has entrusted with the presentment of the negotiable instrument or the drawing up of the protest (ធ្វើវិរុទ្ធរាង) shall not be deemed to be cases of force majeure.

Liability on a lost or destroyed negotiable instrument may be enforced only if a guarantee is given by the transferring party (ភាគីអនុប្បទាន) of the negotiable instrument which was damaged/lost to the parties liable, against claims by any other person on the negotiable instrument in accordance with

decision of the court, if the holder has received the bill of exchange in good faith or without gross negligence.

### Part 8
### Intervention for Payment

#### Article 115._
The drawer, an endorser or an avalist may specify a person who is to accept or pay in case of need.

A bill of exchange may be accepted or paid by a person who intervenes for the honor of any party liable to be sued.

The person who intervenes may be a third party, the drawee, or a party already liable on the bill of exchange, except the acceptor.

Within two (02) working days, the person who intervenes must give notice of his intervention to the party for whose honor he has intervened. If he fails to comply with this time limit, the person who intervenes shall be liable for any damage caused by his negligence, but the compensation for damage shall not exceed the amount of the bill of exchange.

#### Article 116._
Acceptance by intervention may take place in all cases where the holder has a right of recourse before maturity on a bill of exchange which is capable of acceptance.

Where the bill of exchange specifies a person who is to accept or pay in case of need at the place of payment, the holder may not exercise his rights of recourse before maturity against the person designated and against subsequent endorsers, unless the holder has presented

the bill of exchange to that person, and such presentation must be evidenced by a protest for non-acceptance by the same person.

In other cases of intervention, the holder may refuse acceptance by intervention. Conversely, if he admits such acceptance, he loses his right of recourse before maturity against the person who has given the acceptance and against subsequent endorsers.

### Article 117._
Acceptance by intervention shall be written on the bill of exchange and signed by the person who intervenes. It shall specify the person for whose honor the acceptance is given; in default of this specification, the acceptance is deemed to have been given for the drawer.

### Article 118._
The acceptor by intervention is liable to the holder and to subsequent endorsers of the party for whose honor he has intervened, in the same manner as such party.

Notwithstanding acceptance by intervention, the party for whose honor it has been accepted and subsequent endorsers may require the holder, on paying the amount, to deliver to them the bill of exchange, the protest, and a receipted account, if any, in order to adjust the payment of the amount as specified in Article 108 of this Law.

### Article 119._
Payment by intervention may take place in all cases where the holder has a right of recourse on the bill of exchange at maturity or before maturity.

Payment by intervention must include the whole amount payable by the party for whose honor it is made.

Payment must be made at the latest on the day following the last day on which a protest for non-payment is allowed.

### Article 120._
If the bill of exchange has been accepted by intervention by a person who has his domicile in the place of payment, or if persons having their domicile in the same place have been designated as referees in case of need, the holder must present the bill of exchange to all such persons and, if necessary, cause a protest for non-payment to be made at the latest on the day following the last day on which the protest is allowed.

In default of protest within this time limit, the party who has designated the referee in case of need, or the party for whose honor the bill of exchange has been accepted, and subsequent endorsers, shall be discharged from liability.

### Article 121._
A holder who refuses payment by intervention loses his right of recourse against those who would have been discharged.

### Article 122._
Payment by intervention must be receipted on the bill of exchange, indicating the name of the person for whose honor the payment is made. In default of this indication, the payment is deemed to have been made for the drawer.

The bill of exchange and the protest, if any, must be delivered to the person paying by intervention.

### Article 123._
The person paying by intervention is subrogated to the rights of the holder against the party for whose honor he has paid and against those who are liable to the latter on the bill of exchange. However, he may not endorse the bill of exchange again.

Endorsers subsequent to the party for whose honor payment has been made shall be discharged from liability.

In case of competition for payment by intervention, the payment which effects the greatest number of releases has preference. Any person who intervenes in a manner contrary to this rule, with knowledge of the facts, shall lose his right of recourse against those who would have been discharged.

### Part 9
### Parts of a Set and Copies of a Bill of Exchange

### Article 124._
Where a bill of exchange is drawn in two or more identical parts, each part must be numbered and must refer to the other parts, all parts together constituting a single bill of exchange.

Acceptance and each copy on a bill of exchange drawn in a set may be written on any part, but they relate to the entire bill of exchange and not to any single part individually. Such acceptance or copy must be written on one part only.

The drawer who signs each part is liable on the entire bill of exchange, and not on each part individually.

A party who has sent one part for acceptance must indicate on the other parts the name of the person in whose hands this part is to be found. That person is bound to deliver it to the lawful holder of another part. If he refuses, the holder cannot exercise his right of recourse unless he has a protest drawn up establishing:

- That the part sent for acceptance was not delivered to him on demand

- That acceptance or payment could not be obtained on another part.

### Article 125._
Where the holder of a bill of exchange drawn in a set endorses two or more parts to different persons:

- The holder is liable on all the parts, and each endorser subsequent to the holder is liable on the part he has endorsed as if it were a separate bill of exchange

- The lawful holder of any part has a right to claim the payment made by the drawer to any person, or from the drawer who has not accepted the bill of exchange.

Where the drawer accepts two or more parts, and the parts so accepted come into the hands of different lawful holders, the drawer is liable on all such parts as if they were separate bills of exchange.

### Article 126._
Where the acceptor of a bill of exchange drawn in a set pays it without requiring the part bearing his acceptance to be delivered up to him, and that part is in the hands of a lawful holder at maturity, the acceptor is liable to such holder.

The drawer of a bill of exchange drawn in a set is discharged from liability when he pays any one of the parts presented to him.

Subject to the provisions of Article 125 and Article 126 of this Law, where one part of a bill of exchange is discharged by payment or otherwise, the whole bill of exchange is discharged.

### Article 127._
The holder has the right to make copies of the bill of exchange.

The copy must be a faithful reproduction of the original with the endorsements and all other information contained therein. It must specify where the copy ends.

The copy may be endorsed and guaranteed by an aval in the same manner and with the same effects as the original.

### Article 128._
The copy must specify the person who holds the original. The holder of the original must deliver the original to the lawful holder of the copy.

If the holder of the original refuses, the holder of the copy may exercise his right of recourse against the persons who have endorsed or guaranteed the copy by an aval, unless there is a protest stating that the original has not been delivered on request.

If there is a clause written on the original to the effect that 'henceforth an endorsement on the copy is valid only' or any other equivalent clause, any endorsement signed on the original subsequent to that clause is void.

### Part 10
### Alteration of a Bill of Exchange

### Article 129._
In case of alteration of the text of a bill of exchange, parties who have signed subsequent to the alteration are bound according to the terms of the altered text; parties who have signed before the alteration are bound according to the terms of the original text.

### Part 11
### Limitation of Actions

### Article 130._
All actions arising out of a bill of exchange against the acceptor are barred after three (03) years from the date of maturity.

Actions by the holder against the endorsers and the drawer are barred after one (01) year from the date of a protest drawn up within the proper time limit, or from the date of maturity where there is a clause 'return without costs' or 'without protest' or any other equivalent clause.

Actions by an endorser against other endorsers and against the drawer are barred after six (06) months from the day on which the endorser took up and paid the bill of exchange, or from the day on which he himself was sued.

### Article 131._
Interruption of the limitation of action is only effective against the person in respect of whom the period was interrupted.

### Part 12
### General Provisions

### Article 132._
Where a bill of exchange matures on a legal holiday, payment must be made on the next working day. All actions relating to a bill of exchange, in particular presentation for acceptance and protest, may only be made on a working day.

Where any such action must be taken within a certain period of time, and the last day of this period is a legal holiday, the period is extended until the first following working day. Holidays are included in the computation of the period.

### Article 133._
The day from which a period of time begins to run is not included in the period.

### Article 134._
No days of grace, whether legal or judicial, are permitted.

# Chapter 4
# Promissory Notes

### Part 1
### Issue and Form of a Promissory Note

### Article 135._
A promissory note is an unconditional promise in writing made and signed by a person as the maker, promising to pay a sum of money

certain sum, to, or to the order of, a specified person or to bearer. The promissory note must be paid at maturity, in accordance with the obligations set forth in this Chapter.

### Article 136._
A promissory note is a credit instrument. A promissory note may be written in Khmer or in a foreign language and must contain the following particulars:

- The term 'promissory note' inserted in the body of the instrument and expressed in the language employed in drawing up the instrument

- An unconditional promise to pay a determinate sum of money

- An indication of the time of payment

- An indication of the place where payment is to be made

- The name of the person to whom or to whose order payment is to be made

- An indication of the date and of the place where the promissory note is issued

- The signature of the person who issues the instrument (maker) who makes the promise on the promissory note.

### Article 137._
An instrument in which any of the requirements mentioned in Article 136 of this Law is wanting is invalid as a promissory note, except in the cases specified in the following paragraphs:

1- A promissory note which does not specify the time of payment is deemed to be payable at sight.

2- In default of special mention, the place where the instrument is issued is deemed to be the place of payment and, at the same time, the place of the domicile of the maker.

3- A promissory note which does not specify the place of its issue is deemed to have been made in the place designated beside the name of the maker.

4- A promissory note may be made payable to bearer. A promissory note which does not specify the name of the person to whom or to whose order payment is to be made is deemed to be payable to bearer.

### Article 138._
A promissory note may be payable at the domicile of a third person, or in the locality where the maker has his domicile, or in another locality.

### Article 139._
When a promissory note is payable at sight or at a fixed period after sight, the maker may stipulate that the sum payable shall bear interest. In the case of any other promissory note, this stipulation is deemed to be unwritten.

The rate of interest must be indicated in the promissory note; in default of this, the stipulation is deemed to be unwritten.

Interest runs from the date of issue of the promissory note, unless some other date is specified.

### Article 140._
Where the sum payable by a promissory note is expressed in words and also in figures, and there is a discrepancy between the two, the sum expressed in

the sum expressed by the words shall be the amount payable (តួអក្សរជាចំនួនដែលត្រូវយកជាបានការ) ។

In the event that the sum payable by a promissory note is written multiple times in words or in figures, and when there is a discrepancy, the smallest sum shall be the amount payable (ក្នុងករណីដែលចំនួនប្រាក់ត្រូវទូទាត់សងដោយប័ណ្ណសន្យាតាមបញ្ជា ត្រូវបានសរសេរច្រើនដងជាតួអក្សរ ឬ ជាតួរលេខ និងកាលបើមានចំនួនលំអៀងគ្នា ចំនួនប្រាក់ដែលតូចជាងគេជាចំនួនត្រូវយកជាបានការ) ។

**Article 141.- (មាត្រា ១៤១.-)**

Every obligation of any other person who signed a promissory note shall remain valid, even if the promissory note bears the signature of an incapacitated person, or a forged signature, or the signature of a fictitious person, or a signature which for any other reason cannot make the person who signed or on whose behalf the note was signed liable (រាល់កាតព្វកិច្ចនៃបុគ្គលដទៃទៀត ដែលបានចុះហត្ថលេខាលើប័ណ្ណសន្យាតាមបញ្ជា នៅតែមានតម្លៃដដែល ទោះបីប័ណ្ណសន្យាតាមបញ្ជានោះមានហត្ថលេខារបស់បុគ្គលអសមត្ថភាព ឬ ហត្ថលេខាក្លែងបន្លំ ឬ ហត្ថលេខារបស់បុគ្គលតាមការប្រឌិត ឬ ហត្ថលេខាដែលតាមហេតុផលផ្សេងទៀត មិនអាចធ្វើឱ្យបុគ្គលដែលបានចុះហត្ថលេខា ឬ បានចុះហត្ថលេខាជំនួសមានការទទួលខុសត្រូវ) ។

**Article 142.- (មាត្រា ១៤២.-)**

Any person who signed a promissory note on behalf of another person whom he has no authority at all to represent shall be personally liable as a party to the promissory note, and if he has paid, he shall have the same rights as the person whom he undertook to pay. The same rule shall also apply to a guardian who has acted beyond his authority (បុគ្គលណាដែលបានចុះហត្ថលេខានៅលើប័ណ្ណសន្យាតាមបញ្ជា ក្នុងនាមបុគ្គលមួយទៀតដែលខ្លួនគ្មានសិទ្ធិតំណាងឱ្យទាល់តែសោះ ត្រូវមានការពាក់ព័ន្ធផ្ទាល់ជាភាគីមួយលើប័ណ្ណសន្យាតាមបញ្ជានោះ ហើយបើខ្លួនបានទូទាត់សងប្រាក់ ត្រូវមានសិទ្ធិដូចគ្នានឹងបុគ្គលដែលខ្លួនបានទទួលភារៈបង់រំលោះសងឱ្យ ។ វិធានដូចគ្នានេះក៏អនុវត្តទៅលើអាណាព្យាបាល ដែលបានអនុវត្តហួសសិទ្ធិរបស់ខ្លួនផងដែរ) ។

**Article 143.- (មាត្រា ១៤៣.-)**

If a promissory note was incomplete when issued and has been completed in a manner other than in accordance with the relevant agreement, non-compliance with this agreement cannot be set up as a defense against the holder, unless the holder received the promissory note in bad faith or with gross negligence (បើប័ណ្ណសន្យាតាមបញ្ជាមានលក្ខណៈមិនពេញលេញនៅពេលចេញផ្សាយ ហើយត្រូវបានធ្វើឱ្យពេញលក្ខណៈតាមបែបផ្សេងខុសពីការព្រមព្រៀងដែលពាក់ព័ន្ធ ការមិនគោរពតាមការព្រមព្រៀងនេះ មិនអាចយកជាមូលហេតុប្ដឹងអ្នកកាន់បានឡើយ លើកលែងតែអ្នកកាន់នេះបានទទួលប័ណ្ណសន្យាតាមបញ្ជាមកដោយទុច្ចរិត ឬ មានកំហុសដោយការធ្វេសប្រហែសធ្ងន់ធ្ងរ) ។

# Section 2 (ផ្នែកទី ២)

## Endorsement of Bill of Exchange (វិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា)

**Article 144.- (មាត្រា ១៤៤.-)**

A bill of exchange issued payable to a specified person with or without the express words "to order" may be transferred by endorsement (ប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា ដែលចេញឱ្យទូទាត់សងចំពោះបុគ្គលណាមួយច្បាស់លាស់ ដោយមាន ឬ គ្មានបញ្ជាក់ពាក្យថា សងតាមបញ្ជា អាចត្រូវបានផ្ទេរដោយវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើ) ៕

When the drawer has inserted in a bill of exchange issued payable to a specified person the words "not to order" or an equivalent expression, the bill of exchange may be transferred only in the form and with the effects of an ordinary assignment (នៅពេលដែលអ្នកចេញប័ណ្ណបានបញ្ចូលក្នុងប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា ដែលចេញ ដើម្បីទូទាត់សងប្រាក់ចំពោះបុគ្គលណាមួយច្បាស់លាស់នូវពាក្យ មិនសងតាមបញ្ជា ឬ ពាក្យដែលមានន័យដូចគ្នា ប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជានោះអាចត្រូវបានផ្ទេរស្របតាមទម្រង់ និងមានសុពលភាព ជាការផ្ទេរជាធម្មតាតែប៉ុណ្ណោះ) ៕

A bill of exchange may be endorsed even to the drawer or to any other party to the bill of exchange. These persons may endorse the bill of exchange further (ប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាអាចត្រូវបានធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើ ទោះបីឱ្យទៅអ្នកចេញប័ណ្ណក្តី ឬ ឱ្យទៅភាគីណាមួយផ្សេងទៀតនៃប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជានោះក្តី ៕ បុគ្គលទាំងនេះអាចធ្វើ វិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជានោះបន្តទៀតបាន) ៕

An instrument in the form of a bill of exchange which is payable to the order of the drawer is not a bill of exchange under this chapter, unless it is endorsed by the drawer (ឧបករណ៍ដែលមានទម្រង់ជាប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា ដែលអាចទូទាត់សងបានតាម បញ្ជាអ្នកចេញប័ណ្ណ មិនមែនជាប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាក្រោមជំពូកនេះឡើយ លើកលែង តែប័ណ្ណនេះត្រូវបានធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើដោយអ្នកចេញប័ណ្ណ) ៕

**Article 145.- (មាត្រា ១៤៥.-)**

An endorsement must be unconditional. Any condition to which it is made subject shall be deemed not written (វិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើត្រូវតែបានធ្វើឡើងដោយឥតលក្ខខណ្ឌ ៕ ល័ក្ខខ័ណ្ឌណាមួយដែលត្រូវ បានដាក់ចេញជាកម្រិតចំពោះការធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា មិនត្រូវយកជាបាន ការឡើយ) ៕

A partial endorsement of the sum payable by a bill of exchange shall be void (វិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើមួយផ្នែកនៃចំនួនប្រាក់ក្នុងប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា ត្រូវចាត់ទុកជា មោឃៈ) ៕

An endorsement "to bearer" is equivalent to a blank endorsement (វិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើដែលធ្វើឱ្យ អ្នកកាន់ មានតម្លៃស្មើនឹងវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើទទេ) ៕

**Article 146.- (មាត្រា ១៤៦.-)**

An endorsement must be written on the bill of exchange or on a slip affixed thereto (allonge). It must be signed by the endorser (វិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើត្រូវតែបានធ្វើជាលាយលក្ខណ៍អក្សរ នៅលើខ្នងប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា ឬ នៅលើក្រដាសបិទភ្ជាប់បន្ថែមហៅថា អនុព័ន្ធ ហើយត្រូវតែបានចុះហត្ថលេខាដោយអ្នកធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើ) ៕

An endorsement may leave the endorsee unspecified or it may consist simply of the signature of the endorser (blank endorsement) (វិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើ អាចមិនបញ្ជាក់ពីអត្ថគាហក ឬ អាចមានតែហត្ថលេខារបស់អ្នកធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើប៉ុណ្ណោះហៅថា វិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើទទេ) ៕

**Article 147.- (មាត្រា ១៤៧.-)**

An endorsement transfers all the rights arising out of a bill of exchange (វិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើ ផ្ទេរសិទ្ធិទាំងអស់នៃប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាឱ្យទៅអ្នកកាន់) ៕

If the endorsement is in blank, the holder may: (បើជាវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើទទេ អ្នកកាន់អាច ៖)
- Fill up the blank either with his own name or with the name of some other person (- បំពេញកន្លែងទទេ ឬ ដោយឈ្មោះរបស់ខ្លួនផ្ទាល់ ឬ ដោយឈ្មោះរបស់បុគ្គលដទៃទៀត)
- Endorse the bill of exchange in blank or to some other person (- ធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើបន្តប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាជាចំហ ឬ ឱ្យបុគ្គលដទៃទៀត)
- Transfer the bill of exchange to a third person without filling up the blank and without endorsing it (- ផ្ទេរប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាឱ្យទៅតីយបុគ្គល ដោយមិនចាំបាច់បំពេញកន្លែងទទេ និងមិនចាំបាច់ធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើ) ៕

**Article 148.- (មាត្រា ១៤៨.-)**

In the absence of any contrary stipulation, the endorser guarantees payment (បើគ្មានបដិញ្ញាផ្ទុយទេ អ្នកធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើត្រូវទទួលខុសត្រូវលើការទូទាត់សងប្រាក់) ៕

He may prohibit any further endorsement; in this case, he does not guarantee payment to the persons to whom the bill of exchange is subsequently endorsed (អ្នកធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើអាចហាមឃាត់ការធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើណាមួយបន្តទៀត ហើយក្នុងករណីនេះ បុគ្គលដែលបានហាមឃាត់ មិនទទួលខុសត្រូវទូទាត់សងប្រាក់ទៅបុគ្គលទាំងឡាយណា ដែលប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាត្រូវបានធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើបន្តឱ្យទៅនោះឡើយ) ៕

An endorsement to bearer on a bill of exchange makes the endorser liable in accordance with the provisions on the right of recourse, but that bill of exchange cannot be converted into a bill of exchange payable to bearer (វិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើលើប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ញាចំពោះអ្នកកាន់ នាំឱ្យអ្នកធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើមានការទទួលខុសត្រូវស្របតាមបទប្បញ្ញត្តិស្តីពីសិទ្ធិធ្វើតុល្យវិធិយ ប៉ុន្តែប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជានោះ មិនអាចប្រែក្លាយទៅជាប័ណ្ណសងចំពោះអ្នកកាន់ ដើម្បីសងចំពោះអ្នកកាន់ឡើយ) ៕

**Article 149.- (មាត្រា ១៤៩.-)**

The owner of a bill of exchange who has the right to endorse it is the lawful holder (ម្ចាស់ប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាដែលអាចធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើបាន ជាអ្នកកាន់ស្របច្បាប់)

if the latter establishes his title to the bill through an uninterrupted series of endorsements, even if the last endorsement is in blank. In this connection, cancelled endorsements shall be deemed not to have been written. Where an endorsement in blank is followed by another endorsement, the person who signed this last endorsement shall be deemed to have acquired the bill by the endorsement in blank (បើបុគ្គលនេះ បានក្លាយជាម្ចាស់ប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា តាមរយៈការធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើជាបន្តបន្ទាប់ ទោះបីវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើចុងក្រោយជាវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើទទេក៏ដោយ ៕ ក្នុងករណីនេះ វិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើដែលលុបចោលមិនត្រូវយកជាបានការឡើយ ៕ កាលបើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើទទេត្រូវបានបន្តដោយវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើមួយទៀត បុគ្គលដែលចុះហត្ថលេខាធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើចុងក្រោយនេះ ត្រូវបានចាត់ទុកថាបានទទួលប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា តាមរយៈវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើទទេ) ៕

Where a person has been dispossessed of a bill of exchange, by any event whatsoever, whether it is a bill payable to bearer or an endorsable bill to which the holder establishes his right in the manner mentioned in the preceding paragraph, the holder who has acquired the bill is not bound to give up the bill, unless he has acquired it in bad faith or has been guilty of gross negligence (នៅពេលដែលបុគ្គលណាមួយបានបាត់បង់ការកាន់កាប់ប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា ដោយមូលហេតុណាក៏ដោយ ទោះបីជាប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាចេញឱ្យទៅអ្នកកាន់ក្តី ឬ អាចធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើបានក្តី ដែលអ្នកកាន់មានសិទ្ធិជាម្ចាស់ដូចមានចែងនៅក្នុងកថាខណ្ឌខាងលើនៃមាត្រានេះ អ្នកកាន់ដែលទទួលបានប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាមកជាកម្មសិទ្ធិ មិនត្រូវបានបង្ខំឱ្យបោះបង់ចោលប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជានោះឡើយ លើកលែងតែប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជានោះ បានមកដោយទុច្ចរិត ឬ ដោយមានកំហុសពីការធ្វេសប្រហែសធ្ងន់ធ្ងរ) ៕

**Article 150.- (មាត្រា ១៥០.-)**

Persons sued on a bill of exchange cannot set up against the holder defenses founded on their personal relations with the drawer or with previous holders, unless the holder, in acquiring the bill, has knowingly acted to the detriment of the debtor (បុគ្គលទាំងឡាយដែលត្រូវប្តឹងអំពីប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា មិនអាចតតាំងអ្នកកាន់ដោយសំអាងលើទំនាក់ទំនងផ្ទាល់ខ្លួនជាមួយអ្នកគូរ ឬ ជាមួយអ្នកកាន់មុនៗបានឡើយ លើកលែងតែក្នុងការទទួលប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា អ្នកកាន់បានដឹងច្បាស់ថានឹងនាំឱ្យខូចប្រយោជន៍ដល់កូនបំណុល) ៕

The preceding paragraph of this Article does not apply to a bill of exchange issued payable to a specified person containing the words "not to order" or an equivalent expression (កថាខណ្ឌខាងលើនៃមាត្រានេះ មិនអនុវត្តចំពោះប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាដែលចេញ ដើម្បីទូទាត់សងទៅបុគ្គលណាមួយច្បាស់លាស់ ដែលក្នុងនោះមានពាក្យថា មិនសងតាមបញ្ជា ឬ ប្រយោគណាមួយដែលមានន័យដូចគ្នា) ៕

**Article 151.- (មាត្រា ១៥១.-)**

Where an endorsement contains the words "value in collection", "for collection", "by procuration" or any other phrase implying a simple mandate, the holder may exercise all rights arising out of the bill of exchange, but he can only endorse it in the capacity of an agent (កាលបើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើមានពាក្យថា តម្លៃសម្រាប់ទារបំណុល ឬ សម្រាប់បណ្តារក្សីយ ឬ តាមការប្រគល់សិទ្ធិ ឬ ប្រយោគណាមួយផ្សេងទៀតដែលបញ្ជាក់ពីការប្រគល់សិទ្ធិសាមញ្ញ អ្នកកាន់អាចអនុវត្តសិទ្ធិទាំងអស់នៃប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា ប៉ុន្តែអ្នកកាន់នេះអាចធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើបន្តក្នុងនាមជាភ្នាក់ងារតែប៉ុណ្ណោះ) ៕

In this case, the parties liable may set up defenses against the holder, and the holder may also set up defenses against the endorser (ក្នុងករណីនេះ ភាគីពាក់ព័ន្ធទាំងឡាយអាចតតាំងអ្នកកាន់បាន ហើយអ្នកកាន់នេះ ក៏អាចតតាំងអ្នកធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើបានផងដែរ) ៕

A mandate contained in an endorsement does not terminate by reason of the death of the party giving the mandate or of his becoming incapacitated (ការប្រគល់សិទ្ធិដែលមាននៅក្នុងវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើ មិនអាចរលត់បានដោយហេតុផលនៃមរណភាពរបស់ភាគីដែលបានផ្តល់ការប្រគល់សិទ្ធិ ឬ ដោយហេតុផលដែលបុគ្គលនេះបានក្លាយជាអសមត្ថភាពនោះឡើយ) ៕

When a bill of exchange is presented to a bank for clearing, that bill of exchange is a payment order governed by Chapter 4 on payment operations of this Law. In the event of inconsistency regarding the bill of exchange between the provisions of this Chapter and Chapter 4 of this Law, the provisions of this Chapter shall prevail (នៅពេលដែលប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា ត្រូវបានបង្ហាញឱ្យធនាគារធ្វើបេឡាកិច្ច ប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជានោះជាបញ្ជាទូទាត់ដែលគ្រប់គ្រងដោយជំពូកទី ៤ ស្តីពីប្រតិបត្តិការទូទាត់សងប្រាក់នៃច្បាប់នេះ ៕ ក្នុងករណីមិនមានសង្គតិភាពអំពីប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជារវាងបទប្បញ្ញត្តិនៃជំពូកនេះ និងជំពូកទី ៤ នៃច្បាប់នេះ បទប្បញ្ញត្តិនៃជំពូកនេះ ត្រូវយកជាបានការ) ៕

**Article 152.- (មាត្រា ១៥២.-)**

Where an endorsement contains the statement "value in security", "value in pledge", or any other statement implying a pledge, the holder may exercise all rights arising out of the bill of exchange, but an endorsement by him has only the effects of an endorsement in the capacity of an agent (កាលបើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើមួយមានសេចក្តីបញ្ជាក់ថា តម្លៃសម្រាប់ធានា ឬ តម្លៃសម្រាប់ដាក់បញ្ចាំ ឬ សេចក្តីបញ្ជាក់ណាមួយផ្សេងទៀតដែលមានន័យថាសម្រាប់ដាក់បញ្ចាំ អ្នកកាន់អាចអនុវត្តសិទ្ធិទាំងអស់នៃប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជានោះ ប៉ុន្តែវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើដែលខ្លួនបានធ្វើឡើង មានប្រសិទ្ធិភាពត្រឹមតែជាវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើដោយភ្នាក់ងារតែប៉ុណ្ណោះ) ៕

The parties liable cannot set up against the holder defenses founded on their personal relations with the endorser, unless the holder, in receiving the bill, has knowingly acted to the detriment of the debtor (បណ្តាភាគីដែលពាក់ព័ន្ធមិនអាចតតាំងអ្នកកាន់បាន ដោយសំអាងលើទំនាក់ទំនងផ្ទាល់របស់ខ្លួនជាមួយអ្នកធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើឡើយ លើកលែងតែក្នុងការទទួលប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា អ្នកកាន់បានដឹងច្បាស់ថានឹងនាំឱ្យខូចប្រយោជន៍ដល់កូនបំណុល) ៕

**Article 153.- (មាត្រា ១៥៣.-)**

An endorsement after maturity has the same effects as an endorsement before maturity. Nevertheless, an endorsement after protest for non-payment, or after the expiration of the limit of time for presentment for payment, shall: (វិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើដែលធ្វើឡើងក្រោយពីការធ្វើវិរុទ្ធភណ្ឌ ឬ ក្រោយពីការធ្វើប្រតិសេធដែលសមមូលគ្នា ឬ ក្រោយពីផុតរយៈពេលកំណត់សម្រាប់បង្ហាញឱ្យទូទាត់សងប្រាក់អាចនឹង ៖)
- Operate only as an ordinary assignment (- អនុវត្តបានតាមការផ្ទេរធម្មតាតែប៉ុណ្ណោះ)
- Not apply to the transfer of rights under Article 148 and Article 149 of this Law (- មិនអនុវត្តក្នុងការផ្តល់សិទ្ធិក្រោមមាត្រា ១៤៨ និងមាត្រា ១៤៩ នៃច្បាប់នេះ) ៕

In the absence of proof to the contrary, an undated endorsement shall be deemed to have been made on the bill of exchange before protest for non-payment, or before the expiration of the limit of time for presentment for payment referred to in the first paragraph of Article 153 (បើគ្មានភស្តុតាងផ្ទុយទេ វិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើដែលគ្មានចុះកាលបរិច្ឆេទ ត្រូវចាត់ទុកថាបានធ្វើនៅលើប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ហាមុនធ្វើវិរុទ្ធភណ្ឌ ឬ ប្រតិសេធដែលសមមូលគ្នា ឬ មុនផុតរយៈពេលកំណត់ ដែលបានយោងក្នុងកថាខណ្ឌទី ១ នៃមាត្រា ១៥៣ នេះ) ៕

# Section 3 (ផ្នែកទី ៣)

## Liabilities of the Drawer of a Bill of Exchange (ការទទួលខុសត្រូវរបស់អ្នកចេញប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា)

**Article 154.- (មាត្រា ១៥៤.-)**

By issuing a bill of exchange, the drawer undertakes to pay the amount at maturity (ក្នុងការចេញប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា អ្នកចេញប័ណ្ណសន្យាទូទាត់សងប្រាក់នៅពេលដល់កំណត់ប្រណិធាន) ៕

In default of payment, the holder has a direct right of recourse against the drawer to demand everything that can be recovered under Article 175 and Article 176 of this Law (បើមិនបានទូទាត់សងប្រាក់ អ្នកកាន់អាចធ្វើតុល្យវិធិយដោយផ្ទាល់លើប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាទៅនឹងអ្នកចេញប័ណ្ណ ដើម្បីទាមទារនូវអ្វីដែលអាចធ្វើបានស្របតាមមាត្រា ១៧៥ និងមាត្រា ១៧៦ នៃច្បាប់នេះ) ៕

**Article 155.- (មាត្រា ១៥៥.-)**

A bill of exchange payable at a fixed period after sight must be presented for visa within one year of its date (ប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាដែលត្រូវទូទាត់សងប្រាក់នៅក្នុងរយៈពេលកំណត់មួយក្រោយការបង្ហាញ ត្រូវតែបានបង្ហាញឱ្យធ្វើទិដ្ឋាការនៅក្នុងរយៈពេលមួយឆ្នាំ គិតពីកាលបរិច្ឆេទចេញប័ណ្ណ) ៕

The drawer may abridge or extend this period (អ្នកចេញប័ណ្ណអាចបង្រួញ ឬ ពន្យារយៈពេលកំណត់នេះបាន) ៕

These periods may also be abridged by the endorsers (រយៈពេលកំណត់ទាំងនេះ ក៏អាចត្រូវបានបង្រួញដោយអ្នកធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើទាំងឡាយផងដែរ) ៕

**Article 156.- (មាត្រា ១៥៦.-)**

A visa must be written on the bill of exchange using the words "seen for visa" or an equivalent expression, and must be signed by the drawer (ទិដ្ឋាការត្រូវសរសេរនៅលើប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា ដោយប្រើពាក្យថា បានធ្វើទិដ្ឋាការ ឬ ពាក្យផ្សេងទៀតដែលមានន័យដូចគ្នា ហើយត្រូវចុះហត្ថលេខាដោយអ្នកចេញប័ណ្ណ) ៕

A visa must be dated on the day it is made, unless the holder requires it to be dated on the day of presentment for payment. If the visa is undated, the holder, in order to preserve his right of recourse against the endorsers and the drawer, must establish such omission by a protest made within the proper time (ទិដ្ឋាការត្រូវតែបានចុះកាលបរិច្ឆេទនៃថ្ងៃដែលបានធ្វើ លើកលែងតែអ្នកកាន់ទាមទារថា ថ្ងៃនោះជាថ្ងៃដែលបង្ហាញឱ្យទូទាត់សងប្រាក់ ៕ បើទិដ្ឋាការនោះគ្មានចុះកាលបរិច្ឆេទទេ អ្នកកាន់ដើម្បីរក្សាសិទ្ធិឧត្តរាស័យរបស់ខ្លួនទៅនឹងអ្នកធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើ និងអ្នកចេញប័ណ្ណ ត្រូវធ្វើឱ្យស្របច្បាប់ចំពោះកំហុសគ្រោតគ្រាតទាំងនោះ ដោយវិរុទ្ធភណ្ឌដែលធ្វើឡើងនៅក្នុងពេលវេលាសមស្រប) ៕

Refusal by the drawer to write a dated visa must be established by a protest, which determines the date of presentment for payment as the commencement of the period (រយៈពេលកំណត់ក្រោយការបង្ហាញដែលប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាត្រូវទូទាត់សងប្រាក់ ត្រូវគិតពីថ្ងៃធ្វើទិដ្ឋាការដែលបានចុះហត្ថលេខាដោយអ្នកចេញប័ណ្ណ នៅលើប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា ៕ ការបដិសេធរបស់អ្នកចេញប័ណ្ណក្នុងការផ្តល់ទិដ្ឋាការដោយគ្មានចុះកាលបរិច្ឆេទ ត្រូវតែបញ្ជាក់ដោយការធ្វើវិរុទ្ធភណ្ឌ ដែលជាកាលបរិច្ឆេទកំណត់ថ្ងៃចាប់ផ្តើមនៃរយៈពេលកំណត់ បន្ទាប់ពីថ្ងៃបង្ហាញឱ្យទូទាត់សងប្រាក់) ៕

# Section 4 (ផ្នែកទី ៤)

## Aval of a Bill of Exchange (រូបិយបដិភោគប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា)

**Article 157.- (មាត្រា ១៥៧.-)**

Payment of a bill of exchange may be guaranteed by an aval for the whole or part of its amount (ការទូទាត់សងប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាអាចត្រូវបានធានាដោយរូបិយបដិភោគនៅលើចំនួនប្រាក់ទាំងអស់ ឬ មួយផ្នែកនៃប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា) ៕

This guarantee may be given by a third person or even by a person who has signed the bill of exchange (ការធានានេះអាចត្រូវបានផ្តល់ដោយតតិយបុគ្គល ឬ ដោយបុគ្គលណាមួយដែលបានចុះហត្ថលេខាលើប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា) ៕

**Article 158.- (មាត្រា ១៥៨.-)**

An aval may be written on the bill of exchange itself or on an allonge (រូបិយបដិភោគអាចត្រូវបានធ្វើឡើងនៅលើប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាផ្ទាល់ ឬ នៅលើអនុព័ន្ធ) ៕

An aval is expressed by the words "good as aval" or by any other equivalent formula, and must be signed by the giver of the aval, who is called the avalist (រូបិយបដិភោគត្រូវបានបញ្ជាក់ដោយពាក្យថា ល្អសម្រាប់រូបិយបដិភោគ ឬ ដោយរូបមន្តណាមួយផ្សេងទៀត ដែលមានន័យដូចគ្នា និងត្រូវតែចុះហត្ថលេខាដោយអ្នកផ្តល់រូបិយបដិភោគហៅថារូបិយបដិភោគី) ៕

Every signature on the face of a bill of exchange, other than that of the drawer, shall be deemed to be an aval (រាល់ហត្ថលេខាដែលបានចុះនៅខាងមុខប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា ក្រៅពីហត្ថលេខារបស់អ្នកគូរ ត្រូវបានចាត់ទុកថាជាហត្ថលេខាធ្វើរូបិយបដិភោគ) ៕

An aval must specify for whose account it is given. In default of this option, it shall be deemed to be given for the drawer (រូបិយបដិភោគត្រូវតែបញ្ជាក់ឱ្យបានច្បាស់លាស់ នូវបុគ្គលណាមួយដែលជាបុគ្គលទទួលផលពីរូបិយបដិភោគនោះ ៕ បើគ្មានសេចក្តីបញ្ជាក់នេះទេ រូបិយបដិភោគត្រូវបានចាត់ទុកថាធ្វើឱ្យអ្នកគូរ) ៕

**Article 159.- (មាត្រា ១៥៩.-)**

The avalist is bound in the same manner as the person who received the guarantee (រូបិយបដិភោគីមានកាតព្វកិច្ចដូចបុគ្គលដែលបានទទួលការធានាដែរ) ៕

His undertaking is valid even if the obligation which he has guaranteed is void for any reason other than defect of form (ការទទួលខុសត្រូវរបស់ខ្លួននៅតែមានតម្លៃ ទោះបីកាតព្វកិច្ចដែលបានធានានោះ មោឃៈដោយសារហេតុផលផ្សេងៗ ក្រៅពីវិការខាងទម្រង់ក៏ដោយ) ៕

When he pays the bill of exchange, the avalist has the rights arising out of the bill of exchange against the person guaranteed and against those who are liable to the latter on the bill of exchange (នៅពេលទូទាត់សងប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា រូបិយបដិភោគីមានសិទ្ធិទាំងឡាយនៃប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជានេះ ទល់នឹងបុគ្គលដែលត្រូវបានធានា និងបុគ្គលដែលពាក់ព័ន្ធជាមួយបុគ្គលដែលត្រូវបានធានានោះ នៅលើប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា) ៕

# Section 5 (ផ្នែកទី ៥)

## Maturity of a Bill of Exchange (ឥណប្រណិធានប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា)

**Article 160.- (មាត្រា ១៦០.-)**

A bill of exchange may be drawn: (ប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា អាចត្រូវចេញដើម្បី ៖)
- At sight (- ទូទាត់សងប្រាក់នៅពេលបង្ហាញ)
- At a fixed period after sight (- ទូទាត់សងប្រាក់នៅក្នុងរយៈពេលកំណត់មួយ ក្រោយការបង្ហាញ)
- At a fixed period after date (- ទូទាត់សងប្រាក់នៅក្នុងរយៈពេលកំណត់មួយ ក្រោយកាលបរិច្ឆេទចេញប័ណ្ណ)
- At a fixed date (- ទូទាត់សងប្រាក់នៅកាលបរិច្ឆេទមួយជាក់លាក់) ៕

A bill of exchange may be drawn payable by installments, each installment to be paid at a fixed date, with (ប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាអាចត្រូវបានចេញដើម្បីឱ្យទូទាត់សងប្រាក់ជាច្រើនដំណាក់កាល ដែលដំណាក់កាលនីមួយៗត្រូវធ្វើនៅកាលបរិច្ឆេទមួយជាក់លាក់ ដោយមាន)

provided that, in case of default in payment of any installment, the remaining balance shall become due and payable in full (ល័ក្ខខ័ណ្ឌថា ក្នុងករណីខកខានទូទាត់សងប្រាក់នៅដំណាក់កាលណាមួយ ចំនួនប្រាក់ដែលនៅសល់ត្រូវដល់កាលកំណត់សងទាំងស្រុង) ៕

Bills of exchange containing other maturities shall be void (ប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាទាំងឡាយ ដែលមានឥណប្រណិធានផ្សេងពីនេះត្រូវបានចាត់ទុកជាមោឃៈ) ៕

**Article 161.- (មាត្រា ១៦១.-)**

A bill of exchange payable at sight is payable on presentment. It must be presented for payment within one year of its date of issue. The drawer may abridge or extend this period. These periods may also be abridged by the endorsers (ប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាទូទាត់សងនៅពេលបង្ហាញ ត្រូវតែបានទូទាត់សងនៅពេលបង្ហាញ ហើយត្រូវតែបានបង្ហាញនៅពេលណាមួយក្នុងរយៈពេលមួយ (០១) ឆ្នាំ គិតពីកាលបរិច្ឆេទចេញផ្សាយ ៕ អ្នកចេញប័ណ្ណអាចបង្រួញ ឬ ពន្យារយៈពេលនេះបាន ៕ រយៈពេលកំណត់នេះក៏អាចត្រូវបានបង្រួញ ដោយអ្នកធ្វើវិប្បញ្ញត្តិលើផងដែរ) ៕

The drawer may prescribe that a bill of exchange payable at sight shall not be presented for payment before a specified date. In this case, the period for presentment begins from that date (អ្នកចេញប័ណ្ណអាចបញ្ជាក់ថា ប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាដែលត្រូវទូទាត់សងនៅពេលបង្ហាញ មិនត្រូវបានបង្ហាញឱ្យទូទាត់សងនៅមុនថ្ងៃកំណត់ឡើយ ៕ ក្នុងករណីនេះ រយៈពេលកំណត់សម្រាប់បង្ហាញត្រូវចាប់ផ្តើមពីថ្ងៃកំណត់នោះ) ៕

**Article 162.- (មាត្រា ១៦២.-)**

The maturity of a bill of exchange payable at a fixed period after sight is determined either by the date of the visa or by the date of the protest (ឥណប្រណិធាននៃប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាទូទាត់សង នៅក្នុងរយៈពេលកំណត់មួយក្រោយការបង្ហាញ ត្រូវកំណត់ដោយកាលបរិច្ឆេទធ្វើទិដ្ឋាការ ឬ កាលបរិច្ឆេទធ្វើវិរុទ្ធភណ្ឌ) ៕

In the absence of protest, an undated visa by the drawer is deemed to have been written on the last day of the period allowed for presentment for visa (បើគ្មានវិរុទ្ធភណ្ឌទេ ទិដ្ឋាការដែលពុំបានចុះកាលបរិច្ឆេទដោយអ្នកគូរ ត្រូវចាត់ទុកថាបានធ្វើនៅថ្ងៃចុងក្រោយនៃរយៈពេលកំណត់បង្ហាញឱ្យធ្វើទិដ្ឋាការ) ៕

**Article 163.- (មាត្រា ១៦៣.-)**

Where a bill of exchange is drawn payable at one or more months after date or after sight, the bill of exchange matures on the corresponding date in the month in which payment must be made. If there is no corresponding date, the bill of exchange matures on the last day of that month (នៅពេលដែលប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាត្រូវចេញ ដើម្បីឱ្យទូទាត់សងក្នុងរយៈពេលមួយខែ ឬ ច្រើនខែក្រោយកាលបរិច្ឆេទនៃការចេញ ឬ ក្រោយការបង្ហាញ ប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាមានឥណប្រណិធាន នៅកាលបរិច្ឆេទដែលត្រូវគ្នានៅក្នុងខែដែលត្រូវទូទាត់សងប្រាក់ ៕ បើគ្មានកាលបរិច្ឆេទត្រូវគ្នាទេ ប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាមានឥណប្រណិធាននៅថ្ងៃចុងក្រោយនៃខែនោះ) ៕

Where a bill of exchange is drawn payable at one or more months and a half after date or after sight, the entire months must first be calculated (កាលបើប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាត្រូវបានចេញដើម្បីឱ្យទូទាត់សងប្រាក់ ក្នុងរយៈពេលមួយខែកន្លះ ឬ ច្រើនខែកន្លះ ក្រោយកាលបរិច្ឆេទនៃការចេញប័ណ្ណ ឬ ក្រោយការបង្ហាញ រយៈពេលពេញលេញនៃខែទាំងនោះ ត្រូវតែបានយកមកគិតជាមុន) ៕

If maturity is fixed at the commencement, in the middle or at the end of the month, the first, fifteenth or last day of the month shall be understood (បើឥណប្រណិធានត្រូវបានកំណត់នៅដើមខែ ពាក់កណ្តាលខែ ឬ នៅដំណាច់ខែ ត្រូវយកថ្ងៃទីមួយ ថ្ងៃទីដប់ប្រាំ ឬ ថ្ងៃដាច់ខែមកគិត) ៕

The expressions "eight days" or "fifteen days" indicate not one or two weeks, but eight or fifteen actual days (ពាក្យថា ប្រាំបីថ្ងៃ ឬ ដប់ប្រាំថ្ងៃ មិនមានន័យថាមួយសប្តាហ៍ ឬ ពីរសប្តាហ៍ទេ គឺវាជារយៈពេលប្រាំបីថ្ងៃ ឬ ដប់ប្រាំថ្ងៃគិតប្រាកដ) ៕

The expression "half-month" means fifteen days (ពាក្យថា កន្លះខែ មានន័យថាដប់ប្រាំថ្ងៃ) ៕

**Article 164.- (មាត្រា ១៦៤.-)**

Where a bill of exchange is payable at a fixed date or at a fixed period after date or after sight, at a place where the calendar is different from that in the place of issue, unless there is a stipulation to the contrary on the bill of exchange or a simple condition indicating an intention to adopt some other rule, the date of maturity shall be determined according to the calendar of the place of payment (កាលបើប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាត្រូវទូទាត់សងប្រាក់នៅថ្ងៃកំណត់មួយ ឬ នៅរយៈពេលកំណត់មួយ បន្ទាប់ពីថ្ងៃកំណត់ នៅកន្លែងដែលមានប្រតិទិនខុសពីប្រតិទិននៃកន្លែងចេញផ្សាយ លើកលែងតែមានបដិញ្ញានៅក្នុងប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា ឬ មានល័ក្ខខ័ណ្ឌសាមញ្ញ ដែលប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាចង្អុលបង្ហាញអំពីបំណងក្នុងការជ្រើសរើសយកវិធានផ្សេងពីនេះ ថ្ងៃឥណប្រណិធានត្រូវបានកំណត់ទៅតាមប្រតិទិននៃកន្លែងទូទាត់សងប្រាក់) ៕

# Section 6 (ផ្នែកទី ៦)

## Payment (ការទូទាត់សងប្រាក់)

**Article 165.- (មាត្រា ១៦៥.-)**

The holder of a bill of exchange payable on a fixed date or at a fixed period after date or after sight must present the bill of exchange for payment either on the day on which it is payable or on one of the two business days which follow (អ្នកកាន់ប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាដែលត្រូវទូទាត់សងប្រាក់នៅកាលបរិច្ឆេទមួយជាកំណត់ ឬ នៅក្នុងពេលកំណត់មួយក្រោយកាលបរិច្ឆេទចេញប័ណ្ណ ឬ ក្រោយការបង្ហាញ ត្រូវបង្ហាញនៅថ្ងៃទូទាត់សងប្រាក់ ឬ នៅថ្ងៃណាមួយរវាងពីរ (០២) ថ្ងៃធ្វើការបន្ទាប់) ៕

Presentment of a bill of exchange through a clearing house or an electronic system as provided in an agreement or regulations issued by the National Bank of Cambodia constitutes an official presentment for payment (ការបង្ហាញប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជាតាមរយៈសភាទូទាត់ ឬ តាមរយៈប្រព័ន្ធអេឡិចត្រនិកដូចដែលមានចែងនៅក្នុងកិច្ចព្រមព្រៀង ឬ បទបញ្ជាដែលចេញដោយធនាគារជាតិនៃកម្ពុជា គឺជាការបង្ហាញឱ្យទូទាត់សងប្រាក់ជាផ្លូវការ) ៕

**Article 166.- (មាត្រា ១៦៦.-)**
The maker (អ្នកចេញប័ណ្ណ) who pays a promissory note payable to order (ប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា) may require that the promissory note payable to order be surrendered to him/her, upon acknowledgment of receipt of payment by the holder (អ្នកកាន់).

The holder may not refuse a partial payment (ការទូទាត់ប្រាក់មួយផ្នែក) of the amount of the promissory note payable to order.

In the case of a partial payment, the maker may require that such partial payment be specified on the promissory note payable to order and that a receipt (បង្កាន់ដៃទទួលប្រាក់) therefor be delivered to him/her.

**Article 167.- (មាត្រា ១៦៧.-)**
When a promissory note payable to order is not presented for payment within the period determined by Article 165 of this Law, the debtor (កូនបំណុល) is authorized to deposit the amount thereof with the competent authority (អាជ្ញាធរមានសមត្ថកិច្ច), in accordance with the determination of the National Bank of Cambodia (ធនាគារជាតិនៃកម្ពុជា), at the charge and risk of the holder.

**Article 168.- (មាត្រា ១៦៨.-)**
The holder of a promissory note payable to order may not be forced to accept payment before maturity (ដល់កំណត់ប្រណិធាន).

The maker who pays before maturity shall do so at his/her own risk (ហានិភ័យដោយខ្លួនឯងផ្ទាល់).

The maker who pays at maturity is validly discharged from his/her obligation, unless there is fraud (ការក្លែងបន្លំ) or gross negligence (ការធ្វេសប្រហែសធ្ងន់ធ្ងរ). The maker is obliged to verify the regularity of the sequence of endorsements (លំដាប់វិបដិលេខ), but not the signatures of the endorsers (ហត្ថលេខាអ្នកធ្វើវិបដិលេខ).

**Article 169.- (មាត្រា ១៦៩.-)**
When a promissory note payable to order is made in a currency (រូបិយប័ណ្ណ) which is not that of the place of payment (កន្លែងត្រូវទូទាត់សងប្រាក់), the amount may be paid in the currency of the country

of the place of payment, according to the exchange rate at the date of maturity. If the payment is not made upon presentation, the holder may require that the payment of the amount of the promissory note payable to order be made in the currency of the country of the place of payment, according to the exchange rate at the date of maturity or at the date of payment, at his/her option.

Every usage of payment customs (ទម្លាប់ទូទាត់សងប្រាក់) shall be applied for determining currency value. However, the maker may stipulate that the amount to be paid shall be calculated according to the exchange rate specified on the promissory note payable to order.

The above rules shall not apply to a promissory note payable to order:
- Which is made in a currency accepted in the Kingdom of Cambodia (ព្រះរាជាណាចក្រកម្ពុជា)
- In which the maker has specified that payment must be made in a specific currency. In this case, the promissory note payable to order shall be paid in the specified currency.

If the amount of the promissory note payable to order is specified in a currency having the same denomination (នាមចារីកម្ម) but having a different value between the country of payment and the country of issue or any other country, unless otherwise specified, the amount of the promissory note payable to order shall be deemed to be the amount payable in the currency of the country of payment. When the currency of the country of payment does not have the same denomination, the payment must be made in the currency which is widely used in the place of payment.

When the promissory note payable to order is paid at a bank, the indication of the account to be credited with the amount to be paid shall be deemed as an authorization to pay in the currency of the account.

### Section 7: Recourse in Case of Non-Payment (ផ្នែកទី ៧ ឧបាស្រ័យចំពោះការមិនទូទាត់សងប្រាក់)

**Article 170.- (មាត្រា ១៧០.-)**
The holder of a promissory note payable to order may exercise his/her right of recourse (ឧបាស្រ័យ) against the endorsers (អ្នកធ្វើវិបដិលេខ), the maker (អ្នកចេញប័ណ្ណ), and other parties liable (ភាគីពាក់ព័ន្ធដទៃទៀត):

- At maturity, if payment has not been made;

- Before maturity, in the case of bankruptcy (ធនក្ស័យ) of the maker, or in the case where the maker has suspended payments (បញ្ឈប់មិនឱ្យទូទាត់សងប្រាក់), whether or not such suspension is declared by a court decision, or at any time when the execution (វិសក្កកម្ម) of property against the maker has been unsuccessful.

**Article 171.- (មាត្រា ១៧១.-)**
The default of payment must be evidenced by a formal protest (វិរុទ្ធសារ) in accordance with the law.

A protest for non-payment of a promissory note payable to order payable on a fixed date or within a fixed period after date or after sight must be made on one of the two (02) working days following the day on which the promissory note payable to order is payable. In the case of a promissory note payable to order payable at sight, the protest must be made within the period determined for presentation for payment. A protest for refusal of a visa (បដិសេធមិនផ្តល់ទិដ្ឋាការ) may be made without presentation for payment and must be made within the period determined for presentation for payment.

If the maker has suspended payments or if the execution of property has been unsuccessful, the holder may only exercise his/her right of recourse after the

presentation of the promissory note payable to order to the maker for payment and after the protest has been made.

If the maker is declared bankrupt, the presentation of the judgment or decision of the court declaring bankruptcy is sufficient to enable the holder to exercise his/her right of recourse.

The protest must be signed by a legally authorized officer to certify the authenticity of the document. Until there is a legally authorized officer, such officer must be appointed by the National Bank of Cambodia (ធនាគារជាតិនៃកម្ពុជា). The protest must be accompanied by a copy or the original of the promissory note payable to order and must clearly specify the name of the person making the protest, the place and date, the reasons for the protest, the demand made and the answer given if any, or the fact that the maker could not be found. The protest must be made at the place of refusal of payment or at any other place in the Kingdom of Cambodia (ព្រះរាជាណាចក្រកម្ពុជា) situated within a distance not exceeding ten (10) kilometers from the place of presentation and of refusal of payment.

The protest may be replaced by a written declaration signed by the maker certifying all the information as determined in paragraph 5 of this Article.

**Article 172.- (មាត្រា ១៧២.-)**
The holder must give notice of non-payment to his/her endorser (អ្នកធ្វើវិបដិលេខ) and to any avalist (បដិយបនីករ) of the maker, within four (04) working days following the day of protest, or, in case of a stipulation "return without costs" (បង្វិលវិញដោយគ្មានសោហ៊ុយ) or "without protest" (បង្វិលវិញដោយគ្មានវិរុទ្ធសារ) or any other clause of similar meaning, within four working days following the day of presentation. Within two (02) working days following the day on which he/she receives the notice, each endorser must in turn give the same notice to his/her endorser, about the notice received, specifying the name and address

of his/her endorser, who has given notice of non-payment previously in succession until the maker has received it. The periods of time specified above shall run from the day of receipt of the preceding notice.

In accordance with the paragraph above of this Article, when a notice is given to a person who has signed the promissory note payable to order, such notice must also be given to the corresponding avalist (បដិយបដិភារគី) within the same period of time.

Where an endorser has not specified his/her address, or has specified it illegibly, it is sufficient if notice is given to the preceding endorser.

The person who must give notice may do so in any form, even by a simple return of the promissory note payable to order.

The person who must give notice must prove that he/she has given notice within the specified period. This period shall be deemed to have been observed if the letter of notice has been posted within the specified period.

A person who fails to give notice within the period specified above shall be liable for the damage resulting from his/her negligence, if any, but the amount of liability shall not exceed the amount of the promissory note payable to order.

**Article 173.- (មាត្រា ១៧៣.-)**
By a stipulation "return without costs" (បង្វិលវិញដោយគ្មានសោហ៊ុយ) or "without protest" (បង្វិលវិញដោយគ្មានវិរុទ្ធសារ) or any other clause of similar meaning, written and signed on the promissory note payable to order, the endorser or the avalist (រូបិយបដិភារគី) may dispense the holder from making a protest in order to exercise his/her right of recourse.

Such stipulation does not dispense the holder from presenting the promissory note payable to order within the specified periods, or from the requirement of giving notices. The costs

resulting from the presentation of proof of non-compliance with the periods of time by the holder shall be borne by the person who made the stipulation against the holder.

A stipulation written by an endorser or an avalist (រូបិយបដិភារគី) shall apply only to the endorser or the avalist. Whether or not there is a stipulation, if the holder makes a protest, the costs of protest may be recovered from all persons who have signed the promissory note payable to order.

**Article 174.- (មាត្រា ១៧៤.-)**
The maker, the endorsers, and the person guaranteeing by making the aval (រូបិយបដិភារគី) of a promissory note payable to order shall be jointly and severally liable to the holder.

The holder has the right to bring action against these persons who are parties to the promissory note payable to order, individually or jointly, without being required to observe the order in which they have become liable.

The same right shall belong to any person who has signed the promissory note payable to order and has subsequently taken it up and paid it.

An action brought against one of the parties liable does not prevent actions against the other parties, even though they are subsequent to the person first sued.

**Article 175.- (មាត្រា ១៧៥.-)**
The holder may recover from the person against whom he/she exercises his/her right of recourse the unpaid amount of the promissory note payable to order with interest, if interest is specified on the promissory note payable to order, interest calculated at the rate determined by the National Bank of Cambodia (ធនាគារជាតិនៃកម្ពុជា) from the date of maturity, and all costs incurred.

If the right of recourse is exercised before maturity, a discount (អប្បហារ) shall be deducted from the amount of the promissory note payable to order. This discount shall be calculated

according to the official discount rate of the bank, at the date of recourse at the place of residence of the holder.

**Article 176.- (មាត្រា ១៧៦.-)**
A party who has taken up and paid a promissory note payable to order may recover from the parties liable to him/her: the entire sum he/she has paid; interest on the said sum calculated at the rate determined by the National Bank of Cambodia (ធនាគារជាតិនៃកម្ពុជា) from the date of payment; and all costs incurred.

**Article 177.- (មាត្រា ១៧៧.-)**
Each party liable against whom the right of recourse is exercised may require, against payment, the surrender of the promissory note payable to order together with the protest and a receipt.

Each endorser who has taken up and paid a promissory note payable to order may cancel his/her own endorsement and those of subsequent endorsers.

**Article 178.- (មាត្រា ១៧៨.-)**
Unless there is an agreement to the contrary, any person having the right of recourse may pay himself/herself by means of drawing a redraft (រូបិយណត្តិ) payable at sight on one of the parties liable to him/her and payable at the place of residence of that party.

In addition to the sums guaranteed as specified in Articles 175 and 176 of this Law, the redraft (រូបិយណត្តិ) shall include brokerage fees and brokerage tax.

If the redraft is drawn by the holder, the amount payable shall be determined according to the rate of interest for a redraft payable at sight drawn at the place where the original promissory note payable to order was payable at the place of residence of the party liable. If the redraft is drawn by an endorser, the amount payable shall be

determined according to the rate of interest for a redraft payable at sight drawn at the place where the drawer of the redraft has his/her place of residence, on the same place as the party liable.

**Article 179.- (មាត្រា ១៧៩.-)**
The holder loses his/her right of recourse against the endorsers and other parties liable, except for the maker, after the expiration of the periods of time determined:

- For the presentation of a promissory note payable to order payable at sight or within a fixed period after sight;

- For making the protest for non-payment;

- For the presentation for payment in the case of a stipulation "return without costs" (បង្វិលវិញដោយគ្មានសោហ៊ុយ) or "without protest" (បង្វិលវិញដោយគ្មានវិរុទ្ធសារ) or any other clause of similar meaning.

If the stipulation regarding the period of presentation is contained in an endorsement, such period shall be effective only in respect of the endorser.

**Article 180.- (មាត្រា ១៨០.-)**
When the presentation of the promissory note payable to order for payment or the making of the protest within the specified period is prevented by an insurmountable obstacle, which constitutes a case of force majeure (ប្រធានសក្តិ), the periods of time shall be extended.

The holder is obliged to give notice of the force majeure without delay to his/her endorser and to certify this notice, dated and signed, on the promissory note payable to order or on the allonge (អនុប័ត្រ). For other matters, the provisions of Article 172 of this Law shall apply.

When the force majeure has ceased, the holder must present the promissory note payable to order for payment without delay and, if necessary, make the protest.

If the force majeure continues to exist for more than thirty (30) days after maturity, the right of recourse may be exercised, and neither the presentation for payment nor the making of a protest shall be necessary.

For promissory notes payable to order payable at sight or within a fixed period after sight, the period of thirty (30) days shall run from the date on which the holder has given notice of the force majeure to his/her endorser, even if this notice is given before the expiration of the period for presentation. For promissory notes payable to order payable within a fixed period after sight, the period of thirty (30) days specified above must be added to the period after sight specified in the promissory note payable to order.

Facts which are purely personal to the holder or to the person whom he/she has authorized to present the promissory note payable to order for payment or to make the protest shall not be deemed to constitute cases of force majeure.

Liability on a promissory note payable to order which is lost or destroyed may be enforced only if there is a guarantee from the accommodation party (ភាគីអនុគ្គហប័ណ្ណសងតាមបញ្ជា) who pays the damages to the parties liable, in respect of claims of other persons on the promissory note payable to order by a court decision, if the holder has acquired the promissory note payable to order in good faith or without gross negligence.

### Section 8: Intervention for Payment (ផ្នែកទី ៨ ការធ្វើអន្តរាគមន៍ដើម្បីទូទាត់សងប្រាក់)

**Article 181.- (មាត្រា ១៨១.-)**
The maker, the endorser, or the avalist (រូបិយបដិភារគី) may specify the name of the person who is to pay in case of need.

Payment of a promissory note payable to order may be made by a person who intervenes for payment on behalf of any debtor against whom a right of recourse exists.

The person who intervenes may be a third party (តតិយភាគី) or a party already liable on the promissory note payable to order.

Within two (02) working days, the person who has intervened must give notice to the party for whom he/she has intervened (ភាគីដែលខ្លួនបានធ្វើអន្តរាគមន៍សម្រាប់). If he/she fails to comply with this period of time, the person who has intervened shall be liable for any damages resulting from his/her negligence, but the compensation for damages shall not exceed the amount of the promissory note payable to order.

**Article 182.- (មាត្រា ១៨២.-)**
Payment by intervention may be made in all cases where the holder has a right of recourse on the promissory note payable to order at maturity or before maturity.

The payment must include the entire sum payable by the party for whom he/she has intervened (ភាគីដែលខ្លួនធ្វើអន្តរាគមន៍សម្រាប់).

The payment must be made at the latest on the day following the last day on which a protest for non-payment could be made.

**Article 183.- (មាត្រា ១៨៣.-)**
If persons residing at the place of payment are designated as referees in case of need (អ្នកទូទាត់សងក្នុងករណីចាំបាច់), the holder must present the promissory note payable to order to all these persons and, if necessary, make a protest for non-payment, at the latest on the day following the last day allowed for making the protest.

In the absence of a protest within this period of time, the party who has designated the referee in case of need (ភាគីដែលបានចាត់តាំងអ្នកទូទាត់សងក្នុងករណីចាំបាច់ខាងលើ) and the subsequent endorsers shall be discharged from liability.

Article 184.-

The holder who refuses payment by intervention loses their right of recourse against those persons who would have been discharged by such payment.

Article 185.-

Payment by intervention must be authenticated by a receipt given on the promissory note, specifying for whose honour payment is made. In the absence of such specification, payment shall be deemed to have been made for the honour of the maker of the promissory note.

The promissory note and the protest, if any, must be delivered to the person paying by intervention.

Article 186.-

The person paying by intervention shall acquire all the rights of the promissory note against the party for whose honour they paid and against those who are liable to the latter on the promissory note. However, they may not endorse the promissory note again.

Endorsers subsequent to the party for whose honour payment was made shall be discharged from liability by intervention.

In the case of competition for payment by intervention, the payment which discharges the greatest number of parties shall have priority. Any person who intervenes in contravention of this rule, with full knowledge of the facts, shall lose their right of recourse against those who would have been discharged by intervention.

### Section 9

#### Copies

Article 187.-

Every holder of a promissory note has the right to make copies of it.

A copy must reproduce the original exactly, with endorsements and all other declarations to be found therein. It must specify where the copy ends.

A copy may be endorsed and guaranteed by aval in the same manner and with the same effects as the original.

Article 188.-

A copy must specify who is the holder of the original instrument. The holder of the original must deliver the said instrument to the lawful holder of the copy.

If the holder of the original refuses to deliver it, the holder of the copy may only exercise their right of recourse against the persons who have endorsed or guaranteed the copy by aval after having a protest drawn up stating that the original has not been delivered to them on their demand.

After the last endorsement made before the making of the copy, the original may specify that "from this point on an endorsement signed on the copy is valid only" or any other equivalent formula. An endorsement afterwards signed on the original is null and void.

### Section 10

#### Alterations of Promissory Notes

Article 189.-

In case of alteration of the text of a promissory note, parties who have signed subsequent to the alteration are bound according to the terms of the altered text. Parties who have signed before the alteration are bound according to the terms of the original text.

### Section 11

#### Limitation of Actions

Article 190.-

All actions arising from a promissory note against the maker are barred after three years from the date of maturity.

Actions by the holder against the endorsers are barred after one year from the date of a protest drawn up within the proper time, or from the date of maturity where there is a clause "return without costs" or "without protest" or any other equivalent formula.

Actions by endorsers against other endorsers are barred after six months from the day on which the endorser has taken up and paid the promissory note, or from the day on which they themselves were sued.

Article 191.-

Interruption of the period of limitation of action as provided in Article 190 of this Law has effect only against the person in respect of whom the period has been interrupted.

### Section 12

#### Calculation of Limits

Article 192.-

Payment of a promissory note which falls due on a legal holiday will be made on the next business day. All actions relating to promissory notes, especially presentation for payment and protest, may only be made on a business day.

When these actions must be taken within a certain limit of time of which the last day is a legal holiday, such limit of time is extended until the first business day which follows. Legal holidays are included in the calculation of the limit of time.

Article 193.-

Limits of time specified in this Chapter shall not include the day on which the period begins.

Article 194.-

No days of grace, whether legal or judicial, shall be allowed.

## Chapter 5

### Payment Transactions

#### Section 1

##### Scope of Application

Article 195.-

This Chapter applies to payment transactions in national currency or in any foreign currency. This Chapter defines the rights and obligations of participants and applies

to the organization, supervision and management of payment systems in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

Article 196.-

A natural or legal person may open accounts in national currency and in foreign currency, and may make payment transactions through these accounts under the conditions agreed in accordance with the provisions of Section 7 of this Chapter. A natural or legal person may have one or more accounts in one or more banks.

The above paragraph of this Article does not waive, and must comply with, any obligation required by law relating to the use of local currency, such as reporting or restrictions by law relating to specific classes of accounts or foreign currencies.

Article 197.-

A payment order may be made by any customer from within or from outside the Kingdom of Cambodia, and received by any bank located inside or outside the Kingdom of Cambodia.

A payment order received by a branch or separate office of a bank located in the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be subject to the provisions of this Chapter and, where there are no provisions, shall be subject to other laws applicable in the Kingdom of Cambodia. This paragraph applies to transactions made from within or from outside the Kingdom of Cambodia, unless there is an agreement to the contrary between the sender and the receiving bank.

A payment order received by a branch or separate office of a bank located outside the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be subject to the laws applicable in the place where that branch or separate office is established, unless

an agreement to the contrary between the sender and the receiving bank.

The rights and obligations between the bank and its customer relating to accounts and payment orders, inward or outward, shall be subject to the law applicable in the place where the branch or separate office maintaining those accounts is located, unless there is an agreement to the contrary between the bank and its customer.

The timing of payment by the payer to the payee in a payment transaction shall be subject to the law applicable in the place where the branch or separate office of the beneficiary's bank is located.

A payment order may be sent to the National Bank of Cambodia to execute payment from or to accounts opened there. In this case, this Chapter shall apply to the National Bank of Cambodia as if it were a bank.

Whenever a receiving bank agrees to execute an instruction which is not a payment order because it imposes conditions, such instruction shall also be deemed to be a payment order. This Chapter applies to payment transactions, but does not apply to the obligations:

- of the receiving bank to transmit any conditions to the beneficiary's bank, and

- of the beneficiary's bank to execute those conditions.

Article 198.-

The authority of the sender in a credit transfer is as follows:

1- In a credit transfer, the sender may revoke the authority given to the receiving bank by giving a written, electronic or oral notice of revocation of authority to the receiving bank, if there is a prior written agreement to do so. The notice must be given before the payment transaction is completed and in such time and manner as to give the beneficiary's bank an opportunity

to act on it. The beneficiary's bank shall reject any payment order of the sender for which a notice of revocation of authority has been received.

2- In a credit transfer, a notice of death or a court decision of legal incapacity of the sender received by the beneficiary's bank has the same effect as a notice of revocation of authority of the sender under paragraph 1 of this Article.

3- A customer whose:

- account has been debited for the amount of the payment order of the credit transfer

- disputes the authority of the sender of the transaction, and

- objects in writing to the debit within four (04) days after having knowledge of such debit in their account, may have the right to reverse the debit, unless the beneficiary's bank has provided evidence of the authority granted by the customer to the sender of the transaction.

4- By accepting a credit transfer, the beneficiary's bank is obligated to make payment to the beneficiary.

5- This Article does not apply to the authority granted by the beneficiary to the sender under a single revocable authority.

### Section 2

#### Payment Orders

Article 199.-

A payment order is an instruction of a sender to a receiving bank to be used for the transfer of funds to a beneficiary in a payment transaction.

A payment order may be made in writing, electronically, or may be made

orally, if there is a prior written agreement between the parties to the payment order. If there is a similar prior agreement which is part of the terms and conditions of an agreement, subsequent communications between the two parties to also revoke it may also be made orally.

A payment order must identify the beneficiary and the beneficiary's bank correctly. Whenever a payment order identifying a beneficiary is processed in an automatic payment transaction system by specifying the name and account number, the receiving bank does not need to verify the consistency between the name and the account number, and may make payment based solely on the account number. Whenever any employee responsible for executing the payment order notices that the name and the account number are inconsistent, the receiving bank must reject that payment order.

The National Bank of Cambodia may issue regulations specifying conditions under which payment orders and other communications, including revocation orders, may be made orally.

Article 200.-

In a credit transfer, unless otherwise agreed, the receiving bank is obligated to execute the payment order of the sender only if the sender's account has a sufficient balance.

In a credit transfer, the originating bank must execute the payment order of the sender after having final evidence of the authority granted by the beneficiary and a guarantee of reimbursement from the sender. Unless otherwise agreed, the originating bank has no obligation to provide funds to the sender or to voluntarily credit the account provisionally before the completion of the credit transfer. If the account of the sender is credited by the originating bank before the completion of the credit transfer, such crediting

the credit shall be provisional, and the originating bank's account may be debited back for the amount equal to the credit if the credit transfer is not completed as provided in Article 210 of this Law.

A receiving bank must execute only those payment orders that:

- are properly completed and authorized or otherwise binding on the party

- properly identify the beneficiary's bank

- clearly describe the beneficiary.

A receiving bank that does not execute a payment order of the sender must notify the sender immediately of its rejection or non-acceptance.

Unless otherwise agreed, and regardless of any fee agreed upon or customary, the receiving bank must execute the payment order for the amount of money ordered by the sender.

The receiving bank must execute the instructions contained in a payment order on the business day the bank receives the instruction, or on the effective date of the payment order, if any, which is typically a later date. The receiving bank may set a cut-off time for the execution of payment orders. Any payment order received after this cut-off time shall be deemed to be received on the next business day of the bank.

An originating bank in a credit transfer and a beneficiary's bank in a credit transfer that have received more than one payment order during the bank's business day, or other lawful orders to withdraw funds from an account, may execute those orders in any sequence, unless otherwise provided in other laws.

The receiving bank must exercise ordinary care in executing a payment order. The receiving bank must exercise care in interpreting ambiguous payment orders, act in good faith, and execute the payment order in accordance with its reasonable meaning without being required to determine whether such interpretation is contrary to the intent of the sender.

in receiving payment orders and acting in payment transactions, and notwithstanding any agreement to the contrary, the receiving bank must comply with this Law, as well as all relevant regulations or instructions and other laws. The bank must act with care and good faith in accordance with general banking standards, and must assist other participants in completing the payment transaction properly. Within the framework provided in this Law or other laws, the bank must maintain confidentiality and act in the best interests of the sender.

Subject to the confidentiality obligations under applicable laws, a receiving bank that has been ordered to route a payment transaction through an intermediary bank or to a beneficiary's bank that it deems unreliable under the circumstances, must notify the sender immediately of its impediment. However, if the receiving bank, despite having received instructions to route the transaction contrary to its notice, may reject the payment order or, notwithstanding any other provisions of this Law, may execute the order at the sender's own risk.

Article 201.-

Unless otherwise agreed between the sender and the receiving bank, a payment order may be rejected or revoked. A payment order shall be revoked by the sender when the revocation order is authorized or properly verified by or on behalf of the sender, and is received by the receiving bank at a time and in a manner that allows the bank to cancel or amend the payment order before execution.

Even if the sender's payment order has already been executed, the bank that received and executed the order may, at the sender's request, issue a cancellation or amendment order to its receiving bank, as provided in the paragraph above of this Article. An order

stop order issued by the sender to the beneficiary bank in a payment transaction shall be effective only when that stop order reaches the beneficiary bank before the payment transaction has been completed.

A stop order must clearly identify the payment order to be stopped and must be transmitted to the receiving bank in writing, electronically, or orally if there is a prior written agreement between the parties to the payment order.

A payment order that has not been executed by the receiving bank shall be withdrawn in the following cases:

- At the end of the fifth business day of the receiving bank, or within a shorter period as agreed between them, after receiving the payment order or on the effective date of the payment order, whichever is later

- When the receiving bank receives notice of death or a court decision on the legal incapacity of the sender; or

- When the receiving bank is closed by the supervisory authority.

The stop or withdrawal of a payment order shall not be effective after the payment transaction has been finally completed.

#### Article 202.-

Interbank payment of a payment order and the time for payment shall be determined as follows:

1- Payment for interbank payment order may be made:

a- On the settlement accounts of the banks at the National Bank of Cambodia, which in this case shall be subject to any regulations that

issued by the National Bank of Cambodia;

b- By debiting or crediting an account other than the settlement account, which each bank maintains at another bank or at a third bank. Such debiting or crediting may be done for each individual payment order separately or for a batch of payment orders, which in any case must also be done at the time of payment;

c- By any other means that can settle the obligation.

2- The time for payment in paragraph 1 of this Article shall be as follows:

a- Under paragraph 1-a, when the payment is completed under any regulations issued by the National Bank of Cambodia;

b- Under paragraph 1-b, by debiting an account, when the debit item is recorded in the account;

c- Under paragraph 1-b, by crediting an account, when the credit item recorded in the account is used, or if not used, at the start of the next business day when the credit item is available for use and the bank has knowledge of the credit to its account;

d- Under paragraph 1-c, in accordance with the law governing the time of settlement of the obligation.

3- This paragraph shall apply when the payment under paragraph 1-b of this Article occurs in accordance with an agreement or rules governing interbank payment settlement outside the National Bank of Cambodia and for bilateral or multilateral net payment orders. Notwithstanding paragraph 2-b of this Article, such payment occurs when the payment settlement is completed under the agreement or rules.

4- Regulations issued by the National Bank of Cambodia may specify or govern the circumstances in which payment under paragraph 1-b or c of this Article may occur, as well as modify the time for payment.

5- Notwithstanding anything contained in any law, whether or not related to proceedings for the insolvency of a bank, it shall not:

- Have retroactive effect on rights and obligations arising from or related to the routing and payment of payment orders, other than at the time such insolvency proceedings are commenced; or

- Affect the validity and enforceability of a payment order received by the receiving bank before the commencement of such insolvency proceedings and before bilateral or multilateral net payment settlement, as well as before the provision of any security.

6- For the purposes of paragraph 5 of this Article and paragraph 2 of Article 227 of this Law, insolvency proceedings are collective measures prescribed by law to liquidate, suspend operations, or reorganize a bank, whether voluntary or involuntary, and the time of commencement of insolvency proceedings is the time when the relevant judicial or administrative authority announces its decision.

### Section 3

#### Third Party Processors

#### Article 203.-

In performing any part of a payment transaction, a bank may use a third party processor. As authorized by the bank, the third party processor may act on behalf of the authorizing bank as:

- Communications processor;

- Interbank settlement processor which may transmit further information on interbank payment to other banks, including the National Bank of Cambodia, where the payment is also completed;

- Management and operation agent for banks with customer accounts; and/or

- Location for transmitting and receiving payment orders sent or received by the bank, where bank customers can contact directly for transmitting and receiving payment orders.

#### Article 204.-

Third party processors shall comply with all obligations, conditions, and restrictions determined by regulations of the National Bank of Cambodia. Such regulations may provide for licensing, regulation, supervision and may apply to all types of third party processors. Different types of third party processors, as determined by the National Bank of Cambodia, may be subject to different obligations.

A bank may act as a third party processor on behalf of another bank.

#### Article 205.-

Apart from the liability between the bank and the third party processor, the bank authorizing the third party processor to act on its behalf shall be fully liable for the acts or omissions of such third party processor.

To ensure compliance with this law and other legal obligations, the bank must ensure that the third party processor operates under its full control and supervision with respect to all matters in which this person acts on its behalf, and the bank cannot disclaim such control and

supervision.

#### Article 206.-

A third party processor must reject a payment order sent directly to it by a customer of a bank, except:

a- Such payment order is sent under authorization from the bank;

b- In the case of a credit transfer;

- The third party processor acts on behalf of the bank as a management or operations agent for the customer's account, and such customer's account has a sufficient balance;

- The third party processor is authorized by the bank.

A customer's payment order executed by the third party processor shall be deemed to be an order from the customer to its bank. In accordance with this payment order, the bank sends the payment order to the third party processor for onward transmission to the receiving bank.

When a bank authorizes a third party processor to act as a location for receiving payment orders sent to the bank, any payment order received by the third party processor shall be deemed to be received by the receiving bank.

### Section 4

#### Completion of Credit Transfer and Discharge

#### Article 207.-

A credit transfer is completed in the following cases:

1- A credit transfer shall be completed when the beneficiary's bank receives payment. For an in-house transfer, the bank receives payment when it has debited the account of the sender of the transfer operation.

of the payment order. For interbank credit transfers, the beneficiary's bank receives payment when the interbank payment, including the payment order of the sending bank, is completed.

2- A credit transfer is completed at the beginning of the bank's business day following the day the beneficiary's bank received the payment order or following the effective date, whichever is later, if at that time the sender has sufficient funds to credit at the beneficiary's bank, and unless the beneficiary's bank rejects the payment order not later than one hour thereafter.

3- When the beneficiary does not have an account with the beneficiary's bank beforehand, or when that account has been closed or blocked for incoming payments, the credit transfer is completed when the beneficiary's bank pays the beneficiary.

#### Article 208.-

Upon completion of the credit transfer, the beneficiary's bank becomes a debtor to the beneficiary for the amount of the payment order it received, and after deducting reasonable service fees, the bank must pay the beneficiary immediately.

Payment to the beneficiary by the beneficiary's bank must be made by crediting the beneficiary's account not later than the bank's business day following the completion of the credit transfer. When the beneficiary does not have an account or the account cannot be identified with sufficient clarity, or when so ordered, the beneficiary's bank must immediately notify the beneficiary that it holds the funds in hand and must pay the beneficiary.

When the beneficiary or the beneficiary's account is not clearly identified in the payment order received by the beneficiary's bank, thereby causing doubt

about the identity of the beneficiary, the beneficiary's bank shall not accept the payment order and must notify the sender of such rejection.

When the beneficiary's bank has paid the beneficiary or has promised in advance to pay the beneficiary, such payment or promise shall be final and irrevocable, and the credit transfer shall be deemed completed, although the sender may remain indebted to its receiving bank. The promise to pay by the beneficiary's bank may be made directly with the beneficiary or through an interbank agreement.

#### Article 209.-

When a credit transfer is made to satisfy an obligation that the sender of the transaction owes to the beneficiary, unless otherwise agreed between both parties, the obligation shall be discharged when the payment transaction is completed. The discharge is limited to the amount paid in the payment transaction.

Discharge shall occur before the completion of the payment transaction when the beneficiary agrees to a payment guarantee or when such payment guarantee is confirmed by the beneficiary's bank to the beneficiary, except in the circumstances specified in paragraph 3 of Article 207 of this Law.

The guarantor or the beneficiary's bank that has guaranteed or confirmed the payment under the paragraph above shall assume the obligation to pay and become a debtor to the beneficiary for the guaranteed or confirmed amount.

### Section 5

#### Completion of Debit Transfer and Discharge

#### Article 210.-

A debit transfer is completed in the following cases:

1- A debit transfer shall be completed when the beneficiary's bank credits the beneficiary's account as specified in the payment order it received and has not reversed the credit and has not rejected the payment order by the end of the bank's business day following receipt of the payment order. When the payment order is sent to the beneficiary's bank to credit an account opened at that bank, the debit transfer shall be completed at the end of the bank's business day following receipt of the payment order, even if before the credit is recorded in the beneficiary's account, and if up to that time there has been no rejection of that payment order.

2- Until the end of the bank's business day following receipt of the payment order, the beneficiary's bank may reject the payment order. The beneficiary's bank has the right to receive from its sender payment to refund the amount of the payment order to that sender by notifying its rejection to the sender and the sending bank in the transaction, if these two are different banks. The rejection and the right to payment from the sender occur automatically by law when the receiving bank is closed by the supervisory authority before the end of the bank's business day following receipt of the payment order.

3- Upon receipt of the notice of rejection, the first sending bank that is not the originator's bank must notify its sender and has the same right to receive payment from that sender. The first sending bank that is not the originator's bank must notify its sender of the rejection not later than the bank's business day following the day it received the notice of rejection.

4- Upon receipt of the notice of rejection, the originator's bank must immediately notify the originator of the rejection not later than the next business day of the bank and may reverse the credit recorded

provisionally in the account of the originator, or may otherwise recover from the originator any payment previously made on that payment order.

5- Notwithstanding paragraph 2 of this Article, the beneficiary's bank shall be liable to the beneficiary for wrongful non-payment when it has rejected the payment order unlawfully or without lawful reason:

- Notwithstanding knowledge of the beneficiary's rights regarding the debit transfer;

- Notwithstanding clear identification in the payment order received by the bank maintaining the beneficiary's account; and

- Notwithstanding the availability of sufficient balance in the account.

6- The time limits specified in Article 210 and Article 211 of this Law may be shortened by agreement or by regulations.

#### Article 211.-

Upon completion of a debit transfer, the beneficiary's bank and each receiving bank that has received payment from its receiving bank shall be liable to its sender for the amount of payment it received. Payment by the beneficiary's bank to its sender must be in an amount equal to the debit item recorded in the beneficiary's account and must be made not later than the bank's business day following completion of the debit transfer. Payment by other receiving banks must be made not later than the bank's business day following receipt of their payment.

Upon receiving payment and as a form of registration described in paragraph 3 of Article 198 of this Law, the originator's bank shall be indebted to the originator for the amount of payment it received. In this case, the provisional credit

credits given to the originator shall become final. Before the credit becomes final, there shall be no claim against the originator's bank to provide funds to the originator, unless otherwise agreed; any provision of funds before completion of the debit transfer shall be provisional until such completion.

#### Article 212.-

When a debit transfer by authorization is made to satisfy an obligation that the beneficiary owes to the originator, unless otherwise agreed between both parties, the obligation shall be discharged when the payment transaction is completed. The discharge is limited to the amount paid at the completion of the transaction.

Discharge shall occur before the completion of the payment transaction if and when the payment guarantee is agreed by the originator or is confirmed by the originator's bank to the originator.

The guarantor or the originator's bank that has guaranteed or confirmed the payment under the paragraph above shall assume the obligation to pay and become a debtor to the originator for the guaranteed or confirmed amount.

### Section 6

#### Liability, Damages, and Compensation

#### Article 213.-

The liability of the sender shall be determined as follows:

1- A person as a sender shall be liable for the payment order or stop order accepted by the receiving bank, when the payment order or order

cancellation thereof is:

a- Issued by that person to the receiving bank;

b- Issued by a person authorized by that person to the receiving bank;

c- Agreed by the receiving bank in compliance with an appropriate security procedure having commercial characteristics agreed upon between the bank and that person, to verify the authenticity of the payment order ordered by that person to the bank; or

d- Confirmed and agreed by that person.

2- Paragraph 1(c) of this Article shall not apply, and the person making the payment order shall not be held liable, when that person shows evidence that they did not receive benefit from the payment order or revocation order, and the unauthorized issuance of the payment order or revocation order was not caused by:

- The person making it, or other persons who are trusted at all times to perform work for this person, related to payment transactions or security procedures; or

- Any person who obtained from the person making it, or from a source under the control of this person, the access to transmit messages or information, including access to computer software programs or similar means, which easily breached the security procedure.

3- For the purposes of paragraph 1(c) of this Article, a security procedure shall be deemed commercially reasonable if the procedure was chosen by the customer contrary to the bank's recommendation proposing an appropriate procedure, and was rejected by the customer.

#### Article 202.-

Whenever a credit transfer is revoked, the sender shall not prejudice the receiving bank and shall indemnify the bank for any losses up to the amount of the payment order, commercially reasonable expenses, costs, and interest.

In a credit transfer, even if the receiving bank has executed and completed the payment transaction, the sender shall not be liable to the receiving bank for a payment order transmitted to the receiving bank in compliance with a security procedure to prevent errors, and based on authorization by customary law, the receiving bank shall have the right to recover from the beneficiary the amount paid by the sender by mistake in the following cases:

- The sender had no intention to make the payment order, the sender intended to order payment to a beneficiary other than the beneficiary in the payment order, or the payment order had an amount greater than the amount the sender intended to pay; and

- The sender shows evidence of compliance with the security procedure, and that the error would have been detected had the receiving bank also complied with the security procedure.

In a debit transfer, each sender guarantees to the receiving bank that their payment order is properly authorized and in accordance with the effective and valid authorization of the originator and the beneficiary.

The sender of a payment order and the beneficiary who authorizes the originator to make a debit transfer, including the issuance of debt instruments, must always exercise caution to prevent fraud and unauthorized issuance or modification of a payment order, and to ensure that the payment order is clear and has a definite meaning.

#### Article 203.-

Whenever a credit transfer is not completed, without regard to any error caused by any party participant in the payment transaction:

1- The originator shall not be liable to the originator's bank and shall have the right to have its account re-credited for the amount debited to pay the payment order. Furthermore, the originator shall have the right to claim from the originator's bank commercially reasonable expenses, costs, and interest on the re-credited amount.

2- The originator's bank and any intermediary bank that executed the order of its sender shall likewise be released from liability to their receiving bank and shall have the right to recover compensation for themselves from that bank, excluding expenses and other costs.

#### Article 204.-

A receiving bank shall be liable for commercially reasonable expenses, costs, interest, and damages, including any losses from exchange rate fluctuations if any, resulting from its delay or error in executing a payment order. The originator may claim such losses either from the originator's bank or from the receiving bank that caused the delay or error. The beneficiary may claim losses either from the beneficiary's bank or from the receiving bank that caused the delay or error. The receiving bank that caused the delay or error shall be liable to indemnify the originator's bank or the beneficiary's bank for the amount of damages they paid.

In addition, a receiving bank that:

- Failed to notify the sender immediately of its rejection of a payment order; or

committed gross negligence in executing or failing to execute a payment order,

shall be liable to the originator for actual foreseeable damages, not exceeding the amount of the originator's payment order, whether or not the originator is its sender, provided that the originator is also free of negligence. Where applicable, the sender may also be entitled to a refund of the payment order amount, in accordance with Article 203 of this Law.

The originator shall have the right to recover under paragraph 1 of this Article the full amount from the originator's bank or from the bank that committed gross negligence. Subject to agreement between the banks, the bank liable under this Article shall have the right to claim contribution from other parties, in accordance with the degree to which their fault caused the damage.

In a debit transfer, the liability under this Article and any relevant limits shall be without prejudice to the liability of the beneficiary's bank for error in refusing to pay under Article 210(5).

#### Article 205.-

Whenever a credit transfer is completed with an error by a receiving bank that is at fault, such as when payment to the beneficiary was not made as specified in the originator's payment order, in addition to the compensation under Article 204 of this Law, if applicable, the following rules shall apply:

1- When the amount paid to the beneficiary is greater or less than the amount of the originator's payment order, the credit transfer shall be deemed

deemed completed to the extent of the amount paid to the beneficiary. On each payment order, each sender shall be liable to its receiving bank for the amount of its payment order that leads to:

a- When the amount paid to the beneficiary exceeds the amount of the originator's payment order, the bank at fault shall have the right to recover the excess amount from the beneficiary by an action governed by the law of the place of the beneficiary's bank's domicile; and

b- When the amount paid to the beneficiary is less than the amount of the originator's payment order, the bank at fault must pay the deficiency to the beneficiary and shall have the right to retain or receive payment from its sender for the amount of the originator's payment order.

2- When payment is made to a beneficiary who is not the beneficiary named in the originator's payment order, and the bank at fault is not the beneficiary's bank of the originator's payment order, the originator and other senders prior to the bank at fault, and the beneficiary of the originator's payment order, may treat the credit transfer as not completed. The bank at fault shall have the right to recover from the beneficiary the amount paid by an action governed by the law of the place of the beneficiary's bank's domicile.

3- The bank at fault shall be liable to the participants for the principal, interest, and commercially reasonable expenses such as banking fees or information-related expenses arising from such error.

4- For the purposes of this Article, an error may occur or be caused inadvertently, by negligence, or by fraud.

#### Article 206.-

When a debit transfer is completed not in accordance with the instructions in the originator's payment order, due to an error of a receiving bank that is at fault, in addition to the compensation under Article 210(5) and Article 204 of this Law, if applicable, the following rules shall apply:

1- When the debit to the account of the beneficiary of the originator's payment order is less than the amount of the payment order, the bank at fault shall recover the deficiency from the beneficiary and deliver it to the originator.

2- When the debit to the account of the beneficiary of the originator's payment order is greater than the amount of the payment order, the beneficiary's bank shall credit the account of the beneficiary for the excess amount. The bank at fault must pay the excess amount to the beneficiary's bank and recover the excess amount from the originator or other participants, to the extent of the excess amount retained.

3- When a debit is recorded in the account of a beneficiary other than the beneficiary named in the originator's payment order, the beneficiary's bank must credit that beneficiary's account for the amount debited and must debit the account of the beneficiary named in the originator's payment order for the amount of the payment order.

4- When a debit is recorded in the account of a beneficiary at a beneficiary's bank other than the bank in the originator's payment order, that beneficiary's bank must re-credit the beneficiary's account for the amount paid and recover this amount from the bank at fault. The bank at fault shall recover the amount of the originator's payment order from the beneficiary of the

originator.

5- Under this Article, the recovery of funds must be made through a debit transfer, and the refund of funds through a credit transfer.

6- The bank at fault shall be liable to any participant for the principal, interest, and commercially reasonable expenses such as banking fees or information-related expenses arising from such error.

7- For the purposes of this Article, an error may occur or be caused inadvertently, by negligence, or by fraud.

### Section 7

#### Bank Accounts

#### Article 207.-

Subject to the terms of the agreement and except in the circumstances specified in paragraph 2 of this Article, a sufficient credit balance may be withdrawn by the customer or used by the bank to make payment under a payment order. The bank shall not be forced to agree to allow the customer to withdraw funds from a deposit account exceeding the credit balance limit.

The paragraph above of this Article shall not apply when:

- The credit balance in the account is subject to a dispute, seizure, or similar procedure by a creditor;

- The account has been closed, suspended, blocked, or frozen; and

- In executing the payment order or withdrawal order, the bank would violate any law.

The customer and the bank may agree that funds transmitted and received as deposits at the bank for the customer must be distributed according to the customer's instructions to one or more accounts of that customer. Each account shall

subject to separate accounting registry concerning the movement of funds received and paid by the bank for the customer.

#### Article 208.-

Disclosure of information under this Article must be made in writing or electronically, as agreed.

The bank must disclose information to existing and future customers in a pre-prepared and easy-to-understand format concerning the procedures and terms for executing payment transactions, including the bank's charges and, if applicable, the time required to complete the payment transaction.

For any payment transaction or other debit or credit item recorded in the account, the bank must provide the customer with clear information that enables the customer to identify the withdrawal, deposit, or payment transaction, including references, dates, charges, and exchange rates if any.

Except for accounts with passbooks, the bank must regularly provide the customer, at intervals of not more than one month, with an account statement showing the debits and credits recorded since the date of the last statement and also providing the closing balance.

For accounts with passbooks, the bank must facilitate the recording of new transactions in the account sequentially in that passbook. This recording must show each debit or credit item made to the account since the last recording and also show the closing balance. The customer must bring the passbook to the bank for regular recording.

The customer must exercise general care and diligence in reviewing those periodic statements as well as new recordings in the passbook, and notify the bank of any irregularities or errors in the recording

debit or credit item. The bank must assist the customer in tracing and investigating irregularities or disputed debit or credit items, by providing all relevant information available and, if possible, making the necessary corrections and adjustments to the account.

#### Article 209.-

Disclosure of information shall be governed by the following methods:

1- The bank must maintain confidentiality and shall not disclose any information about the account to any person other than the customer who is the account holder, except:

a- When disclosure is required by law;

b- When the interest of the bank requires disclosure;

c- When disclosure is made with the consent of the customer;

d- When disclosure is made upon special request from other banks under a customer reference in the ordinary course of business; or

e- When the customer fails to comply with their obligations to the bank.

2- Disclosure under paragraph 1(b) of this Article shall be made only:

- When there is litigation in which the bank is a party;

- Between banks related to each other through a joint ownership structure, but only to the extent reasonably necessary to protect the bank and its affiliates against loss in providing banking services; or

- in connection therewith and where necessary, the sale of the bank itself or a part of its assets.

3- No disclosure shall be made under paragraph 1(d) of this Article if the customer withdraws consent to such disclosure, unless the bank has explained to the customer their right to withdraw such consent. This explanation may be provided to the customer at the time of opening the account with the bank.

4- Disclosure under paragraph 1(e) of this Article shall be limited to providing information to a bank or any credit agency, with the approval of the National Bank of Cambodia, when there is a default and when the customer's documents for payment do not provide sufficient security, or otherwise, fail to satisfy the bank's request within thirty (30) days after a written request to pay the outstanding obligation.

5- Damages arising to the customer for breach of obligations under this Article shall not be limited only to the loss of the amount of money, and may include compensation for loss of benefits, disturbance, or damage caused.

#### Article 210.-

The bank shall not unilaterally amend the terms of the agreement, unless it has given notice to the customer at least thirty (30) days before the amendment becomes effective. Any amendment contrary to this provision shall not be valid.

#### Article 211.-

In order to be a valid representation of the customer, the agreement must be proper and clearly specify the rights and responsibilities, and must be in the language

simple, using legal and technical language only when necessary.

A bank must exercise constant care in executing payment orders and all other
instructions of its customers.

Customers must exercise care to prevent fraud and unauthorized issuance
or modification of payment orders and other instructions to the bank, and
to ensure that those instructions are clear and certain.

Banks and customers must perform their obligations under this Law, including
regulations and their agreements, in good faith.

Subject to any limitation or special restriction determined by this Law or
other laws, the bank shall be liable to the customer, and the customer
shall be liable to the bank for any loss or damage caused by a breach of
obligation under this Law.

Article 224.-

A bank may deduct fees from the funds or securities deposited in providing
services to the customer, such as safekeeping, transmission, and transfer
of funds as deposits to banks.

A bank may consolidate multiple bank accounts of a customer and deduct credit
balances from one account to:

- pay in accordance with the customer's payment order, if the account to be debited for
that payment order does not have a sufficient balance

- pay a debt that the customer owes and must pay to this bank,
if, in each of the above cases, the bank did not know that the funds in the credit balance
being debited were kept for another person.

Unless otherwise agreed, a bank may use funds, securities, or credit balances
under this Article only when those funds, securities, or credit balances are
available for immediate withdrawal by the customer.

#### Section 8
#### Operation of Payment Systems

Article 225.-

The National Bank of Cambodia has the right to open accounts for customers in its books,
including payment accounts and receiving deposits from national, foreign, domestic banks and
currencies, in accordance with the procedures and conditions as specified by regulations or as
agreed.

Article 226.-

The National Bank of Cambodia shall define, organize, decide, publicize,
and monitor the implementation of payment system policies aimed at serving the
maximum public interest. The payment system policy must be consistent with international
standards. This policy shall promote the safety and efficiency of the payment system, manage risk,
and promote competition in the payment service market that is consistent with the general stability
of the financial system.

Article 227.-

The National Bank of Cambodia may:

- establish and maintain information for the financial system in the Kingdom of
Cambodia

- operate, monitor, follow up the implementation, and regulate the national
payment system for payment transactions and cash withdrawals, including clearing
and settlement systems for domestic currency and foreign currency in the Kingdom of
Cambodia

- maintain a registry for securities issued, establish a center

depositing securities, maintaining accounts for deposited securities, as well as operating,
monitoring, and regulating clearing and settlement systems for securities

- promote, organize, and participate in payment, clearing, and settlement systems
in domestic currency and foreign currency

- assist banks in organizing and using instruments for processing and receiving payment
orders for customers of those banks, as well as for clearing and settlement of interbank payments,
including payments by check and other payment instruments in domestic currency and foreign currency

- regulate, monitor, and follow up the implementation, as well as determine the
conditions and obligations for licensing or registering operators of payment systems and payment
services, including for money transfer, cash withdrawal, cash withdrawal by check, safekeeping
of currency, money exchange, and issuance of payment cards and other payment instruments, and

- establish procedures and issue regulations related to those regulations and the
implementation of its payment system policies under Article 226 of this Law.

Regulations issued pursuant to this Article may validate bilateral and multilateral
interbank clearing agreements, as well as interbank settlement rules, and may specify in particular
that these agreements or rules may prevail over other laws with inconsistent provisions, whether
or not related to insolvency proceedings. Those regulations may also provide prior protection
or risk reduction associated with failure to complete payment obligations resulting from multilateral
interbank clearing.

Without prejudice to the generality of this Article, for the operation of payment systems,
payment services, and all clearing and settlement systems under this Article

the National Bank of Cambodia shall have sufficient power to regulate those operations,
as provided in paragraph 11 of Article 7 of the Law on the Organization and Conduct of the
National Bank of Cambodia.

Article 228.-

A bank must have the capacity to send, receive, and process payment orders and instructions
of relevant customers, including cash withdrawals, and may also participate in payment transactions.
A bank may perform this work through one or more third-party processors as agreed between
the bank and the third-party processor, and subject to obligations, conditions, and restrictions
that may be specified in regulations issued by the National Bank of Cambodia.

Article 229.-

Except as otherwise permitted in any Article, the provisions of this Law may not be varied or
rejected by bilateral or multilateral agreement, except that such agreement may:

1- increase the rights of the customer, provided that such increase does not affect
the rights of other persons;

2- provide appropriate standards specifying the ordinary care of each party, as well
as appropriate methods for providing instructions and any orders under this Law, as well as
agreements;

3- vary the rights and obligations between and among banks, provided that such variation
does not affect the rights of any person who is not a party to the agreement, including non-bank
customers.

#### Chapter 6
#### Penal Provisions

Article 230.-

The National Bank of Cambodia has the right to impose disciplinary sanctions as required
and applied to all persons for failing to pay their debts related to negotiable instruments and
payment orders.

A violation of the regulations provided in the paragraph above of this Article shall be subject
to the following sanctions:

- a warning

- a reprimand

- a prohibition or restriction on the issuance of any or all types of negotiable instruments
or on the granting of the right to make debit transfers for a period not exceeding twelve (12) months

- a fine of five percent (5%) to one hundred percent (100%) of the face value of the
instrument or payment order that was not paid, or the instrument or power to issue, without
prejudice to the prohibition or restriction.

When there are insufficient funds to pay a valid check, the drawee bank that does not pay
this check must record the event of non-payment and report it within two (02) days regarding the
non-payment to the National Bank of Cambodia. The drawer of the check shall not be permitted
to issue checks or be granted the right to make debit transfers for a period of twelve (12) months.
Sufficient funds in each case mean the actual funds that create a positive balance in the account
or are provided by an overdraft facility that the drawer receives from the drawee bank.

The National Bank of Cambodia shall maintain a registry of all bank accounts that

through which checks are issued and debit transfers are made, as well as reporting all events,
and issuing warnings, reprimands, prohibitions, and restrictions under this Article. When issuing
warnings, reprimands, prohibitions, or restrictions, the National Bank of Cambodia shall notify
each bank where the person subject to those sanctions has an account, and shall provide this information
to the bank upon request.

A bank, upon knowing of the prohibition or restriction imposed under paragraph 3 of this
Article, shall require the person subject to the prohibition or restriction to immediately return the
unwritten and unused checks, as well as the authority to clear checks via debit transfer that the
bank has provided to him. A bank, upon knowing of the prohibition or restriction, must be liable
to the holder of the check and the initiator of the debit transfer that has been issued or authorized
by the person subject to the prohibition or restriction, for the total amount of the check or the
debit transfer, if the unwritten check or authority to clear checks via debit transfer was provided to
the person subject to the prohibition or restriction before the bank knew that the prohibition or
restriction had entered into force.

The prohibition or restriction on the issuance of checks or on the granting of the right
to make debit transfers shall be withdrawn when the National Bank of Cambodia has received
notice that payment has been made at the request of the holder. Such payment shall include
bank fees to be paid to the drawee bank, interest, and damages at a minimum rate of five percent
(5%) of the principal amount to be paid to the holder, and a fine of one million (1,000,000) Riels to
be paid to the National Bank of Cambodia. The National Bank of Cambodia shall issue regulations
on the implementation of this obligation.

Article 231.-

Any person who commits an offense of:

- any provision of this Law

- any regulation, instruction, or rule issued by the National Bank of Cambodia related
to any order specified in those regulations, instructions, or rules

- refusing to provide information upon a lawful request permitted under this Law

shall be subject to a fine of five (05) million Riels to ten (10) million Riels or imprisonment
from two (2) years to five (5) years, or both.

Any person who:

- violates the prohibition or restriction imposed under Article 230 of this Law

- grants authority to make a transfer of funds from his account with the intent that
the payment order will not be paid, or

- forges or falsifies a negotiable instrument, an authority to clear checks via debit transfer,
or a payment order, and holds or uses it knowing that the negotiable instrument, authority, or
payment order is forged or falsified

shall be subject to a fine of five (05) million to fifty (50) million Riels and imprisonment from
two (2) years to five (5) years.

Any person who:

- intends to cause harm to others by contesting or withdrawing funds from an account
for payment of a check

- owes a debt on a negotiable instrument with the intent not to pay that instrument
when it is due

- fails to fulfill obligations on a negotiable instrument when it is due, with the intent and
purpose to cause harm to others

shall be subject to a fine of two (02) million to six (06) million Riels and imprisonment from one
(1) year to three (3) years, without prejudice to the obligation to pay the amount of the
negotiable instrument to the holder.

No lawsuit may be brought against the National Bank of Cambodia, officers, staff,
or any director of the National Bank of Cambodia, or any person acting under the direction of the
National Bank of Cambodia, for any act done or omitted in good faith in the exercise of any power or
obligation intended to be or actually implemented under this Law.

Article 232.-

In this Chapter, negotiable instruments such as checks, bills of exchange, and promissory
notes include any instruments that are used or deemed to perform the same function.

For violations by legal entities, any penalty under this Chapter may be applied to any director
and officer according to the hierarchy of that legal entity.

When the violation is committed by any agent, the penalty under this Chapter may be
required for or applied to the principal, the agent, or both.

Penalties under this Chapter may be cumulative and are without prejudice to penalties
under other laws.

#### Chapter 7
#### Final Provisions

Article 233.-

The National Bank of Cambodia shall issue regulations, rules, orders, and instructions,
as well as operating procedures for the implementation of this Law, in particular for:

- a unified structure for identifying and classifying accounts

- models of payment orders

- standards for transmitting payment orders

- forms of checks, bills of exchange, and promissory notes, as well as forms of other
documents to be issued under this Law or by a party or competent officer, such as notices or
irregularities and related procedures

- duties, procedures, and practices of banks in receiving payment orders and other
instructions from customers, and in processing payment transactions

- bank account agreements and information that banks must provide to customers

- information necessary for the appropriate and effective implementation of monetary policy,
which banks must provide to the National Bank of Cambodia, and

- other matters provided for in separate provisions of this Law.

These regulations may be made for consumer protection, integrity, and development
of payment systems and the use of negotiable instruments in the Kingdom of Cambodia. For this
purpose, regulations may restrict, limit, or prohibit the use of checks or other payment orders for
specified types of accounts and persons, or impose conditions for such use. Regulations issued
under this Law may also limit the liability of obligated persons, or limit and condition the rules
for such liability, in connection with negotiable instruments and payment transactions, or to any

accounts used primarily for personal or family purposes and not for business purposes.

Regulations issued by the National Bank of Cambodia under this Law may specify standards
to measure the performance of obligations under this Law and modify the time frame or any final
deadline for doing or not doing activities specified by this Law, for all types or specific types of
participants in negotiable instruments or payment transactions.

Regulations issued by the National Bank of Cambodia under this Law shall be final and binding
on all parties processing payment orders, and shall supersede agreements that are inconsistent,
including interbank settlement rules to the extent of such inconsistency.

Regulations, rules, orders, and instructions issued by the National Bank of Cambodia under
this Law may govern procedures and provide for the timing, rights, and remedies in the event of various
emergencies such as computer breakdown or system failure that disrupts payment operations or the
execution of payment orders. Those regulations, rules, orders, and instructions may suspend all
obligations and deadlines for the duration of such emergency.

All regulations and announcements for general application issued by the National Bank of
Cambodia shall be published in the Royal Gazette. Such regulations and announcements shall enter into
force on the date of publication or on a later date as specifically determined.

Article 234.-

Any provisions that contradict this Law shall be considered null and void. In case of
conflict between this Law and other laws, except as otherwise specifically provided in this
Law, the provisions of this Law shall prevail.
