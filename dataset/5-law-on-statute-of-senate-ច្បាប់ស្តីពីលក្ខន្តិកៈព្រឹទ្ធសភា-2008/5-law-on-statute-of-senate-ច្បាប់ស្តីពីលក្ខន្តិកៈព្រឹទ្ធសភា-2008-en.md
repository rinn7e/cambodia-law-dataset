The Law on Statute of Members of the Senate, which was adopted by the National Assembly on December 06, 2007 during the 7th session of the 3rd legislature, and was entirely approved by the Senate without any amendment on December 27, 2007 during the 4th plenary session of the 2nd legislature, the provisions of which are as follows:

# Law

# on

# Statute of Members of the Senate

# Chapter 1

## General Provisions

### Article 1

This Law aims to determine the parliamentary immunity, rank, privileges, incompatibility, and other necessary conditions to guarantee for the members of the Senate in performing their duties in accordance with the Constitution, laws, and legal instruments of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Article 2

Each member of the Senate is a representative of the entire Khmer nation, not only of their own region.

### Article 3

Members of the Senate have a mandate of 6 (six) years and their mandate shall expire when the new members of the Senate take office.

# Chapter 2

## Senate Immunity

### Article 4

Members of the Senate shall enjoy parliamentary immunity. Parliamentary immunity is divided into two parts:

- The guarantee for expressing opinions or casting votes in any adoption by members of the Senate within the framework of performing their roles as members of the Senate is absolute immunity.

- The guarantee against prosecution, arrest, or detention of members of the Senate under any circumstance is relative immunity.

### Article 5

Members of the Senate shall not abuse this parliamentary immunity in a manner that affects the honor of other individuals, the good customs of society, public order, and national security.

### Article 6

Members of the Senate must respect the Internal Regulations of the Senate of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Article 7

A member of the Senate who has committed a criminal offense may be prosecuted, arrested, detained, or held in custody in accordance with the law and procedures only if their parliamentary immunity has been lifted beforehand.

### Article 8

Any request to lift the parliamentary immunity of any member of the Senate shall be made by the Prosecutor General attached to the Court of Appeal through the Minister of Justice, accompanied by an explanatory statement, and sent to the President of the Senate.

### Article 9

The lifting of parliamentary immunity during the session of the Senate shall follow the procedures below:

- The Senate may meet in closed session upon the request of the President, or of at least one-tenth of the members of the Senate, the King, the Prime Minister, or the President of the National Assembly.

- The meeting must be attended by more than two-thirds of the entire members of the Senate.

- The adoption must be by a two-thirds majority vote of the entire members of the Senate.

### Article 10

For the lifting of parliamentary immunity between Senate sessions, the Standing Committee of the Senate shall meet and approve in accordance with the Internal Regulations of the Senate.

### Article 11.-

The decision of the Standing Committee of the Senate shall be submitted to the upcoming session of the Senate for approval by a two-thirds majority of the entire Senate members.

### Article 12.-

In case any senator commits a flagrant felony, the competent ministry may accuse, arrest, detain or temporarily hold the senator and must immediately report to the Senate or to the Standing Committee of the Senate during the interval between Senate sessions for decision.

### Article 13.-

In all of the above cases, the detention or prosecution of any senator shall be suspended if the Senate decides to suspend by a three-quarters majority of the entire Senate members.

### Article 14.-

A senator whose parliamentary immunity is lifted and who is under court prosecution shall have the same rights and privileges as general senators.

A senator who is under arrest, detention or temporary confinement shall lose the right to attend the meetings of the Senate and various commissions, but shall have the right to receive remuneration, except for session allowances, mission allowances and meeting representation allowances.

A senator against whom a final judgment or final verdict decides as a convict sentenced to imprisonment shall completely lose all rights, privileges and membership as a senator.

### Article 15.-

If any senator is a convict and is pardoned by the King, that senator shall regain the validity of their parliamentary immunity and privileges.

### Article 16.-

Any senator against whom a final judgment or final verdict decides as innocent shall automatically regain their parliamentary immunity and privileges.

## Chapter 3

### Incompatibility

### Article 17.-

The mandate of a senator is incompatible with active public service, with the mandate of a member of the National Assembly, and with the mandate of a member of other institutions as stipulated in the Constitution and laws in force.

## Chapter 4

### Remuneration, Rank and Privileges

### Article 18.-

The Senate shall have an autonomous budget for its functioning.

Senators shall receive remuneration.

Senators shall have the rank and privileges equivalent to the highest-ranking senior official of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

Every senator shall receive social security coverage during their mandate.

In the event of a senator's demise during their mandate, the Standing Committee of the Senate shall be responsible for organizing the funeral and providing remuneration as well as all other allowances to the family of the deceased for another period of one year, counting from the date of the demise.

The Standing Committee of the Senate shall organize the procedures for remuneration, social security regime, health insurance regime, retirement pension regime, mission regime and working conditions of senators.

### Article 19.-

Senators shall receive a retirement pension.

The retirement pension of senators shall be paid at the General Secretariat of the Senate.

The retirement pension can be received at only one place.

The Standing Committee of the Senate shall decide on the retirement of a senator upon their request.

### Article 20.-

Conditions under which former senators receive retirement pensions:

A- Being a former senator, a former member of the National Assembly counting from the year 1993 who has served as a senator, or a former member of the Constitutional Council counting from the year 1998 who has served as a senator.

B- Being 60 (sixty) years of age or older.

C- Not receiving a retirement pension from any other state institution.

### Article 21.-

The formula for calculating the retirement pension scheme of former senators shall be based on the remuneration and all other allowances for a period of one year of each senator in the final year, set at 100% (one hundred percent).

The monthly retirement pension shall be determined based on the length of service as follows:

- From 1 year to 3 years: receiving an index of 0.5%

- More than 3 years to under 6 years: receiving an index of 1%

- From 6 years to under 11 years: receiving an index of 2%

- From 11 years to under 16 years: receiving an index of 3%

- From 16 years and above: receiving an index of 4%

Former senators shall receive the monthly retirement pension until their demise, unless they are re-elected as a senator.

### Article 22.-

Former senators who are not re-elected, after reaching the age of 60 (sixty) years, can receive the retirement pension, starting to be paid for a period of only one year.

### Article 23.-

Senators shall have a number of personal assistants to help with their work under contract staff conditions determined by the Standing Committee of the Senate, with the remuneration being the responsibility of the Senate budget.

In order to facilitate the performance of work, the General Secretariat of the Senate shall assist senators in accordance with the procedures determined in the Internal Regulations of the Senate.

### Article 24.-

Incumbent senators shall receive:

- Parliamentary immunity identification card

- Senate insignia for wearing on shirts

- Inscribed insignia of the Senate

- Senate vehicle license plate

- Personal diplomatic passport for self, spouse, and children under 18 years old

- Senate uniform

- Two decorations of honor per mandate

- Insurance for medical service fees in state hospitals within the Kingdom of Cambodia, paid by the budget of the Senate.

### Article 25.-

Civil servants or members of the armed forces who are elected as senators shall automatically be promoted in rank and class, and shall maintain their seniority for the purpose of receiving retirement pensions.

### Article 26.-

Incumbent male and female senators shall receive the title of His Excellency or Her Excellency. Former male and female senators, after the completion of their mandate, shall maintain the title of His Excellency, Former Senator, or Her Excellency, Former Senator.

## Chapter 5

### Final Provisions

### Article 27.-

Any provisions contrary to this Law shall be abrogated.
