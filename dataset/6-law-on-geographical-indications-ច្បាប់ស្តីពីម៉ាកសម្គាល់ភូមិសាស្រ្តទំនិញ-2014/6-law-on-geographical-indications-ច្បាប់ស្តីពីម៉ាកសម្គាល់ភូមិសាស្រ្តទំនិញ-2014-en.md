### Chapter 1
### General Provisions

#### Article 1.- Purpose

This Law aims to protect the intellectual property of producers and operators, protect consumers of products bearing geographical indications of goods, preserve national identity, strengthen traditional knowledge and know-how, and contribute to poverty reduction.

#### Article 2.- Goal

This Law has the goal to determine the management, registration, recognition, and protection of geographical indications of goods in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 3.- Scope

This Law applies to the registration and protection of geographical indications of goods in the Kingdom of Cambodia for agricultural products, processed food products, handicraft products, or other goods produced in a specific geographical area in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

#### Article 4.- Definitions

Key technical terms used in this Law shall be defined as follows:

Control refers to internal control and external control. Control refers to the verification of compliance of a product with the book of specifications by the geographical indication association of goods and the designated independent certification body.

Use of a geographical indication of goods refers to the use of the sign, production, marketing, export, and import of geographical indication goods.

Evocation refers to a situation where the use of a word or image to identify a product, or the inclusion of a part of the name or image of a registered geographical indication of goods, causes consumers to have the impression that it is the geographical indication goods.

Reputation refers to the widespread recognition of the goods and the name of the goods in the production or trade sector through the use of the relevant goods.

Comparable goods refers to any goods that are related to the objective characteristics or use which are similar to those of the goods.

Geographical indication goods refers to goods bearing a registered geographical indication.

Generic name refers to a name that the general public generally knows as indicating a type of goods.

Opposition refers to the opposition against the registration of any geographical indication by an interested person.

Appeal refers to the appeal against the final decision of the Ministry of Commerce or the Board of Geographical Indications.

Operator refers to the person involved in the collection, processing, commercialization, or distribution of geographical indication goods.

Official Gazette refers to the official gazette of the Ministry of Commerce.

Mark refers to any visible sign capable of distinguishing the goods or services of each enterprise.

Geographical Indication refers to a name, symbol and/or other images which are distinctive, representing the origin of a geographical area, and such name, symbol and/or image is used to identify goods originating from that geographical area, which provides the foundation for the quality, reputation or other characteristics of the goods originating from that geographical area.

Hearing refers to the dispute resolution meeting of the registrar before deciding on any opposition of the opposer.

Counter-statement refers to the submission of response and evidence by any party to contest the statement of the counterparty.

Book of Specifications refers to the document clearly defining key points regarding the geographical area of production, conditions of production, and quality control process of the geographical indication goods, etc., compiled by the applicant.

Certification Body refers to a public institution or private entity that issues a certificate certifying compliance in accordance with the Book of Specifications.

# Chapter 2
## Competent Authority

### Article 5.- Duties of the Ministry of Commerce

The Ministry of Commerce is the competent institution to manage, maintain, and register geographical indications in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Article 6.- Board of Geographical Indications

There shall be established a Board of Geographical Indications to study and promote potential

goods that can be registered as geographical indication goods in the Kingdom of Cambodia, and resolve appeals concerning the registration of geographical indication goods.

The organization and functioning of the Board of Geographical Indications shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

# Chapter 3
## Registration Procedure of Geographical Indications

### Section 1: Applicant and Application

#### Article 7.- Applicant

A geographical indication association, or a group representing producers, or an organization representing producers and/or operators who may benefit from such geographical indication, has the right to file an application for the registration of the geographical indication.

The registration of Cambodian geographical indications shall be made through a geographical indication association. The applicant is the owner of the rights to the geographical indication after registration.

Groups of producers, operators, institutions and/or interested persons wishing to register a Cambodian geographical indication shall organize and establish a geographical indication association based on the geographical area and a specific type of goods, and shall have articles of association to manage and administer its operations on a non-profit basis. Membership of the association shall be open. The geographical indication association must be recognized by the competent authority. The articles of association of the association must be deposited with the Ministry of Commerce. The association must ensure that its members comply with the Book of Specifications and other obligations determined by the laws and regulations in force.

#### Article 8.- Application for Registration

The application for registration of a geographical indication shall be submitted to the Ministry of Commerce, accompanied by the Book of Specifications and relevant files. The application forms, Book of Specifications templates, and relevant files for registration shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Commerce.

### Section 2
#### Application Examination Procedure

#### Article 9.- Examination of the Application for Registration

The Ministry of Commerce shall examine the application and respond within a maximum period of 45 (forty-five) days starting from the date of receipt of the application.

After having examined and found that the application is correctly completed in accordance with the prescribed conditions, the Ministry of Commerce shall officially issue a letter of acknowledgment of the filing of the application.

In case it is found that the application is not yet fully completed in accordance with the prescribed conditions, the Ministry of Commerce shall issue a letter to the applicant to make completions or corrections by specifying the deficiencies. The applicant may complete or correct the application within a period of 06 (six) months starting from the date of issuance of the letter. The application for registration shall be deemed abandoned if there is no completion or correction, or if no response or positive explanation is provided within the above-determined period. The Ministry of Commerce may extend an additional period of 06 (six) months for the correction of the application, if there is a reasonable request from the applicant.

The Ministry of Commerce shall examine the completed or corrected documents and shall respond within a maximum period of 45 (forty-five) days starting from the date of receipt of the completed or corrected documents.

#### Article 10._ Geographical Indications that Cannot be Registered

Geographical Indications cannot be registered if:

a- The indication is contrary to laws, regulations, moral values, society, religion, national traditions, or public order.

b- The indication misleads or confuses the public as to the characteristics, such as the type, quality, place of origin, production process, or usage.

c- The indication is used as the name of a plant variety or animal breed.

d- The indication is a generic name.

#### Article 11._ Substantive Examination of the Application for Registration

The Ministry of Commerce shall examine the substance of the application on the following key points:

1- The accuracy of the details in the application as provided in Article 7 and Article 8 of this Law.

2- The points as provided in Article 4 under the definition of Geographical Indication and Article 10 of this Law.

3- The actual situation at the production site for Cambodian Geographical Indications.

4- For identical existing Geographical Indications applying again, they shall comply with the conditions determined by the Ministry of Commerce.

In the substantive examination of the application, the Ministry of Commerce may invite the applicant or relevant persons to explain or provide additional clarifications. In case of necessity, the Ministry of Commerce

...may request opinions from experts in relevant fields for examination, consideration, and decision.

#### Article 12._ Registration and Publication in the Official Gazette

The Ministry of Commerce shall register and issue a certificate to the applicant after finding that the application is completed in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

After registration, the Ministry of Commerce shall publish the registration of the Geographical Indication of goods in the Official Gazette in order to allow interested parties to file an opposition against the registration.

#### Article 13._ Correction of Recordal

The Ministry of Commerce may correct various errors in recordal made in accordance with this Law or relevant regulations.

#### Article 14._ National Logo for Certifying Geographical Indications

The Ministry of Commerce shall organize and establish a national logo for certifying all Geographical Indication goods registered in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Section 3

### Opposition

#### Article 15._ Period of Opposition

Any interested party may file an opposition against the registration of the Geographical Indication of goods with the Ministry of Commerce within a period of 90 (ninety) days starting from the date of publication of the registration as provided in Article 12 of this Law.

#### Article 16._ Grounds of Opposition

An opposition against the registration of a Geographical Indication of goods shall be based on any of the following grounds:

a- It does not comply with Article 4 under the definition of Geographical Indication of goods of this Law.

b- The registered Geographical Indication of goods is a Geographical Indication of goods that cannot be registered as provided in Article 10 of this Law and other relevant regulations.

#### Article 17.- Opposition Procedure

The Ministry of Commerce shall notify the applicant in writing of the opposition. The applicant shall have 90 (ninety) days from the date of notification to file a counter-statement. The Ministry of Commerce may extend the period for another 90 (ninety) days upon request of the applicant.

The Ministry of Commerce shall notify its decision, with statement of appropriate reasons, to the applicant and the opponent.

The applicant and/or the opponent may appeal against the decision of the Ministry of Commerce in accordance with Article 18 of this Law.

In case of necessity or upon request of any party, the Ministry of Commerce shall organize a hearing on the opposition.

The decision on the opposition shall be published in the Official Gazette.

The detailed procedure of the opposition shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Commerce.

#### Chapter 4
#### Appeal

#### Article 18.- Right and Timeframe of Appeal

Any interested person may appeal against the decision of the Ministry of Commerce to the Board of Appeal for Geographical Indications or to the competent court.

Any interested person may appeal against the decision of the Board of Appeal for Geographical Indications to the competent court.

The appeal shall be filed within 60 (sixty) days from the date of receipt of the notification of the decision.

The decision of the Board of Appeal for Geographical Indications on the appeal shall be published in the Official Gazette and publicly disseminated.

#### Chapter 5
#### Registration of Foreign Geographical Indications

#### Article 19.- Conditions for Registration of Foreign Geographical Indications

The registration of a foreign geographical indication may be done only if that geographical indication has been registered in accordance with the regulations in the country of origin.

The Ministry of Commerce shall not register in the Kingdom of Cambodia any foreign geographical indication which is not protected, or has ceased to be protected, or is no longer used in its country of origin.

The Ministry of Commerce may register a foreign geographical indication only if the application is filed by the legal representative or administrative representative or foreign authority of that country through a legal representative agent as prescribed in Chapter 11 of this Law.

#### Article 20.- Registration Procedure of Foreign Geographical Indications

The application for registration of a foreign geographical indication shall be filed with the Ministry of Commerce. The registration procedure of a foreign geographical indication shall comply with the provisions set forth in Article 7, Article 8, Article 9, Article 10, Article 11, Article 12, and Article 13 of this Law.

After filing the application, the Ministry of Commerce has the right to require the applicant to submit any other information relating to the registration abroad which may serve as a basis for registration in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 21.- Opposition and Appeal against Foreign Geographical Indications

The provisions relating to the timeframe, conditions, opposition procedure, and appeal procedure of this Law shall apply to foreign geographical indications.

#### Chapter 6
#### Effects of Registration

#### Article 22.- Rights Derived from Registration

When a geographical indication is registered at the Ministry of Commerce in accordance with the provisions of this Law, members of the geographical indication association who are producers and/or operators complying with the book of specifications have the right to use that geographical indication. The right of use of a geographical indication cannot be transferred.

The owner of a geographical indication has the right to file a complaint to the competent court against any person who has used their geographical indication in infringement without the owner's consent, and also has the right to file a complaint against any member of the geographical indication association who has used the geographical indication in infringement as prescribed in Article 23 of the Law

... on goods originating from other geographical areas as well.

The rights conferred by the registration of a geographical indication do not extend to the marketing of goods in the Kingdom of Cambodia by the registered owner or with the consent of the owner of that geographical indication.

#### Article 23.- Protection of Cambodian and Foreign Geographical Indications

Cambodian and foreign geographical indications registered in the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be protected against:

A- Any direct or indirect misuse for commercial purposes of a registered geographical indication for goods which are identical or comparable to the registered goods, in order to benefit or may benefit from the reputation of the geographical indication.

B- Any unauthorized use, imitation, evocation, or translation of a geographical indication, even if the true origin of the goods is accompanied by words such as "style", "under type", "type", "method", "manner", "imitation", or translations of these words, or similar terms that mislead the public.

C- Any falsification or misleading indication as to the origin, provenance, or special quality of the goods displayed on any packaging, advertising, or other documents relating to the goods, which is liable to convey a false impression as to the origin of the product.

D- Any other practices liable to mislead the public as to the true origin of the goods.

#### Article 24.- Duration of Protection

The registration of a geographical indication shall be valid from the date of filing the application for registration, unless it is cancelled or invalidated in accordance with this Law.

#### Article 25.- Recognition of Registration

Geographical indications which have been duly registered prior to the entry into force of this Law shall remain valid.

### Chapter VII
### Control of Conformity of the Book of Specifications in the Registration of Geographical Indications of Goods

#### Article 26.- Control of Conformity

The applicant may select a registered certification body or control body legally established in the Kingdom of Cambodia and accredited by the Ministry of Commerce. The certification body or control body shall be a competent public authority or a private body officially accredited by an international standardization organization or any body having a reliable control system.

In the post-registration control, the Ministry of Commerce may invite the right holder or any related person to provide explanations or additional evidence. If necessary, the Ministry of Commerce may request opinions from experts in the relevant fields for examination, consideration, and decision.

The certification body or control body shall verify the conformity of the geographical indication goods with the book of specifications. The certification body or control body shall submit an annual report to the Ministry of Commerce every year, including a list of producers, operators, products, quantities certified, and measures taken, if any. The Ministry of Commerce shall determine the date for submitting the report based on the harvest season or the end of the fiscal year. For documents in languages other than Khmer, a Khmer translation with certification of accuracy must be attached.

In the event that one or more producers or operators fail to comply with the requirements in the book of specifications, the certification body or control body shall report to the Ministry of Commerce to implement appropriate measures as follows:

1- Notice of error and guidance.

2- Warning.

3- Rejection of certification of any batch of products.

4- Temporary suspension of the right to use the geographical indication.

5- Permanent prohibition of the use of the geographical indication.

The geographical indication association shall comply with the decision of the Ministry of Commerce.

The geographical indication association shall report to the Ministry of Commerce on the implementation of the measures of the Ministry.

#### Article 27.- Effectiveness of the Certifying Body or the Compliance Control Body

The Ministry of Commerce shall examine the effectiveness of the certifying body or control body. In case of lack of effectiveness, the Ministry of Commerce has the special authority in writing, with statement of reasons for the certifying body or the control body, and may allow the applicant to apply for the selection of a new certifying body or control body.

In case of necessity, the Ministry of Commerce may determine additional regulations for control after registration of geographical indications.

### Chapter 8
### Amendment, Invalidity and Cancellation of Geographical Indication from the Registry

#### Article 28.- Amendment

After registration, the right holder may request to amend the book of specifications, especially on points related to the development of technical and scientific knowledge and the determination of geographical boundaries, etc.

Any interested person may apply to the Ministry of Commerce to amend the registration and/or the book of specifications.

The amendment of the book of specifications may be made only if it does not affect the essential points of the decision to register the geographical indication.

#### Article 29.- Grounds for Invalidity and Cancellation of Geographical Indication from the Registry

Any interested person may submit observations to the Ministry of Commerce in order to provide guidance to the association.

Any interested person may request the Ministry of Commerce to invalidate or cancel the registration of the geographical indication.

The Ministry of Commerce may consider the registration invalid if:

1- Any condition of Article 4 concerning the definition of geographical indication, Article 7 and Article 10 of this Law is not fulfilled.

2- It is convinced, based on evidence, that the registered owner of the geographical indication is not the legitimate owner of the geographical indication.

The Ministry of Commerce has the right to cancel the registered geographical indication in the event that

1- The registered owner of the geographical indication requests cancellation.

2- The registered geographical indication goods have lost their special characteristics as geographical indication goods.

3- The right holder does not respect and does not comply with the conditions specified in the application.

4- A foreign geographical indication which is not protected or has ceased to be protected, or has not been subsequently used in its country of origin.

5- The contact address in the Kingdom of Cambodia of the geographical indication owner cannot be contacted at least three times by all means within a period of three months.

6- The registered owner of the geographical indication does not comply with the conditions of the control of supporting documents as provided in Article 17 of this Law.

7- The right holder acts contrary to the laws and regulations related to geographical indications.

#### Article 30.- Effect of Invalidity and Cancellation of Registration

The Ministry of Commerce shall examine observations, requests for invalidity, or requests for cancellation by reviewing actual evidence and analyzing in order to reach a decision to maintain, amend, or cancel the geographical indication from the registry in accordance with the laws and regulations in force.

The procedures for the decision to maintain, amend, or cancel the geographical indication from the registry shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Commerce.

The invalidity of the registration of the geographical indication shall be effective from the date of registration.

The cancellation of the geographical indication shall be effective from the date of the decision.

All invalidations and cancellations of geographical indications shall be recorded and published in the Official Gazette and disseminated to the public immediately.

### Chapter 9
### Geographical Indication and Recognition or Refusal of Trademark

#### Article 31.- Geographical Indication and Trademark

The Ministry of Commerce shall refuse the registration of any trademark which is identical or similar to, and leads to confusion with, a geographical indication which has been filed prior to the filing date of the trademark application, for the same goods as provided in Article 23 of this Law.

Once a geographical indication has been registered in accordance with this Law, any application for registration of a trademark that is identical or similar to any of the situations in Article 23 of this Law and relates to the same type of goods shall be rejected.

The Ministry of Commerce must reject the application for trademark registration anew, if such application violates any of the provisions set forth in Article 23 of this Law. This decision of rejection shall be made by the Ministry of Commerce ex-officio or at the request of an interested person.

The use of a trademark related to any point of Article 23 of this Law which has been registered in good faith prior to the date of protection of the geographical indication, or prior to the filing date of the application for registration of the geographical indication in the Kingdom of Cambodia, may be permitted to continue to be used, provided that there are no grounds for invalidation.

# Chapter 10
## Provisional Measures and Border Measures

### Article 32.- Application of Provisional Measures and Border Measures

All provisions concerning provisional measures and border measures provided in the Law on Marks, Trade Names and Acts of Unfair Competition, as well as other regulations related to provisional measures and border measures, shall apply mutatis mutandis to geographical indications.

# Chapter 11
## Agent

### Article 33.- Representation

In case the enterprise or the principal place of business of the applicant is located outside the Kingdom of Cambodia, the applicant shall be represented by an agent residing and practicing in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Article 34.- Qualifications

The representative who acts as an agent in applying for registration of a geographical indication in the Kingdom of Cambodia must have sufficient qualifications, as provided in the Declaration on the Legal Representative Agent of Trademark Owners in the Kingdom of Cambodia of the Ministry of Commerce.

# Chapter 12
## Regulations and Administrative Guidelines

### Article 35.- Implementing Regulations

The Minister of Commerce shall issue necessary regulations providing detailed instructions on the implementation of this Law.

Applicants for registration of geographical indication protection shall pay administrative fees for registration of the geographical indication and other related fees, which shall be determined by a Joint Declaration of the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance and the Minister of Ministry of Commerce.

# Chapter 13
## Implementation of International Treaties and Interpretation

### Article 36.- Implementation

The provisions of international treaties concerning geographical indications to which the Kingdom of Cambodia is a party shall also be implemented in conjunction with this Law.

In case of conflict with the rules of this Law, the provisions of those international treaties shall be considered as the primary provisions.

# Chapter 14
## Detention and Confiscation

### Article 37.- Detention and Confiscation

The competent court has the power to order the detention of goods if there is a suspicion that those goods infringe the rights of the right holder.

The provisions of paragraph 1 above shall also apply if there is a suspicion that materials and equipment mainly used in the production of those goods infringe the rights of the right holder.

Goods that infringe the rights of the right holder which are imported, exported, sold, or offered for sale shall be confiscated by the competent authority in accordance with the decision of the court, whether or not anyone has been convicted of the offense.

# Chapter 15
## Penal Provisions

### Article 38.- Offense of Counterfeiting Geographical Indications

Shall be punished by imprisonment from 1 (one) year to 5 (five) years and a fine from 2,000,000 (two million) Riels to 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels for:

- Any incorrect direct or indirect commercial use of a registered geographical indication for identical goods or goods comparable to the registered goods, in order to benefit or possibly benefit from the reputation of the geographical indication of the goods

- Any unauthorized use, imitation, evoking, or translation of the geographical indication of goods, even if the true origin of the goods is accompanied by words such as "style", "style/type", "type", "method", "style", "imitation", or translation of these words, or similar words that cause public confusion

- Any falsification or misleading of the origin, source, or special quality of goods shown on packaging of all kinds, or on advertising materials, or on other documents related to the goods, which may lead to misunderstanding of the origin of the product

- Other acts that may mislead the general public about the true origin of the goods.

#### Article 39.- Criminal Liability of Legal Entities

Legal entities may be declared criminally liable under the conditions set forth in Article 42 (Criminal liability of legal entities) of the Penal Code for the offenses set forth in Article 38 (Counterfeiting of geographical indication of goods) above.

Legal entities shall be punished by a fine of 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels to 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels, as well as one or more of the following additional penalties:

1- Dissolution in the manner prescribed by Article 170 (Dissolution of legal entity and liquidation of legal entity) of the Penal Code.

2- Placement under judicial supervision in the manner prescribed by Article 171 (Placement under judicial supervision) of the Penal Code.

3- Prohibition from performing one or more activities in the manner prescribed by Article 172 (Prohibition from performing activities) of the Penal Code.

4- Exclusion from public procurement in the manner prescribed by Article 173 (Exclusion from public procurement) of the Penal Code.

5- Confiscation of objects or instruments which are the subject of the offense in the manner prescribed by Article 178 (Confiscation of ownership, sale, destruction of confiscated objects) and Article 179 (Confiscation and rights of third parties) of the Penal Code.

6- Confiscation of proceeds or property arising from the offense in the manner prescribed by Article 178 (Confiscation of ownership, sale, destruction of confiscated objects) and Article 179 (Confiscation and rights of third parties) of the Penal Code.

7- Posting of the sentencing decision in the manner prescribed by Article 180 (Posting of the decision) of the Penal Code.

8- Publication of the sentencing decision in the print media or publication by all means in the manner prescribed by Article 181 (Publication of the decision by telecommunication, audiovisual media, etc.) of the Penal Code.

### Chapter 16
### Transitional Provisions

#### Article 40.- Transitional Provisions

Legal regulations currently in force shall continue to be effective until they are replaced by new legal regulations.

### Chapter 17
### Final Provisions

#### Article 41.- Abrogation

Any provisions of other laws in force at the time this Law is implemented which are contrary to the provisions of this Law shall be considered to have no effect to the extent of their contradiction with this Law, from the date this Law is implemented.
