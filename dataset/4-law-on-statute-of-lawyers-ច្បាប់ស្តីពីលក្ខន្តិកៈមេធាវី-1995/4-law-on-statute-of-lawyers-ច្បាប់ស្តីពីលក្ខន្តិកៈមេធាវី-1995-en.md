#### DO HEREBY PROMULGATE

The Law on the Statute of Lawyers, which was adopted by the National Assembly on June 15, 1995, its full text being as follows:

#### Law

#### on the

#### Statute of Lawyers

#### Chapter 1: General Provisions

#### Article 1.-

The legal profession is an independent and free profession that serves the field of justice.

The legal profession may only it be practiced within the framework of the Bar Association.

#### Article 2.-

Lawyers may represent clients with their consent or assist in defending clients before all courts and at all stages of legal proceedings, unless otherwise provided by law, especially in civil, commercial, administrative, labor, and social security matters.

In criminal cases, lawyers assist in defending the accused and may not represent the accused except in cases where special legal provisions apply. Lawyers may represent persons liable in civil proceedings and civil plaintiffs.

Lawyers may also assist in defending clients before disciplinary councils.

#### Article 3.-

Lawyers provide advice and draft various documents in the legal field.

Lawyers may be appointed by parties or by a judge to fulfill functions as

conciliator or mediator. Lawyers may be appointed as arbitrators only if permitted by law.

#### Article 4.-

Other than lawyers who are members of the Bar Association, no person may engage in the profession of providing legal consultation or drafting legal documents for a fee, except where such legal consultation or drafting of documents is an incidental part of their profession or is a function authorized by law.

#### Article 5.-

Foreign lawyers who are registered with a foreign Bar Association whose home country recognizes their right to practice law have the right to practice in association with Khmer lawyers and assist Khmer lawyers before the courts or other institutions of the Kingdom of Cambodia. Such lawyers may not represent clients.

#### Article 6.-

Foreign lawyers may practice law in the territory of the Kingdom of Cambodia only with the approval of the Council of the Khmer Bar Association, based on sufficient qualifications of that lawyer, and only if the home country of that foreign lawyer provides reciprocal opportunities to Khmer lawyers. Such approval may be revoked in case of professional misconduct in practicing law within the territory of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

A decision to refuse or revoke permission by the Bar Council to practice law must be notified within 15 days to the Prosecutor General at the Court of Appeal and to the person concerned. Such a decision may be appealed within 2 months after receiving the notification.

#### Article 7.-

Foreign lawyers who are permitted to practice this profession may not engage in client solicitation or commercial advertising.

#### Chapter 2: Organization of the Legal Profession

#### Section 1: The Bar Association

#### Article 8.-

The Bar Association of the Kingdom of Cambodia is an organization that brings together all lawyers who have established offices in the Kingdom of Cambodia. Each lawyer must be a full member of the Bar Association after being registered on the Bar Roll.

#### Article 9.-

The Bar Association is led by a President and managed by a Bar Council. The President of the Bar Association is elected for a 2-year term. This term may be renewed only once through an election.

#### Article 10.-

The number of members of the Bar Council is as follows:

- 5 members in case the Bar Association has no more than 30 members.

- 9 members if there are 31 to 50 members.

- 13 members if there are 51 to 200 members.

- 19 members if there are 201 to 500 members.

- 27 members if there are 501 to 1,000 members.

- 33 members if there are more than 1,001 members.

#### Article 11.-

Members of the Bar Council are elected for a 3-year term. This term may be renewed once, but cannot be consecutive. Only after a 3-year period following the end of their term may former members of the Bar Council stand for re-election. The President of the Bar Association may be elected as a member of the Bar Council immediately after finishing their term without having to wait for 3 years.

#### Article 12.-

If the President of the Bar Association or any member of the Bar Council dies or resigns during their term, an election must be organized to choose a replacement to complete that term.

#### Article 13.-

The Bar Association is not under the control of any political party, religious organization, or any other organizations.

The expression of ideological, religious, or political opinions is prohibited.

The Bar Association has an autonomous budget but may not engage in profit-making activities.

#### Section 2: Elections

#### Article 14.-

The President of the Bar Association and members of the Bar Council are elected separately inside the General Assembly of the Bar Association.

Those eligible to vote are all lawyers who are registered on the

Bar Association and have not lost their right to vote through any disciplinary sanction.

#### Article 15.-

Those eligible to stand for election as the President of the Bar Association are lawyers who have been registered on the Bar Roll for at least 3 years and have not lost their right to vote through any disciplinary sanction.

Those eligible to stand for election as members of the Bar Council are lawyers who have been registered on the Bar Roll for at least 2 years and have not lost their right to vote.

#### Article 16.-

Elections are organized by the General Assembly of the Bar Association every 2 years for the President of the Bar Association and every 3 years for the members of the Bar Council.

This election shall be conducted by secret ballot and individual nomination, and may be held in two rounds. The first round shall be decided by an absolute majority of all members of the Bar Association, and the second round by simple majority of members present. In case of a tie, the oldest lawyer shall be elected.

#### Article 17.-

The procedures for the election, the invitation to the meeting, and the quorum shall be determined by the Internal Regulations. Lawyers who have the right to vote or the Prosecutor General at the Court of Appeal may challenge the results of this election before the Court of Appeal.

The complaint must be filed by a lawyer within 15 days after the election, or by the Prosecutor General at the Court of Appeal within 15 days.

starting from the date of receiving notification of the election results.

#### Section 3: General Assembly

#### Article 18.-

The ordinary General Assembly of the Bar Association meets once a year, convened by the President of the Bar Association, based on an agenda determined by the Bar Council.

The General Assembly may make suggestions or file complaints to the Bar Council, which must be resolved within 3 months.

An extraordinary General Assembly may be held if 30% of the lawyers registered on the Bar Roll request it with a proposed agenda at least 15 days before the meeting date.

The organization and functioning of the General Assembly of the Bar Association shall be determined in the Internal Regulations of the Bar Association.

#### Section 4: The Bar Council

#### Article 19.-

The Bar Council considers and resolves all issues related to the practice of the legal profession. The Bar Council ensures the fulfillment of duties and the protection of the rights of lawyers. The Bar Council has the following primary duties:

- Drafting Internal Regulations and the Code of Ethics.

- Deciding on the registration of internships and registration on the Bar Roll.

- Deciding on all authorization requests made by lawyers.

- Reviewing all agreements and documents that lawyers are required to submit for review and providing advice and guidance on those agreements and documents.

- Monitoring the regular maintenance of accounting records by lawyers.

- Ensuring compliance with discipline and the imposition of disciplinary measures.

- Ensuring the organization and management of general services of the Bar Association.

- Managing the assets and budget of the Bar Association, determining the amount of contributions from each lawyer, and paying collective insurance premiums for the legal profession.

- Managing the fund of the Bar Association.

- Providing opinions on all matters related to the field of justice and the rights of lawyers in the field of defense, especially when invited by public authorities.

#### Article 20.-

The Bar Council may meet only if more than half of its members are present. The Bar Council shall make decisions by a majority vote.

#### Article 21.-

Decisions of the Bar Council that have the nature of regulations must be notified to the Prosecutor General at the Court of Appeal by registered mail or by hand delivery with an acknowledgement of receipt. This also applies to decisions on measures such as registration or requests to be placed outside the Bar Roll. The decisions mentioned in the above paragraph must be communicated to the members of the Bar Association through appropriate means.

#### Article 22.-

All decisions of the Bar Council concerning a specific lawyer must be notified to that lawyer by registered mail or by hand delivery with an acknowledgement of receipt.

#### Article 23.-

Decisions of the Bar Council that are outside its competence or contrary to existing laws or regulations may be annulled by the Court of Appeal upon a request from the Prosecutor General at the Court of Appeal.

#### Article 24.-

A lawyer may challenge any decision of the Bar Council that is detrimental to their professional interests. If the decision has not been notified to the lawyer, the lawyer must request the Bar Council to review the decision again before filing an appeal. The Bar Council must decide on this request within 2 months after inviting the lawyer to present their observations. The new decision must be notified to the person concerned. If no new decision is made within the specified period, the previous decision shall be considered null and void.

#### Article 25.-

Complaints against decisions of the Bar Council must be submitted to the Court of Appeal. The complaint may be filed with the clerk of the Court of Appeal or by registered mail with an acknowledgement of receipt. The period for filing an appeal is 2 months from the date of receiving the notification.

#### Article 26.-

The Bar Council shall submit to the Minister of Justice for review

and publicly publish an annual report on the activities of the Bar Association. The Bar Council must present the financial situation and provide various information to the Minister of Justice upon his request.

#### Section 5: President of the Bar Association

#### Article 27.-

The President of the Bar Association has the following roles:

- Leading the Bar Association.

- Presiding over the General Assembly of the Bar Association and the Bar Council.

- Ensuring the interests of the profession and the interests of all members.

- Acting as a conciliator and, if necessary and upon request from the parties, acting as an arbitrator in disputes between lawyers, or between lawyers and clients.

- Representing the legal profession before third parties and public authorities.

- Representing the Bar Association in legal cases concerning the Bar Association after receiving authorization from the Bar Council.

In voting, the vote of the President of the Bar Association does not have casting power.

#### Article 28.-

The President of the Bar Association may delegate any or all of their powers to one or more members of the Bar Council for a limited period.

In case of impediment, the President of the Bar Association shall be replaced by a member of

the Bar Council who is the oldest in age.

#### Section 6: Fund of the Bar Association

#### Article 29.-

The Fund of the Bar Association is derived from the contributions of all members and various other donations. Within this Fund, a special fund must be established to provide fees to lawyers who defend poor persons.

This special fund may receive gifts or aid from private individuals, international organizations, or governments of various countries for the purpose of defending poor persons.

All lawyers have the obligation to help defend poor persons according to the procedures set out in the Internal Regulations and must help protect their clients' interests.

#### Article 30.-

Poor persons are defined as persons who have no assets, no income, or whose income is insufficient to support their livelihood.

The determination of poverty shall be made by the President of the Court and the Chief Prosecutor after conducting an investigation at the local level.

#### Chapter 3: Admission to the Legal Profession and the Bar Roll

#### Section 1: Conditions

#### Article 31.-

Persons who may be admitted to practice the legal profession as lawyers must fulfill the following conditions:

1- Must be of Khmer nationality.

2- Must have a Bachelor of Laws degree or a legal diploma declared as equivalent.

3- Must have a Certificate of Aptitude for the Legal Profession (CAPA). This certificate shall be issued by the Lawyer Training Center. The organization and functioning of this center shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

4- Must have never been convicted of a felony or misdemeanor, or have never been subject to disciplinary or administrative sanctions such as removal from the roll, dismissal from office, or loss of title for acts contrary to honor or for integrity issues. Must not have personal bankruptcy declared by a court.

#### Article 32.-

The following are exempted from the requirement for a Certificate of Aptitude for the Legal Profession and a Bachelor of Laws degree:

- Judges who have fulfilled their professional functions for more than 5 years, and former judges who have a secondary legal diploma and have fulfilled their professional functions for more than 2 years.

The following are exempted from the requirement for a Certificate of Aptitude for the Legal Profession:

- Holders of a Bachelor of Laws degree who have fulfilled professional functions in legal or judicial fields for more than 2 years.

- Lawyers of Khmer origin who are registered on a Bar Roll abroad.

- Holders of a Doctorate of Laws.

#### Section 2: Admission to Practice the Legal Profession

#### Article 33.-

Admission to practice the legal profession as a lawyer shall be decided by the Bar Council after verifying fulfillment of the legal conditions set out in this law and after receiving the opinion of the Prosecutor General at the Court of Appeal.

The decision shall be notified to the party concerned and to the Prosecutor General at the Court of Appeal.

The Bar Council may not reject an application without having summoned the party concerned to present their observations at least 10 days in advance. This summons shall be delivered by registered mail or by hand delivery with an acknowledgement of receipt.

The decisions of the Bar Council may be challenged under the conditions set out in Article 25 above.

#### Article 34.-

Lawyers who are admitted to practice the legal profession must first take an oath before the Court of Appeal in the presence of the President of the Bar Association, with the following text:

"I swear to practice my profession with dignity, conscience, integrity and humanity."

with an independent spirit and in respect for the Constitution and laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Section 3: Internship

#### Article 35.-

Newly registered lawyers must complete a 1-year internship according to the procedures determined by the Internal Regulations of the Bar Association, except for those who are exempted from internship. The methods of the internship include:

- Receiving additional training organized by the Bar Association.

- Participating effectively in work as an associate in a law office.

#### Article 36.-

The internship shall be conducted under the supervision of a lawyer appointed by the Bar Council as an internship supervisor.

#### Article 37.-

Upon completion of the internship, the Bar Council shall decide on the registration on the Bar Roll based on the report of the internship supervisor. The Bar Council may decide to extend the internship for a period not exceeding 1 year for any intern who has not yet demonstrated sufficient aptitude. A decision not to register on the Bar Roll shall result in the removal of the lawyer from the Bar. A decision not to register on the Bar Roll or to extend the internship may only be made after summoning the party concerned to present their observations, under the conditions set out in Article 33.

This decision may be challenged according to the conditions and procedures set out in Articles 24 and 25.

#### Article 38.-

Every year, the Bar Council shall finalize the Bar Roll and the list of lawyer interns, and send these lists to the Prosecutor General and all jurisdictions.

The lists include:

- The names of lawyers registered on the Bar Roll and their addresses;

- The names and addresses of lawyer interns.

#### Article 39.-

The order of registration of lawyers shall be determined by the date of taking the oath as determined by the Bar Council that decided on the registration.

#### Section 4: Omission from the Roll (Off-Roll Status)

#### Article 40.-

For legitimate reasons, a lawyer may request to be omitted from the Bar Roll for a maximum period of two years at a time.

#### Article 41.-

The Bar Council may decide to place a lawyer on the off-roll list if the lawyer is in a situation of incompatibility or if the lawyer is unable to practice their profession.

In case of a complaint against the above decision, the procedures set out in Article 33 shall apply.

#### Article 42.-

A lawyer who is omitted from the Bar Roll may not practice their profession nor use the title of lawyer, and is exempted from all professional obligations. However, they remain under the control of the Bar Association.

The omission from the Roll does not prevent disciplinary prosecution.

#### Section 5: Honorary Lawyer Status

#### Article 43.-

The Bar Council may grant the title of Honorary Lawyer to lawyers who have retired from the profession after having practiced for at least 15 years.

#### Article 44.-

Honorary lawyers remain members of the Bar Association and may participate in the General Assembly with the right to provide opinions and attend various ceremonies.

Furthermore, honorary lawyers may provide advice to clients and may be appointed as conciliators, mediators, or arbitrators.

#### Article 45.-

The Bar Council may revoke the title of Honorary Lawyer if there are reasons that affect the honor or dignity of the lawyer. In this case, the procedures set out in Article 33 shall apply.

#### Chapter 4: Forms of Practice

#### Section 1: Individual or Collective Practice

#### Article 46.-

Lawyers may practice their profession individually or within the framework of a group or a law firm established in accordance with the law.

These firms must have the status of a civil company and consist exclusively of lawyers as members. Honorary lawyers may remain members of their original firm.

Successors (Heirs) who are entitled to inherit from a deceased lawyer may continue to hold shares for a maximum period of 3 years. Within these 3 years, they must sell those shares to a lawyer or back to the law firm.

#### Article 47.-

Law groups and firms are governed by the law on companies and groups, except where those regulations conflict with the principles of governance of the legal profession.

#### Article 48.-

Lawyers are permitted to enter into agreements with members of other liberal professions to pool resources. These agreements must not conflict with the rules of the legal profession, particularly those concerning professional secrecy.

#### Article 49.-

All agreements entered into by a lawyer with another lawyer, with a foreign lawyer, or with a member of another liberal profession must be submitted to the Bar Council for review and approval, and the Prosecutor General at the Court of Appeal must be notified. In this case, the procedures set out in Article 33 shall apply.

#### Section 2: Collaboration (Associate Status)

#### Article 50.-

A lawyer may practice their profession as an associate of another lawyer.

#### Article 51.-

An associate lawyer performs various tasks in the name and under the responsibility of their colleague (confrere). They may not perform tasks without the consent of the colleague, but they have full freedom in choosing the means to perform their work. They may refuse any assignment that they consider contrary to their conscience.

#### Article 52.-

In all cases, collaboration cannot prevent an associate lawyer from establishing their own office or from respecting the obligations and rules of the legal profession. They shall act in their own name when defending their own clients.

The collaboration agreement must be drawn up in writing and submitted to the Bar Council.

#### Chapter 5: Professional Rules and Discipline

#### Section 1: Incompatibility

#### Article 53.-

The legal profession is incompatible with public office and commercial activities, whether practiced directly or indirectly.

#### Article 54.-

A lawyer who takes office in the Royal Government or is elected as a Member of Parliament may remain a member of the Bar Association but must cease practicing the profession until the end of their term or mandate.

#### Article 55.-

Lawyers who are former civil servants may represent clients in cases against the ministry or administrative body they previously served under only after 5 years have elapsed from the date they left their position.

#### Section 2: Discipline

#### Article 56.-

The professional rules of lawyers are determined by the Internal Regulations of the Bar Association. These rules ensure that lawyers respect the principles of their oath.

#### Article 57.-

Any act of individual solicitation, advertisement, or persistent seeking of cases from clients is prohibited.

Only collective professional publicity is permitted. This publicity must be accurate and must not affect the dignity of the legal profession.

#### Article 58.-

Lawyers must maintain absolute professional secrecy (attorney-client privilege). Based on their conscience and with the client's consent, the lawyer shall determine what needs to be raised to protect the interests of the client for whom they are responsible.

A lawyer may not violate professional secrecy and cannot be compelled to violate professional secrecy, even before a court.

Professional secrecy covers consultations and advice, all informal documents drafted by the lawyer for their client, and all correspondence between the lawyer and their client.

#### Section 3: Disciplinary Sanctions

#### Article 59.-

Any lawyer who violates professional rules or commits any act that affects morality or professional honor shall be subject to disciplinary sanctions, even if the act was committed outside of professional practice.

#### Article 60.-

Disciplinary action shall be initiated by the Bar Council on its own motion, or upon a complaint from a client, or upon a complaint from the Prosecutor General at the Court of Appeal. The Bar Council shall appoint a lawyer as a rapporteur. The complaint must be notified in advance to the lawyer concerned, who must be informed of the file and may present their defense. If an investigation is conducted, the resulting information must도 be provided to the lawyer concerned. The rapporteur's report must be sent to the lawyer concerned along with a summons, and the file must be made available for their review. The summons must be sent by registered mail with an acknowledgement of receipt or by a written notice at least 15 days before the meeting date. The lawyer concerned may choose a lawyer to defend them. However, they may not be represented by any other person unless special leave is granted beforehand for legitimate reasons.

If there is no response to a complaint from the Prosecutor General or a third party within 2 months, the lack of response from the Bar Council shall be considered as a rejection of the complaint. This rejection may be appealed within 2 months after the expiration of the initial 2-month period.

#### Article 61.-

Decisions of the Bar Council, which must state the reasons, shall be notified to the lawyer concerned and the Prosecutor General within 15 days of the decision date by registered mail or by hand delivery with an acknowledgement of receipt.

#### Article 62.-

An appeal may be filed by the lawyer concerned or by the Prosecutor General to the Court of Appeal within the timeframe and form set out in Article 25. The appeal hearing shall be held in camera unless there is a decision to the contrary.

of the Court of Appeal upon the explicit request of the lawyer concerned.

The Bar Association is not a party to this case, but the President of the Bar Association must present their observations and the Prosecutor General must make their closing remarks at that time.

#### Article 63.-

Disciplinary sanctions include:

- Warning;

- Reprimand;

- Suspension from practice for a period not exceeding 2 years;

- Removal from the Bar Roll or from the list of interns, or revocation of the honorary title.

#### Article 64.-

The suspension from practice may be suspended (stayed). However, if within 5 years after the stay is granted, the lawyer is subject to a new disciplinary sanction, the suspension shall be applied by adding the new sentence to the previous stayed sentence.

#### Article 65.-

All sanctions may be supplemented by a ban on serving as President of the Bar Association or as a member of the Bar Council for a period not exceeding 5 years. The disciplinary decision shall also determine whether the decision should be made public if necessary.

#### Article 66.-

The Bar Council may temporarily suspend a lawyer from their functions if they are charged with a criminal offense or a disciplinary offense by virtue of a regulation or a complaint from the Prosecutor General at the Court of Appeal.

If the Bar Council does not respond to the Prosecutor General's request within 20 days, the lack of response shall be considered as a rejection of the request.

#### Article 67.-

A temporary suspension may not be decided without having summoned the lawyer concerned to present their defense. The summons shall be sent by registered mail or delivered by hand with an acknowledgement of receipt at least 5 days before the meeting date. The temporary suspension may be appealed under the conditions set out in Article 62. The appeal does not stay the execution of the suspension decision.

The Prosecutor General at the Court of Appeal shall ensure and monitor the execution of disciplinary sanctions and temporary suspensions.

#### Section 4: Fees

#### Article 68.-

Lawyers shall receive fees based on an agreement with the client, considering the scope of work, the expertise demonstrated, the difficulty of the case, the results achieved, or based on an hourly rate schedule determined annually by the Bar Association. Lawyers must issue a receipt to the client upon receiving fees.

#### Article 69.-

Disputes regarding fees, besides being subject to civil litigation, may be submitted to the President of the Bar Association for arbitration if the parties agree. The decision shall be notified to the parties by registered mail or hand delivery.

by hand with an acknowledgement of receipt. The appeal must be filed within 2 months after receiving the notification of the decision. The appeal shall be filed at the Registry of the Court of Appeal or sent by registered mail or delivered by hand with an acknowledgement of receipt. The appeal shall be heard by the President of the Court of Appeal or by a person authorized by the President.

#### Chapter 6: Accounting, Use of Funds, Guarantees and Insurance

#### Article 70.-

Lawyers registered on the Bar Roll are not authorized to personally use funds belonging to their clients or funds held for their clients while performing their missions for clients. These funds include security deposits for litigation and sums recovered after winning a case. Lawyers must deposit these funds into a special account opened by the Bar Association, named the "Bar Association Settlement Office" (CARPA).

Lawyers may not keep these funds in that account for longer than the strictly necessary time to complete the task entrusted by the client. Lawyers may not deduct their fees from these funds unless they have obtained written consent from the client.

Lawyers may use the funds deposited by clients to pay court costs and fees for other legal professionals involved in the client's case.

#### Article 71.-

Lawyers may be appointed as escrow agents (fiduciary/depositary) by agreement between parties or by a judge. The agreement or decision on the escrow must specify the terms, particularly the interest terms and the procedures for terminating the escrow.

terms. Funds or objects must be kept in the Bar Association Settlement Office.

#### Article 72.-

Lawyers must maintain separate professional accounting records in accordance with the law and internal regulations. Payments from client funds or escrowed objects must be made from a separate account.

Lawyers must present their professional accounts and, if applicable, their personal accounts to the inspectors appointed by the Bar Council.

#### Article 73.-

Lawyers must demonstrate that they have paid for professional indemnity insurance through the Bar Association to cover their professional liability and to guarantee the restitution of funds and valuables they have received. These insurance premiums shall be included in the contributions determined by the Bar Association.

#### Chapter 7: Replacement and Administrative Management

#### Article 74.-

A lawyer who is temporarily unable to practice their profession may appoint a substitute with the approval of the President of the Bar Association. If no substitute is appointed, the President of the Bar Association shall appoint one by decree.

#### Article 75.-

A decision of suspension from practice for a period of time or temporary suspension from practice

of practice, or omission from the Bar Roll, a lawyer shall be appointed to manage the substitute law office. If that manager is unable to fulfill their duties, the President of the Bar Association shall issue a decision to appoint a replacement.

#### Article 76.-

If a lawyer dies or ceases their professional practice, the President of the Bar Association shall appoint a lawyer to take charge of the remaining files, settle the accounts, and close the law office.

#### Chapter 8: Penal Provisions

#### Article 77.-

Any person who violates Articles 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 13 and 57 shall be punished by a fine from 1,000,000 Riels to 5,000,000 Riels. If the offense is committed by a foreigner, that person may also be expelled from the territory of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

In case of recidivism, the fine shall be doubled, and the offender may be punished by imprisonment from 3 to 6 months.

In the case of the above offenses, the Bar Council may bring a civil action.

#### Article 78.-

Any person who violates Article 58 shall be punished according to the provisions of the Criminal Law.

#### Chapter 9: Transitional Provisions

#### Article 79.-

The role of defending clients in criminal cases may be performed by defenders who are not lawyers, in accordance with the criminal procedure in force.

#### Article 80.-

The following may be selected to take the entrance examination for the lawyer training course:

- Court defenders who have the capacity to fulfill this function and who have effectively and continuously performed this function for 2 years in provincial or municipal courts. These defenders must have at least 2 years of higher education.

- Civil servants who have served for at least 2 years and who hold a law degree.

Holders of a Bachelor of Laws degree or a law degree declared as equivalent are exempted from the entrance examination.

#### Article 81.-

Persons eligible to register for the entrance examination for the lawyer training course, as set out above, must have never been sentenced to imprisonment for any criminal offense.

#### Article 82.-

The lawyer training course shall last at least 8 months. The curriculum and procedures for the training course, as well as the curriculum and procedures for the examination to verify the results of the training, shall be determined by a Proclamation (Prakas) of the Minister of Justice if the Bar Council is not yet in place.

Those who pass the examination shall obtain the Certificate of Aptitude for the Legal Profession.

#### Article 83.-

The following are exempted from the requirement for a Certificate of Aptitude for the Legal Profession and from the internship:

- Holders of a Bachelor of Laws degree or a law degree declared as equivalent, who have fulfilled professional functions in legal or judicial fields for at least 2 years.

- Holders of a Doctorate of Laws.

- Judges or former judges who have fulfilled their professional functions for at least 2 years.

#### Article 84.-

Before the establishment of the Bar Association, the Prosecutor General at the Court of Appeal shall be responsible for receiving and reviewing applications for admission to the legal profession. They shall summon applicants who fulfill all conditions to take the oath before the Court of Appeal to fulfill their status as lawyers and to participate in the constitutive General Assembly of the Bar Association.

#### Article 85.-

The General Assembly shall organize the election under the responsibility of the most senior lawyer to elect the President of the Bar Association and the Bar Council.

#### Article 86.-

One year later, a new election shall be organized to elect a new President of the Bar Association and a new Bar Council by all lawyers registered on the Bar Roll.

#### Article 87.-

If necessary, the mandate of the Bar Council and the President of the Bar Association elected for the first time shall be extended until the announcement of the results of the election of the new President of the Bar Association and the new Bar Council.

#### Article 88.-

Notwithstanding Articles 14 and 15, those entitled to vote and to stand for election as set out in Article 84 are all lawyers registered on the Bar Roll and on the list of interns, without regard to seniority.

#### Article 89.-

These transitional provisions shall remain in effect until December 31, 1997.

#### Chapter 10: Final Provisions

#### Article 90.-

Any provisions contrary to this Law are hereby repealed.
