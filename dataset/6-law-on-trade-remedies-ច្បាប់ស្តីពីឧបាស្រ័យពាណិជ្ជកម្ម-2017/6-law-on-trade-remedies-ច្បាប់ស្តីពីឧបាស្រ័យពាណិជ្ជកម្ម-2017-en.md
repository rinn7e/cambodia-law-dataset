# Law on Trade Remedies

# Chapter 1
## General Provisions

### Article 1. - Purpose

This Law has the purpose to protect and address negative impacts on Cambodian industries caused by unfair import trade and import surges by using trade remedy measures, in accordance with the relevant agreements of the World Trade Organization.

### Article 2. - Objective

This Law has the objective to determine the principles, mechanisms, procedures, and measures related to trade remedies.

### Article 3. - Scope

This Law has the scope of application to dumping, subsidies and countervailing measures, and safeguard measures related to trade in the Kingdom of Cambodia, in accordance with the relevant agreements of the World Trade Organization.

### Article 4. - Definitions

Key terms used in this Law are defined in the Glossary which is an Annex to this Law.

# Chapter 2
## National Committee on Trade Remedies

### Article 5. - National Committee on Trade Remedies

The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall be established, led by the Minister of Ministry of Commerce and with the participation of relevant ministries and institutions, to perform duties related to trade remedies in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

The organization and functioning of the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall be determined by Sub-decree.

# Chapter 3
## Procedures for Trade Remedies

### Section 1
#### Anti-dumping Measures

### Article 6. - Identification of Dumping

Any products under investigation and circulating for commercial purposes in the Kingdom of Cambodia at an export price lower than their normal value in the country of origin shall be considered as dumped products.

### Article 7. - Normal Value Based on Price in the Exporting Country or Country of Origin

The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall determine the normal value of the product under investigation based on the comparable price paid or payable in the ordinary course of trade for the like product to consumers in the exporting country.

### Article 8. - Normal Value Based on Export Price to a Third Country or Constructed Value

When there are no sales of the like product in the domestic market of the exporting country, or when such sales exist but cannot be used as a basis for comparison due to market situation or because the volume of sales is low in the exporting country, the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall determine the normal value of the product under investigation based on either of the following (1) or (2):

1- Based on the comparable price of the like product when exported from the country of origin to a third country, which indicates that the price of the like product can be representative for comparison; or

2- Based on the cost of production in the country of origin, plus a reasonable amount for administrative, selling, and other costs, as well as profits.

### Article 9. - Export Price

The export price shall be determined as follows:

A- The export price is the price actually paid or payable for the product under investigation when sold for export from the exporting country to Cambodia.

b- In cases where there is no export price or where the National Committee on Trade Remedies determines that the export price is unreliable because of an association or a compensatory arrangement between the exporter and the importer or a third party, as follows:

1- The export price may be constructed on the basis of the price at which the imported products are first resold to an independent buyer, or

2- The National Committee on Trade Remedies may determine the export price on any reasonable basis, if the products are not resold to an independent buyer or are resold in a state different from that when imported.

c- When the National Committee on Trade Remedies determines the normal value based on the country of origin, the export price must be the price actually paid or payable for the product under investigation when sold for export to the country of origin.

#### Article 10.- Adjustment of Normal Value and Export Price

The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall make a fair comparison between the export price and the normal value. This comparison shall be made at the same level of trade, normally at the ex-factory level, and in respect of sales made at as nearly as possible the same time. Due allowance shall be made in each case for differences which affect price comparability.

#### Article 11.- Individual Dumping Margins

The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall determine an individual margin of dumping for each known exporter or producer concerned of the product under investigation. However, the National Committee on Trade Remedies may limit its examination and the determination of individual dumping margins to a reasonable number of interested parties or products under investigation.

#### Article 12.- Determination of Injury

A determination of material injury shall be based on positive evidence and involve an objective examination of the following:

a- The volume of the dumped imports

b- The effect of the dumped imports on prices in the domestic market for like products, and

c- The impact of such imports on domestic producers of such products.

#### Article 13.- Examination of Volume of Dumped Imports and Effect on Prices in Cambodia

a- With regard to the volume of the dumped imports, the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall consider whether there has been a significant increase in dumped imports, either in absolute terms or relative to production or consumption in Cambodia.

b- With regard to the effect of the dumped imports on prices in the Cambodian market, the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall consider whether:

1- There has been a significant price undercutting by the dumped imports as compared with the price of a like product in the domestic market, or

2- The effect of such imports is otherwise to depress prices to a significant degree or to prevent price increases, which otherwise would have occurred, to a significant degree.

c- In addition to the factors set forth in paragraphs "a" and "b" of this Article, the National Committee on Trade Remedies may consider other factors that cause significant changes to the price of like products in the domestic market.

#### Article 14.- Cumulation

Where imports of a like product from more than one country are simultaneously subject to anti-dumping investigations, the National Committee on Trade Remedies may cumulatively assess the effects of such imports on the Cambodian industry only if the National Committee on Trade Remedies determines that:

a- The margin of dumping established in relation to the imports from each country is more than de minimis and the volume of imports from each country is not negligible; and

b- A cumulative assessment of the effects of the imports is appropriate in light of the conditions of competition between the imported products and the conditions of competition between the imported products and the domestic like product.

#### Article 15.- Examination of the Impact of Dumped Imports on Cambodian Industry

a- The examination of the impact of the dumped imports on the domestic industry concerned shall include an evaluation by the National Committee on Trade Remedies of all relevant economic factors and indices having a bearing on the state of the industry, including:

1- Actual and potential decline in sales, profits, output, market share, productivity, return on investments, or utilization of capacity

2- Factors affecting domestic prices

3- The magnitude of the margin of dumping, and

4- Actual and potential negative effects on cash flow, inventories, employment, wages, growth, ability to raise capital or investments.

No single factor of the above factors can necessarily give decisive guidance, but the totality of factors considered must lead to the conclusion that dumping has occurred and is causing material injury to the Cambodian industry.

b- The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall assess the effect of the dumped imports in relation to the Cambodian production of the like product when available data permit the separate identification of that production on the basis of such criteria as the production process, producers' sales and profits. If such separate identification of that production is not possible, the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall assess the effects of the dumped imports by the examination of the production of the narrowest group or range of products, which includes the like product, for which the necessary information can be provided.

#### Article 16.- Threat of Material Injury

a- The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall determine a threat of material injury based on facts and not merely on allegation, conjecture or remote possibility. The change in circumstances which would create a situation in which the dumping would cause injury must be clearly foreseen and imminent.

b- The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall determine the existence of a threat of material injury based on a number of factors such as the following:

1- A significant rate of increase of dumped imports into the Cambodian market indicating the likelihood of substantially increased imports.

2- Sufficient freely disposable, or an imminent, substantial increase in, capacity of the exporter indicating the likelihood of substantially increased dumped imports to the Cambodian market.

3- Import for sale at a price that causes the price of like domestic products to decrease or not to increase, and such import price leads to an increase in the need/demand for more imports.

4- Inventories of the product under investigation.

c- If the dumped imports threaten to cause any injury, the National Committee on Trade Remedies must examine and take measures with the utmost caution.

#### Article 17.- Causal Relationship

a- The National Committee on Trade Remedies must demonstrate that the dumped imports cause material injury within the meaning of this Law. The demonstration of a causal relationship between the dumped imports and the injury to the Cambodian industry shall be based on all relevant evidence collected by the Committee.

b- The National Committee on Trade Remedies must also examine factors other than the dumped imports which at the same time are causing injury to the Cambodian industry, and the injury caused by these other factors must not be attributed to the dumped imports. Those other factors include:

1- The volume and prices of imports not sold at dumped prices.

2- Contraction in demand or changes in the patterns of consumption.

3- Trade restrictive practices of and competition between foreign and domestic producers.

4- Competition between foreign producers and domestic producers.

5- Development of technology, and

6- Export capacity from abroad and the productivity of the Cambodian industry.

#### Article 18.- Procedures for Initiating and Conducting Investigation

a- The National Committee on Trade Remedies may initiate an investigation on dumping upon a written application by or on behalf of the Cambodian industry, or may initiate the investigation on its own motion. The application to initiate the investigation shall include evidence of dumping, injury, and a causal relationship as defined in this Law. The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall determine the procedures for initiating the investigation, including determining the criteria to accept for consideration the applications made directly by or on behalf of the Cambodian industry. The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall not publicize the application for the investigation of dumping

price before the initiation of the investigation, but shall immediately publish a notice of the decision to initiate the investigation.

b- The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall make preliminary and final determinations regarding dumping and injury. After a preliminary determination of dumping, the National Committee on Trade Remedies may apply provisional measures, if the National Committee can determine that such measures are necessary to prevent injury being caused during the investigation.

c- The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall determine the principles and conditions related to the acceptance of voluntary price undertakings in cases where the exporter agrees to revise the selling prices of the product to eliminate the dumping. The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall determine the procedures for resolution in case of violation of the price undertakings. The investigation may be terminated upon withdrawal of the application, or when the National Committee on Trade Remedies examines and decides that there is no sufficient evidence of dumping or injury to justify proceeding with the case. The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall publish a notice of the decision to terminate the investigation.

d- The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall determine the procedures for maintaining confidentiality of all information submitted to the Committee by interested parties and requested to be kept confidential. The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall maintain a public file containing non-confidential information obtained during the investigation. Upon request, the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall organize a public hearing in which all interested parties may participate. All interested parties, as well as representatives of the Cambodian industry using the product, and other users, may submit written comments during the investigation and hearings.

e- Detailed procedures related to the initiation and investigation shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 19.- Investigation Leading to the Imposition of Anti-dumping Duties

Upon completion of the anti-dumping investigation by the National Committee on Trade Remedies with a determination of injury and dumping, the anti-dumping measures shall be implemented in accordance with this Law.

#### Article 20.- Cambodian Public Interest and Lesser Duty Rule

a- The amount of the anti-dumping duty shall not exceed the margin of dumping established in accordance with this Law. When all requirements for the imposition of anti-dumping duties have been fulfilled, the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall verify that the imposition of such measures is in the interest of Cambodia. The consideration of the Cambodian interest shall include:

- the interests of the domestic industry concerned

- the domestic competition situation in respect of the product under investigation

- the interests of users for industrial purposes, and

- the interests of ultimate consumers.

In cases where the imposition of the anti-dumping duty serves the public interest, the duty rate shall be lower than the dumping margin.

b- The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall also examine whether an anti-dumping duty set lower than the full dumping margin is sufficient to eliminate the injury to the Cambodian industry. When the National Committee on Trade Remedies determines that such a duty rate is sufficient to eliminate the injury, the final anti-dumping duty to be imposed shall not exceed this lower rate.

#### Article 21.- Determination and Collection of Anti-dumping Duties

a- Anti-dumping duties shall be collected on an ad valorem or specific basis in addition to the customs duties on the imported product. This anti-dumping duty shall be collected by the Customs Administration of Cambodia in appropriate amounts on a case-by-case basis and on a non-discriminatory basis on imports of the product from all sources found to be dumped and causing injury, except for imports from which price undertakings have been accepted in accordance with this Law.

b- In an anti-dumping investigation, the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall determine an individual anti-dumping duty rate for each known exporter or producer of the imported dumped product. In cases where the number of producers is so large that it makes it impracticable to conclude the investigation in a timely manner, the National Committee on Trade Remedies may determine an average anti-dumping duty rate.

C- The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall refund the tax paid in excess of the dumping margin and temporarily suspend the implementation of anti-dumping measures when market conditions have changed and the application of anti-dumping duties does not serve the interests of Cambodia.

#### Article 22.- Retroactive Application

Anti-dumping duties shall be collected on products under investigation that were imported for consumption during a period of not more than 90 (ninety) days prior to the date of application of provisional measures, but shall not be collected on products imported prior to the date of initiation of the investigation, in cases where the National Committee on Trade Remedies determines that the dumped product:

A- Has a history of dumping which caused injury, or that the importer was aware of, or should have known that, the exporter was dumping which caused injury, and

B- The injury is caused by massive dumped imports of a product in a relatively short period of time which, in light of the timing and the volume of the dumped imports and other circumstances (such as a rapid increase in the total volume of imports), is likely to seriously undermine the remedial effect of the definitive anti-dumping duty to be applied. In this case, the Committee shall provide the concerned importers with an opportunity to explain the reasons for such massive rapid increase.

#### Article 23.- Collection of Anti-Dumping Duties on Dumped Goods During the Period of Application of Provisional Measures

A- Anti-dumping duties may be collected retroactively for the period during which provisional measures were applied in cases where:

1- The National Committee on Trade Remedies has made a final determination of injury, or

2- The final determination is based on the ground that, during the period of application of provisional measures, the effect of the dumped imports still caused material injury.

B- If the final determination of injury finds only a threat of material injury or material retardation of the establishment of an industry, anti-dumping duties shall not be applied retroactively.

C- In cases where the anti-dumping duty is higher than the provisional duty paid or the amount estimated for the purpose of the security, the difference

the difference shall not be collected. In cases where the definitive anti-dumping duty is lower than the provisional duty paid or the amount estimated for the purpose of the security, the difference shall be refunded to the importer or the anti-dumping duties shall be recalculated.

D- Other than the determination in paragraph "A" above, if the National Committee on Trade Remedies makes a determination of threat of injury or material retardation of the establishment of an industry, the anti-dumping duties required to be paid shall be calculated only from the date of the determination of threat of injury or material retardation of the establishment of that industry. The security provided during the period of application of provisional measures shall be refunded to the exporter not later than 10 (ten) working days.

E- In cases where the National Committee on Trade Remedies makes a negative final determination, the security provided during the period of application of provisional measures shall be refunded to the exporter not later than 10 (ten) working days.

#### Article 24.- Principles Concerning Duration and Review of Anti-Dumping Duties and Price Undertakings

Anti-dumping duties shall remain in force only as long as and to the extent necessary to counteract dumping which causes injury to the Cambodian industry.

#### Article 25.- Review

The anti-dumping duties shall be terminated within 5 (five) years from the date of their imposition, or from the date of the most recent review covering both dumping and injury. Within 90 (ninety) days prior to the date of expiry of the anti-dumping duty measures, the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall publish a notice of expiry in the official gazette. The duties may not expire if the National Committee on Trade Remedies determines that the expiry of the duty would be likely to lead to continuation or recurrence of dumping and injury, based on a review initiated before the date of expiry, either on the initiative of the National Committee on Trade Remedies or upon a duly supported request in writing made by or on behalf of the industry

of Cambodia within 45 (forty-five) days from the date of publication of the notice of the impending expiry of the anti-dumping measures. The duties may remain in force pending the outcome of the review.

#### Article 26.- Review under Changed Circumstances

A- The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall review the need for the continued imposition of the anti-dumping duties, where warranted, on its own initiative or upon a written request by any interested party which submits positive information substantiating the need for such review, provided that a reasonable period of at least 1 (one) year has elapsed since the imposition of the definitive anti-dumping duty. Upon initiating the review, the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall publish a notice in the official gazette.

B- The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall determine that the request for review must be accompanied by sufficient information. In conducting a review under this Article, the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall determine whether the continued imposition of the anti-dumping duty is necessary to offset dumping or whether the injury would be likely to continue or recur if the duty were removed or varied, or both. If the National Committee on Trade Remedies conducts a review and determines that the anti-dumping duty is no longer warranted, the anti-dumping duty shall be terminated immediately.

C- The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall determine the procedure for calculating individual dumping margins for any exporter or producer in the exporting country concerned who has not exported the product to the Kingdom of Cambodia during the period of investigation, but can show that they have the intention to export the product to the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 27.- Duration and Review of Price Undertakings

The provisions of this Law regarding the duration and review of anti-dumping measures shall apply to the determination of duration and review of the acceptance of price undertakings.

#### Article 28.- Evidence and Procedure

A- The provisions of this Law relating to evidence and procedure shall be applied to reviews in accordance with this Chapter.

B- Any review shall be carried out expeditiously and shall be concluded within 12 (twelve) months from the date of initiation of the review.

### Part 2

### Subsidies and Countervailing Measures

#### Article 29.- Subsidies and Countervailing Measures

Subsidies and countervailing measures shall be determined by Sub-decree, in accordance with this Law and the provisions of the Agreement on Subsidies and Countervailing Measures of the World Trade Organization.

### Part 3

### Safeguard Measures and Procedures

#### Article 30.- Serious Injury and Causal Link

A- A determination concerning the increase in imports of the product under investigation that has caused or threatens to cause serious injury to a domestic industry shall be based on an evaluation of all relevant factors of an objective and quantifiable nature having a bearing on the situation of that industry, including:

1- The rate and amount of the increase in imports of the product under investigation in absolute or relative terms to domestic production of like or directly competitive products.

2- The share of the domestic market taken by increased imports of the product under investigation.

3- The impact of the increase in imports of the product under investigation on the domestic industry as demonstrated by relevant indicators, including production, capacity utilization, productivity, profits and losses, and employment.

4- Other factors other than increased imports of the product under investigation which at the same time are causing or threatening to cause serious injury to the domestic industry.

B- The National Committee on Trade Remedies may determine that the increased imports of the product under investigation have caused or are threatening to cause serious injury to the domestic industry only if the Committee finds a causal link between the increased imports and the actual serious injury or threat of serious injury.

C- When factors other than increased imports of the product under investigation are causing or threatening to cause injury to the domestic industry at the same time, such injury shall not be attributed to increased imports.

#### Article 31.- Threat of Serious Injury and Causal Link

A- A determination of a threat of serious injury caused by increased imports by the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall be based on facts and not merely on allegation, conjecture or remote possibility.

B- In determining whether an increase in imports threatens to cause serious injury, the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall analyze the following factors, in addition to the factors set forth in Article 30, Paragraph A of this Law:

1- The actual export capacity or potential export capacity of a country, producing countries, or the country of origin of the product.

2- Any increase of inventories in the Kingdom of Cambodia and in the exporting country.

3- The export potential of the product under investigation that increases the volume of imports into the Cambodian market.

4- Other factors that the National Committee on Trade Remedies deems relevant.

#### Article 32.- Initiation and Investigation of Safeguard Measures

A- In conducting an investigation of safeguard measures, the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall follow the principles and procedures set forth in Article 18 of this Law.

B- The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall determine procedures for the initiation and termination of safeguard investigations and shall issue a public notice of these procedures. An applicant may withdraw the application before the initiation of the investigation.

C- Before a safeguard investigation is completed, the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall take provisional safeguard measures in accordance with the determination and findings, and where there is sufficient evidence showing that increased imports have caused or threaten to cause serious injury, and that delay would cause damage which it would be difficult to repair.

D- Safeguard measures and procedures for implementation shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 33.- Determination of Serious Injury or Threat of Serious Injury and Causal Link

A- The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall rely on objective evidence obtained from the investigation to determine whether increased imports of the product under investigation have caused or are threatening to cause serious injury to the domestic industry, in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

B- Upon making a determination of the results, whether positive or negative, regarding serious injury or threat of serious injury, the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall promptly publish a notice of such results in the official gazette. The content of the notice shall comply with the requirements of the notice of initiation of safeguard investigation.

C- The Royal Government shall promptly notify the World Trade Organization if a determination is made that increased imports have caused or threaten to cause serious injury to the domestic industry.

#### Article 34.- General Principles of Definitive Safeguard Measures

A- In cases where the National Committee on Trade Remedies determines that increased imports of the product under investigation are in such increased quantities and under such conditions as to cause or threaten to cause serious injury to the domestic industry, which is unforeseen as a result of developments and of the effect of the obligations under the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade 1994, and to protect public interest, the National Committee on Trade Remedies

Trade Remedies shall propose to the Royal Government to apply definitive safeguard measures. The proposed definitive safeguard measures include increasing customs duty rates to exceed the bound rates or implementing quantitative restrictions on imports.

B- The duration and level of any definitive safeguard measure shall not exceed what is necessary to prevent or remedy serious injury and to adjust the situation.

C- In deciding to propose to the Royal Government to take definitive safeguard measures, the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall consider:

1- Other members of the World Trade Organization (WTO) that are adversely affected by the application of safeguard measures on their trade interests may request compensation from Cambodia, by demanding Cambodia to open market access with greater concessions for their other export products; or

2- In the event that Cambodia does not agree to provide compensation as specified in paragraph 1, the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall weigh the costs and benefits arising from retaliatory measures that the WTO member may suspend concessions substantially equivalent to the concessions that the member has provided to Cambodia under the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade (GATT) 1994, unless such suspension is disapproved by the Council for Trade in Goods of the World Trade Organization. The right of suspension of concessions that may be exercised by such member country shall not be exercised during the first 3 (three) years that the safeguard measure is in effect, if the safeguard measure is taken as a result of an absolute increase in imports.

D- After providing recommendations on the decision to apply definitive safeguard measures, the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall publish a notice on the application of definitive safeguard measures in accordance with the requirements of the notice of investigation on safeguard measures.

#### Article 35.- Notice and Consultation with Members of the World Trade Organization

A- The Royal Government shall notify the World Trade Organization, after deciding on the proposal to apply definitive safeguard measures, regarding:

1- Evidence of serious injury or threat of serious injury caused by the increase in imports;

2- A detailed description of the product under investigation, the form, level, and duration of the proposed measure;

3- The proposed date of application of the measure and the proposed timetable for the progressive liberalization of the measure, if any.

B- Prior to the application of definitive safeguard measures, the Royal Government of Cambodia shall provide adequate opportunity for consultations with those member countries having a substantial interest as exporters of the product under investigation, with the aim of reviewing the information notified to the Committee on Safeguards of the World Trade Organization regarding the findings of serious injury or threat of serious injury caused by increased imports and the proposed safeguard measures. The Royal Government of Cambodia shall provide opportunity for exchanging views on the measures to reach an understanding on ways to achieve the objective set out in paragraph "C" of this Article.

C- When applying definitive safeguard measures, the Royal Government of Cambodia shall endeavor to maintain a substantially equivalent level of concessions and other obligations in force under the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade (GATT) 1994 between Cambodia and the exporting member countries which may be affected by the application of such measures.

D- Maintaining a substantially equivalent level of concessions and other obligations under the agreement between Cambodia and the affected member countries shall be achieved through consultations on trade compensation, such as opening market access for other products, due to the adverse effects resulting from the application of definitive safeguard measures on the trade of those member countries.

E- The Royal Government shall notify the World Trade Organization of the results of the consultations, including any trade compensation provided.

#### Article 36.- Form and Application of Definitive Safeguard Measures

A- Definitive safeguard measures shall be in the form of tariff increases or quantitative import quotas. Tariff increases may be in the form of a tariff-rate quota, where a higher than normal tariff rate is applied to a specified annual volume of imports.

B- Definitive safeguard measures shall apply to all imports of the product under investigation, regardless of the source of import or the date of import, whether before or after this measure enters into force

C- The Customs Administration shall be responsible for collecting revenues generated from the application of definitive safeguard measures in the form of tariff increases. The Ministry of Commerce shall be responsible for the administrative procedures in allocating quantitative limits under definitive safeguard measures applied in the form of quantitative restrictions and tariff-rate quotas.

#### Article 37.- Non-Application of Definitive Safeguard Measures to Certain Developing Countries

A- The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall not apply definitive safeguard measures to imports of a product under investigation originating from any developing country member of the World Trade Organization (WTO) as long as its share of imports of the product under investigation in Cambodia does not exceed 3% (three percent) of the total import value of the product under investigation.

B- Even if imports from each developing country member represent less than 3% (three percent) of the total import value of the product under investigation, the definitive safeguard measure shall apply to imports from those developing countries if their collective share of imports from those developing countries exceeds 9% (nine percent) of the total import value of the product under investigation.

C- The Royal Government shall notify the World Trade Organization of the non-application of definitive safeguard measures to imports originating from developing country members.

#### Article 38.- Duration of Application of Definitive Safeguard Measures

A- Definitive safeguard measures shall be applied for a specific period not exceeding 4 (four) years, unless extended in accordance with this Law.

B- The total duration of a definitive safeguard measure, including the period of initial application and any extension thereof, shall not exceed 10 (ten) years.

#### Article 39.- Progressive Liberalization of Definitive Safeguard Measures

Definitive safeguard measures with a duration of application exceeding 1 (one) year shall be progressively liberalized at regular intervals during the period of application, in accordance with the schedule published in the notice of application of definitive safeguard measures by the National Committee on Trade Remedies.

#### Article 40.- Review of Definitive Safeguard Measures

a- In the case where the duration of the implementation of the definitive safeguard measure exceeds 3 (three) years, the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall conduct a review of the general situation of the implementation of the definitive safeguard measure, not later than mid-term of the duration of the implementation of the definitive safeguard measure. The review shall examine the effectiveness of the implementation of the definitive safeguard measure on the Cambodian industry and the progress of the implementation of the adjustment plan of the Cambodian industry. The results of the review shall be published in the official bulletin of the National Committee on Trade Remedies. Based on the results of this review, the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall decide to maintain or withdraw completely the definitive safeguard measure, or to accelerate the liberalization of the definitive safeguard measure.

b- A summary notice on the results of the review regarding the maintaining, the liberalization, or the complete withdrawal of the definitive safeguard measure shall be published in the official bulletin of the National Committee on Trade Remedies. The content of the notice shall comply with the requirements of the notice on the safeguard investigation.

c- The Royal Government shall notify the World Trade Organization of the results of this review, including any withdrawal or modification of the definitive safeguard measure.

#### Article 41.- Extension of the Definitive Safeguard Measure

a- The Cambodian industry may submit a written request not less than 6 (six) months before the expiry of the initial definitive safeguard measure to the National Committee on Trade Remedies to extend the initial definitive safeguard measure, accompanied by evidence showing that its industry is progressively implementing the adjustment plan.

After receiving the request from the Cambodian industry or upon initiating the extension of the measure, the National Committee on Trade Remedies shall conduct a clear investigation to ensure that the extension of this measure is proper. The procedures for the initial measure as provided in this Law shall apply to the investigation and determination. The safeguard measure may be extended only once for a period not exceeding 6 (six) years.

b- The National Committee on Trade Remedies may extend the definitive safeguard measure only if the investigation has been conducted as provided in paragraph "a" above and the Committee has determined that the definitive safeguard measure continues to be necessary to prevent or remedy serious injury and there is evidence that the Cambodian industry is making adjustments.

c- The definitive safeguard measure that has been extended shall not be more restrictive than it was at the date of the initial implementation. During the extension period of the definitive safeguard measure, the National Committee on Trade Remedies must continue to liberalize the measure progressively in accordance with the adjustment program published in the notice of the implementation of the definitive safeguard measure. The content of the notice shall comply with the requirements of the notice on the safeguard investigation.

d- In extending the definitive safeguard measure, the Royal Government shall maintain a level of concessions and other obligations substantially equivalent to that provided under the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade 1994 between Cambodia and the exporting member countries which would be affected by the implementation of these measures.

e- The requirements concerning notifications to the World Trade Organization and consultations with the exporting member countries affected by the implementation of the measures as provided in this Law shall apply in their entirety to the extension of this definitive safeguard measure.

#### Article 42.- Re-application of the Definitive Safeguard Measure

a- The National Committee on Trade Remedies shall not apply a new safeguard measure to the import of a product that has been subject to a definitive safeguard measure previously applied for a period of 2 (two) years from the date of expiration of the first definitive safeguard measure. Under circumstances where the initial definitive safeguard measure was applied for more than 4 (four) years, the National Committee on Trade Remedies must wait until at least half of the duration of the initial definitive safeguard measure has elapsed before a new safeguard measure can be applied.

b- A safeguard measure with a duration of 180 (one hundred and eighty) days or less may be re-applied to imports subject to investigation which had previously been subject to an initial safeguard measure if:

1- At least 1 (one) year has elapsed since the date of introduction of the initial safeguard measure on that import product; and

2- Such safeguard measure has not been applied on the imported product more than twice in the period of 5 (five) years prior to the date of the implementation of the new definitive safeguard measure.

### Chapter 4
### Judicial Appeal

#### Article 43.- Judicial Appeal

a- An interested party that participated in the investigation, refund procedures, or reviews conducted by the National Committee on Trade Remedies by providing information, written submissions, or participating in public hearings may file an appeal with the competent court to review any activities of the National Committee on Trade Remedies relating to final determinations, reviews of determinations, and determinations on refunds.

b- The appeal provided in paragraph "a" above shall be filed not later than 30 (thirty) days after the date of publication of the determination in the official bulletin of the National Committee on Trade Remedies.

c- In the case where the trade remedy measure is subject to judicial review, the Customs Administration shall suspend the collection of the trade remedy duty at the port of entry for the imported goods subject to the appeal if the importer provides security through cash deposit or guarantee in accordance with the customs law, equivalent to the amount of such duty. The trade remedy duty at the port of entry shall be collected in accordance with the final decision of the court.

### Chapter 5
### Penalty Provisions

#### Article 44.-

a- Any violation of the provisions of this Law shall be penalized under the laws in force of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

b- If the National Committee on Trade Remedies finds that the information provided to support the investigation, the implementation of anti-dumping measures, or safeguard measures was fraudulent, the anti-dumping measures or safeguard measures shall be withdrawn, or the investigation shall be terminated.

### Chapter 6
### Final Provisions

#### Article 45.-

Any provisions that contradict this Law shall be null and void.
