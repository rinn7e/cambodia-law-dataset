### Promulgates

The Law on Public Procurement, which was adopted by the National Assembly on April 19, 2023 at the 9th session of the 6th legislature, and which was reviewed in its entirety on its form and legal aspects by the Senate on May 2, 2023 at the 10th plenary session of the 4th legislature, the full text of which is as follows:

### Law

### on

### Public Procurement

### Chapter 1
### General Provisions

#### Article 1 .-

This Law defines the rules, methods, procedures, and general structure for managing and implementing all public procurement, with the objective of ensuring that procurement for purchasing, construction, repairs, services, and consulting services is conducted in a transparent, accountable, integral, competitive, fair, efficient, effective, sustainable, quality, economical, and timely manner, and to ensure a unified public procurement system in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 2 .-

This Law applies to all public procurement in the Kingdom of Cambodia, regardless of the source of funding, with the exception of:

1- Procurement under projects financed by development partners, which shall be implemented in accordance with the guidelines and procedures specified in the financing agreements. If the agreement does not specify any other procurement guidelines and procedures, the public procurement process shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

2- Procurement to be implemented under the Law on Public-Private Partnerships.

3- Procurement affecting national defense and public security confidentiality, which requires the decision of the Prime Minister.

#### Article 3 .-

Key terms used in this Law are defined in the Glossary, which is the Annex of this Law.

### Chapter 2
### Public Procurement Governing Body

#### Article 4 .-

The Ministry of Economy and Finance is the governing body for public procurement, performing the functions of regulator and supervisor of the implementation of public procurement in the Kingdom of Cambodia, and having the following roles and duties:

- Formulate and disseminate policy, law, and legal instruments related to public procurement

- Control, analyze, monitor, and evaluate the implementation of laws and legal instruments related to public procurement

- Organize procurement skills training for procurement officials and other relevant individuals

- Manage the list of bidders

- Conduct ex-post procurement review

- Resolve complaints related to the implementation of public procurement

- Take measures and decisions on offenses against laws and legal instruments related to public procurement

- Manage data, prepare and publish public procurement reports.

#### Article 5 .-

The Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance has the duty to issue legal instruments to guide the implementation of public procurement for procurement implementing entities. In particular, for procurement implementing entities that are public enterprises, in order to ensure efficiency, effectiveness, and competition, the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance may issue additional legal instruments for the implementation of public procurement.

#### Article 6 .-

The General Department of Public Procurement performs the function of secretariat to the Ministry of Economy and Finance in managing public procurement in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

The roles and duties of the General Department of Public Procurement shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Chapter 3
### Procurement Implementing Entities

#### Article 7 .-

Procurement implementing entities shall be responsible for implementing public procurement correctly in accordance with the provisions of this Law and other relevant legal instruments.

#### Article 8 .-

Procurement implementing entities shall establish a procurement committee to be responsible for reviewing procurement plans and bidding documents, evaluating and awarding contracts, managing contract implementation, and resolving procurement complaints within their jurisdiction. The procurement committee shall be led by a key focal leader assigned by the head of the procurement implementing entity. The head of the procurement implementing entity shall approve the contract award decision and sign the contract. The head of the procurement implementing entity has the authority to delegate power to subordinate leaders to approve and sign contracts on behalf of the head of the procurement implementing entity.

The organization and functioning of the procurement committee shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 9 .-

Procurement implementing entities shall establish a procurement unit that has a clear structure and sufficient staff with public procurement skills and decision-making authority to be responsible for implementing all public procurement.

The procurement unit has a structure under the direct authority of the head of the entity and serves as the secretariat to the procurement committee. The procurement unit shall be led by a chief who shall not have other roles and duties related to financial affairs in their own entity. The chief of the procurement unit serves as the secretariat of the procurement committee.

The organization and functioning of the procurement unit shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 10 .-

Procurement implementing entities which are fully delegated budget-approving units may be permitted to establish a procurement group to implement public procurement instead of establishing a procurement committee and a procurement unit.

The criteria, organization, and functioning of the procurement group shall be determined by declaration of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

### Chapter 4
### Procurement Methods for Goods, Works, and Services

#### Article 11 .-

Procurement methods for goods, works, and services shall be carried out in accordance with Articles 12, 13, and 14 of this Law.

The conditions, threshold, procedures, and granting of preferential treatment for the implementation of each procurement method shall be determined by declaration of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 12 .-

The public competitive bidding method is the primary procurement method for selecting suppliers of goods, contractors of works, or service providers, opening to all domestic and international bidders. The public competitive bidding method is a procurement method that has a high level of transparency and shall be encouraged for use in public procurement. Public competitive bidding methods include:

A. International Competitive Bidding: This method shall be used for procurement projects that have large contract value and/or high technical complexity.

B. Domestic Competitive Bidding: This method shall be used in cases where there are sufficient domestic products, production, services, or construction capacity.

#### Article 13 .-

Other competitive bidding methods which are not public competitive bidding include:

A. Restricted Bidding: This method shall be used for procurement projects that are technically complex and require bidders to have specific skills and capacity to implement. This method requires pre-qualification.

B. Price Inquiry: This method shall be used for procurement items that are readily available in the domestic market and have a medium contract value.

C. Price Quotation: This method shall be used for procurement items that are readily available in the domestic market and have a small contract value.

#### Article 14 .-

Special procurement methods are procurement methods for selecting suppliers of goods, contractors of works, or service providers without competitive bidding. This method can only be implemented with prior permission from the Ministry of Economy and Finance. Special procurement methods include:

A. Direct Contracting: This method allows direct negotiation with suppliers of goods, contractors of works, or service providers under any of the following circumstances:

- The procurement item is of single-source origin

- The procurement item has already been subject to a public competitive bidding process once, and there is an urgent need and insufficient time for a second public competitive bidding process

- The second public competitive bidding process was unsuccessful, even though the bidding documents were revised after the failure of the first bidding process

- Urgent need during natural disasters or other emergencies, and the timeframe is too short to wait for other methods to be used

- For research, experiment/drawing lessons, or development purposes.

B. Contract Extension: This method allows direct negotiation with suppliers of goods, contractors of works, or service providers under the following circumstances:

- The procurement item is identical to the procurement item in the original contract obtained through competitive bidding

- Within a period of not exceeding 6 (six) months after the original contract enters into force and within the same budget year

- The unit price is equal to or lower than the unit price in the original contract, and

- The total value does not exceed 25 (twenty-five) percent of the original contract value.

C. Contracting with Specialized Units of State Institutions: This method allows direct negotiation with specialized units of state institutions that have sufficient resources, machinery, equipment, materials, and labor to implement the contract.

D. Contracting with Communities: This method allows direct negotiation with communities, aiming to encourage local communities to use local labor, knowledge, and materials to participate in community development.

### Chapter 5
### Procurement of Consulting Services

#### Article 15 .-

Consultants may be selected to provide specialized services for project management, implementation of key contracts, or to provide expert advice in institutional capacity building or capacity enhancement. Consultants may be consulting firms or individual consultants.

#### Article 16 .-

Consulting firms shall be selected when the procurement implementing entity has requirements for multiple consulting services that are interrelated at the same time. The selection of consulting firms shall be based on the technical proposal and the financial proposal of each consulting firm.

#### Article 17 .-

Individual consultants shall be selected based on the qualifications of the consultant to provide services to the procurement implementing entity.

#### Article 18 .-

Consultant selection methods include:

- Quality-Based Selection

- Quality- and Cost-Based Selection

- Selection under a Fixed Budget

- Least-Cost Selection

- Selection Based on Consultant's Qualifications

- Single-Source Selection

- Individual Consultant Selection.

#### Article 19 .-

The Quality-Based Selection method shall be used for selecting consulting firms. This method shall be implemented for assignments that are interrelated at the same time and focus primarily on the technical quality of the service, and shall be selected for the following assignments:

- Highly specialized or complex assignments for which it is difficult to define precise terms of reference and assignment outputs, and where the assignment can be carried out in different ways

- Assignments that have a significant social, economic, and/or environmental impact, which require a consulting firm with high expertise and capacity to implement.

#### Article 20.-

Selection Method Based on Quality and Cost shall be used to select consulting firms. This method shall be applied for large assignments and assignments where the scope of work, terms of reference, time frame, and deliverables can be clearly defined. The use of this method shall balance quality and cost, whereby a higher weight must be assigned to quality over cost in accordance with the level of complex technology of the services.

#### Article 21.-

Selection Method Based on a Fixed Budget shall be used to select consulting firms. This method shall be applied for assignments that are simple, where the scope of work can be clearly defined, and where the budget is fixed. The use of this method shall specify the available budget in order for the consulting firms to submit technical proposals in accordance with that budget.

#### Article 22.-

Selection Method Based on the Lowest Price shall be used to select consulting firms. This method shall be applied for assignments that are standard or routine tasks that have been well-implemented and already have standards.

#### Article 23.-

Selection Method Based on Consultant’s Qualifications shall be used to select consulting firms. This method shall be applied for small assignments or specialized assignments that require consultants with specific skills and capacity to execute the contract.

#### Article 24.-

Single-Source Selection Method shall be used to select consulting firms or individual consultants under special circumstances. This method may only be applied upon prior authorization from the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

Special circumstances appropriate for this method include:

- Continuation of previous work to be performed by the same consulting firm or individual consultant

- Cases of extreme urgency requiring immediate response, or

- Cases where there is only one consulting firm or one individual consultant who is fully qualified and has exceptional experience to implement the project.

#### Article 25.-

Individual Consultant Selection Method shall be used to select individual consultants directly. The application of this method shall evaluate the knowledge, professional skill level, experience, and other relevant skills of the consultant.

#### Article 26.-

Expressions of Interest (EOI) notices for all selection methods of consulting services, except for the single-source selection method, shall be publicly advertised to ensure fair competition among domestic and international consultants.

#### Article 27.-

The conditions and procedures for the selection of consultants under the selection methods in Article 18 of this Law shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

### Chapter 6

### Qualifications of Bidders

#### Article 28.-

All bidders shall be provided with fair and equal opportunities to participate in all public procurements in accordance with the conditions specified for each procurement method, except for those bidders who are unqualified, blacklisted by the Ministry of Economy and Finance, or debarred by a court.

#### Article 29.-

Bidders shall register their categorization and classification at the Ministry of Economy and Finance to be eligible to participate in public procurement.

The Ministry of Economy and Finance shall carry out the categorization and classification of bidders based on their capacity level to ensure contract performance.

The procedures for the categorization and classification of bidders shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 30.-

Prequalification of bidders shall be conducted by the procuring entity only for procurement projects of a complex technical nature.

The procedures for prequalification of bidders shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 31.-

The procuring entity shall review and conduct post-qualification of the leading bidder prior to the decision to award the contract.

The conditions and procedures for post-qualification shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 32.-

The Ministry of Economy and Finance shall blacklist any bidder, supplier, or contractor upon finding that such bidder, supplier, or contractor has provided false or incorrect evidence during the complaint process in the public procurement proceedings, or violated the provisions of this Law or other regulations related to public procurement.

The Ministry of Economy and Finance shall remove the name of a bidder, supplier, or contractor from the blacklist upon the expiration of their blacklisting period.

The blacklisting and removal from the blacklist shall be publicly disclosed and copied to all procuring entities in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

The procedures for blacklisting, removal, and disclosure of the blacklist shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

### Chapter 7

### Procurement Planning

#### Article 33.-

Every procuring entity shall prepare an annual procurement plan. The procurement plan shall be consistent with the annual budget package.

All expenditure projects that must implement public procurement shall be recorded and included in the procurement plan. In cases where they are not recorded in the procurement plan, no expenditure authorization shall be granted.

The conditions and procedures for the preparation, review, and approval of the procurement plan shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 34.-

The procuring entity shall package procurements for procurement items of the same type, identical or similar characteristics, based on actual needs for a single procurement activity, in order to enhance the quality of competition, save expenses, and save time.

#### Article 35.-

Splitting projects into smaller packages for the purpose of avoiding procurement methods is prohibited.

#### Article 36.-

Adjustment to the procurement plan shall be allowed in case of necessity. Any adjustment to the procurement plan due to a lack of sufficient funds for contract performance or to meet already approved contracts is prohibited.

Adjustments to the procurement plan shall be submitted for review and decision to the Ministry of Economy and Finance. The Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance may issue regulatory legal instruments determining conditions that allow procuring entities to adjust their procurement plans without having to request review and decision from the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 37.-

The procuring entity shall send the annual procurement plan to the Ministry of Economy and Finance for review and approval before December 31, prior to the fiscal year of implementation.

#### Article 38.-

The procuring entity shall implement the procurement in accordance with the annual procurement plan approved by the Ministry of Economy and Finance and shall regularly submit procurement implementation reports to the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

### Chapter 8

### Procurement Implementation Procedures

#### Article 39.-

Procurement implementation shall comply with the following stages:

- Preparation of bidding documents

- Advertising of invitation for bids

- Sale of bidding documents and receipt and custody of bid proposals

- Bid opening

- Bid evaluation

- Contract award

- Contract management.

#### Article 40.-

The procurement unit of the procuring entity shall prepare bidding documents in accordance with the provisions of this Law, clearly specifying requirements and evaluation criteria for selecting bidders that are verifiable and relevant to the procurement object. Bidding documents shall be submitted to the chairperson of the procurement committee for review and signature. For large-scale procurement projects that are strategic, have specific technical features for management, and projects with high analytical structures, the procuring entity shall request an endorsement visa on the bidding documents from the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

Standard bidding documents shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 41.-

All bid proposals shall be accompanied by bid security, except for bid proposals of procurements implemented under the price inquiry method and the consultant selection method.

The modalities and procedures for bid security shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 42.-

The procurement unit of the procuring entity shall prepare bid invitation notices for advertisement to select bidders. The bid invitation notice shall contain the following key information:

- Description of the type and quantity of goods, construction works, repairs, or services

- Name, address, email, and telephone number of the procurement unit of the procuring entity

- Procurement method to be implemented

- Various requirements regarding the qualifications of bidders

- Date and time for the sale of bidding documents, receipt, and opening of bid proposals

- Requirements for bid security and performance security.

#### Article 43.-

Bid invitation notices and any amendments shall be publicly advertised on the public procurement website of the Ministry of Economy and Finance, the website of the respective procuring entity, and in newspapers, and shall be posted on the information board of the procuring entity.

#### Article 44.-

The procurement unit of the procuring entity shall prepare sufficient bidding documents for sale to persons who wish to participate in the bidding. The sale of bidding documents shall be conducted widely at a specific location within the procuring entity and through the electronic system of the procuring entity.

The modalities of sale and the determination of the selling price of bidding documents shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 45.-

If there is a written request for clarification from any bidder regarding the bidding documents, the procuring entity shall respond in writing to that requesting bidder and copy the response to all bidders who have received the bidding documents. In case of necessity, the procuring entity shall hold a pre-bid meeting before the deadline for submission of bid proposals to provide clarifications, by inviting the bidders who have received the bidding documents to attend.

#### Article 46.-

The procurement unit of the procuring entity shall receive, register, and keep the bid proposals submitted in sealed envelopes in a secure place, without altering the original condition of the bid envelopes, prior to the bid opening date.

#### Article 47.-

All bid proposals shall be opened publicly immediately after the deadline for submission of bid proposals. The key information of each bid proposal shall be announced in front of the participants of the bid opening and shall be recorded in the bid opening minutes, then copied to all bidders and representatives present at the bid opening ceremony.

If in the initial bidding process, less than three bid proposals are received, the procurement committee of the procuring entity shall not open those bid proposals and shall return them to the bidders.

In cases where the initial bidding process receives less than three bid proposals as specified in the second paragraph above, receives no bid proposals, or there is no bid proposal that responds to the bidding conditions, the bidding process shall be re-conducted.

In the re-bidding process, regardless of the number of bid proposals received, those bid proposals shall be opened in accordance with the specified date.

#### Article 48.-

The procurement committee of the procuring entity shall examine and evaluate all bid proposals confidentially in accordance with the conditions and evaluation criteria specified in the bidding documents. Additional conditions and evaluation criteria not specified in the bidding documents shall not be permitted for use in the evaluation.

The procedures for bid evaluation shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 49.-

The contract award shall be made to the bidder who has submitted a bid proposal that:

- is substantially responsive to the conditions specified in the bidding documents

- has the lowest evaluated price, and

- is qualified after post-qualification review.

#### Article 50.-

Prior to starting the examination and evaluation of bid proposals for each procurement, the chairperson, vice-chairperson, and members of the procurement committee and the procurement group who are involved in the evaluation and contract award shall sign a declaration on the procurement code of ethics as specified in Chapter 11 of this Law.

#### Article 51.-

Upon completion of the evaluation and selection of the bidder, the procuring entity shall issue a notice of intent to award the contract to the winning bidder and copy it to all unsuccessful bidders. The procuring entity shall allow a standstill period of 10 (ten) working days after issuing the notice of intent to award the contract, to allow unsuccessful bidders to request clarification or file a complaint regarding the bidding results.

If there is a written request for clarification from any unsuccessful bidder regarding the reasons for the rejection of their bid proposal, the procuring entity shall provide a written clarification within 5 (five) working days after receiving the request for clarification.

For large-scale procurement projects that are strategic, have specific technical features for management, and projects with high analytical structures, the notice of intent to award the contract shall be issued after obtaining agreement from the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 52.-

After a period of 10 (ten) working days from the date of issuing the notice of intent to award the contract, if there is no complaint or after all complaints have been resolved, the procurement unit of the procuring entity shall prepare the procurement contract as specified in Chapter 9 of this Law, and invite the winning bidder to sign the contract, accompanied by the performance security.

In cases where a complaint is filed during the period specified in the paragraph above, the procuring entity shall take immediate measures to resolve it. If the complaint cannot be resolved, the procuring entity shall request a decision from the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 53.-

The signing of the procurement contract shall be made between the head of the procuring entity or a high-ranking official delegated in accordance with Article 8 of this Law, and the winning bidder.

#### Article 54.-

The procuring entity shall manage the contract to ensure that contract implementation is carried out correctly in accordance with the conditions specified in the contract.

#### Article 55.-

The types of large-scale procurement projects that are strategic, have specific technical features for management, and projects with high analytical structures, which must request review and decision from the Ministry of Economy and Finance, shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

#### Article 56.-

The implementation of public procurement shall comply with the context of sustainable procurement. Sustainable procurement requires technical features, requirements, and criteria that prioritize environmental protection and social and economic impact management.

The conditions, rules, and procedures for implementing sustainable procurement shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

#### Article 57.-

Electronic Government Procurement (e-GP) system is established as a tool to build the system and to facilitate the public procurement process.

Key principles of the electronic government procurement system include, among others:

- Electronic communications shall have equal legal value to paper-based communications.

- Requirements to ensure the sustainability and interoperability of the software and hardware used to support the electronic government procurement system.

- Requirements to ensure the accuracy, integrity, and confidentiality of the transmission and storage of relevant documents.

The rules and procedures for implementing electronic government procurement shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

### Chapter 9

### Procurement Contract

#### Article 58.-

The preparation of a procurement contract shall comply with the provisions of this Law and be consistent with the conditions specified in the bidding documents, as well as the standard contract template determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 59.-

The procurement contract shall be written in Khmer. In case of necessity, the contract may be written in a foreign language, but must be translated from the Khmer version. In case of discrepancy between the Khmer and foreign language versions, the Khmer version shall be the official document.

#### Article 60.-

All procurement contracts shall be paid in Riels, except for procurement contracts implemented for projects abroad, which may be permitted to be paid in foreign currencies.

#### Article 61.-

The technical specifications of goods, works, services, and terms of reference for consulting services specified in the contract must be identical to the technical specifications and terms of reference specified in the bidding documents.

#### Article 62.-

The contract price shall not exceed the bid price after arithmetical correction. The contract price shall be fixed during the contract implementation stage, except under special circumstances, including economic and financial crises causing high inflation and cases of force majeure. Special circumstances under which the contract price may be adjusted shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 63.-

All contracts shall require performance security, except for contracts obtained through the lowest price procurement method, contracts with consulting firms or individual consultants, contracts with specialized entities of state institutions, and contracts with communities. The winning bidder shall post performance security before the contract becomes effective. The amount of performance security shall be equal to the amount specified in the bidding documents.

The performance security shall be forfeited to the state budget if the winning bidder refuses to perform the contract that has entered into force or violates contract conditions, resulting in the termination of the contract by the procuring entity before its expiration.

The performance security shall remain valid until the expiration of the warranty period of goods, works, or services.

The modalities and procedures for performance security shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 64.-

The delivery and acceptance of goods, works, or services shall be conducted properly in terms of size, quantity, quality, technical specifications, and in accordance with the schedule specified in the delivery and acceptance schedule in the contract, and shall be subject to inspection by the delivery and acceptance committee.

The organization and functioning of the delivery and acceptance committee shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 65.-

The procuring entity shall make payments for the contract price to the supplier or contractor based on the payment terms in the contract.

#### Article 66.-

The winning bidder may subcontract parts of the project to a third party, subject to prior written approval from the procuring entity. In cases where the procurement contract is subject to review and decision by the Ministry of Economy and Finance, the procuring entity shall request approval from the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

In cases where the winning bidder is permitted to subcontract to a third party, the winning bidder shall remain liable to the procuring entity for the performance of the entire contract.

#### Article 67.-

All public procurement transactions shall be subject to tax procedures and modalities as specified and shall receive exemptions or incentives in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

## Chapter 10

### Control of Procurement Contracts and Financial Audit

#### Article 68 .-

Public procurement is subject to ex-post control of procurement contracts by the Ministry of Economy and Finance in accordance with the provisions of this Law and other relevant regulations in force.

Rules and procedures for the ex-post control of procurement contracts shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 69 .-

The Ministry of Economy and Finance has the right to conduct unannounced on-site inspections during the implementation of procurement contracts in cases where irregularities are found, such as well-founded suspicions or complaints at any stage of public procurement implementation, aiming to ensure compliance and efficiency in the implementation of procurement contracts.

Rules and procedures for unannounced on-site inspections shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 70 .-

Public procurement contracts of the procurement entities are subject to audit and financial inspection in accordance with laws and regulations in force.

## Chapter 11

### Procurement Code of Conduct

#### Article 71 .-

In the implementation of procurement, the chairperson, vice-chairperson, and members of the procurement committee, the procurement unit, procurement officers, bidders, as well as other officials and persons involved in procurement shall respect the Procurement Code of Conduct.

The Procurement Code of Conduct shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Economy and Finance.

## Chapter 12

### Resolution of Complaints and Disputes

#### Article 72 .-

Bidders have the right to protest to the procurement entity by submitting a written complaint regarding any decision made at each stage of the procurement process prior to signing the contract.

The respective procurement entity has the competence to receive, review, and resolve complaints of bidders.

Bidders who do not agree with the decision of the respective procurement entity may appeal to the Ministry of Economy and Finance for review and resolution.

Procedures and formalities for complaints and complaint resolutions at the procurement entities and the Ministry of Economy and Finance shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 73 .-

Bidders who do not agree with the decision of the Ministry of Economy and Finance may appeal to the competent court of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 74 .-

Any disputes arising during the implementation of the procurement contract, the contracting parties shall resolve them in accordance with the provisions and mechanisms specified in the contract.

## Chapter 13

### Penal Provisions

#### Article 75 .-

Sanctions under this Law include administrative sanctions and criminal penalties.

A. Administrative sanctions include:

- Written warning

- Temporary suspension in public procurement

- Suspension of activities, suspension, revocation, or cancellation of the contract

- Removal from the bidder list

- Blacklisting of bidders.

Written warnings, removal from the bidder list, temporary suspension in public procurement, and suspension of activities, suspension, revocation, or cancellation of the contract fall under the competence of the procurement entity.

Blacklisting of bidders falls under the competence of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

Offenses subject to administrative sanctions shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Economy and Finance.

In the event that any person is dissatisfied with the decision on administrative sanctions as provided in this Law, they have the right to file an appeal to court within a maximum period of 30 (thirty) days from the date of receipt of the notification letter of that decision.

B. Criminal penalties include:

- Fines

- Imprisonment.

#### Article 76 .-

Public servants of all levels, citizens holding public mandates by election, who commit offenses against the provisions of this Law or are found to be involved in irregularities in the public procurement process, shall be subject to administrative sanctions in accordance with the laws and regulations in force. These administrative sanctions shall not prevent prosecution and criminal penalties in accordance with the laws and regulations in force of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 77 .-

Bidders, contractors, or suppliers who are found to be involved in corruption, coercion, collusion, fraud, obstruction, or stealth in the public procurement process, or who provide falsified or incorrect evidence when filing a protest in the public procurement process, shall be terminated immediately from the ongoing public procurement or have their active contract cancelled by the procurement entity. These sanctions shall not prevent the imposition of other administrative sanctions under the competence of the Ministry of Economy and Finance, nor shall they prevent prosecution and criminal penalties in accordance with the laws and regulations in force of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 78 .-

Any person who commits an act in the public procurement bidding process in order to obstruct bidders or undermine the bidding process through gifts, promises, agreements, benefits, or other fraudulent/dishonest means, shall be sentenced to imprisonment from 1 (one) year to 3 (three) years and a fine of 4,000,000 (four million) Riels to 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels.

#### Article 79 .-

Any person who commits an act in the public procurement bidding process to obstruct the freedom of bidding by using violence, coercion, or threat against bidders, suppliers, or contractors, shall be sentenced to imprisonment from 2 (two) years to 5 (five) years and a fine from 8,000,000 (eight million) Riels to 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels.

#### Article 80 .-

The offenses defined in Article 78 and Article 79 of this Law are corruption offenses under the exclusive investigative jurisdiction of the Anti-Corruption Unit in investigating and building case files for referral to court.

Other entities and individuals who are aware of any corruption offenses specified in this Law and other laws and regulations in force of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall report the facts of those offenses to the Anti-Corruption Unit or its municipal/provincial offices.

#### Article 81 .-

Attempted misdemeanor offenses specified in Article 78 and Article 79 of this Law shall be punished in the same manner as the completed misdemeanor offenses.

#### Article 82 .-

For the misdemeanor offenses specified in Article 78 and Article 79 of this Law, one or more additional penalties as specified in Article 53 (Types of additional penalties) of the Penal Code may be imposed.

#### Article 83 .-

Legal entities may be declared criminally responsible under the conditions specified in Article 42 (Criminal responsibility of legal entities) of the Penal Code for the misdemeanor offenses specified in Article 78 and Article 79 of this Law.

Legal entities committing the offenses specified in Article 78 and Article 79 of this Law shall be punished by a fine of 40,000,000 (forty million) Riels to 200,000,000 (two hundred million) Riels, along with one or more additional penalties specified in Article 168 (Additional penalties applicable to legal entities) of the Penal Code.

#### Article 84 .-

The implementation of the provisions of Chapter 13 of this Law does not prevent the application of other criminal laws in cases where the acts defined as offenses under this Law are also offenses defined under other criminal laws.

## Chapter 14

### Transitional Provisions

#### Article 85 .-

Any regulations related to the implementation of the Law on Public Procurement promulgated by Royal Decree No. NS/RKM/0112/004 dated January 14, 2012, and those related to the public procurement sector currently in force, shall continue to be effective until replaced by new regulations in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

## Chapter 15

### Final Provisions

#### Article 86 .-

The Law on Public Procurement promulgated by Royal Decree No. NS/RKM/0112/004 dated January 14, 2012, and any regulations contrary to this Law, shall be repealed.

#### Article 87 .-

This Law shall be declared as urgent.

## Annex of the Law on Public Procurement

### Glossary

- **Procurement Package** refers to the grouping of procurement items of the same, similar, or comparable nature into a single group for a single procurement execution.

- **Bidding / Tendering** refers to the public procurement process in which bid dossiers/proposals are submitted, received, and evaluated for the purpose of contract award.

- **Ex-post Control of Procurement Contracts** refers to the inspection by the Ministry of Economy and Finance on the compliance of the implementation of procurement contracts carried out by the respective procurement entity, to monitor and evaluate contract performance and the need for improvement and enhancement of efficiency and capacity in implementing the law and relevant regulations.

- **Competition** refers to the use of procurement procedures involving participation from bidders to select the most suitable, fully responsive bid dossier at the best price.

- **Economy / Savings** refers to the most appropriate use of budget in expenditure, which also takes into account relevant factors in implementing procurement procedures to select suppliers or contractors.

- **Procurement / Procurement Process** refers to the public procurement process from the preparation of bid dossiers until the receipt of goods, works, services, or consulting services.

- **Procurement Code of Conduct** refers to the code that participants involved in public procurement must understand and implement in good faith in accordance with legal regulations in force, and perform properly in accordance with their roles and duties.

- **Accountability** refers to the responsibility of the procurement entity in making decisions and taking actions during the public procurement process.

- **Sustainability** refers to the minimization of potential impacts on the environment, ecosystem, and the continuity of socio-economic development.

- **Transparency** refers to open access to information related to public procurement and the right to participate in the public procurement process in accordance with public procurement methods and procedures.

- **Consultant** refers to a consulting firm or an individual consultant whose business is to provide intellectual services. A consulting firm may be a commercial enterprise or other legal entity.

- **Goods** refers to liquids, solids, and gases that have monetary value or economic utility, such as machinery, equipment, materials, supplies, foodstuff, medicine, and other products, including electricity.

- **Blacklist** refers to the list of bidders barred from participating in public procurement processes, as decided by the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

- **Efficiency** refers to targeted expenditures in relation to the costs for which procurement procedures must be implemented.

- **Procurement Plan** refers to the determination of the needs of the procurement entity within a specific period and the scheduling and procedures for executing procurement, with the aim of meeting usage needs in a timely manner when required.

- **Procurement Officer** refers to the chairperson, vice-chairperson, and all officers of the procurement unit of the procurement entity.

- **Procurement Subject Matter** refers to the items of goods, works, services, or consulting services that are the subject of bidding, price consultation, price standardization, or price negotiation.

- **Procurement** refers to the purchase or acquisition of goods, works, services, or consulting services through the methods specified in this Law.

- **Sustainable Procurement** refers to a procurement process that takes into account the minimization of potential impacts on the environment, ecosystem, and sustainable community development.

- **Public Procurement** refers to procurement carried out by the procurement entity or the fully-delegated authorizing entity.

- **Procurement Entity** refers to ministries, institutions, fully-delegated authorizing entities, capital, provincial, municipal, district, khan, commune, sangkat administrations, public enterprises, public administrative institutions, and other financially autonomous entities that execute public procurement.

- **Integrity** refers to the implementation of the fundamental principles and professional code of conduct that cannot be compromised in public procurement, across both the legal system and institutions.

- **Services** refers to activities involving the use of human labor, equipment, or technology that benefit public institutions, but does not include construction works and consulting services.

- **Consulting Services** refers to expert services provided by a consulting firm or an individual consultant related to any project or for institutional strengthening or capacity building.

- **Works / Construction** refers to all activities related to construction work, repair, rehabilitation, installation, excavation, piling, and similar works, which involve the use of labor, machinery, equipment, and technology.

- **Bid Dossier / Bid Proposal** refers to the set of documents, including the bid dossier completed by the bidder and other information as required in the bidding documents, submitted by the bidder to the procurement entity for the purpose of supplying goods, executing construction, providing services, or consulting services.

- **Effectiveness** refers to the scope or level of achieving the final results in response to the core objective of implementing public procurement.

- **Sub-authorizing Budgeting Entity with Full Delegation** refers to a budgeting entity to which the primary authorizing officer has delegated full authority to command revenues and expenditures of the entity, without requiring the clearance of the finance department and the inspection and decision of the primary authorizing officer.

- **Collusion / Collusive Act** refers to a plan or arrangement between two or more bidders designed to artificially increase bidding prices, without genuine competition, or to influence the actions of any party in the public procurement process or to affect contract performance.

- **Coercion / Coercive Act** refers to threatening or harming, directly or indirectly, any person or their property to influence their participation in the public procurement process or to affect contract performance.

- **Fraud / Fraudulent Act** refers to a misrepresentation of facts in order to influence the public procurement process.

- **Bidder** refers to any natural or legal person who intends to participate in public procurement and has submitted a bid proposal or a request to express interest.

- **First-choice Bidder** refers to a bidder who has participated in the bidding process and whose bid proposal has been evaluated by the procurement committee as the most responsive prior to post-qualification.

- **Contractor** refers to any natural or legal person who signs a contract for construction works, repairs, services, or consulting services with the respective procurement entity.

- **Supplier** refers to any natural or legal person who signs a contract for selling goods with the respective procurement entity.

- **Bidding Documents** refers to the set of documents prepared and initially provided by the procurement unit of the procurement entity to bidders for use in preparing their bid proposals, which includes the bid application, invitation to bid, request for submission of bid proposals, invitation to participate in pre-qualification, instructions to bidders, technical specifications, scope of work, terms of reference (TOR), and contract conditions.
