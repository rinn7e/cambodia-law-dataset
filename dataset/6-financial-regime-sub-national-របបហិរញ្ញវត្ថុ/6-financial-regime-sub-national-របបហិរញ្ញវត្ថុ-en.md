## Promulgates

The Law on Financial Regime and Property Management of Sub-National Administrations, which was adopted by the National Assembly on May 11, 2011, during the 6th session of the 4th legislature, and which was entirely approved by the Senate as to its form and legal substance without any amendment on May 31, 2011, during the 9th plenary session of the 2nd legislature, and the full text of which is as follows:

# Law

## On

# Financial Regime and Property Management of Sub-National Administrations

# Chapter I

## General Provisions

### Article 1.-

This law aims to define the financial regime and property management of Sub-National Administrations in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Article 2.-

This law aims to establish financial resources in order to enable Sub-National Administrations to have appropriate capacity for sustainable local development based on democratic principles and to promote the implementation of the Royal Government's decentralization and deconcentration policies in order to reduce the poverty of citizens.

### Article 3.-

This law shall apply to the financial regime and property management of Capital, Province, Municipality, District, and Khan Administrations in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

This law shall not apply to the financial regime and property management of Commune and Sangkat Administrations, except for provisions specified in this law.

### Article 4.-

Capital, Province, Municipality, District, Khan, Commune, and Sangkat Administrations, hereinafter referred to as "Sub-National Administrations", are public legal entities having their own property, financial resources, and budgets.

In the framework of this law, the term "Sub-National Administrations" refers to Capital, Province, Municipality, District, and Khan Administrations.

### Article 5.-

In managing financial affairs and the budget, Sub-National Administrations must follow the basic principles as stated in Article 2, Article 5, and Article 6 of the Law on Public Finance System.

# Chapter Ii

## Competence and Management of Finance and Property of Sub-National Administrations

### Article 6.-

Each Council shall represent and act on behalf of citizens, and shall manage public financial affairs in order to promote sustainable local development based on democratic principles within its jurisdiction. The Council shall manage and administer financial affairs with efficiency, transparency, and accountability to its citizens and to the Royal Government, with the Ministry of Economy and Finance acting as the chief of staff.

### Article 7.-

In managing finance and property, each Council of the Sub-National Administration shall be responsible for the following:

- Reviewing and adopting the development plan, three-year rolling investment program, and medium-term expenditure framework

- Reviewing and adopting the budget strategic plan and the annual budget draft submitted by the Governor, and having the right to make budget reallocations, provided that such changes do not violate laws, regulations, or budget preparation principles

- Reviewing and adopting budget adjustments upon request of the Governor

- Reviewing and adopting the annual financial report

- Reviewing and adopting the property inventory clearance of the Sub-National Administration in accordance with the rules and regulations in force

- Monitoring and supervising the financial and budget implementation of the Governor

- Reviewing and adopting contracts and agreements that create financial commitments or benefits extending beyond the current budget implementation year.

### Article 8.-

The Governor of the Board of Governors of Capital, Province, Municipality, District, and Khan shall act as the representative of ministries and institutions of the Royal Government within the jurisdiction of each Council, in the sector of

finance. The Governor of the Board of Governors, on behalf of their Council, shall be the direct executor of the Council's bylaws and decisions in implementing this law.

### Article 9.-

The Governor of the Board of Governors of the Sub-National Administration shall be responsible for the following tasks:

- Preparing the development plan, three-year rolling investment program, and medium-term expenditure framework which must be updated on an ongoing basis every year

- Preparing the budget strategic plan and the annual budget draft

- Implementing the action plan and the annual budget approved by the Council, in accordance with financial management rules and procedures in force

- Regularly reporting to the Council on the implementation of the action plan and the annual budget

- Preparing the annual financial report, the annual budget revenue and expenditure execution report, and other reports to be submitted to the Council for review and adoption as determined

- Regularly reporting to the Ministry of Economy and Finance and relevant ministries and institutions on the implementation and financial situation of the Sub-National Administration after various reports have been approved by the Council

- Managing public property within the jurisdiction of the Council and maintaining property allocated by the national level to be held within the framework of laws and regulations in force.

### Article 10.-

The Finance Director of the Sub-National Administration shall be responsible for directly assisting the Council and the Governor of the Board of Governors of the Sub-National Administration in achieving all tasks specified in Article 7 and Article 9 of this law.

The Finance Director shall perform the above role with coordination from the Administration Director and in cooperation with various units of the Sub-National Administration.

The Finance Director of the Sub-National Administration shall be responsible for financial management under the authority of the Governor of the Board of Governors of the Sub-National Administration.

The Finance Director shall be appointed by the Council, after receiving approval from the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance upon request of the Minister of the Ministry of Interior.

The procedures for selection, appointment, and definition of qualifications of the Finance Director shall be determined by an inter-ministerial proclamation of the Minister of the Ministry of Interior and the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

### Article 11.-

For the financial sector within the framework of all provisions in Chapter III of this law, the Governor of the Board of Governors, on behalf of the Council, is the primary authorizer (ordonnateur principal) who prepares and implements the budget of the Sub-National Administration.

In implementing the budget in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Public Finance System, the Governor of the Board of Governors, on behalf of the Council, shall be the person certifying revenue, committing expenditure, liquidating accounts, and ordering payment based on the approved budget of the Sub-National Administration, and shall manage, open, and close administrative accounts.

### Article 12.-

The Governor of the Board of Governors of the Sub-National Administration may delegate in writing the authority to approve invoices to any Deputy Governor, as a delegated authorizer (ordonnateur délégué) acting under the responsibility and supervision of the Governor. The authorizer and the delegated authorizer must deposit their official specimen signatures with the public accountant.

### Article 13.-

Khans and Sangkats under a Capital shall have their budgets included in the Capital's budget. The Khan Governor and the Sangkat Chief of the Capital shall be the delegated authorizers from the Capital Governor for their respective budgets.

Sangkats under a Municipality shall have their budgets included in the Municipality's budget. The Sangkat Chief of the Municipality shall be the delegated authorizer from the Municipal Governor for their respective budget.

The structure, format, and procedures for incorporating the budget of the Khan and Sangkat into the Capital budget, and the Sangkat budget into the Municipal budget, shall be determined by a proclamation of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance, after consultation with the Minister of the Ministry of Interior.

### Article 14.-

The custody of cash of the Sub-National Administration shall be ensured by the public accountant of the Treasury, who receives execution instructions from the authorizer of the Sub-National Administration, in strict compliance with the principles of public accounting control and management in force.

### Article 15.-

The Treasury at the sub-national level shall perform the duties of the public accountant of the Sub-National Administration and shall implement all tasks as follows:

- Collecting taxes and revenues of the Sub-National Administration

- Paying expenses authorized by the Governor of the Board of Governors of the Sub-National Administration

- Managing and disbursing the budget of the Sub-National Administration

- Preparing financial accounting reports on a regular basis

- Keeping and storing documents, receipts, and accounting ledgers of the Sub-National Administration.

### Article 16.-

The Director of the Department of Economy and Finance, the Director of the Treasury, and the Director of the Tax Branch or Tax Office located at the sub-national level shall be the delegated representatives of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance in order to assist the Council and the Governor of the Board of Governors of the Sub-National Administration in implementing this law.

# Chapter Iii

## Budget of Sub-National Administrations

### Section 1

#### Budget Management Principles

### Article 17.-

Sub-National Administrations must have financial resources and an appropriate budget to implement all roles and tasks for economic and social development within their jurisdiction. The annual budget draft of the Sub-National Administration must plan and authorize all resources and burdens, ensuring the balance of the budget.

The budget of the Sub-National Administration shall be subject to all provisions of the Law on Public Finance System.

### Article 18.-

The budget of the Sub-National Administration must be prepared and adopted annually. The fiscal year of the Sub-National Administration's budget shall begin on January 1 and end on December 31.

The budget of the Sub-National Administration must be prepared and adopted with a real balance between revenue and expenditure in accordance with the budget format and classification determined by a proclamation of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

### Article 19.-

The budget of the Sub-National Administration must be prepared, adopted, and implemented in accordance with the following principles:

- Full information must be disseminated regarding the preparation and implementation of the budget

- All revenues and expenditures must be part of a single budget

- The budget must include all revenues and expenditures of the Sub-National Administration

- Revenues must be collected and presented as a gross amount without any deduction of expenditure

- No specific revenue shall be allocated to any specific expenditure, except with permission specified by separate regulations

- Total projected expenditure must be fully guaranteed by total projected revenue

- No revenue or expenditure shall be collected or utilized outside of the approved budget.

### Article 20.-

Each Sub-National Administration must prepare, adopt, and implement a medium-term expenditure framework.

The medium-term expenditure framework must specify realistic forecasts based on medium-term revenue and the rules by which these revenues are allocated to various expenditure items of the Sub-National Administration.

The format and procedures for preparation and implementation of the medium-term expenditure framework shall be determined by a proclamation of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

### Article 21.-

The Sub-National Administration has no right to borrow money from others, lend money to others, issue bonds, or provide guarantees or letters of guarantee, and to perform any act directly or indirectly that may lead to the occurrence of

any debt or other financial burden, directly or indirectly, to the Kingdom of Cambodia.

For receiving grants, the Sub-National Administration must obtain prior agreement from the Minister of the Ministry of Interior and the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

### Article 22.-

The Sub-National Administration must manage and utilize its budget with efficiency, transparency, and accountability to its citizens and to the Royal Government, with the Ministry of Economy and Finance acting as the chief of staff.

## Section 2

### Financial Burdens and Resources of Sub-National Administrations

### Article 23.-

Budget expenditures of the Sub-National Administration include:

- Administrative operating expenditures

- Expenditures in implementing mandatory functions

- Expenditures in implementing optional functions, and

- Expenditures in implementing other roles and tasks determined by laws or other legal instruments in force.

### Article 24.-

Budget revenues of the Sub-National Administration include:

- Local source revenues

- National level revenues

- Other revenues determined by laws or other legal instruments.

### Article 25.-

Local source revenues include tax revenues and non-tax revenues:

1- Local tax revenues are tax and duty revenues to be created and determined by law within the framework of tax laws for the entire benefit of the budget

of the sub-national administrations. The classification and proportion of local tax and duty revenues among each sub-national administration shall be determined by sub-decree.

2- Non-tax revenues at the local level include:

- Revenues obtained from the lease of property of sub-national administrations, service fees, and other revenues

- The allocation of non-tax revenues at the local level among each sub-national administration and the maximum price list for collecting revenues from service fees and other revenues shall be determined by sub-decree upon the proposal of the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance, after agreement from the Minister of Ministry of Interior

- Donations obtained from domestic and foreign sources within the jurisdiction of each council

- Revenues from other sources determined by law or other legal instruments.

#### Article 26._

Sources of revenue from the national level include:

- Shared revenues

- Funds transferred from the national budget, and

- Service fees obtained from agency functions performed by the council on behalf of ministries and institutions of the Royal Government.

#### Article 27._

Shared revenue is revenue that must be shared between the national level and the relevant sub-national administrations. The sources of revenue and the allocation proportion shall be determined by law or sub-decree upon the proposal of the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance, after agreement from the Minister of Ministry of Interior.

#### Article 28._

Funds transferred from the national budget include conditional transfer funds and unconditional transfer funds.

Sub-national administrations have the right to receive annually from the national budget conditional transfer funds and unconditional transfer funds, which shall be made in phases for each year.

Procedures and formats for transferring funds from the national budget to sub-national administrations shall be determined by sub-decree upon the proposal of the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance, after agreement from the Minister of Ministry of Interior.

#### Article 29._

Conditional transfer funds are funds that sub-national administrations must use:

- To manage, allocate, and perform one or more mandatory functions transferred to sub-national administrations through delegation of functions or delegation of authority, or

- To continue to manage or allocate and perform one or more optional functions previously performed by ministries and institutions of the Royal Government, or

- For one or more specified mandatory purposes.

Formats, rules, and conditions for transferring conditional funds shall be determined by sub-decree upon the proposal of the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance, after agreement from the Minister of Ministry of Interior.

#### Article 30._

Unconditional transfer funds are funds that sub-national administrations must use to:

- Fulfill legal obligations

- Perform functions and duties in creating, promoting, and sustaining livelihoods for democratic development

- Cover their administrative expenses, and

- Choose, manage, allocate, and perform various optional functions.

Sub-national administrations have the right to receive annually from the national budget unconditional transfer funds based on criteria and formula with transparency.

Formats, rules, and conditions for transferring unconditional funds shall be determined by sub-decree upon the proposal of the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance, after agreement from the Minister of Ministry of Interior.

#### Article 31._

Municipal and district funds with separate accounts at the National Bank shall be established to receive unconditional transfer funds from the national budget and resources from other sources for

benefit of the municipal and district administrations.

Unconditional transfer funds to be transferred from the national budget to the municipal and district funds shall be made in phases within each year based on a formula or a specific rate and shall be predictable for the medium term.

The allocation of resources from the municipal and district funds to each municipal and district administration shall be made annually based on criteria and formula with transparency.

The establishment and implementation rules of the municipal and district funds shall be determined by sub-decree upon the proposal of the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance, after agreement from the Minister of Ministry of Interior.

#### Article 32._

Sub-national administrations may receive revenues from the implementation of agency functions on behalf of ministries and institutions of the Royal Government.

#### Article 33._

Sub-national administrations may receive agency functions on behalf of the Royal Government or ministries and institutions of the Royal Government based on laws or other legal instruments.

Sub-national administrations may discuss and agree with ministries, institutions, or departments and units of the national administration to receive and implement any task through a contract or agreement which clearly defines the tasks to be performed, the execution timeframe, the results to be achieved, and the service fees that the executing agent shall receive.

Formats and rules for using the budget for implementing the above agency functions shall be determined by proclamation of the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance, after agreement from the Minister of Ministry of Interior.

### Section 3

### Preparation and Approval of Budget of Sub-national Administrations

#### Article 34.-

The budget strategic plan and budget draft of sub-national administrations shall be prepared by the governor of the board of governors and approved by the council, in accordance with the procedures determined by proclamation of the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance.

All budget drafts of sub-national administrations are a budget package to be submitted for review and approval by the National Assembly and the Senate within the framework of reviewing and approving the annual financial law. After the annual financial law has been reviewed and approved by the National Assembly and the Senate, the budget package of the sub-national administrations shall be allocated to each council by proclamation of the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 35._

The budget draft of sub-national administrations shall be prepared according to the following calendar:

1- Budget Strategic Planning (from March to May)

The Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance shall issue guidelines on budget strategic planning to all ministries and institutions and all units in order to notify each council of its programs and sector budget drafts to be implemented within the jurisdiction of each council. On behalf of the council, the governor of the board of governors of the sub-national administration shall prepare its budget strategic plan based on the development plan, investment program, and medium-term expenditure framework of each sub-national administration, and then submit it to its council for review, discussion, and approval, and shall send it to the Provincial Department of Economy and Finance for consolidation and submission to the Ministry of Economy and Finance no later than May 15.

2- Budget Package Preparation (from June to September)

In the first week of June, the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance shall prepare a circular on the technical guidelines for budget draft preparation, specifying the formats, procedures for budget draft preparation, and relevant documents to be attached, and send them to sub-national administrations to prepare their annual revenue and expenditure budget drafts. Each governor of the board of governors of the sub-national administration shall prepare its annual revenue and expenditure budget draft based on the technical circular for budget draft preparation and the policy priorities of its administration, integrating all budget sources to be implemented within its jurisdiction.

The budget draft of sub-national administrations shall be prepared by the governor of the board of governors of each sub-national administration with the assistance of the finance chief of the council and officials of the Provincial Department of Economy and Finance. Prior to the meeting of its council, the governor of the board of governors of each sub-national administration shall publicly disseminate the budget draft to gather participatory comments from citizens and stakeholders within their jurisdiction, and shall

review and revise this budget draft to submit to its council for discussion and approval in a public meeting no later than June 30.

For the preparation of municipal and district budget drafts, the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance may delegate responsibility to the provincial governor of the board of governors to assist in providing technical support to ensure their budget drafts are correct and in compliance with the provisions of this law, regulations, formats, and procedures determined at the national level. The provincial governor of the board of governors shall report to its council meeting on this technical support process.

The provincial governor of the board of governors shall ensure that officials of the Provincial Department of Economy and Finance consolidate the provincial budget draft, municipal budget drafts, and district budget drafts within their province into the formats determined by the Ministry of Economy and Finance, attached with the budget drafts and explanatory notes of the province, municipalities, and districts, and send these drafts and documents to the Ministry of Economy and Finance no later than July 15.

The Ministry of Economy and Finance shall consolidate all budget drafts of sub-national administrations for each year.

Throughout the entire month of August, the Ministry of Economy and Finance shall conduct a legality review and verify compliance of the budget of the capital, budgets of the provinces, budgets of the municipalities, and budgets of the districts.

The Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance may delegate the legality review to its specialized units or any authority of the sub-national administration as necessary.

The legality review process shall involve the participation of the capital, provincial, municipal, and district governors of the boards of governors representing the councils that own the budgets, representatives from the Ministry of Interior, and other members determined by the Ministry of Economy and Finance. In this legality review process, the Ministry of Economy and Finance or the authority delegated by the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance shall verify the principles of legality review as follows:

- The budget draft has been prepared in accordance with the budget classification formats, forms, and procedures determined

- Public participation and input from various stakeholders in the budget preparation process

- Budget allocation for credits is in accordance with the principles of necessary expenditure

- The budget draft is planned according to the conditions attached to the use of resources from the national level as predetermined

- Ensure a real balance between budget revenues and expenditures

- Discussion and approval of the budget draft in a public meeting of the council.

In the event that the Ministry of Economy and Finance or the authority delegated by the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance finds that the budget draft of any sub-national administration is not consistent with the legality review principles as stated above, the Ministry of Economy and Finance or the authority delegated by the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance shall decide to make adjustments and notify in writing the governor of the board of governors of the sub-national administration to revise and adjust in accordance with the procedures in force.

3- Budget Approval of Sub-national Administrations (from October to December)

In the first week of October, the Ministry of Economy and Finance shall submit the budget draft of sub-national administrations, which is a chapter of the draft annual financial law, to the Council of Ministers for review and decision, and then submit it to the National Assembly in the first week of November for review and approval, and submit it to the Senate in the first week of December, so that this draft law shall be finally adopted before December 25.

#### Article 36._

If the annual budget draft of a sub-national administration is not approved before January 1 of the fiscal year to be implemented, the governor of the sub-national administration has the right to collect revenues under the conditions approved in the previous year and ensure monthly expenditures at the level of 1/12 of the previous year's budget.

### Section 4

### Budget Implementation of Sub-national Administrations

#### Article 37._

To serve revenue and expenditure operations, each sub-national administration shall open a deposit account at the relevant National Bank.

#### Article 38._

The budget of a sub-national administration may be adjusted during the fiscal year to respond to changes in economic and financial conditions that affect the initial forecast of

of revenues and expenditures. Budget adjustments shall be prepared, reviewed, approved, and endorsed in accordance with the procedures set forth in Article 35 of this law.

#### Article 39._

Internal credit transfers within the budget of a sub-national administration shall be made under conditions determined by proclamation of the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance. The Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance may delegate to the sub-national administration the authority to adjust its credits to address urgent needs in case of necessity.

#### Article 40._

Unforeseen expenditure credits may be used to guarantee expenditures for which allocation was not specifically determined when the credits were approved, or for supplementary credits to benefit other expenditure categories of the sub-national administration.

The governor shall request approval from its council when there is a need to use unforeseen expenditure credits.

#### Article 41._

Projects or programs whose implementation is not yet completed within the fiscal year shall be integrated to continue implementation by carrying forward the expenditure plans to the budget of the following year.

#### Article 42._

In the event that the implementation of the budget of a sub-national administration results in an imbalance, the sub-national administration shall take ownership to implement all kinds of measures to ensure the re-establishment of budget balance. At the end of the fiscal year, if the actual revenues of the budget of the sub-national administration exceed the planned annual revenues, the expenditure credits may be increased or adjusted to benefit other budgets of sub-national administrations by proclamation of the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance in accordance with the specified excess revenue level. At the end of the year, the budget surplus of the sub-national administration shall be recorded into the result account of the National Bank.

#### Article 43._

The annual financial report of the sub-national administration is subject to internal audit and audit by competent institutions. The purpose of internal financial audit and audit is to ensure transparency, accountability, and efficiency of financial information sources and the evaluation of budget implementation of the sub-national administration under the public financial management system.

Financial inspection and audit reports, which include the comments of the council of the sub-national administration, shall be publicly disseminated.

#### Article 44._

Financial operations and accounting shall be executed in accordance with all rules on financial control, public procurement management, and general public accounting regulations to be determined by law and other legal instruments.

#### Article 45._

The budget settlement of sub-national administrations is a chapter of the general state budget settlement law. In compliance with the principle of unified accounting, the governor of the board of governors of the sub-national administration has the duty to prepare and provide the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance with reports on budget implementation and other necessary information at the end of each fiscal year.

#### Article 46._

At the end of the fiscal year, the governor of the board of governors of each sub-national administration shall reconcile their administrative accounts with the management accounts maintained by the public treasurer of the Treasury, and prepare reports on budget implementation and other necessary information to submit to the council for review and approval, and send them to the Provincial Department of Economy and Finance to consolidate into a joint report for each capital and province, and then send it to the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance to consolidate into a draft settlement of the budget of sub-national administrations and integrate it into a chapter of the draft general state budget settlement law to be submitted for review and approval by the National Assembly and the Senate.

### Chapter 4

### Management of Property of Sub-national Administrations

#### Article 47._

The property of sub-national administrations includes property transferred to them and property acquired by themselves. The State shall transfer and grant to sub-national administrations the right of possession and use of certain public properties of the State and private properties of the State which are immovable properties located within the jurisdiction of each council in accordance with state determination.

The transfer or granting of state property to sub-national administrations shall be made by sub-decree upon the proposal of the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance, after agreement from the Minister of Ministry of Interior. The transfer or granting shall be executed in a revocable manner.

#### Article 48._

The transfer of property from the national level for the benefit of sub-national administrations for public use or for fulfilling the needs of public service shall be aligned with the functions transferred to each sub-national administration.

#### Article 49._

State property transferred or granted to sub-national administrations is property that sub-national administrations may manage, use, and generate revenue from. The sub-national administrations shall maintain and protect such property and have no right to sell, lease, transfer ownership, or transfer the use thereof without prior approval from the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance, after agreement from the Minister of Ministry of Interior.

#### Article 50._

Property acquired by sub-national administrations themselves is their own property, over which they have autonomy to manage and use to generate revenue within the framework determined by law or other legal instruments.

#### Article 51._

Sub-national administrations shall prepare an inventory of all properties of the sub-national administration and update it annually, and submit it to the Ministry of Economy and Finance to be integrated into the state property master inventory.

### Chapter 5

### Special Provisions

#### Article 52._

In implementing this law, to align with the necessary requirements and based on the concrete situations of each sub-national administration including commune and sangkat administrations, the Minister of Ministry of Economy and Finance shall consult with the Minister of Ministry of Interior to propose sub-decrees or issue separate proclamations for each of those sub-national administrations.

### Chapter 6

### Penalty Provisions

#### Article 53._

Members of councils and public officials of all types and levels currently serving, and those who have previously served, who are found to have failed to comply with the provisions of this law in performing their functions and duties assigned, shall be subject to disciplinary penalties in accordance with the laws and legal instruments in force, without prejudice to criminal penalties and other civil liabilities, and must fully compensate the budget of the sub-national administration for all losses and damages of property or public funds, including interest on the value of losses and damages caused by this mistake.

### Chapter 7

### Transitional Provisions

#### Article 54._

Laws and legal instruments related to the financial regime and property of provinces and municipalities previously implemented shall remain in force until new laws and legal instruments are in force.

to replace in accordance with the spirit of the Law on Administrative Management of the Capital, Provinces, Municipalities, Districts, and Khan, the Law on Public Finance System, and this law.

### Chapter 8

### Final Provisions

#### Article 55._

The Law on Financial Regime and Property of Provinces and Municipalities, promulgated by Royal Kram No. NS/RKM/0298/03 dated February 25, 1998, all provisions amending the Law on Financial Regime and Property of Provinces and Municipalities, and all provisions contrary to this law shall be repealed.

#### Article 56._

This law is declared urgent.
