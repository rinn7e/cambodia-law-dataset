# LAW
# ON
# THE STATUTE OF JUDGES AND PROSECUTORS

## Chapter 1
## General Provisions

#### Article 1.-
This law defines the statute of judges and prosecutors and other principles related to judges and prosecutors, for the purpose of guaranteeing the independence of the judiciary in accordance with the Constitution of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 2.-
This law has the scope to apply to judges and prosecutors throughout the Kingdom of Cambodia, unless there is a separate law specifying otherwise.

#### Article 3.-
The technical terminology in this law has the following meaning:

- Judge refers to professional judges, including but not limited to, trial judges and investigating judges.

- Prosecutor refers to deputy prosecutors, prosecutors, deputy general prosecutors and general prosecutors.

- Magistrate refers to judges and prosecutors.

#### Article 4.-
Individuals selected to perform the function of judges and prosecutors must be persons of competence, integrity and high moral character.

#### Article 5.-
During their career, a judge may be appointed as a prosecutor and a prosecutor may also be appointed as a judge again.

#### Article 6.-
Judges and prosecutors shall receive a guarantee of protection of their security, personal safety and that of their families, in case of necessity, upon their request.

Judges and prosecutors shall not be liable under criminal or civil law for any decisions made within the framework of their competence and functions as a judge or prosecutor. This principle shall not apply in the case where the judge or prosecutor uses their decision by committing a serious abuse with malicious intent in the exercise of their competence and functions assigned by law.

#### Article 7.-
All judges and prosecutors are within the central administrative framework of the Ministry of Justice and may be assigned to work at the Ministry of Justice. In the case of being assigned to work at the Ministry of Justice, the judge must request for approval from the Supreme Council of the Magistracy.

During the period of working at the Ministry of Justice, judges and prosecutors shall be under the Statute of Prosecutors and receive salary and functional allowance as prosecutors serving in the prosecution office attached to the courts. In the case where the salary and functional allowance are different, the person has the right to choose either the salary or functional allowance that is higher.

## Chapter 2
## Judges
### Section 1
### General Provisions

#### Article 8.-
All judges shall decide cases impartially, based on the principle of respect for law without receiving pressure, threats or concealment, direct or indirect orders from any party in the case or from any person.

#### Article 9.-
Judges shall be appointed to serve in a court for a period of 4 (four) years.

A judge shall absolutely not be transferred to another location without their consent.

during the period of performance as stated in paragraph 1 above, even if that location has a higher rank. However, the Supreme Council of the Magistracy may transfer a judge to work in another location in the case where the judge has a second-degree disciplinary penalty as stated in Article 55 of this law or due to the necessary requirements for the smooth running of justice.

### Section 2
### Seniority and Grade

#### Article 10.-
Judge framework has 3 (three) ranks:

- Supreme Judge

- Senior Judge

- Junior Judge.

#### Article 11.-
The grades for supreme judge rank, senior judge rank, and junior judge rank shall be determined by Royal Decree.

### Section 3
### Uniform

#### Article 12.-
Judge uniform includes:

- Uniform during hearing and uniform during performance of other functions besides hearing.

- Official uniform for various national ceremonies.

These uniforms shall be determined by Royal Decree upon request of the Supreme Council of the Magistracy.

#### Article 13.-
The color of uniform that all judges must wear during hearings is purple.

The uniform during performances of other functions besides hearing is clothing or other insignia. The use of this clothing or insignia shall be determined by the Supreme Council of the Magistracy.

The uniform that all judges must wear during parades or in various national ceremonies if officially required is official clothing.

### Section 4
### Salary, Other Benefits, Rewards and Leaves

#### Article 14.-
Judges shall receive a salary scaled according to their rank and grade, functional allowance and other allowances, and other rewards in accordance with the provisions in force.

#### Article 15.-
Salary, functional allowance and other allowances such as mission allowance, housing allowance, living cost allowance, transfer of work location allowance, hospital treatment expenses and allowance for long-term chronic illness treatment of judges shall be determined by Sub-Decree.

#### Article 16.-
Judges shall have 30 (thirty) days of annual leave in addition to public holidays determined by the Royal Government. The working period giving the right to this annual leave shall be calculated from January 1st.

Judges may request leave multiple times or all at once in one year. This leave can be extended until the end of February of the following year.

The request for annual leave must be submitted to the President of the Court at least 3 (three) months before the leave.

Female judges have the right to 90 (ninety) days of maternity leave.

During the above leave period, judges shall receive a salary, functional allowance and other allowances.

#### Article 17.-
A judge who has worked for 2 (two) full years, with no interruption or with interruption but completed 2 (two) years, may be granted leave for medical treatment for a period of 6 (six) months without salary deduction.

If leave for medical treatment continues from 6 (six) months to 1 (one) year, the salary shall be reduced to half. If leave for medical treatment is required for more than 1 (one) year, the judge must request a leave of absence without salary as per Article 45 of this law. In case of not requesting a leave of absence without salary, the judge shall be retired due to loss of professional fitness as per Article 66 of this law.

A judge who is ill may request sick leave from the Ministry of Justice and must provide a medical certificate as proof. The Ministry of Justice may have competent specialist doctors perform inspections at any time if deemed necessary.

#### Article 18.-
For judges who have not taken annual leave, the period of untaken leave can be added to the medical treatment period above without salary deduction.

### Section 5
### Selection of Judges

#### Article 19.-
Candidates allowed to take the entrance examination for student judges are those who meet the following conditions:

1- Must have Khmer nationality from birth.

2- Be at most 35 (thirty-five) years old for student candidates and at most 40 (forty) years old for civil servant candidates, as of the exam date.

3- Must have at least a Bachelor's degree in Law.

4- Never been convicted of a misdemeanor or a felony.

5- Have sufficient physical fitness to perform the work.

#### Article 20.-
The determination of the number of selections for student judges shall be made by Proclamation of the Minister of Justice after consultation with all members of the Supreme Council of the Magistracy.

#### Article 21.-
Candidates who pass the entrance exam as student judges shall receive professional training organized by the Ministry of Justice.

The organization and functioning of this professional training shall be determined by Proclamation of the Minister of Justice.

#### Article 22.-
Formality, procedures, dates, and venues, as well as the board of examiners for the competitive entrance exam for student judge selection, shall be determined by Proclamation of the Minister of Justice.

#### Article 23.-
The selection for inclusion in the judge framework can be made through internal competitive examination from among civil servants and court clerks who have at least a Bachelor's degree in Law and have at least 5 (five) years of experience in the field of law or justice, and who are at most 45 (forty-five) years old as of the exam date. Lawyers who have at least a Bachelor's degree in Law and have performed their profession for at least 5 (five) years may register to participate in the competitive examination according to the provisions of paragraph 1 above, if the lawyer is at most 45 (forty-five) years old as of the exam date.

Individuals who pass the exam for inclusion in the judge framework according to the provisions of paragraph 1 above shall receive professional training organized by the Ministry of Justice.

The formality and procedures for the above internal competitive examination and the number for selection into the judge framework as per this Article, as well as the organization and functioning of the training, shall be determined by Proclamation of the Minister of Justice after consultation with all members of the Supreme Council of the Magistracy.

### Section 6
### Internship and Confirmation in the Judge Framework

#### Article 24.-
Student judges selected according to the provisions of Article 19 and Article 23 of this law and who have successfully completed professional training shall be appointed by Royal Decree as internship judges in the first grade of junior judge rank. Internship judges must perform an internship for a period of 1 (one) year starting from the date of appointment.

The formality of the internship and the authority of the internship judge shall be determined by Proclamation of the Minister of Justice after consultation with all members of the Supreme Council of the Magistracy.

Appointment as an internship judge has effect on the arrangement of salary and the calculation of seniority.

#### Article 25.-
Internship judges who have successfully completed the internship shall be confirmed in the judge framework and have the first grade of junior judge rank.

The Supreme Council of the Magistracy may decide to let an internship judge continue the internship for 1 (one) additional year if the internship judge was not successful in the internship or had an interruption without performing the internship totaling more than 30 (thirty) days.

In the case where an internship judge stops for over 30 (thirty) days without legal permission, or an internship judge who is not successful again in the additional internship, their name shall be removed from the judge framework after a decision from the Supreme Council of the Magistracy.

#### Article 26.-
An internship judge who does not have sufficient fitness or sufficient health to perform their functions according to the conditions defined by law, or has shortcomings in performing the work fully, may have their name removed from the judge framework at any time after a decision from the Supreme Council of the Magistracy.

The removal or confirmation in the judge framework must be decided within 3 (three) months at the latest after the completion of the internship.

### Section 7
### Promotion of Grade and Seniority

#### Article 27.-
Promotion of grade and rank shall be made according to selection turns and seniority turns, in accordance with the table of each grade and rank.

The conditions for promotion of grade or rank by selection turns and seniority turns shall be determined by Royal Decree.

#### Article 28.-
Promotion of grade and rank of judges shall be based on bases including:

- Proper respect for work discipline.

- Good fulfillment of tasks.

- Good conduct and morality, not affecting the honor of the magistracy.

- Attending various training courses or completing studies in higher education institutions.

During the period of work, judges who strive for further studies and obtain a postgraduate degree shall be encouraged by being promoted 1 (one) additional grade.

#### Article 29.-
For each grade and rank of the hierarchy, the number of judges planned for promotion of grade or rank for each year will be determined according to work requirements and the national budget project.

#### Article 30.-
A judge within the same rank can be promoted one grade at a time.

In case of necessity, a judge may cross ranks through an examination.

#### Article 31.-
Every year, in the first week of January, each judge shall receive a private bulletin (self-assessment form) that the judge must complete based on the provided questions.

The private bulletin must be scored with comments and clear reasons.

Regarding the scoring, it shall be implemented as follows:

1- Judges of the Court of First Instance and the Vice-President of the Court of First Instance shall be scored by the President of the Court of First Instance.

2- Judges of the Appeal Court and the Vice-President of the Appeal Court shall be scored by the President of the Appeal Court.

3- The President of the Court of First Instance, the President of the Appeal Court, Judges of the Supreme Court and the Vice-President of the Supreme Court shall be scored by the President of the Supreme Court.

4- Judges who have been transferred to perform duties at other institutions shall be scored by the head of that institution.

#### Article 32.-
Registration in the promotion table is only possible for judges who have served after being confirmed in the framework and have been in their rank or grade for at least 2 (two) years as of April 13th of each year.

#### Article 33.-
The composition of the Promotion Commission for Grade and Rank includes:

- Secretary of State of the Ministry of Justice (01 person): President

- Vice-President of the Supreme Court (01 person): Vice-President

- Deputy General Prosecutor attached to the Supreme Court (01 person): Vice-President

- President of the Phnom Penh Appeal Court: Member

- General Prosecutor attached to the Phnom Penh Appeal Court: Member

- President of the Phnom Penh Court of First Instance: Member

- Prosecutor attached to the Phnom Penh Court of First Instance: Member

- Secretary General of the Supreme Council of the Magistracy: Secretary

The Minister of Justice may appoint some other members as necessary as assistants for this Commission.

#### Article 34.-
The list of candidates proposed for grade promotion for each rank shall be prepared according to seniority for each grade table.

This list must be posted for information to the persons concerned at the courts.

#### Article 35.-
Any judge with sufficient seniority for promotion of grade or rank but whose name is missing from the above list may file a complaint to request registration to the Minister of Justice within 30 (thirty) days at the latest from the date of receiving information about the promotion list. After this period, the complaint shall be considered null and void.

#### Article 36.-
The private bulletins of judges registered in each grade list and the private bulletins of judges who filed complaints must be sent to the Promotion Commission for Grade and Rank as stated in Article 33 of this law within the second week of February of each year.

#### Article 37.-
The President of the Promotion Commission for Grade and Rank shall invite the members of the Commission to meet. This Commission must meet before the Khmer Traditional New Year.

#### Article 38.-
After reviewing the private bulletins and deciding on complaints, and after hearing all explanations from the President, each member shall award a score of 0 (zero) to 20 (twenty) to each candidate judge. Registration in the candidate list must follow the order of merit based on the total score result. If two candidates receive the same score, the older candidate shall be registered first in the list.

#### Article 39.-
The Promotion Commission for Grade and Rank must prepare lists according to the judicial hierarchy for promotion of grade and rank and in grade order in the registered list to submit to the Supreme Council of the Magistracy for review and opinion. The Secretary General of the Supreme Council of the Magistracy must prepare the list of judges who received the approval of the Supreme Council of the Magistracy for promotion of grade and rank to the Minister of Justice to prepare the draft Royal Decree for submission to the King.

#### Article 40.-
Judges who are under investigation for disciplinary offenses or criminal offenses shall be suspended from the right to be registered in the promotion list for grade or seniority.

#### Article 41.-
In the case where a candidate is not promoted in grade or rank, that candidate has priority to be registered first in the promotion list for grade or rank in the following year.

#### Article 42.-
Promotion of grade or rank and increase in salary and seniority shall be calculated from April 13th of each year.

### Section 8
### Appointment, Transfer, Detachment, Outside Status, Leave of Absence and Removal of Judges

#### Article 43.-
The Supreme Council of the Magistracy decides and submits requests to the King regarding appointment, transfer, detachment, inclusion in outside status, leave of absence and removal of all judges.

#### Article 44.-
A judge may be allowed to be detached or put in outside status from the judge framework upon their own request or the request of another institution with the judge's consent.

In case of necessity to temporarily perform duties in public office positions, Member of Parliament, Senator, member of the Royal Government, Undersecretary of State, other elected mandated positions or advisory positions of all hierarchies, a judge may request detachment. The permission for detachment shall be made upon the request of the judge or another institution with the judge's consent. During the detachment period, the judge continues to receive salary...

including the right to promotion of grade or rank and the right to retirement, but without the functional allowance and other rewards of their original function.

In case of necessity to temporarily perform duties in a public enterprise or in an international organization, a judge may request to be in outside status. The permission for being in outside status shall be made upon the request of the judge or another institution with the judge's consent. A judge placed in outside status has no right to receive salary, functional allowance and other allowances as defined in Article 15 of this law.

A judge may have their name removed from the judge framework in case they desert work or are absent for more than 30 (thirty) days by absence as per Article 52 of this law or by the judge's permanent resignation.

Resignation of a judge must be made in writing by the judge. This request must be submitted up to the Supreme Council of the Magistracy for review and decision.

#### Article 45.-
In case of necessity for personal, family reasons or incompatibility, a judge may request a leave of absence without salary for a specified period upon the judge's request. Permission for this leave of absence can be made for a period of 1 (one) year or 2 (two) years at a time. This permission can be granted only twice, and the total period of permission shall not exceed 4 (four) years.

The condition for allowing a leave of absence without salary upon the judge's request is only if the judge has performed functions in the judge framework for at least 4 (four) years first, regardless of judges who have been subjected to a second-degree disciplinary penalty under point (b) of Article 55 of this law.

This request must be submitted to the Supreme Council of the Magistracy for review and decision.

#### Article 46.-
A judge who is currently in their first leave of absence without salary, if intending to request an extension or return to work, must submit a request 2 (two) months before the expiry of the leave period. Any judge who fails to submit a request for extension or fails to return to work within 30 (thirty) days at the latest from the expiry of the leave period...

otherwise, they shall be considered as having deserted work and their name shall be removed from the judge framework from that date onwards.

#### Article 47.-
A judge sent for an internship to perfection in their profession shall be considered as a judge in active service.

#### Article 48.-
In of case of removal from the judge framework in implementation of the provisions of this law, the person has the right to receive all the money that the retirement fund has already deducted from their salary, according to the law or regulations of the retirement fund.

#### Article 49.-
The President of the Supreme Court has the highest grade of Supreme Judge rank. The Vice-President and all judges of the Supreme Court have Supreme Judge rank and their number shall not exceed 25 (twenty-five) persons including the President of the Supreme Court. However, based on necessity, the number of judges of the Supreme Court may be determined above this by Royal Decree upon the request of the Minister of Justice.

The President of the Appeal Court has Supreme Judge rank. The Vice-President and all judges of the Appeal Court have Supreme Judge rank or Senior Judge rank.

The President of a Court of First Instance has Senior Judge rank. The Vice-President and judges of a Court of First Instance have Senior Judge rank or Junior Judge rank.

### Section 9
### Obligations of Judges

#### Article 50.-
Judges shall have the responsibility for assigned tasks and must strictly respect their code of ethics.

In their personal life, judges must not commit any act that leads to the degradation of their honor and profession and the prestige of the magistracy.

In the political field, judges must maintain a strictly neutral attitude.

#### Article 51.-
All judges must not desert their work or be absent without prior permission from their immediate superior.

#### Article 52.-
Desertion of work or unauthorized absence without reason from 15 (fifteen) days or more shall be subject to disciplinary penalties of withholding salary or being placed on leave of absence without salary, starting from the day of desertion or unauthorized absence. If the desertion or unauthorized absence without reason exceeds 30 (thirty) days and the judge still fails to return to work after formal notification, their name shall be removed from the framework.

#### Article 53.-
Judges currently performing duties at all levels of courts must request prior permission from the Supreme Council of the Magistracy before publishing or requesting the publication, through all means, of articles or various writings related to their functions.

The violation of the provisions of this Article shall be subject to disciplinary penalty without prejudice to criminal prosecution, if any.

This provision shall not apply to the general provision of information regarding the progress of cases at courts provided it does not affect professional secrecy as defined by law. This provision of information shall be made through officials of the administrative unit of the court.

### Section 10
### Disciplinary Sanctions

#### Article 54.-
Faults of a judge in performing professional obligations and in affecting the honor, integrity and dignity by deviating from the code of ethics for judges and prosecutors shall lead to disciplinary sanctions as per the provisions of this law. However, the use of discretion by judges and prosecutors in implementing the law shall not be considered a disciplinary fault.

Disciplinary sanctions are not an obstacle to criminal prosecution if any crime has been committed.

A judge may be temporarily suspended from duty by the President of the Disciplinary Council of the Supreme Council of the Magistracy in cases where the judge is currently subject to disciplinary proceedings by the Disciplinary Council of the Supreme Council of the Magistracy.

#### Article 55.-
Disciplinary sanctions have 2 (two) levels:

1- First-degree sanctions:

a- Verbal reprimand.

b- Written reprimand recorded in the personal file.

c- Suspension from registration in the promotion list for grade or seniority for a period not exceeding 2 (two) years, or removal from the promotion list in case of prior registration.

2- Second-degree sanctions:

a- Forced transfer, and may be required to receive additional training.

b- Suspension from work without salary for a period not exceeding 1 (one) year.

c- Reduction of one or more grades or reduction of rank.

d- Forced retirement.

e- Forced dismissal from function.

f- Expulsion from the framework.

Second-degree sanctions shall be made by Royal Decree after a decision from the Supreme Council of the Magistracy.

#### Article 56.-
In case of forced dismissal from function or expulsion from the framework in accordance with the provisions of this law, the person has the right to receive all the money that the retirement fund has already deducted from their salary, according to the law or regulations of the retirement fund.

### Section 11
### Prosecution through Judicial Channels

#### Article 57.-
A judge who has committed any criminal offense may be subject to prosecution through judicial channels.

The Minister of Justice may decide to transfer jurisdiction to another Court of First Instance other than the Court of First Instance where the judge is performing their duties.

In cases where any judge has committed a criminal offense and the prosecutor decides to prosecute, the prosecutor must inform the Minister of Justice of that prosecution within 72 hours at the latest.

If any judge is arrested or detained, the competent authority must inform the Minister of Justice of that arrest or detention.

The Minister of Justice must immediately provide the above information to the Supreme Council of the Magistracy.

#### Article 58.-
A judge subject to prosecution for a felony or misdemeanor shall be automatically suspended from duty temporarily until there is a final court decision on the sentence. This judge shall receive their normal salary until there is a court judgment deciding they are guilty. This salary shall be temporarily suspended by a decision of the Minister of Justice from the date the court judgment decides they are guilty, even if there is an appeal against this court judgment.

#### Article 59.-
In cases where a judge prosecuted through judicial channels is granted a non-suit (dismissal of charges) or is finally acquitted of charges, the judge shall be readmitted to work immediately with their grade and rank seniority and retirement reckoned, and shall receive all the previously suspended salary.

This provision shall also apply in cases where the judge was not removed from the judge framework in compliance with the provisions of Article 61 of this law.

#### Article 60.-
Notwithstanding a court decision to dismiss charges, to acquit or to sentence, if the person has committed a fault against the code of ethics for judges and prosecutors, they may be subject to additional disciplinary prosecution by the Supreme Council of the Magistracy.

#### Article 61.-
Sentence for a felony or intentional misdemeanor without suspension of sentence results in removal from the judge framework, reckoned from the date the judgment or decree becomes final.

### Section 12
### Retirement

#### Article 62.-
All judges must retire upon reaching the age of 60 (sixty).

All judges of the Supreme Court aged 60 (sixty) and above shall retire upon their own request. However, upon reaching 65 (sixty-five) years of age, the Supreme Council of the Magistracy must decide on the continuation of work for all Supreme Court judges upon their own request.

The Minister of Justice shall propose a draft Royal Decree to the King regarding the above matters.

#### Article 63.-
A judge who has worked a full 30 (thirty) years may request to retire before the age of 60 (sixty).

Judges who have worked for 30 (thirty) years or more have the right to receive full seniority retirement pension based on the final salary.

Judges who have worked for 20 (twenty) years or more up to under 30 (thirty) years have the right to receive a proportional retirement pension based on the final salary.

Judges who have worked for under 20 (twenty) years shall receive only the accumulated retirement pension fund and other lump-sum allowances.

The above period of work must also include any period of work as a public official before the appointment as a judge.

The retirement pension of judges is paid at the General Secretariat of the Supreme Council of the Magistracy.

The retirement pension can be received at only one location.

The implementation of the retirement regime shall be determined in detail by Sub-Decree.

#### Article 64.-
A judge who has worked a full 30 (thirty) years shall no longer have retirement funds deducted. However, if a judge has worked for less than 30 (thirty) years, retirement funds must be deducted until they have worked for a full 30 (thirty) years to receive the full seniority retirement pension.

#### Article 65.-
A judge's job seniority must be calculated from the day the person entered state service, regardless of the period.

#### Article 66.-
Any judge who cannot perform their professional tasks due to the loss of professional fitness shall be retired. The decision on the loss of professional fitness shall be made by the Supreme Council of the Magistracy based on forensic evidence from a specialist doctor appointed by the Ministry of Health.

In cases where this loss of professional fitness is clearly caused by the performance of functions or results from exceptional dedication to work of public interest, that judge shall be retired with a special right to receive an invalidity retirement pension equal to the full seniority retirement pension, regardless of the period of work. The judge can receive this retirement pension only if the illness or disability is the direct and clear cause of the invalidity or incapacity to perform work.

The cause, source, type and traces of the injury or illness must be confirmed by a forensic document from a specialist doctor appointed by the Ministry of Health.

If a judge dies due to work performance in the above conditions, the spouse and dependent minor children can receive an allowance equal to 12 (twelve) months of the judge's salary and other lump-sum allowances.

Apart from this, the state must support children who are still minors and the spouse, except if the spouse remarries.

#### Article 67.-
In cases of loss of professional fitness as defined in Article 66 above, the Minister of Justice must make a report to the Supreme Council of the Magistracy to review and decide on retirement.

The Minister of Justice shall propose a draft Royal Decree to the King regarding the above matter after receiving the decision of the Supreme Council of the Magistracy.

### Section 13
### Honorary Title

#### Article 68.-
Except in cases of forced retirement, upon retirement, a judge shall receive the title of Honorary Judge and receive a rank one grade higher than their rank at the time of active service, provided that the judge has performed their functions in that rank for at least 1 (one) year before retirement. This honorary title shall be established by Royal Decree.

The Supreme Council of the Magistracy shall propose a request to the King regarding the above matter.

#### Article 69.-
A retired judge has full right to retain this honorary title.

This honorary title may be revoked by the Supreme Council of the Magistracy upon the request of the Minister of Justice if there is a cause affecting the honor or dignity of the magistracy.

### Section 14
### Oath of Allegiance

#### Article 70.-
Before entering service, a judge must take an oath at the Royal Palace. The content of the oath is attached as an annex to this law.

#### Article 71.-
The taking of the above oath must be recorded in the minutes by an official of the Ministry of the Royal Palace and sent to the Supreme Council of the Magistracy to be stored in the judge's personal file.

#### Article 72.-
Any judge who refuses to take the oath cannot perform functions as a judge and may have their name removed from the judge framework.

A judge who insults or makes disparaging remarks about the oath they have already taken shall be subject to disciplinary penalty.

#### Article 73.-
When appearing as a witness before a court, a judge must take an oath according to their religious customs and also according to the court's formalities.

## Chapter 3
## Prosecutors

### Section 1
### General Provisions

#### Article 74.-
The General Prosecution Office attached to the Supreme Court shall be led by one (01) General Prosecutor and assisted by Deputy General Prosecutors and a number of prosecutors as required. The General Prosecutor, Deputy General Prosecutors and prosecutors of the General Prosecution Office attached to the Supreme Court represent the General Prosecution Office attached to the Supreme Court.

The General Prosecution Office attached to the Appeal Court shall be led by one (01) General Prosecutor and assisted by Deputy General Prosecutors and a number of prosecutors as required. The General Prosecutor, Deputy General Prosecutors and prosecutors of the General Prosecution Office attached to the Appeal Court represent the General Prosecution Office attached to the Appeal Court where they perform their duties.

The Prosecution Office attached to the Court of First Instance shall be led by one (01) Prosecutor and assisted by a number of Deputy Prosecutors as required. The Prosecutor and Deputy Prosecutors of the Prosecution Office attached to the Court of First Instance represent the Prosecution Office attached to the Court of First Instance where they perform their duties.

Only the Prosecution Office has the right to initiate criminal action. Prosecutors are responsible for searching for criminal offenses, deciding on criminal prosecution and requesting the implementation of the law before investigating jurisdictions and trial jurisdictions.

In civil cases and other legal cases, representatives of the Prosecution Office may participate in judicial procedures and provide opinions if they find it necessary to protect the public interest.

#### Article 75.-
The main principles of the Prosecution Office include, among others, the following:

1- The Prosecution Office is a unified body where all prosecutors must be under hierarchical leadership and supervision. The Minister of Justice is the head of the hierarchy of the Prosecution Office and may exercise presidency through instructions in writing to the Prosecution Offices of all levels. The General Department of Prosecution and Criminal Affairs of the Ministry of Justice acts as the secretariat for the Minister of Justice for this task. The General Prosecutor attached to the Supreme Court is the head of the hierarchy of the General Prosecution Office attached to the Supreme Court. Prosecutors and Deputy General Prosecutors of the General Prosecution Office attached to the Supreme Court must perform work under the leadership and joint responsibility of the General Prosecutor who is the head of their hierarchy. The General Prosecutor attached to an Appeal Court is the head of the hierarchy of the General Prosecution Office attached to the General Prosecution Office attached to the Appeal Court. Prosecutors and Deputy General Prosecutors of the General Prosecution Office attached to the Appeal Court must perform work under the leadership and joint responsibility of the General Prosecutor who is the head of their hierarchy. The Prosecutor attached to a Court of First Instance is the head of the hierarchy of the Prosecution Office attached to the Court of First Instance. All Deputy Prosecutors of the Prosecution Office attached to the Court of First Instance must perform work under the leadership and joint responsibility of the Prosecutor who is the head of their hierarchy.

2- The Prosecution Office is an indivisible body which requires prosecutors performing functions at the Prosecution Office of any court to have joint duties in the same functions and they can replace one another.

Based on this principle, several representatives of the Prosecution Office may continue work from one another in the same case as assigned by the head of the hierarchy.

3- The Prosecution Office is an independent body that is not under the authority of the court it is attached to. This court has no power to supervise the work of the prosecutor. Even if a prosecutor commits an error in performing their functions, the court must not, in its decision, include any reprimand towards the prosecutor. This prohibition applies to both criminal cases and other legal cases. However, the court can make a proposal to the head of the prosecutor's hierarchy regarding the prosecutor's error.

#### Article 76.-
Every year:

- The Prosecutor attached to a Court of First Instance must submit a report on crimes and their work activities to the Minister of Justice and a copy to the General Prosecutor attached to the competent Appeal Court.

- The General Prosecutor attached to an Appeal Court must submit a report on crimes, their work activities and work activities of all Prosecution Offices attached to the Courts of First Instance within their territorial jurisdiction to the Minister of Justice and a copy to the General Prosecutor attached to the Supreme Court.

- The General Prosecutor attached to the Supreme Court must submit a report on the work activities of the General Prosecution Office attached to the Supreme Court to the Minister of Justice.

#### Article 77.-
All prosecutors must perform their functions and roles based on the principle of respect for the law without accepting pressure, threats or concealment, direct or indirect commands from any party in the case or from any individual, except as provided for in Article 75 of this law.

### Section 2
### Rank and Grade

#### Article 78.-
The prosecutor framework has 3 (three) ranks:

- Supreme Prosecutor

- Senior Prosecutor

- Junior Prosecutor

#### Article 79.-
The grades for Supreme Prosecutor rank, Senior Prosecutor rank and Junior Prosecutor rank shall be determined by Royal Decree.

### Section 3
### Uniforms

#### Article 80.-
The provisions of Chapter 2 (Judges), Section 3 (Uniforms) of this law, which apply to judges, shall also apply to prosecutors.

### Section 4
### Salary, Various Allowances, Rewards and Annual Leaves

#### Article 81.-
The provisions of Chapter 2 (Judges), Section 4 (Salary, various Allowances, Rewards and Annual Leaves) of this law, which apply to judges, shall also apply to prosecutors.

### Section 5
### Selection of Prosecutors

#### Article 82.-
Selection and training of prosecutors shall be implemented together with the selection and training of judges in accordance with the provisions of Chapter 2 (Judges), Section 5 (Selection of Judges) of this law.

### Section 6
### Internship and Confirmation in the Prosecutor Framework

#### Article 83.-
Prosecutor students who have successfully completed professional training must be appointed by Royal Decree as candidate prosecutors in the lowest grade of Junior Prosecutor rank. Candidate prosecutors must perform an internship for a period of 1 (one) year, reckoned from the date of appointment.

The performance of the internship by candidate prosecutors shall be determined by a Prokas of the Minister of Justice after consultation with all members of the Supreme Council of the Magistracy.

The appointment as a candidate prosecutor has effect on salary arrangement and job seniority calculation.

#### Article 84.-
Candidate prosecutors who have successfully completed their internship shall be confirmed in the prosecutor framework and hold the lowest grade of Junior Prosecutor rank. The Supreme Council of the Magistracy submits the request to the King regarding this confirmation in the prosecutor framework upon the request of the Minister of Justice.

The Minister of Justice may decide for a candidate prosecutor to continue the internship for 1 (one) additional year if that candidate prosecutor fails to succeed in the internship or has an aggregate absence from the internship of not more than 30 (thirty) days.

In cases where a candidate prosecutor takes leave for more than 30 (thirty) days without legal permission, or a candidate prosecutor who fails again in the additional internship, their name shall be removed from the prosecutor framework in compliance with the provisions of Article 89 of this law.

#### Article 85.-
A candidate prosecutor who lacks sufficient capacity or has insufficient health to perform their functions according to the conditions defined by law, or has made errors in fulfilling their duties, may have their name removed from the prosecutor framework at any time in compliance with the provisions of Article 89 of this law.

The removal or confirmation in the prosecutor framework must be carried out within 3 (three) months at the latest after the end of the internship.

### Section 7
### Promotion of Grade and Rank

#### Article 86.-
The provisions from Article 27 to Article 30, Article 32 to Article 38, and Article 40 to Article 42 of Chapter 2 (Judges), Section 7 (Promotion of Grade and Rank) of this law, which apply to judges, shall also apply to prosecutors.

#### Article 87.-
Every year, in the first week of January, each prosecutor must receive an individual bulletin/form which the prosecutor must complete according to the list of questions provided.

The individual bulletin must be graded with clear comments and reasons.

Regarding the grading, it shall be implemented as follows:

1- Prosecutors of the Prosecution Office attached to the Court of First Instance shall be graded by the Prosecutor of the Prosecution Office attached to the Court of First Instance.

2- Prosecutors and Deputy General Prosecutors attached to the Appeal Court shall be graded by the General Prosecutor attached to the Appeal Court.

3- Prosecutors attached to the Court of First Instance, General Prosecutors attached to the Appeal Court, Prosecutors and Deputy General Prosecutors attached to the Supreme Court shall be graded by the General Prosecutor attached to the Supreme Court.

4- Prosecutors transferred to perform functions at other various institutions shall be graded by the head of that institution.

#### Article 88.-
The Commission for Promotion of Grade and Rank must prepare the list according to the prosecutor cohort for promotion of grade and rank and according to the order in the registration list to submit to the Supreme Council of the Magistracy for review and opinion. The Secretary General of the Supreme Council of the Magistracy shall prepare the registration list of prosecutors to be promoted in grade and rank for the Minister of Justice to prepare a draft Royal Decree for presentation to the King.

### Section 8
### Appointment, Transfer, Detachment, Outside Status, Leave of Absence and Removal of Prosecutors

#### Article 89.-
The Supreme Council of the Magistracy submits requests to the King regarding the appointment, transfer, detachment, inclusion in outside status, leave of absence and removal of all prosecutors in the Kingdom of Cambodia, upon the request of the Minister of Justice. The Supreme Council of the Magistracy must be consulted as a whole regarding the above request.

The Minister of Justice shall propose the draft Royal Decree to the King regarding the above matters.

#### Article 90.-
A prosecutor may be allowed to be detached or put in outside status from the prosecutor framework upon their request or the request of another institution with the prosecutor's consent.

In case of necessity to temporarily perform duties in public office positions, Member of Parliament, Senator, member of the Royal Government, Undersecretary of State, other elected mandated positions or advisory positions of all hierarchies, a prosecutor may request detachment. The permission for detachment shall be made upon the request of the prosecutor or another institution with the prosecutor's consent. During the detachment period, the prosecutor shall continue to receive salary, as well as the right to promotion of grade or rank and the right to retirement, but without the functional allowance and other rewards of their original functions.

In case of necessity to temporarily perform duties in a public enterprise or international organization, a prosecutor may request to be in outside status. The permission for inclusion in outside status shall be made upon the request of the prosecutor or another institution with the prosecutor's consent. A prosecutor placed in outside status has no right to receive salary, functional allowance and other allowances as determined in Article 81 of this law.

A prosecutor may be temporarily suspended from duty by the Minister of Justice in cases where the prosecutor is currently in disciplinary proceedings of the Disciplinary Council of the Supreme Council of the Magistracy.

A prosecutor may have their name removed from the prosecutor framework in case the prosecutor has a second-degree disciplinary penalty or when they have deserted work or been absent...

for more than 30 (thirty) days by implementing Article 52 of this law or if the person requests to resign permanently.

The resignation of a prosecutor must be made in writing by the prosecutor. This request must be submitted to the Minister of Justice to review and proceed according to procedures.

#### Article 91.-
In case of necessity for personal, family reasons or incompatibility, a prosecutor may request a leave of absence without salary for a specified period upon the prosecutor's request. Permission for the leave of absence without salary can be granted for a period of 1 (one) year or 2 (two) years at a time. This permission can be granted only twice, and the total period of permission shall not exceed 4 (four) years. The conditions for allowing a leave of absence without salary upon the prosecutor's request is only if the prosecutor has performed functions in the prosecutor framework for at least 4 (four) years first, regardless of prosecutors who have been subjected to a second-degree disciplinary penalty.

The request for leave of absence must be made in writing by the prosecutor. This request must be submitted to the Minister of Justice to review and proceed according to the procedures.

#### Article 92.-
A prosecutor currently in their first leave of absence without salary, if intending to request an extension or return to work, must submit a request 2 (two) months before the expiry of the leave period. Any prosecutor who fails to submit a request for an extension of the leave of absence or fails to return to perform their functions within 30 (thirty) days at the latest from the expiry of the leave of absence shall be considered as having deserted work and their name shall be removed from the framework from that date onwards.

#### Article 93.-
A prosecutor sent for an internship to perfection in their profession shall be considered as a prosecutor in active service.

#### Article 94.-
In cases of removal from the prosecutor framework in compliance with the provisions of this law, the person has the right to receive all the money that the retirement fund has already deducted from their salary, according to the law or regulations of the retirement fund.

#### Article 95.-
The General Prosecutor attached to the Supreme Court holds the highest grade of Supreme Prosecutor rank. The Deputy General Prosecutors and all prosecutors attached to the Supreme Court hold Supreme Prosecutor rank.

The General Prosecutor attached to an Appeal Court holds Supreme Prosecutor rank. All Deputy General Prosecutors and prosecutors attached to an Appeal Court hold Supreme Prosecutor rank or Senior Prosecutor rank.

The Prosecutor attached to a Court of First Instance holds Senior Prosecutor rank. All Deputy Prosecutors attached to a Court of First Instance hold Senior Prosecutor rank or Junior Prosecutor rank or higher.

### Section 9
### Obligations of Prosecutors

#### Article 96.-
Prosecutors must take responsibility for assigned tasks and strictly respect their code of ethics.

In their personal life, prosecutors must not commit any acts that lead to the degradation of their honor and dignity and the prestige of the Prosecution Office.

Prosecutors currently performing functions at Prosecution Offices attached to courts of all levels must request prior permission from the Minister of Justice before publishing or requesting the publication, through all means, of articles or various writings that may affect the prestige of the Prosecution Office or the judiciary. This provision shall not apply to public statements made by a prosecutor in compliance with the Code of Criminal Procedure.

In the political field, prosecutors must maintain a strictly neutral attitude.

Violation of the provisions of paragraphs 2 and 3 of this Article shall be subject to disciplinary penalty, without prejudice to criminal prosecution, if any.

#### Article 97.-
All prosecutors must not desert their work or be absent without prior permission from their immediate superior.

Requests for permission for absence from the Prosecutor attached to the Court of First Instance, the General Prosecutor attached to the Appeal Court and the General Prosecutor attached to the Supreme Court shall be decided by the Minister of Justice.

#### Article 98.-
Desertion of work or unauthorized absence without valid reason from 15 (fifteen) days onwards shall be subject to a penalty of salary suspension or placement on leave of absence without salary, calculated from the day of desertion or unauthorized absence. If the desertion of work or unauthorized absence without valid reason exceeds 30 (thirty) days and the prosecutor still does not return to perform their work after two injunctions, they shall be removed from the framework.

### Section 10
### Disciplinary Sanctions

#### Article 99.-
The provisions of Chapter 2 (Judges), Section 10 (Disciplinary Sanctions) of this law, which apply to judges, shall also apply to prosecutors.

### Section 11
### Prosecution through the Courts

#### Article 100.-
The provisions of Chapter 2 (Judges), Section 11 (Prosecution through the Courts) of this law, which apply to judges, shall also apply to prosecutors.

### Section 12
### Retirement

#### Article 101.-
Prosecutors of both sexes shall retire upon reaching the age of 60 (sixty) years.

Specifically, all prosecutors of the General Prosecution Office attached to the Supreme Court who are 60 (sixty) years of age or older shall retire upon their own request. However, upon reaching the age of 65 (sixty-five) years, the Supreme Council of the Magistracy shall decide on the continuation of work for all prosecutors of the General Prosecution Office attached to the Supreme Court upon their request.

The Minister of Justice shall propose a draft Royal Decree to the King regarding the above matters.

#### Article 102.-
The provisions from Article 63 to Article 67 of Chapter 2 (Judges), Section 12 (Retirement) of this law, which apply to judges, shall also apply to prosecutors.

### Section 13
### Honorary Title

#### Article 103.-
Except in cases of forced retirement, upon retirement, a prosecutor shall receive the title of Honorary Prosecutor and receive a rank one grade higher than their rank at the time of active service, provided that the prosecutor has performed their functions in that rank for at least 1 (one) year before retirement. This honorary title shall be established by Royal Decree.

The Supreme Council of the Magistracy shall propose a request to the King regarding the above matter.

#### Article 104.-
A retired prosecutor has full right to retain this honorary title.

This honorary title may be revoked by the Supreme Council of the Magistracy upon the request of the Minister of Justice if there is a cause affecting the honor or dignity of the magistracy.

### Section 14
### Oath of Allegiance

#### Article 105.-
The provisions of Chapter 2 (Judges), Section 14 (Oath of Allegiance) of this law, which apply to judges, shall also apply to prosecutors.

## Chapter 4
## Incompatibility

#### Article 106.-
Performing functions as a judge or a prosecutor at any court or prosecution office attached to a court is incompatible with holding public office, the function of Member of Parliament, Senator...

Member of the Royal Government, Undersecretary of State, other elected mandated positions or advisory positions of all hierarchies, unless there are provisions to the contrary.

A judge or prosecutor currently performing functions at any court or prosecution office attached to a court cannot perform the function of a lawyer, commercial business or any other activity which leads to a conflict of interest or which affects the honor of the magistracy. However, judges and prosecutors can perform the function of a lecturer at educational institutions.

#### Article 107.-
A judge or prosecutor who has been allowed a leave of absence, if having the intention to work in the private sector, must notify the Minister of Justice. The Minister of Justice may object and not allow such work if they find that such activities are contrary to honor or primary dignity, or degrade the prestige of the functions of a judge or prosecutor.

In case of a violation of this Article, the judge or prosecutor who was placed on leave shall be subject to disciplinary penalty as provided in Article 55 of this law.

## Chapter 5
## Transitional Provisions

#### Article 108.-
Judges and prosecutors who were appointed before this law enters into force and are in the framework managed by the Supreme Council of the Magistracy shall legally receive the status of the judge and prosecutor framework according to the provisions of this law, except for judges of military courts and prosecutors attached to military courts.

A commission to review the criteria for integration into the grade and rank of judges and prosecutors shall be established by Royal Decree upon the request of the Minister of Justice, after consultation with all members of the Supreme Council of the Magistracy.

The above integration shall be carried out by Royal Decree upon the request of the Minister of Justice.

#### Article 109.-
The provisions of Chapter 2 (Judges), Section 12 (Retirement) and Chapter 3 (Prosecutors)

Section 12 (Retirement) of this law shall be implemented after the integration is made into the judge and prosecutor frameworks.

While waiting for the above integration, the retirement of judges and prosecutors shall be carried out in compliance with the legal norms that existed before this law was implemented.

#### Article 110.-
In the first 5 (five) years after this law enters into force, the provisions of Article 49 and Article 99 of this law shall not be implemented, except for determining the number of judges at the Supreme Court.

In the first 05 (five) years after this law enters into force, judges of the Appeal Court and prosecutors of the General Prosecution Office attached to the Appeal Court who reach retirement age may be allowed to continue work upon their own request; this continuation of work is for 1 (one) year at a time, but not exceeding 3 (three) times. The permission for this continuation of work shall be granted by Royal Decree upon the request of the Minister of Justice after consultation with all members of the Supreme Council of the Magistracy.

## Chapter 6
## Final Provisions

#### Article 111.-
All provisions contrary to the provisions of this law shall be abrogated.
