# PROMULGATES

The Law on Investment of the Kingdom of Cambodia, which was adopted by the National Assembly on September 09, 2021, at its 6th session of the 6th legislature, and was examined and approved in its entirety as to its form and legality by the Senate on September 20, 2021, at its 7th plenary session of the 4th legislature, and which the Constitutional Council declared consistent with the Constitution in Decision No. 212/002/2021 KBCh.Ch dated September 29, 2021, and whose full text is as follows:

# Law on Investment of the Kingdom of Cambodia

# CHAPTER 1

# GENERAL PROVISIONS

### Article 1 .-

The purpose of this Law is to establish an open, transparent and predictable legal framework that is favorable for investment in order to attract and promote quality, efficient and effective investments by Cambodian or foreign nationals for the socio-economic development in the Kingdom of Cambodia through:

1- Increasing Cambodia's competitiveness to enable its economic structure to diversify and be resilient to regional and global crises.

2- Modernizing and increasing the productivity of local industries and strengthening connectivity in regional and global supply chains by promoting capital inflows, transfer of technology, knowledge and know-how.

3- Establishing a transparent, predictable and non-discriminatory investment incentive regime that supports socio-economic policies.

4- Providing protection of the rights and legal interests of investors in the Kingdom of Cambodia through the establishment of a comprehensive and balanced legal framework consistent with the national interest.

### Article 2 .-

This Law applies to all Qualified Investment Projects, Expanded Qualified Investment Projects and investment projects that have only received investment guarantees after being registered with the Council for the Development of Cambodia or the Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committees.

### Article 3 .-

The key terms used in this Law are defined as follows:

**Investment Project** refers to a Qualified Investment Project, an Expanded Qualified Investment Project and an investment project that has only received investment guarantees.

**Qualified Investment Project** abbreviated as **QIP** refers to an investment project that has received a Registration Certificate from the Council for the Development of Cambodia or a Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committee.

**Export Qualified Investment Project** refers to a **QIP** that sells or transfers a proportion of its products to purchasers or recipients outside the Kingdom of Cambodia.

**Supporting Industry Qualified Investment Project** refers to a **QIP** whose products, in a certain proportion, are supplied to export industries.

**Domestically Oriented Qualified Investment Project** refers to a **QIP** that does not serve exports.

**Expansion of a Qualified Investment Project** abbreviated as **EQIP** refers to the expansion of a **QIP** in any form, including the expansion of existing production, expansion through diversification of product lines within the same product line, expansion through the installation of modern technology that increases productivity or protects the environment, expansion of infrastructure serving local logistics, or expansion in other forms to be determined by Sub-Decree.

**Investment Project Receiving Only Investment Guarantees** abbreviated as **IPROG** refers to an investment project registered with the Council for the Development of Cambodia or a Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committee and clearly identified as an **IPROG**, but which does not receive fiscal incentives.

**Working Day** refers to the official working days of the Royal Government of Cambodia.

**Production Inputs** refers to raw materials, semi-finished products and accessories, including goods, to be used in the production process, excluding alcoholic products and vehicle spare parts.

**Cambodian Legal Entity** refers to a company that has its place of business and is registered in the Kingdom of Cambodia and in which at least 51% (fifty-one percent) of the shares are held by persons of Cambodian nationality.

**Construction Equipment** refers to equipment installed, created or manufactured for use in connection with a construction to increase the quality, comfort and convenience of using the construction, including but not limited to light bulbs, cables, fiber optic cables, washbasins, faucets, water tanks, elevators and pipes, excluding air conditioners. The import of air conditioners for activities or investment projects in any specific sector shall receive separate incentives to be determined in the Law on Financial Management or in a Sub-Decree.

**Person** refers to a natural person or a legal entity.

**Registration Certificate** refers to a certificate issued by the Council for the Development of Cambodia or a Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committee to certify the registration of an investment project.

**Investor** refers to a person carrying out an investment project registered with the Council for the Development of Cambodia or a Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committee.

**Investment Activity** refers to business activities in the Kingdom of Cambodia that are established or acquired or sold or donated or expanded or merged and authorized in writing by the competent authorities in accordance with the laws and regulations in force.

**Investment Project Registration Application** refers to an application submitted to the Council for the Development of Cambodia or a Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committee by any person for the purpose of establishing a **QIP** and an **IPROG**. This application may include one or more phases, including an application for the expansion of a **QIP**.

**Production Equipment** refers to machinery, mechanical equipment and other tools used as a major basis in the production line, excluding vehicles. The import of vehicles for activities or investment projects in any specific sector shall receive separate incentives to be determined in the Law on Financial Management or in a Sub-Decree.

**Construction Material** refers to construction materials that are transformed or used entirely in the construction of various structures, including construction equipment, for a **QIP** to carry out its investment activities during the initial construction phase or expansion phase.

**Construction Equipment, Construction Material and Production Equipment** as stated in this Article shall meet the technical requirements and quantities appropriate to the investment project.

**Applicant** refers to a person who submits an investment project registration application to the Council for the Development of Cambodia or a Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committee.

**Sub-Decree** refers to the Sub-Decree on the Implementation of the Law on Investment of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

# CHAPTER 2

# THE COUNCIL FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF CAMBODIA

### Article 4 .-

The Council for the Development of Cambodia, abbreviated as "**CDC**", is established as an executive body that functions as the staff and the "One-Stop Service" of the Royal Government on the mission of leading and managing...

### Article 5 .-

The CDC is chaired by the Prime Minister and consists of one or more Vice-Chairs and a number of members as necessary. By exercising his/her delegated powers, the Prime Minister may delegate powers to any dignitary who is a member of the Royal Government as a leader of the **CDC** to lead on his/her behalf at any level or in any area of the **CDC's** work in order to ensure the effectiveness of the mission defined in Article 4 of this Law, including the management and allocation of the budget and human resources in accordance with the laws and regulations in force.

The appointment of the composition of the **CDC** shall be determined by Royal Decree.

### Article 6 .-

The **CDC** has the following structure:

- General Secretariat of the **CDC**
- Cambodian Rehabilitation and Development Board, abbreviated as "**CRDB**"
- Cambodian Investment Board, abbreviated as "**CIB**".

The General Secretariat of the **CDC**, the **CRDB** and the **CIB** shall be led by a Secretary General of the **CDC** and assisted by a number of Deputy Secretaries General as necessary.

The **CDC** may establish additional structures and mechanisms by Sub-Decree based on necessity.

### Article 7 .-

The **CDC** has its own separate budget which is part of the national budget, and has civil servants and contract officials in accordance with the laws and regulations in force.

### Article 8 .-

The organization and functioning of the **CDC** shall be determined by Royal Decree.

# CHAPTER 3

# INVESTMENT MECHANISM AT MUNICIPAL-PROVINCIAL LEVEL

### Article 9 .-

In order to increase the efficiency of providing services to investors, reviewing and deciding on private investments and resolving disputes related to investment projects, delegation of power shall be made to the Municipal-Provincial Administrations in accordance with the decision of the Royal Government through the establishment of a Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committee as a staff to the Municipal-Provincial Administration. The power, scale of investment and the organization and functioning of the Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committee shall be determined by a separate Sub-Decree.

# CHAPTER 4

# REGISTRATION AND IMPLEMENTATION OF INVESTMENT PROJECTS

### Article 10 .-

Any person who intends to carry out a **QIP**, **EQIP** or **IPROG** shall submit a written Investment Project Registration Application to the **CDC** or a Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committee.

The above Investment Project Registration Application may be submitted through an information technology platform.

### Article 11 .-

The Investment Project Registration Application shall be accompanied by a number of information to be determined by Sub-Decree.

### Article 12 .-

Upon receipt of the Investment Project Registration Application, the **CDC** shall review and decide on the application through the "One-Stop Service" mechanism.

The "One-Stop Service" mechanism for reviewing Investment Project Registration Applications, as stated in the first paragraph of this Article, is a mechanism for reviewing and deciding on investment project registration by representatives of relevant ministries and institutions stationed at the **CDC** based on the assignment and delegation of power from the heads of the ministries and institutions under the coordination of the **CDC**.

If the proposed investment project is not on the Negative List to be determined separately by Sub-Decree, the **CDC** shall issue a Registration Certificate to the applicant within 20 (twenty) working days.

 the Registration Certificate shall have a barcode with an identification number or a QR code or other technology systems containing a number of primary data related to the registered investment project for the benefit of various related registrations and for the implementation of the investment project.

Relevant ministries and institutions involved in related registrations or compliance monitoring of the implementation of investment projects shall not require applicants or investors to provide any documents already available in the barcode with an identification number or QR code or other technology systems attached to the Registration Certificate issued by the **CDC**.

### Article 13 .-

Investment projects that have received a Registration Certificate may be implemented automatically. However, the receipt of a Registration Certificate does not exempt the investment project from obtaining various licenses as required by laws and regulations.

All investment projects shall be subject to monitoring and inspection through the "One-Stop Service" mechanism coordinated by the **CDC** to ensure compliance with laws and conditions which are the reasons for obtaining a Registration Certificate.

Investors shall provide reports on the implementation of the investment project according to a specific schedule to be determined by the **CDC**. The detailed form and format of the report template shall be determined by the **CDC's** instructions.

The provision of reports on the implementation of the investment project does not preclude on-site compliance inspections of the project implementation if the **CDC** deems it necessary or if there is any complaint from a person claiming to have been affected by the implementation of the investment project.

### Article 14 .-

The registration procedure for investment projects at the Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committees shall be determined by a separate Sub-Decree.

# CHAPTER 5

# INVESTMENT GUARANTEES AND PROTECTION

### Article 15 .-

Investors shall receive non-discriminatory treatment with respect to compensation for damages or other economic settlements in the event that their investment suffers losses due to armed conflict or civil unrest or a state of emergency, if there are laws and policies of the Royal Government of Cambodia on such settlements or reasonable compensation.

Foreign investors shall not be subject to discrimination on the basis of foreign nationality, except for land ownership as provided in the Constitution of the Kingdom of Cambodia and regulations in force.

### Article 16 .-

The State shall not nationalize in a manner that affects the property of investors in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Article 17 .-

The State shall not take any action that affects an approved investment project, whether directly or indirectly or through measures equivalent to expropriation...

...except for public interest purposes, where such expropriation shall meet the following conditions:

1- Non-discrimination

2- Fair and just compensation, and

3- Compliance with the laws and procedures on expropriation in force.

### Article 18 .-

The Royal Government shall not fix the prices of products or services created by an investment project.

### Article 19 .-

In accordance with the laws and regulations in force, investors have the right to freely purchase foreign currencies and to remit all such foreign currencies abroad to settle financial obligations related to their investment through the banking system which are legitimate operating entities. These transfers include:

1- Contributions to capital, including additional capital contributions

2- Income, capital gains, dividends, royalties, license fees, management fees and technical assistance fees, interest and other income derived from the investment

3- Proceeds from the total or partial sale or liquidation of the company implementing the investment project

4- Payment for imports and repatriation of both principal and interest on loans

5- Payment of compensation in case of civil unrest, expropriation or requisition by the State

6- Payments arising from the settlement of disputes by any means, including court decisions or arbitral awards, and

7- Other income and salaries of employees.

### Article 20 .-

The intellectual property of investors shall be protected in accordance with the laws and regulations related to intellectual property of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Article 21 .-

Land ownership by investors for the purpose of implementing investment projects is permitted for persons of Cambodian nationality in accordance with the laws and regulations in force.

Investors have the right to use land through, inter alia, economic land concessions or perpetual leases and fixed-term leases in accordance with the laws and regulations in force.

### Article 22 .-

Persons considered as investors under this Law have the following rights:

1- The right to hire foreign employees to manage or operate the investment project in a number not exceeding the number set by the laws and regulations in force, in the event that skilled Cambodian employees cannot be found to manage or operate the investment project. The permission to hire foreign employees shall be reviewed based on the actual situation and is not permanent.

2- The right to obtain long-term temporary stay permits for themselves, their spouses and their minor children during the period of operation of their investment project.

3- The right to apply for long-term temporary stay permits for foreign employees and for the spouses and minor children of these foreign employees during the period that the employment contract remains valid.

4- The right to obtain work permits and work books for themselves and for their foreign employees.

The **CDC** or the Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committee shall issue a certificate certifying the status as an investor to persons involved in the investment project upon the investor's request, to be used for applying for long-term temporary stay permits, work permits and work books, and other necessary purposes in accordance with the procedures in force.

Special procedures for applying for work permits and work books as stated in this Article shall be determined by a Joint Proclamation between the **CDC** and the ministry in charge of the labor sector.

Detailed forms and special procedures for applying for long-term temporary stay permits as stated in this Article shall be determined by a separate Sub-Decree.

### Article 23 .-

Investors have the right to receive investment maintenance services for investment projects registered with the **CDC** or the Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committees.

The conditions, forms and procedures for providing investment maintenance services for investment projects as stated in the first paragraph of this Article shall be determined by Sub-Decree.

# CHAPTER 6

# INVESTMENT INCENTIVES

### Article 24 .-

The following investment sectors and activities shall receive investment incentives:

1- High-tech industries involving innovation and research and development

2- New industries or manufacturing with high added value, innovation and high competitiveness

3- Industries serving regional and global supply chains

4- Supporting industries related to agriculture, tourism, manufacturing, industries serving regional and global supply chains and supply chain integration

5- Electrical and electronic industries

6- Spare parts, assembly and installation industries

7- Mechanical and machinery industries

8- Agriculture, agro-industry, agricultural product processing industries and food processing industries serving the domestic market or export

9- Small and medium enterprises in priority sectors and development of small and medium enterprise clusters for small and medium enterprises in the industrial and agricultural services, technology and innovation sectors

10- Tourism industry and tourism-related activities

11- Special Economic Zone development

12- Digital industry

13- Investment in education, vocational training and productivity promotion

14- Investment in the health sector

15- Physical infrastructure development

16- Investment in the logistics sector

17- Investment in environmental management, environmental protection and biodiversity conservation and circular economy

18- Investment in green energy, technology contributing to climate change adaptation and mitigation

19- Other investment sectors and activities not stated in this Law that the Royal Government deems to have socio-economic development potential.

### Article 25 .-

Investment sectors and activities as stated in Article 24 of this Law that are not on the Negative List shall receive basic tax incentives and/or full or partial customs duty incentives after receiving a Registration Certificate certifying the status as a **QIP**.

Investment sectors and activities as stated in Article 24 of this Law that do not receive tax and customs duty incentives shall be determined in the Negative List by Sub-Decree.

### Article 26 .-

Investment activities registered as **QIP** have the right to choose basic incentives between two options as follows:

**1- Option 1**

- Income tax exemption for a period of 3 (three) to 9 (nine) years depending on the investment sector and activity, starting from the time the first income is generated. The investment sectors and activities as well as the income tax exemption period shall be determined in the Law on Financial Management and/or Sub-Decree. After the income tax exemption period expires, a **QIP** is entitled to pay income tax at a progressive rate compared to the total tax to be paid, as follows:
    - 25% (twenty-five percent) for the first 2 (two) years
    - 50% (fifty percent) for the next 2 (two) years, and
    - 75% (seventy-five percent) for the last 2 (two) years.

- Exemption from prepayment of income tax during the income tax exemption period.

- Exemption from minimum tax, subject to an independent audit report.

- Exemption from export tax, except as otherwise provided in other laws and regulations. Or

**2- Option 2**

- The right to deduct capital expenditure through special depreciation as provided in the regulations on taxation in force.

- The right to deduct up to 200% (two hundred percent) of specific expenses for a period of up to 9 (nine) years. The investment sectors and activities, specific expenses and the deduction period shall be determined in the Law on Financial Management and/or Sub-Decree.

- Exemption from prepayment of income tax for a specific period depending on the investment sector and activity, to be determined in the Law on Financial Management and/or Sub-Decree.

- Exemption from minimum tax, subject to an independent audit report.

- Exemption from export tax, except as otherwise provided in other laws and regulations.

In addition to the incentives in the first paragraph of this Article:

**a. Export QIP** and **Supporting Industry QIP** for export industries have the right to import construction materials, construction equipment, production equipment and production inputs with customs duty, special tax and value-added tax as a state charge.

**b. Domestically Oriented QIP** has the right to import construction materials, construction equipment and production equipment with customs duty, special tax and value-added tax as a state charge. Incentives for production inputs shall be determined in the Law on Financial Management and/or Sub-Decree.

**QIP** located in a Special Economic Zone is entitled to the same incentives and protection as other **QIPs** as stated in this Law.

### Article 27 .-

In addition to the basic incentives stated in Article 26 of this Law, investment activities registered as **QIP** shall receive additional incentives as follows:

1- Value-added tax exemption for the purchase of locally produced production inputs for the implementation of the **QIP**.

2- Deduction of 150% (one hundred and fifty percent) from the tax base for any of the following activities:
    a. Research, development and innovation
    b. Human resource development through vocational training and skills training for Cambodian employees
    c. Construction of accommodation, food courts or reasonably priced canteens, nurseries and other welfare facilities for employees
    d. Modernization of machinery to serve the production line
    e. Provision of welfare promotion for Cambodian employees, such as providing comfortable transportation for employees to travel between their residence and the factory, accommodation, food courts or reasonably priced canteens, nurseries and other welfare facilities.

### Article 28 .-

In addition to the incentives provided in Articles 26 and 27 of this Law, specific investment sectors and activities with high potential for contributing to national economic development may receive other specific special incentives to be determined in the Law on Financial Management.

# CHAPTER 7

# ACQUISITION, SALE OR MERGER OF INVESTMENT PROJECTS

### Article 29 .-

Rights, privileges or other rights received by a **QIP** cannot be transferred to any third party, except for transfers through acquisition, sale and merger of investment projects.

### Article 30 .-

Investment projects may be acquired, sold or merged without losing the incentives, investment guarantees and remaining obligations, provided that such acquisition, sale or merger is in accordance with the laws and regulations in force and is made through a written application to the **CDC** or the Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committee, the detailed procedures of which shall be determined by Sub-Decree.

# CHAPTER 8

# NULLIFICATION OF INVESTMENT PROJECTS

### Article 31 .-

An investment project may be nullified in any of the following cases:

1- Inability to continue the implementation of the **QIP**.

2- Dissolution of the legal entity implementing the **QIP**.

3- Failure to fulfill the obligations set forth in laws and regulations in force.

4- At the request of relevant ministries and institutions for projects that have a serious impact, inter alia, on the environment or national security or public interest or people's welfare, or at the request of the investor.

### Article 32 .-

In case of nullification under the conditions stated in point 1 of Article 31 of this Law, the investor shall submit a nullification application directly or through an authorized representative to the **CDC** or the Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committee. The application shall be signed by the investor or authorized representative, stating the reasons for such inability and accompanied by various supporting documents.

In case of nullification due to dissolution as stated in point 2 of Article 31 of this Law, if the dissolution is made through liquidation and voluntary dissolution of the company, the investor shall submit a nullification application directly or through an authorized representative to the **CDC** or the Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committee.

In case of nullification due to dissolution as stated in point 2 of Article 31 of this Law, if the dissolution is made through a court decision, the investor may submit a nullification application directly to the **CDC** or the Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committee, accompanied by the final court decision and relevant documents on the dissolution of the legal entity implementing the **QIP**, and a statement of the relationship of interests with the **QIP** being nullified.

The **QIP** may be nullified at the discretion of the **CDC** or the Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committee in accordance with the conditions stated in points 3 and 4 of Article 31 of this Law.

Detailed procedures for the nullification application and nullification at discretion as stated in points 1 to 4 of Article 31 of this Law shall be determined by Sub-Decree.

### Article 33 .-

Even if an investment project is nullified, the investor is not exempt from tax obligations and other various obligations.

### Article 34 .-

Investors may appeal against the nullification of an investment project by submitting a written appeal to the **CDC** or the Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committee in accordance with the procedures in force.

In case of an appeal, the **CDC** or the Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committee shall make a decision on the appeal.

If dissatisfied with the decision on the appeal, the investor who appealed may file a lawsuit with the competent court of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Article 35 .-

In case an investment project is nullified, the investor may transfer the remaining assets abroad or use them in the Kingdom of Cambodia. In case the investment project has used construction materials...

...construction equipment, production equipment and production inputs imported with customs duty, special tax and value-added tax as a state charge for less than 5 (five) years, the investor has the obligation to pay taxes on those construction materials, construction equipment, production equipment and other production inputs to be determined by Sub-Decree in accordance with the laws and regulations in force.

# CHAPTER 9

# DISPUTES AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

### Article 36 .-

Disputes between an investor and an investor related to an investment project may be resolved through the conciliation mechanism of the **CDC** or the Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committee in accordance with the procedures in force upon a written application to the **CDC** or the Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committee by any of the disputing parties.

Within 30 (thirty) days after receiving a written conciliation application, the **CDC** or the Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committee shall organize a conciliation meeting with the investors and other related entities as necessary to reach a reasonable settlement.

If the above conciliation mechanism is not successful, the dispute may be resolved through:

1- National or international arbitration agreed upon by the disputing parties.

2- Competent court of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

# CHAPTER 10

# IMPLEMENTATION

### Article 37 .-

After this Law comes into force, the Royal Government shall issue a Sub-Decree on the implementation of the Law on Investment of the Kingdom of Cambodia to implement the provisions of this Law effectively and comprehensively.

# CHAPTER 11

# TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

### Article 38 .-

The following Sub-Decrees shall remain in effect until they are replaced by new Sub-Decrees or legal regulations:

1. Sub-Decree No. 111 ANKr.BK dated September 27, 2005 on the Implementation of the Law on the Amendment to the Law on Investment of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

2. Sub-Decree No. 179 ANKr.BK dated June 03, 2021 on the Establishment of the Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committees.

3. Sub-Decree No. 34 ANKr.BK dated April 23, 2007 on the Amendment to Part 1 of Annex 1 of Sub-Decree No. 111 ANKr.BK dated September 27, 2005 on the Implementation of the Law on the Amendment to the Law on Investment of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

4. Sub-Decree No. 60 ANKr.BK dated April 05, 2016 on the Organization and Functioning of the Council for the Development of Cambodia.

5. Sub-Decree No. 33 ANKr.BK dated February 13, 2019 on the Amendment to Article 15 of Sub-Decree No. 111 ANKr.BK dated September 27, 2005 on the Implementation of the Law on the Amendment to the Law on Investment of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

6. Sub-Decree No. 148 ANKr.BK dated December 29, 2005 on the Establishment and Management of Special Economic Zones.

7. Other related legal regulations.

### Article 39 .-

The **CDC** shall continue to perform its duties until it is replaced by a Royal Decree on the Organization and Functioning of the Council for the Development of Cambodia.

### Article 40 .-

All investments that have received incentives and have been authorized under the Law on Investment of the Kingdom of Cambodia promulgated by Royal Kram No. 03/NS/94 dated August 05, 1994 and the Law on the Amendment to the Law on Investment of the Kingdom of Cambodia promulgated by Royal Kram No. NS/RKM/0303/009 dated March 24, 2003 and various Sub-Decrees shall be considered as **QIP** as stated in this Law and related Sub-Decrees.

All investments that have not received incentives or have received only investment guarantees and have been authorized under the Law on Investment of the Kingdom of Cambodia promulgated by Royal Kram No. 03/NS/94 dated August 05, 1994 and the Law on the Amendment to the Law on Investment of the Kingdom of Cambodia promulgated by Royal Kram No. NS/RKM/0303/009 dated March 24, 2003 and various Sub-Decrees shall be considered as **IPROG** as stated in this Law and related Sub-Decrees.

**QIP** that have received income tax exemption before the promulgation of this Law and whose right to receive such exemption has been approved in writing by the **CDC** or the Municipal-Provincial Investment Sub-Committee shall continue to receive the right and income tax exemption only for the remaining period.

# CHAPTER 12

# FINAL PROVISIONS

### Article 41 .-

The Law on Investment of the Kingdom of Cambodia promulgated by Royal Kram No. 03/NS/94 dated August 05, 1994, the Law on the Amendment to the Law on Investment of the Kingdom of Cambodia promulgated by Royal Kram No. NS/RKM/0303/009 dated March 24, 2003 and any provisions that contradict this Law shall be nullified.
