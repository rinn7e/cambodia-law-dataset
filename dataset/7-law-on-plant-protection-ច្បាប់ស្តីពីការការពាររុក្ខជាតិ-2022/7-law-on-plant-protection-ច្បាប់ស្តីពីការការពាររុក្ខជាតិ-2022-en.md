## Promulgate

The Law on Plant Protection and Phytosanitary, which was approved by the National Assembly on April 01, 2022 during the 7th session of the 6th legislature, and entirely reviewed and approved on its form and legal aspects by the Senate on April 20, 2022 during the 8th plenary session of the 4th legislature, and the text of which reads as follows:

# Law

## on

# Plant Protection and Phytosanitary

# Chapter 1
## General Provisions

### Article 1 .-

This Law defines the management of plant health, implementation of plant protection measures and phytosanitary measures, protection of all kinds of plant resources, prevention of introduction, spread and infestation of pests, promotion of agricultural productivity, food security, hygiene, quality and safety of agricultural products, as well as phytosanitary security of plants, plant products and goods subject to phytosanitary inspection, facilitation of trade, import, export, transit of crops, plants and plant products, and contribution to national socio-economic development and promotion of public health and welfare, with sustainable natural resources and agro-ecological systems, to ensure the management of plant protection and phytosanitary in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Article 2 .-

This Law has its scope covering plant protection and phytosanitary in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Article 3 .-

Key terms used in this Law are defined in the Glossary, which is annexed to this Law.

# Chapter 2
## Competent Management Authority

### Article 4 .-

Plant protection and phytosanitary shall be under the general jurisdiction of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

### Article 5 .-

The specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary shall be the National Plant Protection Organization of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, fulfilling the following missions:

1- Study and draft policies, legal instruments, standards, and national strategic plans on plant protection and phytosanitary.

2- Manage the working system within the framework of implementing plant protection measures and phytosanitary measures in accordance with the provisions of this Law, the International Plant Protection Convention, the Agreement on the Application of Sanitary and Phytosanitary Measures of the World Trade Organization, and national, regional, or international standards on phytosanitary measures and relevant regulations in force.

3- Manage the pest situation by implementing plant protection measures or phytosanitary measures for plants, plant products, and goods subject to phytosanitary inspection for trade, import, export, or transit through the Kingdom of Cambodia.

4- Manage and inspect all types of technical services in the implementation of plant protection measures and phytosanitary measures in the Kingdom of Cambodia, ensuring scientific assessments on the quality, efficacy, and risk of all types of plant protection products in accordance with this Law and relevant regulations in force.

5- Perform pest risk analysis, conduct certification by issuing phytosanitary certificates for all goods subject to phytosanitary inspection.

6- Manage phytosanitary information and implement educational dissemination programs to raise public awareness within the framework of implementing plant protection measures and phytosanitary measures.

7- Implement research programs and technical capacity building as a mechanism to support the management of plant protection and phytosanitary.

8- Develop human resource capacity, educate, train and promote qualifications of phytosanitary inspectors to enforce this Law effectively.

9- Cooperate and coordinate with relevant ministries, institutions, the private sector, civil society, and development partners within the framework of managing plant health and bilateral or multilateral phytosanitary to participate in implementing this Law effectively.

10- Conduct international cooperation by serving as the official contact point representing the Kingdom of Cambodia in implementing the International Plant Protection Convention, implementing international or regional instruments, and participating in all international fora related to plant protection and phytosanitary.

11- Facilitate private sector investments within the framework of supporting technical services and capacity building for plant protection measures and phytosanitary measures to promote the quality and safety of agricultural products in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

12- Monitor and inspect all activities in the system of plant protection measures and phytosanitary measures, and take measures to enforce this Law and other regulations under this Law effectively.

13- Fulfill other duties within the scope of this Law.

# Chapter 3
## Development of Plant Protection and Phytosanitary Sector

### Article 6 .-

The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, which has a specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary, shall prepare development plans for the plant protection and phytosanitary sector

which shows national policies, strategies, and development programs for the plant protection and phytosanitary sector with the participation of all stakeholders.

The development plan for the plant protection and phytosanitary sector shall be decided by the Royal Government and published to the public for effective and transparent participation.

### Article 7 .-

The development plan for the plant protection and phytosanitary sector shall set out long-term goals and priorities for the management of plant health and plant resources in the Kingdom of Cambodia, and build professional capacity to fulfill the missions and duties stipulated in this Law.

The development plan for the plant protection and phytosanitary sector shall include the following key programs:

1- Development of plant protection and phytosanitary services by improving inspection, prevention, and eradication of all types of pests, including pathways and pest agents, as well as new invasive pest species.

2- Development of agricultural production systems that ensure food safety through the implementation of Good Agricultural Practices.

3- Development of human resources, physical infrastructure, and strengthening technical capacity in scientific research and sharing traditional knowledge in plant protection and phytosanitary.

4- Improving public-private partnerships within the framework of plant protection and phytosanitary.

5- Strengthening economic, scientific, and technical assistance cooperation at national and international levels for the development of plant protection and phytosanitary.

6- Development of database for plant protection and phytosanitary.

7- Development of food safety addressing the risks of agricultural pesticide residues or substances causing health impacts.

8- Development of phytosanitary security for plant health addressing the risks of infestation spread in domestic, regional, or international trade activities.

9- Other developments within the scope of plant protection and phytosanitary.

Phytosanitary security shall be determined by sub-decree.

#### Chapter 4
#### Plant Protection
#### Section 1
#### Protection of Plants and Plant Resources

##### Article 9 .-
The Royal Government shall establish plant protection measures and plant resources protection measures for the purpose of preventing the occurrence, spreading, and outbreak of all kinds of pests in order to reduce loss of plants and plant resources in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

##### Article 10 .-
The protection of plants and plant resources shall:

1- Implement measures simultaneously and regularly, prioritizing prevention or early control as a main priority, and taking measures to eliminate pests in a timely and prompt manner to ensure the effectiveness of the measures.

2- Implement pest control/suppression measures, prioritizing the collective interest of society or the national interest with long-term importance over private or community interest of immediate importance.

3- Implement integrated pest management (IPM) measures by selecting biological, physical, mechanical, or cultural/agricultural measures and traditional experiences of local communities as key choices, for the purpose of minimizing negative impacts on humans, animals, and the agro-ecosystem.

4- Implement chemical measures as a last resort in accordance with technical standards for the use of plant protection products that can achieve efficacy and economic efficiency of use, and ensure safety for humans, animals, and natural enemies, while minimizing negative impacts on the agro-ecosystem.

Guidelines for plant protection measures shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

##### Article 11 .-
All kinds of plant protection products that can be traded or used in the implementation of plant protection measures or phytosanitary measures in the Kingdom of Cambodia shall undergo a scientific study and evaluation process on quality, efficacy, safety, risk, and acceptance of evaluation from the specialized organization in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary in accordance with the requirements of international standards referring to the type of product, procedures, and relevant regulations in force.

##### Article 12 .-
It is prohibited to import, cultivate, possess, or distribute potential hazardous pests and invasive alien plant species that pose a high risk of damage and destruction to plants, plant resources, and agro-ecosystems in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries shall issue a Prakas to determine the list of prohibited crop species and conditional plant species that can be allowed for commercial trade and cultivation in the Kingdom of Cambodia in accordance with the provisions of this Law and relevant regulations in force.

Physical or legal entities who commercially trade or cultivate crop species or plant species under conditions must have a permit issued by the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

The procedures for conditional plant species that can be commercially traded or cultivated in the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Section 2
#### Prevention and Elimination of Pests

##### Article 13 .-
The specialized entity in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary shall fulfill the role of monitoring the health status of plants and plant resources, by determining measures to prevent and eliminate pests at the national level and participating in the implementation of phytosanitary measures at the international level with the member countries of the international convention on plant protection.

In order to prevent and eliminate pests damaging plants and plant resources, the specialized entity in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary and the Provincial-Municipal Departments of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, in cooperation with relevant specialized entities, shall have the following duties:

1- Observe, search, forecast, and declare timely information on the occurrence, outbreak, and damage level of pests, and disseminate appropriate measures to be implemented to the targets in a timely manner.

2- Monitor the health status of plants and plant resources around the areas under their respective jurisdiction in order to take measures to prevent the spread of pests and eliminate pests.

3- Monitor and inspect the status of pests damaging plants and plant resources, and implement pest elimination measures in plant management areas, agricultural production areas, botanical gardens, nurseries/residential centers implementing crop production, processing sites, packaging sites, and storage sites for commercial purposes, and storage sites for exporting products as

plant products and goods subject to phytosanitary inspection, parking areas of transportation means, and other places in the Kingdom of Cambodia that have activities related to plant resources and plant products, or places that have a risk or are suspected of having a pest risk, by providing instructions or requiring the owner of plants, plant products, and goods subject to phytosanitary inspection or the owner of the respective location to take measures to prevent and eliminate pests or correct the implementation of inappropriate measures for the purpose of preventing negative impacts on the public interest.

4- Promote local public movements, cooperating with territorial authorities at all levels, relevant competent authorities, the private sector, farm owners, owners of locations as described in point 3 above, and other stakeholders to participate and cooperate in prevention and pest elimination operations that destroy plants and plant resources in their respective localities.

5- Perform other duties within the framework of plant and plant resource protection measures.

##### Article 14 .-
The Provincial-Municipal Departments of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries may request intervention assistance for pest elimination from the specialized entity in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary in the event that the respective department cannot manage the pest situation.

The Provincial-Municipal Departments of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries shall have the duty to report to the specialized entity in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary on the status of implementation of the annual plant protection action plan, the status of agricultural production, and the results of pest elimination for each occurrence case per cropping season and annually, as well as other information related to the health of plants and crop products, and plant resources under their respective jurisdiction.

##### Article 15 .-
Physical or legal entities who occupy farms or owners of plant resources in the Kingdom of Cambodia shall have the following obligations:

- Regularly monitor and inspect the presence and status of pests that can destroy plant resources in their farms, and must take pest elimination measures effectively and safely, or prevent the spread or outbreak to plant resources of other owners

- Immediately report to the nearest specialized entity in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary in case of detecting the presence of pests that are spreading or severely destroying plant resources, to request intervention for timely prevention or elimination of the pests.

The obligations of plant resource owners in implementing plant protection measures shall be determined by the guidelines of the specialized entity in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

##### Article 16 .-
The Provincial-Municipal Departments of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, in coordination with the specialized entity in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary, shall monitor, evaluate, and report to the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries on the status of infestation of pests within their respective jurisdiction, based on the conditions for declaring a pest outbreak.

In the event that the pest outbreak situation is severe, the specialized entity in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary shall report to the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries as an emergency case of pest outbreak in order to provide necessary means and resources for taking measures to eliminate pests in this emergency case in an effective and timely manner.

The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries shall issue a Prakas to determine the outbreak area of pests upon the request of the specialized entity in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary.

The conditions for declaration and the right to take measures against pest outbreaks shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

##### Article 17 .-
In order to prevent the spread of pests to other areas, the transportation of plants and plant products crossing or leaving the declared pest outbreak area shall be temporarily suspended and must be placed under the management of the specialized entity in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries until there is a Prakas to lift the pest outbreak area.

Territorial authorities of all levels in the pest outbreak area shall mobilize public movements under their respective jurisdiction to gather state forces and materials for promoting pest elimination and preventing the spread of pests.

In the event that the pest outbreak situation is severe, the respective territorial authorities shall immediately notify the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries directly and must provide information to the nearest specialized entity in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary in order to cooperate in implementing urgent measures.

##### Article 18 .-
After declaring the pest outbreak, in case the infestation has been eliminated and eradicated, the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries shall issue a Prakas to lift the pest outbreak area.

##### Article 19 .-
Physical or legal entities who have sufficient qualifications and professional expertise recognized in accordance with the laws in force shall have the right to request to provide plant protection services or perform interventions related to plant protection measures as follows:

1- Investigate the status of pests destroying crop products or plant products.

2- Provide consulting services on the presence and infestation status caused by pests on crop products or plant products.

3- Provide advice to farm owners on prevention and pest elimination measures that destroy their crop products or plant products.

4- Provide implementation services for prevention measures and carry out pest elimination operations or supply equipment, tools, and plant protection products for use in the implementation of plant protection measures.

##### Article 20 .-
Physical or legal entities providing services within the framework of plant protection measures must obtain a permit issued by the specialized entity in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary and must fulfill the requirements in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

The procedures and requirements for managing plant protection services shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Section 3
#### Prevention of Impacts

##### Article 21 .-
Physical or legal entities implementing plant protection measures or carrying out pest elimination operations must comply with the main technical standards in implementing measures to prevent impacts from plant protection and plant resources as follows:

- Ensure safety for plant or crop health to be protected without affecting the productivity potential or crop yield

- Ensure safety from risk of danger to humans, animals, and the environment surrounding the area where measures are implemented

- Ensure safety of crop products or plant products for use as food for humans, animals, or as raw materials for processing

- Do not cause the emergence of pest individuals that are resistant to plant protection measures.

Technical standards for measures to prevent impacts of plant protection and plant resources shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

##### Article 22 .-
The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries shall determine the standards of the agricultural production system that ensures food safety, including Good Agricultural Practices (GAP) and Organic Agriculture.

The specialized entity in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary shall manage the monitoring and implementation process of the agricultural production system that ensures food safety, along with the certification of crop products or plant products.

The management of the agricultural production system that ensures food safety in the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

##### Article 23 .-
Physical or legal entities implementing plant protection measures or carrying out pest elimination operations in crop production shall have the obligation to participate in ensuring food safety and crop products necessarily.

Crop products harvested from cultivation sites for the purpose of marketing or using as food for humans, animal feed, or as raw materials in food processing must ensure food safety without substances causing impact on health, whether physical, biological, or chemical substances, or have the residue of agricultural pesticide under the maximum residue limit (MRL) determined by technical standards or any standard recognized and used in the region or internationally, or in accordance with the standard requirements of importing countries in the event that the crop product or plant product is exported for use as food.

Maximum residue limits (MRLs) of agricultural pesticides and substances causing impact on health in plant-origin agricultural products shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

##### Article 24 .-
The specialized entity in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary has the authority to inspect the maximum residue limits of agricultural pesticides and substances causing impact on health in plant-origin agricultural products to be exported or imported into the Kingdom of Cambodia and before those products are traded, supplied, distributed, or circulated in the market.

The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries shall cooperate with sub-national administrations to establish collection sites for plant-origin agricultural products in provinces and municipalities for

inspection of the maximum residue limits of agricultural pesticides and substances causing impact on health before those products are allowed to be supplied, distributed to users, or circulated in the market.

The establishment and management of collection sites for plant-origin agricultural products in provinces and municipalities for inspecting the maximum residue limits of agricultural pesticides and substances causing impact on health shall be determined by Prakas of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

##### Article 25 .-
The following activities are prohibited:

1- Conducting plant protection operations that easily cause danger to humans, animals, natural enemies, and the agro-ecosystem in contradiction with technical standards for measures to prevent impacts from plant protection and plant resources.

2- Using plant protection products or substances causing impact on health, whether products formulated with chemical or biological substances, on crop products or plant products to be harvested for supply, distribution, display, or marketing for use as food for humans or animal feed.

3- Bringing plant-origin agricultural products that contain residues of plant protection products or substances causing impact on health exceeding the determined maximum residue limit to supply, distribute, display, or market for use as food for humans or animal feed.

4- Exporting, importing, keeping, transporting, displaying, and using crop seeds that are contaminated with pests at a severe level, or contaminated with phytosanitary pests, or prohibited pests.

#### Chapter 5
#### Phytosanitary
#### Section 1
#### Phytosanitary Inspection

##### Article 26 .-
Phytosanitary measures shall be implemented for the purpose of preventing the spread of phytosanitary pests and regulated pests from one country to another and from one area to another, as well as ensuring the safety of plant-origin agricultural products regarding the maximum residue limits of agricultural pesticides and substances causing impact on health, but those measures must not create disguised obstacles to trade activities.

Phytosanitary measures shall be implemented in accordance with:

- International standards for phytosanitary measures adopted by the International Plant Protection Convention

- Phytosanitary requirements of the importing country

- Phytosanitary requirements of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

Phytosanitary requirements of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

Goods subject to phytosanitary inspection shall be determined by Sub-decree.

##### Article 27 .-
The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries shall organize, strengthen capacity, and develop technical infrastructure that can ensure the effectiveness of the performance of duties of the specialized entity in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary in order to support phytosanitary inspections in compliance with the requirements of implementing international standards for phytosanitary measures and relevant regulations in force.

##### Article 28 .-
Phytosanitary inspections of all exported and imported goods as plants, plant products, and other exported and imported goods subject to phytosanitary inspection shall be determined by inter-ministerial Prakas between the Ministry of Economy and Finance and the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, based on risk management principles and in compliance with regulations in force.

##### Article 29 .-
The specialized entity in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary shall perform the following duties:

1- Inspect plants, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection that are being cultivated or stored in warehouses, to report on the status of occurrence, outbreak, or spread of prohibited phytosanitary pests.

2- Inspect goods as plants, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection to be imported or exported, in order to determine impacts and necessarily certify the pest status of the goods sent through sampling for pest risk analysis.

3- Inspect maximum residue limits of agricultural pesticides and substances causing impact on health in plant-origin agricultural products to be exported or imported in accordance with the phytosanitary requirements of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

4- Prohibit all types of goods, including packaging materials, storage facilities, means of transport, and containers to be exported or imported, where regulated pests are detected.

5- Prohibit waste to be disposed of from aircraft arriving in the Kingdom of Cambodia, and places where plants, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection that have been imported are processed or cleaned, from causing threats to plant resources within the Kingdom of Cambodia.

6- Receive applications for phytosanitary inspection, inspect and verify relevant documents attached to the application for pest risk assessment, issue import phytosanitary certificates, or issue phytosanitary certificates for export from the Kingdom of Cambodia of plants, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection.

7- Investigate, monitor, and update data and information on the status of pests in the Kingdom of Cambodia, and regularly exchange this information with the Departments of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries in the capital and provinces, regional phytosanitary branch offices, border gate phytosanitary stations, and other duty compliance places.

8- Investigate and request relevant information or documents when there are suspected cases of violation of phytosanitary requirements as stipulated in this Law. When necessary, implement post-inspection measures to monitor phytosanitary security in accordance with phytosanitary inspection procedures.

9- Perform other tasks within the framework of inspection and implementation of phytosanitary measures.

#### Article 30 .-

The specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary has the right to reject or take measures within the framework of inspection on goods subject to phytosanitary inspection, as follows:

1- Do not permit the import or export of any goods subject to phytosanitary inspection that fail to meet the phytosanitary measures and requirements as stipulated in this Law.

2- Reject the export of any goods that do not comply with the phytosanitary requirements of the importing country.

3- Declare invalidity of phytosanitary certificates issued by the competent authority of other countries in case of detection of the presence of pests causing danger to the imported goods.

#### Article 31 .-

The specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary shall issue import phytosanitary certificates, or issue phytosanitary certificates for export or re-export from the Kingdom of Cambodia of plants, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection, in accordance with the model certificates used globally under the International Plant Protection Convention.

In case a phytosanitary certificate is lost or damaged due to any specific reason within its validity period, the importing or exporting operator shall report and request the specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary to issue a replacement phytosanitary certificate for the original copy.

#### Article 32 .-

The items subject to the request for export or import permits or phytosanitary certificates for export or import from the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries shall be determined by a sub-decree related to the list of prohibited and restricted goods.

#### Article 33 .-

The specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary and the regional phytosanitary branch offices have the right to use the phytosanitary stamp to mark phytosanitary certificates to be issued for commercial operations of plants, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection in accordance with this Law.

The right to use the stamp and the model of the phytosanitary stamp shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 34 .-

Within the framework of regional and international integration, the specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary shall promote the implementation of electronic phytosanitary certificates in the phytosanitary inspection procedures for imported, exported, or re-exported goods.

The specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary has the right to implement phytosanitary inspection for operators who make electronic declarations about their goods stating that they comply with phytosanitary requirements in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

The conditions and requirements for the implementation of electronic phytosanitary certificates shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 35 .-

The specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary shall manage the phytosanitary database system through networking of the specialized units in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary performing their duties in various locations within the Kingdom of Cambodia, in order to establish a national phytosanitary list.

The establishment of the national phytosanitary list shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 36 .-

Procedures for phytosanitary inspection on imported, exported, re-exported, and transit goods through the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

### Section 2
### Pest Risk Analysis on Exported and Imported Goods

#### Article 37 .-

The specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary shall implement pest risk analysis for plants, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection intended for import.

Pest risk analysis shall be implemented when requesting a phytosanitary certificate for import or prior to the import of goods, in order to identify pests that may pose a risk and to determine pest management measures upon import.

#### Article 38 .-

In case of necessity or when invited by any exporting country to perform phytosanitary inspection duties, phytosanitary inspectors of the specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary may implement pest risk analysis on products intended for import into the Kingdom of Cambodia, perform phytosanitary inspection, and conduct joint certification directly in the territory of the exporting country in accordance with the agreed requirements.

The State Party of the exporting country shall bear all expenses in the process of pest risk analysis on products intended for import into the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 39 .-

The specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary may implement pest risk analysis on products intended for export from the Kingdom of Cambodia, based on the requirements of the importing country or through an agreement instrument between the importing State Party and the exporting State Party.

In case of necessity, the State Party of the importing country may implement pest risk analysis on products intended for export from the Kingdom of Cambodia directly in the Kingdom of Cambodia, in accordance with the agreed requirements.

The Kingdom of Cambodia, as the State Party of the exporting country, shall bear all expenses in the process of pest risk analysis on products intended for export from the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 40 .-

Procedures for implementing pest risk analysis on products intended for import or export shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

### Section 3
### Phytosanitary Inspection on Imported Goods

#### Article 41 .-

For the purpose of preventing the introduction and spread of pests, the specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary shall determine phytosanitary requirements and measures on the import of plants, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection, based on the international technical justification standards in force.

Based on changing conditions or new circumstances, the specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary may modify or withdraw phytosanitary requirements and measures, as well as import prohibitions, as necessary.

The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries may evaluate and accept alternative phytosanitary measures proposed by the specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary of the exporting country that have equivalent values to the phytosanitary measures required under paragraph 1 of this Article.

The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries shall officially publish all phytosanitary requirements, prohibitions, and modifications.

#### Article 42 .-

Prior to performing import operations of goods including plants, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection, the importer shall submit an application for an import phytosanitary certificate to the specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary, attached with relevant documents of the goods to be imported, and must comply with the requirements for

Evaluation on applications shall comply with the international standards in force and implement pest risk analysis in order to determine the phytosanitary requirements to be applied to those goods.

#### Article 43 .-

The importer may import goods into the Kingdom of Cambodia by complying with the following conditions:

1- Must have an import phytosanitary certificate issued by the specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary, and the importer must fulfill all requirements along with paying public service fees for phytosanitary inspection or for other related services in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

2- Must have a phytosanitary certificate issued by the competent authority of the exporting country prior to the goods being imported into the Kingdom of Cambodia.

3- Must attach a certificate of treatment or fumigation of the goods in case it has been implemented by the competent authority of the exporting country, or other relevant documents as necessary.

4- Must import their goods through the permitted border gates specified in the import phytosanitary certificate.

5- Must make a declaration of the arrival of their goods to request phytosanitary inspection in accordance with the procedures in force.

#### Article 44 .-

All goods including plants, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection may be inspected at airports, ports, warehouses, or storage yards based on risk principles in accordance with the regulations in force.

#### Article 45 .-

Under specific circumstances, the phytosanitary inspection of plants, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection may be exempted and permitted for import, based on the following conditions:

1- Results of pest risk analysis confirming the phytosanitary safety and security of the goods.

2- Quality of the goods is recognized by international quality standards.

3- The operator has fulfilled all or equivalent requirements, or has fulfilled requirements through electronic declaration of the goods to be imported.

#### Article 46 .-

In case the results of phytosanitary inspection confirm that the goods have correct and complete documentation, and the goods do not pose any phytosanitary pest risk or any dangerous pest, the importer shall be permitted to import those goods into the Kingdom of Cambodia in accordance with the procedures in force.

#### Article 47 .-

In case the results of phytosanitary inspection show a risk of pest spread from those goods, part of or all of the goods shall be detained in order to decide on taking the following measures:

1- Implement appropriate phytosanitary measures to eliminate the risk.

2- Return (re-export) to the country of origin.

3- Declare as goods that cannot be sold or distributed.

4- Take measures to destroy the goods.

#### Article 48 .-

After the goods are permitted to enter the Kingdom of Cambodia or after treatment has been performed on the imported goods, in case some parts of the goods are damaged during the inspection process, or some goods are found to have not been tested and are facing damage caused by pests, the competent phytosanitary inspectors must take immediate measures to destroy them.

### Section 4
### Phytosanitary Inspection on Exported and Re-exported Goods

#### Article 49 .-

Within a period of not more than 10 (ten) days prior to exporting plants, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection from the Kingdom of Cambodia, in compliance with the phytosanitary conditions of the importing country, the exporter shall:

1- Submit an application for an export phytosanitary certificate.

2- Provide relevant documents in accordance with the requirements.

3- Prepare the goods to be ready for phytosanitary inspection and certification, or testing as necessary.

4- Pay public service fees for phytosanitary inspection or for other related services.

#### Article 50 .-

When goods are transported into the Kingdom of Cambodia and subsequently must be re-exported to another country, the exporter shall:

1- Submit an application for a re-export phytosanitary certificate.

2- Provide all documents required for re-export.

3- Pay public service fees for re-export, including phytosanitary inspection of the goods and issuance of the phytosanitary certificate.

4- Facilitate the phytosanitary inspection of the goods.

#### Article 51 .-

After completing the phytosanitary inspection for the exporter, the specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary shall issue a re-export phytosanitary certificate for the goods, which must strictly declare the country of origin of the transported goods and must be based on the following reasons:

1- The goods show no signs of pest transmission risk.

2- The phytosanitary requirements of the importing country are similar to or less stringent than the requirements set by the exporting country.

3- The documents to be attached are consistent with the phytosanitary conditions of the importing country.

#### Article 52 .-

The exporter may re-export their goods after receiving the re-export phytosanitary certificate in compliance with the requirements set for maintaining the phytosanitary security of the goods after the issuance of the certificate or prior to re-export.

### Section 5
### Phytosanitary Inspection on Transit Goods

#### Article 53 .-

Phytosanitary inspection of plants, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection transported in transit through the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be implemented in accordance with the phytosanitary inspection procedures and relevant regulations in force.

For goods in transit through the Kingdom of Cambodia, an import phytosanitary certificate shall not be required in the following cases:

1- The goods are packaged in a secure condition and pose no risk of potential pest spread within those goods.

2- The goods, together with the attached documents, comply with the phytosanitary conditions of the country of destination.

3- The phytosanitary certificate of the country of origin is consistent with the goods.

#### Article 54 .-

All plants, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection must be packed securely, guaranteed not to be opened or torn, and must be secured in loading means that ensure no leakage during transportation.

All means of transporting goods must ensure that there is no risk of introduction or spread of phytosanitary pests or dangerous pests along the transit route.

#### Article 55 .-

The owner of transit goods through the Kingdom of Cambodia shall notify the specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary prior to the arrival of those goods at the border gate.

Phytosanitary inspection shall be implemented on the means of transport, the exterior of the loading means, and inspection of the goods by verifying with the attached documents, and the inspection work must be promoted quickly to facilitate the continuation of transit transport.

#### Article 56 .-

In case it is found that the goods subject to inspection as stated in Article 55 of this Law do not comply with the specified phytosanitary requirements, those goods must be subject to necessary phytosanitary measures prior to being permitted to continue transit transport.

In case of accidental emergency during the transportation of goods resulting in breakage or opening of packaging materials, the owner of the goods must report immediately to the competent specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary that is nearest, or to the local authority nearest to the location to assist in intervention and immediately implement phytosanitary measures again on the goods, and shall provide a phytosanitary certificate for continuing export or transit transport.

## Chapter 6
## Management of Pest Outbreak and Spread
### Section 1
### Pest Outbreak and Spread Areas

#### Article 57 .-

The specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary has the duty to monitor, research, and examine the status of pests regularly by stages regarding the outbreak

Plants, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection to take measures to prevent the introduction and spread of pests from one area to another, or to take measures to eradicate pests as necessary.

Upon finding any pest source or the spread of a regulated pest or quarantine pest within any area under their jurisdiction, the competent phytosanitary inspector must immediately report this finding to the technical unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary duties, in order to request the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries to issue a declaration defining the surveyed area as a temporary phytosanitary pest infestation area.

Within 3 (three) weeks after the declaration of the temporary phytosanitary pest infestation area, the technical unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary duties must request the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries to issue a declaration declaring the area of infestation or suspected pest infestation as a quarantine area to promote the urgent implementation of phytosanitary measures.

#### Article 58.-

In the place declared as a quarantine area, the technical unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary duties must take the following measures:

1- Publicize in writing the content of the declaration defining the quarantine area, specifying:

- The exact size of the quarantine area and the requirements for implementing phytosanitary measures for the relevant owners or occupiers
- The appropriate duration of the quarantine
- The conditions for re-declaration once the pest is fully eradicated.

2- Post pictures, quarantine area identification signs, notifications, or similar formats around the restricted area.

3- Regularly monitor the pest status in the quarantine area and promote the implementation of phytosanitary measures, not allowing the pest spread to prolong for a long time.

#### Article 59.-

When the pest outbreak is controlled and eradicated, the technical unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary duties shall request the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries to issue a declaration to revoke the pest infestation area or the quarantine

quarantine area, and shall publicize this to farm owners and other affected stakeholders in those areas, or publicize it to the public.

#### Article 60.-

An area shall be declared a pest-free area if the technical unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary duties:

1- Recognizes through clear surveillance that a specific pest is not present in the area.

2- Has eradication measures in place to maintain the pest-free area.

3- Has a monitoring system to verify that the conditions for the pest-free area are maintained.

#### Article 61.-

An area shall be declared an area of low pest prevalence if the technical unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary duties:

1- Recognizes that the regulated pest occurs in the area at low levels.

2- Has eradication measures in place to maintain the low pest level.

3- Has a monitoring system to verify that the presence of the pest is suppressed to a low level.

### Section 2
### Phytosanitary Measures

#### Article 62.-

The technical unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary duties is competent to take phytosanitary measures in any area within the Kingdom of Cambodia where there is an outbreak or suspected outbreak of a regulated pest, a pest-free area, or an area of low pest prevalence as follows:

1- Treat, dispose of, or destroy goods, plants, plant products, or other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection, including treating means of transport, storage facilities, packaging materials, and other related materials, whether or not the pest is present, in order to maintain a pest-free area or to suppress the pest to a low level as necessary.

2- Treat to eradicate pests as necessary.

3- Prohibit or permit under conditions the movement of plants, plant products, or other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection within, to, or from other areas.

4- Prohibit the planting or replanting of plants or crops in areas currently infested by pests.

5- Implement other phytosanitary measures as necessary.

#### Article 63.-

The introduction of specimens of phytosanitary pests or regulated pests from a pest infestation area into non-infested areas within the Kingdom of Cambodia for analysis must be authorized by a competent phytosanitary inspector.

The transport of goods into, out of, or through a phytosanitary pest infestation area or regulated pest area within the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be temporarily suspended until a new declaration revoking the quarantine area is issued by the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries.

#### Article 64.-

Based on the report of the competent phytosanitary inspector who has surveyed, assessed, or confirmed that the pest infestation status in any area is a severe pest outbreak, the technical unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary duties shall report to the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries to request the issuance of a declaration on a phytosanitary emergency status to promote the implementation of emergency phytosanitary measures.

Emergency phytosanitary measures shall be determined by a declaration of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries.

#### Article 65.-

Processed or unprocessed wood products, wood packaging materials, dunnage, or pallets made of wood must undergo phytosanitary treatment in accordance with international standards for phytosanitary measures for wood packaging materials and bear the applicable international mark.

#### Article 66.-

Physical or legal persons who are owners of goods subject to phytosanitary inspection or owners of establishments must facilitate the competent phytosanitary inspectors to enter production establishments, warehouses, storage facilities, means of transport, and all types of storage facilities to perform their duties in phytosanitary inspection or to take phytosanitary measures in accordance with legal provisions.

The owner, possessor, or person responsible for any goods subject to phytosanitary inspection has the obligation to take immediate measures to prevent the spread of any pest when that person sees or knows that their goods are damaged by phytosanitary pests.

Owners of farms, land, or establishments who see or suspect that their property or property under their responsibility is infested or threatened by any phytosanitary pest must immediately notify the nearest technical unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary duties in order to assist in taking urgent phytosanitary measures.

#### Article 67.-

The technical unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary duties has the right to notify any farm owner, landowner, or establishment owner to require them to implement phytosanitary measures within the premises of their property.

In case the owner or occupier of the property refuses to comply with the notice specified in the first paragraph of this Article, the competent phytosanitary inspector has the right to question and clarify to the property owner the necessity of implementing phytosanitary measures that require the destruction of plants, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection to prevent the spread of pests.

#### Article 68.-

The owner of the farm, land, or establishment shall bear the loss without compensation or damage repairs for their plants, plant products, or other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection that are destroyed during the implementation of phytosanitary measures.

#### Article 69.-

During the transport of regulated goods, in case of a traffic accident or if the packaging is torn or leaked, causing the release of those regulated goods, the owner or person responsible for the transport means or the goods must immediately inform the nearest technical unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary duties or the local authority to assist in intervening and taking urgent phytosanitary measures.

#### Article 70.-

Every citizen has the obligation to immediately notify the nearest technical unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary duties or the local authority when encountering the following events:

1- Any land transport means carrying goods subject to phytosanitary inspection that has leaked or scattered in any location within the Kingdom of Cambodia.

2- Any vessel, motorboat, boat, or water transport means carrying goods subject to phytosanitary inspection that has leaked or fallen into the waters of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

3- Any aircraft or air transport means that has dropped goods subject to phytosanitary inspection into the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 71.-

The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries shall determine appropriate locations for establishing phytosanitary quarantine stations to store samples of goods, plants, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection, as well as other imported materials for pest risk analysis, surveillance, testing, treatment, or destruction.

#### Article 72.-

The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries shall coordinate and cooperate with relevant ministries, national institutions, international or regional organizations, non-governmental organizations, and the private sector to update phytosanitary measures, which may vary according to pest risk and outbreak situations, in line with phytosanitary events.

#### Article 73.-

For the purpose of managing the spread of pests in all forms, the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries shall issue declarations on:

1- Measures to prevent the spread of regulated pests and to eradicate regulated non-quarantine pests.

2- Measures to prevent the spread, introduction, and eradication of invasive alien pests in the Kingdom of Cambodia in accordance with the provisions of this Law and relevant regulations in force.

3- Establishment and maintenance measures of pest-free areas and areas of low pest prevalence.

### Chapter 7
### Pest List and Phytosanitary Diagnosis of Pests

#### Article 74.-

The technical unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary duties shall study, prepare, and update the pest list by crop group or geographical area of infestation, and by type of goods subject to phytosanitary inspection, classifying them into pest groups

phytosanitary pests, regulated non-quarantine pests, and quarantine pests, in accordance with the standards of the International Plant Protection Convention.

The list of phytosanitary pests, regulated non-quarantine pests, and quarantine pests shall be used in conducting pest risk analysis of imported goods subject to phytosanitary inspection.

The domestic pest list, which includes all pests that infest all groups of crops or plants present in the Kingdom of Cambodia, shall be used as the basic list for conducting pest risk analysis of goods intended for export from the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 75.-

The technical unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary duties shall compile or update the list of phytosanitary pests, regulated non-quarantine pests, quarantine pests, and domestic pests for official use in phytosanitary management in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

The technical unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary duties shall conduct research through identification and phytosanitary diagnosis of pests, focusing on updating newly present pest species in phytosanitary management, with special attention to invasive alien pests classified in the quarantine pest list.

The pest list along with its updates shall be widely publicized to the technical unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary duties, Provincial-Municipal Departments of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries, farm owners, agricultural communities, and other relevant stakeholders as part of education, training, and raising public awareness.

#### Article 76.-

Within the framework of compiling the pest list of the Kingdom of Cambodia, the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries shall coordinate and cooperate with relevant ministries, institutions, and research establishments to collect data and pest specimens on all groups of crops or plants in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries shall establish a pest library to store all types of pest specimens for research and to use as reference specimens for pest risk analysis.

#### Article 77.-

The pest list for phytosanitary management in the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be determined by a declaration of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries.

#### Article 78.-

Within the framework of scientific phytosanitary management, the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries shall establish pest diagnosis centers and sub-centers under the technical unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary duties as follows:

1- A pest diagnosis center acting as a laboratory to identify and diagnose phytosanitary pests at the national level, in accordance with international standards for phytosanitary measures.

2- Pest diagnosis sub-centers to be established at all regional phytosanitary branch offices with potential for pest spread.

#### Article 79.-

The technical unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary duties is the sole competent authority responsible for and having the right to send pest specimens abroad for identification or re-identification and pest diagnosis.

### Chapter 8
### Research and Capacity Building in Plant Protection and Phytosanitary

#### Article 80.-

The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries shall support and promote the implementation of research and capacity building programs in phytosanitary as follows:

1- Research on phytosanitary database systems.

2- Research on pests, risk analysis, and implementation of phytosanitary measures.

3- Research on hazard risks in the implementation of plant protection measures and phytosanitary measures.

4- Capacity building in phytosanitary and improvement of technical infrastructure for plant protection and phytosanitary.

5- Capacity building in human resources and promoting the qualifications of technical officers managing plant protection and phytosanitary.

6- Public awareness campaigns and education on plant protection and phytosanitary.

7- Other research work within the framework of phytosanitary capacity building.

#### Article 81.-

Physical or legal persons who possess sufficient qualifications and recognized professional expertise in accordance with the law in force have the right to request to provide phytosanitary services or related intervention services for phytosanitary measures as follows:

- All types of phytosanitary treatment services
- Establishment of laboratories and testing stations
- Researchers, analysts, experimenters, technical personnel
- Other services within the framework of phytosanitary measures, in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

Physical or legal persons who perform relevant phytosanitary services as specified in the first paragraph of this Article must hold an authorization letter issued by the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries and meet the requirements in accordance with the procedures and regulations of this Law.

Laboratories and testing stations must be accredited by national or international accreditation bodies in accordance with the law in force.

Researchers, analysts, experimenters, and technical personnel must be recognized by the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries.

Procedures and requirements for managing the provision of phytosanitary services shall be determined by a declaration of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries.

### Chapter 9
### Reserve Fund for Plant Protection and Phytosanitary

#### Article 82.-

A Reserve Fund for Plant Protection and Phytosanitary shall be established under the management of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries and the Ministry of Economy and Finance to be used within the framework of supporting plant protection measures and phytosanitary measures.

The management and allocation of the Reserve Fund for Plant Protection and Phytosanitary shall be determined by a sub-decree.

#### Article 83.-

The Reserve Fund for Plant Protection and Phytosanitary has the following sources of revenue:

1- Various contributions from development partners.

2- Grant projects from various sources.

3- Part of the public service revenue in phytosanitary and plant protection management.

4- Part of the fines obtained from the enforcement of this Law.

5- Other financial sources.

#### Article 48 .-

The emergency fund for plant protection and phytosanitary may be used only for the following activities:

1- Unplanned emergency measures for specific campaigns in preventing the spread, outbreak of pests, eradication, or elimination of pest infestation.

2- Research on specific pests and risk analysis.

3- Establishment of pest-free areas and maintenance.

4- Management of specific situations of pest outbreak areas or regulated areas.

5- Public awareness and promotion campaigns for specific cases of pest spread or outbreak.

6- Fulfillment of international obligations of the competent authority in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary within the framework of implementing the International Plant Protection Convention.

7- Other necessary supporting activities decided by the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries and the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

### Chapter 10
### Phytosanitary Information Management and Dissemination

#### Article 49 .-

The competent authority in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary has the role as the sole national authority in managing phytosanitary information, which shall be considered as relevant information for the national interest.

#### Article 50 .-

Any dissemination, demonstration, or dissemination in other forms regarding the implementation of plant protection measures or phytosanitary measures, the use of plant protection products, and other information that is effective in the implementation of plant protection measures or phytosanitary measures, shall undergo examination, study of technical advice, and acceptance of evaluation from the competent authority in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary.

#### Article 51 .-

The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries shall promote the implementation of public educational awareness and dissemination programs in plant protection and phytosanitary in agricultural production, and in the trade of plants and plant products in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

The competent authority in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary shall widely disseminate, guide the implementation, and integrate plant protection techniques to farmers, farm owners, and local communities on all measures for preventing and controlling pests that destroy plants and crops, and preventing losses in the agricultural production system.

#### Article 52 .-

Procedures for managing phytosanitary information and dissemination related to plant protection measures and phytosanitary measures shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

### Chapter 11
### Obligations of Operators

#### Article 53 .-

Any physical or legal person operating within the framework of the plant protection and phytosanitary measures system or conducting activities related to plants, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection shall be responsible for all their activities and shall facilitate all activities of educational guidance, inspection, and law enforcement by competent phytosanitary inspectors in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

#### Article 54 .-

Operators shall record in the record book of plant protection and phytosanitary services all information and data related to their operations, regarding the implemented plant protection or phytosanitary services, types of plant protection products used, and safety measures implemented in their service provision operations under the provisions of this Law, for tracking and inspection by competent phytosanitary inspectors.

The format of the record book of plant protection and phytosanitary services shall be determined by the decision of the head of the competent authority in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 55 .-

Operators holding permits to provide services within the framework of relevant plant protection and phytosanitary measures system under the provisions of this Law shall have the following obligations:

- Implement in accordance with the orders, conditions, and validity period specified in the permit.

- Be legally responsible under this Law for all operations of their branches and activities of their employees regarding offenses or violations of the provisions of this Law arising from relevant operations.

- Be responsible for the spread of pests or other risk hazards occurring during plant protection or phytosanitary service operations.

- Have technical staff recognized by the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries to be directly responsible for the technical framework in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

- Request prior approval for the transfer of ownership of relevant permits.

- Implement other relevant regulations of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 56 .-

Any physical or legal person requesting to operate relevant service provision and other operations within the framework of the plant protection and phytosanitary measures system under the provisions of this Law shall have the obligation to pay public service fees for each request.

Public service fees based on types of operations and types of plant protection and phytosanitary services shall be determined by joint Prakas between the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries and the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

### Chapter 12
### Phytosanitary Inspectors and Law Enforcement Promotion

#### Article 57 .-

The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries shall promote the enforcement of this Law through the competent authority in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary to ensure the prevention and elimination of the spread of all types of pests and toxic substances in agricultural products and food items of plant origin.

Phytosanitary inspectors of the competent authority in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary shall be appointed by the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries at the request of the head of their competent authority to fulfill the role and duties in promoting the enforcement of this Law.

#### Article 58 .-

The competent authority in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary has the competence to monitor, research, and inspect all activities related to plant resources, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection within their jurisdiction to prevent, control the spread and infestation of pests, eliminate pest risks, and promote the enforcement of this Law at the following locations:

- Regional phytosanitary branch offices and phytosanitary quarantine stations.

- Vegetation cover areas, agricultural production areas, farm premises, residential areas, all types of botanical gardens practicing crop production or arboriculture.

- Locations for buying, selling, storing all types of plant protection products, plant goods, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection.

- Research institutions, laboratories, experimental stations, and service providers related to plant protection measures and phytosanitary measures.

- Parking areas for all forms of transportation and means of storing plant goods, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection.

- Other locations in the Kingdom of Cambodia involving activities related to plant resources and plant products, or locations where there is a risk or suspicion of pest risk, or suspicion of related offenses as provided in this Law.

#### Article 59 .-

In operations to promote law enforcement, competent phytosanitary inspectors shall have the following rights and duties:

1- Require physical or legal persons conducting activities related to plants, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection to provide information and present certificates, permits, and relevant documents in their activities.

2- Take samples of plant protection products, plant parts, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection for technical evaluation and pest risk analysis.

3- Temporarily revoke certificates, permits, or relevant documents and seize evidence in case the holder of such certificate or permit commits an offense under this Law.

4- Take measures to temporarily suspend all relevant activities within the framework of implementing plant protection measures and phytosanitary measures.

5- Prepare and complete case files of offenses, take measures to resolve offenses in accordance with this Law, and compile case files of offenses for submission to the court according to procedures.

6- Fulfill other duties within the framework of promoting the enforcement of this Law.

#### Article 60 .-

Phytosanitary inspectors shall be granted the status of judicial police officers as provided in this Law, and shall act in accordance with the Code of Criminal Procedure of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

The granting of judicial police status to phytosanitary inspectors shall be determined by joint Prakas between the Minister of the Ministry of Justice and the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 61 .-

During law enforcement operations, phytosanitary inspectors shall wear uniforms, insignia, and rank badges, accompanied by mission orders from the competent authority in charge of promoting the enforcement of this Law, and shall have the right to request assistance from relevant competent authorities to join the mission.

Uniforms, insignia, and rank badges of phytosanitary inspectors shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 62 .-

During law enforcement operations, in case of clear evidence or based on results of pest risk analysis regarding any activities and operations that pose a risk of pest spread, competent phytosanitary inspectors shall have the right to take measures to temporarily suspend those activities or operations to make an immediate report and request a decision from the head of their competent authority.

Based on the situation and size of the immediate risk, phytosanitary inspectors have the right to take phytosanitary measures on-site, including pest elimination treatment, destruction, declaring the goods as unsellable or undistributable, as necessary.

#### Article 63 .-

In operations to promote the enforcement of this Law, in case the identity of the offender is unknown, the competent authority in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary shall request the competent prosecutor to destroy the risky evidence in accordance with phytosanitary measures or confiscate the evidence of the offense as state property in accordance with the Code of Criminal Procedure of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

All expenses for taking phytosanitary measures on evidence of offenses shall be borne by the state.

#### Article 64 .-

Any person who is dissatisfied with any measure taken by competent phytosanitary inspectors has the right to file a formal written complaint to the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries within 30 (thirty) days from the date of receiving the inspection report of the competent phytosanitary inspectors.

The Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries shall establish an investigation mechanism for complaints against measures taken by phytosanitary inspectors, and shall issue a decision on the complaint within a period not exceeding 60 (sixty) days from the date of receiving the complaint letter.

In case that person is dissatisfied with the decision of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, that person has the right to appeal to other mechanisms of the Royal Government or appeal to court according to procedures.

#### Article 65 .-

In case a physical or legal person conducting relevant operations within the framework of plant protection and phytosanitary measures causes the spread and infestation of pests that pose a danger to the agro-ecosystem of the agricultural production area and bring serious impacts to the public interest and local communities, the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries has the right to file a lawsuit to demand compensation from the offender.

The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries shall evaluate the impact and size of damage for claiming compensation from the offender, or in case of necessity, with the participation of evaluation from representatives of relevant inter-ministries at the request of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

Expenses for the impact and damage evaluation process shall be borne by the offending physical or legal person, which shall be included in the demanded compensation.

#### Article 66 .-

Procedures and forms for phytosanitary inspectors to use in law enforcement shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

### Chapter 13
### Penal Provisions

#### Article 67 .-

Sanctions under this Law include administrative sanctions and penal sanctions.

Administrative sanctions include:

- Written warning

- Suspension or revocation of permits, certificates, or relevant documents issued under the provisions of this Law

- Temporary suspension of activities.

Penal sanctions include:

- Transaction fine (transitional fine)

- Fine

- Imprisonment.

#### Article 68 .-

Written warning, suspension, revocation of certificates, permits, or relevant documents shall be under the competence of the competent authority in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

Transaction fines for offenses as provided in Article 70, Article 71, and Article 72 of this Law shall be under the competence of competent phytosanitary inspectors of the competent authority in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

Payment of transaction fines shall extinguish the criminal action. In case the offender refuses to pay the transaction fine, competent phytosanitary inspectors shall compile the case file to be forwarded to the competent court according to procedures.

The forms and procedures of transaction fines shall be determined by joint Prakas between the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries and the Minister of the Ministry of Justice.

Payment of transaction fines, management of transaction fine receipts, and management of revenue obtained from transaction fines for offenses under the provisions of this Law shall be determined by joint Prakas between the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries and the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 69 .-

A written warning shall be issued to any physical or legal person conducting activities related to plants, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection who commits any of the following acts:

1- Fail to report or provide immediate notification to the nearest competent authority in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary regarding the occurrence, spread, or outbreak of pests on plants and plant resources within their farm, allowing it to cause spread, destruction, or damage to the public interest or the interest of others in the locality.

2- Refuse to implement educational guidelines of the competent authority in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary regarding principles for plant protection measures or guidelines on participating in public mobilization campaigns in the locality to cooperate in taking prevention and control measures against the spread and eradication of pests destroying plants and plant resources in the locality.

3- Refuse to implement notifications of competent phytosanitary inspectors requiring immediate measures to prevent the spread of pests, eradicate pests, or implement phytosanitary measures within their farms.

4- Refuse to answer questions or facilitate phytosanitary inspection, or refuse to cooperate in implementing phytosanitary measures or fulfilling the law enforcement duties of competent phytosanitary inspectors.

5- Fail to perform obligations as owners of plant resources, owners of all types of locations related to plants and plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection that must implement phytosanitary measures in accordance with this Law.

In case of recidivism, the physical or legal person committing the above acts may have their permits, certificates, or relevant documents issued under the provisions of this Law suspended, revoked, or canceled.

#### Article 70 .-

A transaction fine of 1,000,000 (one million) Riels to 5,000,000 (five million) Riels shall be imposed on any physical or legal person committing any of the following acts:

1- Lack of record book of plant protection and phytosanitary services or lack of records on relevant activities or data of service operations required by this Law.

2- Fail to implement obligations as operators as follows:

2.1- Fail to implement conditions, regulations, and validity period specified in the certificate or permit.

2.2- Fail to request prior approval for the transfer of ownership of relevant certificates or permits.

2.3- Fail to be responsible for all operations of their branches and activities of their employees causing offenses or violations of the provisions of this Law.

2.4- Fail to be responsible for the spread of pests or other risk hazards occurring during relevant operations.

2.5- Lack of recognized technical staff to be directly responsible for the technical framework in relevant service provision operations.

3- Fail to notify or send declaration to competent phytosanitary inspectors at relevant gateways regarding the import, export, or transit of plant goods, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection.

In case of having been subject to a transaction fine once already and continuing to commit the offense as provided in point 1 above, the person shall be subject to double the amount of the previous transaction fine.

In case a physical or legal person who has committed an offense as specified in the above paragraph does not change their behavior, the phytosanitary inspector shall report to the specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries and request a decision to suspend or revoke the permit or certificate.

#### Article 107.-

A transitional fine of 5,000,000 (five million) Riels to 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels shall be imposed on any physical or legal person who commits any of the following acts:

1- Providing plant protection services or phytosanitary services without a permit under the provisions of this Law.

2- Possessing plant protection products or providing plant protection services or phytosanitary services using plant protection products that have not undergone study, evaluation, or do not have an evaluation from the competent specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary.

3- Hiring services or directly transporting goods, plants, plant products, and other goods subject to phytosanitary inspection in violation of phytosanitary measures and prohibition orders in areas declared as pest outbreak areas or prohibited areas.

4- Hiring, using services, or directly advertising the implementation of plant protection measures or phytosanitary measures regarding the use of plant protection products and other products that are effective in the implementation of plant protection measures, without evaluation from the specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary, or in violation of requirements under the provisions of this Law.

5- Refusing to terminate operations of providing related services within the framework of plant protection measures and phytosanitary measures, in violation of a temporary suspension decision of the competent phytosanitary inspector.

6- Circulating plants, plant products, and goods subject to phytosanitary inspection whose packaging or transport means are torn or leaky, without permission for re-inspection and re-implementation of phytosanitary measures from the competent phytosanitary inspector.

7- Hiring, using services, or directly transporting plant goods, plant products, and goods subject to phytosanitary inspection, by evading obligations in plant protection and phytosanitary after receiving the phytosanitary certificate.

8- Hiring, using services, or directly transporting plant goods, plant products, and goods subject to phytosanitary inspection that cause the spread of phytosanitary pests or dangerous pests along transport routes.

In case of having already received a transitional fine once but continuing to commit the offense as specified in Paragraph 1 above, a transitional fine of double the first transitional fine shall be imposed.

In case a physical or legal person who has committed an offense as specified in the above paragraph does not change their behavior, the phytosanitary inspector shall report to the specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries and request a decision to suspend or revoke the permit or certificate.

#### Article 108.-

A transitional fine of 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels to 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels shall be imposed on any physical or legal person who commits any of the following acts:

1- Hiring, using services, or directly disseminating information about all types of pests, or information regarding the outbreak or spread of pests without permission or contrary to the information released by the specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary.

2- Importing or exporting plants, plant products, and goods subject to phytosanitary inspection without a phytosanitary certificate, or contrary to the requirements under the provisions of this Law.

3- Possessing in advance or trading phytosanitary certificate documents without goods or without sufficient goods for import or export of goods subject to phytosanitary inspection contrary to the requirements under the provisions of this Law.

4- Hiring, using services, or directly transporting in transit through the Kingdom of Cambodia plant goods, plant products, and goods subject to phytosanitary inspection that pose a risk of spreading phytosanitary pests or dangerous pests.

5- Trading or cultivating crop varieties defined in the list of crop varieties under conditions without a permit from the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

6- Conducting crop production, processing, trading, circulating in the market, and transporting all types of plant goods, plant products, and goods subject to phytosanitary inspection, knowing that pests are present or spreading.

7- Conducting crop production in violation of prohibitions in implementing plant protection measures that cause danger to humans, animals, natural safety, and ecosystems.

8- Harvesting crop products from cultivation sites to circulate in the market or use as food for humans, animal feed, or as raw materials in food processing, with pesticide residues exceeding the maximum residue limits (MRL) or containing substances that cause adverse effects on health.

9- Exporting, importing, storing, transporting, displaying for sale, and using crop seeds contaminated with high levels of pests or contaminated with phytosanitary pests or other prohibited pests.

In case of having already received a transitional fine once but continuing to commit the offense as specified in Paragraph 1 above, a transitional fine of double the first transitional fine shall be imposed.

In case a physical or legal person who has committed an offense as specified in the above paragraph does not change their behavior, the phytosanitary inspector shall report to the specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries and request a decision to suspend or revoke the permit or certificate.

#### Article 109.-

Shall be punished by imprisonment from 1 (one) year to 5 (five) years and a fine of 25,000,000 (twenty-five million) Riels to 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels for any physical or legal person who commits any of the following acts:

1- Cultivating, importing, possessing, or distributing pathogenic organisms, phytosanitary pests, and invasive introduced species of plants or animals that pose a phytosanitary risk to plants, plant products, and ecosystems in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

2- Sending pest samples abroad that pose a risk to national interests.

3- Disseminating phytosanitary information contrary to official information released by the specialized unit in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary that causes adverse effects on national interests.

4- Intentionally carrying out activities related to plant protection and phytosanitary measures that create conditions for or cause the spread of any pest listed in the prohibited pest list that causes loss of public interest or the interest of others.

5- Intentionally importing into the Kingdom of Cambodia any pest or soil containing pests or posing a pest risk.

6- Intentionally discarding pests or goods contaminated with pests in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 110.-

The court may declare one or more additional penalties as specified in Article 53 (Types of additional penalties) and Article 168 (Additional penalties applicable to legal persons) of the Penal Code for offenses specified in Article 109 of this Law.

### Chapter 14
### Transitional Provisions

#### Article 111.-

All legal instruments related to plant protection and phytosanitary that are currently in force and not contrary to this Law shall continue to be in force until they are replaced by new laws and legal instruments in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

#### Article 112.-

Physical or legal persons possessing valid certificates or permits in the framework of plant protection and phytosanitary before this Law comes into force shall complete their related operations and apply for new permits in accordance with the provisions of this Law within a period of not more than 12 (twelve) months from the date this Law enters into force.

Any provisions that are contrary to this Law shall be abrogated.

### Annex to the Law on Plant Protection and Phytosanitary
### Glossary

1- **Plants Protection**: refers to activities and operations of controlling all types of pests that destroy plants and crop products through prevention, formation, accumulation, spread, and infestation of pests, as well as the implementation of eradication measures, with the aim of protecting plant resources and reducing losses in agricultural production systems and reducing socio-economic impacts.

2- **Phytosanitary Inspection**: refers to research, investigation, inspection, sampling for analysis, and identification of pests on goods subject to phytosanitary inspection to determine the status of pest destruction.

3- **Treatment**: refers to technical operations to kill, inactivate, or remove pests, or to render pests incapable of reproduction and to terminate the life cycle of pests.

4- **Pest Risk Analysis**: refers to the process of evaluation based on biological, scientific, and economic evidence of pests to determine whether a pest should be regulated and the strength of phytosanitary measures to be enforced to prevent it.

5- **Quality Assurance**: refers to a comprehensive work system in agricultural production related to planning for quality assurance, quality control, quality evaluation, quality records, and improvement of the quality of agricultural products and plant protection products to ensure that those products and plant protection services meet the specified quality standards with trust.

6- **Good Agricultural Practices-GAP**: refers to cultivation operations or crop production of all kinds that implement plant protection measures or use appropriate plant protection products technically under actual conditions to manage pests effectively while leaving pesticide residues or toxic substances causing danger to human, animal, and environmental health at a minimum.

7- **Maximum Residue Limit-MRL**: refers to the component or concentration of residue permitted by law or recognized of toxic substance residues remaining on

in or on food, agricultural products of plant origin, animal feed or animal products cultivated or raised, as a result of using chemical substances or substances causing danger to health.

8- **Organic farming**: refers to crop and animal production methods involving the non-use of chemical substances such as pesticides, fertilizers, antibiotics, hormones, and genetically modified organisms (GMOs), etc., which do not comply with organic standards.

9- **Phytosanitary Emergency**: refers to phytosanitary measures established and implemented to respond immediately to any new urgent phytosanitary situation or unexpected situation which can be temporary or complete measures.

10- **Border Check Point**: refers to locations of competent authorities to inspect activities of exit, entry, and transit across the borders of the Kingdom of Cambodia under the following forms:
- As international, and activities of travelers, transport means, and all types of goods at international airports, international ports within the country.
- As international bilateral border areas, and activities of travelers, transport means, and all types of goods at international border gates located on the border line.
- As bilateral border area character, and activities of travelers, transport means, and all types of goods at bilateral border gates located on the border line in accordance with bilateral agreements between bordering countries and the Kingdom of Cambodia.
- As border area character and activities of travelers who are permanent residents in that border area, transport means, and all types of goods at border gates located on the border line in accordance with agreements between bordering countries and the Kingdom of Cambodia.
- Via seaports, freshwater ports, and activities of travelers, transport means, and all types of goods at seaport gates, freshwater port gates located in the country.

11- **Pest Free Area**: refers to an area in which a specific pest does not occur as demonstrated by scientific evidence and in which, this status is officially maintained.

12- Consignment: refers to the quantity of plants, plant products and/or other goods of one or more types being moved from one country to another, subject to phytosanitary inspection requirements and phytosanitary status certification through a single phytosanitary certificate.

13- Regulated Article Consignment: refers to a consignment that is on the regulated list subject to phytosanitary inspection, which has the potential to spread pests or act as a pathway/harbor for pests.

14- Quarantine Goods: refers to all plants, plant products and other regulated articles/materials (Regulated Articles/Materials) that are subject to phytosanitary inspection, including packaging materials, soil liners or parts of soil attached to roots or any part of plants, mixtures of substances, minerals and organisms supporting plants, living or dead pests or beneficial organisms, and all organisms capable of transmitting and spreading pests, and other non-plant origin objects but which can create a habitat for pests in commercial activities related to import, export, transit transport, or domestic circulation.

15- Plants Resources: refers to all classes of plants, species, genetic families, and biological forms of the global plant classification system. Based on origin, reproduction, and habitat, plant resources include plant resources of natural origin and natural reproduction, and plants originating from artificial breeding through all genetic technologies, or originating from cultivation, farming, or crop production.

16- Fumigation: refers to treatment using toxic gas to kill pests.

17- Phytosanitary Procedure: refers to the official methodology for the implementation of phytosanitary measures, including inspection, testing, surveillance, or treatment related to regulated pests.

18- National Phytosanitary Clearing House: refers to the work point or national contact center that manages, stores, and shares phytosanitary information and database systems through networking the work of the specialized entity for phytosanitary prevention and protection performing duties at the gates of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

19- Pest Library: refers to the place for keeping samples of all types of pests for study, research, and use as verification samples in pest risk analysis.

20- Invasive Alien Species: refers to species or genetic families of plant or animal species that have entered a new environment outside their natural distribution range in the past or present, causing spread, invasion, and colonization of the original habitats of native plant communities. Invasive alien species include all parts of germplasm, gametes, seeds, eggs, plant propagules (Propagules), or parts of organisms of these species that can survive and reproduce sequentially and cause invasion.

21- Restricted Plant: refers to plant species that are important for use in production, medical pharmaceuticals for disease treatment, or are important for use in a specific technology, but those plant species may pose risks of danger to humans, animals, and the environment, which can be permitted for cultivation for specific important uses as specified in this Law.

22- Plants Product: refers to products originating from plants that have not yet been processed or are semi-processed, including wood products, processed wood products, forest by-products, and plant derivative products or other products originating from all types of plants that are subject to phytosanitary inspection under the provisions of this Law.

23- Plants Protection Product: refers to one or more active chemical substances, biological control agents (Biological Control Agent), microbial agents (Microbial), semi-chemical agents (Semi Chemical), organisms (Organism), or genetically modified organisms (Genetic Modified Organism) that are used as plant protection products for the purpose of plant protection against all destructive organisms, against decay and infestation by pests, preventing impacts on the growth process of plants, storing plant products, eliminating weeds, repelling or killing pests, eliminating plant disease transmission agents, promoting plant growth, regulating plant physiological functions, increasing productivity and crop yields for soil quality management, and for preserving the quality of wood and processed wood products.

24- Plants Pest: refers to all types of pests that cause destruction and disturbance or threats to plant resources, plants, plant products, or other objects originating from plants.

25- Phytosanitary (Phytosanitary/Plants Quarantine): refers to all activities created to prevent the occurrence and/or spread of phytosanitary pests or to ensure the permanent eradication of pests.

26- Phytosanitary Inspection Officer: refers to the officer appointed to perform phytosanitary inspection work.

27- Technical Justification: refers to the technical interpretation showing the correctness and appropriateness that can be used in accordance with the determination of international phytosanitary standards or phytosanitary requirements of the importing country with the same efficiency.

28- Plants: refers to plants of a specific type, whether originating naturally, artificially or as crops, along with their parts (seeds, roots, tubers, stems, bark, branches, leaves, flowers, mistletoe, etc.) and plant germplasm or plant genetic material, as well as plant samples at all stages of growth of all types, species and genetic families included under the classification of plant resources.

29- Phytosanitary Requirement: refers to the requirement of the phytosanitary authority of each country for the purpose of preventing the introduction, spread and infestation of pests into the country through the import of goods subject to phytosanitary inspection.

30- Phytosanitary Certificate: refers to the certificate issued by the specialized entity in charge of plant protection and phytosanitary of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries after conducting pest risk analysis on the goods subject to phytosanitary inspection, including the import phytosanitary certificate for import into the Kingdom of Cambodia, the phytosanitary certificate for export or re-export from the Kingdom of Cambodia in accordance with the template specified in the International Plant Protection Convention. The phytosanitary certificate is a necessary requirement for operators who perform import, export and transit transport operations through the Kingdom of Cambodia of goods subject to phytosanitary inspection.

31- Phytosanitary Measure: refers to all legal norms, regulations or official procedures and activities implemented for the purpose of preventing the occurrence, transmission and/or spread of phytosanitary pests or reducing the economic impact caused by regulated non-phytosanitary pests.

32- Integrated Pest Management-IPM: refers to a pest management system in crop production by using appropriate techniques based on requirements to reduce the presence of pests and keep them at the lowest level that does not pose an economic risk.

33- Food: refers to agricultural crop products originating from plants, forest by-products or products that are fresh products or primary processed products, and must be used or further processed as food for humans or feed for animals.

34- Plants Quarantine Station: refers to the official station that must be established by this Law for keeping plants or plant products in phytosanitary confinement for observation, further research, testing, or treatment.

35- Phytosanitary Security: refers to the maintenance of goods and the prevention of pest transmission and damage by regulated pests to goods, through the implementation of appropriate phytosanitary measures.

36- Quarantine Pest: refers to a pest that has never been present in the Kingdom of Cambodia or is present in a specific location but has not yet spread, and has been or is being subject to eradication measures or has been permanently eradicated.

37- Regulated Pest: refers to a quarantine pest or a regulated non-quarantine pest.

38- Regulated Non-Quarantine Pest: refers to a non-quarantine pest present in plants for planting and having an impact on the intended use of those plants by causing unacceptable economic impacts, and which is the reason why the importing contracting party has regulated it in its territory.

39- Natural Enemy: refers to an organism that lives at the expense of the food and life of another organism in its natural habitat, which can help limit the population of that organism. Those organisms include plant pathogens, parasites, parasitoids, and predators, biological agents, such as carnivorous plants or parasitic plants.

40- Pest: refers to any species, strain or biotype of organism, including genetically modified organisms of plants, animals or pathogens, which must

be regulated as a pest that damages and invades all types of plants and plant resources. Pests also include invasive alien species (Invasive Alien Species) or harmful organisms (Harmful Organism) of plants or animals, which are species outside their natural distribution range in the past or present that cause harm to plant resources, plant products, or all types of crop products. The list of pests for phytosanitary management in the Kingdom of Cambodia is determined by the provisions of this Law.

41- Endemic Pest: refers to all types, species or lines of plants, animals or pathogens originating or having their natural distribution range within the territory of the Kingdom of Cambodia that can cause harm to plants, plant products, or all types of crop products.

42- Dangerous Pest: refers to pests that cause or can cause serious danger to society, economy, or cause serious destruction to plant resources.

43- National Plant Protection Organization-NPPO: refers to the specialized unit in charge of plant health protection and phytosanitary, or currently called the Department of Crop Protection, Sanitary and Phytosanitary, under the General Directorate of Agriculture of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, which was established in accordance with the International Plant Protection Convention (IPPC) to fulfill national and international framework duties in accordance with the terms of the Sanitary and Phytosanitary Agreement of the World Trade Organization (WTO) or requirements of the importing country that are guaranteed safe for consumers of plant products and ensure phytosanitary security.

44- International Plant Protection Convention (IPPC): refers to the International Plant Protection Convention, adopted at the 6th session of the Conference of the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO) in Rome in November 1951 and entered into force on April 3, 1952, with member parties of many states, and registered under number 1963 deposited at the United Nations on November 29, 1952. The Kingdom of Cambodia was recognized as an Adherence State Party to this Convention on June 10, 1952, after this Convention entered into force. The International Plant Protection Convention started to amend the text of the Convention in the session

the 20th session of the Conference of the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO) in Rome in November 1979, and then fully approved the amendment text in the 29th session of the Conference of the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO) in Rome in November 1997, where 90 contracting states provided their instruments of approval. The Kingdom of Cambodia, represented by the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries as the national focal point for the implementation of this Convention, provided its instrument of approval through Letter No. 3550 MAFF dated July 18, 2005. The International Plant Protection Convention aims to ensure effective common action to prevent the spread and introduction of pests of plants and plant products, and to promote appropriate measures for their control. Contracting parties under this Convention shall adopt the legal, technical and administrative frameworks specified in this Convention and supplementary agreements under this Convention.
