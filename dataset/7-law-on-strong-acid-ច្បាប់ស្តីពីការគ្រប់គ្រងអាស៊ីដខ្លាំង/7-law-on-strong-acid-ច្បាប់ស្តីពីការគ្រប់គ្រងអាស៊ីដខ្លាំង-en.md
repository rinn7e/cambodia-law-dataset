## Proclaims (ប្រកាសឱ្យប្រើ)

The Law on the Management of Strong Acid which was adopted by the National Assembly on November 08, 2011 during its 7th session of the 4th legislature, and which was approved by the Senate on its entire form and legal substance without any amendment on November 30, 2011 during its 10th plenary session of the 2nd legislature, and which has the full content as follows:

# Law

## On

### The Management of Strong Acid

### Chapter 1
### General Provisions

#### Article 1
This Law has the purpose to define the management and control of all types of strong acid that cause danger to human health and life.

#### Article 2
This Law aims to define the prevention, suppression, and crack down on offenses caused by the use of all types of strong acid, which cause danger to human health and life.

#### Article 3
This Law has the scope covering the production, processing, import, export, packaging, transportation, possession, distribution, sale, purchase, storage, and use of all types of strong acid that cause danger to human health and life in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 4
Strong acid in this Law refers to acids, raw materials, and acidic ingredients, whether liquid or solid mixtures, with a pH equal to or below 3 (three), which cause danger to human health and life, as listed in the Annex of this Law, except for sulfuric acid which is a liquid mixture (solution) and has a concentration or density below 33% used for battery filling. The Annex of this Law can be amended by Sub-decree.

### Chapter 2
### Competent Authority for Managing Strong Acid

#### Article 5
The production, processing, import, export, packaging, transportation, possession, distribution, sale, purchase, storage, and use of all types of strong acid must have permission from the competent authority or ministry/department, or as defined by other legal regulations.

**Article 6._**

The issuance of licenses or permits related to the import, export, production, and processing of all types of strong acid is the competence of the Ministry of Industry, Mines and Energy.

For strong acid listed in the annex table, which is to be used for the health sector, it is the competence of the Ministry of Health.

**Article 7._**

The formalities and conditions of sale, purchase, storage, distribution, transportation, packaging, carrying, and use of all types of strong acid shall be determined by sub-decree.

**Article 8._**

Physical or legal entities authorized by law to use all types of strong acid must use those strong acids for the right target and must respect technical standards at the highest level of safety.

**Article 9._**

All types of strong acid, as listed in the annex table of this law, shall be permitted for use only for the purpose of serving the sectors of industry, health, agriculture, handicrafts, technology, science, scientific research, experimentation, testing, and other fields that serve economic and social interests and support legitimate daily livelihoods.

### Chapter III

### Protection of Victims of Strong Acid

**Article 10._**

In the event that there is a victim of strong acid, the relevant competent authorities must cooperate to send the victim for emergency rescue to the nearest health center, public hospital, or other treatment facility.

**Article 11._**

Health centers, public hospitals, or other public treatment facilities must provide rescue and treatment to victims of strong acid free of charge.

The determination of the level of disability of victims of strong acid is the competence of a specialized committee established by the Ministry of Health and in accordance with the provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure.

The State shall provide legal aid to victims of strong acid.

### Article 12

The protection, rehabilitation, and integration of strong acid victims into the community is the competence of the Ministry of Social Affairs, Veterans and Youth Rehabilitation.

### Article 13

The State encourages the participation of philanthropists, associations, national and international non-governmental organizations, and the private sector in helping to support victims of strong acid.

## Chapter 4
### Penal Provisions

### Article 14

Acts of manufacturing, processing, importing, exporting, packaging, transporting, carrying, distributing, selling, buying, storing, and using all types of strong acid without a license or permit, or failing to comply with the provisions of other legal regulations, shall be punished as follows:

1- A fine of 500,000 (five hundred thousand) Riels to 1,500,000 (one million five hundred thousand) Riels, as well as the additional penalty of confiscation of the strong acid, in cases where the strong acid weighs less than 500 (five hundred) grams.

2- A fine of 1,500,000 (one million five hundred thousand) Riels to 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels, as well as the additional penalty of confiscation of the strong acid, in cases where the strong acid weighs 500 (five hundred) grams or more.

### Article 15

Legal entities may be declared criminally responsible under the conditions set forth in Article 42 (Criminal responsibility of legal entities) of the Criminal Code for the offenses stated in Article 14 above.

Legal entities shall be punished with a fine of 5,000,000 (five million) Riels to 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels in cases where the strong acid weighs less than 500 (five hundred) grams.

Legal entities shall be punished by a fine of 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels to 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels in the event that the strong acid has a weight of 500 (five hundred) grams or more.

Legal entities shall also be subject to any one or more additional penalties as provided in Article 168 (Additional Penalties Applicable to Legal Entities) of the Criminal Code.

#### Article 16._

The act of intentional murder by using strong acid shall be punished by imprisonment from 15 (fifteen) years to 30 (thirty) years.

Imprisonment for life shall be imposed in the event that the above felony is committed with:

1- Premeditation or ambush.

2- Torture or acts of cruelty before or during the commission of the murder.

For the above felony, the additional penalties provided in Article 206 (Types and Duration of Additional Penalties) of the Criminal Code may be declared.

#### Article 17._

The act of causing the death of another person through unintentional act, negligence, carelessness, recklessness or breach of security or safety obligations required by law or other regulatory provisions in the management of strong acid shall be punished by imprisonment from 1 (one) year to 5 (five) years and a fine of 2,000,000 (two million) Riels to 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels.

For the above offense, the additional penalties provided in Article 208 (Types and Duration of Additional Penalties) of the Criminal Code may be declared.

#### Article 18._

Legal entities may be declared criminally responsible under the conditions provided in Article 42 (Criminal Responsibility of Legal Entities) of the Criminal Code for the offense provided in Article 17 above.

Legal entities shall be punished by a fine of 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels to 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels, as well as any one or more additional penalties as provided in Article 168 (Additional Penalties Applicable to Legal Entities) of the Criminal Code.

### Article 19.-

Torture and acts of cruelty using strong acid committed against any person shall be punished by imprisonment from 10 (ten) years to 20 (twenty) years.

If the commission of the above crime leads to amputation or permanent disability of the victim, it shall be punished by imprisonment from 15 (fifteen) years to 25 (twenty-five) years.

If the commission of the above crime causes the death of the victim without intent to kill or leads to the suicide of this person, it shall be punished by imprisonment from 20 (twenty) years to 30 (thirty) years.

For the above crime, additional punishments as provided in Article 216 (Additional Punishments: Types and Duration) of the Penal Code may be pronounced.

### Article 20.-

Intentional violence against others using strong acid shall be punished by imprisonment from 2 (two) years to 5 (five) years and a fine of 4,000,000 (four million) Riels to 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels.

If the commission of this act leads to amputation or permanent disability of the victim, it shall be punished by imprisonment from 5 (five) years to 10 (ten) years.

If this act causes the death of the victim without intent to kill, it shall be punished by imprisonment from 10 (ten) years to 20 (twenty) years.

For the above offense, additional punishments as provided in Article 229 (Additional Punishments: Types and Duration) of the Penal Code may be pronounced.

An attempt to commit a misdemeanor as provided in the first paragraph of this Article shall be punished in the same manner as the misdemeanor itself.

### Article 21.-

An act causing injury by negligence, recklessness, carelessness, or breach of security obligations or duty of care required by law or other legal instruments in the management of strong acid, shall be punished by imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 1 (one) year and a fine of 200,000 (two hundred thousand) Riels to 2,000,000 (two million) Riels.

It shall be punished by imprisonment from 6 (six) months to 3 (three) years and a fine of

1,000,000 (one million) Riels to 6,000,000 (six million) Riels when this act leads to amputation of limbs or permanent disability of the victim.

For the above offense, additional penalties as provided in Article 237 (Types and duration of additional penalties) of the Penal Code may be declared.

#### Article 22

A legal entity may be declared criminally responsible under the conditions set forth in Article 42 (Criminal responsibility of legal entities) of the Penal Code for the offense as set forth in Article 21 above.

The legal entity shall be punished by a fine from 5,000,000 (five million) Riels to 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels, as well as any one or more of the additional penalties as set forth in Article 368 (Criminal responsibility of legal entities) of the Penal Code.

#### Article 23

The act of producing food or permitting the production of food using strong acid in violation of technical standards shall be punished by imprisonment from 1 (one) year to 5 (five) years and a fine from 2,000,000 (two million) Riels to 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels.

#### Article 24

A legal entity may be declared criminally responsible under the conditions set forth in Article 42 (Criminal responsibility of legal entities) of the Penal Code for the offense as set forth in Article 23 above.

The legal entity shall be punished by a fine from 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels to 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels, as well as any one or more of the additional penalties as set forth in Article 168 (Additional penalties applicable to legal entities) of the Penal Code.

### Chapter 5
### Transitional Provisions

#### Article 25

After this law enters into force, any physical or legal person who has been and is conducting strong acid business activities without permission, and who intends to continue conducting strong acid business activities, must complete the procedures to request permission to conduct business activities in accordance with the law in force.

#### Article 26._

The provisions as stated in Article 14 of this Law shall be implemented after the sub-decree as defined in Article 7 of this Law enters into force.

### Chapter 6

### Final Provisions

#### Article 27._

Any provisions contrary to this Law shall be considered null and void to the extent of their contradiction to this Law, from the date this Law is implemented.
