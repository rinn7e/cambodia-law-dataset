# PROMULGATE

The Law on Prisons, which was adopted by the National Assembly on November 07, 2011 at the 7th session of its 4th legislature, and which was reviewed and approved by the Senate as to its form and substance without any amendment on November 30, 2011 at its 10th plenary session of the 2nd legislature, the full text of which is as follows:

# LAW ON PRISONS

# Chapter 1
# General Provisions

#### Article 1..
This law has the objective to determine the management of prisons in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 2..
This law has the following target :

- To educate, reform, and rehabilitate in order to reintegrate detainees into society and prevent re-offending.

- Manage detainees to ensure security, safety, health, and humanity in accordance with international principles and United Nations rules for the protection of minors deprived of their liberty.

#### Article 3..
This law has the scope of application to all prisons in the Kingdom of Cambodia, except for military prisons or detention places of judicial police or other detention centers determined by other laws.

#### Article 4..
The key terminologies used in this law have the following definitions:

**Prisons** refers to places used for detaining detainees according to detention warrants issued by judicial authorities, including detention centers and reformatory centers.

**Minors** refers to any individuals who are aged from 14 years old and above to under 18 years old.

**Detainees** refers to the accused, defendants or convicted persons or sentenced persons who are sent for detention in prison according to detention warrants issued by judicial authorities.

**Prison officials** refers to officials who are within the framework of prison officials.

**Prison medical officials** refers to officials who are appointed to provide health care services to detainees in prison and who are recognized or registered as doctors or medical officials at the Ministry of Health.

**Visitors** refers to persons who are permitted to enter into prison for visiting or providing assistance to detainees.

**Official visitors** refers to public officials or representatives of individuals from various organizations who have received permission to conduct monitoring visits to various prisons or provide assistance to detainees.

**National Preventive Mechanism** refers to an independent mechanism, in accordance with the Optional Protocol to the Convention against Torture and Other Cruel, Inhuman or Degrading Treatment or Punishment.

# Chapter 2
# Management Competence

#### Article 5..
The Ministry of Interior shall be the competent institution in managing prisons in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 6..
The General Department of Prisons shall be the secretariat for the Ministry of Interior in managing prisons. The organization and functioning of the General Department of Prisons shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 7..
Prison officials shall be under a separate statute which shall be determined by Royal Decree, in accordance with the common statute of civil servants of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 8..
Prison officials shall have uniforms, rank insignia, equipment, tools and appropriate protective materials.

#### Article 9..
The Ministry of Interior shall delegate functions to the Capital and Provincial Administrations for the management of capital and provincial prisons.

Capital and provincial prisons are under the management of the Capital and Provincial Board of Governors.

# Chapter 3
# Prison Infrastructure

#### Article 10..
Prison infrastructure shall comply with minimum standards for prison construction as determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

#### Article 11..
Detention buildings and rooms shall have adequate light, natural ventilation, sanitation rooms, water supply systems and electricity for use.

#### Article 12..
Prisons shall have health posts, infirmaries for detainees, and shall have prison medical officials on duty regularly.

Prison health services shall be under the coverage of the Ministry of Health.

#### Article 13..
Prisons shall have places for religious worship, libraries, and buildings for education and vocational training.

#### Article 14..
Prisons shall organize exercise areas for detainees.

#### Article 15..
Prisons shall have work spaces and official residences for prison officials during the performance of their office.

#### Article 16..
The creation, naming, determination of location, determination of the number of detainees, renaming, relocation and dissolution of prisons shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

# Chapter 4
# Detention

#### Article 17..
Admission or detention of any person into prison shall be possible only if there is a detention warrant issued by the judicial authority.

#### Article 18..
Before admitting a detainee into prison, the prison director or assigned prison official shall verify the identity of the detainee correctly according to the detention warrant issued by the judicial authority.

#### Article 19..
Prison medical officials shall examine the health of the detainee before admitting them into prison, and shall record health status information of the detainee in the registry.

#### Article 20..
After admitting a detainee into prison, prison officials shall instruct the detainee about the law, internal regulations of the prison, the right to seek legal representation, the right to notify family or relatives or other relevant institutions about the detention and prison location, as well as other rights and duties as stated in this Law.

In the case where the detainee is a foreigner, the prison director shall allow the detainee to notify their embassy or consulate or representative mission. If the detainee is unable to notify them, the prison director shall perform this task.

After receiving these instructions, the detainee shall provide fingerprints and have their photo attached or scanned into the computer for permanent record in the file.

#### Article 21..
Each prison shall have a registry of detainees that certifies the identity of the detainees and is visaed by a representative of the prosecution office on every page.

For female detainees and minor detainees, there shall be separate registries, and special attention shall be paid to the management of these two types of detainees.

#### Article 22..
The registry of detainees shall be examined by a representative of the prosecution office and shall be provided to judges for examination upon request, as well as shown to judicial police officers who perform duties as assigned by judicial authorities.

#### Article 23..
Every prison shall create a personal file for each individual detainee, including minor detainees. All information and relevant documents of the detainee shall be kept confidential in the personal file.

#### Article 24..
Detainees shall be examined and have their health assessed by prison medical officials. Information regarding scars, mental illness, physical disability or other relevant information shall be recorded in the detainee's file.

For participation in labor in prison, during the initial health assessment, prison medical officials shall determine physical capacity and record it in the personal file of the convict, paying attention to the specific and different needs of women and minors.

#### Article 25..
Any torture, inhuman act or use of various instruments to punish detainees shall be prohibited, except for handcuffs that are permitted under Article 50 of this Law.

#### Article 26..
Detainees who are accused, defendants or convicted persons shall be separated from detainees who are sentenced persons.

Female detainees shall be separated from male detainees.

Minor detainees shall be separated from adult detainees.

Detainees with infectious diseases shall be separated from other detainees.

#### Article 27..
After admitting a detainee into prison, the prison director shall arrange for an assessment to determine security classification to ensure security, safety and effectiveness of prison management.

# Chapter 5
# Rights and Living Conditions of Detainees

#### Article 28..
Detainees have the right to complain to the prison director or to the Director General of the General Department of Prisons or to the Prosecutor or to the General Prosecutor attached to the Court of Appeal regarding abuse by other detainees or abuse by prison officials.

A detainee's complaint shall be made through prison officials or official visitors or relative visitors or the detainee's lawyer or a representative of the prosecution office.

Every complaint must be resolved and responded to within a reasonable timeframe.

No detainee shall be punished or discriminated against for having submitted a complaint or a request.

#### Article 29..
Detainees have the right to practice religion according to their beliefs and shall not be forced to practice any religion.

#### Article 30..
Detainees who are not required to work outside for legal or security reasons may be permitted to exercise daily.

#### Article 31..
Detainees who are foreigners have the right to contact their embassy or consulate or representative mission.

All communication between the detainee and lawyer shall be conducted in confidence.

#### Article 32..
Detainees have the right to receive and send correspondence. Correspondence of detainees shall be opened in the presence of an authorized prison official, except for correspondence from a lawyer.

A detainee's correspondence shall be confiscated if it relates to security, safety or public order, both inside and outside the prison.

#### Article 33..
Detainees shall receive living regimes as determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 34..
Providing food to detainees shall take into account nutritional requirements in accordance with health needs as certified by prison medical officials or according to their religious requirements.

Detainees may receive or purchase food with their own funds from external sources, but such food must be inspected by prison officials.

#### Article 35..
Detainees may be permitted to use their own funds to purchase materials or receive materials from family, relatives or from other sources, if there is approval and inspection from the prison director.

Detainees are not allowed to buy, give, lend or sell materials to other detainees.

#### Article 36..
Funds or valuable property personally held by a detainee shall be confiscated, correctly recorded in a registry and kept in a prison account, or handed over to family or relatives for safekeeping if there is consent from the detainee, which shall be confirmed by the detainee's signature or fingerprint.

In case of necessity and with approval from the prison director, the detainee may use funds from their account.

Funds and valuable property of the detainee that the prison is responsible for keeping shall be returned to the detainee upon release.

The detainee may keep medicine as their own property if there is approval from prison medical officials.

#### Article 37..
Detainees are allowed to receive news or important information through broadcasts of radio, television, magazines, newspapers and telephone communication with family as organized by the prison.

Detainees may be allowed to enter the prison library for study and research or may be allowed to borrow books for research in their detention room to increase their knowledge.

The prison shall notify families regarding any illness or death of a detainee, and vice versa.

#### Article 38..
Detainees who are ill shall receive daily health care services from prison medical officials.

Detainees who are seriously ill and require treatment at an external hospital upon the proposal of prison medical officials shall have permission from the competent prosecutor. In the event that a detainee is seriously ill and requires emergency rescue at an external hospital, the prison director shall send the patient to the hospital immediately upon the proposal of prison medical officials, and shall immediately notify the Director General of the General Department of Prisons, the Governor of the Capital or Provincial Board of Governors, the competent prosecutor and the family or relatives or closest friends upon the request of the detainee. After the treatment has improved, as certified by the hospital that provided the treatment, the detainee shall be returned to the prison.

Detainees who have mental illness or physical disability or who are under the influence of drugs shall receive separate health care services.

#### Article 39..
Detention buildings for female detainees shall have female prison officials on duty permanently.

Male prison officials are not permitted to enter female detention areas without being accompanied by female prison officials, except for cases of emergency rescue or for prison security and safety reasons.

#### Article 40..
Female detainees shall be provided with maternal and infant care services before and after giving birth.

Pregnant female detainees are permitted to give birth at an external hospital. In the event that a female detainee gives birth in prison, the birth certificate shall not state that the infant was born in prison.

#### Article 41..
Children living with their mothers are permitted to stay in prison with their mothers up to the age of under 3 (three) years old and shall be provided with food, clothing and health care services.

Children living with their mothers who are aged 3 (three) years old and above, if there is no guardian responsible for their continued care outside, shall be the responsibility of the Ministry of Social Affairs, Veterans and Youth Rehabilitation.

# Chapter 6
# Prison Management

#### Article 42..
The fundamental goal of managing detainees is the education, correction and rehabilitation of detainees, which is the core of the prison reform policy framework.

Disciplinary actions against detainees shall be at the minimum level necessary only to ensure security, safety and order.

#### Article 43..
The prison director shall be directly responsible for managing the prison correctly according to procedures and internal regulations as determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

#### Article 44..
Prison officials may conduct searches of detainees and various areas in the prison to ensure prison security and safety.

Detainees who refuse searches by prison officials shall be considered as committing a violation of prison internal regulations.

#### Article 45..
To ensure security, safety and order of the prison, prison officials and visitors shall be inspected before entering the prison.

#### Article 46..
Keeping or using alcohol in prison is prohibited.

Detainees are not allowed to keep or use any items that may threaten prison security.

Types of prohibited items are determined in prison internal regulations.

#### Article 47..
The prison director may permit prison officials who have been correctly trained to use weapons and technical equipment to ensure security and safety in prison operations.

The prison director shall be responsible for managing weapons and technical equipment safely.

#### Article 48..
The prison director shall immediately report to the prosecutor, the Capital or Provincial Board of Governors and the Director General of the General Department of Prisons regarding any information or incidents that threaten or may threaten prison security and safety.

#### Article 49..
The prison director may use operational forces to maintain prison security and safety. In serious cases, the prison director shall report and request intervention forces from the Unified Command of the Capital or Provincial Administration.

#### Article 50..
The use of handcuffs to punish detainees is prohibited, except for use in the following situations:

- During riots in prison

- During transfer, sending or escorting from prison

- For medical reasons

- To prevent detainees from self-harming or causing injury to others or causing damage to the property of others.

#### Article 51..
Detainees shall respect and follow prison internal regulations and instructions from prison officials.

#### Article 52..
Detainees who have committed violations of internal regulations that seriously affect prison order shall not be considered for commutation of sentence or pardon for a period of one year. In cases of recidivism, they shall be detained in a separate room.

The prison director who issues an order for separate detention shall specify the reasons and immediately report to the Director General of the General Department of Prisons.

Separate detention orders may last from 14 (fourteen) days to 20 (twenty) days. In cases of recidivism, this order may be re-issued.

During the period of punishment by separate detention, detainees are not permitted:

- To meet with family or relatives who come to visit

- To participate in various activities in prison.

#### Article 53..
Physical torture of any form, detention in dark cells, food reduction, collective punishment of detainees and using detainees to torture other detainees shall be prohibited.

#### Article 54..
The General Prosecutor attached to the Court of Appeal, the Prosecutor, the President of the Investigation Chamber and investigative judges shall conduct prison inspections in accordance with the Code of Criminal Procedure. The forms, procedures and functioning of such inspections shall be determined by a Joint Prakas of the Minister of Interior and the Minister of Justice.

The National Preventive Mechanism shall be permitted to conduct monitoring visits to various prisons at any time and conduct confidential interviews with detainees to monitor detention conditions and treatment of detainees, and provide recommendations for improvement aimed at preventing torture, cruel, inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment.

#### Article 55..
Detainees shall be permitted to have family or friends visit them as regulated, unless prohibited by judicial authorities.

The prison director shall publicly post the schedule and duration of visits determined in the prison internal regulations.

#### Article 56..
Embassy or consular officials or representative missions intending to visit detainees who are their citizens or the citizens they represent shall notify the General Department of Prisons in advance.

Foreign family members or friends intending to visit detainees shall apply to the General Department of Prisons through their embassy or consulate or representative mission.

#### Article 57..
Visitors to detainees shall not bring prohibited objects or materials into the prison as determined in the prison internal regulations.

#### Article 58..
Visitors to detainees may be prohibited or stopped or sent out from the prison temporarily in cases of sudden accidental incidents.

In cases where a visitor to a detainee conducts activities that lead to an effect on prison order, security or safety, they shall be arrested and legal measures shall be taken.

#### Article 59..
If official visitors intend to visit any prison, they shall notify the Director General of the General Department of Prisons in advance regarding the purpose and proposal of the visit.

The results of the visit shall be provided orally or in writing to the prison director and the Director General of the General Department of Prisons. Such information shall be considered confidential, unless there is agreement from the Ministry of Interior.

#### Article 60..
Detainees may be allowed to leave the prison temporarily according to court warrants for court hearings, investigation, or for the purpose of presenting evidence.

#### Article 61..
The prison director shall be responsible for security and safety regarding escorting or sending detainees to court or to an external hospital.

#### Article 62..
The transfer of convicted persons from one prison to another shall have permission from the Minister of Interior, and the prison director shall immediately notify the competent prosecutor of the sending jurisdiction and the competent prosecutor of the receiving jurisdiction, at most within 3 (three) days.

The transfer of detainees other than convicted persons from one prison to another shall be notified to the competent prosecutor of the sending jurisdiction and the competent prosecutor of the receiving jurisdiction, or the competent General Prosecutor who is currently handling the case.

In case of necessity, the General Prosecutor or the Prosecutor may issue a warrant to transfer detainees other than convicted persons from one prison to another as required by procedures. This warrant shall be copied for the Ministry of Interior.

The transfer of detainees from one prison to another shall be accompanied by the detainees' property, case file and health documents.

#### Article 63..
In the event that a detainee escapes, the prison director and competent authorities shall immediately notify the Unified Command of the capital or provincial administration, the Director General of the General Department of Prisons and the competent prosecutor of the jurisdiction to track down and re-arrest the detainee in accordance with the Code of Criminal Procedure.

#### Article 64..
In the event that a detainee dies in prison, it shall be considered a suspicious death. Prison officials shall protect the scene where the body is found and maintain any detainees who could be witnesses. The prison director shall notify the prosecutor to take investigative measures.

In the case where the detainee dies in a hospital, the prison director shall notify the prosecutor. If there is suspicion, the prosecutor shall assign technical and scientific police to conduct an examination of the body.

#### Article 65..
The prison director shall immediately notify the family of the deceased or the embassy or consulate or representative mission of the deceased to organize the funeral.

In cases where there is no family or relatives, the prison director shall make a written report on the status of the deceased to the prosecutor, the Director General of the General Department of Prisons and civil registry officials of the deceased's residence. Expenses for the funeral shall be at the cost of the state as determined by Sub-decree.

# Chapter 7
# Education, Correction, Vocational Training and Rehabilitation of Convicts

#### Article 66..
Convicts shall be assigned to participate in legal and social moral education programs organized by the prison.

#### Article 67..
Convicts shall be provided with the means to participate in general education and vocational training programs in the prison.

Education and vocational training programs in prison shall be integrated with national education and vocational training systems.

Minors who are convicts shall be given separate attention regarding their education, vocational training, rehabilitation and integration needs, in cooperation with the Ministry of Social Affairs, Veterans and Youth Rehabilitation and the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sports.

The Ministry of Education, Youth and Sports and the Ministry of Labour and Vocational Training shall cooperate with the Ministry of Interior to organize programs and implement education and vocational training programs for convicts in the prison.

#### Article 68..
Convicts who have low security levels and have been assessed as having physical capacity shall be assigned to do daily labor in the prison or work for the benefit of the community and public interest, or assigned to participate in prison industry, handicraft and agricultural programs.

#### Article 69..
Industry, handicraft and agricultural programs in prison shall be organized in each prison to provide opportunities for convicts to have the capacity for a profession for their livelihood when reintegrating into the community.

#### Article 70..
Convicts who participate in industrial, handicraft and agricultural programs in prison shall be equipped with occupational safety materials and equipment during their work.

#### Article 71..
Following the agreement of the Minister of Interior, the Director General of the General Department of Prisons has the right to sign contracts with any individual or legal entity or national or international organization in order to create labor

for industrial, handicraft and agricultural programs in the prison and having the right to sign contracts to sell products from the prison's industrial, handicraft and agricultural programs.

#### Article 72..
The management and use of income derived from industrial, handicraft and agricultural programs in the prison shall be determined by Sub-decree upon the proposal of the Minister of Interior and the Minister of Economy and Finance.

Procedures for managing industrial, handicraft and agricultural programs in the prison shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

#### Article 73..
Before the end of their sentence by 6 (six) months, convicts shall participate in reintegration programs organized by the prison to help them become citizens who can live well in the national society and local communities.

# Chapter 8
# Commutation of Sentence, Pardon and Release of Convicts

#### Article 74..
The Governor of the Capital or Provincial Board of Governors shall create a committee to monitor and assess the level of correction of convicts during their detention in the prisons under their management. This committee shall make a list of convicts who are qualified to request commutation of sentence and pardon, and submit it to the National Committee through the Director General of the General Department of Prisons at the latest 30 (thirty) days before the scheduled date of each festival.

This committee has the following composition:

- Governor of the Capital or Provincial Board of Governors: President

- Representative of the Prosecution Office attached to the Capital or Provincial Court of First Instance: Vice President

- Head of the Emulation Working Group of the Capital or Provincial Prison: Permanent Vice President

- Deputy Director of the Capital or Provincial Prison in charge of education and transformation: Member

- Deputy Director of the Capital or Provincial Prison in charge of operations: Member
