#### Law

## On

# Civil Fine Procedure

# Chapter 1: General Provisions

#### Article 1.- Purpose

This law determines the procedures for civil fines as provided in the Civil Code, the Code of Civil Procedure, and the Law on Civil Procedure for Non-Litigious Matters.

#### Article 2.- Universal Application

The provisions of the Law on Civil Procedure for Non-Litigious Matters shall also apply to the procedures for civil fines, except where otherwise provided in this law.

# Chapter 2: Competence

#### Article 3.- Competence

Civil fine cases shall fall under the jurisdiction of the Court of First Instance that has territorial jurisdiction as determined in Article 8 (Jurisdiction of the Trial Court determined by domicile, etc.) of the Code of Civil Procedure of the parties to the civil fine procedure, except in cases where there are special provisions in other laws.

# Chapter 3: Procedure

#### Article 4.- Commencement of Procedure

Civil fine procedures shall be initiated at the discretion of the court.

#### Article 5.- Procedure for Judging Civil Fines

1- A judgment on a civil fine shall be made by an order.

2- The order mentioned in paragraph 1 above must be in writing, stating the reasons and the final decision, and the judge must sign the order document.

3- Before issuing an order on a civil fine, the court must hear the opinion of the prosecutor and the statements of the parties.

4- The prosecutor and the parties may appeal against the order on civil fines. An order imposing a civil fine shall not be effective until it becomes final and conclusive.

5- In cases where an order imposing a civil fine has been issued, the court costs necessary for the procedure of the order on civil fines, including the procedure for judging the appeal, shall be borne by the person who received the order, and except in this case, it shall be borne by the State.

6- In cases where there is an appeal from a party against an order imposing a civil fine, if the appellate court vacates the original order and issues a new order on the civil fine, even if this order is an order imposing a civil fine, the court costs determined in paragraph 5 above shall also be borne by the State.

#### Article 6.- Execution of the Order Imposing Civil Fine

1- An order imposing a civil fine shall be executed by the order of the prosecutor. This order has the same effect as an execution document with an executory formula.

2- The execution of an order imposing a civil fine shall be carried out in accordance with Book 6 of the Code of Civil Procedure or other laws or regulations on enforcement procedures, without the need to serve the order before execution.

3- If it is deemed necessary to execute an order imposing a civil fine, the prosecutor may request reports on necessary points from state institutions, public entities, or private entities.

# Chapter 4: Final Provisions

#### Article 7.- Implementation of this Law

This law is proclaimed as urgent.
