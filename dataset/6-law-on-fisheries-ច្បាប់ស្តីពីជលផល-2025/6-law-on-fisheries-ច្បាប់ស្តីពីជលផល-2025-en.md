### Promulgates

The Law on Fisheries, which was approved by the National Assembly on May 22, 2006, during the 4th session of its 3rd legislature, and which was entirely reviewed on its form and legal concept by the Senate on June 16, 2006, during the 3rd plenary session of its 2nd legislature, and which has the following full text:

# Law on Fisheries

# Chapter 1: General Provisions

### Article 1 .-
This Law defines the management, conservation, and development of fishery resources in an effective and sustainable manner, aiming at enhancing food security and national economy, as well as ensuring the protection of rights and interests of fishers, community fisheries, aquaculturists, and traders doing business on fishery catches and fishery products.

### Article 2 .-
This Law has the scope of application on fishery resources, fishery management areas, operations and activities related to fisheries within the sovereignty and competent jurisdiction of the Kingdom of Cambodia, as well as fishing in areas outside the national jurisdiction, in accordance with international conventions, agreements, or instruments to which the Kingdom of Cambodia is a party.

### Article 3 .-
Key terminology used in this Law is defined in the Glossary, which is Annex 1 of this Law.

# Chapter 2: Competent Authorities

### Article 4 .-
The management, conservation, and development of fisheries are under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries. The management, conservation, and development of fisheries located within protected areas shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the Law on Protected Areas.

The Royal Government may determine specific roles, responsibilities, and cooperation for relevant competent ministries and institutions in the fisheries sector in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

### Article 5 .-
The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries shall act as staff of the Royal Government in the management, conservation, and development of fisheries within its jurisdiction, in accordance with the provisions of this Law and other relevant regulations in force.

The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries must have a specialized unit in charge of fisheries to act as staff, which specialized unit shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 6 .-
The Royal Government may transfer certain specific functions and responsibilities in the management, conservation, and development of fisheries to the sub-national administrations in compliance with the relevant laws and legal instruments in force.

The sub-national administrations shall participate in managing, conserving, and developing fisheries located within the competent jurisdiction of each sub-national administration in accordance with various regulations in force.

### Chapter III
### Sustainability of Fisheries Management

#### Article 7.-
Fisheries management shall comply with the following principles:

1- Manage, conserve, develop, and use fisheries resources sustainably based on scientific data and information.

2- Use and allocate fisheries resources equitably.

3- Promote cooperation in fisheries resource management with transparency and inclusiveness.

4- Promote aquaculture development in potential areas.

5- Respect relevant international and regional agreements and instruments to which the Kingdom of Cambodia is a party.

6- Implement appropriate working conditions in fisheries work on fishing vessels or vessels related to fishing to protect workers against forcing people to perform forced labor.

7- Ensure food safety.

8- Improve the livelihoods of fishers.

#### Article 8.-
The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries and the competent sub-national administrations shall receive budget revenues from the fisheries sector as a priority in accordance with financial procedures for use in sustainable management, conservation, and development of fisheries.

#### Article 9.-
July 1 shall be designated as the National Fish Day.

### Chapter IV
### Fisheries Management Area

#### Article 10.-
Fisheries management areas include permanent flooded lands, flooded forest lands, mangrove forest lands, seasonally flooded lands, and lands subject to tidal regimes from the sea, which play an important role in serving all types of fishing and protecting the fisheries ecosystem.

Permanent flooded lands include, among others, seas, rivers, tributaries, lakes, canals, streams, creeks, and reservoirs.

Seasonally flooded lands include, among others, rice fields, ponds, open forests, wetlands, and flooded dams.

Lands subject to tidal regimes from the sea include, among others, lagoons, sandy beaches, mudflats, and rocky shores.

Fisheries management areas may cover public land and private property land, but the management of fisheries resources shall not affect private property rights.

Fisheries management areas shall be divided into freshwater fisheries management areas and marine fisheries management areas.

#### Article 12.-
Freshwater fisheries management areas are defined as follows:

1- Public fishing areas.

2- Fishing areas designated for specific fishing gear.

3- Fishery community areas.

4- Aquaculture development areas.

5- Fishery conservation areas.

6- Conservation and protection areas for Mekong River dolphins.

#### Article 13.-
Marine fisheries management areas are defined as follows:

1- Public fishing areas.

2- Fishery community areas.

3- Aquaculture development areas.

4- Fishery conservation areas.

#### Article 14.-
Procedures and criteria for the establishment or modification of fisheries management areas shall be determined by sub-decree.

The establishment or cancellation of any site within a fisheries management area shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 15.-
The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries has the right to authorize the use of fisheries management areas in accordance with the provisions of this Law to individuals for fishing, business on fisheries products and sub-products, and aquaculture, as well as scientific research on fisheries resources.

Any use of fisheries management areas for purposes other than the fisheries sector shall be decided by the Royal Government upon the proposal of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, with consultation with relevant ministries, institutions, and competent sub-national administrations.

#### Article 16.-
The specialized unit in charge of fisheries may request the registration of any type of land within a fisheries management area as state property.

The granting of ownership or land possession rights by competent authorities in fisheries management areas may only take place if there is an evaluation study by the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, with consultation with relevant competent sub-national administrations, in accordance with the laws and legal instruments in force.

The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries has the right to organize, manage, and administer fisheries management areas to ensure the sustainability of fisheries resources by cooperating with ministries, institutions, sub-national administrations, fishery communities, and relevant stakeholders.

#### Article 17.-

The establishment and management of fishery tourism areas shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

### Chapter V
### Protection and Conservation of Fisheries Resources

#### Article 18.-
The following activities are prohibited in fishery conservation areas:

1- All types of fishing, except with the permission of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries for experiments and technical-scientific research in fisheries.

2- Navigation passing through with fishing gear prepared in a manner intending to fish, or other activities that affect fisheries resources.

3- Building new settlements located closer than 2 (two) kilometers from the boundary of the fishery conservation area, except for the workplaces of competent officers used for law enforcement purposes.

#### Article 19.-
The following activities are prohibited in Mekong River dolphin conservation areas:

1- All types of fishing.

2- Aquaculture.

3- Navigation passing through with gear intending to fish.

4- Navigation with a speed exceeding 30 (thirty) kilometers per hour.

5- Building new settlements.

6- Other activities that affect dolphins.

Fishing in dolphin protection areas using medium-scale and large-scale fishing gear is prohibited, except for fishing with small-scale fishing gear.

The types of small-scale fishing gear permitted for fishing in dolphin protection areas shall be determined in accordance with Article 28 of this Law.

#### Article 20.-
The following activities and use of fishing gear are prohibited:

1- Electro-fishing gear, explosives, or poison.

2- Anchored mosquito net traps or fish traps in deep pools of freshwater fisheries management areas, or coral reefs and seagrass areas of marine fisheries management areas.

3- Fishing by burning forest or pumping dry.

4- Mosquito net fishing gear, except for catching baby shrimp or seed fish.

5- Fishing using marine trawls.

The use of newly created fishing gear or fishing gear not included in Annex 2 of this Law, or new fishing methods that cause damage to the fisheries ecosystem, must be decided by the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

7- Changing, modifying, pulling out, or destroying boundary posts of any site within a fisheries management area.

8- Digging or dredging gravel, stone, sand, or minerals in fishery conservation areas.

9- Using all types of fishing gear and means of transport different from those specified in the permit.

10- Providing incorrect or incomplete information in application forms for permits, certificates, or other documents specified in this Law.

11- Activities that destroy fisheries resources or cause damage to the environment or the fisheries ecosystem.

#### Article 21.-
The following activities may only be carried out with a permit from the specialized unit in charge of fisheries:

1- Catching, buying, selling, stocking, or transporting baby fish, baby mussels, baby crabs, baby clams, baby snails, baby freshwater snails, or other aquatic animal seed from the wild.

2- Catching, buying, selling, harvesting, processing, stocking, transporting, exporting, or importing endangered fisheries resources.

3- Buying, selling, or stocking whole shells of endangered snails, freshwater snails, or mussels.

4- Collecting, harvesting, buying, selling, stocking, or transporting corals.

5- Fishing with trawlers or purse seines using light attraction that cover the marine fisheries management area.

6- Transporting, buying, or selling endangered fisheries resources obtained from aquaculture.

Endangered fisheries resources shall be determined by sub-decree.

#### Article 22.-
The production, purchase, sale, transport, and stocking of freshwater bag nets (yon), freshwater trawling nets, electro-fishing gear, or mosquito net fishing gear are prohibited, except for baby shrimp fishing gear.

The disposal, dumping, discharging, or spreading of solid or liquid waste or toxic substances into fisheries management areas that have a negative impact on aquatic animals and plants, leading to pollution in fisheries management areas, as determined by laws and legal instruments of the Kingdom of Cambodia, as well as international agreements or instruments to which the Kingdom of Cambodia is a party, is prohibited.

#### Article 23.-
Building dams that block, or changing the water flow of rivers, streams, canals, creeks, lakes, channels, or reservoirs that are migration paths of aquatic animals in fisheries management areas, shall be subject to an evaluation study by the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, with consultation with relevant ministries, institutions, and competent sub-national administrations.

#### Article 24.-
Citizens, fishery communities, armed forces, and authorities at all levels have the obligation to participate in preserving and protecting natural habitats, and preventing and suppressing forest fires in flooded forests and mangrove forests.

Guidelines for preventing fires in flooded forests and mangrove forests shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 25.-
Flooded areas within flooded forest and mangrove forest lands shall be protected for replanting or natural regeneration to maintain the balance of the fisheries ecosystem.

#### Article 26.-
The following activities are prohibited:

1- Encroaching, enclosing, and occupying land in flooded forest or mangrove forest lands.

2- Granting ownership or land possession rights in flooded forest or mangrove forest lands without complying with the procedures specified in Article 16 of this Law.

3- Burning, clearing, uprooting, bulldozing, or killing flooded forest or mangrove forest trees.

4- Leaving open flames that can cause forest fires in flooded forests or mangrove forests.

5- Negligence, carelessness, imprudence, or failure to respect obligations, causing forest fires in flooded forests or mangrove forests.

6- Intentionally causing forest fires in flooded forests or mangrove forests.

7- Collecting, stocking, or transporting wood, firewood, or charcoal from flooded forests or mangrove forests for business purposes.

8- Using flooded forest or mangrove forest wood as raw material to provide energy in handicraft workshops, factories, or processing facilities.

#### Article 27.-
The production, purchase, or sale of legal large-scale fishing gear for commercial purposes shall require a permit from the specialized unit in charge of fisheries.

### Chapter VI
### Freshwater Fisheries Management

#### Article 28.-
Freshwater fishing is classified as follows:

1- Small-scale fishing.

2- Medium-scale fishing.

3- Large-scale fishing.

Small-scale, medium-scale, and large-scale fishing shall be determined by the types of fishing gear as specified in Annex 2 of this Law. In case of necessity, the types of fishing gear for small-scale, medium-scale, and large-scale fishing may be modified or additionally determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

Technical specifications of types of fishing gear for small-scale, medium-scale, and large-scale fishing shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 29.-
Small-scale fishing may be conducted at any time and in any place within freshwater fisheries management areas, except for fishery conservation areas and fishery community areas, which are permitted only for community members.

Medium-scale fishing in freshwater fisheries management areas may only be conducted in public fishing areas during the open fishing season.

For small-scale or medium-scale fishing in fishery community areas, it may only be conducted by fishery community members in accordance with the technical conditions for using each fishery community area as determined by the specialized unit in charge of fisheries.

Large-scale fishing in freshwater fisheries management areas may be conducted in designated fishing areas at specific times in accordance with the procedures and provisions of this Law.

Fishing for study, experiment, and technical-scientific research in fisheries may be conducted in any place within freshwater fisheries management areas, provided there is authorization from the specialized unit in charge of fisheries.

#### Article 30.-
Individuals conducting medium-scale or large-scale fishing in freshwater fisheries management areas permitted for fishing shall have a fishing permit, be obligated to pay fishing fees to the State, and comply with the conditions in the fishing permit. For small-scale fishing, a permit is not required.

The formats and procedures for granting fishing permits for medium-scale and large-scale fishing shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

The transfer of fish bag nets (dai) or shrimp bag nets (dai) for fishing may be carried out through investment, public bidding, or lease by agreement for fish or shrimp bag nets that have no bidders.

Procedures for investment, public bidding, or lease by agreement, and the payment of fishing fees, shall be determined by sub-decree.

Physical persons conducting fishing using fish bag nets (dai) or shrimp bag nets (dai) must have a fishing permit, a logbook (load book), and pay fisheries product fees. Every fishing activity using fish or shrimp bag nets must record fishing statistics in the fishing statistics logbook and comply with the conditions in the permit and logbook.

The model of the burden book for fishing lots or aquaculture shall be prepared by the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries with the participation of the Ministry of Economy and Finance, and shall be determined by a Proclamation of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 32 .-
The following activities are prohibited in freshwater fisheries management areas:

1- Medium or large-scale fishing during the closed fishing season.

2- Large-scale fishing in community fishing areas.

3- Fishing by fishing lots or aquaculture outside of designated locations.

4- Fishing or using fishing gear that obstructs navigation channels, or fishing using shelter barriers, row barriers, or net barriers stretching more than two-thirds (two-thirds) of the width of a stream, canal, or river.

5- Fishing using cast nets, anchored gill nets, long drift nets, or any type of three-layer gill nets.

6- Fishing with nets having a mesh size larger than 15 (fifteen) centimeters.

7- Fishing using any type of vibrating line or other means that cause fish to panic.

8- Fishing using gear or fishing using shelter barriers, row barriers, or net barriers contrary to the designated location, length, size, mesh size, or time.

9- Fishing using drag nets or flying nets in freshwater.

10- Installing brush parks or fishing using shelter devices or other means to attract fish or other aquatic animals.

11- Fishing by pumping, splashing, or dewatering any part of a fisheries management area, which may cause or lead to destruction of fisheries resources of public interest.

12- Fishing by damming water channels or using fishing gear that affects fisheries resources.

13- Fishing that obstructs fish migration in the Tonle Sap Lake area.

#### Article 33 .-
Fishing using gear or fishing using shelter barriers, row barriers, or net barriers in freshwater fisheries management areas may be permitted according to the type, size, mesh size of the fishing gear, time, and location permitted for fishing. The length, size, mesh size of the fishing gear, time, and location permitted for fishing shall be determined by a Proclamation of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

Fishing using Manh, Nam, Chon, vibrating nets, drag nets, trawlers, or pushing with engine power may be permitted only in marine fisheries areas under the determination of engine power, time, and location permitted for fishing.

The engine power, time, and location permitted for fishing shall be determined by a Proclamation of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 34 .-
The closed fishing season in freshwater fisheries management areas shall be determined as follows:

1- From the 1st of June to the 30th of September for freshwater fisheries management areas located north of the Chaktomuk parallel.

2- From the 1st of July to the 31st of October for freshwater fisheries management areas located south of the Chaktomuk parallel.

The closed fishing season in other areas shall be determined by a Proclamation of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries based on specific scientific information.

### Chapter 7
### Marine Fisheries Management

#### Section 1
#### Management of Fishing Operations, Fishing Vessels, and Activities Related to Fishing

#### Article 35 .-
Marine fishing operations shall be determined as follows:

1- Small-scale fishing.

2- Medium-scale fishing.

3- Large-scale fishing.

Small-scale, medium-scale, and large-scale fishing operations shall be determined according to the type of fishing gear or fishing vessel as specified in Annex 2 of this Law. In case of necessity, the types of fishing gear for small-scale, medium-scale, and large-scale fishing may be modified or additionally determined by a Proclamation of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

Technical specifications of types of fishing gear for small-scale, medium-scale, and large-scale fishing shall be determined by a Proclamation of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 36 .-
Any person who performs medium-scale or large-scale fishing, or uses a vessel related to medium-scale or large-scale fishing in marine fisheries management areas, must have a permit, have the obligation to pay taxes and fishing fees to the State, and comply with the conditions of the fishing permit. Small-scale fishing operations shall not be required to have a permit.

The formalities and procedures for granting fishing permits for medium-scale and large-scale fishing and activities related to fishing shall be determined by a Proclamation of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

Small-scale fishing may be conducted at any time and in any location within marine fisheries management areas, except for fisheries conservation areas and community fishing areas which are permitted only for members of the community fisheries.

Medium-scale fishing may be conducted at any time in public fishing areas of marine fisheries management areas, except for drag nets, scoop nets, and scoop nets for dragging cockles, mussels, or clams which may be conducted during the fishing season only in specific locations determined by a Proclamation of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

Small-scale or medium-scale fishing in community fishing areas may be conducted only for members of the community fisheries, in accordance with technical specifications of using each community fishing area determined by the specialized unit in charge of fisheries.

Large-scale fishing in marine fisheries management areas may be conducted only in the outer marine fisheries sub-area.

Fishing for study, experimentation, and research of fisheries science and technology may be conducted in any location within marine fisheries management areas, subject to permission from the specialized unit in charge of fisheries.

#### Article 37 .-
Any person who performs fishing using fishing gear stretching across a channel or stream of a fishing area along a waterway, must open the water passage by at least 1/2 (one-half) of the width of the channel or stream during low tide to allow watercrafts to travel, and must remove all fishing gear before the expiration date of the permit.

#### Article 38 .-
The following activities are prohibited in marine fisheries management areas:

1- Fishing or using fishing gear that obstructs navigation channels.

2- Fishing or performing other activities in coral reef or seagrass areas that affect the growth of coral reefs or seagrass.

3- Anchoring vessels by casting anchor in coral reef areas.

4- Damaging seagrass or coral reefs by other activities.

5- Fishing using drift nets or drag nets with a mesh size smaller than 1,5 (one and a half) centimeters, except for shrimp fishing nets.

6- Fishing using trawl nets, scoop nets, or scoop nets for dragging cockles, mussels, or clams in seasonal fishing areas outside of designated locations.

7- Foreign fishing vessels or vessels related to fishing performing activities contrary to designated locations.

The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries may permit activities specified in point 3 of the first paragraph above.

The conditions of management and conservation of marine fisheries shall be determined by a Sub-Decree.

#### Article 41 .-
Fishing vessels or vessels related to fishing shall be classified as follows:

1- Small-scale vessels.

2- Medium-scale vessels.

3- Large-scale vessels.

Types of fishing vessels or vessels related to fishing are specified in Annex 2 of this Law. In case of necessity, the types of fishing vessels or vessels related to fishing may be modified by a Proclamation of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 42 .-
The import of large-scale vessels for fishing or activities related to fishing must have approval in principle from the Royal Government of Cambodia.

The import of medium-scale vessels for fishing or activities related to fishing must have permission from the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

The procedures for requesting the import of fishing vessels or vessels related to fishing shall be determined by a Proclamation of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 43 .-
Medium-scale or large-scale vessels, prior to being granted a permit to perform fishing or activities related to fishing in Cambodia's marine fisheries management areas or areas outside national jurisdiction, must undergo inspection and technical evaluation of fishing gear and related accessories from the specialized unit in charge of fisheries.

Procedures for inspection and technical evaluation of fishing gear and related accessories shall be determined by a Proclamation of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 44 .-
Any person who performs fishing or activities related to fishing outside national jurisdiction using Cambodian flagged medium-scale or large-scale vessels, must:

1- Have a permit from the specialized unit in charge of fisheries after receiving guidelines and training related to international regulations that the fisher must implement.

2- Comply with management and conservation measures of the relevant regional fisheries management organization in which the fishing vessel or vessel related to fishing operates.

3- Comply with international standards concerning working and living conditions and safety at sea for fishing vessels or vessels related to fishing, and ensure that persons on board the fishing vessels or vessels related to fishing have insurance coverage for loss of life, disability, or use of forced labor.

4- Comply with conditions for vessel marking and identification of fishing vessels determined by the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

5- Comply with reporting requirements on catch, fishing gear, vessel location, fishing zone, or activities related to fishing.

6- Comply with requirements for installing and operating vessel tracking devices on the fishing vessel or vessel related to fishing.

7- Comply with requirements for using fishing ports, including landing and transshipment of catch and fisheries products.

8- Report on operational locations of fishing, purchasing, and selling of fisheries products, as well as other related activities.

9- Keep the original permit or its copy operating in the fishing vessel or vessel related to fishing at all times during the validity of the permit, and the vessel master must present it to the competent fisheries officer upon request.

#### Article 45 .-
The specialized unit in charge of fisheries shall not permit fishing or activities related to fishing in Cambodia's marine fisheries management areas or areas outside national jurisdiction in any of the following cases:

1- The vessel is not properly registered or is registered in multiple countries.

2- The person or vessel is suspected of performing illegal, unreported, and unregulated fishing or activities related to fishing, or is included in the list of illegal, unreported, and unregulated vessels.

3- The vessel does not comply with international standards concerning working and living conditions or safety at sea.

4- The person or fishing vessel has been penalized under the law of the flag State by suspension or revocation of the permit.

5- The applicant has direct relationships with persons or vessels that have committed fisheries offenses or failed to comply with transitional fine decisions or court decisions.

#### Article 46 .-
In case of international agreements or treaties on fishing or activities related to fishing to which the Kingdom of Cambodia is a party, foreign fishing vessels or foreign vessels related to fishing may operate in designated fishing areas.

#### Article 47 .-
Foreign fishing vessels or foreign vessels related to fishing with a permit to perform fishing or activities related to fishing may enter designated fishing ports, and must notify the specialized unit in charge of fisheries 24 (twenty-four) hours in advance.

In case permission to enter port is granted, prior to departure, notification must be given 12 (twelve) hours in advance to the specialized unit in charge of fisheries.

## Section 2
## Monitoring, Control and Surveillance

#### Article 48 .-
Any person using fishing vessels or vessels related to fishing in Cambodia's marine fisheries management areas or areas outside national jurisdiction, must mark and identify the vessel clearly in compliance with the laws and regulations in force.

#### Article 49 .-
Any person using large-scale fishing vessels or vessels related to fishing in Cambodia's marine fisheries management areas may operate only if the fishing gear is marked and identified.

Technical specifications for marking and identifying fishing gear shall be determined by a Proclamation of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 50 .-
Any person performing medium-scale or large-scale fishing with a permit to fish in Cambodia's marine fisheries management areas, must record data, statistics, and information on the fishing area, fishing duration, catch, and fishing gear in the logbook of catch, and must provide it to the fisheries inspection officer within the determined timeframe.

Any person using medium-scale or large-scale vessels related to fishing with a permit to perform activities related to fishing in Cambodia's marine fisheries management areas, must record information on activities of packaging, processing, transshipment, or transportation of catch and fisheries products, including provision of vessel services, fuel, and other supplies in the logbook of activities related to fishing, and must provide it to the fisheries inspection officer within the determined timeframe.

#### Article 51 .-
The master of a fishing vessel performing medium-scale or large-scale fishing with a permit to fish in Cambodia's marine fisheries management areas, must:

1- Keep the logbook of catch on board the vessel and record information in Khmer or English.

2- Maintain the logbook of catch and present it to the fisheries inspection officer or observer upon request for inspection or auditing.

3- Submit the logbook of catch to the fisheries inspection officer after completing the landing of catch.

The master of a vessel related to fishing of medium-scale or large-scale with a permit to perform activities related to fishing in Cambodia's marine fisheries management areas, must:

1- Keep the logbook of activities related to fishing on board the vessel and record information in Khmer or English.

2- Maintain the logbook of activities related to fishing and present it to the fisheries inspection officer or observer upon request for inspection or auditing.

3- Submit the fishing activity logbook to the fisheries inspector after the completion of the activity.

#### Article 52.-

The specialized fisheries authority shall establish a database, in consultation with relevant institutions, landing site owners, fisheries products and vessels, and fishers, to collect information and data on fishing.

#### Article 53.-

The specialized fisheries authority shall require operators of foreign fishing vessels or vessels involved in fishing-related activities in designated areas to have observers on board.

The specialized fisheries authority shall establish observer programs to collect, record, and report specific information to monitor fishing activities and fishing-related activities.

The procedures for the implementation of the functions of observers and operators shall be determined by Prokas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries.

#### Article 54.-

Persons using medium or large-scale vessels shall install vessel tracking equipment on fishing vessels and maintain their continuous operation during fishing operations or fishing-related activities in Cambodia's marine fisheries management areas and areas beyond national jurisdiction.

The determination of vessel tracking equipment, technical conditions, and rules for the operation of the vessel tracking system shall be determined by Prokas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries.

#### Article 55.-

Foreign fishing vessels or foreign vessels involved in fishing-related activities that have never used Cambodian fishing ports to land fish, fisheries products, transfer, pack, or process fisheries products, or use fishing port services, including refueling, supply, repair, and maintenance, may be allowed to use fishing ports only if:

1- Entering designated fishing ports for foreign fishing vessels or foreign vessels involved in fishing-related activities, which have been publicly advertised.

2- The vessel operator has notified the specialized fisheries authority at least 24 (twenty-four) hours in advance.

3- The vessel operator presents the authorization letter for the vessel to enter the port to the fisheries inspector upon arrival.

#### Article 56.-

Fisheries inspectors shall deny foreign fishing vessels or foreign vessels involved in fishing-related activities from port entry or using Cambodian fishing ports in the event of:

1- Having evidence confirming that the vessel has conducted fishing or fishing-related activities contrary to conservation and management measures.

2- The vessel is listed as an illegal, unreported, and unregulated fishing vessel.

Notwithstanding the cases under paragraph 1 of this Article, the fisheries inspector has the right to allow the above vessel to enter Cambodian fishing ports in the event that:

1- Conducting vessel inspection and taking appropriate measures in accordance with the provisions of this Law to prevent, deter, and eliminate illegal, unreported, and unregulated fishing and fishing-related activities.

2- In case of force majeure or distress, the vessel may enter Cambodian fishing ports for the duration of the actual event.

Fisheries inspectors, in cooperation with relevant competent authorities, may prohibit foreign fishing vessels or foreign vessels involved in fishing-related activities from entering Cambodian fishing ports in the event that the vessel operates contrary to the conservation and management measures of a regional fisheries management organization or of a flag State that is a non-member or a non-cooperating party of the regional fisheries management organization, unless it is verified that the catch on board the vessel was caught in accordance with the conservation and management measures.

#### Article 57.-

The specialized fisheries authority may deny foreign fishing vessels or foreign vessels involved in fishing-related activities that have never used Cambodian fishing ports from landing fish, fisheries products, transferring, packing, or processing fisheries products, or using fishing port services, including refueling, supplying, repairing, and maintaining, in the event that:

1- The vessel has an authorization to fish or conduct fishing-related activities issued by the flag State that has expired.

2- The vessel does not have an authorization to fish or conduct fishing-related activities subject to this Law.

3- There is evidence showing that the catch on board the vessel was obtained from fishing in excess of the authorization conditions within the jurisdiction of the coastal State.

4- The flag State of the foreign fishing vessel or the foreign vessel involved in fishing-related activities fails to respond to a request from the specialized fisheries authority, even if the fisheries products on board the vessel were obtained from fishing in accordance with the requirements of a regional fisheries management organization.

5- There are clear grounds that the fishing vessel or vessel involved in fishing-related activities has conducted illegal, unreported, and unregulated fishing or fishing-related activities, unless the person using the vessel has evidence showing that the activities were conducted in compliance with conservation and management measures.

Notwithstanding the provisions of the above paragraph of this Article, the specialized fisheries authority may allow the vessel to use fishing port services for the safety of the vessel and the health of the crew, which is an essential requirement, or for discharging waste on board the vessel at designated locations.

#### Article 58.-

In the event that a foreign fishing vessel or a vessel involved in fishing-related activities is denied port entry or use of Cambodian fishing port services, the specialized fisheries authority shall immediately notify the flag State, coastal State, or relevant regional fisheries management organizations, or international organizations as necessary and appropriate.

In the event that a foreign fishing vessel or a vessel involved in fishing-related activities is denied the use of Cambodian fishing port services after inspection, the specialized fisheries authority shall transmit the inspection report to the flag State, and, as necessary, to the coastal State, regional fisheries management organizations, relevant international organizations, or the State of nationality of the master of the vessel.

#### Article 59.-

Fisheries inspectors shall prioritize the inspection of foreign fishing vessels or foreign vessels involved in fishing-related activities at Cambodian fishing ports as follows:

1- Vessels that have previously been denied entry or use of fishing ports under the jurisdiction of another State.

2- Requests for inspection from a State or a regional fisheries management organization, particularly requests accompanied by evidence showing that the vessel is involved in illegal, unreported, and unregulated fishing or fishing-related activities.

3- Other vessels for which there are clear grounds for suspecting that they are involved in illegal, unreported, and unregulated fishing or fishing-related activities.

4- Vessels that have violated other laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 60.-

During the inspection of foreign fishing vessels or foreign vessels involved in fishing-related activities at Cambodian fishing ports, fisheries inspectors shall:

1- Present identification documents to the master of the vessel before conducting the inspection.

2- Invite a representative of the flag State of the fishing vessel or vessel involved in fishing-related activities to participate in the inspection as necessary.

3- Avoid interfering with the master's communication with the flag State authorities in accordance with international law.

4- Inspect all areas of the vessel, such as catch, fishing gear, equipment, and documents or records on board the vessel, relevant to verifying compliance with conservation and management measures.

5- Require the master of the vessel to cooperate and facilitate the provision of necessary information, showing relevant equipment and documents as required.

6- Make best efforts to avoid delaying, interfering with, or causing difficulties that would affect the quality of the catch on board the vessel.

7- Make best efforts to communicate with the master or key crew members, providing an interpreter if necessary.

8- Conduct the inspection in a fair, transparent, and non-discriminatory manner.

#### Article 61.-

Cambodian fishing vessels or Cambodian vessels involved in fishing-related activities in areas beyond

1- Co-operate fully in inspections at the fishing ports of other States in accordance with the laws and procedures of the port State.

2- Be prohibited from landing, transshipping, packaging, or processing fish, fisheries products, or using other services at fishing ports of other States that are recognized by regional fisheries management organizations, in the event of operating contrary to regional or international instruments related to port State measures.

When there are clear grounds for suspecting that a Cambodian fishing vessel or a Cambodian vessel involved in fishing-related activities has conducted illegal, unreported, and unregulated fishing or fishing-related activities, and is seeking entry to or is in the fishing port of another State, the specialized fisheries authority shall request that State to inspect the vessel or take appropriate measures in accordance with regional or international instruments.

When there are clear grounds for suspecting that a Cambodian fishing vessel or a Cambodian vessel involved in fishing-related activities has conducted illegal, unreported, and unregulated fishing or fishing-related activities, the specialized fisheries authority shall conduct a full and rapid investigation. In the event of sufficient evidence, immediate measures shall be taken in accordance with this Law.

### Section 3
### Landing and Transshipment of Fish and Fisheries Products

#### Article 62.-

The landing of fish and fisheries products by medium or large-scale fishing vessels or vessels involved in fishing-related activities shall be conducted at fishing ports or landing sites of fish and fisheries products determined by the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries. For the landing of fish and fisheries products by small-scale fishing vessels or vessels involved in fishing-related activities, it may take place anywhere, except in prohibited places determined by the specialized fisheries authority and publicly advertised.

Operators of foreign medium or large-scale fishing vessels or vessels involved in fishing-related activities shall, after landing fish and fisheries products, submit a landing declaration of fish and fisheries products to the specialized fisheries authority.

The determination of fishing ports shall be determined by inter-ministerial Prokas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries and the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

The formalities and procedures for using fishing ports or landing sites of fish and fisheries products by medium or large-scale fishing vessels or vessels involved in fishing-related activities shall be determined by Prokas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries.

#### Article 63.-

The transshipment of fish and fisheries products by medium or large-scale fishing vessels or vessels involved in fishing-related activities in Cambodian marine fisheries management areas or in Cambodian fishing ports or landing sites of fish and fisheries products shall be prohibited, unless the fishing vessel or vessel involved in fishing-related activities has a transshipment authorization from the specialized fisheries authority.

The transshipment of fish and fisheries products from Cambodian fishing vessels or Cambodian vessels involved in fishing-related activities in areas beyond national jurisdiction or in foreign fishing ports shall require a transshipment authorization from the specialized fisheries authority and shall be transshipped at:

1- The high seas under the management of a regional fisheries management organization, in accordance with the transshipment conditions determined by that regional organization.

2- Seas within the sovereignty or jurisdiction of another State, only with the authorization of the competent authority of that State.

3- Foreign fishing ports designated for foreign fishing vessels or foreign vessels involved in fishing-related activities.

Operators of Cambodian or foreign fishing vessels or vessels involved in fishing-related activities shall, after completing the transshipment of fish and fisheries products, submit a transshipment declaration of fish and fisheries products to the specialized fisheries authority.

The procedures, requirements, and formalities for submitting declarations of landing of fish and fisheries products or transshipment of fish and fisheries products shall be determined by Prokas of the Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries.

### Section 4
### Protection of Persons Working on Fishing Vessels and Vessels Involved in Fishing-Related Activities

#### Article 64.-

Persons using foreign fishing vessels or foreign vessels involved in fishing-related activities may recruit Cambodian citizens to work on fishing vessels or vessels involved in fishing-related activities in the exclusive economic zone or areas beyond national jurisdiction, and shall comply with the conditions as provided for in the laws and regulations in force of the Kingdom of Cambodia and international conventions, agreements, or instruments to which the Kingdom of Cambodia is a party.

#### Article 65.-

Persons using Cambodian fishing vessels or Cambodian vessels involved in fishing-related activities may recruit persons to work on fishing vessels or vessels involved in fishing-related activities in Cambodian marine fisheries management areas or areas beyond national jurisdiction, and shall comply with the conditions as provided for in the laws and regulations in force of the Kingdom of Cambodia and international conventions, agreements, or instruments to which the Kingdom of Cambodia is a party.

## Chapter 8
## Fisheries Communities

#### Article 66.-

Every Cambodian citizen has the equal right to request the establishment of a fisheries community on a voluntary basis to participate in the management, conservation, development, and use of fisheries resources in local fisheries management areas sustainably in order to improve the livelihood of the local citizens.

A fisheries community is an agricultural cooperative established and managed in accordance with the Law on Agricultural Cooperatives.

#### Article 67.-

The fisheries specialized unit has the duty to determine the boundaries of the community fishery area to ensure an appropriate size, based on the potential of fisheries resources and the traditional use needs of the community, with consultation with relevant competent authorities, in order to request a decision to establish the community fishery area from the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

The fisheries specialized unit has the right to issue a letter transferring the community fishery area for the community to use in the first year, in accordance with the technical conditions for using the community fishery area. In case of necessity, the fisheries specialized unit has the right to withdraw the community fishery area.

#### Article 68.-

The community fishery shall use its community fishery area sustainably, in accordance with the conditions specified in the letter transferring the community fishery area determined by the fisheries specialized unit.

The fisheries specialized unit shall monitor and inspect the implementation of technical conditions for using the community fishery area.

#### Article 69.-

The community fishery has no right to sell, rent, exchange, donate, sharecrop, transfer, or mortgage the community fishery area, or take the community fishery area as private property, or enter into any agreement related to the community fishery area with any individual.

For research study or development in the community fishery area, the community fishery shall request the fisheries specialized unit to represent them in entering into agreements with individuals, after obtaining consent from the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

### Chapter IX
### Aquaculture Management

#### Article 70.-

Freshwater and marine aquaculture shall be classified into small scale, medium scale, or large scale.

Small-scale aquaculture is not required to have a permit, except for the breeding or hatching of newly imported aquaculture species, but must be registered, comply with technical regulations and requirements, and provide information on activities to the fisheries specialized unit for recording in the database system.

Individuals who carry out medium-scale or large-scale aquaculture must have a permit from the fisheries specialized unit and must comply with technical regulations and requirements, as well as the conditions in the permit.

Technical conditions and requirements for aquaculture shall be determined by a Prokas of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

The formalities and procedures for granting, transferring, and terminating aquaculture permits shall be determined by a Prokas of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 71.-

Aquaculture of aquatic species that are endangered fisheries resources must have a permit from the fisheries specialized unit.

Commercial import and export of endangered fisheries resources obtained from aquaculture must comply with the provisions of this Law and must conform to the CITES Convention for species listed in the CITES Convention list.

The procedures for granting permits for aquaculture of endangered fisheries resources shall be determined by a Prokas of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 72.-

Investments in medium-scale and large-scale aquaculture in fisheries management areas that are not private property must obtain approval in principle from the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries and a permit from the fisheries specialized unit, in accordance with the laws and regulations in force.

#### Article 73.-

Individuals who carry out medium-scale and large-scale aquaculture shall comply with the following conditions:

1- Ensure hygiene, quality, and safety of aquaculture.

2- Use quality aquatic breeds for aquaculture.

3- Record all aquaculture statistics in the aquaculture logbook and report to the fisheries specialized unit as scheduled.

4- Report and provide samples of aquatic species suspected of having diseases or infectious epidemics to the fisheries specialized unit for laboratory research to confirm health, quality, safety, and pathology.

5- Comply with technical regulations and requirements of aquaculture.

6- Comply with other requirements in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

#### Article 74.-

Individuals who import, export, and transit aquatic breeds must have a permit from the fisheries specialized unit.

All imports and transits of aquatic breeds must have a health certificate.

All transits of aquatic breeds must comply with the conditions in international agreements related to transit goods transport, treaties, conventions, and international instruments to which the Kingdom of Cambodia is a party.

The procedures for issuing health certificates, permits for import, export, and transit of aquatic breeds shall be determined by a Prokas of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 75.-

Individuals who produce, import, transit, store, repackage, or supply, distribute, or wholesale, retail aquaculture feed and veterinary drugs must have a permit from the fisheries specialized unit.

All production or import of aquaculture feed and veterinary drugs must be registered in compliance with the provisions of this Law. All types of aquaculture feed and veterinary drugs applying for registration must undergo analysis and verification of ingredients or active ingredients at a laboratory recognized by the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

Aquaculture feed and veterinary drugs with insufficient data for registration application evaluation are required to undergo efficacy and safety testing at a laboratory or testing station recognized by the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

The fisheries specialized unit shall determine the efficacy testing procedures and guidelines for the analysis of aquaculture feed and veterinary drugs.

The procedures for granting permits and managing the production, import, transit, storage, repackaging, supply, distribution, or wholesale, retail, and registration of aquaculture feed and veterinary drugs shall be determined by a Prokas of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 76.-

The following activities are prohibited:

1- Releasing any type of non-native aquatic species into fisheries management areas, unless permitted by the fisheries specialized unit.

2- Producing, importing, exporting, distributing, or using aquaculture feed or veterinary drugs containing prohibited or abnormal chemical substances that cause danger to public health.

3- Discharging or dumping toxic substances, aquaculture waste, veterinary drugs, and equipment/materials into fisheries management areas without permission and without improving water quality in accordance with technical regulations and requirements, causing poisoning or danger to aquatic animals and plants.

4- Using juvenile fish or other aquatic animals obtained from illegal, unreported, and unregulated fishing to produce feed, fishmeal, or aquaculture feed for aquaculture.

5- Harvesting, purchasing, selling, donating, or distributing aquaculture products that are diseased, toxic, or contain chemical residues exceeding the set limits.

6- Falsifying the origin, identity, trade name, packaging, ingredients, or compound elements of aquaculture feed and veterinary drugs used in aquaculture.

7- Carrying out aquaculture of prohibited aquatic species.

#### Article 77.-

The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries shall issue a Prokas on the prevention or eradication of any aquatic animal disease agent, upon request of the fisheries specialized unit.

The fisheries specialized unit shall disseminate measures for the prevention, management, and eradication of infectious epidemics of aquatic animals.

In case of an outbreak of infectious epidemics of aquatic animals that poses a severe risk to public health, the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries shall cooperate with the Ministry of Health and relevant ministries and institutions to take measures to prevent and control the spread.

### Chapter X
### Management of Fisheries Harvest and Products

#### Article 78.-

The fisheries specialized unit has the duty to be responsible for the management and inspection of the quality and safety of fisheries harvest and products in the business of fisheries harvest and products, transport, export, import, and transit.

The procedures and criteria for the management and inspection of the quality and safety of fisheries harvest and products in the commercial business of fisheries harvest and products, transport, export, import, and transit shall be determined by a Prokas of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 79.-

Individuals who carry out businesses in fisheries harvest and products must obtain a permit from the fisheries specialized unit, except for family-scale businesses which are not required to have a permit.

For endangered fisheries harvest and products listed in the appendices of the CITES Convention, they must comply with the CITES Convention and relevant regulations in force.

#### Article 80.-

Individuals who carry out businesses in fisheries harvest and products shall comply with the following conditions:

1- Implement hygiene principles and good manufacturing practices.

2- Ensure hygiene standards and food safety.

3- Ensure correct packaging and trade labeling on fisheries harvest and products.

4- Maintain a logbook for traceability of fisheries harvest and products.

5- Take responsibility for unsafe fisheries harvest and products.

6- Report unsafe fisheries harvest and products to the fisheries specialized unit or the nearest competent fisheries officers in order to take action according to procedures.

7- Comply with other conditions in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

#### Article 81.-

Individuals who commercially import fisheries harvest and products must have a permit from the fisheries specialized unit, a certificate of origin, and a health certificate in accordance with the procedures and requirements of management and quality and safety inspection of fisheries harvest and products.

Individuals who commercially export and transit fisheries harvest and products must have a permit from the fisheries specialized unit for products defined in the list of prohibited and restricted goods, and must have a health certificate in accordance with the requirements of the importing country.

The import, export, and transit of endangered fisheries harvest and product species listed in the appendices of the CITES Convention must have a permit from the fisheries specialized unit, in accordance with the CITES Convention and relevant regulations in force.

The transport of fisheries harvest and products commercially within the Kingdom of Cambodia must have a permit.

All transits of fisheries harvest and products must comply with the conditions specified in international agreements related to transit goods transport, or conventions, treaties, and relevant international instruments to which the Kingdom of Cambodia is a party.

#### Article 82.-

The following activities are prohibited:

1- Falsifying permits, certificates, or relevant documents required by this Law.

2- Falsifying analysis results, samples, or health diagnostic test results of aquatic species.

3- Carrying out business in fisheries harvest and products that are in stock, unhygienic, or deteriorated in quality.

4- Carrying out business, transport, export, import, or transit of fisheries harvest and products obtained from illegal, unreported, and unregulated fishing.

5- Carrying out business, transport, or import of fisheries harvest and products commercially without a permit, or in violation of the conditions specified in the permit, or in violation of the requirements for management and quality and safety inspection of fisheries harvest and products.

6- Importing fisheries harvest and products commercially without a certificate of origin or a health certificate.

7- Exporting or transiting fisheries harvest and products commercially without a permit for products specified in the list of prohibited and restricted goods.

8- Using food additives exceeding the permitted level or prohibited chemical substances on fisheries harvest and products and aquaculture products as specified in the requirements for management and quality and safety inspection of fisheries harvest and products.

9- Purchasing, selling, donating, or distributing fisheries harvest and products containing chemical residues exceeding the set limits.

### Chapter XI
### Public Service Fees

#### Article 83.-

Individuals who apply for permits or certificates for business activities and services related to fisheries as specified in the provisions of this Law have the duty to pay public service fees.

Public service fees for each type shall be determined by an inter-ministerial Prokas between the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries and the Minister of Economy and Finance.

### Chapter XII
### Fisheries Inspection

#### Article 84.-

The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries shall appoint national fisheries inspection officers.

The competent sub-national administrations shall appoint sub-national fisheries inspection officers within their jurisdictions.

of its own. The appointment of fisheries inspectors shall be made based on qualifications determined by a declaration of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

National and sub-national fisheries inspectors shall receive judicial police status to inspect fisheries offenses as specified in this Law, in accordance with the provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure. The Governor of the Board of Governors of Capital, Provinces shall submit requests to grant judicial police status to sub-national fisheries inspectors within their jurisdiction to the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries for further procedures.

The granting of judicial police status and the formalities and procedures for granting judicial police status to fisheries inspectors shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure.

The determination of the jurisdiction for fisheries inspectors who have received judicial police status shall be determined by the decision granting judicial police status.

#### Article 85 .-

Fisheries inspectors have the following rights:

- To inspect and monitor fishing and activities related to fishing or aquaculture, or business, or transport, export, import and transit of fisheries, fisheries products or production, export, import, transit, storage, repackaging or supply, distribution, wholesale, retail of aquatic animal feed or veterinary drugs for aquatic animals, as well as the quality and safety of fisheries, fisheries products, aquatic animal health, residues of veterinary drugs for aquatic animals, or the use of related documents

- To take measures to temporarily suspend fishing and activities related to fishing or aquaculture, or fisheries business, fisheries products, or transport, export, import and transit of fisheries, fisheries products or production, export, import, transit, storage, repackaging or supply, distribution, wholesale, retail of aquatic animal feed or veterinary drugs for aquatic animals, and then report as soon as possible to the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries for further measures.

The formalities and procedures for conducting fisheries inspection shall be determined by a declaration of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 86 .-

Fisheries inspectors who have received judicial police status may exercise powers in accordance with the Code of Criminal Procedure and laws, as well as legal instruments in force, including:

- Search, seize evidence, summon or arrest, and implement other procedural matters

- Temporarily maintain and manage evidence related to fisheries offenses

- Return seized evidence to the possessor or original owner

- Investigate and collect evidence in accordance with the provisions of this Law

- Take other measures within the framework of the implementation of this Law.

In order to operate the inspection of offenses, fisheries inspectors who have received judicial police status must act in accordance with the Code of Criminal Procedure and may request assistance from sub-national authorities, armed forces

and other relevant institutions to participate in investigating, preventing and suppressing fisheries offenses as specified in this Law.

#### Article 87 .-

When operating to implement this Law, fisheries inspectors shall have a mission order.

The uniform, insignia and rank of fisheries inspectors shall be determined by a sub-decree.

The use of uniform, insignia and rank of fisheries inspectors shall be determined by a declaration of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

#### Article 88 .-

Fisheries inspectors who have received judicial police status have the right to use weapons in accordance with laws and legal instruments in force.

#### Article 90 .-

The evidence of fisheries offenses as specified in this Law includes:

១- Endangered fisheries resources, young or breeding aquatic animals, prohibited fishing gear, explosives, and all types of toxic substances.

២- Fisheries, fisheries products, mud/silt residues, veterinary drugs for aquatic animals or aquatic animal feed, and prohibited chemical substances.

៣- Means of transport, machinery, equipment, materials, or electronic devices used as means for committing fisheries offenses.

៤- Weapons or explosives used as means for committing fisheries offenses.

៥- Assets/property obtained as a result of fisheries offenses.

For evidence that cannot be kept for a long time, which may be damaged immediately or lose quality, or cause serious disaster to fisheries resources, or cause danger to public health, fisheries inspectors have the right to release, destroy, or keep for public interest, with a proper report and approval from the prosecutor.

#### Article 91 .-

The costs of evidence and keeper service, costs of transport, maintenance, and destruction of seized evidence shall be borne by the offender. In case the identity of the offender is unknown, all costs shall be borne by the State.

#### Article 92 .-

Evidence related to fisheries offenses as specified in this Law may be confiscated as State property, destroyed, or returned to the possessor or original owner, in accordance with the provisions of this Law and other relevant laws.

Fisheries inspectors who have received judicial police status shall return seized evidence to the possessor or original owner after the transactional fine has been paid and with the approval of the prosecutor, except for evidence that is an endangered fisheries resource, young or breeding aquatic animals, fishing gear

prohibited, all types of toxic substances, prohibited chemical substances, and weapons or explosives used as means for
committing fisheries offenses and assets/property obtained as a result of fisheries offenses.

### Chapter 13
### Penal Provisions

#### Article 93 .-

Sanctions in this Law include administrative sanctions and penal sanctions.

#### Article 94 .-

Administrative sanctions include:

- Written warning

- Temporary suspension of activities, suspension or temporary revocation of certificates or permits

- Cancellation or revocation of certificates or permits

- Other administrative sanctions determined by a declaration of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

The decision to impose administrative sanctions is the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries or the specialized unit in charge of fisheries, or the sub-national administration according to their jurisdiction.

Offenses subject to administrative sanctions under this Law shall be determined by a declaration of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

Any person who is dissatisfied with the decision on administrative sanctions as specified in paragraph 1 above has the right to appeal to the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries within 30 (thirty) days from the date of receipt of the decision. The Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries must decide on the appeal within 30 (thirty) days from the date of receipt of the appeal. Any person who is dissatisfied with the decision of the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries has the right to appeal to a competent court in accordance with the legal procedures in force within 30 (thirty) days from the date of receipt of the decision. The filing of an appeal by the above person does not have the effect of suspending the implementation of the decision on administrative sanctions.

#### Article 95 .-

Penal sanctions include:

- Transactional fine

- Fine

- Imprisonment

The procedures for transactional fines under this Law shall be determined by a sub-decree.

The decision to impose transactional fines for offenses as specified in this Law is the jurisdiction of fisheries inspectors of the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries or fisheries inspectors of the sub-national administration who have received judicial police status.

Payment of a transactional fine shall extinguish the criminal action. The payment of the transactional fine shall not be an obstacle to the implementation of administrative sanctions and other penal sanctions in force under this Law and other criminal laws.

In case the offender refuses to pay the transactional fine, fisheries inspectors who have received judicial police status shall build and send the case file to the competent court in accordance with the procedures in force.

The formalities and procedures for transactional fines, payment of transactional fines, management of receipts for transactional fines, and allocation of revenues from transactional fines shall be determined by an inter-ministerial declaration between the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries and the Minister of Economy and Finance.

The Royal Government may decide to reward officers who have participated in operations to suppress offenses specified in this Law.

#### Article 96 .-

Shall be subject to imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 1 (one) year and a fine from 1,000,000 (one million) Riels to 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels for any person who commits any of the following acts:

១- Destroy sea grass or coral reefs.

២- Encroach, enclose to possess, burn, clear, reclaim, bulldoze, or displace flooded forest or mangrove forest of less than 1 (one) hectare.

៣- Negligently, carelessly, imprudently, or failing to respect obligations causing fire in flooded forest or mangrove forest.

៤- Conduct fisheries business, or fisheries products that are counterfeited/falsified.

៥- Discharge, dump toxic substances, aquaculture waste, veterinary drugs for aquatic animals, or equipment/materials into the fisheries management area without permission or without improving water quality in accordance with technical standards or requirements, causing poisoning or danger to aquatic animals or aquatic plants.

៦- Keep electro-fishing gear for the purpose of illegal fishing.

#### Article 97 .-

Shall be subject to imprisonment from 1 (one) year to 5 (five) years and a fine from 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels to 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels for any person who commits any of the following acts:

១- Conduct medium or large-scale fishing in fisheries conservation areas or Mekong River dolphin conservation areas.

២- Conduct fishing by pumping, draining, or submerging any part of the fisheries management area, which may cause or result in damage to fisheries resources of public interest.

៣- Conduct fishing by using electro-fishing gear, explosives, or toxic substances.

៤- Produce, import, supply, or sell electro-fishing gear.

៥- Clear, bulldoze, build dams, barriers, or block waterways of fisheries communities, or take that area as private property.

៦- Produce, import, export, distribute, or use aquatic animal feed or veterinary drugs for aquatic animals containing abnormal growth promoters that cause danger to public health.

៧- Encroach, enclose to possess, burn, clear, reclaim, bulldoze, or displace flooded forest or mangrove forest of 1 (one) hectare or more.

៨- Intentionally cause fire in flooded forest or mangrove forest.

៩- Change, displace, pull out/dig up, or destroy boundary posts of the fisheries management area.

១០- Dig, excavate, or take gravel, stone, soil, sand, or minerals in fisheries conservation areas.

១១- Conduct fishing that destroys natural habitat, fisheries resources, or fisheries ecosystems within the fisheries management area.

១២- Harvest, buy, sell, donate, or distribute fisheries or fisheries products knowing that they are diseased, toxic, or contain chemical residues exceeding limits, affecting public health.

១៣- Falsify the origin, identity, trade name, packaging, substances, or ingredients of aquatic animal feed or veterinary drugs for aquatic animals used in aquaculture.

For the offenses specified in point 3 of Article 97 of this Law, which are committed by public officials or citizens holding public mandates by election, they shall be defined as corruption offenses subject to penal sanctions under the Law on Anti-Corruption and shall fall under the direct jurisdiction of the Anti-Corruption Unit in investigating and building the corruption case file to the court.

The investigation of corruption offenses in the fisheries sector and the building of corruption case files in this sector to the court shall be the exclusive jurisdiction of the Anti-Corruption Unit, in accordance with the Law on Anti-Corruption.

#### Article 98 .-

Shall be subject to imprisonment from 5 (five) years to 10 (ten) years for any person who commits any act that destroys fisheries resources in the fisheries management area, resulting in serious impact on the environment or ecosystem within the fisheries management area.

#### Article 99 .-

For individuals who commit offenses specified in Article 96, Article 97, and Article 98 of this Law, the court may declare one or more additional penalties as follows:

១- Confiscation of equipment, materials, or objects used during the commission of the offense or kept for committing the offense.

២- Confiscation of objects or funds obtained as a result of the offense.

៣- Confiscation of assets/property obtained as a result of the offense.

៤- Temporary closure of the enterprise or establishment used for committing the offense.

៥- Publication of the sentencing decision.

៦- Prohibition on the possession or carrying of weapons, explosives, and all types of ammunition.

7- Publication of the sentencing decision by all audiovisual telecommunication means.

The substance, forms, and procedures for the implementation of these additional penalties shall comply with the provisions of the Penal Code.

#### Article 100.-

A legal entity may be declared criminally responsible under the conditions stipulated in Article 42 (Criminal Liability of Legal Entities) of the Penal Code for the offenses specified in Article 96, Article 97, and Article 98 of this Law.

A legal entity shall be punished with a fine from 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels to 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels, as well as one or more additional penalties as stipulated in Article 168 (Additional Penalties Applicable to Legal Entities) of the Penal Code, for the offense specified in Article 96 of this Law.

A legal entity shall be punished with a fine from 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels to 500,000,000 (five hundred million) Riels, as well as one or more additional penalties as stipulated in Article 168 (Additional Penalties Applicable to Legal Entities) of the Penal Code, for the offense specified in Article 97 of this Law.

A legal entity shall be punished with a fine from 500,000,000 (five hundred million) Riels to 1,000,000,000 (one billion) Riels, as well as one or more additional penalties as stipulated in Article 168 (Additional Penalties Applicable to Legal Entities) of the Penal Code, for the offense specified in Article 98 of this Law.

#### Article 101.-

The implementation of the provisions of Chapter 13 of this Law does not obstruct the application of other criminal laws in cases where the acts defined as offenses under this Law are also offenses stipulated under other criminal laws.

### Chapter 14
### Transitional Provisions

#### Article 102.-

All legal instruments related to the Law on Fisheries prior to the entry into force of this Law shall continue to remain in force until they are replaced by new legal instruments, in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

#### Article 103.-

Persons who are holding and using legal documents under the Law on Fisheries prior to the entry into force of this Law shall continue to implement them until their expiration. Any application for extension of validity or new applications shall be implemented in accordance with this Law.

### Chapter 15
### Final Provisions

#### Article 104.-

All Laws on Fisheries prior to the entry into force of this Law shall cease to have effect and be replaced.

Provisions defining fishery offenses as specified in the Law on Fisheries prior to the entry into force of this Law shall continue to remain in force after the entry into force of this Law for offenses committed before this Law enters into force, except for the application of the more lenient provisions of the criminal law as specified in the Penal Code.

### Annex of the Law on Fisheries
### Glossary

**Coastal Fishing Area** refers to the marine fishery management area extending from the highest high-tide line to the line with water depth of approximately 20 meters. The criteria for determining the coastal fishing area shall be determined by sub-decree as specified in Article 14.

**Area Designated for the Use of Specific Fishing Gear** refers to a fishing area for public bidding, lease, or investment, by specifying the types of fishing gear, such as bamboo traps (Tru), bamboo traps with fence (Tru Sangkong), or bamboo traps (Bangkang).

**Offshore Fishing Area** refers to the marine fishery management area extending from the outer boundary of the coastal fishing area to the outer boundary of the Exclusive Economic Zone of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

**Public Fishing Area** refers to the fishery management area located outside of fishery conservation areas, community fishing areas, aquaculture development areas, areas designated for the use of specific fishing gear, areas for management and protection of the Mekong River dolphins, and freshwater fishery management areas.

**Community Fishing Area** refers to a part of the fishery management area with defined boundaries and size, allocated to a fishery community for exclusive and sustainable use; within the community fishing area, it includes fishing areas, conservation areas, fishery protection areas, fishery safe haven areas, and aquaculture areas.

**Fishery Safe Haven Area** refers to a part of the community fishing area for protecting one or more types of aquatic animals during a specific period of their life cycle in order to ensure the sustainability of fishery stocks.

**Fishery Protection Area** refers to a part of the community fishing area adjacent to or near the fishery conservation area, where only small-scale fishing is permitted. The fishery protection area serves as a buffer zone.

**Fishery Conservation Area** refers to a part of the fishery management area with defined boundaries and size for conserving aquatic resources, or breeding, growing, or breeding; no type of fishing and activity affecting aquatic resources is permitted, except for technical and scientific fishery research.

**Mekong River Dolphin Conservation Area** refers to a part of the fishery management area designated as the core zone of the management and protection area for the Mekong River dolphins in Kratie and Stung Treng provinces.

**Mekong River Dolphin Protection Area** refers to a part of the fishery management area designated as the buffer zone of the management and protection area for the Mekong River dolphins in Kratie and Stung Treng provinces.

**Landing Site for Fisheries and Fishery Products** refers to the land along the coast or adjacent to the water body which serves as a place for unloading or trading fisheries and fishery products from vessels to the mainland.

**Fishing** refers to the catching, gathering, attracting, extracting, searching for, or harvesting of aquatic animals by using devices, equipment, or other fishing means.

**Small-Scale Fishing** refers to fishing as a traditional family characteristic, using small-scale fishing gear or small-scale paired vessels combined with small-scale gear, such as bamboo traps (Lop), hooks and lines (Sontouch), scoop nets (Dangkang), trap nets (Saiyin), bamboo traps (Tru), and dome-shaped traps (Kum).

**Medium-Scale Fishing** refers to fishing by using medium-scale fishing gear or medium-scale fishing vessels or small-scale paired vessels combined with medium-scale gear, such as seine nets (Oun), purse seine (Manh), scoop nets (Neam), marine trawls (Oun Os at sea), flying trawls (Oun Hoh), and climbing-perch traps (Oun Kranh Krem).

**Large-Scale Fishing** refers to fishing by using large-scale fishing gear or large-scale fishing vessels or medium-scale vessels combined with large-scale gear, such as fish push nets (Chhnang Trei), shrimp push nets (Chhnang Bangkang), and marine trawls using engine power exceeding 500 (five hundred) horsepower.

**Illegal, Unreported and Unregulated Fishing (IUU)** refers to fishing activities that are contrary to national or international management and conservation measures, as follows:

១- Illegal Fishing refers to activities that are:
 - conducted by national or foreign vessels in waters under national jurisdiction, without permission or in contravention of national laws and regulations
 - conducted by vessels flying the flag of States that are parties to a relevant regional fisheries management organization but operate in contravention of the conservation and management measures adopted by that organization and by which the States are bound, or relevant provisions of international law
 - in violation of national laws or international obligations, including those undertaken by cooperating States to a relevant regional fisheries management organization.

២- Unreported Fishing refers to fishing activities that:
 - have not been reported, or have been misreported, to the competent national authority, in contravention of national laws and regulations
 - have been conducted in the area of competence of a relevant regional fisheries management organization which have not been reported or have been misreported, in contravention of the reporting procedures of that organization.

៣- Unregulated Fishing refers to fishing activities:
 - conducted in the area of application of a regional fisheries management organization by vessels without nationality, or by those flying the flag of a State not party to that organization, or by a fishing entity, in a manner that is inconsistent with or contravenes the conservation and management measures of that organization
 - in areas or for fish stocks in relation to which there are no applicable conservation or management measures and where such fishing activities are conducted in a manner inconsistent with State responsibilities for the conservation of living marine resources under international law.

**Fishing in Combination with Pang (fences)** refers to fishing by using bamboo traps (Lop), trap fences (Lop Nor), in combination with Pang, mosquito nets, nylon threads, or seine net fences.

**Transshipment of Fisheries and Fishery Products** refers to the transfer of fisheries and fishery products directly from one vessel to another within a fishery management area, or at port, or at landing sites for fisheries and fishery products.

**Landing of Fisheries and Fishery Products** refers to the bringing of fisheries and fishery products in or unloading them onto the land or at landing sites for fisheries and fishery products.

**Aquaculture Practice** refers to the implementation of aquaculture activities on aquatic resources, which complies with the principles of food safety, animal health and welfare, and the environment.

**Hygiene Practice** refers to the hygiene conditions and necessary measures to ensure food safety at all stages of the food chain, including production, packaging, handling, preservation, and transportation of fisheries and fishery products.

**Transactional Fine** refers to a monetary penalty imposed by a fishery inspection officer who has acquired the status of a judicial police officer.

**Court Fine** refers to a monetary penalty imposed by a judicial institution.

**Fishery Port** refers to a port with infrastructure for fishing vessels or vessels involved in fishing to dock in order to unload fisheries, fishery products, or to use other services such as the supply of fishing gear, fuel, and ice.

**Transit of Fisheries and Fishery Products** refers to the transportation of fisheries and fishery products through the Kingdom of Cambodia.

**Fisheries** refers to the protection, conservation, and development of aquatic resources, fishing activities, activities related to fishing, harvesting of aquatic resources, aquaculture, processing and business, and the export and import of fisheries and fishery products.

**Dai Fish Net (Bag Net)** refers to a fishing gear combined with rafts or boats attached to poles to open the mouth of the Dai net, which has mesh sizes varying from large to small toward the codend of the Dai net, used for catching fish migrating from the Tonle Sap Lake and the Tonle Sap River.

**Area Beyond National Jurisdiction** refers to the marine area located outside the Exclusive Economic Zone, including the high seas and the marine areas of other States.

**Exclusive Economic Zone** refers to the sea within the national jurisdiction, extending adjacent to the baseline to the outer sea for a distance of 200 (two hundred) nautical miles (Nautical Mile) or 370.4 (three hundred seventy point four) kilometers.

**Fishery Tourism Zone** refers to a part of the fishery management area designated as a tourism zone for recreational use, such as coral reef areas, seagrass beds, flooded forests, mangrove creeks, and natural lakes.

**Aquaculture Development Zone** refers to a part of the fishery management area designated as a zone for aquaculture development (land, water surface, or marine territory) for aquaculture.

Habitat refers to the place of shelter for aquatic animals in the fisheries management area, including flooded forest, mangrove forest, coral reef, sea grass, deep pool, lake, reservoir, and conservation areas.

Floodplain refers to flooded lowlands that receive seasonal water regimes, including plains and grasslands.

Fishery Resources refers to freshwater and marine organisms, both animals and plants, living or non-living, including fish, molluscs, amphibians, insects, reptiles, mammals, and other invertebrates originating in water, aquatic flora, coral reefs, flooded forests, and mangrove forests, etc.

Mangrove Forest Domain refers to land that receives tidal water regimes of the sea, covered by mangrove forests and flooded areas that require protection.

Flooded Forest Domain refers to state public land flooded during the rising water season, covered by flooded forests and flooded areas that require protection.

Fishing Vessel refers to a vessel intended to use fishing gear and materials for fishing.

Cambodian Fishing Vessel refers to a marine fishing vessel registered as a Cambodian fishing vessel in accordance with the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

Foreign Fishing Vessel refers to a marine fishing vessel registered as a marine fishing vessel by a foreign competent authority to obtain legality for fishing.

Vessel Related to Fishing refers to a vessel used to support fishing activities, including vessels for transporting, transferring, processing, packaging catch/fishery products, or vessels transporting fuel, fishing gear, and providing other services to support fishing vessels.

Illegal, Unreported and Unregulated Fishing Vessel List (IUU vessel List) refers to the list of illegal, unreported, and unregulated fishing vessels determined by regional fisheries management organizations, showing vessel identities such as name, flag, and call sign.

Operator refers to a person responsible for the operation, direction, and management of a vessel, including the owner, charterer, and beneficiary of the vessel.

Fishery Product Declaration refers to a document for declaring the source of fishery products during landing or transshipment of fishery products.

Vessel Monitoring System refers to equipment for tracking the activities of fishing vessels or vessels related to fishing.

Endangered Fishery Resource Species refers to species of aquatic animals and aquatic flora whose stocks have decreased to a level of concern, such as dolphins, giant barbs, otters, royal turtles, and dugongs.

Harvesting Fishery Products refers to fishing catches or fishery resources harvested from the wild or aquaculture, including animals and aquatic plants.

Fishery Products refers to products processed from fisheries.

Mangrove Forest refers to all types of plants growing in areas that receive tidal water regimes of the sea, including mangrove trees, basak, kranhib, smae, chheu ph'or, ambol, ampov, prong, and chak.

Flooded Forest refers to all types of plants growing in areas flooded seasonally, including reang, sandan, romdeng, tros, tamour, phkaol, phgneur meas, anhchanh, pring, sno, kak, and submerged grass.

Fishing Closed Season refers to the determined period for prohibiting fishing.

Pathology refers to the scientific study of the causes and aspects of disease, focusing on the study of symptoms, disease transmission, disease spread, management, and treatment of disease.

Aquatic Life refers to aquatic animals and aquatic flora.

Aquaculture refers to activities of breeding, hatching, raising, and feeding aquatic animals or planting, cultivating, and breeding aquatic flora.

Aquatic Animals refers to animals whose origin and life cycle are in water, living in water permanently or both in water and on land, including fish, lobsters, shrimp, crabs, snails, freshwater clams, cockles, blood clams, squid, turtles, snakes, frogs, mammals, etc.

Aquatic Flora refers to aquatic plants that grow and develop in water, including sedge, water lilies, romchong, water hyacinth, water spinach, water mimosa, yayoung, water caltrops, seaweed, duckweed, and phytoplankton.

Activities Related to Fishing refers to any operation supporting or preparing for fishing, including landing, transshipment, transportation, packaging, processing of catch or fishery products which have not previously landed at any port, as well as providing personnel, fuel, fishing gear, and other supplies at sea.

Fishery Community refers to a group of Cambodian citizens who voluntarily agree to participate in managing, maintaining, protecting, conserving, developing, and using fishery resources sustainably in the fisheries management area, in accordance with legal regulations.

CITES refers to the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora.

Fishing Lot Specifications, License or Receipt refers to the book for determining specific or private fishing conditions, including type of fishing gear, date of operation, payment of fishing fee, and manner of using fishing gear and other obligations of the licensee.

Logbook of Activities Related to Fishing refers to a book issued by the specialized unit in charge of fisheries for recording information about packaging, processing, transshipment, or transportation of catch, fishery products, provision of services at sea, fuel, and other supplies.

Fishery Logbook refers to a book issued by the specialized unit in charge of fisheries for recording fishing catch, information, and fishing data.

Sanitary Standards refers to necessary technical conditions to ensure sanitation at all stages of the production chain of fisheries business or trade to ensure food safety.

Regional Fisheries Management Organization refers to an organization responsible for formulating regulations and taking measures for the management and conservation of fishery resources in international waters.

Fisheries and Fishery Products Business refers to the purchase, sale, distribution, storage, or processing of fish and fishery products.

Fishing Vessel Observer refers to a person designated by the specialized unit in charge of fisheries to monitor fishing activities and activities related to fishing.

Vessel Tracking Device refers to a device installed on a fishing vessel or a vessel related to fishing to track the activities of the fishing vessel or vessel related to fishing.

Aquatic Animal Medicine refers to any substance or mixture of multiple substances originating from chemical substances, biological products, or micro-organisms for treating aquatic animals.
