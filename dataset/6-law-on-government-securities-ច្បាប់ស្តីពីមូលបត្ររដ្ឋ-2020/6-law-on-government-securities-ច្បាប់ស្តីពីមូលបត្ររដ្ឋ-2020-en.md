# Law (ច្បាប់)
## On (ស្តីពី)
### Government Securities (មូលបត្ររដ្ឋ)

[IMAGE: Seal of the Ministry of Royal Palace]

# Chapter 1

## General Provisions

### Article 1 .-

This Law aims to provide a general framework for managing the issuance, trading, and management of government securities in a transparent, accountable, efficient, and effective manner, as well as to ensure the sustainability of public debt management, aiming to contribute to:

1- Strengthening the cash management requirements of the Royal Government.

2- Responding to financing needs for economic and social development.

3- Developing the securities market and financial market in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

4- Supporting priority and other necessary projects.

### Article 2 .-

This Law has the scope of application to the issuance, trading, and management of government securities within and outside the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Article 3 .-

The definitions of technical terms used in this Law are as follows:

1- Account refers to the general account and special account of the General Department of National Treasury as set out in the Law on Public Finance System in force and other special accounts created by the law of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

2- Coupon refers to the interest to be paid in accordance with the terms of government securities issued under this Law.

3- Subscription refers to the submission of a request to purchase government securities issued under this Law.

4- Government securities refer to bonds, notes, and other financial instruments that certify the recognition of debt issued on behalf of the Royal Government under this Law, including:

A- Treasury bills with a maturity of less than one year.

B- Treasury bonds with a maturity of one year or more.

C- Other government securities.

All above-mentioned government securities do not include securities issued by public enterprises or public administrative institutions of the Royal Government that are not under the scope of this Law.

5- Minister refers to the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

6- Royal Government refers to the Royal Government of the Kingdom of Cambodia (រាជរដ្ឋាភិបាល សំដៅដល់រាជរដ្ឋាភិបាលនៃព្រះរាជាណាចក្រកម្ពុជា).

7- Government Securities Certificate refers to a written financial instrument with a guarantee of full payment obligation from the Royal Government, specifying the duration, conditions, and other obligations of such securities (វិញ្ញាបនបត្រមូលបត្ររដ្ឋ សំដៅដល់ឧបករណ៍ហិរញ្ញវត្ថុជាលាយលក្ខណ៍អក្សរដោយមានការធានានូវការបំពេញកាតព្វកិច្ចទូទាត់ពេញលេញពីរាជរដ្ឋាភិបាល ដោយមានចែងអំពីរយៈពេល លក្ខខណ្ឌ និងកាតព្វកិច្ចផ្សេងៗទៀតរបស់មូលបត្រនោះ).

8- Maturity refers to the date on which the securities are due for repayment (ឥណប្រតិទាន សំដៅដល់កាលបរិច្ឆេទដែលមូលបត្រត្រូវដល់កាលកំណត់សង).

# Chapter 2 (ជំពូកទី ២)
# Issuance and Management of Government Securities (ការបោះផ្សាយនិងការគ្រប់គ្រងមូលបត្ររដ្ឋ)

**Article 5.- (មាត្រា ៥ .-)**

The issuance of government securities shall be implemented as follows (ការបោះផ្សាយមូលបត្ររដ្ឋ ត្រូវអនុវត្តដូចខាងក្រោម៖):

a- Under the limitation of the Law on Finance and under the authority of the Prime Minister, only the Minister is authorized to issue government securities in accordance with the Law on Public Finance System or other laws and regulations in force. The Minister may provide advances, repurchase, and repay all government securities that have matured or have not yet matured. The issuance of government securities must clearly define the expenditure targets for the state's financing requirements (ក- ស្ថិតក្រោមការកម្រិតកំណត់នៃច្បាប់ហិរញ្ញវត្ថុ និងក្រោមអំណាចរបស់នាយករដ្ឋមន្ត្រី មានតែរដ្ឋមន្ត្រីមួយគត់ដែលអាចបោះផ្សាយមូលបត្ររដ្ឋដោយអនុលោមតាមច្បាប់ស្តីពីប្រព័ន្ធហិរញ្ញវត្ថុសាធារណៈឬច្បាប់និងបទប្បញ្ញត្តិពាក់ព័ន្ធជាធរមាន។ រដ្ឋមន្ត្រីអាចផ្តល់ប្រាក់វិវត្ត ទិញមកវិញ និងទូទាត់សង នូវរាល់មូលបត្ររដ្ឋដែលដល់ឬមិនទាន់ដល់ឥណប្រតិទាន។ ការបោះផ្សាយមូលបត្ររដ្ឋត្រូវកំណត់ឱ្យបានច្បាស់លាស់នូវមុខសញ្ញាចំណាយនៃតម្រូវការហិរញ្ញប្បទានរបស់រដ្ឋ។).

b- All operations related to the issuance and management of government securities shall be determined by the Prakas of the Minister (ខ- រាល់ប្រតិបត្តិការពាក់ព័ន្ធនឹងការបោះផ្សាយនិងការគ្រប់គ្រងមូលបត្ររដ្ឋ ត្រូវកំណត់ដោយប្រកាសរបស់រដ្ឋមន្ត្រី។).

c- As provided in the Law on the Organization and Conduct of the National Bank of Cambodia and the Law on the Issuance and Trading of Non-Government Securities, all technical work and operations related to government securities may be executed by the National Bank of Cambodia and/or the Cambodia Securities Market as determined by the Prakas of the Minister, unless otherwise provided by other laws. The National Bank of Cambodia shall establish procedures to separate operations related to government securities from monetary policy functions (គ- ដូចមានចែងក្នុងច្បាប់ស្តីពីការរៀបចំនិងការប្រព្រឹត្តទៅនៃធនាគារជាតិនៃកម្ពុជា និងច្បាប់ស្តីពីការបោះផ្សាយនិងការជួញដូរមូលបត្រមហាជន រាល់ការងារបច្ចេកទេសនិងប្រតិបត្តិការទាក់ទងនឹងមូលបត្ររដ្ឋអាចអនុវត្តដោយធនាគារជាតិនៃកម្ពុជា និង/ឬទីផ្សារមូលបត្រកម្ពុជា តាមការកំណត់ដោយប្រកាសរបស់រដ្ឋមន្ត្រីលើកលែងតែមានចែងក្នុងច្បាប់ផ្សេងទៀត។ ធនាគារជាតិនៃកម្ពុជាត្រូវបង្កើតនីតិវិធីដើម្បីបែងចែកឱ្យដាច់ដោយឡែកពីគ្នារវាងប្រតិបត្តិការទាក់ទងនឹងមូលបត្ររដ្ឋ និងមុខងារគោលនយោបាយរូបិយវត្ថុ។).

Government securities may be issued in national currency or foreign currency (មូលបត្ររដ្ឋអាចត្រូវបានបោះផ្សាយជារូបិយវត្ថុជាតិឬជារូបិយបណ្ណបរទេស។).

Government securities may be issued domestically or abroad, as determined by the Prakas of the Minister (មូលបត្ររដ្ឋអាចត្រូវបានបោះផ្សាយក្នុងប្រទេសឬនៅក្រៅប្រទេស តាមការកំណត់ដោយប្រកាសរបស់រដ្ឋមន្ត្រី។).

Government securities issued for sale are the sole responsibility of the Royal Government (មូលបត្ររដ្ឋដែលត្រូវបានបោះផ្សាយលក់ គឺជាការទទួលខុសត្រូវទាំងស្រុងរបស់រាជរដ្ឋាភិបាល។).

**Article 6.- (មាត្រា ៦ .-)**

The total amount of government securities issuance shall be determined in the Law on Finance for each annual financial management (ទំហំប្រាក់សរុបនៃការបោះផ្សាយមូលបត្ររដ្ឋ ត្រូវកំណត់ក្នុងច្បាប់ហិរញ្ញវត្ថុសម្រាប់ការគ្រប់គ្រងហិរញ្ញវត្ថុប្រចាំឆ្នាំនីមួយៗ។).

The Prime Minister shall make a proposal to the National Assembly to request approval on the issuance of government securities in addition to the total amount already approved by the National Assembly, specifying the purpose of such additional issuance of government securities.

### Article 6 .-

Government securities may be issued in a certificate form or an electronic form, which can be presented and printed in a readable format.

### Article 7 .-

Direct participants in the primary market may be banking and financial institutions licensed by the National Bank of Cambodia, or securities companies licensed by the Securities and Exchange Commission of Cambodia, or other institutions as determined by Prakas of the Minister.

Direct participants in the primary market may bid and/or underwrite government securities on their own behalf and/or as agents for other persons.

### Article 8 .-

Government securities may be publicly offered in the primary market through bidding or other methods as determined by Prakas of the Minister.

### Article 9 .-

To serve the public interest, and to ensure the efficiency and transparency of the issuance and sale of government securities, the Minister shall determine by Prakas:

a- Methods and mechanisms for the issuance and sale of government securities.

b- Forms and procedures related to the management of government securities to be issued.

c- Direct participants and those eligible to participate in the primary market.

d- Appointment of underwriters.

e- Custody of government securities at any custodian operator and registration for trading government securities in the operating securities market.

f- Repayment of principal and coupon rates of government securities, and other important components.

### Article 10 .-

Only the current account of the National Treasury at the National Bank of Cambodia shall be used by the Minister for the purpose of managing government securities.

Revenue from the issuance of government securities shall be transferred to the current account for revenue from the issuance of government securities, which is a revenue sub-account of the current account of the National Treasury. Revenue from the issuance of government securities shall be considered as national budget revenue.

Expenses on coupon rate payments and principal payments, or the repurchase of government securities, shall be made through the current account of the National Bank of Cambodia and shall be determined in the state expenditure budget.

### Article 11 .-

The right to claim compensation for the amount of government securities shall be exercised within a period of 5 (five) years for the principal and coupon, starting from the maturity date. This limitation period shall be suspended or interrupted in accordance with the Civil Code and provisions in force.

Public assets and state revenues shall be protected from seizure and other prohibitions.

### Article 12 .-

All trading transactions of government securities shall be subject to the procedures of the certificated form in force and may receive incentives and tax concessions in accordance with the laws and regulations in force.

# Chapter 3

## Secondary Market of Government Securities

### Article 13 .-

The trading, transfer, or pledging of government securities by the National Bank of Cambodia, or by participants in the interbank market, or the derivative securities market, or by other eligible participants, as well as the relevant mechanisms, shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister.

Participants as specified in the first paragraph of this Article may trade, transfer, or pledge government securities for themselves and/or as agents for other persons.

The rights and responsibilities of participants as specified in the first paragraph of this Article shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister.

# Chapter 4

## Penal Provisions

### Article 14 .-

Any person who counterfeits government securities in any form shall be held responsible for the acts they committed and shall be punished in accordance with the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Article 15 .-

Civil servants or agents who are involved in the implementation of bidding or involved in other obligations and fail to perform their duties in accordance with the provisions of this Law shall be held responsible for the acts they committed and shall be punished in accordance with the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

# Chapter 5

## Final Provisions

### Article 16 .-

The Law on Government Securities, promulgated by Royal Kram No. NS/RKM/0107/001 dated January 10, 2007, and any provisions contrary to this Law, shall be abrogated.
