### Decides

### Chapter 1 General Provisions

#### Article 1 ..

This Sub-Decree determines the organization and functioning of the Ministry of Justice.

### Chapter 2 Mission, Roles, Duties, and Structure

#### Article 2 ..

The Ministry of Justice has the competence to lead and manage the justice sector of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 3 ..

The Ministry of Justice has the following roles and duties:

- Participate in protecting the independence of judges in the performance of their functions

- Ensure justice and equality for every individual before the law

- Organize and inspect the administrative functioning of court bodies and prosecution bodies

- Organize, lead, manage, and inspect prosecution bodies

- Ensure the functioning of court bodies

- Ensure the enforcement of laws and all decisions of court bodies and prosecution bodies

- Monitor and inspect the enforcement of laws and all decisions of court bodies and prosecution bodies

- Organize, manage, and compile decisions of court bodies

- Conduct inspections in investigating complaints requesting intervention from individuals or legal entities involved in disputes in court bodies and prosecution bodies and according to necessary requirements

- Ensure the functioning of the General Secretariat of the Supreme Council of the Magistracy

- Supervise prosecutors and prisons in law enforcement

- Draft laws and other legal instruments in the field of justice

- Receive, review and provide advice on draft laws and legal instruments of other ministries and institutions

- Prepare and manage instructions on the implementation of laws related to the field of justice

- Lead and manage the Royal Academy for Judicial Professions

- Lead, manage and develop judicial police and out-of-court dispute resolution

- Manage and coordinate work related to legal aid and the right to access justice

- Lead and manage judicial professionals and those who serve in work related to the field of justice

- Prepare, lead, manage and inspect evidence, seized assets and proceeds of crime

- Research and investigate crimes affecting state security and other crimes that seriously affect the economy and society as assigned by the Royal Government

- Research, train, educate and disseminate laws related to the field of justice

- Monitor training, education and dissemination of laws related to the field of justice

- Establish international relations and cooperation in relevant legal and justice fields

- Manage and issue criminal record extracts

- Receive and manage clemency requests in accordance with procedures determined by law

- Receive and manage requests for conditional release in accordance with procedures determined by law

- Manage all civil servants serving under the Ministry

- Manage court officials and other professionals related to the field of justice

- Manage the budget of the Ministry of Justice, judicial organizations, prosecution organizations and the Supreme Council of the Magistracy

- Prepare monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, nine-month and annual performance reports for the Royal Government

- Perform other duties assigned by the Royal Government.

#### Article 5 .-

The Ministry of Justice has the following structure:

- Cabinet of the Minister

- General Secretariat

- General Department of Judicial Administrative Affairs

- General Department of Civil Affairs

- General Department of Prosecutorial and Criminal Affairs

- General Department of Criminal Justice Affairs

- General Department of International Affairs and Judicial Development

- General Inspectorate of Judicial Affairs

- Internal Audit Department

- Information Unit.

#### Article 5 ..

The General Secretariat is led by a General Secretary, with a rank equivalent to Director General, and assisted by a number of Deputy General Secretaries, with a rank equivalent to Deputy Director General.

The General Department is led by a Director General and assisted by a number of Deputy Directors General.

The General Inspectorate of Judicial Affairs is led by an Inspector General, with a rank equivalent to Director General, and assisted by a number of Deputy Inspectors General, with a rank equivalent to Deputy Director General.

The organizational chart of the Ministry of Justice is annexed to this Sub-Decree.

#### Article 6 ..

The organization and functioning of departments and units below the department level of the Ministry of Justice shall be determined by a declaration (Prokas) of the Minister of the Ministry of Justice.

### Chapter 3
### Cabinet of the Minister

#### Article 7 ..

The Cabinet of the Minister is responsible for all missions determined by Sub-Decree No. 11 ANKR.BK dated January 31, 2017 on the Organization and Functioning of the Cabinets of the Prime Minister, Deputy Prime Ministers, Senior Ministers, Ministers and Secretaries of State as Heads of Institutions, and other regulations in force.

### Chapter 4
### General Secretariat

#### Article 8 ..

The General Secretariat plays a chief-of-staff role for the Ministry on support services to ensure the normal functioning of the Ministry of Justice and to manage administration, personnel, finance, and planning, statistics, archives, information technology, and digital equipment of the Ministry and various units under the Ministry of Justice.

The General Secretariat has 4 departments under its supervision:

- Department of Personnel Affairs

- Department of Financial Affairs

- Department of Administrative Affairs

- Department of Information Technology and Digital Equipment.

#### Article 9 ..

The Department of Personnel Affairs is responsible for:

- Preparing action plans and work programs of the Department, and proposing measures to strengthen the efficiency of the Department's work

- Managing all personnel and civil servants of the Ministry of Justice by communicating with the Ministry of Civil Service and other relevant institutions

- Preparing procedures for recruitment, appointment, promotion of ranks/grades, discipline, transfer, and retirement as determined by law

- Preparing all administrative files for managing the careers of civil servants of the Ministry of Justice

- Preparing procedures and submitting proposals to award work medals and various decorations to leadership, civil servants of the Ministry, and experts of the Ministry who have good work achievements

- Determining frameworks and the number of personnel, as well as collecting relevant information of units under the Ministry

- Preparing personnel statistics and executive information charts

- Participating in preparing the annual budget project and budget strategic plan of the General Secretariat

- Preparing monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, nine-month, and annual performance reports for leadership

- Performing other duties assigned by the Minister.

The Department of Personnel Affairs is led by a Director and assisted by a number of Deputy Directors.

#### Article 10 ..

The Department of Financial Affairs is responsible for:

- Preparing action plans and work programs of the Department, and proposing measures to strengthen the efficiency of the Department's work

- Managing the budget and finance of the Ministry of Justice and various units under the Ministry of Justice, with the exception of the budget and finance of the General Department of Judicial Administration

- Preparing the budget strategic plan of the Ministry

- Collecting needs, preparing income lists, preparing budget expenditure programs of the Ministry, managing materials, equipment, organizing repairs and construction of infrastructure of the Ministry and various units under the Ministry of Justice, unless otherwise specified in separate regulations

- Collecting and coordinating all revenues and expenditures of the Ministry

- Directly managing the revenues of the Ministry

- Managing movable property, immovable property, inventory of materials and making the inventory list of the Ministry

- Managing daily list of vouchers/checks and physical accounting

- Managing and allocating revenue appropriately in accordance with financial law

- Preparing information technology systems for financial activities and account management

- Implementing public procurement of the Ministry

- Taking charge of printing work in cooperation with relevant units

- Proposing plans for economic, social affairs and public investment of the Ministry

- Proposing the Ministry's budget and monitoring its implementation

- Participating in preparing the annual budget project and budget strategic plan of the General Secretariat

- Preparing monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, nine-month, and annual performance reports for leadership

- Performing other duties assigned by the Minister.

The Department of Financial Affairs is led by a Director and assisted by a number of Deputy Directors.

#### Article 11 ..

The Department of Administrative Affairs is responsible for:

- Preparing action plans and work programs of the Department, and proposing measures to strengthen the efficiency of the Department's work

- Coordinating administrative management activities of the Ministry

- Ensuring administrative efficiency, security and managing social work of the Ministry

- Organizing meetings, congresses, conferences, workshops, protocol work and various ceremonies of the Ministry

- Organizing maintenance of various infrastructures of the Ministry

- Organizing security, order and hygiene of the Ministry

- Managing and circulating various administrative documents of the Ministry

- Managing the archiving of administrative documents of the Ministry

- Preparing services for receiving and providing information

- Coordinating and communicating with journalists and various media networks

- Cooperating, preparing and printing the information bulletin of the Ministry

- Summarizing and preparing work and activity reports of the General Secretariat and the Ministry

- Collecting, receiving and managing statistical lists from all specialized units of the Ministry to transmit the Ministry's work statistics to Ministry leadership, relevant institutions, the Office of the Council of Ministers, and the Royal Government

- Participating in preparing the annual budget project and budget strategic plan of the General Secretariat

- Preparing monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, nine-month, and annual performance reports for leadership

- Performing other duties assigned by the Minister.

The Department of Administrative Affairs is led by a Director and assisted by a number of Deputy Directors.

#### Article 12 ..

The Department of Information Technology and Digital Transformation is responsible for:

- Preparing action plans and work programs of the Department, and proposing measures to strengthen the work efficiency of the Department

- Implementing instructions and decisions of the Royal Government on work related to the development of information and communication technology sector

- Developing and strengthening information and communication technology strategies and policies to support the development of digital systems to be more efficient and transparent

- Managing general work related to the development of information technology and telecommunication systems for the justice sector

- Organizing, managing network infrastructure, repairing, and maintaining computers, IT equipment, and computer software of the Ministry, aiming to ensure the efficiency of managing necessary data for promoting the justice system

- Creating and managing websites and other social media networks of the Ministry

- Proposing plans for IT strategy and digital transformation projects

- Cooperating with the unit in charge of international cooperation to conduct cooperation within national, regional, and international frameworks on work related to the development of information and communication technology sector for the Ministry and the entire justice system

- Training and disseminating the use and management of information technology systems and computer software to officers of the Ministry and officers of the justice sector

- Collecting and compiling various work documents for dissemination through information technology systems

- Organizing, compiling, and managing database systems of the Ministry, and participating in defining development programs

- Organizing, managing, and developing the library of the Ministry

- Creating security systems and keeping documents safely for the Ministry and judicial bodies

- Participating in preparing annual budget projects and budget strategic plans of the General Secretariat

- Preparing reports on monthly, quarterly, semesterly, nine-month, and annual work activities for the leadership

- Performing other tasks assigned by the Minister.

The Department of Information Technology and Digital Transformation shall be led by a Director and assisted by a number of Deputy Directors as assistants.

### Chapter 5
### General Department of Judicial Administration

#### Article 13 ..

The General Department of Judicial Administration shall serve as a staff to the Ministry in coordinating, managing, and supervising the administrative units of judicial bodies and prosecution secretariats, as well as affairs

public affairs, procedural administration, financial affairs, human resource management affairs, bailiff affairs, clerk secretariat affairs, and archiving and sending affairs, and promoting the use of technology as a supporting tool for administrative work, registration, and case management, aiming to enhance and ensure the smooth functioning of justice.

The General Department of Judicial Administration has 6 subordinate departments:

- Department of Technical Affairs and Judicial Administration Modernization

- Department of Financial Affairs of Courts

- Department of Human Resource Management of Judicial Administration

- Department of Bailiff Affairs

- Department of Clerk Secretariat Affairs

- Department of Archiving and Sending Affairs.

#### Article 14 ..

The Department of Technical Affairs and Judicial Administration Modernization is responsible for:

- Preparing action plans and work programs of the Department, and proposing measures to strengthen the work efficiency of the Department

- Communicating and coordinating administrative work and judicial administration IT affairs with central entities and judicial administration units

- Coordinating and managing administrative support services for the functioning of judicial bodies and prosecution secretariats

- Coordinating, managing, and guiding judicial administrative work including public affairs, registration systems, archiving systems, case management, and procedural administration for judicial bodies and prosecution secretariats

- Participating in coordinating the management of archiving, bail/security deposition, and sending at various courts

- Ensuring efficiency, administrative security, order, and social work management of judicial bodies and prosecution secretariats

- Preparing legal instruments related to the organization and functioning of the administrative section of judicial bodies

- Supervising the administrative functioning of administrative units of judicial bodies and prosecution secretariats

- Monitoring, supervising, and evaluating the efficiency of administrative work management, management, and handling of court cases of judicial bodies and prosecution secretariats

- Reviewing and providing comments on all requests for intervention related to judicial administration units, and being able to conduct inspections as necessary

- Developing and managing other necessary information and technology affairs to facilitate and ensure the efficiency of the management system and administrative functioning of judicial bodies and prosecution secretariats

- Collecting, receiving, and managing statistical data of court cases at courts of all levels

- Collecting and compiling all decisions related to court cases at courts of all levels for publication and dissemination as necessary, in cooperation with the unit in charge

- Organizing meetings, congresses, conferences, and various workshops to collect information and experience in developing the administrative management system of judicial bodies and prosecution secretariats, and information technology system for case management

- Analyzing research and producing new information technology programs, and modernizing information technology to better serve court administrative management with speed and transparency

- Cooperating with relevant units in managing and developing criminal and civil case registration programs of courts to be better and meet necessary needs

- Receiving, compiling, archiving, and preparing for publication of reports, statistics, and data on personnel work, administrative work, and finance of judicial administration units

- Coordinating and managing information technology support services for court administrative operations through information technology systems at courts

- Organizing, developing, and managing the website as well as social media networks of the General Department

- Organizing and managing internet connection network infrastructure and other services related to information and communication technology infrastructure

- Organizing and managing intranet connection network infrastructure with equipment for document management at courts

- Managing the data center and data recovery which are achievements

- Managing repairs and maintenance of infrastructure, computers, and various information and communication technology equipment

- Cooperating with the unit in charge of international cooperation to conduct cooperation with foreign countries, development partners, organizations, and various entities on work related to administrative affairs, case management, collection and compilation of judgments, verdicts, and decisions of courts of all levels, preparation and development of information technology systems supporting the operation and functioning of courts and judicial administration units, and public affairs of judicial bodies and prosecution secretariats

- Advising and consulting on finding solutions for security, communication, and information sections

- Participating in training and various dissemination programs at both national and international levels related to security, communication, and information sections

- Participating in preparing annual budget projects and budget strategic plans of the General Department

- Summarizing and preparing reports on work and various activities of judicial bodies and prosecution secretariats

- Preparing reports on monthly, quarterly, semesterly, nine-month, and annual work activities for the leadership

- Performing other tasks assigned by the Minister.

The Department of Technical Affairs and Judicial Administration Modernization shall be led by a Director and assisted by a number of Deputy Directors as assistants.

#### Article 15 ..

The Department of Financial Affairs of Courts is responsible for:

- Preparing action plans and work programs of the Department, and proposing measures to strengthen the work efficiency of the Department

- Coordinating, preparing, and managing budget and finance for the functioning of judicial bodies and prosecution secretariats

- Coordinating and managing financial support services for the functioning of judicial bodies and prosecution secretariats

- Participating in analyzing budget planning projects of judicial bodies and prosecution secretariats

- Managing, supervising, and guiding judicial bodies and prosecution secretariats on financial affairs

- Coordinating, collecting, and managing revenue of administrative units of judicial bodies and prosecution secretariats obtained from, inter alia, court fees, tax on applications, and other revenues as determined by law

- Coordinating, supervising, and managing movable property, immovable property, inventory materials, and preparing inventory lists of judicial bodies and prosecution secretariats

- Coordinating and supervising public procurement affairs of judicial bodies and prosecution secretariats

- Preparing projects for economic, social, and public investment plans of judicial bodies and prosecution secretariats

- Participating in organizing dissemination and training work on skills related to court financial affairs

- Participating in preparing annual budget projects and strategic plans of the General Department

- Preparing reports on monthly, quarterly, semesterly, nine-month, and annual work activities for the leadership

- Performing other tasks assigned by the Minister.

The Department of Financial Affairs of Courts shall be led by a Director and assisted by a number of Deputy Directors as assistants.

#### Article 16

The Department of Human Resources Management of the Court Administration shall be responsible for:

- Formulating action plans and work programs of the Department, as well as proposing measures to strengthen the work efficiency of the Department;

- Managing all personnel and officers of the court administration organizations and legal experts;

- Coordinating and managing support services in terms of personnel for the functioning of judicial bodies and prosecution institutions;

- Preparing master plans to determine the human resources needs and development of judicial bodies and prosecution institutions;

- Determining the requirements, staff frames, and number of personnel, as well as collecting relevant information of judicial bodies and prosecution institutions;

- Reviewing, providing opinions on, and preparing requests for principles on recruitment, appointment, promotion of ranks, classes, disciplinary actions, transfers, awarding decorations, retirement, or termination of employment for administrative officials and personnel of the court administration organizations of judicial bodies and prosecution institutions in accordance with the law;

- Participating in preparing and implementing plans, dissemination programs, training, and upgrading the capacity of administrative officials and personnel of the court administration of the courts of adjudication;

- Formulating, determining, and managing the remuneration and other allowances of judicial bodies and prosecution institutions;

- Monitoring, inspecting, and evaluating the efficiency of personnel management of judicial bodies and prosecution institutions;

- Cooperating with the unit in charge of international cooperation in coordinating, planning, and managing the departure of personnel and administrative officials of the court administration organizations for study and internships abroad;

- Studying and researching the functioning of court administration systems of other countries;

- Participating in preparing the annual budget project and strategic plans of the General Department;

- Preparing monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, and annual reports on work activities for the leadership;

- Implementing other tasks assigned by the Minister.

The Department of Human Resources Management of the Court Administration shall be led by a Director and assisted by a number of Deputy Directors as assistants.

#### Article 17

The Department of Bailiffs shall be responsible for:

- Formulating action plans and work programs of the Department, as well as proposing measures to strengthen the work efficiency of the Department;

- Coordinate, manage, and inspect all bailiffs in the Kingdom of Cambodia;

- Formulate statutes and legal normative documents related to the profession of bailiffs;

- Cooperate with relevant units in organizing training and upgrading programs to strengthen the capacity of bailiffs;

- Coordinate and ensure the smoothness and efficiency of the practice of the bailiff profession;

- Evaluate the needs of bailiffs;

- Participate in preparing and recruiting bailiffs;

- Review, provide opinions on, and prepare requests for principles on recruitment, appointment, promotion of ranks, classes, disciplinary actions, transfers, awarding decorations, retirement, or termination of practice as a bailiff in accordance with the law;

- Coordinate, review, and resolve various issues related to the practice of the bailiff profession;

- Conduct economic analysis of bailiff professional activities and study and research the evolution of the bailiff profession;

- Prepare statistics and summary reports on bailiff affairs;

- Participate in preparing the annual budget project and the strategic budget plan of the General Department;

- Prepare monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, and annual reports on work activities for the leadership;

- Implement other tasks assigned by the Minister.

The Department of Bailiffs shall be led by a Director and assisted by a number of Deputy Directors as assistants.

#### Article 18

The Department of Court Clerks shall be responsible for:

- Formulating action plans and work programs of the Department, as well as proposing measures to strengthen the work efficiency of the Department;

- Coordinating, managing, and inspecting all court clerks in the Kingdom of Cambodia;

- Managing prospectively the work and profession of court clerk officers;

- Managing the administrative aspect of court clerk officers;

- Formulating statutes and legal normative documents related to the body of court clerks;

- Coordinating and ensure the smoothness and efficiency of the practice of the court clerk profession;

- Studying and evaluating the needs of court clerks;

- Participate in preparing and recruiting court clerks;

- Cooperate with relevant units in organizing training and upgrading programs to strengthen the capacity of court clerks;

- Review, provide opinions on, and prepare requests for principles on recruitment, appointment, promotion of ranks, classes, disciplinary actions, transfers, awarding decorations, retirement, or termination of practice as a court clerk in accordance with the law;

- Coordinate, review, and resolve various issues related to the practice of the court clerk profession;

- Promote the value of competence and the evolution of the court clerk profession;

- Prepare statistics and summary reports on court clerk affairs;

- Participate in preparing the annual budget project and the strategic budget plan of the General Department;

- Prepare monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, and annual reports on work activities for the leadership;

- Implement other tasks assigned by the Minister.

The Department of Court Clerks shall be led by a Director and assisted by a number of Deputy Directors as assistants.

#### Article 19

The Department of Custody and Consignment shall be responsible for:

- Formulating action plans and work programs of the Department, as well as proposing measures to strengthen the work efficiency of the Department;

- Formulating legal norms, templates, and procedures related to custody, guarantee, and consignment affairs, in accordance with the Civil Code, the Code of Civil Procedure, the Code of Criminal Procedure, and other regulations;

- Organizing, managing, arranging, and inspecting the registration system for custody, guarantee, and consignment affairs, in accordance with the Civil Code, the Code of Civil Procedure, the Code of Criminal Procedure, and other regulations;

- Participating in reviewing and providing opinions on draft laws and various legal normative documents related to custody, guarantee, and consignment affairs;

- Participating in formulating and implementing plans and educational and dissemination programs to citizens and various organizations regarding the registration system for custody, guarantee, and consignment affairs;

- Participating in preparing and implementing training plans on skills related to custody, guarantee, and consignment affairs;

- Developing and managing information technology affairs and other necessary technologies to facilitate and ensure the efficiency of the registration management system for custody, guarantee, and consignment affairs;

- Compiling, arranging the publication, and disseminating various documents related to custody, guarantee, and consignment affairs, in cooperation with the unit in charge as necessary;

- Monitoring, inspecting, and evaluating the efficiency of custody, guarantee, and consignment management;

- Participating in preparing the annual budget project and the strategic budget plan of the General Department;

- Preparing monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, and annual reports on work activities for the leadership;

- Implementing other tasks assigned by the Minister.

The Department of Custody and Consignment shall be led by a Director and assisted by a number of Deputy Directors as assistants.

### Chapter 6
#### General Department of Civil Affairs

#### Article 20

The General Department of Civil Affairs shall act as a staff of the Ministry in coordinating and managing civil affairs, family affairs, registration of matrimonial property, registration of legal entities as determined by law, commercial affairs, labor affairs and cross-sectoral affairs, bankruptcy affairs, temporary administrators and liquidators, execution of civil decisions affairs, alternative dispute resolution and legal aid, notary affairs, as well as formulating strategies for the development of technical work in the civil field.

The General Department of Civil Affairs has 5 subordinate departments:

- Department of Civil Legal Affairs and Cross-Sectoral Affairs

- Department of Bankruptcy Affairs, Temporary Administrators and Liquidators

- Department of Execution of Civil Decisions Affairs

- Department of Out-of-Court Dispute Resolution and Legal Aid

- Department of Notaries.

#### Article 21

The Department of Civil Legal Affairs and Cross-Sectoral Affairs shall be responsible for:

- Formulating action plans and work programs of the Department, as well as proposing measures to strengthen the work efficiency of the Department;

- Formulating visions, strategies, and action plans to strengthen, reform, and develop civil law;

- Reviewing and providing opinions on various issues related to civil affairs and cross-sectoral affairs;

- Proposing programs to draft laws and various legal normative documents related to the civil field;

- Participating in studying, researching, and preparing legal norms to operationalize commercial courts, labor courts, and other specialized courts;

- Preparing drafts of laws and various legal normative documents related to civil affairs and cross-sectoral affairs, registration of matrimonial property, and registration of legal entities;

- Organizing, managing, arranging, and inspecting the registration system of matrimonial property contracts and registration of legal entities;

- Developing and managing information technology affairs and other necessary technologies to facilitate and ensure the efficiency of the registration system for matrimonial property and registration of legal entities;

- Reviewing and providing opinions on draft laws and legal normative documents of ministries and institutions related to the civil field.

- Cooperating with the unit in charge of international cooperation to conduct international cooperation with foreign countries, development partners, and various organizations on national and international legislative work related to the civil sector

- Participating with relevant ministries and institutions in consulting and negotiating on international treaties, conventions, or agreements related to the civil sector

- Reviewing, monitoring, and evaluating the implementation of laws and regulations related to the civil sector and cross-sectoral matters before the court

- Reviewing and providing comments on various issues related to the implementation of the Civil Code, Code of Civil Procedure, laws, and regulations in the civil sector

- Preparing explanatory notes on legal provisions in the civil sector

- Reviewing and providing comments on various requests for intervention in civil cases

- Monitoring, inspecting, and evaluating the effectiveness of registration management and legal entity registration work

- Participating in preparing and implementing plans and educational, dissemination, and training programs on laws related to the civil sector, matrimonial property registration, and legal entity registration

- Collecting and compiling national and international laws and legal instruments in the civil sector

- Participating in preparing the annual budget plan and budget strategic plan of the General Department

- Preparing reports on work activities on a monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, nine-month, and annual basis for the leadership

- Performing other tasks assigned by the Minister.

The Department of Civil and Cross-Sectoral Legal Affairs is led by a Director and assisted by a number of Deputy Directors as assistants.

### Article 22 .-

The Department of Insolvency, Provisional Administrator, and Liquidator Affairs is responsible for:

- Preparing action plans and work programs of the Department, as well as proposing plans to strengthen the work efficiency of the Department

- Managing, reviewing, and providing comments on work related to insolvency, provisional administrator, and liquidator affairs

- Preparing draft laws and relevant legal instruments to manage the profession of provisional administrators and liquidators, including defining conditions, formats, and procedures for granting and managing licenses or certificates for provisional administrators and liquidators

- Preparing and coordinating the management, issuance, and revocation of licenses or certificates for provisional administrators and liquidators

- Preparing the national list of provisional administrators and liquidators

- Preparing and drafting the code of ethics for provisional administrators and liquidators

- Monitoring and inspecting the performance of functions as provisional administrators and liquidators

- Reviewing and providing comments on disciplinary actions against provisional administrators and liquidators

- Reviewing and providing comments on draft laws and legal instruments prepared by various ministries and institutions related to insolvency, provisional administrator, and liquidator affairs

- Studying, researching, and compiling national and international laws and legal instruments related to insolvency, provisional administrator, and liquidator affairs

- Studying to manage the remuneration rates for provisional administrators and liquidators

- Cooperating with the unit in charge of international cooperation to conduct international cooperation with foreign countries, development partners, and various organizations on national and international legislative work related to insolvency, provisional administrator, and liquidator affairs

- Participating in reviewing and providing comments on the preparation of international agreements related to insolvency, provisional administrator, and liquidator affairs

- Reviewing and providing comments on various requests for intervention in cases related to insolvency and liquidation affairs

- Participating in preparing and implementing plans and educational, dissemination, and training programs on laws related to insolvency, provisional administrator, and liquidator affairs

- Participating in preparing and implementing training and capacity building programs related to insolvency and liquidation affairs

- Participating in preparing the annual budget plan and budget strategic plan of the General Department

- Preparing reports on work activities on a monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, nine-month, and annual basis for the leadership

- Performing other tasks assigned by the Minister.

The Department of Insolvency, Provisional Administrator, and Liquidator Affairs is led by a Director and assisted by a number of Deputy Directors as assistants.

### Article 23 .-

The Department of Civil Judgment Execution Affairs is responsible for:

- Preparing action plans and work programs of the Department, as well as proposing plans to strengthen the work efficiency of the Department

- Reviewing and providing comments on various issues related to the execution of civil judgments of courts at all levels

- Coordinating and monitoring the execution of civil judgments of courts at all levels in accordance with laws and legal instruments in force

- Reviewing and providing comments on various requests for intervention related to the execution of civil judgments of courts at all levels

- Participating in preparing and implementing plans and educational, dissemination, and training programs on laws related to the execution of civil judgments of courts at all levels

- Participating in preparing the annual budget plan and budget strategic plan of the General Department

- Preparing reports on work activities on a monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, nine-month, and annual basis for the leadership

- Performing other tasks assigned by the Minister.

The Department of Civil Judgment Execution Affairs is led by a Director and assisted by a number of Deputy Directors as assistants.

### Article 24 .-

The Department of Out-of-Court Dispute Resolution and Legal Aid is responsible for:

- Preparing action plans and work programs of the Department, as well as proposing measures to strengthen the work efficiency of the Department

#### Article 46.-

Civil servants of the General Inspectorate of Judicial Affairs may obtain the status of judicial police officers in accordance with the law and legal instruments in force. The appointment of civil servants of the General Inspectorate of Judicial Affairs as judicial police officers shall be made by a Proclamation (Prakas) of the Minister of Justice.

### Chapter 11
### Department of Internal Audit

#### Article 47.-

The Department of Internal Audit is directly under the Minister of Justice and is responsible for implementing duties in accordance with Sub-Decree No. 40 ANKR.BK dated February 15, 2005 on the Organization and Functioning of Internal Audit at Institutions, Ministries, and Public Enterprises, and other regulations in force.

The Department of Internal Audit is led by a Director and assisted by a number of Deputy Directors as assistants.

### Chapter 12
### Information Unit

#### Article 48.-

The Information Unit is responsible for the following tasks:

- Serving as a technical staff on spokesmanship and information dissemination

- Research and compile all national and international daily information related to the field of law and justice

- Cooperate with relevant ministries and institutions to collect information related to the field of law and justice, and prepare documents to clarify to the public in a timely and effective manner

- Serve as a single window for receiving requests and providing various information within the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Justice, in accordance with the laws and procedures in force

- Prepare monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, nine-month, and annual reports on work activities for the leadership

- Perform other tasks assigned by the Minister.

The Information Unit is led by a Chief who has the rank equivalent to a Department Director, and is assisted by a number of Deputy Chiefs who have the rank equivalent to a Deputy Department Director as assistants.

### Chapter 13 Financial Control Unit

#### Article 49 .-

The Financial Control Unit is established by the Ministry of Economy and Finance under the conditions of the provisions specified in Sub-Decree No. 81 dated November 16, 1995 on the Establishment of Financial Control Institutions on State Budget Expenditure in Ministries and other regulations in force.

### Chapter 14 Transitional Provisions

#### Article 50 .-

All legal instruments related to the appointment of leadership, as well as legal instruments related to the establishment or organization and functioning of various units under the Ministry of Justice existing prior to this Sub-Decree, shall continue to remain in force until new legal instruments are created to replace them in accordance with the substance of this Sub-Decree.

### Chapter 15 Final Provisions

#### Article 51 .-

Sub-Decree No. 240 ANKR.BK dated August 29, 2014 on the Organization and Functioning of the Ministry of Justice and any provisions contrary to this Sub-Decree shall be abrogated and replaced by this Sub-Decree.

#### Article 52 .-

Minister in charge of the Office of the Council of Ministers, Minister of Economy and Finance, Minister of Justice, Minister of Civil Service, Ministers of all ministries, and heads of all relevant institutions shall be responsible for implementing this Sub-Decree according to their respective duties from the date of signature.
