#### Promulgates

The Law on Trusts, which was adopted by the National Assembly on December 13, 2018 at the 1st session of its 6th legislature, and which was reviewed and entirely approved by the Senate as to its form and legal spirit on December 24, 2018 at its extraordinary plenary session of the 4th legislature, the text of which is as follows:

# Law

## on

# Trusts

### Chapter 1

### General Provisions

#### Article 1.-

This law aims to manage trusts established in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 2.-

This law has the objective to determine rules, procedures for establishment, registration, management, and supervision of trusts.

#### Article 3.-

This law has the scope of application to trusts established in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 4.-

Key terms used in this law are defined in the glossary, which is the annex of this law.

### Chapter 2

### Trusts

#### Section 1

#### Parties, Establishment, and Effect of Trusts

#### Article 5.-

The parties to a trust mainly consist of three (3) parties: the trustor, the trustee, and the beneficiary.

A trust may have other participating parties, such as a contributor to the trust and a substitute trustor.

#### Article 6.-

A trust is established by a trustor or by regulations in force and shall have a purpose not contrary to public order.

The forms of trust establishment are as follows:

- In the case where a trust is created by a trustor, the trustor shall transfer his/her trust fund property to the trustee through a trust instrument specifying the specific purpose of the trust and/or identifying the beneficiary.

- In the case where a trust is created by provisions in force, the trust property shall be automatically transferred to the trustee in order to manage and dispose of the property in accordance with the provisions in force, with the specific purpose of the trust and/or the identity of the beneficiary specified.

#### Article 8 .-

A trust shall have effect when the trustee agrees to accept the appointment in writing.

### Section 2
### Purpose of Trusts

#### Article 9 .-

Trusts shall be classified into 4 (four) types as follows:

- Commercial trust

- Public trust

- Social trust

- Personal trust.

#### Article 10 .-

A commercial trust is created for the purpose of seeking profit for the benefit of the trust contributor or any specific person designated by the trust contributor, including:

1- Pension fund, education fund, and other similar funds that any person transfers properties to a trustee, and the properties are transferred back to that person or transferred to another person which meets some conditions as follows:

a- There is a transfer of property or funds into a trust by any person to the trustee on a regular basis, and the trustee manages and disposes of this fund for the specific benefit of this person or other persons, and

b- When the property or funds of other trust contributors are pooled in an investment scheme and are invested by the trustee for the benefit of all trust contributors according to the specified conditions, and

c- When the property and proceeds from the investment are identified, managed, and reported separately to that person.

2- Escrow services in which there is a transfer of property or funds by any person to the trustee, and the trustee shall transfer that property back to that person or transfer them to another person when the following conditions are met:

a- There is a safekeeping of property or funds by any person, such as a notary public or lawyer's office or accounting firm, or

b- There is a holding of property or capital funds for the benefit of investors, buyers of immovable property, and for other commercial transactions, or

c- Other escrow services that receive authorization by decisions of other regulators, such as the National Bank of Cambodia, the Securities and Exchange Commission of Cambodia, or the Ministry of Economy and Finance, to manage activities within their roles and duties.

3- Investor share schemes or investor benefits when the trustee manages property or funds to invest on behalf of that investor, and

4- For other purposes:

a- When there is a transfer of property or funds by any person to the trustee for the benefit of the contributor or other persons designated by the contributor, and

b- When the provisions in force provide for rules on the operation and management of trust activities.

Public trust is created for the benefit of Cambodian citizens in general.

This trust includes:

1- The development of banking and microfinance sector in Cambodia when:

a- A development partner provides property or funds for the benefit of Cambodian citizens, and those property or funds have been and are managed and disposed of in banking and microfinance institutions or other entities, and

b- Subject to conditions specified in the trust instrument or provisions in force.

2- Other public interests when the relevant regulator appoints a trustee to manage and protect the guarantee fund under the conditions specified in the trust instrument or provisions in force. These conditions include:

a- Funds provided to the Royal Government or provided by the Royal Government of the Kingdom of Cambodia to fulfill any specific purpose of the donor, and the donor requires that these funds are managed, used, and benefited in accordance with their purpose, or

b- To protect the interests of children or other incompetent persons, or

c- Estate that becomes state property under the provisions in force.

#### Article 12 .-

A social trust is created when the trustor makes a gift of his/her property to the trustee to be used for general interest in society, such as for cultural, educational, humanitarian, religious, or scientific purposes.

1- Special funds:

a- Funds created by the trustor through a gift or a will

b- Through receiving property from the trust contributor during lifetime or receiving property through the will of the trust contributor, and

c- Property is transferred to the trustee to fulfill social interest purposes, and

d- Non-profit or no commercial enterprise operations, and

e- Unless otherwise provided in the trust instrument, the initial property and other additional property transferred by the trust contributor to any fund shall be preserved to fulfill the trust purpose, and only the proceeds from that property are permitted to be distributed.

2- Funds for the benefit of cultural, educational, humanitarian, religious, or scientific organizations:

Must be created or received by a person, including a gift or a will, and this property is transferred to serve

purposes for the benefit of, inter alia, culture, education, humanity, religion, or science.

Article 13 .-
A private trust is created for the benefit of the trustor or specific persons designated by the trustor under the following conditions:

1- The trustor is a natural person who donates their property or funds to the trustee for their own benefit or for the benefit of other persons; or

2- The transfer of property or funds by the trustor:

a- In the event that the trustor is alive, the trustor must execute a trust instrument certified by witnesses, a lawyer, a notary, or the commune/sangkat administration, or by other persons as specified in the regulations in force.

b- In the event that the trustor makes a will, upon the death of the trustor through this will, a trust is created in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Code.

Article 14 .-
The organization and functioning of trusts as provided in Article 10, Article 11, Article 12, and Article 13 shall be determined by relevant regulations.

#### Section 3
#### Amendment of Purpose and Duration of Trust

Article 15 .-
The trustee may request an amendment of the purpose of the trust to the relevant regulator and notify the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

The applicant must specify:

- The reasons why the trust has not been implemented in accordance with the original purpose;
- The consent of the trustor;
- The unanimous consent of the trustees;
- The consent of the person in charge; and
- Other conditions of exemption as provided in the regulations in force.

Article 16 .-
The duration of each trust shall be specified in the trust instrument or in the regulations in force.

For a private trust, the duration shall not exceed 100 (one hundred) years from the date of creation of the trust.

In the event that the trust property is to be distributed after the dissolution of the trust, but the beneficiary has not yet obtained authorization or the right to own the land property, such trust property shall become the property of the State until the beneficiary has the full right to own the land in accordance with the laws in force of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Chapter 3
### Trustor, Trustee and Beneficiary

#### Section 1
#### Trustor

Article 17 .-
The trustor has, inter alia, the following rights:

- To receive information related to the administration of the trust, including, inter alia, financial reports, audit reports, and other relevant documents

- To appoint and remove the trustee, in accordance with the conditions specified in the trust instrument.

Article 18 .-
The trustor has, inter alia, the following obligations:

- To transfer trust property or funds to the trustee for administration and disposal on behalf of and for the benefit of the beneficiary

- To participate in coordinating the transfer of trust property to the trustee for administration and disposal

- To notify the Ministry of Economy and Finance in writing of any change in the trustor or contributor of the trust, or any modification in the conditions of the trust instrument

- To comply with the conditions specified in the trust instrument and the regulations in force.

Article 19 .-
In the event that the trustor does not have the ability or capacity to exercise their rights and obligations, the relevant rights and obligations shall devolve upon the natural person or legal person who has the competence to perform certain substitute functions as the successor trustor.

#### Section 2
#### Trustee

Article 20 .-
Persons who may be appointed as a trustee include:

1- A natural person or a group of natural persons who have full capacity under the Civil Code; or

2- A legal person or a group of legal persons legally established in the Kingdom of Cambodia; or

3- A mixed group of natural persons and legal persons.

The above-mentioned persons may be appointed as trustees unless they are prohibited from acting as trustees in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

For a trust in which the trustor is a natural person, the trustor and/or contributor of the trust and the beneficiary may become the trustee. However, in order to avoid conflicts of interest, the trustor and/or contributor of the trust and the beneficiary cannot become the trustee at the same time, and another co-trustee must be appointed.

Article 21 .-
The trustee has, inter alia, the following rights:

- To receive remuneration or various fees for the administration of the trust, which may vary according to their work experience and/or professional qualifications

- To manage and dispose of the trust property in accordance with the purpose and conditions of the trust instrument

- To make expenses in appropriate activities in accordance with the purpose and conditions of the trust instrument

- To seek assistance or other funds for the benefit of the trust

- To represent the trustor or beneficiary before the law or competent authorities; and

- To comply with the conditions specified in the trust instrument or the regulations in force.

Article 22 .-
The trustee has, inter alia, the following obligations:

- To apply for registration of the trust with the Ministry of Economy and Finance in accordance with the forms and procedures specified in Article 24 of this Law

- To clearly segregate their personal property from the trust property

- To manage the trust property with care and prudence

- Maintain honesty and integrity in the management of trust property;

- Cannot delegate their duties to another person;

- Manage and dispose of the trust property effectively and efficiently for the benefit of the beneficiary;

- Register the inventory of trust property;

- Report and provide information related to trust management to the trustor, beneficiary, and relevant regulator;

- Keep minutes of meetings, prepare annual accounts, and respond to the relevant regulator;

- Take appropriate measures to protect trust property and the beneficiary;

- Pay all types of taxes related to trust property;

- Enter into insurance contracts for liability in the capacity as trustee;

- Provide financial reports regularly to the relevant regulator and the Ministry of Economy and Finance;

- Cannot accept benefits from third parties in case of a conflict of interest; and

- Comply with the conditions determined by the trust instrument or regulations in force.

#### Article 23 .-

The trustee is liable for the following activities:

- Causing loss or reduction of the trust without proper justification;

- Failing to generate profit for the trust in cases where the trust property could have generated profit;

- Using the trust contrary to the purpose of the trust;

- Fault committed intentionally or through negligence in the performance of their work;

- Other liabilities according to the conditions of the trust instrument or regulations in force.

#### Article 24 .-

In exercising their rights and duties, if the trustees cannot agree by a majority, the trustees must have:

- Unanimity of opinion in writing;

- Notify immediately this disagreement in writing to the Ministry of Economy and Finance or the relevant regulator.

- The relevant regulator shall resolve this dispute with the participation of a representative of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 25 .-

A trustee may be replaced by another person in accordance with the conditions and procedures specified in the trust instrument or regulations in force.

In the absence of provisions regarding the conditions and procedures specified in the first paragraph, the trustee shall be replaced in accordance with the following conditions:

1- If the trustee is a physical person, this trustee shall be replaced:

a- Automatically when he/she dies, becomes physically or mentally incapacitated, is declared missing by the competent authority, commits a misdemeanor or felony, or according to other conditions determined by the regulations in force of the relevant regulator.

b- By a decision of the absolute majority of all trustees when the trustee retires or refuses to perform duties or is unfit to perform duties.

2- If the trustee is a legal entity, this trustee shall be replaced when the trustee ceases its activities and is struck off the registry or dissolved.

The relevant regulator or the person issuing the decision to appoint a replacement trustee must notify the Ministry of Economy and Finance in writing.

### Section 3
### Beneficiary

#### Article 26 .-

A beneficiary may be a person legally created in the Kingdom of Cambodia or may be the trustor.

A beneficiary may receive benefits from the trust from the effective date of the trust, unless otherwise provided by relevant regulations.

In case there are multiple beneficiaries, these beneficiaries may receive the same or different benefits from each other, in accordance with the manner specified in the trust instrument.

In the absence of an arrangement for the manner of receiving benefits as specified in the paragraph above, it shall be presumed that these beneficiaries receive equal benefits.

#### Article 27 .-

A beneficiary has, inter alia, the following rights:

- Receive benefits as specified in the trust instrument;

- Demand that the trustee fulfill the obligations determined in the trust instrument and in accordance with the regulations in force;

- Receive information related to the management and disposal of the trust; and

- Other rights as specified in the trust instrument and regulations in force.

## Chapter 4
## Registration of Trust, Control and Termination of Trust

### Section 1
### Registration of Trust

#### Article 28 .-

All trusts shall be valid only if they are registered with the Ministry of Economy and Finance within a period of not more than 3 (three) months after the date of creation of the trust.

The detailed conditions and procedures of registration shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 29 .-

Before registering for trust operations with the Ministry of Economy and Finance in accordance with Article 28 of this Law, the creation of a trust for any purpose of this Law must obtain prior approval in principle from the relevant regulator.

The operation of the trust in accordance with the purpose of this Law must be licensed by the relevant regulator in accordance with the regulations in force.

#### Article 30 .-

The Ministry of Economy and Finance shall record, manage and keep data and information of the trustor, trustee, beneficiary, other relevant persons, annual operations, replacement of trustee, and ownership information related to registration.

The conditions and procedures for annual operations and notifications of replacement of trustee shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

### Section 2
### Supervision and Termination of Trust

#### Article 31 .-

Any operation of the trust shall be subject to the supervision of the relevant regulator in order to protect the use of the trust property in accordance with the trust purpose.

In case of necessity and unless otherwise determined by the relevant regulator, the Ministry of Economy and Finance may require an audit on the management of trust property by an independent auditor. The independent auditor must be approved by the Ministry of Economy and Finance, and the audit expenses shall be borne by the trust.

At the request of the Ministry of Economy and Finance, and unless otherwise determined by the relevant regulator, the trustee shall permit supervision over trust records and provide financial reports or other information.

#### Article 32 .-

The trust shall be terminated in the following cases:

- The arrival of the timeframe specified in the trust instrument;

- The purpose of the trust has been achieved;

- Unanimous decision from the trustor;

- By a decision of a competent court; or

- Other cases as specified in the trust instrument.

#### Article 33 .-

Upon the termination of the trust, the trustee must submit a report on the management of the trust to the Ministry of Economy and Finance or the relevant regulator with details of the remaining trust property, income and other necessary information, in accordance with the regulations in force.

The trustee shall continue its execution activities until the completion of the liquidation, and the liquidator who is responsible shall supervise the trust.

The remaining trust property after liquidation shall be distributed or provided to the trustor or other persons as specified in the trust instrument, or kept as State property.

## Chapter 5
## Dispute Resolution and Inspection

### Section 1
### Dispute Resolution

#### Article 34 .-

Disputes related to trust operations shall be resolved by the Trust Dispute Resolution Board of the Ministry of Economy and Finance, unless otherwise determined by the relevant regulator.

The decision of the Trust Dispute Resolution Board is mandatory before filing a lawsuit to the competent court within 30 (thirty) days from the date of the decision.

The organization and functioning of this Dispute Resolution Board shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

### Section 2
### Inspection

#### Article 35 .-

The Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance has the right to appoint trust inspection officers to monitor, research, inspect, and promote the implementation of this Law.

The trust inspection officers have the right to conduct inspections on trust operations and perform other duties assigned by the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

The trust inspection officers have the right to monitor, inspect, research, and investigate offenses in the trust sector.

#### Article 36 .-

Trust inspection officers shall be qualified as judicial police officers to inspect offenses specified in this Law, in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Code.

The formalities and procedures for granting qualification to trust inspection officers shall be determined by inter-ministerial Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Justice and the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 37 .-

Within the framework of operations related to the examination and investigation of offenses specified in this Law, trust inspection officers have the power to search, seize evidence, summon relevant persons, and perform other procedures in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Code. In case of necessity to detain a suspect, trust inspection officers must obtain prior authorization from the prosecutor, even if such authorization is oral.

Every operation of trust inspection officers in examining, investigating offenses, and detaining suspects shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Code.

Trust inspection officers have the right to request assistance from sub-national authorities, armed forces, or other relevant competent authorities to participate in suppressing offenses specified in this Law.

#### Article 38 .-

In the process of trust inspection, in case there is evidence or verified analytical results confirming that trust operations are conducted contrary to the declaration and authorization in the trust instrument, trust inspection officers have the right to take measures to temporarily suspend them.

#### Article 39 .-

Any person who is dissatisfied with any measure of the trust inspection officers may file an appeal within 30 (thirty) days to the Ministry of Economy and Finance, starting from the date of receiving the decision.

The Ministry of Economy and Finance shall decide on the appeal within a maximum of 30 (thirty) days starting from the date of receiving the appeal.

In case that person is dissatisfied with the decision of the Ministry of Economy and Finance, that person has the right to file a lawsuit to the court in accordance with the procedures within 30 (thirty) days starting from the date of receiving the decision.

#### Article 40 .-

In performing law enforcement operations, trust inspection officers must wear uniforms, badges, and ranks, and must have a mission order.

The forms of uniforms, badges, and ranks of trust inspection officers shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 41 ._

The formalities and procedures for trust inspection shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 42 ._

Trust inspection officers who perform their functions and duties correctly in accordance with the law shall be protected by the law.

### Chapter 6

### Penal Provisions

#### Article 43 ._

A trustee who operates without applying for trust registration, as determined in Article 28 of this Law, shall be fined by the trust inspection officer from 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels to 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels and shall immediately cease trust activities and then be required to register the trust at the Ministry of Economy and Finance in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

In case of recidivism, shall be punished by a fine from 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels to 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels and/or imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 1 (one) year.

#### Article 44 ._

A trustee shall be fined by the trust inspection officer from 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels to 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels for the following acts:

- Failing to report and provide information related to trust management to the trustor, beneficiary, and relevant regulator.

- Failing to regularly submit financial reports to the relevant regulator and the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

In case of recidivism, shall be punished by a fine from 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels to 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels and/or imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 1 (one) year.

#### Article 45 ._

Shall be punished under the Penal Code of the Kingdom of Cambodia for any person who commits offenses related to trust among the following acts:

- 1- Abuse of trust or abuse of power in performing the functions of a trustee, with the intention to obtain personal interest or interest for other parties, or with the intention to cause damage to the interest of relevant parties.

- 2- Fraud in the trust sector committed by a trustee.

- 3- Obstructing or interfering with the inspection officers in performing their functions as set out in Article 38 of this Law.

- 4- Forging documents, or misleading the Ministry of Economy and Finance, or disseminating or providing false information committed by a trustee, leading to damage to the interests of relevant parties.

- 5- Destroying evidence of offenses in the trust sector with the intention of obstructing the investigation of trust inspection officers or other relevant competent officers.

#### Article 46 ._

A trustee who intentionally provides false information in the initial registration and/or in the subsequent registration of trusts, in accordance with the provisions of this Law, shall be punished by a fine from 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels to 200,000,000 (two hundred million) Riels and/or imprisonment from 6 (six) months to 3 (three) years.

#### Article 47 ._

Any person or trustee who participates in committing offenses related to money laundering and terrorist financing shall be punished in accordance with the Law on Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the Financing of Terrorism and the criminal provisions in force.

#### Article 48 ._

A trustee who breaches professional confidentiality, causing damage to the interests of relevant parties, shall be punished by a fine from 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels to 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels and/or imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 1 (one) year.

#### Article 49 ._

Legal entities may be declared criminally responsible under the conditions stipulated in Article 42 on the Criminal Liability of Legal Entities of the Penal Code, for the offenses set out in the second paragraph of Article 43, the second paragraph of Article 44, and points 1, 4 and 5 of Article 45, Article 46 and Article 48 of this Law.

Legal entities shall be punished by a fine from 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels to 200,000,000 (two hundred million) Riels, and shall not be allowed to act as a trustee in the Kingdom of Cambodia, as well as one or more additional penalties as set out in Article 168 on Additional Penalties Applicable to Legal Entities of the Penal Code.

#### Article 50 ._

For physical persons who commit offenses as set out in the second paragraph of Article 43, the second paragraph of Article 44, and Article 45 to Article 48 of this Law, the court may declare one or more additional penalties as set out in Article 53 (Types of Additional Penalties) of the Penal Code, including the decision to not allow them to act as a trustee in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

The substance, formalities and procedures for the implementation of these additional penalties shall comply with the provisions of the Penal Code.

#### Article 51 ._

For any offenses not provided for in this Law, the provisions in force shall apply.

#### Article 52 ._

Any person directly affected by a decision of the trust inspection officer as set out in this Chapter may file a written complaint to the Ministry of Economy and Finance within 30 (thirty) days from the date of receipt of the decision.

The Ministry of Economy and Finance shall decide on the complaint within a maximum of 30 (thirty) days from the date of receipt of the complaint.

In case that person is dissatisfied with the decision of the Ministry of Economy and Finance, that person has the right to file a complaint to the court in accordance with the procedures within 30 (thirty) days from the date of receipt of the decision.

#### Article 53 ._

The provisions of the Chapter on Penalties (General Provisions) of Title 1 (General Provisions), Chapter on Penalties (Physical Compulsion) of Title 3 (Physical Compulsion) of Book 8 of the Code of Criminal Procedure shall apply in cases where the decision on the imposition of the fine has become final.

### Chapter 7

### Transitional Provisions

Within a period of 3 (three) months after this Law enters into force, any person who has been and is currently managing a trust shall register the trust as set out in Article 28 of this Law.

# ANNEX OF THE LAW ON TRUSTS

# GLOSSARY

The essential key terms used in the Law on Trusts are defined as follows:

1- Civil Code refers to the Civil Code of the Kingdom of Cambodia promulgated by Royal Kram No. NS/RKM/1207/030 dated 08 December 2007.

2- Criminal Code refers to the Criminal Code of the Kingdom of Cambodia promulgated by Royal Kram No. NS/RKM/1109/022 dated 30 November 2009.

3- Code of Criminal Procedure refers to the Code of Criminal Procedure of the Kingdom of Cambodia promulgated by Royal Kram No. NS/RKM/0607/016 dated 10 August 2007.

4- Property refers to movable and immovable property defined in Article 120 of the Civil Code.

5- Trust Property refers to the fund property transferred into the trust and any benefits derived from that fund property.

6- Trust Asset refers to property or fund transferred to a trustee for management and disposal for the benefit of the beneficiary, in accordance with the terms specified in the trust instrument or regulations in force.

7- Trustee refers to a person who receives the right to manage and dispose of the trust asset from the trustor for the benefit of the beneficiary, in accordance with the trust instrument or regulations in force.

8- Trust refers to the management and disposal of the trust asset provided by the trustor for the benefit of the beneficiary, in accordance with the trust instrument or regulations in force.

9- Trustor refers to a person who creates and sets the terms of a trust, and provides fund property or increases it in the trust.

10- Person refers to any physical person or legal entity established under the law of the Kingdom of Cambodia, whether for profit or non-profit.

11- Gift refers to a contract whereby one party expresses their will to provide the other party with property free of charge, and the other party agrees to accept it.

12- Will refers to a testamentary instruction made by a person in the form prescribed by law to express their last wishes regarding the disposal of their property, among others, after death.

13- Trust Instrument refers to:

A- Legal document determining, among others, the creation, management, disposal, and termination of a trust, made between the trustor or the contributor to the trust, and the trustee, certified by a notary, lawyer, or yuttisangkat, or by any other person specified under the regulations in force.

B- Will made in accordance with the provisions on testaments of the Civil Code.

14- Real Right refers to the right to control a specific object directly which can be asserted against other persons.

15- Custody service refers to the transfer of fund property by any person to a trustee to keep and hand over to another person or return to that person, in accordance with the conditions specified in the trust instrument.

16- Beneficiary refers to a person who receives benefits from the trust.

17- Trust Contributor refers to a person who contributes to a trust or an existing trust and has the right to participate in instructing the conditions of the contribution payment to be managed and disposed of by the trustee, and the conditions of benefits received by the beneficiary.

18- Gift refers to the voluntary transfer of property from one person to another without consideration or legal obligation.
