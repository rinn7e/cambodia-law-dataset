#### HEREBY DECIDES

**Article 1 .-**
The purpose of this Sub-Decree is to determine the monthly salary tax thresholds and the annual income tax thresholds.

**Article 2 .-**
This Sub-Decree applies to the taxable monthly salary of resident employees and the taxable annual income of sole proprietorships and the distributive share of each partner of a partnership that is not considered a legal entity.

**Article 3 .-**
1- The monthly salary tax thresholds and tax rates for resident employees are determined as follows:

| No. | Monthly Taxable Salary Threshold (in Riel) | Tax Rate |
| :---: | :--- | :---: |
| 1 | From 0 to 1,500,000 | 0% |
| 2 | From 1,500,001 to 2,000,000 | 5% |
| 3 | From 2,000,001 to 8,500,000 | 10% |
| 4 | From 8,500,001 to 12,500,000 | 15% |
| 5 | Over 12,500,000 | 20% |

2- Based on evidence of family status, resident employees are entitled to reductions in the tax base as follows:

- a- Dependent child: 150,000 Riels per child per month.

- b- Housewife spouse: 150,000 Riels for only one person per month.

In the event that both spouses in a family are employed, the reduction as stated in paragraph (a) of this section shall be granted to only one of the spouses.

**Article 4 .-**
The annual income tax thresholds and tax rates for sole proprietorships and the distributive share of each partner of a partnership that is not considered a legal entity are determined as follows:

| No. | Annual Taxable Income Threshold (in Riel) | Tax Rate |
| :---: | :--- | :---: |
| 1 | From 0 to 18,000,000 | 0% |
| 2 | From 18,000,001 to 24,000,000 | 5% |
| 3 | From 24,000,001 to 102,000,000 | 10% |
| 4 | From 102,000,001 to 150,000,000 | 15% |
| 5 | Over 150,000,000 | 20% |

**Article 5 .-**
Any provisions contrary to this Sub-Decree shall be abrogated.

**Article 6 .-**
The Minister in charge of the Office of the Council of Ministers, the Minister of Economy and Finance, ministers of all ministries, and heads of all relevant institutions shall be responsible for implementing this Sub-Decree according to their respective duties from the date of signature.
