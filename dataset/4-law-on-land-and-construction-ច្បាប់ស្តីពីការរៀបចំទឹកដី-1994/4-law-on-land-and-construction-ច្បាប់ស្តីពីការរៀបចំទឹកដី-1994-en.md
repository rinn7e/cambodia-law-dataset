#### Promulgated

The Law adopted by the National Assembly on May 24, 1994, regarding Land Management, Urbanization and Construction, as follows:

### Chapter 1
### Objectives

#### Article 1._

The Law on Land Management, Urbanization and Construction has the objective to promote the organization and improvement of urban and rural areas of the Kingdom of Cambodia in order to ensure the appropriate development of the country in the spirit of:

- Respecting collective and individual interests, private rights, legal provisions, and inspecting construction.

- Ensuring the development of construction in all urban and rural areas based on the geography and special characteristics of each rural area.

- Ensuring the value of natural assets, cultural assets and ensuring development in the economic sector, tourism sector, and maintaining environmental quality.

#### Article 2._

This Law on Land Management, Urbanization and Construction shall be based on land law in compliance with the current land law. In case the land law is modified or changed, this Law on Land Management, Urbanization and Construction shall also be adjusted to be consistent with that land law.

#### Article 3._

In order to achieve the objectives stated above, a Committee for Land Management, Urbanization and Construction shall be established, with members and appointment procedures and organization to be determined by Sub-decree.

For Phnom Penh Capital, a Committee for Land Management, Urbanization and Construction for Phnom Penh Municipality shall be established. This Committee is led by the Chairman of the National Committee for Land Management, Urbanization and Construction and includes the Governor of Phnom Penh Municipality or the Governor, Vice-Governors, as well as competent technical units as members. The specific composition and procedures of the Committee for Land Management, Urbanization and Construction for Phnom Penh Municipality shall be determined by Sub-decree.

For provinces and cities, a Sub-committee for Land Management, Urbanization and Construction shall be established, led by the Provincial/City Governor and includes competent provincial/city technical units as members. The specific composition and procedures of the Sub-committee for Land Management, Urbanization and Construction shall be determined by the decision of the Chairman of the National Committee for Land Management, Urbanization and Construction after the approval of this National Committee.

### Chapter 2
### Documents Related to Land Management, Urbanization and Construction

#### Article 4._

All documents related to land management, urbanization and construction shall be prepared on a case-by-case basis as appropriate to the conditions and special characteristics of the land, province, city, or area in the spirit of protecting heritage, the environment, protecting natural resources, as well as ensuring economic development.

#### Article 5._

The Committee for Land Management, Urbanization and Construction for Phnom Penh Capital or the Sub-committee for Land Management, Urbanization and Construction for each province and city shall draw up a Master Plan for the organization and development of its own capital, province, or city. This Master Plan must have the approval of the National Committee for Land Management, Urbanization and Construction and shall be determined by Sub-decree.

In order to protect heritage, the environment, and to ensure economic development for any special area determined by the Council of Ministers, the National Committee for Land Management, Urbanization and Construction has the duty to prepare a Master Plan for that special area for the Council of Ministers to approve.

### Chapter 3
### Land Use Plan and Construction Plan

#### Article 6._

Land use plans and construction plans shall be organized for the capital, provinces, and cities. These plans must be respected by private individuals as well as public authorities in their construction activities.

#### Article 7._

Land use plans and construction plans must clearly specify areas to be used for national defense, agriculture, commerce, industry, handicrafts, culture, tourism, religion, as well as for administrative buildings and public facilities. These plans also determine the potential for construction.

#### Article 8._

Land use plans and construction plans must comply with the Master Plan as stated in Article 5 of this Law.

#### Article 9._

Land use plans and construction plans for each capital, province and city must be approved by the Committee for Land Management, Urbanization and Construction of the Capital or the Sub-committee for Land Management, Urbanization and Construction of the Province or City, and then must be approved by the National Committee for Land Management, Urbanization and Construction. All plans must be complied with by private individuals as well as all public authorities.

#### Article 10._

The Royal Government shall prepare special regulations and urbanization services to protect and enhance resorts or immovable properties that provide benefits in terms of archaeology, history, culture, aesthetics or technology. The list of these immovable properties or resorts shall be determined by Sub-decree. Land registration, transformation, and management of these immovable properties and resorts shall be determined by the Law on the Protection of Cultural Heritage.

Special regulations and urbanization services to protect and enhance resorts or immovable properties that provide benefits in terms of archaeology, history, culture, aesthetics or technology must be inscribed in the Master Plan for Land Management, Urbanization and Development and inscribed in the Land Use and Construction Plan.

The Royal Government shall determine by Sub-decree the list of immovable properties that are beneficial for archaeology, history, culture, aesthetics and technology in order to ensure the management of these immovable properties according to the provisions of the Law on the Protection of Cultural Heritage.

### Chapter 4
### Excavation and Construction Work

#### Article 11._

Any excavation or survey that affects archaeology must have permission from the Royal Government.

For all construction, in case of discovery of any object beneficial for archaeology or history, the person in charge of the site has the obligation to report immediately to the competent authorities. The competent authorities shall suspend the construction immediately.

#### Article 12._

All private persons or institutions as well as public authorities are prohibited from building on land that has a public character as follows:

- Water reservoir and dam sites
- Reserved agricultural land and forested areas
- Archaeological and historical resorts
- Public parks, gardens and development areas
- Land reserved for roads, land reserved for railway stations, and land reserved for railways and airports
- Rivers, seas, capes, streams as well as all these shores
- Generally on any land where legal provisions prohibit construction.

The conditions for managing those areas, construction conditions or ancient settlements, [or] temporary [settlements] may be authorized by Sub-decree.

#### Article 13._

Construction work and settlement must be carried out in compliance with the documents related to land management, urbanization and construction, and in compliance with the Master Plan stated in Articles 4 and 5 of this Law.

#### Article 14._

All construction, all changes of appearance or changes of use of any construction must have permission from the competent authorities.

The application for a construction permit must include necessary documents and plans. The procedures for implementing this Article shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 15._

The necessary documents and plans attached to the application for a construction permit for large buildings must be prepared by a Cambodian architect whose diploma is recognized by the competent institutions.

The conditions required regarding diplomas and the recognition measures mentioned above shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 16._

The application for a construction permit must be submitted by the owner or any person who has received rights from the owner. This application for a permit must be attached with documents proving the owner's ownership.

#### Article 17._

No permit shall be granted for any construction that is not appropriate to the Master Plan or the Land Use and Construction Plan.

No permit shall be granted if that construction project affects public security, affects the environment, affects public hygiene, construction or immovable properties that are beneficial for archaeology, history, culture, aesthetics, technology, or affects areas with natural resources.

No permit shall be granted if there is no provision for sufficient public infrastructure or facilities for the size of the requested construction.

#### Article 18._

In case where there is no Land Use and Construction Plan, the provincial/city authorities shall establish a committee to examine applications and issue construction permits.

#### Article 19._

For administrative construction projects, the unit requesting construction must have prior permission from the competent authorities.

That permission can be granted only for construction that is necessary for administrative operations.

This permission cannot lead to a change in the determined land use, and cannot change the public character and general benefits of that area.

#### Article 20._

All constructions built by private investors on state land according to any contract must be returned to the state upon the expiration of the period stated in that contract.

#### Article 21._

Unless there is a valid reason specified in this law, any civil servant who refuses to accept an application for construction or issue a construction permit or objects to the issuance of a construction permit within a maximum of 45 days shall be punished according to legal provisions.

Punishment shall also apply to any agent who has examined construction projects and issued construction permits that are not consistent with the regulations.

Supervisory agents who fail to object when they see construction on any site that does not comply with the approved project shall be punished according to legal provisions.

Administrative sanctions to be applied to agents who commit faults as described above shall be determined by administrative law.

As for constructors who do not comply with the construction permit, they shall be ordered to demolish or modify the construction to be consistent with the construction permit within a maximum of 30 days starting from the date of receiving the official order.

Civil servants who collaborate in the violation shall be prosecuted before the court.

When construction errors are found, the site must be closed and, if necessary, construction materials and equipment at that location shall be confiscated by court order. In case the constructor suffers damage that is not their fault, the victim can exercise their right to sue in court for compensation.

#### Article 22._

The owner of a construction, before demolishing their existing construction, must have a demolition permit from the competent authorities.

The forms for demolishing and destroying construction shall be determined by Sub-decree.

Permission for demolition and destruction of construction can be refused in case that demolition and destruction are related to social, technical or heritage issues recorded by the state.

#### Article 23._

Any provisions in previous laws regarding land management, urbanization and construction that are contrary to this Law shall be null and void.
