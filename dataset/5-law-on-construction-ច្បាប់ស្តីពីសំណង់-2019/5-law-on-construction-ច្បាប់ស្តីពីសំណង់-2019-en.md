The Law on Construction which was adopted by the National Assembly on October 07, 2019, at its 3rd plenary session of the 6th legislature, and which was reviewed and approved entirely as to its form and legal concepts by the Senate on October 18, 2019, at its extraordinary session of the 4th legislature, and whose full text is as follows:

# Law
# on
# Construction

# Chapter 1
# General Provisions

### Article 1 .-
The purpose of this law is to ensure:

- Quality, security, and safety of construction; and the protection of property and well-being of construction owners, construction users, and the general public;

- Aesthetics and healthy environment for sustainable living, aimed at enhancing public welfare;

- Accountability and efficiency in the work and professional practice in the construction sector;

- Increased investor confidence in the construction sector and promotion of the real estate market with economic and social efficiency.

### Article 2 .-
This law aims to define principles, construction technical regulations, rules, and procedures for the management of the construction sector in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Article 3 .-
This law has a scope of application to the construction sector in the Kingdom of Cambodia, except for types of construction defined in separate legal instruments.

### Article 4 .-
Key terms used in this law are defined in the glossary, which is an annex to this law.

# Chapter 2
# Management Competence

### Article 5 .-
The Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction has the competence to manage the construction sector.

The Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction may assign or delegate the competence to manage the construction sector to the Capital, Provincial, Municipal, District, and Khan administrations.

# Chapter 3
# Principles

### Article 6 .-
All construction must comply with the following principles:

- Protection of public interests and individual rights;

- Efficiency, sustainability, and inclusiveness;

- Land management and urban planning;

- Green development, natural resource and environmental protection;

- Maintenance and promotion of national identity.

# Chapter 4
# Technical Regulations for Construction

### Article 7 .-
All construction work shall comply with the construction technical regulations.

Inspection of compliance with construction technical regulations shall be carried out by licensed or authorized inspectors and certifiers provided by the Ministry of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

Conditions and procedures for construction inspection and certification shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 8 .-
All construction must have structural components that can safely support loads according to the functional use of the construction, as defined in the construction technical regulations.

The classification, type, and size of construction required to undergo inspection and certification of structural safety shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

### Article 9 .-
All construction shall comply with fire safety regulations as defined in the construction technical regulations and regulations related to fire prevention and firefighting.

The classification, type, and size of construction required to undergo inspection and certification of fire safety on the construction shall be determined by inter-ministerial Prakas of the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction and the Minister of Interior.

### Article 10 .-
National construction technical regulations shall be promulgated by Sub-decree upon the request of the National Council for Construction Technical Regulations.

Other construction technical regulations besides national construction technical regulations shall be promulgated by Prakas of the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

Construction technical regulations for construction materials, construction equipment, and construction products that require specific precautions shall be promulgated by Prakas of the Minister in charge of the sector

Industry, upon the request of the Ministry of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction and according to the decision of the National Standards Council.

Construction technical regulations for construction materials, construction equipment, and other construction products that have not yet been defined as Cambodian Standards shall be promulgated by Prakas of the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

### Article 11 .-
The National Council for Construction Technical Regulations shall be established, with the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction as the Chairperson and representatives from relevant ministries, institutions, and the private sector as members.

The National Council for Construction Technical Regulations shall have a General Secretariat as its secretariat.

The organization and functioning of the National Council for Construction Technical Regulations shall be determined by Sub-decree.

# Chapter 5
# Management of Professionals in the Construction Sector

### Article 12 .-
Physical persons who can perform functions as professionals in the construction sector must be registered with the respective professional board of the construction sector.

The organization and functioning of each professional board related to the construction sector shall be determined by Royal Decree.

### Article 13 .-
Professionals in the construction sector must strictly adhere to their professional code of ethics.

The code of ethics for professionals in the construction sector shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 14 .-
The management of other experts in the construction sector for whom a professional board has not yet been established shall be determined by Sub-decree.

# Chapter 6
# Management of Professional Practice and Business Practice in the Construction Sector

### Article 15 .-
Professionals in the construction sector who can practice their profession independently must have a license provided by the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

Professionals in the construction sector who perform functions as technical directors of companies conducting business in the construction sector are not required to have a license provided by the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

Technical directors of companies conducting business in the construction sector must perform their functions independently in accordance with the professional code of ethics and construction technical regulations in force.

### Article 16 .-
Professionals in the construction sector shall lose their legal capacity to practice their profession in any of the following cases:

- No longer fulfilling requirements related to professional practice;

- Having committed professional misconduct leading to the suspension or revocation of the professional license;

- Having health issues that may affect professional practice, certified by a specialist doctor or having been declared the commencement of curatorship or guardianship by a court;

- Having been declared bankrupt by a court;

- Having been convicted of a felony in the Kingdom of Cambodia or in any country where they have resided.

The suspension of a professional license in the construction sector shall not exceed 6 (six) months, counting from the day of suspension.

The legal capacity for professional practice in the construction sector may be reinstated after a period of 5 (five) years, counting from the day of license revocation.

In cases where legal capacity for professional practice is lost due to conviction for a felony in the Kingdom of Cambodia or in any country where they have resided, this legal capacity for professional practice may be reinstated after the person concerned has been rehabilitated in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Code.

### Article 17 .-
All business operations in the construction sector must have a license provided by the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction or a permit from the Capital or Provincial administration, depending on the type of business.

### Article 18 .-
Companies conducting business in the construction sector shall lose their legal capacity to conduct business in any of the following cases:

- No longer fulfilling any requirements allowing the company to obtain a business license;

- Having been removed from the commercial register;

- Having been declared dissolved or nullified by a court;

- Having been declared bankrupt or undergoing court-ordered liquidation;

- Having a director or technical director who has been declared under curatorship or guardianship by a court, unless such director or technical director is replaced within 30 (thirty) days from the date of the court's declaration;

- Having a director or technical director who has been declared bankrupt or undergoing court-ordered liquidation by a court, unless such director or technical director is replaced within 30 (thirty) days from the date of the court's declaration;

- Having a director or technical director who has been convicted of a felony in the Kingdom of Cambodia or in any country where they have resided, unless such director or technical director is replaced within 30 (thirty) days from the date of the final judgment or verdict.

The suspension of a business license in the construction sector shall not exceed 1 (one) year, counting from the day of suspension.

The legal capacity for conducting business in the construction sector may be reinstated after a period of 5 (five) years, counting from the day of license revocation.

Any individual or professional who has been declared bankrupt or undergoing court-ordered liquidation by a court may regain the legal capacity to serve as a director or technical director of a company conducting business in the construction sector after a period of 5 (five) years, counting from the date of the court's declaration of bankruptcy or liquidation.

Any individual or professional who has been convicted of a felony in the Kingdom of Cambodia or in any country where they have resided may regain the legal capacity to serve as a director or technical director of a company conducting business in the construction sector after the person concerned has been rehabilitated in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Code.

### Article 19 .-
Individuals who hold a license for professional practice or business practice in the construction sector must enter into an insurance contract with an insurance company operating in the Kingdom of Cambodia and hold an insurance certificate to guarantee their liability in their professional or business practice.

The coverage of the insurance contract is intended to guarantee the fulfillment of obligations as stated in the contract between the professional or business practitioner and their client.

The minimum amount [of insurance] to guarantee liability in professional or business practice in the construction sector shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 20 .-
The conditions and procedures for granting, suspending, and revoking professional licenses and other licenses or permits for business operations in the construction sector shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 21 .-
Applications for professional licenses or other permits for professional practice or business operations in the construction sector shall be subject to service fees.

Service fees for professional licenses and other permits for professional practice or business operations in the construction sector shall be determined by inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of Economy and Finance and the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

Service fees for professional licenses and other permits for professional practice or business operations in the construction sector shall be for the benefit of the national budget and sub-national budgets.

# Chapter 7
# Use of Construction Materials, Construction Equipment and Construction Products, and Construction Laboratories

### Article 22 .-
Construction materials, construction equipment, and construction products necessary to ensure construction quality and construction users' safety must have accreditation or compliance certification from the Ministry of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction in accordance with construction technical regulations or Cambodian Standards approved by the National Standards Board, with the National Standard Mark affixed or printed on them and having a license to use the Cambodian Standard Mark.

The forms and procedures for accreditation or compliance certification and the types of construction materials, construction equipment, and construction products necessary to ensure construction quality and safety for construction users shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

### Article 23 .-
The production, distribution, importation, sale, supply, or use of construction materials, construction equipment, or construction products without the Cambodian Standard Mark, or without accreditation, or without compliance certification in accordance with construction technical regulations is prohibited in cases where construction materials, construction equipment,

or the construction products are required to have a Cambodian Standard Mark affixed, or recognition certification, or compliance certification in accordance with construction technical regulations.

The use of construction materials, construction equipment, and construction products that do not comply with construction technical regulations is prohibited.

The forms and procedures for inspecting compliance of the use of construction materials, construction equipment, and construction products in accordance with construction technical regulations shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

### Article 24 .-
The Ministry of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction shall establish a National Construction Laboratory.

The organization and functioning of the National Construction Laboratory shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 25 .-
Construction laboratories for business purposes must have a license provided by the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

The establishment of construction laboratories for study, research, and training purposes must have a permit from the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

The conditions and procedures for granting, suspending, and revoking permits to establish construction laboratories for study, research, and training purposes shall be determined by Sub-decree.

# Chapter 8
# Construction, Repair, or Demolition Permit

### Article 26 .-
All construction or demolition work must have prior authorization from the competent authorities.

### Article 27 .-
Construction or demolition work in situations of emergency or disaster for rescue purposes or to protect life or prevent serious impact or damage to health or property in an emergency does not require a prior permit.

Within no more than 30 (thirty) days at the latest after the emergency situation has ended, the owner of a construction built during such an emergency must apply for a construction usage certificate according to the conditions and procedures in force.

Within no more than 30 (thirty) days at the latest after the emergency situation has ended, the owner of a construction who has carried out demolition during the emergency must notify the competent authorities in writing.

### Article 28 .-
Construction or demolition work that does not require a permit includes:

- Small-scale construction that does not affect public security, safety, and order;

- Wooden houses, houses with a concrete ground floor and wooden upper floor, and one-story wooden or concrete houses in rural areas or farmlands that do not affect tourist resorts, heritage areas, conservation areas, or protected areas;

- Construction serving national security and national defense.

The types and sizes of construction or demolition work that do not require a permit shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 29 .-
No permit is required for any repair, modification, or installation of construction equipment in cases where such work does not affect the supporting structure, external aesthetics, or the overall or partial functional use of the construction, and does not affect public security, safety, and order. In this case, the construction owner must notify the competent authorities in advance.

The types and sizes of repair, modification, or installation of construction equipment that do not require a permit shall be determined by Sub-decree.

The forms and procedures for prior notification of repair, modification, or installation of construction equipment for which no permit is required shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

### Article 30 .-
The conditions and procedures for granting, suspending, and revoking construction, repair, or demolition permits shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 31 .-
Construction, repair, or demolition permits do not exempt the person who obtained such permits from obligations and liability under construction technical regulations and other regulations in force.

Any construction or demolition work for which a permit is not required does not exempt the construction owner from obligations and liability under the law and other regulations in force.

# Chapter 9
# Management of Design Projects

### Article 32 .-
All design projects used for construction or demolition work must be signed for responsibility by a designer who has a license or permit provided by the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

### Article 33 .-
All design projects used for construction or demolition work must comply with construction technical regulations and land management and urban planning regulations.

Design projects for construction or demolition of all types, except for construction for personal use, must be organized to have infrastructure, equipment, and services for persons with disabilities, such as roads and ramps for wheelchairs, handrails, elevators, restrooms, special parking spaces for persons with disabilities, and various signs.

Compliance of design projects with construction technical regulations and land management and urban planning regulations must be certified by a licensed or authorized inspector and certifier provided by the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

Design projects for construction or demolition that require a permit must have compliance certification with construction technical regulations by a licensed or authorized inspector and certifier provided by the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

### Article 34 .-
The construction owner must ensure that construction or demolition work that requires a permit is implemented correctly according to the design project that has received approval from the competent authorities melalui/through the permit.

Before implementing construction or demolition work, the competent authorities may allow the modification of a design project that has already received approval.

Authorization for modification of a design project shall be done through a design project modification permit.

The conditions and procedures for authorizing the modification of a design project shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 35 .-
Every work of a project designer that is original, in whole or in part, and has received approval from the competent authorities through a construction permit, shall be protected from plagiarism in accordance with the regulations in force.

A design project may not be implemented at another location without authorization from the designer who signed the project and the owner of the construction.

The implementation of a design project having a special architectural value at another location is prohibited. Design projects with special architectural value shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

# Chapter 10
# Management of Construction or Demolition Sites

### Article 36 .-
All construction or demolition work must be carried out by a builder/contractor who has a license provided by the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction or a permit from the Capital or Provincial administration.

A builder/contractor is not required to have a license provided by the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction or a permit from the Capital or Provincial administration, provided that the builder/contractor fulfills all of the following conditions:

- Being a professional in the construction sector;

- Constructing or demolishing a building that is their own residence; and

- Constructing or demolishing according to their professional skills.

### Article 37 .-
All construction or demolition work must be implemented in accordance with:

- The design project;

- Construction technical regulations;

- Other regulations in force.

All construction or demolition work that requires a permit must be implemented according to the design project that has received approval from the competent authorities through the permit.

### Article 38 .-
All construction work that requires a permit must have a prior construction site opening permit.

The conditions and procedures for issuing a construction site opening permit shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 39 .-
The builder/contractor must ensure public security, safety, and order as well as the environment at their construction site.

### Article 40 .-
Construction owners who are required to have a permit must provide reports on the dynamics of construction or demolition work to the competent authorities.

The report on the dynamics of construction or demolition work must be prepared by the builder/contractor and concurred with by the construction owner.

The forms and procedures for providing reports on the dynamics of construction or demolition work shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

### Article 41 .-
During site operations, all construction or demolition work that requires a permit must be inspected by a licensed or authorized inspector and certifier provided by the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

The inspector and certifier of construction or demolition work must prepare reports and issue certificates of compliance of the work with the design project that has received approval from the competent authorities and with construction technical regulations.

### Article 42 .-
In necessary cases to ensure public security, safety, and order, the competent authorities may assign construction inspection officials to inspect construction or demolition work.

Construction owners, construction users, and individuals involved in construction work, including real estate developers, builders/contractors, and construction inspectors and certifiers, must cooperate with the construction inspection officials.

The competent authorities may decide to suspend, rectify, stop, require demolition, or take other necessary measures if it is found that construction or demolition work does not comply with construction technical regulations and other regulations in force.

### Article 43 .-
The conditions and procedures for the management and inspection of construction or demolition work shall be determined by Sub-decree.

# Chapter 11
# Use of Construction

### Article 44 .-
The use of construction that requires a construction permit must be authorized by the competent authorities through a construction usage certificate.

The construction owner must apply for a construction usage certificate before using or allowing the use of that construction.

All constructions that require a construction permit must have an inspection and compliance certification with construction technical regulations by a licensed or authorized inspector and certifier provided by the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction before the competent authorities authorize the occupancy of the construction.

### Article 45 .-
The competent authorities may authorize the temporary occupancy of a construction for a certain period, for the whole or part of a construction project, in cases where the whole or part of that construction project can be used safely.

### Article 46 .-
The conditions and procedures for granting, suspending, and revoking a construction usage certificate shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 47 .-
Constructions used for purposes other than residential must have a quality and safety inspection within a maximum of 5 (five) years from the date of the construction usage certificate issuance. This quality and safety inspection must be continued regularly every 5 (five) years at the latest.

Constructions used for residential purposes must have a quality and safety inspection within a maximum of 10 (ten) years from the date of the construction usage certificate issuance. This quality and safety inspection must be continued regularly every 10 (ten) years at the latest.

The inspection and issuance of certificates for the quality and efficiency of construction fire prevention and extinguishing systems must be conducted every 2 (two) years.

Construction equipment that is prone to causing danger must have an inspection every 1 (one) year. The types of construction equipment prone to causing danger shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

Construction owners or construction managers have the obligation to submit the results of the construction quality and safety inspection to the competent authorities within 1 (one) month after the deadline required for the implementation of the construction quality and safety inspection.

### Article 48 .-
The competent authorities may require a construction quality and safety inspection in cases where there is a risk to human life or property, or an impact on public security or order.

### Article 49 .-
The construction quality and safety inspection shall be conducted by construction inspection officials or by a licensed construction inspector and certifier provided by the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

All costs for the construction quality and safety inspection shall be borne by the construction owner.

For co-owned buildings, all private unit owners shall jointly bear all costs for the construction quality and safety inspection in proportion to the size of their private units.

### Article 50 .-
The forms and procedures for the construction quality and safety inspection shall be determined by inter-ministerial Prakas of the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction and the Minister of Interior.

# Chapter 12
# Dangerous Constructions

### Article 51 .-
The Ministry of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction and the Capital, Provincial, Municipal, District, and Khan administrations have the duty to inspect and monitor any construction that is in a dangerous condition to take various necessary measures to ensure public safety, security, and order.

### Article 52 .-
For constructions in a dangerous condition, the construction owner and construction manager have the obligation to report to the competent authorities and implement all measures to avert danger as determined by the competent authorities.

In necessary cases to avert danger, the competent authorities shall take urgent measures, such as cordoning off the area, prohibiting entry, prohibiting use, and requiring the demolition of the construction.

All costs for averting danger shall be borne by the construction owner.

### Article 53 .-
The degree of danger, the issuance of measures to avert danger, and the procedures for managing dangerous constructions shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

# Chapter 13
# Construction or Demolition Without or Contrary to a Permit

### Article 54 .-
The competent authorities shall take measures to stop construction or demolition work carried out without a permit in cases where such construction or demolition work is required to have a permit.

### Article 55 .-
The competent authorities shall take measures to suspend construction or demolition work that has been carried out contrary to the permit and require the construction owner to rectify it in accordance with the permit.

### Article 56 .-
The competent authorities shall take measures to rectify or demolish any construction built without a permit or contrary to the permit in cases where such construction is required to have a permit.

The competent authorities shall take measures to prohibit any use, business operation, management, and other transactions related to construction without a permit or contrary to the permit in cases where such construction is required to have a permit.

# Chapter 14
# Construction Inspection

### Article 57 .-
The Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction shall appoint construction inspection officials to monitor, investigate, research, inspect, collect evidence, and promote the implementation of this law.

Construction inspection officials shall be granted the qualifications of judicial police to examine offenses as stated in this law in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Code.

The forms and procedures for granting judicial police qualifications to construction inspection officials shall be determined by inter-ministerial Prakas of the Minister of Justice and the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

### Article 58 .-
While performing duties to implement the law, construction inspection officials must wear uniforms and have identification marks and mission order.

The uniforms, identification markers, and rank insignia of construction inspection officials shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 59 .-
Construction inspection officials have the following duties and rights:

- To inspect, stop, take temporary measures, and impose transitional fines for construction offenses as stated in this law;

- To temporarily revoke relevant permits or licenses in cases where the holder of such permits or licenses violates this law;

- To seize material evidence and build case files for construction offenses;

- To take other measures within the framework of the implementation of this law.

The forms and procedures for construction inspection shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

### Article 60 .-
Construction inspection officials may enter construction sites at any time while the sites are in operation or enter buildings during working hours to perform their duties.

### Article 61 .-
All construction inspection operations in examining offenses shall be conducted in accordance with the Criminal Procedure Code.

Construction inspection officials may request assistance from local authorities at all levels and armed forces units or other relevant competent authorities to participate in suppressing offenses as stated in this law.

In cases of flagrante delicto or absence of a construction or demolition permit, the relevant competent authorities must immediately provide information to the nearest competent construction inspection officials for further action according to the procedure.

# Chapter 15
# Incompatibility

### Article 62 .-
Civil servants who have the authority to inspect and/or decide on design projects cannot be the designer or the inspector and certifier of the design project that they themselves have designed or inspected and certified.

### Article 63 .-
Construction inspection officials, construction inspection officers, and inspectors and certifiers cannot be the construction owner, or the spouse, or blood relatives, or relatives by consanguinity up to the 3rd degree, or relatives by affinity up to the 3rd degree of the construction owner, or a person who has a financial interest related to the construction project.

### Article 64 .-
Performing the function of a design project designer is incompatible with performing the function of an inspector and certifier of one's own design project.

### Article 65 .-
Performing the function of a builder/contractor is incompatible with performing the function of an inspector and certifier of one's own construction work.

### Article 66 .-
An inspector and certifier of a design project or construction work cannot be the spouse, blood relative, or co-blood relative up to the 3rd degree of the construction owner.

Shareholders of a company that is the construction owner, staff of a company that is the construction owner, or persons who have a financial interest related to the construction project cannot perform the function of an inspector and certifier of that design project or construction work.

# Chapter 16
# Construction or Demolition Contract

### Article 67 .-
A construction or demolition contract must be made in writing and must include at least the following minimum content:

- Identification of the parties to the contract;

- Location, size, and type of work;

- Contract price, and the schedule and method of payment;

- Guarantees from the parties to the contract for the implementation of contractual obligations;

- Technical conditions and safety conditions of the work;

- Conditions related to the supply of construction materials, construction equipment, or tools and machinery for construction or demolition work and for installation on the construction;

- Conditions for modification and termination of the contract;

- Insurance or other provisions related to liability for damages caused by the work;

- Assertion of compliance of the design project, which is the object of the construction or demolition contract, with regulations in force;

- Starting and ending dates of the work and the handover date.

- Conditions related to cases of force majeure;

- Dispute resolution related to the contract.

### Article 68 .-
For construction or demolition contracts for private residential buildings, abusive clauses shall be considered null and void.

Abusive clauses in construction or demolition contracts for private residential buildings include:

1- Clauses requiring the construction owner to grant a mandate to the contractor to seek necessary loans for financing the construction;

2- Clauses requiring the construction owner to make full advance payment of the contract price in order for the contractor to handover the building to the construction owner;

3- Clauses prohibiting the construction owner or their representative from entering the site to inspect before the payment of the contract price at each stage and before handover;

4- Clauses requiring the contractor to implement construction or demolition work according to the construction, repair, or demolition permit where technical conditions have changed significantly and differ from the technical conditions of the project that is the object of the contract;

5- Clauses exempting the contractor from the obligation to fully implement the work within the timeframe determined in the contract by recognizing various reasons for delay, except for reasons of force majeure and reasons caused by the construction owner themselves.

### Article 69 .-
The parties to a construction or demolition contract may include conditions precedent regarding the obligations or rights arising from the contract, as follows:

1- Land acquisition or real rights permitting construction, in cases where the construction owner has received a promise to sell;

2- Obtaining a construction, repair, or demolition permit or other authorizations from the competent authorities. In this case, the construction owner must clearly specify the date of the permit application;

3- Obtaining a loan to finance the construction or demolition work;

4- Obtaining insurance for damages caused by the construction or demolition work;

5- Obtaining a guarantee for the supply of construction materials, construction equipment, or construction products for the building.

### Article 70 .-
The obligation to complete the work which is the object of the construction or demolition contract without defects includes:

1- The construction work is implemented in accordance with the permit, construction technical regulations, and other regulations and instructions in force;

2- The construction work is implemented using appropriate methods and with professional expertise;

3- Construction materials, construction equipment, or construction products supplied for the work are suitable for the functional use in accordance with the design project and are of the quality required by construction technical regulations;

4- Construction materials, construction equipment, or construction products supplied for the work must be new, unless otherwise specified in the contract.

### Article 71 .-
The warranty period for defects of work that is the object of the construction contract, counting from the handover date, is determined as follows:

a- At least 10 (ten) years for structural components made of reinforced concrete, steel-concrete, or steel;

b- At least 5 (five) years for external walls, windows, doors, and building roofs;

c- At least 2 (two) years for electrical, plumbing, and mechanical work, and other related work.

The parties to a construction contract may agree to a longer warranty period for defects than the periods specified in the above paragraphs.

Any agreement on a warranty period for defects shorter than the periods specified in the above paragraphs shall be null and void.

# Chapter 17
# Insurance

### Article 72 .-
A builder/contractor must have site liability insurance for construction or demolition work which is the object of the construction or demolition contract in order to provide compensation for damage, loss of property, injury, disability, or loss of life caused by that work to persons performing the work and third parties.

The competent authorities must ensure that the builder/contractor has entered into an insurance contract with an insurance company before authorizing the opening of the construction site.

### Article 73 .-
In the case where a builder/contractor enters into a subcontracting contract, the obligation to enter into site liability insurance shall be borne by that subcontractor. The site liability insurance must cover all operations of that subcontractor.

In the case where a construction owner enters into contracts with several builders/contractors for construction work on the same construction, each of those builders/contractors shall enter into site liability insurance individually.

### Article 74 .-
In the case of construction operations for real estate development projects, the real estate developer must enter into site liability insurance with an insurance company directly.

### Article 75 .-
The types and sizes of construction projects that require insurance for construction work shall be determined by Sub-decree.

# Chapter 18
# Liability of Persons Involved in Construction

### Article 76 .-
A design project designer shall be liable for compensation for damages caused to others if the construction design project has defects, except for cases where the designer can provide evidence that their design project complied with the standard regulations in force.

### Article 77 .-
During the implementation of construction work, the construction owner and builder/contractor shall be jointly and severally liable for compensation for damages caused to others in cases where the construction work or the management of the construction site has defects.

### Article 78 .-
A construction inspector and certifier shall be liable for compensation for damages caused to others due to defects in their inspection and certification work.

### Article 79 .-
The construction owner, construction manager, and construction lessee shall be jointly and severally liable for compensation for damages caused to others in cases where the use or management of the construction has defects.

### Article 80 .-
The right to claim compensation for damages caused by defects in design project study, construction or demolition work, or construction site management, construction inspection and certification, construction use, and construction management shall be extinguished by the statute of limitations if:

- A period of 3 (three) years has passed since the victim or their legally designated representative knew that they could claim compensation for damages; or

- A period of 10 (ten) years has passed since the damage occurred.

### Article 81 .-
A producer of construction materials, construction equipment, or construction products shall be liable for compensation for damages caused to others due to defects in their products, except where the producer can provide any of the following evidence:

1- At the time when the construction materials, construction equipment, or construction products were marketed, scientific and technical knowledge could not detect the defects that caused the damage;

2- The construction materials, construction equipment, or construction products were produced in accordance with mandatory technical regulations.

### Article 82 .-
The right to claim compensation for damages caused by defects in construction materials, construction equipment, or construction products shall be extinguished by the statute of limitations if:

1- A period of 3 (three) years has passed since the victim or their legal representative knew that they could claim compensation for damage; or

2- A period of 10 (ten) years has passed since the construction materials, construction equipment, or construction products were first delivered by the producer. In cases where the construction materials, construction equipment, or construction products contain substances affecting human health, this period shall be increased by 20 (twenty) years.

# Chapter 19
# Dispute Resolution in the Construction Sector
# Section 1
# Complaints in the Construction Sector

### Article 83 .-
Any person having an interest in a decision of a competent authority in the construction sector has the right to file a written complaint within 60 (sixty) days from the date of receiving notification of that decision to the competent authority that issued the decision or to the Ministry of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

The competent authority receiving the complaint must issue a written decision on the complaint, stating the reasons, within 30 (thirty) working days from the date of receiving the complaint.

In cases where the competent authority receiving the complaint does not decide on the complaint within the timeframe specified in the above paragraph, the complainant may file a complaint with the Ministry of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction within 60 (sixty) days from the date the timeframe expires.

The Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction must issue a written decision stating the reasons within 30 (thirty) working days from the date of receiving the complaint.

Any person dissatisfied with the decision on the complaint has the right to file a lawsuit in court within 30 (thirty) days from the date of receiving the notification of that decision.

In cases where the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction does not decide on the complaint within the timeframe specified in the above paragraph, the complainant may file a lawsuit in court within 30 (thirty) days from the date the timeframe expires.

### Article 84 .-
Any person having an interest in a decision of a competent authority in the construction sector must file a written complaint within 60 (sixty) days from the date of receiving notification of that decision to the Ministry of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction before filing a lawsuit in court.

The Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction must issue a written decision stating the reasons within 30 (thirty) working days from the date of receiving the complaint.

In cases where any person is dissatisfied with the decision of the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction, that person has the right to file a lawsuit in court in accordance with procedures within 30 (thirty) days from the date of receiving notification of that decision.

### Article 85 .-
Any person dissatisfied with any measure taken by a construction inspection official may file a written complaint with the Ministry of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction within 60 (sixty) working days from the date of receiving notification of that measure, or may file a lawsuit in court in accordance with the procedures in force within 30 (thirty) days from the date of receiving notification of that measure.

The Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction must issue a decision on the complaint, stating the reasons, within 30 (thirty) working days from the date of receiving the complaint.

In cases where any person is dissatisfied with the decision of the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction, that person has the right to file a complaint in court in accordance with procedures within 30 (thirty) days at the latest after receiving the notification of that decision.

In cases where the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction does not decide on the complaint within the timeframe specified in the above paragraph, the complainant may file a lawsuit in court within 30 (thirty) days from the date the timeframe expires.

# Section 2
# Dispute Resolution Related to the Exercise of Personal Rights in the Construction Sector and Construction or Demolition Contracts

### Article 86 .-
Any person who suffers damage or disturbance or becomes a victim of damage due to the exercise of rights or obligations of any person in the construction sector may submit a request to the Construction Dispute Resolution Commission for conciliation before filing a lawsuit in court.

The request for conciliation must be made in writing within 60 (sixty) days from the date of suffering the impact.

The Construction Dispute Resolution Commission must conduct conciliation with the parties to the dispute within 60 (sixty) days from the date of receiving the request.

In cases where the Construction Dispute Resolution Commission decides to reject the request for conciliation, the Construction Dispute Resolution Commission must issue a written notification to the requester. In this case, the requester has the right to file a lawsuit in court in accordance with the procedures in force.

In cases where the Construction Dispute Resolution Commission does not implement the conciliation procedure within the timeframe specified in the above paragraph, the requester has the right to file a lawsuit in court according to the procedures in force.

In cases where the parties to a dispute cannot reach a unanimous agreement to terminate the dispute through conciliation by the Construction Dispute Resolution Commission, the requester has the right to file a lawsuit in court in accordance with the procedures in force, counting from the date the timeframe expires.

### Article 87 .-
A party or parties to a construction or demolition contract may submit a request to the Construction Dispute Resolution Commission to conciliate and resolve disputes related to the implementation of that construction or demolition contract.

The request for conciliation must be made in writing within 30 (thirty) days from the date the dispute arose.

The Construction Dispute Resolution Commission must conduct conciliation with the parties to the dispute and issue a written decision stating the reasons within 30 (thirty) working days from the date of receiving the request for conciliation.

The parties to a dispute may decide to stop accepting the conciliation and dispute resolution of the Construction Dispute Resolution Commission at any stage of the conciliation process; however, the parties to the dispute must notify the Construction Dispute Resolution Commission in writing about the withdrawal from the conciliation and dispute resolution of the Construction Dispute Resolution Commission before filing a lawsuit in court.

### Article 88 .-
The statute of limitations for the extinction of the right to file a lawsuit shall be suspended in cases where a request for conciliation is submitted to the Construction Dispute Resolution Commission.

### Article 89 .-
The Construction Dispute Resolution Commission has the following structure:

1- National Construction Dispute Resolution Commission;

2- Capital and Provincial Construction Dispute Resolution Commissions;

3- Municipal, District, and Khan Construction Dispute Resolution Commissions.

The organization and functioning of the Construction Dispute Resolution Commission shall be determined by Sub-decree.

# Chapter 20
# Penal Provisions

### Article 90 .-
Sanctions in this law include written warnings, suspension or revocation of professional or business licenses or certificates in the construction sector, transitional fines, prohibition from professional practice, compulsory demolition and/or reconstruction of parts to their original state, fines, and imprisonment.

### Article 91 .-
Written warnings and the suspension or revocation of professional or business licenses or certificates in the construction sector are the authority of the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

The Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction may delegate the authority mentioned in this article to the Governors of the Capital and Provinces, Municipalities, Districts, and Khans.

### Article 92 .-
The imposition of transitional fines is the authority of construction inspection officials.

The payment of transitional fines shall extinguish the public action.

In cases where the offender refuses to pay the transitional fine, the construction inspection official may build a case file for the offense and send it to the competent court.

Offenses subject to transitional fines shall be determined by Sub-decree.

Procedures for transitional fines, payment of fines, management of fine payment receipts, and the management of proceeds from fines as provided for in this law shall be determined by inter-ministerial Prakas of the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction, the Minister of Justice, and the Minister of Economy and Finance.

The Royal Government may decide to provide incentives to officials who participate in cracking down on offenses in the construction sector.

### Article 93 .-
A fine of 5,000,000 (five million) Riels to 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels shall be imposed on any person who is not an engineer, architect, or other construction sector expert but performs a professional function in the construction sector.

Imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 1 (one) year and double the fine shall be imposed for repeat offenses committed within 1 (one) year.

### Article 94 .-
A fine of 4,000,000 (four million) Riels to 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels shall be imposed on any engineer, architect, or other construction expert who performs a professional function in the construction sector without being registered with a professional council.

Imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 1 (one) year and double the fine shall be imposed for repeat offenses committed within 1 (one) year.

### Article 95 .-
A fine of 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels to 40,000,000 (forty million) Riels shall be imposed on any person who independently practices a profession in the construction sector without a license.

Imprisonment from 1 (one) year to 3 (three) years and double the fine shall be imposed for repeat offenses committed within 1 (one) year.

### Article 96 .-
Imprisonment from 1 (one) year to 3 (three) years and a fine of 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels to 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels shall be imposed on any person who conducts business in the construction sector without a permit and causes injury or health damage to others.

### Article 97 .-
A fine of 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels to 40,000,000 (forty million) Riels shall be imposed on any person who defies a decision of the competent authority to suspend, rectify, stop, or demolish a construction, or a decision to prohibit the use of a construction.

Imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 3 (three) months and double the fine shall be imposed on any person who defies a second decision of the competent authority to suspend, rectify, stop, or demolish a construction, or a decision to prohibit the use of a construction.

### Article 98 .-
Imprisonment from 1 (one) year to 3 (three) years and a fine of 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels to 40,000,000 (forty million) Riels shall be imposed on any person who uses or installs construction materials, construction equipment, or construction products necessary for ensuring construction quality and safety for users which do not comply with construction technical regulations or technical orders for those materials, equipment, and products that are mandatory to comply with determined standards, and causes injury or health damage to others.

### Article 99 .-
Imprisonment from 1 (one) year to 3 (three) years and a fine of 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels to 40,000,000 (forty million) Riels shall be imposed on any design project designer who has designed a design project without complying with construction technical regulations where the implementation of that design project causes injury or health damage to others.

### Article 100 .-
Imprisonment from 1 (one) year to 3 (three) years and a fine of 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels to 40,000,000 (forty million) Riels shall be imposed on any builder/contractor who has conducted construction or demolition work without complying with the design project and construction technical regulations, causing injury or health damage to others.

### Article 101 .-
Imprisonment from 2 (two) years to 5 (five) years and a fine of 30,000,000 (thirty million) Riels to 60,000,000 (sixty million) Riels shall be imposed on any inspector and certifier who has certified a design project or construction or demolition work as correct when in fact that design project or construction or demolition work does not comply with construction technical regulations and/or the approved design project, causing injury or health damage to others.

### Article 102 .-
Imprisonment from 1 (one) year to 3 (three) years and a fine of 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels to 40,000,000 (forty million) Riels shall be imposed on any person who uses or allows for business use a construction that does not have a construction usage certificate, causing injury or health damage to others.

### Article 103 .-
The offenses stated in Articles 95, 96, 97, 98, 99, 100, 101, and 102 of this law shall be punished by imprisonment from 5 (five) years to 10 (ten) years when the offense causes amputation or permanent disability to the victim.

The offenses stated in Articles 95, 96, 97, 98, 99, 100, 101, and 102 of this law shall be punished by imprisonment from 7 (seven) years to 15 (fifteen) years when the offense causes the death of a person.

### Article 104 .-
Punishments as provided in Articles 97, 98, 99, 100, 101, 102, and 103 of this law shall be imposed when the offenses mentioned in those articles are committed by a technical director of a company conducting business in the construction sector.

### Article 105 .-
A fine of 40,000,000 (forty million) Riels to 80,000,000 (eighty million) Riels shall be imposed on any legal entity that conducts business in the construction sector without a license.

A legal entity may be declared to be criminally liable under the conditions provided for in Article 42 (Criminal Liability of Legal Entities) of the Criminal Code for the offenses provided for in Articles 97, 98, 99, 100, 101, 102, and 103 of this law.

The legal entity shall be punished by a fine of 60,000,000 (sixty million) Riels to 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels and one or more of the following additional penalties:

1- Dissolution according to the form determined by Article 170 (Dissolution and Liquidation of Legal Entities) of the Criminal Code;

2- Judicial supervision according to the form determined by Article 171 (Judicial Supervision) of the Criminal Code;

3- Prohibition from performing one or more activities according to the form determined by Article 172 (Prohibition of Activities) of the Criminal Code;

4- Exclusion from public procurement according to the form determined by Article 173 (Exclusion from Public Procurement) of the Criminal Code;

5- Posting of the sentencing decision according to the form determined by Article 180 (Posting of Decisions) of the Criminal Code;

6- Publication of the sentencing decision in print media or broadcast by any means according to the form determined by Article 181 (Publication of Decisions by Audio-visual Telecommunication Means, etc.) of the Criminal Code.

# Chapter 21
# Transitional Provisions

### Article 106 .-
Within 2 (two) years after this law enters into force, construction owners who completed construction without a permit or constructed differently from the permit before this law entered into force, must apply

for a construction usage certificate from the competent authority in the case where that construction requires a construction permit.

During the timeframe specified above, all penal provisions in force related to the construction sector shall continue to be implemented for constructions that were completed without a permit or constructed differently from the permit before this law entered into force.

### Article 107 .-
The competent authority may grant a construction usage certificate for constructions that were completed without a permit or constructed differently from the permit before this law entered into force, in the case where that construction does not cause danger to users and the public and does not affect public order.

The form and procedures for granting a construction usage certificate for constructions completed before this law entered into force shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

### Article 108 .-
Within 2 (two) years after this law enters into force, construction laboratories that have been and are operating or conducting business must apply for an operation permit or business license in accordance with the provisions of this law.

### Article 109 .-
If necessary, the Royal Government may require professionals or companies conducting business in the construction sector to place a security deposit at the National Bank of Cambodia to guarantee their liability in their professional or business practice. Professionals or companies conducting business in the construction sector may withdraw the security deposit when they stop their professional or business practice, and when the license expires or is revoked by the competent authority.

Service recipients of professionals or companies conducting business in the construction sector who suffer damages shall have second-tier priority after the wages of workers and employees over the security deposit mentioned in the above paragraph.

The conditions and procedures for the security deposit, withdrawal of the security deposit, and the minimum amount to be deposited at the National Bank of Cambodia for each type of license to guarantee professional or business liability in the construction sector shall be determined by Sub-decree.

# Chapter 22
# Final Provisions

### Article 110 .-
Any provisions that are contrary to this law shall be null and void.

This law shall be promulgated as urgent.

# Annex to the Law on Construction
# Glossary

1- **Construction work** refers to design project study, surveying work, building work, demolition work, construction site management, inspection and certification work, testing work, construction project management, or inspection work for construction quality and safety.

2- **Design work** refers to drawing work, research and data analysis for architectural and engineering work, planning work, preparation of cost estimation lists, preparation of technical instructions, and detailed drawing work.

3- **Surveying work** refers to study and analysis of data and information obtained from measuring, surveying, mapping, and observation for use in planning, design project study, and building work.

4- **Building work** refers to earthwork, construction of new buildings, repair work, modification work, and installation work.

5- **Repair work** refers to repair of any part of a construction or complete repair of a construction that has been damaged for any reason, including replacement of old decayed parts that may cause accidental danger, and improvements, or installation of other internal equipment while maintaining aesthetics and original form and without affecting structural components.

6- **Modification work** refers to change of function, overall form or any part of a construction.

7- **Demolition work** refers to removal or extraction of any part or extraction of whole construction or construction wreckage work.

8- **Certification work** refers to the inspection, analysis, and certification of design projects, calculations, and technical instructions for construction or demolition work, and implementation of construction or demolition work regarding compliance with construction technical regulations and other regulations in force, in order to ensure safety and well-being in construction or demolition and in construction use.

9- **Testing work** refers to the study, analysis, and measurement calculations in the fields of geology, structural components, construction equipment and machinery, and construction materials, equipment, and products.

10- **Construction project management** refers to work implemented by a construction project manager on behalf of the construction owner to ensure that the construction or demolition project proceeds efficiently in terms of time, cost, quality, and safety.

11- **Revocation** refers to a decision to abrogate a license or permit in the construction sector by an authority or competent official.

12- **Construction contract** refers to a contract between a construction owner and a builder/contractor, and a contract between a builder/contractor and a subcontractor for the implementation of construction or demolition work.

13- **Design document** refers to technical documents for construction work, including architectural drawings, structural drawings, mechanical system drawings, electrical system drawings, plumbing system drawings, fire safety system drawings, site plans, documents, and other technical instructions for construction or demolition work and construction use.

14- **Contract fee** refers to fees or other remuneration that a construction owner must pay to a builder/contractor as determined in the construction contract.

15- **Force majeure** refers to events that occur beyond one's will, which cannot be foreseen and cannot be overcome.

16- **Building technical regulations** refers to technical standards, technical conditions, and technical rules of a mandatory nature for the implementation of construction work which are approved by the competent institution.

17- **Spatial and urban regulations** refers to regulations provided for in legal norms on spatial planning and urbanization, spatial plans, land use master plans, land use plans, and detailed urban plans.

18- **Load** refers to weight, pressure, or all forces that press upon or affect structural components.

19- **Construction equipment** refers to equipment that is assembled, created, or produced for use or installation in construction to increase the quality, comfort, and ease of construction use, such as lamps, electrical wires, optical cables, lavatories, faucets, toilets, air conditioners, elevators, and pipes.

20- **Construction product** refers to finished or semi-finished products resulting from creation or production using construction materials and specifically intended for use as structural components, such as tiles, bricks, mortar, concrete, columns, walls, roofs, concrete floors, concrete sewage pipes, wall putty, paint, wallpaper, and tile adhesive.

21- **Producer** refers to a manufacturer, an importer, or any person who places their trademark on a product or acts as a seller.

22- **Construction owner** refers to an owner of a construction or a real estate developer who has constructed on their own land or on others' land with permission from the landowner, or a perpetual lessee who has constructed on the land of a perpetual lessor.

23- **Construction controller** refers to a professional official who is assigned by the competent authority to inspect construction or demolition work and to inspect construction quality and safety.

24- **Defect** refers to a deviation from the normal state.

25- **Occupancy certificate** refers to a permit granted by a competent authority to a construction owner to use, lease, or conduct business in that construction.

26- **Dangerous building** refers to any part of a construction, construction materials, or equipment installed in a construction that may cause danger to neighboring constructions, or to the life, body, or health of users of the construction, neighbors, and the public.

27- **Construction** refers to the process of construction work, buildings, structural components, or all architectural structures of a permanent or temporary nature, created using construction materials, construction equipment, or construction products.

28- **Construction material** refers to raw materials for melting, mixing, assembling, or use as structural components, construction equipment, or construction products, such as sand, stones, gravel, cement, steel, glass, plastic, soil, ceramics, and wood.

29- **Builder** refers to a person who contracts for construction or demolition work, a skilled builder or demolisher, or a construction team.

30- **Real estate developer** refers to a merchant, investor, or company that conducts business in constructing buildings for sale to seek profit.

31- **Competent authority** refers to the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction, and Governors of the Board of Governors of the Capital and Provinces, and Municipalities, Districts, and Khans.

32- **Construction professional** refers to an architect or engineer who is registered with the Board of Architects of Cambodia or the Board of Engineers of Cambodia, or any other professional registered with other relevant professional boards in the construction sector.

33- **Tradesperson** refers to a construction technician (skilled worker/craftsperson) who has received training from a relevant technical school or who has the skill and experience in implementing construction work, or a professional in the construction sector for whom a professional board has not yet been established.

34- **Building manager** refers to a person who has received authorization or the right to manage a construction from a construction owner for a determined period.
