# Law on Peaceful Demonstration

## Chapter 1
## General Provisions

#### Article 1._
This law aims to determine the organization and functioning of peaceful demonstration in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 2._
This law aims to ensure the protection of the freedom of expression of Cambodian citizens through peaceful demonstration, but this right shall not be abused to affect the rights, freedoms and honors of others, good customs of the national society, public order and national security.

#### Article 3._
This law has the scope of application to all gatherings or marches for peaceful demonstration in the Kingdom of Cambodia, but does not apply to:

1- Meetings or gatherings or marches in any election campaign.

2- Gatherings inside and outside the fences of factories or enterprises or various institutions related to labor disputes which shall be included in the scope of the Labor Law.

3- Marches, parades, funeral processions and other gatherings for the purpose of serving religion, arts, culture, national tradition and educational dissemination activities for social benefits.

#### Article 4._
Peaceful demonstration refers to the gathering or march by a group of people to demand, protest or publicly express sentiments, ideas or their will peacefully or by other peaceful means.

## Chapter 2
## Procedures for Notification of Peaceful Demonstration

#### Article 5._
Any group of people intending to organize a peaceful demonstration in any public place shall notify in writing to the competent territorial authorities of the Capital or Province managing that place.

#### Article 6._
The notification shall specify:

- Surname, given name and permanent address for communication of three (3) leaders, with copies of Cambodian national identity cards attached.

- Purpose of the peaceful demonstration.

- Date, time and duration of the peaceful demonstration, the place and routes to be used for activities, the number of participants, as well as the number of vehicles by each type.

All notification applicants shall ensure to respect and fulfill their roles and responsibilities as stated in Article 16 of this law.

#### Article 7._
The notification shall be submitted to the Capital Hall or Provincial Hall at least five (5) working days prior to the scheduled date of the peaceful demonstration.

#### Article 8._
Upon receiving the notification, the Capital Hall or Provincial Hall:

1- Issue a written receipt to the person who submitted the notification.

2- Disseminate this notification immediately in an easily visible place in the administrative building or on its official website.

3- Send copies of this notification to the local police commissioner, the Royal Gendarmerie office, and other relevant competent authorities involved in the demonstration site.

#### Article 9._
The competent territorial authorities of the Capital or Province shall respond positively to the notification in writing, except when:

- The peaceful demonstration is held on the days of the King's Birthday Ceremony, the King's Coronation Ceremony, the Water Festival, the Moon Salutation and Ak Ambok, the National Independence Day, the Khmer Traditional New Year, and the Pchum Ben Festival.

- There is clear information that the demonstration will cause any danger or will seriously affect security, safety and public order.

#### Article 10._
The competent territorial authorities of the Capital or Province shall examine and respond within a maximum of three (3) working days from the date the notification was submitted. If the competent territorial authorities of the Capital or Province fail to respond by the scheduled time of the peaceful demonstration, it means the competent territorial authorities of the Capital or Province have agreed.

#### Article 11._
In case the competent territorial authorities of the Capital or Province have clear information that...

this peaceful demonstration will cause any danger or will seriously affect security, safety and public order, the competent territorial authorities of the Capital or Province shall respond by immediately notifying the applicant so that they can meet with the local authorities and other relevant authorities to consider and discuss for a resolution.

#### Article 12._
After the discussion, if both parties still cannot reach an agreement, the competent territorial authorities of the Capital or Province shall request a decision from the Minister of Interior.

#### Article 13._
The decision of the Minister of Interior shall be made in writing and shall be provided to the applicant at least twenty-four (24) hours before the scheduled time of the peaceful demonstration. Copies of the decision, including details of the agreed amendments, shall be provided to the relevant competent authorities and to other authorities whose areas might be affected by this peaceful demonstration.

#### Article 14._
The full specification and guarantee as stated in paragraph 1 of Article 6 of this Law are not required, only a written notification signed or thumbprinted and accompanied by copies of Cambodian national identity cards of three (3) leaders to the local competent territorial authorities of the Capital or Province at least twelve (12) hours during working days or thirty-six (36) hours during holidays before the peaceful demonstration, if the peaceful demonstration is held at the following places:

1- At public expression places established legally according to laws or regulations, and where the peaceful demonstration has a maximum of two hundred (200) participants for each Capital or Province.

2- At private property or collective property with the consent of the owner or the management committee responsible for that collective property, and the peaceful demonstration has a maximum of two hundred (200) participants.

However, in case there are two (2) or more different groups wishing to hold a peaceful demonstration at the same place and time, the competent territorial authorities of the Capital or Province shall decide that the group who submitted the notification first shall have the priority, or may allow the other group to hold it at another place at least five hundred (500) meters away.

The peaceful demonstration as stated in this article can only take place between 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM only.

## Chapter 3
## Responsibilities of Demonstration Leaders and Competent Authorities

#### Article 15._
For a peaceful demonstration that has been agreed upon and involves a march on public roads, the territorial authorities of the Commune, Sangkat, District, Khan, Municipality, Province, Capital, or officials appointed as representatives shall cooperate with the local police and the Royal Gendarmerie to prevent any possible bad consequences by determining the aforementioned routes to facilitate and ease traffic for vehicles to avoid serious disruption to work processes, businesses, services, commerce, or other trades.

#### Article 16._
Leaders of a peaceful demonstration shall be responsible for taking appropriate measures to ensure that the demonstration will take place peacefully and according to the following conditions:

1- Notify the participants about their responsibilities as stated in point 3 of this Article.

2- Discuss and cooperate with local officials and relevant competent authorities to ensure the demonstration takes place peacefully.

3- Maintain order and good discipline correctly according to the scheduled time and place from the start to the end of the peaceful demonstration.

#### Article 17._
The competent authorities shall take measures to protect the spirit of peaceful demonstration to ensure security, safety and public order are well maintained and shall not interfere with the process of the peaceful demonstration.

#### Article 18._
When there is a request for assistance from the leaders of a peaceful demonstration, the competent authorities shall make efforts to carefully respond to reasonable requests based on law to ensure the exercise of the right to peaceful demonstration and the exercise of the right to freedom of public expression with dignity.

#### Article 19._
Competent authorities appointed to maintain security, safety and public order at peaceful demonstration sites shall wear proper uniforms, including name tags and identification numbers displayed on the front, and must strictly maintain dignified behavior.

#### Article 20._
Peaceful demonstrations that have been agreed upon, including cases where the competent territorial authorities of the Capital or Province failed to respond as stated in Article 10 of this Law, shall not be dispersed.

In case a peaceful demonstration becomes violent, the competent authorities shall take appropriate measures to prevent and stop this demonstration immediately.

Even if a demonstration is conducted peacefully, if no notification was submitted according to Article 5, Article 6 and Article 14 of this Law, the competent authorities may take measures to stop that demonstration.

## Chapter 4
## Procedures for Resolving Offenses and Penal Provisions

#### Article 21._
A written warning shall be issued to the leaders of a peaceful demonstration who have violated Article 5, Article 16 and Article 20, paragraphs 2 and 3 of this Law.

#### Article 22._
A written warning shall be issued to any territorial authority official or competent authority official who has violated Article 17, Article 18, Article 19 and Article 20, paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Law.

#### Article 23._
In case any demonstration involves carrying instruments that could cause danger or affect others, or committed any act that interferes with the rights and freedoms of others, the competent authorities shall seize those instruments or prohibit such acts from continuing. If those individuals are stubborn and refuse to hand over...

give it up or refuse to stop committing such acts, the competent authorities shall temporarily detain them until the peaceful demonstration is completely finished, then they will be released, unless there is any other offense.

#### Article 24._
In case any demonstrator carries weapons or explosives, the competent authorities shall seize those weapons or explosives and detain them, then send them to the competent institutions for resolution according to the laws in force.

#### Article 25._
Any person who takes the opportunity during a peaceful demonstration to commit theft, robbery or cause damage to private property or public property shall be punished according to the criminal law in force.

#### Article 26._
If the process of the peaceful demonstration causes violence resulting in damage to private property or public property, the repair of the damage shall be the responsibility of the perpetrator and the accomplice.

In case those individuals do not have the ability to repair the damage, the competent authorities shall compile a case file and send it to the court.

#### Article 27._
If the process of the peaceful demonstration causes violence, any person who commits an offense resulting in injury or death to competent authorities or demonstrators or other persons shall be punished according to the criminal law in force.

## Chapter 5
## Transitional Provisions

#### Article 28._
Within six (6) months after this Law enters into force, each Capital and Province shall create a "Public Expression Place" by choosing a suitable open space or center with high safety, and where it is easy to hear and see within its jurisdiction for peaceful demonstrations or public expression as stated in Article 14 of this Law.

## Chapter 6
## Final Provisions

#### Article 29._
The Law on Demonstration adopted by the National Assembly of the State of Cambodia on December 27, 1991, promulgated by Decree No. 114 Kr. dated December 28, 1991, and any provisions contrary to this Law shall be null and void.

#### Article 30._
This Law shall be declared as urgent.
