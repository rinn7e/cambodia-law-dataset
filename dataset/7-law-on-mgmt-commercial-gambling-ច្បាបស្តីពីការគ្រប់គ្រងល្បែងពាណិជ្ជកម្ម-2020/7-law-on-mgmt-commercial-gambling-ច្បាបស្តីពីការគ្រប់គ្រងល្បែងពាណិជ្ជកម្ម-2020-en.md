### Promulgates

The Law on the Management of Commercial Gambling, which was adopted by the National Assembly on October 5, 2020 at the 5th session of the 6th legislature, and which was fully reviewed in its form and legal aspects by the Senate on October 26, 2020 at the extraordinary session of the 4th legislature, the entire text of which is as follows:

# Law

# on

# the Management of Commercial Gambling

### Chapter 1
### General Provisions

#### Article 1.-

This Law has the purpose to ensure the management of integrated commercial gambling centers and commercial gambling in order to contribute to promoting economic growth, enhancing tourism, creating jobs, collecting revenue, and maintaining safety and social security by defining competence, rules, and procedures for managing integrated commercial gambling centers and commercial gambling, rights and obligations of physical and legal persons in directly operating or participating in commercial gambling, and defining ownership of integrated commercial gambling centers and commercial gambling establishments, as well as establishing standards for integrated commercial gambling centers and commercial gambling of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 2.-

This Law has the scope of application to integrated commercial gambling centers and commercial gambling in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 3.-

Key terms used in this Law shall have the meanings as defined in the Glossary, which is the Annex of this Law.

### Chapter 2
### Integrated Resort Management and Commercial Gambling Committee (CGMC)

### Section 1
### Competence of the CGMC

#### Article 4.-

The Integrated Resort Management and Commercial Gambling Committee (CGMC) is established, having competence to propose policies, issue regulations, manage, inspect, license, collect revenue, and take all necessary measures related to the management of integrated commercial gambling centers and commercial gambling.

#### Article 5.-

The CGMC shall consist of 11 (eleven) members as follows:

1- Minister of Economy and Finance - Chairperson

2- Secretary of State of the Ministry of Interior, 1 (one) person - Vice-Chairperson

3- Secretary of State of the Ministry of Economy and Finance, 1 (one) person - Member

4- Secretary of State of the Office of the Council of Ministers, 1 (one) person - Member

- Secretary of State of the Ministry of Tourism, 1 (one) person - Member

- Secretary of State of the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications, 1 (one) person - Member

- Secretary of State of the Ministry of Justice, 1 (one) person - Member

Representative of the Council for the Development of Cambodia, 1 (one) person - Member

- Commissioner-General of the National Police of the Ministry of Interior - Member

- Secretary-General of the CGMC - Member

- Deputy Secretary-General of the CGMC - Member

The actual composition of the CGMC shall be appointed by Sub-decree, upon the proposal of the Chairperson of the CGMC.

#### Article 6.-

The CGMC has the following duties and functions:

A. Act as the regulator and rule-maker for the integrated commercial gambling center and commercial gambling sectors.

B. Propose development policies for integrated commercial gambling centers and commercial gambling.

C. Grant casino licenses, direct gambling licenses, games of chance licenses, other commercial gambling licenses, special staff licenses, commercial gambling promoter licenses, and licenses for manufacturers, installers, importers, sellers, or distributors of gaming equipment and software, as well as modification, extension, suspension, shutdown of operations, temporary suspension, revocation, and return of those licenses.

D. Approve the types of games permitted for operation in casinos or in other commercial gambling establishments, as well as the rules of play and gaming rules.

E. Issue guidelines to commercial gambling operators on specific types of any game that is not permitted to operate in commercial gambling establishments.

F. Approve standards for internal control, administrative procedures, and accounting policies for casinos and other commercial gambling establishments.

G. Define measures to protect vulnerable people, including children, from gambling and prevent money laundering and terrorism financing related to commercial gambling.

H. Review and decide on all complaints related to gambling operations within the extent permitted by this Law.

#### Article 7.-

The Vice-Chairperson and members of the CGMC shall have a term of 5 (five) years and can be reappointed, except for the members who are the Secretary-General and Deputy Secretary-General, whose terms are not limited.

Any Vice-Chairperson or member of the CGMC may lose their position in the following cases:

1- Request for resignation.

2- Loss of capacity to perform duties.

C. Loss of qualifications as defined in Article 8 of this Law.

D. Termination of position by decision of the CGMC by a two-thirds (2/3) majority of the total members of the CGMC due to misconduct in their duties.

E. Imprisonment for any felony or misdemeanor.

Any Vice-Chairperson or member of the CGMC who is charged with any felony or misdemeanor shall be temporarily suspended from their duties.

The replacement of a Vice-Chairperson and members of the CGMC who lose their membership shall follow the same procedures as the appointment.

#### Article 8.-

The CGMC shall organize regular meetings every month. The CGMC may convene extraordinary meetings at any time depending on necessity, upon the invitation of the Chairperson, or upon the request of the Secretary-General of the CGMC, or upon the request of members representing at least 1/3 (one-third) of the total members. Meetings of the CGMC may proceed only with the presence of more than half of the total members. Members of the CGMC who are absent cannot be represented. If the meeting does not reach a quorum, the meeting shall be postponed to a later date determined by the Chairperson, and the same agenda shall be discussed. Decisions of the CGMC are valid only when approved by a majority of the members present. In the case of a tie vote, the Chairperson's vote shall be decisive.

The remuneration of the Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson, and members of the CGMC shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Economy and Finance, upon the proposal approved by the plenary meeting of the CGMC.

#### Article 9.-

The CGMC shall have its logo and seal for use in its operations.

### Section 2
### Jurisdiction of the CGMC

#### Article 10.-

The CGMC may issue legal instruments, including Prakas, decisions, and rules related to:

A. The procedure and form of applications for the development of integrated commercial gambling centers.

B. The procedure and form of all types of license applications.

C. The determination of minimum services and standards that each integrated commercial gambling center and commercial gambling establishment must have, such as gaming areas, hotels, restaurants, and/or supermarkets, etc.

D. The rules of play and gaming rules.

E. Internal control, accounting, and auditing systems.

F. Promotion of commercial gambling.

G. Management and extension of credit to gaming patrons by casino operators and commercial gambling promoters.

H. Other regulations not contrary to this Law.

#### Article 11.-

The CGMC has the following enforcement rights:

A. Direct investigations in cases where there are complaints regarding violations of the provisions of this Law and other regulations related to this Law.

B. Require commercial gambling operators, owners of integrated commercial gambling centers, casino owners, and related parties to provide documents or information as necessary.

C. Conduct on-site inspections, including casinos or other commercial gambling establishments, manufacturing, sales, or distribution sites of gaming equipment and gaming software, as necessary without prior notice.

D. Enter into contracts or memorandums of understanding with royal government institutions and/or international institutions for the purposes of information sharing, technical cooperation, joint investigations, and other duties in compliance with this Law.

E. Determine licensing fees and other fees related to the management and control of commercial gambling, including commissions for payment to commercial gambling promoters.

F. Other powers necessary to ensure the effective implementation of this Law.

#### Article 12.-

The CGMC shall have a General Secretariat as its staff, which is led by 1 (one) Secretary-General and 1 (one) Deputy Secretary-General.

The Minister of Economy and Finance and the Minister of Interior shall jointly propose the appointment of the Secretary-General and Deputy Secretary-General of the CGMC to the Prime Minister.

The Secretary-General representing the Ministry of Economy and Finance shall be responsible for overall management and management of collection of obligatory revenue from gambling, fees, licensing fees, and other revenues defined by this Law.

The Deputy Secretary-General representing the Ministry of Interior shall be responsible for the field of security, public order, and gaming technology.

The structure and functioning of the General Secretariat of the CGMC shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 13.-

The Secretary-General and Deputy Secretary-General of the CGMC shall have the following qualifications:

A. Have Cambodian nationality, good character, honesty, and integrity.

B. Hold the status of civil servants who have served and have a seniority of 20 (twenty) years or more, and come from the Ministry of Economy and Finance and the Ministry of Interior.

C. Not involved in commercial gambling activities in the Kingdom of Cambodia and/or have no direct financial interest with casino operators, owners of integrated commercial gambling centers or commercial gambling establishments, or related parties.

### Section 3
### Financial Resources of the CGMC

#### Article 14.-

The CGMC shall have an autonomous budget to support the effective performance of its functions and duties.

The sources of revenue of the CGMC are as follows:

A. License fees and service fees for the review and evaluation of applications for casino licenses, direct gambling licenses, games of chance licenses, other commercial gambling licenses, special employee licenses, commercial gambling promoter licenses, and licenses for manufacturers, installers, importers, sellers, or distributors of gaming equipment and software, as provided in this Law.

B. Fees on gaming tables, gaming machines, gaming equipment, and all types of gaming software.

C. Fees from various fines.

D. Analysis services and supplementary service fees of the CGMC.

E. Legal revenue authorized by the Royal Government.

F. Other legal revenue that the CGMC shall receive to serve its objectives, other than the fund for serving the development of integrated commercial gambling centers.

#### Article 15.-

The financial resources of the CGMC shall be used for the following purposes:

A. Expenses for supporting the CGMC.

B. Research relevant to the development of integrated commercial gambling centers, the commercial gambling sector, and responsible commercial gambling operations.

C. Serving social work to maximize prevention of negative impacts from commercial gambling.

D. Other necessary expenses as decided by the CGMC.

The management of the state budget and property of the CGMC shall be determined by sub-decree, in accordance with the laws and regulations on public finance.

#### Article 16.-

The CGMC is subject to audits and inspections in accordance with the laws in force. The CGMC shall prepare its annual financial reports in accordance with the standards, formats, and accounting procedures in force.

### Chapter 3
### Commercial Gambling Zones and the Framework for the Development of Integrated Commercial Gambling Centers and Commercial Gambling

#### Section 1
#### Commercial Gambling Zones

##### Article 17.-

Commercial gambling zones shall be divided into:

1. Prohibited zones. And

2. Permitted zones.

Casino games and direct gambling shall only be permitted to operate within the casino premises.

##### Article 18.-

A prohibited zone is a geographical area that is prohibited for all types of commercial gambling or for certain types of commercial gambling, due to historical, cultural, religious reasons, or due to necessity or other reasons. The actual location of the prohibited zone and the types of commercial gambling that are subject to prohibition shall be determined by sub-decree.

##### Article 19.-

A permitted zone is any geographical area other than prohibited zones.

##### Article 20.-

Commercial gambling establishments that hold legal licenses before this Law enters into force and are located in prohibited zones shall be considered as exceptional cases. These commercial gambling establishments may be converted into integrated commercial gambling centers by fulfilling all necessary conditions and must comply with the regulations on the development and ownership of integrated commercial gambling centers as provided in Section 2 and Section 3 of this Chapter.

#### Section 2
#### Framework for the Development of Integrated Commercial Gambling Centers

##### Article 21.-

The development and ownership of integrated commercial gambling centers shall be implemented under integrated commercial gambling center development agreements, in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

Owners of integrated commercial gambling centers are permitted to apply for casino licenses under the provisions of Section 2, Chapter 4 of this Law, or may contract with one or more casino operators to run commercial gambling in their integrated commercial gambling centers in accordance with the content and conditions of the relevant integrated commercial gambling center development agreements and in compliance with the provisions of this Law.

An owner of an integrated commercial gambling center may enter into one or more integrated commercial gambling center development agreements, but each integrated commercial gambling center development agreement is permitted to develop only one integrated commercial gambling center.

#### Article 22.-

Commercial gambling may operate within free trade zones of airports, international ports, and cruise ships located in permitted zones with prior written permission from the CGMC on a case-by-case basis.

A casino operator may operate commercial gambling under the provisions of the first paragraph above only if there is a casino operation agreement with the owner, concessionaire, or manager of the airport, international port, or cruise ship, as the case may be.

### Section 3
### Approval on the Development and Ownership of Integrated Commercial Gambling Centers

#### Article 23.-

The CGMC shall determine, as necessary, the minimum investment amount for the development project that the applicant is obliged to implement in accordance with the integrated commercial gambling center development agreement to obtain the right to develop and own the integrated commercial gambling center.

The minimum investment amount of the development project, as provided in the first paragraph above, which is decided by the CGMC, shall be based on the size of the approved integrated commercial gambling center, and on the stages of development of the integrated commercial gambling center and commercial gambling and the level of achievement.

#### Article 24.-

Only legal entities registered for commerce and tax under the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia may submit applications for the development of integrated commercial gambling centers, and such legal entities must have exclusive ownership rights over one or more of their locations.

An application for the development of an integrated commercial gambling center may also be made by a legal entity that has co-ownership of any location of more than one for submitting the application, when the development of the integrated commercial gambling center is permitted.

#### Article 25.-

Before submitting an application for the development of an integrated commercial gambling center, it is required to request and obtain approval in principle

of the development of the integrated commercial gambling center in advance from the Prime Minister before submitting the application for the development of the integrated commercial gambling center to the Chairperson of the CGMC for review and decision.

Applications for the development of integrated commercial gambling centers to be submitted to the CGMC shall be accompanied by:

A. Documents and information in the format and according to procedures determined by the CGMC.

B. Application form fees in the amount and according to the format determined by the CGMC. And

C. A deposit for the CGMC's expenses in conducting studies, research, investigations, or hiring necessary experts in the process of evaluating the application from start to finish, in the amount and format determined by the CGMC.

#### Article 26.-

After receiving an application for the development of an integrated commercial gambling center, the CGMC shall:

A. Conduct studies, research, and inquiries as necessary.

B. Require the applicant to provide additional relevant information or documents as necessary.

C. Require the applicant to provide authorization documents or necessary relevant consent documents to perform studies, research, and inquiries or to obtain relevant information and documents.

D. Require the applicant to provide various guarantees. And

* E. Hire experts or consultants to evaluate the application, which expenses shall be borne by the applicant.

If any change occurs regarding the information and documents required by the CGMC or in relation to any major part of the application before the application is approved or rejected, the applicant shall immediately notify the CGMC in writing of such change. During the period the application is being processed, the CGMC may make recommendations to the applicant to modify certain key contents, including the development project and the original heritage restoration project, and the applicant shall be given an opportunity to comment on those recommendations. If the applicant withdraws their application, the CGMC has the right to retain all handling fees/charges. In this case, the CGMC shall transfer to the applicant the deposited and unused amount of money, in accordance with point (C), paragraph 3 of Article 25 of this Law. The CGMC's certification of its study and research expenses is sufficient proof for this type of expense, which is the responsibility of the applicant.

Applications, all relevant information and documents provided to the CGMC by the applicant, related legal entities, or physical persons shall be considered confidential, and this information and these documents shall not be publicly disclosed to any person other than the applicant, the legal representative, or the attorney of the applicant, except as otherwise explicitly provided in the law or determined separately by the CGMC.

#### Article 27.-

In reviewing the application, the CGMC shall consider the following evaluation criteria:

The applicant:

A. Has financial resources or the ability to obtain sufficient financial resources to guarantee financial sustainability and to guarantee the operation and management of the integrated commercial gambling center. And

B. Has sufficient business capacity and experience or the ability to contact and use the services of legal entities or physical persons with sufficient business capacity and experience in successfully developing and operating the integrated commercial gambling center.

The integrated commercial gambling center proposed for development shall have:

A. Quality, special features, and attractiveness.

B. Architectural layout designs that comply with the regulations for integrated commercial gambling center zones, matching the surrounding natural landscapes, as well as harmonizing with surrounding structures and environments.

C. Suitable attractiveness and services in comparison with existing integrated commercial gambling centers based on trends and proper distinct styles, as well as in comparison with regional and international integrated commercial gambling centers.

D. Attractiveness and services at a suitable level that can meet market demands.

E. Contribution to promoting the reputation of the Kingdom of Cambodia as an international tourism and entertainment destination. And

F. A suitable scale of capital for establishing the integrated commercial gambling center, including the capacity to generate jobs for citizens in appropriate quantities and qualities.

The CGMC may determine other additional evaluation criteria for reviewing the application.

#### Article 28.-

The CGMC has the right to approve or reject the application. The CGMC has the duty to notify the applicant in writing of such approval or rejection. Applications for the development of integrated commercial gambling centers shall be approved with content and conditions and for the location determined in the integrated commercial gambling center development agreement which has been discussed and agreed upon with the applicant.

The integrated commercial gambling center shall receive development rights after:

A. The deposit determined by the CGMC has been paid to guarantee obligations as the owner of the integrated commercial gambling center in implementing the integrated commercial gambling center development agreement; and

B. The integrated commercial gambling center development agreement has been signed by the CGMC upon the decision of the Prime Minister and publicly disclosed.

#### Article 29 .-

The conditions of the Integrated Resort development agreement shall comply with the provisions of this Law and all other applicable regulations in force, and shall at least specify the following:

a. The amount and phases of development.

b. Architectural design and construction schedule.

c. Development and/or construction milestones to be achieved and the project completion date.

d. The location size as a percentage of the total area of the Integrated Resort to be used as casino location, hospitality location, shopping mall, restaurant, entertainment center, and exhibition/convention center, as the case may be.

e. The number of hotels, hotel ratings, and minimum number of rooms of each hotel.

f. The number of floors and total area of each casino.

g. Entertainment centers and other attractions of the Integrated Resort.

h. Other development obligations other than commercial gambling. And

i. Obligations to maintain and secure public risk insurance.

The Integrated Resort development agreement shall specify the cases leading to the amendment or termination of the agreement by the Royal Government, and the cases allowing the CGMC to impose penalties based on that agreement. The Integrated Resort development agreement may specify the obligations of the Integrated Resort owner in developing within the permitted zone:

a. Entertainment centers and attractions other than commercial gambling in order to contribute to making the Integrated Resort an international tourist entertainment destination. And

b. Infrastructure contributing to social-economic development in the permitted zone.

#### Section 4
#### Casino Development Framework

#### Article 30 .-

Only legal entities registered for commercial and tax purposes under the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia may apply for casino development, and such legal entities must have ownership or any legal rights over one or more locations of their own.

Casino development and ownership shall be implemented under the Casino Development Agreement to be entered into between the applicant and the CGMC in accordance with the provisions of this Law. The procedures and formalities for casino development applications shall be determined by the CGMC. The applicant for casino development shall request and obtain prior approval in principle for casino development from the Prime Minister before submitting the casino development application to the CGMC for review and decision.

A casino shall obtain development rights after:

a. Receiving approval in principle for the development of the Integrated Resort as provided in Article 28 of this Law from the Prime Minister.

b. Paying the deposit set by the CGMC to guarantee the obligations of the casino owner in implementing the casino development agreement. And

c. The casino development agreement has been signed by the CGMC and published publicly.

The conditions of the casino development agreement shall comply with the provisions of this Law or other relevant regulations, and shall at least specify the following:

a. The amount and phases of development.

b. Architectural design and construction schedule.

c. Development and/or construction milestones to be achieved and the project completion date.

d. The location size as a percentage of the total area of the casino to be used as gaming location, hospitality location, hotel, and restaurant.

e. Hotel ratings and minimum number of rooms of the hotel.

f. The number of floors and total area of the casino.

g. Other development obligations other than commercial gambling. And

h. Obligations to maintain and secure public risk insurance.

#### Section 5
#### Casino Operation Agreement

#### Article 31 .-

A Casino Operation Agreement is an instrument governing the relationship between the casino operator and the Integrated Resort owner or between the casino operator and the casino owner. The Casino Operation Agreement shall not exceed the validity of the casino license.

The parties to the Casino Operation Agreement may determine the content and conditions of this agreement which are subject to the approval of the CGMC, without prejudice to the provisions of this Law and other relevant provisions as specified in Article 10 and Article 11 of this Law.

The Casino Operation Agreement shall be approved by the CGMC. In this approval, the CGMC may determine any contents and conditions related only to public policy, but they must not contradict any provisions of this Law.

The provisions of this Article shall not apply to the Integrated Resort owner or the casino owner who has obtained a casino license in accordance with the transitional provisions of this Law.

Prior approval from CGMC is required in the following cases:

a. Amendment of the Casino Operation Agreement.

b. Addition of annexes or supplementary agreements, and modification of such annexes or supplementary agreements.

c. Separate agreements between the Integrated Resort owner or casino owner and the casino operator regarding their relationship under the spirit of the Casino Operation Agreement; or

d. Separate agreements under which the casino operator transfers all or part of its rights and obligations under the spirit of the Casino Operation Agreement.

The content and conditions of the Casino Operation Agreement may be modified as determined by the CGMC by providing a written notification of the proposed amendment to the casino operator and the Integrated Resort owner or casino owner when absolutely necessary to comply with any amendment of the casino license as provided in Article 43 of this Law.

The Integrated Resort owner or casino owner and the casino operator may comment on the CGMC's proposed amendment, and the CGMC shall consider such comments before making a decision on the amendment. The CGMC shall notify the casino operator or Integrated Resort owner or casino owner in writing of the CGMC's decision on the amendment, which shall become effective on the date specified in the notification.

#### Article 32 .-

If permitted in the Casino Operation Agreement, the casino operator may enter into a casino management contract or be a party to an amendment contract to transfer all or part of its obligations in the Casino Operation Agreement to a casino manager. The casino operator shall notify the CGMC in writing at least 30 days prior to entering into the contract or becoming a party to such amendment contract, attaching all copies of the casino management contract or amendment contract, and shall specify the key points of the contract in a form and procedure determined by the CGMC.

Prior to entering into the contract or becoming a party to the amendment contract, the CGMC may require the casino operator to conduct an investigation into the qualifications of the casino manager. During this period, the casino operator shall not enter into the casino management contract or amendment contract to transfer obligations until the approval from the CGMC is obtained.

The casino manager must have qualifications of a relevant party, and in this case, the provisions of Section 3 of Chapter 5 shall apply.

Without prejudice to the liability of the casino manager under the provisions of this Law and in addition to the specifications above, the casino operator entering into the contract or being a party to the casino management contract must always be jointly liable in fulfilling its obligations towards the owner

of the Integrated Resort or the casino owner under the Casino Operation Agreement and obligations under this Law.

#### Article 33 .-

The Casino Operation Agreement shall terminate automatically when the casino license is surrendered or revoked, and shall be effective from the date the surrender or revocation takes effect in accordance with Article 90 of this Law. The Casino Operation Agreement may be terminated at any time by either party to the agreement, regardless of the reason for termination, by giving a prior written notice of at least 180 days to the other party, unless both parties have agreed on a longer period.

Upon receipt or provision of the notice of termination of the Casino Operation Agreement, or upon receipt of the notice of surrender or revocation of the casino license in accordance with Article 90 of this Law, the Integrated Resort owner or casino owner shall immediately notify the CGMC in writing of such termination, surrender, or revocation. Meanwhile, the owner must replace the terminated casino operator with a new casino operator or directly assume the duties as the casino operator, in the case where the owner already holds a casino license or will obtain a casino license.

An application for or amendment of a casino license, or approval of a Casino Operation Agreement made for the purpose of the second paragraph above, shall be treated as a priority task of the CGMC. The decision on such application shall be made within a period not exceeding the notice period of the existing Casino Operation Agreement, or not exceeding the date of surrender or revocation of the casino license as determined by the CGMC.

If there is no appropriate replacement before the expiry of the prior notice of the existing Casino Operation Agreement, or before the date of surrender or revocation of the casino license, the Integrated Resort owner or casino owner, the old casino operator, and the new casino operator shall cooperate under the supervision of the CGMC to ensure that the transfer of casino management occurs with minimum impact on casino operations.

If there is no appropriate replacement before the expiry of the prior notice of the existing Casino Operation Agreement, or before the date of surrender or revocation of the casino license, the CGMC shall appoint one (1) casino manager under reasonable conditions until there is a suitable replacement, and the casino management shall be transferred to the new casino operator in accordance with the provisions of the fourth paragraph above.

Without prejudice to the right to receive compensation for damages of either party, no Casino Operation Agreement shall be renewed after its termination, unless the parties to the Casino Operation Agreement have agreed in writing in advance.

### Chapter 4
### Licensing Regime
#### Section 1
#### License

#### Article 35 .-

The CGMC may issue licenses according to the types of commercial gambling and related activities as follows:
- Casino license
- Direct game license
- Game of chance license
- Other commercial gambling license
- Special employee license
- Commercial gambling promoter license; and
- Manufacturer, installer, importer, seller, and/or distributor of gaming equipment and/or software license.

Legal entities may operate commercial gambling only after obtaining a prior license in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

No person may represent themselves as a representative of the license holder and commercial gambling operator unless permitted in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

All types of commercial gambling launched by commercial gambling operators must obtain prior permission.

Licensing under this Law shall be made under the condition that the license holder has the obligation to maintain their appropriate qualifications as a license holder during the period in which the license is valid.

#### Section 2
#### Casino License

#### Article 36 .-

The minimum capital for casino gambling operations shall be determined by Sub-decree. The minimum capital for direct games, games of chance, and other commercial gambling operations shall be determined by a Prakas of the CGMC.

Prior approval from the CGMC is required in the following cases:

a. Modification of the successor of the company of the commercial gambling operator.

b. Transfer in all forms and images of the qualifying shares of the commercial gambling operator or the qualifying transfer of the legal entity which is the qualifying shareholder of the commercial gambling operator.

c. Requesting the right to sell qualifying shares of a commercial gambling operator; and

d. Transferring or granting disposal rights in any form of a casino or other commercial gambling establishment, in whole or in part, or any operation of a casino or other commercial gambling establishment.

Qualifying shareholdings of legal entities listed on the Cambodia Securities Exchange and/or various international securities markets, such as the securities markets of Hong Kong, Shanghai, Tokyo, New York, Singapore, and/or London, are exempted from requesting prior permission from the CGMC, which is a requirement under point (b) of the second paragraph above. After obtaining any permission as specified in the second paragraph above, or after company registration, the commercial gambling operator must notify the CGMC in writing within 30 (thirty) days.

#### Article 37 .-

Only integrated resort owners who are legal entities registered for commerce and tax under the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia and have entered into a casino operation agreement with the integrated resort owner may apply for a casino license.

The first paragraph above shall also apply to casino owners who are legal entities registered for commerce and tax under the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia and have entered into a casino operation agreement with the casino owner.

An application for a casino license shall be submitted to the CGMC, accompanied by:

a. Documents and information in the form and procedure determined by the CGMC.

b. Formal fees in the amount and manner determined by the CGMC.

c. Deposit for CGMC expenses in studying, researching, investigating, or hiring experts, etc., which are necessary during the evaluation process of the application from start to finish, in the amount and manner determined by the CGMC; and

d. Business guarantee deposit as determined by the CGMC.

#### Article 38 .-

After receiving the casino license application, the CGMC shall:

a. Study, research, and inquire as necessary.

b. Require the applicant to provide additional relevant information or documents as necessary.

c. Require the applicant to provide necessary authorization documents or consent letters in order for the CGMC to conduct research and inquiries, or to obtain relevant information and documents.

d. Require the applicant to provide various necessary evidence; and

e. Hire experts or consultants to evaluate the application, which expense shall be borne by the applicant.

If any change occurs related to the information and documents required by the CGMC, or related to any significant part of the application, before the application is accepted or rejected, the applicant shall immediately notify the CGMC in writing of such change.

During the processing of the application, the CGMC may make recommendations to the applicant to modify certain key contents of their application, and the applicant shall be given an opportunity to comment on those recommendations.

If the applicant withdraws their application, the CGMC has the right to retain all formal fees. In this case, the CGMC must transfer to the applicant the deposited amount that has not been used, in accordance with this Law.

The CGMC's certification of its study and research expenses shall be sufficient evidence for this type of expense, which is the responsibility of the applicant.

All applications, information, and relevant documents provided to the CGMC shall be considered confidential, and this information and documents shall not be disclosed to any person other than the applicant, their legal representative, or their lawyer, unless determined otherwise by the applicant or the CGMC.

#### Article 39 .-

The CGMC shall consider and decide on the license application based on the following criteria:

1. The applicant must have:

a. Appropriate business structure and company articles of association.

b. Financial resources, including access to sufficient financial resources to ensure financial sustainability and guarantee the operation and management of the casino; and

c. Sufficient business capacity and commercial gambling experience, or have contracted for the services of a legal entity or physical person who has sufficient business capacity and commercial gambling experience in successfully establishing and operating a casino.

2. Qualifying shareholders or key personnel of the applicant:

a. Are persons of good reputation, good character, honesty, and integrity; and

b. Are persons of sound financial standing and appropriate stability.

3. Casino operation agreement between the casino operator and the integrated resort owner or casino owner that:

a. Allows the CGMC to exercise effective control over the casino operations and over the casino operator.

b. Has no negative impact on the commercial gambling market by causing unfair competition to existing casino operators and future operations; and

c. Complies with all regulations of this Law.

#### Article 40 .-

The CGMC has the right to approve or reject the application. The CGMC has the duty to notify the applicant in writing of its decision.

The casino license shall be approved in accordance with the terms and conditions determined by the CGMC, without contradicting any provisions of this Law.

The casino license must specify the types of commercial gambling and the maximum number of gaming tables, gaming machines, equipment, and gaming programs of commercial gambling that the casino operator runs in that casino.

The casino license shall be effective after:

a. Payment of the license fee and deposit in the amount and manner determined by the CGMC for the purpose of guaranteeing the legal obligations and license obligations of the casino operator.

b. The integrated resort casino specified in the license receives permission to open the casino; and

c. The casino license shall be published publicly.

#### Article 41 .-

The casino license is granted for a specific period specified in the license, which shall not exceed 20 (twenty) years, counting from the date of the public publication of the casino license.

The validity of the casino license as specified in the first paragraph above applies only to casino licenses located in the integrated resort. For casinos not located in the integrated resort, the license is granted for a period of not more than 5 (five) years.

The casino operator may submit an application to renew the validity of the casino license to the CGMC at least 6 (six) months prior to the expiration of the license, in which case the provisions of Article 37 to Article 48 of this Law shall be applied with necessary modifications.

In case the casino operator does not submit an application to renew the validity of the casino license within the time period specified in the third paragraph above, or the renewal application is rejected, the provisions of the third to sixth paragraphs of Article 35 of this Law shall apply.

#### Article 42 .-

A casino license that does not have active commercial gambling operations cannot be pledged, mortgaged, and/or transferred.

A casino license with active commercial gambling operations may be pledged, mortgaged, and/or transferred for commercial purposes. Any transfer of a casino license with active commercial gambling operations may only be made if the transfer is made to a legal entity managed by persons of integrity and good character, and who are not involved in money laundering or terrorist financing activities.

Any transfer of a casino license with active commercial gambling operations must have prior approval from the CGMC.

#### Article 43 .-

The contents and conditions of the casino license may be modified in the following cases:

a. There is a written request from the casino operator to the CGMC; or

b. Upon the decision of the CGMC within the context of administrative sanctions against the casino operator who violates the contents and conditions of the casino license.

The casino operator shall be given an opportunity to comment on the requested modification or the modification determined by the CGMC, as the case may be. The modification determined by the CGMC must be notified in writing to the casino operator and shall be valid from the date determined in the notification.

#### Article 44 .-

The development agreement of the integrated resort and the casino development agreement must determine the important measures under which each casino proposes to operate and open to the public, as specified in point (c) of the first paragraph of Article 29 and point (c) of the fourth paragraph of Article 30 of this Law.

The integrated resort owner and the casino owner must notify the CGMC in writing of the achievements made at each stage, as specified in point (c) of the first paragraph of Article 29 and point (c) of the fourth paragraph of Article 30 of this Law, and must also confirm the complete preparedness for opening the casino operations to the public and request permission from the CGMC to open such operations.

Within 15 (fifteen) working days after receiving the notification, the CGMC shall arrange for an on-site inspection. Within 15 working days after the completion of the inspection, the CGMC must notify the integrated resort owner or casino owner in writing of the results of the inspection by:

a. Permitting the opening of the casino operation and determining the date of such opening; or

b. Rejecting with justification of the reasons and issues that the integrated resort owner or casino owner must resolve before obtaining permission to open the casino operation.

In case the opening of the casino operation is rejected under point (b) of the third paragraph above, the integrated resort owner or casino owner must resolve the issues specified in the notification of the CGMC. After resolving those issues, the integrated resort owner or casino owner must notify the CGMC in writing to request a second inspection. The second and third paragraphs of this Article shall be applied to the second inspection.

In case the opening of the casino operation is permitted under point (a) of the third paragraph of this Article, the integrated resort owner or casino owner may open the casino operation and commercial gambling operations in the casino in accordance with the date determined in the notification of the CGMC.

#### Article 45 .-

A licensed casino operator must operate continuously for the public 24 hours a day, every day throughout the year. However, the casino operator may temporarily suspend or close operations in the following situations:

a. In case the suspension or closure of operations does not exceed 7 days and with prior approval from the CGMC; or

b. In case of force majeure, for a period not exceeding the duration of such force majeure event.

The casino operator must temporarily suspend or close operations for religious or cultural reasons based on conditions determined by the CGMC or as necessary.

The casino operator must also temporarily suspend or close the casino operations upon the decision of the CGMC, even if the casino operator appeals against the decision of the CGMC related to the suspension or revocation of the license.

### Section 3

### Special Personnel License

#### Article 46 .-

A physical person shall not perform any function of the special personnel functions unless they hold a special personnel license in accordance with the provisions of this Law. Each holder of a special personnel license must only perform the functions specified in their own special personnel license, in accordance with the conditions of the special personnel license. The commercial gambling operator shall be responsible for employing the services of their employees in accordance with this regulation.

For casinos, special personnel refers to individuals who work in the casino and have managerial capacity with decision-making authority related to the management of the casino operations or personnel who have functions and duties as follows:

a. Operating games in the casino.

b. Circulation of cash or gaming chips/tokens within the casino premises.

c. Exchanging cash or gaming chips/tokens for gaming guests in the casino.

d. Counting cash or gaming chips/tokens within the casino premises.

e. Maintaining security and monitoring surveillance in the casino.

f. Operation, maintenance, installation, or repair of gaming tables, gaming machines, or commercial gambling equipment, etc. in the casino.

g. Managing and supervising all the above activities.

d. Other activities related to casino operations as determined by the CGMC through written notification to the casino operator.

For other commercial gambling establishments besides casinos, special staff shall be determined by a Prakas of the CGMC.

#### Article 47 .-

Employees of commercial gambling establishments classified as special staff must apply for a special staff license from the CGMC.

The application for a special staff license shall be submitted to the CGMC accompanied by:

a. Documents and information in the format and procedure determined by the CGMC; and

b. License fees and administrative service fees in the amount and manner determined by the CGMC.

Procedures, formalities, and conditions for granting, transferring, and/or renewing the validity of the license shall be determined by a Prakas of the CGMC.

### Section 4
### Commercial Gambling Promoter License

#### Article 48 .-

Only legal entities holding a commercial gambling promoter license in accordance with the provisions of this Law may carry out commercial gambling promotion activities.

Commercial gambling promoters are authorized to bring gambling patrons to play at casinos or other commercial gambling establishments by acting as an agent for one or more casino operators or commercial gambling establishments, and to coordinate arrangements, transportation, accommodation, entertainment, and other benefits for the gambling patrons in exchange for commissions or other service fees. Commercial gambling promoters are also authorized to provide credit for gambling in their own name or as an agent of the casino operator or commercial gambling establishment.

Commercial gambling promoters may only bring and provide credit to gambling patrons who are non-residents or do not have Khmer nationality.

#### Article 49 .-

Before starting operations, the commercial gambling promoter must obtain a license from the CGMC.

Only legal entities registered for business and registered for tax under the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia may apply for a commercial gambling promoter license. This license application shall be submitted to the CGMC accompanied by:

a. Documents and information in the format and procedure determined by the CGMC; and

b. License fees and administrative service fees in the amount and manner determined by the CGMC.

Procedures, formalities, and conditions for granting, transferring, and/or renewing the validity of the license shall be determined by a Prakas of the CGMC.

#### Article 50 .-

Commercial gambling promoters shall be qualified as a relevant party and, after obtaining the license in accordance with Article 49 of this Law, shall be subject to the inspection and management of the CGMC. Contracts between commercial gambling promoters and casino operators shall be made in writing and qualify as controlled contracts as provided in Section 3 of Chapter 5 of this Law.

Commercial gambling promoters shall also be subject to internal control obligations as provided in Article 58 of this Law.

### Section 5
### Licenses for Direct Gambling, Chance-Based Gambling and Other Commercial Gambling

#### Article 51 .-

Types and number of direct gambling licenses, chance-based gambling licenses, and other commercial gambling licenses shall be determined by a Prakas of the CGMC.

Procedures, formalities, obligations, and conditions for granting, transferring, and renewing the validity of the licenses as set forth in the first paragraph above shall be determined by a Prakas of the CGMC, and the conditions for granting the license shall also specify the security deposit to guarantee the business operations which direct gambling operators, chance-based gambling operators, and other commercial gambling operators must maintain in an amount and manner determined by the CGMC.

The operation, management, and control of direct gambling, chance-based gambling, and other commercial gambling shall be determined by a Prakas of the CGMC.

### Section 6
### Licenses for Manufacturers, Installers, Importers, Sellers or Distributors of Gaming Equipment and/or Software

#### Article 52 .-

Licenses for manufacturers, installers, importers, sellers, and distributors of gaming equipment and/or software shall be determined by the CGMC.

Procedures, formalities, obligations, and conditions for granting, transferring, and renewing the validity of the license as set forth in the first paragraph above shall be determined by a Prakas of the CGMC, and the conditions for granting the license shall also specify the security deposit to guarantee the business operations that the operator must maintain in an amount and manner determined by the CGMC.

The operation, management, and control over the manufacturing, installation, importation, sale, or distribution of gaming equipment and/or software shall be determined by a Prakas of the CGMC.

### Chapter 5
### Control and Management of Commercial Gambling Operations and Relevant Parties

### Section 1
### Commercial Gambling Operations

#### Article 53 .-

A commercial gambling operator has the right to acquire shares, including qualified shares, in other commercial gambling operators, whether located inside or outside the Kingdom of Cambodia.

A special staff of a commercial gambling operator may not be a qualified shareholder or special staff of another commercial gambling operator, or of an owner of an affiliated commercial gambling center, or a casino owner, or a casino manager, or of any other relevant party, either directly or indirectly.

#### Article 54 .-

Commercial gambling operators shall have the obligation to exercise internal control over the following points:

a. Anti-money laundering and combating the financing of terrorism;

b. Anti-bribery and anti-corruption;

c. Securing gambling revenue and controlling access to restricted areas;

d. Keeping accounts and records; and

e. Obligations to provide internal audit reports and compliance obligations.

The internal control of commercial gambling operators shall comply with the legal framework in force and the minimum standards of internal control determined by the CGMC.

#### Article 55 .-

Commercial gambling operators shall keep and maintain accounting books in compliance with accounting standards in force, showing the transactions and financial status of their commercial gambling operations. The accounting books shall ensure that the preparation of financial statements is done accurately and timely, and facilitates effective control, investigation, and auditing of the financial statements.

#### Article 56 .-

Without prejudice to regular internal audits, commercial gambling operators shall ensure an annual independent audit of financial statements and compliance with internal control procedures in accordance with the minimum internal control standards determined by the CGMC, and the independent auditor must be accredited by the CGMC to audit the financial statements of the commercial gambling operator.

The CGMC may impose additional obligations on auditors related to the auditing of commercial gambling operators as necessary. The cost of this service shall be borne by the commercial gambling operator.

The independent audit specified in the first paragraph above shall be conducted in accordance with the Law on Accounting and Auditing as well as relevant regulations in force, and the commercial gambling operator shall submit the audit report to the CGMC.

#### Article 57 .-

Without prejudice to the obligation to submit annual financial reports and declaration of commercial gambling tax revenue, commercial gambling operators shall have the obligation to submit to the CGMC quarterly reports and other information related to their business operations, in accordance with the timeframe and format determined by the CGMC. In addition to the quarterly reports and information mentioned above, the CGMC may require the commercial gambling operator to provide documents, records, reports, and other information in accordance with its written instructions as necessary.

#### Article 58 .-

Casino operators shall also have internal control obligations to:

a. Ensure that casino operations run normally and without interruption;

b. Ensure that there is an electronic security system and surveillance system inside the casino and in the area outside the casino entrance, and there shall be a professional private security team capable of protecting the public and property at all times of casino operation;

c. Ensure that persons or establishments involved in casino operations who receive income directly or indirectly from the casino or who have supplied goods or services for casino operations are appropriate relevant parties, and the contracts of the casino operator with the relevant parties are appropriate controlled contracts; and

d. Ensure that the special staff of the casino operator and the casino employees are not, at the same time, civil servants or CGMC officers.

Casino operators shall immediately inform the CGMC by all means of the following cases:

a. Incidents or circumstances that affect the casino operations or the management of the casino operator;

b. Incidents or circumstances that affect the credibility and integrity of the qualified shareholders, special staff, or agents, and the adequacy of the financial status of the casino operator and other major incidents, including any incidents that may cause the casino operator to become bankrupt;

c. Any dispute with the casino operator or special staff, and disputes between the qualified shareholders of the casino operator; and

d. Misconduct or behavior against the casino operator by public servants, members of the security forces, or CGMC officers.

The first and second paragraphs above shall also apply to direct gambling operators, chance-based gambling operators, and other commercial gambling operators.

#### Article 59 .-

The CGMC may issue written instructions to casino operators, direct gambling operators, chance-based gambling operators, and other commercial gambling operators regarding the operation, control, or management of casino operations or other gambling operations, provided that such instructions shall not contradict the provisions of this Law and the casino license, direct gambling license, chance-based gambling license, or other commercial gambling licenses. The above instructions also include instructions on the implementation, modification, termination, or suspension of any activity related to casino operations and/or other commercial gambling operations. Casino operators, direct gambling operators, chance-based gambling operators, and other commercial gambling operators must comply with the instructions of the CGMC upon receipt of those instructions or on the date specified in those instructions.

### Section 2
### Gambling Patrons

#### Article 60 .-

Gambling patrons, as customers of casinos or other commercial gambling establishments, shall have the following rights:

a. Receive full winnings when winning a game in an appropriate and fair manner;

b. Receive winnings in cash or by any means permitted by laws or regulations in force of the Kingdom of Cambodia;

c. File a complaint to the CGMC regarding failure to receive full winnings or fraudulent activities of casino operators, direct gambling operators, chance-based gambling operators, and other commercial gambling operators;

d. Have other legal rights as set forth in the rules of play determined by the CGMC.

Gambling patrons must strictly comply with various regulations related to gambling patrons determined by the CGMC.

Gambling patrons shall not:

a. Play directly against each other between gambling patrons in casinos or other commercial gambling establishments;

b. Perform any activity that affects social order and security in and around the premises of commercial gambling establishments.

### Section 3
### Relevant Parties and Controlled Contracts

Any person who is not a commercial gambling operator and receives income directly or indirectly from commercial gambling, or who supplies goods or services for commercial gambling operations, shall be considered

as a related party. This related party shall be subject to inspection and administrative sanctions or transitional fines by the CGMC in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

The CGMC may issue additional guidelines as necessary regarding the general definition of related parties as defined in the first paragraph above, for the purpose of classifying or categorizing natural or legal persons who qualify as related parties for the purposes of this Law.

The CGMC may grant exemptions from any obligations or provisions of this section related to the classification or category of related parties through written notice to the commercial gambling operator.

### Article 62.-

Contracts or amended contracts of a casino operator with related parties shall be considered controlled contracts, and these contracts or amended contracts shall be subject to the inspection of the CGMC in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

The CGMC shall issue additional guidelines as necessary regarding the general definition of controlled contracts as defined in the first paragraph above, for the purpose of defining the classification or type of contracts that qualify as controlled contracts for the purposes of this Law. The CGMC shall also determine the minimum threshold of transaction volume for the consideration of contracts, and contracts that exceed this minimum threshold shall be considered controlled contracts.

### Section 4
#### Inspection of Commercial Gambling Operations and Related Parties

### Article 63.-

The CGMC may conduct investigations on a case-by-case basis into the suitability of commercial gambling operators or related parties regarding commercial gambling operations, the receipt of direct or indirect revenue from commercial gambling, or the supply of goods or services for commercial gambling operations.

Commercial gambling operators or related parties shall be deemed suitable if the person has:

A. Good reputation, character, honesty, and integrity.

B. Strong financial standing and appropriate stability; and

C. Sufficient business capability and experience to run the business successfully to a level that ensures that the commercial gambling operations of the casino operator, direct gambling operator, lottery operator, other commercial gambling operators, or related parties will not cause any serious negative impact on the reputation of the Kingdom of Cambodia as a destination for attracting national tourists, or on the reliability or integrity of the commercial gambling sector.

In assessing the suitability of a commercial gambling operator or a related party, the CGMC shall also consider the results of the suitability assessment of that commercial gambling operator or related party conducted by a foreign authority with expertise and a high reputation in this field.

In conducting suitability investigations as provided in paragraph 1 above, and subject to consideration of a number of essential factors to determine to its satisfaction the suitability of the commercial gambling operator or related party, the CGMC may:

A. Compel the conduct of investigation and examination under oath.

B. Require the commercial gambling operator or related party to provide or prepare, or require them to urge other related legal or natural persons to provide or prepare, various necessary information and documents.

C. Require the commercial gambling operator or related party to urge other related legal or natural persons to grant consent for CGMC officials to conduct investigations or receive other necessary information and documents; and

D. Use the services of external advisors and experts, the costs of which shall be borne by the commercial gambling operator or related party.

If it is found that the commercial gambling operator or related party may not be suitable, and that the current or future activities of the commercial gambling operator or related party may cause serious negative impact on the reputation of Cambodia as a destination for attracting international tourists, or on the reliability or integrity of the commercial gambling sector, the CGMC may issue a written notice specifying that:

A. For casino operators, direct gambling operators, lottery operators, and other commercial gambling operators, the casino license, direct gambling license, lottery license, or other commercial gambling licenses shall be suspended in accordance with the provisions of this Law; or

B. For related parties, activities related to commercial gambling shall be suspended until there is a new determination by the CGMC that the related party is suitable.

In the event of a suspension of the activities of a related party as provided in point (B) of paragraph 5 above, the CGMC shall issue a written notice to each gambling operator, requiring them to terminate business relations with the suspended related party until a new written notice is issued regarding the cancellation of the suspension.

### Article 64.-

After the CGMC determines the unsuitability of a commercial gambling operator, the CGMC may issue a written notice by:

A. Requiring the commercial gambling operator to take measures to improve its unsuitability within a specific reasonable timeframe as determined in the notice; or

B. Revoking the casino license, direct gambling license, lottery license, or other commercial gambling licenses in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

After determining the unsuitability of a related party, the CGMC may issue a written notice that may require the related party, within a specific reasonable timeframe, to:

A. Take necessary improvement measures to ensure that the related party is suitable; or

B. Terminate all controlled contracts to which that related party is a party or a beneficiary; and

C. Terminate participation in commercial gambling operations, the receipt of direct or indirect revenue from commercial gambling, or the supply of goods or services to commercial gambling operators.

Even if the controlled contract to which the related party is a party or a beneficiary is not terminated as specified in the notice of paragraph 2 above, such contract shall be deemed terminated at the time specified in the notice. In the event that a controlled contract is terminated in accordance with this Article, paragraph 3 of Article 66 of this Law shall apply.

### Article 65.-

A commercial gambling operator who is a party to a controlled contract or an amended contract shall notify the CGMC in writing within 15 working days after signing the controlled contract or amended contract, enclosing a full copy of the controlled contract or amended contract, and shall specify key points of the contract in the format and procedure determined by the CGMC.

Upon receiving the notice in paragraph 1 above and based on consideration of a number of essential factors to determine to its satisfaction the suitability of the controlled contract or amended contract, the CGMC may:

A. Compel the conduct of investigation, research, or inquiry.

B. Require the commercial gambling operator or related party to provide or prepare, or require them to urge other related legal or natural persons to provide or prepare, various necessary information and documents.

C. Require the commercial gambling operator or related party to urge other related legal or natural persons to grant consent for CGMC officials to conduct investigations or inquiries, or receive other necessary information and documents.

D. Use the services of external advisors and experts, the costs of which shall be borne by the casino operator, direct gambling operator, lottery operator, other commercial gambling operators, or the related party.

Controlled contracts shall be deemed unsuitable if the terms and content of the contract, or other various conditions, including the suitability of each related party to the contract, may cause serious negative impact on the reliability, integrity, robustness, and suitability of the financial status of the casino operator, direct gambling operator, lottery operator, or other commercial gambling operators, or on the stability of those commercial gambling operations.

If it is found that a controlled contract may cause any serious negative impact on the reliability, integrity, robustness, and suitability of the financial status of a commercial gambling operator, or on the stability of commercial gambling operations, which could lead to irreparable damage to the reputation of Cambodia as a destination for attracting international tourists, the CGMC may issue a written notice to each commercial gambling operator or related party requiring the suspension of the execution of rights and obligations under the controlled contract until a new determination is made as to whether the contract is suitable or unsuitable, and no party to the contract may give effect to any provision of the contract during the suspension period.

### Article 66.-

After determining the unsuitability of a controlled contract, the CGMC may issue a written notice to the casino operator, direct gambling operator, lottery operator, other commercial gambling operators, and related parties by:

A. Requiring the improvement of the controlled contract within a reasonable timeframe as specified in the notice to ensure that the contract is suitable to the satisfaction of the CGMC; or

B. Requiring the termination of the contract within a reasonable timeframe as specified in the notice.

If the contract is not amended or terminated as required in the notice in accordance with the paragraph above, such contract shall still be deemed terminated during the timeframe specified in the notice.

If the contract is amended or terminated in accordance with paragraphs 1 and 2 of this Article:

A. The parties to the contract shall have no rights or obligations under the duties of the controlled contract or any part of the amended contract that has been rejected due to such amendment.

B. The amendment or termination of the contract shall not affect the acquired rights or responsibilities of any party to the contract or any party receiving rights through the contract that arose before the amendment or termination took effect.

C. The parties to the contract shall not be liable for breach of the contract arising solely from such amendment or termination; and

D. The CGMC shall not bear any liability arising from such amendment or termination of the contract.

### Chapter 6
#### Powers of Judicial Police Officers

### Article 67.-

Judicial police officers of the General Commissioner of National Police of the Ministry of Interior shall have the authority to inspect and suppress offenses as provided in this Law.

### Chapter 7
#### Limitations on Gambling

### Section 1
#### Restrictions on Gambling

### Article 68.-

Entry into gambling in casinos or commercial gambling establishments shall be strictly prohibited for the following persons:

A. Persons deemed to have mental disorders, financial incapacity, or declared bankrupt by the court.

B. Persons under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

C. Persons carrying weapons of all kinds.

D. Persons under 18 years of age; and

E. General Cambodian citizens, including members of the armed forces, public servants, and elected officials.

Entry into gambling in casinos or commercial gambling establishments shall be prohibited for employees of the casino operator or commercial gambling operator of the commercial gambling establishment where they are currently working.

The restrictions on entering gambling in casinos or commercial gambling establishments as specified in paragraph 1 above shall not apply to lottery games.

### Article 69.-

Commercial gambling operators, other than lottery operators, shall take appropriate measures to ensure that persons restricted from gambling as provided in Article 68 of this Law do not enter to gamble in their casinos or commercial gambling establishments in violation of the restriction conditions.

Commercial gambling operators, other than lottery operators, shall have necessary mechanisms to effectively manage entry into gambling, including:

A. Maintaining updated reports on the restrictions of guests entering gambling in their casinos or commercial gambling establishments.

B. Maintaining a sufficient number of competent security guards to effectively inspect entry into gambling.

C. Arranging adequate monitoring mechanisms to effectively inspect entry into gambling; and

D. Demarcating gambling areas clearly and posting appropriate markers/signs to indicate those gambling locations.

Commercial gambling operators, other than lottery/lucky draw operators, shall:

a. Use necessary security measures to prevent persons restricted from gambling from entering their casinos or other commercial gambling establishments and expel those persons from the gambling premises or make them leave the gambling premises by other means.

b. Notify the CGMC immediately after determining that a person restricted from gambling has gambled or intended to enter and gamble in their casinos or other commercial gambling establishments. and

c. Close all accounts at casinos or commercial gambling establishments of persons restricted from gambling and remove those persons' names from the guest member list or from other reward programs.

#### Article 70 .-

Any person who violates the provisions on gambling restrictions shall be subject to confiscation as state property of winnings from gambling that have been paid, are being paid, or will be paid during that restricted period.

### Section 2
### Prohibition of Activities

#### Article 71 .-

Casino operators, live gambling operators, lottery/lucky draw gambling operators, or other commercial gambling operators shall determine the conditions for allowing gambling guests to be present in or enter and play gambling in their casinos or other commercial gambling establishments.

Upon request, commercial gambling operators shall provide clear information to gambling guests who intend to gamble in casinos or in other commercial gambling establishments about the conditions and rules of gambling at that place.

Commercial gambling operators have the right to refuse entry to any person into their casinos or other commercial gambling establishments if it is deemed that the person does not meet the conditions of that casino or commercial gambling establishment. This prohibition shall not apply to CGMC officers in the performance of their duties.

#### Article 72 .-

Casino operators, live gambling operators, lottery/lucky draw gambling operators, and/or other commercial gambling operators shall ensure that no person commits any of the following activities in their casinos or other commercial gambling establishments:

a. Any activity that causes instability and insecurity in the gambling operation.

b. Gambling by coercion or fraud.

c. Physical gestures/services that are disorderly or theft, etc. and

d. Violation of technical conditions and regulations of the casino or other commercial gambling establishments.

Without prejudice to paragraph 4 of this Article, commercial gambling operators shall have the obligation to use their security forces as necessary to expel persons involved in activities as described in paragraph 1 above from their casinos or other commercial gambling establishments and prohibit such persons from entering the casinos or other commercial gambling establishments for a period of 24 hours, unless otherwise instructed by the CGMC.

Immediately after expelling any person from a casino or other commercial gambling establishment in accordance with paragraph 2 above, the commercial gambling operator shall notify the CGMC, providing all information related to the expelled person and specifying the details of the facts relied upon to expel that person from the casino or other commercial gambling establishment. The CGMC may issue a prohibition letter in accordance with the provisions of Article 68 of this Law.

If there is a suspicion that any person is involved in activities as specified in points (a) and (b) of paragraph 1 above, casino operators, live gambling operators, lottery/lucky draw gambling operators, or other commercial gambling operators shall notify the CGMC immediately. In case of necessity, those commercial gambling operators may use their security forces to take appropriate measures to ensure security and order at the premises and shall notify the CGMC immediately to proceed with further legal procedures.

Upon receiving a request for intervention as provided in paragraphs 3 and 4 above, judicial police officers of the General Commissioner of National Police of the Ministry of Interior shall take appropriate measures and may request cooperation from relevant competent officials or cooperate with the prosecutor as necessary.

### Section 3
### Limitation of Advertising

#### Article 73 .-

All advertisements of commercial gambling shall require permission and approval from the CGMC.

### Section 4
### Management of Gambling Rules, Equipment, and/or Software

#### Article 74 .-

The CGMC may provide different approvals for types of gambling and gambling rules, depending on the request of the commercial gambling operator, differences in time, place, and circumstance.

Commercial gambling operators shall not operate any gambling in commercial gambling establishments unless:

a. There is prior approval on the type of gambling from the CGMC, which remains valid.

b. That gambling is operated in accordance with the way of play and gambling rules approved by the CGMC. and

c. That gambling is operated by special staff holding a license.

#### Article 75 .-

Before granting approval on equipment and/or gambling software, CGMC officers may conduct investigations to verify suitability for use in casinos or other commercial gambling establishments, and all costs of the investigation shall be borne by the applicant. The CGMC may grant approval to a specific equipment and/or gambling software or to a group of gambling equipment and may grant such approval with conditions as necessary.

Regulations made under this Law may set standards and/or gambling software for the manufacture, installation, import, distribution, or supply of equipment and/or gambling software for use in casinos or other commercial gambling establishments.

Without prejudice to the provisions of other laws, the possession of equipment or/and gambling shall be deemed unlawful, unless the gambling equipment and/or software is certified as approved by the CGMC for a specific commercial gambling establishment.

#### Article 76 .-

Casino operators, live gambling operators, lottery/lucky draw gambling operators, and other commercial gambling operators shall ensure that:

a. The layout of the internal premises of the casino or other commercial gambling establishments, including the location of gambling machines for play, etc., complies with the determination of the CGMC, which may be made as necessary.

b. Major modifications to the layout of the internal premises of the casino or other commercial gambling establishments shall be notified in advance to the CGMC.

#### Article 77 .-

Gambling chips of each commercial gambling establishment shall be identified by the specific symbol of that commercial gambling establishment for identification purposes and shall meet the technical conditions determined by the CGMC. A commercial gambling establishment shall not use gambling chips of another commercial gambling establishment.

#### Article 78 .-

The types and number of equipment and/or gambling software to be manufactured, installed, imported, distributed, including re-exported, and destroyed shall be permitted by the CGMC and shall comply with the provisions of this Law. The CGMC may issue legal instruments related to the registration of certain types of gambling equipment such as gambling tables and other commercial gambling machines. Equipment or/and gambling software permitted for use in casinos or other commercial gambling establishments shall have technical specifications in compliance with legal instruments determined by the CGMC.

#### Article 79 .-

Commercial gambling operators may operate test gambling for training and instructing staff or testing gambling machines or equipment and/or gambling software, only with prior agreement from the CGMC.

#### Article 80 .-

The CGMC has the right to inspect equipment and audio and video recordings of safety equipment installed in the casino or commercial gambling establishment.

The installation of automated teller machines (ATMs) within the gambling area of the casino or other commercial gambling establishments is prohibited.

## Chapter 8
## Obligatory Income from Gambling, License Fees, and Other Obligations

### Section 1
### General Principles

#### Article 81 .-

Income from gambling shall be subject to obligatory income from gambling in accordance with this Article and Article 82 of this Law. Casino operators, live gambling operators, lottery/lucky draw gambling operators, and other commercial gambling operators shall pay obligatory income from gambling, fees, and expenses as follows:

a. Obligatory income from gambling as provided in this Law or in relevant regulations.

b. License fees as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 35 of this Law.

c. Fees for the transfer of license ownership.

d. Equipment software gambling fees, and/or

e. Various procedural expenses depending on the actual cases of commercial gambling.

The obligation to pay fees and/or expenses as provided in points (b), (c), (d), and (e) of paragraph 1 above shall also apply to manufacturers, installers, importers, sellers, distributors of equipment, gambling software, buyers of commercial gambling, and/or other relevant parties. The conditions and deadlines for fulfilling the obligatory income from gambling as provided in point (a) of paragraph 1 above shall be determined by the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance. For the conditions and deadlines for paying fees and/or expenses as provided in points (b), (c), (d), and (e) of paragraph 1 above shall be determined by the CGMC.

Commercial gambling operators and relevant persons shall have the obligation to provide the CGMC with quarterly and annual declarations of obligatory income from gambling in the form, format, and time determined by the CGMC.

In addition to the obligatory income from gambling as provided in paragraph 1 of this Article, real estate owners or location owners, casino entrepreneurs, casino owners, casino operators, live gambling operators, operators

lottery/lucky draw gambling, other commercial gambling operators and/or relevant parties shall comply with fiscal obligations in accordance with tax laws and regulations, unless there is a special determination in this Law.

### Section 2
### Obligatory Income from Gambling

#### Article 82 .-

Casino operators and/or casino owners and/or integrated resort commercial gambling center owners shall fulfill the quarterly obligatory income from gambling during the period of holding the license. The rate of obligatory income from gambling is determined as follows:

a. Casinos located in an integrated resort commercial gambling center shall be 4 (four) percent for special gambling guests and 7 (seven) percent for regular gambling guests.

b. Casinos not located in an integrated resort commercial gambling center shall be 7 (seven) percent.

The basis for calculating the obligatory income from gambling is the gross gaming revenue. The gross gaming revenue is calculated as follows:

a. Gross revenue received or to be received from all types of gambling operated in the casino or other commercial gambling establishments.

b. Deducting all winnings of the gambling guests.

#### Article 83 .-

Live gambling operators, lottery/lucky draw gambling operators, and other commercial gambling operators shall fulfill the quarterly obligatory income from gambling during the period of holding the license. The base rate of calculation and other conditions of the obligatory income from gambling in this Article shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 84 .-

The fulfillment of obligatory income from gambling shall be done by self-declaration of the operator on a monthly basis, at the latest by the 20th of the following month, and shall be subject to audit.

## Chapter 9
## Reconciliation Measures and Dispute Resolution

#### Article 85 .-

For disputes related to commercial gambling activities, any party to the dispute may bring the dispute to the CGMC for reconciliation, except for criminal offenses.

#### Article 86 .-

Upon receiving the complaint, the CGMC shall question both parties regarding the subject of the dispute and then initiate mediation to resolve the dispute of each party, based on laws, rules, and other regulations. The CGMC shall organize the reconciliation of the disputing parties on a voluntary basis with the assistance of CGMC officers. This reconciliation minutes shall be made in writing and the original copy shall be kept at the CGMC.

The conciliation conducted before the CGMC shall have the force of an enforceable title. In case the conciliation or mediation is not successful between the disputing parties, the CGMC official shall draw up a record of non-conciliation/non-settlement for the disputing parties, and any disputing party who is dissatisfied has the right to file an appeal to arbitration or to a competent court.

The procedures for dispute resolution shall be determined by a Proklam of the CGMC.

### Chapter 10
### Administrative Sanctions and Penal Provisions
#### Section 1
#### Administrative Sanctions

#### Article 87 ._

Administrative sanctions under this Law include written warning, suspension of license, and revocation of license.

The imposition of administrative sanctions shall be the competence of the CGMC.

The modalities and procedures for imposing administrative sanctions shall be determined by a Proklam of the CGMC.

Other than the casino license, the written warning, suspension of license, and revocation of license shall be determined by a Proklam of the CGMC.

#### Article 88 ._

A written warning shall be issued to casino operations that commit any of the following acts:

a. Acting contrary to the instructions of the CGMC, as provided in Article 89 of this Law.

b. Advertising commercial gambling without the approval of the CGMC, as provided in Article 73 of this Law.

c. Operating trial games for training and instructing staff, or testing gaming machines or equipment and/or gaming systems without prior consent from the CGMC, as provided in Article 79 of this Law.

#### Article 89 ._

The casino license shall be suspended in the following cases:

a. Special staff commit an offense against the second paragraph of Article 43 of this Law.

b. Casino operations commit an offense against Article 80 of this Law.

c. Commercial gambling operations commit an offense against Article 54, Article 55, Article 56, Article 57, Article 58, or the second paragraph of the first paragraph of Article 60, Article 66, or Article 77 of this Law.

d. Casino operations commit an offense as provided in the fourth paragraph of Article 63 of this Law.

e. Casino operations do not comply with the written warning of the CGMC.

#### Article 90 ._

The casino license shall be revoked for committing any of the following acts:

a. Transferring the commercial gambling license or commercial gambling operations in whole or in part by any method or form without the permission of the CGMC.

b. Failing to pay tax obligations, compulsory gambling revenue, license fees, or other financial obligations in the amount and within the time limit as provided in Article 82, Article 83, and Article 84 of this Law.

c. Committing an offense as provided in the first paragraph of Article 65 of this Law.

d. Continuing to commit or failing to remedy serious offenses and license conditions.

e. Continuing to commit the offenses provided in Article 89 of this Law.

f. Continuing to operate the casino during the suspension of the license.

#### Article 91 ._

Any person directly affected by the decision on administrative sanctions of the CGMC as provided in this Chapter has the right to file a complaint to the court within a maximum of 30 (thirty) days from the date of receiving this decision.

### Section 2
### Transactional Penalty

#### Article 92 ._

Offenses subject to transactional fines shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

The imposition of transactional fines shall be the competence of the CGMC.

The payment of the transactional fine shall extinguish the public prosecution.

In case the offender refuses to pay the transactional fine, the case file of the offense shall be forwarded to the court for processing according to the procedures.

The conditions, modalities, and procedures for transactional fines shall be determined by a Proklam of the CGMC.

The Royal Government may decide to award incentives to officials participating in the suppression of offenses in the sector of management of integrated resorts and commercial gambling.

The rate of the reward and the procedures for managing and using the reward shall be determined by a Proklam of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

### Section 3
### Penal Provisions

#### Article 93 ._

Shall be punished under the Penal Code in force of the Kingdom of Cambodia for any person who commits any of the following activities in the sector of management of integrated resorts and commercial gambling:

a. Organizing, directing, operating, or participating in commercial gambling operations without a license.

b. Manufacturing, assembling, importing, selling, distributing, or re-exporting gaming equipment and/or software without the permission of the CGMC.

c. Falsifying or using falsified documents related to the sector of management of integrated resorts and commercial gambling.

d. Falsifying public documents or using falsified public documents issued by public administration in accordance with this Law.

e. Fraud.

f. Breach of trust.

### Chapter 11
### Transitional Provisions

#### Article 94 ._

Commercial gambling operations that obtained a commercial gambling license before the entry into force of this Law shall fulfill all conditions in accordance with the provisions of this Law, including conditions to be determined by a Sub-decree and legal instruments of the CGMC.

Existing agreements between the Royal Government of Cambodia and commercial gambling operators, which were made before the entry into force of this Law, shall continue to be fully implemented until those agreements expire, by guaranteeing the following principles:

1. All rights of investors granted by the Royal Government under those agreements shall be fully respected by the CGMC and relevant stakeholders.

2. The management of investment projects shall be implemented in accordance with the terms and substances of those agreements. And

3. The implementation of this Law, under any circumstances, shall not adversely affect the various rights obtained by investors.

#### Article 95 ._

Compulsory gambling revenue for casino operations, betting operations, games of chance operations, and other commercial gambling operations shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of this Law from the fiscal year 2021.

#### Article 96 ._

The existing structure, governance, and management shall continue to operate to support the fulfillment of functions of the Secretariat of the CGMC in accordance with the provisions of this Law and shall be permanently terminated when the Secretariat of the CGMC confirms that it can operate on its own, not exceeding 6 (six) months from the date of the entry into force of this Law.

### Chapter 12
### Final Provisions

#### Article 97 ._

Any provisions contrary to this Law shall be abrogated.

### Glossary

The legal terms used in this Law shall have the following meanings:

Casino Owner refers to a legal entity that owns one or more casinos for casino operations.

Integrated Resort Owner refers to a legal entity that owns one or more integrated resorts to operate and develop commercial gambling.

Force Majeure refers to an event or circumstance that is uncontrollable, and which is not the fault or negligence of the affected party, and is an event or circumstance that the party cannot prevent despite reasonable efforts, including:

a. Riot, war, invasion, acts of foreign enemies, hostilities, civil war, rebellion, coup d'état, military mutiny or usurpation of power, and/or confiscation or occupation of property by competent authorities.

b. Epidemic/pandemic and the effects of radiation from nuclear weapons.

c. Pressure waves caused by aircraft or devices traveling at sonic or supersonic speeds.

d. Earthquake, flood, fire, or other natural disasters, but excluding weather conditions, regardless of their severity.

e. National strike or industrial dispute, or strike or industrial dispute caused by employees who are not employees of the affected party, or of its subcontractors or suppliers, which activities affect essential parts of the work, but excluding industrial disputes directly related to the performance of work and the contract.

Chips refers to tokens or plaques used in place of real currency or banknotes for gambling purposes.

Commercial Gambling Advertisement refers to activities that can attract the interest of the public or any group of people to encourage them to gamble in casinos or other commercial gambling establishments frequently.

Gambling refers to playing casino games, live games, games of chance, or other commercial gambling games with the intention of winning money or things of monetary value, based purely on chance or chance combined with skill.

Casino refers to the location of some commercial gambling operations, including poker, blackjack, baccarat, roulette, slot machines and/or other commercial gambling games, with direct interaction between the game operator and the player in accordance with the determination of the CGMC.

Casino Operation Contract refers to a commercial agreement concluded between the casino operator and the integrated resort owner or casino owner, whereby, upon receiving approval from the CGMC, the casino operator shall operate the casino within the premises or any part of the casino.

Integrated commercial gambling resort or one or more casinos of the owner of the integrated commercial gambling resort or the owner of that casino shall comply with the substance and conditions agreed upon by the parties and approved by the Commercial Gambling Management Commission of Cambodia.

Casino Development Agreement refers to the agreement established between a private legal entity and the Commercial Gambling Management Commission of Cambodia under that agreement. The private legal entity has the obligation to develop the casino based on the substance and conditions agreed with the Commercial Gambling Management Commission of Cambodia.

Integrated Commercial Gambling Center Development Agreement refers to the agreement established between a private legal entity and the Commercial Gambling Management Commission of Cambodia with the approval of the Royal Government under that Integrated Commercial Gambling Center Development Agreement. The private legal entity has the obligation to develop the integrated commercial gambling center based on the substance and conditions agreed with the Royal Government.

Casino Management Contract refers to a commercial gambling contract established between a casino operator and a casino manager, in which the casino operator transfers some or all of their obligations under the casino operation agreement to that casino manager.

Control Contract refers to the contract of a commercial gambling operator with related parties regarding the supply of goods or services, in part or in whole, for commercial gambling operations or other issues determined to be subject to control for the purposes of this Law.

Commercial Gambling Management Commission of Cambodia (CGMC) refers to the regulator and supervisor managing and controlling, as well as collecting revenues from integrated commercial gambling centers and commercial gambling established under Part 1 and Part 2, Chapter 2 of this Law.

Qualified Investment Project refers to an investment project that has received a Final Registration Certificate as provided in the Law on the Amendment to the Law on Investment of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

Commercial Gambling Establishment refers to the place or location of commercial gambling operations, including casino games, betting games, games of chance, or other commercial gambling.

Total revenue from gambling refers to the total of:

a. Gross revenue received or to be received from all types of games operated in casinos or other commercial gambling establishments.

b. Subtracting all winnings of the gambling patrons.

Gaming Table refers to a table used for playing games in casinos, including baccarat, blackjack, roulette, lotto, and other commercial gaming tables permitted by the Commercial Gambling Management Commission.

Key Employee refers to an employee working in a commercial gambling establishment who has managerial authority and decision-making power regarding commercial gambling operations, or who has functions and duties as provided in Part 3, Chapter 4 of this Law.

Excluded Person refers to a person who is not permitted to enter or be present in a casino or other commercial gambling establishment, as determined in Part 1, Chapter 7 of this Law.

Casino Operator refers to a legal entity holding a casino license.

Commercial Gambling Operator refers to a legal entity that has received a license for commercial gambling operations.

Casino Operation refers to operations related to gaming activities in a casino as follows:

a. Operating games in a casino.

b. Managing and supervising the operation of games in a casino.

c. Money counting in a casino and in operations related to the casino.

d. Accounting procedures in a casino and in operations related to the casino.

e. Use of vault within the casino premises. and

f. Other operations having an impact arising from activities in the casino.

Related Party refers to a physical person or legal entity who is not a commercial gambling operator and intends to be involved in commercial gambling operations, receiving direct or indirect income from commercial gambling, or who supplies goods or services for commercial gambling operations.

Patron refers to a person who has the right to enter and play games in a casino or other commercial gambling establishment, as permitted under this Law. Patrons include regular patrons and special patrons, which shall be determined by a Prakas of the CGMC.

Qualifying Share refers to direct or indirect holding of shares in an establishment with a size of at least 10 (ten) percent of the total shares of that establishment.

Qualifying Shareholder refers to a holder of qualifying shares.

Gaming Machine refers to any device, whether fully or partially mechanical or electronic, manufactured to be used for playing games of pure chance or games of chance combined with skill and other machines as determined by the CGMC.

Integrated Commercial Gambling Resort refers to the development site approved by the Royal Government, which includes, among others, hotel grounds, restaurants, shopping centers, hotels, sports centers, conference centers, exhibition centers, cultural arts, casinos and/or recreation centers, theme parks, duty-free shops, and where one or more casino operations can be conducted within that integrated commercial gambling resort.

Casino Game refers to a commercial game permitted to operate in a casino, including jackpot, blackjack, baccarat, roulette, slot machine and/or other games using cards, dice

dice, mechanical devices, electromechanical or electronic devices and/or other gaming devices and/or software as determined by the CGMC, excluding betting, games of chance, and other commercial games.

Game of Chance refers to a commercial game operated outside a casino based on pure chance or on a combination of chance and a portion of the patron's skill, such as lottery, lotto, and/or other games of chance determined by the CGMC.

Commercial Gambling refers to casino games, betting games, games of chance, and/or other commercial games operated for the purpose of making a profit or for commercial purposes. Commercial gaming that does not involve the requirement to pay money or items of monetary value in order to have the possibility or chance of winning money or items of monetary value shall not be considered "commercial gambling" for the purposes of this Law.

Betting Game refers to a commercial game based on predicting the outcome of a race, competition, or any unplanned event.

Any Legal Right over a Location refers to a private lease right, permanent lease right, or any rights that allow the holder of the right to develop and own an integrated commercial gambling resort or casino on that specific location during a specific period of time and under conditions determined by the CGMC.

Casino Manager refers to a physical person or legal entity who assumes, in part or in whole, the obligations under a casino operation agreement from a casino operator or a person designated by the CGMC to manage one or more casinos owned by the casino operator, in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

Commercial Gambling Promoter refers to a legal entity whose primary business is to act as an agent of one or more commercial gambling operators to attract patrons to play games in the commercial gambling establishment of that operator, receiving a commission or any benefit.

Casino License refers to a license granted by the CGMC related to "casino games" operated in casinos under Part 2, Chapter 4 of this Law.

Key Employee License refers to a license granted by the CGMC under Part 3, Chapter 4 of this Law.

Gaming Equipment and/or Software refers to equipment and/or software or materials, including gaming cards, used for operating games or activities related to gaming, including gaming tables, gaming machines, electronic systems, safety and surveillance systems, and parts or accessories for equipment and/or software, casino chips, and other gaming equipment and/or software or materials as determined by the CGMC.
