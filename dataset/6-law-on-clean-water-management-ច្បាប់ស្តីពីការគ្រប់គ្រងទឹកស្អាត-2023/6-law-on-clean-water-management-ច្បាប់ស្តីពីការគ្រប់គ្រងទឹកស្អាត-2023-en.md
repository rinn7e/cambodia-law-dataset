The Law on Clean Water Management, which was adopted by the National Assembly on February 8, 2023 at its 6th legislature's extraordinary session, and entirely reviewed and approved on its legal form and substance by the Senate on February 20, 2023 at its 4th legislature's extraordinary session, and which has the full text as follows:

# LAW

# ON

# CLEAN WATER MANAGEMENT

# Chapter 1
# General Provisions

## Article 1.- Purpose

This Law defines the legal framework for the management and development of clean water supply service in the Kingdom of Cambodia, aiming to ensure quality, safety, sustainability, and affordable price, as well as to contribute to promoting public welfare and the livelihood of citizens in an inclusive manner.

## Article 2.- Scope

This Law applies to commercial clean water supply services throughout the Kingdom of Cambodia.

## Article 3.- Definitions

Key terms used in this Law are defined in the Glossary, which is an Annex to this Law.

# Chapter 2
# Management Authority

## Article 4.- Management Authority

The provision of clean water supply service is under the management authority and direct responsibility of the State. The State may grant rights to the private sector to provide clean water supply services to the public and to production and service sectors in accordance with laws and legal instruments in force.

The management of clean water supply service shall be under the authority of the Ministry in charge of clean water sector.

The Minister of the Ministry in charge of clean water sector may delegate the management of clean water supply service to sub-national administrations in accordance with regulations and procedures in force.

Any person conducting clean water supply business must obtain authorization granted by the Ministry in charge of clean water sector and must comply with regulations and procedures in force.

## Article 5.- Policy and Plan

The Ministry in charge of clean water sector shall formulate policies and development plans for clean water sector based on studies and research using technical, economic, social, and environmental information in order to determine strategic plans, master plans, and investment plans for leading and managing the clean water sector and managing commercial clean water supply businesses in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

The Ministry in charge of clean water sector shall mobilize to the fullest extent resources, such as human, financial, and technical resources, to develop the clean water sector in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

# Chapter 3
# Principles

## Article 6.- Principles

The provision of clean water supply service shall be based on the following principles:

- Ensuring transparency and equity in clean water supply service provision;

- Ensuring equality and non-discrimination between service providers and clean water supply consumers;

- Encouraging national and international investors to participate in clean water sector investments;

- Providing clean water supply service based on 4 conditions: clean water must have quality, safety, sustainability, and affordable price.

# Chapter 4
# Clean Water Development Fund

## Article 7.- Clean Water Development Fund

The Clean Water Development Fund is established to ensure the maintenance, improvement, and development of clean water supply systems with efficiency and sustainability, especially in developing and remote areas.

The Clean Water Development Fund shall come from state budget, assistance, donations, contributions from individuals, development partners, and other legal sources.

The organization and functioning of the Clean Water Development Fund shall be determined by Sub-decree.

# Chapter 5
# Management of Clean Water Supply Business
## Section 1
## Licensing of Clean Water Supply Business

## Article 8.- General Conditions of Licenses

Any natural person or legal entity wishing to provide clean water supply services in a specific defined area within the Kingdom of Cambodia must apply for a license to operate a clean water supply business from the Ministry in charge of clean water sector in accordance with the provisions of this Law and regulations in force.

## Article 9.- Types of Licenses

The licenses referred to in Article 8 above are of two types: Clean Water Supply Business License and Wholesale Clean Water Supply License. The conditions and procedures for granting a clean water supply business license shall be determined by Sub-decree.

## Article 10.- Feasibility Study

Any natural person or legal entity wishing to apply for a clean water supply business license must apply for an authorization letter to conduct a feasibility study of a clean water supply business project in any specific area determined by the Ministry in charge of clean water sector.

In case any area has an urgent and necessary demand for clean water supply to the citizens, the Minister of the Ministry in charge of clean water sector has the right to decide to waive the feasibility study.

The forms and procedures for applying for an authorization letter granting the right to conduct a feasibility study of a clean water supply business project shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry in charge of clean water sector.

## Article 11.- Response and Grant of Licenses

After receiving the application for a clean water supply business license that has been duly and completely filled out according to the forms, the Ministry in charge of clean water sector must issue a receipt of the application within 7 (seven) working days.

The Ministry in charge of clean water sector shall implement the procedure for review and grant of the license within a maximum of 60 (sixty) working days after the date of issuing the receipt of the application.

In case the application is rejected, the Ministry in charge of clean water sector must specify the reasons for such rejection.

## Article 12.- Licensing Procedure

The grant of clean water supply business licenses for areas where clean water supply is to be operated shall be implemented through a competitive bidding method or direct award method.

The criteria for determining the areas subject to licensing through competitive bidding or direct award shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry in charge of clean water sector.

## Article 13.- Validity of Licenses

Clean water supply business licenses granted to natural persons or legal entities shall have a specific validity determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry in charge of clean water sector.

This license may be adjusted before its expiration in case the licensee acts contrary to the conditions specified in the license. In this case, the licensee shall be solely responsible for any risks that may occur in connection with its clean water supply business.

## Article 14.- Transfer, Sale, Lease, and Pledging of Licenses

The transfer, sale, lease, and pledging of clean water supply business licenses must be authorized by the Ministry in charge of clean water sector. The transfer, sale, lease, and pledging of clean water supply business licenses shall be implemented in accordance with the procedures determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry in charge of clean water sector.

For the public interest, the Ministry in charge of clean water sector may authorize, reject, or impose certain conditions, such as:

- Transfer of functions in accordance with the principles specified in the clean water supply business license;

- Ensuring that business activities increase the capacity, capability, and efficiency of clean water supply;

- The transfer operation of clean water supply business must comply with tax regulations in force.

Article 15.- Performance of Duties by Agent

An operator may delegate to any agent the performance of all or part of its technical duties related to clean water supply services, only with the permission of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

All regulations and conditions related to operators shall also apply to the agent performing these duties within the scope of the delegated work.

#### Section 2
#### Certificate of Authorization for Clean Water Supply Operation

Article 16.- Certificate of Authorization for Clean Water Supply Operation

An operator who has obtained a clean water supply business license must apply for a certificate of authorization for clean water supply operation from the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector prior to conducting clean water supply business.

The forms and procedures for issuing the certificate of authorization for clean water supply operation shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

Article 17.- Validity of the Certificate of Authorization for Clean Water Supply Operation

The certificate of authorization for clean water supply operation shall have a specific validity period determined by the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

Within 90 (ninety) days prior to the expiration date of the certificate of authorization for clean water supply operation, the operator must apply to the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector to renew the validity.

The procedures for applying to renew the certificate of authorization for clean water supply operation shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

#### Section 3
#### Public Service Fees

Article 18.- Payment of Public Service Fees

Applying for an authorization letter to study, establish or manage a clean water supply business project, a clean water supply business license, and a certificate of authorization for clean water supply operation shall be subject to the payment of public service fees.

These public service fees shall be determined by a joint Prakas between the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector and the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Section 4
#### Request for Service Area Extension

Article 19.- Service Area Extension

The extension of the clean water supply service area shall require prior permission from the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

The operator may apply for permission to extend the service area to adjacent areas where no other operators are conducting clean water supply business.

An operator's application for extending the clean water supply service area may be granted, provided that the operator has covered at least 80 (eighty) percent of its clean water supply service area and implemented 100 (one hundred) percent of the construction of the treatment system according to the plan.

The forms and procedures for permitting the extension of the clean water supply area shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

#### Section 5
#### Conditions for Clean Water Supply Business Operation

Article 20.- Conditions for Clean Water Supply Business Operation

The conditions for clean water supply business operation are an annex to the clean water supply business license, which shall be determined by the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector to require the operator to implement in conducting its business.

The conditions for clean water supply business operation shall define the obligations, technical conditions, and business plan, including financial reports, that the operator must respect and implement when they obtain the clean water supply business license in a specific service area.

The conditions for clean water supply business operation, which are an annex to the clean water supply business license, shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector. The renewal, suspension, revocation, and cancellation of the clean water supply business license shall review the operator's compliance with the conditions for clean water supply business operation, which are an annex to the clean water supply business license.

The forms and procedures for the renewal, suspension, revocation, and cancellation of the clean water supply business license, in accordance with the conditions specified in paragraph 3 (three) above, shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

#### Chapter 6
#### Water Quality Standards

Article 21.- Clean Water Quality Standards

The operator must supply clean water that ensures quality and safety for public health, by complying with Cambodian standards for clean water quality and safety requirements, in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

The Ministry in charge of the clean water sector shall guide and disseminate the Cambodian standards for clean water quality and safety requirements to operators to ensure consistency in the use of clean water under a single clean water quality standard and to improve the efficiency of clean water production to guarantee public health.

#### Section 1
#### Water Quality Inspection

Article 22.- Water Quality Inspection

An operator who has obtained a clean water supply business license must have a laboratory or water quality measuring equipment to inspect water quality as determined by the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

The Ministry in charge of the clean water sector may designate specialized officers to collect samples for analysis and testing of water quality at all clean water business locations of the operator, when necessary.

#### Chapter 7
#### Land Use, Water Sources, and Shared Infrastructure

Article 23.- Land Use

The determination and use of land for installing or constructing clean water supply systems shall comply with applicable laws and regulations.

For public interest, the operator may obtain permission from the legal owner of the location where installation, construction, or repair of the clean water supply system is to be carried out.

The operator shall make efforts to resolve through negotiation and coordination with the owner, by paying reasonable and fair compensation to obtain the right of entry, transit, and/or land use for installing, constructing, or repairing the clean water supply system.

In case permission cannot be obtained from the owner, the operator shall apply to the competent authority to resolve this issue for the public interest.

Article 24.- Impact on Clean Water Supply System from Other Infrastructure

For all construction or repair of shared public or private infrastructure that may affect the clean water supply system infrastructure, the owner, project owner, or project developer must conduct an assessment of the impacts on the clean water supply system infrastructure and integrate it into their project in order to be able to resolve those impacts appropriately.

The owner, project owner, or project developer of construction or repair of infrastructure that may affect the clean water supply system infrastructure must notify the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector and the operator in the construction or repair area in writing, showing the construction or repair plan at least 30 (thirty) days prior to the start of the project implementation. Upon receiving this notice, the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector or the relevant operator must provide the master plan of the clean water supply network or treatment system, or other conditions, in order to cooperate, coordinate, resolve, and protect the clean water supply system from impacts in the project implementation areas, with the owner, project owner, or project developer, and the relevant competent ministries, institutions, and local authorities.

In case of any repair or construction that causes damage to the clean water supply system infrastructure, the repairing or constructing party must provide reasonable actual compensation for the damage to ensure that the clean water supply services remain continuous, stable, serving social livelihoods, and public welfare, which are indispensable basic needs.

Article 25.- Management, Maintenance, and Protection of Water Sources

The operator may use water sources in accordance with favorable geographical conditions and technical means for treatment. The use of this water source must respect and cooperate with the administration

sub-national level and/or relevant ministries and institutions in managing, maintaining, and protecting water sources in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

The Ministry in charge of the clean water sector, the Ministry in charge of the water resources sector, relevant ministries and institutions, and sub-national administrations shall cooperate in taking
measures to maintain and protect water sources to serve clean water supply.

#### Chapter 8
#### Tariff of Clean Water

#### Section 1
#### Determination of Clean Water Tariff

Article 26.- Determination of Clean Water Tariff

The determination of clean water tariff shall be based on the policy of the Royal Government, geographical conditions of each area, water sources, treatment systems, technology, demographics, and other physical infrastructure, and shall be based on clear considerations of production cost factors in order to ensure the interests of both consumers and service providers, particularly to facilitate people with low income.

The Ministry in charge of the clean water sector has the authority to determine and modify the clean water tariff as necessary in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

The forms and procedures for determining the clean water tariff shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

#### Section 2
#### Modification of Clean Water Tariff

Article 27.- General Review of Clean Water Tariff

The clean water tariff shall be reviewed every 5 (five) years through the same process as the previous determination. According to any necessary requirements, the modification of the clean water tariff may be done before the 5 (five) year period, in consultation with the operator.

Article 28.- Review of Clean Water Tariff in Special Cases

In special cases, the operator may request a review of the clean water tariff determination in the following cases:

- Cases of force majeure

- Political, economic, or financial crisis in the country

- Public unrest, labor disputes, or industrial sector unrest

- Inability or insecurity of materials, equipment, labor, or restriction or limitation on the use of water resources or labor

- Any event where a reputable insurance company cannot provide insurance coverage

- Other events that seriously affect the operation of the clean water supply business.

The Ministry in charge of the clean water sector may review the clean water tariff in special cases as specified in paragraph 1 above.

#### Chapter 9
#### Obligations and Rights of Operators

#### Section 1
#### Obligations of Operators

Article 29.- General Obligations

Operators must implement the policy program of the Royal Government of the Kingdom of Cambodia by ensuring four conditions: clean water quality, safety, sustainability, and reasonable price.

The operator has the following obligations:

- Prepare production plans, business plans, and short, medium, and long-term investment plans required by the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector

- Supply clean water to the best of their capacity and ability to meet the consumption needs of customers without discrimination

- Install clean water supply connection facilities upon customer request in a timely manner and in accordance with the determined plans

- Manage and maintain the clean water supply system

- Be responsible for ensuring clean water quality in accordance with applicable regulations

- Strictly comply with the principles of determining clean water tariffs and apply other service fees in accordance with the decision of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector

- Manage waste from clean water production in accordance with applicable laws and regulations

- Pay attention to and participate in conserving and developing water sources for treatment to produce clean water within their business location to ensure sustainability in clean water supply

- Provide information reports and financial reports as required by the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector and/or relevant competent institutions

- Strictly comply with all conditions specified in the clean water supply business license and other relevant regulations concerning clean water supply business operation

- Provide cooperation for inspections by inspection officers in the clean water sector.

Article 30.- Compliance with Technical Regulations

Clean water supply shall comply with technical regulations determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

Article 31.- Provision of Data and Information on Clean Water Supply

Operators must record data and information on production and business status and input them into the data management system of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

Financial data and information provided to the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector shall not be disclosed to the public, except upon request of the operator concerned or with the authorization of the relevant competent authority.

Article 32.- Emergency Measures

Operators must regularly monitor the clean water supply system and water quality to ensure that they do not pose a risk to public health.

In the event that the clean water supply system or water quality poses a risk for any reason, the operator must take immediate necessary measures, including notifying clean water consumers at a specific time in the risk area to prepare for temporary coping during clean water shortages, draining water, cleaning, and improving the clean water supply system to restore it to normal condition.

Article 33.- Water Quality Testing

Operators must test the quality of water sources, water in treatment systems, water in the clean water supply network, and water in connection facilities for necessary parameters to monitor their clean water supply system to ensure clean water quality standards in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 21 of this Law.

Article 34.- Sanitation Measures for Clean Water Supply System

Operators must take disinfection measures or other measures to maintain sanitation for treatment systems, clean water supply networks, and clean water supply connection facilities, in accordance with technical regulations determined by the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

#### Section 2
#### Rights of Operators

Article 35.- Basic Rights of Operators

Operators who have correctly implemented this Law shall be guaranteed basic rights as follows:

- Conduct exclusive clean water supply business within their service area

- Receive incentives in accordance with investment policies of the Royal Government and relevant policies concerning financial support

- Receive the right to manage and protect the clean water supply system within their service area

- Take necessary and appropriate measures within their authority to ensure clean water supply operations, such as temporary suspension or permanent disconnection of clean water supply to customers who:
    - Refuse to pay or delay payment of clean water usage fees or other service fees
    - Steal clean water or connect to the clean water network without permission, or cause other problems that obstruct the clean water supply, until the customer has paid the clean water usage fees, or corrected the water meter to its original state, or replaced it with a new one.

#### Section 3
#### Association of Clean Water Supply Service Providers

Article 36.- Association of Clean Water Supply Service Providers

Operators have the right to establish an association of clean water supply service providers in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. After registration, the association must deposit one copy of the Prakas on the registration of the association and its statute with the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

Membership in the association of clean water supply service providers shall be voluntary and open to public legal entities, public or private enterprises, and any individuals with knowledge or experience related to the clean water supply sector in accordance with the statute of the association.

The association of clean water supply service providers is a cooperation partner with the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector in promoting and developing the clean water supply sector.

#### Chapter 10
#### Management, Inspection of Clean Water Supply Systems, and Competence of Inspection Officers

Article 37.- Management and Inspection

Operators must organize clean water supply systems in compliance with requirements for water quality, quantity, and pressure according to geographical conditions, from water sources, treatment systems, to clean water supply connection facilities, in accordance with technical regulations determined by the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

The Ministry in charge of the clean water sector shall issue a written order to the operator to correct the clean water supply system or change the technical manager to protect public health, in case it is found that the system does not comply with the technical regulations specified in this Law and applicable regulations, or the clean water supply technical manager has made errors in implementing work and regulations or instructions issued by the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

Article 38.- Appointment of Inspection Officers

The Ministry in charge of the clean water sector shall appoint inspection officers to monitor, investigate, research, inspect, collect evidence, and promote the implementation of this Law.

Inspection officers shall obtain the status of judicial police to investigate offenses as specified in this Law, in accordance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Code.

Inspection officers may inspect the clean water supply system at any time.

In operations to investigate offenses, inspection officers must follow the Criminal Procedure Code and may request assistance from local authorities at all levels and armed forces units or other relevant competent authorities to participate in suppressing offenses as specified in this Law.

In performing operations to implement this Law, inspection officers must wear uniforms and carry mission letters at all times.

Uniforms, badges, and identification cards of inspection officers shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

The forms and procedures for granting judicial police status to inspection officers shall be determined by a joint Prakas between the Minister of the Ministry of Justice and the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

Article 39.- Powers of Inspection Officers

Inspection officers shall have the following powers:

(a) Enter the clean water business premises of the operator by presenting the mission order issued by the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector to:
- Inspect and take samples of water and raw materials or other substances used in the clean water supply system
- Inspect the implementation of technical conditions of the clean water supply system
- Inspect clean water tariffs and clean water supply service quality, including water pressure, water quantity, and supply duration
- Inspect treatment systems, clean water supply networks, and waste discharges.

(b) Monitor and research equipment or chemical substances, apply labels, place seals on evidence of offenses, or take other necessary measures in case there is suspicion of offenses under this Law.

(c) Require the operator to provide information or present the clean water supply business license and the certificate of authorization for clean water supply operation, as well as documents related to their business.

(d) Invite relevant persons to provide information, clarification, documents, or other objects for investigation purposes.

(e) Take measures to temporarily suspend the clean water supply when investigation shows evidence of non-compliance with technical regulations and poses risks to the clean water supply system or causes danger to human, animal, plant health, the environment, and social security, and then report as soon as possible to relevant competent ministries and institutions to take further measures under applicable laws.

(f) Submit proposals to the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector to request approval for the suspension, revocation, or cancellation of the certificate of authorization for clean water supply operation, clean water supply business license, or related documents in case the operator violates this Law.

(g) Implement interim fines in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

(h) Fulfill other duties assigned by the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

The forms and procedures for conducting inspections in the clean water sector shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

#### Chapter 11
#### Emergency Measures

Article 40.- Events constituting an Emergency

Events considered as an emergency in accordance with this Law include:

- Insufficient clean water supply due to force majeure, such as severe drought or flood
- Insufficient clean water supply due to unrest in the industrial sector or in society, demonstrations, strikes, or temporary suspension of enterprises
- Clean water supply or clean water supply systems that affect or threaten public safety or public health
- Placing the nation in a state of emergency.

Article 41.- Emergency Measures

In case of emergency, the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector and relevant ministries and institutions shall take measures and issue guidelines to manage those emergencies.

Operators must comply with all determinations and guidelines of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector to manage the clean water supply situation.

#### Chapter 12
#### Dispute Resolution

Article 42.- Complaint Against Rejecting Decisions of the Ministry in charge of the Clean Water Sector

Any person who is dissatisfied with the rejection of the issuance of authorization letters to study, establish or manage projects, licenses, certificates of authorization, or applications for extension of clean water supply service areas, has the right to file a written complaint within 60 (sixty) days from the date of receipt of the rejection notice to the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector prior to filing a lawsuit in court.

The Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector shall issue a written decision specifying the reasons within 30 (thirty) working days from the date of receipt of the complaint.

In case any person is dissatisfied with the decision of the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector, that person has the right to file a lawsuit in court according to procedures within 60 (sixty) days from the date of receipt of the notification of that decision.

In case the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector does not make a decision on the complaint within the period specified in the paragraph above, the complainant may file a lawsuit in court within 60 (sixty) days from the date of expiration of that period.

Article 43.- Complaint Against Administrative Sanction Decisions

Any person who is dissatisfied with the suspension, revocation, or cancellation of the authorization letter to study, establish or manage clean water supply business projects, certificates of authorization for clean water supply operation, or clean water supply business licenses, has the right to file a written complaint within 60 (sixty) days from the date of receipt of the notification of such suspension, revocation, or cancellation to the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector prior to filing a lawsuit in court.

The Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector shall issue a written decision specifying the reasons within 30 (thirty) working days from the date of receipt of the complaint.

In case any person is dissatisfied with the decision of the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector, that person has the right to file a lawsuit in court according to procedures in force within 60 (sixty) days from the date of receipt of the notification of that decision.

In case the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector does not make a decision on the complaint within the period specified in the paragraph above, the complainant may file a lawsuit in court within 60 (sixty) days from the date of expiration of that period.

Article 44.- Complaint Against Administrative Measures of Inspection Officers

Any person who is dissatisfied with any administrative measure of the inspection officers may file a written complaint to the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector within 60 (sixty) working days from the date of receipt of the notification of such measure.

The Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector shall issue a decision on the complaint specifying the reasons within 30 (thirty) working days from the date of receipt of the complaint.

In case any person is dissatisfied with the decision of the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector, that person has the right to file a lawsuit in court according to procedures in force within a maximum of 60 (sixty) days from the date of receipt of the notification of that decision.

In case the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector does not make a decision on the complaint within the period specified in the paragraph above, the complainant may file a lawsuit in court within 60 (sixty) days from the date of expiration of that period.

Article 45.- Dispute Mediation Between Operators

For all disputes between operators related to clean water supply businesses, the disputing parties may file a complaint to the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector for mediation before filing a lawsuit to an arbitration center or a competent court, except for disputes related to the clean water supply service area boundary, which the disputing parties must file with the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

In case of dissatisfaction with the decision of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector related to the clean water supply service area boundary, the disputing party may file a lawsuit in a competent court according to procedures in force within a maximum of 30 (thirty) days from the date of receipt of the notification of this decision.

Article 46.- Dispute Between Operator and Clean Water Consumer

In case the operator and the clean water consumer have a dispute with each other regarding the clean water supply services or clean water usage, the disputing parties may file a written complaint to the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector or the Capital/Provincial Department in charge of the clean water sector.

The Ministry in charge of the clean water sector or the Capital/Provincial Department in charge of the clean water sector shall issue a written decision within 30 (thirty) working days from the date of receipt of the complaint.

In case any person is dissatisfied with the decision of the official in charge of the clean water sector of the capital or province, they have the right to appeal to the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector within 30 (thirty) working days starting from the date of receipt of the notification letter of that decision. In this case, the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector must issue a decision in writing within 30 (thirty) working days starting from the date of receipt of the complaint.

Any person dissatisfied with the decision on the complaint has the right to file a lawsuit in court in accordance with the procedures in force within 60 (sixty) days starting from the date of receipt of the notification letter of that decision.

In case the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector does not make a decision on the complaint within the period specified in the second paragraph above, the complainant may file a lawsuit in court in accordance with the procedures in force within 60 (sixty) days starting from the date of expiry of the specified period.

### Chapter 13 Penal Provisions

#### Article 47 .- Types of Sanctions

Sanctions in this law include administrative sanctions and criminal sanctions.

Administrative sanctions include:

- Written warning

- Suspension, revocation, or cancellation of the permit granting the right to study the feasibility of clean water supply business project, clean water supply business license, or certificate of authorization for clean water supply operation. In case of suspension, revocation, or cancellation of clean water supply business license, the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector shall take various measures to ensure the sustainability of the clean water supply. Acts subject to administrative sanctions shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

The decision to impose administrative sanctions is under the competence of the Ministry in charge of clean water sector.

Criminal sanctions include:

- Transactional fine

- Fine

- Imprisonment.

Other offenses subject to transactional fine in accordance with this law shall be determined by sub-decree.

The decision to impose a transactional fine is under the competence of the clean water inspector who is authorized as a judicial police officer.

Payment of transactional fine shall extinguish the criminal action.

Payment of transactional fine does not release the offender from the obligation to implement administrative measures and administrative sanctions imposed in accordance with this law.

In case the offender refuses to pay the transactional fine, the clean water inspector who is authorized as a judicial police officer shall build a case file and send it to the competent court.

The regime and procedure of the transactional fine shall be determined by a joint Prakas between the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector and the Minister of the Ministry of Justice.

Payment of the transactional fine, management of the transactional fine payment receipt, and management of the revenue from the transactional fine for the specified offenses in accordance with the provisions of this law shall be determined by a joint Prakas between the Minister of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector and the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

The Royal Government may decide to reward and encourage officials who have participated in cracking down on offenses in the clean water sector.

#### Article 48 .- Conducting Feasibility Studies of Clean Water Supply Business Projects without Authorization

A transactional fine of 2,000,000 (two million) Riels to 5,000,000 (five million) Riels shall be imposed on any physical or legal person who conducts feasibility studies of clean water supply business projects without a permit granting the right to study the feasibility of clean water supply business projects from the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

#### Article 49 .- Transactional Fine for Physical Persons

A transactional fine of 2,000,000 (two million) Riels to 5,000,000 (five million) Riels shall be imposed on any physical person operator of a clean water supply business who commits any of the following acts:

1- Operating a clean water supply business using an expired clean water supply business license as specified in the first paragraph of Article 13 of this law.

2- Operating a business using an expired certificate of authorization for clean water supply operation as specified in the first paragraph of Article 17 of this law.

3- Supplying clean water that does not comply with Cambodian standards for clean water and clean water safety as specified in the first paragraph of Article 26 of this law.

4- Failing to properly record and enter all data and information on production and business status into the data management system of the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector as specified in the first paragraph of Article 31 of this law.

5- Failing to test the quality of water sources, water in the treatment system, water in the clean water distribution network, and water in the transportation means of the clean water supply service in front of the meter of their clean water supply system, at the time required by standards and as determined by the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector as specified in Article 33 of this law.

Failing to take measures to disinfect or implement sanitary measures in the treatment system, distribution network, and transportation means of the clean water supply service as specified in Article 34 of this law.

A transactional fine of 5,000,000 (five million) Riels to 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels shall be imposed on any physical person operator of a clean water supply business who commits any of the following acts:

1- Transferring, selling, leasing, or pledging the usufruct of the clean water supply business license without authorization from the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector as specified in the first paragraph of Article 14 of this law.

2- Allowing any third party to perform all or part of the technical work related to the clean water supply service on their behalf without authorization from the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector as specified in the first paragraph of Article 15 of this law.

3- Operating a clean water supply business without a certificate of authorization for clean water supply operation as specified in the first paragraph of Article 16 of this law.

4- Expanding the service area without authorization as specified in Article 19 of this law.

5- Failing to have a laboratory or water quality testing equipment as determined by the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector as specified in the first paragraph of Article 22 of this law.

6- Selling clean water at a price other than that set by the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

7- Setting up a clean water supply system that does not comply with technical regulations as specified in Article 30 of this law.

8- Providing data and information to the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector or competent officials knowing that this information is incorrect or with the intention of falsifying data and information.

9- Failing to take urgent measures as specified in Article 36 of this law.

10- Failing to comply with a written order to rectify the clean water supply system or change the technical manager as specified in the second paragraph of Article 37 of this law.

11- Failing to comply with measures in an emergency as specified in the second paragraph of Article 41 of this law.

The Ministry in charge of the clean water sector may decide to impose additional administrative sanctions as specified in this law and legal instruments in accordance with this law on physical persons who have committed the offenses specified in the first and second paragraphs of this article.

#### Article 50 .- Transactional Fine for Legal Persons

For any legal person operator of a clean water supply business who commits any of the offenses specified in the first and second paragraphs of Article 49 of this law, the transactional fine shall be doubled.

The Ministry in charge of the clean water sector may decide to impose additional administrative sanctions as specified in this law and legal instruments in accordance with this law on legal persons who have committed the offenses specified in the first and second paragraphs of Article 49 of this law.

#### Article 51 .- Failing to Notify about Impact on Infrastructure of Clean Water Supply System

A transactional fine of 2,000,000 (two million) Riels to 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels shall be imposed on any physical person or private legal person who is a landowner or project owner or implementer of construction or repair of various infrastructures, failing to notify in advance the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector and

operators located in the construction or repair area, regarding this construction or repair project, in accordance with the second paragraph of Article 24 of this law, in case they know that the implementation of this project leads to impact on the infrastructure of the clean water supply system.

#### Article 52 .- Cases of Repeat Offenses

Any person who has already been ordered to pay a transactional fine as specified in Article 48 to Article 51 of this law and who commits the same offense again, the transactional fine shall be doubled.

#### Article 53 .- Operating Clean Water Supply Business without License

Shall be punished by imprisonment from 3 (three) months to 1 (one) year and a fine of 5,000,000 (five million) Riels to 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels for any person who operates a clean water supply business without a license from the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

#### Article 54 .- Non-Compliance with Clean Water Quality Standards

Shall be punished by imprisonment from 6 (six) months to 2 (two) years and a fine of 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels to 40,000,000 (forty million) Riels for any clean water supply business operator who fails to comply with Cambodian standards for clean water quality and safety requirements, causing an impact on the health of users.

Shall be punished by imprisonment from 2 (two) years to 5 (five) years and a fine of 40,000,000 (forty million) Riels to 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels if the act specified in the first paragraph above causes serious impact on the health of users.

Shall be punished by imprisonment from 7 (seven) years to 15 (fifteen) years if the act specified in the first paragraph above causes loss of life of users.

#### Article 55 .- Intentional Damage to Clean Water Supply Infrastructure

Shall be punished by imprisonment from 6 (six) months to 2 (two) years and a fine of 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels to 40,000,000 (forty million) Riels for any person who intentionally damages any part of the infrastructure of the clean water supply system.

#### Article 56 .- Taking Water for Use or Re-distribution without Authorization

Shall be punished by imprisonment from 6 (six) months to 3 (three) years and a fine of 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels to 60,000,000 (sixty million) Riels for any person who takes water from the clean water supply system by any means for use or re-distribution without prior authorization from the operator.

#### Article 57 .- Altering or Changing Water Meter without Authorization

Shall be punished by imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 6 (six) months and a fine of 2,000,000 (two million) Riels to 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels for any person who alters or changes a water meter or water measuring device without prior authorization from the operator.

#### Article 58 .- Water Pollution

Shall be punished by imprisonment from 6 (six) months to 2 (two) years and a fine of 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels to 40,000,000 (forty million) Riels for any person who intentionally uses any substance to pollute water in a water source or in any part of the clean water supply system.

Shall be punished by imprisonment from 2 (two) years to 5 (five) years and a fine of 40,000,000 (forty million) Riels to 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels if the act specified in the first paragraph above causes impact on the health of users. Shall be punished by imprisonment from 5 (five) years to 10 (ten) years in case this act is committed by an organized group.

Shall be punished by imprisonment from 7 (seven) years to 15 (fifteen) years if the act specified in the first paragraph above causes loss of life of users. Shall be punished by imprisonment from 15 (fifteen) years to 30 (thirty) years in case this act is committed by an organized group.

#### Article 59 .- Sanctions for Legal Persons

Legal persons may be declared criminally responsible as specified in Article 42 (Criminal Liability of Legal Persons) of the Criminal Code for offenses specified in Article 53 to Article 58 of this law.

Legal persons shall be punished by a double fine, as well as one or more additional penalties, as specified in Article 168 (Additional Penalties Applicable to Legal Persons) of the Criminal Code.

#### Article 60 .- Offenses Committed by Inspectors

Any inspector who abuses their power contrary to the purpose of this law, causing impact on the rights, physical integrity, or damage to the property of others, shall be responsible for their acts in accordance with the laws and regulations in force of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

Any inspector who commits a mistake in the implementation of their work shall receive administrative disciplinary sanctions in accordance with the laws and regulations in force, without prejudice to other criminal penalties.

#### Article 61 .- Implementation of Other Criminal Laws

The implementation of the provisions of Chapter 13 of this law does not preclude the implementation of other criminal laws in case the act defined as an offense specified in this law is an offense prescribed in other criminal laws.

### Chapter 14 Transitional Provisions

#### Article 62 .- Transitional Phase for Persons without License

In order to comply with the provisions of this law, any person who is currently operating a clean water supply business without a license shall apply for a license within 3 (three) months after receiving an official notification letter from the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector or the entity delegated by the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

#### Article 63. - Extension of Validity of Existing Licenses

The validity of clean water supply business licenses obtained before the entry into force of this Law shall continue until their expiration date. Within 3 (three) months prior to the expiration of the validity, the license holder shall apply for a new license to replace the old license that is about to expire, in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

#### Article 64. - Extension of the Legal Effect of Existing Legal Instruments

Legal instruments related to clean water management and clean water supply service provision shall continue to be in force until they are replaced by new legal instruments in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

### Chapter 15 Final Provisions

#### Article 65. - Abrogation

Any provisions contrary to this Law shall be abrogated.

### Annex to the Law on Clean Water Management

### Glossary

1- Extension of service area refers to increasing the clean water distribution network outside the service area in which one is currently supplying clean water.

2- Feasibility study refers to studying geographical, demographic, water source, technical, and financial factors to demonstrate the feasibility of clean water supply service in a specific area.

3- Service area refers to an area defined geographically in which the operator has the exclusive right to supply clean water to customers in accordance with the terms of the license granted by the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector.

4- Clean water tariff refers to the amount in Riel that the operator is allowed to collect from their customers for the provision of clean water supply service per unit of cubic meter, equal to 1,000 (one thousand) liters.

5- Other service fees refer to fees collected by the operator from customers in addition to the clean water tariff in accordance with the water subscription agreement, which may include connection fees, disconnection and reconnection fees, repair fees, late payment fines, or other fees allowed under this law and regulations in force.

6- Raw water refers to water obtained from various water sources that has not yet undergone treatment.

7- Clean water refers to water that has undergone treatment and has quality, safety, and can be supplied to customers.

8- Water source refers to surface water including seas, rivers, streams, creeks, ravines, valleys, canals, lakes, ponds, pools, puddles, reservoirs, groundwater flowing underground in spaces of rocks and soil particles that move from one place to another, rainwater, and accumulation of water in other forms.

9- Treatment refers to the process of modifying water quality from raw water to clean water through various water purification technologies.

10- Treatment system refers to the infrastructure for the process of modifying water quality, ranging from the raw water pumping station to the clean water storage reservoir.

11- Clean water supply system refers to the treatment system, clean water distribution network, and various transportation means of clean water supply services.

12- Person refers to a physical or legal person.

13- Means of service connection refers to clean water pipe connecting to the clean water distribution network and other water supply connection equipment up to the clean water meter at the customer's location.

14- Clean water distribution network refers to the distribution pumping station, distribution booster station, main pipes, sub-pipes, and elevated water tank.

15- Certificate of authorization for clean water supply operation refers to the letter issued by the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector, authorizing the operator to use their clean water supply system to provide clean water supply services in their service area.

16- Disinfection measures refers to cleaning the clean water supply system using chemicals or technologies to kill pathogens in the clean water supply system.

17- Operator refers to any person who has obtained a clean water supply business license.

18- Clean water supply service refers to the supply of clean water provided by the operator to customers on a business basis.

19- Clean water supply business license refers to the letter issued by the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector, granted to the operator, defining the terms, rights, and responsibilities for investment, business operation, and clean water supply services in a specific service area with clearly defined boundaries.

20- Wholesale clean water supply license refers to the letter issued by the Ministry in charge of the clean water sector, granted to the operator, defining the terms, rights, and responsibilities for investment, production operation, and distribution of clean water in the form of a wholesaler to the franchisee for re-distribution.
