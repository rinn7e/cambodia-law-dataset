### PROMULGATE

The Law on Nationality, which was adopted by the National Assembly on May 31, 2018 during the 10th session of the 5th legislature, and which was reviewed and approved in its entirety by the Senate on June 11, 2018 during the 1st plenary session of the 4th legislature, the contents of which are as follows:

# LAW ON NATIONALITY

## CHAPTER 1
## GENERAL PROVISIONS

### ARTICLE 1.- (New)
The purpose of this Law is to ensure the exercise of the jurisdiction of the Kingdom of Cambodia over natural persons in order to determine the rights to obtain and the rights to renounce Khmer nationality for persons living in the territory of the Kingdom of Cambodia or abroad and who have fulfilled the conditions of this Law.

### ARTICLE 2.-
This law aims to determine:

- Obtaining Khmer nationality by birth
- Obtaining Khmer nationality by full adoption
- Obtaining Khmer nationality by marriage
- Obtaining Khmer nationality by naturalization
- Loss of Khmer nationality.

### ARTICLE 3.-
This Law has the scope of application for natural persons born or living in the territory of the Kingdom of Cambodia or abroad.

### ARTICLE 4.-
Key terms used in this Law are defined as follows:

1- Khmer Nationality refers to the legal and political bond that links a natural person to the Kingdom of Cambodia.

2- Claiming Khmer Nationality refers to the right to apply for Khmer nationality arising from a legal marriage bond between a foreigner and a Khmer citizen.

3- Naturalization refers to the granting of Khmer nationality through the application of a foreigner based on the conditions of residence, investment, cash donation, and special merits or special skills.

## CHAPTER 2
## COMPETENT INSTITUTIONS

### ARTICLE 5.-
The Ministry of Interior has the competence to lead the implementation of the Law on Nationality.

## CHAPTER 3
## GENERAL PRINCIPLES

### ARTICLE 6.-
Any person having Khmer nationality is a Khmer citizen.

Only Khmer citizens have the right to receive and hold Khmer nationality identity cards and passports of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### ARTICLE 7.- (New)
A Khmer citizen cannot be deprived of nationality, exiled, or extradited to any foreign country, unless there is a mutual agreement.

### ARTICLE 8.-
Khmer citizens living abroad:

1. Shall be protected by the State through all diplomatic means.

2. Cannot lose their nationality unilaterally.

## CHAPTER 4
## KHMER NATIONALITY BY BIRTH AND KHMER NATIONALITY BY FULL ADOPTION

### Section 1
### Khmer Nationality by Birth

### ARTICLE 9.-

1- Khmer nationality shall be granted regardless of the place of birth to:

- A child born of a father or mother having Khmer nationality.

- A child whose father or mother did not acknowledge, but after the court issued a verdict that the child was indeed born to a father or mother having Khmer nationality.

2- Khmer nationality shall be granted by being born in the Kingdom of Cambodia to:

- A child born of foreign parents who were born and living legally in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

- A child born of unknown parents and a newborn baby who was found in the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be considered to have been born in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Section 2
### Khmer Nationality by Full Adoption

### ARTICLE 10.-
A foreign child who has been fully adopted by a Khmer spouse shall receive Khmer nationality when the full adoption is established in accordance with the law in force.

## CHAPTER 5
## KHMER NATIONALITY BY MARRIAGE

### ARTICLE 11.-
A foreign woman or man who marries a Khmer citizen can claim Khmer nationality provided that the woman or man has lived together for a period of three years after the registration of the marriage and has resided for at least 12 (twelve) full months in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

Claiming Khmer nationality as a family cluster is allowed for a foreign mother or father whose children are minors.

### ARTICLE 12.-
A foreigner who intends to claim Khmer nationality by marriage must fulfill the following conditions:

1- Have good behavior and morality.

2- Have never been convicted of any felony or misdemeanor.

3- Have a residence in the Kingdom of Cambodia at the time of applying to claim Khmer nationality by marriage.

4- Be able to speak Khmer, write Khmer, and have some knowledge of Khmer history.

- 5- Have intelligence and physical fitness that do not cause danger or burden to the nation.

### ARTICLE 13.-
A foreigner who applies to claim Khmer nationality by marriage can apply to change their name to a Khmer name. In this case, the new name must be specified in the application to claim Khmer nationality.

### ARTICLE 14.-
In case the competent authority rejects the claim for Khmer nationality, the person concerned has the right to appeal.

### ARTICLE 15.-
Forms and procedures for claiming Khmer nationality, as well as appeal procedures, shall be determined by Sub-degree.

The granting of Khmer nationality in the case of claiming Khmer nationality shall be decided by Royal Decree.

### ARTICLE 16.- (New)
Persons who have been allowed to obtain Khmer nationality by marriage must take an oath before the Supreme Court.

The content of this oath shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### ARTICLE 17.-
A Khmer citizen does not lose Khmer nationality due to marriage with a foreigner.

## CHAPTER 6
## NATURALIZATION

### ARTICLE 18.-
A foreigner can apply for Khmer nationality through naturalization.

Naturalization is not a right of the applicant, but merely a favor of the Kingdom of Cambodia. In any case, the application may be rejected at the discretion.

### ARTICLE 19.-
A foreigner who can apply for naturalization must fulfill the following conditions:

1- Have good behavior and morality.

2- Have never been convicted of any felony or misdemeanor.

3- Have a residence in the Kingdom of Cambodia and have resided for 7 (seven) consecutive years from the date of receiving the residence permit issued within the framework of the Law on Immigration, for...

...immigrant foreigners.

4- Have a residence in the Kingdom of Cambodia and have a residence permit issued within the framework of the Law on Immigration for foreigners defined in Articles 21 and 22 of this Law.

5- Be able to speak Khmer, write Khmer, and have some knowledge of Khmer history, and show evidence that they can live harmoniously in Khmer society and accept Khmer customs and traditions.

6- Have intelligence and physical fitness that do not cause danger or burden to the nation.

### ARTICLE 20.-
For any immigrant foreigner who was born in the Kingdom of Cambodia, the duration of consecutive residence of 7 (seven) years as stated in point 3 of Article 19 of this Law shall be reduced to 3 (three) years.

### ARTICLE 21.-
Any foreigner who has received a proper investment authorization in accordance with the law from the competent institution and has spent capital to implement an actual investment project in the Kingdom of Cambodia, that foreigner can apply for Khmer nationality through naturalization in case that foreigner has fulfilled the conditions as stated in points 1, 2, 4, 5 and 6 of Article 19 of this Law and has resided legally for at least 12 (twelve) full months in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

The amount of money, priority sectors, and types of investment projects, as well as other conditions, shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### ARTICLE 22.-
Any foreigner who has made a cash donation to the national budget for the benefit of socio-economic development or humanitarian sector in the Kingdom of Cambodia, that foreigner can apply for Khmer nationality in case that foreigner has fulfilled the conditions as stated in points 1, 2, 4 and 6 of Article 19 of this Law and has resided legally for at least 6 (six) full months in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

The amount of cash donation to the national budget or humanitarian sector shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### ARTICLE 23.-
Any foreigner who has shown evidence of having special merits or any special skill for the benefit of the Kingdom of Cambodia and has been recognized by the Royal Government of the Kingdom of Cambodia, can apply for Khmer nationality through naturalization in case that foreigner has fulfilled the conditions as stated in points 1, 2 and 6 of Article 19 of this Law.

### ARTICLE 24.-
A foreigner who has a spouse or children who are minors and who intends to apply for Khmer nationality through naturalization can apply as a family cluster.

### ARTICLE 25.-
A foreigner can apply to change their name to a Khmer name. In this case, the new name must be specified in the naturalization application.

### ARTICLE 26.-
The forms and procedures for naturalization applications shall be determined by Sub-decree.

The granting of Khmer nationality by naturalization shall be decided by Royal Decree.

### ARTICLE 27.- (New)
Persons who have been allowed to obtain Khmer nationality by naturalization must take an oath before the Supreme Court.

The content of this oath shall be determined by Sub-decree.

## CHAPTER 7
## LOSS OF KHMER NATIONALITY

### ARTICLE 28.- (New)
Any person who has Khmer nationality and is at least 18 (eighteen) years old can apply to renounce Khmer nationality in case that person has another nationality and without coercion.

Renunciation of Khmer nationality as a family cluster of a Khmer citizen who is a mother or father having minor children is allowed.

### ARTICLE 29.- (New)
Forms and procedures for renouncing nationality shall be determined by Sub-decree.

Permission to renounce Khmer nationality shall be decided by Royal Decree.

## CHAPTER 8
## PENAL PROVISIONS

### ARTICLE 30.-
Any foreigner who holds and uses a Khmer nationality identity card or a passport of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be punished by imprisonment from 5 (five) years to 10 (ten) years.

Any government official or person who produces a Khmer nationality identity card or a passport of the Kingdom of Cambodia for any person who does not have Khmer nationality shall be punished the same as stated in the first paragraph of this Article.

### ARTICLE 31.-
Any person who forges a Khmer nationality identity card or a passport of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be punished by imprisonment from 5 (five) years to 10 (ten) years.

Acts of scratching, erasing, adding text, or misrepresenting information in a Khmer nationality identity card or a passport of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall also be considered as forgery.

### ARTICLE 32.-
Any person who uses a forged Khmer nationality identity card or a forged passport of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be punished by imprisonment from 2 (two) years to 5 (five) years and a fine from 4,000,000 (four million) Riels to 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels.

### ARTICLE 33.-
Instigators and accomplices shall be held criminally responsible for the offenses stated in Articles 30, 31, and 32 of this Law, in accordance with the conditions stated in the Penal Code.

### ARTICLE 34.- (New)
For a natural person who commits an offense as stated in Articles 30, 31, and 32 of this Law, the court may declare one or more additional penalties as stated in Article 53 (Types of additional penalties) of the Penal Code.

### ARTICLE 35.-
A legal entity may be declared criminally responsible in accordance with the conditions stated in Article 42 (Criminal responsibility of legal entities) of the Penal Code for the offenses stated in Article 30...

...Articles 31 and 32 of this Law.

Legal entities shall be punished by a fine from 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels to 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels, and one or more additional penalties as stated in Article 168 (Additional penalties applicable to legal entities) of the Penal Code.

## CHAPTER 9
## INTER-PROVISIONS

### ARTICLE 36.-
All legal documents related to the implementation of the Law on Nationality promulgated by Royal Decree No. NS/RKM/1096/30 dated October 9, 1996 shall remain in force until there are new legal documents to replace them, in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

## CHAPTER 10
## FINAL PROVISIONS

### ARTICLE 37.-
The Law on Nationality promulgated by Royal Decree No. NS/RKM/1096/30 dated October 9, 1996 shall be abrogated and replaced by this Law.

### ARTICLE 38.-
This Law shall be declared as urgent.

### PROMULGATE

The Law on the Amendment of the Law on Nationality, which was adopted by the National Assembly on August 25, 2025 during the 4th session of the 7th legislature, and which was reviewed and approved in its entirety by the Senate on September 1, 2025 during the extraordinary session of the Senate of the 5th legislature, the contents of which are as follows:

# LAW ON THE AMENDMENT OF THE LAW ON NATIONALITY

### ARTICLE ONE.-
Articles 1, 7, 16, 27, 28, 29, and 34 of the Law on Nationality promulgated by Royal Decree No. NS/RKM/0618/008 dated June 21, 2018, are amended as follows:

### ARTICLE 1.- (New)
The purpose of this Law is to ensure the exercise of the jurisdiction of the Kingdom of Cambodia over natural persons in order to determine the obtaining of Khmer nationality, as well as the loss of Khmer nationality through renunciation and deprivation for persons living in the territory of the Kingdom of Cambodia or abroad and who have fulfilled the conditions of this Law.

### ARTICLE 7.- (New)
A Khmer citizen cannot be exiled or extradited to any foreign country, unless there is a mutual agreement.

### ARTICLE 16.- (New)
Persons who have been allowed to obtain Khmer nationality by marriage must take an oath before the Supreme Court.

The content of this oath is attached as an annex to this Law.

A Khmer nationality identity card and a passport of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be provided after the completion of the oath procedure before the Supreme Court.

### ARTICLE 27.- (New)
Persons who have been allowed to obtain Khmer nationality by naturalization must take an oath before the Supreme Court.

The content of this oath is attached as an annex to this Law.

A Khmer nationality identity card and a passport of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be provided after the completion of the oath procedure before the Supreme Court.

### ARTICLE 28.- (New)
A Khmer citizen who is at least 18 (eighteen) years old can voluntarily apply to renounce Khmer nationality in case that person has another nationality.

Renunciation of Khmer nationality as a family cluster of a Khmer citizen who is a mother or father having minor children is allowed.

Forms and procedures for renouncing Khmer nationality shall be determined by Sub-decree.

Permission to renounce Khmer nationality shall be decided by Royal Decree.

### ARTICLE 29.- (New)
A Khmer citizen can be deprived of Khmer nationality in any of the following cases:

1- Committing treason or activities in collaboration with a foreign country that harm the core interests of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

2- Activities that harm national sovereignty, territorial integrity, and national security.

3- Voluntarily joining the military of a foreign country, fulfilling a role as a public official of a foreign country, and refusing to stop despite orders from the Royal Government of Cambodia.

4- Obtaining Khmer nationality through fraud, deception, or providing false information.

5- Being convicted of a felony or misdemeanor related to the violation of the King, affecting national security, or acts of terrorism.

Deprivation of Khmer nationality by birth can be carried out only if the Khmer citizen is at least 18 (eighteen) years old and has another nationality or has a basis to believe that they can obtain another nationality or receive protection from a foreign state. Deprivation of Khmer nationality by birth shall be carried out after receiving the advice of a national committee, the creation and operation of which shall be determined by Sub-decree at the request of the Minister of Interior.

Forms and procedures for deprivation of Khmer nationality shall be determined by Sub-decree.

Deprivation of Khmer nationality shall be decided by Royal Decree.

### ARTICLE 34.- (New)
For a natural person who commits an offense as stated in Articles 30, 31, and 32 of this Law, the court may declare one or more additional penalties as stated in Article 53 (Types of additional penalties) of the Penal Code.

### ARTICLE TWO.-
This Law shall be declared as urgent.

# ANNEX

## CONTENT OF OATH FOR PERSONS ALLOWED TO OBTAIN KHMER NATIONALITY BY MARRIAGE AND BY NATURALIZATION

The content of the oath is as follows:

"I solemnly swear that I have loyalty and love for my Khmer nation and motherland, and I am determined to make all kinds of sacrifices to protect and respect the Constitution and laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia, both at present and in the future.

I solemnly swear that I will position myself as a good Khmer citizen and dare to sacrifice everything to contribute to protecting the foundation of national independence, national sovereignty, territorial integrity, peace, stability, security, and socio-economic development, as well as promoting the prestige of the Kingdom of Cambodia, both at present and in the future."
