# HEREBY PROMULGATE

The Law on the Organization and Functioning of the Non-Bank Financial Services Authority, which was adopted by the National Assembly on November 24, 2020 at the 5th session of the 6th legislature, and entirely reviewed on its form and legal concepts by the Senate on December 21, 2020 at the 6th plenary session of the 4th legislature, and declared constitutional by the Constitutional Council in its Decision No. 213/013/2020 CC.D of December 30, 2020, and the full text of which is as follows:

LAW

ON

THE ORGANIZATION AND FUNCTIONING OF THE NON-BANK FINANCIAL SERVICES AUTHORITY

# Chapter 1
# General Provisions

**Article 1.-**

The Non-Bank Financial Services Authority, abbreviated as "N.B.F.S.A.", is established with independence in performing its functions by integrating the mechanisms for managing and supervising the non-bank financial sectors, including the insurance and private pension sector, the securities sector, the social security sector, the trust sector, the accounting and auditing sector, and the real estate business, pawn and security by transfer sector, in order to strengthen and ensure the efficiency of the management, supervision, and development of the non-bank financial sectors, including promoting the development and use of financial technology in the non-bank financial sectors in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

**Article 2.-**

This Law applies to all operations in the non-bank financial sectors in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

# Chapter 2
# Mission, Roles, and Functions

**Article 3.-**

The regulatory jurisdiction of the Ministry of Economy and Finance relating to the non-bank financial sectors, as well as the regulatory jurisdiction of the Securities and Exchange Commission of Cambodia and the regulatory jurisdiction of the National Accounting Council, as defined in relevant laws and regulations, are transferred to the responsibility of the N.B.F.S.A.

**Article 4.-**

The N.B.F.S.A. has the following roles and functions:

- To act as the regulator for the insurance and private pension sector, the securities sector, the social security sector, the trust sector, the accounting and auditing sector, and the real estate business, pawn and security by transfer sector, of which the implementation of the roles of the N.B.F.S.A. in each sector shall be defined in Chapter 3, Section 3, Section 4, Section 5, Section 6, Section 7, and Section 8 of this Law;

- To submit proposals on policies and strategic plans for the development of the non-bank financial sectors and non-bank financial technology to the Royal Government;

- To guide, monitor, and follow up the implementation of strategic plans and action plans for implementing policies and development strategies for the non-bank financial sectors;

- To strengthen market confidence, protect investors, and prevent financial crimes in the non-bank financial sectors by initiating investigations, collecting information from all relevant individuals and institutions, and being able to take measures to freeze assets or cash in bank accounts of relevant individuals in case of suspected offenses committed in the non-bank financial sectors, in accordance with the laws in force;

- To contribute to maintaining and strengthening financial stability;

- To promote the development of financial innovation.

# Chapter 3
# Management and Structure

# Section 1
# Board of the Non-Bank Financial Services Authority

**Article 5.-**

The N.B.F.S.A. shall be governed by the Board of the Non-Bank Financial Services Authority, hereinafter referred to as the "Board".

**Article 6.-**

The Board consists of the following composition:

1. Minister of Economy and Finance - Chairperson

2. Representative of the Ministry of Economy and Finance, 1 person - Vice-Chairperson

3. Representative of the Ministry of Economy and Finance, 1 person - Member

4. Representative of the National Bank of Cambodia, 1 person - Member

5. Representative of the Office of the Council of Ministers, 1 person - Member

6. Representative of the Ministry of Commerce, 1 person - Member

7. Representative of the Ministry of Justice, 1 person - Member

8. Secretary General of the General Secretariat of the N.B.F.S.A. - Member

9. Director General of the Insurance Regulator of Cambodia - Member

10. Director General of the Securities Regulator of Cambodia - Member

11. Director General of the Social Security Regulator - Member

12. Director General of the Trust Regulator - Member

13. Director General of the Accounting and Auditing Regulator - Member

14. Director General of the Real Estate Business and Pawn Regulator - Member

15. Prominent individuals in the financial sector, proposed by the Minister of Economy and Finance, 3 persons - Members.

**Article 7.-**

The actual composition of the Board shall be determined by Sub-decree upon the proposal of the Minister of Economy and Finance and Chairperson of the Board. The Vice-Chairperson and members of the Board representing ministries and institutions, as defined in Article 6 above, shall have the rank and position of Secretary General or Deputy Secretary General or Vice-Chairperson of the institution or above.

The Vice-Chairperson and members of the Board shall have a mandate of 5 (five) years. During the performance of their duties, the Vice-Chairperson and members may be removed or replaced. The Vice-Chairperson and members of the Board may be reappointed for only one additional mandate upon the proposal of the Minister of Economy and Finance and Chairperson of the Board upon the expiration of their mandate.

The Board may establish specialized committees as necessary by decision of the Chairperson of the Board.

**Article 8.-**

The Board has the following roles and duties:

- To review and propose policies and development strategies for the non-bank financial sectors, including financial technology for the non-bank financial sectors

- To formulate strategic plans and action plans for implementing policies and development strategies for the non-bank financial sectors

- To review and monitor the implementation of the strategic plans and action plans of the N.B.F.S.A.

- To review and decide on the preparation of Prakas, circulars, and other guidelines to manage the non-bank financial sectors

- To review and decide on the conditions, procedures, and rules for granting, suspending, and revoking licenses and/or certificates, permits, recognitions, and registrations in the non-bank financial sectors

- To promote the implementation of and encourage the compliance with the provisions of this Law

- To oversee the implementation of roles and duties of the subordinate regulators

- To review and approve the budget proposal for the functioning of the N.B.F.S.A. and its subordinate regulators and units of the N.B.F.S.A.

- To review and decide on the internal regulations of the N.B.F.S.A.

**Article 9.-**

The Minister of Economy and Finance and Chairperson of the Board shall regularly report on the work status focusing on policies and strategies, as well as various challenges, including the progress of non-bank financial services work, to the Head of the Royal Government.

**Article 10.-**

The N.B.F.S.A. shall have its logo and seal for performing its duties.

**Article 11.-**

The N.B.F.S.A. has the following subordinate units as secretariats:

- General Secretariat

- Insurance Regulator of Cambodia

- Securities Regulator of Cambodia

- Social Security Regulator

- Trust Regulator

- Accounting and Auditing Regulator

- Real Estate Business and Pawn Regulator

- Internal Audit Unit.

**Article 12.-**

Each Regulator of the N.B.F.S.A. shall perform its regulatory functions in accordance with the laws and relevant regulations in force.

Each Regulator of the N.B.F.S.A. shall have a separate budget and account for the implementation of its roles.

The Social Security Regulator shall use a joint budget package with the General Secretariat of the National Social Protection Council to perform its daily duties.

The General Secretariat and each Regulator of the N.B.F.S.A. shall request reviews and approvals of their annual budgets from the Ministry of Economy and Finance through the Board. The annual budget of the N.B.F.S.A. and its subordinate units is a separate budget package within the framework of the program budget of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

The organization and functioning of the General Secretariat, each Regulator, and the Internal Audit Unit of the N.B.F.S.A. shall be determined by Sub-decree.

**Article 13.-**

The General Secretariat of the N.B.F.S.A. shall be led by 1 (one) Secretary General, assisted by a number of Deputy Secretaries General as necessary.

Each Regulator of the N.B.F.S.A. shall be led by 1 (one) Director General, assisted by a number of Deputy Directors General as necessary.

The Internal Audit Unit of the N.B.F.S.A. shall be led by 1 (one) Head, assisted by a number of Deputy Heads as necessary.

Candidates for Secretary General, Director General, Deputy Secretary General, Deputy Director General, and Head and Deputy Head of the Internal Audit Unit of the N.B.F.S.A. shall be selected and proposed by the Minister of Economy and Finance to the Head of the Royal Government.

## Section 2

## General Secretariat of the N.B.F.S.A.

**Article 14.-**

The General Secretariat shall perform its function as secretariat for the N.B.F.S.A.

## Section 3

## Insurance Regulator of Cambodia

**Article 15.-**

The Insurance Regulator of Cambodia shall exercise the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Economy and Finance as defined in the Law on Insurance promulgated by Royal Decree No. NS/RKM/0814/021 dated August 4, 2014, and other relevant regulations in the insurance sector.

## Section 4

## Securities Regulator of Cambodia

**Article 16.-**

The Securities and Exchange Commission of Cambodia shall be converted into the Securities Regulator of Cambodia in accordance with the provisions of this Law. The Securities Regulator of Cambodia shall perform the functions of the Securities and Exchange Commission of Cambodia as defined in the Law on Public Securities promulgated by Royal Decree No. NS/RKM/1007/028 dated October 19, 2007, and other relevant regulations in the securities sector.

**Article 17.-**

The Director General of the Securities Regulator of Cambodia shall perform the functions of the Director General of the Securities and Exchange Commission of Cambodia as defined in the Law on Public Securities promulgated by Royal Decree No. NS/RKM/1007/028 dated October 19, 2007, and other relevant regulations in the securities sector.

## Section 5

## Social Security Regulator

**Article 18.-**

The General Secretariat of the National Social Protection Council shall perform the additional role as the Social Security Regulator in accordance with the provisions of this Law. The Social Security Regulator shall implement the roles and duties as defined in the Law on Social Security promulgated by Royal Decree No. NS/RKM/1119/018 dated November 2, 2019, and other relevant regulations in the social security sector.

**Article 19.-**

The Secretary General of the National Social Protection Council shall perform the additional roles and duties as the Director General of the Social Security Regulator.

## Section 6

## Trust Regulator

**Article 20.-**

The Trust Regulator shall exercise the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Economy and Finance as defined in the Law on Trusts promulgated by Royal Decree No. NS/RKM/0119/002 dated January 2, 2019, and other relevant regulations in the trust sector.

## Section 7

## Accounting and Auditing Regulator

**Article 21.-**

The National Accounting Council shall be converted into the Accounting and Auditing Regulator in accordance with the provisions of this Law. The Accounting and Auditing Regulator shall perform the functions and duties of the National Accounting Council as defined in the Law on Accounting and Auditing promulgated by Royal Decree No. NS/RKM/0416/006 dated April 11, 2016, and other relevant regulations in the accounting and auditing sector.

## Section 8

## Real Estate Business and Pawn Regulator

**Article 22.-**

The Real Estate Business and Pawn Regulator shall implement the roles and duties as defined in Article 5 of the Law on Financial Management for the Year 2007 promulgated by Royal Decree No. NS/RKM/1206/039 dated December 29, 2006, and other relevant regulations in the real estate, pawn, and security by transfer sectors.

# Chapter 4

# Penalty Provisions

**Article 23.-**

All regulations that have been and are being implemented relating to the insurance and private pension sector, the securities sector, the social security sector, the trust sector, the accounting and auditing sector, the real estate sector, the pawn sector, and the security by transfer sector, shall continue to be in force until there are new regulations to replace them in accordance with this Law.

**Article 24.-**

In order to ensure the continuity of work relating to the jurisdiction of the Non-Bank Financial Services Authority, the Ministry of Economy and Finance, and the National Accounting Council shall perform functions, roles, duties, and jurisdiction as defined in relevant laws and regulations in force, until each Regulator of the N.B.F.S.A. begins performing its roles and duties in accordance with the sectoral regulations and in compliance with this Law.

The Securities and Exchange Commission of Cambodia shall continue its normal operations until there is an organization of the Board composition upon the proposal of the Chairperson of the Board.

# Chapter 5

# Final Provisions

**Article 25.-**

Any provisions contrary to this Law shall be null and void.
