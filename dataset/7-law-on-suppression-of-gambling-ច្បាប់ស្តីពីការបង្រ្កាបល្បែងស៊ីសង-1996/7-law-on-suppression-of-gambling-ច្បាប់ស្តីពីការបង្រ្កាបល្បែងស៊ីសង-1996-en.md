### Promulgate

The Law on the Suppression of Gambling, which was adopted by the National Assembly during the 5th session of its 1st legislature, the text of which reads as follows:

# Law

## On the

## Suppression of Gambling

### Chapter 1

#### General Provisions

**Article 1:** The playing of all kinds of gambling shall be strictly prohibited in all places throughout the Kingdom of Cambodia, except for those permitted by the Royal Government.

**Article 2:** Any authorization to open gambling games other than that permitted by the Royal Government shall be considered null and void.

### Chapter 2

#### Competent Institution

**Article 3:** The suppression and prevention of all kinds of gambling games is the competence of the Ministry of Interior.

# Chapter 3

## Penal Provisions

### Article 4

Any person who gambles shall be punished by a fine of ten thousand Riels (10,000 Riels) to fifty thousand Riels (50,000 Riels) or imprisonment of one week to one month.

In case of recidivism, the offender shall be punished by a fine of fifty thousand Riels (50,000 Riels) to five hundred thousand Riels (500,000 Riels) and imprisonment of one month to one year.

### Article 5

Shall be punished by a fine of five million Riels (5,000,000 Riels) to twenty million Riels (20,000,000 Riels) and imprisonment of one year to five years for anyone who opens a gambling place, manages/possesses gambling, or organizes and acts as a gambling agent.

In case of recidivism, the above fine and imprisonment shall be applied to the maximum.

### Article 6

Whenever there is one or more minors in the gambling circle, the penalties stipulated in Article 4 and Article 5 of this Law shall be applied to the maximum.

### Article 7

In any case, money placed in the gambling circle, money found on the person of the gambling organizer, on the person of the gambling boss, on the person of the gambling agent, on the person of the gambling helper, on the person of the person in charge of accounting, on the person of the guard/clerk, and on the person of the gambler, shall be confiscated by the competent agents, who shall make a proper report and submit it to the court.

The evidence (materials) used for serving the gambling shall also be confiscated as mentioned above.

### Article 8

The production, import for business purposes, or sale of gambling equipment of all kinds without permission from the Royal Government shall be punished by a fine of five hundred thousand Riels (500,000 Riels) to five million Riels (5,000,000 Riels) and imprisonment of one month to one year.

The above-mentioned equipment as evidence shall be confiscated. In case of recidivism, the maximum of the above-mentioned fine and imprisonment shall be imposed.

**Article 9:**
The money and evidence confiscated as stated in Article 7 and Article 8 of this Law must be forwarded to the court for a decision of confiscation as state property or for destruction.

As for the rewards for competent agents, units, and individuals who participated in the operation, they shall be determined by sub-decree.

**Article 10:**
Apart from the Royal Government, no authority whatsoever has the right to issue a permit/license to open gambling games anywhere whatsoever.

Any official or government agent who still issues a permit or allows gambling games shall be punished with imprisonment from one year to five years.

**Article 11:**
Any person who acts to protect/defend gambling rings or gambling dens of all kinds anywhere whatsoever shall be punished with imprisonment from one year to five years.

### Chapter 4
#### Final Provisions

**Article 12:**
Any provisions that are contrary to this Law shall be abrogated.

**Article 13:**
This Law shall be promulgated as urgent.
