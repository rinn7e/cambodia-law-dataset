# Law on Civil Registration, Vital Statistics and Identification

# Chapter 1 General Provisions

### Article 1.- Purpose

This law defines rules and procedures related to civil registration, residence registration, vital statistics collection, personal identification registration, and the organization and management of the population register for the purpose of:

a- Ensuring that civil registration is mandatory, universal, continuous, and permanent.

b- Ensuring that personal identification registration is universal, continuous, and permanent.

c- Ensuring that residence registration is mandatory, universal, continuous, and permanent.

d- Ensuring that everyone has a legal identity through civil registration and identity registration by providing each individual with a personal identification number.

e- Ensuring respect for human rights, access to all types of services, and providing a basis for development planning and monitoring of social progress.

### Article 2.- Scope

This law applies to all persons living in the territory of the Kingdom of Cambodia and Cambodian citizens living abroad.

### Article 3.- Definitions

Key terms used in this law are defined in the Glossary annexed to this law.

# Chapter 2 Civil Registration

## Section 1 General Rules

### Article 4.- Vital Events Subject to Registration

Vital events that are required to be registered include: live birth, death, and marriage.

2- Besides the vital events defined in paragraph 1 above, other vital events shall also be registered, including: child recognition, certification of paternity, certification of maternity, adoption, termination of adoption, divorce, annulment of marriage, and other vital events that the law and other legal norms require to be registered in accordance with the provisions of this law.

## Section 2 Civil Registers

### Article 5.- Types of Civil Registers

Civil registers include the live birth register, death register, and marriage register.

### Article 6.- Form of Civil Registers

1- Civil registers may be kept in paper and/or electronic format.

2- The form and information contained in the civil register and the forms used for notification, reporting, and registration of vital events shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior, unless separate provisions are stated in this law.

## Section 3 Civil Registration Officers

### Article 7.- National Civil Registration Officer

1- The Director General of the General Department of Identification of the Ministry of Interior is the National Civil Registration Officer.

2- In the case that the Director General of the General Department of Identification of the Ministry of Interior is absent, a Deputy Director General who receives authority as Acting Director General has the power and responsibility as the National Civil Registration Officer.

### Article 8.- Capital/Provincial Civil Registration Officers

1- The Governor of the Board of Governors of the Capital/Province is the Capital/Provincial Civil Registration Officer within his/her jurisdiction.

2- In the case that the Governor of the Board of Governors of the Capital/Province is absent, a Deputy Governor of the Capital/Province who receives authority as Acting Governor of the Board of Governors of the Capital/Province has the power and responsibility as the Capital/Provincial Civil Registration Officer and shall officially report to the National Civil Registration Officer.

### Article 9.- Municipality/District/Khan Civil Registration Officers

1- The Governor of the Board of Governors of the Municipality/District/Khan is the Municipality/District/Khan Civil Registration Officer within his/her jurisdiction.

2- In the case that the Governor of the Board of Governors of the Municipality/District/Khan is absent, a Deputy Governor of the Municipality/District/Khan who receives authority as Acting Governor of the Board of Governors of the Municipality/District/Khan has the power and responsibility as the Municipality/District/Khan Civil Registration Officer and shall officially report to the Capital/Provincial Civil Registration Officer.

### Article 10.- Commune/Sangkat Civil Registration Officers

1- The Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief is the Commune/Sangkat Civil Registration Officer within his/her jurisdiction.

2- In the case that the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief is absent, the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief shall delegate authority in writing to a subordinate assistant of the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief according to the hierarchy and shall officially report to the Municipality/District/Khan Civil Registration Officer.

3- In the case that the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief is absent due to resignation or termination of position during a transitional period where a new Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief has not yet been appointed, the Acting Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief is the Commune/Sangkat Civil Registration Officer.

### Article 11.- Civil Registration Officers at Representative Missions of the Kingdom of Cambodia

The Minister of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation shall appoint 1 (one) officer as the Civil Registration Officer at each representative mission of the Kingdom of Cambodia abroad and shall notify the Minister of Interior about this appointment.

## Section 4 Competence of Civil Registration Officers

### Article 12.- Competence of the National Civil Registration Officer

The National Civil Registration Officer has the following competence:

a- Managing and preserving all types of civil registers that have been implemented.

b- Ensuring the implementation of provisions and other legal norms on civil registration that are under this law.

c- Issuing principles, instructions, and forms and procedures in accordance with the determination in the provisions of this law.

d- Supervising and instructing civil registration officers at all levels, including civil registration officers at representative missions of the Kingdom of Cambodia abroad, on the implementation of civil registration work.

e- Developing plans and providing training to civil registration officers.

f- Encouraging Capital/Provincial Civil Registration Officers and Civil Registration Officers at representative missions of the Kingdom of Cambodia abroad to provide regular and accurate civil registration reports as determined.

g- Reporting all cases of violation of legal provisions committed by civil registration officers to the Minister of Interior for legal action.

h- Reviewing, deciding, and authorizing the correction of civil registration data or authorizing the re-registration of civil status in accordance with the provisions of this law.

i- Reviewing and deciding on civil registration in cases where civil registration officers at other levels cannot make a decision.

j- Reviewing and deciding on complaints against administrative actions, including decisions or delays or negligence in administrative actions by civil registration officers at all levels and civil registration officers at representative missions of the Kingdom of Cambodia regarding civil registration.

k- Issuing copies of birth, death, and marriage certificates in cases where there is a request.

l- In cases of necessity, requesting principles from the Minister of Interior to establish civil registration offices at health facilities or other places where vital events may occur, by arranging for civil registration staff or delegating the rights and duties of civil registration officers to health officials.

m- Fulfilling other necessary duties as determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 13.- Competence of Capital/Provincial Civil Registration Officers

Capital/Provincial Civil Registration Officers have the following competence:

a- Issuing copies of birth, death, and marriage certificates upon request.

b- Correcting errors in the civil register that are within the competence of civil registration officers, based on the provisions of this law or other laws or legal norms.

c- Monitoring, supervising, and supporting the work of Municipality/District/Khan Civil Registration Officers to ensure the implementation of provisions and other legal norms on civil registration that are under this law.

d- Developing plans and providing training to civil registration officers within its jurisdiction.

e- Encouraging civil registration officers within its jurisdiction to provide regular and accurate civil registration reports as determined.

f- Reviewing and deciding on civil registration in cases where Municipality/District/Khan Civil Registration Officers cannot make a decision.

g- Reviewing and deciding on complaints against administrative actions, including decisions or delays or negligence in administrative actions by Municipality/District/Khan Civil Registration Officers regarding civil registration.

h- Fulfilling other necessary duties as determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 14.- Competence of Municipality/District/Khan Civil Registration Officers

Municipality/District/Khan Civil Registration Officers have the following competence:

a- Issuing copies of birth, death, and marriage certificates upon request.

b- Correcting errors in the civil register that are within the competence of civil registration officers, based on the provisions of this law or other laws or legal norms.

c- Monitoring, supervising, and supporting the work of Commune/Sangkat Civil Registration Officers to ensure the implementation of provisions and other legal norms on civil registration that are under this law.

d- Supporting and strengthening the capacity of civil registration officers within its jurisdiction.

e- Encouraging civil registration officers within its jurisdiction to provide regular and accurate civil registration reports as determined.

f- Reviewing and deciding on civil registration in cases where Commune/Sangkat Civil Registration Officers cannot make a decision.

g- Reviewing and deciding on complaints against administrative actions, including decisions or delays or negligence in administrative actions by Commune/Sangkat Civil Registration Officers regarding civil registration.

h- Fulfilling other necessary duties as determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 15.- Competence of Commune/Sangkat Civil Registration Officers

Commune/Sangkat Civil Registration Officers have the following competence:

a- Reviewing and recording necessary information regarding live birth, death, and marriage.

b- Issuing original birth, death, and marriage certificates and copies.

c- Correcting errors in the civil register that are within the competence of civil registration officers based on the provisions of this law or other laws or legal norms.

d- Providing authorization for cremation or burial.

e- Recording, correcting, or annulling civil status registers according to final court decisions and/or according to decisions of higher-level civil registration officers that have implementation effect.

f- Providing regular and accurate civil registration reports as determined.

g- Disseminating information to citizens about their rights and duties regarding civil registration and creating favorable conditions for citizens in civil registration.

h- Collaborating with local authorities, health officials, education officials, social affairs officials, and officials of other relevant ministries and institutions regarding civil registration work in cases of necessity.

i- Conducting mobile civil registration within its jurisdiction to achieve complete civil registration.

j- Fulfilling other necessary duties as determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 16.- Competence of Civil Registration Officers at Representative Missions of the Kingdom of Cambodia

Civil Registration Officers at representative missions of the Kingdom of Cambodia have the following competence:

a- Reviewing and recording necessary information regarding live birth, death, and marriage.

b- Issuing original birth, death, and marriage certificates and copies.

c- Correcting errors in the civil register that are within the competence of civil registration officers, based on the provisions of this law or other laws or legal norms.

d- Providing regular and accurate civil registration reports as determined.

e- Fulfilling other necessary duties as determined by Sub-decree.

## Section 5 Live Birth Registration

### Article 17.- Live Birth Registration

Every live birth occurring in the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be reported and registered in accordance with the provisions of this law.

### Article 18.- Place of Live Birth Registration

The reporter shall apply for live birth registration at any civil registration office of:

a- The Commune/Sangkat where the live birth occurred.

b- The Commune/Sangkat where the reporter resides.

c- Other places as determined by legal norms of the Ministry of Interior.

### Article 19.- Time Period for Live Birth Registration

1- The reporter shall apply for live birth registration within 30 (thirty) days from the date the infant was born.

2- The Ministry of Interior may extend the defined period for applying for live birth registration for specific geographical areas or indigenous ethnic groups or for other reasons in necessary cases, by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

### Article 20.- Reporter of Live Birth

1- The person responsible as the live birth reporter is determined according to the following priorities:

a- The mother of the infant.

b- The father of the infant.

c- The general guardian of the infant's parents. (Note: Transcribed as "parents of the infant", but logically refers to the infant's guardian).

d- Relatives of the person caring for the infant.

e- Minister of a Ministry or head of an institution or representative or other person as determined by this law or other laws.

2- In necessary cases, reporters other than the persons defined above shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 21.- Notification of Live Births Occurring in Health Facilities

1- The head or the person assigned the duty of the health facility where a live birth occurs shall provide a document certifying the live birth to the reporter according to the priorities defined in Article 20 (Reporter of Live Birth) of this law.

2- If an infant is born outside a health facility and the mother and infant travel to a health facility within 48 (forty-eight) hours, the head or the person assigned the duty of the health facility shall provide a document certifying the live birth to the reporter according to the priorities defined in Article 20 (Reporter of Live Birth) of this law.

3- In the cases defined in paragraph 1 and paragraph 2 above, the head or the person assigned the duty of the health facility shall send a copy of that live birth certificate to the competent civil registration officer.

4- The forms and procedures for providing live birth certificates to the competent civil registration officer shall be determined by an Inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of Interior and the Minister of Health.

### Article 22.- Notification of Live Births Occurring Outside Health Facilities

If a live birth occurs outside a health facility and the mother and infant do not travel to a health facility within 48 (forty-eight) hours, the person or institution aware of this live birth event may be required to provide information or documents as defined in the Inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of Interior and the Minister of Health.

### Article 23.- Application for Live Birth Registration

1- The reporter may apply for live birth registration in writing, orally, or in electronic form.

2- In the application for live birth registration, the reporter shall fill in the following points:

a- The name of the infant.

b- The gender of the infant.

c- Date of birth, time, and place of the infant's live birth.

d- Name, date of birth, personal identification number, nationality, and residence of the infant's parents.

e- Name, date of birth, personal identification number, and current address of the reporter, in cases where the reporter is not the mother or father.

f- Date, month, year when the application was made.

g- Other points as determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

3- The form and layout of the application shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

### Article 24.- Documents Attached to the Application for Live Birth Registration

1- The reporter shall attach the live birth certificate defined in paragraph 1 or paragraph 2 of Article 21 (Notification of Live Births Occurring in Health Facilities) of this law to the application for live birth registration. If the reporter does not have that live birth certificate, the reporter shall attach other documents that can certify the infant's live birth.

2- The reporter shall also attach documents that can prove the identity of the infant's parents to the application for live birth registration.

3- Other documents that can certify the infant's live birth as defined in the second phrase of paragraph 1 and documents that can prove the identity of the infant's parents as defined in paragraph 2 above shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

### Article 25.- Live Birth Registration Procedure

1- Upon receiving the application for live birth registration and attached documents defined in Article 23 (Application for Live Birth Registration) and Article 24 (Documents Attached to the Application for Live Birth Registration) of this law, the civil registration officer shall examine, verify, and record that information in the live birth register.

2- Minimum information for live birth registration includes: name, gender, date of birth, and place of birth of the born person.

3- If the civil registration officer examines and finds that the application for live birth registration lacks any information among the minimum information defined in paragraph 2 above, the officer...

...shall demand the reporter to provide the missing information within a reasonable period. In case the reporter does not provide the missing information within the reasonable period determined by the civil registration officer, the civil registration officer may dismiss that application for live birth registration.

4- The civil registration officer shall not reject an application for live birth registration due to lack of information related to the parents.

5- If the civil registration officer examines and finds that the application for live birth registration lacks some information among the points defined in paragraph 2 of Article 23 (Application for Live Birth Registration), other than the minimum information defined in paragraph 2 above, or lacks attached documents defined in Article 24 (Documents Attached to the Application for Live Birth Registration) of this law, the civil registration officer shall register the live birth according to the available information. In this case, the civil registration officer shall demand the reporter to provide additional information within a reasonable period.

6- Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph 4 above, the civil registration officer may register by discretion with authorization from a higher-level civil registration officer, if the civil registration officer has received the minimum information defined in paragraph 2 above through notification from relevant competent institutions or other methods.

### Article 26.- Provision of Personal Identification Number

1- After the civil registration officer has received and recorded information regarding a live birth, the civil registration officer shall request the National Center for Population Identification Management of the National Authority for Population Identification Management as stated in Chapter 6 (Integrated Population Identification System) of this law to provide a personal identification number to the born person.

2- The provision of a personal identification number shall be implemented according to Chapter 6 (Integrated Population Identification System) of this law.

3- After receiving the personal identification number, the civil registration officer shall provide the personal identification number of the born person to the reporter.

### Article 27.- Birth Certificate

1- After the live birth registration is completed, the competent civil registration officer shall issue 1 (one) original birth certificate to the reporter free of charge.

2- Information on the original birth certificate includes:

a- Name of the born person.

b- Gender of the born person.

c- Name of the father and/or mother of the born person.

d- Place of birth and date of birth of the born person.

e- Other information as necessary as determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

### Article 28.- Copy of Birth Certificate and Extract of Birth Register

1- Persons who can request a copy of a birth certificate include:

a- The holder of the birth certificate.

b- Direct relatives and spouse of the holder of the birth certificate.

c- A legal representative or a representative by mandate of the persons above as stated in points a and b.

d- Other persons with legal interest as determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

2- If the civil registration officer finds that the person requesting a copy of the birth certificate is not a person defined in paragraph 1 above or the request is made for improper purposes such as the purpose of affecting the privacy of the holder of the birth certificate, the civil registration officer shall reject that request and provide reasons for the rejection. This rejection may be appealed in accordance with the provisions of Section 13 (Appeals) of this Chapter.

3- Competent authorities or courts may request in writing a copy of a birth certificate or an extract of the birth register in necessary cases to fulfill their duties based on the law or other legal norms. In cases of such requests, the competent civil registration officer shall provide the requested documents promptly and free of charge.

### Article 29.- Form of Birth Certificate and Copy of Birth Certificate

1- Birth certificates and copies of birth certificates shall have security features to prevent forgery.

2- The form of birth certificates and copies of birth certificates shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

### Article 30.- Live Birth Registration for Abandoned Infants

1- If any person finds an infant in a place where he/she has been abandoned, that person shall provide information about that event to the Commune/Sangkat civil registration officer of the Commune/Sangkat where the infant was found without delay.

2- The person who finds the infant in that place shall provide:

a- The date and place where he/she found the infant.

b- The gender of the infant.

c- An estimate of the date of birth of the infant, if possible.

d- The name and residence of the person who found the infant.

3- After receiving the information in paragraph 2 above, the civil registration officer shall record the information and register the infant's live birth, assigning a name to the infant, even if no reporter has filed an application. In case of necessity, if an estimate of the infant's date of birth cannot be made, the civil registration officer shall consult with a health official to determine the date of birth of the found infant. If the place of birth of the infant is not clear, the place where the infant was found shall be presumed to be the infant's place of birth.

4- After the infant has been registered for birth, the Commune/Sangkat civil registration officer of the place where the infant was found shall send the infant to a public center for child welfare along with the birth certificate for further action according to legal procedures.

5- The guardian for the minor in respect of the found infant may apply to the competent civil registration officer to correct or supplement the infant's information in the infant's live birth register. The provisions of Section 10 (Correction of Civil Registers) of this Chapter shall not apply to this case.

6- An infant found in the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be presumed to have Khmer nationality by birth.

### Article 31.- Annullment of Live Birth Register of Abandoned Infants

In the case that an abandoned infant has been registered for live birth in accordance with Article 30 (Live Birth Registration for Abandoned Infants) of this law, but later if the civil registration officer finds that the infant has already been registered for live birth before, the new birth register created in accordance with this law shall be nullified, and the civil registration officer shall confiscate the new birth certificate and copies of the new birth certificate that were issued.

### Article 32.- Live Birth Registration for Children in Public Centers for Child Welfare

1- Live birth registration for children in public centers for child welfare shall be implemented according to Article 23 (Application for Live Birth Registration), Article 24 (Documents Attached to the Application for Live Birth Registration), and Article 25 (Live Birth Registration Procedure) of this law.

2- The head of a public center for child welfare shall ensure that all children living in that public center for child welfare are registered for birth. The head of the public center for child welfare shall fulfill his/her duties as a reporter by implementing the provisions of Article 20 (Reporter of Live Birth) of this law.

### Article 33.- Annullment of Live Birth Registration of Children in Public Centers for Child Welfare

In the case that a child in a public welfare center has already been registered for birth in accordance with Article 32 (Live Birth Registration for Children in Public Centers for Child Welfare) of this law, but later if the civil registration officer finds that the child has already been registered for birth before, the new birth register created in accordance with this law shall be nullified, and the civil registration officer shall confiscate the new birth certificate and copies of the new birth certificate that were issued.

### Article 34.- Live Birth Registration for Infants Born to Women Detained in Prisons or Youth Rehabilitation Centers

The head of a prison or the head of a youth rehabilitation center shall ensure that all infants born to women detained in prison or center for youth rehabilitation are registered for birth. The head of the prison or the head of the youth rehabilitation center shall have the role of reporter.

### Article 35.- Live Birth Registration for Infants Born in Moving Vehicles

1- If any woman traveling to the Kingdom of Cambodia or within the Kingdom of Cambodia by train, ship, aircraft, or other public vehicle gives birth in that vehicle, the Commune/Sangkat where the train, ship, aircraft, or vehicle stops and the mother disembarks after giving birth shall be considered the place where the live birth event occurred.

2- If any woman having Khmer nationality and traveling from the Kingdom of Cambodia by train, ship, aircraft, or other public vehicle gives birth in that vehicle, the place where the train, ship, aircraft, or vehicle stops and the mother disembarks after giving birth shall be considered the place where the live birth event occurred.

3- In the cases defined in paragraph 1 and paragraph 2 above, the reporter shall register the live birth at any place as defined in Article 18 (Place of Live Birth Registration) of this law.

### Article 36.- Reporting of Live Births of Infants of Khmer Citizens Born Outside the Kingdom of Cambodia

1- If a Khmer citizen gives birth to an infant outside the Kingdom of Cambodia, the reporter as defined in Article 20 (Reporter of Live Birth) of this law may apply for live birth registration with the competent civil registration officer at the representative mission of the Kingdom of Cambodia in the country where the infant was born, providing all attached documents as follows:

a- Evidence certifying the live birth or a birth certificate from the country where the infant was born.

b- Other attached documents as determined by legal norms of the Ministry of Interior.

2- If there is no representative mission or it is not possible to file an application for live birth registration or there is no possibility to register the live birth with the civil registration officer at the representative mission of the Kingdom of Cambodia in that country, the reporter may apply for live birth registration with the competent civil registration officer after returning to the Kingdom of Cambodia, providing all attached documents as follows:

a- Evidence certifying the live birth or a birth certificate from the country where the infant was born.

b- Other attached documents as determined by legal norms of the Ministry of Interior.

3- In the cases defined in paragraph 1 and paragraph 2 above, the civil registration officer shall record the live birth event and issue a birth certificate in accordance with Article 25 (Live Birth Registration Procedure) and Article 27 (Birth Certificate) of this law.

### Article 37.- Live Birth Registration for Infants Born to Foreign Parents

Live births of infants born to foreign parents in the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be registered in accordance with the provisions of Section 5 (Live Birth Registration) of this Chapter.

### Article 38.- Live Birth Registration During the One-Year Grace Period

1- If the reporter has not applied for live birth registration within 30 (thirty) days from the date of the infant's birth or another period as defined in paragraph 2 of Article 19 (Time Period for Live Birth Registration) of this law, the reporter shall apply for live birth registration during a grace period of 1 (one) year from the date the infant was born. The reporter shall also specify the reason for failing to apply for registration within 30 (thirty) days or another period as defined in Article 19 (Time Period for Live Birth Registration) of this law after the infant's birth.

2- Upon receiving the application for registration as defined in paragraph 1 above, the civil registration officer shall perform live birth registration in accordance with Article 25 (Live Birth Registration Procedure) of this law. The civil registration officer shall not reject an application for live birth registration based on the reasons for explaining the delay in applying for registration.

### Article 39.- Live Birth Registration After the One-Year Grace Period

1- If the reporter has not applied for live birth registration after the one-year grace period, in addition to the attached documents stated in Article 24 (Documents Attached to the Application for Live Birth Registration) of this law, the reporter shall provide any additional information or documents as follows:

a- Assurance before the civil registration officer of witnesses to this live birth.

b- Child health card.

c- Other documents or information certifying the live birth event as determined by legal norms of the Ministry of Interior.

2- Upon receiving information and documents as stated in paragraph 1 above, the civil registration officer shall perform that live birth registration. The civil registration officer shall not reject an application for live birth registration because the grace period has expired.

### Article 40.- Reminders and Mobile Live Birth Registration in Localities

1- When the civil registration officer becomes aware that a live birth event has occurred through notification from relevant authorities or other sources, and the civil registration officer knows that the reporter has not yet applied for live birth registration, the civil registration officer shall remind the reporter to apply for registration, setting a reasonable period.

2- The Commune/Sangkat civil registration officer may move within his/her jurisdiction to register live births that have not yet been registered, in accordance with the provisions of Section 5 (Live Birth Registration) of this Chapter.

## Section 6 Death Registration

### Article 41.- Death Registration

Every death occurring in the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be reported and registered in accordance with the provisions of this law.

### Article 42.- Place of Death Registration

The reporter shall apply for death registration at any civil registration office of:

a- The Commune/Sangkat where the death event occurred.

b- The Commune/Sangkat where the deceased resided.

c- Other places as determined by legal norms of the Ministry of Interior.

### Article 43.- Time Period for Death Registration

The reporter shall apply for death registration within 15 (fifteen) days from the date the death event occurred.

### Article 44.- Reporter of Death

1- The person responsible as the death reporter is determined according to the following priorities:

a- For the death of a minor:
- Mother or father or guardian for the minor
- Closest relatives of the deceased
- Minister of a Ministry, head of an institution, or representative, or other person as determined by this law or other laws.

b- For the death of an adult:
- Spouse of the deceased
- Closest relatives of the deceased
- Minister of a Ministry, head of an institution, or representative, or other person as determined by this law or other laws.

c- For deaths due to abnormal causes or in cases of natural disasters or other emergencies, it shall be reported by the person or relevant competent authorities as determined by the provisions of this law and legal norms following this law.

2- If the reporter is a minor or a person under general guardianship, the forms and procedures shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

### Article 45.- Notification of Deaths Occurring in Health Facilities

1- The head or the person assigned the duty of the health facility where a death occurs shall provide one copy of the death certificate to the reporter according to the priorities defined in Article 44 (Reporter of Death) of this law.

2- The head or the person assigned the duty of the health facility shall provide a death certificate to the reporter if the death of that person occurred before reaching the health facility and they traveled to reach the health facility.

3- In the cases defined in paragraph 1 and paragraph 2 above, the head or the person assigned the duty of the health facility shall send a copy of that death certificate to the competent civil registration officer.

4- The forms and procedures for providing death certificates to the competent civil registration officer shall be determined by an Inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of Interior and the Minister of Health.

### Article 46.- Notification of Deaths Occurring Outside Health Facilities

1- If a death occurs outside a health facility, the reporter shall report the death to the competent authorities. The competent authorities shall cooperate with a health facility to perform an examination so that the health facility can certify the death and provide a death certificate to the reporter.

2- The health facility shall send 1 (one) copy of the death certificate to the competent civil registration officer.

3- The forms and procedures for providing death certificates to the competent civil registration officer shall be determined by an Inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of Interior and the Minister of Health.

### Article 47.- Notification of Deaths Caused by Natural Disasters or Large-Scale Emergencies

1- In case Article 45 (Notification of Deaths Occurring in Health Facilities) and Article 46 (Notification of Deaths Occurring Outside Health Facilities) of this law cannot be applied to deaths leaving corpses caused by natural disasters or large-scale emergencies, the procedures for notifying the competent civil registration officer about deaths occurring due to natural disasters or large-scale emergencies shall be determined by Sub-decree.

2- Natural disasters or large-scale emergencies as defined in paragraph 1 above shall be declared by the Royal Government.

### Article 48.- Medical Certificate of Cause of Death in Cases of Normal Death

1- When a death of any person occurs in a health facility or while that person is traveling to a health facility, the head or the person assigned the duty of the health facility shall determine the cause of death, if possible.

2- When a death of any person occurs outside a health facility, the head or the person assigned the duty of the health facility shall determine the cause of death, if possible.

3- The forms and procedures for determining the cause of death shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Health.

4- The head or the person assigned the duty of the health facility shall ensure the transmission of information regarding the cause of death to the competent civil registration officer. The transmission procedure shall be determined by an Inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of Interior and the Minister of Health.

### Article 49.- Medical Certificate of Cause of Death in Cases of Abnormal or Suspicious Death

1- The head of a health facility, medical practitioners, civil registration officers, or relevant persons who suspect that any death is caused by abnormal causes shall report this case to the competent authorities for investigation.

2- After completing the investigation regarding the cause of death, the competent authorities shall determine the cause of death according to the form determined by the Ministry of Health.

3- The competent authorities defined in paragraph 2 above shall ensure that the information regarding the cause of death is transmitted to the competent civil registration officer.

4- The procedures for transmitting information as defined in paragraph 3 above shall be determined by an Inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of Interior and the Minister of Health.

### Article 50.- Medical Certificate of Cause of Death in case of Natural Disasters or Large-Scale Emergencies

In case Article 48 (Medical Certificate of Cause of Death in Cases of Normal Death) and Article 49 (Medical Certificate of Cause of Death in Cases of Abnormal or Suspicious Death) of this law cannot be applied due to deaths caused by natural disasters or large-scale emergencies, the procedure for providing a medical certificate of the cause of death shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 51.- Determination of Cause of Death by Oral Inquiry

1- In case the cause of death cannot be determined according to the provisions of Article 48 (Medical Certificate of Cause of Death in Cases of Normal Death), the cause of death may be determined by oral inquiry procedures.

2- The oral inquiry procedures shall be determined by an Inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of Interior and the Minister of Health.

### Article 52.- Application for Death Registration

1- The reporter may apply for death registration in writing, orally, or in electronic form.

2- In the application for death registration, the reporter shall fill in the following points:

a- Name of the deceased.

b- Gender of the deceased.

c- Date of birth of the deceased.

d- Date, month, year, time, and place where the deceased died.

e- Name, date of birth, and current address of the reporter, and the relationship between the reporter and the deceased.

f- Date, month, year when the application was made.

g- Other points as determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

3- The form and layout of the application shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

### Article 53.- Documents Attached to the Application for Death Registration

1- The reporter shall attach the death certificate defined in paragraph 1 or paragraph 2 of Article 45 (Notification of Deaths Occurring in Health Facilities) or Article 46 (Notification of Deaths Occurring Outside Health Facilities) or Article 47 (Notification of Deaths Caused by Natural Disasters or Large-Scale Emergencies) of this law to the application for death registration.

2- The reporter shall also attach documents that can prove the personal identity of the deceased to the application for death registration.

### Article 54.- Death Registration Procedure

1- Upon receiving the application for death registration and attached documents defined in Article 52 (Application for Death Registration) and Article 53 (Documents Attached to the Application for Death Registration) of this law, the civil registration officer shall examine, verify, and record that information in the death register.

2- Minimum information for death registration includes the name, gender, date, and place where the deceased died.

3- If the civil registration officer examines and finds that the application for death registration lacks any information among the minimum information defined in paragraph 2 above, the civil registration officer shall demand the reporter to provide the missing information, setting a reasonable period.

4- The competent civil registration officer shall not reject death registration because of the lack of a medical certificate of the cause of death.

5- If the civil registration officer examines and finds that the application for death registration lacks some information among the points defined in paragraph 2 of Article 52 (Application for Death Registration) of this law, other than the minimum information defined in paragraph 2 above, or lacks attached documents defined in Article 53 (Documents Attached to the Application for Death Registration) of this law, the civil registration officer shall register the death according to the available information. In this case, the civil registration officer shall demand the reporter to provide additional information, setting a reasonable period.

6- Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph 3 above, the civil registration officer may register by discretion with authorization from a higher-level civil registration officer, if the civil registration officer has received the minimum information defined in paragraph 2 above through notification from relevant competent institutions or other methods.

### Article 55.- Death Certificate

1- After the death registration is completed, the competent civil registration officer shall issue 1 (one) original death certificate to the reporter free of charge.

2- Information on the original death certificate includes:

a- Name of the deceased.

b- Date of birth of the deceased.

c- Gender of the deceased.

d- Place and date of the deceased's death.

e- Other information as necessary as determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

### Article 56.- Copy of Death Certificate and Extract of Death Register

1- Persons who can request a copy of a death certificate and an extract of the death register:

a- Relatives of the deceased.

b- A legal representative or a representative by mandate of the relatives of the deceased.

c- Other persons with legal interest as determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

2- If the civil registration officer finds that the person requesting a copy of the death certificate and an extract of the death register is not a person defined in paragraph 1 above or the request is made for improper purposes such as the purpose of affecting the interests of the relatives of the deceased, the civil registration officer shall reject that request and provide reasons for the rejection. This rejection may be appealed in accordance with the provisions of Section 13 (Appeals) of this Chapter.

3- Competent authorities or courts may request in writing a copy of a death certificate or an extract of the death register in necessary cases to fulfill their duties based on the law or other legal norms. In cases of such requests, the competent civil registration officer shall provide the requested documents promptly and free of charge.

### Article 57.- Form of Death Certificate and Copy of Death Certificate

1- Death certificates and copies of death certificates shall have security features to prevent forgery.

2- The form of death certificates and copies of death certificates shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

### Article 58.- Closing the Administrative Identity of the Deceased

1- After death registration, the national civil registration officer shall ensure verification between the death register and other civil registers of the deceased to record in those other civil registers that the person has died. After recording in the other civil registers, if there is a request for a copy of other civil certificates of the deceased, those copies shall state that the person is deceased.

2- The layout and procedure for verification between the death register and other civil registers as defined in paragraph 1 above shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

3- The national civil registration officer shall provide information to the National Center for Population Identification Management of the National Authority for Population Identification Management. The National Center for Population Identification Management of the National Authority for Population Identification Management shall close the personal identification number of the deceased as stated in Chapter 6 (Integrated Population Identification System) of this law.

### Article 59.- Procedure for Registering the Death of Foreigners

The death of a foreigner occurring in the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be registered according to the procedures stated in Section 6 (Death Registration) of this Chapter. The competent civil registration officer shall provide the death certificate as soon as possible to facilitate international transportation and cremation or burial.

### Article 60.- Registration of the Death of Unidentified Persons

1- In case of an unidentified corpse, the person who finds the corpse shall provide information to the competent authorities for investigation.

2- The competent authorities shall provide information and documents to the competent civil registration officer as a basis for registering the death of that unidentified person as follows:

a- Information about the death as much as possible according to the death certificate determined by the Inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of Interior and the Minister of Health.

b- One copy of the medical certificate of the cause of death, if any.

c- Investigation report number.

d- Other information as determined by legal norms of the Ministry of Interior.

3- In the cases defined in paragraph 2 above, even if the minimum information defined in paragraph 2 of Article 54 (Death Registration Procedure) of this law is missing, the civil registration officer shall register that death.

### Article 61.- Registration of Persons Deemed Deceased

1- The competent civil registration officer shall register the death of a person deemed deceased after receiving a court declaration of disappearance in accordance with Article 441 (Conditions for Declaration of Disappearance), Article 442 (Public Notice), and Article 443 (Effect of Declaration of Disappearance) of the Civil Code.

2- The competent civil registration officer shall invalidate the death register after receiving a court decision on the cancellation of the declaration of disappearance in accordance with Article 444 (Management When the Missing Person Returns) of the Civil Code. In this case, the death register recorded in accordance with paragraph 1 above shall be kept in accordance with Section 11 (Updating of Civil Registers, Maintenance, Retention, Transmission of Registers, Information, and Reporting on Civil Registration Work) of this Chapter.

### Article 62.- Reporting of Death Occurring Outside the Kingdom of Cambodia

1- If any Khmer citizen, whether having received or not received a personal identification number under this law, or a person who is not a Khmer citizen who has received a personal identification number under this law, dies outside the Kingdom of Cambodia:

a- The reporter shall apply for death registration with the competent civil registration officer at the representative mission of the Kingdom of Cambodia in the country where the death occurred, providing the following attached documents:
- Death certificate or other evidence of death from the country where the death occurred.
- Other attached documents as determined by legal norms of the Ministry of Interior.

b- If there is no representative mission or it is not possible to report or there is no possibility to report this death to the competent representative mission of the Kingdom of Cambodia, the reporter shall apply for death registration with the competent civil registration officer after returning to the Kingdom of Cambodia, providing the following attached documents:
- Death certificate or other evidence of death from the country where the death occurred.
- Other attached documents as determined by legal norms of the Ministry of Interior.

2- In cases defined in paragraph 1 above, the competent civil registration officer shall verify and record information related to the death event as stated in the death certificate or other evidence certifying the death from the country where the death event occurred and issue a death certificate in accordance with Article 55 (Death Certificate) of this law.

### Article 63.- Authorization for Cremation or Burial

1- The competent civil registration officer shall issue an authorization for cremation or burial. No corpse shall be cremated or buried without an authorization for cremation or burial issued by the competent civil registration officer, unless otherwise provided in this law or other laws.

2- After the civil registration officer has received the application for death registration defined in Article 52 (Application for Death Registration) attached with the documents defined in Article 53 (Documents Attached to the Application for Death Registration) of this law, the civil registration officer shall issue an authorization for cremation or burial.

3- Notwithstanding the provisions in paragraph 2 above, the competent civil registration officer may issue an urgent authorization for cremation or burial upon receiving information and certification of the necessity for cremation or burial from the competent authorities.

4- If an urgent authorization for cremation or burial is issued in accordance with paragraph 3 above, the competent civil registration officer shall continue to follow up with the family of the deceased to ensure that the appropriate reporter has applied for death registration in accordance with Article 52 (Application for Death Registration), Article 53 (Documents Attached to the Application for Death Registration), and Article 54 (Death Registration Procedure) of this law.

5- The person responsible for the place where cremation or burial takes place shall require the person requesting the cremation or burial to show the authorization for cremation or burial.

### Article 64.- Death Registration During the One-Year Grace Period

1- If the reporter has not applied for death registration within 15 (fifteen) days from the date the death event occurred, the reporter shall apply for death registration during a grace period of 1 (one) year from the date the death event occurred. The reporter shall also attach documentation justifying the reason for failing to apply for registration within 15 (fifteen) days after the death event occurred.

2- Upon receiving the application for registration as defined in paragraph 1 above, the civil registration officer shall perform death registration in accordance with Article 54 (Death Registration Procedure) of this law. The civil registration officer shall not reject an application for death registration based on the reasons for explaining the delay in applying for registration.

### Article 65.- Death Registration After the One-Year Grace Period

1- If the reporter has not applied for death registration after the one-year grace period, in addition to the attached documents defined in Article 53 (Documents Attached to the Application for Death Registration) of this law, the reporter shall provide any additional information or documents as follows:
- Assurance before the civil registration officer of witnesses to this death.
- Other documents or information certifying the death event as determined by legal norms of the Ministry of Interior.

2- The reporter shall also attach documentation justifying the reason for failing to apply for registration within the one-year grace period.

3- Upon receiving information and documents as stated in paragraph 1 above, the civil registration officer shall perform that death registration. The civil registration officer shall not reject an application for death registration because the grace period has expired.

### Article 66.- Reminders and Mobile Death Registration in Localities

1- When the civil registration officer becomes aware that a death event has occurred through notification from relevant authorities or other sources, and the reporter has not applied for death registration within the period as defined in Article 43 (Time Period for Death Registration) of this law, the civil registration officer shall remind the reporter to apply for registration, setting a reasonable period.

2- The Commune/Sangkat civil registration officer may move within his/her jurisdiction to register deaths that have not yet been registered, in accordance with the provisions of Section 6 (Death Registration) of this Chapter.

## Section 7 Marriage Registration

### Article 67.- Place to Apply for Marriage

A man and a woman who intend to marry each other shall apply for marriage with the civil registration officer of the Commune/Sangkat where either the man or the woman resides.

### Article 68.- Application for Marriage

1- A man and a woman who intend to marry each other shall apply for marriage with the civil registration officer as defined in Article 67 (Place to Apply for Marriage) of this law. The form of the marriage application shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

2- The marriage application shall be attached with documents proving identity and other documents as determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

3- If the man or the woman has not reached majority but has attained the age of marriage eligibility in accordance with Article 948 (Marriageable Age) of the Civil Code, the person with parental authority or the guardian for that minor man or woman shall provide consent before the civil registration officer or in writing.

4- The civil registration officer shall examine and verify the application and attached documents in accordance with Article 946 (Receipt of Marriage Notification) of the Civil Code.

### Article 69.- Public Notification of Marriage

1- The civil registration officer shall publicly announce the marriage by posting 1 (one) copy of the notice at the woman's residence and another 1 (one) copy at the Commune/Sangkat hall of the woman's residence, and shall send 2 (two) copies of the notice to the civil registration officer of the Commune/Sangkat where the man resides to post 1 (one) copy at the man's residence and another 1 (one) copy at the Commune/Sangkat hall of the man's residence.

2- The form and information of this notice shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

3- The public announcement of the marriage shall be made continuously for 10 (ten) days for the purpose of allowing persons with interests in this matter to object to the marriage within those 10 (ten) days. If there is no objection within those 10 (ten) days, the marriage can take place at any time within 6 (six) months from the date of the marriage application.

4- If the man and the woman who applied for marriage have not come to register the marriage before the competent civil registration officer, the competent civil registration officer may remind them by notifying the man and the woman.

### Article 70.- Marriage Registration Before the Civil Registration Officer

1- A marriage is considered legal only if the man and the woman have registered the marriage before the civil registration officer at the Commune/Sangkat that received the marriage application.

2- Information or points related to the marriage shall be entered into the marriage register... signed by the civil registration officer and signed or thumb-printed by the persons representing the spouses and with 2 (two) witnesses who are of legal age.

### Article 71.- Provision of Marriage Certificate

After the marriage registration is completed, the competent civil registration officer shall issue 1 (one) original marriage certificate to the couple free of charge.

### Article 72.- Issuance of Copy of Marriage Certificate and Extract of Marriage Register

1- Persons who can request a copy of a marriage certificate include:

a- The owners of the marriage certificate.

b- Direct blood relatives of the owners of the marriage certificate.

c- A legal representative or a representative by mandate of the persons above as stated in points (a) and (b).

d- Other persons with legal interest as determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

2- If the civil registration officer finds that the person requesting a copy of the marriage certificate is not a person defined in paragraph 1 above or the request is made for improper purposes such as the purpose of affecting the privacy of the owners of the marriage certificate, the civil registration officer shall reject that request and provide reasons for the rejection. This rejection may be appealed in accordance with the provisions of Section 13 (Appeals) of this Chapter.

3- Competent authorities or courts may request in writing a copy of a marriage certificate or an extract of the marriage register in necessary cases to fulfill their duties based on the law or other legal norms. In cases of such requests, the competent civil registration officer shall provide the requested documents promptly and free of charge.

### Article 73.- Form of Marriage Certificate and Copy of Marriage Certificate

1- Marriage certificates and copies of marriage certificates shall have security features to prevent forgery.

2- The form of marriage certificates and copies of marriage certificates shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

### Article 74.- Marriage Registration of Khmer Citizens Abroad

Application for marriage between a Khmer citizen and a Khmer citizen, or between a Khmer citizen and a foreigner legally residing abroad, may be made before the competent civil registration officer at the representative mission of the Kingdom of Cambodia in that country.

### Article 75.- Recognition of Marriage of Khmer Citizens Abroad

1- A marriage between a Khmer citizen and a Khmer citizen or between a Khmer citizen and a foreigner that has been performed according to the forms defined in the foreign law applied at the place where the marriage was performed shall be recognized for registration in the Kingdom of Cambodia if that marriage does not contradict the legal provisions of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

2- The marriage registration as defined in paragraph 1 above can be recorded at the represented mission of the Kingdom of Cambodia having jurisdiction in the country where the marriage was performed or recorded at the Commune/Sangkat where the competent officer resides at the residence of the spouses, through showing the marriage certificate from that country and other necessary information as determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

3- After completing the marriage registration, the civil registration officer at the competent representative mission of the Kingdom of Cambodia or the competent civil registration officer shall issue 1 (one) original marriage certificate to the couple.

### Article 76.- Marriage Between a Khmer Citizen and a Foreigner in the Kingdom of Cambodia

1- A marriage between a Khmer citizen and a foreigner entering or residing in the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be performed in accordance with the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

2- In cases defined in paragraph 1 above, if the foreigner has no residence in the Kingdom of Cambodia, the public notification defined in Article 69 (Public Notification of Marriage) of this law shall be done by posting 1 (one) copy of the notice at the residence of the Khmer citizen and another 1 (one) copy at the Commune/Sangkat hall of the residence of the Khmer citizen.

3- The forms, layout, and procedure for marriage between a Khmer citizen and a foreigner shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 77.- Marriage Between Foreigners in the Kingdom of Cambodia

1- Foreigners legally entering or residing in the Kingdom of Cambodia may apply for marriage with other foreigners legally entering or residing in the Kingdom of Cambodia, by complying with the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

2- If a foreigner and a foreigner marry under the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia, the registration of the foreigners shall be written in Khmer, accompanied by Latin characters.

## Section 8 Registration of Divorce, Nullity, and Cancellation of Marriage

### Article 78.- Registration of Divorce, Nullity, and Cancellation of Marriage

1- In case a decision on divorce, nullity, or cancellation of marriage has become final, the person who applied to the court shall report it to the civil registration officer of the Commune/Sangkat where the applicant resides, attached with a certified copy of that court decision and certification of the date that the court decision became final, within 15 (fifteen) days from the date that court decision became final.

2- In case the decision as defined in paragraph 1 above has become final at a Capital/Provincial Court of First Instance, the administration secretariat of the Capital/Provincial Court of First Instance that issued that decision shall notify that decision to the Capital/Provincial civil registration officer where that court of first instance is located.

3- In case the decision defined in paragraph 1 above has become final at the Appeal Court or the Supreme Court, the administration secretariat of the Appeal Court or the administration secretariat of the Supreme Court that issued that decision shall notify that decision to the national civil registration officer.

### Article 79.- Procedure for Registering Divorce, Nullity, and Cancellation of Marriage

After receiving the report defined in paragraph 1 or the notification defined in paragraph 2 or paragraph 3 of Article 78 (Registration of Divorce, Nullity, and Cancellation of Marriage) of this law, the competent civil registration officer shall examine and verify that information against the marriage register of both persons and record in that marriage register.

### Article 80.- Recognition and Registration of Divorce Abroad

If a couple who married under the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia has divorced outside the Kingdom of Cambodia in accordance with the laws of that country, this divorce shall be considered recognized and valid in the Kingdom of Cambodia. The person who has divorced...

shall provide the decision or certification of divorce from the competent institution in that foreign country to the competent civil registration officer. The competent civil registration officer shall examine and verify the information against the marriage register of both persons and record in that marriage register.

## Section 9 Other Civil Registrations

### Article 81.- Registration According to Court Decisions

1- In case a court decision as defined below has become final, the person who applied to the court shall report it to the civil registration officer of the Commune/Sangkat where the person's birth was registered, or the Commune/Sangkat of the applicant's residence, or the Commune/Sangkat determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior, attached with a certified copy of that court decision and certification of the date that the court decision became final, within 15 (fifteen) days from the date that court decision became final:

a- Rejection of paternity as defined in Article 999 (Action for Denial of Paternity) of the Civil Code.

b- Recognition as defined in Article 1001 (Action for Acknowledgment) of the Civil Code.

c- Determination of the existence of filiation as defined in Article 1002 (Action for Determination of Filiation) of the Civil Code.

d- Determination of the existence of maternity as defined in Article 1004 (Action for Determination of Maternity by Child) or Article 1005 (Action for Determination of Maternity by Mother) of the Civil Code.

e- Full adoption as defined in Article 1007 (Forms for Establishing Full Adoption) of the Civil Code.

f- Dissolution of full adoption as defined in Article 1016 (Dissolution of Full Adoption) of the Civil Code.

g- Simple adoption as defined in Article 1020 (Application for Simple Adoption) of the Civil Code.

h- Dissolution of simple adoption as defined in Article 1028 (Application for Dissolution of Simple Adoption) of the Civil Code.

2- In case the decision as defined in paragraph 1 above has become final at a Capital/Provincial Court of First Instance, the administration secretariat of the Capital/Provincial Court of First Instance that issued that decision shall notify that decision to the Capital/Provincial civil registration officer where that court of first instance is located.

3- In case the decision defined in paragraph 1 above has become final at the Appeal Court or the Supreme Court, the administration secretariat of the Appeal Court or the administration secretariat of the Supreme Court that issued that decision shall notify that decision to the national civil registration officer.

4- In case the civil registration officer receives the report defined in paragraph 1 above, the civil registration officer shall register or correct the register according to that report, and shall withdraw the old birth certificate and issue a new birth certificate.

5- The form, layout, and procedure for registration in cases defined in paragraph 1 above shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

6- The provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 40 (Reminders and Mobile Birth Registration in Localities) of this law shall also apply equally to the registration defined in paragraph 1 above.

### Article 82.- Registration Related to Voluntary Recognition

1- In case a father voluntarily recognizes a child in accordance with the provisions of Article 993 (Voluntary Acknowledgment) of the Civil Code, that father shall report it to the civil registration officer of the Commune/Sangkat where the child's birth was registered.

2- In case a father recognizes a fetus in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 995 (Acknowledgment by Father) of the Civil Code, that father shall report it to the competent civil registration officer of the Commune/Sangkat of the mother's residence with the mother's consent. In this case, if the fetus that was recognized by the father dies before birth, the mother or the father of that fetus, in addition to recording information about the stillborn infant, shall notify that event to the civil registration officer of the Commune/Sangkat where the father reported the recognition.

3- The provisions of paragraph 4 of Article 81 (Registration According to Court Decisions) of this law shall also apply equally to the case defined in paragraph 1 above.

4- In case a father recognizes a child who has died in accordance with the provisions...

death) of the Civil Code, that father shall report it to the civil registration officer of the Commune/Sangkat where the child's birth was registered, stating the date, month, and year that the child died and showing evidence of that child.

5- In case a father recognizes a child by will in accordance with the provisions of the second phrase of paragraph 1 of Article 997 (Form of Acknowledgment) of the Civil Code, the executor of the will shall report it to the civil registration officer of the Commune/Sangkat where the child's birth was registered, attached with a certified copy of that will, within 15 (fifteen) days from the date the executor assumes his/her duties.

6- The form, layout, and procedure for registration in cases defined in paragraphs 1 to 5 above shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

### Article 83.- Registration of Dissolution of Relationship by Marriage due to Death of a Spouse

1- In case one spouse has died, and if the surviving spouse intends to end the relationship by marriage in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 941 (Dissolution of Relationship by Marriage) of the Civil Code, that surviving spouse shall express the will to end the relationship by marriage before the civil registration officer of the surviving spouse's residence. This provision does not prevent the surviving spouse from expressing the will to end the relationship by marriage before the civil registration officer to whom he/she reports the death of that spouse.

2- In case of expressing the will to end the relationship by marriage as defined in paragraph 1 above, the competent civil registration officer shall record it in the marriage register of both the surviving and deceased spouses.

### Article 84.- Registration of Disinheritance

1- In case a court decision to disinherit a presumed heir in accordance with Article 1151 (Disinheritance of Heir) and Article 1152 (Disinheritance of Heir by Will) of the Civil Code, or a court decision cancelling the disinheritance of an heir in accordance with Article 1154 (Cancellation of Disinheritance of Heir) of the Civil Code, has become final, the person who applied to the court shall report it to the civil registration officer of the Commune/Sangkat of the applicant's residence, attached with a certified copy of that court decision and certification of the date that the court decision became final, within 15 (fifteen) days from the date that court decision became final.

2- In case the decision defined in paragraph 1 above has become final at a Capital/Provincial Court of First Instance, the administration secretariat of the Capital/Provincial Court of First Instance that issued that decision shall notify that decision to the Capital/Provincial civil registration officer where that court of first instance is located.

3- In case the decision defined in paragraph 1 above has become final at the Appeal Court or the Supreme Court, the administration secretariat of the Appeal Court or the administration secretariat of the Supreme Court that issued that decision shall notify that decision to the national civil registration officer.

4- In case there is a report as defined in paragraph 1 above, the competent civil registration officer shall record it in the birth register of the deceased person (testator).

## Section 10 Correction of Civil Registers

### Article 85.- Persons Who Can Request Correction

Persons who can request corection of civil registers include:

a- The owner of the civil register.

b- Father, mother, or guardian for a minor, in case of requesting correction of the minor's birth register.

c- Direct blood relatives and spouse of the deceased, in case of requesting correction of the death register.

d- Any persons having legal interest in correcting the civil register as determined by the Minister of Interior.

### Article 86.- Correction of Technical Errors by Civil Registration Officers

1- In case the civil registration has an error, and that error is a technical error of the competent civil registration officer, the civil registration officer may correct that technical error upon application or by discretion.

2- Technical errors defined in paragraph 1 above refer to data written differently from information by the civil registration officer, as follows:

a- Spelling mistakes in the surname and/or given name in Khmer and Latin characters of the owner or father or mother, meaning the sound is the same but the spelling is different.

b- Writing the wrong gender from the attached documents at the time of registration.

c- Writing the date, month, or year of birth of the owner or father or mother differently from the attached documents.

d- Error in writing the day, month, and year of birth according to the solar calendar.

e- Error in writing the day, month, and year of birth according to the lunar calendar.

f- Error in administrative geographical location of the place of birth, place of death, or residence.

g- Other technical errors determined by inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of Interior and the Minister of Justice.

3- Modalities and procedures for correcting technical errors shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

#### Article 87.- Correction of Civil Status Registers by Court Decision

1- Apart from technical errors of the competent civil status officer as defined in Article 86 (Correction of technical errors by civil status officer) of this Law, correction of civil status registers shall be made through an application to the court.

2- In the case of an application as defined in paragraph 1 above, the provisions of the Law on Non-Litigious Civil Procedures shall also apply.

3- In the case where the court's decision on correction has become final, the applicant to the court shall report to the civil status officer of the commune/Sangkat where the civil status register to be corrected is registered, or the commune/Sangkat of the applicant's residence, or the commune/Sangkat determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior, by attaching a certified copy of that court decision and a certificate of the date on which that court decision became final, within 15 (fifteen) days from the date the court decision became final.

4- In the case where the court's decision on correction has become final at the Court of First Instance of the Capital/Province, the administrative secretariat of the Court of First Instance of the Capital/Province that issued the decision shall notify the capital/provincial level civil status officer where that Court of First Instance is located about that decision.

5- In the case where the decision defined in paragraph 1 above has become final at the Appeal Court, the General Secretariat of the Appeal Court that issued the decision shall notify the national level civil status officer about that decision.

6- If the civil status officer has received the notification defined in paragraph 4 and paragraph 5 above, even if there is no reporting from the applicant to the court as defined in paragraph 3 above, that civil status officer may also make the correction. In this case, the civil status officer shall notify the applicant to the court.

7- The competent civil status officer shall correct the civil status register according to the court decision and shall make a marginal note in the civil status register while preserving the original data.

#### Article 88.- Change of Family Name after Marriage or after Divorce, etc.

If any person applies to change their family name after marriage or to change back to their original family name after divorce or annulment of marriage, the competent civil status officer shall change the family name and make a marginal note in the civil status register while preserving the original data.

#### Article 89.- Change of Given Name by Court

1- In the case where the name of any person affects their personal dignity, is contrary to public order and good customs, or there is another special necessity for the change, that person may apply for a change of name to the competent court of their residence by specifying the reasons for such change.

2- The provisions of the Law on Non-Litigious Civil Procedures shall also apply to the case of an application for a change of name based on the provisions of paragraph 1 above.

3- In the case where the court's decision on the change of name has become final, the applicant to the court shall report to the civil status officer of the commune/Sangkat where the civil status register to be corrected is registered, by attaching a certified copy of that court decision and a certificate of the date on which that court decision became final, within 15 (fifteen) days from the date the court decision became final.

4- In the case where the court decision on the change of name has become final at the Court of First Instance of the Capital/Province, the administrative secretariat of the Court of First Instance of the Capital/Province that issued the decision shall notify the capital/provincial level civil status officer where that Court of First Instance is located about that decision.

5- In the case where the court decision on correction has become final at the Appeal Court, the General Secretariat of the Appeal Court that issued the decision shall notify the national level civil status officer about that decision.

6- The competent civil status officer shall change the name in the civil status register according to the court decision and shall make a marginal note in the civil status register while preserving the original data.

#### Article 90.- Notification Procedure

The notification procedure defined in paragraph 4 and paragraph 5 of Article 87 (Correction of Civil Status Register by Court Decision), Article 88 (Change of Family Name after Marriage or after Divorce, Death, and Annulment of Marriage), paragraph 2 and

paragraph 3 of Article 81 (Registration according to Court Decision), paragraph 2 and paragraph 3 of Article 84 (Registration of Disinheritance), paragraph 4 and paragraph 5 of Article 87 (Correction of Civil Status Register according to Court Decision), paragraph 4 and paragraph 5 of Article 89 (Change of Name by Court) of this Law, shall be determined by inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of Interior and the Minister of Justice.

### Section 11 Updating Civil Status Registers, Maintenance, Storage, Submission of Registers, Information and Reports on Civil Status Work

#### Article 91.- Updating Civil Status Registers

1- In the case where there is any civil status registration based on the provisions from Section 3 (On Civil Status Officers) to Section 10 (Correction of Civil Status Registers), the competent civil status officer shall update all other relevant registers.

2- The national level civil status officer shall ensure that the updating defined in paragraph 1 above is made completely and accurately.

3- Forms, modalities and procedures for updating civil status registers shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

#### Article 92.- Maintenance and Storage of Registers

Procedures for maintenance and storage of civil status registers at sub-national and national levels shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

#### Article 93.- Re-establishment of Civil Status Registers

In the case where civil status registers are lost or destroyed by disaster entirely, the national level civil status officer shall take measures to have the competent civil status officer re-establish those civil status registers.

#### Article 94.- Submission of Registers and Civil Status Reports

1- Commune/Sangkat civil status officers shall submit civil status registers to the town/district/khan civil status officer and/or the capital/provincial level civil status officer and/or the national level civil status officer.

2- Commune/Sangkat civil status officers shall submit monthly reports on civil status work to the town/district/khan civil status officer and/or the capital/provincial level civil status officer.

3- Town/district/khan civil status officers shall submit reports on civil status work to the capital/provincial level civil status officer and/or the national level civil status officer.

4- Capital/provincial level civil status officers shall submit reports on civil status work to the national level civil status officer.

5- Modalities and timing for the submission of registers and reports defined in paragraph 1, paragraph 2, paragraph 3, and paragraph 4 above shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

#### Article 95.- Submission of Registers and Civil Status Reports of Diplomatic Missions

The civil status officer of the diplomatic mission of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall submit the civil status registers and reports on civil status work to the national level civil status officer in accordance with the modalities and timing determined by inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of Interior and the Minister of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation.

#### Article 96.- Data Submission

The national level civil status officer shall ensure that necessary data relating to vital events is submitted to the National Center for Population Management (NCPM) as stated in Chapter 6 (Integrated Population Management System) of this Law.

### Section 12 Legal Effect of Civil Status Registers and Certificates

#### Article 97.- Legal Effect of Civil Status Registers and Certificates

Civil status registers and certificates issued by the competent civil status officer shall be presumed to be valid legal evidence for:

a- The registration of the vital event related to that civil status certificate.

b- The information contained in that civil status register and certificate.

### Section 13 Complaint

#### Article 98.- Complaint against actions including decisions, delays or negligence in handling by civil status officer

1- Any person who has applied to the civil status officer for registration of a vital event, correction of civil status registers, or other requests authorized under this Chapter, may file a complaint against actions including decisions, delays, or negligence in handling.

2- Modalities and procedures for filing a complaint defined in paragraph 1 above shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

3- The provisions of paragraph 1 above do not prevent direct filing of a complaint to the court.

## Chapter 3 Vital Statistics

### Section 1 General Provisions

#### Article 99.- Basic Principles

Vital statistics shall be implemented according to the following basic principles:

- Ensure the production of high-quality, accurate, complete, timely, and reliable vital statistics.

- Ensure the provision of necessary information on population statistics to support administrative work and various planning.

### Section 2 Compilation, Production and Dissemination of Vital Statistics

#### Article 100.- Competence of the National Institute of Statistics

The National Institute of Statistics (NIS) of the Ministry of Planning is competent to collect, process, compile, analyze, and produce vital statistics for the entire nation and by geographic location of national and sub-national administration, as well as to publish and disseminate vital statistics.

#### Article 101.- Data Sources of Vital Statistics

1- Data sources of vital statistics come from the collection during the process of registration of live births, deaths, marriages, and other vital events, and from the recording of stillbirths.

2- The National Institute of Statistics of the Ministry of Planning may use the above data sources and other information to supplement information on vital registration.

#### Article 102.- Submission of Information on Vital Registration and Stillbirths

1- The Ministry of Interior shall ensure the submission of necessary information from the vital registration to the National Institute of Statistics of the Ministry of Planning on a regular and timely basis. The Ministry of Interior shall not submit information relating to personal identity, such as names, from the vital registration to the National Institute of Statistics of the Ministry of Planning. The timing, modalities, and information to be submitted shall be determined by inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of Interior and the Minister of Planning.

2- The Ministry of Health shall ensure the submission of information on stillbirths to the National Institute of Statistics of the Ministry of Planning on a regular and timely basis. The Ministry of Health shall not submit information relating to personal identity, such as names of parents, to the National Institute of Statistics of the Ministry of Planning. The timing, modalities, and information to be submitted shall be determined by inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of Health and the Minister of Planning.

#### Article 103.- Compilation, Production, Publication and Dissemination of Vital Statistics

1- The National Institute of Statistics of the Ministry of Planning shall compile, produce, and publish vital statistics in a timely, accurate, complete, and regular manner, and shall share vital statistics reports according to the standards of produced vital statistics, at least annually for each calendar year, and shall disseminate vital statistics on its website and through other appropriate means.

2- Apart from laws and other legal instruments in force, necessary procedures for the compilation, production, publication, and dissemination of vital statistics shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Planning.

### Section 3 Recording and Certification of Stillbirths

#### Article 104.- Recording of Stillbirths

Health facilities shall record information on each stillbirth as follows:

a- Date and place of delivery of a stillbirth.

b- Sex, weight, gestational age, and body length of the fetus, if known.

c- Certification of the cause of stillbirth, if possible.

d- Mother's date of birth and residence.

e- Other information determined by Prakas of the Minister of Health.

## Chapter 4 Residence Registration

### Section 1 General Provisions

#### Article 105.- Basic Principles

Residence registration shall be implemented according to the following basic principles:

- Ensure the counting of people and updating the addresses of every person living in the Kingdom of Cambodia to contribute to maintaining security, safety, public order, economic and social development, administrative work, and various planning.

- Ensure that every person has the right to residence registration.

- Strengthen the efficiency of establishing a system for collecting and managing data relating to the residence of every person accurately and uniformly throughout the country.

- Ensure that data relating to residence is used as a basis for providing public services.

### Section 2 Competence and Residence Registration Officers

#### Article 106.- Competence

Residence registration and management of residence shall be under the competence of the Ministry of Interior.

#### Article 107.- Residence Registration Officers

1- The Chief of Administrative Police Post of the commune/Sangkat is the Residence Registration Officer.

2- In the case where the Chief of Administrative Police Post of the commune/Sangkat is absent, deceased, or terminated from the position, the Deputy Chief of Administrative Police Post of the commune/Sangkat who receives the rights as the Acting Chief of Administrative Police Post of the commune/Sangkat is the Residence Registration Officer.

3- The provisions in paragraph 1 and paragraph 2 above do not prevent the Minister of Interior from appointing other residence registration officers in case of necessity.

#### Article 108.- Responsibilities of Residence Registration Officers

Residence Registration Officers shall be responsible for:

a- Examining, recording and registering residence within their jurisdiction.

b- Issuing residence identification documents.

c- Correcting, deleting residence registration, and annulling and withdrawing residence identification documents.

d- Cooperating with relevant authorities on the management of residence and residence registration.

e- Conducting regular administrative inspections of residences within their jurisdiction to verify changes of members living in each residence in order to update the residence register and residence registration completely.

f- Disseminate and promote awareness to citizens about their rights and obligations regarding residence registration.

g- Provide reports relating to residence registration and residence management through the hierarchy in a timely and accurate manner according to the determination.

h- Perform other necessary duties as determined by the Minister of Interior.

### Section 3 Residence Registration

#### Article 109.- Residence Register

1- The residence register may be in paper and/or electronic format.

2- The format of the residence register and the information to be collected and recorded in the residence register shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

#### Article 110.- Residence Registration

1- Every person shall register their residence at the residence where they are currently living in order to receive recognition of residence in that residence.

2- Every person may register their residence at only one place, even if that person has more than one residence.

3- The person responsible for applying for residence registration of each residence has the obligation to register every member living permanently with them.

#### Article 111.- Persons Responsible for Applying for Residence Registration

1- Persons responsible for applying for residence registration include:

a- Representative of each residence.

b- Owner of the residence or the lessor or representative, in the case where the residence is subject to lease.

c- Head of the entity or manager or representative, in the case of collective residence.

d- Actual residents.

e- Other persons or head of the entity or manager or representative as authorized by this Law or other laws.

2- The Ministry of Interior may determine additional persons responsible for applying for residence registration in case of necessity.

#### Article 112.- Place of Residence Registration

Places of residence registration include:

a- The Administrative Police Post of the commune/Sangkat where the person is living.

b- Other places as determined by legal instruments of the Ministry of Interior.

#### Article 113.- Reporting for Residence Registration

1- The person responsible for applying for residence registration shall report to the Residence Registration Officer about members who come to live in the residence and shall apply for residence registration.

2- The person responsible for applying for residence registration shall report about moving out, moving in, and the death of members living permanently with them to the Residence Registration Officer and shall apply for residence registration.

#### Article 114.- Issuance of Residence Identification Documents

1- After completion of residence registration, the Residence Registration Officer shall issue 1 (one) original copy of residence identification document to the person responsible for applying for residence registration as stated in Article 111 (Persons responsible for applying for residence registration) of this Chapter.

2- If the Residence Registration Officer finds that the person applying for residence registration has invalid attached documents, the Residence Registration Officer may refuse the application and shall provide reasons for the refusal in writing. This refusal may be complained against as stated in Section 17 (Complaint) of this Chapter.

#### Article 115.- Application for Residence Certificate

1- Persons who may apply for a residence certificate include:

a- The subject of the residence certificate.

b- Representative of the subject of the residence certificate.

c- Representative determined by law or representative by mandate of the person stated in point a and b.

d- Other persons who are considered to have legitimate interests as determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

2- The Residence Registration Officer may refuse the application for a residence certificate and shall provide reasons for the refusal in writing. This refusal may be complained against as stated in Section 17 (Complaint) of this Chapter.

3- Competent authorities or courts may obtain a residence certificate for legal purposes.

#### Article 116.- Modalities and Procedures for Residence Registration

Modalities and procedures for residence registration and the issuance of residence identification documents shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

#### Article 117.- Format of Residence Identification Document

1- Residence identification documents shall have security features to prevent forgery.

2- The format of residence identification documents shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

### Section 4 Correction, Annulling, Deletion of Residence Register and Withdrawal of Residence Identification Document

#### Article 118.- Persons who may apply for Correction

Persons who may apply for correction of the residence register include:

a- The subject of the residence register.

b- Parents, in the case where the subject of the application is a minor.

c- Representative determined by law or representative by mandate of the person stated in point a and b.

d- Other persons who have legitimate interests in correcting the residence register as determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

#### Article 119.- Correction of Errors in Residence Register

1- The Residence Registration Officer who manages or has access to the residence register may correct errors upon application or at the discretion of the Residence Registration Officer. This correction shall be recorded and a marginal note made in the residence register while preserving the original data.

2- If the Residence Registration Officer finds that the correction application is invalid or has insufficient attached documents, the Residence Registration Officer may refuse the application for correction in writing and shall specify the reasons. This refusal may be complained against as stated in Section 17 (Complaint) of this Chapter.

3- Modalities and procedures for correcting errors in the residence register shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

#### Article 120.- Deletion of Residence Registration, Annulling of Residence Identification Documents and Withdrawal of Residence Identification Documents

Residence registration shall be deleted in any of the following conditions:

- It is found that the residence registration is contrary to the procedures determined by law.

- It is found that the residence registration is in more than one place.

- It is found that the person who has registered their residence has moved their residence or is deceased.

2- Residence identification documents shall be annulled in any of the following cases:

- Residence identification documents which have been issued contrary to the procedures determined by law or by violation.

- Residence identification documents which have been issued by a person without legal capacity.

- Residence identification documents which have been issued but there is no basis in the register.

- Residence identification documents which are found to have been altered or erased of data by the person themselves.

3- Residence identification documents shall be withdrawn in the case of deletion or annulment as defined in paragraph 1 and paragraph 2 above.

4- The provisions in paragraph 1 and paragraph 2 above do not prevent the Minister of Interior from issuing a Prakas determining other conditions.

5- Modalities and procedures for deleting residence registration, annulling residence identification documents, and withdrawing residence identification documents shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

### Section 5 Storage, Maintenance and Submission of Information from Residence Register

#### Article 121.- Storage, Maintenance and Submission of Information from Residence Register

1- In the case where the residence registration is in paper format, the residence register shall be stored at the Administrative Police Post of the commune/Sangkat or other places as determined by legal instruments of the Ministry of Interior.

2- In the case where the residence registration is in electronic format, the residence register shall be stored in the residence registration system and necessary information relating to residence registration shall be submitted to the National Center for Identification Management.

3- Storage, maintenance, and submission of information from the residence register shall ensure the confidentiality of personal identity information and ensure security.

4- Modalities and procedures for storage and maintenance of the residence register shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

### Section 6 Legal Effect of Residence Register and Residence Identification Documents

#### Article 122.- Legal Effect of Residence Register and Residence Identification Documents

1- Residence register and residence identification documents shall be presumed to be valid legal evidence for:

a- Residence registration relating to residence identification documents made correctly in accordance with the conditions and procedures defined in this Law.

b- Information contained in the residence register and residence identification documents made correctly in accordance with this Law.

2- Residence registration is not a condition for automatic granting of nationality, granting ownership of real estate, and legal rights of foreigners to reside in the Kingdom of Cambodia. Acquisition of all these rights shall be determined by separate laws.

### Section 7 Complaint

#### Article 123.- Complaint against Actions including Decisions, Delays, or Negligence in Actions by Residence Registration Officer

1- Any person who has applied to the Residence Registration Officer for residence registration, correction of residence register, or other requests authorized under this Chapter, may file a complaint against actions including decisions, delays, or negligence in handling.

2- Modalities and procedures for filing a complaint defined in paragraph 1 above shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

3- The provisions of paragraph 1 above do not prevent direct filing of a complaint to the court.

## Chapter 5 Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration

### Section 1 General Provisions

#### Article 124.- Basic Principles

Khmer Nationality Identity Card registration shall be implemented according to the following basic principles:

- Ensure that persons holding Khmer nationality have the right to Khmer Nationality Identity Card registration.

- Strengthen the efficiency of establishing a system for collecting and managing personal identity data of persons holding Khmer nationality accurately through information and communication technology systems and strengthen the quality of digital systems to avoid duplication of Khmer Nationality Identity Cards in the Khmer Nationality Identity Card management system.

- Data relating to Khmer Nationality Identity Card registration shall be used as a basis for verifying and certifying the accuracy of personal identity data of persons holding Khmer nationality when applying for public services.

- Ensure participation in the implementation of rights and obligations as Khmer citizens in accordance with various laws and legal instruments and to contribute to maintaining security, safety, public order, and economic and social development.

### Section 2 Competence and Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration Officers

#### Article 125.- Competence

Khmer Nationality Identity Card registration shall be under the competence of the Ministry of Interior.

#### Article 126.- Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration Officers

Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration Officers include:

a- Khmer Nationality Identity Card officers of the Administrative Police Post of the commune/Sangkat.

b- Khmer Nationality Identity Card officers of the town/district/khan Police Inspectorate.

c- Khmer Nationality Identity Card officers of the capital/provincial Police Commissariat.

d- The provisions in paragraph 1, paragraph 2, and paragraph 3 above do not prevent the Minister of Interior from appointing other officers to handle Khmer Nationality Identity Card registration work in case of necessity.

#### Article 127.- Responsibilities of Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration Officers

Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration Officers shall be responsible for:

a- Examining, recording, and registering or rejecting applications for Khmer Nationality Identity Card registration within their jurisdiction.

b- Issue Khmer Nationality Identity Cards according to the determination of the Ministry of Interior.

c- Prepare modalities relating to corrections, annulment, deletion of Khmer Nationality Identity Card register, and withdrawal of Khmer Nationality Identity Cards to be submitted to the competent Khmer Nationality Identity Card registration officer for examination and decision.

d- Cooperate with relevant authorities on Khmer Nationality Identity Card registration.

e- Disseminate and promote awareness to citizens about their rights and obligations regarding the registration and use of Khmer Nationality Identity Cards.

f- Provide reports relating to Khmer Nationality Identity Card registration through the hierarchy in a timely and accurate manner according to the determination.

g- Perform other necessary duties as determined by the Minister of Interior.

### Section 3 Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration

#### Article 128.- Khmer Nationality Identity Card Register

1- The Khmer Nationality Identity Card register shall be in electronic format.

2- The format and information contained in the Khmer Nationality Identity Card register shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 129.- Khmer Nationality Identity Card

1- The Khmer Nationality Identity Card is an official document for certifying the identity of each person holding Khmer nationality, which cannot be transferred from one person to another or be used to create security interests.

2- The format, features, information, determination of validity, and age for issuing Khmer Nationality Identity Cards shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 130.- Place of Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration

Places for Khmer Nationality Identity Card registration include:

a- The commune/Sangkat of the person's residence.

b- Other places as determined by legal instruments of the Ministry of Interior.

#### Article 131.- Application for Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration and Duplicates

1- Any person holding Khmer nationality and reaching the age as determined in paragraph 2 of Article 129 (Khmer Nationality Identity Card) of this Law may apply for Khmer Nationality Identity Card registration.

2- A person of Khmer nationality may apply for only one Khmer Nationality Identity Card.

3- A person of Khmer nationality may apply for a re-issuance of a Khmer Nationality Identity Card in the case where their Khmer Nationality Identity Card is damaged, lost, expired, or data is corrected.

4- The application for Khmer Nationality Identity Card registration and for duplication shall be in writing and attached with necessary documents as determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

#### Article 132.- Issuance of Khmer Nationality Identity Cards to Subjects

1- After completion of the Khmer Nationality Identity Card registration, the Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration Officer shall issue the Khmer Nationality Identity Card to the subject.

2- If the Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration Officer finds that the application for Khmer Nationality Identity Card registration and attached documents lack any information as determined in paragraph 4 of Article 131 (Application for Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration and Duplicates) of this Law, the Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration Officer shall require the applicant to provide the missing information within a reasonable timeframe. In the case where the applicant fails to provide the missing information within the reasonable timeframe determined by the Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration Officer, the Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration Officer may reject the application for Khmer Nationality Identity Card registration.

3- Modalities and procedures for issuing Khmer Nationality Identity Cards and duplicates shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

### Section 4 Correction, Annulling, Deletion of Register and Withdrawal of Khmer Nationality Identity Cards

#### Article 133.- Correction of Personal Identity Data in the Register and Khmer Nationality Identity Cards

1- The subject may apply for correction of identity data in the register and their Khmer Nationality Identity Card in the case where the civil status register has been corrected in accordance with the provisions of Section 10 (Correction of Civil Status Register) of Chapter 2 (Civil Status Registration) or in the case where the Khmer Nationality Identity Card register has records different from the civil status register.

2- Any person who has acquired Khmer nationality and has not registered in the civil status register may apply for correction of their identity data in the register and Khmer Nationality Identity Card based on the content in the Royal Decree on their acquisition of nationality.

3- The applications stated in paragraph 1 and paragraph 2 above shall be submitted to the Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration Officer who has authority over the Khmer Nationality Identity Card register. The Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration Officer who has authority over the Khmer Nationality Identity Card register shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

4- Necessary information to be written in the application for correction of the Khmer Nationality Identity Card and attached documents shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

5- If the Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration Officer who has authority over the Khmer Nationality Identity Card register as determined in paragraph 2 above finds that the application for correction of the Khmer Nationality Identity Card lacks any necessary information as determined in paragraph 3 above, the Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration Officer who has authority over the Khmer Nationality Identity Card register shall require the applicant to provide the missing information within a reasonable timeframe. In the case where the applicant fails to provide the missing information within the reasonable timeframe determined by the Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration Officer who has authority over the Khmer Nationality Identity Card register, the Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration Officer who has authority over the Khmer Nationality Identity Card register may reject the application for correction.

6- Modalities and procedures for correction of personal identity data in the register and Khmer Nationality Identity Cards shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

#### Article 134.- Deletion of Khmer Nationality Identity Card Register, Annulling and Withdrawal of Khmer Nationality Identity Cards

1- The Khmer Nationality Identity Card register shall be deleted in any of the following conditions:

- It is found that the Khmer Nationality Identity Card registration is contrary to the procedures determined by law.

- It is found that the person holding the Khmer Nationality Identity Card is deceased.

- It is found that the person holding the Khmer Nationality Identity Card has lost Khmer nationality.

2- Khmer Nationality Identity Cards shall be annulled in any of the following cases:

- Khmer Nationality Identity Cards which have been issued contrary to the procedures determined by law or by violation.

- Khmer Nationality Identity Cards which have been issued by a person without legal capacity.

- It is found that any person is using more than 1 (one) valid Khmer Nationality Identity Card.

- It is found that the person holding the Khmer Nationality Identity Card is deceased.

- It is found that the person holding the Khmer Nationality Identity Card has lost Khmer nationality.

3- Khmer Nationality Identity Cards shall be withdrawn in cases of deletion or annulment as defined in paragraph 1 and paragraph 2 above.

4- The provisions in paragraph 1 and paragraph 2 above do not prevent the Minister of Interior from issuing a Prakas determining other conditions.

5- Modalities and procedures for deleting the Khmer Nationality Identity Card register, annulling Khmer Nationality Identity Cards and withdrawing Khmer Nationality Identity Cards shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

### Section 5 Submission and Maintenance of Khmer Nationality Identity Card Data

#### Article 135.- Submission and Maintenance of Khmer Nationality Identity Card Data

1- Data in the Khmer Nationality Identity Card register shall be automatically submitted to the National Center for Identification Management as stated in Chapter 6 (Integrated Population Identification System) of this Law.

2- Personal identity data of persons registered in the Khmer Nationality Identity Card register shall be maintained with the highest security as stated in Chapter 7 (Use of Data, Personal Privacy and Data Security) of this Law.

3- Personal data recorded and stored in the Khmer Nationality Identity Card register shall be protected and managed by the State.

### Section 6 Legal Effect of Register and Khmer Nationality Identity Cards

#### Article 136.- Legal Effect of Register and Khmer Nationality Identity Cards

The register and Khmer Nationality Identity Cards shall be presumed to be valid legal evidence for:

a- Khmer Nationality Identity Card registration made correctly in accordance with the conditions and procedures defined in this Law.

b- Personal data contained in the Khmer Nationality Identity Card register, personal information displayed on Khmer Nationality Identity Cards, and data stored in the electronic chip of the Khmer Nationality Identity Cards, which are correctly made in accordance with this Law.

### Section 7 Complaint

#### Article 137.- Complaint against Actions including Decisions, Delays or Negligence in Actions by Khmer Nationality Identity Card Officers

1- Any person who has applied to the Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration Officer for Khmer Nationality Identity Card registration, correction of the Khmer Nationality Identity Card register, or other requests authorized under this Chapter, may file a complaint against actions including decisions, delays, or negligence in handling.

2- Modalities and procedures for filing a complaint defined in paragraph 1 above shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

3- The provisions of paragraph 1 above do not prevent direct filing of a complaint to the court.

## Chapter 6 Integrated Population Identification System

### Section 1 General Provisions

#### Article 138.- Basic Principles

The Integrated Population Identification System shall be implemented according to the following basic principles:

- Ensure the centralization of digital population data following the principles of interoperability for managing population data and integrating inter-ministerial governance mechanisms, for various institutions and systems to use as a primary and unique trusted source of population data.

- Ensure the quality and principle of uniqueness of personal identity information and ensure that authorized institutions or persons have the ability to search for accurate and true data about individuals.

- Ensure that persons holding Khmer nationality and all persons who are born and/or reside in the Kingdom of Cambodia have a unique and permanent individual identification code.

### Section 2 Integrated Population Identification System

#### Article 139.- Integrated Population Identification System

1- The Integrated Population Identification System is the core individual identity database system.

2- The establishment and development of the Integrated Population Identification System shall use information and communication technology to connect and interact with the civil status registration system, Khmer Nationality Identity Card management system, passport management system, residence management system, nationality data management system and other systems.

3- The Integrated Population Identification System shall be under the management of the National Center for Identification Management.

#### Article 140.- Functions of the Integrated Population Identification System

1- The Integrated Population Identification System has the following functions:

a- Continuously transmit and update personal identity data in the population register.

b- Automatically generate internal identification codes.

c- Facilitate interaction between the civil status registration system, Khmer Nationality Identity Card management system, passport management system, residence management system, nationality data management system and other systems for recording, storing, updating and verifying personal identity data.

d- Categorize personal identity data from the population register.

2- In case of necessity and to ensure high efficiency in managing the Integrated Population Identification System, the National Authority for Identification Management has the right to add more functions to the Integrated Population Identification System.

### Section 3 Personal Identification Number

#### Article 141.- Creation of Personal Identification Number

1- The personal identification number shall be created by the Integrated Population Identification System through random and automatic selection for every person holding Khmer nationality and all persons who are born and/or reside in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

2- The format and detailed sequence of the personal identification number shall be determined by Sub-decree upon the proposal of the National Authority for Identification Management.

#### Article 142.- Provision of Personal Identification Number

1- Each person shall be provided with a permanent personal identification number for use until the person is deceased.

2- Every person born in the Kingdom of Cambodia and Khmer citizens born abroad after the effective date of this Law shall be provided with a personal identification number through application for live birth registration. For Khmer citizens born before the effective date of this Law, the personal identification number shall be provided through application for Khmer Nationality Identity Card registration. For immigrants residing in the Kingdom of Cambodia or persons naturalized as Khmer citizens, the personal identification number shall be provided through immigrant registration or nationality registration.

3- Procedures for providing the personal identification number to each person shall be determined by Sub-decree upon the proposal of the National Authority for Identification Management.

#### Article 143.- Closing of Personal Identification Number

1- The use of a personal identification number shall be closed in the following cases:

a- When the holder of the personal identification number is deceased.

b- When it is found that there is fraud in nationality registration, civil status registration, or immigrant registration.

c- When the personal identification number has been suspended in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 144 (Suspension of Personal Identification Number) of this Law, and this personal identification number has not been reopened in accordance with Article 145 (Reopening of Personal Identification Number) of this Law within the timeframe determined in paragraph 2 of Article 144 (Suspension of Personal Identification Number) of this Law.

2- A personal identification number that has been closed cannot be reused for another person.

#### Article 144.- Suspension of Personal Identification Number

1- The use of a personal identification number shall be suspended in the following cases:

a- When a foreigner loses the right of residence or the right of entry/exit in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

b- When any person has renounced Khmer nationality and no longer resides in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

2- A personal identification number which has been suspended shall be stored for a timeframe determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 145.- Reopening of Personal Identification Number

A personal identification number which has been closed or suspended shall be reopened when the National Center for Identification Management finds that any of the reasons for closing or suspension as determined in paragraph 1 of Article 143 (Closing of Personal Identification Number) or paragraph 1 of Article 144 (Suspension of Personal Identification Number) of this Law have not occurred.

#### Article 146.- Modalities and Procedures for Closing, Suspension, and Reopening

Modalities and procedures for closing, suspension, and reopening of a personal identification number shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Section 4 Population Register

#### Article 147.- Population Register of the Integrated Population Identification System

The population register is the primary and unique source of population data as defined in Article 148 (Personal Identity Data in the Population Register) of this Law.

#### Article 148.- Personal Identity Data in the Population Register

Personal identity data in the population register include:

a- Personal identification number.

b- Biographical information:

- Name

- Sex

- Date of birth

- Place of birth

- Name and personal identification number of mother

- Name and personal identification number of father

- Nationality

- Ethnicity, in cases where there is clear information

- Current address

- Marital status

- Mobile phone number and/or email, if any

- Other necessary biographical information shall be determined by the National Authority for Identification Management.

c- Biometric data:

- Digital photo

- Fingerprint scan

- Iris scan

- Besides the digital photo, fingerprint scan and iris scan above, the National Authority for Identification Management may determine other biometric data as necessary and in accordance with actual circumstances. In cases where any person's biometric data cannot be captured due to physical disability not meeting technical standards, the National Authority for Identification Management shall issue instructions to determine substitute data for the biometric data that cannot be captured.

d- Information on personal identity documents already registered.

#### Article 149.- Change, Correction, and Updating of Data

The change, correction, and updating of data registered in the population register shall be determined by instructions of the National Authority for Identification Management.

#### Article 150.- Management, Storage, and Distribution of Personal Identification Data

1- The National Center for Identification Management shall be responsible for managing, storing, and distributing personal identification data in the population register with the highest security as stated in Chapter 7 (Use of Data, Personal Privacy and Data Security) of this Law.

2- Personal identification data recorded and stored in the population register shall be protected and managed by the State.

### Section 5 National Authority for Identification Management

#### Article 151.- Establishment of National Authority

The National Authority for Identification Management shall be established to lead, manage, and

ensure the organization and updating of personal identity records and the use of data. The National Authority for Identification Management has the National Center for Identification Management as its General Secretariat.

2- The organization and functioning of the National Authority for Identification Management shall be determined by Royal Decree.

## Chapter 7 Use of Data, Personal Privacy and Data Security

### Section 1 Use of Data

#### Article 152.- Right to Access Personal Identity Information

1- Every person has the right to access information relating to their personal identity which is recorded and stored in the population register, civil status register, Khmer Nationality Identity Card register, residence register, nationality register and passport register through filing an application as determined by the competent authority.

2- The application for information relating to personal identity as determined in paragraph 1 above may be made by the subject, direct ancestors, direct descendants, or spouse of the subject or any person having a legitimate interest.

3- In cases where the subject is a minor or under general guardianship, the application for information relating to personal identity may be made by the person holding parental power or the guardian for the minor or the person under general guardianship.

4- The application for information relating to the personal identity of a person shall be attached with documents that can certify the identity of the applicant and/or the applicant's representative.

5- Modalities and procedures for filing an application for information relating to personal identity shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

6- The competent authority may reject the application for information relating to the personal identity of a person in cases where the application does not meet all determined conditions. Every rejection of an application for information relating to the personal identity of a person shall be accompanied by an explanation of the reasons.

#### Article 153.- Extracts of Personal Identity Information

1- Extracts of information provided to the applicant shall be identical to the information recorded and stored in the population register, civil status register, Khmer Nationality Identity Card register, residence register, nationality register and passport register.

2- Every extract of information relating to the personal identity of a person provided to the applicant shall be certified by the competent authority.

#### Article 154.- Correction of Extracts of Information

1- In cases where the extract of information provided to the applicant is incomplete or incorrect, the applicant may apply to the competent authority for completion or correction of that information by attaching official documents or evidence. If the applicant is not the subject and the subject is still alive, the competent authority may complete or correct the information only upon notification and consent of the subject, provided the subject is an adult. In cases where the subject is a minor or under general guardianship, the competent authority shall notify and receive consent from the person holding parental power or the guardian for the minor or the person under general guardianship.

2- The application for completion or correction of information as determined in paragraph 1 above shall be made as follows:

a- In cases of application for completion or correction of information in the civil status register, the applicant shall file an application in accordance with the provisions of Article 84 (Persons who may apply for correction) and Article 87 (Correction of Civil Status Register by Court Decision) of Chapter 2 (Civil Status Registration) of this Law.

b- In cases of application for completion or correction of information in the residence register, the applicant shall file an application in accordance with the provisions of Article 119 (Correction of errors in residence registration) of Chapter 4 (Residence Registration) of this Law.

c- In cases of application for completion or correction of information in the Khmer Nationality Identity Card register, the applicant shall file an application in accordance with the provisions of Article 133 (Correction of personal identity data in the register and Khmer Nationality Identity Cards) of Chapter 5 (Khmer Nationality Identity Card Registration) of this Law.

d- In cases of application for completion or correction of information in other registers besides those defined in points a, b and c above, the applicant shall file an application to the institution or unit managing those registers for correction.

e- In cases where there is incomplete or incorrect information on the extract of information issued by the National Center for Identification Management and the applicant cannot identify the source of that incomplete or incorrect information, the applicant shall file an application to the National Center for Identification Management. After the National Center for Identification Management examines and finds that the source of the incomplete or incorrect information is in any of the registers, the National Center for Identification Management shall notify the applicant and instruct them to file an application for completion or correction to the competent authority in accordance with the provisions of points a, b or c above.

3- The competent authority may reject a proposal for completion or correction of personal identity information with a written explanation of the reasons.

#### Article 155.- Evidence of Population Register

Data in the population register is an official source of personal data.

#### Article 156.- Use of Data in the Population Register

1- Competent authorities of public institutions may use data in the population register for performing public functions assigned by law to those institutions, including for administrative tasks, economy, taxation, statistics, education, health, social affairs, security, justice, elections and other tasks serving the public interest. Data shared for performing public functions shall be limited to the scope that is necessary and proportional to the specific use. Data shall be shared in a manner that ensures the confidentiality of personal identity data, in accordance with Article 159 (Confidentiality of Personal Identity Data) of this Law.

2- Public and private service providers may use the population register or other components of the Integrated Population Identification System to verify identities. This identity verification service shall provide a response message of either "correct" or "incorrect" only, and there shall be no sharing of personal identity data as part of the response to the identity verification request.

#### Article 157.- Generation of Statistical Data from Population Register

1- The Director of the National Center for Identification Management may decide to authorize or reject a proposal from any person, institution, or unit to generate statistical data

from the population register. In cases where the Director of the National Center for Identification Management authorizes the generation of statistical data from the population register, the use of that data shall be subject to an agreement between the National Center for Identification Management and the applicant and shall maintain personal privacy, security and confidentiality.

2- Statistical data generated from the population register and provided to the applicant shall not contain any information that can identify an individual which has the risk of revealing the identity of that person.

#### Article 158.- Complaint against Decisions or Rejections relating to the Right to Access Personal Identity Information

1- Every person has the right to file a complaint against decisions or rejections relating to the right to access personal identity information as determined in Article 152 (Right to Access Personal Identity Information) or for correction of extracts of information as determined in Article 154 (Correction of Extracts of Information) of this Law.

2- Complaints as determined in paragraph 1 above shall be made as follows:

a- Complaints relating to decisions or rejections by the General Department of Identification shall be filed to the Minister of Interior within 30 (thirty) working days from the date of receipt of the decision or rejection. The Minister of Interior shall make a decision on the complaint within 30 (thirty) working days from the date of receipt of the complaint. Procedures for filing a complaint to the Minister of Interior shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Interior.

b- In cases where any person is dissatisfied with the decision of the Minister of Interior, that person may file a lawsuit to the competent court within 30 (thirty) working days from the date of receipt of the decision or rejection.

3- Complaints against decisions or rejections by the National Center for Identification Management as defined in point e of paragraph 2 of Article 154 (Correction of Extracts of Information) of this Law shall be filed directly to the competent court within 30 (thirty) working days from the date of receipt of the decision or rejection.

### Section 2 Personal Privacy Protection

#### Article 159.- Confidentiality of Personal Identity Data

1- Information contained in the registers shall be protected. The confidentiality of physical person's private information which is recorded and stored in the population register, civil status register, residence register, Khmer Nationality Identity Card register and other personal identity registers under this Law shall be protected.

2- Name lists of persons in the population register, civil status register, residence register, Khmer Nationality Identity Card register and other personal identity registers under this Law shall not be disseminated or shown by any person, institution or unit that has not received authorization from the law, or from legal instruments or decisions of the Minister of Interior.

### Section 3 Data Security

#### Article 160.- Data Security during Collection, Submission, Use and Storage of Data

#### Article 161.- Right of Public Officials to Use Personal Identity Data

1- The right of public officials to use personal identity data shall be limited to only the personal identity data that is necessary for performing the public functions of that public official.

2- Modalities, procedures and conditions for the use of personal identity data by public officials as defined in paragraph 1 above shall be determined by Sub-decree.

## Chapter 10 Penalties

#### Article 167.- Failure to Register in Civil Status and Other Registers

Any registration officer who intentionally refuses without reason to register civil status, residence, or Khmer Nationality Identity Cards required by this Law, or refuses to record information relating to civil status, residence, or Khmer Nationality Identity Cards required by this Law, shall be punished by imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 2 (two) years and a fine from 100,000 (one hundred thousand) Riels to 2,000,000 (two million) Riels.

#### Article 168.- Intentional Damage, Destruction, or Loss of Registers

Any competent officer in charge of maintaining civil status registers, residence registers, Khmer Nationality Identity Card registers or population registers required by this Law, who intentionally damages, destroys, or causes the loss of those registers, shall be punished by imprisonment from 5 (five) years to 10 (ten) years.

#### Article 169.- Intentional Deletion or Modification of Information in Registers or Entry of False Data in Registers

Any competent officer in charge of managing civil status registers, residence registers, Khmer Nationality Identity Card registers or population registers, who intentionally deletes or modifies information in the register or enters false data into those registers, shall be punished by imprisonment from 5 (five) years to 10 (ten) years.

#### Article 170.- Intentional Access to Registers

1- Any officer without authority to access civil status registers, residence registers, Khmer Nationality Identity Card registers or population registers, who intentionally accesses those registers, or any competent officer who intentionally accesses those registers beyond their authority, or any competent officer who allows another person to access or share information from those registers, shall be punished by imprisonment from 6 (six) months to 2 (two) years and a fine from 1,000,000 (one million) Riels to 4,000,000 (four million) Riels.

2- In cases where the acts defined in paragraph 1 above cause damage to the registers or operating systems, they shall be punished by imprisonment from 2 (two) years to 5 (five) years and a fine from 4,000,000 (four million) Riels to 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels.

### Annex to the Law on Civil Registration, Vital Statistics and Identification Glossary

1- Civil Status Statistics Compilation refers to the process of summarizing and condensing information on life events through classification and preparation of data tables by type or group to produce civil status statistics in accordance with predetermined tabulations.

2- Live Birth refers to the complete expulsion or extraction from its mother of a product of conception, irrespective of the duration of pregnancy, which, after such separation, breathes or shows any other evidence of life, such as beating of the heart, pulsation of the umbilical cord, or definite movement of voluntary muscles, whether or not the umbilical cord has been cut or the placenta is attached.

3- Stillbirth refers to the death of a product of conception prior to the complete expulsion or extraction of the product of conception (such as fetus, placenta, umbilical cord, amniotic fluid and amniotic sac) from its mother irrespective of the duration of pregnancy. The death is indicated by the fact that after such separation the product of conception does not breathe or show any other evidence of life, such as beating of the heart, pulsation of the umbilical cord, or definite movement of voluntary muscles.

4- Personal Identity Data refers to information that can identify an individual.

5- Complaint refers to an administrative complaint through administrative channels by any person against an action, including decisions or delays or negligence in acting by the competent authority.

6- Biometric Data refers to the measurement of biological characteristics and other characteristics such as fingerprint, iris, face, digital signature, etc., that can be captured digitally and used to identify individuals.

7- Death refers to the permanent disappearance of all evidence of life at any time after live birth has taken place.

8- Health Facility refers to public health facilities and private health facilities.

9- Public Health Facility refers to national hospitals, including military hospitals, police hospitals and referral hospitals of the capital, province, municipality, district, khan, health centers and health posts.

10- Private Health Facility refers to private hospitals, polyclinics, maternity clinics and obstetric-gynecological clinics and private clinics.

11- Medical Certificate of Cause of Death refers to a form filled out by a medical professional as determined by law for the purpose of recording and certifying the cause and type of death, based on the International Classification of Diseases (ICD).

12- Personal Identification Number refers to the identification number for each person.

13- Representative Office of the Kingdom of Cambodia refers to Embassies, Consulate Generals and Consulates.

14- Civil Status Statistics refers to statistics on life events of citizens.

15- Civil Status Certificate refers to a document in paper or electronic form issued by a civil status register officer containing information identical to all or part of the information in civil status records, and after being issued by the civil status register officer, it shall have full legal effect and validity as defined in the civil status records.

16- Identity refers to the basic characteristics of an individual's identity, including name, sex, place of birth, and date of birth.

17- Legal Identity refers to the basic characteristics of an individual's identity, including name, sex, place of birth, and date of birth, which are assigned and issued by the civil status register officer through live birth registration. In cases where there is no live birth registration, legal identity can be assigned by the identification authority recognized by law.

18- Current Address refers to the place of residence where a person is living in a specific administrative geography such as house number, street name, group, village, commune, sangkat, municipality, district, khan, capital, province in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

19- Marriage refers to a special contract arising from the mutual consent between a man and a woman who intend to live together and help each other, and that man and woman must meet the conditions as defined in the Civil Code.

20- Representative of Residence refers to any person who acts as the representative of that residence for the purpose of residence registration.

21- Reporter refers to any person or institution determined by law to report to the competent civil status register officer about life events that have occurred and provide all information and characteristics of that event as a basis for the competent civil status register officer to register that event legally.

22- Privacy refers to the private part of life that every person has the right to be protected by law.

23- Residence Identification Document refers to administrative documents used for the purpose of residence registration and management of every person living in the Kingdom of Cambodia. Residence identification documents may be in the form of a book, a card or a certificate and other administrative documents certifying the current address of one or more persons.
