### HEREBY PROMULGATE

The Law on Tourism, which was adopted by the National Assembly on April 28, 2009 during the 2nd session of the 4th legislature, and which was approved in its entirety by the Senate on its form and legal concept without any amendment on May 27, 2009 during its extraordinary session, and whose entire content is as follows:

# Law on Tourism

# Chapter 1
## General Provisions

### Article 1._

This Law has the objective to define the rules and mechanisms for the management and development of the tourism sector of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Article 2._

This Law aims to:

- Manage and develop the tourism sector with efficiency, quality, and sustainability in order to contribute to poverty reduction;

- Contribute to the protection and preservation of natural resources, culture, and customs which are the basis of the tourism sector;

- Ensure and promote the quality of tourism services in the Kingdom of Cambodia through the establishment of a quality assurance system, by providing security, safety, comfort, and increasing tourist satisfaction;

- Derive maximum benefits from the tourism sector, ensuring equitable distribution and preventing negative impacts of the tourism sector;

- Promote broad cooperation, find ways to promote Cambodia's tourism to be more effective, with participation from the public and private sectors;

- Develop human resources in the tourism sector;

- Contribute to building friendship and international understanding through the tourism industry.

### Article 3._

This Law has a scope of application covering all tourism activities in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Article 4._

Key terms used in this Law shall be defined in the Annex.

# Chapter 2
## Tourism Development Policy and Plan

### Article 5._

The Ministry of Tourism shall prepare the tourism development policy and plan for the Kingdom of Cambodia in consultation with relevant ministries, institutions, sub-national administrations, tourism industry associations, and the private sector.

The tourism development plan includes the national tourism development plan, regional tourism development plans, and tourism development plans of sub-national administrations.

Sub-national administrations shall prepare tourism development plans for their respective jurisdictions.

### Article 6._

The national tourism development plan shall:

- A- Define the national policy, strategy, and plan for the development and promotion of the tourism sector of Cambodia.

- B- Propose all measures to reduce poverty and ensure the sustainability of the national tourism development.

- C- Integrate with ASEAN tourism plans and various international tourism plans.

### Article 7._

Regional tourism development plans and tourism development plans of sub-national administrations shall:

- A- Define strategies to develop the tourism sector by region and at the sub-national administration level

- B- Propose various measures to reduce poverty and to ensure the sustainability of development and management of the tourism sector in regions and at sub-national administration levels.

Regional tourism development plans shall be prepared in alignment with the national tourism development plan. Tourism development plans of sub-national administrations shall be prepared in alignment with regional tourism development plans.

### Article 8._

National and regional tourism development plans shall be submitted to the Royal Government for approval and integration into the National Strategic Development Plan, in order to become the general policy and strategy of the Royal Government for the tourism sector in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

Tourism development plans shall be reviewed and updated as necessary, at least once every 05 (five) years.

# Chapter 3
## Competence for Management

### Article 9._

The Ministry of Tourism has the competence to lead and manage the work of the tourism sector and to perform other duties effectively in accordance with national and international practices.

The Ministry of Tourism shall lead and monitor the implementation of tourism development plans. Since the tourism sector affects and is affected by the work and competence of many other ministries, institutions, and authorities, the division of roles of the Ministry of Tourism and relevant institutions shall be made in accordance with the principles specified in the articles of this Law.

### Article 10._

The Ministry of Tourism shall have the primary responsibility to issue key regulations in the tourism industry to promote the effective implementation of the tourism development plan.

These key areas include:

- Tourism promotion inside and outside the Kingdom of Cambodia

- Quality assurance in the tourism sector

- Standards of the tourism sector

- Tourism licenses

- Tourism business activities

- Guidance to stakeholders in the tourism sector

- Management and control of tourism information

- International cooperation in the tourism sector with neighboring countries and the world.

The Ministry of Tourism shall consult with relevant ministries, institutions, and authorities in the event that any ministry, institution, or authority has an interest in consulting on any part of the tourism industry.

### Article 11._

The Ministry of Tourism shall share responsibility with other relevant ministries, institutions, and authorities in issuing key regulations that affect or are affected by the tourism industry.

These key areas include:

- Tourism industry associations

- Environmental protection

- Protection and presentation of natural heritage

- Protection and presentation of cultural heritage

- Tourism sector investment and incentives

- Management and privatization of state-owned tourism enterprises

- State land management leased for tourism purposes

- Prevention of sexual exploitation

- Tourism product development

- Ecotourism

- Community-based tourism

- Poverty reduction

- Food hygiene and safety

- Tourist safety and security

- Tourism police.

The Ministry of Tourism, ministries, institutions, and relevant authorities shall cooperate to ensure that various sectors within the tourism industry are managed and controlled effectively and in response to the tourism development plan.

#### Article 12.-

Ministries, institutions, and relevant authorities whose work impacts the tourism industry and the tourism development plan shall consult on tourism professional aspects with the Ministry of Tourism. Sectors that affect or are affected by the tourism industry may include:

- Land use planning

- Physical planning and development plan

- Infrastructure and public services

- Provision of ownership, lease, or concession for tourism purposes

- Formulation of tax policies on tourism businesses

- Visa

- Aviation policy.

To ensure effective implementation, all construction projects serving tourism activities shall be carried out in compliance with the tourism development plan and shall obtain professional tourism advice from the Ministry of Tourism.

The Ministry of Tourism shall provide technical advice to the Capital, Provincial, and Local Tourism Management and Development Committees, the Cambodia Tourism Marketing and Promotion Board, tourism industry associations, and tourism operators.

#### Article 13.-

In case of necessity, the Ministry of Tourism along with relevant ministries, institutions, and authorities may jointly issue various regulations in order to arrange and manage the work as provided in Article 11 and Article 12 of this Law effectively.

### Chapter 4

### Institutions and Finance

#### Article 14.-

The Ministry of Tourism shall strengthen and expand its resource mobilization flow to respond to the tourism development plan.

#### Article 15.-

The Ministry of Tourism shall establish the National Institute of Tourism. The National Institute of Tourism shall serve as a center for education and research on the tourism sector for the purpose of formulating tourism policies, strategies, and development plans in an effective, quality, and sustainable manner. The organization and functioning of this National Institute of Tourism shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 16.-

The Ministry of Tourism shall establish a Tourism Vocational School. This Tourism Vocational School has the mission of educating and training vocational specialists in the field of tourism

hotels and hospitality aimed at serving the tourism industry. The organization and functioning of this Tourism Vocational School shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 17.-

The Ministry of Tourism shall establish the University of Tourism and Hospitality. The University of Tourism and Hospitality is a higher education institution that provides education and training of human resources in all skills of the tourism and hospitality sectors. The organization and functioning of the University of Tourism and Hospitality shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 18.-

The Ministry of Tourism, in cooperation with the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sports, ministries, institutions, and relevant authorities, shall recognize the valuable role of the private sector in vocational education and training and skills in tourism, hotel, and hospitality as determined in various regulations stated in Chapter 5 of this Law.

#### Article 19.-

The Ministry of Tourism shall facilitate all work in creating statutes and/or statutes, internal regulations, and monitor the activities of the tourism industry association.

The tourism industry association shall have statutes and/or statutes, or internal regulations in compliance with the laws and other regulations in force.

#### Article 20.-

The Ministry of Tourism shall establish the Cambodia Tourism Marketing and Promotion Board, abbreviated as "C.T.B.", in the form of a partnership between the private sector and the public sector. The organization and functioning of the Cambodia Tourism Marketing and Promotion Board shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 21.-

Financing for supporting the development and management of the tourism sector shall come from:

- Annual national budget of the Ministry of Tourism

- Cooperation financing from various development partners

- Contributions from philanthropists, clients, and tourism industry associations

- Contributions from the Cambodia Tourism Marketing and Promotion Board

- Contributions from tourism operators

- Other financing sources or legal incomes.

The management and use of the budget for tourism development shall be determined by a joint declaration (Prakas) between the Minister of the Ministry of Tourism and the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

### Chapter 5

### Quality, Standards and Tourism Licenses

#### Article 22.-

The Ministry of Tourism shall monitor the management of all tourism businesses and activities through the issuance of licenses, establishment of rating systems, setting minimum standards, and implementation systems in order to create, improve, and maintain the quality of tourism services in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 23.-

The Ministry of Tourism shall determine standards and requirements on tourism service techniques in compliance with the best practice conditions in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

All tourism businesses shall comply with the standards and requirements determined by declaration (Prakas) of the Minister of the Ministry of Tourism.

#### Article 24.-

The Ministry of Tourism shall establish a classification system for hotels and tourist accommodation services and other tourism services in accordance with international standards and the best practices in the Kingdom of Cambodia. The classification system for hotels and tourist accommodation services shall be determined by sub-decree. The classification system for tourism services shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Tourism.

#### Article 25.-

After the classification system has been established for any tourism business activity, the tourism license issued under this Law shall specify the class grade of that business.

#### Article 26.-

The Ministry of Tourism shall introduce measures for establishing other tourism service quality assurances, such as special labels, quality certification marks, quality recognition, awards, and incentives.

The Ministry of Tourism shall consult with the tourism industry and other stakeholders in designing, developing, and implementing those measures.

The specifications of special labels, quality certification marks, quality recognition, awards, and incentives shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Tourism.

#### Article 27.-

In order to encourage quality assurance of products and services in the tourism industry, the Ministry of Tourism shall establish a special environmental label called "Green Mekong" to be awarded as an incentive to any tourism operator who has properly implemented the environmental standards of the Ministry of Environment. Only tourism operators who

received the special environmental label can use this label to stick on products and services served to the public and their customers.

#### Article 28.-

Tourism operators who own a special environmental label provided by the Ministry of Tourism may receive benefits and priority incentives from the Royal Government before other tourism operators. Among those benefits and incentives are:

- Right to operate businesses in certain special zones

- Reduction of tourism license fee

- Publicity on the website system of special award recipients

- Publicity in the book of special award recipients

- Receiving priority in promotional campaigns.

#### Article 29.-

Tourism operators who provide services may receive special awards from the Ministry of Tourism or the Royal Government or from the King. Detailed procedures and criteria for this special award shall be determined by sub-decree.

Only tourism operators who have received this special award may use this special award in communication with the public and their customers.

#### Article 30.-

Tourism business activities, as set out in Chapter 3 of this Law, shall be classified by type and responsibility of the Ministry of Tourism as follows:

**A- Tourism business activities in the sector of which the responsibility is in force as set out in Article 10 of this Law, include, among others, the following:**

- Tourist guides

- Tour operators and tourism agents

- Tourism information and promotion

- Tourism accommodation services

- Tourism resorts

- Tourism attraction destinations

- Tourism transport services

- Tourism eating houses and restaurants

- Tourism vocational schools

- Tourism entertainment centers

- Adult tourism entertainment centers

- Tourism sports activities

- Tourism events and exhibitions.

**B- Tourism business activities in the sector of which the responsibility is preventive as set out in Article 11 of this Law, include, among others, the following:**

- Eating houses and restaurants serving domestic guests

- Domestic passenger transport services

- Natural protected areas

- Mekong River dolphin areas

- Ecotourism activities

- Local community-based tourism activities

- Souvenir sales services

- Educational institutions for tourism, hotel and hospitality.

**C- Tourism business activities in the sector of which the responsibility is professional consultation as set out in Article 12 of this Law, include, among others, the following:**

- Passenger transport by air

- National cultural heritage sites

- National natural heritage sites

- Conference, exhibition, event, and national/international festival organizing services.

The Ministry of Tourism may adjust the types of tourism business activities and its responsibilities in accordance with needs or requirements defined in the Tourism Development Plan.

#### Article 31.-

In order to implement in accordance with Article 10 of Chapter 3 and the provisions set out in Chapter 5 of this Law, the Minister of the Ministry of Tourism shall issue Prakas to manage the licensing of tourism business activities under the areas for which the Ministry of Tourism is primarily responsible.

The Ministry of Tourism as well as relevant ministries, institutions, and authorities shall cooperate to issue joint Prakas to manage the areas of joint responsibility in accordance with Article 11 of Chapter 3 and the provisions of Chapter 5 of this Law.

#### Article 32.-

The Minister of the Ministry of Tourism shall issue Prakas on the types of tourism business activities that require a license from the Ministry of Tourism or from the sub-national administration.

No person may operate a tourism business if that person has not obtained a tourism license from the Ministry of Tourism or from the sub-national administration. In the event that multiple types of businesses are operated by a single person from a single location, each type of business must have a separate license, just as if different businesses were operated from different locations.

In the event that a person has one type of tourism business and has multiple branches, each branch must have a tourism license.

#### Article 33._

Tourism licenses issued by the Ministry of Tourism or sub-national administration do not exempt tourism operators from the obligation to register their businesses or obtain permission from ministries, institutions, and relevant authorities required by laws and regulations in force. The Ministry of Tourism shall cooperate with ministries, institutions, and relevant authorities to increase the efficiency of the tourism licensing process.

#### Article 34._

The Ministry of Tourism, in consultation with the private sector, ministries, institutions, and relevant authorities, shall determine minimum standards and other criteria for each type of tourism license established to create, improve, and maintain the quality of tourism services.

The criteria shall be consistent with each type of tourism license and shall relate to some or all of the following objectives:

a- Nationality, qualifications, experience, knowledge, and character of the operators and key personnel.

b- Location, buildings, equipment, registration, and financial capacity of the operators.

The criteria for each type of tourism license shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Tourism.

#### Article 35._

Criteria for issuing tourism licenses to tour guides shall include the following:

a- Must be a Cambodian national and be at least 18 years of age

b- Possess a tour guide professional certificate from a training recognized by the Ministry of Tourism.

Other criteria for issuing tourism licenses to tour guides shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 36._

An application for a tourism license or renewal of a tourism license shall be made by the tourism operator in a standard application package, including supporting documents and other information determined by Prakas of the Minister of Tourism.

The application for a tourism license must be accompanied by a licensing fee determined by a joint Prakas of the Minister of Tourism and the Minister of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 37._

An application for a tourism license shall be processed for decision when the Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration finds that the applicant has correctly filled out the sample application form for a tourism license.

#### Article 38._

The Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration shall review and evaluate the application for a tourism license based on the information received or by requesting additional information as necessary.

The Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration shall notify the applicant for a tourism license of the decision to approve or not approve within 28 (twenty-eight) days from the date of receipt of the complete application. In case of disapproval of the tourism license, the Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration must specify the reasons in writing to the applicant.

Officials of the Ministry of Tourism or officials of the sub-national administration responsible for reviewing and evaluating tourism license applications who have a personal interest shall not be allowed to participate in the approval process for such applications.

#### Article 39._

Licenses issued under this law shall contain conditions that the recipient must comply with. These conditions are as follows:

a- Comply with plans, standards on building construction, fire safety, food health, sanitation, labor, taxes, laws, and other regulations related to activities, premises, equipment, employees, and operations of the tourism business.

b- Comply with standards and requirements issued by the Ministry of Tourism as stated in Article 23 of this law.

c- Comply with the classification system issued by the Ministry of Tourism as stated in Article 24 and Article 25 of this law.

d- Comply with quality assurance measures issued by the Ministry of Tourism as stated in Article 28 of this law, and do not use information about special logos, quality certification logos, quality recognition, awards, or incentives that the business does not legitimately possess.

e- If the tourism business is classified under any level as stated in Article 24 and Article 25 of this law, that classification must be displayed in an easily visible place within the business location of the licensee, and must be stated in all marketing, advertising, and correspondence.

f- Comply with the professional code of conduct and professional code of ethics issued by the Ministry of Tourism or by the tourism industry association with the approval of the Ministry of Tourism for that type of tourism business.

g- Provide written information to the Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration within 14 (fourteen) days after any change of detail related to the business of the licensee.

h- Provide the Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration in a timely manner with statistics and other information related to the tourism business upon request and requirement by the Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration.

i- Display the tourism license in an easily visible place within the business premises of the licensee.

j- Ensure that the registration number and type of tourism license are specified in all correspondence with relevant partners and business partners.

Regarding other special conditions required for each type of tourism license shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Tourism.

#### Article 40._

Travel agencies and tour operators, in order to guarantee their business and protect customers, shall deposit a financial guarantee in the bank as cash or as a bank guarantee in an amount determined by Prakas of the Minister of Tourism.

Procedures and requirements on management of the guarantee deposit and changes to the bank guarantee deposit shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Tourism.

#### Article 41._

If the applicant meets the criteria and requirements as stated in Chapter 5 of this law, the Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration shall issue a license with a minimum validity of 01 (one) year to the applicant.

If the applicant does not fulfill the conditions as stated in Chapter 5 of this law, but if the Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration agrees to accept a written commitment from the applicant to implement the criteria and requirements

Under that requirement, the Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration may issue a temporary license with a validity of 90 (ninety) days after the applicant has paid the license fee.

Within the period specified in the contract, if it is found that the temporary licensee has complied with all the criteria and requirements, the Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration shall decide to grant a full license to the applicant. On the contrary, in the event that the applicant fails to fulfill the conditions of the contract and continues to operate the business under the temporary license that has expired, it shall be considered as operating a business without a license.

#### Article 42._

The licensee must apply for license renewal at least 30 (thirty) days before the expiration of the license's validity, accompanied by the license fee, as well as documents and other information as determined in the Prokas of the Minister of the Ministry of Tourism.

#### Article 43._

The tour guide license issued under this Law shall not be transferred or sold, because this license is based on the individual qualifications and experience of the licensee.

Other tourism licenses issued under this Law may be transferred or sold under the following conditions:

a- The licensee must submit an application for transfer or sale of the license to the Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration in accordance with the prescribed form and accompanied by the service fee for transfer or sale of the license, documents, as well as other information determined by the Ministry of Tourism.

b- The person receiving the license from the transfer or purchase of the license must fulfill all the requirements regarding the issuance of the license of the Ministry of Tourism as stated in Chapter 5 of this Law.

c- This transfer or sale of the tourism license may only take place if the licensee has fulfilled the tax obligations in accordance with the law in force and has resolved all other issues with their relevant parties, or the person receiving the license from the transfer or purchase of the license agrees in writing to fulfill the tax obligations or resolve those remaining issues, unless there is a written agreement otherwise between the relevant parties.

d- In the event that the license is a temporary license, the person receiving the license from the transfer or purchase of the license must provide the Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration with written consent to fulfill the remaining conditions within the remaining period.

#### Article 44._

If the Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration finds that the tourism licensee has provided false information related to the application, or has failed to comply with all or any of the conditions of the license, the Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration may revoke, suspend, or downgrade the license to a temporary license by giving 30 (thirty) days written notice in advance to the licensee. The notice of revocation, suspension, or downgrading of this tourism license must state clear reasons.

In an emergency situation where the Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration finds that the continuation of a tourism business operation poses an immediate serious risk to health, safety, or security, the Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration may decide to suspend or revoke that license immediately, stating clear reasons.

The revocation, suspension, or downgrading of the license as stated in this Article shall not exempt the licensee from liability for offenses and penalty provisions set forth in Chapter 10 of this Law and other laws in force.

The conditions and procedures for revocation, suspension, or downgrading of a tourism license to a temporary license shall be determined by Prokas of the Minister of the Ministry of Tourism.

#### Article 45._

In the event that the tourism licensee fails to comply with the criteria or other requirements for the license due to the death of the tourism licensee or the manager, or due to legal reasons beyond the control of the tourism licensee and which are acceptable, the Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration may issue a temporary tourism license to the tourism licensee, heir, or person responsible for the dissolution of the company in order to facilitate and rectify the issues in an orderly manner or to transfer that business.

#### Article 46._

Any person who considers that they have suffered grievance due to a decision of the Ministry of Tourism or of the sub-national administration regarding the refusal, suspension, revocation, downgrading, or other related decisions as stated in Chapter 5 of this Law, may submit a written protest to the Minister of the Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration within 30 (thirty) days after receiving written notice of any decision as described above.

After receiving the written protest, the Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration may suspend its decision and make a new decision within 60 (sixty) days.

#### Article 47._

The Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration shall maintain a registry of tourism licenses and shall publish all decisions, including denials, issuances, revocations, suspensions, and downgrades of tourism licenses in their bulletin and/or website.

The public may inspect the registry of tourism licenses at the Ministry of Tourism or the offices of the sub-national administration at any time during working hours and may request copies of those documents.

### Chapter 6
### Obligations of Relevant Parties in the Tourism Sector

#### Article 48._

Tourism operators, including agents, employees, and all contractors, shall have the following obligations:

a- Comply with laws and regulations in force relating to standards, codes of professional conduct, codes of ethics, and various conditions of the tourism license.

b- Comply with laws and regulations that affect or are affected by the tourism sector, such as urbanization zoning, building construction, safety, fire, security, public order, environment, health, hygiene, food, labor, tax obligations, accounting and finance, protection of cultural heritage, natural heritage, and the law on land traffic.

c- Protect the national interest and the interest of tourists, and contribute to the preservation of natural resources, not polluting or destroying the environment, protecting and conserving the cultural heritage, arts, traditions, and customs of the Cambodian people in all of their tourism activities.

d- Provide tourists with true and clear information, and be responsible for providing services

...as promised, and have appropriate measures to care for property, protect security and safety of tourists during the time they are under their control.

e- File a complaint or report immediately to the tourist police, competent authorities or the nearest tourism competent authority in case of knowing or having suspicion of committing offenses, drug trafficking and drug use, human trafficking and detention, child trafficking, sexual exploitation, dissemination of pornographic images and materials, or committing other criminal offenses.

f- Managers of hotels or tourist accommodation services must record the identity of customers and other information upon check-in.

g- Cooperate in inspections as stated in Article 60 of this Law.

h- Properly practice the professional code of ethics of the World Tourism Organization.

i- Strictly implement the conditions stated in Article 38 and other conditions stated in Chapter 5 of this Law.

### Article 49._

Competent officials involved in the tourism sector shall have the following obligations:

a- Provide tourists with free and unbiased information.

b- Help and assist with honesty and provide warmth in the capacity of a host.

c- Facilitate the travel of tourists, and simplify paperwork and various procedures.

d- Not discriminate against nationality, race, color, and religion.

e- Take necessary measures to protect and preserve the property of tourists and maintain security and safety.

f- Properly practice the professional code of ethics of the World Tourism Organization.

### Article 50._

Tourists or excursionists shall have the following obligations:

a- Respect the laws and regulations in force of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

b- Respect Khmer traditions, customs and culture, and shall not participate in any activity that violates or damages Khmer culture.

c- Shall not cause damage to the environment or cultural heritage and natural heritage.

d- Shall not participate in any activity involving the trafficking and use of drugs, human trafficking and detention, child trafficking, sexual exploitation, dissemination of pornographic images and materials, money laundering, and causing insecurity in society.

e- Shall not affect social order and general welfare.

f- Shall not threaten security or be involved with terrorist groups.

g- Strictly implement the professional code of conduct and professional code of ethics issued by the Ministry of Tourism or established by tourism industry associations and approved by the Ministry of Tourism.

h- Properly practice the professional code of ethics of the World Tourism Organization.

i- For foreign tourists or excursionists, they must not use tourism destinations as an opportunity to seek employment or settlement.

### Article 51._

The Ministry of Tourism shall organize, establish and encourage the creation of a professional code of conduct and professional code of ethics, with consultation with relevant ministries, institutions, authorities, tourism industry associations and other stakeholders.

### Article 52._

The Ministry of Tourism may transfer the responsibility for the establishment of the professional code of conduct and professional code of ethics to relevant tourism industry associations when those tourism industry associations are established with sufficient representation and efficiency. If the Ministry of Tourism requests the tourism industry association to create or improve the professional code of conduct and professional code of ethics, the tourism industry association must accept this work by consulting with its members and other stakeholders, and submit the draft professional code of conduct and professional code of ethics to the Ministry of Tourism for review and decision.

### Article 53._

The professional code of conduct and professional code of ethics issued by the Ministry of Tourism shall bind the stakeholders, and can be used as a condition for issuing tourism licenses upon the request of the Ministry of Tourism.

The professional code of conduct and professional code of ethics established by the tourism industry association and approved by the Ministry of Tourism shall bind its members, and the association must take appropriate measures to ensure that its members comply.

# Chapter 7
## Tourism Dispute Resolution

### Article 54._

The Ministry of Tourism shall organize and establish a commission called the "Tourism Dispute Resolution Commission" to coordinate and resolve disputes and perform obligations in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

The Minister of the Ministry of Tourism shall issue a Prakas to publicize the types of disputes that the Ministry is responsible for resolving.

### Article 55._

The types of disputes that the Ministry of Tourism is responsible for coordinating and resolving include disputes between tourism business operators, their agents, employees, contractors, competent officials involved in tourism, tourists, and other stakeholders.

The Tourism Dispute Resolution Commission shall consider and resolve disputes fairly. If the Commission cannot resolve the dispute, any party may proceed with the case to court for a legal solution.

The expenses and service fees of the Tourism Dispute Resolution Commission shall be determined by a joint Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Tourism and the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

# Chapter 8
## Prohibition of Minors

### Article 56._

Minors shall not be allowed to enter the premises of adult tourism entertainment centers.

### Article 57._

Managers and licensees of adult tourism entertainment centers must prohibit and take appropriate measures to ensure that no minors are present on the premises, and must inspect and verify the identity cards of all young customers to ensure compliance with the obligations under this Article.

The modalities for the implementation of Article 56 and Article 57 of this Law shall be determined by Sub-decree.

# Chapter 9
## Investigation, Inspection and Law Enforcement

#### Article 58.-

The Ministry of Tourism shall lead the inspection work:

a- Send officers to conduct inspection and control on business establishments, premises, or materials that have been used or are suspected of being used for tourism business purposes, and which have been used or are suspected of being used in violation of the law. In conducting inspections, the Ministry of Tourism may cooperate and coordinate with officials from other ministries, institutions, and competent authorities.

b- If the business premises are also used as a residence, the inspection can only be conducted during business hours.

#### Article 59.-

Inspection officers shall present their identity card, mission order, or mission authorization letter before starting the inspection. Tourism inspection officers shall have the status of judicial police.

#### Article 60.-

License holders, tourism operators, and persons occupying the business location, premises, or financial materials subject to inspection shall cooperate with tourism inspection officers by providing accurate, true, sufficient information, and explanation for any inquiries in order to ensure that inspection officers can perform their duties. No person shall obstruct, hinder, or interfere with the inspection work as provided in this Law.

#### Article 61.-

If the inspection officer finds any violation of the provisions of this Law, the inspection officer may take measures in accordance with this Law and, in case of necessity, request cooperation from the relevant competent authorities. In serious cases, a report shall be made immediately to the Ministry of Tourism to take measures according to the law.

In case of detecting or having reasonable grounds to believe that a criminal offense has been committed, the inspection officer shall report immediately to the prosecutor or the competent judicial police officer of the relevant area in order to take action according to legal procedures.

#### Article 62.-

The Ministry of Tourism or the sub-national administration has the right to take necessary measures to immediately halt and close any business whose business license has been revoked or suspended, or any business operating without a tourism license.

# Chapter 10
## Penalty Provisions

#### Article 63.-

Interim fines determined by this Law shall be executed by tourism inspection officers and/or tourism police. The procedures and conditions for interim fines shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Tourism.

The division of competence between tourism inspection officers and tourism police shall be determined by a joint Prakas of the Minister of Tourism and the Minister of Interior.

#### Article 64.-

The imposition of interim fines shall not release the offender from civil liability and/or criminal liability as provided by laws and regulations in force.

#### Article 65.-

Any person who operates a tourism business without a tourism license shall be subject to an interim fine in cash from a minimum of 2,000,000 (two million) Riels to a maximum of not exceeding 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels. The amount of the interim fine for each type of tourism business shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of Tourism.

#### Article 66.-

Violations of obligations as provided in Article 48 of this Law may be subject to interim fines. The acts subject to, and the amount of, this interim fine shall be determined in detail by a Prakas of the Minister of Tourism.

#### Article 67.-

Competent officials who fail to comply with the provisions of this Law shall be subject to administrative sanctions, without prejudice to other sanctions in accordance with the laws and regulations in force.

#### Article 68.-

Tourists or other travelers who violate Article 50 of this Law may have their tourism visa revoked and be expelled or prohibited from being present in tourism destination areas of the Kingdom of Cambodia for a specified period of time not exceeding 05 (five) years or permanently.

#### Article 69.-

Tourists or other travelers who cause damage to the environment, cultural heritage, or natural heritage shall be liable in accordance with laws and regulations in force.

#### Article 70.-

Managers or owners of adult entertainment tourism centers who violate Article 47 of this Law shall be subject to an interim fine in cash from 1,000,000 (one million) Riels to 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels, without prejudice to other administrative measures. In case of repeated offenses, the fine shall be doubled.

#### Article 71.-

Tourism operators who do not permit or obstruct tourism inspection officers from performing their duties as provided in this Law shall be subject to an interim fine in cash from 1,000,000 (one million) Riels to 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels, without prejudice to other administrative measures. In case of repeated offenses, the fine shall be doubled.

#### Article 72.-

Any person who instigates an offense or protects a tourism business that violates the provisions of this Law shall face administrative sanctions or criminal liability and civil compensation as provided by laws and other regulations in force.

#### Article 73.-

Any person who coerces or deceives in selling fake tourism products or services to guests shall be subject to an interim fine of double the selling price of the tourism products or services. If such act constitutes a criminal offense, it shall be punished under the criminal law in force. In this case, the Ministry of Tourism may revoke, suspend, or convert the tourism license into a temporary license.

### Article 74._

Any person who falsifies a tourism license or other public documents shall be punished in accordance with the criminal law in force.

# Chapter 11
## Transitional Provisions

### Article 75._

Tourism licenses issued by the Ministry of Tourism prior to the entry into force of this Law shall remain valid and effective until their expiration date as specified on those tourism licenses.

Any tourism operators who operate tourism service businesses without a tourism license shall submit an application for a tourism license within 03 (three) months after the entry into force of this Law.

# Chapter 12
## Final Provisions

### Article 76._

Any provisions contrary to this Law shall be abrogated.

### Article 77._

This Law shall be declared as urgent.

# Appendix

Definitions of certain words and terms used in this Law are as follows:

- Incentive: refers to the reward provided by the Ministry of Tourism under Article 26 to encourage tourism businesses or individuals with outstanding achievements in organizing tourism exhibitions, products or tourism services, and that meet the quality assurance standards of tourism products or services.

- Sole Responsibility: refers to the direct management competence of the Ministry of Tourism in being responsible for leading and managing the development of key sectors of the tourism industry, and leading the issuance of regulatory management and tourism licensing, as stipulated in Article 10, Article 30 (a) under Chapter 3 and Chapter 5 of the Law on Tourism.

- Shared Responsibility: refers to the management competence of the Ministry of Tourism to cooperate with relevant ministries, institutions, and authorities in the shared responsibility of managing sectors that affect or are affected by the tourism industry, by issuing joint regulations to improve management efficiency, as stipulated in Article 11, Article 30 (b) under Chapter 3 and Chapter 5 of the Law on Tourism.

- Responsibility by Consensus: refers to the responsibility of the Ministry of Tourism to ensure the sustainability of tourism development by providing professional advice on tourism to relevant ministries, institutions, and authorities whose work affects or is affected by the tourism industry, as defined in Article 12, Article 30 (c) under Chapter 3 and Chapter 5 of the Law on Tourism.

- Quality Recognition: refers to the certificate issued by the Ministry of Tourism or by a tourism industry association to certify and recognize the quality of tourism products or services that meet the quality assurance standards defined by the Ministry of Tourism.

- Travel Agent: refers to any person who organizes and sells tour packages and aggregates other tourism services for sale directly or through a travel agent or other contracted parties.

- Tourism License Fee: refers to the fee to be paid to obtain a tourism license from the Ministry of Tourism or sub-national administrations.

- Code of Professional Conduct: refers to regulations defined for each type of profession in the tourism industry, which are restrictive or mandatory in nature, including penalties for those who commit violations or fail to comply with the profession they are practicing.

- Code of Professional Ethics: refers to advisory rules established for each type of profession in the tourism industry, characterized as highly decent behavior coupled with education, guidance, and explanation to tourism operators, with the aim of promoting excellent professional practices and compliance with professional ethics.

- Cambodia Tourism Research and Promotion Board: refers to a legal entity serving as a partnership between the private and public sectors, established to strengthen, expand, and effectively implement tourism research and promotion activities of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

- Tour Package: refers to a package of tourism services organized by a tour operator, which includes at least two of the essential services such as tourist accommodation, transport services, food and beverage services, sightseeing, tour guide services, and other related services, sold at a specific price and within a specific duration of operation.

- Tourism Purpose: refers to the main purposes such as rest, leisure, recreation, religion, sports activities, business, research, visiting relatives and friends, medical treatment, missions, workshops, conferences, and other lawful tourism purposes.

- Tourism Attraction Destination: refers to any place that has tourist attraction, tourism facilities, comfortable services, and accessibility.

- Tourism Dispute Resolution Commission: refers to an organization established by the Ministry of Tourism based on necessity to coordinate, resolve disputes, and ensure the performance of contractual obligations under the provisions of the Law on Tourism.

- Tourism and Hospitality Training Institution: refers to educational institutions such as universities, colleges, and institutes established by the public sector, private sector, joint venture, or non-governmental organizations that provide training in tourism, hotel, and hospitality skills.

- Tourism Zone: refers to areas in the Kingdom of Cambodia selected, organized, and developed for tourism purposes and activities.

- Tourism: refers to the activities of persons traveling to and staying in places outside their usual environment for a specific period of not more than one consecutive year for leisure, recreation, business, and other lawful tourism purposes.

- Tourist: refers to any person, either Cambodian or foreign national, who arrives at a place outside their usual environment and stays there for at least one night and not exceeding the designated duration for leisure, recreation, business, and other lawful tourism purposes, and whose visit is not related to establishing residence or performing any activities for which they receive remuneration at that place.

- Person: refers to a natural person or a legal entity.

- Tourism Operator: refers to any person who operates, practices a profession in, or runs a tourism service business.

- Tourism Product: refers to existing resources or created resources such as cultural heritage, natural resources, service facilities, goods, services...

Other products which can meet the needs, desires, and tourism activities.

- National Tourism Development Plan: refers to the entire plan for the management and development of national tourism, incorporating policies, plans, and strategies prepared by the Ministry of Tourism.

- Regional Tourism Development Plan: refers to the general plan for the management and development of tourism in a specific designated region, incorporating policies, plans, and strategies prepared by the Ministry of Tourism.

- Sub-national Administration Tourism Development Plan: refers to the general plan for the management and development of tourism in a municipality, province, town, district, or khan, incorporating policies, plans, and strategies prepared by that municipality, province, town, district, or khan and the Ministry of Tourism.

- Tourism Attraction: refers to cultural heritage, natural resources, events, businesses, premises, equipment/facilities, or other attractions with special characteristics to serve recreation, leisure, entertainment, and other lawful tourism purposes.

- Travel Agent: refers to a person of Khmer nationality who runs a business helping to sell tourism services, such as transport tickets, tourism accommodation services, and other tourism services, including tour packages created by tour operators.

- Visitor: refers to a person who travels for recreation, leisure, entertainment, business, and other lawful tourism purposes, as a tourist or excursionist.

- Tour Guide: refers to a person who practices the profession of receiving and guiding national and international tourists and providing explanations/interpretations about the geography, nature, history, culture, traditions, arts, customs, and socio-economy of the visited places.

- Tourism Entertainment Center: refers to all service locations that operate business serving recreation, leisure, and entertainment according to lawful tourism purposes, and those types of businesses are determined by a declaration of the Minister of Ministry of Tourism.

- Adult Tourism Entertainment Center: refers to all service locations that operate business serving recreation, leisure, and entertainment for adults, and those types of businesses are determined by a sub-decree.

- Food and Beverage Service Establishment: refers to eateries, restaurants, buildings, or locations for banquets or clubs/lounges that operate to serve food and beverage services or other entertainment services for guests.

- Tourism Accommodation Service Establishment: refers to hotels, apartments, motels, guesthouses, villas, bungalows, campsites, and other tourism accommodation lodging places.

- Award: refers to the award sponsored by the Ministry of Tourism under Article 26 of this Law, for the recognition of tourism businesses or individuals with outstanding achievements in implementing product quality assurance standards or tourism services determined by the Ministry of Tourism.

- Tourism Resort: refers to a location with tourism attractions, such as culture, history, nature, coastal areas, or artificially created/developed sites, which is permitted to be organized for business to serve recreation, leisure, and entertainment according to lawful tourism purposes.

- Sub-national Administration: in this Law, refers to capital administration, provincial administrations, municipal administrations, district administrations, and khan administrations.

- Most Favorable Conditions in the Kingdom of Cambodia: refers to all conditions that are guaranteed to ensure quality, security, safety, and harmonization that are not contrary to the environment, culture, traditions, public order, and the nation.

- National Institute of Tourism: refers to a study and research center in the tourism sector aiming to formulate tourism policies, strategies, and development plans with efficiency, quality, and sustainability.

- Tourism Industry Association: refers to a legal entity representing groups of tourism businesses that have similar types and activities, established to promote and protect the common interests of their groups.

- University of Tourism and Hospitality: refers to a higher education institution responsible for educating and training human resources in all skills of the tourism, hotel, and hospitality sectors.

- Vocational School of Tourism: refers to an educational institution established by the public sector, private sector, joint public-private partnership, or non-governmental organization, responsible for educating and training vocational specialists in tourism, hotel, and hospitality, aiming to serve the tourism industry.

- Domestic Transport Service: refers to a business established to provide land and water transport services for the general public, including tourists.

- Tourism Transport Service: refers to a business established to provide transport services for tourism by land and water, including sea cruise ships.

- Community-Based Tourism: refers to a tourism site or attraction organized by the local community itself or through cooperation to strengthen local development, aiming to reduce poverty, promote the livelihood of the community, and serve the interests of local residents through sustainable tourism development.

- Quality Certification Label: refers to a label determined by the Ministry of Tourism under Article 26 of this Law to identify the quality of tourism products/services, for use in each type of various businesses in the tourism industry.

- Special Label: refers to a label determined by the Ministry of Tourism under Article 26 of this Law to identify special characteristics and other special qualities of tourism products that are found to comply with the quality assurance standards of tourism products/services.

- Minor: refers to a person under 18 years of age.

- Tourism Business: refers to a business in tourism services as stated in Article 30 of the Law on Tourism.

- Tourism License: refers to a license issued by the Ministry of Tourism or sub-national administrations under Article 30 of this Law, as a full license or temporary license.

- Tourism Eatery and Restaurant: refers to a building or location established mainly to serve food and beverage services for guests.

- Eatery and Restaurant Serving Domestic Guests: refers to a building or location established mainly to serve food and beverage services for the general public.

- Ecotourism: refers to a form of nature-based tourism that aims to conserve the natural environment and local culture, help improve the livelihoods and well-being of the local community, provide good experiences to tourists, and involve responsible travel, and enjoyment, appreciation, and learning from nature.

- Eco-label: refers to a special environmental label determined by the Ministry of Tourism under Article 27 for ecotourism products.

- Excursionist: refers to any person, Cambodian or foreigner, who travels to any area for recreation, leisure, entertainment, business, and other lawful tourism activities for a period of less than one year.

- Tourism Industry: refers to a sector of the service industry established to supply the needs of tourists and society, collectively comprising all services directly and indirectly related to the development of the tourism sector and helping to make tour packages feasible.
