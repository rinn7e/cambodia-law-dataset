### Promulgates

The Law on Roads, which was adopted by the National Assembly on April 3, 2014, at its 2nd session of the 5th legislature, and which was reviewed and entirely approved by the Senate as to its form and legal aspects on April 11, 2014, at its 3rd extraordinary session, the full text of which is as follows:

# Law on Roads

# Chapter 1
# General Provisions

### Article 1.-

This Law aims to manage and develop the road infrastructure sector and to ensure traffic safety in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Article 2.-

This Law has the following objectives:

- Protect public property, protect and improve road quality, maintain order, and facilitate travel and transport by land

- Determine policies, strategies, and development plans for the construction, repair, and maintenance of road infrastructure, as well as determine technical rules and regulations

- Promote and encourage the private sector to participate in the construction, repair, maintenance, and development of the road infrastructure sector

- Promote cooperation and national, regional, sub-regional, and international integration in the road infrastructure sector

- Encourage the study and research of technology and the transfer of new technologies for the development of road infrastructure in the Kingdom of Cambodia

- Develop human resources in the road infrastructure sector in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Article 3.-

This Law has the scope of application to all activities related to the road infrastructure sector in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Article 4.-

Technical terms used in this Law are defined in the Glossary, which is the Annex of this Law.

# Chapter 2
# Competent Authorities for Road Management

### Article 5.-

Competent authorities for road management shall be determined as follows:

- 1- Ministry of Public Works and Transport has the competence to manage expressways, national roads, provincial roads, and other roads assigned by the Royal Government.

- 2- Ministry of Rural Development has the competence to manage rural roads and other roads assigned by the Royal Government.

- 3- Sub-national administrations have the competence to manage capital roads, municipal roads, and urban roads in provinces, which are constructed, repaired, and maintained using the budget of the sub-national administrations and budget mobilized from other sources, and other roads transferred from the Ministry of Public Works and Transport and the Ministry of Rural Development to sub-national administrations, as well as other roads after approval from the Royal Government.

The allocation of competence to manage other roads shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 6.-

Sub-national administrations have the right to manage and administer roads under their competence in accordance with point 3 of Article 5 of this Law. For all planning, road development, and design studies that require high technology, sub-national administrations may request support from the Ministry of Public Works and Transport or the Ministry of Rural Development according to the ministry's expertise and must comply with technical regulations, urban planning regulations, and heritage regulations, and in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

# Chapter 3
# Types, Names and Classification of Roads

### Article 7.-

Roads in the Kingdom of Cambodia are classified into types as follows:

- 1- Expressways.

- 2- National roads.

- 3- Provincial roads.

- 4- Rural roads.

- 5- Capital roads, municipal roads, and urban roads in provinces.

- 6- Other types of roads determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 8.-

Expressways, national roads, provincial roads, capital roads, municipal roads, urban roads in provinces, and rural roads must have road names and road codes and shall be determined by Sub-decree.

The naming of roads or numbering of roads shall be determined as follows:

- 1- Road names may be named after heroes, dignitaries, benefactors, historical names, physical geographical names, cultural names, ancient names, or customary names.

- 2- Road numbers must be assigned according to a methodology that can clearly identify the road location, road type, and road priority.

- 3- Road names or road numbers connecting to international roads shall be determined by the decision of the Royal Government. Roads connecting to international roads must have both the national road name and number, and the international road name and number.

The formalities and procedures for naming or numbering roads and changing road names or numbers shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 9.-

Competent authorities for road management shall prepare and classify road classifications based on administrative geographical management, road functions, and road technical regulations, and must conduct a road inventory consultation with relevant competent road management authorities at least once every 5 (five) years to classify roads and change road classifications.

Road functions, road classification, and changes to road classifications shall be determined by Sub-decree.

# Chapter 4
# Road Development and Maintenance

### Article 10.-

Competent authorities for road management shall prepare a road development master plan, design plans, and determine priorities for construction, rehabilitation, improvement, expansion, and maintenance projects, as well as other road infrastructure development activities, to submit for approval from the Royal Government.

The formalities and methods of establishing the road development master plan, design plan preparation, and project prioritization shall be determined by inter-ministerial declaration (Prakas) between the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport and the Minister of the Ministry of Rural Development.

### Article 11.-

For all construction, expansion of roads, intersections, interchanges, bridges, flyovers, bridges

crossing over, underground roads or underpasses, underwater roads, roads above or below railways, especially roads with traffic congestion on a daily basis, public written notification must be made, and construction design proposals must be submitted to the relevant competent authorities. In this case, if such construction or expansion of the road affects legal property, the owner may receive fair and just compensation in accordance with the Law on Expropriation.

### Article 12.-

Any permanent construction on land reserved for road infrastructure development, especially on the road right-of-way (road shoulder land), is prohibited, except for construction under certain necessary projects such as facilities serving national defense and national security, facilities serving management, or road construction and other public service construction facilities, which must request approval in principle from the Royal Government upon the proposal of the competent road management authority.

Road right-of-way land may be used for other temporary purposes, provided that it does not affect facilities and other structures for road traffic safety.

The management of the use of road right-of-way land along the road network shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 13.-

Competent road management authority or private sector partners who receive rights to construct or develop new roads must equip sufficient road facilities in accordance with technical regulations before officially putting them into use.

### Article 14.-

The construction of new roads must reserve road right-of-way land and land for other necessary road infrastructure structures.

In particular, the construction of roads in cities, towns (Borey), and new development areas must construct sidewalks (pedestrian ways), pathways for disabled persons, and other necessary road infrastructure structures that ensure traffic safety.

The reservation of road right-of-way land and land for road infrastructure structures above must be publicly disseminated, and map layouts of the reserved road right-of-way land and land for road infrastructure structures must be provided to the Ministry of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction and the Ministry of Interior to cooperate in taking protective measures.

### Article 15.-

For all construction of roads, intersections, interchanges, bridges, flyovers, overpasses, underground roads, underpasses, underwater roads, or railway crossings, it must be constructed in accordance with technical regulations as provided in Article 23 of this Law.

### Article 16.-

Roads must be maintained regularly. Road maintenance shall be categorized as periodic maintenance, routine maintenance, and emergency repair.

Roads officially put into use must be properly managed and maintained in accordance with the technical regulations and maintenance procedures as provided in Article 23 of this Law, by keeping the roads in good condition, conducting audits, and regularly inspecting road infrastructure components.

### Article 17.-

The competent road management authority shall establish weighing stations at necessary locations, parking stations, parking bays, rest areas, and leisure areas along the road network. In case of necessity, the competent road management authority may construct toll stations along the road network to serve transportation services, road protection and maintenance, after receiving approval from the Royal Government.

All structures as provided in the 1st paragraph above must comply with technical regulations as provided in Article 23 of this Law.

### Article 18.-

For all road infrastructure development and maintenance projects that receive approval in principle from the Royal Government, the competent road management authority may implement these projects by itself or permit the private sector to participate in road infrastructure development and maintenance in accordance with the Law on Public Procurement.

In cases where a concessionaire or road construction company implements a development project or road maintenance project, they must comply with the provisions of this Law, the Law on Concessions, and other relevant regulations in force.

### Article 19.-

Concessionaires or road construction companies must cooperate with relevant ministries and institutions that perform roles within their competence as managers, controllers, auditors, or road dispute resolvers, and must regularly report on construction, road maintenance, traffic accidents, and submit urgent reports in the event of any occurrence related to the roads.

# Chapter 5
# Technical Standards and Technical Regulations for Road Infrastructure

### Article 20.-

The competent road management authority shall organize its organizational structure to respond to the road infrastructure development plan and public service needs.

### Article 21.-

The competent road management authority shall establish a National Institute for Road Infrastructure Development. This Institute has the role to study, research, experiment, train, and act as a management center to collect and disseminate information related to the road infrastructure sector.

The organization and functioning of the National Institute for Road Infrastructure Development shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 22.-

The competent road management authority shall establish a national laboratory for the road sector. This laboratory has the role to test quality, evaluate construction materials, and other material substances used in implementation work, and issue quality certificates related to the public works sector.

The organization and functioning of the national laboratory for the road sector shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 23.-

The Ministry of Public Works and Transport has the competence to prepare technical regulations for road infrastructure and other technical requirements to comply with international technical regulations for the construction of road infrastructure, as follows:

- 1- Technical regulations on road construction, bridge construction.

- 2- Technical regulations on road equipment, drainage facilities, and civil structures.

- 3- Technical regulations on safety and maintenance of heavy machinery.

- 4- Technical regulations on occupational safety for public works construction workers.

- 5- Technical regulations and procedures for road maintenance and repair.

- 6- Technical regulations on construction of vehicle weighing stations, parking stations, parking bays, rest areas, and leisure areas along the road network.

- 7- Other technical regulations serving the public works sector in terms of road infrastructure.

Each technical regulation shall be determined by the declaration of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

# Chapter 6
# Use of Roads

### Article 24.-

All road users and persons involved with roads must comply with the provisions of this Law and the Law on Land Traffic.

This Law shall have priority in the event that the provisions of the Law on Land Traffic are inconsistent with the provisions of this Law.

### Article 25.-

Users of transport vehicles on roads must comply with the weight limits as provided in Article 26 of this Law, and must enter for weight inspection at the vehicle weighing stations located along each road network, as well as comply with technical standards of vehicle size limits.

Technical standards of vehicle size limits shall be determined by the declaration of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport and the declaration of the Minister of the Ministry of Rural Development.

The inspection of transport vehicles exceeding the weight limits is the competence of the Committee for Directing the Inspection of Overweight Transport Vehicles.

The organization and functioning of the Committee for Directing the Inspection of Overweight Transport Vehicles shall be determined by Sub-decree.

### Article 26.-

The maximum transport weight on the road network is as follows:

1- On expressways, national roads, provincial roads, capital roads, municipal roads, urban roads in provinces, and rural roads, it shall be implemented as follows:

A- The maximum weight limit distributed on the axles of trucks, trailers, or semi-trailers shall be limited to:

- 6 (six) tons for a single axle with 2 (two) wheels, located at the steering part of the truck

- 11 (eleven) tons for a single axle with 4 (four) wheels, located at the steering part of the truck

- 10 (ten) tons for a single axle with 4 (four) wheels

- 19 (nineteen) tons for a tandem axle with 8 (eight) wheels

- 24 (twenty-four) tons for a triple axle with 12 (twelve) wheels.

B- The maximum allowable gross weight limit of trucks shall be limited to:

- 16 (sixteen) tons for a truck with 2 (two) axles, which are a single front axle of the truck with 2 (two) wheels and a single rear axle of the truck with 4 (four) wheels

- 25 (twenty-five) tons for a truck with 3 (three) axles, which are a single front axle of the truck with 2 (two) wheels and a tandem rear axle of the truck with 8 (eight) wheels

- 30 (thirty) tons for a truck with 4 (four) axles, which are a tandem front axle of the truck with 4 (four) wheels and a tandem rear axle of the truck with 8 (eight) wheels.

C- The maximum allowable gross weight limit of trailer-towed trucks shall be limited to:

- 35 (thirty-five) tons for a trailer-towed truck with 4 (four) axles, which are a single front axle of the truck with 2 (two) wheels, a single rear axle of the truck with 4 (four) wheels, and a tandem rear axle of the trailer with 8 (eight) wheels

- 40 (forty) tons for a trailer-towed truck with 5 (five) axles or more.

D- The maximum allowable gross weight limit of semi-trailer-towed trucks shall be limited to:

- 35 (thirty-five) tons for a semi-trailer-towed truck with 4 (four) axles, which are a single front axle of the truck with 2 (two) wheels, a single rear axle

Vehicles with 4 (four) axles and a twin axle at the rear of the semi-trailer with 8 (eight) wheels:

- 40 (forty) tons for semi-trailer truck combinations with 5 (five) axles.

For the weight of vehicles and vehicle combinations of trailers or semi-trailers not specified above, special permission must be requested from the competent authority managing the road.

2- All wheel axle loads of vehicles as specified in point 1 above shall have a pressure on the road surface of not more than 5 (five) kg/cm².

#### Article 27._

Road users, when arriving at a ferry crossing, steep slopes, or roads requiring high caution, must respect and follow the instructions of the ferry crossing agents or safety agents stationed at those destinations.

Regulations on the use of ferry crossings, steep slopes, or roads requiring high caution shall be determined by a declaration (Prakas) of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport and a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Rural Development.

#### Article 28._

The organization of various activities such as cultural, sporting, exhibitions, and other festivals on the road must comply with the following instructions:

1- The organizer must submit a proposal to use the road, accompanied by a traffic safety plan, to the sub-national administration. This activity can take place after obtaining permission from the sub-national administration according to the level of the event and as necessary.

2- The sub-national administration must disseminate information regarding the road closure plan in a timely manner and take appropriate measures to protect public order and traffic safety.

3- The sub-national administration must cooperate with the organizer to arrange prevention and ensure traffic safety.

The rules and procedures for using the road shall be determined by an inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of the Ministry of Interior and the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport, or an inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of the Ministry of Interior and the Minister of the Ministry of Rural Development.

#### Article 29._

The use of roads and other road-related activities along the streets in the capital, cities, and urban areas shall be implemented as follows:

A- Road shoulders and pavements/sidewalks shall be used only for the purpose of pedestrian traffic.

B- Road shoulders and pavements/sidewalks may be used for other purposes in compliance with the provisions of Article 28 of this Law.

C- The following activities shall not be carried out:

- Parking vehicles or means of transportation improperly along roads, shoulders, and pavements/sidewalks other than the designated locations.

- Constructing barriers across, speed bumps, or similar objects on the road without permission.

- Carrying out other activities contrary to this Law.

#### Article 30._

In order to ensure safe traffic on expressways, national roads, and provincial roads, all construction of structures with exit and entry access connected to the above roads shall be implemented as follows:

1- The construction of structures such as gas stations, gas stations (LPG), vehicle washing locations, garages, or other similar structures where people gather moderately must be located at a distance of at least 15 (fifteen) meters from the boundary line of the road right-of-way.

2- The construction of commercial centers, stadiums, arenas, theaters, hospitals, educational establishments, markets, flea markets, commodity exhibitions, or other activities where many people gather must be located at a distance of at least 50 (fifty) meters from the boundary line of the road right-of-way.

3- The specifications as set forth in points 1 and 2 above shall not apply to any structures previously constructed in accordance with the regulations in force.

4- The distance specifications as set forth in points 1 and 2 above for rural roads shall be separately determined by a Sub-decree.

#### Article 31._

All construction of exit and entry access points of structures connecting to the road must obtain written permission from the competent authority managing the road.

The owner of the structure who obtains permission shall bear the responsibility for the construction, repair, and maintenance of this access point in accordance with the specified technical conditions.

#### Article 32._

All construction of technical infrastructure networks, such as electric power lines, fiber optic cables, telecommunication networks, sewage pipes, clean water distribution pipes, or oil and gas distribution pipes, crossing or along the road right-of-way must obtain written permission from the competent authority managing the road.

The rules and procedures for permitting the construction of technical infrastructure networks shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport or a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Rural Development.

#### Article 33._

The permit holder for the construction of technical infrastructure networks, as specified in Article 32 of this Law, must arrange to set up traffic safety equipment or signs within the construction site.

After completion of the work site, the permit holder must restore the road right-of-way or any affected part of the road to its original condition in accordance with road technical standards, and shall be responsible for all damage caused by the construction of that technical infrastructure network.

#### Article 34._

Road right-of-way lands shall not be permitted for other purposes other than the construction, widening, maintenance, protection, and development of roads, and planting trees and gardens to improve aesthetics and the environment.

The temporary use of road right-of-way lands for installing technical infrastructure networks or organizing other activities is subject to service fees for the benefit of the national budget.

This service fee shall be determined by an inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance and the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport, or an inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance and the Minister of the Ministry of Rural Development.

#### Article 35._

The temporary use of road right-of-way lands, as specified in Article 34 of this Law, shall be terminated and the condition of that road right-of-way land shall be restored to its original state in the event of necessary requirements for the construction, maintenance, protection, and development of the road.

The State shall not be responsible for any damage to property or structures of individuals or enterprises built on the road right-of-way lands.

#### Article 36._

All vehicles traveling on roads shall pay a contribution for road maintenance.

The modalities of the contribution payment shall be determined by law or Sub-decree.

#### Article 37._

Every person has the obligation to participate in preserving and protecting the road infrastructure, and must immediately report to the competent authority managing the road or the nearest local authority any cases of destruction or cases that could cause danger to the road infrastructure and traffic safety.

### Chapter 7
Prohibitions

#### Article 38._

Any activities that cause obstacles or damage to the road infrastructure and traffic safety shall be prohibited as follows:

1- Intentional destruction of the components of the road infrastructure:

- Excavating, cutting, lifting, or drilling the road, shoulders, or road right-of-way lands without permission.

- Excavating soil or sand near the embankment slope or dam slope of the road, causing subsidence or erosion of the road embankment.

- Driving vehicles or steel-tracked vehicles directly, or moving heavy objects on the road surface, causing damage to the road surface.

- Importing vehicles, machinery (onto the road right-of-way).

- Constructing in the road right-of-way, filling in drainage channels intended for road safety.

2- Creating obstacles hindering traffic safety:

- Making the road surface slippery, sticky, and causing tilting or collapse of road equipment/facilities.

- Discharging wastewater, soapy water, acid, or other chemical substances onto the road surface, shoulders, or road right-of-way lands, causing damage to the road.

- Burning trash, grass, or other objects that cause smoke blocking the traffic space or damaging the road surface.

- Discharging flood water, irrigation drainage water, or other water sources onto the road.

- Driving vehicles or other means of transport carrying goods that drop, spill, leak gravel, sand, soil, waste, or other objects on the road surface.

- Tying, releasing, or leading cows, buffaloes, horses, and other beasts of burden on the road, shoulders, or road right-of-way lands, causing disruption or traffic accidents.

- Washing vehicles or other means of transport, or repairing vehicles or other means of transport on the roadway, shoulders, or road right-of-way lands, causing disruption to traffic flow or damage to the road surface.

3- Using road right-of-way lands contrary to the legislative purpose:

- Constructing booths, vending, constructing structures, pitching tents, or organizing other ceremonies on the roadway, shoulders, or road right-of-way lands, which disrupts public order, aesthetics, and traffic safety.

- Displaying materials or various signs that block the view or reduce the caution of drivers, or create obstacles for pedestrians.

- Constructing protruding walls or any structure on the road edges or road shoulders.

- Landfilling higher than the road without a proper drainage system.

- Constructing roads or connecting roads to main roads without legal permission.

4- All other activities causing damage to the road, posing danger to people and means of transport on the road.

### Chapter 8
Road Licenses

#### Article 39._

Doing business in the road infrastructure sector, road maintenance, and establishing road sector laboratories must have a license.

The conditions and procedures for applying for a license to conduct business in the road infrastructure sector, road maintenance, and establishing road sector laboratories shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

#### Article 40._

Applying for a license in the road infrastructure sector, road maintenance, and establishing laboratories related to the road sector is subject to licensing fees.

Licensing fees shall be determined by an inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance and the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport, or an inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance and the Minister of the Ministry of Rural Development, and shall be allocated for the benefit of the national budget.

### Chapter 9
Budget for Road Development and Maintenance

#### Article 41._

At the end of each year, the Ministry of Public Works and Transport, the Ministry of Rural Development, and the sub-national administrations must prepare a budget plan for road development and maintenance within the framework of their budget packages.

#### Article 42._

The budget for road development and maintenance shall be subject to the Law on Finance and shall have sources from

resources from:

- National budget

- Cooperative financing from various development partners

- Financing from the private sector

- Charitable donations from philanthropists, national and international organizations, associations, or communities

- Profits from investment and lawful businesses in the road sector

- Transitional fines

- Income from other lawful sources.

### Chapter 10
Road Inspection

#### Article 43._

The competent authority managing roads has the right to appoint road inspection officials to monitor, investigate, inspect, and expedite the enforcement of the law.

Road inspection officials shall be granted the status of judicial police to investigate offenses as set forth in this Law in compliance with the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Code.

The formalities and procedures for granting judicial police status to road inspection officials shall be determined by an inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of the Ministry of Justice and the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport, or an inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of the Ministry of Justice and the Minister of the Ministry of Rural Development.

#### Article 44._

Road inspection officials have the right to request cooperation and assistance from relevant competent institutions to participate in suppressing offenses as set forth in this Law.

#### Article 45._

During law enforcement operations, road inspection officials must wear uniforms, hold identity cards, and have mission authorization letters.

The uniforms and identity cards of road inspection officials shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

#### Article 46._

Road inspection officials have the right to inspect and take measures as follows:

1- Enter construction and maintenance sites and conduct regular inspections of road infrastructure components and private laboratories doing business in the road sector.

- Receive authority and obtain document samples from supply sources and various material production locations that serve the construction of road infrastructure under technical construction and environmental conditions that may cause disaster and impact the road infrastructure.

- Inspect, halt, and apply transitional fines on all disasters and impacts to the road infrastructure.

- Temporarily revoke relevant permits or licenses in the event that the holder of the permit or license violates this Law.

Procedures for road inspection shall be determined by an inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport and the Minister of the Ministry of Rural Development.

#### Article 47._

Any person who is dissatisfied with any measure of the competent road inspection official may lodge a protest/appeal within 30 (thirty) working days to the competent authority managing roads, counting from the date of receiving the decision.

The Minister, Head of Institution, must decide on the appeal within a maximum of 45 (forty-five) working days, counting from the date of receiving the complaint.

In the event that the person is dissatisfied with the decision of the Minister, Head of Institution, that person has the right to appeal to other mechanisms of the Royal Government or to the court according to the procedures.

### Chapter 11
Penal Provisions

#### Article 48._

Sanctions for offenses under this Law include written warning, revocation or suspension of certificates or licenses, transitional fines, monetary fines, and imprisonment.

#### Article 49._

Written warnings, revocations, or suspensions of certificates and licenses shall be under the authority of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport and the Ministry of Rural Development.

The execution of transitional fines shall be the authority of the road inspection officials within the territorial boundary of the unit to which they are assigned to serve.

#### Article 50._

Road inspection officials have the right to impose transitional fines on offenses as set forth in Article 52 to Article 70 of this Law.

Transitional fines can be applied in the event that the offender confesses to the offense and agrees to pay the transitional fine in compliance with the penal provisions of this Law. Payment of the transitional fine shall extinguish the criminal action.

In the event that the offender refuses to pay the transitional fine, the competent road inspection official must compile the offense file and forward it to the court according to the procedures.

In the event that the identity of the offender is unknown, the road inspection official must request the competent prosecutor to confiscate the instrumentalities/exhibits of the offense as state property. Any person with a relevant interest has the right to appeal the prosecutor's decision to the civil court.

Procedures for determining transitional fines on offenses as set forth in this Law shall be determined by an inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance and the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport, or an inter-ministerial Prakas between the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance and the Minister of the Ministry of Rural Development.

#### Article 51._

Transitional fines, fines imposed by a court judgment or final ruling, or proceeds from the sale of confiscated exhibits that the court decides to confiscate as state property must be paid into the national budget.

The Royal Government may decide to award incentives to officials who participated in suppressing specific offenses that caused serious disaster to the road infrastructure.

#### Article 52._

Any person who obtains the right to construct or develop a new road who:

1- Fails to equip the road infrastructure according to the construction design plan, shall be subject to a transitional fine of 5,000,000 (five million) Riels, and shall be required to completely equip the road infrastructure in accordance with the construction design plan specified in the contract.

2- Equips the road infrastructure insufficiently according to the construction design plan and not in compliance with technical regulations, shall be subject to a transitional fine of 2,000,000 (two million) Riels, and shall be required to completely equip the road infrastructure in accordance with the construction design plan specified in the contract.

3- Equips the road infrastructure sufficiently according to the construction design plan, but not in compliance with technical regulations, shall be subject to a transitional fine of 1,000,000 (one million) Riels, and shall be required to completely equip the road infrastructure in accordance with the construction design plan specified in the contract.

#### Article 53._

Legal entities may be declared criminally responsible under the conditions stipulated in Article 42 (Criminal Liability of Legal Entities) of the Criminal Code for offenses set forth in Article 52 above.

Legal entities shall be punished according to the following cases:

1- In the case of violating point 1 of Article 52 above, they shall be subject to a transitional fine of 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels.

2- In the case of violating point 2 of Article 52 above, they shall be subject to a transitional fine of 5,000,000 (five million) Riels.

3- In the case of violating point 3 of Article 52 above, they shall be subject to a transitional fine of 2,000,000 (two million) Riels.

#### Article 54._

Any person who obtains the right to construct roads, intersections, grade-separated intersections, multi-level roads, flyovers, overpasses, underpasses, underwater tunnels, or crossings between roads and railways, who fails to comply with technical regulations specified in the construction design plan shall be subject to a transitional fine of 5,000,000 (five million) Riels, and shall be required to rebuild in accordance with the designated design plan.

#### Article 55._

Legal entities may be declared criminally responsible under the conditions stipulated in Article 42 (Criminal Liability of Legal Entities) of the Criminal Code for offenses set forth in Article 54 above.

Legal entities shall be punished with a transitional fine of 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels, and shall be required to rebuild in accordance with the designated design plan.

#### Article 56._

Any person who obtains the right to implement a development project or road maintenance project who:

1- Charging road use fees without permission from the competent institution, shall be subject to a transitional fine of 5,000,000 (five million) Riels. In the event of having already been subject to a transitional fine once and continuing to commit the offense, they shall be subject to a double transitional fine.

2- Failing to publicly disseminate the road use fees approved by the competent institution as specified, shall be subject to a transitional fine of 2,000,000 (two million) Riels, and shall be required to republish. In the event of having already been subject to a transitional fine once and continuing to commit the offense, they shall be subject to a double transitional fine.

3- Refusing to reduce road use fees when there is already an agreement with the competent institution regarding the reduction of road use fees, shall be subject to a transitional fine of from 2,000,000 (two million) Riels. In the event of having already been subject to a transitional fine once and continuing to commit the offense, they shall be subject to a double transitional fine.

#### Article 57._

Legal entities may be declared criminally responsible under the conditions stipulated in Article 42 (Criminal Liability of Legal Entities) of the Criminal Code for offenses set forth in Article 56 above.

Legal entities shall be punished according to the following cases:

1- In the case of violating point 1 of Article 56 above, they shall be subject to a transitional fine of 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels.

2- In the case of violating point 2 of Article 56 above, they shall be subject to a transitional fine of 5,000,000 (five million) Riels.

3- In the case of violating point 3 of Article 56 above, they shall be subject to a transitional fine of 5,000,000 (five million) Riels.

In the event of having already been subject to a transitional fine once and continuing to commit the offense, they shall be subject to a double transitional fine.

#### Article 58._

Private enterprises or road construction companies that do not cooperate with or obstruct competent officials performing their duties as specified in Article 19 of this Law, shall be subject to a transitional fine of 2,000,000 (two million) Riels.

In the event of having already been subject to a transitional fine once and continuing to commit the offense, they shall be subject to a double transitional fine.

#### Article 59._

Legal entities may be declared criminally responsible under the conditions stipulated in Article 42 (Criminal Liability of Legal Entities) of the Criminal Code for offenses set forth in Article 58 above.

Legal entities shall be punished with a transitional fine of 5,000,000 (five million) Riels.
In the event of having already been subject to a transitional fine once and continuing to commit the offense, they shall be subject to a double transitional fine.

#### Article 60._

Users of heavy transport vehicles carrying goods on roads who:

1- Do not enter to be weighed at weighing stations along the road network, shall be subject to a transitional fine of 500,000 (five hundred thousand) Riels.

2- Overload not exceeding 5 (five) percent of the weight limit, shall be given a written warning.

3- Overload from 5 (five) percent to 10 (ten) percent of the weight limit, shall:

A- Unload the excess goods and detain the vehicle for 10 (ten) days and be subject to a transitional fine of 100,000 (one hundred thousand) Riels per 1 (one) ton of excess weight.

B- Confiscate and suspend the driver's license for 10 (ten) days.

4- Overload from 10 (ten) percent to 20 (twenty) percent of the weight limit, shall:

A- Unload the excess goods and detain the vehicle for 1 (one) month and be subject to a transitional fine of 200,000 (two hundred thousand) Riels per 1 (one) ton of excess weight.

B- Confiscate and suspend the driver's license for 6 (six) months.

5- Overload by more than 20 (twenty) percent of the weight limit, shall:

A- Unload the excess goods and detain the vehicle for 1 (one) year and be subject to a transitional fine of 300,000 (three hundred thousand) Riels per 1 (one) ton of excess weight.

B- Confiscate and suspend the driver's license for 2 (two) years.

In the event of an offense of overloading the total weight limit, as well as an offense of overloading the maximum axle weight, fines shall be imposed for both cases.

In the event of having already been subject to a transitional fine once but continuing to commit the offense, a double transitional fine shall be imposed, and the transport business of the company shall be temporarily suspended for a period of 1 (one) year.

The vehicle owner shall be responsible for loading and unloading goods and paying the rental fee for storage and maintenance of goods and vehicle by themselves.

#### Article 61._

Legal entities may be declared criminally responsible under the conditions set forth in Article 42 (Criminal Liability of Legal Entities) of the Penal Code for offenses specified in Article 60 above.

Legal entities shall be punished with a double transitional fine for offenses specified in Article 60 above. In the event of having already been subject to a transitional fine once but continuing to commit the offense, a transitional fine of double the amount of the transitional fine as specified in the first paragraph of this section shall be imposed.

#### Article 62._

Any person who fails to comply with the instructions of safety agents stationed at targets as specified in Article 27 of this Law shall be subject to a transitional fine of 10,000 (ten thousand) Riels.

#### Article 63._

Any person who constructs a barrier across, a speed bump, or other obstacles on the road without permission shall be subject to a transitional fine of 1,000,000 (one million) Riels and shall be required to remove such constructed equipment.

#### Article 64._

Any person who performs any public works within the road body, roadway, shoulder, roadside, and remaining land of the road right-of-way without permission from the competent authority managing the road shall be subject to a transitional fine of 500,000 (five hundred thousand) Riels.

#### Article 65._

Legal entities may be declared criminally responsible under the conditions set forth in Article 42 (Criminal Liability of Legal Entities) of the Penal Code for offenses which

specified in Article 64 above.

Legal entities shall be punished with a transitional fine of 1,000,000 (one million) Riels.

#### Article 66._

Any person who modifies the road right-of-way to make access roads to residences, buildings, factories, enterprises, or other places adjacent to the road without permission from the competent authority managing the road shall be subject to a transitional fine of 50,000 (fifty thousand) Riels.

In the event of having already been subject to a transitional fine once but continuing to commit the offense, a double transitional fine shall be imposed.

#### Article 67._

Any person who makes the road slippery, sticky, pours liquid waste on the road, scatters materials and objects on the road, or displays materials or various signs that block the driver's vision or create obstacles for travelers during travel shall be subject to a transitional fine of 50,000 (fifty thousand) Riels.

In the event of having already been subject to a transitional fine once but continuing to commit the offense, a double transitional fine shall be imposed.

#### Article 68._

Any person who alters, removes, relocates, writes on, or damages traffic signs, traffic lights, kilometer posts, or direction signs shall be subject to a transitional fine of 100,000 (one hundred thousand) Riels.

In the event of having already been subject to a transitional fine once but continuing to commit the offense, a double transitional fine shall be imposed.

#### Article 69._

Any person who operates a business in road infrastructure, maintenance, or opens a road laboratory without a license shall be subject to a transitional fine of 5,000,000 (five million) Riels.

In the event of having already been subject to a transitional fine once but continuing to commit the offense, a double transitional fine shall be imposed.

#### Article 70._

Any person who:

1- Organizes activities and various ceremonies on the road without permission from the sub-national

administration shall be subject to a transitional fine of 100,000 (one hundred thousand) Riels.

2- Organizes activities and various ceremonies on the road with or without permission from the sub-national administration and unintentionally causes damage to the road or road facilities, shall be required to repair them to their original state and shall be punished in accordance with Article 419 (Other damages caused by negligence or non-compliance with regulations) of the Penal Code.

#### Article 71._

Any person who unlawfully constructs a solid, permanent structure on land reserved for road infrastructure development shall receive a written warning and be required to remove such structure.

In case of refusal to remove, they shall be punished with imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 1 (one) year and a fine of 100,000 (one hundred thousand) Riels to 2,000,000 (two million) Riels.

#### Article 72._

Any person who constructs various structures without complying with points 1 and 2 of Article 30 of this Law shall be punished with a fine of 100,000 (one hundred thousand) Riels to 1,000,000 (one million) Riels and shall be required to remove such structures.

In case of refusal to remove, they shall be punished with imprisonment from 6 (six) months to 2 (two) years and a fine of 1,000,000 (one million) Riels to 4,000,000 (four million) Riels.

#### Article 73._

Any person who modifies the road drainage system and elevates access roads connecting to the main road without permission from the competent authority managing the road shall be subject to a fine of 1,000,000 (one million) Riels to 4,000,000 (four million) Riels.

In case of refusal to remove, they shall be punished with imprisonment from 6 (six) months to 2 (two) years and a fine of 1,000,000 (one million) Riels to 4,000,000 (four million) Riels.

#### Article 74._

Any person who encroaches upon, occupies, or uses by constructing structures or works on the road reserve land shall be punished with a fine of 100,000 (one hundred thousand) Riels to 1,000,000 (one million) Riels and shall be required to remove those structures or works.

In case of refusal to remove, they shall be punished with imprisonment from 6 (six) months to 2 (two) years and a fine

to a fine of 1,000,000 (one million) Riels to 4,000,000 (four million) Riels.

#### Article 75._

Heavy transport vehicle users who transport goods on roads carrying excess weight that causes road subsidence or structural cracking across the road or bridge collapse shall be punished with imprisonment from 6 (six) months to 2 (two) years and a fine of 1,000,000 (one million) Riels to 4,000,000 (four million) Riels.

Competent officials who permit overloaded vehicles to travel on the road network, resulting in road subsidence, road cracking across the road, or bridge collapse, shall be punished as a principal.

#### Article 76._

Legal entities may be declared criminally responsible under the conditions set forth in Article 42 (Criminal Liability of Legal Entities) of the Penal Code for offenses specified in Article 75 above.

Legal entities shall be punished with a fine of 5,000,000 (five million) Riels to 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels, as well as one or more of the following additional penalties:

1- Dissolution in accordance with the formalities specified in Article 170 (Dissolution of Legal Entities and Liquidation of Legal Entities) of the Penal Code.

2- Judicial supervision in accordance with the formalities specified in Article 171 (Placement under Judicial Supervision of Court) of the Penal Code.

3- Prohibition from performing one or more activities in accordance with the formalities specified in Article 172 (Prohibition on Activity) of the Penal Code.

4- Exclusion from public procurement in accordance with the formalities specified in Article 173 (Exclusion from Public Procurement) of the Penal Code.

5- Posting of the sentencing decision in accordance with the formalities specified in Article 180 (Posting of Decision) of the Penal Code.

6- Publication of the sentencing decision in written media or broadcasting through all

means in accordance with the formalities specified in Article 181 (Publication of Decision by Audiovisual Telecommunication Means) of the Penal Code.

#### Article 77._

Any person who performs public works without permission from the competent authority managing the road and causes damage to the road body, roadway, shoulder, roadside, and remaining land of the road right-of-way shall be required to repair them to their original state and shall be punished with imprisonment from 2 (two) years to 5 (five) years and a fine of 4,000,000 (four million) Riels to 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels.

#### Article 78._

Legal entities may be declared criminally responsible under the conditions set forth in Article 42 (Criminal Liability of Legal Entities) of the Penal Code for offenses specified in Article 77 above.

Legal entities shall be punished with a fine of 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels to 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels, as well as one or more of the following additional penalties:

1- Dissolution in accordance with the formalities specified in Article 170 (Dissolution of Legal Entities and Liquidation of Legal Entities) of the Penal Code.

2- Judicial supervision in accordance with the formalities specified in Article 171 (Placement under Judicial Supervision of Court) of the Penal Code.

3- Prohibition from performing one or more activities in accordance with the formalities specified in Article 172 (Prohibition on Activity) of the Penal Code.

4- Exclusion from public procurement in accordance with the formalities specified in Article 173 (Exclusion from Public Procurement) of the Penal Code.

5- Posting of the sentencing decision in accordance with the formalities specified in Article 180 (Posting of Decision) of the Penal Code.

6- Publication of the sentencing decision in written media or broadcasting through all means in accordance with the formalities specified in Article 181 (Publication of Decision by Audiovisual Telecommunication Means) of the Penal Code.

### Chapter 12
#### Transitional Provisions

#### Article 79._

Legal normative documents related to the road sector that are currently in force shall remain valid until new legal normative documents are issued to replace them in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

#### Article 80._

Within 01 (one) year after this Law enters into force, laboratories related to the road sector that have been and are operating businesses shall apply for a license in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

#### Final Provisions

#### Article 81._

Other laws and legal normative documents that are in force at the date of implementation and contradict the provisions of this Law shall be considered null and void to the extent of such contradiction, starting from the date of implementation.

### Glossary

1- Road refers to the entire area including the road body, roadway, shoulder or sidewalk, bridge, flyover, overpass, tunnel, and ferry crossing connecting the road.

2- Roadway refers to the portion of the road reserved for all types of vehicles to travel.

3- Traffic lane refers to a section of the roadway, marked by line markings or dividing the roadway along the length of the roadway, with sufficient width to allow a single line of all types of vehicles to travel.

4- Sidewalk/Pedestrian path refers to a part of the road constructed or prepared for people to walk safely.

5- Shoulder refers to the sides of the roadway outside urban areas where vehicles can park in case of necessity.

6- Sidewalk/Footpath refers to the sides of the roadway in urban areas prepared for pedestrians, tree planting, and installation of public utility equipment.

7- Roundabout refers to an intersection with a circular island in the center, traffic direction signs, and a roadway circling around with arrows indicating the direction of traffic.

8- Ferry crossing refers to a specific land area adjacent to the water or a ferry terminal prepared safely for transport and crossing from one side to the other side of a canal, lake, stream, river, and sea by using a ferry pier or ferry bridge, with ferry vessels such as boats, ferries, towed ferries, etc. The ferry terminal is prepared by constructing access roads, vehicle parking waiting areas, waiting areas for passengers, management agents to direct traffic, and other equipment serving the ferry crossing.

9- Road facilities refer to parking lots, halting places, traffic lights, speed limit signs, road direction signs, spacing signs, markings, posts, or road lane barrier facilities

roundabout, traffic divider islands, curbstones, walls, safety barriers, kilometer posts, boundary marking posts, as well as traffic signs and other equipment installed along the road.

10- Drainage facilities refer to canals, ditches, reservoirs, wastewater treatment plants, rainwater drainage systems, or various parts etc. prepared along the road to protect the road, road facilities, and traffic safety.

11- Road dividers refer to equipment for dividing the roadway into two sections for traffic flow in opposite directions or dividing it into traffic lanes. Dividers include both mobile and fixed types.

12- Road structures/Civil works refer to bridges, culverts, retaining walls, drainage systems, and other physical structures that provide safety, efficiency, and sustainability to the road.

13- Road construction refers to roads, vehicle stopping areas, road parking lots, road facilities, civil structures, and other constructions serving the road.

14- Road infrastructure refers to the road right-of-way, road construction, bus stations (bus terminals), parking areas, parking lots, roadside service stations, and rest/leisure areas.

15- Road right-of-way refers to the land on both sides of the road centerline, including the road width, reserved for protection, management, development, and maintenance of the road, ensuring traffic safety, and for the use of other physical infrastructure works, as defined by the road right-of-way boundary line.

16- Road right-of-way boundary line refers to the boundary line of the road right-of-way points at a designated distance from the road centerline.

17- Dimensions or limits refer to the size limits on height and weight capacity of roads, bridges, ferry crossings, tunnels, etc. to ensure that goods transport vehicles cross safely.

18- Level intersection refers to the intersection of two or more roads at the same grade level, without taking into account the height of the roadway axes. Level intersections can have the shape of +, X, Y, T, or roundabout, etc.

19- Grade-separated intersection/Interchange refers to the intersection of two or more roads at different height levels, without taking into account the height of the roadway axes. Grade-separated intersections can have the shape of +, X, and ★.

20- Maximum allowable gross vehicle weight refers to the sum of the vehicle's weight, the weight of passengers, and the weight of goods carried. If the vehicle has a trailer, the maximum allowable gross weight of the vehicle is the sum of the maximum gross weights of each vehicle.

21- Expressway refers to a road designed for vehicle traffic, with facilities dividing the roadway into two opposite directions or dividing it into lanes, with no level intersections, or with minimal intersections where necessary, and fully equipped with road facilities to ensure continuous traffic flow, safety, and to reduce travel time, and has designated access and exit points for vehicles.

22- Road user refers to vehicle drivers, pedestrians, business operators on the road, persons driving herds of animals or animal users, and other users of the road for other purposes.

23- National road refers to a road with high traffic volume:

a- Road connecting Phnom Penh Capital to the town of each province throughout the country.

b- Road connecting the town of one province to the town of another province.

c- Road connecting a national road to another national road.

d- Road branching from a national road to the town of a province.

e- Road branching from a national road or connecting from a provincial town to important areas such as ports, railway stations, airports, special economic zones, tourist resorts, border crossings, and other areas with significant economic potential.

f- Important national roads with high traffic volume determined by the Royal Government upon the proposal of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

24- Provincial road refers to a road with medium traffic volume:

a- Road connecting a provincial town to a district or khan town.

b- Road branching from a national road to a provincial road.

c- Road branching from a provincial road or municipal road to a district or khan town.

d- Road connecting a provincial town to a provincial road.

e- Road branching from a provincial road to another provincial road.

f- Road branching from a provincial road, municipal road, or other urban roads in the province to important areas such as ports, railway stations, airports, special economic zones, tourist resorts, bilateral border crossings, and other areas with medium economic potential.

25- Capital, municipal, and provincial urban roads refer to roads located within the administrative geography of the capital and provinces, including the roadway, sidewalk, and pedestrian path.

26- Rural road refers to:

a- Road connecting a national road or provincial road to a town, district/khan town, commune/sangkat center, and to villages.

b- Road connecting a town, district/khan town to another town, district/khan town.

c- Road connecting a town, district/khan town to a commune/sangkat center.

d- Road connecting a commune/sangkat center to another commune/sangkat center.

e- Road connecting a commune/sangkat center to villages.

f- Road connecting a village to another village.

g- Road within a village.

27- Bus terminal refers to a parking place for passengers embarking/disembarking and loading/unloading goods.

28- Weigh station refers to a place prepared for checking vehicle weights.

29- Technical regulations refer to technical norms, technical standards, and instruction documents regarding technical works, safety conditions, and traffic safety conditions.

30- Road stakeholders refer to managers, constructors, inspectors, financiers, or concessionaires of road development and maintenance, transporters, road users, residents along the road, and suppliers of materials, machinery, or road facilities.

31- Roadside service station refers to places serving road traffic such as fuel stations, management and information stations, leisure areas, rest areas, vehicle repair garages, etc.

32- Rest area refers to a place for travelers to rest, with dining services, restrooms, and notification/information areas.

33- Service area refers to a place for public services located outside the right-of-way of major roads such as expressways or national roads, for drivers and travelers to rest, with hotels, restaurants, safe road networks, parking lots, fuel stations, information centers, vehicle repair garages, clean water supply networks, but it is not a tourist resort.

34- Parallel/Overlapping roads refer to two or more roads located on the same level or different levels, running in parallel directions, with different road types and functions.

35- Bridge refers to a structure crossing over water to convey traffic from one location to another.

36- Flyover/Viaduct refers to a bridge crossing over land to convey traffic from one location to another, bypassing all types of structures at different locations and height levels.

37- Overpass refers to a bridge crossing over land to convey traffic from one location to another, crossing over roads or railways at the same location.

38- Tunnel refers to a route used for traffic located underground or through a mountain, equipped with safety walls.

39- Copy refers to duplication from the original text or original law for archival purposes.
