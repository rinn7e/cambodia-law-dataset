### HEREBY PROMULGATE

The Law on Nationality, which was adopted by the National Assembly on May 31, 2018, during the 10th session of the 5th legislature, and which was reviewed and approved entirely by the Senate on its form and legal concepts on June 11, 2018, during the 1st plenary session of the 4th legislature, the whole text of which is as follows:

# Law
# On
# Nationality

### Chapter 1
### General Provisions

**Article 1.-**
This Law aims to ensure the implementation of the jurisdiction of the Kingdom of Cambodia over natural persons in order to determine the rights to acquire and the rights to renounce Khmer nationality for those living in the territory of the Kingdom of Cambodia or abroad and who have fulfilled the conditions of this Law.

**Article 2.-**
This Law aims to determine the:

- Acquisition of Khmer nationality by birth;

- Acquisition of Khmer nationality by full adoption;

- Acquisition of Khmer nationality by marriage;

- Acquisition of Khmer nationality by naturalization;

- Loss of Khmer nationality.

**Article 3.-**
This Law has the scope of application to natural persons who are born or live in the territory of the Kingdom of Cambodia or abroad.

**Article 4.-**
Key terms used in this Law are defined as follows:

1- Khmer Nationality refers to the legal and political bond that connects a natural person to the Kingdom of Cambodia.

2- Claiming Khmer Nationality refers to the right to request Khmer nationality arising from a legal marriage bond between a foreigner and a Khmer citizen.

3- Naturalization refers to the granting of Khmer nationality through the request of a foreigner based on the conditions of residence, investment, cash donation, and special merits or special achievements.

### Chapter 2
### Competent Institutions

**Article 5.-**
The Ministry of Interior has the competence to lead the implementation of the Law on Nationality.

### Chapter 3
### General Principles

**Article 6.-**
Any person who possesses Khmer nationality is a Khmer citizen.

Only Khmer citizens have the right to receive and hold a Khmer nationality identity card and a passport of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

**Article 7.-**
A Khmer citizen shall not be deprived of nationality, exiled, or extradited to any foreign country, unless there is a mutual agreement.

**Article 8.-**
Khmer citizens living abroad:

1. Shall be protected by the State through all diplomatic means.

2. Cannot lose their nationality involuntarily.

### Chapter 4
### Khmer Nationality by Birth and Khmer Nationality by Full Adoption

#### Section 1
#### Khmer Nationality by Birth

**Article 9.-**

1- Khmer nationality shall be acquired, regardless of the place of birth, by:

- Children born to a father or mother who has Khmer nationality.

- Children whose father or mother is not recognized, but if a court issues a judgment that the child was indeed born to a father or mother who has Khmer nationality.

2- Khmer nationality shall be acquired by birth in the Kingdom of Cambodia by:

- Children born to foreign parents who were born and are living legally in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

- Abandoned children or newborns found in the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be deemed to have been born in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Section 2
#### Khmer Nationality by Full Adoption

**Article 10.-**
A foreign child who is fully adopted by a Khmer citizen spouse shall acquire Khmer nationality when the full adoption is established in accordance with the law in force.

### Chapter 5
### Khmer Nationality by Marriage

**Article 11.-**
A foreign woman or man who marries a Khmer citizen may claim Khmer nationality only if the woman or man has lived together for a period of three years after registering the marriage and has resided for at least 12 (twelve) full months in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

The claim for Khmer nationality as a family group is allowed for a foreign mother or father with minor children.

**Article 12.-**
A foreigner who may claim Khmer nationality by marriage shall fulfill the following conditions:

1- Have good conduct and morality.

2- Have never been convicted of any felony or misdemeanor.

3- Have a residence in the Kingdom of Cambodia at the time of applying for Khmer nationality by marriage.

4- Be able to speak Khmer, write Khmer, and have some knowledge of Khmer history.

5- Have intelligence and physical fitness that will not cause danger or burden to the nation.

**Article 13.-**
A foreigner who claims Khmer nationality by marriage may apply to change their name to a Khmer name. In this case, the new name must be specified in the application for Khmer nationality.

**Article 14.-**
In case the competent authority rejects the claim for Khmer nationality, the applicant has the right to appeal.

**Article 15.-**
The formalities and procedures for claiming Khmer nationality, as well as the appeal procedure, shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

The granting of Khmer nationality in cases of claiming Khmer nationality shall be decided by a Royal Decree.

**Article 16.-**
A person who has been permitted to acquire Khmer nationality by marriage shall take an oath before the Supreme Court.

The content of this oath shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

**Article 17.-**
Khmer citizens do not lose their Khmer nationality due to marriage with a foreigner.

### Chapter 6
### Naturalization

**Article 18.-**
A foreigner may apply for Khmer nationality through naturalization.

Naturalization is not a right of the applicant but a favor of the Kingdom of Cambodia only. In any case, the application may be rejected at discretion.

**Article 19.-**
A foreigner who may apply for naturalization shall fulfill the following conditions:

1- Have good conduct and morality.

2- Have never been convicted of any felony or misdemeanor.

3- Have a residence in the Kingdom of Cambodia and have resided for a continuous period of 7 (seven) years with a residence card issued within the framework of the Law on Immigration for...

immigrant foreigners.

4- Have a residence in the Kingdom of Cambodia and have a residence card issued within the framework of the Law on Immigration for foreigners defined in Article 21 and Article 22 of this Law.

5- Be able to speak Khmer, write Khmer, and have some knowledge of Khmer history, and show evidence of being able to live in Khmer society harmoniously as well as having adapted to Khmer customs and traditions.

6- Have intelligence and physical fitness that will not cause danger or burden to the nation.

**Article 20.-**
For any immigrant foreigner who was born in the Kingdom of Cambodia, the period of continuous residence of 7 (seven) years as stated in Point 3 of Article 19 of this Law shall be reduced to 3 (three) years.

**Article 21.-**
Any foreigner who has received authorization for investment correctly according to the law from a competent institution and has spent capital to implement an actual investment project in the Kingdom of Cambodia may apply for Khmer nationality through naturalization in the case that the foreigner has fulfilled the conditions stated in Points 1, 2, 4, 5, and 6 of Article 19 of this Law and has resided legally for at least 12 (twelve) full months in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

The amount of money, priority sectors, and types of investment projects, as well as other conditions, shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

**Article 22.-**
Any foreigner who has made a cash donation to the national budget for the benefit of socio-economic development or humanitarian sectors in the Kingdom of Cambodia may apply for Khmer nationality in the case that the foreigner has fulfilled the conditions stated in Points 1, 2, 4, and 6 of Article 19 of this Law and has resided legally for at least 6 (six) full months in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

The amount of the cash donation to the national budget or humanitarian sectors shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

**Article 23.-**
Any foreigner who has shown evidence of having provided special merits or special achievements for the benefit of the Kingdom of Cambodia and has been recognized by the Royal Government of Cambodia may apply for Khmer nationality through naturalization in the case that the foreigner has fulfilled the conditions stated in Points 1, 2, and 6 of Article 19 of this Law.

**Article 24.-**
A foreigner who has a spouse or minor child and who intends to apply for Khmer nationality through naturalization may apply as a family group.

**Article 25.-**
A foreigner may apply to change their name to a Khmer name. In this case, the new name must be specified in the application for naturalization.

**Article 26.-**
The formalities and procedures for applying for Khmer nationality through naturalization shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

The granting of Khmer nationality through naturalization shall be decided by a Royal Decree.

**Article 27.-**
A person who has been permitted to acquire Khmer nationality through naturalization shall take an oath before the Supreme Court.

The content of this oath shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

### Chapter 7
### Loss of Khmer Nationality

**Article 28.-**
Any person who possesses Khmer nationality and is at least 18 (eighteen) years of age may apply to renounce Khmer nationality in the case that the person has another nationality and without coercion.

The application to renounce Khmer nationality as a family group is allowed for a Khmer citizen mother or father with minor children.

**Article 29.-**
The formalities and procedures for applying to renounce nationality shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

The permission to renounce Khmer nationality shall be decided by a Royal Decree.

### Chapter 8
### Penal Provisions

**Article 30.-**
Any foreigner who holds and uses a Khmer nationality identity card or a passport of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be punished by imprisonment from 5 (five) years to 10 (ten) years.

Any official, civil servant, or person who produces a Khmer nationality identity card or a passport of the Kingdom of Cambodia for any person who does not have Khmer nationality shall be punished the same as stated in the first paragraph of this Article.

**Article 31.-**
Any person who forges a Khmer nationality identity card or a passport of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be punished by imprisonment from 5 (five) years to 10 (ten) years.

The acts of scratching, erasing, adding text, or damaging the truth of the information in the Khmer nationality identity card or the passport of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall also be considered as forgery.

**Article 32.-**
Any person who uses a forged Khmer nationality identity card or a forged passport of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be punished by imprisonment from 2 (two) years to 5 (five) years and a fine from 4,000,000 (four million) Riels to 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels.

**Article 33.-**
Instigators and accomplices shall be held criminally responsible for the offenses stated in Articles 30, 31, and 32 of this Law in accordance with the conditions stated in the Criminal Code.

**Article 34.-**
For natural persons who commit the offenses stated in Articles 30, 31, and 32 of this Law, the court may declare one or more additional penalties as stated in Article 53 (Types of Additional Penalties) of the Criminal Code.

**Article 35.-**
Legal entities may be declared criminally responsible under the conditions stated in Article 42 (Criminal Responsibility of Legal Entities) of the Criminal Code for the offenses stated in Articles 30...

Articles 31 and 32 of this Law.

Legal entities shall be punished by a fine from 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels to 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels, and one or more additional penalties as stated in Article 168 (Additional Penalties applicable to Legal Entities) of the Criminal Code.

### Chapter 9
### Transitional Provisions

**Article 36.-**
All legal instruments related to the implementation of the Law on Nationality promulgated by Royal Kram No. NS/RKM/1096/30 dated October 9, 1996, shall remain in effect until new legal instruments come to replace them in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

### Chapter 10
### Final Provisions

**Article 37.-**
The Law on Nationality promulgated by Royal Kram No. NS/RKM/1096/30 dated October 9, 1996, is hereby repealed and replaced by this Law.

**Article 38.-**
This Law is declared as urgent.
