# Promulgates

The Law on Administrative Management of Commune-Sangkat, which was adopted by the National Assembly on January 12, 2001, during its 5th plenary session of the 2nd legislature, and which was approved by the Senate on its form and legal aspects in its entirety on February 06, 2001, during its 4th plenary session of the 1st legislature, and which was declared by the Constitutional Council to be consistent with the Constitution according to Decision No. 041/003/2001 KBTh.Ch dated February 28, 2001, the full text of which is as follows:

Chapter 1
General Provisions

#### Article 1.-

This law aims to determine the administrative management of all communes and sangkats in the Kingdom of Cambodia according to the decentralization policy.

#### Article 2.-

Commune-Sangkat are legal entities.

#### Article 3.-

The system of local governance shall be implemented at the level of commune-sangkat.

#### Article 4.-

The power of leadership and management of commune-sangkat shall be derived from general, universal, free, fair and just elections by equal, direct and secret ballot within the framework of each commune-sangkat.

#### Article 5.-

Commune-Sangkat shall manage local affairs in their territory with autonomy in accordance with the Constitution, laws, royal decrees, sub-decrees, proclamations and relevant legal instruments.

Legislative power and executive power in local management shall be granted to commune-sangkat, and such power shall be exercised in accordance with the Constitution, laws, royal decrees, sub-decrees, proclamations and relevant legal instruments.

#### Article 6.-

The establishment, dissolution, modification of boundaries and change of name of commune-sangkat in cases where the boundaries of the relevant province or municipality are not affected, shall be determined by a sub-decree at the request of the Minister of Interior.

In cases where the boundaries of the relevant province or municipality are affected, the establishment, dissolution, modification of boundaries and change of name of commune-sangkat shall be determined by a royal decree at the request of the Prime Minister based on the proposal of the Minister of Interior.

#### Article 7.-

Whenever any boundary dispute arises after a commune or sangkat has been established, dissolved or modified, the Minister of Interior shall issue a proclamation to resolve such dispute in accordance with the law and regulations in force.

#### Article 8.-

In any commune or sangkat where the election for the commune-sangkat council cannot be organized due to force majeure, the Royal Government shall report to the National Assembly.

After reporting to the National Assembly, the Royal Government shall instruct the Ministry of Interior to organize the temporary management of those communes or sangkats, and the election for the commune-sangkat councils shall be conducted when the situation in those communes or sangkats returns to normal.

# Chapter 2
# Commune-Sangkat Councils

#### Article 9.-

Each commune and sangkat shall have a council called the commune-sangkat council.

The commune-sangkat council is a body representing the citizens in its commune or sangkat and has the mission to serve the general interest in its commune or sangkat.

#### Article 10.-

The commune-sangkat council shall be elected by the citizens of its commune or sangkat in accordance with the procedures determined by the Law on the Election of Commune-Sangkat Councils.

#### Article 11.-

The commune-sangkat council has a mandate of 5 years and shall expire when the new commune-sangkat council takes office.

During this transitional period, the commune-sangkat council whose mandate has expired shall have the duty to perform daily tasks only.

#### Article 12.-

The commune-sangkat council shall consist of 5 to 11 members based on its population and geography.

The actual number of council members for each commune or sangkat shall be determined by a sub-decree at the request of the Minister of Interior.

The determined number of council members may be changed by a sub-decree at the request of the Minister of Interior based on changes in population and geography.

Additional members of the commune-sangkat council shall be elected according to the same procedures as other members.

#### Article 13.-

The commune-sangkat council shall have a chairman called the Chairman of the Commune-Sangkat Council.

The Chairman of the Commune-Sangkat Council has the following duties:

- Preside over various sessions of the commune-sangkat council.

- Ensure that the commune-sangkat council meets at least once a month.

- Ensure that the sessions of the commune-sangkat council proceed correctly according to the rules and internal regulations of the commune-sangkat council and the provisions as stated in Article 23 of this law.

- Maintain order and discipline during the session.

#### Article 14.-

Cambodian citizens of both sexes who have the right and intention to stand as candidates for the commune-sangkat council election shall fulfill the following main conditions:

- Have Khmer nationality by birth

- Be able to read and write Khmer script

- Have registered in the voter list in the commune or sangkat where they intend to stand as a candidate

- Be at least 25 years of age on the day of the commune-sangkat council election

- Possess sufficient qualifications in accordance with the conditions determined by the Law on the Election of Commune-Sangkat Councils.

#### Article 15.-

The commune-sangkat council shall be elected through a proportional representation system.

#### Article 16.-

A member of the commune-sangkat council shall lose their membership in any of the following cases:

- Death

- Loss of mental capacity with certification from the competent ministry or institution

- Written resignation from being a member of the commune-sangkat council

- Convicted by the court to imprisonment for any felony or misdemeanor

- Dismissed through the disciplinary system of the internal regulations of the commune-sangkat council in accordance with the conditions stated in Article 58 of this law.

- The entire commune-sangkat council is dissolved

- The member loses membership from their political party.

Whenever a member of the commune-sangkat council loses their membership, a replacement candidate from the same candidate list following the next order shall be selected without having to conduct a re-election.

#### Article 17.-

A re-election shall be organized within a maximum period of 120 days to elect the commune-sangkat council in any of the following cases:

- Newly established commune or sangkat according to the conditions stated in Article 6 of this law

- The commune-sangkat council is dissolved according to the conditions stated in Article 57 and Article 58 of this law.

The re-election as stated above shall be organized in accordance with the rules and procedures stated in the Law on the Election of Commune-Sangkat Councils.

The commune-sangkat council resulting from the re-election as stated above shall have a mandate equal to the remaining mandate of the previous commune-sangkat council.

The re-election shall not be permitted if the remaining mandate of the commune-sangkat council is not more than 180 days.

During this period, the Minister of Interior shall appoint the composition and determine the competence and mode of leadership for managing that commune or sangkat until there is an election to elect the commune-sangkat council for the next mandate.

#### Article 18.-

The modification of boundaries of any commune or sangkat can be performed only in cases where such modification does not cause a change in the number of elected commune-sangkat council members.

The establishment of any commune or sangkat can be performed only in cases where such establishment does not cause a change in the number of elected commune-sangkat council members.

In cases where the establishment of a new commune or sangkat, or the dissolution or modification of boundaries causes a change in the number of elected commune-sangkat council members, the establishment, dissolution or modification of boundaries shall enter into force for the purpose of the commune-sangkat council election for the next mandate.

#### Article 19.-

Within 14 days after the official announcement of the election results, the first meeting of the commune-sangkat council shall be organized under the chairmanship of an official appointed by the Minister of Interior to declare the validity of the commune-sangkat council members and to announce the names of the Chairman of the Commune-Sangkat Council, the Commune Chief (Mekhum), the Sangkat Chief (Chao Sangkat), and the Deputy Chiefs and Assistant Sangkat Chiefs.

#### Article 20.-

Before beginning its work, each commune-sangkat council shall prepare and adopt internal regulations to determine all procedures for its meetings and its mode of work.

The Minister of Interior shall issue instructions on various principles as a basis for the preparation of the above internal regulations.

#### Article 21.-

The commune-sangkat council shall meeting at least once a month.

The commune-sangkat council shall meet in the commune office or sangkat office, or in case of necessity, the chairman of the meeting may choose any place within its commune or sangkat for the meeting.

#### Article 22.-

The meeting of the commune-sangkat council shall be valid only if more than half of its total members are present.

The commune-sangkat council shall vote to approve by an absolute majority of its total members to decide on any of the following matters:

- Approval of the commune-sangkat development plan

- Approval of the commune-sangkat budget

- Determination of local taxes, non-tax revenues and various service fees

- Approval of internal regulations and commune-sangkat legal acts (Keti)

- Other matters as determined by the Minister of Interior.

During the approval process, if there is a tie vote, the chairman of the meeting shall have the casting vote.

#### Article 23.-

The meeting of the commune-sangkat council shall be public and democratic.

The commune-sangkat council may hold meetings in secret.

The Minister of Interior shall issue instructions on the mode of the above meetings.

#### Article 24.-

Members of the commune-sangkat council have the freedom to express their views in the meeting of the commune-sangkat council.

A member of the commune-sangkat council, regardless of whom, shall not be prosecuted, arrested, detained or imprisoned for expressing views or opinions in the meeting of the commune-sangkat council.

# Chapter 3
# Administrative Structure of Commune-Sangkat

#### Article 25.-

The commune-sangkat administration shall be led and managed by the commune-sangkat council.

The commune-sangkat council shall have a chairman.

The chairman of the commune-sangkat council is the Commune Chief (Mekhum) or the Sangkat Chief (Chao Sangkat).

#### Article 26.-

The Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief shall have assistants as follows:

For the Commune Chief, there are two assistants:

- First Deputy (Chum-top) 1 person

- Second Deputy (Chum-top) 1 person.

For the Sangkat Chief, there are two assistants:

- First Assistant Sangkat Chief 1 person

- Second Assistant Sangkat Chief 1 person.

#### Article 27.-

The Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief has the right to appoint various committees to provide advice and assist in various tasks as necessary.

The above committees may have any member of the commune-sangkat council as the chairman, or may have any citizen who has the right to vote in its commune or sangkat other than a member of the council as the chairman.

#### Article 28.-

Each commune or sangkat shall have one staff member from the administrative framework of the Ministry of Interior to serve as the Commune or Sangkat Clerk (Stien).

The Commune-Sangkat Clerk shall be appointed by the Minister of Interior and has the duty to assist in commune-sangkat tasks and to ensure the continuity of administrative work in the commune or sangkat.

The Commune-Sangkat Clerk shall be changed in cases where there is a request from the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief in accordance with the decision of the commune-sangkat council.

#### Article 29.-

Communes and sangkats may recruit non-civil service staff to assist in their work according to need and necessity of the work and according to the decision of the commune-sangkat council during its mandate, and may continue their work according to the decision of the commune-sangkat council of the next mandate.

#### Article 30.-

Aiming at improving the efficiency of commune-sangkat management, each commune-sangkat council shall organize the selection of a Village Chief (Me Phum) for each village under its commune or sangkat.

The Village Chief shall organize the appointment of a Deputy Village Chief and a member as assistants.

The Minister of Interior shall issue instructions on the mode and procedures for selecting the Village Chief, taking office, changing the Village Chief, and appointing assistants to the Village Chief.

#### Article 31.-

The Village Chief has the following duties:

- Implement the duties assigned by the commune-sangkat council or the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief to ensure security, public order and socio-economic development in the village

- Provide advice to the commune-sangkat council on various issues related to the interests in their village

- Propose advice to the commune-sangkat council, the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief to organize and coordinate tasks in their village.

The Minister of Interior shall issue additional instructions on the mode of work and duties of the village appropriately.

#### Article 32.-

The Chairman of the Commune-Sangkat Council who is the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief shall be selected from the candidate at the top of the candidate list that received the most votes.

The Chairman of the Commune-Sangkat Council who is the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief has a mandate equal to the mandate of the members of the commune-sangkat council.

#### Article 33.-

The First Deputy (Chum-top) or First Assistant Sangkat Chief shall be selected from the candidate at the top of the candidate list that received the second most votes.

The Second Deputy (Chum-top) or Second Assistant Sangkat Chief shall be selected from the candidate at the top of the candidate list that received the third most votes.

#### Article 34.-

In case the official results of the election of commune-sangkat council members provide seats to only one candidate list which fulfills the qualifications to receive all seats in the council, the members from that single list shall be selected to take the positions of Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief, First Deputy or First Assistant Sangkat Chief, and Second Deputy or Second Assistant Sangkat Chief.

#### Article 35.-

In case the official results of the election of commune-sangkat council members provide seats to only two candidate lists which fulfill the qualifications to receive seats in the council, the council members shall be selected from the candidate whose name is next to the candidate at the top of the list that received the first most votes to take the position of First Deputy or First Assistant Sangkat Chief, and select the council member who is the candidate at the top of the list that received the second most votes to take the position of Second Deputy or Second Assistant Sangkat Chief.

#### Article 36.-

The position of Chairman of the Commune-Sangkat Council who is the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief shall be lost in case the person loses their membership in the commune-sangkat council in accordance with the conditions stated in the first paragraph of Article 16 of this law.

#### Article 37.-

The position of Chairman of the Commune-Sangkat Council, the position of Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief, and the position of member of the commune-sangkat council shall all be lost at the same time if any of these positions is lost.

In the above case, the commune-sangkat council member whose name is next in the same candidate list as the former Chairman of the Commune-Sangkat Council who was the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief who lost the position, shall be selected to take the position of Chairman of the Commune-Sangkat Council who is the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief, and a new commune-sangkat council member shall be selected to fill the vacant seat in accordance with the conditions stated in the second paragraph of Article 16 of this law.

In case the candidate list that holds the position of Chairman of the Commune-Sangkat Council who is the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief has run out of council members, a member of the commune-sangkat council shall be selected from any candidate list based on the conditions stated in the second paragraph of Article 16 of this law, and this council member shall take the position of Chairman of the Commune-Sangkat Council who is the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief replacing the one who lost the position.

Based on the above conditions, the loss of position and the taking of the position of Chairman of the Commune-Sangkat Council who is the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief shall be made by a proclamation of the Minister of Interior within a maximum period of 14 days.

#### Article 38.-

The position of First Deputy or First Assistant Sangkat Chief, or the position of Second Deputy or Second Assistant Sangkat Chief, shall be lost from the position in case the person loses their membership in the commune-sangkat council based on the conditions stated in the first paragraph of Article 16 of this law.

In case the position of First Deputy or First Assistant Sangkat Chief, or the position of Second Deputy or Second Assistant Sangkat Chief is lost, the position of the person as a member of the commune-sangkat council shall also be lost at the same time.

In the above case, a member of the commune-sangkat council who is the candidate whose name is next in the same candidate list as the First Deputy or First Assistant Sangkat Chief, or the Second Deputy or Second Assistant Sangkat Chief shall be selected to take the position replacing the one who lost the position, and a new commune-sangkat council member shall be selected to fill the vacant seat based on the conditions stated in the second paragraph of Article 16 of this law.

In case the candidate list that holds the position of First Deputy or First Assistant Sangkat Chief, or the Second Deputy or Second Assistant Sangkat Chief has run out of council members, a candidate shall be selected from that list to be a member of the commune-sangkat council based on the conditions stated in the second paragraph of Article 16 of this law, and that council member shall take the position of First Deputy or First Assistant Sangkat Chief, or the Second Deputy or Second Assistant Sangkat Chief replacing the one who lost the position.

Based on the above conditions and the second paragraph of Article 33 of this law, the loss of position and the taking of the position of First Deputy or First Assistant Sangkat Chief, or Second Deputy or Second Assistant Sangkat Chief shall be made by a proclamation of the Minister of Interior within a maximum period of 14 days.

#### Article 39.-

The Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief shall fulfill the following roles and duties:

- Implement the decisions of the commune-sangkat council

- Implement the provisions and various principles received from the commune-sangkat council

- Report on the activities of the work to the commune-sangkat council at least once a month

- Provide various suggestions and assist the commune-sangkat council in preparing financial plans and in organizing the implementation of the roles, functions, and powers of the council.

#### Article 40.-

The Deputy (Chum-top) or Assistant Sangkat Chief is the assistant to the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief in implementing duties assigned by the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief, and replaces the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief in accordance with the hierarchy of deputies or assistant sangkat chiefs in case the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief is absent.

The Deputy or Assistant Sangkat Chief shall divide duties as follows:

- The First Deputy or First Assistant Sangkat Chief is responsible for assisting the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief on economic and financial affairs.

- The Second Deputy or Second Assistant Sangkat Chief is responsible for assisting the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief on administrative, social, public service and public order affairs.

The Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief may assign additional duties beyond the above division of duties to the Deputy or Assistant Sangkat Chief.

# Chapter 4
# Roles, Functions and Powers of Commune-Sangkat Administration

#### Article 41.-

The commune-sangkat administration has the role of promoting and supporting good governance by organizing and using various resources sustainably to meet local basic needs in its commune or sangkat, serving the common interest of citizens and respecting national interest in accordance with the general policy of the state.

#### Article 42.-

The commune-sangkat administration has two types of roles:

- Role in local management for the benefit of the commune or sangkat and citizens within its commune or sangkat.

- Role as an agent representing the state under the appointment or delegation of power by the state authorities.

#### Article 43.-

In its role in local management, the commune-sangkat administration has the duties to:

- Ensure security, public order and public orderliness

- Organize and manage necessary public services and see that those tasks function well

- Encourage the creation of well-being and welfare for citizens

- Promote economic and social development and improve the living standards of citizens

- Protect and preserve the environment, natural resources, culture and national heritage

- Reconcile views of citizens to have mutual understanding and tolerance

- Fulfill other tasks to respond to the needs of citizens.

#### Article 44.-

In its role as an agent of the state, the commune-sangkat administration has the duty to implement in accordance with the laws, royal decrees, sub-decrees, proclamations and relevant legal instruments.

In this case, the state authorities may delegate power to the commune or sangkat by attaching capacity building and providing means, materials and budget for implementation.

The above delegation of power shall be granted only to the competence of the commune-sangkat council.

#### Article 45.-

The commune-sangkat administration has no power to decide on the following sectors:

- Forestry

- Post and telecommunications

- National defense.

- National security

- Currency

- Foreign policy

- Fiscal policy

- And other sectors as stated in laws and relevant legal instruments.

#### Article 46.-

The roles, functions, and powers of the commune or sangkat as stated in Articles 42, 43 and 44 of this law shall be implemented only within the territorial boundaries of its own commune or sangkat.

In case there are issues related to the competence between two or more communes or sangkats, the Minister of Interior shall take action to facilitate reconciliation between the relevant administrations.

#### Article 47.-

The roles, functions, and powers of the commune-sangkat administration as stated in Articles 42, 43, 44 and 45 of this law can be further determined in detail by a sub-decree at the request of the Minister of Interior.

#### Article 48.-

The commune or sangkat has legislative power and executive power.

The legislative power of the commune or sangkat shall be exercised through the commune-sangkat legal acts (Deika) to manage various affairs within the framework of its roles, functions, and powers.

The commune-sangkat Deika shall be made based on the decision of the commune-sangkat council and shall be signed by the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief.

The commune-sangkat administration shall publicly and immediately disseminate the adopted Deika within its commune or sangkat.

#### Article 49.-

The commune-sangkat Deika is valid and enforceable only within its own territorial boundaries.

The commune-sangkat Deika shall not be contrary to international treaties/conventions recognized by the Kingdom of Cambodia, the spirit of the Constitution, laws, royal decrees, sub-decrees, proclamations, and relevant legal instruments.

The Deika issued by a commune-sangkat administration that is contrary to international treaties/conventions recognized by the Kingdom of Cambodia, the spirit of the Constitution, laws, royal decrees, sub-decrees, proclamations, and relevant legal instruments shall be considered null and void.

The Minister of Interior shall notify the nullity in writing to the relevant commune or sangkat and shall inform the public.

#### Article 50.-

The Minister of Interior shall provide instructions on the form and mode for the commune or sangkat to prepare its Deika in accordance with the situation in its own commune or sangkat.

#### Article 51.-

The commune-sangkat council may delegate its power to the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief in accordance with the instructions of the Minister of Interior, except for the following matters:

- Approval of the commune-sangkat budget

- Determination of local taxes, non-tax revenues, and various service fees

- Voting to approve internal regulations and commune-sangkat Deika

- Approval of the commune-sangkat development plan

- Other issues as determined by the Minister of Interior.

 can Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief to whom the power has been delegated by the commune-sangkat council cannot further delegate this power to any other person.

#### Article 52.-

The commune-sangkat council that has delegated power to the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief remains responsible for the tasks performed under that delegation.

The withdrawal of delegated power does not affect the validity of the tasks already performed.

# Chapter 5
# Monitoring, Inspection and Intervention

#### Article 53.-

The Minister of Interior shall determine various procedures for monitoring and inspection of the general activities of the commune or sangkat to:

- Inspect the legality of work management, exercise of power and fulfillment of roles and duties of each commune or sangkat

- Monitor the evolution of the capacity of various communes and sangkats

- Assess the intervention and support/assistance to the commune or sangkat to strengthen the management capacity, exercise of power and fulfillment of roles and duties.

In the monitoring, inspection and intervention for the commune or sangkat, the Minister of Interior may delegate power to state representatives at provincial, municipal, district and khan levels to assist in this task based on the requirements of the work.

#### Article 54.-

Any commune or sangkat that fails to fulfill the duties required by law, the Minister of Interior shall intervene to ensure immediate fulfillment of the duties required by the law.

The duties required by law are those that the commune-sangkat council has the obligation to fulfill in accordance with the rules of law, royal decrees, sub-decrees, proclamations, relevant legal instruments or internal regulations and commune-sangkat Deika.

#### Article 55.-

Monitoring, inspection and intervention include:

- Investigation and evaluation

- Issuing written instructions to the commune-sangkat council to fulfill the duties required by law

- Providing assistance in continuing the fulfillment of duties required by law.

#### Article 56.-

The intervention as stated in the first paragraph of Article 54 of this law shall be reasonable and proportionate to the scale of the duties that the commune-sangkat council has failed to fulfill, by attaching important and necessary instructions and support so that the commune-sangkat council can normally fulfill its duties as required by law.

The Minister of Interior has the right to enter into a written agreement with any individual or organization to provide written instructions to the state representatives at provincial, municipal, district and khan levels to perform necessary tasks to intervene on its behalf in any matter.

#### Article 57.-

In case the intervention does not receive results regarding any duty or certain duties required by law within a period of 6 months, and the general duties required by law cannot be fulfilled by the commune-sangkat council, the Minister of Interior shall dissolve the commune-sangkat council and conduct a re-election in accordance with the conditions stated in this law and the law on the election of members of commune-sangkat council.

#### Article 58.-

In case any commune-sangkat council performs activities contrary to the Constitution or the policy of the Royal Government, the Minister of Interior shall immediately dissolve that commune-sangkat council and shall conduct a re-election of that commune-sangkat council in accordance with the conditions stated in this law and the law on the election of members of commune-sangkat council.

#### Article 59.-

An entity under the Ministry of Interior shall be established to serve as the secretariat for local administration affairs.

The organization and functioning of this entity shall be determined by a sub-decree at the request of the Minister of Interior.

# Chapter 6
# Commune-Sangkat Development Plan

#### Article 60.-

The commune-sangkat council shall prepare, approve and implement the commune-sangkat development plan with the aim of determining the vision, programs and development duties of its own commune or sangkat.

#### Article 61.-

The commune-sangkat development plan shall be in accordance with the national socio-economic development plan.

#### Article 62.-

The commune-sangkat development plan shall be approved by the commune-sangkat council in the first year of its mandate and shall be reviewed to provide new data regularly every year by the commune-sangkat council.

#### Article 63.-

In preparing the commune-sangkat development plan, the commune-sangkat council shall take the following measures:

- Assess the level of development and development activities within its commune or sangkat

- Collect needs in the commune or sangkat and determine priorities for those needs based on its own resources and the resources received

- Prepare programs that can respond to various needs through service delivery and development, and these programs shall be included in the annual budget of the commune or sangkat

- Provide services and development in accordance with its budget

- Monitor and evaluate the results of the implementation of the development plan

- Use various results to assess the new level of development within its commune or sangkat

- Report on the results of the implementation of the development plan to its citizens.

The commune-sangkat council shall lead the work in accordance with its commune-sangkat development plan.

#### Article 64.-

The commune-sangkat development plan shall be prepared in writing with maps, statistics and other relevant documents attached.

The commune-sangkat development plan shall also determine various methods and means to create favorable conditions for the participation of citizens in its commune or sangkat during the whole process.

#### Article 65.-

In case any commune-sangkat development plan is prepared not in accordance with the conditions stated in this law, the Minister of Interior shall instruct the commune-sangkat council to adjust its plan appropriately within a maximum period of 45 days after receiving a copy of that plan.

The commune-sangkat council shall adjust its commune-sangkat development plan appropriately in accordance with the instructions of the Minister of Interior.

#### Article 66.-

The commune-sangkat council shall monitor, inspect and evaluate all implementation of the plan, programs, projects and various service delivery with transparency and high responsibility.

#### Article 67.-

The commune-sangkat council remains responsible for monitoring, inspection and evaluation of plans, programs and various projects, even if those tasks are implemented by any entity that it has selected.

#### Article 68.-

The Minister of Interior shall provide instructions to the commune-sangkat council on various methods for implementing the plan and for monitoring and evaluating the use of the fund for the commune-sangkat development plan.

#### Article 69.-

The commune-sangkat council shall make an annual report on:

- Priority tasks and goals for service delivery and development projects of the commune or sangkat during the past fiscal year

- Results of the implementation of priority tasks and goals for service delivery and all development projects of the commune or sangkat.

- Measures taken to improve various sectors that had gaps during past implementation

- Priority tasks and goals for service delivery and development projects of the commune or sangkat during the current fiscal year.

The commune-sangkat council shall approve this report within a maximum period of 45 days after the end of each fiscal year.

#### Article 70.-

The annual report as stated in Article 69 of this law, after being approved by the commune-sangkat council, shall:

- Be copied to the Minister of Interior

- Be kept for the public

- Be copied and distributed to organizations, associations and key public figures at cost price.

#### Article 71.-

The commune-sangkat council shall consider various views provided on the report as stated in Articles 69 and 70 of this law, and shall accept good views to be included in the commune-sangkat development plan.

#### Article 72.-

The Minister of Interior shall consult and discuss with relevant ministries and institutions to issue instructions on the practical implementation of various provisions stated in Chapter 6 of this law.

# Chapter 7
# Commune-Sangkat Finance

#### Article 73.-

The commune or sangkat has financial resources, a budget and its own property.

#### Article 74.-

The commune or sangkat has the right to receive direct revenues from tax revenues, non-tax revenues and various service fees.

The above taxes include land tax, property tax and rental tax.

In case the Ministry of Economy and Finance collects direct revenues of the commune or sangkat, the collection shall be done on behalf of the commune or sangkat.

The types, rates and mode of collection of the above tax revenues, non-tax revenues and various service fees shall be determined by law.

#### Article 75.-

The commune or sangkat has the right to receive shared resources from state revenues for its budget.

These shared resources include:

- Transfer of a portion of the fund from specific state tax and non-tax revenue sources

- Surcharges and specific shared resources or various gifts received from state revenue sources.

The above shared resources shall be provided to the commune or sangkat with accountability.

The organization and procedures for the transfer of the above shared funds shall be determined by a sub-decree.

#### Article 76.-

Each commune or sangkat has the right to receive service fee resources for implementing the role as an agent of the state as delegated by the Royal Government or any ministry or institution of the Royal Government.

The above service fees are additional funds to the revenue of the commune or sangkat or other shared funds that the commune or sangkat receives from state revenue sources.

The Royal Government or any ministry or institution of the Royal Government shall provide additional service fees to the commune or sangkat that has implemented roles as agent representing the state as stated above.

In case the delegation of power and roles of the state is permanent, the Royal Government shall organize a stable and long-term financial resource to be provided to the commune or sangkat.

The organization of the above service fees shall be determined by a sub-decree.

#### Article 77.-

The state shall establish a Commune-Sangkat Fund.

The Commune-Sangkat Fund is established with the objective of transferring national revenues and other revenues to the commune-sangkat budget.

The transfer of national revenues to the above fund shall be done according to a formula or a specific rate for a period of at least 3 years and not more than 5 years as determined by a sub-decree.

The practical procedures for implementing this article shall be determined by a sub-decree.

#### Article 78.-

The Commune-Sangkat Fund comes from:

- Sharing of a specific portion of state revenue from the annual state budget as determined by a sub-decree

- Grants and loans from domestic and foreign sources.

The annual sharing of resources to the commune or sangkat through the Commune-Sangkat Fund shall be done in accordance with a formula determined by a sub-decree.

The mode of management, rules for use and procedures for disbursement from the Commune-Sangkat Fund shall be determined by a sub-decree.

#### Article 79.-

The commune or sangkat shall prepare and approve its budget every year.

The commune-sangkat budget shall be prepared in form and approved by or before the date in accordance with the national budget system.

The commune-sangkat budget shall be balanced between total revenues and total expenditures.

The commune or sangkat may increase expenditures only in accordance with its revenues.

The commune-sangkat budget shall reflect the priorities and needs of the commune-sangkat development plan and investment programs.

The procedures for preparation and approval of the above budget shall be determined by a sub-decree.

#### Article 80.-

The commune or sangkat has no right to borrow money.

The commune or sangkat cannot guarantee any financial claims or obligations.

#### Article 81.-

The commune or sangkat has the right to receive transfer of a portion of state property as its own property.

The commune or sangkat shall properly manage property it has acquired on its own and property provided by the state.

The commune or sangkat may use and derive revenue from property provided by the state for its management.

The commune or sangkat shall maintain and protect property provided by the state to remain intact, and the commune or sangkat has no right to sell or transfer ownership or change the use of such property without prior approval from the Minister of Economy and Finance after having received the agreement of the Minister of Interior.

The mode of management and use of the above property shall be determined by a sub-decree.

#### Article 82.-

The commune or sangkat shall organize a system for management, monitoring and inspection of its finance, which includes:

- Financial standards and practical implementation of accounting

- Asset management and organization

- Various auditing duties

- Financial management with transparency, efficiency and accountability.

The system for management, monitoring and inspection of the commune-sangkat finance shall be determined by a sub-decree.

#### Article 83.-

The financial regime and property management of the commune or sangkat shall be under the inspection of the Ministry of Economy and Finance after having consulted and agreed on the principles with the Ministry of Interior.

# Chapter 8
# Penalties

#### Article 84.-

A member of the commune-sangkat council who fails to comply with the provisions of this law shall be subject to disciplinary action without prejudice to other criminal penalties.

Disciplinary actions include:

- Reprimand in the meeting of the commune-sangkat council with clear reasons

- In case of continued misconduct, suspension of a portion or all of the allowance.

The disciplinary action requiring the suspension of a portion or all of the allowance as stated above can be implemented only with the prior approval of the Minister of Interior at the request of the commune-sangkat council.

 the suspended allowance shall be kept for the benefit of the commune-sangkat budget.

In case the above two disciplinary actions have been implemented but the person concerned still fails to reform, the commune-sangkat council may terminate that person from the position of member of commune-sangkat council by a vote of the absolute majority of all members of the whole commune-sangkat council, and a new member shall be selected to replace that person in accordance with the conditions stated in the second paragraph of Article 16 of this law.

In case the chairman of the commune-sangkat council, who is the Commune Chief or Sangkat Chief, is subject to disciplinary action, a member of the council and an official appointed by the Minister of Interior shall be selected to lead the meeting of the commune-sangkat council to implement the above disciplinary action.

The official appointed by the Minister of Interior to lead the meeting above has no right to vote.

# Chapter 9
# Inter-Provisions

#### Article 85.-

In order to have a basis for organizing the election of members of the commune-sangkat council and for managing the commune or sangkat, the Minister of Interior shall issue a proclamation on the number, names and boundaries of the commune or sangkat within the territory of the Kingdom of Cambodia at least 9 months before the date of the first election of members of the commune-sangkat council.

#### Article 86.-

After the first election of members of the commune-sangkat council, each commune-sangkat council shall re-organize the structures under its own commune or sangkat according to the procedures stated in Article 30 of this law.

#### Article 87.-

In order to create favorable conditions for managing the commune or sangkat according to the decentralization system, an inter-ministerial committee called "National Committee for Support to Commune or Sangkat" shall be established with the Minister of Interior as chairman.

The National Committee for Support to Commune or Sangkat shall be established by a Royal Decree at the request of the Prime Minister.

The National Committee for Support to Commune or Sangkat shall be established after this law comes into force and shall end its mandate at least 9 months before the date of the next election of members of the commune-sangkat council.

The National Committee for Support to Commune or Sangkat may be divided into sub-committees as necessary.

The National Committee for Support to Commune or Sangkat shall use the entity established in accordance with Article 59 of this law as its secretariat.

#### Article 88.-

The National Committee for Support to Commune or Sangkat has the competence to make proposals and comments to the Ministry of Interior and the Royal Government on the following issues:

- Research and redefine boundaries of all communes and sangkats within the territory of the Kingdom of Cambodia

- Divide functions, powers and various duties between the Royal Government and the commune or sangkat, as well as organize cooperation and coordination between two or more communes or sangkats

- Training and capacity building for the commune-sangkat council

- Organize revenues and taxes for the commune or sangkat

- Technical support to the commune or sangkat

- Search for means to provide information and public administration communication regarding the process of the commune-sangkat management system according to democracy and decentralization

- Provide comments to the Royal Government on the implementation of decentralization policy under the power of this law and provide comments on public administration reform

- Prepare draft legal instruments for the implementation of this law

- Joint comments on sub-decrees related to the work at the commune-sangkat level.

- Determine the transitional administration regime for the commune or sangkat that does not have sufficient capacity to implement the decentralization policy.

#### Article 89.-

After the first election of members of the commune-sangkat council, the Minister of Interior, based on the proposals and comments of the National Committee for Support to Commune or Sangkat, may propose to establish a commune or sangkat, dissolve a commune or sangkat, adjust boundaries of a commune or sangkat, change the name of a commune or sangkat in accordance with the conditions stated in Articles 6 and 7 of this law for the purpose of organizing the second election of members of the commune-sangkat council.

# Chapter 10
# Final Provisions

#### Article 90.-

Any provisions contrary to this law shall be considered null and void.
