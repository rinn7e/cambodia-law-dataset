# LAW
# ON
# PROLIFERATION OF WEAPONS OF MASS DESTRUCTION FINANCING

### CHAPTER 1
### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### ARTICLE 1: PURPOSE

This Law aims to ensure the combatting of financing for the proliferation of weapons of mass destruction by defining measures to freeze, prevent, suppress, and eliminate the financing of the proliferation of weapons of mass destruction.

#### ARTICLE 2: SCOPE

This Law applies to all activities related to the financing of the proliferation of weapons of mass destruction within the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### ARTICLE 3: DEFINITIONS

Key terms used in this Law are defined as follows:

1- Assets refers to funds, property, financial resources, and economic resources of all kinds, whether tangible or intangible, movable or immovable, currently existing or that may exist in the future, regardless of how they were obtained, provided they fall into the following categories:

a- Cash.

b- Virtual currency or digital currency, including cryptocurrency.

c- Credits, traveler's checks, bank checks, postal orders.

d- Precious metals and precious stones.

e- Immovable property, movable property, and vessels.

f- Shares, securities, bonds, and instruments (Bill of exchange or draft).

g- Liquidation rights, letters of guarantee, performance bonds, and other financial promises.

h- Letters of credit, bill of lading, transport documents, sales invoices.

i- Financial instruments for export.

j- Natural resources.

k- Labor resources, such as crew services.

l- Other economic resources that can be used to obtain funds, goods, or services.

m- Documents or legal instruments in any form, including electronic or digital, as evidence of ownership or interest or title to assets.

n- Interest, dividends, income, or increased value from any part of the assets.

2- Designated Person or Entity refers to a natural person or entity designated by the United Nations Security Council or a Committee of the Security Council pursuant to Article 4 of this Law or by a court pursuant to Article 5 of this Law.

3- Entity refers to legal persons, non-corporate entities, groups, associations, organizations, institutions, and other arrangements.

4- Asset Freezing refers to the prevention of sale, provision, pledge, transfer, conversion, commercial exploitation, relocation, or use of assets.

5- Weapons of Mass Destruction refers to atomic or nuclear explosive weapons, radioactive material weapons, chemical weapons, and biological weapons that can kill or cause catastrophe to humans, animals, or plants, including other weapons that will be produced in the future with characteristics identical to atomic or nuclear bombs or other weapons as described above.

6- Cambodia Financial Intelligence Unit refers to the Cambodia Financial Intelligence Unit as stated in the Law on Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the Financing of Terrorism and which performs additional functions as stated in this Law.

7- Supervisory Ministries or Institutions refers to the National Bank of Cambodia, the Securities and Exchange Commission of Cambodia, and other ministries or institutions that supervise reporting entities.

### CHAPTER 2
### DESIGNATION AND ASSET FREEZING

#### ARTICLE 4: DESIGNATION BY THE UNITED NATIONS SECURITY COUNCIL

After receiving a resolution designating a person or entity on the list by the United Nations Security Council or its Committee, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation shall immediately transmit the resolution designating the individuals or entities on the list as stated in the Annex of this Law to the Ministry of Justice, and the Ministry of Justice shall immediately transmit those documents to the court to request a decision to freeze all assets as follows:

a- Assets owned or controlled by the designated person or entity, whether in whole or in part, directly or indirectly, and not only those assets that can be linked to a specific activity, illicit plan, or threat.

b- Assets of a person or entity representing or acting under the direct instruction of the designated person or entity.

c- Newly increased assets or assets derived from assets specified in paragraphs (a) and (b).

d- Names designated on the list by the United Nations Security Council or its Committee, in accordance with the resolutions of the United Nations Security Council as stated in the Annex of this Law.

The asset freezing decision issued by the court shall have no fixed duration, unless the decision is cancelled.

#### ARTICLE 5: DESIGNATION BY THE COURT

The Minister of Justice may issue a mandate to the Prosecutor General attached to the Court of Appeal or the Prosecutor attached to the Court of First Instance to request the court to designate a person or entity on the list and freeze their assets.

The court shall issue a decision to designate a person or entity on the list and freeze their assets if there are reasonable grounds to believe that the person or entity meets the conditions detailed in paragraph 32 of Resolution No. 2270 of the United Nations Security Council and its subsequent resolutions.

The asset freezing decision issued by the court shall apply to the following assets:

a- Assets owned or controlled by the designated person or entity, whether in whole or in part, directly or indirectly, and not only those assets that can be linked to a specific activity, illicit plan, or threat.

b- Assets of a person or entity representing or acting under the direct instruction of the designated person or entity.

c- Newly increased assets or assets derived from assets specified in paragraphs (a) and (b).

d- Names designated on the list by the United Nations Security Council or its Committee, in accordance with the resolutions of the United Nations Security Council as stated in the Annex of this Law.

The decision to designate on the list and freeze assets issued by the court shall have no fixed duration, unless the decision is cancelled.

#### ARTICLE 6: ASSET FREEZING

Any person or entity who is in possession of assets that are frozen by the power of a court decision as specified in Article 4 or Article 5 of this Law, shall freeze those assets without delay and without prior notice.

### CHAPTER 3
### REQUEST FOR DELISTING AND AUTHORIZATION TO USE ASSETS

#### ARTICLE 7: REQUEST TO THE UNITED NATIONS SECURITY COUNCIL FOR DELISTING

Any person or entity affected by a resolution designating them on the list as specified in Article 4 of this Law, may file a complaint for delisting as specified in the Annex of this Law, to the United Nations Security Council or its Committee for review and decision on the case.

When the United Nations Security Council or its Committee decides to delist a person or entity, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation shall immediately transmit the resolution to delist the individuals or entities to the Ministry of Justice, and the Ministry of Justice shall immediately transmit those documents to the court to request the court to issue a decision to unfreeze the assets.

#### ARTICLE 8: REQUEST TO THE COURT FOR DELISTING AND UNFREEZING OF ASSETS

Any person or entity affected by a court decision to designate them on the list and freeze their assets as specified in Article 5 of this Law, may file a complaint with the court to request delisting and unfreezing of assets in accordance with the legal procedures.

When the court issues a decision to delist and unfreeze assets, the court shall notify the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation through the Ministry of Justice, so that the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation can notify the United Nations Security Council or its Committee of the decision to delist the person or entity and unfreeze the assets.

#### ARTICLE 9: AUTHORIZATION TO USE FROZEN ASSETS

Any person or entity in possession of frozen assets may be authorized to use and manage the frozen assets.

The use and management of frozen assets shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

### CHAPTER 4
### PROHIBITIONS AND OBLIGATIONS

#### ARTICLE 10: PROHIBITION ON THE USE OF ASSETS

No person or entity subject to the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall provide all or part of assets, directly or indirectly, or use those assets for the benefit of the following persons or entities:

a- Designated person or entity.

b- Person or entity owned or controlled, directly or indirectly, by a designated person or entity.

c- Person or entity representing or acting under the direct instruction of a designated person or entity, or an entity owned or controlled by a designated person or entity.

A reporting entity as specified in Article 11 of this Law may be authorized to accept payments on cards or other instruments arising from contract obligations into a frozen account, provided that those deposits are also frozen.

#### ARTICLE 11: REPORTING ENTITIES

Reporting entities as defined in this Law include:

a- Banks, including branches of foreign banks.

b- Non-bank financial institutions, including securities firms and insurance companies.

c- Microfinance institutions.

d- Credit cooperatives.

e- Pledging companies, pension and investment funds, investment companies, and investment fund management companies.

f- Money exchange businesses.

g- Remittance services.

h- Gaming and betting operations.

i- Real estate agents, companies, and developers of buildings and land.

j- Precious metal and precious stone dealers.

k- Post offices that perform payment transactions.

l- Lawyers, notaries, accountants, auditors, investment advisors, and asset managers when they prepare or carry out transactions related to activities for their clients.

m- Casinos and other gaming and betting institutions.

n- Non-governmental organizations and foundations performing business activities and foundations.

o- Other persons or entities as determined by the Cambodia Financial Intelligence Unit within the scope of this Law.

#### ARTICLE 12: REPORTING OBLIGATIONS OF REPORTING ENTITIES

A reporting entity that has frozen assets in accordance with Article 6 of this Law shall report within no more than 3 (three) days regarding the asset freezing and any attempts to carry out transactions on the frozen assets to the Cambodia Financial Intelligence Unit.

A reporting entity that is requested to provide assets to a designated person or entity in violation of Article 10 of this Law shall report this request without delay to the Cambodia Financial Intelligence Unit.

The reporting entity shall submit a report on the asset freezing to the Cambodia Financial Intelligence Unit. The Cambodia Financial Intelligence Unit shall copy the report on asset freezing to the Ministry...

Reporting entities who have submitted a report in accordance with this Article shall provide additional information related to the report promptly upon request from the Cambodia Financial Intelligence Unit or the Ministry of Justice.

#### ARTICLE 13: REPORTING OBLIGATIONS OF PERSONS OR ENTITIES OTHER THAN REPORTING ENTITIES

A person or entity other than a reporting entity that has frozen assets in accordance with Article 6 of this Law shall report within no more than 3 (three) days regarding the asset freezing and any attempts to carry out transactions on the frozen assets to the Ministry of Justice.

A person or entity other than a reporting entity that is requested to provide assets to a designated person or entity in violation of Article 10 of this Law shall report this request without delay to the Ministry of Justice.

The person or entity other than a reporting entity shall submit a report on the asset freezing to the Ministry of Justice, and the Ministry of Justice shall copy the report on asset freezing to the Cambodia Financial Intelligence Unit. The Ministry of Justice may determine the format of the report on asset freezing.

A person or entity other than a reporting entity that has submitted a report in accordance with this Article shall provide additional information related to the report promptly upon request from the Ministry of Justice or the Cambodia Financial Intelligence Unit.

### CHAPTER 5
### CONFISCATION OF FROZEN ASSETS

#### ARTICLE 14: CONFISCATION OF FROZEN ASSETS

Confiscation of frozen assets shall be carried out in accordance with a court decision upon a request from the Prosecutor General attached to the Court of Appeal or the Prosecutor attached to the Court of First Instance after receiving an instruction from the Minister of Justice or based on a request from the holder of the frozen assets.

The court may issue a decision ordering the confiscation of frozen assets if there is a reasonable risk that those assets may be lost.

The court may appoint a manager to take care of and manage the confiscated assets.

The assets confiscated in accordance with this Article shall be kept by the manager appointed by the court until those assets are no longer subject to asset freezing as specified in Article 4 or Article 5 of this Law.

### CHAPTER 6
### MANAGEMENT MECHANISM

#### ARTICLE 15: NATIONAL COORDINATION COMMITTEE

The Royal Government shall establish a National Coordination Committee in charge of developing and issuing leading policies and coordinating the implementation of this Law.

The organization and functioning of the National Coordination Committee shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

#### ARTICLE 16: MONITORING AND INSPECTION

Supervisory ministries and institutions shall monitor reporting entities under their supervision to ensure compliance with this Law.

The Ministry of Justice shall monitor compliance and disseminate this Law to other persons or entities that are not reporting entities.

#### ARTICLE 17: DISCLOSURE OF INFORMATION

The Ministry of Justice, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation, the Cambodia Financial Intelligence Unit, and supervisory ministries and institutions may disclose information obtained from the implementation of this Law to national ministries and institutions or international agencies or institutions, for the purpose of strengthening law enforcement, inspection, or regulation.

#### ARTICLE 18: LEGAL INSTRUMENTS FOR LAW ENFORCEMENT

The procedures for combatting the financing of the proliferation of weapons of mass destruction shall be determined by a Sub-decree upon the proposal of the Minister of Justice.

The Ministry of Justice may issue instructions to persons or entities that are not reporting entities on the implementation of this Law.

Supervisory ministries and institutions may issue instructions to reporting entities for the purpose of implementing this Law.

### CHAPTER 7
### PENAL PROVISIONS

#### ARTICLE 19: OFFENSE OF VIOLATING THE FREEZING DECISION

Without prejudice to other criminal offenses, any person who fails to comply with a court decision as specified in Article 6 of this Law shall be punished by imprisonment from 2 (two) years to 5 (five) years and a fine from 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels to 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels.

#### ARTICLE 20: OFFENSE OF PROVIDING ASSETS

Without prejudice to other criminal offenses, any person who provides all or part of assets, directly or indirectly, or uses those assets for the benefit of the persons or entities specified in Article 10 of this Law shall be punished by imprisonment from 7 (seven) years to 15 (fifteen) years and a fine from 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels to 200,000,000 (two hundred million) Riels.

#### ARTICLE 21: OFFENSE OF VIOLATING REPORTING OBLIGATIONS

Without prejudice to other criminal offenses, any person who fails to report in accordance with Article 12 or Article 13 of this Law shall be punished by imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 6 (six) months and a fine from 100,000 (one hundred thousand) Riels to 1,000,000 (one million) Riels.

#### ARTICLE 22: LIABILITY OF LEGAL ENTITIES

A legal entity may be declared criminally liable as specified in Article 42 (Criminal Liability of Legal Entities) of the Criminal Code for the offenses specified in Article 19 of this Law. The legal entity shall be punished by a fine from 30,000,000 (thirty million) Riels to 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels, and one or more additional penalties as specified in Article 168 (Additional Penalties Applicable to Legal Entities) of the Criminal Code.

A legal entity may be declared criminally liable as specified in Article 42 (Criminal Liability of Legal Entities) of the Criminal Code for the offenses specified in Article 20 of this Law. The legal entity shall be punished by a fine from 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels to 400,000,000 (four hundred million) Riels, and one or more additional penalties as specified in Article 168 (Additional Penalties Applicable to Legal Entities) of the Criminal Code.

A legal entity may be declared criminally liable as specified in Article 42 (Criminal Liability of Legal Entities) of the Criminal Code for the offenses specified in Article 21 of this Law. The legal entity shall be punished by a fine from 30,000,000 (thirty million) Riels to 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels, and one or more additional penalties as specified in Article 168 (Additional Penalties Applicable to Legal Entities) of the Criminal Code.

### CHAPTER 8
### FINAL PROVISIONS

#### ARTICLE 23: ABROGATION

All provisions contrary to this Law shall be abrogated.

#### ARTICLE 24: PROMULGATION
