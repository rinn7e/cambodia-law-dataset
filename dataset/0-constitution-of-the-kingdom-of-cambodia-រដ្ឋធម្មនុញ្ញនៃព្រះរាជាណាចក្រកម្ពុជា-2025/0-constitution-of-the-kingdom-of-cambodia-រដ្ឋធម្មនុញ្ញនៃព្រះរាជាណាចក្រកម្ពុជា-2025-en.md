# Preamble

We, the Cambodian People

Who have had a grand civilization, a prosperous and vast nation, glorious and high prestige, shining like a radiant diamond,

Have fallen into terrible circumstances during the last two decades, passing through grief, destruction, decay and great regret,

Have awakened, stood up, united with strong will and determination to strengthen national unity, preserve and protect Cambodia's territory, precious sovereignty and the noble Angkor civilization, rebuilding the nation into an "Island of Peace" again based on a liberal multi-party democratic system, guaranteeing human rights, respecting the law, and taking high responsibility for the nation's future destiny to reach progress, growth and eternal prosperity.

## In this resolute will

We inscribe in the Constitution of the Kingdom of Cambodia as follows:


# Chapter 1: Sovereignty

## Article 1

Cambodia is a Kingdom in which the King shall fulfill His functions according to the Constitution and the principles of liberal multi-party democracy.

The Kingdom of Cambodia is an independent, sovereign, peaceful, permanently neutral and non-aligned State.

## Article 2

The territorial integrity of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be absolutely inviolable within its borders as determined in the 1/100,000 scale map made between the years 1933-1953 and internationally recognized between the years 1963-1969.

## Article 3

The Kingdom of Cambodia is an indivisible State.

## Article 4

The motto of the Kingdom of Cambodia is: Nation, Religion, King.

## Article 5

The official language and script shall be the Khmer language and script.

## Article 6

Phnom Penh is the capital of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

The national flag, national anthem and national emblem shall be defined in Annexes 1, 2 and 3.

# Chapter 2: The King

## Article 7

The King of Cambodia shall reign but shall not govern.

The King shall be the Head of State for life.

The person of the King shall be inviolable.

## Article 8

The King shall be a symbol of national unity and continuity.

The King shall be the guarantor of national independence, sovereignty and territorial integrity of the Kingdom of Cambodia, the protector of rights and freedoms of all citizens and the guarantor of international treaties.

## Article 9

The King shall serve as a high arbiter to ensure the regular execution of public powers.

## Article 10

The Cambodian monarchy shall be an elective monarchy.

The King shall not have any power to appoint a heir to the throne.

## Article 11 (New)

In the event that the King cannot perform His duties as Head of State as usual due to serious illness certified by a group of medical experts selected by the President of the Senate, the President of the National Assembly and the Prime Minister, the President of the Senate shall perform the duties of the Head of State on His behalf as Regent.

In the event that the President of the Senate is unable to perform the duties of the Head of State on behalf of the King as Regent when He is seriously ill as specified in the paragraph above, the President of the National Assembly shall perform these duties.

The position of Head of State on behalf of the King as Regent may be transferred to other persons in the same circumstances as specified above, according to the following order of precedence:

A- First Vice President of the Senate

B- First Vice President of the National Assembly

C- Second Vice President of the Senate

D- Second Vice President of the National Assembly

## Article 12 (New)

When the King dies, the President of the Senate shall take over the duties of Acting Head of State in the capacity of Regent of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

In the event that the President of the Senate is unable to perform the duties of Acting Head of State on behalf of the King when He dies, the taking over of duties as Acting Head of State in the capacity of Regent shall follow paragraphs 2 and 3 of Article 11 (New).

## Article 13 (New)

Within a maximum period of 7 days, the new King of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be chosen by the Throne Council.

The composition of the Throne Council consists of:

- President of the Senate

- President of the National Assembly

- Prime Minister

- Supreme Patriarchs of the Mohanikay and Thammayut orders

- First and Second Vice Presidents of the Senate

- First and Second Vice Presidents of the National Assembly

The organization and functioning of the Throne Council shall be determined by law.

## Article 14

The person elected as King of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be a member of the Cambodian Royal Family, who is at least 30 years old, and a descendant of King Ang Duong, or...

Norodom or Sisowat.

Before ascending the throne, the King shall take an oath of allegiance as specified in Annex 4.

## Article 15

The wife of the King shall have the royal title of Queen of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

## Article 16

The Queen of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall not have the right to engage in politics, to take the role of Head of State or Head of the Royal Government, or to hold any administrative or political positions.

The Queen of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall devote her efforts to fulfilling her royal role in serving the interests of society, humanitarianism, religion, and helping the King in His royal duties, protocol, and diplomacy.

## Article 17

The provision that the King reigns but does not govern, as stated in the first paragraph of Article 7 of this Constitution, shall not be amended.

## Article 18 (New)

The King shall communicate with the Senate and the National Assembly by royal messages.

These royal messages shall not be subject to discussion or debate by the Senate and the National Assembly.

## Article 19 (New-Two)

The King shall appoint the Prime Minister and the Council of Ministers according to the procedures stipulated in Article 119 (New-Two).

## Article 20

The King shall grant an official audience twice a month to the Prime Minister and the Council of Ministers to hear their reports on the state of the nation.

## Article 21

At the proposal of the Council of Ministers, the King shall sign royal decrees appointing, transferring or terminating the duties of high-ranking civil and military officials, ambassadors and envoys extraordinary and plenipotentiary.

Upon proposal by the Supreme Council of the Magistracy, the King shall sign royal decrees appointing, transferring or dismissing judges of the judiciary.

## Article 22 (New)

When the nation faces danger, the King shall make a public proclamation placing the country in a state of emergency, after unanimous agreement from the Prime Minister, the President of the National Assembly and the President of the Senate.

## Article 23

The King shall be the Supreme Commander of the Royal Cambodian Armed Forces.

A Commander-in-Chief of the Royal Cambodian Armed Forces shall be appointed to command the Royal Cambodian Armed Forces.

## Article 24 (New)

The King shall serve as Chairman of the Supreme Council of National Defense to be established by law.

The King shall declare war after approval by the National Assembly and the Senate.

## Article 25

The King shall receive credentials of ambassadors and envoys extraordinary and plenipotentiary from foreign countries accredited to the Kingdom of Cambodia.

## Article 26 (New)

The King shall sign and ratify international treaties and conventions after their approval by the National Assembly and the Senate.

## Article 27

The King shall have the right to grant pardons or commutations of sentences.

## Article 28 (New)

The King shall sign royal decrees promulgating the Constitution and laws adopted by the National Assembly and reviewed by the Senate, and shall sign royal decrees at the proposal of the Council of Ministers.

When the King is ill and requires medical treatment abroad, the King has the right to delegate His power to sign the aforementioned royal decrees to the Acting Head of State via a royal message of delegation.

## Article 29 (New)

The King shall establish and confer national medals and decorations.

The King shall decide on military and civil rank promotions as determined by law.

## Article 30 (New)

In the absence of the King, the President of the Senate shall serve as Acting Head of State.

In the event that the President of the Senate is unable to perform the duties of Acting Head of State in the King's absence, the taking over of duties as Acting Head of State shall follow paragraphs 2 and 3 of Article 11 (New).


# Chapter 3: Rights and Duties of Khmer Citizens

## Article 31

The Kingdom of Cambodia shall recognize and respect human rights as stipulated in the United Nations Charter, the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, the covenants and conventions related to human rights, women's rights and children's rights.

Every Khmer citizen shall be equal before the law, enjoying the same rights, freedom and fulfilling the same duties regardless of race, color, sex, language, belief, religion, political tendency, national origin, social status, wealth or other status. The exercise of personal rights and freedom by any individual shall not adversely affect the rights and freedom of others. The exercise of such rights and freedom shall be in accordance with the law.

## Article 32

Every individual shall have the right to life, liberty and security of person.

Capital punishment shall be abolished.

## Article 33 (New)

Khmer citizens shall not be exiled or deported to any foreign country unless there is a mutual agreement on extradition.

Khmer citizens residing abroad shall be protected by the State.

The acquisition and loss of Khmer nationality, including the deprivation of Khmer nationality, shall be determined by law.

## Article 34 (New) (One)

Khmer citizens of either sex shall have the right to vote and to stand as candidates for election.

Khmer citizens of either sex of at least 18 years of age shall have the right to vote.

Khmer citizens of either sex of at least 25 years of age may stand as candidates for election to the National Assembly.

Khmer citizens of either sex of at least 40 years of age may stand as candidates for election to the Senate.

Provisions depriving the right to vote and the right to stand as a candidate shall be determined by law.

## Article 35

Khmer citizens of either sex shall have the right to participate actively in the political, economic, social and cultural life of the nation.

Any suggestions from citizens shall be given full consideration and resolved by State organizations.

## Article 36

Khmer citizens of either sex shall have the right to choose any employment according to their ability and to the needs of society.

Khmer citizens of either sex shall receive equal pay for equal work.

Housework shall be considered equal in value to work for which payment is received outside the home.

Khmer citizens of either sex shall have the right to social security and other social benefits as determined by law.

Khmer citizens of either sex shall have the right to form trade unions and to be members of them.

The organization and operation of trade unions shall be determined by law.

## Article 37

The right to strike and non-violent demonstration shall be exercised in the framework of law.

## Article 38

The law shall prohibit all physical abuse of any individual.

The law shall protect the life, honor and dignity of citizens.

The prosecution, arrest or detention of any person shall not be done except in accordance with the law.

Coercion, physical ill-treatment or any act that increases the severity of the penalty applied to detainees or prisoners shall be prohibited.

The perpetrators, co-perpetrators and accomplices shall be punished according to the law.

Confessions obtained through physical or mental coercion shall not be admissible as evidence of guilt.

Any doubt shall be resolved in favor of the accused.

Any accused person shall be presumed innocent as long as the court has not yet rendered a final judgment.

Every person has the right to defend himself through the judicial system.

## Article 39.-

Khmer citizens have the right to denounce, complain or claim for damages caused by illegal acts of state organizations, social organizations and their staff.

The settlement of complaints and claims for damages is the competence of the courts.

## Article 40.-

The freedom of movement and the right to settle dwellings in legal circumstances of citizens shall be respected.

Khmer citizens may settle abroad or return home.

The right to the inviolability of the home and the privacy of correspondence by mail, telegram, telex, facsimile and telephone shall be guaranteed.

Search of the residence, property and person must be in accordance with the provisions of the law.

## Article 41.-

Khmer citizens shall have freedom of expression, freedom of the press, freedom of publication and freedom of assembly.

No person may abuse these rights to the detriment of the honor of others, to the detriment of the good customs of society, public order and national security.

The regime of the press shall be established by law.

## Article 42.- New

Khmer citizens shall have the right to establish associations and political parties.

These rights shall be determined by law.

Political parties shall put the national interest first and shall not engage in any activity, whether direct or indirect, which affects the interests of the Kingdom of Cambodia and the Khmer people.

All Khmer citizens may participate in mass organizations for mutual assistance, to protect national achievements and social order.

## Article 43.-

Khmer citizens of both sexes shall have the full right to belief.

Freedom of belief and religious practice shall be guaranteed by the State on the condition that they do not affect other beliefs or religions, public order and security.

Buddhism shall be the State religion.

## Article 44.-

All persons, individually or collectively, shall have the right to ownership.

Only Khmer legal entities and citizens of Khmer nationality shall have the right to own land.

Legal private ownership shall be protected by law.

The right to confiscate properties from any person shall be exercised only in the public interest as provided for under the law and shall require fair and just compensation in advance.

## Article 45.-

All forms of discrimination against women shall be abolished.

The exploitation of women's work shall be prohibited.

Men and women shall have equal rights in all fields especially in marriage and family matters.

Marriage shall be conducted according to conditions determined by law based on the principle of mutual consent between one husband and one wife.

## Article 46.-

The commerce of human beings, exploitation by prostitution and obscenity which affects the dignity of women shall be prohibited.

The termination of a woman's employment because of her pregnancy shall be prohibited.

Women shall have the right to maternity leave with full pay and with no loss of seniority or other social benefits.

The State and society shall provide opportunities for women, especially those in rural areas without support, to have a profession, to receive medical care, to send their children to school and to have decent living conditions.

## Article 47.-

Parents shall have the duty to take care of and educate their children to become good citizens.

Children shall have the duty to take care of and nurture their elderly parents according to Khmer customs.

## Article 48.-

The State shall guarantee the protection of the rights of children as stated in the Convention on the Rights of the Child, especially the right to life, the right to education, the right to protection during wartime, and protection against all forms of economic exploitation or sexual abuse.

The State shall prevent children from working in any way that would interfere with their education or harm their health or well-being.

## Article 49.- New

Every Khmer citizen shall respect the Constitution and the laws.

Every Khmer citizen shall put the national interest first and shall not engage in any activity, whether direct or indirect, which affects the interests of the Kingdom of Cambodia and the Khmer people.

Every Khmer citizen shall have the duty to contribute to nation-building and to defend the motherland.

The duty to defend the motherland shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the law.

## Article 50.-

Khmer citizens of both sexes shall respect the principles of national sovereignty and liberal multi-party democracy.

Khmer citizens of both sexes shall respect public property and legal private ownership.

# Chapter IV
## On the Political Regime

### Article 51.- New

The Kingdom of Cambodia adopts a policy of liberal multi-party democracy.

Khmer citizens are the masters of their own country's destiny.

All power belongs to the people. The people exercise their power through the National Assembly, the Senate, the Royal Government and the Courts.

The legislative, executive and judicial powers shall be separate.

### Article 52.-

The Royal Government of Cambodia shall be committed to protecting the independence, sovereignty and territorial integrity of the Kingdom of Cambodia, and implementing a policy of national reconciliation to ensure national unity and to preserve the good customs and traditions of the nation.

The Royal Government of Cambodia shall preserve legality and ensure public order and security. The State shall prioritize the livelihood and well-being of the people.

### Article 53.- New

The Kingdom of Cambodia shall always maintain a policy of permanent neutrality and non-alignment.

The Kingdom of Cambodia lives in

peaceful co-existence with its neighbors and with all other countries throughout the world.

The Kingdom of Cambodia shall never invade any country, nor interfere in any other country's internal affairs, directly or indirectly, and shall solve any problems peacefully with due respect for mutual interests.

The Kingdom of Cambodia shall not join in any military alliance or military agreement which is incompatible with its policy of neutrality.

The Kingdom of Cambodia shall not permit any foreign military base on its territory and shall not have its own military base abroad, except within the framework of a United Nations request.

The Kingdom of Cambodia reserves the right to receive foreign assistance in military equipment, armaments, ammunition, training of its armed forces, and other assistance for self-defense and to maintain public order and security within its territory.

## Article 54.-

The manufacturing, use and storage of nuclear, chemical or biological weapons shall be absolutely prohibited.

## Article 55.-

Any treaty and agreement which is incompatible with the independence, sovereignty, territorial integrity, neutrality and national unity of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be annulled.


# Chapter V
## On the Economy

### Article 56.-

The Kingdom of Cambodia shall adopt a market economy system.

The organization and functioning of this economic system shall be determined by law.

### Article 57.-

Tax collection shall be authorized only by law.

The national budget shall be determined by law.

The monetary management regime and the financial system shall be determined by law.

### Article 58.-

State property includes land, mineral resources, mountains, sea, seabed, sub-seabed, coastline, airspace, islands, rivers, canals, streams, lakes, forests, natural resources, economic and cultural centers, national defense bases and other constructions determined as State property.

The management, use and disposal of State property shall be determined by law.

## Article 59.-

The State shall protect the environment and balance of abundant natural resources and establish a precise plan for the management of land, water, air, wind, geology, ecological system, mines, energy, petrol and gas, rocks and sand, gems, forests and forest by-products, wildlife, fish and aquatic resources.

## Article 60.-

Citizens shall have the right to sell their own products.

The obligation to sell products to the State or the temporary use of private property or products by the State shall be prohibited, except in special cases authorized by law.

## Article 61.-

The State shall promote economic development in all sectors, especially in agriculture, handicrafts, industry, starting from remote areas, by focusing on water, electricity, roads and means of transport, modern technology and credit systems.

## Article 62.-

The State shall pay attention to helping resolve means of production, protecting the prices of products for farmers and crafters, and helping find markets for selling products.

## Article 63.-

The State shall pay attention to managing the market, helping to ensure that the living standard of the citizens is at an appropriate level.

## Article 64.-

The State shall ban and severely punish any person who imports, manufactures, or sells narcotics, counterfeit goods, or expired goods which harm the health and life of the consumers.


# Chapter VI
## On Education, Culture and Social Affairs

### Article 65.-

The State shall protect and promote the right of citizens to receive quality education at all levels and shall take all necessary measures step by step to ensure that this education reaches every citizen.

The State shall pay attention to physical education and sports for the well-being of all Khmer citizens.

### Article 66.-

The State shall establish a comprehensive and unified education system throughout the country which guarantees the principles of educational freedom and equality in education to ensure that every citizen has equal opportunity to build their life.

### Article 67.-

The State shall implement modern curricula and pedagogical principles, including technology and foreign languages.

The State shall manage public and private educational institutions and classes at all levels.

## Article 68.-

The State shall provide free primary and secondary education to all citizens in public schools.

Citizens shall receive at least nine years of education.

The State shall help promote and develop Pali schools and Buddhist education.

## Article 69.-

The State shall have the duty to preserve and develop national culture.

The State shall have the duty to protect and promote the Khmer language as required.

The State shall have the duty to preserve and protect ancient monuments and artifacts and to restore historical sites.

## Article 70.-

Any offense affecting or relating to cultural heritage and artistic heritage shall be severely punished.

## Article 71.-

The perimeter of national heritage sites as well as heritage sites listed as World Heritage shall be considered neutral zones where no military activity is allowed.

## Article 72.-

The health of the people shall be guaranteed.

The State shall pay attention to disease prevention and medical treatment.

Poor people shall receive free medical examinations

free of charge in public hospitals, infirmaries and maternity wards.

The State shall establish infirmaries and maternity wards in rural areas.

## Article 73.-

The State shall pay attention to children and mothers. The State shall establish nurseries and help support women with many children in their care and without support.

## Article 74.-

The State shall help support disabled persons and families of combatants who sacrificed their lives for the nation.

## Article 75.-

The State shall establish a social security system for workers and employees.


# Chapter VII
## On the National Assembly

### Article 76.- New

The National Assembly shall consist of at least 120 members.

The members of the National Assembly shall be elected by free, universal, equal and direct suffrage by secret ballot.

Members of the National Assembly may be re-elected.

Khmer citizens of both sexes who have the right to vote, who are at least 25 years of age and who have Khmer nationality at birth shall be eligible to be candidates for the National Assembly.

The procedures and process of the election shall be determined by the Election Law.

### Article 77.-

The members of the National Assembly represent the entire Khmer nation and not only the citizens from their constituencies.

Any imperative mandate shall be null and void.

### Article 78.-

The term of the National Assembly is five years and shall expire when the new National Assembly takes office.

The National Assembly shall not be dissolved before the end

of its term, unless the Royal Government is unseated twice within a period of 12 months.

In this case, the King shall dissolve the National Assembly, upon the proposal of the Prime Minister and after the approval of the President of the National Assembly.

The election of a new National Assembly shall take place no later than 60 days from the date of dissolution.

During this period, the Royal Government shall only be responsible for day-to-day affairs.

In time of war or in other special circumstances where an election cannot be held, the National Assembly may extend its term for one year at a time, upon the proposal of the King.

Such an extension shall require the approval of at least two-thirds of the total number of members of the National Assembly.

## Article 79.-

The mandate of a member of the National Assembly shall be incompatible with the exercise of any active public function and with the function of a member of any other institution provided for in the Constitution, except when the member exercises functions in the Royal Government.

In this case, the member shall retain his status as a regular member of the National Assembly but shall not hold any position on the Standing Committee or other commissions of the National Assembly.

## Article 80.-

Members of the National Assembly shall enjoy parliamentary immunity.

No member of the National Assembly shall be prosecuted, detained or imprisoned because of opinions expressed or votes cast in the exercise of his functions.

Any prosecution, arrest, detention or imprisonment of a member of the National Assembly shall only be made with the authorization of the National Assembly, or by its Standing Committee between sessions, except in case of flagrante delicto. In the latter case, the competent ministry shall immediately report to the National Assembly or to its Standing Committee for decision.

The decision of the Standing Committee shall be submitted to the next session of the National Assembly for approval by a two-thirds majority of the total number of members of the National Assembly.

In all of the above cases, the detention or prosecution of any member of the National Assembly shall be suspended if the National Assembly so decides by a three-fourths majority of its total members.

## Article 81.-

The National Assembly shall have an autonomous budget for its functioning.

Members of the National Assembly shall receive remuneration.

## Article 82.- New (One)

The National Assembly shall hold its first session no later than sixty days after the election upon the convocation of the King.

Before starting its work, the National Assembly shall decide on the validity of each member's mandate and shall elect separately its President, Vice-Presidents and all members of various commissions of the National Assembly by an absolute majority of all members of the National Assembly.

The President of the National Assembly shall be elected from among the members of the National Assembly who have only Khmer nationality.

The National Assembly shall adopt its internal regulations by an absolute majority of all its members.

All members of the National Assembly shall take an oath before taking office as specified in Annex 5 of the Constitution.

## Article 83.-

The National Assembly shall meet in ordinary session twice a year.

Each session shall last at least three months. Upon the request of the King or the proposal of the Prime Minister or at least one-third of the members of the National Assembly, the Standing Committee of the National Assembly shall convene an extraordinary session.

In this case, the specific agenda of the extraordinary session shall be published to the people along with the date of the meeting.

## Article 84.-

Between sessions, the Standing Committee of the National Assembly shall manage the work of the National Assembly.

The Standing Committee of the National Assembly consists of the President of the National Assembly, the Vice-Presidents, and the Presidents of all commissions of the National Assembly.

## Article 85.-

The sessions of the National Assembly shall be held in the capital of the Kingdom of Cambodia in the assembly hall of the National Assembly, unless otherwise decided in the convocation due to circumstances.

Apart from the above cases, and apart from the place and date determined in the convocation, any session of the National Assembly shall be considered illegal and void.

## Article 86.-

In circumstances of a state of emergency, the National Assembly shall meet every day. The National Assembly has the right to decide to end the above special circumstances when the situation permits.

If the National Assembly cannot meet due to necessity, such as when foreign forces occupy the territory, the proclamation of the state of emergency shall be automatically extended.

During a state of emergency, the National Assembly shall not be dissolved.

## Article 87.-

The President of the National Assembly shall preside over the sessions of the National Assembly, receive draft laws and resolutions adopted by the National Assembly, ensure the implementation of the internal regulations of the National Assembly and manage all international relations of the National Assembly.

In case the President of the National Assembly is unable to perform his duties due to illness, or because he fulfills the function of Acting Head of State or as Regent, or is on a mission abroad, a Vice-President of the National Assembly shall perform the duties on his behalf.

In case of resignation or death of the President or Vice-Presidents, the National Assembly shall elect a new President or Vice-Presidents.

## Article 88.- New (Two)

The sessions of the National Assembly shall be held in public.

The National Assembly may meet in secret session at the request of the President or of at least one-tenth of its members, or of the King, or of the Prime Minister.

The sessions of the National Assembly shall be valid only if there is:

a- A quorum of more than two-thirds of the total number of members of the National Assembly for any vote requiring a two-thirds majority.

b- A quorum of more than half of the total number of members of the National Assembly for any vote requiring an absolute majority.

## Article 89.- New

Upon the request of at least one-tenth of its members, the National Assembly may invite a member or several members of the Royal Government accompanied by high officials as experts in any field to clarify issues of particular importance to the National Assembly.

## Article 90.- New (Two)

The National Assembly is an organ which has legislative power and performs its duties as provided for in the Constitution and laws in force.

The National Assembly shall adopt the national budget, State planning, State loans, State lending, various financial commitments and the creation, modification or abolition of taxes.

The National Assembly shall approve the administrative accounts.

The National Assembly shall adopt the law on general amnesty.

The National Assembly shall approve or annul international treaties or conventions.

The National Assembly shall adopt the law on the declaration of war.

The above-mentioned adoptions shall be made by an absolute majority of the total number of members of the National Assembly.

The National Assembly shall pass a vote of confidence in the Royal Government by an absolute majority of the total number of members of the National Assembly.

## Article 91.- New

Members of the Senate, members of the National Assembly and the Prime Minister have the right to initiate legislation.

Members of the National Assembly have the right to propose amendments to the laws. However, these proposals cannot be accepted if they aim to reduce public income or increase the burden on citizens.

## Article 92.-

Any adoption by the National Assembly that contradicts the principles of maintaining and protecting the independence, sovereignty and territorial integrity of the Kingdom of Cambodia, and which leads to the disruption of political unity or the administrative management of the nation, shall be considered void. The Constitutional Council is the only body competent to decide on this nullity.

## Article 93.- New

A law passed by the National Assembly and reviewed by the Senate and signed by the King for its promulgation shall come into force in Phnom Penh ten days after its promulgation and throughout the country twenty days after its promulgation. However, if the law is declared urgent, it shall come into force throughout the country immediately after its promulgation.

The law signed and promulgated by the King shall be published in the Royal Gazette and disseminated throughout the country within the time frame specified above.

## Article 94.-

The National Assembly shall establish various necessary commissions. The organization and functioning of the National Assembly shall be determined by the internal regulations of the National Assembly.

## Article 95.-

In case of death, resignation or loss of membership of a member of the National Assembly occurring at least six months before the end of the term, a replacement shall be appointed in accordance with the internal regulations of the National Assembly and the Election Law.

## Article 96.-

Members of the National Assembly have the right to put questions to the Royal Government. These questions shall be in writing and submitted through the President of the National Assembly.

The answers shall be given by one or several ministers depending on whether the issues raised come under the responsibility of one or several ministers. If the issues concern the general policy of the Royal Government, the Prime Minister shall answer in person.

The answers by the ministers or by the Prime Minister may be given orally or in writing.

The above answers shall be given within seven days after the receipt of the questions.

In case of an oral answer, the President of the National Assembly shall decide whether or not to open a debate. If there is no debate, the answer of the minister or the Prime Minister shall terminate the questioning.

If a debate is opened, the questioner, other members and the relevant minister or the Prime Minister may discuss and exchange views within a period not exceeding one session.

The National Assembly shall reserve one day per week for answering questions.

However, the session for answering questions shall not lead to any type of vote.

## Article 97.-

The commissions of the National Assembly may invite any minister to clarify issues relating to his field of responsibility.

## Article 98.- New (One)

The National Assembly may dismiss a member of the Royal Government or the Royal Government from office by passing a motion of censure by an absolute majority of the total number of members of the National Assembly.

If the Royal Government is dismissed by the National Assembly, the outgoing Royal Government shall only be responsible for day-to-day affairs until a new Royal Government takes office.

A motion of censure against the Royal Government shall be submitted to the National Assembly by at least one-third of its members before the National Assembly can discuss it.

# Chapter 8 New
## On the Senate

## Article 99.- New

The Senate is an organ which has legislative power and performs its duties as provided for in the Constitution and laws in force.

The number of members of the Senate shall not exceed half of the total number of members of the National Assembly.

Some members of the Senate shall be appointed and some shall be elected by indirect election.

Members of the Senate may be re-appointed and re-elected. Candidates for the Senate shall be Khmer citizens of either sex, have the right to vote, be at least 40 years of age and have Khmer nationality from birth.

## Article 100.- New

Two members of the Senate shall be appointed by the King.

Two members of the Senate shall be elected by the National Assembly by a relative majority.

All other members of the Senate shall be elected by indirect election.

## Article 101.- New

The organization, procedures and functioning of the appointment and election of members of the Senate, as well as the determination of the electors, the electoral college and the electoral districts shall be determined by a law.

## Article 102.- New (One)

The term of the Senate is six years and shall expire when the new Senate takes office.

In time of war or in other special circumstances where an election cannot be held, the Senate may extend its term for one year at a time, upon the proposal of the King.

Such an extension shall require the approval of at least two-thirds of the total number of members of the Senate.

In the above-mentioned circumstances, the Senate shall meet every day. The Senate has the right to decide to end the above special circumstances when the situation permits. However, the decision to end the state of emergency shall be made jointly with the National Assembly in a joint session of the National Assembly and the Senate, unless Article 86 of the Constitution is applied.

If the Senate cannot meet due to necessity, such as when foreign forces occupy the territory, the proclamation of the state of emergency shall be automatically extended.

## Article 103.- New

The mandate of a member of the Senate shall be incompatible with the exercise of any active public function, with the function of a member of the National Assembly and with the function of a member of any other institution provided for in the Constitution.

## Article 104.- New

Members of the Senate shall enjoy parliamentary immunity.

No member of the Senate shall be prosecuted, detained or imprisoned because of opinions expressed or votes cast in the exercise of his functions.

Any prosecution, arrest, detention or imprisonment of a member of the Senate shall only be made with the authorization of the Senate, or by its Standing Committee between sessions, except in case of flagrante delicto. In the latter case, the competent ministry shall immediately report to the Senate or to its Standing Committee for decision.

The decision of the Standing Committee of the Senate shall be submitted to the next session of the Senate for approval by a two-thirds majority of the total number of members of the Senate.

In all the cases mentioned above, the detention or prosecution of any Senator shall be suspended if the Senate has expressed its opinion to suspend it by a three-fourths majority vote of the total number of Senators.

#### Article 105.-New

The Senate shall have an autonomous budget for its operation.

Senators shall receive remuneration.

#### Article 106.-New (Two)

The Senate shall hold its initial session no later than sixty days after the election, upon the convocation by the King.

Before starting its work, the Senate shall declare the validity of the mandate of each member and shall elect separately its President, Vice-Presidents, and all members of various commissions of the Senate by an absolute majority vote of the total number of Senators.

The President of the Senate shall be elected from among the Senators who possess only Khmer nationality.

All Senators shall take an oath before taking office, as provided in Annex 7 of the Constitution.

#### Article 107.-New

The Senate shall meet in ordinary sessions twice a year.

Each session shall last at least three months. If requested by the King or proposed by the Prime Minister or at least one-third of the Senate members, the Senate shall convene an extraordinary session.

#### Article 108.-New

In between Senate sessions, the Permanent Committee of the Senate shall be responsible for managing the work.

The Permanent Committee of the Senate consists of: the President of the Senate, the Vice-Presidents of the Senate, and the chairpersons of all Senate commissions.

#### Article 109.-New

The Senate sessions shall be held in the capital of the Kingdom of Cambodia in the Senate's hall, unless otherwise decided in the convocation notice due to necessary circumstances.

Apart from the cases mentioned above and outside the place and date specified in the invitation, any meeting of the Senate shall be considered illegal and completely null and void.

#### Article 110.-New

The President of the Senate shall chair the sessions of the Senate, receive laws and resolutions adopted by the Senate, ensure the implementation of the Internal Regulations of the Senate, and manage all international relations of the Senate.

In the event that the President of the Senate is busy and unable to perform their duties due to illness, performing the function of Acting Head of State or Regent, or fulfilling a mission abroad, one of the Vice-Presidents shall be responsible for managing the work in their place.

In the case where the President or a Vice-President of the Senate resigns from office or passes away, the Senate shall elect a new President or Vice-President.

#### Article 111.-New (Two)

Senate sessions shall be held in public.

The Senate may meet in secret session upon the request of the President or at least one-tenth of its members, the King, the Prime Minister, or the President of the National Assembly.

Senate sessions shall be valid only if there is:

A- A quorum of more than two-thirds of the total number of Senators for any adoption that requires a two-thirds majority vote of the total number of Senators.

B- A quorum of more than half of the total number of Senators for any adoption that requires a relative majority or an absolute majority vote of the total number of Senators.

The number of votes required for the National Assembly to approve a law as specified in the Constitution shall also apply to the Senate.

#### Article 112.-New

The Senate shall be responsible for coordinating the work between the National Assembly and the Royal Government.

#### Article 113.-New

The Senate shall examine and provide its opinion within a maximum of one month on draft laws or proposed laws that have already been adopted by the National Assembly in the first reading, as well as on all matters submitted by the National Assembly for examination. If the matter is urgent, this period shall be reduced to five days.

If the Senate provides a favorable opinion or fails to provide any opinion within the specified period, the law already adopted by the National Assembly shall be promulgated.

If the Senate requests amendments to the draft law or proposed law, the National Assembly shall immediately consider it for a second reading. The National Assembly shall examine and decide only on the provisions or points that the Senate requested to amend, by rejecting them entirely or accepting some of them.

The back-and-forth process between the Senate and the National Assembly shall be conducted within one month. This period shall be reduced to ten days if the matter concerns the national budget and finance, and to only two days if it is urgent.

If the National Assembly lets the specified period expire or extends the period set for examination, the principled period for the National Assembly and the Senate shall be equally extended.

If the Senate rejects the draft law or proposed law as null and void, the National Assembly cannot consider it for a second reading before a period of

one month. This period shall be reduced to fifteen days if the matter concerns the national budget and finance, and to only four days if it is urgent.

In this second reading of the draft law or proposed law, the National Assembly shall adopt it by an open vote and by an absolute majority.

Any draft law or proposed law decided as mentioned above shall be promulgated.

#### Article 114.-New (One)

The Senate shall create the necessary commissions. The organization and functioning of the Senate shall be determined by the Internal Regulations of the Senate. These Internal Regulations shall be approved by an absolute majority vote of the total number of Senators.

#### Article 115.-New

In the event of the death, resignation, or loss of membership of a Senator occurring at least six months before the end of the legislature, a replacement for that member shall be appointed or elected according to the conditions set forth in the Internal Regulations of the Senate and in the law on the appointment and election of Senators.

# Chapter 9 New
# The Congress of the National Assembly and the Senate

#### Article 116.-New

In case of necessity, the National Assembly and the Senate may meet together as a Congress to resolve important national issues.

#### Article 117.-New

Important national issues as mentioned in Article 116 New above, as well as the organization and functioning of the Congress, shall be determined by a law.


# Chapter 10 New
# The Royal Government

#### Article 118.-New (One)

The Council of Ministers is the Royal Government of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

The Council of Ministers shall be led by one Prime Minister, assisted by Deputy Prime Ministers, and shall also include Senior Ministers and Ministers as members.

#### Article 119.-New (Two)

Upon the proposal of the party with the most seats in the National Assembly, the King shall appoint a dignitary as Prime Minister to form the Royal Government. This proposal from the party with the most seats in the National Assembly shall be copied to the President of the National Assembly.

The dignitary appointed as Prime Minister must be a representative in the National Assembly possessing only Khmer nationality and who has been elected from the party with the most seats in the National Assembly.

This appointed dignitary shall lead their colleagues, who are representatives or members of parties represented in the National Assembly assigned to various positions in the Royal Government, to seek a vote of confidence from the National Assembly.

The vote of confidence shall be conducted on the entire list of the composition of the Council of Ministers.

When the National Assembly has granted its confidence, the King shall issue a Royal Decree appointing the entire Council of Ministers.

Before taking office, the Council of Ministers shall take an oath as provided in Annex 6.

At the end of each legislature of the National Assembly, the outgoing Royal Government shall only be responsible for managing day-to-day affairs until a new Royal Government takes office.

#### Article 120.-New (Article 101 Old)

The functions of members of the Royal Government shall be incompatible with professional activities in commerce or industry and with holding any public office.

#### Article 121.-New (Article 102 Old)

Members of the Royal Government shall be collectively responsible to the National Assembly for the general policy of the Royal Government.

Each member of the Royal Government shall be individually responsible to the Prime Minister and to the National Assembly for their own conduct.

#### Article 122.-New (Article 103 Old)

Members of the Royal Government cannot invoke written or oral orders from anyone as a ground to exempt themselves from their responsibilities.

#### Article 123.-New (Article 104 Old)

The Council of Ministers shall meet every week in a plenary session or a study session.

Sessions shall be led by the Prime Minister. The Prime Minister may assign a Deputy Prime Minister to lead study sessions.

Minutes of all sessions of the Council of Ministers shall be submitted to the King for information.

#### Article 124.-New (Article 105 Old)

The Prime Minister may delegate their powers to Deputy Prime Ministers or any member of the Royal Government.

#### Article 125.-New (One)

In the event that the Prime Minister is temporarily absent, the Prime Minister shall appoint a Deputy Prime Minister to serve as Acting Prime Minister to lead the Council of Ministers in their place on a temporary basis.

If the office of Prime Minister becomes vacant permanently due to death or resignation, a new Council of Ministers shall be urgently appointed under the conditions provided in this Constitution. The President of the National Assembly shall urgently convene a session of the National Assembly and shall organize other necessary procedures promptly within a maximum of 7 days after receiving a proposal from the party with the most seats in the National Assembly.

In the event that the office of Prime Minister becomes vacant permanently due to death, the outgoing Council of Ministers under the leadership of the Deputy Prime Minister appointed as Acting Prime Minister

shall only lead the daily tasks until the new Council of Ministers takes office.

The resignation of the Prime Minister shall be made by a letter of resignation to be submitted to the King and copied to the President of the National Assembly. In this case, the outgoing Council of Ministers shall continue to lead the work of the Royal Government until the new Council of Ministers takes office.

#### Article 126.-New (Article 107 Old)

Each member of the Royal Government shall be punished for crimes or misdemeanors committed in the performance of their duties.

In this case and in the case of serious misconduct in the performance of their duties, the National Assembly may decide to file a complaint with a competent court.

The National Assembly shall decide on this matter by secret ballot by an absolute majority vote of all members of the National Assembly.

#### Article 127.-New (Article 108 Old)

The organization and functioning of the Council of Ministers shall be determined by a law.

# Chapter 11 New
# Of the Judicial Power

#### Article 128.-New (Article 109 Old)

The Judicial Power shall be an independent power.

The Judicial Power shall guarantee impartiality and protect the rights and freedoms of the citizens.

The Judicial Power shall cover all cases, including administrative cases.

This power shall be vested in the Supreme Court and in courts of all sectors and all levels.

#### Article 129.-New (Article 110 Old)

The adjudication of justice shall be done in the name of the Khmer people in accordance with the procedures and laws in force.

Only judges shall have the right to adjudicate. Judges shall perform this duty with strict respect for the law and with all their heart and conscience.

#### Article 130.-New (Article 111 Old)

No organization of the Legislative or Executive Power may receive any judicial power.

#### Article 131.-New (Article 112 Old)

Only the prosecution organization shall have the right to file criminal lawsuits.

#### Article 132.-New (Article 113 Old)

The King shall be the guarantor of the independence of the Judicial Power.

The Supreme Council of the Magistracy shall assist the King in this task.

#### Article 133.-New (Article 114 Old)

Judges shall not be removed from office. However, the Supreme Council of the Magistracy shall decide on disciplinary action against judges who have committed misconduct.

#### Article 134.-New (Article 115 Old)

The Supreme Council of the Magistracy shall be established by an organic law which shall determine its composition and functions.

The Supreme Council of the Magistracy shall be under the chairmanship of the King. The King may appoint a royal representative to chair this Supreme Council of the Magistracy.

The Supreme Council of the Magistracy shall submit proposals to the King on the appointment of judges and prosecutors to all courts.

The Supreme Council of the Magistracy, in its disciplinary function for judges and prosecutors, shall meet under the chairmanship of the President of the Supreme Court or the Prosecutor General of the Supreme Court, depending on the cases related to judges or prosecutors.

#### Article 135.-New (Article 116 Old)

The statute of judges and prosecutors and the organization of the judiciary shall be determined in separate laws.

# Chapter 12 New
# Of the Constitutional Council

#### Article 136.-New

The Constitutional Council shall have the competence to guarantee protection of the respect for the Constitution, to interpret the Constitution and the laws adopted by the National Assembly and definitively reviewed by the Senate.

The Constitutional Council shall have the right to examine and decide on contested cases related to the election of members of the National Assembly and the election of members of the Senate.

#### Article 137.-New (One)

The Constitutional Council shall consist of nine members with a nine-year mandate. One-third of the members of the Constitutional Council shall be replaced every three years. Three members shall be appointed by the King, three members shall be elected by the National Assembly, and three other members shall be appointed by the Supreme Council of the Magistracy.

The President shall be elected by the members of the Constitutional Council from among its members who possess Khmer nationality by birth and who have only Khmer nationality. The President shall have a casting vote in the event of a tie.

#### Article 138.-New (Article 119 Old)

Members of the Constitutional Council shall be chosen from among dignitaries with a higher education degree in law, administration, diplomacy, or economics and who have extensive professional experience.

#### Article 139.-New

The function of a member of the Constitutional Council shall be incompatible with the function of a member of the Senate, a member of the National Assembly, a member of the Royal Government, a sitting judge, any position in the public service, the post of President or Vice-President of a political party, or the post of President or Vice-President of a trade union.

#### Article 140.-New

The King, the Prime Minister, the President of the National Assembly, one-tenth of the members of the National Assembly, the President of the Senate, or one-fourth of the members of the Senate may send laws adopted by the National Assembly to the Constitutional Council for review before they are promulgated.

The Internal Regulations of the National Assembly, the Internal Regulations of the Senate, and all organic laws must be sent to the Constitutional Council for review before they are promulgated. The Constitutional Council shall decide within a maximum of thirty (30) days whether the laws, the Internal Regulations of the National Assembly or the Internal Regulations of the Senate are constitutional or not.

#### Article 141.-New

After a law is promulgated, the King, the President of the Senate, the President of the National Assembly, the Prime Minister, one-fourth of the members of the Senate,

one-tenth of the members of the National Assembly or the courts may ask the Constitutional Council to review the constitutionality of that law.

Citizens shall have the right to appeal against the constitutionality of laws through members of the National Assembly, the President of the National Assembly, members of the Senate, or the President of the Senate as mentioned in the above paragraphs.

#### Article 142.-New (Article 123 Old)

A provision of any article declared by the Constitutional Council to be unconstitutional shall not be promulgated or implemented.

The decision of the Constitutional Council shall be final and without appeal.

#### Article 143.-New (Article 124 Old)

The King shall consult the Constitutional Council on all proposals for amendments to the Constitution.

#### Article 144.-New (Article 125 Old)

An organic law shall determine the organization and functioning of the Constitutional Council.


# Chapter 13 New
# Of the Administration

#### Article 145.-New (One)

The territory of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be divided into municipalities, provinces, cities, districts, sections, communes and quarters.

#### Article 146.-New (One)

Municipalities, provinces, cities, districts, sections, communes and quarters shall be governed in accordance with the conditions provided in an organic law.


# Chapter 14 New
# Of the National Congress

#### Article 147.-New (Article 128 Old)

The National Congress shall enable the citizens to be directly informed about various matters of national interest and to raise issues and requests to the State authorities for resolution.

Khmer citizens of both sexes shall have the right to participate in the National Congress.

#### Article 148.-New (Article 129 Old)

The National Congress shall meet once a year in early December upon the convocation of the Prime Minister.

The National Congress shall proceed under the chairmanship of the King.

#### Article 149.-New

The National Congress shall adopt requests to the Senate, the National Assembly and the State authorities for consideration.

The organization and functioning of the National Congress shall be determined by a law.


# Chapter 15 New (Two)
# Of the Election Organizing Body

#### Article 150.-New (Two)

The National Election Committee is the body competent to organize, manage and oversee the election of members of the Senate and the election of members of the National Assembly, as well as other elections as determined by law.

The National Election Committee shall exercise its competence independently and neutrally to ensure that elections are conducted freely, fairly and justly, in accordance with the principles of liberal multi-party democracy.

The function of a member of the National Election Committee shall be incompatible with holding any position in the public service and with the function of a member of other bodies provided for in this Constitution. Members of the National Election Committee may not be members of a political party or leaders of non-governmental organizations, associations, trade unions or any commercial company.

The National Election Committee shall have an autonomous budget for its operation.

#### Article 151.-New (Two)

The National Election Committee shall consist of nine members with a five-year mandate. Four members shall be chosen by the party leading the Royal Government, four members shall be chosen by the parties having seats in the National Assembly that are not involved in the Royal Government, and one member shall be chosen by agreement among all political parties having seats in the National Assembly.

The Standing Committee of the National Assembly shall organize the selection of the members of the National Election Committee in an open and transparent manner. The Standing Committee of the National Assembly shall prepare the list of members of the National Election Committee and submit it to the National Assembly for a vote of confidence by an absolute majority of the total number of members of the National Assembly.

The members of the National Election Committee shall be appointed by Royal Decree.

In the event that any member of the National Election Committee loses their membership, the Standing Committee of the National Assembly shall initiate procedures to select a new member for replacement within a maximum of 15 days from the date of the loss of membership. The detailed procedures for selecting a new replacement shall be determined in a separate law.

In the event that the selection of the members of the National Election Committee is not organized, the members of the National Election Committee

already in place shall continue to perform their duties and shall have the competence to organize the elections in accordance with the law.

The organization and functioning of the National Election Committee shall be determined by a law.


# Chapter 16 New (Two)
# On the Force, Revision and Amendment of the Constitution

#### Article 152.-New (Two Article 150 New)

This Constitution shall be the supreme law of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

Laws and decisions by state institutions must absolutely conform to the Constitution.

#### Article 153.-New (Two Article 151 New)

The initiative to revise or amend the Constitution shall be the prerogative of the King, the Prime Minister and the President of the National Assembly at the proposal of one-fourth of the total number of members of the National Assembly.

The revision or amendment of the Constitution shall be effected by a constitutional law adopted by the National Assembly by a two-thirds majority vote of the total number of members of the National Assembly.

#### Article 154.-New (Two Article 152 New)

Revision or amendment of the Constitution shall be prohibited when the nation is in a state of emergency, as provided for in Article 86.

#### Article 155.-New (Two Article 153 New)

Revisions or amendments of the Constitution shall be prohibited if they affect the system of liberal multi-party democracy and the regime of constitutional monarchy.

# Chapter 17 New
# Transitional Provisions

#### Article 156.-New (Two Article 154 New)

This Constitution, after its adoption, shall be immediately promulgated by the King of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 157.-New (Two Article 155 New)

After this Constitution comes into force, the Constituent Assembly shall become the National Assembly.

The Internal Regulations of the National Assembly shall come into force after being adopted by the National Assembly.

In the event that the National Assembly cannot yet function, the President, the First Vice-President and the Second Vice-President of the Constituent Assembly shall participate in the Throne Council if the situation in the country so requires.

#### Article 158.-New (Two Article 156 New)

After this Constitution comes into force, the King shall be elected under the conditions provided for in Articles 13 New and 14.

#### Article 159.-New (Two Article 157 New)

The first mandate of the Senate shall be for 6 years and shall expire when the new Senate takes office.

For the first mandate of the Senate:

- The Senate shall have a total of sixty-one members.

- The King shall appoint two members of the Senate, as well as the President, the First Vice-President and the Second Vice-President of the Senate.

- Other members of the Senate shall be appointed by the King upon the proposal of the President of the Senate and the President of the National Assembly from among the members of the political parties having seats in the National Assembly.

- Joint sessions of the National Assembly and the Senate shall be led by Co-Presidents.

#### Article 160.-New (Two Article 158 New)

All laws and normative documents in Cambodia that guarantee the protection of state property, rights, freedoms and legal private property of persons and which are in the national interest shall continue to be effective until new texts are enacted to amend or abrogate them, except for any provisions that are contrary to the spirit of this Constitution.

# Additional Constitutional Law
## Aiming to
### Guarantee the Normal Functioning of National Institutions

#### Article 1.-

This Constitutional Law aims to guarantee, in all circumstances, the smooth functioning of national institutions based on the fundamental principles of liberal multi-party democracy and in accordance with the requirements of necessity.

#### Article 2.-

At the beginning of each new legislature, the National Assembly, under the chairmanship of the most senior member, before starting its work and after deciding on the validity of each member's mandate, may begin to adopt texts of a constitutional nature or laws in order to implement the goals stated in Article 1.

These texts, after being voted and adopted by the National Assembly, must immediately receive urgent action from the most senior member of the National Assembly through the procedures defined for those texts until they are promulgated.

#### Article 3.- New (One)

In the event that the procedures of Article 82 New (One) and Article 119 New (Two) of the Constitution cannot be achieved, the National Assembly, upon the proposal of the political party with the most seats in the National Assembly, may proceed with a package vote to elect the President and Vice-Presidents of the National Assembly, as well as the Chairmen and Vice-Chairmen of the Commissions, and at the same time vote of confidence for the Royal Government.

#### Article 4.- New (One)

The preparation of the list of names of the composition for the election and the vote of confidence shall be implemented as follows:

- The list of names of the composition of the President and Vice-Presidents of the National Assembly, as well as the Chairmen and Vice-Chairmen of all specialized Commissions of the National Assembly, shall be prepared and proposed by the political party with the most seats in the National Assembly and shall be sent to the most senior member of the National Assembly. The President of the National Assembly shall be elected from among the members of the National Assembly who have Khmer nationality as their sole nationality.

- Upon the proposal from the political party with the most seats in the National Assembly, the King shall appoint one dignitary as Prime Minister to form the Royal Government. This dignitary to be appointed as Prime Minister must be a Member of Parliament having Khmer nationality as his sole nationality and chosen from the political party with the most seats in the National Assembly. This appointed dignitary shall prepare the various positions in the Royal Government and then submit the list of all members of the Royal Government to the most senior member of the National Assembly.

- The Dean of the National Assembly shall urgently and immediately prepare a single package list comprising the composition of the President and Vice-Presidents of the National Assembly, as well as the Chairpersons and Vice-Chairpersons of all specialized commissions of the National Assembly, together with the Prime Minister-designate appointed by the King and all members of the Royal Government, to be submitted to the National Assembly for a vote. The Dean of the National Assembly shall lead the National Assembly session to urgently vote on the approval of this list.
- The Dean of the National Assembly shall be chosen by identifying the eldest member of the National Assembly among all members. In the event that this Dean of the National Assembly is unable to perform their duties, the position of Dean shall be given to other members of the National Assembly in order of seniority.

#### Article 5.-
In the process of the package vote, there shall be no debate or discussion, nor shall there be any explanation of the vote after the announcement of the results of the package vote.
Members of the National Assembly shall vote by indicating "yes" or "no" for the single package list submitted by the Dean of the National Assembly. This vote shall be conduct by a show of hands.

#### Article 6.-New of the Additional Constitutional Law
The above package vote shall be decided by an absolute majority vote of the total number of members of the National Assembly.
 

In the event that the first round of voting is not successful, the same procedure shall be applied to subsequent rounds of voting.

#### Article 7.-
This Additional Constitutional Law shall be promulgated as urgent and shall be applied from the beginning of the current legislature.

# Articles amended for the first time

#### Article 28.-
The King shall sign the Royal Decree promulgating the Constitution and laws adopted by the National Assembly, and shall sign the Royal Proclamations at the request of the Council of Ministers.
 


# Articles amended for the second time

#### Article 11.-
In the event that the King cannot perform His duties as Head of State in a normal manner due to a serious illness, as certified by a group of specialized doctors chosen by the President of the National Assembly and the Prime Minister, the President of the National Assembly shall perform the duties of Head of State on His behalf as "Regent".

#### Article 12.-
When the King dies, the President of the National Assembly shall assume the duties of Acting Head of State as Regent of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 13.-
Within a period of no more than seven days, the new King of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be chosen by the Throne Council.
The composition of the Throne Council shall consist of:
- The President of the National Assembly
- The Prime Minister
- The Supreme Patriarchs of the Mahanikaya and Dhammayuttikanikaya sects
- The First and Second Vice-Presidents of the National Assembly.
The organization and functioning of the Throne Council shall be determined by a law.
 

#### Article 18.-
The King shall communicate with the National Assembly by Royal Message. This Message shall not be subjected to any debate by the National Assembly.

#### Article 22.-
When the nation faces danger, the King shall make a public proclamation placing the country in a state of emergency after unanimous agreement from the Prime Minister and the President of the National Assembly.

#### Article 24.-
The King shall serve as the Chairperson of the Supreme Council of National Defense, which shall be established by a law.
The King shall declare war after approval by the National Assembly.

#### Article 26.-
The King shall sign international treaties and conventions and shall ratify them after approval by the National Assembly.

#### Article 28 New.-
The King shall sign the Royal Decree promulgating the Constitution and laws adopted by the National Assembly and shall sign the Royal Proclamations at the request of the Council of Ministers.
During a period when the King is ill and must receive medical treatment abroad, the King has the right to delegate His power to sign the aforementioned Royal Decrees or Royal Proclamations to the Acting Head of State to sign on His behalf by a Royal Message of delegation of power.
 

#### Article 30.-
During the period of the King's absence, the President of the National Assembly shall assume the duties of Acting Head of State.

#### Article 34.-
Khmer citizens of both sexes shall have the right to vote and to stand as candidates for election.
Khmer citizens of both sexes who are at least 18 years old shall have the right to vote.
Khmer citizens of both sexes who are at least 25 years old may stand as candidates for election.
Provisions restricting the right to vote and the right to stand as a candidate shall be defined in the Electoral Law.

#### Article 51.-
The Kingdom of Cambodia adopts a policy of liberal multi-party democracy.
Khmer citizens are the masters of their own country's destiny.
All power belongs to the citizens. Citizens exercise their power through the National Assembly, the Royal Government, and the Judiciary.
Legislative, Executive, and Judicial powers shall be separate.
 

#### Article 90.-
The National Assembly is the only organ having legislative power. This power shall not be delegated by the National Assembly to any other organ or individual.
The National Assembly shall adopt the national budget, state plans, state loans, state lending, financial guarantees, and the creation, amendment, or annulment of taxes.
The National Assembly shall approve administrative accounts.
The National Assembly shall adopt laws on general amnesty.
The National Assembly shall approve the adoption or annulment of international treaties or conventions.
The National Assembly shall adopt laws on the declaration of war.
The above adoptions shall be decided by an absolute majority vote of the total number of members of the National Assembly.
The National Assembly shall pass a vote of confidence in the Royal Government by a two-thirds majority vote of the total number of members of the National Assembly.

#### Article 91.-
Members of the National Assembly and the Prime Minister shall have the right to initiate legislation.
Members of the National Assembly shall have the right to propose amendments to laws, but these proposals shall not be acceptable if they aim to reduce public income or increase the burden on citizens.

#### Article 93.-
A law adopted by the National Assembly and signed by the King for promulgation shall enter into force in Phnom Penh
 

within ten days after its promulgation, and throughout the country within twenty days after its promulgation.
However, if the law is declared as urgent, it shall enter into force immediately throughout the country after its promulgation.
Laws signed and promulgated by the King shall be published in the Royal Gazette and circulated throughout the country within the abovementioned periods.

Khmer citizens may participate in mass organizations to assist each other, to protect national achievements, and to maintain social order.

#### Article 49.-

Every Khmer citizen shall have the obligation to contribute to nation-building and to defend the motherland.
The obligation to defend the motherland shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of law.
 

#### Article 53.-
The Kingdom of Cambodia shall always maintain a policy of permanent neutrality and non-alignment. The Kingdom of Cambodia shall coexist peacefully with its neighbors and all other countries throughout the world.
The Kingdom of Cambodia shall never invade any country, nor interfere in any other country's internal affairs, directly or indirectly, and shall settle any problems by peaceful means and show mutual respect for each other's interests.
The Kingdom of Cambodia shall not join in any military alliance or military agreement which is incompatible with its policy of neutrality.
The Kingdom of Cambodia shall not permit any foreign military base on its territory and shall not have its own military base abroad, except within the framework of a United Nations request.
The Kingdom of Cambodia reserves the right to receive foreign assistance in the form of military equipment, weapons, ammunition, training of its armed forces, and other assistance for self-defense and for maintaining public order and security within its territory.

(Royal Decree No. NS/RKM/0605/018, dated June 19, 2005)

#### Article 111.- New (Two)
The Senate sessions shall be held in public.
The Senate may meet in closed sessions at the request of the President or at least one-tenth of its members, the King, the Prime Minister or the President of the National Assembly.
Senate sessions shall be valid only if there is:
 

A- A quorum of more than two-thirds of all members of the Senate for any vote requiring a two-thirds majority of all members of the Senate.
B- A quorum of more than half of all members of the Senate for any vote requiring a simple majority or an absolute majority of all members of the Senate.
The number of votes required for adoptions by the National Assembly as determined in the Constitution shall also be applied to the Senate.
(Royal Decree No. NS/RKM/0306/006, dated March 09, 2006)

#### Article 114.- New
The Senate shall establish such various Commissions as it deems necessary. The organization and functioning of the Senate shall be determined by its Internal Rules. These Internal Rules shall be adopted by a two-thirds majority vote of all members of the Senate.
(Royal Decree No. NS/RKM/0399/01, dated March 08, 1999)

#### Article 114.- New (One)
The Senate shall establish such various Commissions as it deems necessary. The organization and functioning of the Senate shall be determined by its Internal Rules. These Internal Rules shall be adopted by an absolute majority vote of all members of the Senate.
(Royal Decree No. NS/RKM/0306/006, dated March 09, 2006)
 

#### Article 99.-
The Council of Ministers is the Royal Government of the Kingdom of Cambodia.
The Council of Ministers shall be led by one Prime Minister, assisted by Deputy Prime Ministers and along with Senior Ministers, Ministers and Secretaries of State as members.
(Royal Decree dated September 24, 1993)

#### Article 119.- New (One)

This dignitary appointed as Prime Minister must be a Representative who has Khmer nationality by birth only. This appointed dignitary shall present colleagues who are Representatives or members of the party represented in the National Assembly for various ministerial posts in the Royal Government, and shall seek a vote of confidence from the National Assembly. #### Article 119.- New (Two)
Upon the proposal of the party with the largest number of seats in the National Assembly, the King shall appoint a dignitary as Prime Minister to form the Royal Government. This proposal from the party with the largest number of seats in the National Assembly shall be copied to the President of the National Assembly. This dignitary appointed as Prime Minister must be a Representative who has Khmer nationality by birth only and who is elected from the party with the largest number of seats...

This appointed dignitary shall present colleagues who are Representatives or members of the party represented in the National Assembly for various ministerial posts in the Royal Government, and shall seek a vote of confidence from the National Assembly. #### Article 106.-
If the office of the Prime Minister is permanently vacant, a new Council of Ministers shall be appointed under the conditions stipulated in this Constitution. If the vacancy is temporary, an acting Prime Minister shall be appointed.

#### Article 125.- New (One)
In the event that the Prime Minister is temporarily absent, the Prime Minister shall appoint a Deputy Prime Minister to serve as acting Prime Minister to fulfill the task of leading the Council of Ministers on his behalf temporarily.
If the office of the Prime Minister is permanently vacant due to death or resignation, a new Council of Ministers shall be immediately appointed under the conditions stipulated in this Constitution. The President of the National Assembly shall immediately convene the National Assembly and take other necessary measures urgently within seven days at the latest after receiving the proposal from the party with the largest number of seats in the National Assembly.
In the event that the office of the Prime Minister is permanently vacant due to death, the outgoing Council of Ministers under the leadership of the Deputy Prime Minister appointed as acting Prime Minister shall only perform day-to-day work until a new Council of Ministers takes office.
The resignation of the Prime Minister shall be made by a letter of resignation addressed to the King, with a copy to the President of the National Assembly. In this case, the outgoing Council of Ministers shall continue leading the work of the Royal Government until a new Council of Ministers takes office.
(Royal Decree No. NS/RKM/0822/007, dated August 06, 2022)
 

#### Article 118.-
The Constitutional Council shall consist of nine members with a nine-year mandate. One-third of the members shall be replaced every three years. Three members shall be appointed by the King, three members shall be elected by the National Assembly, and three others shall be appointed by the Supreme Council of the Magistracy.
The President shall be elected by the members of the Constitutional Council. The President shall have a casting vote in the event of a tie.
(Royal Decree dated September 24, 1993)
