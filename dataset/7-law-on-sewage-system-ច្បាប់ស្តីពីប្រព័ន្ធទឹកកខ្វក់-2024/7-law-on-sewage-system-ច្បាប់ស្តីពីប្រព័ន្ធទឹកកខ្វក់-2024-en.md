## HEREBY PROMULGATE

The Law on Sewage System, which was adopted by the National Assembly on October 31, 2024, at the 3rd session of the 7th legislature, and entirely reviewed by the Senate on its form and legal aspects on November 13, 2024, at the 2nd plenary session of the 5th legislature, the full text of which is as follows:

# LAW

## ON

# SEWAGE SYSTEM

# Chapter 1
# General Provisions

**Article 1 .-**

This law defines the management, development, construction, repair, maintenance, operation, and use of sewage systems in order to ensure the efficiency, quality, safety, and sustainability of sewage systems and environmental protection, contributing to the improvement of public welfare in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

**Article 2 .-**

This law has the scope of application to sewage systems in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

**Article 3 .-**

Key terms used in this law are defined in the Glossary, which is an annex to this law.

# Chapter 2
# Competent Management Authority

**Article 4 .-**

The development of sewage systems is under the direct responsibility of the State. The State may grant rights to the private sector to develop and manage the operation of sewage systems in accordance with the laws and legal instruments in force.

The Ministry of Public Works and Transport has the competence to manage the development of sewage systems in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

Sub-national administrations have the competence to manage sewerage systems and wastewater treatment systems within their jurisdiction and other sewage system tasks as assigned or delegated by the ministry or institution in charge of sewage system development, upon approval from the Royal Government.

The division of functions and duties on the management of sewerage systems and wastewater treatment systems of each institution shall be determined by a sub-decree.

# Chapter 3
# Principles

**Article 5 .-**

This law has the following basic principles:

- Wastewater management shall comply with the required standards of the Environment and Natural Resources Code, laws, and legal instruments in force.

- Development, construction, repair, maintenance, and operation of sewage systems shall comply with legal instruments and technical regulations of sewage systems, in accordance with the geography, layout, and national economic situation.

- Investment in sewage systems shall be promoted and encouraged in all forms.

- Provision and use of sewerage systems shall be transparent, efficient, and sustainable.

- Management and use of existing sewage systems shall be consistent.

# Chapter 4
# Sewage System Development Plan

## Section 1
## Preparation of Master Plan for Sewage System Development

**Article 6 .-**

The Ministry of Public Works and Transport shall cooperate with relevant ministries, institutions, and sub-national administrations to prepare the master plan for sewage system development throughout the Kingdom of Cambodia.

The master plan for sewage system development shall comply with the geographical situation of each area based on key foundations of urban development, downtowns, rural areas, communities, residential clusters, sewage discharge zones, rainwater runoff along road networks, and population growth rate in each area with demands and requirements for construction, use, and discharge of wastewater.

The master plan for sewage system development shall be prepared consistently with the national policy on land use planning of the Kingdom of Cambodia, the national policy on clean water supply and sanitation, and other relevant national policies, and shall be monitored, reviewed, and re-evaluated at least every 10 (ten) years in accordance with urban development, downtowns, rural areas, communities, population growth, and practical construction and usage needs.

The master plan for sewage system development shall be decided by the Royal Government.

**Article 7 .-**

Priority areas necessary for preparing the master plan for sewage system development include:

1- Areas with water pollution control.

2- Areas protecting water quality and quantity in natural zones.

3- New development areas, new cities, borey construction areas, commercial zones, and new land subdivision areas.

4- Combined commercial and production centers, tourism, and service areas.

5- Special economic zones, industrial parks, innovation parks, or agro-industrial zones.

The designation of priority areas of the master plan for sewage system development shall be decided by the Royal Government.

**Article 8 .-**

The preparation of the master plan for sewage system development shall focus on the following points:

- Coverage areas of the sewage system and conditions for treatment and discharge

- Definition of policy directions and implementation strategies for wastewater treatment and management

- Preparation of detailed implementation plans

- Preparation of concrete action projects of sewage systems in various areas and expansion of the scope of reorganization of sewerage systems

- Project planning, design drawings, structure, and capacity of the sewage system

- Study of existing structural site conditions, topography, mixture flow, water source currents, and other natural conditions within the project area

- Land use and development of the sewage system

- Improvement or upgrading of the sewage system and water use

- Research, development, and human resources training related to sewage system works

- Estimation of cost and expenditure requirements for project implementation, including cost-benefit analysis and other related aspects of sewage system development.

Conditions and procedures for the preparation of the master plan for sewage system development shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Public Works and Transport.

### Section 2 Project Planning for Sewage System Development

#### Article 9 ._

Project planning for sewage system development shall be implemented according to the following stages:

1- Basic information and data.

2- Procedures of design project study workflow.

3- Source and quality of sewage source.

4- Source and construction of manholes.

5- Bedding, connection, and installation of sewers.

Detailed project planning for sewage system development shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Public Works and Transport.

#### Article 10 ._

Sub-national administrations shall plan projects for the development of sewage and wastewater treatment systems in accordance with the geographical conditions within their jurisdiction, by requesting technical advice from the Ministry of Public Works and Transport and relevant ministries and institutions, and shall comply with the master plan for sewage system development approved by the Royal Government.

If necessary, sub-national administrations may request the Ministry of Public Works and Transport to plan projects for the development of sewage and wastewater treatment systems within their jurisdiction.

#### Article 11 ._

The Ministry of Public Works and Transport shall cooperate with relevant ministries and institutions to monitor, inspect, and evaluate the planning of projects for the development of sewage and wastewater treatment systems throughout the Kingdom of Cambodia.

Conditions and procedures for monitoring, inspecting, and evaluating the planning of projects for the development of sewage and wastewater treatment systems shall be determined by Sub-decree.

# Chapter 5 Types of Wastewater Treatment Systems and Sewage Systems

#### Article 12 ._

The Ministry of Public Works and Transport shall classify the types of wastewater treatment systems and sewage systems according to their specific functions in accordance with the technical regulations for sewage systems as provided in Article 70 of this Law, and shall compile an inventory of wastewater treatment systems and sewage systems, in consultation with relevant ministries, institutions, and sub-national administrations, at least once every 5 (five) years, to classify wastewater treatment systems and sewage systems or to modify wastewater treatment systems and sewage systems.

#### Article 13 ._

Wastewater treatment systems shall be classified into wastewater treatment plants, wastewater filtration ponds, and other infrastructure supporting the sewage system.

The classification of the types and sizes of each wastewater treatment system shall be determined by joint Prakas of the Minister of Public Works and Transport and the Minister of Environment.

#### Article 14 ._

Sewage systems shall be classified into main sewer networks and branch sewer networks, according to the type and size of each sewer network.

The classification of sewage systems, types, and sizes of each sewer network shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Public Works and Transport.

# Chapter 6 Sewage System Development

## Section 1 Construction, Rehabilitation, and Expansion of Sewage Systems

#### Article 15 ._

The State promotes and encourages in all forms the participation in the development, construction, rehabilitation, and expansion of sewage systems throughout the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 16 ._

The authority in charge of sewage system management shall promote the construction, rehabilitation, and expansion of sewage and wastewater treatment systems in the capital, and various urban areas of provinces, municipalities, districts, and khans, to a sufficient extent to create demand for use and provide sewage system services appropriate to the geographical conditions of each area, in compliance with the master plan for sewage system development.

Every authority in charge of sewage system management or private sector that has obtained the rights to develop, construct, rehabilitate, or expand sewage systems shall implement them in accordance with the construction design project and contract, and shall comply with the technical regulations for sewage systems as provided in Article 70 of this Law.

All development, construction, rehabilitation, and expansion of sewage systems as provided in paragraph 1 above shall be classified by type and size as provided in Article 13 and Article 14 of this Law.

#### Article 17 ._

The person who obtains a construction project shall commence implementation after public notification or receipt of a notification letter from the authority in charge of sewage system management. Before and during the construction process, the person implementing the construction project shall comply with occupational safety regulations and traffic safety measures, including installing warning signs, temporary barriers, traffic equipment, and shall comply with measures that guarantee traffic safety.

The person implementing the construction project shall conduct inspections in accordance with each point specified in the contract when the project is completed. In case the project is interrupted, the person implementing the construction project shall prepare a detailed report on the changes of the project to the competent institution.

#### Article 18 ._

The authority in charge of sewage system management shall preserve documents related to the sewage system project and record the information in their digital information technology system and archive after the construction project, rehabilitation project, or expansion project has been completed.

The information and documents related as provided in paragraph 1 above that must be recorded and preserved are as follows:

- Layout plan of the sewage system area

- Location of land area for burial of the sewer distribution network

- Horizontal position or vertical position of any part of the network, including materials, equipment, and components used for the sewer network

- Area for wastewater treatment system and sewer network equipment associated with water levels or elevations, and maps showing the infrastructure of the sewage system

- Location and layout plan for sewage inflow

- Analysis data of pollutant levels and quantity of sewage inflow

- Starting date of construction and final completion date of construction

- Other issues recorded during operation, maintenance, and project implementation.

## Section 2 Land Use

#### Article 19 ._

The authority in charge of sewage system management may use land to serve the construction of sewage system engineering structures included in the development plan of the sub-national administration, and right-of-way land along roads, railways, navigation routes, airways, waterways, maritime routes, and/or private land, after the construction project has obtained written approval.

The authority in charge of sewage system management shall request land from relevant ministries, competent institutions, or landowners to arrange and preserve those lands for the construction of sewage system engineering structures in compliance with the provisions of this Law and legal regulations in force.

#### Article 21 ._

The authority in charge of sewage system management shall specify clear reasons for the use of land, as provided in Article 20 of this Law, in the construction of sewage system engineering structures.

The reasons as provided in paragraph 1 above are as follows:

1- Serve the public interest and national interest.

2- Promote public health and the environment.

3- Enhance the quality of service in the capital, provinces, municipalities, districts, khans, and communities.

4- Contribute to attracting tourists to increase national and community economic benefits.

5- Forecast the start of operations, levels, methods, and duration of construction.

6- Provide other necessary reasons.

#### Article 22 ._

The authority in charge of sewage system management shall provide information and written notification to property owners and/or right holders and sewage system users in the area about the necessary requirements for land use and reasons as provided in Article 20 and Article 21 of this Law, in order to cooperate in resolving impacts related to the project.

Property owners and/or right holders who are affected by the above development project shall receive prior, fair, and just compensation, and shall comply with the Law on Expropriation.

#### Article 23 ._

Property owners and/or right holders of all locations shall not change or modify sewage networks and wastewater engineering structures that are in daily use within their locations or public locations without permission from the authority in charge of sewage system management.

## Section 3 Construction of Sewage Systems in New Development Areas

#### Article 24 ._

The creation of new development areas, such as cities, satellite cities, ports, airports, railway stations, special economic zones, industrial parks, residential estates, commercial areas, agro-industrial areas, and other areas, shall construct adequate sewage systems in accordance with technical conditions and technical regulations for sewage systems as provided in Article 70 of this Law, in order to meet the requirements of use and provide sewage system services in those new development areas before connecting to the public sewage system.

Technical conditions for the construction of sewage systems in new development areas shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 25 ._

New development areas shall construct branch sewer networks to prevent rainwater from flowing into wastewater filtration ponds and to facilitate filtration, unless decided otherwise by the Royal Government.

## Section 4 Construction of Sewage Facilities of Building Sites

#### Article 26 ._

All construction of buildings including business and production or other similar sites shall construct adequate and appropriate wastewater treatment tanks, sewage networks, and other wastewater engineering structures in accordance with the technical regulations for sewage systems as provided in Article 70 of this Law, before connecting to the sewer network.

Individuals who are owners of building sites including business and production or other similar sites shall prepare a detailed design project for the construction of wastewater treatment tanks, sewage networks, and other wastewater engineering structures, to be included in the design project used for construction works to request permission from relevant competent ministries, institutions, or authorities in compliance with the Law on Construction.

#### Article 27 ._

The construction of wastewater treatment tanks and/or sewage networks located near buildings shall be prohibited in the following cases:

1- May cause problems to the building foundation or other nearby building foundations.

2- May disturb clean water systems or underground electricity, and/or other underground networks.

3- Located near protection, safety, security, and public health systems, which may easily cause accidental danger.

The construction of wastewater treatment tanks and/or sewage networks above shall fulfill the following conditions:

- With approval from the engineer in charge

- Design and ensure that building foundations are not disturbed or affected by the construction of wastewater treatment tanks and/or sewage networks

- Design and ensure that clean water or underground electricity services are not affected

- Design and ensure that safety, security, and public health are protected in compliance with the laws in force.

All exemptions and construction near buildings or near clean water systems or underground electricity, and/or other underground networks to change, relocate, replace, or repair sewage networks shall have a detailed written technical report submitted to the authority in charge of sewage system management.

#### Article 28 ._

The construction of wastewater treatment tanks and/or sewage networks located under buildings may be permitted in the following cases:

1- The building is required to construct a wastewater treatment tank and/or sewage network.

2- The location of the wastewater treatment tank and sewage network has no option for construction other than under the building.

The construction of wastewater treatment tanks and/or sewage networks located under buildings shall fulfill the following conditions:

- With approval from the engineer in charge

- Sewage networks shall be placed under the building in accordance with technical features

- Sewage networks shall have a technical safety distance between each building foundation and the highest part of the sewage network

- Shall not increase or add weight load on the sewage network

- Sewer network testing and proper inspection shall be conducted before construction.

In case the construction of wastewater treatment tanks and/or sewage networks is located under an already constructed building, the person implementing the construction project shall be responsible for the technical level during the construction of the wastewater treatment tanks and/or sewage networks.

#### Article 29 ._

The construction of wastewater treatment tanks and/or sewage networks on sloped locations, upper floors, and/or building balconies shall fulfill the following conditions:

- With approval from the engineer in charge

- The building is required to construct a wastewater treatment tank and/or sewage network

- The building has no space or no option of other spaces for the construction of a wastewater treatment tank and/or sewage network.

To protect the infrastructure of wastewater treatment tanks to be strong, the person implementing the construction project shall inspect the feasibility of construction options in accordance with a number of conditions as follows:

- Avoid steep sloped lands

- Sewage networks shall be constructed descending down in accordance with the flat surface conditions of the slope

- Ensure that the load from above or inside the sewage network does not block or reduce water flow

- Use materials, equipment, and construction methods in compliance with the requirements of technical regulations for sewage systems as provided in Article 70 of this Law and legal regulations in force.

#### Article 30 ._

Detailed technical features of the construction of wastewater treatment tanks and/or sewage networks under buildings, upper floors, or on building roofs, outside of buildings, near buildings, and building blocks, shall be determined by joint Prakas of the Minister of Public Works and Transport and the Minister of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction.

## Section 5 Modification of Sewage Facilities

#### Article 31 ._

The modification of wastewater treatment tanks or sewage networks shall require prior application for permission from the authority in charge of sewage system management and shall clearly specify the location of the wastewater treatment tanks or sewage networks.

The modification of sewage networks and manhole covers shall fulfill the following conditions:

- Sewage networks and manhole covers shall be appropriate to the weight of transport vehicles or traffic crossing regularly

- Sewage networks and manhole covers shall be resistant to reactions from various chemical and biological substances arising from atmospheric conditions or other substances.

#### Article 32 ._

The authority in charge of sewage system management shall permit the modification of wastewater treatment tanks or sewage networks in case it is found that the proposed modification design project has fulfilled the following conditions:

- Correct calculation of dimensions and sizes of wastewater treatment tanks or sewage networks

- Correct calculation of types and qualities of wastewater treatment tanks or sewage networks

- Correct calculation of quantity, water discharge, and water flow

- Correct calculation of geographical conditions of the slope

- Compliance with the type of sewage network.

Technical conditions of calculation as provided in paragraph 1 above shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Public Works and Transport.

# Chapter 7 Use of Sewage Systems

## Section 1 Network Connection

#### Article 33 ._

Individuals who are owners of locations situated in areas with existing and newly constructed sewage systems, and in areas where sewage systems have just been constructed or arranged, shall apply for connection of sewage pipes flowing from their locations with the sewage network in the area.

Applying for connection of sewage pipes flowing from one's location with the sewage network shall be exempted in case the building location is far from the sewage network and cannot be connected, and shall fulfill the required technical conditions as provided in this Law and legal regulations in force.

Procedures and formats of the application for connection with the sewage network shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of Public Works and Transport.

#### Article 34 ._

Disturbing or causing disturbance to the process of any sewage network connection without permission from the authority in charge of sewage system management shall be prohibited.

#### Article 35 ._

The authority in charge of sewage system management or the operator shall construct the sewage network and establish connection points along the sewage network.

The person who is the owner of the location shall be responsible for arranging sewage pipes or rainwater pipes up to the connection point along the sewage network and shall comply with technical conditions determined by the authority in charge of sewage system management.

#### Article 36 ._

The authority in charge of sewage system management or the operator may expand the connection with the sewage network to be more comprehensive and shall ensure that:

1- Every starting point of the sewage network in any area can be connected directly or can be distributed according to the sewage system in the wastewater reservoir area.

2- Sewage networks are constructed in accordance with the requirements of the starting point of the sewage network in the wastewater reservoir area.

The authority in charge of sewage system management or the operator shall properly monitor and inspect the starting points of connections of each sewage network.

#### Article 37 ._

All connections with the sewage network at intersections, bends, ascending or descending slopes, and grade-varying points shall fulfill the following conditions:

- Use connection sewer pipes that are quality-compliant with the technical regulations for sewage systems as provided in Article 70 of this Law and have appropriate dimensions with the sewage network level or the joint sewage network

- Place sewer pipes with smaller diameters higher than larger sewer pipes, in case the connection sewer pipes have different diameters

- Inspect technical features of connections according to each format and properly use materials, equipment, and components for connecting each sewage network

- Arrange separate sewage networks or options by any appropriate means in case of connecting sewage networks from intersections and bends connected to the sewage network, descending or ascending slopes, or sewage networks varying according to different grade levels.

#### Article 38 ._

The arrangement of sewage pipe connections of buildings from two or more buildings or a group of buildings with the sewage network shall fulfill the following conditions:

- Must have a wastewater treatment tank with a connection point for each building or group of buildings in accordance with technical specifications.

- Sewage treatment tanks and networks can support the discharge of sewage from all buildings.

- There must be sufficient evidence and correct technical conditions according to the topography of each building site that can be connected, or there is no other option for installing the connection.

For connections of junctions, bends, vertical drops, slopes, and level changes, they must be implemented in accordance with the conditions stipulated in Article 37 of this Law.

#### Article 39 .-

The connection of sewage networks from special economic zones, industrial parks, or enterprise parks to the sewer network must have permission from the authority managing the sewage system and a written agreement between the authority managing the sewage system and the site owner, which determines the connection location, technical requirements, time frame, level of pollutants, and wastewater samples flowing into the connection point, and must comply with the provisions of this Law and legal documents in force.

#### Article 40 .-

Technical conditions of connection to the sewer network shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

#### Article 41 .-

The person constructing or installing the connection must use sewer materials, equipment, and assemblies of proper quality as technically specified, and must connect or install sewer materials, equipment, and assemblies in accordance with technical regulations of the sewage system, as well as provide relevant documents proving the evaluation of materials, equipment, and assemblies, water flow rate, and pollutant levels of discharged water to the authority managing the sewage system.

#### Article 42 .-

The person constructing or installing the connection cannot connect or install sewer materials, equipment, and assemblies to the sewer network before inspection by competent agents or responsible institutions. Competent agents or responsible institutions have the right to make corrections in case they find that the use of materials, equipment, and assemblies to connect to the sewer network is not correct according to the technical regulations of the sewage system.

#### Article 43 .-

All connections to the sewer network must use materials, equipment, and assemblies in accordance with technical regulations of the sewage system.

Sewer materials, equipment, and assemblies connected to the sewer network shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

Excavation or tunneling and road drilling across for connection to the sewer network on the road reserve must request permission from the road management authority and must fulfill the following conditions:

- Avoid affecting the infrastructure of the sewer system or other physical infrastructure.

- Resolve immediately any disruptions before continuing work.

- Connect to the sewer network in accordance with the technical regulations of the sewage system and test the discharge of sewage after the sewer connection is completed.

- Facilitate traffic order, prevent various accidents that may occur unexpectedly, and comply with instructions of competent agents or responsible institutions.

- Restore the site to its original condition or better than its original condition.

#### Article 45 .-

All connections to the sewer network must pay public service fees.

Public service fees for connection to the sewer network shall be determined by inter-ministerial Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance, the Minister of the Ministry of Interior, and the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

### Section 2
### Use of Sewer System

#### Article 46 .-

The authority managing the sewage system or the operator must disseminate to the public information about sewage system zones or sewer networks, the start date of operations, connection methods to the sewer network in accordance with the provisions of this Law and legal documents in force, before starting the operation of the sewage collection system, sewage treatment plant, and/or sewer system.

The above dissemination shall be exempted for any location or geographical area where there are existing and operating sewage treatment systems, sewage treatment plants, and/or sewer systems.

#### Article 47 .-

All discharges of sewage or wastewater after primary treatment from any site to the sewage treatment plant must be discharged through the sewer system.

For areas or sites that do not yet have a system, the owner of the site must construct or install a septic tank as stipulated in Section 4 of this Chapter.

All discharges of sewage or wastewater after primary treatment through the sewer system must pay sewer use service fees in accordance with the provisions determined in Chapter 11 of this Law.

#### Article 48 .-

Obligations of the site owner discharging sewage to the connection point as stipulated in the second paragraph of Article 38 of this Law shall be implemented according to the following measures:

1- Discharge of sewage from residential buildings:

a- In areas where a new sewer system has just been constructed, which has a combined underground sewer system and sewage treatment plant, sewage must be discharged directly to the connection point.

b- In cities, towns, and other areas where there is a separate sewer system, sewage and rainwater must be discharged into the connection points of their respective separate sewer networks.

2- Discharge of sewage from other sources other than residential buildings:

a- Each owner of a site that has a collective treatment facility or its own sewage treatment system for primary treatment must discharge sewage in accordance with the standards set for wastewater discharge to the connection point.

b- Each site owner must apply to the environmental agency to analyze the pollutant levels discharged to the connection point, and must submit the test results to the authority managing the sewage system.

The competent agency or responsible institution shall be responsible for inspecting and monitoring the discharge of sewage to ensure compliance with technical regulations of the sewage system and legal documents in force.

#### Article 49 .-

Any person who is the owner of a site intending to change the alignment of the sewer axis, divert the sewer network, or modify sewage structures within their site for sewage discharge, must implement the following:

1- Apply for written permission from the authority managing the sewage system, specifying reasonable grounds and ensuring implementation in accordance with technical regulations of the sewage system.

2- Avoid causing damage, affecting, or destroying sewage structures or other physical infrastructure in that site.

3- Reorganize and improve the site to be as good as its original condition or better than its original condition.

All expenses for changing the alignment, drainage, and relocation shall be borne by the person who is the owner of the site.

#### Article 50 .-

The authority managing the sewage system or the operator must arrange for a sewer use contract with the person who is the owner of the site who discharges sewage through the sewer system.

The format and procedure of the sewer use contract shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

#### Article 51 .-

The authority managing the sewage system or the operator has the right to temporarily suspend, close, or terminate the sewer use service after giving written notice 2 (two) times to the person who is the owner of the site in the event that such person discharges sewage incorrectly causing clogging or breakage of sewer pipes or sewer network and refuses to repair or improve their sewer pipes or sewer network.

In addition to the case stipulated in the first paragraph above, the authority managing the sewage system or the operator may also request the clean water authority or clean water supplier to temporarily suspend the supply of clean water

temporarily to repair and improve the sewer pipes or sewer network, and must take temporary sewage discharge measures to minimize the impact on production activities and daily activities of the site owner and to reduce environmental pollution.

#### Article 52 .-

Any person who uses the sewer system must participate in maintaining the sewer pipes or sewer network and sewer equipment by themselves, and must not allow any person to discharge sewage into their sewer pipes or sewer network.

Particularly, the person who uses the sewer system whose site is located in an area with a sewage treatment pond in each town must implement the following:

1- Do not allow construction waste, soil, sand, or waste to flow into the sewer network.

2- Sewage discharged from all parts of the site must be sent into the sewer network or sewer system constructed in each town.

#### Article 53 .-

The authority managing the sewage system must make immediate corrections if they find that the quality of water after treatment discharged from the sewage treatment system or sewage treatment plant to public water areas does not comply with the water quality standards of public water areas as stipulated in the regulations of the Environment and Natural Resources Code and legal documents in force.

### Section 3
### Protection of Sewer System

#### Article 54 .-

Obstructing the sewer network without permission from the authority managing the sewage system shall be prohibited.

Obstructing the sewer network may occur if permission is obtained from the authority managing the sewage system for any of the following reasons:

1- Any part of the sewer network is clogged and needs to be pumped and cleaned.

2- Any part of the sewer network is broken or collapsed so that traffic or passage cannot proceed and needs to be repaired.

3- Any part of the sewer network needs to be modified or replaced.

#### Article 55 .-

Disconnecting any part of the sewer network shall be prohibited.

Disconnecting any part of the sewer network shall be exempted in the following cases:

1- Taking emergency rescue measures from floods.

2- Construction and expansion of road or railway infrastructure or other public service infrastructure.

3- Construction of infrastructure serving national defense, security, and public order.

4- Other relevant activities according to the regulations of the Royal Government.

Disconnecting any part of the sewer network as stipulated in point 2, point 3, and point 4 of the second paragraph above must take measures to construct an alternative sewer network to facilitate the discharge of sewage.

#### Article 56 .-

All activities shall be prohibited as follows:

1- Intentionally destroying or damaging the equipment of the sewage system or sewer network by causing disruption such as creating flow holes or reducing the flow of sewage into the sewage system or sewer network.

2- Violating technical regulations in the construction, repair, maintenance, and protection of the sewage system and/or sewer network infrastructure.

3- Excavating, digging, or drilling through, modifying, or changing sewage structures or sewage system infrastructure.

4- Constructing anything on or pressing from above the sewer system where those structures may cause partial obstruction of sewage flow or complete obstruction of the sewage system or sewer network.

5- Placing materials or debris into manholes through the sewage system or sewer network.

6- Modifying the ground surface above the sewage system which causes disruption to the process of water discharge into the distributor pipe assembly or manholes.

7- Unintentionally causing damage to the sewage system infrastructure, such as causing breakage or collapse of the sewer system or sewer network.

8- Connecting or modifying the sewage system and using materials, equipment, and assemblies without the consent and permission of competent agents, responsible institutions, or technicians.

9- Disrupting or obstructing the inspection work of the sewage system and/or sewer network infrastructure by competent agents, responsible institutions, or technicians.

10- Constructing sewage storage tanks without correct technical conditions and causing impacts on public health or the environment.

11- Discharging wastewater or sewage containing substances that do not properly meet the discharge standards of waste or sewage into the sewage system or sewer network.

### Section 4
### Construction and Repair of Septic Tanks

#### Article 57 .-

Any person who is the owner of a site must construct or install a septic tank to store and discard sewage in any of the following cases:

1- Areas or sites without a sewer network or without space to arrange a sewer network.

2- Areas or sites with dilapidated sewer networks that have an excessive amount of water.

3- Areas or sites with highly permeable soil conditions where the efficiency of treatment cannot cope with the infiltration of water into the ground.

4- Areas or sites that are impermeable or rock layers that prevent water infiltration.

5- Areas or sites with thin soil layers unsuitable for discharging sewage.

6- Other environmental areas or sites where it is absolutely prohibited to construct sewage system infrastructure.

#### Article 58 .-

The authority managing the sewage system must not allow the site owner to construct or install a septic tank in areas or sites where there is sufficient sewage system infrastructure, in the event that the inspection finds that the discharge of sewage in that area or site does not affect the network, cause clogging, or disrupt the water flow through the sewer network.

#### Article 59 .-

All construction, installation, repair, and modification of septic tanks must ensure structured quality and sewage filtration functions that are easy to pump, clean, and maintain.

Construction, installation, repair, and modification of septic tanks must comply with technical regulations of the sewage system as stipulated in Article 40 of this Law and legal documents in force.

Requirements for construction, installation, repair, and modification of septic tanks shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

### Section 5
### Management and Recycling of Sludge

#### Article 60 .-

The authority managing the sewage system must operate the pumping and cleaning of sludge, especially in sewage treatment systems, sewage treatment plants, pumping stations, sewer systems, and other sewage structures at least once (1) a year to protect the sewage system infrastructure in good condition and in accordance with the technical lifespan.

All pumping and cleaning of sludge as stipulated in the first paragraph above must avoid clogging the daily discharge flow of water used in the area where sewage must be discharged through the sewer system, and avoid causing impacts on human and animal health and damage to the environment in that area.

Technical characteristics of pumping and cleaning sludge shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

#### Article 61 .-

The collection, transportation, treatment, recycling/use, and preservation or storage of sludge must fulfill the following conditions:

1- Preserve and treat or recycle sludge at sites approved by the competent authority or competent ministries and institutions, and absolutely prohibit throwing away untreated sludge into public water areas, public areas, or private properties.

2- Treat sludge according to technical methods recognized by the authority managing the sewage system and relevant ministries and institutions.

3- Recycle and use sludge in accordance with the provisions of this Law and legal documents in force.

4- Arrange for structures, number of storage tanks, storage capacity, milling machines, odor-retention tanks, and odor-elimination tanks in accordance with the provisions of this Law and legal documents in force.

Technical conditions of collection, transportation, treatment, recycling/use, and preservation or storage of sludge shall be determined by Sub-decree.

## Chapter 8
## Maintenance of Sewage System

#### Article 62 .-

Maintenance of the sewage system must be carried out regularly and divided into periodic maintenance, daily maintenance, and emergency repair.

#### Article 63 .-

The authority managing the sewage system must maintain the sewage system properly to ensure the sewage system operates well, avoiding risks to public health and impacts on water quality of public water areas.

All maintenance of the sewage system must comply with technical regulations of the sewage system as stipulated in Article 40 of this Law, and must regularly inspect the infrastructure components of the sewage system.

Form and procedures of the maintenance of the sewage system shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

#### Article 64 .-

The authority managing the sewage system must conduct technical inspection of the sewage system at least once every 3 (three) years to inspect and evaluate the quality and function of the sewage system under its responsibility.

The authority managing the sewage system must prepare plan designs and repair, maintenance, or improvement plans for the damaged sewage system after receiving the technical inspection results as stipulated in the first paragraph above.

Conditions of inspection and evaluation of each quality and function of the sewage system shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 65 .-

All sewage system maintenance projects approved in principle by the Royal Government may be implemented by the authority managing the sewage system itself or allow the private sector to participate in implementation in accordance with the Law on Public Procurement and must comply with the provisions of this Law and legal documents in force.

#### Article 66 .-

The authority managing the sewage system may delegate the task of inspecting and evaluating the quality and function of each sewage system to engineers registered and recognized by the Board of Engineers of Cambodia.

In the event that the infrastructure of the sewage system is under the responsibility of any ministry or institution, that ministry or institution may delegate the task of inspecting and evaluating the quality and function of each sewage system to engineers registered and recognized by the Board of Engineers of Cambodia.

#### Article 67 .-

The authority managing the sewage system or the operator may delegate the task of maintaining the network, replacing or modifying sewer materials, equipment, and assemblies to technicians who have undergone technical training and are officially recognized by the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

Form and procedures for training network maintenance, replacement, or modification technicians for sewer materials, equipment, and assemblies shall be determined by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

#### Article 68 .-

The authority managing the sewage system must preserve documents and record in IT systems or digital systems data about sewage structures, sewer materials, equipment, and assemblies replaced, maintained, quantity, and quality of sewage transmitted into sewers or discharged after treatment from the sewage treatment system, sewage treatment plant, or sewage treatment pond.

The authority managing the sewage system must prepare network layout designs of the sewer system or sewer network on maps for each area, which must be publicly posted in their office.

Information to be publicly posted includes the following:

1- Clear definition of sewer system or sewer network lines in each location.

2- Geographical location and surface area of the sewage treatment system or sewage treatment plant or sewage treatment pond, manholes, and connection points with the sewer system or sewer network, and discharge locations of treated sewage.

3- Schedule of maintenance and repairs, including the use of materials, equipment, and assemblies, and sewage discharge.

4- Other information that is necessary for management, protection, and maintenance of the sewage system infrastructure in that area.

### Article 69 .-

The authorities managing the sewage system shall be classified as authorities responsible for monitoring and inspecting each area, as stated in points 1 and 2 of the third paragraph of Article 68 of this Law, in order to disseminate and educate citizens to participate in the maintenance of the sewage system infrastructure and to take measures to prevent prohibited activities as stated in Article 56 of this Law.

## Chapter 9
## Technical Regulations for Sewage System

### Article 70 .-

The Ministry of Public Works and Transport shall prepare and establish technical regulations for sewage systems in accordance with new technologies and other technical requirements corresponding to international technical regulations in order to serve sewage system works and operations.

The technical regulations for sewage systems include, among others:

- Technical regulations on architectural drawings for sewage treatment

- Technical regulations on sewer system drawings

- Technical regulations on construction of sewage collection pipes

- Technical regulations on construction of earth sewers

- Technical regulations on manholes

- Technical regulations on sewer connections

- Technical regulations on construction of toilets and septic tanks

- Technical regulations on manhole covers and grates

- Other technical regulations related to the sewage system.

Each technical regulation for sewage systems shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

## Chapter 10
## Sewage System Business Operations
### Section 1
### Running Sewage System Businesses

### Article 71 .-

Any physical or legal person running a sewage system business shall obtain a permit or license for running a sewage system business, issued by the Ministry of Public Works and Transport and sub-national administrations.

The conditions and procedures for granting a permit or license for running a sewage system business shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

### Article 72 .-

Applications for running a sewage system business shall be subject to public service fees.

These public service fees shall be determined by a joint Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance, the Minister of the Ministry of Interior, and the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

### Article 73 .-

All sewage system business operations must have trained technicians, sufficient equipment according to their functions, safe operating measures, and sufficient technical mechanisms.

Particularly, the modification of vehicles used for pumping and transport operations shall comply with the regulations on vehicle technical inspection determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

### Article 74 .-

Guidelines on the necessary conditions and safety measures for operating pumping and transport businesses shall be determined by a joint Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport and the Minister of the Ministry of Environment.

### Article 75 .-

Persons running a pumping and transport business shall have the following obligations towards their workers and employees:

១- Provide training on safety measures in pumping, transporting, and discharging sewage to locations or sites determined by the competent authorities.

២- Provide safety equipment directly to the personnel operating the pumping and transport.

៣- Provide instructions on standards for using pumping and transport equipment and safety protection equipment.

៤- Provide regular instructions on how to inspect and maintain pumping and transport equipment.

Guidelines on the use of safety equipment shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

### Article 76 .-

In discharging or draining sewage and/or sludge obtained from pumping and transport, persons running a sewage system business shall implement the following rules:

១- Must discharge or drain sewage and/or sludge at locations permitted by ministries, institutions, or competent local authorities, and must comply with the conditions required by the ministries, institutions, and competent authorities at those locations.

២- For the discharge of sewage from sewage transport vehicles into the sewer system, prior permission from the sewage system management authority must be obtained.

### Article 77 .-

Persons running a business of pumping, transporting sewage and/or sludge from pumped sewage, or pumping and cleaning sludge, shall meet the conditions set forth in point 1 of the first paragraph of Article 61 of this Law, and shall implement the rules set forth in point 1 of Article 76 of this Law.

### Section 2
### Rights and Obligations of Operators and Users

### Article 78 .-

Operators who receive the rights to manage, maintain, and operate sewage systems shall have the following rights:

- Manage and operate the sewage system in accordance with the investment agreement

- Propose adjustments to legal instruments related to sewage system operations to the sewage system management authority for review and decision

- Participate in discussions and provide opinions on development plans for the sewage treatment system and/or sewer system within the administrative jurisdiction they have invested in

- Collect sewer system user fees in accordance with the provisions of this Law

- Receive compensation for any damage caused by stakeholders who have acted contrary to the investment agreement or contrary to the provisions of this Law

- Other rights specified in the investment agreement and in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

Operators who receive the rights to manage, maintain, and operate sewage systems shall have the following obligations:

- Manage assets in accordance with the investment agreement

- Implement the procedures and rules set forth in this Law

- Resolve incidents occurring during the investment period until the termination of the agreement

- Prepare proposals for sewer system user fees and submit them to the sewage system management authority for review and decision

- Create database of households and locations connected to sewer pipes and discharging sewage into the sewer system under their management

- Cooperate with water utility authorities or clean water suppliers to collect sewer system user fees

- Provide information on connection contracts with the sewer system upon the request of the sewage system management authority

- Protect the infrastructure of the sewage system properly and safely, in accordance with the provisions of this Law and the investment agreement.

- Compensate users for any damage in accordance with the provisions of this Law and the legal instruments in force

- Report on work activities in a timely manner as specified in the investment agreement to the sewage system management authority

- Respect and fulfill other obligations in accordance with the provisions of this Law and the legal instruments in force.

### Article 80 .-

Sewer system users shall have the following rights:

- Receive sewer system services in accordance with the contract

- Request the sewage system management authority or the operator to resolve the connection of sewer pipes to the sewer network as quickly as possible

- Receive regular information related to the use of and user fees for the sewer system from the sewage system management authority or the operator

- Complain and claim compensation for damage from the sewage system management authority or the operator who violates the contract or acts contrary to the provisions of this Law and the legal instruments in force

- Other rights specified in the contract and in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

Sewer system users shall have the following obligations:

- Pay sewer system user fees regularly and on time

- Discharge sewage into the sewer system in compliance with the contract, the provisions of this Law, and the legal instruments in force

- Provide immediate information to the sewage system management authority or the operator about incidents or phenomena that could cause damage to the sewer system

- Connect sewer pipes from their households and/or locations to the sewer network as specified in the contract and in accordance with the provisions of this Law

- Refund any damage arising from use that is contrary to the contract, the provisions of this Law, and the legal instruments in force

- Fulfill other obligations specified in the contract and in accordance with the provisions of this Law.

## Chapter 11
## Sewer System User Fees

### Article 81 .-

Sewer system user fees shall be determined based on the following principles:

១- Appropriate to the stages of social development, national socio-economy, and investment levels, improving service quality to strengthen the management, operation, and maintenance capacity of sewage systems

២- Promote economic efficiency and profitability to increase investment and ensure the effectiveness of implementing legal provisions.

- Promote public interests and establish an equitable operational framework for poor customers and vulnerable groups

- Ensure sufficient business income to guarantee efficient, equitable, simple, and low-cost service delivery

- The state shall subsidize the costs of management, operation, and maintenance in the event that the sewer system user fee income is lower than the actual costs, in accordance with the laws in force.

### Article 82 .-

The determination of sewer system user fee rates may be adjusted in the following cases:

១- When there are changes in investment in new technical methods and technologies for sewage treatment and service quality

២- Changes in state mechanisms and policies

៣- Socio-economic development conditions of the country, urban areas, and improvement in citizens' income

៤- Fluctuations in raw material prices in global, regional, and domestic markets

៥- Global or domestic financial and economic crises, or other force majeure cases that seriously affect the income of citizens.

All proposals to adjust the above sewer system user fee rates shall be submitted to the Royal Government for approval.

### Article 83 .-

The calculation of sewer system user fee rates shall be implemented as follows:

១- For sewage discharge from households, it shall be calculated proportionally to the volume of clean water used

២- For sewage discharge from other sources besides households, it shall be calculated proportionally to the volume of clean water used and the level of pollutants in the sewage discharged into the sewer system

៣- Other methods determined by a decision of the Royal Government.

Sewer system user fee rates shall be determined by a joint Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance, the Minister of the Ministry of Interior, and the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

### Article 84 .-

The sewage system management authority shall cooperate with the ministry in charge of clean water sector to coordinate sewer system user services.

Sewer system user fees collected shall be used to pay for management contracts, operations, and to strengthen investment capacity in development, construction, repair, maintenance of sewage systems, installation of equipment, and new technologies.

The rules and procedures for collecting sewer system user fees shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

## Chapter 12
## Budget for Construction and Maintenance of Sewage System

### Article 85 .-

The budget for construction and maintenance of sewage systems shall be sourced from:

- National budget

- Development assistance from various development partners

- Financing from the private sector

- Charitable donations from philanthropists, national organizations, international organizations, associations, or communities

- Profits from investment and legal business operations in sewage systems

- Other legal sources of income.

### Article 86 .-

The Ministry of Economy and Finance shall examine the possibility of providing sufficient budget packages to the sewage system management authority to support the development, construction, repair, maintenance, and management of sewage systems throughout the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Article 87 .-

The management and allocation of budget for the development, construction, repair, and maintenance of sewage systems shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

## Chapter 13
## Sewage System Inspection

### Article 88 .-

The Ministry of Public Works and Transport shall appoint sewage system inspection officers to monitor, inspect, and evaluate construction, installation, modification, repair, maintenance, connection, and operation of sewage systems, and other activities related to sewage system infrastructure, in accordance with the provisions of this Law. The rights and duties of sewage system inspection officers shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

Sewage system inspection officers shall obtain the status of judicial police officers to inspect offenses as specified in this Law, in accordance with the provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure.

In conducting operations to inspect offenses, sewage system inspection officers who have obtained the status of judicial police officers shall perform their duties in accordance with the Code of Criminal Procedure.

The rules and procedures for granting the status of judicial police officers to sewage system inspection officers shall be determined by a joint Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Justice and the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

### Article 89 .-

Sewage system inspection officers who have obtained the status of judicial police officers shall have the following rights and duties:

- Enter construction sites to monitor, inspect, and perform duties within their competence, in accordance with the provisions of this Law

- Take measures to temporarily suspend or halt the improper discharge of sewage that does not meet sewage discharge standards and sewer connections without a permit or not complying with the technical regulations of sewage systems

- Cooperate with other competent officers of ministries, institutions, and competent authorities to prevent the unauthorized discharge or drainage of sewage or discharge that does not comply with the sewage discharge standard limits

- Implement transitional fines in accordance with the provisions of this Law

- Take other measures within the framework of implementing the provisions of this Law.

The formats and procedures for conducting sewage system inspections shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

### Article 90 .-

Sewage system inspection officers may enter construction, installation, modification, repair, maintenance, connection, and operation sites of sewage systems, and other activities related to sewage system infrastructure, to perform their duties without prior notice or written notice of a scheduled date.

Sewage system inspection officers have the right to request cooperative assistance from relevant competent ministries, institutions, and local authorities to participate in preventing and suppressing offenses as stated in the provisions of this Law.

### Article 91 .-

During law enforcement operations, sewage system inspection officers must wear uniforms, badges, and rank insignia, and carry a mission permit.

The uniforms, badges, and rank insignia of sewage system inspection officers shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

## Chapter 14
## Penal Provisions

Sanctions in this Law include administrative sanctions and criminal sanctions.

Administrative sanctions include:

- Written warning

- Temporary suspension

- Requirement for improvement

- Requirement to replace materials, equipment, or sewer components

- Requirement to set up a shared collective collection tank or own sewage treatment system

- Requirement for restitution

- Suspension, revocation, or cancellation of permits or licenses granted in accordance with this Law.

Other actions subject to administrative sanctions shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

The decision to impose administrative sanctions is within the competence of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

Any person dissatisfied with the decision on administrative sanctions, except for the written warning as stated in the first paragraph above, has the right to appeal in writing to the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport within 30 (thirty) days from the date of receiving the decision. The Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport shall decide on this appeal within 30 (thirty) days from the date of receiving the appeal. Persons dissatisfied with the decision of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport on the appeal have the right to file a lawsuit in the competent court in accordance with the procedures within 30 (thirty) days from the date of receiving the decision. The filing of the lawsuit by the person above does not have the effect of suspending the execution of the decision on administrative sanctions.

### Article 92 .-

Criminal sanctions include:

- Transitional fines

- Fines

- Imprisonment.

Offenses subject to transitional fines in accordance with this Law shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

The decision to impose transitional fines is within the competence of sewage system inspection officers who have obtained the status of judicial police officers.

Payment of transitional fines shall extinguish the criminal lawsuit. The payment of transitional fines is not an obstacle to the enforcement of administrative sanctions and criminal sanctions, in accordance with the provisions of this Law and other criminal laws in force.

In case the offender refuses to pay the transitional fine, sewage system inspection officers who have obtained the status of judicial police officers shall compile the case file of the offense and send it to the court in accordance with procedures.

The rules and procedures for transitional inspection, payment of transitional fines, management of receipts

The payment of transitional fines and the management of revenue received from transitional fines for offenses as stipulated in the provisions of this Law shall be determined by a joint declaration of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport and the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

The Royal Government may provide incentives to officers who have participated in cracking down on offenses as stipulated in this Law.

#### Article 96 ._

Sewage system inspection officers or competent officers who fail to implement the provisions of this Law and other relevant regulations in force in the performance of their duties shall be held administratively responsible in accordance with the law and other legal instruments in force. Administrative responsibility shall not be an obstacle to prosecution, criminal penalty, and civil liability in accordance with this Law and other legal instruments in force.

#### Article 97 ._

Any person who has obtained the right to develop, construct, restore or expand the sewage system, and fails to comply with the construction plan projects, contracts, and sewage system technical regulations as stipulated in Article 17 of this Law, shall be required to make immediate improvements.

In case of stubbornness, the offender shall be punished by imprisonment from 6 (six) months to 2 (two) years and a fine of 2,000,000 (two million) Riels to 8,000,000 (eight million) Riels.

#### Article 98 ._

Any person who implements construction projects and fails to comply with work safety regulations or public safety measures as stipulated in Article 18 of this Law, causing injury to others and resulting in the victim's occupational disability for a period equal to or exceeding 8 (eight) days starting from the date of the incident, shall be punished by imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 1 (one) year and a fine of 400,000 (four hundred thousand) Riels to 4,000,000 (four million) Riels.

This offense shall be punished by imprisonment from 1 (one) year to 5 (five) years and a fine of 4,000,000 (four million) Riels to 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels in any of the following cases:

- Causes the death of another person

- Causes injury to several people

- Causes permanent disability to the victim.

#### Article 99 ._

Any person who does not construct a horizontal sewer line in a new development area as stipulated in Article 25 of this Law, shall be required to make improvements.

In case of stubbornness, the offender shall be punished by imprisonment from 6 (six) months to 2 (two) years and a fine of 2,000,000 (two million) Riels to 8,000,000 (eight million) Riels.

#### Article 100 ._

Any sewage system inspection officer or competent officer who, within the framework of their functions or on the occasion of performing their functions, revokes a permit or license for operating a sewage system business without clear reasons, shall be punished by imprisonment from 6 (six) days to 1 (one) month and a fine of 100,000 (one hundred thousand) Riels to 500,000 (five hundred thousand) Riels.

In case the above offense causes damage or business loss, the respective entity shall be responsible for compensating for such damage or loss in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Code and other laws in force.

In case any sewage system inspection officer or competent officer abuses their power by obtaining benefits in performing their duties or on the occasion of performing their duties for the offense as stipulated in the first paragraph above, they shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of Article 35 (Abuse of Power) of the Law on Anti-Corruption, which is a corruption offense and shall be under the sole jurisdiction of the Anti-Corruption Unit in investigating and building the corruption case to court.

Other entities and individuals who are aware of corruption offenses stipulated in this Law and in other laws and regulations in force of the Kingdom of Cambodia, shall report to the Anti-Corruption Unit or the Capital-Provincial Anti-Corruption Office regarding the facts of those offenses.

#### Article 101 ._

Any person who modifies the sewer system and manhole covers without complying with the conditions as stipulated in the second paragraph of Article 31 of this Law, shall be required to improve or replace those equipment or components.

In case of stubbornness, the offender shall be punished by imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 1 (one) year and a fine of 400,000 (four hundred thousand) Riels to 4,000,000 (four million) Riels.

#### Article 102 ._

Any person who fails to connect or disrupts the connection process of any sewer network without authorization as stipulated in Article 34 of this Law, shall be required to suspend the activity temporarily.

In case of stubbornness, the offender shall be punished by imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 1 (one) year and a fine of 400,000 (four hundred thousand) Riels to 4,000,000 (four million) Riels.

#### Article 103 ._

Any person who connects a sewer network from a Special Economic Zone, industrial park, or agro-industrial park to the public sewer network without authorization, as stipulated in Article 35 of this Law, shall be punished by imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 1 (one) year and a fine of 500,000 (five hundred thousand) Riels to 4,000,000 (four million) Riels.

#### Article 104 ._

Any person who digs to bury, digs to unearth, digs to fill, or sets boundary posts without authorization, as stipulated in Article 44 of this Law, shall be punished by imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 1 (one) year and a fine of 400,000 (four hundred thousand) Riels to 4,000,000 (four million) Riels.

#### Article 105 ._

Any person who discharges sewage from sources other than residential dwellings and fails to comply with point 2-a of the first paragraph of Article 48 of this Law, shall be required to organize their own collective basin or sewage treatment system immediately.

In case of stubbornness, the offender shall be punished by imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 1 (one) year and a fine of 400,000 (four hundred thousand) Riels to 4,000,000 (four million) Riels.

#### Article 106 ._

Any person who destroys or intentionally damages sewer system equipment or sewer networks by causing disruption or violating technical regulations in the construction, repair, maintenance, and protection of the infrastructure of the sewage system and/or sewer system, as stipulated in point 1 or point 2 of Article 56 of this Law, shall be punished by imprisonment from 6 (six) months to 2 (two) years and a fine of 2,000,000 (two million) Riels to 8,000,000 (eight million) Riels.

#### Article 107 ._

Any person who operates the processing or storage of sludge and fails to comply with the conditions as stipulated in point 1 of the first paragraph of Article 61 of this Law, shall be required to make immediate improvements.

In case of stubbornness, the offender shall be punished by imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 1 (one) year and a fine of 400,000 (four hundred thousand) Riels to 4,000,000 (four million) Riels.

#### Article 108 ._

For any person who commits an offense as stipulated in the second paragraph of Article 97, Article 98, the second paragraph of Article 99, the second paragraph of Article 101, the second paragraph of Article 102, Article 103, Article 104, the second paragraph of Article 105, Article 106, and the second paragraph of Article 107 of this Law, the court may declare one or more additional penalties as stipulated in Article 53 (Types of Additional Penalties) of the Criminal Code.

The substance, modality, and procedure for the implementation of these additional penalties shall comply with the provisions of the Criminal Code.

#### Article 109 ._

Legal entities may be declared criminally responsible under the conditions stipulated in Article 42 (Criminal Liability of Legal Entities) of the Criminal Code for offenses stipulated in Article 97 to Article 99 and from Article 101 to Article 107 of this Law.

Legal entities shall be punished by a fine of 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels to 200,000,000 (two hundred million) Riels, as well as one or more additional penalties as stipulated in Article 168 (Additional Penalties Applicable to Legal Entities) of the Criminal Code.

#### Article 110 ._

The implementation of the provisions of Chapter 14 of this Law shall not interfere with the implementation of other criminal laws in case the act defined as an offense stipulated in this Law is an offense defined in other criminal laws.

### Chapter 15
### Transitional Provisions

#### Article 111 ._

Legal instruments related to the sewage system shall remain valid until new legal instruments are introduced to replace them, in compliance with the provisions of this Law.

#### Article 112 ._

Any person who operates a sewage system business shall apply for a permit or license in accordance with the provisions of this Law within 1 (one) year after this Law enters into force.

### Chapter 16
### Final Provisions

#### Article 113 ._

Any provisions that contradict this Law shall be abrogated.

### Annex to the Law on Sewage System
### Glossary

1- Modification: refers to changing or adjusting any part of the original structure in the study layout, including additions or removals of any construction components.

2- Sewage System Work: refers to work related to the field of design layout study, construction, restoration, expansion, modification, installation, repair, and maintenance of sewage systems.

3- Sewer System Usage: refers to the payment of fees for using, repairing, restoring, expanding manholes, sewer networks, open channels, storage basins, and operating sewage treatment plants.

4- Collection and Transportation of Sewage: refers to the transportation of wastewater and sludge pumped, collected, and removed from toilets, manholes, septic tanks, sewage treatment systems, or sewage treatment plants, sewage treatment basins, or sewage filtration basins by various means of transport.

5- Water Quality Testing: refers to verifying sewage quality against defined standards when discharged into the system or from a sewage treatment system or sewage treatment plant, sewage treatment basin or sewage filtration basin into public water areas.

6- Sewage System Business Operation: refers to any person operating a business related to the construction, restoration, expansion, repair, and maintenance of sewage systems, as well as the collection and transportation of sewage or sludge.

7- Sewage Network Apparatus: refers to sewage treatment basins and various equipment installed or constructed to collect, transport, treat, and discharge sewage from buildings or discharge industrial wastewater to the connection point of the sewer system.

8- Connection: refers to connecting the public sewer network with the sewer system.

9- Public Water Area: refers to water sources for public use, such as rivers, tributaries, streams, canals, creeks, lakes, ponds, pools, seas, territorial waters, canal systems, water basins, groundwater, etc.

10- Sewage: refers to water whose original characteristics have been modified by human activities, discharged from various locations into the sewer network or public water areas, including rainwater flowing through the sewer network.

11- Wastewater: refers to sewage discharged from locations into the sewer system, and treated sewage discharged from sewage treatment plants or sewage filtration basins and other wastewater cleaning equipment.

12- Location: refers to public places, public buildings, residential dwellings, collective dwellings, residential gated communities (Borey), co-owned buildings, commercial and service areas, satellite cities, entertainment centers, or other similar locations.

13- Public Place: refers to museums, commercial exhibition venues, conference halls or meeting halls, educational establishments, entertainment centers, public parks, places of worship, or other places for gatherings or assemblies.

14- Satellite City: refers to mixed-use development areas consisting of modern dwellings, residential gated communities (Borey), apartments, condominium buildings, schools, supermarkets, hospitals, commercial buildings, service buildings, entertainment centers, as well as other similar structures.

15- Sludge Management Site: refers to locations for keeping or storing sludge collected and removed from sewer systems, sewage treatment systems, sewage treatment plants, sewage treatment basins, septic tanks, and business or production sites for recycling and reuse.

16- Reclaimed Water: refers to water that has been treated or filtered and has a quality complying with standards defined by legal instruments in force, and which can be reused for industry, handicrafts, agriculture, or other purposes.

17- Sewer Network: refers to horizontal sewer networks and vertical sewer networks.

18- Combined Sewer Network: refers to sewer networks that collect and transport or discharge sewage and rainwater together.

19- Separate Sewer Network: refers to sewer networks that collect and transport or discharge sewage and rainwater separately.

20- Main Sewer Network: refers to combined sewer networks and separate sewer networks connected to sewage storage basins, pumping stations, sewage treatment plants, or sewage filtration basins.

21- Branch Main Sewer Network: refers to main sewer networks and separate sewer networks connected to the main sewer network to collect and receive sewage and/or rainwater from lateral sewer networks.

22- Lateral Sewer Network: refers to combined sewer networks and separate sewer networks connected to the main sewer network or branch main sewer network.

23- Sub-lateral Sewer Network: refers to combined sewer networks and separate sewer networks connected to the lateral sewer network and receiving sewage from locations or discharge sources.

24- Interceptor Sewer Network: refers to sewer networks constructed to intercept sewage volume into sewage storage basins, sewage treatment plants, or sewage filtration basins and/or discharge water directly into natural storage basins or public water areas.

25- Sewer System: refers to public sewer systems and private sewer systems consisting of combined sewer networks and/or separate sewer networks, main sewer networks, branch main sewer networks, lateral sewer networks, sub-lateral sewer networks, interceptor sewer networks, as well as manholes.

26- Public Sewer System: refers to sewer systems developed, constructed, restored, installed, repaired, maintained, and managed by the competent authority in charge of sewage system management.

27- Private Sewer System: refers to sewer networks constructed, installed, repaired, maintained, and operated through private sector investment.

28- Sewage System: refers to sewer systems, sewage treatment systems, or sewage treatment plants, sludge management sites, sewage system work, sewage artwork structures, or sewage network apparatus, and other activities related to sewage.

29- Sewage Treatment System: refers to canal water storage basins, sewage treatment plants or sewage filtration basins, pumping stations, open channels, main sewer networks, branch main sewer networks, and sewage artwork structures established based on technological features or natural features.

30- Operator: refers to an entity established by the state or a private company that has obtained the right to manage, maintain, and operate the sewage system in accordance with this Law and legal instruments in force.

31- Sewage System Technical Regulations: refers to technical codes or technical standards or various technical rules for implementing sewage system work, prepared and put into use as determined by the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

32- Competent Agent: refers to an officer appointed by the competent authority in charge of sewage system management to monitor and inspect the alignment of system lines or sewer networks, locations of sewage treatment systems, or sewage treatment plants, or sewage filtration basins, manholes, connection points, and other related activities.

33- Sewer: refers to closed sewers and open sewers or open channels functioning to collect and transport or discharge sewage and/or rainwater.

34- Residential Dwelling: refers to a house for private personal lodging.

35- Sewage Artwork Structure: refers to discharge canals, discharge dams/barriers, sewage storage basin walls, assemblies, sewage treatment equipment, and other equipment serving the sewage treatment process.

36- Sewage Treatment Plant: refers to a cluster of collective sewage treatment basins that treat wastewater before discharging.

37- Sludge: refers to waste from septic tanks, sewage treatment systems, and sewage filtration basins.

38- Competent Authority for Sewage System Management: refers to the Ministry of Public Works and Transport and sub-national administrations.

39- Manhole: refers to a shaft constructed on the sewer network as an access point to inspect, repair, maintain, clean, and/or pump out sludge accumulated in the sewer network. Man

hole, lateral, or branching from the sewer network, which may vary in diameter, size, volume, elevation, direction, and has a nearly straight length within appropriate spacing.

40- Sewage Treatment Basin: refers to equipment or structures that treat or primary neutralize sewage from residential sources, shared buildings, services and handicrafts, or any location before discharging into the sewer network.

41- Septic Tank: refers to basins receiving sewage from toilets and bathrooms to intercept and convert organic waste in sewage before discharging into the sewer network.

42- Sewage Filtration Basin: refers to public sewage storage basins and natural filtration basins.

43- Sewage Storage Basin: refers to storage basins for mixed wastewater, such as rainwater runoff, wastewater from residential dwellings, agricultural wastewater, and industrial wastewater, created specifically to serve sewage treatment.

44- Natural Filtration Basin: refers to filtration basins that clean sewage in stages and have natural flow, storing water for a period of time to utilize sunlight, wind or mechanical ventilation, and natural bacteria in synthesizing wastewater to eliminate micro-organisms present in the wastewater before discharging to public water areas.

45- Technician: refers to sewage system work technicians who have undergone training and obtained proper certification recognized by the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.
