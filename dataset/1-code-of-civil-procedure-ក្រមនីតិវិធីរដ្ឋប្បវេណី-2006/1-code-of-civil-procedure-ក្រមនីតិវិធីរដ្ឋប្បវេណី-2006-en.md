The Code of Civil Procedure, which was adopted by the National Assembly on May 26, 2006, at its 4th session of the 3rd legislature, and which was reviewed and agreed upon by the Senate as to its form and legal concepts without any modification on June 23, 2006, at its 1st plenary session of the 2nd legislature, the full text of which is as follows:

# CODE OF CIVIL PROCEDURE

# BOOK 1
## GENERAL PROVISIONS

### CHAPTER 1
### PURPOSE OF THE CODE OF CIVIL PROCEDURE, PURPOSE OF CIVIL SUITS, RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE COURT AND THE PARTIES

#### Article 1.- Purpose of the Code of Civil Procedure

Procedures related to civil suits shall be conducted in accordance with the provisions of this Code, except where otherwise specifically provided for by other laws.

#### Article 2.- Purpose of Civil Suits, Right to Standing in Court

1- Civil suits have the purpose of having the court resolve civil disputes based on the law in order to protect the rights of private persons.

2- Every person is guaranteed the right to standing in court regarding civil disputes.

#### Article 3.- Right to Demand Inquiry, Adversarial Principle

1- No party may be judged without being examined or summoned.

2- In all cases, the court must respect the adversarial principle.

#### Article 4.- Responsibilities of the Court and the Parties

The court must strive to ensure that civil suits proceed fairly and quickly, while the parties must conduct civil suits in good faith and honestly.

#### Article 5.- Language Used in Court, Right of the Parties to Request an Interpreter

1- In court, the Khmer language shall be used.

2- Parties who are unable to understand the Khmer language may request a qualified interpreter, and if they are unable to provide one themselves, the State

must find a substitute.

#### Article 6.- Participation of the Prosecutor

1- In cases where it is deemed necessary to protect the public interest, the court must notify the prosecutor that a complaint has been received.

2- Even if no notification as provided in paragraph 1 above is given by the court, the prosecutor may participate in the proceedings of a civil suit and express opinions if they deem it necessary to protect the public interest.

### CHAPTER 2
## COURT

### SECTION 1
### JURISDICTION OF THE TRIAL COURT

#### Article 7.- Definition of Court with Jurisdiction

A court with jurisdiction is a court that can legally receive a complaint, judge a case, and issue a judgment or decree.

#### Article 8.- Jurisdiction of the Trial Court Determined by Residence, etc.

Suits against defendants as defined in the points below must be filed in the Court of First Instance that has jurisdiction to judge at the place defined in each point below:

A- Natural person:

- The defendant's residence.

- The place of stay, if there is no residence or if the residence in the Kingdom of Cambodia is uncertain.

- The last residence, if there is no place of stay or if the place of stay is uncertain.

B- Domestic legal person:

- The location of the office or the principal place of business.

- The residence of the representative or the person in charge of management, if there is no office or place of business.

C- Foreign legal person:

- The location of the office or the principal place of business.

- The residence of the representative in the Kingdom of Cambodia or the person in charge of management in the Kingdom of Cambodia, if there is no office or place of business in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 9.- Jurisdiction of the Trial Court Determined by Property, etc.

In addition to the court defined in Article 8 (Jurisdiction of the trial court determined by residence, etc.) above, a complaint defined in the points below may also be filed in the court that has jurisdiction at the place defined in each point:

A- Suit demanding performance of an obligation: The place where the obligation is to be performed.

B- Suit demanding payment of a bill of exchange or check: The place of payment of the bill or check.

C- Suit demanding performance of an obligation against a person who has no residence in the Kingdom of Cambodia or whose residence is uncertain: The place where the object of the claim or the object of the security guaranteeing the claim, or the defendant's attachable property, is located.

D- Suit demanding performance of an obligation against a legal person that has no office or place of business in the Kingdom of Cambodia or whose office or place of business is uncertain

uncertain: the place where the object of the claim or the object of the security guaranteeing the claim, or the defendant's attachable property, is located.

E- Suit against a person having an office or place of business related to the activities of that office or place of business: The location of that office or place of business.

F- Suit related to a tortious act: The place where the tortious act occurred.

G- Suit related to immovable property: The place where that immovable property is located.

H- Suit related to registration: The place where registration is to be made.

I- Suit related to inheritance: The residence of the decedent at the time of commencement of succession, or the place of stay if the decedent had no residence in the Kingdom of Cambodia or whose residence was uncertain, or the last residence if the decedent had no place of stay in the Kingdom of Cambodia or whose place of stay was uncertain.

#### Article 10.- Special Jurisdiction of the Trial Court over Divorce Cases and Cases Concerning Paternity

1- Suits for divorce and suits demanding confirmation of the existence or non-existence of paternity shall be under the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of First Instance having jurisdiction at the residence of a party to the status relationship involved in the suit, or the residence at the time of that party's death.

2- If the residence as defined in paragraph 1 above is not in the Kingdom of Cambodia or if the residence in the Kingdom of Cambodia is uncertain, the jurisdiction of the trial court shall be determined by the place of stay. If there is no place of stay or if the place of stay is uncertain, the jurisdiction shall be determined by the last residence.

#### Article 11.- Jurisdiction of the Trial Court for Multiple Claims Joined Together

Regarding a suit with multiple claims that fall under the jurisdiction

different trial courts, the suit may be filed in any court that has jurisdiction over one of the claims, by joining the other claims together.

#### Article 12.- Determination of Court with Jurisdiction

In cases where a court with jurisdiction cannot exercise its right to judge due to law or fact, or where the jurisdiction of a trial court cannot be determined because the jurisdictional boundaries of the court are uncertain, the Supreme Court shall determine the court with jurisdiction by a ruling upon request.

#### Article 13.- Agreement on Determination of Court with Jurisdiction

1- An agreement determining the court with jurisdiction for trial shall be effective only if it is made between merchants, between legal persons, or between a merchant and a legal person, and only for the trial of first instance.

2- The agreement provided in paragraph 1 above shall be effective only if it is made regarding a suit based on a specific legal relationship and is made in writing.

#### Article 14.- Jurisdiction of Trial Court Arising from Lack of Objection by the Defendant

When a defendant has already made a defense on the merits of the case in the preparatory proceedings for oral argument or has answered with oral arguments on the merits of the case at the court of first instance without objecting to the lack of jurisdiction of that court, such court shall have jurisdiction to judge.

#### Article 15.- Case of Exception to the Application of Exclusive Jurisdiction of the Trial Court as Defined by Law

The provisions of Article 13 (Agreement on Determination of Court with Jurisdiction) and Article 14 (Jurisdiction of trial court arising from lack of objection by the defendant) above shall not apply to cases where the law provides

exclusive jurisdiction over the suit.

#### Article 16.- Examination of Evidence by Discretion

The court may examine evidence on issues related to the jurisdiction of the trial court on its own discretion.

#### Article 17.- Time Basis for Determining Jurisdiction of the Trial Court

The jurisdiction of the trial court shall be determined as of the time the complaint is filed.

#### Article 18.- Disposition of Suits with Lack of Jurisdiction of Trial Court

1- If a court finds that the suit in whole or in part does not fall under its jurisdiction, the court shall transfer the suit to a court with jurisdiction upon request of a party or on its own discretion.

2- A request for transfer of a suit must be made in writing, except for cases where the request is made on a court date. When making such a request, the reasons for the request must be clearly stated.

#### Article 19.- Transfer to Avoid Delay, etc.

1- Even if a suit falls under its jurisdiction, if a court of first instance deems it necessary to avoid excessive delay in the proceedings or to ensure equity between the parties, considering the residence of the parties and witnesses to be examined, the location of objects to be inspected, and other circumstances, the court may transfer the suit in whole or in part to another court with jurisdiction upon request of a party or on its own discretion.

2- In case of a request as provided in paragraph 1 above, the court shall decide after hearing the opinion of the other party. In case where the court decides on a transfer as provided in paragraph 1 above on its own discretion, the court may hear the opinions of the parties.

#### Article 20.- Limitation of Transfer in Cases of Exclusive Jurisdiction as Defined by Law

The provisions of Article 19 (Transfer to avoid delay, etc.) above shall not apply to cases where the suit is under the exclusive jurisdiction defined by law of the court handling the suit.

#### Article 21.- Complaint of Objection

An appeal may be filed against a ruling to transfer or a ruling dismissing a request for transfer.

#### Article 22.- Effect of Transfer Ruling, etc.

1- A final ruling for transfer shall be binding on the court to which the suit is transferred.

2- The court to which a suit is transferred may not transfer the case to another court.

3- When a ruling for transfer becomes final, the suit shall be deemed to have been filed in the court to which it is transferred at the time it was originally filed.

4- When a ruling for transfer becomes final, the court that decided on the transfer must send the case file to the court to which the suit is transferred.

### SECTION 2
## STRUCTURE OF THE COURT

#### Article 23.- Trial of First Instance by a Single Judge or by a Collegiate Body of Judges

1- At the Court of First Instance, cases shall be handled by a single judge, except for cases provided in paragraph 2 below.

2- The following cases shall be handled by a collegiate body of judges:

A- Cases where the value of the object of the suit is not less than 5,000,000 (five million) Riels, and which the court recognizes as should be judged and tried by a collegiate body of judges, considering the number of parties and other issues.

B- Cases that the law defines as having to be judged and tried by a collegiate body of judges.

3- In a collegiate body of judges as defined in paragraph 2 above, there shall be 3 (three) judges, and one shall be appointed as the presiding judge of the collegiate body.

4- A ruling to have a case judged and tried by a collegiate body of judges based on point A of paragraph 2 above shall be made by the collegiate body.

#### Article 24.- Deliberation of the Collegiate Body

1- The deliberation of the collegiate body shall not be public.

2- The deliberation shall be opened and led by the presiding judge of the collegiate body.

3- Each judge must express their opinion in the deliberation.

4- The process of deliberation, the opinions of each judge, and the number of votes for an opinion shall be kept secret.

#### Article 25.- Decision of the Collegiate Body

1- Decisions shall be made by a majority vote of the judges in the collegiate body.

2- Each judge in the collegiate body shall have equal voting rights.

### SECTION 3
### ALLOCATION OF CASES, DISQUALIFICATION OF JUDGES, AND OBJECTIONS

#### Article 26.- Allocation of Cases

1- The allocation of cases in a court and the order of substitution for cases where a judge is unavailable shall be determined in advance each year by a ruling of the President of that court.

2- Each case shall be allocated automatically to each judge in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 above.

3- The allocation of cases in a court and the order of substitution for cases where a judge is unavailable as defined in paragraph 1 above shall not be changed during that year, except in cases where a judge has too much work, or cases where a judge must leave or transfer to another court, or cases where a judge is continuously unavailable due to reasons such as long-term leave, etc. Changes in such cases shall be determined by a ruling of the President of the court.

#### Article 27.- Disqualification of a Judge from Trial Jurisdiction

1- A judge shall be disqualified from performing their duties in the following cases:

A- When the judge or the judge's spouse or former spouse is a party to the case.

B- When the judge is a blood relative within the 6th (sixth) degree or a relative by marriage within the 3rd (third) degree or a former relative by marriage within the 3rd (third) degree of any party.

C- When the judge is a guardian of any party.

D- When the judge in charge of the case had or is having a dispute in court with any party.

E- When the judge served as a witness or an expert in the case.

F- When the judge is a representative, or served as a representative or an assistant to a party in the case.

G- When the judge participated in an arbitral award in the case, or participated in a judgment in the first instance that was appealed, or a judgment in the appellate instance that was appealed, or expressed an advisory opinion on points of law regarding the case.

2- In cases where there are grounds for disqualification as defined in paragraph 1 above, the court shall decide on the disqualification upon request or on its own discretion.

#### Article 28.- Challenge to a Judge

1- In cases where there are circumstances that give rise to doubt as to the impartiality of a judge in judging, a party may challenge that judge.

2- In cases where a party has already answered with oral arguments before a judge or has made a defense in the preparatory proceedings for oral argument before a judge, that party may not challenge that judge. However, this provision shall not apply to cases where the party was unaware of the grounds for challenge or cases where the grounds for challenge occurred later.

#### Article 29.- Withdrawal of a Judge

In cases defined in paragraph 1 of Article 27 (Disqualification of a Judge from Trial Jurisdiction) or paragraph 1 of Article 28 (Challenge to a Judge) of this Code, a judge may withdraw with permission from the President of the court to which they are attached.

#### Article 30.- Decision on Request for Disqualification or Challenge

1- Regarding the disqualification of a judge or challenge to a judge, the collegiate body

at the court to which that judge belongs, shall decide by a ruling.

2- A judge may not participate in a decision regarding their own disqualification or challenge. However, the judge may express their opinion regarding the request for disqualification or challenge.

3- A request for disqualification or a challenge must be made by first submitting a written statement of the grounds.

4- When a request for disqualification or a challenge is made, the proceedings shall be stayed until the ruling on the motion becomes final. However, this provision shall not apply to emergency actions such as the preservation of evidence, etc.

5- No appeal may be filed against a ruling that finds the request for disqualification or challenge to be well-founded.

6- An appeal may be filed against a ruling that finds the request for disqualification or challenge to be groundless.

#### Article 31.- Identical Application to Court Clerks

The provisions of this Section shall also apply to court clerks. In this case, the decision regarding the request for disqualification or challenge shall be made by the court to which the clerk belongs.

### CHAPTER 3
## PARTIES

### SECTION 1
### CAPACITY OF PARTIES AND CAPACITY TO SUE

#### Article 32.- Capacity of Parties, Capacity to Sue, Legal Representation...

defined for persons who lack the capacity to sue.

1- Persons who can have rights and obligations under the provisions of the Civil Code and other laws may be a plaintiff or a defendant in a civil suit.

2- Persons who have the capacity to perform acts by themselves under the provisions of the Civil Code and other laws may file a complaint by themselves as a plaintiff or may answer a suit as a defendant, or perform other necessary litigation acts.

3- Minors and persons under general guardianship may perform valid litigation acts only if they are performed by a legal representative. However, this provision shall not apply to cases where a minor may perform acts independently.

4- The provisions of the Civil Code and other laws shall apply to legal representatives and their representation rights.

#### Article 33.- Special Provisions Concerning Litigation Acts of Persons under Curatorship and Legal Representatives

1- Persons under curatorship or legal representatives may perform litigation acts regarding a suit or an appeal to a higher court filed by the other party, without the consent of the curator or the supervisor of the guardian, or other authorization.

2- Persons under curatorship or legal representatives must have special authorization to perform the following litigation acts:

A- Withdrawal of the complaint, settlement, abandonment of the claim, or acknowledgement of the claim.

B- Withdrawal of an appeal or a motion for review.

#### Article 34.- Special Provision Concerning Capacity to Sue of Foreigners

A foreigner shall be deemed to have the capacity to sue if that person...

shall be deemed to have the capacity to sue according to the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia, even if that person lacks the capacity to sue according to the laws of their own country.

#### Article 35.- Measures in Case of Lack of Capacity to Sue, etc.

1- In cases where there is a lack of capacity to sue or legal representation as defined by law, or necessary authorization to perform litigation acts, the court shall order the defect to be corrected, specifying a period of time. In this case, if any damage might occur due to delay, the court may allow the person with the defect to perform litigation acts temporarily.

2- Litigation acts performed by a person lacking capacity to sue or lacking legal representation as defined by law, or lacking necessary authorization to perform litigation acts, shall have retroactive effect from the time they were performed, if they are subsequently acknowledged by the party who has acquired capacity to sue, or by a representative who has acquired legal representation as defined by law or authorization.

3- Legal representation as defined by law or necessary authorization to perform litigation acts must be confirmed by a written document.

#### Article 36.- Special Representative

1- In cases where there is no legal representative as defined by law, or cases where the legal representative is unable to exercise the representation rights, a person who intends to perform litigation acts against a person lacking capacity to sue may apply to the court where the suit is filed to appoint a special representative, by first explaining that damage might occur due to the delay.

2- The court may replace a special representative at any time.

3- The decision regarding the appointment or replacement of a special representative shall also be notified to the special representative.

4- A special representative must have the same authorization as a guardian to perform litigation acts.

#### Article 37.- Notification of Termination of Legal Representation Rights

1- The termination of legal representation rights shall not be effective unless notification of such termination is given by the principal or the representative to the other party.

2- The person who notifies of the termination of legal representation rights must report to the court about such notification in writing.

#### Article 38.- Identical Application to Representatives of Legal Persons

The provisions of this Code regarding legal representation and legal representatives shall also apply to representatives of legal persons.

### SECTION 2
## JOINT SUIT

#### Article 39.- General Conditions for Joint Suit

If there are any of the following grounds between multiple persons, those persons may sue or be sued as joint parties:

A- When the rights or obligations that are the object of the suit are identical.

B- When the rights or obligations that are the object of the suit are based on the same grounds of fact and law.

C- When the rights or obligations that are the object of the suit are of the same type and are based on grounds of fact and law of the same type.

#### Article 40.- Status of Joint Parties in Normal Joint Suit

Litigation acts performed by one of the joint parties, and litigation acts of the other party performed against one of the joint parties...

and grounds that occur with respect to one of the joint parties shall have no effect on the other joint parties.

#### Article 41.- Provisions on Judging Compulsory Joint Suits

1- In cases where the rights or obligations that are the object of the suit must be decided jointly for all joint parties to be final, notwithstanding the provisions of Article 40 (Status of Joint Parties in Normal Joint Suit) above, litigation acts performed by one of the joint parties shall have effect only in cases where they are beneficial to all.

2- In cases defined in paragraph 1 above, litigation acts of the other party performed against one of the joint parties shall have effect on all of them.

3- In cases defined in paragraph 1 above, if there are grounds for staying or suspending the litigation proceedings with respect to one of the joint parties, such stay or suspension shall have effect on all of them.

#### Article 42.- Selection of Parties

1- Multiple persons who have a common interest may select one or more persons from among them to be the plaintiff(s) or defendant(s) for all of them.

2- If plaintiff(s) or defendant(s) are selected according to paragraph 1 above while a suit is pending in court, the other parties shall automatically withdraw from that suit.

3- The persons who made the selection of plaintiff(s) or defendant(s) according to paragraph 1 above may revoke the selection or change the selected parties.

4- Among the selected parties, if anyone loses...

the status of a selected party due to death or other reasons, the other selected parties may perform litigation acts for all.

### SECTION 3
## INTERVENTION IN A SUIT

#### Article 43.- Assistive Intervention

Any third party who has a legal interest in the outcome of a suit may intervene in the suit to assist one of the parties.

#### Article 44.- Application for Assistive Intervention

1- An application for assistive intervention must be made to the court where the litigation acts are to be performed, specifying which party in which suit the intervention is for, and clearly stating the reasons for the intervention.

2- An application for assistive intervention may be made together with litigation acts that the intervening party may perform.

#### Article 45.- Objection to Assistive Intervention, etc.

1- If a party objects to the assistive intervention, the court shall decide whether or not to allow the intervention by a ruling. In this case, the intervening party must first provide a statement of the reasons for the intervention.

2- The objection defined in paragraph 1 above may not be made after a party has made a defense in the preparatory proceedings for oral argument or has made an oral argument without raising such objection.

3- Regarding the ruling defined in paragraph 1 above, an appeal may...

may be appealed.

#### Article 46.- Litigation Acts of Assistive Intervenor

1- An assistive intervenor may submit defensive or protective measures, file objections, appeal to a higher court, request a retrial, and perform other litigation acts related to the suit. However, this provision shall not apply to acts that the person being assisted cannot perform at the time of the intervention.

2- If a litigation act performed by an assistive intervenor is in conflict with a litigation act of the party being assisted, such act shall have no effect.

3- Notwithstanding an objection to the assistive intervention, the assistive intervenor may perform litigation acts until the court decision to not allow the intervention becomes final.

4- Litigation acts of an assistive intervenor shall also have effect, even if the court decision to not allow the intervention becomes final, if the party invokes such acts.

#### Article 47.- Effect of Judgment on Assistive Intervenor

Regarding a suit with assistive intervention, if the judgment that is disadvantageous to the party being assisted has become final, the findings of such judgment shall be binding on the assistive intervenor in their relation with the assisted party, except in the following cases:

A- Where the assistive intervenor was unable to perform litigation acts by virtue of the second sentence of paragraph 1 of Article 46 (Litigation Acts of Assistive Intervenor) above.

B- Where a litigation act of the assistive intervenor had no effect by virtue of paragraph 2 of Article 46 (Litigation Acts of Assistive Intervenor) above.

C- Where the assisted party obstructed a litigation act of the assistive intervenor.

D- Where the assisted party failed to perform, intentionally or by negligence, a litigation act that the assistive intervenor was unable to perform.

#### Article 48.- Assistive Intervention with Status of Joint Party

1- If the judgment is binding on the assistive intervenor and the other party of the assisted person, the status of the assistive intervenor in the suit shall be the same as that of a joint party as defined in the provisions of Article 41 (Provisions on Judging Compulsory Joint Suits) of this Code.

2- In cases defined in paragraph 1 above, paragraph 2 of Article 46 (Litigation Acts of Assistive Intervenor) and points B and C of Article 47 (Effect of Judgment on Assistive Intervenor) above shall not apply.

#### Article 49.- Intervention as a Joint Party

1- In cases where the rights or obligations that are the object of the suit must be decided jointly for one of the parties and a third party to be final, that third party may intervene in the suit as a joint party.

2- The provisions of Article 44 (Application for Assistive Intervention) of this Code shall also apply to the application for intervention in a suit according to the provisions of paragraph 1 above.

3- An application for intervention in a suit according to the provisions of paragraph 1 above must be made in writing.

4- The written application defined in paragraph 3 above must be sent to the other joint parties and to the opposite party.

#### Article 50.- Notice of Suit

1- A party may give notice of a suit to a third party who is able to intervene in that suit, while the suit is pending in court.

2- Notice of a suit shall be given by submitting to the court a written document stating the reasons for the notice and the status of the proceedings. The court shall send this document to the person to be notified and to the opposite party of the suit.

3- Even if the person who receives the notice of a suit does not intervene, for the purpose of the application of the provisions of Article 47 (Effect of Judgment on Assistive Intervenor) of this Code, that person shall be deemed to have intervened at the time when they were able to intervene.

#### Article 51.- Suit with Plaintiff and Defendant as Joint Defendants

1- A third party who claims that the whole or part of the rights that are the object of a suit between other parties belong to them, may file a suit against both parties of that suit as joint defendants, to the court of first instance where the suit between the other parties is pending, while that suit is pending in any court.

2- In case of a suit defined in paragraph 1 above, if the court of first instance is handling the suit between the other parties, the court that received the suit from the third party may consolidate both suits.

### SECTION 4
## REPRESENTATIVE BY MANDATE AND ASSISTANT

#### Article 52.- Litigation Acts Performed by Representative by Mandate

1- A party may perform all litigation acts by themselves or through a representative by mandate whom they have selected.

2- Even if a party has selected a representative by mandate, that party may attend court and make oral arguments by themselves.

3- Litigation acts performed by a representative by mandate shall have the same effect as those performed by the party themselves. However, admissions and other statements on facts made by the representative by mandate shall have no effect if the party revokes or corrects them immediately.

#### Article 53.- Qualifications of Representative by Mandate

1- A representative by mandate as defined in Article 52 (Litigation Acts Performed by Representative by Mandate) above must be a lawyer, except for cases defined in the following points:

A- Where this Code or other laws allow a third party who is not a lawyer to perform litigation acts on behalf of a party.

B- Where a party is the state, an administrative legal entity, or other state institution, and that institution or its representative appoints an employee of that institution as a representative.

C- Where the court allows representation by a third party who is not a lawyer in a case where the value of the object of the suit is less than 1,000,000 (one million) Riels.

2- The power of a representative by mandate must be confirmed in writing.

3- The court may revoke the permission defined in point C of para-

of paragraph 1 above at any time.

#### Article 54.- Powers of Representative by Mandate

1- A representative by mandate has the power to perform all litigation acts, including filing a complaint, answering a suit, filing a counterclaim, intervening in a suit, appealing to a higher court, entering into a settlement, abandoning a claim, or acknowledging a claim, regarding the case for which they were appointed.

2- Powers granted for a suit include the power to apply for provisional measures, compulsory execution, selection of sub-representatives, and receiving payments.

3- In cases where the representative by mandate is a lawyer, the party may not limit their powers, except for cases related to filing a counterclaim, withdrawal of a complaint, settlement, abandonment of a claim, acknowledgement of a claim, withdrawal from the suit, filing an appeal to a higher court, withdrawal of an appeal from a higher court, or selection of sub-representatives.

4- In cases where the representative by mandate is not a lawyer, the party may also have that representative perform only specific litigation acts as determined separately by the party.

5- The provisions of the four paragraphs above shall not apply to representatives authorized by law to perform litigation acts on behalf of a party.

#### Article 55.- Cases where Representation by Mandate Does Not Terminate

The powers of a representative by mandate shall not terminate even in cases of death of the party, loss of capacity to sue, or replacement of the legal representative, etc.

#### Article 56.- Grounds for Termination of Representation by Mandate

1- The powers of a representative by mandate shall terminate due to grounds such as death...

of the representative, loss of qualifications of the representative, or completion of the mandate task, etc.

2- A party may revoke the powers of a representative by mandate at any time, and a representative by mandate may resign as representative at any time.

3- The termination of powers of a representative by mandate shall not be effective unless notification of such termination is given by the principal or the representative to the other party.

#### Article 57.- Measures in Case of Lack of Representation Rights

1- In cases where there is a doubt as to the lack of representation rights of a representative by mandate, the court shall investigate the existence of the representation rights on its own discretion, even if such doubt arises at any stage of the suit.

2- If there is a lack of representation rights, the court shall instruct the defect to be corrected, specifying a period of time. In this case, the court may allow the person with the defect to perform litigation acts temporarily.

3- Litigation acts performed by a person with a lack of representation rights shall have retroactive effect from the time they were performed, based on the ratification of the party.

#### Article 58.- Assistant

1- If it is necessary to clarify the relations of a suit, a party or a representative by mandate may attend court with an assistant, with permission from the court.

2- The permission defined in paragraph 1 above may be revoked at any time.

3- Statements made by an assistant shall be deemed to be statements made by the party or the representative by mandate themselves, except in cases where the party or the representative...

mandate revokes or corrects them immediately.

### CHAPTER 4
## COURT COSTS

### SECTION 1
### MEANING AND TYPES OF COURT COSTS

#### Article 59.- Scope and Amount of Civil Court Costs to be Borne by Parties or Other Persons

In accordance with the provisions of Article 64 (Shares to be Borne and Reimbursement of Paid Court Costs) of this Code, parties or other persons shall bear the burden of paying court costs defined in Article 61 (Taxes on Application) and Article 62 (Court Costs Other Than Taxes) and party costs defined in Article 63 (Party Costs) of this Code.

#### Article 60.- Calculation of Value of the Object of the Suit, Calculation of Value in Case of Multi-Point Claims

1- The value of the object of the suit shall be calculated based on the benefits claimed in the suit. In cases where there are multiple claims in a single suit, the total value of those claims shall be deemed the value of the object of the suit. However, this provision shall not apply to each claim in cases where the claimed benefit is identical for each of those claims.

2- In cases where the claim for benefits, compensation for damage, penalty for breach of contract, or expenses is a secondary object of the suit, such values shall not be added to the calculation of the value of the object of the suit.

3- In cases where there is difficulty in calculating the value defined in para-

para 1 above, the court shall determine that value at its reasonable discretion. If that value cannot be calculated, it shall be deemed to be 5,500,000 (five million five hundred thousand) Riels.

#### Article 61.- Taxes on Application

1- When filing a complaint, a tax in an amount calculated according to the value of the object of the suit as defined in each of the following points must be paid to the court:

A- For the portion of the value of the object of the suit up to 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels: for every 100,000 (one hundred thousand) Riels, 1,000 (one thousand) Riels must be paid.

B- For the portion of the value of the object of the suit exceeding 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels up to 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels: for every 100,000 (one hundred thousand) Riels, 700 (seven hundred) Riels must be paid.

C- For the portion of the value of the object of the suit exceeding 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels up to 1,000,000,000 (one billion) Riels: for every 100,000 (one hundred thousand) Riels, 300 (three hundred) Riels must be paid.

D- For the portion of the value of the object of the suit exceeding 1,000,000,000 (one billion) Riels: for every 100,000 (one hundred thousand) Riels, 100 (one hundred) Riels must be paid.

2- The value of the object of the suit, which is the basis for calculating the tax defined in paragraph 1 above, shall be calculated in accordance with the provisions of Article 60 (Calculation of Value of the Object of the Suit, Calculation of Value in Case of Multi-Point Claims) of this Code.

3- For filing an appeal, a tax of 1.5 times the tax calculated according to paragraphs 1 and 2 above must be paid, and for filing a final appeal (Sartoke), a tax of 2 times the tax calculated according to paragraphs 1 and 2 above must be paid to the court.

4- For filing a request for retrial, 10,000 (ten thousand) Riels must be paid to the court.

5- For filing an application for a demand for payment, a tax of 1/2 of the amount calculated according to paragraph 1 above based on the value of the object of the claim must be paid. If it is deemed that a suit has been filed according to the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 327 (Effect of Objection to Demand for Payment Made Before Declaration of Provisional Execution) or Article 331 (Transfer of Demand for Payment Proceedings to Suit Proceedings by Objection to Demand for Payment Made After Declaration of Provisional Execution) of this Code, the person who filed the application for demand for payment must pay the amount of tax calculated by subtracting the tax they paid for the application for demand for payment from the amount of tax calculated according to paragraph 1 above.

6- In cases of filing an application for a court decision other than those defined in paragraph 1 and paragraphs 3 to 5, 5,000 (five thousand) Riels must be paid to the court.

7- Payment of taxes must be made in cash at the court's application receiving desk. An application for which tax has not been paid at the time of filing shall be deemed an illegal application.

8- In the following cases, upon application of the person who paid, the court shall reimburse the taxes as defined in the following points:

A- Case of overpayment: reimburse the overpaid tax.

B- Where a settlement is reached before oral argument, where a complaint is withdrawn before the end of the first date for oral argument, and where a decision to dismiss an application without oral argument becomes final: reimburse half of the tax paid.

#### Article 62.- Court Costs Other Than Taxes

1- Parties or persons related to the case must pay the following court costs to...

in an amount determined by the court:

A- Amount necessary for the court to conduct examination of evidence, transmission of documents, or other litigation acts under civil procedure.

B- Amount equal to the necessary travel and accommodation expenses of judges and clerks in cases where examination of evidence, factual research, or other activities are conducted outside the court.

2- The party or person related to the case who must pay court costs as defined in paragraph 1 above, regarding costs for acts performed upon application, is the applicant; and regarding costs for acts performed on the court's own motion, is the person determined by the court to pay.

3- For acts for which court costs must be paid according to paragraph 1 above, the court shall direct the party or the person related to the case to pay that amount in advance.

4- In cases where the court has directed payment of court costs in advance according to paragraph 3 above, but the person fails to pay in advance, the court may refuse to perform the act for which the cost was required.

5- For court costs defined in paragraph 1 that were not paid in advance, the court may collect them from the person liable to pay based on a court ruling, in accordance with Article 64 (Shares to be Borne and Reimbursement of Paid Court Costs) of this Code.

#### Article 63.- Party Costs

In addition to the costs defined in Article 61 (Taxes on Application) and Article 62 (Court Costs Other Than Taxes) above, an amount determined by the court as reasonable, considering the overall nature of the case or the financial means of the parties, etc., among the following points, shall also be deemed party costs:

A- Fees for preparing documents such as complaints, other applications, or docu-

other documents, such as preparatory documents, etc., and the cost of submitting those documents to the court.

B- Travel expenses, daily allowances, and accommodation expenses for parties and representatives to appear on the defined dates.

C- Other expenses that the court recognizes as necessary for carrying out the litigation proceedings.

### SECTION 2
## BURDEN OF COURT COSTS

#### Article 64.- Shares to be Borne and Reimbursement of Paid Court Costs

1- Court costs shall be borne by the losing party. In cases where a party loses only part of the case, the court shall decide the burden to be borne by each party at its discretion.

2- Notwithstanding paragraph 1 above, the court may, depending on the circumstances, have the winning party bear all or part of the court costs in cases where the winning party performed litigation acts that were unnecessary for the suit or defense, or caused a delay in the litigation proceedings.

3- Joint parties shall bear the court costs in equal shares. However, the court may, depending on the circumstances, order joint parties to bear the court costs jointly and severally, or order a party who performed unnecessary litigation acts for the suit or defense to bear a larger share of the court costs than the other joint parties.

4- In cases where a legal representative, a representative by mandate, or a court clerk has intentionally or by gross negligence caused unnecessary court costs to arise, the court handling the suit may, upon application or on its own motion, order that person to reimburse those court costs by a ruling.

5- Regarding the ruling defined in paragraph 4 above, an appeal may be filed.

#### Article 65.- Decision on Burden of Court Costs

1- In a decision to conclude a case, the court shall decide on the burden of all court costs at that level of trial at its discretion.

2- In cases where a higher court changes a decision on the merits, the court shall re-decide on the burden of all court costs. This provision shall also apply to cases where the court that received a remanded or transferred suit makes a decision to conclude the case.

3- In cases where a case is concluded without a court decision, the court of first instance shall, upon application, order the burden of court costs by a ruling. However, for a suit that has been settled, court costs shall be borne as agreed upon, and if there is no agreement, each party shall bear their own costs.

#### Article 66.- Procedure to Finalize Amount of Court Costs

1- The amount of court costs to be borne shall be determined by the clerk of the court of first instance, upon application, after the decision on the burden has become enforceable.

2- In cases defined in paragraph 1 above, if both parties bear court costs, the amounts to be borne by each party shall be deemed to be set off against each other to the extent that they match.

3- The disposition on the application defined in paragraph 1 above shall take effect after notification is given in a manner that can be deemed appropriate.

4- An objection to the disposition defined in paragraph 3 above must be made within 1 (one) week after receiving notification.

in cases where the court recognizes that the objection has correct grounds, the court shall determine the amount of the burden of court costs by itself.

5- The period of time defined in paragraph 4 above may not be extended.

### SECTION 3
## SECURITY FOR COURT COSTS

#### Article 67.- Ruling Ordering Security to be Provided

1- If a plaintiff has no domicile, office, or place of business in the Kingdom of Cambodia, the court shall, upon application of the defendant, order the plaintiff to provide security for court costs by a ruling. This provision shall also apply to cases where the security is insufficient.

2- If the defendant has made a defense in the preparatory proceedings for oral argument or has made an oral argument on the merits of the suit after knowing that there are grounds for providing security, the defendant may not make the application defined in paragraph 1 above.

3- A defendant who has made the application defined in paragraph 1 above may refuse to defend the suit until the plaintiff provides security.

4- The court shall determine the amount of security and the period for providing security in the ruling defined in paragraph 1 above.

5- The amount of security shall be determined based on the total amount of court costs that the defendant is expected to spend at all trial levels.

6- Regarding the ruling on the application defined in paragraph 1 above, an appeal may be filed.

#### Article 68.- Effect of Failure to Provide Security

If the plaintiff fails to provide security within the defined period, the court may dismiss the complaint by a judgment without oral argument. However, this provision shall not apply if security is provided before the judgment is issued.

### SECTION 4
## LITIGATION AID

#### Article 69.- Granting Litigation Aid

1- For a person who lacks the financial means to pay the costs necessary for preparing and conducting litigation proceedings, or a person who would suffer extreme hardship in maintaining their livelihood due to such payment, the court may, upon application, grant litigation aid by a ruling. However, this provision shall not apply to cases where it is clear that there is no possibility of winning the case.

2- The ruling to grant litigation aid shall be made for each level of trial.

3- Regarding the grounds for granting litigation aid, a preliminary statement must be made.

#### Article 70.- Content of Litigation Aid

1- A ruling to grant litigation aid shall have effect as defined in the following points:

A- Postponement of payment of court costs.

B- Exemption from payment of court costs. However, this decision can only be made if the court finds it appropriate, considering the financial means of the party, etc., after the postponement period has expired.

2- A ruling to grant litigation aid may have effect only for the person...

who received that ruling.

3- In cases where it is found that a person who received a ruling to grant litigation aid did not meet the conditions defined in Article 69 (Granting Litigation Aid) above or has lost those conditions, the court handling the case may, upon application of a person with an interest or on its own motion, revoke the ruling to grant litigation aid and order that person to pay the costs that were postponed or exempted at any time by a ruling.

4- Regarding the ruling defined in Article 69 (Granting Litigation Aid) of this Code and this Article, an appeal may be filed.

### CHAPTER 5
## SECURITY OF A SUIT

#### Article 71.- Method of Providing Security and Change of Security

1- Providing security shall be done by depositing cash or negotiable instruments that the court recognizes as appropriate at a depository, or by a method of reliance or negotiable instruments that the court recognizes as appropriate at the court, or other defined methods.

2- The court may, upon application of the person who provided the security, order a change of security by a ruling. However, this provision shall not preclude the parties from changing the security to another security by contract.

#### Article 72.- Rights of the Person Benefiting from Security

A person for whose benefit security has been provided has a priority right over other creditors in receiving payment from the cash or negotiable instruments deposited or provided according to the provisions of Article 71 (Method of Providing Security...

and Change of Security) above, in order to receive payment for the secured claim.

#### Article 73.- Cancellation of Security

1- The holder of the security right in this Article refers to the person for whose benefit the security was provided.

2- If the person who provided security proves that the grounds for requiring security have ceased to exist, the court shall issue a ruling to cancel the security upon application.

3- The provisions of paragraph 2 above shall also apply to cases where the person who provided security proves that they have obtained the consent of the holder of the security right for its cancellation.

4- After the grounds for enforcing the security right have arisen, if the court notifies the holder of the security right to exercise their rights within 2 (two) weeks upon application of the person who provided the security, but the holder fails to do so, they shall be deemed to have consented to the cancellation of the security.

5- Regarding the ruling defined in paragraphs 2 and 3 above, an appeal may be filed.

### BOOK 2
## PROCEDURE OF FIRST INSTANCE TRIAL

### CHAPTER 1
## COMPLAINT

#### Article 74.- Interest of the Suit

A suit may be filed in cases where the suit has a specific legal dispute as its object and such dispute can be resolved by a judgment.

#### Article 75.- Method of Filing Complaint and Matters to be Stated in Complaint

1- Filing a suit shall be done by submitting a written complaint to the court.

2- The following matters must be stated in the complaint:

A- Name or appellation and address of the parties, and name and address of the legal representative.

B- Substance of the judgment the plaintiff claims the court to issue, and the necessary facts to identify the claim.

3- In the complaint, in addition to the matters defined in point (B) of paragraph 2 above, the plaintiff should clearly state the facts that are the grounds for the claim, and regarding matters requiring proof by evidence, should state the important facts among all facts related to the grounds for the claim and the evidence.

4- A complaint that contains statements about methods of defense or protection shall also be treated as a preparatory document.

#### Article 76.- Suit Claiming for Future Performance

A suit claiming for future performance may be filed only if there is

necessity of having that claim made beforehand.

#### Article 77.- Joinder of Multiple Claims in a Single Suit

Multiple claims may be joined in a single suit only if such claims are to be resolved through the same type of procedure.

#### Article 78.- Examination of Complaint

1- In cases where a complaint contradicts the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 75 (Method of Filing Complaint and Matters to be Stated in Complaint) above, the court shall specify a reasonable period of time and order the defect to be corrected within that period. This provision shall also apply to cases where the tax for filing the suit has not been paid in accordance with Article 61 (Taxes on Application) of this Code.

2- In cases defined in paragraph 1 above, if the plaintiff fails to correct the defect, the court shall dismiss the complaint by a ruling.

3- Regarding the ruling defined in paragraph 2 above, an appeal may be filed.

#### Article 79.- Service of Complaint

1- The complaint must be served on the defendant.

2- The provisions of Article 78 (Examination of Complaint) above shall also apply to cases where the complaint cannot be served. These provisions shall also apply to cases where the plaintiff fails to pay the costs for service in advance.

#### Article 80.- Determination of Date for First Preparatory Proceedings for Oral Argument

1- When the complaint is filed, the court shall promptly determine the date for preparatory proceedings for oral argument and summon the parties.

2- The date defined in paragraph 1 above shall be set within 30 (thirty) days after the complaint is filed, except in cases where there are special grounds.

#### Article 81.- Dismissal of Suit Without Oral Argument

In cases where a suit is unlawful and its defects cannot be corrected, the court may dismiss the suit by a judgment without oral argument.

#### Article 82.- Dismissal of Suit in Case of Failure to Pay Summoning Costs in Advance

1- In cases where the court has ordered the plaintiff to pay in advance the costs necessary for summoning the parties to appear on a date in accordance with the provisions of this Code, and specifies a reasonable period, but the plaintiff fails to pay in advance, the court may dismiss the suit by a ruling, provided the defendant does not object.

2- Regarding the ruling defined in paragraph 1 above, an appeal may be filed.

#### Article 83.- Prohibition of Double Suits

Regarding a case pending in court, the parties may not file another suit.

#### Article 84.- Addition or Change of Object of Suit

1- The plaintiff may add to or change the object of the suit up to the conclusion of oral argument, provided that the basis of the claim is not changed. However, this provision shall not apply to cases that would cause extreme delay in the litigation proceedings due to such addition or change.

2- The addition or change of the object of the suit must be done in writing.

3- The document defined in paragraph 2 above must be served on the other party.

4- If the addition or change of the object of the suit is found to be improper, the court shall issue a ruling not to permit such addition or change, upon application or on its own motion.

#### Article 85.- Suit for Interlocutory Declaratory Judgment

1- In cases where a decision depends on the existence or non-existence of a legal relation which has become a point of dispute in the course of the suit, the plaintiff or the defendant may expand the claim or file a counterclaim for a declaratory judgment on such legal relation. However, this provision shall not apply if such claim for declaratory judgment is subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of another court under the law.

2- The provisions of paragraphs 2 and 3 of Article 84 (Addition or Change of Object of Suit) of this Code shall also apply to the expansion of a claim under paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 86.- Counterclaim

1- The defendant may file a counterclaim in the court where the original suit is pending up to the conclusion of oral argument, provided that the claim of the counterclaim is related to the claim of the original suit or to a method of defense for the original suit. However, this provision shall not apply if the claim of the counterclaim is subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of another court under the law, or if the counterclaim would cause extreme delay in the litigation proceedings.

2- The provisions concerning a suit shall apply to a counterclaim.

#### Article 87.- Time of Interruption of Prescription, etc.

judicial claims necessary for the interruption of prescription or for compliance with a legal period shall take effect when the complaint is filed or when the documents defined in paragraph 2 of Article 84 (Addition or Change of Object of Suit) or paragraph 2 of Article 84 (Addition or Change of Object of Suit) applicable mutatis mutandis under the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 85 (Suit for Interlocutory Declaratory Judgment) of this Code are submitted to the court.

#### Article 88.- Assignment of Object of Dispute, etc.

1- Even if a suit is pending, the parties to the suit shall not be prevented from assigning or transferring the object of dispute or the rights or obligations that are the object of the suit.

2- The assignment or transfer defined in paragraph 1 above shall not affect the suit. The person who assigned or transferred according to paragraph 1 above shall remain a party to the suit after the transfer.

3- The provisions of Article 48 (Intervention as a Joint Party) of this Code shall apply to cases where the person who received the assignment or transfer defined in paragraph 1 above becomes an intervening party.

### CHAPTER 2
## ORAL ARGUMENT AND PREPARATION

### SECTION 1
### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 89.- Conduct of Litigation Proceedings by Presiding Judge

1- The presiding judge shall conduct and manage the preparatory proceedings for oral

argument and must conduct and manage the oral argument.

2- The presiding judge may permit any person to speak and may forbid any person who fails to follow their orders from speaking.

#### Article 90.- Right to Issue Clarification, etc.

1- In order to clarify factual and legal points related to the suit, the court may question the parties, or require the parties to make assertions or provide evidence on those points during the preparatory proceedings for oral argument or the oral argument.

2- A party may request the court to ask necessary questions to the other party during the preparatory proceedings for oral argument or the oral argument.

3- Outside of the court dates, the court may direct the parties to prepare for providing clarifications on the next date, by specifying the points the court finds necessary to be clarified.

#### Article 91.- Objection to Conduct of Litigation Proceedings

In cases where a party objects to the conduct of preparatory proceedings for oral argument or oral argument, or to the court's management under the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 90 (Right to Issue Clarification, etc.) above, the court shall decide on that objection by a ruling.

#### Article 92.- Duty of Parties to Inquire

Parties must beforehand inquire in detail into the facts relations regarding witnesses and other evidence, in order to make sufficient and clear assertions and clarifications in the suit.

#### Article 93.- Time for Submitting Methods of Suit or Defense

Methods of suit or defense must be submitted to the court at a time that is rea-

sonable according to the progress of the suit.

#### Article 94.- Dismissal of Methods of Suit or Defense Submitted Late or Not at Appropriate Time

1- For methods of suit or defense submitted to the court late or not at an appropriate time due to intent or gross negligence of a party, the court may issue a ruling to dismiss such methods upon application or on its own motion, if the court finds that such submission would cause delay in concluding the litigation proceedings.

2- The provisions of paragraph 1 above shall also apply to methods of suit or defense whose meaning is unclear, in cases where a party fails to provide necessary clarification or fails to appear on the date summoned for clarification.

#### Article 95.- Facts that Can Be Basis of Judgment

The court cannot use any facts that have not been asserted by any party as the basis of a judgment.

#### Article 96.- Deemed Admission

1- In cases where a party does not clearly indicate an intent to contest facts asserted by the other party during preparatory proceedings for oral argument or oral argument, that party shall be deemed to have admitted those facts. However, this provision shall not apply if the court, considering the progress and substance of the case, finds that the party has contested the facts.

2- In cases where a party makes a statement that they do not know about the facts asserted by the other party, that party shall be presumed to have contested those facts.

#### Article 97.- Attempted Settlement

The court may attempt a settlement even if the suit is at any stage

at any stage.

#### Article 98.- Loss of Right to Object to Litigation Proceedings

1- In cases where a party or the court has performed a litigation act that contradicts the provisions of litigation procedure, a party may object to that act in court and may assert the nullity of that act.

2- In cases where a party knows or could know about the illegality defined in paragraph 1 above but fails to object at a reasonable time, that party shall lose the right to object. However, this provision shall not apply to cases where this right cannot be waived.

#### Article 99.- Separation and Consolidation of Cases

1- The court may order the separation or consolidation of cases by a ruling, or may revoke such a ruling.

2- In cases where the court has ordered the consolidation of cases involving different parties, if a party who did not have the opportunity to examine a witness because the witness had already been examined before the consolidation applies to examine that witness, the court must examine that witness.

#### Article 100.- Participation of Interpreters, etc.

1- If a person involved in civil procedure cannot understand Khmer, or is deaf or mute, the court shall have a qualified interpreter participate with that person. However, the court may question a deaf person in writing or allow a mute person to make a statement in writing.

2- The provisions concerning expert witnesses in this Code shall also apply to interpreters, except where contradictory to the nature of their role.

### SECTION 2
## PREPARATORY DOCUMENTS

#### Article 101.- Preparatory Documents

1- During preparatory proceedings for oral argument and oral argument, the court may order the parties to submit preparatory documents.

2- Preparatory documents shall contain statements about methods of suit or defense, and statements regarding the methods of suit or defense of the other party.

3- In the first preparatory document submitted by the defendant to the court, the defendant shall state their response to the substance of the judgment claimed by the plaintiff in the complaint, and whether they admit or deny the facts stated in the complaint, as well as facts for contesting those stated in the complaint, etc.

#### Article 102.- Time for Submitting Preparatory Documents, etc.

The court may specify the period for submitting the defendant's first preparatory document, or preparatory documents containing assertions about specific points or requests for examination of evidence related to specific points.

### SECTION 3
## PREPARATORY PROCEEDINGS FOR ORAL ARGUMENT

#### Article 103.- Purpose of Preparatory Proceedings for Oral Argument

In preparatory proceedings for oral argument, the court shall organize the parties' assertions, clarify the points of dispute where the parties disagree, and organize the evidence related to those points, so that the case can be adjudicated thoroughly during the oral argument procedure.

#### Article 104.- Attempted Settlement in Preparatory Proceedings for Oral Argument

In preparatory proceedings for oral argument, the court shall first attempt a settlement, except when it finds it inappropriate.

#### Article 105.- Date of Preparatory Proceedings for Oral Argument

1- Preparatory proceedings for oral argument shall be conducted on a date on which both parties can attend.

2- The date of preparatory proceedings for oral argument need not be open to the public. However, the court may permit persons it finds appropriate to attend as observers.

#### Article 106.- Litigation Acts, etc. in Preparatory Proceedings for Oral Argument

On the date of preparatory proceedings for oral argument, the court may issue rulings concerning applications for examination of evidence or other rulings that can be made outside of the date for oral argument, and may examine documentary evidence to the extent necessary for organizing the points of dispute and evidence.

#### Article 107.- Confirmation of Facts to Be Clarified, etc.

Upon concluding the preparatory proceedings for oral argument after finishing the organization of points of dispute and evidence, the court shall confirm with the parties the facts that are to be clarified in the subsequent examination of evidence.

#### Article 108.- Effect of Conclusion of Preparatory Proceedings for Oral Argument

After the conclusion of preparatory proceedings for oral argument, the parties may not submit new methods of suit or defense. However, this provision shall not apply to cases where such methods relate to matters that the court must investigate on its own motion, or cases where the submission of such new methods does not cause delay

in the litigation proceedings, or where a party has made a preliminary statement that they could not submit such methods before the conclusion of preparatory proceedings for oral argument without gross negligence.

#### Article 109.- Record of Preparatory Proceedings for Oral Argument

The court shall have the clerk dynamic a record of the preparatory proceedings for oral argument for each date.

#### Article 110.- Successive Matters of Record of Preparatory Proceedings for Oral Argument

1- The following matters shall be recorded in the record of preparatory proceedings for oral argument:

A- Case identifier.

B- Names of judges and clerks.

C- Names of parties, representatives, assistants, and interpreters who appeared.

D- Time and place.

2- The presiding judge and the clerk shall sign the record defined in paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 111.- Essential Matters of Record of Preparatory Proceedings for Oral Argument

In the record of preparatory proceedings for oral argument, a summary of actions taken on each date shall be recorded, and in particular, the following matters shall be clearly recorded:

A- Substance of the judgment the plaintiff claims the court to issue, and the defendant's response to such claim.

B- Substance of the facts asserted and applications for examination of evidence.

C- Admission or denial by the parties regarding facts asserted by the other party, and admission or denial of the authenticity

docuement.

D- Withdrawal of complaint, settlement, waiver of claim, and admission of claim.

E- Matters the court ordered to be recorded and matters the court permitted to be recorded upon request of the parties.

F- Decisions made on that date which were not made in writing.

#### Article 112.- Objection to Entry in Record of Preparatory Proceedings for Oral Argument, etc.

1- In cases where a party or other interested person objects to an entry in the record of the preparatory proceedings for oral argument, such objection shall be noted in the record.

2- Compliance with provisions concerning the procedure for preparatory proceedings for oral argument can be proved only by the record of the preparatory proceedings for oral argument. However, this provision shall not apply in cases where the record has been lost or destroyed.

### SECTION 4
## ORAL ARGUMENT

#### Article 113.- Setting Date for Oral Argument

When the preparatory proceedings for oral argument have been completed, the court shall set a date for oral argument and shall summon the parties to appear.

#### Article 114.- Necessity of Oral Argument

1- In order to issue a judgment on a suit, the court must conduct oral argument. However, regarding a case or matter to be decided by a ruling, the court shall determine whether or not to conduct oral argument.

2- In cases where oral argument is not conducted in accordance with the second sentence of paragraph 1 above, the court may question the parties.

3- The provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 above shall not apply to cases where there are special provisions in the law.

#### Article 115.- Oral Argument

1- Oral argument shall be conducted on a date on which both parties can be present.

2- Oral argument shall be conducted in an open courtroom. However, this provision shall not apply to cases where publicity would disrupt public order or where there are special provisions in the law.

#### Article 116.- Statement on Results of Preparatory Proceedings for Oral Argument and Oral Argument

1- During the oral argument procedure, the parties shall make a statement about the results of the preparatory proceedings for oral argument.

2- The statement defined in paragraph 1 above shall be made by clearly identifying the facts to be confirmed through subsequent examination of evidence during the oral argument.

3- During the oral argument, the parties may make additional assertions

regarding facts and submit further evidence. However, this provision shall not apply to cases where such assertion of facts or submission of evidence contradicts Article 108 (Effect of Conclusion of Preparatory Proceedings for Oral Argument) of this Code.

4- After examining evidence on the date for oral argument, if the court finds it especially necessary, it may conduct preparatory proceedings for oral argument again by a ruling.

5- The court may allow each party to make a final argument before concluding the oral argument.

#### Article 117.- Record of Oral Argument

1- The court shall have the clerk dynamic a record of oral argument for each date.

2- The provisions of Article 112 (Objection to Entry in Record of Preparatory Proceedings for Oral Argument, etc.) of this Code shall also apply to the record of oral argument.

#### Article 118.- Successive Matters of Record of Oral Argument

1- The following matters shall be recorded in the record of oral argument:

A- Case identifier.

B- Names of judges and clerks.

C- Name of the prosecutor who attended, if any.

D- Names of parties, representatives, assistants, and interpreters who appeared.

E- Time and place of oral argument.

F- Whether the oral argument was conducted in public, or the facts and grounds for not conducting it in public if it was not.

2- The presiding judge and the clerk shall sign the rec-

grounds defined in paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 119.- Essential Matters of Record of Oral Argument

1- In the record of oral argument, a summary of each oral argument shall be recorded, and in particular, the following matters shall be clearly recorded:

A- Withdrawal of complaint, settlement, waiver of claim, admission of claim, and confession.

B- Statements of witnesses, parties themselves, and expert witnesses.

C- Whether or not witnesses, parties themselves, and expert witnesses took an oath, and the reasons for not having witnesses or expert witnesses take an oath.

D- Results of inspection.

E- Matters the court directed to be recorded and matters the court permitted to be recorded upon request of the parties.

F- Decisions made which were not made in writing.

G- Announcement of decisions.

2- Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph 1 above, in cases where a suit is concluded other than by a judgment, statements of witnesses, parties themselves, and expert witnesses, and results of inspection, may be omitted from the record with the court's permission. However, this provision shall not apply if a party requests such matters to be recorded in the record within 1 (one) week from the day they became aware of the conclusion of the suit.

#### Article 120.- Recording by Audiotape, etc. instead of Record

1- Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 119 (Essential Matters of Record of Oral Argument) above, with the court's permission, the clerk may record the statements of witnesses, the parties themselves, or expert witnesses on audiotape or videotape in substitute for a written record. In this case, if the court permits, the parties may express their opinions.

2- In cases defined in paragraph 1 above, if a party requests up to the conclusion of the suit, the clerk must prepare a written document recording the statements of the witnesses, the parties themselves, or expert witnesses. This provision shall also apply to cases where a higher court finds it necessary when the suit is pending before it.

#### Article 121.- Use of Documents, etc. as Part of Record and Annexation

Documents, photographs, audiotapes, videotapes, or other objects that the court finds appropriate may be made part of the record of oral argument by treating them as portions of the record and annexing them to the case file.

#### Article 122.- Restriction on Photography, etc. in Courtroom

Photography, stenography, audiotaping, videotaping, or broadcasting in the courtroom may not be performed without the court's permission.

## CHAPTER 3
# EVIDENCE

### SECTION 1
## GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 123.- Principle of Adjudication Based on Evidence

1- The court shall recognize facts based on evidence. However, the court may consider the entire circumstances presented during oral argument.

2- Facts admitted by a party in court and facts found by the court to be obvious need not be recognized based on evidence.

3- A party may withdraw an admission in the following cases:

A- The other party does not object.

B- The admission is contrary to the truth and was made by mistake.

C- The admission was caused by a criminal act of another person.

#### Article 124.- Examination of Evidence

1- Examination of evidence shall be performed upon application by a party.

2- If the court cannot determine whether or not to admit an assertion of facts based on the evidence for which the parties have applied for examination, or if it otherwise finds it necessary, the court may examine evidence on its own motion.

#### Article 125.- Application for Examination of Evidence

1- A party applying for examination of evidence must specifically identify the facts to be proved.

2- A party applying for examination of evidence should clearly explain the facts to be proved and the relationship between those facts and the evidence.

3- Application for examination of evidence may also be made before the date of preparatory proceedings for oral argument or before the date of oral argument.

4- Application to examine witnesses and the parties themselves should, if possible, be made together at once.

#### Article 126.- Adoption of Evidence

The court shall examine evidence for which a party has applied for examination. However, this provision shall not apply to evidence that has no relationship with the facts to be proved and other evidence that the court finds unnecessary.

#### Article 127.- Concentrated Examination of Evidence

1- The court shall ensure that the examination of evidence can be performed in a concentrated manner on the first date for oral argument after completing the organization of points of dispute and evidence.

2- Examination of witnesses or the parties themselves shall, if possible, be performed in a concentrated manner.

3- Documents intended to be used in the examination of witnesses, etc., shall be submitted within a reasonable time before the commencement of the examination of witnesses, etc., except for documents to be used as evidence to impeach the credibility of statements of witnesses, etc.

#### Article 128.- Right of Parties to Participate

1- The court shall ensure the parties have the opportunity to be present to participate in the examination of evidence.

2- Examination of evidence may be performed even if a party fails to appear on the date set.

#### Article 129.- Examination of Evidence in Foreign Country

1- Regarding examination of evidence to be performed in a foreign country, the court shall entrust the task to the competent ministry or institution of that country, or to the ambassador, minister, or consul of the Kingdom of Cambodia residing in that country.

2- Examination of evidence performed in a foreign country shall be valid even if it violates the law of that country, provided that such examination does not violate this Code.

#### Article 130.- Entrustment of Investigation

The court may entrust necessary investigations to domestic or foreign institutions.

#### Article 131.- Preliminary Evidence

Preliminary evidence shall be provided by evidence that can be examined immediately.

### SECTION 2
## EXAMINATION OF WITNESSES

#### Article 132.- Duties of Witnesses

1- Except as otherwise provided in this Code or other laws, the court may examine any person as a witness.

2- The court may, by a ruling, order that a witness who refuses to appear in court without a justifiable reason be brought to the court.

3- If a witness refuses to appear in court without a justifiable reason, the court may, by a ruling, impose a fine of not more than 1,000,000 (one million) Riels.

4- An objection may be filed against the rulings defined in paragraphs 2 or 3 above.

#### Article 133.- Examination of Civil Servants as Witnesses

1- In cases where a civil servant or former civil servant is to be examined as a witness regarding confidential matters in their official capacity, the court must obtain the consent of the institution that manages that witness.

2- The managing institution defined in paragraph 1 above refers to the Senate for members or former members of the Senate, the National Assembly for members or former members of the National Assembly, and the Council of Ministers for members or former members of the Government.

3- The consent defined in paragraph 1 above cannot be refused except in cases where it could harm the public interest or cause serious obstacles to the performance of state duties.

#### Article 134.- Right to Refuse Testimony

1- If providing testimony relates to facts that could lead to the witness, the witness's spouse, or other relatives of the witness being prosecuted or punished, the witness may refuse to testify. This provision shall also apply to testimony regarding facts that would harm the reputation of the persons mentioned above or cause a serious breakdown in family relationships.

2- A witness may refuse to testify in the following cases:

A- In cases as defined in paragraph 1 of Article 133 (Examination of Civil Servants as Witnesses) above.

B- In cases where doctors, dentists, midwives, nurses, pharmacists, lawyers, religious officials, or persons who formerly held such positions, are to be examined about facts they learned in the performance of their duties and which are required to be kept confidential.

C- In cases where the examination relates to matters of technical or professional secrecy.

3- The provisions of paragraph 2 above shall not apply to cases where the witness has been released from the duty of secrecy.

#### Article 135.- Ruling on Refusal to Testify

1- The grounds for refusal to testify shall be supported by preliminary evidence.

2- Except in the case of item A of paragraph 2 of Article 134 (Right to Refuse Testimony) above, the court where the suit is pending shall examine the parties and determine by a ruling whether the refusal to testify is justifiable.

3- The parties and the witness may file an objection against the ruling defined in paragraph 2 above.

4- The provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 132 (Duties of Witnesses) above shall also apply to cases where a witness refuses to testify without a justifiable reason after a ruling by the court determining that there are no grounds for refusal to testify has become final and binding.

#### Article 136.- Examination of Witness Outside of Court

1- The court may examine a witness outside of court in the following cases:

A- The witness has no duty to appear in the court where the suit is pending, or has a justifiable reason that prevents them from appearing in court.

B- The witness's appearance in the court where the suit is pending would involve unreasonable expense in terms of time or costs.

C- The parties do not object.

2- Regarding the provisions of paragraph 1 above, the court may have a commissioned judge or an entrusted judge conduct the examination of a witness outside of court.

3- In cases where a commissioned judge or an entrusted judge examines a witness, that judge shall perform the duties of the court or the presiding judge.

#### Article 137.- Oath

1- The court shall have a witness take an oath, except as otherwise provided.

2- In cases where the witness to be examined is under 16 (sixteen) years of age or a person who does not understand the meaning of an oath, the court may not let that witness take an oath.

3- When examining a witness who falls under the conditions defined in Article 134 (Right to Refuse Testimony) of this Code and who does not exercise the right to refuse to testify, the court may decide not to have the witness take an oath.

4- A witness's oath shall be taken before the examination. However, it may be taken after the examination if there are special reasons.

5- The form of the oath shall follow the provisions in the Annex of this Code.

6- Before the oath is taken, the presiding judge shall explain the purpose of the oath and notify the witness of the punishment for providing false evidence.

#### Article 138.- Method of Examining Witness

1- Application for examination of a witness shall be made by clearly identifying the witness.

2- The person applying for examination of a witness shall attach a document specifying the points to be examined.

3- A witness must be summoned at least 2 (two) weeks before the date of examination.

4- A writ of summons for a witness shall state the following matters and shall be accompanied by a document specifying the points to be examined:

A- Names or titles and addresses of the parties.

B- Time and place to appear.

C- Punishments defined by law for failure to appear.

5- Examination of a witness shall be conducted in the following order: the presiding judge, the party who applied for the examination, and the other party. Associate judges may examine the witness after notifying the presiding judge.

6- If it finds it appropriate, the court may change the order of examination defined in paragraph 5 above.

7- A witness may not testify by reading from a document as a basis. However, this provision shall not apply to cases where the court gives permission.

8- If the court finds it necessary, it may permit a witness who has been examined or a witness who is waiting to be examined to remain in the courtroom.

9- When the court recognizes that a witness cannot testify sufficiently in the presence of a particular observer due to fear caused by that person, the court may order the observer to leave the courtroom while the witness testifies.

10- If the court finds it necessary, it may order the simultaneous examination of multiple witnesses.

#### Article 139.- Restriction on Questions

1- Questions shall, as much as possible, be specific and clear.

2- Except for cases with justifiable reasons, the following questions may not be asked:

A- Questions that insult or confuse the witness.

B- Leading questions.

C- Repetitive questions.

D- Questions that have no relationship with the points of dispute.

E- Questions requesting the witness's own opinion.

F- Questions requesting an opinion on facts that the witness did not directly experience.

3- The court may restrict questions upon application or on its own motion if it finds that the questions violate the provisions of paragraph 2 above.

4- A party may object to a restriction made in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 3 above.

### SECTION 3
## EXAMINATION OF PARTIES

#### Article 140.- Examination of Parties

1- The court may examine the parties. In this case, the court may have the parties take an oath.

2- During the examination of a party, if that party fails to appear or refuses to take an oath or testify without a justifiable reason, the court may recognize the assertions of the other party regarding the points of examination as being true.

3- If a party who has taken an oath makes a false statement, the court may, by a ruling, impose a fine of not more than 2,000,000 (two million) Riels.

4- An objection may be filed against the ruling defined in paragraph 3 above.

#### Article 141.- Mutatis Mutandis Application of Provisions on Examination of Witnesses, etc.

1- The provisions of Article 136 (Examination of Witness Outside of Court), Article 137 (Oath) (except for paragraphs 1 and 3), Article 138 (Method of Examining Witness) (except for paragraphs 8 and 10), and Article 139 (Restriction on Questions) of this Code shall apply mutatis mutandis to the examination of parties.

2- If the court finds it necessary, it may order the simultaneous examination of a party with the other party or a witness.

#### Article 142.- Examination of Statutory Representative

The provisions of this Code regarding the examination of parties shall apply mutatis mutandis to a statutory representative who represents a party in a suit. However, this provision shall not preclude the examination of the party themselves.

### SECTION 4
## EXPERT TESTIMONY

#### Article 143.- Application for Expert Testimony and Points for Expert Testimony

1- The court may order expert testimony upon application by a party.

2- When applying for expert testimony, the party shall attach a document specifying the points for which expert testimony is requested. However, if there is an unavoidable reason, such document may be submitted within a period determined by the court.

3- The court shall hear the opinion of the other party regarding the application defined in paragraph 2 above.

4- The court shall determine the points for expert testimony based on the document defined in paragraph 2 and considering the opinion defined in paragraph 3 above. In this case, the court shall provide a document specifying the points for expert testimony to the expert.

#### Article 144.- Designation of Experts and Duty to Provide Expert Testimony

1- Experts shall be designated by the court.

2- Persons who have the knowledge and experience necessary for providing expert testimony have the duty to provide expert testimony.

3- Persons may not serve as experts in the following cases:

A- Providing expert testimony would lead to the expert, their spouse, or other relatives being prosecuted or punished. This provision shall also apply to cases where providing expert testimony would harm the reputation of the persons mentioned above or cause a serious breakdown in family relationships.

B- The points for expert testimony relate to confidential matters in their official capacity as civil servants or former civil servants. However, this provision shall not apply to cases where these persons have obtained the consent of the managing ministry or institution. The managing ministry or institution cannot refuse consent except in cases where it could harm the public interest or cause serious obstacles to the performance of state duties.

C- The points for expert testimony relate to facts learned by doctors, dentists, midwives, nurses, pharmacists, lawyers, religious officials, or persons who formerly held such positions, in the performance of their duties and which are required to be kept confidential.

D- The points for expert testimony relate to matters of technical or professional secrecy.

4- The managing institution defined in item B of paragraph 3 refers to the Senate for members or former members of the Senate, the National Assembly for members or former members of the National Assembly, and the Council of Ministers for members or former members of the Government.

is the Council of Ministers.

5- The provisions of items B, C, and D of paragraph 3 above shall not apply to cases where the expert has been released from the duty of secrecy.

#### Article 145.- Challenge to Expert

1- If there are circumstances that prevent an expert from providing impartial expert testimony, a party may challenge the expert before the expert gives their conclusion on the points for expert testimony. Even after the expert has given their conclusion, this provision shall apply mutatis mutandis to cases where the grounds for the challenge arose later or the party learned of the grounds after the expert gave their conclusion.

2- An application for challenge must be made in writing, except in cases where the application is made on the date of preparatory proceedings for oral argument or the date of oral argument.

3- A party applying for a challenge must provide preliminary evidence of the grounds for the challenge.

4- No appeal may be filed against a ruling determining that a challenge is justifiable.

5- An objection may be filed against a ruling determining that a challenge is not justifiable.

#### Article 146.- Form of Expert's Conclusion and Questions by Expert, etc.

1- The court may have the expert express their opinion in writing or orally.

2- If necessary for providing expert testimony, an expert may participate in the hearing and request the court to examine a witness or a party, or with the court's permission, directly examine those persons.

#### Article 147.- Mutatis Mutandis Application of Provisions on Examination of Witnesses and Form of Oath

The provisions of Section 2 (Examination of Witnesses) of Chapter 3 of Book 2 of this Code shall apply mutatis mutandis to expert testimony, except as otherwise provided. However, the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 132 (Duties of Witnesses) shall not apply.

### SECTION 5
## DOCUMENTARY EVIDENCE

#### Article 148.- Application for Examination of Documentary Evidence, etc.

1- Application for examination of documentary evidence shall be made by providing the document held by the party or by applying to the court for an order for the holder of the document to produce it.

2- Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph 1 above, application for examination of documentary evidence may be made by applying to the court to request the holder of the document to send it.

3- If the court finds it necessary, it may retain the document provided or sent.

#### Article 149.- Annexation of Translation, etc.

1- In cases where an application is made for examination of documentary evidence written in a foreign language, the party applying shall attach a translation of the portions for which examination is requested.

2- In cases where the other party has an opinion regarding the accuracy of the translation defined in paragraph 1 above, that party shall submit their opinion to the court in writing.

#### Article 150.- Duty to Produce Document

1- The holder of a document may not refuse to produce the document, except as otherwise provided in this Code or other laws.

2- The holder of a document may refuse to produce the document if the document falls under any of the following items:

A- The document records facts that would lead to the holder, their spouse, or other relatives being prosecuted or punished, or facts that would harm the reputation of the persons mentioned above or cause a serious breakdown in family relationships.

B- The document relates to confidential matters in the official capacity of a civil servant and producing the document would cause serious obstacles to the performance of state duties.

C- The document records facts learned by doctors, dentists, midwives, nurses, pharmacists, lawyers, religious officials, or persons who formerly held such positions, in the performance of their duties and for which they have not been released from the duty of secrecy, or matters relating to technical or professional secrecy for which they have not been released from the duty of secrecy.

#### Article 151.- Application for Order to Produce Document

1- An application for an order to produce a document shall clearly state the following matters:

A- Title and summary of the document.

B- Holder of the document.

C- Facts to be proved.

2- An application for an order to produce a document shall be made in writing.

3- If the other party has an opinion regarding the application defined in paragraph 2 above, that party shall submit their opinion to the court in writing.

#### Article 152.- Order to Produce Document, etc.

1- If the court finds that the application for an order to produce a document is justifiable, the court shall, by a ruling, order the holder of the document to produce it. In this case, if the court finds that there are portions of the document that are unnecessary for examination or for which it cannot be recognized that there is a duty to produce, the court may order the production of the document while excluding those portions.

2- If the court intends to order a third party to produce a document, the court shall examine that third party.

3- An objection may be filed against a ruling on an application for an order to produce a document.

#### Article 153.- Effect of Failure by Party to Comply with Order to Produce Document, etc.

1- If a party fails to comply with an order to produce a document, the court may recognize the assertions of the other party regarding the contents of that document as being true.

2- The provision of paragraph 1 above shall also apply to cases where a party destroyed a document that they had a duty to produce or otherwise made it unusable, for the purpose of preventing its use by the other party.

3- In the cases defined in paragraphs 1 and 2 above, if the other party experiences extreme difficulty in making specific assertions regarding

the contents of the document and in clarifying the facts to be proved by the document, even by using other evidence, the court may recognize the assertions of the other party regarding those facts as being true.

#### Article 154.- Fine for Third Party's Non-compliance with Order to Produce Document

1- If a third party fails to comply with an order to produce a document, the court may, by a ruling, impose a fine of not more than 2,000,000 (two million) Riels.

2- An objection may be filed against the ruling defined in paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 155.- Authenticity of Document

1- A party who has applied for examination of documentary evidence shall prove that the document was authentically created.

2- If a document, based on its form and contents, is recognized as having been created by a civil servant in their official capacity, it shall be presumed to be an authentic public document.

3- If there is doubt regarding the authenticity of a public document, the court may, on its own motion, inquire with the relevant ministry or institution.

4- A private document bearing the signature of the party themselves or their representative shall be presumed to be authentic.

5- The provisions of paragraphs 2 and 3 above shall also apply to documents recognized as having been created by a foreign ministry or institution.

#### Article 156.- Proof by Comparison of Handwriting

1- Authenticity of a document may also be proved by comparison of handwriting which

can be in handwriting.

2- The provisions of Article 148 (Application for Examination of Documentary Evidence, etc.), Article 152 (Order to Produce Document, etc.), and paragraphs 1 and 2 of Article 153 (Effect of Failure by Party to Comply with Order to Produce Document, etc.) of this Code shall apply mutatis mutandis to the production or sending of documents or other objects bearing handwriting to be used for comparison.

3- If there is no suitable handwriting of the other party to be used for comparison, the court may, by a ruling, order that party to write characters to be used for comparison.

4- If the other party fails to comply with the ruling defined in paragraph 3 above without a justifiable reason, the court may recognize the assertions of the applicant for examination regarding the authenticity of the document as being true. This provision shall also apply to cases where the handwriting is falsified.

5- If a third party fails to comply with an order to produce a document as defined in paragraph 1 of Article 153 (Effect of Failure by Party to Comply with Order to Produce Document, etc.) of this Code, which applies mutatis mutandis under the provisions of paragraph 2 of this Article, without a justifiable reason, the court may, by a ruling, impose a fine of not more than 2,000,000 (two million) Riels.

6- An objection may be filed against the ruling defined in paragraph 5 above.

#### Article 157.- Fine for Contesting Authenticity of Document

1- If a party or their representative contests the authenticity of a document contrary to the truth, intentionally or through gross negligence, the court may
```

impose a fine of not more than 1,000,000 (one million) Riels by a ruling.

2- An objection may be filed against the ruling defined in paragraph 1 above.

3- In the case defined in paragraph 1 above, if the party or their representative who contested the authenticity of the document admits its authenticity while the suit is pending, the court may, depending on the circumstances, revoke the ruling defined in paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 158.- Mutatis Mutandis Application to Objects Equivalent to Documents

The provisions of this Section shall apply mutatis mutandis to objects other than documents, such as drawings, photographs, audiotapes, videotapes, and other objects created for providing information.

#### Article 159.- Handling of Application for Examination of Documentary Evidence Transcribed from Audiotapes, etc.

A party who applies for examination of documentary evidence by submitting a written transcript of an audiotape, videotape, or other object that can regularly record images by other means, shall, if requested by the other party to provide a duplication of such objects, provide such duplication to the other party.

#### Article 160.- Submission of Document Explaining Meaning of Audiotape, etc.

1- A party who applies for examination of evidence consisting of an audiotape, videotape, or other object that can regularly record images by other means, shall, if requested by the court or the other party, submit a transcript of the contents of such object or a document explaining its meaning.

2- If the other party has an opinion regarding the explanation of the meaning in the document

a document writing their opinion.

### SECTION 6
## INSPECTION

#### Article 161.- Application for Inspection

An application for inspection shall be made by indicating the object to be inspected.

#### Article 162.- Production of Objects for Inspection, etc.

1- The provisions of Article 148 (Application for Examination of Documentary Evidence, etc.), Article 152 (Order to Produce Document, etc.), and Article 153 (Effect of Failure by Party to Comply with Order to Produce Document, etc.) of this Code shall apply mutatis mutandis to the production or sending of objects for inspection.

2- If a third party fails to comply with an order to produce an object for inspection as defined in paragraph 1 of Article 152 (Order to Produce Document, etc.) of this Code, which applies mutatis mutandis under the provisions of paragraph 1 of this Article, without a justifiable reason, the court may, by a ruling, impose a fine of not more than 2,000,000 (two million) Riels.

3- An objection may be filed against the ruling defined in paragraph 2 above.

### SECTION 7
## PRESERVATION OF EVIDENCE

#### Article 163.- Preservation of Evidence

1- If the court finds that there are circumstances where it will be difficult to use evidence unless the examination of such evidence is conducted in advance, the court may, upon application, conduct the examination of such evidence in advance in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter.

2- The results of examination of evidence conducted in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 above shall be effective in a suit on the merits.

3- In the oral argument of the suit on the merits, the party shall make a statement regarding the results of the examination of evidence conducted in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 164.- Competent Court

1- An application for preservation of evidence made after a suit is filed shall be made to the court of the instance where that evidence is to be used.

2- An application for preservation of evidence made before a suit is filed shall be made to the first instance court that has jurisdiction over the residence of the person to be examined or the holder of the document, or at the place where the object to be inspected is located.

3- In case of urgency, even after a suit is filed, an application for preservation of evidence may be made to the first instance court defined in paragraph 2 above.

#### Article 165.- Method of Applying for Preservation of Evidence

1- An application for preservation of evidence shall be made in writing.

2- The application defined in paragraph 1 above shall state the following matters:

A- Name and address of the other party.

B- Facts to be proved.

C- Evidence to be preserved.

D- Reasons for preservation of evidence.

3- Preliminary evidence of reasons for preservation of evidence shall be provided.

#### Article 166.- Procedure When Other Party Cannot Be Identified

An application for preservation of evidence may be made even if the other party cannot be identified. In this case, the court may appoint a special representative for the person who is to become the other party.

#### Article 167.- Preservation of Evidence on Court's Own Motion

While a suit is pending, the court may, if it finds it necessary, issue a ruling for preservation of evidence on its own motion.

#### Article 168.- Appeal

An objection may be filed against a ruling dismissing an application for preservation of evidence.

#### Article 169.- Summons for Date

On the date of examination of evidence, the court shall summon the applicant and the other party. However, this provision shall not apply in case of urgent necessity.

#### Article 170.- Costs for Preservation of Evidence

Costs related to preservation of evidence shall be part of the court costs.

#### Article 171.- Re-examination at Oral Argument

If a party requests the examination of a witness who was examined in proceedings for preservation of evidence during the oral argument, the court shall examine that witness.

#### Article 172.- Creation of Record of Preservation of Evidence

In the case of examination of evidence for preservation, the court that conducted the examination shall send the record of that examination to the court where the record of the suit on the merits is kept.

### CHAPTER 4
## SUSPENSION AND TERMINATION OF SUIT PROCEEDINGS

#### Article 173.- Suspension and Succession of Suit Proceedings

1- Suit proceedings shall be suspended when any of the following grounds occurs:

A- Death of a party.

B- Dissolution of a legal person that is a party, by merger of legal persons.

C- Loss of capacity to sue by a party, or death or extinction of authority of a statutory representative.

D- Death or loss of qualification by other reasons of a person who has the qualification defined and sues in their own name on behalf of another.

E- Death or loss of qualification by other reasons of all selected parties under the provisions of Article 42 (Selected Parties) of this Code.

2- When any of the grounds defined in paragraph 1 above occurs, the persons who shall succeed to the suit proceedings are:

A- For death of a party, the heirs or other persons required by law or other norms to succeed to the suit.

B- For dissolution of a legal person that is a party by merger, the legal person that succeeds to the rights and obligations after the merger.

C- For loss of capacity to sue, death of a statutory representative, or extinction of authority, the new statutory representative or the party who has regained capacity to sue.

D- For death or loss of qualification of a person suing in their own name on behalf of another, a person who has the same qualification.

E- For death or loss of qualification of all selected parties under Article 42, the remaining selected parties or a newly selected party under Article 42.

3- The provisions of paragraph 1 above shall not apply to cases where there is an attorney for the suit. However, the attorney shall report the occurrence of any of the grounds defined in paragraph 1 above to the court.

4- Notwithstanding the occurrence of the ground defined in item A of paragraph 1 above, an heir may not succeed to the suit proceedings during the period in which they may waive the inheritance.

#### Article 174.- Automatic Termination of Suit Proceedings

If a party dies or a legal person is dissolved by merger and there is no successor to the rights or obligations that are the subject of the suit, or if such rights or obligations devolve upon a single person, the suit proceedings shall terminate. In this case, the court shall issue a judgment declaring the termination of the suit.

#### Article 175.- Procedure for Succession

1- An application for succession to suit proceedings may be made by the person who intends to succeed or by the other party.

2- If an application for succession to suit proceedings is made, the court shall notify the other party.

3- If an application for succession to suit proceedings is made, the court shall investigate on its own motion, and if it finds that the application is without justifiable grounds, it shall dismiss the application by a ruling. An objection may be filed against this ruling.

4- In the case defined in paragraph 3 above, if the court finds that the application is justifiable, it shall permit the succession to suit proceedings by a ruling.

5- If an application for succession to suit proceedings is made after a judgment has been rendered while proceedings were suspended, the court that rendered the judgment shall rule on the application.

#### Article 176.- Order for Succession on Court's Own Motion

Even if neither party applies for succession to suit proceedings, the court may, on its own motion, order the succession to suit proceedings by a ruling.

#### Article 177.- Stay of Suit Proceedings

1- If the court cannot perform its duties due to a natural disaster or other reasons, suit proceedings shall be stayed until such reasons cease to exist.

2- If a party cannot continue suit proceedings due to an obstacle of indefinite duration, the court may, by a ruling, order the stay of suit proceedings. In this case, once the obstacle ceases to exist, the court may revoke the ruling.

3- If a criminal case related to the facts forming the basis of a claim in a civil case is pending, the court may, by a ruling, order the stay of the civil suit proceedings. In this case, once the criminal case is concluded, the court may revoke the ruling.

#### Article 178.- Effect of Suspension or Stay of Suit Proceedings

1- While suit proceedings are suspended or stayed, the parties and the court may not
```

perform any suit activities. However, the court may render a judgment even if suit proceedings are suspended.

2- When suit proceedings are suspended or stayed, any period shall cease to run. In this case, such period shall start to run anew from the time when notification of succession to suit proceedings is given or the proceedings are resumed.

### CHAPTER 5
## DECISIONS

### SECTION 1
## GENERAL PROVISIONS ON DECISIONS

#### Article 179.- Types of Decisions

1- A judgment is a decision made by the court in a formal document of judgment session in accordance with the form defined by law, based on oral argument, except as otherwise provided in this Code or other laws, and which becomes effective upon pronouncement based on such document.

2- A ruling is a decision that the court or a judge may make without going through oral argument, and which is not a judgment.

### SECTION 2
## GENERAL PROVISIONS ON JUDGMENTS

#### Article 180.- Final Judgment

1- If the court finds that the trial should be concluded based on the results obtained from oral argument and examination of evidence, the court shall conclude the
```

oral argument and render a final judgment.

2- If the court finds that the trial should be concluded for one claim among multiple joined claims, the court may render a judgment on the merits for that single claim.

3- If the court finds it necessary for rendering a final judgment, it may order the resumption of oral argument that has been concluded.

#### Article 181.- Interlocutory Judgment

If there is a dispute regarding any of the facts defined in the following items, the court may conclude the trial for those facts alone and render an interlocutory judgment:

A- Existence of a claim in cases where there is a dispute over both the existence and the amount of the claim.

B- A means of objection or defense that can be adjudicated independently.

C- Existence or non-existence of requirements for the suit.

D- Facts relating to the termination of the suit.

#### Article 182.- Matters to be Adjudicated by Judgment

1- The court shall render a judgment on all claims requested by the parties.

2- The court may not render a judgment on any matters not requested by the parties.

3- The court shall rule on the obligation to bear court costs even if there is no application by a party.

#### Article 183.- Omission of Adjudication

If the court omits to adjudicate on a part of a claim, that part of the claim shall remain pending in that court.

#### Article 184.- Principle of Free Evaluation of Evidence

In rendering a judgment, the court shall decide whether or not the allegations of facts are true, by considering the results of the examination of evidence and the whole tenor of the oral argument in accordance with its free conviction.

#### Article 185.- Principle of Direct Trial

1- Only a judge who participated in the oral argument that forms the basis of the judgment may render the judgment.

2- If a judge is replaced before the conclusion of oral argument, the parties shall state the results of the oral argument conducted before the replacement.

3- If a single judge is replaced or if a majority of the judges in a collegiate court are replaced, the court shall examine or re-examine witnesses already examined if a party so requests.

### SECTION 3
## PRONOUNCEMENT OF JUDGMENT

#### Article 186.- Effectiveness of Judgment

A judgment shall become effective upon pronouncement.

#### Article 187.- Date of Pronouncement

Pronouncement of judgment shall be made within one month from the date of conclusion of oral argument. However, this shall not apply to cases that are particularly complex or have other special circumstances.

#### Article 188.- Method of Pronouncement

1- Pronouncement of a judgment shall be made on the date of pronouncement in an open court session by the presiding judge reading the formal part of the judgment based on the original document of judgment. However, if the presiding judge is unable to do so, another judge of the collegiate court may read it.

2- Pronouncement of a judgment may be made even if the parties are not present in the courtroom.

3- If the presiding judge finds it appropriate, they may read the reasons for the judgment or explain the contents of the reasons orally.

#### Article 189.- Document of Judgment

1- The document of judgment shall state the following matters:

A- The court.

B- The date of conclusion of oral argument.

C- Names and addresses of the parties, and names and addresses of statutory representatives.

D- Facts and points of dispute.

E- Reasons.

F- Formal part of the decision.

2- Regarding facts and points of dispute, the major points shall be stated based on the statements of the parties.

3- The judges who rendered the judgment shall sign and seal the document of judgment.

4- If any judge of a collegiate court is unable to sign and seal the document of judgment, the other judges shall sign and seal it and state the reasons for the inability.

#### Article 190.- Service of Document of Judgment

1- The document of judgment shall be served on the parties within two weeks from the date of pronouncement.

2- Service defined in paragraph 1 above shall be made by an authenticated copy of the judgment.

### SECTION 4
## EFFECT OF JUDGMENT

#### Article 191.- Binding Force of Judgment

Except as provided in Article 192 (Ruling for Correction) of this Code, the court that rendered a judgment may not revoke or modify it.

#### Article 192.- Ruling for Correction

1- If a judgment contains a calculation error, a clerical error, or any other similar obvious error, the court may, upon application or on its own motion, issue a ruling for correction of the judgment.

2- An objection may be filed against a ruling for correction of a judgment. However, this provision shall not apply if a lawful appeal has been filed against that judgment.

3- A ruling for correction shall be appended to the original document of judgment and authenticated copies thereof. However, if the court finds it appropriate, it may issue the ruling as a separate document instead of appending it, and serve a certified copy of the ruling on the parties.

#### Article 193.- Time of Finality of Judgment

1- A judgment shall not become final before the expiration of the period defined for
```

filing of a lawful appeal or a lawful objection to a higher court.

2- The finality of a judgment shall be obstructed by the filing of an appeal to a higher court or an objection within the period defined in paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 194.- Res Judicata of Matters Adjudicated by Judgment

1- When a judgment becomes final, the effect of res judicata shall occur.

2- Res judicata defined in paragraph 1 above shall definitively determine the legal relationship at the time of conclusion of oral argument.

3- The scope of res judicata defined in paragraph 1 above is limited to the matters adjudicated in the formal part of the decision regarding claims requested by a complaint or a counterclaim, and such effect shall not extend to the adjudications in the reasons for the judgment.

4- Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph 3 above, if a defendant claims a set-off, the adjudication in a final judgment that the claim used for the set-off does not exist shall have the effect of res judicata for the amount for which the set-off was claimed.

#### Article 195.- Time of Occurrence of Executory Effect

Except as otherwise provided by law, a judgment may be executed from the time when res judicata occurs as defined in Article 194 (Res Judicata of Matters Adjudicated by Judgment) of this Code.

#### Article 196.- Declaration of Provisional Execution

1- Regarding a judgment rendering a decision on a claim for property rights, if the court finds
```

that it is necessary, the court may declare that the judgment may be executed provisionally, with or without security, upon application or on its own motion.

2- The court may declare that a party may avoid provisional execution by providing security, upon application or on its own motion.

3- Declarations regarding provisional execution shall be stated in the formal part of the judgment. This provision shall apply mutatis mutandis to the declaration defined in paragraph 2 above.

4- If the court omits to rule on an application for a declaration of provisional execution, or if the court should have made such declaration on its own motion but failed to do so, the court shall issue a supplemental ruling upon application or on its own motion. This provision shall apply mutatis mutandis to cases where the court omits to rule on an application defined in paragraph 2 above.

#### Article 197.- Loss of Effect of Declaration of Provisional Execution and Restoration to Original State

1- A declaration of provisional execution shall lose its effect upon pronouncement of a judgment by a higher court modifying such declaration or the judgment on the merits, to the extent of such modification.

2- When modifying a judgment on the merits, the court shall, upon application by the defendant, order in its judgment that the plaintiff return the performance rendered by the defendant based on the declaration of provisional execution and compensate for any damage sustained by the defendant from the provisional execution or for avoiding it.

3- In the case where only the declaration of provisional execution is modified, the provisions of paragraph 2 above shall apply to
```

a judgment modifying the judgment on the merits at a later time.

#### Article 198.- Subjective Scope of Effect of Final Judgment

A final judgment shall be effective against the following persons:

A- The parties.

B- A third person in case where a party becomes a plaintiff or a defendant for such third person.

C- A successor to the rights or obligations of the persons defined in the two items above, after the suit is filed.

D- A person who possesses the object which is the subject of the claim, for the interest of any of the persons defined in the three items above.

#### Article 199.- Effect of Final Judgment of Foreign Court

A final judgment of a foreign court shall be effective only if all of the following requirements are met:

A- The jurisdiction of the foreign court is recognized by law or by a treaty or convention to which the Kingdom of Cambodia is a party.

B- The losing defendant received service of summons or orders necessary for the commencement of proceedings, or, though not receiving them, responded to the suit.

C- The contents of the judgment and the suit proceedings are not contrary to public order or good customs of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

D- There is a guarantee of reciprocity between the Kingdom of Cambodia and the foreign country.

### SECTION 5
## JUDGMENT BY DEFAULT

#### Article 200.- Judgment by Default Against Plaintiff
```

1- If the plaintiff fails to appear on the first date for the preparatory proceedings for oral argument, the court shall dismiss the plaintiff's claim by a judgment by default.

2- If the plaintiff fails to appear on a date for continuing the preparatory proceedings for oral argument, the court may immediately conclude the preparatory proceedings and designate the first date for oral argument.

3- If the plaintiff fails to appear on a date for oral argument, the court shall dismiss the plaintiff's claim by a judgment by default.

#### Article 201.- Judgment by Default Against Defendant

1- If the defendant fails to appear on the first date for preparatory proceedings for oral argument, the court shall immediately conclude the preparatory proceedings and designate the first date for oral argument.

2- If the defendant fails to appear on a date for oral argument, the court shall deem that the defendant has admitted the facts alleged by the plaintiff, and if it finds the plaintiff's claim to be well-grounded, it shall render a judgment by default in favor of the plaintiff; however, if it finds the claim to be without grounds, it shall dismiss the claim. This provision shall not apply if the defendant had disputed the plaintiff's allegations on previous dates for preparatory proceedings or for oral argument.

#### Article 202.- Cases Where Judgment by Default Cannot Be Rendered

The court may not render a judgment by default in any of the following cases:
```

A- The party who failed to appear did not receive service of a lawful summons.

B- The party who failed to appear has a justifiable reason that makes it impossible to appear, such as a natural disaster or other unavoidable circumstances.

C- The suit is unlawful.

D- The contents of the statement made by the plaintiff on the date of oral argument when the defendant failed to appear were not notified to the defendant before that date.

#### Article 203.- Adjournment of Date

1- If the court finds that the period for summons was too short, or if it finds that a party was unable to appear through no fault of their own, the court may adjourn the date for preparatory proceedings or for oral argument.

2- If the court adjourns the date under paragraph 1 above, it shall summon the party who failed to appear to the new date.

#### Article 204.- Objection

1- If a party failed to appear on the date in time due to an unforeseeable or unavoidable reason and has received a judgment by default, that party may file an objection against that judgment.

2- The objection defined in paragraph 1 above shall be made within two weeks from the date of service of the judgment by default. This period may not be extended.

3- If a judgment by default is served by public notice or by service in a foreign country, the court shall specify the period for filing an objection in that judgment by default.
```

#### Article 205.- Method of Filing Objection

1- An objection shall be filed by submitting a written objection to the court that rendered the judgment by default.

2- The written objection shall state the following matters:

A- Names and addresses of the parties, and names and addresses of statutory representatives.

B- Identification of the judgment by default against which the objection is filed.

C- The intent to file an objection against the judgment defined in item B above.

D- Reasons for failing to appear on the date in time.

#### Article 206.- Examination and Service of Written Objection

1- If the written objection does not comply with the provisions of Article 205 (Method of Filing Objection) of this Code, the court shall specify a reasonable period and order the correction of the defects within that period.

2- In the case defined in paragraph 1 above, if the party filing the objection fails to correct the defects, the court shall dismiss the objection by a ruling.

3- An objection may be filed against the ruling defined in paragraph 2 above.

4- If there are no defects in the written objection, the court shall serve it on the other party, designate a new date for adjudicating the objection, and summon the parties.

#### Article 207.- Trial and Decision on Objection

1- The court shall, on its own motion, investigate whether the objection was filed in accordance with the form and within the period defined by law, and whether
```

and whether the reasons for the objection are correct.

2- The court shall dismiss the objection by a ruling if it finds that the objection was not filed lawfully; however, if it finds that there is no justifiable reason for the objection, it shall dismiss the objection by a ruling.

#### Article 208.- Effect of Sustained Objection

1- If the objection is sustained, the suit proceedings shall be restored to their original state as of the time the failure to appear occurred.

2- In the case defined in paragraph 1 above, the court shall designate a new date for preparatory proceedings or for resuming oral argument, and shall notify the parties.

#### Article 209.- New Judgment

1- If a judgment rendered based on a new trial after the resumption of proceedings has the same result as the judgment by default, the court shall render a new judgment declaring that the judgment by default is upheld.

2- If a judgment rendered based on a new trial after the resumption of proceedings does not have the same result as the judgment by default, the court shall revoke the judgment by default in the new judgment.

#### Article 210.- Costs Incurred Due to Failure to Appear

If a judgment by default was rendered in accordance with the form defined by law, the costs incurred due to the failure to appear shall be borne by the party who failed to appear, even if the judgment is revoked based on an objection, except in cases where such costs were incurred due to the inappropriate litigation conduct of the other party.
```

#### Article 211.- Second Judgment by Default

1- If the party who filed an objection fails to appear on the first date after the resumption of proceedings, the court shall dismiss the objection by a second judgment by default, except in cases defined in Article 202 (Cases Where Judgment by Default Cannot Be Rendered) and paragraph 2 of Article 203 (Adjournment of Date) of this Code.

2- No objection may be filed against a second judgment by default defined in paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 212.- Mutatis Mutandis Provisions

1- Provisions regarding the withdrawal of an appeal shall apply mutatis mutandis to the withdrawal of an objection.

2- The provisions of this Section shall apply mutatis mutandis to a counterclaim.

### SECTION 6
## RULINGS

#### Article 213.- Notification of Ruling

1- A ruling shall become effective only if notified by an appropriate method.

2- If a ruling is made in writing, the judge who made the ruling shall sign and seal it.

3- When notifying a ruling, the court clerk shall clearly record the circumstances and method of notification in the case file.

#### Article 214.- Revocation of Rulings Relating to Direction of Proceedings

A ruling relating to the direction of proceedings may be revoked at any time.
```

#### Article 215.- Objection to Action of Court Clerk

Regarding an objection to an action of a court clerk, the court to which the clerk belongs shall decide by a ruling.

#### Article 216.- Mutatis Mutandis Application of Provisions on Judgment

Provisions regarding a judgment shall apply mutatis mutandis to a ruling, except where such provisions are inconsistent with the nature of a ruling.

# CHAPTER 6
## TERMINATION OF SUIT PROCEEDINGS OTHER THAN BY DECISION

#### Article 217.- Withdrawal of Complaint

1- A plaintiff may withdraw the whole or a part of a complaint until the judgment becomes final.

2- After the defendant has submitted a preparatory document on the merits, or made a statement on the merits in the preparatory proceedings for oral argument or in the oral argument, the withdrawal of the complaint shall not become effective unless the defendant consents thereto. However, this provision shall not apply to the withdrawal of a counterclaim in cases where the plaintiff has withdrawn the original complaint.

3- Withdrawal of a complaint shall be made in writing. However, on a date for preparatory proceedings for oral argument, on a date for oral argument, or on a date for conciliation, the withdrawal of a complaint may be made orally.

4- In the case defined in the first sentence of paragraph 2 above, if the withdrawal of a complaint is made in writing, the court shall serve the document on the defendant; and if the withdrawal of a complaint is made orally on a date for preparatory proceedings or for
```

oral argument or date for conciliation, the court shall serve a copy of the record of such date on the defendant. However, this provision shall not apply if the withdrawal of the complaint was made orally on a date for preparatory proceedings, date for oral argument, or date for conciliation, and the defendant was present on that date.

5- If the defendant does not file an objection within two weeks after receiving the service of the written withdrawal of the complaint, the defendant shall be deemed to have consented to the withdrawal. This provision shall also apply if the withdrawal was made orally on a date for preparatory proceedings, date for oral argument, or date for conciliation, where the defendant fails to file an objection within two weeks from the date of withdrawal if the defendant was present on that date, or within two weeks from the service of the copy of the record defined in paragraph 4 above if the defendant was not present on that date.

#### Article 218.- Effect of Withdrawal of Complaint

1- The part of the complaint that has been withdrawn shall be deemed to have never been filed.

2- A party who has withdrawn a complaint after a final judgment on the merits was rendered may not file the same suit again.

#### Article 219.- Constructive Withdrawal of Complaint

In the case where both parties fail to appear on a date for preparatory proceedings or for oral argument and do not apply for designation of a new date within one month, the complaint shall be deemed to have been withdrawn. This provision shall also apply to
```

cases where both parties fail to appear on a date for preparatory proceedings for oral argument or on a date for oral argument twice in succession.

#### Article 220.- Conciliation on Suit

1- Parties may conclude a conciliation on a date for preparatory proceedings for oral argument, on a date for oral argument, or on a date for conciliation.

2- Conciliation defined in paragraph 1 above may be conducted outside the courthouse if the court finds it appropriate.

#### Article 221.- Waiver or Acknowledgment of Claim

Waiver of a claim or acknowledgment of a claim shall be made on a date for preparatory proceedings for oral argument, on a date for oral argument, or on a date for conciliation.

#### Article 222.- Effect of Record of Settlement, etc.

If a settlement, waiver of a claim, or acknowledgment of a claim is recorded in the record, such record shall have the same effect as a final judgment.

# CHAPTER 7
## SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR SMALL CLAIMS

#### Article 223.- Purpose of Proceedings

The purpose of proceedings for small claims is to resolve disputes promptly through simplified procedures.
```

#### Article 224.- Requirements for Small Claims Suit, etc.

1- Regarding a suit seeking payment of money not exceeding 1,000,000 (one million) Riels, which is the value of the subject matter of the suit, the plaintiff may request trial and decision under small claims proceedings.

2- The statement requesting trial and decision under small claims proceedings shall be made at the time the complaint is filed.

#### Article 225.- Oral Filing of Complaint; Matters to be Clarified at Filing

1- A complaint for a small claim may be filed orally.

2- Regarding a complaint for a small claim, notwithstanding the provisions of item B, paragraph 2 of Article 75 (Method of Filing Complaint and Matters to be Stated in Complaint) of this Code, it shall be sufficient if the major points of the dispute are clarified.

3- If the plaintiff files a complaint orally, the court clerk shall record the plaintiff's statement in a record. In this case, such record shall be deemed to be the complaint.

#### Article 226.- Explanation of Proceedings

1- The court clerk shall deliver to the parties a document explaining the meaning of the trial and decision proceedings for small claims, at the time of summoning for the first date for oral argument in a small claims suit.

2- At the beginning of the date defined in paragraph 1 above, the court shall explain to the parties the following matters:

A- Except in special circumstances, the court shall conclude the trial on the first date for oral argument.
```

B- Regarding a final judgment in a small claims suit, the parties may not file an appeal, except for an objection to a judgment by default.

C- Examination of evidence may be conducted only for evidence that can be examined immediately.

D- The defendant may make a statement to transfer the suit to ordinary proceedings. However, this provision shall not apply if the defendant has already made statements on the merits on the first date for oral argument or after such date has concluded.

#### Article 227.- Prohibition of Counterclaim

In a small claims suit, No counterclaim may be filed.

#### Article 228.- Designation of Date for Oral Argument

1- When a complaint is filed, the court shall designate a date for oral argument promptly and summon the parties.

2- The date defined in paragraph 1 above shall be within thirty days from the date of filing the complaint, except in special circumstances.

#### Article 229.- Principle of Trial in a Single Day

1- In a small claims suit, the court shall conclude the trial on the first date for oral argument, except in special circumstances.

2- The parties shall produce all means of objection or defense before or on the date defined in paragraph 1 above. However, this provision shall not apply if the oral argument is continued.
```

#### Article 230.- Order for Appearance of Party Himself

Even if a mandate representative is appointed, the court may order the party herself or her statutory representative to appear.

#### Article 231.- Limitation on Examination of Evidence

Examination of evidence may be conducted only for evidence that can be examined immediately.

#### Article 232.- Application for Examination of Witness and Examination of Witness

1- A party does not need to submit a document defining the matters for examination when applying for examination of a witness.

2- The court may examine a witness without requiring an oath.

#### Article 233.- Transfer of Suit to Ordinary Proceedings by Defendant's Statement

1- The defendant may make a statement to transfer the suit to ordinary proceedings. However, this provision shall not apply if the defendant has already made statements on the merits on the first date for oral argument.

2- The statement defined in paragraph 1 above shall be made in writing, except when made on a date.

3- When a statement defined in paragraph 1 above is made, the court clerk shall immediately notify the plaintiff that the suit has been transferred to ordinary proceedings by that statement. However, this provision shall not apply if the statement was made on a date when the plaintiff was present.

#### Article 234.- Transfer of Suit to Ordinary Proceedings by Court Ruling

1- The court shall issue a ruling that the trial and decision on the suit shall be conducted under ordinary proceedings in the following cases:
```

A- Where the plaintiff requested trial and decision under small claims proceedings in violation of the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 224 (Requirements for Small Claims Suit, etc.).

B- Where the court is unable to summon the defendant to the first date for oral argument, unless such summons is made by service by public notice.

C- Where the court finds that the trial and decision under small claims proceedings are inappropriate.

2- When a ruling defined in paragraph 1 above is made, the court clerk shall immediately notify the parties thereof.

#### Article 235.- Time of Transfer of Suit to Ordinary Proceedings, etc.

1- When a statement defined in paragraph 1 of Article 233 (Transfer of Suit to Ordinary Proceedings by Defendant's Statement) or a ruling defined in paragraph 1 of Article 234 (Transfer of Suit to Ordinary Proceedings by Court Ruling) of this Code is made, the suit shall be transferred to ordinary proceedings.

2- When a suit is transferred to ordinary proceedings, the date already designated for the small claims suit shall be deemed to have been designated for the preparatory proceedings for oral argument of the ordinary proceedings.

#### Article 236.- Judgment and Pronouncement of Judgment

1- The court shall pronounce the judgment immediately after the conclusion of the oral argument, except where it finds it inappropriate.

2- In the case defined in paragraph 1 above, the pronouncement of judgment may be made without the original of the judgment. In this case, the court shall pronounce the judgment by stating the formal part of the decision and the gist of
```

and the reasons.

3- When pronouncing the judgment in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 above, the court shall have the court clerk record the matters defined in each of the following items in the record of the date for oral argument on which the judgment was pronounced, in lieu of preparing a judgment document:

A- Names and addresses of the parties, and names and addresses of statutory representatives.

B- The judgment sought by the plaintiff.

C- The formal part of the decision.

4- On the judgment document of a small claims suit or the record in lieu thereof, it shall be written that it is a judgment of a small claims suit.

#### Article 237.- Grace Period for Payment by Judgment

1- Where the court renders a judgment sustaining the plaintiff's claim, if it finds it especially necessary in consideration of the defendant's financial capacity and other circumstances, the court may determine item A, item B, or item C together with item A or item B, regarding the payment of the money sustained by the court, within a period not exceeding three years from the date of pronouncement of judgment:

A- Determination of a period for payment.

B- Determination of payment in installments.

C- Waiver of the obligation to pay damages for delay after the filing of the complaint, on the condition that payment is made in accordance with the period determined under item A, or for the benefit of the period determined under item B if the installments are paid without default under paragraph 2 below.

2- When determining payment in installments
```

established under item B of paragraph 1 above, the court shall determine the loss of the benefit of the period in case the defendant fails to pay.

#### Article 238.- Prohibition of Appeals

Regarding a final judgment in a small claims suit, no appeal may be filed. However, this provision shall not apply to an objection.

#### Article 239.- Non-applicable Provisions

The provisions from Article 103 (Purpose of Preparatory Proceedings for Oral Argument) to Article 112 (Objection to Record of Preparatory Proceedings, etc.) of this Code shall not apply to the small claims proceedings defined in this Chapter 7.

# CHAPTER 8
## DATES, PERIODS, AND SERVICE

### SECTION 1
## DATES AND PERIODS

#### Article 240.- Designation of Date

1- A date shall be designated by the court upon application or on its own motion. However, a date for proceedings to be conducted by a commissioned judge or an assigned judge shall be designated by such judge.

2- If it is unavoidable, a date may be designated on a Saturday, Sunday, or any other holiday defined by law or regulation.

#### Article 241.- Change of Date

1- If a party applies for a change of date, that party shall clarify the reasons why the change of date is necessary.
```

2- A change of date for preparatory proceedings for oral argument or for oral argument shall not be permitted, except in cases where unavoidable reasons exist.

3- A change of date for the first preparatory proceedings for oral argument may be permitted if the parties agree thereto.

#### Article 242.- Summons for Date

Summons for a date shall be made by service of a writ of summons or by notifying the date to a person who has appeared before the court in connection with the suit.

#### Article 243.- Calculation of Periods

1- Calculation of periods shall be made in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Code regarding periods.

2- In a decision designating a period, if the starting time is not specified, the period shall run from the time the decision becomes effective.

3- If the last day of a period falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or any other holiday defined by law or regulation, the period shall expire on the day following such holiday.

#### Article 244.- Extension of Periods

1- Only if it is unavoidable, the court may extend a period defined by law or a period already designated by the court. However, this provision shall not apply to cases where there are special provisions in the law.

2- Only if it is unavoidable, a commissioned judge or an assigned judge may extend a period already designated.
```

#### Article 245.- Subsequent Completion of Litigation Act

1- If a party was unable to comply with a period defined in the second sentence of paragraph 1 of Article 244 (Extension of Periods) above through no fault of their own, the party may complete the litigation act that should have been performed within that period, within one week after the reason for non-compliance has ceased to exist. However, for a party residing in a foreign country, such period shall be two months.

2- The period defined in paragraph 1 above may not be extended.

### SECTION 2
## SERVICE

#### Article 246.- Principle of Service by Court's Motion, etc.

1- Service shall be made on the court's own motion, except where there are special provisions in the law.

2- Affairs regarding service shall be performed by the court clerk.

3- Service shall be executed by a postal worker, a bailiff, or a court clerk.

#### Article 247.- Principle of Service by Delivery

1- Service shall be made by delivery of the document to be served to the person to whom service is to be made, except where there are special provisions in the law.

2- The documents to be served shall be copies of all relevant documents, except where there are special provisions in the law.

3- If the person receiving service is illiterate, the official in charge of service should explain the contents of the document at the time of delivery.

#### Article 248.- Service on Person Lacking Capacity to Sue, etc.

1- Service on a person lacking capacity to sue shall be made on her statutory representative.
```

2- If there are several persons who have the right to represent jointly, it shall be sufficient to make service on only one of them.

3- Service on a person in prison shall be made on the warden of the prison.

#### Article 249.- Place of Service

Service shall be made at the domicile, residence, place of business, or office of the person on whom service is to be made. However, service on a statutory representative may also be made at the place of business or office of the principal.

#### Article 250.- Notification of Place of Service, etc.

1- A party, statutory representative, or mandate representative may notify the court where the suit is pending of the place where she/he is to receive service within the Kingdom of Cambodia or of the person who is to receive service.

2- A party, statutory representative, or mandate representative may notify a change of the notified place where service is to be received or of the notified person who is to receive service.

3- The notification defined in paragraphs 1 and 2 above shall be made in writing.

4- Notwithstanding the provisions of Article 249 (Place of Service) above, if a notification of a place to receive service is made under paragraph 1 above, service shall be made at that place.

#### Article 251.- Service at Place of Encounter

Notwithstanding the provisions of Article 249 (Place of Service) above, if it is unclear whether the person on whom service is to be made has a domicile, residence, place of business, or office in the Kingdom of Cambodia, service may be made at the place where such person is encountered, except where service is to be made on a person who has notified
```

the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 250 (Notice of place for service, etc.) above.

These provisions shall apply mutatis mutandis to cases where a person who has a clearly defined domicile, residence, place of business, or office within the Kingdom of Cambodia, or a person who has given notice in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 250, refuses to receive the service.

#### Article 252.- Service of documents to the recipient and service by leaving the document

1- If the person to be served is not met at the place for service, the official in charge of service may deliver the document to a servant or other person who works there, or to a person who lives with the person to be served, who has reasonable judgment for receiving such document.

2- If the person to be served or the person who is to receive the document under the provisions of the preceding paragraph 1 refuses to receive the service without a justifiable reason, the document may be left at the place for service.

#### Article 253.- Service to be made in a foreign country

1- With respect to service to be made in a foreign country, the court shall entrust the ministry or institution with jurisdiction in that country, or the ambassador, charge d'affaires, or consul of the Kingdom of Cambodia residing in that country, to perform the service.

2- If service in a foreign country was made in accordance with the procedures to be followed by the commissioned judge or the judge who received the commission, such judge may entrust the service in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 254.- Report on service, etc.

1- The official in charge of service who has performed the service shall prepare a report on such service and submit it to the court.

2- The report defined in paragraph 1 above shall state the following points:

(a)- Identification of the case and the document served;

(b)- The person to receive the service;

(c)- The date and place of service;

(d)- The method of service;

(e)- The measures taken under paragraph 3 of Article 247 (Principle of service by delivery) of this Code in cases where the recipient is illiterate;

(f)- The signature or thumbprint of the person who received the document;

(g)- The circumstances of refusal to receive the service in cases where the person to be served or the person who is to receive the delivery of the document under the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 252 (Service of documents to the recipient and service by leaving the document) of this Code refuses to receive the service;

(h)- The circumstances in cases where the service was made in accordance with the provisions of Article 252 (Service of documents to the recipient and service by leaving the document) of this Code;

(i)- The signature of the person who performed the service.

3- In cases where service was attempted but could not be completed, the official in charge of service shall prepare a report on those circumstances and the points defined in paragraph 2 above from point (a) to point (d) and point (i), and submit it to the court.

#### Article 255.- Conditions for service by public notification

1- In the following cases, the court clerk may perform service by public notification upon motion and with the permission of the court:

(a)- Even after making efforts to search for the domicile or residence of the party or other places where service was to be made, the location remains unknown;

(b)- Service cannot be made in accordance with the provisions of Article 252 (Service of documents to the recipient and service by leaving the document) of this Code.

(c)- Service cannot be made in accordance with the provisions of Article 253 (Service to be made in a foreign country) of this Code, or it is recognized that service cannot be made in accordance with such provisions, in cases where service is to be made in a foreign country;

(d)- Service was entrusted to the ministry or institution with jurisdiction in the foreign country to be performed in accordance with the provisions of Article 253 (Service to be made in a foreign country) of this Code, but no document certifying the completion of such service has been received within 6 (six) months from the date of such entrustment.

2- In the cases defined in paragraph 1 above, if the court finds it necessary to avoid delay in the proceedings, the court may order the court clerk to perform service by public notification, even if no motion has been made.

3- In the cases defined in point (b) of paragraph 1 above, the court clerk should notify the party to be served of the fact that service by public notification has been performed, using a reasonable method.

4- Second and subsequent services by public notification to the same party shall be made by the court clerk's discretion. However, this provision shall not apply to the cases defined in point (c) of paragraph 1.

#### Article 256.- Method of service by public notification

1- Service by public notification shall be performed by posting a notice on the notice board of the court stating that the court clerk is keeping the document to be served and is ready to deliver it to the person to be served at any time. However, for service of a summons by public notification, the summons itself shall be posted on the notice board.

2- In cases where service by public notification is performed in accordance with the provisions of point (b) of paragraph 1 of Article 255 (Conditions for service by public notification) of this Code, the posting of the notice defined in paragraph 1 above may be done at

that the court recognizes as appropriate, in addition to the place defined in paragraph 1 above.

3- The court may publish a notice in the Royal Gazette or a newspaper that service by public notification has been performed. With respect to service to be made in a foreign country, the court clerk may give notification of the fact that service by public notification was performed, in lieu of publication in the Royal Gazette or a newspaper.

#### Article 257.- Time when service by public notification becomes effective

1- Service by public notification shall become effective upon the lapse of 2 (two) weeks from the date on which the posting was made in accordance with the provisions of Article 256 (Method of service by public notification) above. However, with respect to service by public notification defined in paragraph 4 of Article 255 (Conditions for service by public notification) of this Code, it shall become effective on the day following the date of posting.

2- With respect to service by public notification performed for service to be made in a foreign country, the period defined in paragraph 1 above shall be 6 (six) weeks.

### CHAPTER 9
### INSPECTION OF CASE RECORDS

#### Article 258.- Request for inspection of case records, etc.

1- A party to the lawsuit and a third party who has made a preliminary showing of interest in the lawsuit may request the court to inspect the case records, copy the case records, be delivered an authenticated copy, a certified copy, or an extract of the case records, or to be issued a certificate regarding matters relating to the lawsuit, upon payment of the fees. With respect to an authenticated copy, a certified copy

or an extract of the case records, the court clerk shall certify that the document is an authenticated copy, a certified copy, or an extract, and sign it.

2- The provisions of paragraph 1 above shall not apply to audio tapes, video tapes, or other objects on which information is recorded by similar methods in the case records. In such cases, if there is a request from a party to the lawsuit or a third party who has made a preliminary showing of interest in the lawsuit, the court shall permit the reproduction of those objects.

3- A request for inspection, copying, or reproduction of case records cannot be made if it would interfere with the preservation of the case records or the court's performance of its duties.

## BOOK 3
## APPEALS TO SUPERIOR COURTS

### CHAPTER 1
### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### Article 259.- Types of appeals to superior courts

1- The types of appeals to superior courts against judicial decisions that have not yet become final and binding are as follows:

(a)- An appeal (Uksor) against a judgment of a first instance court, or a final appeal (Satuk) in cases where there is an agreement between the parties as defined in point (a) of paragraph 1 of Article 260 (Judgments against which an appeal may be filed, etc.) of this Code;

(b)- A final appeal (Satuk) against a judgment of an appellate court;

(c)- A complaint (Chomtoah) against a ruling.

2- A complaint (Chomtoah) may be filed only where there is a provision in the law.

3- No further complaint may be filed against a ruling made on a complaint (Chomtoah).

### CHAPTER 2
### APPEALS (UKSOR)

#### Article 260.- Judgments against which an appeal may be filed, etc.

1- An appeal may be filed against a final judgment of a first instance court, except for the following cases:

(a)- Both parties have agreed not to file an appeal while reserving the right to file a final appeal after the final judgment is rendered;

(b)- The final judgment was rendered in a civil or commercial case where the value of the subject matter of the dispute does not exceed 5,000,000 (five million) Riels.

2- The agreement defined in point (a) of paragraph 1 above shall not be effective unless it is made in writing.

#### Article 261.- Restriction on appeal against a decision on court costs

No appeal may be filed independently against a decision on the burden of court costs.

#### Article 262.- Decisions subject to adjudication by the appellate court

Decisions made prior to the final judgment shall be subject to adjudication by the appellate court. However, this provision shall not apply to decisions against which no appeal (upaly) may be filed and decisions against which an appeal may be filed by a complaint (Chomtoah).

#### Article 263.- Waiver of right to appeal

1- A party may waive the right to appeal.

2- After an appeal has been filed, the statement of waiver defined in paragraph 1 above shall be made together with a withdrawal of the appeal.

#### Article 264.- Period for filing an appeal

1- An appeal shall be filed within 1 (one) month from the date on which service of the judgment was received, or the date on which notice was given of a ruling to dismiss or reject an objection to a default judgment. However, this shall not prevent an appeal from being effective if filed before such period begins.

2- The period defined in paragraph 1 above may not be extended.

#### Article 265.- Procedure for filing an appeal

1- To file an appeal, a party shall file a petition for appeal with the court of first instance. In this case, the court of first instance shall promptly forward the petition for appeal and the case records to the appellate court.

2- The petition for appeal shall state the following points:

(a)- The names or titles and addresses of the parties, and the name and address of any legal representative;

(b)- Identification of the judgment of first instance and the fact that an appeal is being filed against such judgment.

3- In cases where the grounds for requesting the reversal or modification of the judgment of first instance are not clearly stated in the petition for appeal, the appellant should submit a document in writing stating such grounds to the appellate court within 30 (thirty) days from the date on which the petition for appeal was filed.

#### Article 266.- Right of the appellate court to examine the petition for appeal

1- In cases where the petition for appeal is in violation of the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 265 (Procedure for filing an appeal) of this Code, the appellate court shall fix a reasonable period and order the appellant to correct the defect within that period. This provision shall also apply to cases where the fee for filing the petition for appeal has not been paid in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 4 of Article 61 (Taxes for filing a motion) of this Code.

2- In the cases defined in paragraph 1 above, if the appellant fails to correct the defect, the appellate court shall reject the petition for appeal by a ruling.

3- A complaint (Chomtoah) may be filed against the ruling defined in paragraph 2 above.

#### Article 267.- Service of the petition for appeal

1- The petition for appeal shall be served on the appellee.

2- The provisions of Article 266 (Right of the appellate court to examine the petition for appeal) above shall also apply to cases where the petition for appeal cannot be served, including cases where the necessary costs for serving the petition for appeal have not been paid in advance.

#### Article 268.- Dismissal of an appeal without oral argument

In cases where an appeal is unlawful and the defect cannot be corrected, the appellate court may dismiss the appeal by a judgment without oral argument.

#### Article 269.- Withdrawal of an appeal

1- An appeal may be withdrawn until the final judgment of the appellate court is pronounced.

2- The withdrawal of an appeal shall be made in writing. However, at the date of the preparatory proceedings for oral argument, the date of oral argument, or the date of settlement, the withdrawal of the appeal may be made orally.

3- The part of the appeal that has been withdrawn shall be deemed not to have been pending from the beginning of the appellate proceedings.

4- The provisions of Article 219 (Constructive withdrawal of a petition) of this Code shall also apply to the constructive withdrawal of an appeal.

#### Article 270.- Incidental appeal

1- The appellee, even if the right to appeal has expired, may

file an incidental appeal until the oral argument is concluded.

2- An incidental appeal shall lose its effect in cases where the appeal is withdrawn or in cases where the appeal is dismissed as being unlawful. However, an incidental appeal that satisfies the requirements for an appeal shall be deemed to be an independent appeal.

3- The provisions regarding appeals shall also apply to incidental appeals.

#### Article 271.- Declaration of provisional execution by the appellate court

1- The appellate court may, upon motion, declare provisional execution by a ruling, limited to those parts [of the judgment] for which no petition for appeal (upaly) has been filed against the judgment of first instance.

2- No appeal (upaly) may be filed against a judicial decision of the appellate court regarding provisional execution.

#### Article 272.- Scope of oral argument, etc.

1- Oral argument shall be conducted only within the scope of the request for modification of the judgment of first instance.

2- The parties shall make statements regarding the results of the oral argument in the first instance proceedings.

#### Article 273.- Mutatis mutandis application of provisions regarding first instance proceedings

Except where there are special provisions, the provisions of Book 2 (First Instance Proceedings) of this Code shall apply mutatis mutandis to appellate proceedings. However, this shall not apply to Article 80 (Fixing the date for the first preparatory proceedings for oral argument), Article 104

(Attempt at settlement during preparatory proceedings for oral argument), Section 5 (Default judgments) of Chapter 4 of Book 2, and Chapter 7 (Special provisions regarding small claims cases) of Book 2 of this Code.

#### Article 274.- Effectiveness of procedural acts in the first instance, etc.

1- Procedural acts performed in the first instance proceedings shall remain effective in the appellate proceedings.

2- Preparatory proceedings for oral argument conducted in the first instance proceedings shall remain effective in the appellate proceedings.

#### Article 275.- Restriction on assertion of lack of jurisdiction of the first instance court

In appellate proceedings, a party may not assert lack of jurisdiction of the court of first instance. However, this shall not apply to exclusive jurisdiction.

#### Article 276.- Filing of a counterclaim

1- A counterclaim may be filed in appellate proceedings only with the consent of the other party.

2- If the other party, without filing an objection, presents oral argument on the merits of the counterclaim, such party shall be deemed to have consented to the filing of the counterclaim.

#### Article 277.- Dismissal of an appeal

1- The appellate court shall dismiss the appeal if it finds that the judgment of first instance is correct.

2- Even in cases where the grounds for the judgment of first instance are incorrect, the appellate court shall dismiss the appeal if it finds that the conclusion of the judgment is correct based on other grounds.

#### Article 278.- Extent of modification of the judgment of first instance

Modification of the judgment of first instance may be made only to the extent of the appeal or incidental appeal.

#### Article 279.- Reversal of the judgment of first instance

1- The appellate court shall reverse the judgment of first instance in the following cases:

(a)- If it finds that the judgment of first instance is incorrect;

(b)- If the proceedings in the court of first instance were in serious violation of the law.

2- In the cases defined in paragraph 1 above, the appellate court shall adjudicate the case, except for the cases defined in Article 280 (Remand) or Article 281 (Transfer for reason of lack of exclusive jurisdiction of the court of first instance) of this Code.

#### Article 280.- Remand

1- In cases where the appellate court reverses a judgment of first instance that dismissed a lawsuit on the ground that the lawsuit was unlawful, the appellate court shall remand the case to the court of first instance. However, this shall not apply to cases where further oral argument regarding the case is not necessary.

2- In cases other than those defined in the first sentence of paragraph 1 above, if the appellate court reverses the judgment of first instance and finds it necessary to conduct further oral argument on the case, the appellate court may remand the case to the court of first instance.

3- If a case is remanded on the ground that the proceedings in the court of first instance were in violation of the law, such proceedings shall be deemed to have been reversed by the remand.

#### Article 281.- Transfer for reason of lack of exclusive jurisdiction of the court of first instance

If the appellate court reverses the judgment of first instance on the ground that the case was not within the exclusive jurisdiction of the court of first instance, the appellate court shall transfer the case to the court with jurisdiction by a judgment.

#### Article 282.- Declaration of provisional execution in a judgment of the appellate court

With respect to a judgment for a claim for payment of money, the appellate court shall, upon motion, declare that the judgment may be provisionally executed without the provision of security, except where the court finds such declaration unnecessary. However, if the appellate court finds it appropriate, the court may make the provisional execution conditional upon the provision of security.

### CHAPTER 3
### FINAL APPEAL (SATUK)

#### Article 283.- Court of final appeal

1- A final appeal may be filed with the Supreme Court against a final judgment rendered in the second instance by an appellate court. This provision shall also apply to a final judgment rendered in the first instance by an appellate court in accordance with the provisions of special laws.

2- With respect to a final judgment rendered in the first instance by a court of first instance, in cases where the parties have agreed not to file an appeal while reserving the right to file a final appeal after the judgment is rendered, a final appeal may be filed directly with the Supreme Court.

#### Article 284.- General grounds for final appeal

A final appeal may be filed on the ground of a violation of the Constitution, laws, or regulations that affects the judgment.

#### Article 285.- Absolute grounds for final appeal

1- In any of the following cases, a ground for final appeal shall be deemed to exist:

(a)- The court that rendered the judgment was not constituted in accordance with the law;

(b)- A judge who was not permitted by law to participate in rendering the judgment participated therein;

(c)- Violation of the provisions regarding exclusive jurisdiction;

(d)- Lack of authority of a legal representative, representative by mandate, or necessary authorization for a representative to perform procedural acts;

(e)- Violation of the provisions regarding the public nature of oral argument;

(f)- The judgment does not state its grounds, or the grounds stated are contradictory.

2- The provisions of point (d) of paragraph 1 above shall not apply in cases where there has been subsequent ratification in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 35 (Measures in cases of lack of capacity to perform procedural acts, etc.) or paragraph 3 of Article 57 (Measures in cases of lack of authority of representation) of this Code.

#### Article 286.- Mutatis mutandis application of provisions regarding appeals

The provisions of Chapter 2 of Book 3 above shall apply mutatis mutandis to final appeals and final appeal proceedings, except where there are special provisions.

#### Article 287.- Procedure for filing a final appeal

To file a final appeal, a party shall file a petition for final appeal with the court of first instance. In this case, the court of first instance shall promptly forward the petition for final appeal and the case records to the court of final appeal.

#### Article 288.- Service of notice of receipt of petition for final appeal, etc.

1- In cases where a petition for final appeal has been filed, the court of final appeal shall serve a notice of receipt of the petition for final appeal on the parties, except where the petition for final appeal is dismissed.

2- When the court of final appeal serves a notice of receipt of the petition for final appeal on the appellee in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 above, the court of final appeal shall also serve the petition for final appeal together with such notice.

#### Article 289.- Statement of grounds for final appeal

1- If the grounds for final appeal are not stated in the petition for final appeal, the appellant shall submit a statement of grounds for final appeal to the court of final appeal within 30 (thirty) days after receiving service of the notice of receipt of the petition for final appeal.

2- In cases where a final appeal is filed on the grounds defined in Article 284 (General grounds for final appeal) above, the appellant shall state the grounds for final appeal by specifying the provisions of the Constitution, laws, or regulations and the reasons for the violation thereof. In this case, if the reasons concern procedural rules, the facts constituting the violation shall be clearly stated.

3- In cases where a final appeal is filed on any of the grounds defined in paragraph 1 of Article 285 (Absolute grounds for final appeal) of this Code, the appellant shall state the grounds for final appeal by specifying...

the points defined in paragraph 1 of Article 285 and the facts constituting such points.

#### Article 290.- Dismissal of a petition for final appeal

In any of the following cases, the court of final appeal shall dismiss the petition for final appeal by a ruling:

(a)- If the petition for final appeal is unlawful and the defect cannot be corrected;

(b)- If a statement of grounds for final appeal has not been submitted in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 289 (Statement of grounds for final appeal) above, or if the statement of grounds for final appeal is in violation of the provisions of paragraph 2 or 3 of Article 289.

#### Article 291.- Order to correct

1- If the statement of all grounds for final appeal in the petition for final appeal or in the statement of grounds for final appeal submitted within the period defined in paragraph 1 of Article 289 (Statement of grounds for final appeal) of this Code clearly violates the provisions of paragraph 2 or 3 of Article 289, the court of final appeal shall fix a reasonable period and order the appellant to correct the defect within that period by a ruling.

2- The ruling to dismiss the petition for final appeal defined in point (b) of Article 290 (Dismissal of a petition for final appeal) above shall be made in cases where the appellant fails to correct the defect within the period defined in the order made in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 292.- Service of a copy of the statement of grounds for final appeal

If the court of final appeal does not issue a ruling to dismiss the petition for final appeal in accordance with the provisions of Article 290 (Dismissal of a petition for final appeal) of this Code...

the petition for final appeal shall be served with a copy of the statement of grounds for final appeal on the appellee. However, this provision shall not apply in cases where the court of final appeal conducts proceedings and reaches a decision without oral argument and considers it unnecessary to serve a copy of the statement of grounds for final appeal.

#### Article 293.- Order to submit preparatory documents

The court of final appeal may fix a reasonable period and order the appellee to submit the first preparatory documents.

#### Article 294.- Dismissal of a petition for final appeal without oral argument

The court of final appeal may dismiss the petition for final appeal by a judgment without oral argument if it considers that the petition for final appeal is groundless after examining the petition for final appeal, the statement of grounds for final appeal, the first preparatory documents submitted by the appellee, or any other documents.

#### Article 295.- Scope of investigation

The court of final appeal shall conduct an investigation only to the extent of the final appeal based on the grounds for final appeal.

#### Article 296.- Binding effect of fact-finding in the original judgment or the judgment of first instance

1- Facts that have been legally finalized in the original judgment or in the judgment of first instance shall be binding on the court of final appeal.

2- In cases where a petition for final appeal is filed in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 283 (Court of final appeal) of this Code, the court of final appeal may not reject the judgment of first instance on the ground that the fact-finding is finalized

in that judgment of first instance is contrary to the Constitution or law or regulations.

#### Article 297.- Cases of exception for application to points investigated ex officio

The provisions of Article 295 (Scope of investigation) and Article 296 (Binding effect of fact-finding in the original judgment or the judgment of first instance) above shall not apply to points that the court must investigate ex officio.

#### Article 298.- Declaration of provisional execution

The court of final appeal may, upon motion, declare provisional execution by a ruling only for the part for which no final appeal has been filed in the original judgment or the judgment of first instance.

#### Article 299.- Reversal and remand to the original court, etc.

1- In cases where there are grounds defined in Article 284 (General grounds for final appeal) or Article 285 (Absolute grounds for final appeal) of this Code, the court of final appeal shall reverse the original judgment or the judgment of first instance and remand the case to the original court or transfer the case to another court of the same rank, except for cases defined in Article 300 (Reversal and decision by the court of final appeal itself) of this Code.

2- The court that has received the case by remand or transfer in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 above shall make a decision based on new oral argument. In this case, the decision on facts and law that the court of final appeal took as the grounds for reversal shall be binding on the court that received the remanded or transferred case.

3- A judge who was involved in the original judgment or the judgment of first instance may not participate in the decision defined in paragraph 2 above.

#### Article 300.- Reversal and decision by the court of final appeal itself

The court of final appeal shall reach a decision on the case by itself in the following cases:

(a)- Where the court of final appeal reverses the original judgment or the judgment of first instance on the ground that the court of original instance made an error in the application of the Constitution or law or regulations with respect to the facts that have been finalized, and the court of final appeal is able to make a decision on the case based on such facts.

(b)- Where the court of final appeal reverses the original judgment or the judgment of first instance on the ground that the case is not within the jurisdiction of the court.

### Chapter 5
### COMPLAINT AGAINST A RULING

#### Article 301.- Method of filing a complaint against a ruling

1- In order to file a complaint against a ruling, a party shall submit a petition for complaint against a ruling to the original court. In this case, the original court shall promptly send the petition for complaint against a ruling and the case record to the court of complaint against a ruling.

2- If the grounds for demanding the annulment or modification of the original ruling are not clearly stated in the petition for complaint against a ruling, the person filing the complaint should submit a written statement describing such grounds to the court of complaint against a ruling within 2 (two) weeks from the date the petition for complaint against a ruling was submitted.

#### Article 302.- Recourse against a ruling of a commissioned judge or an assigned judge

1- A party who does not agree with a ruling of a commissioned judge or an assigned judge may file an objection with the court that received the case...

only in cases where the law permits a complaint against a ruling, if such ruling is a ruling of the court with which the case is pending. However, in cases where the court with which the case is pending is the Supreme Court or the Appeal Court, a party may file an objection only if the law would permit a complaint against a ruling if the ruling had been a ruling of a court of first instance.

2- A complaint against a ruling may be filed against a ruling on the motion mentioned in paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 303.- Period for filing a complaint against a ruling

1- A complaint against a ruling shall be filed within 1 (one) week from the date on which notice of the ruling was received.

2- The period mentioned in paragraph 1 above may not be extended.

#### Article 304.- Mutatis mutandis application of provisions on appeals or final appeals

1- The provisions regarding appeals and the procedures for trial of an appeal shall apply mutatis mutandis to a complaint against a ruling made by a court of first instance and the procedure of the court of complaint against a ruling, except where such provisions are inconsistent with the nature of the complaint or the procedure.

2- The provisions regarding final appeals and the procedures for trial of a final appeal shall apply mutatis mutandis to a complaint against a ruling made by an Appeal Court as a court of first instance and the procedure related to such complaint against a ruling, except where such provisions are inconsistent with the nature of the complaint or the procedure.

#### Article 305.- Stay of effect of original ruling

1- A complaint against a ruling shall stay the effect of the original ruling.

2- The court of complaint against a ruling or the court that issued the original ruling may order a temporary stay of compulsory execution based on the original ruling

or order any other necessary disposition until a ruling is made on the complaint against the ruling.

#### Article 306.- Optional oral argument and examination of the person filing a complaint against a ruling

1- A decision on a complaint against a ruling may be made without oral argument.

2- In cases where the court of complaint against a ruling does not conduct oral argument, the court may examine the person who filed the complaint against a ruling or any other person interested in the complaint.

## BOOK 4
## RETRIAL

### SINGLE CHAPTER
### RETRIAL

#### Article 307.- Grounds for retrial (One)

1- A party may file a petition for retrial against a final judgment or decree that has become finalized, in any of the following cases:

(a)- If the court that issued the judgment or decree was not organized in accordance with the law;

(b)- If a judge who was not permitted by law to participate in issuing a judgment or decree participated in issuing such judgment or decree;

(c)- If there was a lack of legal representation required by law, or if a representative lacked the necessary authorization for performing a procedural act, or if there was a lack of representative authority by mandate;

(d)- If a judge who participated in issuing the judgment or decree committed a crime related to his or her duties with respect to the case;

(e)- If a party confessed or was prevented from producing evidence or a defense that would have influenced the judgment or decree, due to a crime committed by another person;

(f)- If a document or any other object that became evidence for the judgment or decree was forged or altered;

(g)- If a false statement of a witness, expert, interpreter, or a party or representative who was sworn in, became evidence for the judgment or decree.

(g)- If a false statement of a witness, expert, interpreter, or a party or representative who was sworn in, became evidence for the judgment or decree.

(h)- If a judgment or order in a civil or criminal case, or an administrative disposition, on which the judgment or decree was based, has been changed by a subsequent judgment, order or administrative disposition;

(i)- If there was an omission of judgment on material facts that would have influenced the judgment or decree;

(j)- If the judgment or decree against which a petition for retrial is filed conflicts with a previous judgment or decree that has become finalized.

However, this provision shall not apply if the party asserted such grounds in an appeal to a higher court, or if the party knew of such grounds but failed to assert them.

2- With respect to the grounds defined in points (d) through (g) of paragraph 1 above, a petition for retrial may be filed only in the following cases:

(a)- Where a judgment of conviction or a decision to impose a fine has become finalized with respect to the act to be punished;

(b)- Where a finalized judgment of conviction or a finalized decision to impose a fine cannot be obtained for reasons other than the absence of evidence.

3- If a judgment on the merits of a case has been made in an appeal instance, a petition for retrial may not be filed against the judgment of the court of first instance.

#### Article 308.- Grounds for retrial (Two)

In cases where any of the grounds defined in paragraph 1 of Article 307 (Grounds for retrial (One)) above exist with respect to a decision that was the basis of the judgment or decree, even if there are means of recourse

by themselves, a party may use such grounds as grounds for a petition for retrial against the judgment or decree.

#### Article 309.- Court with jurisdiction over a petition for retrial

1- A petition for retrial shall be under the exclusive jurisdiction of the court that issued the judgment or decree against which the petition for retrial is filed.

2- In cases where a petition for retrial is filed against multiple judgments or decrees regarding the same case issued by courts of different ranks, the higher court shall have jurisdiction over all such judgments or decrees.

3- A judge who participated in issuing the judgment or decree against which the petition for retrial is filed may not participate in the trial and decision of such petition for retrial.

#### Article 310.- Procedure for a petition for retrial

The provisions regarding the procedure for appeals at each instance shall apply mutatis mutandis to the procedure for a petition for retrial, except where such provisions are inconsistent with the nature of the petition for retrial.

#### Article 311.- Period for a petition for retrial

1- A petition for retrial shall be filed within 30 (thirty) days from the date on which the party became aware of the grounds for retrial after the judgment or decree has become finalized. However, this provision shall not apply to cases where the petition for retrial is based on the grounds defined in point (c) or (j) of paragraph 1 of Article 307 (Grounds for retrial (One)) of this Code.

2- The period defined in paragraph 1 above may not be extended

be extended.

3- A party may not file a petition for retrial if 5 (five) years have passed from the date the judgment or decree became finalized, or from the date the grounds for retrial arose if such grounds arose after the judgment or decree became finalized. However, this provision shall not apply to cases where the petition for retrial is based on the grounds defined in point (c) or (j) of paragraph 1 of Article 307 (Grounds for retrial (One)) of this Code.

#### Article 312.- Points to be stated in a petition for retrial

1- The following points shall be stated in a petition for retrial:

(a)- The name or trade name and address of the party, and the name and address of the legal representative;

(b)- An indication of the judgment or decree against which the petition for retrial is filed, and a demand for a retrial against such judgment or decree;

(c)- The facts constituting the grounds for retrial.

2- A copy of the judgment or decree against which the petition for retrial is filed shall be attached to the petition for retrial.

#### Article 313.- Amendment of grounds for retrial

A party who has filed a petition for retrial may amend the grounds for retrial.

#### Article 314.- Dismissal of a petition for retrial and rejection of a petition for retrial

1- In cases where a petition for retrial is unlawful, the court...

the petition for retrial shall be dismissed by a ruling.

2- If there are no grounds for retrial, the court shall reject the petition for retrial by a ruling.

3- Once the ruling defined in paragraph 2 above has become finalized, a party may not file another petition for retrial based on the same grounds.

4- A complaint against a ruling may be filed against the rulings defined in paragraphs 1 and 2 above.

#### Article 315.- Ruling to commence a retrial

1- If there are grounds for retrial, the court shall issue a ruling to commence the retrial.

2- The court shall examine the other party before issuing a ruling as defined in paragraph 1 above.

3- A complaint against a ruling may be filed against the ruling defined in paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 316.- Review and decision on the merits

1- The court shall review and decide on the merits of the case within the scope of the recourse, once the ruling to commence the retrial has become finalized.

2- In cases defined in paragraph 1 above, if the court considers the original judgment or decree to be correct, it shall reject the petition for retrial.

3- Except in cases defined in paragraph 2 above, the court shall annul the original judgment or decree and make a new decision.

#### Article 317.- Petition for retrial against a ruling

1- With respect to a ruling against which a complaint against a ruling can be filed and which has become

finalized, a petition for retrial may be filed.

2- The provisions from Article 307 (Grounds for retrial (One)) to Article 316 (Review and decision on the merits) above shall apply mutatis mutandis to the petition defined in paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 318.- Petition for retrial by a third party

1- If a plaintiff and a defendant obtained a judgment or decree by conspiracy with the intent to infringe upon the rights or interests of a third party, such third party may seek recourse by filing a petition for retrial against the finalized judgment or decree.

2- In a petition for retrial as defined in paragraph 1 above, the plaintiff and the defendant shall be joint defendants.

3- The provisions of Article 41 (Provisions on trial of mandatory joinder) of this Code shall apply mutatis mutandis to the joint defendants as defined in paragraph 2 above and the third party who filed the petition for retrial as defined in paragraph 1 above.

## BOOK 5
## DEMAND PROCEDURE

### SINGLE CHAPTER
### DEMAND PROCEDURE

#### Article 319.- Conditions for a demand ruling

With respect to a claim for payment of a sum of money, the court may, upon motion of the creditor, issue a demand ruling. However, this may be done only if service of such ruling can be made in the Kingdom of Cambodia by methods other than service by public notice.

#### Article 320.- Motion for issuance of a demand ruling

1- A motion for the issuance of a demand ruling against a debtor as defined in the following points shall be filed with the court of first instance having jurisdiction over the area defined in each of the following points:

(a)- Natural person: his or her residence, or if he or she has no residence or the residence is unknown in the Kingdom of Cambodia, his or her place of stay.

(b)- Domestic legal person: its principal office or principal place of business, or if it has no office or place of business, the residence of its representative or the person in charge of its affairs.

(c)- Foreign legal person: its principal office or principal place of business in the Kingdom of Cambodia, or if it has no office or place of business in the Kingdom of Cambodia, the residence of its representative in the Kingdom of Cambodia or the person in charge of its affairs in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

2- A motion for the issuance of a demand ruling regarding a claim as defined in

the following points may also be filed with the court of first instance having jurisdiction over the area defined in each of the following points:

(a)- For a claim against a person having an office or a place of business, where such claim relates to the affairs of such office or place of business: the place where such office or place of business is located.

(b)- For a claim for payment of money on a bill of exchange or a check, and any supplementary claim related thereto: the place of payment of such bill of exchange or check.

2- The jurisdiction defined in paragraph 1 or 2 above is exclusive jurisdiction.

#### Article 321.- Mutatis mutandis application of provisions on petitions

The provisions regarding a petition shall apply mutatis mutandis to a motion for a demand ruling, unless such provisions are inconsistent with the nature of such motion.

#### Article 322.- Dismissal of a motion

In cases where a motion for a demand ruling is inconsistent with the provisions of Article 319 (Conditions for a demand ruling) or Article 320 (Motion for issuance of a demand ruling) above, or where it is clear from the content of the motion that the claim is groundless, the court shall dismiss such motion by a ruling. This provision shall also apply to a part of a claim for which a demand ruling cannot be issued.

#### Article 323.- Issuance of a demand ruling, etc.

1- A demand ruling shall be issued without examining the debtor.

2- The debtor may file an objection to the demand ruling with the court that issued such ruling.

#### Article 324.- Points to be stated in a demand ruling

1- A demand ruling shall state the following points:

(a)- The name or trade name and address of the party, and the name and address of the legal representative;

(b)- The demand for which the creditor requested the issuance of a ruling and the facts necessary to justify such demand;

(c)- An order to pay a specific sum of money.

2- The demand ruling defined in paragraph 1 above shall also state that if the debtor does not file an objection to the demand within 2 (two) weeks from the date of service of such ruling, the court shall ex officio declare provisional execution.

#### Article 325.- Service of a demand ruling

1- The court shall notify the creditor of the demand ruling and shall also serve such ruling on the debtor.

2- A demand ruling shall take effect upon service on the debtor.

3- If service of a demand ruling cannot be made because the debtor's residence, place of stay, office, or place of business, or the residence of the person in charge of its affairs as reported by the creditor, is unknown, the court shall notify the creditor of such fact. In this case, if the creditor fails to report the place where service is to be made, other than the place previously reported, within 2 (two) months from the date the creditor received the notice, the creditor shall be deemed to have withdrawn the motion for a demand ruling.

4- The period defined in the second sentence of paragraph 3 above may not be extended.

#### Article 326.- Dismissal of an objection to a demand ruling filed before the declaration of provisional execution

1- If the court considers an objection to a demand ruling filed before the declaration of provisional execution to be unlawful, the court shall dismiss such objection by a ruling.

2- A complaint against a ruling may be filed against the ruling defined in paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 327.- Effect of an objection to a demand ruling filed before the declaration of provisional execution

1- If a lawful objection to a demand ruling is filed before a declaration of provisional execution, the demand ruling shall lose its effect within the scope of the objection.

2- In the cases defined in paragraph 1 above, with respect to the claim relating to the objection to the demand ruling, a lawsuit shall be deemed to have been filed with the court defined in Article 320 (Motion for issuance of a demand ruling) of this Code at the time the motion for the issuance of the demand ruling was filed. In this case, the costs of the demand procedure shall be part of the court costs.

#### Article 328.- Declaration of provisional execution

1- If the debtor fails to file an objection to the demand ruling before the declaration of provisional execution within 2 (two) weeks from the date of service of the demand ruling, or if the ruling dismissing the objection to the demand ruling before the declaration of provisional execution

has become finalized, the court shall ex officio declare provisional execution, including the costs of the demand procedure in the demand ruling.

2- The declaration of provisional execution shall be stated in the demand ruling and served on the parties.

3- The provisions of Article 157 (Loss of effect of declaration of provisional execution and restoration of status quo ante) and paragraph 2 of Article 325 (Service of a demand ruling) of this Code shall apply mutatis mutandis to the declaration of provisional execution defined in paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 329.- Objection to a demand ruling filed after the declaration of provisional execution

1- An objection to a demand ruling under paragraph 2 of Article 323 (Issuance of a demand ruling, etc.) of this Code against a demand ruling accompanied by a declaration of provisional execution shall be filed within 2 (two) weeks from the date of service of such demand ruling.

2- The period defined in paragraph 1 above may not be extended.

#### Article 330.- Dismissal of an objection to a demand ruling filed after the declaration of provisional execution

1- If the court considers an objection to a demand ruling filed after the declaration of provisional execution to be unlawful, the court shall dismiss such objection by a ruling.

2- A complaint against a ruling may be filed against the ruling defined in paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 331.- Transition from demand procedure to lawsuit upon objection to a demand ruling filed after declaration of provisional execution

If a lawful objection to a demand ruling is filed after the declaration of provisional execution, the claim relating to such objection shall be deemed to have been filed as a lawsuit with the court that issued the demand ruling at the time the motion for issuance of the demand ruling was filed. In this case, the costs of the demand procedure shall be part of the court costs.

#### Article 332.- Judgment in a lawsuit transferred after declaration of provisional execution

1- If the judgment in a lawsuit transferred under Article 331 (Transition from demand procedure to lawsuit upon objection to a demand ruling filed after declaration of provisional execution) above is consistent with the demand ruling, the court shall recognize the demand ruling accompanied by the declaration of provisional execution in its judgment. However, this provision shall not apply to cases where the demand procedure was conducted unlawfully.

2- In the judgment of a lawsuit transferred under Article 331 of this Code, the court shall annul the demand ruling accompanied by the declaration of provisional execution, except where the court recognizes the demand ruling accompanied by the declaration of provisional execution in accordance with paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 333.- Effect of a demand ruling

If no objection is filed against a demand ruling accompanied by a declaration of provisional execution, or if a ruling dismissing an objection against a demand ruling accompanied by a declaration of provisional execution has become finalized, such demand ruling shall have the same effect as a finalized judgment.

# BOOK 6
# COMPULSORY EXECUTION

### CHAPTER 1
### GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### SECTION 1
#### CONCEPT

#### Article 334.- Concept

A person who has a claim or real right defined in the Civil Code or other laws may seek the realization of such right through compulsory execution in accordance with the provisions of this Book, except as otherwise provided by special provisions in other laws.

#### Article 335.- Mutatis mutandis application of provisions of Book 2 to Book 4

The provisions from Book 2 to Book 4 of this Code shall apply mutatis mutandis to the procedure for compulsory execution, except as otherwise specially provided.

#### SECTION 2
#### EXECUTION ORGANS

#### Article 336.- Execution organs

1- Compulsory execution shall be carried out by the execution organ upon motion of a party.

2- The execution organs are the execution court and the bailiff.

3- The court of first instance that is to carry out compulsory execution under the provisions of this Book shall be the execution court.

4- A decision on a motion for recourse against an execution measure carried out by a bailiff shall be made by the court of first instance to which such bailiff belongs.

#### Article 337.- Decisions of execution court, etc.

1- A decision of the execution court shall be made by a ruling.

2- The execution court may, when it considers it necessary for an execution measure, examine any person having an interest or any other person concerned.

3- The provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 above shall apply mutatis mutandis to cases where the court of first instance is to decide on a motion for recourse against an execution measure carried out by a bailiff.

#### Article 338.- Safeguards for performance of duties by bailiff, etc.

1- If a bailiff encounters resistance in the performance of his or her duties, the bailiff may use force or seek assistance from the police or other competent forces to overcome such resistance.

2- If a person other than a bailiff who performs duties related to compulsory execution upon order of the execution court encounters resistance in the performance of his or her duties, such person may seek assistance from a bailiff.

#### Article 339.- Observers

When entering a residence to perform his or her duties, if the bailiff or a person other than a bailiff performing duties related to compulsory execution upon order of the execution court does not find the master of the residence, his or her representative, or a relative, servant, or employee living there who has suitable discretion, he or she shall cause a commune or quarter official, a police officer, or any other person who may be recognized as a suitable witness to be present. This provision shall also apply to cases where a bailiff uses force or receives assistance from the police or other competent forces as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 338 (Safeguards for performance of duties by bailiff, etc.) of this Code.

#### Article 340.- Execution on holidays or at night

1- A bailiff or a person other than a bailiff performing duties related to compulsory execution upon order of the execution court shall obtain permission from the court of first instance to which he or she belongs or the execution court that issued the order, to enter a residence to perform his or her duties on Sundays or other legal holidays, or during the period from 6 p.m. to 6 a.m. of the following day.

2- When performing his or her duties, the bailiff or the person other than a bailiff performing duties related to compulsory execution upon order of the execution court shall show the document certifying the permission obtained under paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 341.- Carrying an identification card, etc.

When performing his or her duties, a bailiff or a person other than a bailiff performing duties related to compulsory execution upon order of the execution court shall carry a document certifying his or her identity or status and shall show it to any person having an interest if requested by such person.

#### Article 342.- Requesting assistance from competent ministries

The execution court may, when it considers it necessary for compulsory execution, request the assistance of competent ministries or agencies.

#### Article 343.- Special provisions on complaints against a ruling in compulsory execution procedure

1- Notwithstanding the provisions of Article 305 (Stay of execution of original ruling) of this Code, a complaint against a ruling as defined in this Book shall not stay the execution of the original ruling.

2- Until the decision on the complaint against the ruling takes effect, the court handling the complaint may issue any of the following rulings:

(a)- Order a stay of effect of the original ruling, with or without security.

(b)- Order a stay of the compulsory execution procedure, in whole or in part, with or without security.

#### Article 344.- Objection to execution

1- An objection may be filed with the execution court against an execution measure carried out by the execution court against which no complaint against a ruling can be filed.

2- An objection may be filed with the court of first instance to which the bailiff belongs against an execution measure carried out by a bailiff, or against a bailiff's delay or negligence in carrying out an execution measure.

3- The provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 343 (Special provisions on complaints against a ruling in compulsory execution procedure) of this Code shall apply mutatis mutandis to cases where an objection to execution is filed.

#### Article 345.- Complaint against a ruling of annulment, etc.

1- A complaint against a ruling may be filed against the following rulings:

(a)- A ruling of the execution court annulling the compulsory execution procedure;

(b)- A ruling of the court of first instance ordering the bailiff to annul the compulsory execution procedure;

(c)- A ruling of the court of first instance dismissing an objection to an annulment of the compulsory execution procedure by a bailiff.

2- A ruling against which a complaint against a ruling can be filed under paragraph 1 above shall not take effect until it has become finalized.

#### Article 346.- Exclusive jurisdiction

The jurisdiction of the court defined in this Book is exclusive jurisdiction.

### SECTION 3
### PARTIES TO EXECUTION AND REPRESENTATIVES

#### Article 347.- Names of the parties

In this Book, the party filing a motion for compulsory execution shall be called the "execution creditor" and the other party shall be called the "execution debtor".

#### Article 348.- Mandated representative in compulsory execution procedure

1- The following provisions shall apply to representation by mandate in procedures related to compulsory execution:

(a)- The provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 53 (Qualifications of mandated representative) of this Code shall apply to the procedure for a lawsuit or a complaint against a ruling as defined in this Book 6.

(b)- Except in cases defined in point (a) above, in procedures carried out by an execution court or a court of first instance for compulsory execution, a person other than those who can be mandated representatives under paragraph 1 of Article 53 (Qualifications of mandated representative) of this Code may be a mandated representative with the permission of the execution court or the court of first instance.

(c)- In procedures carried out by a bailiff, any person may be a mandated representative.

2- The execution court or the court of first instance may revoke the permission defined in point (b) of paragraph 1 above at any time.

### SECTION 4
### CONDITIONS FOR COMPULSORY EXECUTION

#### Article 349.- Method of filing a motion for compulsory execution

1- A motion for compulsory execution shall be filed in writing.

2- A motion for compulsory execution shall state the following points and shall be accompanied by an authenticated copy of a title of execution having executory force:

(a)- The name or trade name and address of the execution creditor and the execution debtor, and the name and address of the legal representative;

(b)- An indication of the title of execution;

(c)- A demand for execution through direct compulsion, substitute execution, or indirect compulsion;

(d)- An indication of the property that is the object of compulsory execution and the method of compulsory execution requested by the execution creditor, in cases where execution through direct compulsion is requested;

(e)- The substance of the decision requested by the creditor, in cases where execution through substitute execution or indirect compulsion is requested.

3- In cases where the execution creditor requests compulsory execution for only a part of the claim for payment of money stated in the title of execution, the execution creditor shall state the claim and its scope in the motion for compulsory execution.

#### Article 350.- Title of execution

1- Compulsory execution shall be carried out based on a title of execution.

2- The following documents shall be titles of execution:

(a)- A judgment or decree ordering performance that has become finalized;

(b)- A judgment or decree ordering performance accompanied by a declaration of provisional execution;

(c)- A ruling ordering performance; however, in the case of a ruling that does not take effect unless it has become finalized, such ruling shall be limited to one that has become finalized;

(d)- A demand ruling accompanied by a declaration of provisional execution;

(e)- An order of a court clerk as provided in paragraph 1 of Article 66 (Procedure for final determination of amount of court costs) of this Code;

(f)- An authenticated document prepared by a notary with respect to a claim for payment of a fixed sum of money, provided that such document contains a statement that the debtor will be immediately subject to compulsory execution;

(g)- A judgment or decree of a foreign court for which a judgment for execution has become finalized under Article 352 (Judgment for execution of judgment or decree of a foreign court) of this Code;

(h)- An arbitral award for which a ruling for execution has become finalized under Article 353 (Ruling for execution of arbitral award) of this Code;

(i)- A protocol as provided in Article 222 (Effect of protocol on settlement, etc.) of this Code and any other protocol having the same effect as a finalized judgment.

#### Article 351.- Scope of persons by and against whom compulsory execution can be carried out

1- Compulsory execution may be carried out by or against the persons defined following as the execution creditor or the execution debtor:

(a)- The party specified in the title of execution;

(b)- A third party, in cases where the party specified in the title of execution is a party for the benefit of such third party.

(c)- A person who succeeded to the rights or obligations of a person specified in point (a) or (b) above after the title of execution was prepared. However, with respect to titles of execution specified in points (a), (b), (g), and (h) of Article 350 (Titles of execution) of this Code, this includes persons who succeeded to the rights or obligations after the lawsuit was filed.

2- Compulsory execution based on a title of execution specified in point (a), (b), (c), (h), or (i) of Article 350 (Titles of execution) of this Code may also be carried out against a person who possesses the object of the claim on behalf of a person specified in each point of paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 352.- Judgment for execution of judgment or decree of foreign court

1- To carry out compulsory execution based on a judgment or decree of a foreign court, a judgment for execution shall be obtained from a court of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

2- A lawsuit seeking a judgment for execution of a foreign judgment or decree shall be under the jurisdiction of the court having jurisdiction over the execution debtor's place as provided in Article 8 (Jurisdiction of the court of trial determined by residence, etc.) of this Code. If no court having jurisdiction can be determined under Article 8, the lawsuit shall be under the jurisdiction of the court of first instance having jurisdiction over the place where the object of the claim or any seizable property of the execution debtor is located.

3- If it cannot be proven that the foreign judgment or decree has become finalized, or if such judgment or decree fails to meet any of the conditions specified in Article 199 (Effect of finalized judgment of foreign court) of this Code, the court shall dismiss the lawsuit specified in paragraph 2 above.

4- A judgment for execution shall be issued without examining the merits of the foreign judgment or decree.

5- In the judgment for execution, the court shall declare that compulsory execution based on the foreign judgment or decree is permitted.

#### Article 353.- Ruling for execution of arbitral award

1- To carry out compulsory execution based on an arbitral award, whether it is a domestic or foreign arbitral award, a ruling for execution shall be obtained from a court in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

2- A party filing a motion for a ruling for execution of an arbitral award shall submit the following documents:

(a)- The original arbitral award duly authenticated or a duly certified copy thereof;

(b)- The original arbitration agreement or a duly certified copy thereof.

3- The court may refuse to enforce an arbitral award if the party against whom the award is invoked proves any of the following grounds:

(a)- A party to the arbitration agreement was under some incapacity, or the arbitration agreement is invalid under the law to which the parties have subjected it or, failing any indication thereon, under the law of the country where the award was made;

(b)- The party against whom the award is invoked was not given proper notice of the appointment of an arbitrator or of the arbitral proceedings or was otherwise unable to present his or her case;

(c)- The arbitral award deals with a dispute not contemplated by or not falling within the terms of the submission to arbitration...

...to which the arbitration relates or contains a decision on a matter beyond the scope of the submission to arbitration;

(d)- The composition of the arbitral tribunal or the arbitral procedure was not in accordance with the agreement of the parties or, failing such agreement, was not in accordance with the law of the country where the arbitration took place;

(e)- The arbitral award has not yet become binding on the parties or has been set aside or suspended by a court of the country in which, or under the law of which, that award was made.

4- The court may also refuse to enforce an arbitral award if it finds that:

(a)- The subject matter of the dispute is not capable of settlement by arbitration under the law of the Kingdom of Cambodia;

(b)- The recognition or enforcement of the award would be contrary to public order.

5- A motion for a ruling for execution of a domestic arbitral award shall be under the jurisdiction of the court having jurisdiction over the execution debtor's place as provided in Article 8 (Jurisdiction of the court of trial determined by residence, etc.) of this Code. If no court having jurisdiction can be determined under Article 8, the motion shall be under the jurisdiction of the court of first instance having jurisdiction over the place where the object of the claim or any seizable property of the execution debtor is located.

6- A motion for a ruling for execution of a foreign arbitral award shall be under the jurisdiction of the Appeal Court.

7- A ruling for execution shall be issued without examining the merits of the arbitral award, except for the grounds specified in paragraphs 3 and 4 above.

8- In the ruling for execution, the court shall declare that compulsory execution based on [the arbitral award is permitted.]

...based on the arbitral award.

9- A complaint against a ruling may be filed against a ruling on a motion for a ruling for execution.

10- A ruling for execution shall not take effect until it has become finalized.

#### Article 354.- Necessity of grant of execution and granting organ

1- Compulsory execution shall be carried out based on an authenticated copy of a title of execution to which the grant of execution is attached. However, where the documents specified following are used as titles of execution, compulsory execution by or against the party specified in the title of execution shall be carried out based on an authenticated copy of such title of execution:

(a)- A finalized judgment in a small claim case;

(b)- A judgment in a small claim case accompanied by a declaration of provisional execution;

(c)- A demand ruling accompanied by a declaration of provisional execution.

2- The grant of execution shall be provided by the court clerk of the court having the case records, upon motion. However, with respect to the title of execution specified in point (f) of paragraph 2 of Article 350 (Titles of execution) of this Code, the grant of execution shall be provided by the notary who keeps the original document.

#### Article 355.- Motion for grant of execution

1- A motion for grant of execution shall be filed in writing stating the following points:

(a)- The name or trade name and address of the execution creditor and the execution debtor, and the name and address of the legal representative;

(b)- An indication of the title of execution;

(c)- Facts and grounds if the claim for grant of execution is to be made...

...under Article 356 (Special grant of execution) or paragraph 1 of Article 358 (Multiple grants of execution, etc.) of this Code.

2- In cases where the title of execution is a decision that does not take effect until it has become finalized, if the execution creditor files a motion for grant of execution for such title, the execution creditor shall attach to the motion a certificate of finalization of such decision. However, this provision shall not apply to cases where it is clear from the case record that the decision has already become finalized.

3- The provisions of paragraph 1 above shall apply mutatis mutandis to cases where the execution creditor requests the issuance of additional authenticated copies of titles of execution defined in the second sentence of paragraph 1 of Article 354 (Necessity of grant of execution and granting organ) of this Code.

#### Article 356.- Special grant of execution

1- In cases where the claim specified in the title of execution is subject to a condition precedent or an uncertain time of maturity, the court clerk or the notary may provide the grant of execution only if the execution creditor proves the fulfillment of the condition or the arrival of the time.

2- To carry out compulsory execution by or against a person other than the party specified in the title of execution, the execution creditor shall obtain a grant of execution that states such fact.

3- In cases where it is clear that compulsory execution can be carried out by or against a person other than the party specified in the title of execution, or where the execution creditor proves such fact by documentary evidence, the court clerk or the notary may provide the grant of execution defined in paragraph 2 above.

4- In cases where a court clerk provides a grant of execution under paragraph 1 or 3 above, the court clerk shall obtain the permission of the court to which he or she belongs.

#### Article 357.- Matters to be stated in the grant of execution

1- The grant of execution shall be provided by adding the following information at the end of the authenticated copy of the title of execution:

(a)- The persons who can demand compulsory execution based on the title of execution and the other party;

(b)- The facts, in cases where the grant of execution is provided under Article 356 (Special grant of execution) of this Code;

(c)- The scope of compulsory execution, if the grant of execution is provided for only part of the claim specified in the title of execution;

(d)- The facts, in cases where the grant of execution is provided under paragraph 1 of Article 358 (Multiple grants of execution, etc.) of this Code.

2- The court clerk or the notary shall sign the grant of execution and state the date of provision.

#### Article 358.- Multiple grants of execution, etc.

1- Additional grants of execution may be provided only if multiple authenticated copies of the title of execution with the grant of execution are necessary to obtain full satisfaction of the claim, or if the authenticated copy has been lost.

2- If a grant of execution is provided under paragraph 1 above, the court clerk or the notary shall notify the execution debtor of the facts and grounds thereof, and the number of authenticated copies for which the grant of execution was provided.

3- The provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 above shall apply mutatis mutandis...

...to cases where additional authenticated copies of titles of execution defined in the second sentence of paragraph 1 of Article 354 (Necessity of grant of execution and granting organ) of this Code are issued.

#### Article 359.- Entries in original title of execution

1- Upon providing a grant of execution, the court clerk or the notary shall enter the following information in the original title of execution:

(a)- The fact that the grant of execution was provided, the date of provision, and the number of authenticated copies for which the grant of execution was provided;

(b)- The scope of compulsory execution, if the grant of execution was provided for only part of the claim specified in the title of execution;

(c)- The facts and the names of the execution creditor or execution debtor, if the grant of execution was provided under paragraph 2 of Article 356 (Special grant of execution) of this Code.

2- If a court clerk has issued additional authenticated copies of a title of execution under the second sentence of paragraph 1 of Article 354 (Necessity of grant of execution and granting organ) of this Code, the court clerk shall enter the fact of such issuance, the date, and the number of authenticated copies issued in the original judgment, decree, or demand ruling.

#### Article 360.- Service of title of execution, etc.

Compulsory execution may commence only if an authenticated copy or certified copy of the title of execution or of the decision destined to become a title of execution upon finalization has been served on the execution debtor in advance. In cases where a grant of execution was provided under Article 356 (Special grant of execution) of this Code, a certified copy of the grant of execution and a certified copy of the documents submitted by the execution creditor under...

...the provisions of Article 356 shall also be served in advance.

#### Article 361.- Conditions for commencement of execution to be examined by execution organ

1- In cases where the claim specified in the title of execution is subject to a certain time of maturity, the execution organ may commence compulsory execution only if such time has arrived.

2- With respect to compulsory execution based on a title of execution that requires security for such execution, the execution organ may commence compulsory execution only if the execution creditor submits a document certifying that such security has been provided.

3- In cases where the performance of the execution debtor is to be provided in exchange for a counter-performance, the execution organ may commence compulsory execution only if the execution creditor proves that he or she has provided the counter-performance or has offered to provide it.

#### Article 362.- Motion for objection to grant of execution, etc.

1- An objection to a measure taken regarding a motion for grant of execution may be filed with the court to which the court clerk belongs if the measure was taken by a court clerk, or with the court of first instance having jurisdiction over the place where the notary's office is located if the measure was taken by a notary.

2- A decision on the motion specified in paragraph 1 above shall be made by a ruling.

3- The provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 above shall apply mutatis mutandis to cases where the issuance of additional authenticated copies of titles of execution defined in the second sentence of paragraph 1 of Article 354 (Necessity of grant of execution and granting organ) of this Code was requested.

### SECTION 5
### LAWSUITS RELATING TO EXECUTION

#### Article 363.- Lawsuit for objection to claim

1- An execution debtor who has an objection to the existence or scope of the claim specified in a title of execution may file a lawsuit for objection to the claim to seek a declaration that compulsory execution based on such title of execution is not permitted. However, this provision shall not apply to titles of execution specified in point (b) or (d) of paragraph 2 of Article 350 (Titles of execution) of this Code that have not yet become finalized.

2- Grounds for objection to a finalized judgment or decree shall be limited to those that arose after the conclusion of oral argument.

3- The lawsuit specified in paragraph 1 above shall be under the jurisdiction of the following courts, according to the type of title of execution:

(a)- Title of execution specified in points (a) through (d), or points (g) through (i), of paragraph 2 of Article 350 (Titles of execution) of this Code: The court of first instance where the adjudication procedure took place;

(b)- Title of execution specified in point (e) of paragraph 2 of Article 350 (Titles of execution) of this Code: The court to which the court clerk belongs;

(c)- Title of execution specified in point (f) of paragraph 2 of Article 350 (Titles of execution) of this Code: The court having jurisdiction over the defendant's place as provided in Article 8 (Jurisdiction of the court of trial determined by residence, etc.) of this Code. If no court having jurisdiction can be determined under this Article, the court of first instance having jurisdiction over the place where the object of the claim or any seizable property of the execution debtor is located.

#### Article 364.- Objection to granting of execution formula

1- In cases where an execution formula has been granted in accordance with the provisions of Article 356 (Special execution formula) of this Code, the execution debtor who has an objection regarding the fulfillment of conditions, regarding the arrival of an uncertain date, or regarding the possibility of compulsory execution through the substitution of a person other than the parties specified in the execution document as the execution creditor or the execution debtor, may file an objection to the granting of the execution formula in order to prevent the allowance of compulsory execution, based on a certified copy of the execution document attached to that execution formula.

2- The provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 363 (Objection to claim) of this Code shall also apply *mutatis mutandis* to the objection specified in paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 365.- Objection by a third party

1- A third party who has ownership or other rights that prevent the alienation or delivery of the object which is the subject of compulsory execution may file an objection to demand that compulsory execution not be allowed against the execution creditor.

2- The objection specified in paragraph 1 above regarding compulsory execution by an execution court that has become an execution organ shall be under the jurisdiction of that execution court, while for compulsory execution by a bailiff who has become an execution organ, it shall be under the jurisdiction of the first instance court where that bailiff is located.

### Section 6
### Stay and Revocation of Compulsory Execution

#### Article 366.- Decision to stay execution due to filing of motion for new trial, etc.

1- The court may order a temporary stay of compulsory execution, upon motion and with or without providing security, in the following cases:

a- In case a motion for a new trial has been filed, if the circumstances asserted as the grounds for the objection appear to have legal merit and there is *prima facie* evidence of the facts.

b- In case an appeal to the Supreme Court (Sa Tuk) has been filed against a judgment or decision attached with a declaration of provisional execution, if there is *prima facie* evidence of circumstances that would be grounds for the reversal of the original judgment or decision.

c- In case an appeal (Utdor) has been filed against a judgment attached with a declaration of provisional execution, or in case an objection has been filed against a demand for payment (Dika Samrech Dak Teun) attached with a declaration of provisional execution, if there is *prima facie* evidence of circumstances that would be grounds for the revocation or modification of the original judgment or the demand for payment.

d- In case an opposition (Bandung Toas) has been filed against a default judgment attached with a declaration of provisional execution, if there is *prima facie* evidence of circumstances that would be grounds for the revocation or modification of that default judgment.

2- In the cases specified in each item of paragraph 1 above, the court may, upon motion and upon the provision of security, order the revocation of the execution measures already taken, together with the order for temporary stay of compulsory execution.

3- In case an appeal to the Supreme Court has been filed against a judgment or decision attached with a declaration of provisional execution, or in case an appeal has been filed against a judgment attached with a declaration of provisional execution, if the case file for the appeal is still at the original court, that court shall make a decision on the motion specified in paragraphs 1 and 2 above.

#### Article 367.- Decision to stay execution due to filing of objection to claim, etc.

1- In case an objection to claim, an objection to the granting of an execution formula, or an objection by a third party has been filed, if there is *prima facie* evidence of the grounds for the objection asserted, the court that received the objection may, upon motion and with or without providing security, order a temporary stay of compulsory execution until a decision as specified in paragraph 1 of Article 368 (Decision to stay execution in the final judgment, etc.) of this Code is made in the final judgment.

2- In the case specified in paragraph 1 above, the court that received the objection may, upon motion and upon the provision of security, order the revocation of the execution measures already taken, together with the order for temporary stay of compulsory execution.

3- In case an objection to claim or an objection to the granting of an execution formula has been filed, if there is *prima facie* evidence of the grounds for the objection asserted and there is an urgent situation, the execution court or the first instance court where the bailiff is located may issue the order specified in paragraph 1 or 2 above, while specifying a period within which a certified copy of the decision made according to paragraph 1 above must be submitted. This decision may also be made before the filing of the objection specified in paragraph 1 above.

4- If the period specified in paragraph 3 above has elapsed, or if the decision made according to paragraph 1 or 2 above is submitted to the execution court or the bailiff within that period, the order specified in paragraph 3 above shall lose its effect.

#### Article 368.- Decision to stay execution in the final judgment, etc.

In the final judgment on an objection to claim, an objection to the granting of an execution formula, or an objection by a third party, the court that received the objection may order the measures specified in paragraphs 1 and 2 of Article 367 (Decision to stay execution due to filing of objection to claim, etc.) of this Code, or may revoke, modify, or approve the decision already made according to the provisions of said paragraph 1 or 2. This decision shall become effective from the time the final judgment on the objection specified in this Article is pronounced.

#### Article 369.- Decision to stay execution due to filing of motion to object against granting of execution formula, etc.

In case a motion to object against the granting of an execution formula, etc. has been filed, if it is deemed necessary, the court may, upon motion, order a stay of execution with or without providing security until a decision on that objection is made.

#### Article 370.- Stay and revocation of compulsory execution

1- An execution court or a bailiff shall stay compulsory execution and revoke the execution measures already taken if any of the following documents is submitted:

a- A certified copy of a decision that revokes an execution document other than a certified copy of a document specified in item (f) of paragraph 2 of Article 350 (Execution documents) of this Code, or a certified copy of a decision that revokes a declaration of provisional execution.

b- A certified copy of a decision that orders that compulsory execution shall not be allowed.

c- An exemplification (Anusar) of a final judgment or a final decision which declares that a settlement or an acknowledgement relating to the execution document is void.

d- A certified copy of a record or other document prepared by a court clerk which certifies that an execution document specified in items (b) through (e) of paragraph 2 of Article 350 (Execution documents) of this Code has lost its effect due to the withdrawal of the motion or for other reasons.

e- A certified copy of a record of settlement in the procedure for the decision that orders that compulsory execution shall not be carried out or that the motion for compulsory execution is withdrawn.

f- A document certifying the provision of security in order to be released from compulsory execution.

g- A certified copy of a decision that orders a stay of compulsory execution and the revocation of execution measures.

2- An execution court or a bailiff shall stay compulsory execution if any of the following documents is submitted. However, in the case specified in item (b) below, the effect of the stay shall be limited to two (2) months:

a- A certified copy of a decision that orders a temporary stay of compulsory execution.

b- A document stating that the creditor has received payment or has agreed to a stay of performance after the execution document was created.

3- The provisions of Article 345 (Complaint against a ruling of revocation, etc.) of this Code shall not apply to cases where execution measures are revoked according to the provisions of paragraph 1 above.

### Section 7
### Execution Costs and Inspection of Execution Case File

#### Article 371.- Meaning of execution costs and procedural costs

The meanings of the terms specified in each of the following items shall be in accordance with the provisions of each item:

a- Execution costs: Refers to the application fee specified in Article 372 (Application fee), costs other than the application fee specified in Article 373 (Costs other than application fee), and party costs specified in Article 374 (Party costs) of this Code.

b- Procedural costs: Refers to the costs incurred for the common benefit among the execution costs specified in item (a) above.

#### Article 372.- Application fee

1- In case a motion for compulsory execution is submitted to an execution court, the execution creditor shall pay a fee of 10,000 (ten thousand) Riels to the court.

2- In case a motion for compulsory execution is submitted to a bailiff, the execution creditor shall pay a fee according to separate provisions.

#### Article 373.- Costs other than application fee

The execution creditor shall pay an amount determined by the court as necessary costs for the delivery of documents or other activities for the procedure of compulsory execution.

#### Article 374.- Party costs

In addition to the costs specified in Article 372 (Application fee) and Article 373 (Costs other than application fee) of this Code, the costs that the court recognizes

...as necessary for carrying out compulsory execution shall be considered party costs.

#### Article 375.- Advance payment of costs

1- When a motion for compulsory execution is submitted to an execution court, the person who submitted the motion shall pay in advance an amount determined by the execution court as necessary costs for the procedure of compulsory execution. This provision shall also apply to cases where the costs paid in advance are insufficient and the execution court orders the advance payment of the deficit.

2- If the person who submitted the motion does not pay the costs in advance, the execution court may dismiss the motion for compulsory execution or may revoke the procedure for compulsory execution.

3- An appeal (Bandung Toas) may be filed against a ruling of dismissal made according to the provisions of paragraph 2 above.

#### Article 376.- Burden of execution costs

1- Execution costs shall be borne by the execution debtor.

2- In case of compulsory execution against a monetary claim, execution costs may be collected simultaneously in the execution procedure without the need for an execution document.

#### Article 377.- Provision of security

In order to provide security according to the provisions of this Code, a party shall deposit money or negotiable instruments recognized as appropriate by the court, with the court that ordered the provision of security or with the execution court.

#### Article 378.- Inspection of compulsory execution case file, etc.

1- A person who has provided *prima facie* evidence of having an interest in a compulsory execution carried out by an execution court may demand to inspect the case file, to copy the case file, or to be issued a certified copy or an authenticated copy

...or an extract of the case file, or may demand that the court issue a certificate concerning matters relating to the case, upon payment of a fee.

2- A person who has provided *prima facie* evidence of having an interest in a compulsory execution carried out by a bailiff may demand to inspect the case file, to copy the case file, or to be issued a certified copy, an authenticated copy, or an extract of the case file, or may demand that the bailiff issue a certificate concerning matters relating to the case, upon payment of a fee.

## Chapter 2
## Compulsory Execution regarding Monetary Claims

### Section 1
### Assets Subject to Seizure

#### Article 379.- Principle

Seizure may be carried out against all tangible or intangible assets belonging to the execution debtor, except in cases where there are special provisions in this Code or other laws.

#### Article 380.- Movables exempt from seizure

The following movables may not be seized:

a- Clothing, bedding, tables, cabinets, cooking utensils, and eating utensils necessary for the daily life of the execution debtor and relatives living and sharing a livelihood with the execution debtor.

b- Foodstuffs and fuel necessary for the livelihood for two (2) months of the execution debtor and relatives living and sharing a livelihood with the execution debtor.

c- Living support, education support, health support, and other money or objects provided by the State or local authorities to protect the livelihood of the execution debtor.

d- Cash less than or equal to 1,000,000 (one million) Riels, other than the money specified in item (c) above.

e- Tools, land, and livestock necessary for the agricultural work of the person, including fodder for such livestock, as well as seeds or similar agricultural products necessary for continuing agricultural work until the next harvest, for a person engaged in agriculture.

f- Nets or other fishing gear necessary, including bait, fries, or similar aquatic products necessary for catching or raising aquatic products, for a person engaged in fishing.

g- Tools and other objects necessary for technical personnel, artisans, workers, and other persons engaged in a profession or craft using mental or physical strength. However, if such objects are held for the purpose of sale or lease, they may be seized.

h- Buddha statues, religious texts, and other objects necessary for religious ceremonies.

i- Genealogy, diaries, business logs, and similar documents necessary for the execution debtor.

j- Decorations and other certificates of merit of the execution debtor or relatives of such person.

k- Documents and tools necessary for study at a school or other educational institution, for the execution debtor and relatives living and sharing a livelihood with the execution debtor.

l- Objects relating to an invention or a copyrighted work that has not yet been published.

m- Artificial limbs, other artificial organs, and other auxiliary devices for the body necessary for the execution debtor and relatives living and sharing a livelihood with the execution debtor, including various objects for nursing sick persons who are the execution debtor or relatives living and sharing a livelihood with the execution debtor.

n- Firefighting machines or equipment, emergency evacuation equipment, or other equipment for buildings or other structures which various laws or regulations require as fixtures in order to prevent disasters or protect security.

#### Article 381.- Changing the scope of movables exempt from seizure

1- The first instance court where the bailiff is located may, upon motion, order the revocation of all or part of the seizure carried out by the bailiff, or may permit the seizure of movables specified in each item of Article 380 (Movables exempt from seizure) of this Code, by taking into account the livelihood and other circumstances of the execution debtor and the execution creditor.

2- If the circumstances have changed, the first instance court where the bailiff is located may, upon motion, permit the seizure of movables for which the seizure was revoked according to the provisions of paragraph 1 above, or may order the revocation of all or part of the seizure as provided in the same paragraph 1.

3- In case a motion has been filed for an order revoking the seizure according to the provisions of paragraph 1 or 2 above, the first instance court where the bailiff is located may order a stay of compulsory execution, with or without providing security, until said order becomes effective.

4- An appeal (Bandung Toas) may be filed against a ruling of dismissal of the motion specified in paragraph 1 or 2 above, or against a ruling permitting seizure according to the provisions above.

#### Article 382.- Claims exempt from seizure

1- Salary, wages, and claims of a similar nature to salary may be seized, from the amount remaining after deducting taxes and social security contributions from the amount the execution debtor is entitled to receive at the time of payment, only in accordance with the following items:

a- The portion less than or equal to 200,000 (two hundred thousand) Riels per month may not be seized.

b- For the portion exceeding 200,000 (two hundred thousand) Riels up to 600,000 (six hundred thousand) Riels per month, no more than 1/4 (one-fourth) of this portion may be seized.

c- For the portion exceeding 600,000 (six hundred thousand) Riels up to 2,000,000 (two million) Riels per month, no more than 1/3 (one-third) of this portion may be seized.

d- For the portion exceeding 2,000,000 (two million) Riels up to 4,000,000 (four million) Riels per month, no more than 1/2 (one-half) of this portion may be seized.

e- For the portion exceeding 4,000,000 (four million) Riels up to 6,000,000 (six million) Riels per month, no more than 2/3 (two-thirds) of this portion may be seized.

f- The portion exceeding 6,000,000 (six million) Riels per month may be seized in its entirety.

2- Claims for living support, education support, health support, and other claims for the purpose of protecting the livelihood of the execution debtor may not be seized.

#### Article 383.- Changing the scope of claims exempt from seizure

1- An execution court may, upon motion, revoke all or part of a seizure order, or may issue an order to seize all or part of a claim that is exempt from seizure according to the provisions of Article 382 (Claims exempt from seizure) of this Code, by taking into account the livelihood and other circumstances of the execution debtor and the execution creditor.

2- If the circumstances have changed, an execution court may, upon motion, seize a claim for which the seizure order was revoked according to the provisions of paragraph 1 above, or may revoke all or part of the seizure order according to the provisions of the same paragraph.

3- In case a motion has been filed as specified in paragraph 1 or 2 above, the execution court may order a prohibition on the third party debtor specified in paragraph 2 of Article 402 (Meaning of execution against claims and execution court) of this Code from making payments or other performances, with or without providing security, until said order becomes effective.

4- An appeal (Bandung Toas) may be filed against a ruling of dismissal of the motion for revocation of the seizure order according to the provisions of paragraph 1 or 2 above.

### Section 2
### Execution against Movables

#### Article 384.- Commencement of execution against movables

1- A motion for compulsory execution against movables shall be made to a bailiff of the first instance court that has jurisdiction over the place where the objects which are the subject of compulsory execution are located.

2- In a motion for execution against movables, in addition to each item specified in paragraph 2 of Article 349 (Method of filing motion for compulsory execution) of this Code...

...the location where the movables to be seized are located shall be specified.

3- Compulsory execution against movables shall be initiated by the seizure of the objects by a bailiff.

4- The movables referred to in this Section include natural fruits that have not yet been separated from the land, if it is clear that they can be harvested within one (1) month.

5- If the execution debtor is encountered at the place of seizure, the bailiff shall demand the execution debtor to perform the obligation voluntarily before initiating the seizure.

6- In execution against movables, a bailiff may receive performance of the obligation and payment of execution costs for the execution creditor.

#### Article 385.- Seizure of movables in the possession of the execution debtor

1- Seizure of movables in the possession of the execution debtor shall be carried out by the bailiff taking possession of the movables.

2- When carrying out the seizure specified in paragraph 1 above, a bailiff may enter the residence of the execution debtor or other places in the possession of the execution debtor, and may search for movables to be seized. In this case, if necessary, the bailiff may take necessary measures to open closed doors, safes, and other containers.

3- When selecting movables to be seized, a bailiff shall consider the interests of the execution debtor, provided that it does not damage the interests of the execution creditor.

4- If deemed appropriate, a bailiff may allow the execution debtor to keep and manage the seized movables. In this case, the seizure shall be effective only if a label or sign is affixed to the seized movables

...or the seizure is indicated by other methods.

5- In case the bailiff has allowed the execution debtor to keep and manage the seized movables according to the provisions of paragraph 4 above, if deemed appropriate, the bailiff may permit the execution debtor to use those movables.

6- If deemed necessary, the bailiff may take over the custody and management of the movables that were allowed to be kept and managed by the execution debtor according to the provisions of paragraph 4 above, or may revoke the permission granted according to the provisions of paragraph 5 above. The provisions of paragraph 2 above shall also apply to cases where the bailiff takes over the custody and management of the movables that were allowed to be kept and managed by the execution debtor.

#### Article 386.- Seizure of movables in the possession of persons other than the execution debtor

The provisions of paragraphs 1, 3, 4, 5, and 6 of Article 385 (Seizure of movables in the possession of the execution debtor) of this Code shall also apply *mutatis mutandis* to the seizure of movables in the possession of the execution creditor or movables in the possession of a third party, if such third party does not refuse to deliver them.

#### Article 387.- Special provisions concerning registered vehicles, etc.

1- In case a car or a motorcycle that has been seized is registered in a registry, the bailiff shall request the competent authority to record the fact of seizure in that registry.

2- The seizure of a car or a motorcycle specified in paragraph 1 above shall become effective when the fact of seizure is recorded in the registry.

3- The provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 above shall also apply *mutatis mutandis* to movables other than cars or motorcycles for which registration is required.

#### Article 388.- Consolidation of cases

1- In case a further motion for execution against movables has been filed against the same execution debtor for whom a seizure has already been carried out at the same location, if there are movables not yet seized, the bailiff shall seize those movables; and if there are no movables that can be seized, the bailiff shall clearly state this fact, and shall consolidate this new execution case with the previous execution case. These provisions shall also apply *mutatis mutandis* to cases where a motion for execution against movables has been filed against an execution debtor for whom a provisional seizure has already been carried out at the same location.

2- If two execution cases against movables are consolidated according to the provisions of the first sentence of paragraph 1 above, the movables seized in the subsequent case shall be deemed to have been seized in the previous case at the time of consolidation, and the motion in the subsequent case shall have the effect of a demand for distribution. If the previous execution creditor withdraws the motion for execution against movables or the procedure initiated by that motion is stayed or revoked, the movables seized in the previous case shall be deemed to have been seized for the subsequent case at the time of consolidation.

3- If a case for provisional seizure and an execution case against movables are consolidated according to the provisions of the second sentence of paragraph 1 above, the movables for which provisional seizure was carried out shall be deemed to have been seized in the execution case against movables at the time of consolidation, and the motion for provisional seizure shall have the effect of a demand for distribution. If the execution creditor withdraws the motion for execution against movables or the procedure initiated by that motion is revoked, the movables seized in the execution case against movables shall be deemed to have been provisionally seized

...in the provisional seizure case at the time of consolidation.

4- When cases are consolidated, the bailiff shall notify the execution creditor, the provisional seizure creditor, and the execution debtor of that fact.

#### Article 389.- Scope of effect of seizure

Seizure shall be effective against natural fruits produced from the seized movables.

#### Article 390.- Order to deliver seized movables

1- If movables that have already been seized come into the possession of a third party, the first instance court where the bailiff is located may, upon motion of the execution creditor, order said third party to deliver the seized movables to the bailiff.

2- The motion specified in paragraph 1 above shall be filed within one (1) week from the date on which the fact that the seized movables are in the possession of a third party was known.

3- An appeal (Bandung Toas) may be filed against a ruling on the motion specified in paragraph 1 above.

4- A ruling based on the provisions of paragraph 1 above may not be executed after two (2) weeks have elapsed from the date on which the ruling was notified to the person who filed the motion.

5- A ruling based on the provisions of paragraph 1 above may be executed before said ruling is served on the third party who is in possession of the seized movables.

6- Costs necessary for the ruling based on the provisions of paragraph 1 above shall be considered common costs for the benefit of the compulsory execution procedure against those movables.

#### Article 391.- Prohibition of excessive seizure

1- Seizure of movables shall not exceed the extent necessary for the satisfaction of the claims of the execution creditor and the execution costs.

2- If it comes to the knowledge of the bailiff after the seizure has been carried out that the seizure has exceeded the extent specified in paragraph 1 above, the bailiff shall revoke the seizure to the extent of such excess.

#### Article 392.- Prohibition of seizure where no surplus is expected

1- A bailiff shall not seize movables if it is estimated that no surplus will remain from the proceeds of the sale of the seized movables after paying the execution costs.

2- A bailiff shall revoke the seizure if it is estimated that no surplus will remain from the proceeds of the sale of the seized movables after paying the claims that have priority over the claim of the execution creditor and the execution costs.

#### Article 393.- Revocation of seizure where sale is expected to be impossible

A bailiff may revoke the seizure of movables that have already been seized if, despite having attempted to sell them by a proper method, they could not be sold and it is estimated that they cannot be sold in the future.

#### Article 394.- Demand for distribution by holders of preferential rights, etc.

A person who has a preferential right or a pledge may demand a distribution by submitting documents proving such right.

#### Article 395.- Method of sale

1- In order to sell the seized movables, a bailiff shall sell them by auction or by sealed bidding.

2- Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph 1 above, if deemed appropriate by taking into account the type and quantity of the movables, etc., a bailiff may carry out the sale of the seized movables by methods other than auction or sealed bidding, or may have a person other than a bailiff carry out the sale of the seized movables, with the permission of the first instance court where the bailiff is located.

3- A bailiff shall give public notice concerning the movables to be sold and the method of sale of such movables by posting it on the court's notice board.

4- The execution debtor may not make an offer to buy those movables.

5- If the bailiff permits the purchase, the purchaser shall pay the price immediately.

6- If those movables are put up for sale again because the purchaser failed to pay the price, the previous purchaser may not make another offer to buy.

7- If movables of high value have been seized, the bailiff shall appoint an appraiser and have said appraiser appraise those movables.

#### Article 396.- Maintenance of order at the place of sale

A bailiff may restrict a person who commits an act that interferes with the proper implementation of the sale, such as obstructing other persons from making an offer to buy or colluding to improperly lower the sale price, or a person who caused such an act to be committed, from entering the place of sale, or may expel such person from the place of sale, or may refuse to accept an offer to buy from such person.

#### Article 397.- Sale while execution is stayed

1- In case a document specified in item (a) or item...

(b) of paragraph 2 of Article 370 (Stay and revocation of compulsory execution) of this Code, if there is a concern that the sale price of the seized movables will decline significantly or if the costs of keeping the movables are disproportionately high, the bailiff may sell the seized movables.

2- If the seized movables are sold according to the provisions of paragraph 1 above, the bailiff shall deposit the proceeds of the sale with the first instance court where the bailiff is located.

#### Article 398.- Scope of creditors entitled to receive distribution

Creditors entitled to receive a distribution are the persons specified in each of the following items:

a- The execution creditor.

b- Creditors who have demanded a distribution by the time the bailiff receives delivery of the money, in case of proceeds of sale.

c- Creditors who have demanded a distribution by the time the money is seized, in case of seized money.

#### Article 399.- Distribution by the bailiff

1- In case there is only one creditor, or in case there are multiple creditors and the proceeds of sale or seized money are sufficient to satisfy all the claims of each creditor and the execution costs, the bailiff shall distribute the money to the creditors and deliver any remaining balance to the execution debtor.

2- Except for cases specified in paragraph 1 above, upon receiving the proceeds of sale or seizing money, the bailiff shall fix a date within two (2) weeks for consultation on the distribution of said proceeds of sale or seized money, and shall notify the creditors of the time and place.

...of each creditor. If the creditors have reached an agreement, the bailiff shall distribute the money in accordance with that agreement.

3- In case a document specified in items (a) through (g) of paragraph 1 of Article 370 (Stay and revocation of compulsory execution) of this Code has been submitted after the purchaser has paid the price, if there are creditors entitled to receive a distribution other than the execution creditor, the bailiff shall distribute the money to those creditors.

4- In case a document specified in item (a) or (b) of paragraph 2 of Article 370 (Stay and revocation of compulsory execution) of this Code has been submitted after the purchaser has paid the price, the bailiff shall also distribute the money.

5- For the purpose of distribution, a claim for which the due date has not yet arrived shall be deemed to have fallen due.

#### Article 400.- Deposit of money by the bailiff

1- In case a distribution is carried out according to the provisions of paragraph 1 or 2 of Article 399 (Distribution by the bailiff) of this Code, if any of the following items applies to the claim of a creditor entitled to receive a distribution, the bailiff shall distribute the amount corresponding to such distribution to the first instance court where the bailiff is located for deposit, and shall report the fact to said court:

a- If the claim is subject to a condition precedent or has an uncertain due date.

b- If the claim is that of a provisional seizure creditor.

c- If a document specified in item (a) of paragraph 2 of Article 370 (Stay and revocation of compulsory execution) of this Code has been submitted.

(d) If an authenticated copy of a ruling ordering a temporary prohibition on the exercise of a preferential right or a pledge relating to the claim has been submitted.

2- A bailiff shall deposit the amount corresponding to the distribution to a creditor who failed to appear to receive the distribution, with the first instance court where the bailiff is located.

#### Article 401.- Notification to the first instance court

In case no agreement is reached through the consultation specified in paragraph 2 of Article 399 (Distribution by the bailiff) of this Code, the bailiff shall report the fact to the first instance court where the bailiff is located.

### Section 3
### Execution against Claims and Other Property Rights

#### Article 402.- Meaning of execution against claims and execution court

1- Compulsory execution against claims for the payment of money or the delivery of movables shall be referred to as "execution against claims."

2- A debtor of a claim to be seized shall be referred to as a "third party debtor."

3- In execution against claims, the first instance court having jurisdiction over the location of the execution debtor specified in Article 8 (Jurisdiction determined by residence, etc.) of this Code shall be the execution court; provided that if there is no such location, the first instance court having jurisdiction over the location of the third party debtor specified in Article 8 shall be the execution court.

4- In case another seizure order has been issued against a claim that has already been seized, if the execution courts that issued the seizure orders are different, an execution court may transfer the case to another execution court.

#### Article 403.- Seizure order

1- A motion for execution against a claim shall be filed in the execution court.

2- In a motion for execution against a claim, in addition to each of the items specified in paragraph 2 of Article 349 (Method of filing motion for compulsory execution) of this Code, the third party debtor shall also be specified.

3- In case execution is sought for only a part of the claim, the execution creditor shall clearly specify the scope of the execution in the motion for execution against a claim.

4- Execution against a claim shall be initiated by a seizure order issued by an execution court.

5- In a seizure order, the execution court shall prohibit the execution debtor from collecting the claim or making any other disposition, and shall prohibit the third party debtor from making payment to the execution debtor.

6- A seizure order shall be issued without examination of the execution debtor or the third party debtor.

7- A seizure order shall be served on the execution debtor and the third party debtor.

8- Seizure shall become effective when the seizure order is served on the third party debtor.

9- When the seizure order has been served on the execution debtor and the third party debtor, the court clerk shall notify the execution creditor of that fact and the date of service.

10- If a seizure order against a claim secured by a hypothec or other registered security right has become effective, upon motion of the execution creditor, the court clerk shall request the competent authority to record the fact that the claim has been seized.

11- An appeal (Bandung Toas) may be filed against a ruling on a motion for a seizure order.

#### Article 404.- Scope of seizure

1- An execution court may issue a seizure order against the entire claim to be seized.

2- If the value of the seized claim exceeds the amount of the claim of the execution creditor and the execution costs, the execution court shall not seize any other claim.

3- In case part of a claim has been seized or has been provisionally seized, if another seizure order is issued which covers the remainder of the claim, the effect of each seizure or provisional seizure shall extend to the entire claim. This provision shall also apply to the effect of seizure in case the entire claim has already been seized or provisionally seized and another seizure order is issued for part of that claim.

#### Article 405.- Demand for statement from the third party debtor

1- Upon motion of the execution creditor, when serving the seizure order, the execution court shall demand the third party debtor to submit a statement within two (2) weeks from the date of service of the seizure order concerning the following matters:

a- Whether the seized claim exists, and if it does, its type, its amount, and its content.

b- Whether the third party debtor intends to make payment, and if so, the extent of such payment or the reason for refusing to make payment.

c- Whether there are persons having priority over the execution creditor...

...with respect to the relating claim, and if so, the type of the right of such person and the scope of the priority.

(d) Whether there is any other seizure or provisional seizure against the relating claim by other creditors.

2- If the third party debtor, either intentionally or through negligence, fails to submit a statement in response to the demand specified in paragraph 1 above, or submits a false statement, the third party debtor shall be liable to compensate for any damage resulting therefrom.

#### Article 406.- Delivery of document certifying claim

1- If there is a document certifying the seized claim, the execution debtor shall deliver that document to the execution creditor.

2- Based on the seizure order, the execution creditor may obtain the delivery of the document specified in paragraph 1 above by way of compulsory execution for delivery of movables as specified in Article 525 (Compulsory execution for delivery of movables) of this Code.

#### Article 407.- Seizure of recurring claims

The effect of a seizure of a claim for salary or other recurring payments shall extend to the payments to be received after the seizure, up to the amount of the claim of the execution creditor and the execution costs.

#### Article 408.- Notice of withdrawal of motion, etc.

1- If a motion for execution against a claim is withdrawn, the court clerk shall notify the third party debtor upon whom the seizure order has been served of such fact.

2- If a ruling to revoke the execution procedure against a claim has been issued...

...the court clerk shall notify the third party debtor upon whom the seizure order has been served of such fact.

#### Article 409.- Demand for distribution

1- A creditor who has an authenticated copy of an enforceable title of execution and a creditor who provides documentation proving that he/she has a preferential right may demand a distribution.

2- When a demand for distribution specified in paragraph 1 above is made, the execution court shall serve the documentation proving such demand on the third party debtor.

3- An appeal (Bandung Toas) may be filed against a ruling to dismiss a demand for distribution.

#### Article 410.- Collection of monetary claims

1- An execution creditor who has seized a monetary claim may collect such claim after one (1) week has elapsed from the date on which the seizure order was served on the execution debtor; provided that the execution creditor may not receive payment exceeding the amount of his/her claim and the execution costs.

2- When the execution creditor has received payment from the third party debtor, his/her claim and the execution costs shall be deemed to have been satisfied to the extent of the amount of the payment received.

3- Upon receiving the payment specified in paragraph 2 above, the execution creditor shall notify the execution court of that fact.

4- The execution creditor shall be liable to the execution debtor to compensate for any damage resulting from his/her failure to exercise the seized claim.

#### Article 411.- Deposit of money by the third party debtor

1- When a monetary claim has been seized, the third party debtor may deposit an amount corresponding to the seized portion of the claim or the entire amount of the claim with the execution court.

2- In the following cases, the third party debtor shall deposit the amount specified below with the execution court:

a- If another seizure order or a provisional seizure order exceeding the remaining portion of the claim has been issued and served on the third party debtor before a lawsuit specified in paragraph 1 of Article 412 (Action for collection) of this Code is served on the third party debtor: the entire amount of the claim.

b- If a document proving that a demand for distribution has been made is served: the seized portion of the claim.

3- When the third party debtor has made a deposit according to the provisions of paragraph 1 or 2 above, he/she shall notify the execution court of that fact.

#### Article 412.- Action for collection

1- If the execution creditor has filed an action against the third party debtor seeking payment of the seized claim, the court where the action is pending may, upon motion of the third party debtor, order other creditors who have seized said claim to join the action as co-plaintiffs by a ruling issued before the end of oral arguments.

2- The effect of a judgment in the action specified in paragraph 1 shall extend to the execution creditors who were ordered to join but failed to do so.

3- In an action specified in paragraph 1, if the third party debtor is obligated to make a deposit according to the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 411 (Deposit of money by the third party debtor) of this Code, and if the third party debtor acknowledges the plaintiff's claim, the court where the action is pending shall state in the definitive ruling of the judgment that the payment relating to the claim shall be made by way of deposit.

#### Article 413.- Order to sell

1- If the seized claim is subject to a condition or a time limit, or is difficult to collect due to being linked to a counter-performance or other reasons, the execution court may, upon motion of the execution creditor, issue an order to have a bailiff sell such claim by a method fixed by the execution court and deliver the proceeds of the sale to the execution court.

2- In case an order is to be issued according to the provisions of paragraph 1 above, the execution court shall examine the execution debtor; provided that this shall not apply if the execution debtor is in a foreign country or his/her residence is unknown.

3- If it is estimated that no surplus will remain from the proceeds of the sale of the seized claim after paying the claims having priority over the claim of the execution creditor and the execution costs, the execution court shall not issue the order specified in paragraph 1 above.

4- An appeal (Bandung Toas) may be filed against a ruling on the motion specified in paragraph 1 above.

5- A ruling on the motion specified in paragraph 1 above shall not become effective until it becomes final and binding.

6- In case an order specified in paragraph 1 above is issued, if deemed necessary, the execution court may appoint an appraiser and order said appraiser to...

...carry out an appraisal of the claim.

7- A bailiff shall not sell the claim if it is estimated that no surplus will remain from the proceeds of the sale of the seized claim after paying the claims having priority over the claim of the execution creditor and the execution costs.

8- In the sale specified in paragraph 1 above, the execution debtor may not make an offer to buy.

9- If the seized claim is sold, the bailiff shall notify the third party debtor of the assignment by a document with a fixed date, on behalf of the execution debtor.

10- If the sale has been completed according to the provisions of paragraph 1 above, with respect to the claim specified in paragraph 10 of Article 403 (Seizure order) of this Code, the execution court shall, upon motion of the purchaser, request the competent authority to register the transfer of the hypothec or other security right to the purchaser who has acquired the claim, and to cancel the registration made according to the provisions of paragraph 10 of Article 403.

#### Article 414.- Execution of seizure order against claim for delivery of movables

1- An execution creditor who has seized a claim for delivery of movables may demand the third party debtor to deliver those movables to the bailiff who accepted the motion of the execution creditor, after one (1) week has elapsed from the date on which the seizure order was served on the execution debtor.

2- Upon receiving the delivery of movables, the bailiff shall sell them by the method for selling movables and deliver the proceeds to the execution court.

#### Article 415.- Scope of creditors entitled to receive distribution

Creditors entitled to receive a distribution shall be the creditors who have carried out a seizure or...

...or a provisional seizure, or have demanded a distribution by the time specified below:

a- The time when the third party debtor has made a deposit according to the provisions of paragraph 1 or 2 of Article 411 (Deposit of money by the third party debtor) of this Code.

b- The time when a complaint for an action for collection is served on the third party debtor.

c- The time when the bailiff receives the proceeds of the sale under an order to sell.

d- The time when the bailiff receives the delivery of movables in case of seizure of a claim for delivery of movables.

#### Article 416.- Compulsory execution against other property rights

Compulsory execution against property rights other than real estate, ships, movables, and claims shall be carried out in accordance with the method for execution against claims, unless otherwise provided.

### Section 4
### Execution against Real Estate

#### Subsection 1
#### Object of execution against real estate, execution organ, method of execution, method of seizure, method of public notice, and preservative measures

#### Article 417.- Object and method of compulsory execution against real estate

1- In this Section 4, real estate refers to registered land, registered buildings, undivided shares of such land or buildings, registered perpetual leases, and usufructs, as well as undivided shares of such rights.

2- Compulsory execution against real estate shall be carried out by the method of compulsory sale.

...accompanied by an authenticated copy of an enforceable title of execution and the following documents:

a- For registered real estate: a certified copy of the register and, in case a person other than the execution debtor is registered as the owner, documents proving that the real estate belongs to the execution debtor.

b- For unregistered land: documents proving that the land belongs to the execution debtor.

#### Article 418.- Execution court

In compulsory execution against real estate, the following courts shall be the execution courts:

a- If registered land or buildings are the objects: the first instance court having jurisdiction over the location.

b- If undivided shares of registered land or buildings, registered perpetual leases, registered usufructs, or undivided shares of such rights are the objects: the first instance court having jurisdiction over the location of the registration.

#### Article 419.- Commencement order, etc.

1- When initiating the procedure for compulsory sale, the execution court shall issue a commencement order for compulsory sale and shall declare in said order that the real estate is seized for the benefit of the execution creditor.

2- The commencement order specified in paragraph 1 above shall be served on the execution debtor.

3- An appeal (Bandung Toas) may be filed against a ruling to dismiss a motion for compulsory execution against real estate.

#### Article 420.- Request for registration of seizure, etc.

1- If a commencement order for compulsory sale has been issued, the court clerk shall immediately request the competent authority to register the seizure based on said commencement order.

2- If the registration of seizure has been made based on the request specified in paragraph 1 above, the competent authority shall send a certified copy of the register to the court clerk.

#### Article 421.- Effect of seizure

1- The effect of seizure of real estate shall extend to things attached to and forming part of the real estate, as well as the natural fruits and appurtenances of the real estate.

2- Seizure shall become effective when the commencement order for compulsory sale is served on the execution debtor; provided that if the registration of seizure is made before the commencement order is served, the seizure shall become effective at the time of the registration.

3- Seizure shall not prevent the execution debtor from using or taking the fruits of the real estate by normal methods; provided that this shall not apply in case a claim for rent of the real estate is seized according to the provisions of Article 516 (Seizure of claims for rent of real estate) or in case a preservative measure is ordered according to the provisions of Article 429 (Preservative measures) of this Code.

#### Article 422.- Time limit for demand for distribution and change of such time limit

1- When the seizure has become effective based on the commencement order for compulsory sale, the execution court shall fix a time limit for a demand for distribution, by taking into account the time necessary for preparing the detailed description of the property...

...unless a duplicate commencement order for compulsory sale has been issued according to paragraph 1 of Article 424 (Duplicate commencement order) of this Code.

2- When the time limit for a demand for distribution has been fixed, the execution court shall give public notice concerning the issuance of a commencement order and the time limit for a demand for distribution, and shall demand the following persons to report to the execution court concerning whether they have a claim and, if so, the cause and the amount of the claim, before the time limit for a demand for distribution:

a- Creditors who registered a provisional seizure before the registration of seizure based on the first commencement order for compulsory sale against the real estate.

b- Creditors having a registered security right before the registration of seizure based on the first commencement order for compulsory sale against the real estate.

c- Competent authorities for taxes or other public charges.

3- In the case specified in paragraph 2 above, the execution court shall demand the persons specified in item (b) of paragraph 2 to report on each of the following matters:

a- Whether they intend to exercise their security right by the time limit for a demand for distribution.

b- That they must file a motion to exercise the security right by the time limit for a demand for distribution if they intend to exercise it.

4- If a ruling to permit the sale is not issued within three (3) months from the time limit for a demand for distribution, the execution court may change the time limit for a demand for distribution.

5- If the time limit for a demand for distribution is changed...

...pursuant to the provisions of paragraph 4 above, the execution court shall give public notice of such change.

#### Article 423.- Duty to report of persons demanded to report on claims

1- A person specified in item (a), (b), or (c) of paragraph 2 of Article 422 (Time limit for demand for distribution and change of such time limit) of this Code who has been demanded to report pursuant to the provisions of paragraph 2 or 3 of Article 422 shall report on the matters specified in the demand before the time limit for a demand for distribution.

2- If there is any change in the principal amount of the claim relating to the report specified in paragraph 1 above, the person who made the report shall report such change.

3- If the person who is required to report pursuant to the provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 above fails to make a report or makes a false report, either intentionally or through negligence, such person shall be liable to compensate for any damage resulting therefrom.

#### Article 424.- Duplicate commencement order

1- If another motion for compulsory execution is filed against the real estate for which a commencement order for compulsory sale has already been issued, and if such motion is accepted, the execution court shall issue a new commencement order for compulsory sale. In this case, the execution court shall notify the execution creditor relating to the previous commencement order of such fact.

2- If the motion for compulsory sale relating to the previous commencement order is withdrawn, or if the procedure for compulsory sale based on the previous commencement order is revoked, the execution court shall continue the procedure based on the...

...subsequent commencement order.

3- In the case specified in paragraph 2 above, if the subsequent commencement order for compulsory sale is based on a motion filed after the time limit for a demand for distribution, the execution court shall fix a new time limit for a demand for distribution. In this case, the execution court does not need to issue a demand pursuant to the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 422 (Time limit for demand for distribution and change of such time limit) to the persons who have already made a report specified in paragraph 1 of Article 423 (Duty to report of persons demanded to report on claims) of this Code.

4- If the procedure for compulsory sale based on the previous commencement order is stayed, the execution court shall notify the execution creditor relating to the subsequent commencement order of such fact. In this case, the execution court may issue a ruling to continue the procedure based on the subsequent commencement order for compulsory sale which was based on a motion for compulsory execution filed before the time limit for a demand for distribution, upon motion. Provided that this shall not apply if any change would occur to the matters specified in items 1 to 3 of Article 431 (Rights to continue to exist and rights to be extinguished after sale) of this Code if the compulsory sale relating to the previous commencement order were revoked.

5- If a ruling specified in paragraph 4 above is issued, the execution court shall notify the execution debtor of such fact.

6- An appeal (Bandung Toas) may be filed against a ruling to dismiss a motion specified in paragraph 4 above.

#### Article 425.- Duplicate commencement order for exercise of security right and execution against real estate

The provisions from paragraph 1 to paragraph 3 of Article 424 (Duplicate commencement order) of this Code shall also apply in case a motion for compulsory execution is filed against the real estate for which a commencement order for compulsory sale for the exercise of a security right has already been issued.

#### Article 426.- Demand for distribution

1- The following persons may demand a distribution:

(a) A creditor who has an authenticated copy of an enforceable title of execution which allows for compulsory execution according to paragraph 1 of Article 354 (Necessity of execution clause and execution-clause-granting organ) of this Code; provided that this shall not apply to a creditor having a real security right which would continue to exist after the sale of the real estate according to the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 431 (Rights to continue to exist and rights to be extinguished after sale) of this Code.

(b) A creditor who registered a provisional seizure after the registration of seizure based on the commencement order for compulsory sale.

(c) A creditor who provides documentation proving that he/she has a preferential right.

2- A demand for distribution shall be made in writing, stating the cause and the amount of the claim, including interest and other incidental claims.

3- If a demand for distribution is made, the execution court shall notify the execution creditor and the execution debtor of such fact.

4- An appeal (Bandung Toas) may be filed against a ruling to dismiss a demand for distribution.

#### Article 427.- Revocation of procedure for compulsory sale due to loss of real estate, etc.

If it is clear that the real estate has been lost or another circumstance has occurred that prevents the transfer of the real estate by sale, the execution court shall revoke the procedure for...

...compulsory sale.

#### Article 428.- Request for cancellation of registration of seizure

1- If a motion for compulsory sale is withdrawn, or if a ruling to revoke the procedure for compulsory sale has become effective, the court clerk shall request the competent authority to cancel the registration of seizure based on the commencement order.

2- The costs necessary for the request specified in paragraph 1 above shall be borne by the execution creditor who withdrew the motion or the execution creditor for whose benefit the ruling to revoke the procedure was issued.

#### Article 429.- Preservative measures

1- If the execution debtor or the possessor of the real estate commits an act that significantly reduces the value of the real estate or makes it difficult to sell or deliver the real estate, or if there is a concern that such person will commit such act, the execution court may, upon motion of the execution creditor, the highest bidder, or the purchaser, and with or without security, prohibit such person from committing such act, or order such person to perform a specific act, or order such person to surrender the possession of the real estate to a bailiff for storage and management. Provided that a person who is not the execution debtor may be ordered to surrender the possession of the real estate only if his/her right to possess cannot be asserted against the purchaser.

2- In case a ruling is to be issued according to the provisions of paragraph 1 above against a possessor other than the execution debtor, the execution court shall examine said possessor; provided that this shall not apply if there is an urgent necessity to issue the ruling.

3- If circumstances have changed, the execution court may revoke or modify the ruling...

...specified in paragraph 1 above, upon motion.

4- An appeal (Bandung Toas) may be filed against a ruling on the motion specified in paragraph 1 or 3 above.

5- A ruling specified in paragraph 1 above may be executed even before it is served on the other party.

6- The costs necessary for the motion or execution of the ruling specified in paragraph 1 above shall be borne by the party who applied for the motion, but they shall be deemed to be for the common benefit of the procedure for compulsory sale of the real estate.

#### Subsection 2
#### Conditions of sale

#### Article 430.- Appraisal

1- The execution court shall appoint an appraiser and order him/her to appraise the real estate by investigating its condition, relationship of possession, and other current circumstances.

2- In order to request the assistance of a bailiff pursuant to the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 338 (Assistance in performance of duties of bailiff, etc.) of this Code, the appraiser shall obtain permission from the execution court.

3- In carrying out an investigation for appraisal, the appraiser may enter the real estate and ask questions or demand the production of documents from the execution debtor or the third party who possesses the real estate.

4- If necessary, the bailiff whose assistance is requested by the appraiser pursuant to the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 338 (Assistance in performance of duties of bailiff, etc.) of this Code may take necessary measures to open closed doors in order to enter the real estate pursuant to the provisions of paragraph 3 above.

5- The appraiser shall submit the results of the investigation concerning the current condition and the appraisal of the real estate to the execution court in writing.

#### Article 431.- Rights to continue to exist and rights to be extinguished after sale

1- Among the real security rights, real rights of use and usufruct, and leases existing on the real estate, those that can be asserted against the seizure by the execution creditor shall continue to exist after the sale.

2- Rights on the real estate other than those specified in paragraph 1 above shall be extinguished by the sale.

3- Seizures, provisional seizures against the real estate, and provisional dispositions which cannot be asserted against the execution creditor or the provisional seizure creditor shall lose their effect by the sale.

4- If interested persons report an agreement that differs from the provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 above before the minimum sale price is fixed, the change of rights on the real estate by the sale shall depend on such agreement.

#### Article 432.- Ruling to fix minimum sale price, etc.

1- The execution court shall fix the minimum sale price based on the appraisal of the appraiser.

2- If deemed necessary, the execution court may change the minimum sale price.

#### Article 433.- Collective sale

If it is deemed appropriate for one purchaser to purchase the real estate together with other real estate as a whole due to the relationship in their use, even if such pieces of real estate have different execution creditors or execution debtors...

...the execution court may order a collective sale. Provided that if it is possible to satisfy all execution creditors' claims and execution costs by selling one of the several pieces of real estate at the minimum sale price, for which a commencement order for compulsory sale has been issued based on a single motion, the execution court may order a collective sale only if the execution debtor consents.

#### Article 434.- Detailed description of property

1- The execution court shall prepare a detailed description of the property and keep a copy of said detailed description together with a copy of the appraisal report at the execution court for public inspection. The detailed description of the property shall state the following matters:

(a) Description of the real estate.

(b) Rights on the real estate which would continue to exist after the sale according to the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 431 (Rights to continue to exist and rights to be extinguished after sale) of this Code and provisional dispositions which would not lose their effect by the sale.

2- If deemed necessary to prepare the detailed description of the property specified in paragraph 1 above, the execution court may order a bailiff to investigate the current condition of the real estate, such as the shape of the real estate, the status of its possession, the structure of the building if any, etc., and to report the results of the investigation in writing.

3- In carrying out an investigation concerning the current condition of the real estate according to the order specified in paragraph 2 above, a bailiff shall have the same authority as an appraiser specified in paragraph 3 of Article 430 (Appraisal) of this Code. If necessary, the bailiff may take necessary measures...

...to open closed doors.

#### Article 435.- Measures in case of concern about lack of surplus

1- If the execution court finds that no surplus will remain from the minimum sale price of the real estate after paying the execution costs, the execution court shall notify the execution creditor of such fact.

2- The execution court shall revoke the procedure for compulsory sale relating to the motion of the execution creditor if the execution creditor fails to perform any of the following acts:

(a) Making an offer to purchase the real estate at a proposed price in case there is no offer to purchase at a price equal to or exceeding the proposed price. Provided that such offer shall be made within one (1) week from the date on which the execution creditor received the notice specified in paragraph 1 above, and the proposed price shall exceed the estimated execution costs.

(b) Providing security equal to the proposed price specified in item (a) above.

#### Subsection 3
#### Method of sale

#### Article 436.- Method of sale, etc.

1- Sale of real estate shall be conducted by the method of bidding by envelope or oral bidding.

2- The execution court may determine a method of sale other than bidding by envelope or oral bidding, only if it is not possible to sell by bidding by envelope or oral bidding.

3- If the sale is conducted by bidding by envelope or oral bidding...

...the execution court shall fix the date and place of sale and order a bailiff to conduct the sale.

4- In the case specified in paragraph 3 above, the court clerk shall give public notice of the description of the real estate to be sold, the minimum sale price, and the date and place of sale.

5- The provisions of Article 386 (Maintenance of order at place of sale) of this Code shall apply mutatis mutandis to the case specified in paragraph 3 above.

#### Article 437.- Security for offer to purchase

1- A person who intends to make an offer to purchase real estate shall provide security in an amount equal to one-tenth (1/10) of the minimum sale price. Provided that if deemed appropriate, the execution court may fix the amount of security at a higher amount.

2- If an offer to purchase is made by a person other than the highest bidder, the execution court shall immediately return the security provided according to the provisions of paragraph 1 above after the completion of the sale conducted based on the provisions of Article 436 (Method of sale, etc.) of this Code.

#### Article 438.- Prohibition of offer to purchase by execution debtor

The execution debtor may not make an offer to purchase.

#### Article 439.- Determination of highest bidder, etc.

1- When the bidding by envelope or oral bidding is completed, the bailiff shall announce the end of the bidding date after determining the highest bidder and announcing the name or trade name of the purchaser and the bidding price.

2- If the high bidder who bid by envelope...

...is more than one person, the bailiff shall determine the highest bidder by having such persons bid again by envelope. In this case, the bidders may not bid at a price lower than their previous bid.

3- If the bidders specified in paragraph 2 above do not bid again, the highest bidder shall be determined by drawing lots. This provision shall also apply to cases where there are more than one person who bid by envelope at the same highest price in the re-bidding specified in paragraph 2 above.

4- When the bidding by envelope or oral bidding is conducted, the bailiff shall prepare a record of bidding by envelope or oral bidding, stating the following matters, and immediately submit it to the execution court:

(a) Description of the real estate.

(b) Date of the bidding by envelope or oral bidding.

(c) Description of the highest bidder and his/her representative.

(d) Purchase price of the highest bidder.

(e) Facts and circumstances if the highest bidder could not be determined.

(f) The reason and the measures taken if measures specified in Article 386 (Maintenance of order at place of sale) of this Code were taken.

5- The bailiff shall have the highest bidder or his/her representative sign the record of bidding by envelope or oral bidding.

#### Article 440.- Date for ruling on sale

1- The execution court shall open a date for a ruling on the sale and announce whether the sale is permitted or not.

2- At the date fixed for a ruling on the sale, the execution...

...court shall notify interested persons of such date.

#### Article 441.- Statement of opinion on whether sale should be permitted or not

An interested person may state his/her opinion concerning the grounds specified in the items of paragraph 2 of Article 442 (Permission or non-permission of sale) of this Code which affect his/her rights, at the date fixed for a ruling on the sale.

#### Article 442.- Permission or non-permission of sale

1- Except for the cases specified in paragraph 2 below, the execution court shall issue a ruling to permit the sale.

2- The execution court shall issue a ruling not to permit the sale if it finds that any of the following grounds exists:

(a) The procedure for compulsory sale should not have been commenced or continued.

(b) The highest bidder does not have the qualification or capacity to purchase the real estate, or his/her representative does not have the authority to do so.

(c) A motion not to permit the sale has been filed pursuant to the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 445 (Motion not to permit sale, etc. in case real estate is damaged) of this Code.

(d) There is a serious error in the procedure for the ruling to fix the minimum sale price.

(e) There is a serious error in the procedure for the sale.

#### Article 443.- Measure in case of excessive sale

1- In case several pieces of real estate are sold, if the proposed purchase price of one or more of said pieces of real estate is estimated to be sufficient to satisfy the claims of each creditor and all execution costs...

...the execution court shall reserve its ruling to permit the sale of other pieces of real estate.

2- In the case specified in paragraph 1 above, if there are several pieces of real estate for which the proposed purchase price is estimated to be sufficient to satisfy the claims of each creditor and all execution costs, or if there are several groups of real estate for which the total proposed purchase price is estimated to be sufficient, the execution court shall first hear the opinion of the execution debtor as to which real estate or group of real estate should be sold.

3- The high bidder for the real estate for which the ruling to permit the sale is reserved according to the provisions of paragraph 1 above may withdraw his/her offer to purchase by notifying the execution court.

4- When the purchase price has been paid for the real estate for which the ruling to permit the sale was issued, the execution court shall revoke the procedure for compulsory sale relating to the real estate specified in paragraph 3 above.

#### Article 444.- Appeal against ruling to permit or not permit sale

1- An interested person may file an appeal (Bandung Toas) against a ruling to permit or not permit the sale, only if such ruling causes damage to his/her rights.

2- An appeal against a ruling to permit the sale shall be based on any of the grounds specified in the items of paragraph 2 of Article 442 (Permission or non-permission of sale) of this Code or a serious error in the procedure for said ruling.

3- If deemed necessary, the court of appeal may determine the other party to the appellant.

4- A ruling to permit or not permit the sale shall not become effective unless...

...it becomes final and binding.

#### Article 445.- Motion not to permit sale, etc. in case real estate is damaged

1- If the real estate is significantly damaged after the offer to purchase due to natural disaster or other cause not attributable to the high bidder or the purchaser, the high bidder may file a motion to the execution court not to permit the sale before the ruling to permit the sale is issued, or the purchaser may file a motion to the execution court to revoke the ruling to permit the sale before the purchase price is paid after the ruling to permit the sale was issued.

2- An appeal (Bandung Toas) may be filed against a ruling on a motion to revoke the ruling to permit the sale specified in paragraph 1 above.

3- A ruling to revoke the ruling to permit the sale specified in paragraph 1 above shall not become effective until it becomes final and binding.

#### Article 446.- Withdrawal of motion for compulsory sale after offer to purchase

In order to withdraw a motion for compulsory sale after an offer to purchase has been made, the execution creditor shall obtain the consent of the high bidder or the purchaser.

#### Subsection 4
#### Effect of sale

#### Article 447.- Payment of purchase price

1- When a ruling to permit the sale becomes final and binding, the purchaser shall pay the purchase price to the execution court by the time fixed by the execution court. This time shall be within one (1) month from the date on which the ruling to permit the sale became final and binding.

2- The security provided by the purchaser for the offer to purchase shall be applied to the purchase price.

3- If the purchaser is a creditor entitled to receive a distribution from the proceeds of the sale, he/she may pay the purchase price by deducting the amount to be received as distribution on the date of distribution, upon motion to the execution court before the end of the date for a ruling on the sale. In this case, if any objection is raised concerning the amount of distribution to be received by the purchaser, the purchaser shall immediately pay the amount corresponding to the contested portion.

4- The purchaser shall acquire the real estate at the time he/she has paid the purchase price.

#### Article 448.- Request for registration based on payment of purchase price

1- When the purchaser has paid the purchase price, the court clerk shall request the competent authority to perform the following registrations and cancellations of registrations:

(a) Registration of the transfer of the rights acquired by the purchaser.

(b) Cancellation of registration concerning the rights that were extinguished or lost their effect by the sale.

(c) Cancellation of registration concerning the provisional dispositions that lost their effect by the sale.

(d) Cancellation of registration concerning the seizure or provisional seizure.

2- In making the request specified in paragraph 1 above, the court clerk shall attach an authenticated copy of the ruling to permit the sale to the written request.

3- The costs necessary for the request specified in paragraph 1 above shall be borne by the purchaser.

#### Article 449.- Responsibility of purchaser

1- Concerning the security rights which continue to exist after the sale pursuant to the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 431 (Rights to continue to exist and rights to be extinguished after sale) of this Code...

...after the sale) of this Code, if the execution debtor is the debtor of the claim secured by such security rights, the purchaser shall be responsible for paying such claim.

2- In the case specified in paragraph 1 above, if the security right which continues to exist after the sale is a floating mortgage (Hyphoteque), the purchaser shall be responsible for paying the claim secured by said floating mortgage up to the maximum amount.

#### Article 450.- Ruling to order delivery of real estate

1- The execution court may, upon motion of the purchaser who has paid the purchase price, issue a ruling to order the execution debtor or the possessor of the real estate to deliver said real estate to the purchaser. Provided that this shall not apply to a person who is recognized as possessing the real estate based on a right that can be asserted against the purchaser according to the records of the compulsory execution case.

2- A motion specified in paragraph 1 above may not be filed after six (6) months from the date on which the purchaser paid the purchase price.

3- In case a ruling is to be issued against a possessor other than the execution debtor according to the provisions of paragraph 1 above, the execution court shall examine said possessor.

4- An appeal (Bandung Toas) may be filed against a ruling on the motion specified in paragraph 1 above.

5- A ruling specified in paragraph 1 above shall not become effective until it becomes final and binding.

#### Article 451.- Effect of non-payment of purchase price

1- If the purchaser fails to pay the purchase price, the ruling to permit the sale shall lose its effect. In this case, the purchaser may not demand the return of the security provided...

...as security for the offer to purchase.

2- In the case specified in paragraph 1 above, the execution court shall conduct a re-sale.

3- In the re-sale specified in paragraph 2 above, the purchaser specified in paragraph 1 above may not make an offer to purchase.

#### Article 452.- Proceeds of sale

1- The following money shall be treated as the proceeds of the sale for distribution:

(a) Purchase price of the real estate.

(b) The money that the purchaser may not demand the return of pursuant to the provisions of the second sentence of paragraph 1 of Article 451 (Effect of non-payment of purchase price) of this Code.

2- In case several pieces of real estate were sold as a whole pursuant to the provisions of Article 433 (Collective sale) of this Code, if it is necessary to determine the price of each piece of real estate, such price shall be the amount obtained by dividing the total purchase price in proportion to the minimum sale price of each piece of real estate. This provision shall also apply to the allocation of the execution costs to each piece of real estate.

#### Article 453.- Scope of creditors entitled to receive distribution

The creditors entitled to receive a distribution from the proceeds of the sale shall be the following persons:

(a) Execution creditors who filed a motion for compulsory sale or a motion for compulsory sale by exercise of a general preferential right by the time limit for demand for distribution.

(b) Creditors who filed a demand for distribution by the time limit for demand for distribution.

(c) Creditors who registered a provisional seizure before the registration of the seizure which is the basis of the first commencement order for compulsory sale.

### Section 5
### Compulsory execution against ships

#### Article 454.- Meaning of ship, etc.

1- In this Book VI and Book VII, a "ship" refers to a vessel with a gross tonnage equal to or exceeding 20 (twenty) tons, even if not registered in the Kingdom of Cambodia, and a vessel with a gross tonnage under 20 (twenty) tons that has been registered in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

2- In this Book and Book VII, "certificate of ship's nationality, etc." refers to the document certifying the nationality of the ship and other documents necessary for the legal operation of the ship.

#### Article 455.- Motion for compulsory execution against ship and method of execution

1- Compulsory execution against a ship shall be conducted by the execution court by the method of compulsory sale.

2- In a motion for compulsory execution against a ship, in addition to the matters specified in paragraph 2 of Article 349 (Method of filing motion for compulsory execution) of this Code, the execution creditor shall state the location of the ship, the name of the master or the person in charge of the ship, and the current location of said master or person in charge.

3- In the motion specified in paragraph 2 above, in addition to an authenticated copy of the title of execution with an execution clause, the execution creditor shall attach the documents specified below:

(a) For a registered Cambodian ship: an authenticated copy of the registry.

(b) For an unregistered Cambodian ship or a ship other than...

...Cambodian ship: documents certifying that the ship is a ship specified in paragraph 1 of Article 454 (Meaning of ship, etc.) of this Code and documents certifying that the ship belongs to the execution debtor.

#### Article 456.- Execution court

With respect to compulsory execution against a ship, the first instance court having jurisdiction over the place where the ship is located at the time of the issuance of the commencement order for compulsory sale shall have jurisdiction as the execution court.

#### Article 457.- Commencement order, etc.

1- In commencing the procedure for compulsory sale, the execution court shall issue a commencement order for compulsory sale and order a bailiff to confiscate the certificate of ship's nationality, etc., and deliver it to the execution court.

2- In the commencement order specified in paragraph 1 above, the execution court shall declare the seizure of the ship for the benefit of the execution creditor and shall prohibit the execution debtor from allowing the ship to depart.

3- The commencement order specified in paragraph 1 above shall be served on the execution debtor.

4- When a commencement order has been issued according to the provisions of paragraph 1 above for a registered Cambodian ship, the court clerk shall immediately request the competent authority to register the seizure relating to said commencement order.

5- The effect of the seizure shall arise when the service specified in paragraph 3 above is made. Provided that if the registration of the seizure is made before the service of the commencement order for compulsory sale, the effect shall arise at the time of registration.

#### Article 457 (Continuation)

6- If the bailiff confiscates the certificate of ship's nationality, etc., before the service of the commencement order for compulsory sale or the registration of the seizure, the effect of the seizure shall arise at the time of confiscation.

7- An appeal (Bandung Toas) may be filed against a ruling to dismiss a motion for compulsory sale.

#### Article 458.- Fixing of time limit for demand for distribution and change of said time limit

1- When the effect of the seizure based on the commencement order for compulsory sale has arisen, the execution court shall fix the time limit for demand for distribution.

2- If the ruling to permit the sale has not been issued within three (3) months from the day of the time limit for demand for distribution, the execution court may change said time limit for demand for distribution.

#### Article 459.- Ruling to order delivery of certificate of ship's nationality, etc., before filing of motion

1- If there is a risk that compulsory execution against a ship will be significantly difficult unless the certificate of ship's nationality, etc., is confiscated before the filing of a motion for compulsory execution against the ship, the court of first instance having jurisdiction over the place where the ship is registered may, upon motion, issue a ruling to order the execution debtor to deliver the certificate of ship's nationality, etc. to a bailiff. If the ship is not registered, or if there are urgent circumstances, the court of first instance having jurisdiction over the place where the ship is located may issue such ruling.

2- In filing a motion according to the provisions of paragraph 1 above, the creditor shall produce an authenticated copy of the title of execution with an execution clause and make a preliminary showing of the grounds specified in paragraph 1 above.

3- The ruling referred to in the provisions of paragraph 1 above shall be sent to the execution debtor.

4- An objection may be filed against the ruling referred to in the provisions of paragraph 1 above.

5- The ruling referred to in the provisions of paragraph 1 above may be executed even before it is sent to the execution debtor. Necessary expenses for this execution shall be treated as expenses for the common benefit in the compulsory sale procedure.

6- The ruling ordering the delivery of the vessel's certificate of nationality, etc., to the bailiff referred to in the provisions of paragraph 1 above shall not be executed if more than 2 (two) weeks have elapsed since the date on which notice was given to the execution creditor.

7- If the execution creditor fails to submit a document certifying that he/she has filed a motion for compulsory execution against the vessel within 5 (five) days from the date on which he/she received the delivery of the vessel's certificate of nationality, etc., the bailiff shall return the vessel's certificate of nationality, etc., to the execution debtor.

#### Article 460.- Appointment of a custodian, etc.

1- If it is recognized as necessary, the execution court may, upon motion of the execution creditor, appoint a custodian for the vessel for which a ruling to commence a compulsory sale has been issued.

2- The execution court shall supervise the custodian. The custodian shall perform his/her duties with the care of a good manager. In the event that the custodian has completed his/her duties, he/she shall report the accounting of receipts and expenses for the custody to the execution court without delay.

3- The custodian may receive in advance necessary expenses for the custody and remuneration determined by a ruling of the execution court. The expenses incurred by the custodian for the custody of the vessel and the remuneration shall be treated as expenses for the procedure.

4- An objection may be filed against the ruling on the motion specified in paragraph 1 and the ruling referred to in the provisions of paragraph 3 above.

#### Article 461.- Cancellation of compulsory sale procedure by providing security

1- In the event that a document specified in any of the items of paragraph 2 of Article 370 (Stay and cancellation of compulsory execution) of this Code has been submitted with respect to the claim of the execution creditor, if the execution debtor has provided security equivalent to the total amount of the claim of the execution creditor and the claims of creditors who have filed demands for distribution and the expenses for the execution before an offer to purchase is made, the execution court shall, upon motion, cancel the compulsory sale procedure, except for the distribution procedure. If the security is provided before the expiration of the time limit for a demand for distribution, the amount of security to be provided by the execution debtor shall be the total amount of the claims and expenses for the execution of the creditors who have filed demands for distribution up to that time.

2- If the stay of execution by submission of the document specified in paragraph 1 above loses its effect, the execution court shall make a distribution to the creditors specified in paragraph 1 above from the security provided under the provisions of paragraph 1.

3- An objection may be filed against a ruling to dismiss the motion specified in paragraph 1 above.

4- The provisions of Article 71 (Methods of providing security and change of security) and Article 72 (Expenses for providing security) shall apply mutatis mutandis to the provision of security under the provisions of paragraph 1.

(Rights of a person who has received security) of this Code shall apply mutatis mutandis to the provision of security specified in paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 462.- Permission for the vessel to navigate

1- In the event that it is recognized that there is a necessity for business or other reasonable grounds, the execution court may, upon motion of the execution debtor, permit the vessel to navigate, provided that the consent of each creditor and the highest bidder or the purchaser has been obtained.

2- An objection may be filed against the ruling on the motion specified in paragraph 1 above.

3- The ruling referred to in the provisions of paragraph 1 above shall not take effect until it becomes final and binding.

#### Article 463.- Transfer of the case

In the event that a vessel for which a ruling to commence a compulsory sale has been issued is located at a place outside the jurisdictional area, the execution court may transfer the case to the first instance court having jurisdiction over the place where the vessel is located.

#### Article 464.- Cancellation of compulsory sale procedure in the event that the vessel's certificate of nationality, etc., cannot be confiscated

If the bailiff cannot confiscate the vessel's certificate of nationality, etc., within 2 (two) weeks from the date on which the ruling to commence the compulsory sale was issued, the execution court shall cancel the compulsory sale procedure.

#### Article 465.- Cancellation of compulsory sale procedure due to loss or destruction of the vessel, etc.

If an event that makes the transfer of the vessel by sale impossible has occurred because the vessel has been lost or destroyed or due to other reasons, the execution court shall cancel the compulsory sale procedure.

#### Article 466.- Requisition to cancel registration of seizure

1- If a motion for compulsory sale has been withdrawn or if a ruling to cancel the procedure for a compulsory sale has become effective, the court clerk shall request the competent authority to cancel the registration of the seizure relating to that commencement ruling.

2- Necessary expenses for the request referred to in the provisions of paragraph 1 above shall be borne by the execution creditor who has withdrawn the motion or the execution creditor who has received the ruling to cancel the procedure.

#### Article 467.- Valuation

1- The execution court may appoint an appraiser and order the valuation of the vessel.

2- In order to request the assistance of a bailiff referred to in the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 338 (Guarantees for performance of duties of bailiff, etc.) of this Code, the appraiser must obtain permission from the execution court.

3- When conducting research for valuation, the appraiser may enter the vessel or ask questions or demand the presentation of documents to the execution debtor or a third party in possession of the vessel.

4- If necessary, the bailiff whose assistance has been requested by the appraiser under the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 338 (Guarantees for performance of duties of bailiff, etc.) of this Code may take necessary measures to open closed doors in the event that the appraiser enters the vessel under the provisions of paragraph 3 above.

#### Article 468.- Ruling to determine the minimum sales price, etc.

1- If a valuation has been made by an appraiser, the execution court shall determine the minimum sales price based on that valuation.

2- If it is found to be necessary, the execution court may change the minimum sales price.

3- The provisions of Article 435 (Measures in case of fear of no surplus) shall apply mutatis mutandis to the compulsory sale of a vessel.

of this Code shall apply mutatis mutandis to the execution against a vessel.

#### Article 469.- Sales method, etc.

1- A sale of a vessel by methods other than a sale by a bid or an auction shall be conducted by a method determined by the execution court.

2- In the event that a sale is conducted by a bid or an auction, the execution court shall determine the date, time, and place for the sale and shall cause the bailiff to conduct the sale.

3- In the case specified in paragraph 2 above, the court clerk shall publicly notify the description of the vessel to be sold, the minimum sales price, and the date, time, and place of the sale.

4- The provisions of Article 396 (Maintenance of order at the place of sale) of this Code shall apply mutatis mutandis to the case specified in paragraph 2 above.

#### Article 470.- Security for an offer to purchase

1- A person who intends to make an offer to purchase the vessel shall provide security equivalent to 1/10 (one-tenth) of the minimum sales price. However, if the execution court finds it to be reasonable, it may determine the amount of security in excess of 1/10 (one-tenth) of the minimum sales price.

2- If there is an offer to purchase from a person other than the highest bidder, the execution court shall return the security provided under the provisions of paragraph 1 above immediately after the completion of the sale conducted based on the provisions of Article 469 (Sales method, etc.) of this Code.

#### Article 471.- Prohibition of an offer to purchase by the execution debtor

The execution debtor may not make an offer to purchase.

#### Article 472.- Date for the ruling on sale

1- The execution court shall open a date for a ruling on the sale and shall declare whether the sale is permitted or not.

2- When a date for a ruling on the sale has been determined, the execution court shall notify interested persons of that date.

#### Article 473.- Statement of opinions on whether to permit or not permit the sale

Interested persons with respect to the permission or non-permission of the sale of the vessel may state their opinions on the grounds specified in each of the items of paragraph 2 of Article 474 (Permission or non-permission of the sale) of this Code which affect their rights, on the date for the ruling on the sale.

#### Article 474.- Permission or non-permission of the sale

1- Except for the cases specified in paragraph 2 below, the execution court shall issue a ruling to permit the sale.

2- The execution court shall issue a ruling not to permit the sale if it recognizes that there are grounds as specified in the following items:

(a) The procedure for the compulsory sale should not have been commenced or continued;

(b) The highest bidder is not qualified or does not have the capacity to purchase the vessel, or the representative of the highest bidder does not have the authority to do so;

(c) There is a motion not to permit the sale under the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 476 (Motion not to permit the sale, etc. in the event that the vessel has been damaged) of this Code;

(d) There is a serious error in the procedure for the ruling to determine the minimum sales price;

(e) There is a serious error in the sale procedure.

#### Article 475.- Objection to a ruling to permit or not permit the sale

1- Interested persons may file an objection to a ruling to permit or not permit the sale only if such ruling affects their rights.

2- An objection to a ruling to permit the sale must be based on the grounds specified in each of the items of paragraph 2 of Article 474 (Permission or non-permission of the sale) of this Code or on the grounds that there is a serious error in the procedure for the ruling to permit the sale.

3- If it is found to be necessary, the appellate court may determine the other party to the objection.

4- The ruling to permit or not permit the sale shall not take effect until it becomes final and binding.

#### Article 476.- Motion not to permit the sale, etc. in the event that the vessel has been damaged

1- In the event that the vessel has been significantly damaged due to a natural disaster or other reasons not attributable to the fault of the highest bidder or the purchaser after the offer to purchase has been made, the highest bidder or the purchaser may file a motion with the execution court not to permit the sale if it is before the ruling to permit the sale is issued, or a motion to cancel the ruling to permit the sale if it is after the ruling to permit the sale has been issued, provided that it is before the payment of the sales price.

2- An objection may be filed against a ruling on a motion to cancel a ruling to permit the sale under the provisions of paragraph 1 above.

3- A ruling to cancel a ruling to permit the sale based on the motion specified in paragraph 1 above shall not take effect until it becomes final and binding.

#### Article 477.- Withdrawal of motion for compulsory sale after offer to purchase

In order to withdraw a motion for a compulsory sale after an offer to purchase has been made, the execution creditor must obtain the consent of the highest bidder or the purchaser.

#### Article 478.- Deadline for payment of sales price

If the ruling to permit the sale becomes final and binding, the execution court shall determine a deadline for the payment of the sales price and notify the purchaser of that deadline.

#### Article 479.- Payment of sales price

1- The purchaser shall pay the sales price to the execution court before the deadline determined by the execution court under the provisions of Article 478 (Deadline for payment of sales price) of this Code.

2- The amount of security provided by the purchaser for the offer to purchase shall be included in the sales price.

#### Article 480.- Time of acquisition of ownership of the vessel

The purchaser shall acquire ownership of the vessel when he/she has paid the sales price.

#### Article 481.- Effect of failure to pay sales price

1- If the purchaser fails to pay the sales price, the ruling to permit the sale shall lose its effect. In this case, the purchaser may not demand the return of the security provided under the provisions of Article 470 (Security for an offer to purchase) of this Code.

2- In the case specified in paragraph 1 above, the execution court shall conduct a resale of the vessel.

3- In the resale conducted under the provisions of paragraph 2 above, the purchaser specified in paragraph 1 above may not make an offer to purchase.

#### Article 482.- Requisition for registration upon payment of sales price

1- If the purchaser has paid the sales price, the court clerk shall request the competent authority to register and cancel the registration of the items specified below:

(a) Registration of the transfer of ownership acquired by the purchaser;

(b) Cancellation of registration of the seizure or provisional seizure.

2- In order to make a request under the provisions of paragraph 1 above, the court clerk shall attach a certified copy of the ruling to permit the sale to that request.

3- Necessary expenses for the request under the provisions of paragraph 1 above shall be borne by the purchaser.

#### Article 483.- Order for delivery of the vessel

1- The execution court may issue a ruling ordering the execution debtor or the person in possession of the vessel to deliver the vessel to the purchaser upon motion of the purchaser who has paid the sales price. However, this shall not apply to a person who is recognized to be in possession based on a legal ground that can be asserted against the purchaser in the case.

2- If more than 6 (six) months have elapsed since the date on which the purchaser paid the sales price, the purchaser may not file the motion specified in paragraph 1 above.

3- In the event that a ruling is issued against a person in possession other than the execution debtor under the provisions of paragraph 1 above, the execution court shall examine that person.

4- An objection may be filed against a ruling on the motion specified in paragraph 1 above.

5- The ruling referred to in the provisions of paragraph 1 above shall not take effect until it becomes final and binding.

#### Article 484.- Sales price

The money specified below shall be treated as the sales price for distribution:

(a) The sales price of the vessel;

(b) The money that the purchaser cannot demand to be returned under the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 481 (Effect of failure to pay sales price) of this Code.

#### Article 485.- Demand for distribution

1- The persons specified below may file a demand for distribution:

(a) A creditor who has a certified copy of an enforceable title of execution under the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 354 (Necessity of an execution clause and the authority to grant an execution clause) of this Code;

(b) A creditor whose registration of a provisional seizure has been made after the registration of the seizure based on the ruling to commence the compulsory sale;

(c) A creditor who has executed a provisional seizure by means of confiscation of the vessel's certificate of nationality, etc.;

(d) A person who certifies by document a preferential right or a lien on the vessel.

2- An objection may be filed against a ruling to dismiss a demand for distribution.

#### Article 486.- Scope of creditors to receive distribution

Creditors who are to receive a distribution of the sales price are the persons specified below:

(a) The execution creditor;

(b) Creditors who have filed demands for distribution before the deadline for demands for distribution;

(c) A creditor whose registration of a provisional seizure was made before the registration

of the seizure based on the ruling to commence the compulsory sale.

#### Section 6 Distribution Procedure by the Court

#### Article 487.- Court to execute distribution

The court to execute the distribution is the first instance court where the bailiff belongs in the case of compulsory execution against movables, or the execution court in other cases.

#### Article 488.- Preparation for distribution

1- If any of the grounds specified in the following items exists, the court to execute the distribution shall determine the date for the distribution and notify the creditors who are to receive the distribution and the debtor of the time and place:

(a) In compulsory execution against movables, when the ground for the realization of the sales proceeds arises, in the event that notice has been given under the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 400 (Deposit of money with the court by the bailiff) or when notice has been given under the provisions of Article 401 (Notice to the first instance court) of this Code;

(b) In compulsory execution against claims and other property rights, when a deposit of money has been made under the provisions of Article 412 (Collection suit) or when a sale has been conducted by a ruling ordering a sale under the provisions of Article 413 (Ruling ordering a sale) or when the money obtained from the sale has been delivered under the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 414 (Execution against a ruling to seize a claim for delivery of movables) of this Code;

(c) In compulsory execution against immovables, when the sales price has been paid under the provisions of Article 447 (Payment of sales price) or when the ruling to permit the sale becomes final and binding in the case where a motion is made under the provisions of item (3) of paragraph 1 of Article 447 (Payment of sales price) of this Code.

(d) In compulsory execution against a vessel, when the sales price has been paid under the provisions of Article 479 (Payment of sales price) of this Code.

2- When the date for the distribution has been determined, the court clerk shall request each creditor to submit to the court to execute the distribution, within 1 (one) week, a statement of account of the principal of the claim, interest up to the date for the distribution, and expenses for the execution.

#### Article 489.- Execution of distribution

1- In the case specified in paragraph 1 of Article 488 (Preparation for distribution) of this Code, the court to execute the distribution shall make the distribution based on a distribution schedule. However, in the cases specified in paragraph 2 below, it is not necessary to base it on a distribution schedule.

2- In the event that there is only one creditor, or in the event that there are multiple creditors and the amount of money to be distributed is sufficient to fully satisfy the claims of each creditor and the expenses for the execution, the court to execute the distribution shall distribute the money to each creditor and deliver the remaining amount to the execution debtor.

3- In the event that a document specified in items (a) to (g) of paragraph 1 of Article 370 (Stay and cancellation of compulsory execution) of this Code has been submitted after the distribution procedure has commenced, if there are creditors to receive a distribution other than the execution creditor, the court to execute the distribution shall make the distribution to those creditors.

4- In the event that a document specified in items (a) and (b) of paragraph 2 of Article 370 (Stay and cancellation of compulsory execution) of this Code has been submitted after the distribution procedure has commenced, the court to execute the distribution shall also make the distribution.

#### Article 490.- Making a distribution schedule

1- The court to execute the distribution shall make a distribution schedule on the date for the distribution, except for the cases specified in paragraph 2 of Article 489 (Execution of distribution) of this Code.

2- On the date for the distribution, the court shall summon the creditors specified below and the execution debtor:

(a) In compulsory execution against movables: creditors specified in Article 398 (Scope of creditors to receive distribution of money) of this Code;

(b) In compulsory execution against claims and other property rights: creditors specified in Article 415 (Scope of creditors to receive distribution) of this Code;

(c) In compulsory execution against immovables: creditors specified in Article 453 (Scope of creditors to receive distribution) of this Code;

(d) In compulsory execution against a vessel: creditors specified in Article 486 (Scope of creditors to receive distribution) of this Code.

3- With respect to the making of the distribution schedule, the court may examine the creditors and the execution debtor who have appeared and may examine documentary evidence that can be examined immediately, on the date for the distribution.

4- In the distribution schedule, the court shall describe the principal, interest, other incidental claims, and expenses for the execution of each claim, the order and the amount of the distribution, as well as the following amounts:

(a) In compulsory execution against movables: the amount of money obtained from the sale of the seized property and the amount of money seized;

(b) In compulsory execution against claims and other property rights: the amount of money to be distributed.

(c) In compulsory execution against immovables: the amount of money obtained from the sale;

(d) In compulsory execution against a vessel: the amount of money obtained from the sale.

5- The order and the amount of the distribution specified in paragraph 4 above shall be described in accordance with the agreement in the case where an agreement has been reached among all creditors on the date for the distribution, and in other cases, in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Code, laws relating to commerce, or other laws.

6- With respect to the distribution, a claim whose due date has not yet arrived shall be deemed to have become due.

#### Article 491.- Argument against description in the distribution schedule

1- The creditors and the execution debtor who do not agree with the claim or the amount of the distribution of each creditor described in the distribution schedule may state their arguments on the date for the distribution.

2- The court to execute the distribution shall make the distribution only for the part for which there is no argument against the description in the distribution schedule.

3- As a result of the execution of the distribution, if the execution creditor or a creditor who has filed a demand for distribution based on a certified copy of an enforceable title of execution has received the full amount of the claim, the execution debtor may demand the court clerk to deliver the certified copy of the enforceable title of execution that the creditor has provided.

4- Except for the case specified in paragraph 3, when the case has been completed, the creditor specified in paragraph 3 may demand the court clerk to deliver the certified copy of the enforceable title of execution. However, if that creditor is a person who has received a distribution for only a part of the claim

the court clerk shall deliver the certified copy of the enforceable title of execution after endorsing the amount of the distribution that the creditor has received on that document.

#### Article 492.- Creditor's suit against description in the distribution schedule

1- A creditor who has stated an argument against the description in the distribution schedule shall file a suit against the description in the distribution schedule, making the creditor who is the other party to the argument the defendant.

2- The suit specified in paragraph 1 above shall be under the jurisdiction of the court to execute the distribution.

3- If the creditor who has stated an argument against the description in the distribution schedule fails to certify that he/she has filed the suit specified in paragraph 1 within 1 (one) week from the date for the distribution, the argument against the description in the distribution schedule shall be deemed to have been withdrawn.

4- In the judgment of the suit specified in paragraph 1 above, if it is found that the argument is well-grounded, the court shall modify the distribution schedule or cancel the distribution schedule to make a new distribution schedule.

#### Article 493.- Execution debtor's suit against description in the distribution schedule

1- An execution debtor who has stated an argument against the description in the distribution schedule with respect to a creditor who has a certified copy of an enforceable title of execution shall file a suit against the claim, making the creditor who is the other party to the argument the defendant.

2- An execution debtor who has stated an argument against the description in the distribution schedule with respect to a creditor who does not have a certified copy of an enforceable title of execution shall file a suit against the description in the distribution schedule

making the creditor who is the other party to the argument the defendant.

3- The suit specified in paragraph 2 above shall be under the jurisdiction of the court to execute the distribution.

4- If the execution debtor who has stated an argument against the description in the distribution schedule fails to certify that he/she has filed the suit specified in paragraph 1 with the court and fails to provide a certified copy of a ruling ordering the stay of execution relating to his/her suit to the court, or fails to certify that he/she has filed the suit specified in paragraph 2 with the court within 1 (one) week from the date for the distribution, the argument against the description in the distribution schedule shall be deemed to have been withdrawn.

5- In the judgment of the suit specified in paragraph 1 above, if it is found that the argument is well-grounded, the court to execute the distribution shall modify the distribution schedule or cancel the distribution schedule to make a new distribution schedule.

#### Article 494.- Deposit of the amount of the distribution

1- With respect to a claim of a creditor who is to receive a distribution, if any of the following grounds exists, the court to execute the distribution shall deposit the amount of money equivalent to the amount of that distribution:

(a) The claim is subject to a condition precedent or a time of uncertain arrival;

(b) The claim is a claim of a creditor of a provisional seizure;

(c) A document specified in item (a) of paragraph 2 of Article 370 (Stay and cancellation of compulsory execution) of this Code has been submitted;

(d) A certified copy of a ruling ordering a provisional stay of execution of a preferential right, a pledge, or a mortgage relating to the claim has been submitted.

(e) A suit against the description in the distribution schedule has been filed under the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 492 (Creditor's suit against description in the distribution schedule) or paragraph 2 of Article 493 (Execution debtor's suit against description in the distribution schedule) of this Code.

2- The court to execute the distribution shall deposit the amount of money equivalent to the distribution to a creditor who has failed to appear before the court to receive the distribution.

#### Article 495.- Execution of distribution upon final determination of rights, etc.

1- In the event that a deposit has been made under the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 494 (Deposit of the amount of distribution) of this Code, if the ground for the deposit has ceased to exist, the court to execute the distribution shall make a distribution with respect to the deposited money.

2- In the case where a distribution is to be conducted under the provisions of paragraph 1 above, the court to execute the distribution shall modify the distribution schedule for the benefit of creditors who have not stated arguments against the description in the distribution schedule, if any of the following items applies:

(a) If a distribution cannot be made to a creditor for whom a deposit was made based on the grounds specified in items (a) to (d) of paragraph 1 of Article 494 (Deposit of the amount of distribution) of this Code;

(b) If a creditor for whom a deposit was made based on the grounds specified in item (e) of paragraph 1 of Article 494 (Deposit of the amount of distribution) has lost the suit against the description in the distribution schedule filed by the execution debtor under the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 493 (Execution debtor's suit against description in the distribution schedule) of this Code.

### Chapter 3 Special Provisions on Execution of Security Interests

#### Section 1 General Provisions

#### Article 496.- Titles of execution for execution of security interests

Notwithstanding the provisions of Article 350 (Titles of execution) of this Code, the execution of a security interest shall be conducted based on the following titles of execution:

(a) A final and binding judgment which certifies the existence of the security interest, or a document which has the same effect as a final and binding judgment;

(b) A notarized document executed by a notary which certifies the existence of the security interest.

#### Article 497.- Special provisions on jurisdiction over a suit of opposition to a claim and a suit of opposition to the grant of an execution clause relating to a title of execution for execution of a security interest

1- Notwithstanding the provisions of Article 363 (Suit of opposition to a claim) of this Code, a suit of opposition to a claim relating to a title of execution for the execution of a security interest shall be under the jurisdiction of the court specified in the following items according to the types of titles of execution:

(a) For a title of execution specified in item (a) of Article 496 (Titles of execution for execution of security interests) of this Code: the first instance court that conducted the procedure of that suit;

(b) For a title of execution specified in item (b) of Article 496 (Titles of execution for execution of security interests) of this Code: the court having jurisdiction over the place of the defendant specified in Article 8 (Jurisdiction over the court specified

according to the residence, etc.) of this Code. If there is no court having jurisdiction under Article 8, the first instance court having jurisdiction over the place where the object of the security interest is located. If the object of the security interest is a claim, the court having jurisdiction over the place of the third party debtor specified in Article 8 (Jurisdiction of the court determined according to the residence, etc.) of this Code.

2- The provisions of paragraph 1 above shall also apply to a suit of opposition to the grant of an execution clause.

#### Article 498.- Stay and cancellation of execution of security interest

1- The execution court or the bailiff shall stay the procedure of the execution of a security interest and shall cancel the execution measures already taken if any of the following documents is submitted:

(a) A certified copy of a ruling which determines the cancellation of the title of execution specified in item (a) of Article 496 (Titles of execution for execution of security interests) of this Code;

(b) A certified copy of a ruling which determines that the execution of the security interest is not permitted;

(c) A certified transcript of a final and binding judgment which declares that the settlement or acknowledgement relating to the title of execution specified in item (a) of Article 496 (Titles of execution for execution of security interests) of this Code has no effect;

(d) A certified copy of a record of court proceedings which determines the waiver of the execution of the security interest or the withdrawal of the motion for the execution of the security interest;

(e) A certified copy of a ruling which orders the stay of the execution of the security interest and the cancellation of execution measures.

2- The execution court or the bailiff shall stay the procedure of the execution of a security interest if any of the following documents is submitted:

(a) A certified copy of a ruling which orders a provisional stay of the execution of the security interest.

(b) A document which states that the creditor has received satisfaction of the secured claim or has agreed to a stay of performance of the secured claim, after the title of execution specified in Article 496 (Titles of execution for execution of security interests) of this Code has been created.

However, the stay under item (b) above shall be limited to a period of 2 (two) months.

3- The provisions of Article 370 (Stay and cancellation of compulsory execution) of this Code shall not apply to the execution of a security interest.

4- The provisions of Article 349 (Objection to ruling to cancel, etc.) of this Code shall not apply in the case where an execution measure is cancelled under the provisions of paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 499.- Compulsory sale of property subject to a right of retention and compulsory sale for realization

A compulsory sale of property subject to a right of retention and a compulsory sale for realization under the provisions of the Civil Code or other laws shall be conducted in accordance with the provisions of this Code of Civil Procedure regarding a compulsory sale for the execution of a security interest.

#### Section 2 Execution of Security Interests against Movables

#### Article 500.- Conditions for execution of security interests against movables

Even in the case where the execution creditor fails to submit the title of execution specified in Article 496 (Titles of execution for execution of security interests) of this Code to the bailiff, if the movable property that is the object of the security interest has been submitted, or if a document which

certifies that the person in possession of the movable property has consented to the seizure is submitted, the execution of the security interest against the movables may be commenced.

#### Article 501.- Objection to execution of seizure of movables

If the execution of the security interest against movables has been commenced under the provisions of Article 500 (Conditions for execution of security interests against movables) of this Code, the owner of the movable property or the debtor of the secured claim may file a suit of opposition to the execution of the seizure on the ground of the non-existence or extinction of the security interest.

#### Article 502.- Items to be described in the motion for execution of security interest against movables

In a motion for execution of a security interest against movables, the movant shall describe the following items:

(a) Indication of the execution creditor, the owner of the movable property who is the execution debtor, the debtor of the secured claim, and their representatives;

(b) Indication of the security interest and the secured claim;

(c) Indication of the movable property that is the object of the execution of the security interest and the place where that movable property is located;

(d) Facts and the scope of the part of the secured claim, if the execution of the security interest is conducted for only a part of the secured claim.

#### Article 503.- Provisions on compulsory execution against movables not to be applied

The provisions of Article 380 (Property exempt from seizure), Article 381 (Modification of scope of property exempt from seizure), and Article 382 (Prohibition of excessive seizure) of this Code shall not apply to the procedure of execution of a security interest against movables.
*(Note: In the original Khmer text, Article 382 is mistakenly cited as Article 381 for the second time).*

#### Article 504.- Procedure for simplified execution of a pledge

1- A pledgee of movables may file a motion for a ruling to permit the satisfaction of the secured claim by means of the pledged property itself with the first instance court having jurisdiction over the place where the obligation is to be performed, based on the provisions regarding the simplified execution of a pledge specified in the Civil Code.

2- In issuing a ruling on the motion specified in paragraph 1 above, the court shall examine the pledgee, the owner of the pledged property, and the debtor of the secured claim.

3- If the court issues the ruling to permit specified in paragraph 1 above, the court shall appoint an appraiser to appraise the pledged property in advance, based on the provisions regarding the simplified execution of a pledge specified in the Civil Code. However, this shall not apply in the case where the pledgee has filed the motion specified in paragraph 1 above by determining the value of the pledged property based on his/her own appraisal and the owner of the pledged property has agreed to that value.

4- If the court issues the ruling to permit under the provisions of paragraph 1 above, the expenses for the procedure shall be borne by the owner of the pledged property.

5- In the ruling to permit specified in paragraph 1 above, the court shall declare the permission for the satisfaction of the secured claim by means of the pledged property itself at the amount appraised by the appraiser, and at the same time, if the appraised amount exceeds the total amount of the obligation to be performed and the expenses for the procedure, the court shall order the return of the excess amount to the owner of the pledged property.

6- An objection may be filed against a ruling on the motion specified in paragraph 1 above.

7- The ruling specified in paragraph 5 above shall not take effect until it becomes final and binding.

#### Section 3 Execution of Security Interests against Claims and Other Property Rights

#### Article 505.- Items to be described in a motion for execution of a security interest against a claim, etc.

1- In a motion for execution of a security interest against a claim as specified in Article 402 (Definition of execution against a claim and the execution court), notwithstanding the provisions of paragraphs 2 and 3 of Article 403 (Order of seizure) of this Code, the execution creditor shall describe the following items:

(a) Indication of the execution creditor, the execution debtor, the debtor of the secured claim, the third party debtor, and their representatives;

(b) Indication of the security interest and the secured claim;

(c) Indication of the claim that is the object of the execution of the security interest;

(d) Facts and the scope of the part of the secured claim, if the execution of the security interest is conducted for only a part of the secured claim.

2- The movant shall attach a certified copy of the title of execution specified in Article 496 (Titles of execution for execution of security interests) of this Code to the motion specified in paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 506.- Seizure of a claim based on real subrogation

1- With respect to the execution of a security interest conducted by a holder of a security interest against money or other property to be received by the debtor as a result of the sale, lease, loss, or damage of the object of the security interest, or the creation of a real right on the object of the security interest, based on the provisions of the Civil Code or other laws, the provisions of Article 505 (Items to be described in a motion for execution of a security interest against a claim, etc.) of this Code shall be

applied.

2- In the execution of a security interest under the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 506 (Seizure of a claim based on real subrogation), in addition to the items specified in each item of paragraph 1 of Article 505 (Items to be described in a motion for execution of a security interest against a claim, etc.) of this Code, the movant shall describe the relationship between the secured claim and the claim to be seized.

3- With respect to the execution of a security interest against movables, if a holder of a security interest conducts the execution under the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 506 (Seizure of a claim based on real subrogation), notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 505 (Items to be described in a motion for execution of a security interest against a claim, etc.), the execution creditor may attach a certified copy of a document certifying the existence of the security interest to the motion, instead of a certified copy of the title of execution specified in Article 496 (Titles of execution for execution of security interests) of this Code.

#### Article 507.- Execution of security interest against other property rights

The execution of a security interest against the property rights specified in Article 416 (Compulsory execution against other property rights) of this Code shall be conducted in accordance with the procedure for execution against claims specified in Section 3 (Execution against claims and other property rights) of Chapter 2 of Part 6 of this Code, unless otherwise provided for.

#### Article 508.- Provisions not to be applied

The provisions of Article 382 (Claims exempt from seizure) and Article 383 (Modification of scope of claims exempt from seizure), and paragraph 2 of Article 404 (Scope of seizure) of this Code shall not apply to this Section.

#### Section 4 Execution of Security Interests against Immovables

#### Article 509.- Items to be described in a motion for execution of a security interest against an immovable, etc.

1- In a motion for execution of a security interest against an immovable, the movant shall describe the following items:

(a) Indication of the execution creditor, the owner of the immovable property who is the execution debtor, the debtor of the secured claim, and their representatives;

(b) Indication of the security interest and the secured claim;

(c) Indication of the immovable property that is the object of the execution of the security interest;

(d) Facts and the scope of the part of the secured claim, if the execution of the security interest is conducted for only a part of the secured claim.

2- In a motion for execution of a security interest, in addition to a certified copy of the title of execution specified in Article 496 (Titles of execution for execution of security interests) of this Code, the movant shall attach the following documents:

(a) In cases of execution of a security interest other than a preferential right: a certified copy of the register;

(b) In cases of execution of a preferential right: for registered immovable property, a certified copy of the register, and in the case where a person other than the execution debtor is registered as the owner, a document certifying that the immovable property belongs to the execution debtor. For unregistered land, a document certifying that the land belongs to the execution debtor.

#### Article 510.- Survival and extinction of rights after compulsory sale for execution of security interest

1- Real security interests, real rights of use and profit, and leases on an immovable that can be asserted against the security interest of the execution creditor shall survive the compulsory sale for the execution of the security interest.

2- Property rights on the immovable other than those specified in paragraph 1 above shall be extinguished upon the sale.

3- Seizure, execution of provisional seizure, and execution of provisional disposition that cannot be asserted against the creditor who has made a provisional seizure relating to the immovable shall lose their effect upon the sale.

4- If the interested parties have reported that they have reached an agreement different from the provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 above before the minimum sales price is determined, the change in the status of rights on the immovable shall be in accordance with that agreement.

#### Article 511.- Double commencement ruling for execution against immovables and execution of security interest

1- If a motion for execution of a security interest is filed for an immovable for which a commencement ruling for compulsory sale has already been issued under the provisions of Article 419 (Commencement ruling, etc.) of this Code and the court accepts that motion, the execution court shall issue another commencement ruling for compulsory sale. In this case, the execution court shall notify the execution creditor relating to the prior commencement ruling of that fact.

2- If the motion for compulsory sale relating to the prior commencement ruling has been withdrawn or the procedure for compulsory sale based on the prior commencement ruling has been cancelled, the execution court shall continue the procedure based on the commencement ruling for compulsory sale

4- If the procedure for compulsory sale based on the prior commencement ruling has been stayed, the execution court shall notify the execution creditor relating to the subsequent commencement ruling of that fact. In this case, if the security interest of that execution creditor can be asserted against the seizure by the prior execution creditor, the execution court may, upon motion, issue a ruling to continue the procedure based on the subsequent commencement ruling for compulsory sale.

5- If the ruling specified in paragraph 4 above is issued, the execution court shall notify the execution debtor of that fact.

6- An objection may be filed against a ruling to dismiss a motion specified in paragraph 4 above.

#### Article 512.- Double commencement ruling for execution of security interest and execution of security interest

1- If another motion for execution of a security interest is filed for an immovable for which a commencement ruling for execution of a security interest has already been issued and the court accepts that motion, the execution court shall issue another commencement ruling for compulsory sale. In

In this case, the execution court does not need to issue the demand specified in paragraph 2 of Article 422 (Determination of final deadline for demand for distribution and change of the deadline) of this Code to the persons who have made reports under the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 423 (Obligation of persons to whom demand for report of claim has been made).

4- If the procedure for compulsory sale based on the prior commencement ruling has been stayed, the execution court shall notify the execution creditor relating to the subsequent commencement ruling of that fact. In this case, if the security interest of that execution creditor can be asserted against the seizure by the prior execution creditor, the execution court may, upon motion, issue a ruling to continue the procedure based on the subsequent commencement ruling for compulsory sale.

5- If the ruling specified in paragraph 4 above is issued, the execution court shall notify the execution debtor of that fact.

6- An objection may be filed against a ruling to dismiss a motion specified in paragraph 4 above.

#### Article 513.- Survival and extinction of rights after compulsory sale relating to prior execution against immovables or execution of security interest

1- In the case specified in paragraph 1 of Article 511 (Double commencement ruling for execution against immovables and execution of security interest) of this Code, if the subsequent motion for compulsory sale as execution of a security interest is based on a security interest that can be asserted against the seizure by the prior execution creditor, the security interest of that subsequent execution creditor, as well as other security interests, real rights of use and profit, and leases that cannot be asserted against that security interest, shall be extinguished upon the compulsory sale.

2- In the case specified in paragraph 1 of Article 512 (Double commencement ruling for execution of security interest and execution of security interest) of this Code, if the subsequent motion for compulsory sale is based on a security interest that can be asserted against the security interest of the prior execution creditor, the security interest of that subsequent execution creditor, as well as other security interests, real rights of use and profit, and leases that cannot be asserted against that security interest, shall be extinguished upon the compulsory sale.

3- The provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 shall not apply in the case where the subsequent motion is filed after the date for bidding or the date for the opening of bids in the procedure based on the prior motion has commenced.

#### Article 514.- Preparation of property specification, etc. in case of double commencement ruling

interest relating to the immovable or execution of security interest) of this Code, in determining the minimum sales price and preparing the property specification, the execution court shall consider the extinction of the security interest of the execution creditor who filed the subsequent motion, as well as other security interests, real rights of use and profit, and leases that cannot be asserted against that security interest.

2- In the case specified in paragraph 1 above, if it is necessary, the execution court shall redetermine the minimum sales price or redo the property specification.

#### Article 515.- Disposition in case where motion for execution of security interest is filed after the date for bidding or opening of bids in compulsory execution against immovables has commenced

1- In the case specified in paragraph 1 of Article 511 (Double commencement ruling for execution against immovables and execution of security interest) of this Code, if a motion for compulsory sale for the execution of a security interest is filed after the date for bidding or the date for the opening of bids in the prior compulsory execution procedure has commenced, the execution court shall issue a new commencement ruling for compulsory sale and, at the same time, shall stay the subsequent compulsory sale procedure. However, this shall not apply in the case where the security interest cannot be asserted against the seizure by the prior execution creditor.

#### Article 516.- Seizure of claim for rent of immovable

1- In the case where the effect of seizure has occurred for an immovable by a commencement ruling for compulsory sale, if the execution creditor files a motion to seize a claim for the purpose of receiving payment of rent of that immovable, the execution court may issue an order of seizure. This order of seizure shall be issued without examining the execution debtor and the person obligated to pay the rent of that immovable.

2- In the order of seizure specified in paragraph 1 above, the execution court shall prohibit the execution debtor from collecting the claim for rent or making any other disposition thereof, and shall prohibit the person obligated to pay the rent from making payment to the execution debtor, and shall order the person obligated to pay the rent to deposit the amount of money equivalent to the total amount of the seized claim with the execution court.

3- The order of seizure specified in paragraph 1 above shall be served on the execution debtor and the person obligated to pay the rent.

4- The effect of the seizure shall occur at the time when the order of seizure specified in paragraph 1 above is served on the person obligated to pay the rent.

#### Article 517.- Effect of seizure of claim for rent

1- An execution creditor who has seized a claim for the purpose of...

receiving payment of rent of the immovable under the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 516 (Seizure of claim for rent of immovable) of this Code may demand that the person obligated to pay the rent deposit the amount of money equivalent to the total amount of the seized claim with the execution court, if 1 (one) week has elapsed from the date when the order of seizure specified in paragraph 1 above was served on the execution debtor.

2- The seizure under the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 516 (Seizure of claim for rent of immovable) of this Code shall have effect on the rent to be received by the execution debtor after the seizure until the time when the purchaser pays the sales price of the immovable property.

3- The rent deposited with the court by the person obligated to pay the rent shall be included in the sales proceeds.

#### Article 518.- Relationship between seizure of claim for rent of immovable and procedure for execution against claims

1- With respect to a claim for the purpose of receiving payment of rent of an immovable, if an order of seizure specified in Article 403 (Order of seizure) of this Code has been served on the lessee of the immovable before the order of seizure specified in paragraph 1 of Article 516 (Seizure of claim for rent of immovable) takes effect, the order of seizure specified in paragraph 1 of Article 516 (Seizure of claim for rent of immovable) shall not take effect, unless the security interest of the execution creditor who has obtained the order of seizure specified in paragraph 1 of Article 516 (Seizure of claim for rent of immovable) was registered before the service of that order of seizure.

2- A claim for the purpose of receiving payment of rent of an immovable may not be...

seized under the provisions of Article 403 (Order of seizure) of this Code after the order of seizure specified in paragraph 1 of Article 516 (Seizure of claim for rent of immovable) has taken effect on that claim.

#### Article 519.- Scope of creditors who are to receive distribution

In addition to the persons specified in Article 453 (Scope of creditors who are to receive distribution) of this Code, the creditors who are to receive a distribution from the proceeds of a compulsory sale for the execution of a security interest shall include creditors whose security interests are extinguished by the sale. However, this shall not apply to an execution creditor whose security interest was registered after the effect of the seizure occurred.

#### Article 520.- Provisions on execution against immovables not to be applied

The provisions of paragraphs 2 and 3 of Article 389 (Method of filing a motion for compulsory execution), paragraph 3 of Article 417 (Object and method of compulsory execution against immovables), paragraph 1 of Article 431 (Rights to survive and rights to be extinguished upon sale), and Article 453 (Scope of creditors who are to receive distribution) of this Code shall not apply to the execution of a security interest against immovables.

### Chapter 4 Compulsory Execution Relating to Non-monetary Claims

#### Article 524.- Compulsory execution of delivery of immovables, etc.

1- Compulsory execution of the delivery of immovables or vessels in which persons reside shall be conducted by a method in which a bailiff of the first instance court having jurisdiction over the place where the object is situated removes the possession of the execution debtor and causes the execution creditor to acquire possession.

2- Compulsory execution specified in paragraph 1 above may be conducted only when the execution creditor or his representative...

(continued from prior page) ... has appeared at the place of execution.

3- In conducting the compulsory execution specified in paragraph 1 above, a bailiff may enter the immovable, vessel, or other property possessed by the execution debtor and, if necessary, take necessary measures to open closed doors.

4- In the compulsory execution specified in paragraph 1 above, a bailiff shall remove movables that are not the object of execution and shall deliver them to the execution debtor or his representative, or to a relative living with him, or his employee or other person working there who has reasonable discretion. In this case, if the movables cannot be delivered to any of those persons, the bailiff shall keep the movables under his custody.

5- Expenses for custody under the provisions of paragraph 4 above shall be treated as execution expenses.

6- If the movables specified in paragraph 4 above cannot be delivered to the persons specified in that paragraph, the bailiff may sell the movables by the method of execution against movables.

7- If the movables have been sold under the provisions of paragraph 6 above, the bailiff shall deduct the expenses for the sale and custody from the proceeds of the sale and shall deposit the remaining amount with the first instance court to which he belongs.

8- Upon completion of the compulsory execution under the provisions of paragraphs 1 and 4 above, the bailiff shall notify the execution debtor of that fact.

#### Article 525.- Compulsory execution of delivery of movables

1- Compulsory execution of the delivery of movables other than those specified in paragraph 1 of Article 524 (Compulsory execution of delivery of immovables, etc.) of this...

(continued from prior page) ... Code shall be conducted by a method in which a bailiff of the first instance court having jurisdiction over the place where the object is situated removes the movables from the possession of the execution debtor and delivers them to the execution creditor.

2- If the execution creditor or his representative has not appeared at the place of the compulsory execution specified in paragraph 1 above, the bailiff may stay that compulsory execution if he finds it unavoidable considering the type and quantity, etc., of the movables concerned.

3- In the case where the execution creditor or his representative has not appeared at the place of the compulsory execution of the delivery of movables, if the bailiff has removed the movables from the possession of the execution debtor, he shall keep those movables under his custody.

4- The provisions of paragraph 6 of Article 384 (Commencement of execution against movables), paragraph 2 of Article 385 (Seizure of movables in possession of execution debtor), and the provisions of paragraphs 4 through 8 of Article 524 (Compulsory execution of delivery of immovables, etc.) of this Code shall apply mutatis mutandis to the compulsory execution specified in paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 526.- Compulsory execution of delivery in cases where the object is in possession of a third party

1- In the case where a third party possesses the object of compulsory execution and is obligated to deliver that object to the execution debtor, the compulsory execution of the delivery of that object shall be conducted by a method in which the execution court seizes the execution debtor's claim for delivery against the third party and issues an order permitting the execution creditor to exercise that claim.

2- The provisions of paragraphs 3 and 4 of Article 402 (Meaning of execution against claims and execution court), Article 403 (Order of seizure), Article 405 (Demand to third party debtor for report), and Article 406 (Delivery of document

(continued from prior page) ... certifying claim), as well as paragraphs 1, 2, and 4 of Article 410 (Collection of claims for the purpose of receiving payment of money) of this Code shall apply mutatis mutandis to the compulsory execution specified in paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 527.- Substitute execution

1- In the case where an obligation has the nature of performing an act and a third party can perform that act in place of the debtor, compulsory execution of that obligation shall be conducted by a method in which the execution court, upon motion by the execution creditor, issues an order permitting the execution creditor to cause a third party to perform that act at the expense of the execution debtor.

2- The execution court specified in paragraph 1 above shall be the court specified in each item according to the classification of the title of execution specified in item (a) or item (c) of paragraph 3 of Article 363 (Action for objection to a claim) of this Code.

3- Before issuing the order specified in paragraph 1 above, the execution court shall examine the execution debtor.

4- If the execution court issues the order specified in paragraph 1 above, it may, upon motion, order the execution debtor to pay in advance to the execution creditor the expenses necessary for the performance of the act specified in that order.

5- An immediate appeal may be filed against a ruling on the motion for compulsory execution specified in paragraph 1 above or the motion specified in paragraph 4 above.

6- The provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 338 (Assurance of performance of duties of bailiff, etc.) of this Code shall apply mutatis mutandis to the case where the order specified in paragraph 1 above is executed.

7- The provisions of paragraphs 1 through 6 of Article 527 (Substitute execution) shall apply mutatis mutandis to the case where an obligation has the nature of refraining from an act and the execution creditor demands that the result of an act performed in violation of that obligation be removed, or that a construction be erected to prevent such violation, or that other appropriate measures be taken at the expense of the execution debtor.

#### Article 528.- Indirect execution

1- Compulsory execution of an obligation to perform or refrain from an act may also be conducted by a method in which the execution court, upon motion by the execution creditor, issues an order to the execution debtor to pay to the execution creditor a certain amount of money found to be reasonable for the purpose of ensuring the performance of the obligation, according to the period of delay or within a period found to be reasonable for the performance of the obligation, and to order the execution debtor to pay to the execution creditor a certain amount of money found to be reasonable for the purpose of ensuring the performance of the obligation, immediately if the performance is not made within that period.

2- The execution court specified in paragraph 1 above shall be the court specified in each item according to the classification of the title of execution specified in item (a) or item (c) of paragraph 3 of Article 363 (Action for objection to a claim) of this Code.

3- If there has been a change in the circumstances, the execution court may, upon motion by the execution creditor or the execution debtor, change the order issued under the provisions of paragraph 1 above.

4- Before issuing the order specified in paragraph 1 or 3 above, the execution court shall examine the other party of the movant.

5- In the case where the payment of money ordered under the provisions of paragraph 1...

above, if the amount of damage caused by the non-performance of the obligation exceeds that paid amount of money, the execution creditor may claim compensation for such excess amount.

6- An immediate appeal may be filed against a ruling on the motion for compulsory execution specified in paragraph 1 above or the motion specified in paragraph 3 above.

#### Article 529.- Legal fiction of declaration of intent

1- If a judgment or a ruling ordering the execution debtor to make a declaration of intent has become final and binding, or if a title of execution based on a compromise or acknowledgement having the content that the execution debtor shall make a declaration of intent has come into effect, the execution debtor shall be deemed to have made the declaration of intent at the time when the judgment or ruling has become final and binding or at the time when the title of execution has come into effect. However, if the right to demand the declaration of intent is subject to a condition precedent or an uncertain time of maturity, the execution debtor shall be deemed to have made the declaration of intent at the time when the certificate of execution has been issued under the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 356 (Special certificate of execution) of this Code. If the declaration of intent of the execution debtor is to be made in exchange for a counter-performance, the execution debtor shall be deemed to have made the declaration of intent at the time when the certificate of execution has been issued under the provisions of paragraph 2 of that Article.

2- In the case where the declaration of intent of the execution debtor is to be made in exchange for a counter-performance by the execution creditor, the certificate of execution may be issued only when the execution creditor has submitted a document certifying that he has provided or offered that counter-performance.

### Part 7 Preservative Disposition

#### Chapter 1 General Provisions

#### Article 530.- Purpose

If there is a concern that compulsory execution will be impossible or extremely difficult because of a change in the current state of the debtor's property, or if there is a concern that serious damage will occur or an imminent danger will befall the status of either party because of a dispute over a relationship of rights, a person who intends to protect his/her rights may demand a preservative disposition under the provisions of this Part, unless otherwise provided for in other laws.

#### Article 531.- Types of preservative dispositions

A preservative disposition refers to any of the following dispositions as specified in each item below:

(a) Provisional seizure: A disposition that restricts the disposition of the debtor's property in order to preserve compulsory execution of a claim for the purpose of receiving payment of money;

(b) Provisional disposition relating to the object of dispute: A disposition that preserves the current state of the object of dispute in the case where there is a concern that a creditor will be unable to exercise his/her right or will encounter extreme difficulty in exercising it because of a change in the current state of that object;

(c) Provisional disposition determining a provisional status: A disposition that determines a provisional status until a judgment becomes final and...

(continued from prior page) ... binding, in the case where there is a necessity for avoiding serious damage or an imminent danger to the creditor with respect to the legal relationship of dispute.

#### Article 532.- Preservative disposition organ

1- A ruling on a preservative disposition shall be issued by a court upon motion.

2- Execution of a preservative disposition shall be conducted by a court or a bailiff upon motion.

3- The court that is to execute a preservative disposition under the provisions of this Part shall be called the preservative execution court.

4- The provisions of paragraph 4 of Article 336 (Execution organs) of this Code shall apply mutatis mutandis to the execution of a preservative disposition conducted by a bailiff.

#### Article 533.- Exclusive jurisdiction

Jurisdiction of the courts specified in this Part shall be exclusive.

#### Article 534.- Method of filing motion

The following motions shall be filed in writing:

(a) Motion for preservative ruling;

(b) Appeal against a ruling dismissing a motion for preservative ruling;

(c) Motion for objection to preservation;

(d) Motion for revocation of preservative ruling;

(e) Immediate appeal as specified in Article 561 (Immediate appeal) of this Code;

(f) Motion for preservative execution.

#### Article 535.- Decision on preservative procedure, etc.

1- A decision concerning the procedure for a preservative ruling and the procedure conducted by a court for preservative execution shall be made by a ruling.

2- If the preservative execution court finds it necessary in conducting the execution, it may examine interested persons or other related persons.

3- The provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 above shall apply mutatis mutandis to cases where a first instance court is to decide on an incidental matter regarding the disposition of execution conducted by a bailiff.

#### Article 536.- Providing security

In order to provide security under the provisions of this Part, a party shall use the method of depositing money or negotiable instruments found to be reasonable by the court to the court that ordered the security or the preservative execution court.

#### Article 537.- Expenses for preservative disposition, etc.

1- In the case of filing a motion for a preservative ruling, the movant shall pay a tax of 5,000 (five thousand) Riels to the court.

2- When filing a motion for preservative execution with a preservative execution court, the movant shall pay in advance the amount of money determined by the preservative execution court as necessary expenses for the preservative execution procedure.

3- If the movant fails to pay the expenses in advance, the preservative execution court may dismiss the motion for preservative execution or may cancel the preservative execution procedure.

4- An immediate appeal may be filed against a ruling dismissing a motion under the provisions of paragraph 3.

5- In the case of filing a motion for preservative execution with a bailiff, the movant shall pay a tax according to other specified provisions.

#### Article 538.- Inspection of records of preservative disposition, etc.

1- A person who has prima facie shown interest in the case with respect to the procedure for a preservative ruling or the procedure conducted by a court for preservative execution may demand to inspect the record of the case, to copy the record, to be delivered a certified copy, an authenticated copy, or an extract of the record, or to be delivered a certificate of matters relating to the case from the court, upon payment of a tax.

2- A person who has prima facie shown interest in the case with respect to preservative execution conducted by a bailiff may demand to inspect the record of the case, to copy the record, to be delivered a certified copy, an authenticated copy, or an extract of the record, or to be delivered a certificate of matters relating to the case from the bailiff, upon payment of a tax.

#### Article 539.- Application mutatis mutandis of provisions of Part 2 through Part 4

The provisions of Part 2 through Part 4 of this Code shall apply mutatis mutandis to the preservative disposition procedure, unless otherwise provided for.

### Chapter 2 Preservative Ruling

#### Section 1 General Provisions

#### Article 540.- Competent court

1- A case for a preservative ruling shall be under the jurisdiction of the court having jurisdiction over the merits of the case or the first instance court having jurisdiction over the place where the property to be provisionally seized or the object of dispute is situated.

2- The court having jurisdiction over the merits shall be a court of first instance. However, if the merits are pending in an appellate procedure, the court having jurisdiction over the merits shall be the court of appellate instance.

3- If the property to be provisionally seized or the object of dispute is a claim, that claim shall be deemed to be situated at the location specified in Article 8 (Jurisdiction of the first instance court determined by domicile, etc.) of this Code with respect to the third party debtor. However, a claim for the purpose of the delivery of a vessel or a movable shall be deemed to be situated at the place where the object is situated.

4- The provisions of the first sentence of paragraph 3 above shall apply mutatis mutandis to cases where the property to be provisionally seized or the object of dispute is a property right specified in Article 416 (Compulsory execution against other property rights) of this Code and there is a third party debtor or a person equivalent thereto.

#### Article 541.- Items to be described in motion and prima facie showing

1- In a motion for a preservative ruling, the following items shall be described:

(a) Name or title and address of the parties, and the name and address of any legal representative;

(b) Content of the preservative ruling sought by the movant;

(c) The right or legal relationship to be preserved;

(d) The necessity of preservation.

2- With respect to items (c) and (d) of paragraph 1 above, the movant should describe them clearly and specify the evidence for each fact to be proved.

3- With respect to items (c) and (d) of paragraph 1 above, the movant shall make a prima facie showing.

#### Article 542.- Security for preservative ruling

A preservative ruling may be issued contingent upon the provision of security or without requiring security.

#### Article 543.- Service

The court shall serve the preservative ruling on the parties.

#### Article 544.- Appeal against ruling of dismissal

1- An execution creditor may file an immediate appeal against a ruling dismissing a motion for a preservative ruling within 2 (two) weeks from the date of receiving notification thereof.

2- The period specified in paragraph 1 above may not be extended.

### Section 2 Provisional Seizure Ruling

#### Article 545.- Necessity of provisional seizure ruling

1- A provisional seizure ruling may be issued for a claim for the purpose of receiving payment of money in cases where there is a concern that compulsory execution will be impossible or extremely difficult.

2- A provisional seizure ruling may be issued even if the claim specified in paragraph 1 above is subject to a condition or a time of maturity.

#### Article 546.- Object of provisional seizure ruling

The court shall issue a provisional seizure ruling by specifying the object. However, a provisional seizure ruling for movables may be issued...

(continued from prior page) ... without specifying the object.

#### Article 547.- Sum for release from provisional seizure

1- In a provisional seizure ruling, the court shall specify the amount of money to be provided by the debtor in order to obtain the cancellation of the execution of the provisional seizure.

2- The provision of money specified in paragraph 1 above shall be made to the court that issued the provisional seizure ruling or to the preservative execution court.

### Section 3 Provisional Disposition Ruling

#### Article 548.- Necessity of provisional disposition ruling, etc.

1- A court may issue a provisional disposition ruling relating to the object of dispute in cases where there is a concern that a creditor will be unable to exercise his/her right or will encounter extreme difficulty in exercising it because of a change in the current state of that object.

2- A court may issue a provisional disposition ruling determining a provisional status in cases where there is a necessity for avoiding serious damage or an imminent danger to a creditor with respect to a legal relationship of dispute.

3- The provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 545 (Necessity of provisional seizure ruling) of this Code shall apply mutatis mutandis to a provisional disposition ruling.

4- A court may not issue a provisional disposition ruling as specified in paragraph 2 above without...

(continued from prior page) ... a date of oral argument or a date for examination at which the debtor can be present. However, this shall not apply to cases where there are special circumstances in which the purpose of the motion for a provisional disposition ruling cannot be achieved if such a date is held.

#### Article 549.- Method of provisional disposition

The court may order the debtor to perform a certain act or to refrain from performing an act, or order the debtor to provide a counter-performance, or appoint a custodian to hold the object of the provisional disposition, or take any other necessary measures within the limit necessary for achieving the purpose of the motion for a provisional disposition ruling.

### Section 4 Objection to Preservative Ruling

#### Article 550.- Motion for objection to preservative ruling

A debtor may file a motion for objection to a preservative ruling with the court that issued that ruling.

#### Article 551.- Decision on stay of preservative execution, etc.

1- In the case where a motion for objection to a preservative ruling has been filed, the court may, upon motion, order a stay of preservative execution or cancel a disposition of execution already conducted, contingent upon the provision of security, until a decision is made under the provisions of paragraph 3 below on the motion for objection to the preservative ruling, only if a prima facie showing has been made as to facts clearly constituting a ground for revocation of the preservative ruling and as to a concern that irreparable damage will be caused by the preservative execution.

2- In the case where the court of an immediate appeal has issued a preservative ruling, if the record of the case is at the court of first instance, the court of first instance may also make a decision...

(continued from prior page) ... under the provisions of paragraph 1 above.

3- In a ruling on a motion for objection to a preservative ruling, the court shall cancel, change, or acknowledge the decision already made under the provisions of paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 552.- Trial of objection to preservative ruling

A court may not issue a ruling on a motion for objection to a preservative ruling without holding a date for oral argument or a date for examination at which both parties can be present.

#### Article 553.- Conclusion of trial

In order to conclude the trial of an objection to a preservative ruling, the court shall determine the date for conclusion of the trial, allowing a reasonable grace period. However, on the date for oral argument or the date for examination at which both parties can be present, the court may declare immediately that the trial is concluded.

#### Article 554.- Ruling on motion for objection to preservative ruling

1- In a ruling on a motion for objection to a preservative ruling, the court shall acknowledge, change, or revoke the preservative ruling.

2- In a ruling revoking a preservative ruling under the provisions of paragraph 1 above, the court may make the revocation contingent upon the provision of security by the debtor.

3- The provisions of Article 543 (Service) of this Code shall apply mutatis mutandis to the ruling specified in paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 555.- Ruling ordering restitution

If a creditor has received delivery of an object or payment of money, or has used or held in custody an object based on a provisional disposition ruling, the court may order...

the creditor shall return the property delivered by the debtor or refund the money paid by the debtor, or return the property which the creditor is using or keeping under his/her management, upon the request of the debtor, in a ruling to revoke a ruling for provisional disposition, in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 554 (Ruling on request for objection to a ruling of conservatory preservation) of this Code.

#### Article 556.- Effect of a ruling revoking a ruling of conservatory preservation

A ruling revoking a ruling of conservatory preservation under the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 554 (Ruling on request for objection to a ruling of conservatory preservation) of this Code shall not have any effect until it becomes final and binding. However, if it finds that it is particularly necessary, the court may declare that such ruling shall have effect immediately.

#### Section 5

#### Revocation of a Ruling of Conservatory Preservation

#### Article 557.- Revocation of a ruling of conservatory preservation due to failure to file a lawsuit on the merits, etc.

1-The court that issued a ruling of conservatory preservation shall, upon the request of the debtor, order the creditor to file a lawsuit on the merits and to submit a document certifying the filing of such lawsuit within a period of time recognized as reasonable.

2-The period of time specified in the preceding paragraph 1 shall be no less than 2 (two) weeks.

3-If the creditor fails to submit the document specified in paragraph 1 within the specified period of time, the court shall, upon the request of the debtor, revoke the ruling of conservatory preservation in accordance with the provisions of that paragraph.

4-In the case where the lawsuit on the merits specified in paragraph 1 has been withdrawn or dismissed after the document

specified in paragraph 1 above has been submitted, it shall be deemed that the document has not been submitted.

5-For the application of the provisions of paragraphs 1 and 3 above, if there is an arbitration agreement concerning the merits, the commencement of the arbitration procedure shall be deemed as the filing of a lawsuit on the merits.

6-If the arbitration procedure specified in paragraph 5 above has concluded without an arbitral award, the creditor shall file a lawsuit on the merits within the same period of time as specified in the provisions of paragraph 1 starting from the day on which the arbitration procedure concluded.

7-The provisions of paragraph 3 above shall also apply to cases where the creditor fails to file a lawsuit on the merits under the provisions of paragraph 6, and the provisions of paragraph 4 shall also apply to cases where the lawsuit on the merits filed under the provisions of paragraph 6 has been subsequently withdrawn or dismissed.

8-The provisions of Article 543 (Service) of this Code shall also apply to the rulings under paragraphs 3 and 7.

#### Article 558.- Revocation of a ruling of conservatory preservation due to change in circumstances

1-If there has been an extinction of the rights or legal relationship that were to be preserved, or the necessity for conservatory preservation, or any other change in circumstances, the court that issued the ruling of conservatory preservation or the court of the lawsuit on the merits may revoke the ruling of conservatory preservation upon the request of the debtor.

2-Concerning the change in circumstances specified in paragraph 1 above, the debtor shall provide a preliminary showing.

3-The provisions of Article 543 (Service) and paragraph 2 of Article 554 (Ruling

on a motion for objection to a ruling of conservatory preservation) of this Code shall also apply to a ruling on a motion specified in paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 559.- Revocation of a ruling for provisional disposition due to special circumstances

1-If there is a fear that an irreparable damage may occur due to a ruling for provisional disposition or if there are any other special circumstances, the court that issued the ruling for provisional disposition or the court of the lawsuit on the merits may revoke the ruling for provisional disposition, contingent upon the provision of security, upon the request of the debtor.

2-Concerning the special circumstances specified in paragraph 1 above, the debtor shall provide a preliminary showing.

3-The provisions of Article 543 (Service) of this Code shall also apply to a ruling on a motion specified in paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 560.- Mutatis mutandis application of provisions on objection to ruling of conservatory preservation, etc.

1-The provisions of Article 551 (Decision on stay of execution of conservatory preservation, etc.) through Article 553 (Conclusion of trial), Article 555 (Ruling ordering restitution), and Article 556 (Effect of a ruling revoking a ruling of conservatory preservation) of this Code shall also apply to a decision on revocation of a ruling of conservatory preservation, except for a decision made under the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 557 (Revocation of a ruling of conservatory preservation due to failure to file a lawsuit on the merits, etc.).

2-Concerning a decision under the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 551 (Decision on stay of execution of conservatory preservation, etc.) of this Code that is applied mutatis mutandis

in the same manner as in paragraph 1 above, in the case where a motion for revocation of a ruling of conservatory preservation was filed with the court of the lawsuit on the merits, other than the court that issued the ruling of conservatory preservation, and there are records of the case in the court that issued the ruling of conservatory preservation, the court that issued the ruling of conservatory preservation may also make a decision under the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 551.

#### Section 6

#### Immediate Appeal

#### Article 561.- Immediate appeal

1-An immediate appeal may be filed against the following rulings within 2 (two) weeks from the day on which the service thereof was received:

(a) A ruling on a motion for objection to a ruling of conservatory preservation or a motion for revocation of a ruling of conservatory preservation.

(b) A ruling under the provisions of Article 555 (Ruling ordering restitution) of this Code.

(c) A ruling under the provisions of Article 555 (Ruling ordering restitution) of this Code that is applied mutatis mutandis under paragraph 1 of Article 560 (Mutatis mutandis application of provisions on objection to ruling of conservatory preservation, etc.).

However, this provision shall not apply to a decision on a motion for objection to a ruling of conservatory preservation made by a court of immediate appeal.

2-The period of time specified in paragraph 1 above may not be extended.

3-The provisions of Article 543 (Service) and Article 554 (Ruling on motion for objection to preservative ruling) of this Code shall also apply to a ruling on an immediate appeal, and the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 551 (Decision on stay of execution of conservatory preservation, etc.), Article 552 (Trial of objection

of objection to preservative ruling), Article 553 (Conclusion of trial), and Article 555 (Ruling ordering restitution) of this Code shall also apply to a decision on an immediate appeal, and the provisions of Article 317 (Motion for retrial against ruling) of this Code shall also apply to cases where a decision against which an immediate appeal may be filed has become final and binding.

4-Concerning a decision under the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 551 (Decision on stay of execution of conservatory preservation, etc.) of this Code that is applied mutatis mutandis under paragraph 3 above, in the case where there are records of the case in the court of first instance, the court of first instance may also make a decision under the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 551.

#### Chapter 3

#### Execution of Conservatory Preservation

#### Article 562.- Conditions for execution of conservatory preservation

1-Execution of conservatory preservation shall be performed based on an authenticated copy of the ruling of conservatory preservation. However, execution of conservatory preservation against or for a person other than a party specified in the ruling of conservatory preservation shall be performed based on an authenticated copy of the ruling of conservatory preservation with an execution clause attached.

2-Execution of conservatory preservation shall not be performed if 2 (two) weeks have elapsed from the day on which the ruling of conservatory preservation was served on the creditor.

3-Execution of conservatory preservation may be performed even before the ruling of conservatory preservation is served on the debtor.

#### Article 563.- Special case for court with jurisdiction over suit of opposition by third party

Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 365 (Suit of opposition by third party) of this Code, a suit of opposition by a third party concerning a ruling of conservatory preservation issued by an appellate court as the court of execution of conservatory preservation shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the first instance court with jurisdiction over the place where the property to be provisionally seized or the object of the dispute is located.

#### Article 564.- Mutatis mutandis application of provisions of Part 6

Except for cases for which there are special provisions in this Chapter, the provisions of Article 338 (Security for performance of duties of bailiff, etc.) through Article 345 (Appeal against a ruling of cancellation, etc.), the provisions of Article 348 (Representative by mandate in compulsory execution procedure), the provisions of paragraphs 2 and 3 of Article 349 (Method of filing motion for compulsory execution), paragraph 1 of Article 351 (Scope of persons who can perform compulsory execution), paragraph 2 of Article 354 (Necessity of execution clause and institution to grant execution clause), paragraphs 1 and 3 of Article 355 (Motion for grant of execution clause), paragraph 2 through paragraph 4 of Article 356 (Special execution clause), Article 357 (Matters to be stated in execution clause) through Article 359 (Entry into the original of the title of execution), Article 362 (Motion for objection to grant of execution clause, etc.), the provisions of Article 364 (Suit of opposition to grant of execution clause), and Article 365 (Suit of opposition by third party), and the provisions of Article 367 (Decision on stay of execution by filing of suit of opposition to claim, etc.) through Article 370 (Stay and cancellation of compulsory execution) of this Code shall also apply to the execution of conservatory preservation.

#### Article 565.- Execution of provisional seizure of movables

1-Execution of a provisional seizure of movables shall be performed by the method of taking possession of the object of the seizure by a bailiff of the first instance court with jurisdiction over the place where the object of the seizure is located.

2-The bailiff shall deposit the money obtained from the execution of the provisional seizure with the court to which he belongs.

3-If there is a fear that the movable property on which the execution of provisional seizure was performed may sharply decrease in value or if maintenance of such movable property requires excessive expenses, the bailiff shall sell such movable property in accordance with the sale procedure for execution against movables specified in Section 2, Chapter 2 of Part 6 of this Code, upon the request of the creditor of the provisional seizure or the debtor, and deposit the proceeds of such sale with the court to which he belongs.

4-The provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 384 (Commencement of execution against movables), Article 385 (Seizure of movables in possession of execution debtor) through Article 392 (Prohibition of seizure where no surplus is expected) of this Code shall also apply to the execution of a provisional seizure of movables.

#### Article 566.- Execution of provisional seizure against claims and other property rights

1-Execution of a provisional seizure against a claim specified in Article 402 (Definition of execution against claim and execution court) of this Code shall be performed by the court of conservatory preservation issuing a ruling prohibiting the third party debtor from paying the debtor.

2-Concerning the execution of provisional seizure specified in paragraph 1 above, the court which has issued the ruling of provisional seizure shall have the jurisdiction

as the court of execution of conservatory preservation.

3-In the case where the provisional seizure was performed on a claim for payment of money, if the third party debtor has deposited with the court the amount corresponding to the amount of the claim, it shall be deemed that the debtor has deposited the amount corresponding to the amount specified under the provisions of Article 547 (Release from provisional seizure) of this Code. However, this provision shall not apply to the portion exceeding such amount.

4-The provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 shall also apply to the execution of provisional seizure against other property rights.

5-The provisions of paragraphs 2, 3, 6, 7, 8, 10, and 11 of Article 403 (Order of seizure), Article 404 (Scope of seizure) through Article 408 (Notice of withdrawal of motion, etc.), Article 411 (Deposit by third party debtor), and Article 416 (Compulsory execution against other property rights) of this Code shall also apply to the execution of provisional seizure against the claims specified in paragraph 1 above and against other property rights.

#### Article 567.- Execution of provisional seizure of real property

1-Execution of a provisional seizure of real property specified in paragraph 1 of Article 417 (Object and method of compulsory execution against real property) of this Code shall be performed by the method of registration of the provisional seizure.

2-Concerning the execution of provisional seizure, the court that issued the ruling of provisional seizure shall have the jurisdiction as the court of execution of conservatory preservation.

3-A court clerk shall commission the institution with jurisdiction over registration to register the provisional seizure.

4-The provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 420 (Commissioning for registration of seizure

, etc.) the first sentence of paragraph 1 and the provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 421 (Effect of seizure), paragraph 1 of Article 424 (Ruling to commence administration), Article 425 (Ruling to commence administration for execution of security interest and for execution against real property), Article 427 (Cancellation of compulsory sale procedure due to loss of real property, etc.), and Article 428 (Commissioning for cancellation of registration of seizure) of this Code shall also apply to the execution of provisional seizure of real property.

#### Article 568.- Execution of provisional seizure of a ship

1-Execution of a provisional seizure of a ship shall be performed by the method of registration of the provisional seizure or the method of ordering a bailiff to confiscate the ship's nationality certificate, etc. and to submit it to the court of execution of conservatory preservation. These methods may be performed together.

2-Concerning the execution of provisional seizure by the method of registration of the provisional seizure, the court that issued the ruling of provisional seizure shall have the jurisdiction as the court of execution of conservatory preservation, while for the execution of provisional seizure by the method of ordering confiscation of the ship's nationality certificate, etc., the first instance court with jurisdiction over the place where the ship is located shall have the jurisdiction as the court of execution of conservatory preservation.

3-A court clerk shall commission the institution with jurisdiction over registration to register the provisional seizure.

4-The provisions of paragraphs 2 and 3 of Article 455 (Motion for compulsory execution against ship and method of execution), Article 465 (Cancellation of compulsory sale procedure due to loss of ship, etc.), and Article 466 (Commissioning for cancellation of registration of seizure) shall also apply to the execution of provisional seizure by the method of registration, and the provisions of paragraph 3 of Article 455 (Motion for compulsory execution against ship and method of execution), paragraph 7 of Article 457 (Ruling to commence, etc.), Article 460 (Appointment of a manager, etc.), and

Article 462 (Permission for ship to sail) of this Code shall also apply to the execution of provisional seizure by the method of ordering confiscation of the ship's nationality certificate, etc.

#### Article 569.- Cancellation of execution of provisional seizure through deposit of amount to avoid provisional seizure

1-If the debtor has deposited with the court the amount specified under the provisions of Article 547 (Release from provisional seizure) of this Code, the court of execution of conservatory preservation or the first instance court to which the bailiff belongs shall cancel the execution of the provisional seizure.

2-Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 345 (Appeal against a ruling of cancellation, etc.) of this Code that are applied mutatis mutandis under Article 564 (Mutatis mutandis application of provisions of Part 6), a ruling issued under the provisions of paragraph 1 above shall have effect immediately.

#### Article 570.- Execution of provisional disposition

The execution of a provisional disposition shall be performed in the same manner as the execution of a provisional seizure or as compulsory execution.

#### Article 571.- Effect of a provisional disposition prohibiting the exercise of possession

1-If a ruling has been issued which prohibits the debtor from exercising possession of an object, orders the debtor to deliver the object to a bailiff by divesting the debtor of possession, and orders the bailiff to keep the object in his/her custody for the purpose of preserving the right to demand delivery of the object, the bailiff shall publicly announce that the debtor is prohibited from exercising possession of the object and that the bailiff is keeping the object in his/her custody, by posting a public notice in a manner that is not easily removable or by any other appropriate method.

2-If the provisional disposition specified in paragraph 1 above has been executed, the creditor may perform compulsory execution to deliver the object based on the title of execution of the merits. However, the creditor may perform such execution only against a person who possesses the object knowing that the execution of the provisional disposition has been performed.

3-A person who takes possession of the object after the execution of the provisional disposition specified in paragraph 1 above shall be presumed to possess the object knowing that the execution of the provisional disposition has been performed.

#### Book 8

#### Transitional Provisions

#### Chapter 1

#### Transitional Provisions

#### Article 572.- Principles for transitional measures

Concerning lawsuits filed before the date on which this Code was enforced, the procedure related to the lawsuit after the enforcement of this Code shall be continued and concluded in accordance with this Code, except for cases specially provided for in this Book 8 (Transitional Provisions). In such cases, this provision shall not hinder the effect of any procedure performed before the date on which this Code was enforced.

#### Article 573.- Transitional measures concerning jurisdiction

Concerning the jurisdiction over a lawsuit and the transfer of a lawsuit that is pending at the time of enforcement of this Code, it shall be in accordance with the actions already taken.

#### Article 574.- Transitional measures concerning assignment of cases

1-In the case where the lawsuit was filed but this Code is enforced before the assignment of a judge in charge of investigation or a conciliation procedure (hereinafter referred to as the "judge in charge of investigation, etc."), it shall be deemed that the lawsuit was filed at the time of enforcement of this Code, and the provisions of Article 26 (Assignment of cases) of this Code shall apply.

2-In the case where the judge in charge of investigation, etc. has already been assigned but this Code is enforced before the assignment of the presiding judge or the judge in the stage of trial, it shall be deemed that the judge in charge of investigation, etc. has received the assignment of the case under the provisions of Article 26 (Assignment of cases) of this Code. However, if the chief judge of the first instance court is in charge of conciliation

it shall be deemed that the chief judge has received the assignment of the case under the provisions of Article 26 (Assignment of cases).

3-In the case of paragraph 2 above, concerning a case specified in paragraph 2 of Article 23 (First instance trial by a single judge or by a collegiate body of judges) of this Code, it shall be deemed that the lawsuit was filed at the time of enforcement of this Code, and the provisions of Article 26 (Assignment of cases) shall apply, notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph 2 above.

4-After a case has been received for an appellate trial, if this Code is enforced before the assignment of a judge in the stage of trial, it shall be deemed that the case was received at the time of enforcement of this Code, and the provisions of Article 26 (Assignment of cases) of this Code shall apply.

5-The provisions of paragraph 4 above shall also apply to the assignment of cases for a final appeal.

#### Article 575.- Transitional measures concerning a decision on a motion for disqualification or challenge

In a first instance court which does not have enough judges to form a collegiate body to trial and decide on a motion for disqualification or challenge, the provisions of paragraph 1 of Article 30 (Decision on motion for disqualification or challenge) of this Code shall be applied by changing the phrase "the collegiate body of the court to which the judge belongs" to "another judge of the court to which the judge belongs" for a certain period of time.

#### Article 576.- Transitional measures concerning an investigation procedure or procedure after investigation procedure

1-In the case where this Code is enforced while an investigation procedure is being performed, the court shall immediately conclude such investigation procedure and shall determine

the date for the procedure to prepare for oral argument and shall summon the parties without delay.

2-After an investigation procedure has concluded, if this Code is enforced before the date for oral argument, the court shall determine the date for the procedure to prepare for oral argument and shall summon the parties without delay. However, this provision shall not apply to cases where the court performs the procedure to prepare for oral argument under the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 578 (Transitional measures concerning date for trial) of this Code.

3-In the cases of paragraphs 1 and 2 above, the procedural acts performed during the investigation procedure shall be deemed as performed during the procedure to prepare for oral argument within the scope of the court's authority as provided in Article 106 (Procedural acts, etc. in procedure to prepare for oral argument) of this Code.

4-In the case where this Code is enforced after the date for oral argument has started, concerning witnesses who were examined during the investigation procedure by a party or the parties themselves who were not examined during the stage of trial, if a party motions for examination during the oral argument, the court shall perform the examination.

#### Article 577.- Transitional measures concerning court costs

1-Concerning the court costs of a civil lawsuit which a party or other related persons shall bear, in relation to a lawsuit filed before the date on which this Code was enforced, it shall be in accordance with the actions already taken.

2-The provisions on security for court costs shall not apply to a lawsuit filed before the date on which this Code was enforced.

#### Article 578.- Transitional measures concerning date for trial

1-If a date for trial has already been determined when this Code

is enforced, it shall be deemed as the date for oral argument determined under the provisions of this Code.

2-In the case of paragraph 1 above, if the date for trial is the first date after the conclusion of an investigation procedure, the court may perform the procedure to prepare for oral argument before the commencement of the oral argument.

3-In the case of paragraph 2 above, if the court cannot conclude the procedure to prepare for oral argument, the court shall determine another date for the procedure to prepare for oral argument and shall cancel or postpone the date for oral argument.

#### Article 579.- Transitional measures concerning sanctions in examination of evidence

1-The provisions on civil fines specified in Chapter 3 (Evidence) of Book 2 of this Code shall apply to cases where a ruling to examine evidence was issued in accordance with the provisions of this Code, or to cases where a party or his/her representative denies the authenticity of a document after the enforcement of this Code.

2-Concerning the provisions of Chapter 3 (Evidence) of Book 2 which are applied mutatis mutandis under Article 273 (Mutatis mutandis application of provisions on first instance procedure) and Article 286 (Mutatis mutandis application of provisions on appeal) of this Code, they shall also be applied in the same manner as in paragraph 1 above.

#### Article 580.- Transitional measures concerning default judgment

Concerning cases where the plaintiff or the defendant failed to appear on a date for trial under the summons procedure before the date on which this Code was enforced, it shall be in accordance with the actions already taken.

#### Article 581.- Transitional measures concerning permissibility of appeal and period for appeal

1-Whether an appeal may be filed and the period for filing an appeal, against a judgment of first instance or a judgment of appellate court which was rendered based on oral arguments concluded before the date on which this Code was enforced, shall be in accordance with the actions already taken.

2-Whether a final appeal may be filed and the period for filing a final appeal, against a judgment rendered by an appellate court to which a case was remanded by the Supreme Court before the date on which this Code was enforced, shall be in accordance with the actions already taken.

3-Concerning a case where the Supreme Court has quashed a judgment of an appellate court rendered based on oral arguments concluded before the date on which this Code was enforced and has remanded the case to the appellate court, whether a final appeal may be filed and the period for filing a final appeal against the judgment subsequently rendered by the appellate court shall be in accordance with the actions already taken.

4-Concerning decisions other than a judgment of first instance or a judgment of appellate court which were served before the date on which this Code was enforced, whether an appeal may be filed and the period for filing an appeal shall be in accordance with the actions already taken.

#### Article 582.- Transitional measures concerning final appeal and final appeal procedure

1-A final appeal and the procedure for a final appeal against a judgment of an appellate court rendered based on oral arguments concluded before the date on which this Code was enforced shall be in accordance with the actions already taken.

2-A final appeal and the procedure for a final appeal against a judgment rendered by an appellate court to which a case was remanded by the Supreme Court before the date on which this Code was enforced shall be in accordance with the actions already taken.

3-Concerning a case where the Supreme Court has quashed a judgment of an appellate court rendered based on oral arguments concluded before the date on which this Code was enforced and has remanded the case to the appellate court, a final appeal and the procedure for a final appeal against the judgment subsequently rendered by the appellate court shall be in accordance with the actions already taken.

#### Article 583.- Transitional measures concerning the effect of reasons in a judgment of the Supreme Court quashing a judgment of an appellate court

In cases where a final appeal and the procedure for a final appeal are in accordance with the actions already taken under the provisions of Article 582 (Transitional measures concerning final appeal and final appeal procedure) of this Code, if the Supreme Court quashes the original judgment and remands the case to the appellate court or transfers it to another court, the provisions of paragraph 2 of Article 299 (Quashing and remanding, etc.) shall not apply to such judgment.

#### Article 584.- Transitional measures concerning retrial

The provisions of Book 4 (Retrial) of this Code shall apply only to decisions that become final and binding after the date on which this Code was enforced.

#### Article 585.- Transitional measures concerning compulsory execution

1-Concerning a case for compulsory execution for which a motion was filed before the date on which this Code was enforced, it shall be in accordance with the actions already taken, except for provisions that

are provided in Chapter 3 (Compulsory execution through detention of person) of the Law on Civil Procedure enacted prior to this Code.

2-A ruling ordering compulsory execution through detention of a person which was issued in accordance with the provisions of Chapter 3 (Compulsory execution through detention of person) of the Law on Civil Procedure enacted prior to this Code before the date on which this Code was enforced shall lose its effect starting from the date on which this Code was enforced.

3-At the time of enforcement of this Code, if a debtor is under detention through the execution of the provisions of Chapter 3 (Compulsory execution through detention of person) of the Law on Civil Procedure enacted prior to this Code, the presiding judge of the collegiate body which ordered the detention or the chief judge of the court shall immediately order the release of such debtor.

#### Article 586.- Transitional measures concerning conservatory preservation

1-In the case where this Code is enforced before a decision is rendered on a case for conservatory preservation for which a motion was filed before the date on which this Code was enforced, the subsequent procedure shall be continued and concluded in accordance with this Code. In such case, the procedure performed before the date on which this Code was enforced shall not lose its effect.

2-In the case where this Code is enforced after a decision is rendered on a case for conservatory preservation, the subsequent procedure shall be in accordance with the actions already taken.

#### APPENDIX

#### PROCEDURE FOR OATH TAKING

The oath taking shall be led by a court clerk and shall be performed before sacred objects at the location of each court or at any location agreed upon by the parties. If there is no agreement, the court shall decide.

Before starting the oath, the person taking the oath shall light incense, candles, or make offerings to the sacred objects before which he/she is taking the oath. Then, the court clerk shall read the announcement loudly and clearly for the person taking the oath to hear, and then read the **Word of the Oath** for the person taking the oath to repeat.

After the oath has been taken, the court clerk shall make a record of the oath taken, confirming that it was done correctly, and the content of the spoken oath shall also be recorded in that record.
