### PROMULGATE

The Law on the Establishment of the Ministry of Justice, which was adopted by the National Assembly during the 5th session of its 1st legislature, the entire text of which is as follows:

# LAW
# ON
# THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE MINISTRY OF JUSTICE

### Article 1: The Ministry of Justice shall be established under the Royal Government of Cambodia.

Article 2: The Ministry of Justice has the jurisdiction to lead and manage the field of justice in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

Article 3: The Ministry of Justice shall be led by a Minister, who may be assisted by Secretaries of State and/or Under-Secretaries of State as necessary.

Article 4: The organization and functioning of the Ministry of Justice shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

Article 5: Any provisions that are contrary to this Law shall be null and void.

Article 6: This Law shall be promulgated as urgent.

Done at Phnom Penh, January 24, 1996.
