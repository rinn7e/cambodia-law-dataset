### PROMULGATE

The Law on Expropriation, which was adopted by the National Assembly on December 29, 2009 during the 3rd session of its 4th legislature, and which was approved by the Senate on its entire form and legality without any amendment on January 14, 2010 during the 6th session of its 2nd legislature, and which was declared constitutional by the Constitutional Council in its Decision No. 108/001/2010 CC.D dated February 09, 2010, the entire text of which is as follows:

# LAW
# ON
# EXPROPRIATION

# CHAPTER 1
# GENERAL PROVISIONS

### Article 1..

The purpose of this Law is to determine expropriation in the Kingdom of Cambodia by defining the principles, mechanisms, and procedures of expropriation and the provision of fair and just compensation for any construction, rehabilitation, and expansion of public physical infrastructure projects in the public and national interests and for the development of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

### Article 2..

The objectives of this Law are to:

- Ensure reasonable and just deprivation of legal private ownership;

- Ensure reasonable and just prior compensation;

- Serve the public and national interests;

- Develop public physical infrastructure.

### Article 3..

This Law shall apply to expropriation involving public physical infrastructure projects in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

This Law does not cover agreements or memorandums on investment protection between the Royal Government and partner countries that define expropriation. In the absence of such agreements or where the agreements or memorandums do not define expropriation, this Law shall apply.

### Article 4..

Key terms used in this Law are defined as follows:

**Expropriation** refers to the deprivation of ownership of immovable property or real rights to immovable property of natural persons, private legal entities, and public legal entities, including land, buildings, and crops, for the purpose of construction, rehabilitation, and expansion of public physical infrastructure in the public and national interests, and providing prior, fair, and just compensation.

**Owner of Immovable Property and/or Right Holder** refers to natural persons, private legal entities, and public legal entities, including owners, possessors, and all right holders on the land site who are affected by an expropriation project.

**Public or General Interest** refers to the use of land or property by the general public or by state agencies, institutions, or the public.

**National Interest Requirement** refers to activities or projects such as:

- Construction, rehabilitation, maintenance, or expansion of buildings necessary for national defense or security;

- Possession of land or property for the implementation of the policy of protecting territorial integrity.

**Project Implementer** refers to the state, public enterprises and institutions, contractors, or investors.

**Third Party** refers to natural persons or legal entities who receive the right of possession and use of immovable property from the owner of the immovable property.

**Person who causes obstruction** refers to natural persons or private legal entities who are masterminds, accomplices, or instigators who act directly or indirectly with bad intent to obstruct the process of implementing the expropriation program.

### Article 5..

Public physical infrastructure projects include:

a- Construction or expansion of all types of roads, highways, bridges, airports, and ports, including structures and equipment;

b- Construction or expansion of power stations, structures, equipment, and transmission and distribution lines;

c- Construction or expansion of buildings and equipment for post and telecommunications and information technology systems;

d- Construction or expansion of roads, markets, parking lots, parks and public squares;

e- Construction or expansion of irrigation systems, clean water supply systems, sewage systems and other public service areas;

f- Construction or expansion of buildings for education and training, science, culture, health care, social security, and stadiums for public performances;

g- Construction or expansion of refineries and treatment plants, structures, and equipment for nature and environment protection;

h- Construction or expansion of buildings and equipment for research and for mining and other natural resources;

i- Construction or expansion of gas pipelines, oil pipelines, oil refineries, and other networks;

j- Construction or expansion of residential buildings resulting from serious natural disasters such as earthquakes, floods, fires, and landslides, and for resettlement;

k- Construction or expansion of buildings for the protection and welfare of the people;

l- Construction or expansion of border crossing points;

m- Construction, expansion, or organization of buildings necessary for national defense or security.

i- Establishing new conservation areas for natural resources, forests, cultural and archaeological heritage, or environmental protection;

j- Implementing national requirements as determined by the Royal Government.

# CHAPTER 2
# GENERAL PRINCIPLES

### Article 6..

Expropriation may be carried out on a part or the whole of immovable property or on real rights to immovable property.

### Article 7..

Only the state may carry out expropriation for the public and national interests.

Expropriation may be carried out only for the implementation of projects as stated in Article 5 of this Law.

### Article 8..

The state shall purchase the remaining part of the immovable property left from expropriation at a fair and just price upon the request of the owner and/or right holder who can no longer reside near the project, or build a house, or carry out any other business.

### Article 9..

The expropriated immovable property shall be used only for the purpose planned in the declaration of the expropriation project for the public interest or national interest requirement, and shall not be left vacant without a proper reason or transferred to any third party for private interest.

Any immovable property left from the required use shall be given priority for sale to the owner and/or right holder at the compensation rate. In case the owner and/or right holder refuses to buy back the remaining immovable property, such property shall be kept as state property.

### Article 10..

In special and urgent cases required for public safety, such as fighting against fire, flood, forest fire, earthquake, impending war, or terrorist attacks, and other situations as determined by the Royal Government, the Royal Government may carry out temporary expropriation of immovable property or real rights to immovable property without any prior consultation and shall return it to the owner and/or right holder when the expropriation is finished.

In urgent and necessary cases required for the national interest, the Royal Government has the discretionary power to carry out expropriation without having to follow the consultation procedures as stated in Chapter 4 of this Law.

### Article 11..

Before starting the expropriation work, the Royal Government shall plan an adequate annual budget and provide sufficient and timely credit to the Expropriation Committee upon the request of the Ministry of Economy and Finance for the payment of compensation to the owners and/or right holders affected by the expropriation.

# CHAPTER 3
# EXPROPRIATION MECHANISMS

### Article 12..

An Expropriation Committee shall be established and led by a representative of the Ministry of Economy and Finance and consist of representatives from relevant ministries and institutions.

The organization and functioning of the Expropriation Committee shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

### Article 13..

The Expropriation Sub-committee, serving as the secretariat of the Expropriation Committee, shall be established and led by the Governors of the Capital City and Provinces, and consist of representatives from relevant departments and authorities.

The organization and functioning of the Expropriation Sub-committee shall be determined by a Sub-decree as stated in Article 12 of this Law.

### Article 14..

A Complaint Resolution Committee shall be established and led by a representative of the Ministry of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction, and consist of representatives from relevant ministries and institutions.

The organization and functioning of the Complaint Resolution Committee shall be determined by a separate Sub-decree.

# CHAPTER 4
# EXPROPRIATION PROCEDURES
# SECTION 1
# PRE-EXPROPRIATION PROCEDURES

### Article 15..

Expropriation shall be carried out based on public physical infrastructure projects and have the principle approval from the Royal Government upon the request of the line ministries and institutions.

The Expropriation Committee shall prepare the expropriation project proposal to be submitted to the Royal Government for review and decision.

### Article 16..

Before making a proposal for an expropriation project, the Expropriation Committee shall conduct a public survey by recording and analyzing in detail all rights of the owners of immovable property and/or right holders over the immovable property and other assets related to the payment of compensation, as well as recording all relevant issues.

In conducting this survey, the Expropriation Committee shall organize public consultations at the level of the Capital, Provincial, Municipal, District, and Khan authorities with the Commune/Sangkat Councils and representatives of villages or communities affected by the expropriation to provide specific and clear information and receive opinions from all stakeholders regarding the public physical infrastructure project proposal.

To determine the timing of the expropriation or the new locations or compensation, the Expropriation Committee shall interview all stakeholders comprehensively about the issues of immovable property related to the public physical infrastructure project.

After finishing the survey definitively within 30 (thirty) working days, the Expropriation Committee shall make a report containing recommendations to be submitted for approval to the Royal Government.

### Article 17..

Based on the decision of the Royal Government, the Expropriation Committee shall issue a declaration on the expropriation project, which shall inform the owners and/or right holders of the immovable property subject to the deprivation and specify the purpose of the deprivation of ownership and/or rights to the immovable property clearly by implementing the following procedures:

a- Defining the scope of the public physical infrastructure project, the location of the project, and the period for project implementation, as well as the competent authorities who have the right to organize the deprivation of private ownership and/or rights or assets.

b- Determining reasonable and just compensation;

c- Determining the period for complaints;

d- Sending the declaration to every owner of immovable property and/or right holder together with the legal instruments of this Law;

e- Placing signboards indicating that the immovable property is subject to deprivation for the public physical infrastructure project;

f- Disseminating the declaration through the media;

g- Posting the declaration at the Commune/Sangkat office related to the location of the public physical infrastructure project and informing the affected people through the village chiefs.

### Article 18..

After receiving the declaration on the expropriation project, the owner of the immovable property and/or right holder may file a request for an investigation to verify that it is a necessary requirement serving the public interest or national interest, or that the project location can be diverted to another place. This complaint may be made through a lawyer or a representative.

The complaint shall be made in writing within 30 (thirty) working days after receiving the declaration, to the Complaint Resolution Committee as stated in Article 14 of this Law. The main contents of the complaint are:

- Name, address, and phone number of the owner and/or right holder;

- Reasons for the complaint;

- Description of the legal status of the land;

- Interests of the owner and/or right holder on the land subject to deprivation.

However, the owner of the immovable property and/or right holder cannot file a complaint requesting an investigation for major national development projects, bridges, railways, water and electricity networks, oil pipelines, sewage pipelines, cable networks, or main drainage systems and irrigation systems.

After finishing the investigation definitively within 30 (thirty) working days, the Complaint Resolution Committee shall make a report containing recommendations to be submitted for approval to the Royal Government.

# SECTION 2
# EXPROPRIATION PROCESS

### Article 19..

Deprivation of ownership of immovable property and real rights to immovable property may be carried out only after the Expropriation Committee has provided prior, fair, and just compensation to the owner of the immovable property and/or right holder, according to the principles and procedures of compensation payment as stated in Section 3 of Chapter 4 of this Law.

The deprivation may still proceed even if a dispute has not yet been resolved. The owner of the immovable property and/or right holder who has received compensation as determined by the Expropriation Committee still has the right to continue their complaint according to the procedures for dispute resolution as stated in Article 34 of Chapter 6 of this Law.

### Article 20..

Deprivation of ownership of immovable property and other real rights to immovable property shall be carried out by a decision of the Expropriation Committee. The expropriation decision shall specify:

- The ownership of immovable property and real rights to immovable property subject to deprivation;

- The compensation to be paid;

- The date required for the owner and/or right holder to dismantle, vacate the location, and hand over the immovable property to the Expropriation Committee.

### Article 21..

The Expropriation Committee shall inform about the expropriation decision and provide full compensation to the owner and/or right holder before carrying out the expropriation.

# SECTION 3
# PAYMENT OF COMPENSATION FOR EXPROPRIATION

### Article 22..

The compensation to be provided to the owner and/or right holder shall be based on market value or replacement cost as of the date of issuance of the declaration on the expropriation project.

The market value or replacement cost shall be determined by a committee or an independent agency selected by the Expropriation Committee.

### Article 23..

The owner of the immovable property and/or right holder has the right to receive compensation for actual damages on the immovable property from the date of issuance of the declaration on the expropriation project, which is the final date for being entitled to receive fair and just compensation.

### Article 24..

Compensation shall be paid in cash, assets, or replacement rights depending on the actual situation and with the agreement between the owner and/or right holder and the Expropriation Committee.

The forms and procedures for compensation payment shall be determined by a Sub-decree upon the request of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

### Article 25..

The compensation shall be calculated based on the total compensation amount minus the stamp duty and/or land tax for unused land that has not been paid to the state, and the Expropriation Committee shall pay the withheld tax into the national budget according to the procedures in force.

### Article 26..

Compensation for ownership of immovable property and real rights to immovable property shall not take into account any changes in value occurring from the date of issuance of the declaration on the expropriation project, as such changes might be related to changes in land reservation measures in the urban planning that determine land use zones or land use plans properly approved, or such changes result from the declaration on the expropriation project.

### Article 27..

After receiving full compensation, the owner of the immovable property and/or right holder shall be responsible for maintenance, dwelling, possession, and receiving benefits from the rights and immovable property until they are taken over by the Expropriation Committee.

### Article 28..

The Expropriation Committee may take over the immovable property or rights to the immovable property after:

- Implementing the procedures and conditions as stated in Section 1 and Section 2 of Chapter 4 of this Law.

- Payment of compensation has been implemented according to the principles and procedures stated in Section 3 of Chapter 4 of this Law.

### Article 29..

A possessor of immovable property with a proper contract shall receive compensation for various disturbances related to the deprivation, such as dismantling of structures and materials and transportation to a new location.

For a possessor of immovable property who is conducting business, they have the right to receive compensation for the impact on the business and additional support at a fair and just rate on the actual capital expenses for the business activities, calculated as of the date of issuance of the declaration on the expropriation project.

For the deprivation of a location where business activities are being carried out, the owner of the immovable property has the right to receive additional support compensation at a fair and just rate on the value of the assets actually affected, calculated as of the date of issuance of the declaration on the expropriation project.

# CHAPTER 5
# TRANSITIONAL PERIOD FOR POSSESSION AND MANAGEMENT OF IMMOVABLE PROPERTY

### Article 30..

From the date of issuance of the declaration on the expropriation project, the owner of the immovable property and/or right holder shall not sell or transfer the immovable property to others. All documents and procedures contrary to this provision shall be considered null and void.

Regarding the use of the land or related immovable property, the nature and size of use as of the date of issuance of the declaration on the expropriation project shall be maintained, and no further construction shall be allowed on that land. Any activity contrary to this provision shall not entitle the owner and/or right holder to receive compensation for the additional new construction.

### Article 31..

The owner of the immovable property and/or right holder may possess the immovable property for a maximum of 1 (one) month after the Expropriation Committee has paid full compensation in accordance with the procedures stated in Chapter 4 of this Law, unless there is an agreement or permission to the contrary.

In case the owner of the immovable property and/or right holder refuses to vacate the location, the Expropriation Committee may request the competent institutions or public authorities to take measures to require such person to vacate the location where the expropriation has been completed.

# CHAPTER 6
# DISPUTE RESOLUTION

### Article 32..

The Expropriation Committee and the Complaint Resolution Committee have the competence to review and resolve complaints.

### Article 33..

The owner of the immovable property and/or right holder who disagrees with the decision of the Expropriation Committee may file a complaint with the Complaint Resolution Committee.

### Article 34..

In case there is no agreement on the decision of the Complaint Resolution Committee, the owner of the immovable property and/or right holder may file a lawsuit with the competent court regarding incorrect expropriation procedures, deprivation that does not serve the public interest or national interest, and compensation that is not fair and just.

The forms and procedures for lawsuits shall be determined by a Sub-decree.

# CHAPTER 7
# PENAL PROVISIONS

### Article 35..

Competent officials who fail to comply with the provisions of this Law shall be subject to administrative sanctions, without prejudice to other sanctions according to the laws and regulations in force.

### Article 36..

Any person who intentionally obstructs the implementation of the expropriation decision with bad faith, causing obstruction to the progress of implementing projects serving the public interest or national interest, shall be punished by imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 1 (one) year and a fine from 100,000 (one hundred thousand) Riels to 2,000,000 (two million) Riels.

### Article 37..

Any person who illegally possesses or uses the location where expropriation has already been completed shall be punished according to the criminal provisions in force.

# CHAPTER 8
# FINAL PROVISIONS

### Article 38..

Any provisions that are contrary to this Law shall be considered nullified.

### Article 39..

This Law shall be declared urgent.
