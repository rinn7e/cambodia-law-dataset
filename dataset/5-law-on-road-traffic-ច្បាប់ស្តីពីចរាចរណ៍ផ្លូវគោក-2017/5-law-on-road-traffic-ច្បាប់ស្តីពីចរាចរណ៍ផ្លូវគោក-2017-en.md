The Law on the Amendment of Articles 8, 40, 41, 47, 48, 75, 77, 82 and 90 of the Law on Road Traffic promulgated by Royal Code No. NS/RKM/0115/001 dated January 09, 2015, which was adopted by the National Assembly on December 26, 2016 at its 7th session of the 5th legislature, and which was fully reviewed by the Senate on both form and legal concept on January 18, 2017 at its extraordinary session of the 3rd legislature, the full text of which is as follows: (ច្បាប់ស្តីពីវិសោធនកម្មមាត្រា៨ មាត្រា៤០ មាត្រា៤១ មាត្រា៤៧ មាត្រា៤៨ មាត្រា៧៥ មាត្រា៧៧ មាត្រា៨២ និងមាត្រា៩០ នៃច្បាប់ស្តីពីចរាចរណ៍ផ្លូវគោក ដែលត្រូវប្រកាសឱ្យប្រើដោយព្រះរាជក្រមលេខ នស/រកម/០១១៥/០០១ ចុះថ្ងៃទី៩ ខែមករា ឆ្នាំ២០១៥ ដែលរដ្ឋសភាបានអនុម័តកាលពីថ្ងៃទី២៦ ខែធ្នូ ឆ្នាំ២០១៦ នាសម័យប្រជុំរដ្ឋសភាលើកទី៧ នីតិកាលទី៥ ហើយដែលព្រឹទ្ធសភាបានពិនិត្យចប់សព្វគ្រប់លើទម្រង់និងគតិច្បាប់នេះទាំងស្រុងកាលពីថ្ងៃទី១៨ ខែមករា ឆ្នាំ២០១៧ នាសម័យប្រជុំវិសាមញ្ញនីតិកាលទី៣ ហើយដែលមានសេចក្តីទាំងស្រុងដូចតទៅ ៖)

# Law (ច្បាប់)

## On (ស្តីពី)

### Road Traffic (ចរាចរណ៍ផ្លូវគោក)

<!-- Blank Page -->

# Chapter 1 (ជំពូកទី១)

## General Provisions (បទប្បញ្ញត្តិទូទៅ)

#### Article 1 (មាត្រា ១) .-
This Law aims to ensure safety and order of road traffic, and to guarantee the protection of health and lives of humans, animals, property, and environment. (ច្បាប់នេះមានគោលបំណងធានាសុវត្ថិភាព សណ្ដាប់ធ្នាប់ចរាចរណ៍ផ្លូវគោក និងធានាការការពារសុខភាពនិងអាយុជីវិតមនុស្ស សត្វ ទ្រព្យសម្បត្តិ និងបរិស្ថាន។)

#### Article 2 (មាត្រា ២) .-
This Law has the following goals: (ច្បាប់នេះមានគោលដៅដូចតទៅ៖)

- To provide awareness on road traffic safety (ផ្តល់ការយល់ដឹងអំពីសុវត្ថិភាពចរាចរណ៍ផ្លូវគោក)

- To manage road traffic safety (គ្រប់គ្រងសុវត្ថិភាពចរាចរណ៍ផ្លូវគោក)

- To maintain road traffic order (រក្សាសណ្តាប់ធ្នាប់ចរាចរណ៍ផ្លូវគោក)

- To prevent offenses resulting from road use. (ទប់ស្កាត់ការប្រព្រឹត្តបទល្មើសដែលបណ្តាលមកពីការប្រើប្រាស់ផ្លូវថ្នល់។)

#### Article 3 (មាត្រា ៣) .-
This Law applies to all activities related to road traffic in the Kingdom of Cambodia. (ច្បាប់នេះមានវិសាលភាពអនុវត្តចំពោះរាល់សកម្មភាពពាក់ព័ន្ធនឹងចរាចរណ៍ផ្លូវគោកនៅក្នុងព្រះរាជាណាចក្រកម្ពុជា។)

#### Article 4 (មាត្រា ៤) .-
Terms used in this Law are defined as follows: (ពាក្យសព្ទដែលប្រើប្រាស់ក្នុងច្បាប់នេះ មាននិយមន័យដូចខាងក្រោម៖)

- Roadway, called road, refers to the entire space including tunnels, roadways, shoulders, bridges, overpasses, flyovers, tunnels, and ferry crossings connecting roadways. (ផ្លូវថ្នល់ ហៅថាផ្លូវ សំដៅដល់ទំហំទាំងមូលរួមមានផ្លូវរូង ទ្រូងផ្លូវ ចិញ្ចើមផ្លូវ ស្ពាន ស្ពានអាកាស ស្ពានរំលង ផ្លូវរូង និងកំពង់ចម្លងតភ្ជាប់ផ្លូវថ្នល់)

- National Road refers to roads with heavy traffic: (ផ្លូវជាតិ សំដៅដល់ផ្លូវដែលមានចរាចរណ៍ច្រើន៖)

    A. Roads connecting from Phnom Penh Capital to the municipalities of each province nationwide. (ក- ផ្លូវភ្ជាប់ចេញពីរាជធានីភ្នំពេញ ឆ្ពោះទៅកាន់ក្រុងនៃខេត្តនីមួយៗនៅទូទាំងប្រទេស។)

    B. Roads connecting from one provincial municipality to another provincial municipality. (ខ- ផ្លូវភ្ជាប់ចេញពីក្រុងនៃខេត្តមួយ ឆ្ពោះទៅកាន់ក្រុងនៃខេត្តមួយទៀត។)

    C. Roads connecting from national road to national road. (គ- ផ្លូវភ្ជាប់ចេញពីផ្លូវជាតិ ឆ្ពោះទៅកាន់ផ្លូវជាតិ។)

    D. Roads branching from national road to provincial municipalities. (ឃ- ផ្លូវបែកចេញពីផ្លូវជាតិ ឆ្ពោះទៅកាន់ក្រុងនៃខេត្តមួយ។)

    E. Roads branching from national road or connecting from provincial municipalities to major areas such as ports, railway stations, airports, special economic zones, tourist resorts, border crossings, and other areas of economic potential. (ង- ផ្លូវបែកចេញពីផ្លូវជាតិឬផ្លូវភ្ជាប់ចេញពីក្រុងនៃខេត្តមួយ ឆ្ពោះទៅកាន់តំបន់សំខាន់ៗ ដូចជាកំពង់ផែ ស្ថានីយរថភ្លើង អាកាសយានដ្ឋាន តំបន់សេដ្ឋកិច្ចពិសេស រមណីយដ្ឋានទេសចរណ៍ ច្រកទ្វារព្រំដែន តំបន់មានសក្តានុពលសេដ្ឋកិច្ចសំខាន់ៗផ្សេងទៀត។)

    F. Major national roads with heavy traffic as determined by the Royal Government upon request of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport. (ច- ផ្លូវសំខាន់ៗរបស់ជាតិ មានចរាចរណ៍ច្រើនដែលកំណត់ដោយរាជរដ្ឋាភិបាលតាមសំណើរបស់ក្រសួងសាធារណការនិងដឹកជញ្ជូន។)

- Provincial Road refers to roads with medium traffic: (ផ្លូវខេត្ត សំដៅដល់ផ្លូវដែលមានចរាចរណ៍មធ្យម៖)

    A. Roads connecting from a provincial municipality to district or khan headquarters. (ក- ផ្លូវភ្ជាប់ចេញពីក្រុងនៃខេត្តមួយ ឆ្ពោះទៅកាន់ទីរួមស្រុក ខណ្ឌ។)

    B. Roads branching from national road to provincial roads. (ខ- ផ្លូវបែកចេញពីផ្លូវជាតិ ឆ្ពោះទៅកាន់ផ្លូវខេត្ត។)

    C. Roads branching from provincial road or city road to district or khan headquarters. (គ- ផ្លូវបែកចេញពីផ្លូវខេត្តឬផ្លូវក្រុង ឆ្ពោះទៅកាន់ទីរួមស្រុក ខណ្ឌ។)

    D. Roads connecting from a provincial municipality to provincial roads. (ឃ- ផ្លូវភ្ជាប់ចេញពីក្រុងនៃខេត្តមួយ ឆ្ពោះទៅកាន់ផ្លូវខេត្ត។)

    E. Roads branching from a provincial road to provincial roads. (ង- ផ្លូវបែកចេញពីផ្លូវខេត្ត ឆ្ពោះទៅកាន់ផ្លូវខេត្ត។)

    F. Roads branching from a provincial road, city road or various town roads in a province to major areas such as ports, railway stations, airports, special economic zones, tourist resorts, border crossings, bilateral crossings, and other areas of medium economic potential. (ច- ផ្លូវបែកចេញពីផ្លូវខេត្ត ឬផ្លូវក្រុង ឬផ្លូវទីប្រជុំជននានាក្នុងខេត្ត ទៅកាន់តំបន់សំខាន់ៗ ដូចជាកំពង់ផែ ស្ថានីយរថភ្លើង អាកាសយានដ្ឋាន តំបន់សេដ្ឋកិច្ចពិសេស រមណីយដ្ឋានទេសចរណ៍ ច្រកទ្វារព្រំដែន ច្រកទ្វារភាគី តំបន់មានសក្តានុពលសេដ្ឋកិច្ចមធ្យមផ្សេងទៀត។)

- Rural Road refers to: (ផ្លូវជនបទ សំដៅដល់៖)

    A. Roads connecting from national roads or provincial roads to municipalities, district/khan headquarters, towns, communes/sangkats, and to villages. (ក- ផ្លូវដែលភ្ជាប់ពីផ្លូវជាតិ ផ្លូវខេត្ត ឆ្ពោះទៅកាន់ក្រុង ទីរួមស្រុក ខណ្ឌ ទីប្រជុំជន ឃុំ សង្កាត់ និងទៅភូមិ។)

    B. Roads connecting from municipalities, district/khan headquarters to municipalities, district/khan headquarters. (ខ- ផ្លូវដែលភ្ជាប់ពីក្រុង ទីរួមស្រុក ខណ្ឌ ឆ្ពោះទៅកាន់ក្រុង ទីរួមស្រុក ខណ្ឌ។)

    C. Roads connecting from municipalities, district/khan headquarters to commune/sangkat towns. (គ- ផ្លូវដែលភ្ជាប់ពីក្រុង ទីរួមស្រុក ខណ្ឌ ទៅទីប្រជុំជនឃុំ សង្កាត់។)

    D. Roads connecting from commune/sangkat towns to commune/sangkat towns. (ឃ- ផ្លូវដែលភ្ជាប់ពីទីប្រជុំជនឃុំ សង្កាត់ ទៅទីប្រជុំជនឃុំ សង្កាត់។)

    E. Roads connecting from commune/sangkat towns to villages. (ង- ផ្លូវដែលភ្ជាប់ពីទីប្រជុំជនឃុំ សង្កាត់ ទៅភូមិ។)

    F. Roads connecting from village to village. (ច- ផ្លូវដែលភ្ជាប់ពីភូមិទៅភូមិ។)

    G. Roads within a village. (ឆ- ផ្លូវក្នុងភូមិ។)

- Expressway refers to a road designated for motor vehicle traffic, equipped with dividing facilities that separate the roadway into two parts in opposite directions or divide it into lanes, having no flat intersections or with minimal intersections where necessary, and fully equipped with road facilities to ensure uninterrupted traffic flow, safety, and to reduce travel time, and having designated places for motor vehicles to enter and exit the expressway. (ផ្លូវល្បឿនលឿន សំដៅដល់ផ្លូវសម្រាប់យានយន្តធ្វើចរាចរណ៍ មានបរិក្ខារចែកទ្រូងផ្លូវជាពីរចំណែកខុសទិសគ្នា ឬចែកជាគន្លងផ្លូវ គ្មានកន្លែងផ្លូវប្រសព្វលើផ្ទៃរាបជាមួយ ឬត្រូវមានផ្លូវប្រសព្វតិចបំផុតក្នុងករណីចាំបាច់ និងបំពាក់គ្រប់គ្រាន់នូវបរិក្ខារផ្លូវថ្នល់ ដើម្បីធានានូវលំហូរចរាចរណ៍ឥតឈប់ សុវត្ថិភាព និងដើម្បីបន្ថយពេលវេលាធ្វើដំណើរ និងមានកន្លែងកំណត់សម្រាប់ឲ្យយានយន្ត ចេញ និងចូលផ្លូវល្បឿនលឿន)

- Road Facility refers to parking spaces, stopping places, traffic lights, speed limit signs, road signboards, indicator signs, road markings, signposts or barriers or lane-dividing equipment, roundabouts, traffic direction dividing lines such as road tiles, walls, protective fences, kilometer posts, boundary posts, as well as other signs and equipment installed along the road. (បរិក្ខារផ្លូវថ្នល់ សំដៅដល់ទីចំណត ទីសំចត ភ្លើងសញ្ញា ផ្លាកសញ្ញាកំណត់ល្បឿន ផ្លាកសញ្ញាបង្ហាញផ្លូវ សញ្ញាចង្អុលបង្ហាញ គំនូសសញ្ញា បង្គោលសញ្ញា ឬរបាំង ឬបរិក្ខារបែងចែកចំណែកផ្លូវ រង្វង់មូល ខ្សែបែងចែកទិសចរាចរណ៍ ដូចជាក្បឿងផ្លូវ ជញ្ជាំង របងការពារ បង្គោលគីឡូម៉ែត្រ បង្គោលសញ្ញាកំណត់ព្រំ ព្រមទាំងផ្លាកសញ្ញា និងឧបករណ៍បំពាក់ផ្សេងទៀតនៅតាមបណ្តោយផ្លូវថ្នល់)

- Roadway refers to the portion of the road designated for all types of vehicle traffic. (ទ្រូងផ្លូវ សំដៅដល់ចំណែកនៃផ្លូវទុកសម្រាប់ឲ្យយានជំនិះគ្រប់ប្រភេទធ្វើចរាចរណ៍)

- Shoulder refers to the sides of the roadway outside municipal areas, where vehicles can park when necessary. (ជាយផ្លូវ សំដៅដល់ផ្នែកសងខាងនៃទ្រូងផ្លូវនៅក្រៅទីប្រជុំជន ដែលយានជំនិះអាចចតនៅទីនោះបានក្នុងករណីចាំបាច់)

- Sidewalk refers to the sides of the roadway inside municipal areas, designated for pedestrian walking, tree planting, and embedding facilities for public service purposes. (ចិញ្ចើមផ្លូវ សំដៅដល់ផ្នែកសងខាងនៃទ្រូងផ្លូវនៅក្នុងទីប្រជុំជន ដែលរៀបចំទុកសម្រាប់ថ្មើរជើងធ្វើដំណើរ ដាំដើមឈើ និងបង្កប់បរិក្ខារបម្រើផលប្រយោជន៍សេវាសាធារណៈ)

- Lane refers to a subdivision of the roadway, divided by longitudinal markings or lane-dividing equipment along the roadway, having sufficient width to allow a single line of all types of vehicles to travel. (គន្លងផ្លូវ សំដៅដល់ចំណែកមួយនៃទ្រូងផ្លូវ ខណ្ឌដោយគំនូសឬបរិក្ខារបែងចែកគន្លងផ្លូវតាមបណ្តោយទ្រូងផ្លូវ ដែលមានទទឹងគ្រប់គ្រាន់អាចឲ្យយានជំនិះគ្រប់ប្រភេទធ្វើចរាចរណ៍បានមួយជួរ)

- Built-up Area refers to a place where buildings or houses are constructed close to each other, and where the entry or exit has a built-up area sign, the name of the built-up area indicating entry, or the name of the built-up area having a red line on the roadway marking exit from the built-up area along the road, and where there is significant pedestrian and vehicle traffic crossing. (ទីប្រជុំជន សំដៅដល់ទីកន្លែងដែលមានអគារឬគេហដ្ឋានសង់ជិតៗគ្នា ហើយផ្លូវចេញឬចូលនៅទីនោះមានដាក់ផ្លាកសញ្ញាប្រាប់ទីប្រជុំជន ឈ្មោះទីប្រជុំជនសម្គាល់ការចូល ឬឈ្មោះ ទីប្រជុំជនដែលមានគំនូសបន្ទាត់ទ្រូងផ្លូវពណ៌ក្រហមសម្គាល់ការចេញពីទីប្រជុំជនតាមដងផ្លូវ និងមានមនុស្សធ្វើចរាចរណ៍ឆ្លងកាត់ទៅមកច្រើន)

- Intersection refers to a junction where two or more roads cross or meet, having a shared area, regardless of the angle of the axes of the roadways. Intersections can be in the shape of +, X, T, Y or roundabouts, etc. (កន្លែងផ្លូវប្រសព្វឬផ្លូវប្រសព្វ សំដៅដល់កន្លែងប្រសព្វនៃផ្លូវពីរឬច្រើន ដែលមានផ្ទៃតែមួយ និងមិនកំណត់មុំនៃអ័ក្សរបស់ទ្រូងផ្លូវឡើយ។ ផ្លូវប្រសព្វអាចមានរាងជា +, X, T, Y ឬរង្វង់មូលជាដើម)

- Roundabout refers to an intersection with a circular island in the center, equipped with traffic direction signs, and having a circular roadway around it marked by traffic direction indicators. (រង្វង់មូល សំដៅដល់កន្លែងផ្លូវប្រសព្វដែលនៅចំកណ្ដាលមានខឿនជារាងរង្វង់មូល មានដាក់ផ្លាកសញ្ញាបង្ហាញទិសចរាចរណ៍ និងមានទ្រូងផ្លូវជារង្វង់ជុំវិញដោយសញ្ញាបង្ហាញប្រាប់ទិសចរាចរណ៍)

- Private Driveway refers to an entry or exit passage to a private house having a flat surface connecting to a public road, which is not a sidewalk. (ច្រកចេញចូលផ្ទះឯកជន សំដៅដល់ច្រកផ្លូវចេញចូលផ្ទះដែលមានផ្ទៃរាបស្មើភ្ជាប់ជាមួយផ្លូវសាធារណៈ និងមិនមែនជាចិញ្ចើមផ្លូវ)

- Stopping refers to a temporary immobilization of a vehicle on the roadway with the driver remaining at the steering wheel or near the vehicle, to allow passengers or other vehicles to cross, or to load or unload goods or passengers. (ឈប់ សំដៅដល់ការនៅស្ងៀមនៃយានជំនិះនៅលើទ្រូងផ្លូវមួយរយៈពេលខ្លី ដែលមានអ្នកបើកបរស្ថិតនៅជាប់នឹងចង្កូតឬក្បែរយានជំនិះខ្លួន ដើម្បីឲ្យអ្នកដំណើរឬយានជំនិះដទៃទៀតឆ្លងកាត់ ឬដើម្បីអែបខាងដាក់ទំនិញ ឬអ្នកដំណើរឡើងចុះ)

- Parking refers to keeping a vehicle on the roadway for a long or short duration where the driver can walk away from the vehicle. (ចត សំដៅដល់ការទុកឲ្យយានជំនិះនៅលើទ្រូងផ្លូវក្នុងរយៈពេលយូរឬឆាប់ ដែលអ្នកបើកបរអាច ដើរចោលយានជំនិះរបស់ខ្លួនបាន)

- Driver refers to a person who drives a vehicle, including persons riding animals, herding animals, or driving carts on the road. (អ្នកបើកបរ សំដៅដល់អ្នកបញ្ជាយានជំនិះគិតទាំងអ្នកជិះសត្វ អ្នកដឹកសត្វ និងអ្នកបរទេះ នៅលើផ្លូវ)

- Safety Helmet refers to a safety helmet complying with national standards recognized by competent institutions, to be worn when traveling on motorcycles or tricycles with the purpose of reducing the risk of head injuries in the event of traffic accidents. (មួកសុវត្ថិភាព សំដៅដល់មួកសុវត្ថិភាពតាមស្តង់ដារជាតិដែលទទួលស្គាល់ដោយស្ថាប័នមានសមត្ថកិច្ច សម្រាប់ពាក់នៅពេលធ្វើដំណើរតាមទោចក្រយានយន្តឬត្រីចក្រយានយន្តក្នុងគោលបំណងកាត់បន្ថយហានិភ័យនៃការរងរបួសក្បាលនៅពេលមានគ្រោះថ្នាក់ចរាចរណ៍កើតឡើង)

- Technical Equipment refers to devices for testing speed, alcohol levels, drug usage, or devices for collecting images and information, etc. (ឧបករណ៍បច្ចេកទេស សំដៅដល់ឧបករណ៍សម្រាប់ត្រួតពិនិត្យល្បឿន ឬជាតិអាល់កុល ឬសារធាតុញៀន ឬឧបករណ៍ប្រមូលរូបភាពនិងព័ត៌មានជាអាទិ៍)

- Signaling Device refers to a device used by traffic agents to provide signals to command vehicles to stop. This signaling device emits a red light at night. (ឧបករណ៍សញ្ញា សំដៅដល់ឧបករណ៍ប្រើប្រាស់ដោយភ្នាក់ងារចរាចរណ៍សម្រាប់ផ្តល់ជា សញ្ញាក្នុងការបញ្ជាឲ្យឈប់។ ឧបករណ៍សញ្ញានេះបញ្ចេញភ្លើងពណ៌ក្រហមនៅពេលយប់ងងឹត)

- Vehicle refers to any means designed to transport people or goods on roads, or for towing, or being towed, whether with or without an engine. (យានជំនិះឬយាន សំដៅដល់យានសម្រាប់ដឹកមនុស្សឬទំនិញនៅលើផ្លូវ ឬសម្រាប់សណ្ដោង ឬត្រូវគេសណ្ដោងទោះមានឬគ្មានម៉ាស៊ីន)

- Light Passenger Transport Vehicle refers to a passenger vehicle with a capacity from 10 (ten) to 20 (twenty) passengers, including the driver. (រថយន្តដឹកជញ្ជូនអ្នកដំណើរធុនតូច សំដៅដល់រថយន្តដឹកអ្នកដំណើរមានចំណុះចាប់ពី១០ (ដប់)នាក់ ដល់២០(ម្ភៃ)នាក់ ដោយគិតទាំងអ្នកបើកបរ)

- Heavy Passenger Transport Vehicle refers to a passenger vehicle with a capacity of more than 20 (twenty) passengers, including the driver. (រថយន្តដឹកជញ្ជូនអ្នកដំណើរធុនធំ សំដៅដល់រថយន្តដឹកអ្នកដំណើរមានចំណុះលើសពី២០ (ម្ភៃ)នាក់ ដោយគិតទាំងអ្នកបើកបរ)

- Motor Vehicle refers to all types of vehicles propelled by an engine on roads. (យានយន្ត សំដៅដល់យានគ្រប់ប្រភេទ ដែលធ្វើចរាចរណ៍ដោយម៉ាស៊ីននៅលើផ្លូវថ្នល់)

- Family Car refers to a car designed to carry passengers not exceeding 9 (nine) persons, including the driver. This type of vehicle can be used for family purposes. (រថយន្តគ្រួសារ សំដៅដល់រថយន្តដែលមានផ្ទុកមនុស្សមិនលើសពី៩ (ប្រាំបួន) នាក់ ដោយគិតទាំងអ្នកបើកបរ។ រថយន្តប្រភេទនេះអាចត្រូវបានប្រើប្រាស់ជាលក្ខណៈគ្រួសារ)

- Light Vehicle refers to a vehicle with a maximum total weight not exceeding 3.5 (three point five) tons. (រថយន្តធុនស្រាល សំដៅដល់រថយន្តដែលមានទម្ងន់សរុបអតិបរមាមិនលើសពី៣,៥ (បីកន្លះ) តោន)

- Heavy Vehicle refers to a vehicle with a maximum total weight exceeding 3.5 (three point five) tons and above. (រថយន្តធុនធ្ងន់ សំដៅដល់រថយន្តដែលមានទម្ងន់សរុបអតិបរមាលើសពី៣,៥ (បីកន្លះ) តោនឡើងទៅ)

- Bus refers to a heavy passenger transport vehicle serving public transport service on defined routes within capital or cities and charging fares. (រថយន្តក្រុង សំដៅដល់រថយន្តដឹកជញ្ជូនអ្នកដំណើរធុនធំដែលបម្រើសេវាដឹកជញ្ជូនសាធារណៈ លើកំណត់ផ្លូវមានកំណត់ក្នុងរាជធានីឬទីក្រុង និងយកកម្រៃ)

- Modified Agricultural Motor Vehicle refers to a motor vehicle modified for agricultural work, such as rice harvesters, rice threshers, and rice mills, etc. (យានយន្តកសិកម្មកែច្នៃ សំដៅដល់យានយន្តឧបករណ៍កែច្នៃសម្រាប់បម្រើការងារកសិកម្ម ដូចជា យានយន្តច្រូតស្រូវ យានយន្តបោកបែនស្រូវ និងយានយន្តកិនស្រូវ ជាអាទិ៍)

- Special Purpose Vehicle refers to a special motor vehicle equipped with additional equipment or machinery, carrying no passengers or goods outside the cabin, such as concrete mixers or road/bridge construction vehicles, etc. (យានយន្តឧបករណ៍ សំដៅដល់យានយន្តពិសេសដែលមានឧបករណ៍ គ្រឿងចក្របំពាក់បន្ថែមគ្មានផ្ទុកមនុស្ស ឬគ្មានផ្ទុកទំនិញនៅក្រៅកាប៊ីន ដូចជារថយន្តលាយស៊ីម៉ង់ត៍ឬ រថយន្តសម្រាប់បម្រើការងារសាងសង់ផ្លូវ ស្ពានជាអាទិ៍)

- Commercial Transport Vehicle for Goods and Passengers refers to a vehicle operated for commercial transport of goods of others or own, and transport of passengers. (រថយន្តធ្វើអាជីវកម្មដឹកជញ្ជូនទំនិញនិងអ្នកដំណើរ សំដៅដល់រថយន្តធ្វើអាជីវកម្មដឹកជញ្ជូនទំនិញរបស់អ្នកដទៃឬផ្ទាល់ខ្លួន និងដឹកជញ្ជូនអ្នកដំណើរ)

- Road User refers to drivers and pedestrians traveling on roads. (អ្នកប្រើប្រាស់ផ្លូវ សំដៅដល់អ្នកបើកបរនិងថ្មើរជើងដែលធ្វើដំណើរនៅលើផ្លូវ)

- Unladen Weight of Vehicle refers to the weight of the vehicle without passengers or goods, but including fuel, tools, and spare wheel. (ទម្ងន់ទទេរបស់យាន សំដៅដល់ទម្ងន់របស់យានដែលគ្មានមនុស្សឬគ្មានទំនិញ ប៉ុន្តែមានប្រេងឥន្ធនៈសម្រាប់ផ្តល់ឲ្យយាន មានឧបករណ៍ជួសជុល និងកង់បម្រុង)

- Maximum Payload of Vehicle refers to the sum of the weight of passengers and the weight of goods, determined by the vehicle manufacturer. (ទម្ងន់ផ្ទុកអតិបរមារបស់យាន សំដៅដល់ផលបូកទម្ងន់ផ្ទុកមនុស្សនិងទម្ងន់ផ្ទុកទំនិញដែលកំណត់ដោយរោងចក្រផលិតយាន)

- Maximum Total Weight of Vehicle refers to the sum of the unladen weight of the vehicle and the maximum payload of the vehicle. If the vehicle has a trailer, the maximum total weight of the vehicle is the sum of the maximum total weights of each vehicle. (ទម្ងន់សរុបអតិបរមារបស់យាន សំដៅដល់ផលបូកសរុបទម្ងន់ទទេរបស់យានជាមួយនឹងទម្ងន់ផ្ទុកអតិបរមារបស់យាន។ ប្រសិនបើយានមានសណ្តោង ទម្ងន់សរុបអតិបរមារបស់យានគឺជាផលបូកនៃទម្ងន់សរុបអតិបរមារបស់យាននីមួយៗ)

- Maximum Permissible Gross Weight of Vehicle refers to the sum of the unladen weight of the vehicle and the permitted weight of passengers and goods. If the vehicle has a trailer, the maximum permissible gross weight of the vehicle is the sum of the maximum permissible gross weights of each vehicle. (ទម្ងន់សរុបអនុញ្ញាតអតិបរមារបស់យាន សំដៅដល់ផលបូកទម្ងន់ទទេរបស់យានជាមួយនឹងទម្ងន់ផ្ទុកមនុស្សនិងទម្ងន់ផ្ទុកទំនិញអនុញ្ញាត។ ប្រសិនបើយានមានសណ្តោង ទម្ងន់សរុបអនុញ្ញាតអតិបរមារបស់យាន គឺជាផលបូកនៃទម្ងន់សរុបអនុញ្ញាតអតិបរមារបស់យាននីមួយៗ)

- Actual Gross Weight of Vehicle refers to the sum of the unladen weight of the vehicle and the actual weight of passengers and goods. (ទម្ងន់សរុបជាក់ស្តែងរបស់យាន សំដៅដល់ផលបូកទម្ងន់ទទេរបស់យានជាមួយនឹងទម្ងន់ផ្ទុកមនុស្សនិងទម្ងន់ផ្ទុកទំនិញជាក់ស្តែង)

- Goods refers to materials, objects, minerals, stones, gravel, sand, fuel, animals, and other items transported by vehicles. (ទំនិញ សំដៅដល់សម្ភារៈ វត្ថុ រ៉ែ ថ្ម គ្រួស ខ្សាច់ ប្រេងឥន្ធនៈ សត្វ និងវត្ថុផ្សេងៗទៀត ដែលដឹកជញ្ជូនតាមយានជំនិះ)

- Trailer refers to a vehicle designed to be coupled with a motor vehicle or other vehicles. (រ៉ឺម៉ក សំដៅដល់យានសម្រាប់ភ្ជាប់ជាមួយរថយន្តឬយានជំនិះផ្សេងទៀត)

- Semi-trailer refers to a vehicle designed to be coupled with a towing truck. (សឺមីរ៉ឺម៉ក សំដៅដល់យានសម្រាប់ថ្ពក់ភ្ជាប់នឹងក្បាលរថយន្តសណ្ដោង។)

# Chapter 2 (ជំពូកទី២)

## Traffic Signs (សញ្ញាចរាចរណ៍)

#### Article 5 (មាត្រា ៥) .-
Road traffic signs include road traffic signboards, traffic lights, road markings, command signals of traffic agents, and other signs. (សញ្ញាចរាចរណ៍ផ្លូវគោក រួមមាន ផ្លាកសញ្ញាចរាចរណ៍នៅតាមផ្លូវ ភ្លើងសញ្ញាចរាចរណ៍ គំនូសសញ្ញាចរាចរណ៍នៅលើផ្លូវ សញ្ញាបញ្ជារបស់ភ្នាក់ងារចរាចរណ៍ និងសញ្ញាផ្សេងៗទៀត។)

Road traffic signboards, traffic lights, and road markings must be organized as a consistent system along useful locations, which are easy for road users to see. (ផ្លាកសញ្ញាចរាចរណ៍ ភ្លើងសញ្ញាចរាចរណ៍ និងគំនូសសញ្ញាចរាចរណ៍នៅលើផ្លូវ ត្រូវរៀបចំជាប្រព័ន្ធទាក់ទងស៊ីចង្វាក់គ្នាជាហូរហែនៅតាមកន្លែងមានសារប្រយោជន៍ ដែលធ្វើឲ្យអ្នកប្រើប្រាស់ផ្លូវ ងាយស្រួលមើលឃើញ។)

Temporary danger road signs must be placed at an appropriate and sufficient distance according to road type, road class, and road conditions, which can alert the driver in advance. (សញ្ញាគ្រោះថ្នាក់នៅតាមដងផ្លូវដែលមានលក្ខណៈមិនអចិន្ត្រៃយ៍ ត្រូវដាក់ក្នុងចម្ងាយសមស្រប ល្មមគ្រប់គ្រាន់តាមប្រភេទផ្លូវ ចំណាត់ថ្នាក់ផ្លូវ និងស្ថានភាពផ្លូវ ដែលអាចធ្វើឲ្យអ្នកបើកបរមានស្មារតី ប្រុងប្រយ័ត្នជាមុនបាន។)

It is prohibited to write anything else or paint on signboards, or use signboard supports, or on any other materials used for traffic signs, which causes drivers to be unable to see traffic signs clearly, or misunderstand the meaning of traffic signs, or blind the drivers. (ហាមមិនឱ្យដាក់សរសេរអ្វីផ្សេងៗទៀត ឬគូសវាសនៅលើផ្លាកសញ្ញា ឬប្រើប្រាស់ទម្រផ្លាកសញ្ញា ឬនៅលើសម្ភារៈអ្វីផ្សេងៗទៀតដែលប្រើប្រាស់សម្រាប់សញ្ញាចរាចរណ៍ ដែលជាហេតុធ្វើឲ្យអ្នកបើកបរ មិនអាចមើលឃើញសញ្ញាចរាចរណ៍ច្បាស់លាស់ល្អ ឬយល់ខ្លឹមសារសញ្ញាចរាចរណ៍មិនបានត្រឹមត្រូវ ឬធ្វើ ឲ្យអ្នកបើកបរចាំងភ្នែក។)

The images and contents of road traffic signs shall be determined by a declaration (Prakas) of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport. (រូបភាពនិងខ្លឹមសារស្តីពីសញ្ញាចរាចរណ៍ផ្លូវគោក ត្រូវកំណត់ដោយប្រកាសរបស់រដ្ឋមន្ត្រីក្រសួងសាធារណការនិងដឹកជញ្ជូន។)

#### Article 6 (មាត្រា ៦) .-
The order of priority is defined as follows: (លំដាប់សិទ្ធិអាទិភាពត្រូវបានកំណត់ដូចតទៅ៖)

1- Traffic signboards have priority over general traffic regulations. (១- ផ្លាកសញ្ញាចរាចរណ៍មានសិទ្ធិអាទិភាពលើបទប្បញ្ញត្តិចរាចរណ៍។)

2- Traffic lights have priority over traffic signboards. (២- ភ្លើងសញ្ញាចរាចរណ៍មានសិទ្ធិអាទិភាពលើផ្លាកសញ្ញាចរាចរណ៍។)

3- Command signals of traffic agents have priority over traffic lights, road markings, road traffic signboards, and all traffic regulations. (៣- សញ្ញាបញ្ជារបស់ភ្នាក់ងារចរាចរណ៍មានសិទ្ធិអាទិភាពលើភ្លើងសញ្ញាចរាចរណ៍ គំនូសសញ្ញាចរាចរណ៍នៅលើទ្រូងផ្លូវ ផ្លាកសញ្ញាចរាចរណ៍នៅតាមដងផ្លូវ និងបទប្បញ្ញត្តិចរាចរណ៍ទាំងឡាយ។)

# Chapter 3 (ជំពូកទី៣)

## Drivers of Vehicles (អ្នកបើកបរយានជំនិះ)

#### Article 7 (មាត្រា ៧) .-
Driving all types of vehicles in the Kingdom of Cambodia must always keep to the right. Every moving vehicle on the road must have a driver. (ការបើកបរយានជំនិះគ្រប់ប្រភេទក្នុងព្រះរាជាណាចក្រកម្ពុជា ត្រូវប្រកាន់ស្តាំជានិច្ច។ គ្រប់យានជំនិះដែលមានចលនានៅលើផ្លូវថ្នល់ ត្រូវតែមានអ្នកបើកបរ។)

Goods transport vehicles with a total weight of more than 16 (sixteen) tons or passenger transport vehicles carrying passengers from 35 (thirty-five) persons and above, must have 1 (one) driver and 1 (one) assistant driver holding a valid driving license corresponding to the type of vehicle, appropriate for the type of road, travel distance, and travel time. (រថយន្តដឹកទំនិញដែលមានទម្ងន់សរុបលើសពី១៦ (ដប់ប្រាំមួយ) តោន ឬរថយន្តដឹកអ្នកដំណើរដែលផ្ទុកអ្នកដំណើរចាប់ពី៣៥ (សាមសិបប្រាំ) នាក់ឡើង ត្រូវមានអ្នកបើកបរ១ (មួយ) នាក់និងជំនួយការបើកបរ១ (មួយ) នាក់ ដែលកាន់ប័ណ្ណបើកបរត្រឹមត្រូវតាមប្រភេទរថយន្តអមជាមួយស្របតាមប្រភេទផ្លូវ ចម្ងាយផ្លូវ និងពេលវេលាធ្វើដំណើរ។)

#### Article 8 (New) (មាត្រា ៨ .- ថ្មី)
All drivers of all types of motor vehicles must comply with the following measures: (គ្រប់អ្នកបើកបរយានយន្តគ្រប់ប្រភេទ ត្រូវអនុវត្តវិធានការដូចតទៅ៖)

1- Driving licenses are required for drivers of the following motor vehicles: (១- ត្រូវមានប័ណ្ណបើកបរចំពោះអ្នកបើកបរយានយន្តដូចខាងក្រោម៖)

    - All types of cars (រថយន្តគ្រប់ប្រភេទ)

    - Motorcycles with trailers and tricycles (ទោចក្រយានយន្តសណ្ដោងរ៉ឺម៉ក និងត្រីចក្រយានយន្ត)

    - Motor vehicles with cylinder capacity exceeding 125 (one hundred twenty-five) cubic centimeters or electric power exceeding 11 (eleven) kilowatts. For motor vehicles with cylinder capacity up to 125 (one hundred twenty-five) cubic centimeters or electric power up to 11 (eleven) kilowatts, drivers are not required to hold a driving license, but must be at least 15 (fifteen) years of age or older, holding a Khmer Nationality Identification Card or any identification document. (យានយន្តដែលមានទំហំស៊ីឡាំងលើសពី១២៥ (មួយរយម្ភៃប្រាំ) សង់ទីម៉ែត្រគូបឡើងទៅ ឬមានថាមពលអគ្គិសនីលើសពី១១ (ដប់មួយ) គីឡូវ៉ាត់។ ចំពោះយានយន្តដែលមានទំហំស៊ីឡាំងត្រឹម១២៥ (មួយរយម្ភៃប្រាំ) សង់ទីម៉ែត្រគូបចុះក្រោម ឬមានថាមពលអគ្គិសនីត្រឹម១១ (ដប់មួយ) គីឡូវ៉ាត់ចុះក្រោម អ្នកបើកបរមិនតម្រូវឲ្យមានប័ណ្ណបើកបរឡើយ ប៉ុន្តែត្រូវមានអាយុយ៉ាងតិច១៥ (ដប់ប្រាំ) ឆ្នាំឡើងទៅដោយមានអត្តសញ្ញាណប័ណ្ណសញ្ជាតិខ្មែរ ឬឯកសារបញ្ជាក់អត្តសញ្ញាណណាមួយ។)

2- When driving a vehicle, the driver must always be attentive and in a state where they can drive easily and quickly, especially without any obstruction to their movement. (២- នៅពេលបើកបរយានជំនិះ អ្នកបើកបរត្រូវមានស្មារតីប្រុងប្រយ័ត្នជាប់ជានិច្ច ហើយស្ថិតក្នុងស្ថានភាពដែលអាចធ្វើសកម្មភាពបើកបរបានយ៉ាងស្រួល និងឆាប់រហ័ស ជាពិសេសលទ្ធភាពនៃការធ្វើចលនាមិនត្រូវឲ្យមានការរំខានឡើយ។)

3- It is prohibited to disturb residents living along the road and road users. (៣- ហាមធ្វើឲ្យរំខានដល់អ្នករស់នៅជាប់តាមដងផ្លូវនិងអ្នកប្រើប្រាស់ផ្លូវ។)

4- It is prohibited to drink alcohol that leads to an alcohol rate starting from 0.25 (zero point two five) milligrams per liter of air, or starting from 0.50 (zero point five zero) grams per liter of blood and above. (៤- ហាមពិសាគ្រឿងស្រវឹង ដែលបណ្តាលឲ្យមានអត្រាជាតិអាល់កុលចាប់ពី០,២៥ (សូន្យក្បៀសម្ភៃប្រាំ) មីលីក្រាមក្នុងមួយលីត្រខ្យល់ ឬចាប់ពី០,៥០ (សូន្យក្បៀសហាសិប) ក្រាមក្នុងមួយលីត្រឈាមឡើងទៅ។)

5- It is prohibited to drive a vehicle with metal track wheels directly on the roadway. This type of vehicle must be transported by a vehicle with rubber tires. (៥- ហាមបើកបរយានជំនិះដែលមានកង់ច្រវាក់ដែកផ្ទាល់លើទ្រូងផ្លូវ។ យានជំនិះប្រភេទនេះ ត្រូវដឹកតាមយានជំនិះដែលមានប្រភេទកង់កៅស៊ូ។)

6- It is prohibited to use a telephone while driving a vehicle without a hands-free device. (៦- ហាមប្រើទូរស័ព្ទនៅពេលកំពុងបើកបរយានជំនិះ ដោយគ្មានឧបករណ៍សំឡេងភ្ជាប់ទំនាក់ទំនង។)

7- It is prohibited to carry passengers, goods or other objects that obstruct the driver's view. (៧- ហាមផ្ទុកអ្នកដំណើរ ទំនិញ ឬវត្ថុផ្សេងៗដែលនាំឲ្យបាំងភ្នែកអ្នកបើកបរ។)

8- Motorcycles may carry two people and can add one child. Drivers of motorcycles and passengers, as well as children aged 3 (three) years and above, must wear safety helmets properly. Drivers of tricycles and drivers of motorcycles with trailers must wear safety helmets properly. (៨- ទោចក្រយានយន្តអាចផ្ទុកមនុស្សពីរនាក់និងអាចបន្ថែមបានកុមារម្នាក់។ អ្នកបើកបរទោចក្រយានយន្តនិងអ្នករួមដំណើរ ព្រមទាំងកុមារដែលមានអាយុចាប់ពី៣ (បី) ឆ្នាំឡើងទៅ ត្រូវពាក់មួកសុវត្ថិភាពឲ្យបានត្រឹមត្រូវ។ អ្នកបើកបរត្រីចក្រយានយន្តនិងអ្នកបើកបរទោចក្រយានយន្តសណ្ដោងរ៉ឺម៉ក ត្រូវពាក់មួកសុវត្ថិភាពឲ្យបានត្រឹមត្រូវ។)

9- At all times of traveling on the road, drivers and passengers sitting in the front seats of cars must wear seat belts. (៩- នៅគ្រប់ពេលធ្វើចរាចរណ៍តាមដងផ្លូវ អ្នកបើកបរនិងអ្នករួមដំណើរជិះកៅអីមុខនៃរថយន្ត ត្រូវពាក់ខ្សែក្រវាត់សុវត្ថិភាព។)

10- Outside built-up areas, all drivers and passengers in cars must wear seat belts in accordance with the provisions of Article 90 (New) of this Law. (១០- នៅក្រៅទីប្រជុំជនអ្នកបើកបរនិងអ្នករួមដំណើរទាំងអស់តាមរថយន្ត ត្រូវពាក់ខ្សែក្រវាត់សុវត្ថិភាព ស្របតាមបទប្បញ្ញត្តិដូចមានចែងក្នុងមាត្រា៩០ថ្មី នៃច្បាប់នេះ។)

11- Children under 10 (ten) months of age must be placed in a child safety seat designed for infants, with seat belts securing the seat to the rear seat of the car. (១១- កុមារអាយុតិចជាង១០ (ដប់) ខែ ត្រូវដាក់ក្នុងកៅអីសម្រាប់កុមារដេកនិងមានខ្សែក្រវាត់សុវត្ថិភាពចងផ្នែកកៅអីនេះឲ្យជាប់ទៅនឹងកៅអីក្រោយនៃរថយន្ត។)

12- Children from 10 (ten) months to under 5 (five) years of age must sit in a child safety seat with seat belts securing the seat to the rear seat of the car. (១២- កុមារមានអាយុចាប់ពី១០ (ដប់) ខែទៅក្រោម៥ (ប្រាំ) ឆ្នាំ ត្រូវអង្គុយក្នុងកៅអីសម្រាប់កុមារ ដែលមានខ្សែក្រវាត់សុវត្ថិភាពចងផ្នែកកៅអីនេះឲ្យជាប់ទៅនឹងកៅអីក្រោយនៃរថយន្ត។)

13- Children under 10 (ten) years of age are prohibited from sitting in the front seat of a car. (១៣- ហាមកុមារអាយុក្រោម១០ (ដប់) ឆ្នាំ អង្គុយនៅកៅអីមុខនៃរថយន្ត។)

#### Article 9 (មាត្រា ៩) .-
On a roadway with two-way traffic and without traffic direction markings, the driver must use the right half of the roadway, driving close to the right side. (នៅលើទ្រូងផ្លូវដែលមានទិសចរាចរណ៍ពីរ ហើយដែលគ្មានគំនូសសញ្ញាបែងចែកទិសចរាចរណ៍ អ្នកបើកបរត្រូវប្រើប្រាស់ពាក់កណ្តាលទ្រូងផ្លូវផ្នែកខាងស្តាំ ដោយបើកបរកៀកទៅខាងស្តាំ។)

On a one-way roadway without lane markings, the driver must drive close to the right side unless they need to turn left. The driver may use the left side of the roadway when overtaking or stopping. Separately, in built-up areas, the use of roadways shall be determined by a declaration of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport. (នៅលើទ្រូងផ្លូវឯកទិសដែលគ្មានគំនូសសញ្ញាបែងចែកគន្លងផ្លូវ អ្នកបើកបរត្រូវបើកបរកៀកទៅខាងស្តាំលើកលែងតែត្រូវការបត់ឆ្វេង។ អ្នកបើកបរអាចប្រើប្រាស់ទ្រូងផ្លូវខាងឆ្វេងបាននៅពេលជែងឬឈប់។ ដោយឡែកនៅក្នុងទីប្រជុំជន ការប្រើប្រាស់ទ្រូងផ្លូវត្រូវកំណត់ដោយប្រកាសរបស់រដ្ឋមន្ត្រីក្រសួងសាធារណការនិងដឹកជញ្ជូន។)

#### Article 10 (មាត្រា ១០) .-
On a lane designated for a specific type of vehicle, drivers of other types of vehicles must not enter this lane, except when entering or leaving their own lanes. (នៅលើគន្លងផ្លូវតម្រូវសម្រាប់យានជំនិះប្រភេទណាមួយ អ្នកបើកបរយានជំនិះប្រភេទដទៃមិនត្រូវបើកបរចូលក្នុងគន្លងនេះទេ លើកលែងតែត្រូវចាកចេញឬចូលទៅក្នុងគន្លងផ្លូវរបស់ខ្លួន។)

On a two-way roadway with roadway markings, the driver must keep to the right side of the road. (នៅលើទ្រូងផ្លូវដែលមានទិសចរាចរណ៍ពីរ ហើយមានគំនូសបែងចែកទ្រូងផ្លូវ អ្នកបើកបរត្រូវប្រកាន់យកចំណែកផ្លូវខាងស្តាំ។)

On a one-way roadway with two or more lanes, the driver must keep to the rightmost lane. In case of heavy traffic congestion, the driver may drive in any lane. (នៅលើទ្រូងផ្លូវឯកទិសដែលមានគន្លងផ្លូវពីរឬច្រើន អ្នកបើកបរត្រូវប្រកាន់យកគន្លងផ្លូវខាងស្តាំបំផុត។ ក្នុងករណីមានចរាចរណ៍កកស្ទះខ្លាំង អ្នកបើកបរអាចបើកបរបានលើគ្រប់គន្លងផ្លូវ។)

On a two-way roadway with opposing traffic directions and where each direction has two or more lanes, the driver must keep to the rightmost lane. In case of heavy traffic congestion, the driver may drive in any lane of that traffic direction. (នៅលើទ្រូងផ្លូវដែលមានទិសចរាចរណ៍ពីរផ្ទុយគ្នា ហើយនៅលើចំណែកផ្លូវនៃទិសនីមួយៗដែលមានគន្លងផ្លូវពីរឬច្រើន អ្នកបើកបរត្រូវប្រកាន់យកគន្លងផ្លូវខាងស្តាំបំផុត។ ក្នុងករណីមានចរាចរណ៍កកស្ទះខ្លាំងអ្នកបើកបរអាចបើកបរបានលើគ្រប់គន្លងផ្លូវតាមទិសចរាចរណ៍។)

In case of heavy traffic congestion as stipulated in paragraph 3 and paragraph 4 above, the fact that vehicles in one lane travel faster than vehicles in another lane shall not be considered as overtaking. For drivers of vehicles with a maximum total weight exceeding 3.5 (three point five) tons, they must always keep to the rightmost lane, except when turning left or when overtaking where they may use other lanes. Separately, in built-up areas, the use of roadways shall be determined by a declaration of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport. (ក្នុងករណីមានចរាចរណ៍កកស្ទះខ្លាំង ដូចមានចែងនៅកថាខណ្ឌ៣និងកថាខណ្ឌ៤ខាងលើមានន័យថានៅក្នុងគន្លងណាមួយលឿនជាងយានជំនិះនៅក្នុងគន្លងណាមួយទៀត មិនចាត់ទុកជាការជែងឡើយ។ ចំពោះអ្នកបើកបរយានជំនិះដែលមានទម្ងន់សរុបលើសពី៣,៥ (បីកន្លះ) តោន ត្រូវប្រកាន់យកគន្លងផ្លូវខាងស្តាំជានិច្ច លើកលែងមានការបត់ឆ្វេងឬនៅពេលជែងអាចប្រើប្រាស់គន្លងផ្លូវផ្សេងទៀតបាន។ ដោយឡែកនៅក្នុងទីប្រជុំជន ការប្រើប្រាស់ទ្រូងផ្លូវត្រូវកំណត់ដោយប្រកាសរបស់រដ្ឋមន្ត្រីក្រសួងសាធារណការនិងដឹកជញ្ជូន។)

by a declaration of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport. (ដោយប្រកាសរបស់រដ្ឋមន្ត្រីក្រសួងសាធារណការ និងដឹកជញ្ជូន។)

#### Article 11 (មាត្រា ១១) .-
A yellow traffic light indicates caution and the requirement to stop; a green light indicates permission for vehicles to proceed; a red light indicates an absolute prohibition to proceed. (ភ្លើងសញ្ញាចរាចរណ៍បញ្ចេញពន្លឺពណ៌លឿង ជាសញ្ញាឲ្យប្រុងប្រយ័ត្ន ត្រូវឈប់ ពន្លឺពណ៌បៃតងជាសញ្ញាអនុញ្ញាតឲ្យយានជំនិះចេញដំណើរ ពន្លឺពណ៌ក្រហមជាសញ្ញាហាមដាច់ខាតមិនឲ្យទៅ។)

A red traffic light is a prohibition to proceed; drivers must stop in front of the solid stop line across the roadway or in front of the pedestrian crossing markings. If there is no obstacle ahead, the rightmost vehicle may turn right with caution, except when there is a red arrow signal prohibiting right turns. (ភ្លើងសញ្ញាចរាចរណ៍បញ្ចេញពន្លឺពណ៌ក្រហមគឺជាសញ្ញាហាមឃាត់ឲ្យឈប់ អ្នកបើកបរត្រូវឈប់នៅមុខខ្សែបន្ទាត់គំនូសសញ្ញាជាប់កាត់ទទឹងទ្រូងផ្លូវឬមុខគំនូសសញ្ញាសម្រាប់ថ្មើរជើងឆ្លងកាត់។ បើគ្មានឧបសគ្គនៅខាងមុខ យានជំនិះខាងស្តាំបំផុតអាចបត់ស្តាំបានដោយប្រុងប្រយ័ត្ន លើកលែងតែមានភ្លើងសញ្ញាព្រួញក្រហមហាមបត់ស្តាំ។)

#### Article 12 (មាត្រា ១២) .-
Drivers must bypass to the right side of any roundabout or traffic dividing island that forms an obstacle to the straight movement of the vehicle, unless otherwise specified. (អ្នកបើកបរត្រូវវាងតាមស្តាំដៃ នៅគ្រប់រង្វង់មូលឬខឿនបែងចែកទ្រូងផ្លូវ ដែលជាឧបសគ្គនៃចលនាត្រង់ទៅមុខរបស់យានជំនិះ លើកលែងតែមានកំណត់ផ្សេង។)

#### Article 13 (មាត្រា ១៣) .-
Drivers must not drive abreast on the same roadway or lane. Drivers must not drive across or disrupt a military convoy, a police convoy, a delegation convoy, or an official procession. (អ្នកបើកបរមិនត្រូវបើកបរទន្ទឹមគ្នានៅលើទ្រូងផ្លូវឬគន្លងផ្លូវតែមួយទេ។ អ្នកបើកបរមិនត្រូវបើកបរកាត់ ឬដណ្ដើមជួរយោធា ឬជួរនគរបាល ឬជួរគណៈប្រតិភូ ឬជួរដង្ហែផ្លូវការ។)

#### Article 14 (មាត្រា ១៤) .-
The rules for towing must comply with the following provisions: (បែបបទនៃការសណ្ដោង ត្រូវអនុវត្តតាមបញ្ញត្តិដូចខាងក្រោម៖)

- For coupling a towed vehicle, the driver must use a tow rope, tow bar, or coupling device that ensures a secure coupling and ease of turning. (ចំពោះការភ្ជាប់សណ្ដោងយានជំនិះ អ្នកបើកបរត្រូវប្រើខ្សែសាប ឬដងដែក ឬឧបករណ៍សម្រាប់ភ្ជាប់ ដែលធានាភ្ជាប់មាំមួននិងងាយស្រួលក្នុងការបត់បែន)

- It is prohibited for a driver to use a tow rope for vehicles with a maximum total weight exceeding 5 (five) tons. (ហាមអ្នកបើកបរប្រើខ្សែសណ្ដោងចំពោះយានជំនិះដែលមានទម្ងន់សរុបលើសពី៥ (ប្រាំ) តោនឡើងទៅ)

- It is prohibited for a driver to tow more than one vehicle, or to tow a vehicle that already has a trailer or semi-trailer. (ហាមអ្នកបើកបរសណ្ដោងយានជំនិះលើសពីមួយគ្រឿង ឬសណ្ដោងយានជំនិះដែលមានសណ្ដោងរ៉ឺម៉កឬសឺមីរ៉ឺម៉ក)

- It is prohibited to tow a bicycle or a motorcycle. (ហាមសណ្ដោងទោចក្រយានឬទោចក្រយានយន្ត)

- It is prohibited for a driver to tow any type of car with passengers inside, other than the person steering the towed vehicle. (ហាមអ្នកបើកបរសណ្ដោងរថយន្តគ្រប់ប្រភេទ ដែលមានអ្នកដំណើរនៅក្នុងនោះ ក្រៅពីអ្នកកាន់ចង្កូត)

- Cars with a trailer or semi-trailer must have an equilateral triangle sign with two yellow reflectors placed at the front of the vehicle on both sides of the license plate. (រថយន្តដែលមានសណ្ដោងរ៉ឺម៉កឬសឺមីរ៉ឺម៉ក ត្រូវមានសញ្ញាត្រីកោណសមបាតដែលមានកញ្ចក់ចាំងពន្លឺពណ៌លឿងពីរដាក់នៅខាងរថយន្តខាងមុខអមសងខាងផ្លាកលេខ។)

The technical aspects of coupling and uncoupling a trailer or semi-trailer to/from a car or a towing truck shall be determined by a declaration of the Minister of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport. (បច្ចេកទេសនៃការភ្ជាប់សណ្ដោងរ៉ឺម៉កឬសឺមីរ៉ឺម៉ក និងការដោះរ៉ឺម៉កឬសឺមីរ៉ឺម៉កចេញពីរថយន្ត ឬក្បាលរថយន្ត ត្រូវកំណត់ដោយប្រកាសរបស់រដ្ឋមន្ត្រីក្រសួងសាធារណការនិងដឹកជញ្ជូន។)

#### Article 15 (មាត្រា ១៥) .-
Near a bus stop in built-up areas, drivers of all types of vehicles must reduce speed and, if necessary, stop to allow buses to easily pull out, pull in, or stop. (នៅកន្លែងជិតនឹងកន្លែងឈប់រថយន្តក្រុងនៅក្នុងទីប្រជុំជន អ្នកបើកបរយានជំនិះគ្រប់ប្រភេទត្រូវបន្ថយល្បឿន ហើយបើចាំបាច់ត្រូវឈប់ដើម្បីទុកឲ្យរថយន្តក្រុងចេញ ចូល ឬឈប់បានដោយងាយស្រួល។)

#### Article 16

The driving speed of vehicles shall be determined according to the following conditions:

- The determination of speed limits for vehicles shall be specified by Sub-decree.

- A driver shall not drive at an abnormally slow speed without proper justification, which causes disruption to other vehicles. A driver who drives at a slow speed must drive close to the right side to allow drivers who drive at a faster speed to pass easily from the left.

- Any driver who is forced to reduce speed to an abnormally slow speed must notify other drivers by using hazard warning lights and must drive closest to the right side of the road.

- Speed limit regulations do not apply to drivers of vehicles of the Police, Royal Cambodian Armed Forces, delegations, high-ranking leaders, fire engines, hospital ambulances, and some other vehicles sounding sirens or flashing special signal lights while performing their duties.

#### Article 17

In all cases, the driver must always maintain control over their driving speed and must drive at a speed appropriate to the actual conditions to be able to stop the vehicle in a timely and safe manner.

The driver must reduce speed in the following cases:

1- When passing each other on narrow roads.

2- When passing passenger transport vehicles or child transport vehicles that are stopping to pick up or drop off passengers or children.

3- When approaching a bridge or approaching a railway crossing.

4- In bad weather conditions that make it impossible to see far and clearly.

5- When changing traffic direction.

6- At road turns, curves, slippery roads, intersections, or roundabouts.

7- When approaching a high hill or when going down a slope.

8- When passing or overtaking a stopped vehicle convoy, or when passing or overtaking other processions.

9- When people are present or when there are many houses close to the road side.

10- In all cases where there are road signs instructing to reduce speed or warning of danger.

11- Whenever there are police, military, royal gendarmerie, delegation or high-ranking leader vehicles, fire engines, hospital ambulances sounding sirens or flashing signal lights, or other vehicles sounding sirens or flashing signal lights, users of

road users must reduce speed and drive close to the right side, and if necessary, stop at the closest possible edge to facilitate the passage of these vehicles to proceed safely.

12- Places with schools or hospitals located near the road within a distance of not more than 50 (fifty) meters.

#### Article 18

A vehicle travelling behind another vehicle must maintain a sufficient safe distance to avoid a collision when the vehicle in front reduces speed or stops unexpectedly. The safe distance for all types of vehicles travelling on the road shall be determined by the declaration of the Minister of Public Works and Transport.

#### Article 19

In overtaking, the driver must comply with the following:

1- Drivers of all types of vehicles must overtake on the left. However, the driver may overtake on the right if the vehicle being overtaken is signaling a left turn and has stopped or is preparing to turn left.

2- Before overtaking, the driver must be certain that the overtaking does not cause any danger, by complying with the following:

a- Clearly see that there is no obstacle ahead and that it is possible to return to the normal traffic lane after overtaking without disrupting the traffic of others.

b- No vehicle driver behind them has started to request to overtake them.

c- No signal to request to overtake another vehicle from the vehicle driver in front of them.

d- Must be certain that the relative speed between the two vehicles allows overtaking in the shortest possible time.

e- Must signal the request to overtake by using the left turn signal, and if necessary, additionally use alternating low-beam and high-beam headlights at night or the horn during the day, except in built-up areas where the use of the horn to request overtaking is prohibited.

f- Must be certain that the vehicle drivers behind and ahead are aware of their request to overtake.

3- Upon knowing that the vehicle behind requests to overtake, the driver of the vehicle in front must inspect the road section ahead of them; if there is no obstacle, they must give a right turn signal or raise their left hand upwards bending to the right, reduce speed and drive close to the right side, as a signal for the driver of the requesting vehicle behind to overtake. In the event that the front driver finds that the section

road ahead is not clear due to obstacles, they must signal a left turn or extend their left arm straight outwards to indicate that overtaking cannot be performed yet.

4- When overtaking, they must increase speed and maintain a distance from the other vehicle of at least 1 (one) meter.

5- On roads without lane markings, the driver may overtake by using the other side of the road, provided this does not cause danger or disrupt vehicles travelling in the opposite direction.

6- Once past the overtaken vehicle, the driver must signal a right turn to notify that they are returning to their normal traffic lane.

7- In case the road has multiple lanes in the same direction for different routes, a vehicle in one lane travelling faster than a vehicle in another lane shall not be considered as overtaking.

Overtaking is prohibited in the following cases:

- Overtaking in three rows (overtaking a vehicle that is already overtaking)

- Places with a no-overtaking sign

- At road curves or steep hills, unless this overtaking can leave the left side of the center dividing line vacant

- Close to road turns, intersections, or slippery roads

- At the top of a steep slope, narrow roads, or roads with speed reduction signs

- On bridges or when going down a slope

- At construction sites

- Near or at designated pedestrian crossings

- Under bridges or tunnels

- Under poor visibility conditions, such as heavy rain, thick fog, heavy dust, or heavy smoke

- Near or at railway crossings without barriers

- On flooded roads.

#### Article 20

In passing:

1- Drivers of all types of vehicles must pass each other by keeping to their respective right.

2- When passing each other on narrow roads or dangerous roads, drivers must reduce their respective speed. In case of necessity, both vehicles must stop, and if they are large-sized vehicles, whichever vehicle wishes to proceed first must signal for the way by raising a hand or sounding the horn in

during the day or use alternating low-beam and high-beam headlights as a signal at night, and wait for the oncoming vehicle to signal agreement to let them proceed first, before proceeding. However, in the case of different types of vehicles, the driver of the larger vehicle must yield to the vehicle that is smaller than theirs to proceed first.

3- When passing each other on a narrow road where only one vehicle can travel, and there is a passing place, the vehicle that arrives at this place first must stop and wait there to allow the other vehicle to pass easily.

4- In the case of a narrow road where vehicles cannot pass each other and it is necessary for one vehicle to reverse, the following shall be complied with:

a- For vehicles of the same type, whichever vehicle wishes to proceed first must signal for the way and must obtain agreement to reverse from the other vehicle.

b- For vehicles without trailers and vehicles with trailers, the vehicle without a trailer must reverse.

c- For light vehicles and heavy vehicles, the light vehicle must reverse.

d- For trucks and passenger transport vehicles, the truck must reverse.

5- When passing each other on a steep and narrow road, the vehicle going down the slope must stop to allow the vehicle going up the slope to proceed first.

6- In passing each other, whichever vehicle observes an obstacle on its side of the road, such as a damaged road or a stationary vehicle, must stop to allow the other vehicle to proceed first.

7- On a narrow bridge where only one vehicle can drive, large vehicles must yield the right of way to light vehicles to proceed first.

8- Before passing each other at night outside built-up areas and where there is no street lighting, both vehicles must switch off their high-beam headlights and switch on their low-beam headlights.

#### Article 21

Right of way at intersections shall be determined as follows:

1- Every driver, when approaching an intersection, must reduce speed and inspect the traffic on the road they are crossing, and must cross with extreme caution if the road ahead is not clear. If necessary, they must signal to notify drivers coming from other roads by using the horn during the day and using signal lights or flashing signal lights at night. On a road where right of way is yielded to others, the driver must drive at a speed that allows them to stop their vehicle to yield the right of way to vehicles on the priority road.

2- Even if at an intersection there is a green traffic light permitting forward traffic, a driver must not enter the intersection if doing so might require them to stop their vehicle within the intersection due to heavy traffic congestion. In such cases, the driver must stop before the intersection to avoid blocking the traffic of vehicles coming from other directions.

3- At intersections without right of way signs, the driver must yield the right of way to vehicle drivers coming from their right. However, in the case of a T-junction (three-way intersection) without right of way signs, the driver of the vehicle on the ending road must yield the right of way to vehicle drivers on the continuing road to proceed first.

4- A driver approaching a roundabout, regardless of the type of road they are on, must yield the right of way to drivers who are already inside the roundabout which has directional signs to proceed first.

5- Upon arriving at an intersection with right of way priority signs, the driver must yield the road to vehicles on the priority road to proceed first, and may cross the intersection only when they are certain that there is no danger.

6- Upon arriving at an intersection with a 'STOP' sign, the driver must stop completely before the transverse line across the road to yield to vehicles and pedestrians on the road who have the right of way to proceed first, and may cross the intersection only when they are certain that there is no danger.

7- Separately, the driver must yield to service vehicles of the police, military, royal gendarmerie, fire engines, or hospital ambulances that are performing duties by sounding acoustic signals and flashing special light signals to indicate their arrival to proceed first.

8- Vehicles performing duties as listed below have right of way priority in the following order:

- Fire engines

- Hospital ambulances

- Vehicles of the police, military, royal gendarmerie, delegation convoys, high-ranking leader convoys, or vehicle convoys led by traffic police.

9- Before driving out of a private road that is not open to public traffic, such as residential gates, factories, buildings, or other plazas, the driver must exercise extreme caution and must yield the right of way to vehicles or pedestrians traveling on the public road to proceed first.

10- A driver exiting a dirt road or track must yield the right of way to drivers on other roads that are not dirt roads or tracks to proceed first.

11- At intersections, if a road is divided into several traffic lanes, and one or more lanes are reserved for the traffic of certain types of vehicles, the traffic priority rules specified in this Article shall apply to drivers travelling on this type of lane, except as provided in points 7 and 8 above.

#### Article 22

Driving across railway crossings:

1- The driver must reduce speed when approaching a railway crossing.

2- At railway crossings with barriers or gates, the driver must not enter when the barriers are closed, closing, or opening.

3- At railway crossings with a guard, the driver must obey the instructions of the guard and must not cause any obstacle to the closing or opening of the barriers or gates.

4- Whenever a train is approaching, all road users must stop immediately to yield the way to the train to proceed first. All animal herders must keep animals, herds, and beasts of burden away, and absolutely prevent them from crossing the railway track.

5- When a train signals its approaching, every driver must stop at least 2 (two) meters from the barriers or gates, and if there are no barriers or gates, must stop at least 5 (five) meters from the railway track.

6- In the event of a stalled vehicle or animal herd stranded on the railway track, the driver or animal herder must find a way to resolve it to avoid causing an obstacle to the train, or must notify the nearest railway official responsible about the danger that could occur if they cannot avoid the obstacle on the railway track by themselves.

#### Article 23

Stopping and parking on the road shall comply with the following:

1- Every vehicle that stops or parks must not cause disruption to other vehicles traveling on the road.

2- Within built-up areas, every vehicle must stop or park in the direction of its travel, by complying with the following regulations:

- For two-way roads, park or stop on the right side in the direction of their travel

- For one-way roads, park or stop on the right or left side in the direction of travel.

3- Outside built-up areas, vehicles or beasts of burden must stop or park outside the road. If there is no other space where vehicles can park outside the road, they must comply with the regulations

as specified in point 1 above.

4- Outside built-up areas, any vehicle that is broken down or forced to stop or park on the road must use hazard warning lights and/or place at least one warning triangle at a distance of at least 30 (thirty) meters, which must be visible from at least 100 (one hundred) meters. In case the above problem occurs in a place where stopping or parking is prohibited, the driver must notify the nearest competent authority to take further measures.

5- Within built-up areas, every vehicle must stop or park to load or unload goods or passengers at a distance of not more than 25 (twenty-five) centimeters from the edge of the sidewalk, and at least 5 (five) meters from intersections, road bends, or curves, and a distance of at least 10 (ten) meters from intersections, road bends, or curves of a boulevard. Outside built-up areas, all vehicles must stop or park at least 10 (ten) meters from intersections, road bends, or curves.

6- In parking, before walking away from their vehicle, the driver must engage the handbrake or take other measures to prevent any accidental danger during their absence.

7- Stopping or parking of vehicles is prohibited in the following places:

- On roads, sidewalks reserved for pedestrians, and within a distance of at least 5 (five) meters in front of pedestrian crossings across the road or on these crossings

- On pedestrian paths

- On spaces reserved only for the stopping or parking of certain types of vehicles

- Between the edge of the road and a continuous longitudinal line, if the distance between the continuous line and the parked vehicle is less than 3 (three) meters, which prevents another vehicle from passing without risk or crossing the continuous line

- Near traffic light posts or road signs that obstruct the view of road users from seeing these traffic signs

- At any place that obstructs the entry/exit, parking, or stopping of other vehicles

- On bridges, underpasses, tunnels, intersections, railway crossings, curves, roundabouts, sharp curves, or steep slopes where other vehicles cannot be seen from a distance

- Within less than 20 (twenty) meters from a railway crossing

- Within less than 10 (ten) meters in front of and behind a fire hydrant location of fire engines and in front of a fire engine station

- On the entry/exit of public buildings

- On the road in front of the entry/exit of private houses, except for the vehicle of the owner of that same property

- In two parallel rows

- On only one side of the road if the other facing side is a designated parking space for vehicles, except in cases where parking on both sides is permitted

- In places with a no-stopping or no-parking sign

- On the road for more than 24 hours

- Within less than 10 (ten) meters in front of and behind a bus stop sign

- Within less than 1 (one) meter in front of or behind vehicles that are stopped or parked back-to-back

- Near intersections or road bends.

#### Article 24

Opening vehicle doors to get in or out must always be done with caution, especially when opening doors to get in or out on the left side of the vehicle.

#### Article 25

Before moving off from a parking space, the driver must give a clear and sufficient signal to notify others using the road, and must be certain that the departure of the vehicle does not cause any danger to others.

Before changing the vehicle's direction, such as turning left or right, changing lanes, making a U-turn or reversing, pulling over to stop or park, the driver must reduce speed and give a clear and sufficient signal to notify others using the road.

In turning left or right, making a U-turn or reversing, the driver must comply with the following rules:

1- Turning left

- Before turning left, the driver must look behind through the rearview mirror, especially the left mirror, to know the position and movement of traffic behind them

- Must give a left turn signal at a sufficient distance from the turning point and reduce speed, and if there is no obstacle, must drive close to the center dividing line of the road

- When about to turn left, the driver must look left, right, and look left again, especially looking at traffic coming from the opposite direction. If the road is clear, turn with caution

especially for pedestrians who are crossing. If the road is not clear, they must wait and allow vehicles coming from the opposite direction and from the right to proceed first

- Left turns must be made by dodging (passing behind), i.e., turning behind the vehicle coming from the opposite direction, unless instructed otherwise by the traffic police.

2- Turning right

- Before turning right, the driver must look behind through the rearview mirror, especially the right mirror, to know the position and movement of traffic behind them

- If there is no obstacle, they must signal a right turn at a sufficient distance from the turning point, reduce speed, and drive close to the right side

- When about to turn right, the driver must look left and right, and must look behind through the right mirror again, especially to ensure that there is no obstacle to the rear right. If clear, drive as close as possible and be cautious of pedestrians who are crossing.

3- U-turns and Reversing

- A driver who intends to make a U-turn or reverse must ensure that this action does not cause major disruption or danger to other road users

- For reversing, the driver must be cautious and look at the traffic movement behind the vehicle every second; especially before reversing, they must carefully inspect the area behind for small children or small animals

- U-turns are prohibited in the following cases:

  - Near intersections or near railway crossings

  - On road curves where the driver cannot see other vehicles from a distance of 150 (one hundred and fifty) meters

  - Near the top of a hill and on roads going down from a hill

  - In places with a no U-turn sign

  - On one-way roads.

The above rules are exempted when there are commands from the land traffic police or road markings on the road.

#### Article 26

When there are pedestrians crossing, the driver must comply with the following:

1- In all cases, the driver must stop when pedestrians are crossing or about to cross the road at designated pedestrian crossings.

2- At or near intersections, the driver is prohibited from

causing obstruction or block to pedestrians who are crossing the road, even if there are no pedestrian crossing markings.

3- In case there are pedestrian crossing markings at intersections where there are traffic lights or traffic control officers, even if there is a green light or a command from the officer to proceed, the driver must not disrupt or block pedestrians who are crossing on the pedestrian crossing markings. For drivers who turn into another road where pedestrians are crossing, the driver must reduce speed and, if necessary, stop to yield the right of way to pedestrians to complete crossing first.

4- In case there are pedestrian crossing markings at intersections where there are no traffic lights or traffic control officers, the driver must reduce speed or must stop to yield the right of way to pedestrians who are crossing or are about to cross to proceed first.

#### Chapter 4
#### Use of Lights and Signals of Vehicles

#### Article 27

At night or during the day when visibility is insufficient, such as during heavy rain, fog, or when crossing a tunnel, the driver of a vehicle on the road must use signal lights in accordance with the following conditions:

1- In the case of driving a motor vehicle:

a- High-beam headlights: At night, on roads without public street lighting, high-beam headlights must generally be used to see far ahead, but caution must be exercised to avoid disrupting traffic coming from the opposite direction.

It is prohibited to use high-beam headlights in cities or built-up areas with sufficient street lighting, except for signaling to request overtaking or opening the way by alternating with low-beam headlights.

b- Low-beam headlights: Low-beam headlights must be used in the following cases:

- At night, in built-up areas and outside built-up areas where there is public street lighting along the road

- At night, outside built-up areas without public street lighting, when about to pass another vehicle, and when driving closely behind another vehicle

- During the day when visibility is insufficient, such as during heavy rain, fog, or when crossing a tunnel

Low-beam headlights can be used alternately with high-beam headlights as a signal replacing the horn, to request the way or to request overtaking at night.

c- Position lights: At night in built-up areas, all moving vehicles must have at least position lights, without having to use low-beam headlights, if the road is sufficiently lit by street lights. These position lights are used to indicate the presence and size of the vehicle to road users looking from the front.

d- Other lights: At night or during the day when weather conditions are bad and visibility is poor, the driver must use:

- Red lights: to indicate the presence of the vehicle and its width to road users looking from behind

- License plate lights: to allow the identification number of the vehicle to be clearly visible from behind

- Width lights: to show the width of the vehicle or protruding load to other road users

- Turn signal lights: to notify other road users when changing direction to the left or to the right

- Reversing signal lights: to notify road users behind the vehicle when reversing

- Stop lights (Brake lights): to notify road users behind the vehicle when braking to reduce speed or stop the vehicle

- Hazard warning lights: to notify other road users of potential dangers, such as when the driver is forced to drive at a slow speed due to towing a broken-down vehicle or carrying protruding items, and at night or when parked on the road outside built-up areas due to vehicle breakdown, involvement in an accident, or spilled goods that cannot be immediately removed

- Fog lights: to be used during fog or heavy rain. Fog lights can be used instead of low-beam headlights or in addition to low-beam headlights

- Daytime running lights: to be used during the day. All types of motor vehicles can use these lights to make it easier for other vehicles to see them.

2- In the case of driving non-motorized vehicles, drivers of the following types of vehicles must comply as follows:

- Handcarts pulled by humans must have a white or yellow light or reflector in front, and one red light or reflector behind. Both lights must be placed at the end of the cart facing the road

- Carts pulled by animals must have two white or yellow lights or reflectors in front at both ends of the cart, and must have two red lights or reflectors

placed at the rear on both sides of the cart.

- Bicycles and tricycles must have at least one white light or reflector in front, and must have at least one red light or reflector behind

- Trailers towed by motorcycles or bicycles must have two red reflectors or lights placed at the rear on both sides of the trailer

3- In the case of stopping or parking motor vehicles on a road without public street lighting, the following must be used:

- Position lights: to notify road users coming from the front of the vehicle's presence and width

- Red lights: to notify road users coming from behind of the vehicle's presence and width

- License plate lights: to allow the identification number of the vehicle to be clearly visible from behind.

In built-up areas, the use of the signal lights specified in this point 3 is not required if there is street lighting and the illumination is sufficient for road users to clearly see the vehicle from a distance of at least 20 (twenty) meters.

4- In the case of a trailer or semi-trailer detached from a motor vehicle, it must be parked close to the sidewalk or road shoulder, and must use one yellow signal light in front and one red light behind. Each light must be placed at the corner of the vehicle 'corner of the trailer' facing the road along which the trailer or semi-trailer is aligned. In built-up areas, the use of these lights is not required if there is street lighting along the road with sufficient illumination to allow road users to see the vehicle parked on the road from a distance of at least 100 (one hundred) meters.

5- In the event that a vehicle is forced to stop or park on the road, which may cause an obstacle or a traffic hazard, such as near intersections, road bends, curves, slopes, and railway crossings, due to vehicle breakdown or involvement in a traffic accident, and it is impossible to move the vehicle off the road, the driver must notify other drivers of the obstacle by using hazard warning lights and/or placing at least one warning triangle at a distance of 30 (thirty) meters behind the vehicle, which must be visible from a distance of at least 100 (one hundred) meters. In case a vehicle has cargo fallen on the road and the cargo cannot be immediately removed, a signal must be made to notify other drivers by using a warning triangle and hazard warning lights or reflectors, or at least use one warning triangle at a distance of at least 30 (thirty) meters from the fallen cargo.

6- Special signal lights may be used at all times while performing duties for:

fire engines, hospital ambulances, vehicles of the police, military, royal gendarmerie, delegation convoys, high-ranking leader convoys, or vehicle convoys led by land traffic police.

#### Article 28

Use of vehicle acoustic signals (horns) shall comply with the following:

1- The use of the acoustic signal 'horn' is permitted only during the day to notify other road users to avoid potential accidents and to request overtaking outside built-up areas.

2- At night, the horn must be replaced by alternating high-beam and low-beam headlight signals to notify other road users to avoid accidents and to request overtaking.

3- Special acoustic signals may be used at all times while performing duties for: fire engines, hospital ambulances, vehicles of the police, military, royal gendarmerie, delegation convoys, high-ranking leader convoys, or vehicle convoys led by land traffic police.

4- Acoustic signals must be used for a short duration, no longer than necessary, except in case of emergency declarations.

5- Air horns are prohibited in cities, built-up areas, or near hospitals.

#### Article 29

Use of special signal lights and special acoustic signals shall comply with the following:

1- Special signal lights are additional lights that emit flashing or rotating beacon light and have multiple colors, with the following meanings:

a- Blue or red special signal lights must be used only for priority vehicles, such as fire engines, hospital ambulances, vehicles of the police, military, royal gendarmerie, delegation convoys, high-ranking leader convoys, or vehicle convoys led by land traffic police, while performing duties, to notify other road users of their presence.

b- Amber (orange) special signal lights must be used only for public service vehicles, such as street-cleaning vehicles, road maintenance and repair vehicles, and special transport vehicles equipped with these special signal lights, and they must drive at a slow speed.

2- Special acoustic signals are additional acoustic signals used for priority vehicles, such as fire engines, hospital ambulances, vehicles of the police, military, royal gendarmerie, delegation convoys, high-ranking leader convoys, or vehicle convoys led by land traffic police, which have an added siren sound on their vehicles. As for hospital emergency ambulances, they have a two-tone sound rising and falling repeatedly.

3- When encountering vehicles emitting special amber (orange) signal lights while performing their duties, drivers must be cautious in passing and overtaking these vehicles.

4- When encountering priority vehicles performing duties with special signal lights and special acoustic signals, other road users must reduce speed, pull over to the right, or stop to allow these vehicles to pass or cross easily.

Particularly, when performing duties, priority vehicles such as fire engines, hospital ambulances, vehicles of the police, military, royal gendarmerie, delegation convoys, high-ranking leader convoys, or vehicle convoys led by land traffic police, may drive:

- In the opposite direction on one-way roads

- When the traffic lights show a red signal

- At an unlimited speed

- With priority at intersections

- Crossing or driving over a continuous longitudinal dividing line.

In any case, the driver of this type of vehicle must always drive with caution to avoid danger to other road users, and must strictly respect the commands of traffic officers.

#### Article 30

Installing horns, lights, or other decorative lights other than those specified in the technical regulations of the vehicle is prohibited.

#### Chapter 5
#### Pedestrians and Persons Riding, Driving or Leading Animals

#### Article 31

Pedestrians and persons pulling or pushing handcarts must respect traffic lights, road signs, or commands of traffic officers.

Pedestrians and persons pulling or pushing handcarts must comply with the following:

1- It is prohibited to use the road if both sides of the road have sidewalks or pedestrian paths reserved for pedestrian travel.

2- Using the road is permitted if the sidewalk or road shoulder is not available for pedestrian use, and they must walk close to the edge of the road in the direction of traffic.

3- They must cross at marked pedestrian crossings if there are any nearby.

4- At places with designated pedestrian crossings:

- Pedestrians must respect the commands of traffic officers or traffic signals

- At places where there are no traffic lights or traffic control officers, pedestrians must exercise caution before crossing the road.

5- At places where there are no pedestrian crossing markings:

- Before crossing the road, pedestrians must ensure that this action can be done without disrupting vehicle traffic or causing any danger

- When crossing the road, pedestrians are prohibited from crossing at intersections, but must cross perpendicular to the road axis, and are prohibited from lingering or stopping on the road unnecessarily.

6- Children under 6 (six) years of age are prohibited from wandering unattended on the road.

7- Blind persons traveling on or crossing the road must be guided by a person, an animal, or a guiding device.

#### Article 32

Convoys and the use of roads for other purposes authorized by the competent authorities must comply with the following instructions:

a- Convoys:

- Must march along the right side of the road in the direction of traffic, and must leave the left side of the road completely clear

- May march in columns of two, three, or four rows, depending on the width of the road, and must respect traffic regulations

- Must carry a white light in front and a red light at the rear at night or during the day when visibility is insufficient.

b- Use of roads for other necessary purposes may be permitted in accordance with the determination of an inter-ministerial declaration between the Minister of Interior and the Minister of Public Works and Transport, or an inter-ministerial declaration between the Minister of Interior and the Minister of Rural Development.

#### Article 33

Persons riding, driving, or leading animals must comply with the following:

1- Must not cause obstacles or danger to other road users, and must ensure that the number of riders is appropriate to the number of animals so they can control and stop the animals sufficiently and in a timely manner.

2- At night or during the day when visibility is poor, a white light must be used in front and a red light at the rear.

3- When stopping on the road, they must not cause disruption to other vehicles traveling.

4- In built-up areas, persons riding, driving, or leading animals on the road must stop according to the direction of their travel, by complying with the following:

- For two-way roads, stop on the right side in the direction of their travel

- For one-way roads, stop on the right or left side in the direction of travel, except where there is a prohibitive sign.

5- Allowing animals to wander unattended on the road is prohibited.

#### Chapter 6
#### Cases of Traffic Accidents

#### Article 34

All cases of traffic accidents are under the jurisdiction of the land traffic police. In every traffic accident case, the land traffic police must make every effort to:

1- Maintain public order at the accident scene.

2- Take immediate measures to rescue the victims.

3- Maintain security for all parties involved and prevent conflicts arising from the traffic accident.

4- Take professional measures to gather evidence and information to write a traffic accident report.

#### Article 35

In the event of a traffic accident, the parties involved in the accident must comply with the following:

1- Immediately stop their vehicles, without causing or risking any danger to others.

2- Must not alter the accident scene or erase traces or marks that are useful for finding the person responsible for the accident.

3- Immediately notify the local authority or the nearest land traffic police officers.

4- Must not use violence in the event of disputes between the parties involved.

5- Must not leave the accident scene before mutual agreement or without authorization from the land traffic police.

For eyewitnesses, they must immediately notify the local authority or the nearest land traffic police officers.

#### Article 36

If an accident occurs causing only property damage, both parties may settle the matter by mutual agreement without requesting intervention from land traffic police officers.

#### Article 37

In the event of injury or loss of life, any driver or road user involved in the accident, or anyone witnessing this event, must arrange to notify the local authority or land traffic police officers, or immediately send the victims to the nearest hospital from the accident scene.

#### Article 38

For traffic accidents in which one party is a diplomatic agent or mission, the land traffic police must copy the report and make a report on this accident to send to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation to arrange for a representative to cooperate with the land traffic police to inspect and propose appropriate measures.

#### Chapter 7
#### Vehicle Management and Transportation

#### Article 39

National and international driving licenses for drivers of all types of motor vehicles shall be issued by the Ministry of Public Works and Transport, except for driving licenses for drivers of military and all types of police vehicles, especially for national defense purposes such as tanks, armored vehicles, weapon-equipped vehicles, pursuit vehicles, three-wheeled motorcycles, and highway patrol motorcycles which are state property under the management of the Ministry of Interior or the Ministry of National Defense, which shall be issued by the respective ministry.

The procedure for issuing international driving licenses shall be determined by a declaration of the Minister of Public Works and Transport.

The Ministry of Public Works and Transport must provide the statistical list of driving license holders to the Ministry of Interior once a month.

#### Article 40 .. New

National driving licenses for driving motor vehicles in the Kingdom of Cambodia are divided into 5 (five) categories as follows:

**Category A**: Motorcycles with a cylinder capacity exceeding 125 (one hundred and twenty-five) cubic centimeters or electric power exceeding 11 (eleven) kilowatts, motorcycles with trailers, and tricycles.

**Category B**: Passenger vehicles carrying not more than 9 (nine) passengers, including the driver, and goods transport vehicles with a maximum total weight not exceeding 3.5 (three and a half) tons

- Vehicles in this category can tow a trailer with a maximum total weight not exceeding 0.75 (zero point seventy-five) tons.

**Category C**: Goods transport vehicles with a maximum total weight exceeding 3.5 (three and a half) tons and can tow a trailer with a maximum total weight not exceeding 0.75 (zero point seventy-five) tons.

**Category D**: Passenger vehicles:

**D1**: Passenger vehicles carrying from 10 (ten) to 20 (twenty) passengers, including the driver, and can tow a trailer with a maximum total weight not exceeding 0.75 (zero point seventy-five) tons.

**D2**: Passenger vehicles carrying more than 20 (twenty) passengers, including the driver, and can tow a trailer with a maximum total weight not exceeding 0.75 (zero point seventy-five) tons.

**Category E**: Vehicles of category B, C, and D towing a trailer with a maximum total weight exceeding 0.75 (zero point seventy-five) tons, which are divided into three subcategories: Category E(B), Category E(C), and Category E(D).

#### Article 41 .. New

Various conditions related to driving licenses shall be determined as follows:

1- A driver has the right to hold only one driving license. The forms, procedures, and rights of issuing driving licenses and demerit point licenses shall be determined by a declaration of the Minister of Public Works and Transport.

2- Disabled persons have the right to drive special vehicles for the disabled, and the conditions for issuing driving licenses for the disabled shall be determined by an inter-ministerial declaration of the Minister of Public Works and Transport and relevant ministries.

3- The age of drivers shall be determined according to the category of driving license as follows:

- At least 18 (eighteen) years of age for Category A and B

- At least 22 (twenty-two) years of age for Category C and D1

- At least 24 (twenty-four) years of age for Category D2 and E.

4- A holder of a Category B driving license can drive agricultural vehicles or machinery vehicles with a maximum speed not exceeding 40 (forty) kilometers per hour, but cannot drive other categories of vehicles.

5- For drivers of machinery vehicles with a maximum speed exceeding 40 (forty) kilometers per hour, they must hold a Category C driving license.

6- To be eligible to take the exam for a Category C driving license, one must first hold a Category B or D1 driving license.

7- Holders of Category C or D2 driving licenses can drive Category B vehicles.

8- A holder of a Category C driving license can drive a Category D1 vehicle if they have passed the Category C driving license exam after holding a Category D1 driving license. However, a holder of a Category D2 driving license can drive Category B, C, or D1 vehicles.

9- To be eligible to take the exam for a Category D1 driving license, one must first hold a Category B driving license.

10- To be eligible to take the exam for a Category D2 driving license, one must first hold a Category B, C, or D1 driving license.

11- To be eligible to take the exam for a Category E driving license, one must first hold a Category B, C, or D2 driving license.

12- A holder of a Category E driving license has the right to drive a trailer-towing vehicle with a maximum total weight exceeding 0.75 (zero point seventy-five) tons in accordance with their driving license category, such as E(B), E(C), or E(D).

#### Article 42

Holders of all driving license categories have the right to drive motor vehicles up to the age of 65 (sixty-five) years. From the age of 65 (sixty-five) onwards, driving license holders may be permitted to drive, but they must undergo a medical examination and renew their driving license, which will be valid for 2 (two) years at a time.

The validity of driving license categories A, B, D, and E is as follows:

1- Category A and B driving licenses are valid for a period of 10 (ten) years.

2- Category C, D, and E driving licenses are valid for a period of 5 (five) years.

3- Driving license holders must apply to renew their driving license before expiration, accompanied by a medical certificate certifying they are fit to drive. Failure to renew the driving license will be subject to a fine in accordance with the provisions of this law.

The medical examination must have a certificate certifying fitness to drive from a doctor at a hospital or health center designated by the Ministry of Health.

#### Article 43

Every driving license must have a demerit point system with a total of 12 (twelve) points, and this score shall be reduced when the driver commits traffic offenses as follows:

a- Deduction of 1 (one) point for:

- Not wearing a seat belt or safety helmet

- Using high-beam headlights when encountering vehicles coming from the opposite direction

- Driving over the center longitudinal dividing line of the road

- Driving at night without sufficient lights corresponding to the type of vehicle

- Carrying passengers exceeding the limit.

b- Deduction of 2 (two) demerit points for:

- Driving over the speed limit by 20 (twenty) to 29 (twenty-nine) kilometers per hour

- Speeding up when being overtaken

- Making incorrect left or right turns

- Making a U-turn at prohibited places

- Violating conditions of the driving license

- Turning left, right, or stopping without giving signals

- Overtaking or passing incorrectly

- Driving without maintaining a safe distance, causing an accident

- Negligent driving causing disturbance to other road users

- Failing to signal properly when starting to move

- Operating a vehicle with non-compliant technical specifications

- Loading cargo incorrectly

- Parking on pedestrian crossings

- Stopping or parking on road markings on the roadway

- Allowing a child under 10 (ten) years of age to sit in the front seat of a car

- Failing to place a child under 5 (five) years of age in a child safety seat or child car bed

- Evading traffic police officers performing inspections.

c- Deduction of 3 (three) demerit points for:

- Stopping or parking at dangerous locations

- Overtaking dangerously

- Overtaking each other in prohibited areas

- Changing direction without signaling

- Crossing a solid dividing line in the middle of the road

- Driving over the speed limit by 30 (thirty) to 39 (thirty-nine) kilometers per hour

- Driving with an alcohol concentration from 0.25 (zero point twenty-five) to 0.39 (zero point thirty-nine) milligrams per liter of air or from

0.50 (zero point fifty) to 0.79 (zero point seventy-nine) grams per liter of blood.

- Racing on the road without permission

- Failure to stop or failure to give right of way at crossings

- Disobeying red traffic lights

- Disobeying stop signs or priority signs

- Disobeying traffic control signals of traffic police.

D- Deduct 4 (four) points:

- Driving over the speed limit from 40 (forty) to 49 (forty-nine) kilometers per hour

- Driving without respecting the right of way at intersections

- Driving side-by-side

- Causing traffic congestion

- Failure to give right of way to vehicles that have the right of way

- Cargo falling from the vehicle while driving

- Driving against the traffic direction

- Reckless driving: chasing, cutting off, or crowding.

E- Deduct 6 (six) points:

- Driving over the speed limit by 50 (fifty) kilometers per hour or more

- Driving with an alcohol concentration of 0.40 (zero point forty) milligrams per liter of breath or 0.80 (zero point eighty) grams per liter of blood or more, or under the influence of drugs

- Fleeing the scene of a traffic accident

- Using fake license plates and vehicle registration certificates

- Driving in violation of traffic laws and causing unintentional injury to others, resulting in the victim's inability to work for a duration equal to or exceeding 8 (eight) days from the date of the incident, or death

- Repeated offense of using an expired driving license or a license not corresponding to the vehicle type

- Refusing to undergo alcohol or drug testing

- Carrying passengers on top of cargo.

In case of committing a single offense, the points deducted shall not exceed 8 (eight) points.

#### Article 44.- Disciplinary Actions Related to Point System

- In the event that all points are depleted, the driving license shall be invalidated. If the driver wishes to obtain a new driving license, they must re-learn and re-take the driving license exam after a minimum period of 6 (six) months from the date the driving license was invalidated, except in cases where the driver unintentionally causes injury to others resulting in the victim's inability to work for a duration equal to or exceeding 8 (eight) days from the date of the incident, or death, the driver may re-learn and re-take the driving license exam after a minimum period of 5 (five) years from the date the driving license was invalidated.

- In the event that some points have been deducted, but within 3 (three) years following the last point deduction, the driver does not commit any further offense to deduct points, the driver shall automatically receive a new points card with 12 (twelve) points.

- In the event that some points have been deducted, but the driver wishes to restore points, they must undergo a 2 (two) day refresher course organized by the Ministry of Public Works and Transport. During this course, points may be increased by 4 (four) points, but the total points shall not exceed 11 (eleven) points. The driver may attend the course multiple times to increase points within a maximum period of 2 (two) years.

The procedures for deducting and restoring driving license points shall be determined by a joint declaration (Prokas) of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport and the Ministry of Interior.

#### Article 45.-

Except where there is a bilateral or multilateral agreement or international convention recognizing driving licenses mutually with the Kingdom of Cambodia, foreigners who enter and reside legally and wish to drive on the roads in the Kingdom of Cambodia must possess a Cambodian driving license through one of two methods:

1- Must take the driving license exam similar to Cambodian citizens.

2- Must swap their country's driving license for a Cambodian driving license by submitting an application to the Ministry of Public Works and Transport and fulfilling the following conditions:

A- Must have a valid passport and visa.

B- Must have a valid driving license.

C- Have a clear residential address certified by the commune chief or quarter chief (Sangkat).

D- Have a physical fitness certificate.

In the event that the driving license is not written in English or French, it must be translated into Khmer, English, or French, with certification from the embassy, consulate, or mission office of their country.

The validity of a driving license of a foreigner which has been swapped for a Cambodian driving license is 1 (one) year. The validity of a driving license of a foreigner who has passed the exam is the same as that of Cambodian citizens.

Foreigners holding driving licenses of categories D and E cannot swap them for Cambodian driving licenses of categories D and E, but can swap them for driving licenses of categories C and B.

#### Article 46.-

Every driving school must have a business license for driving school operations, must have a curriculum, and must be under the management and inspection of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

Every instructor at a driving school must have a driving instructor certificate issued by the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

The conditions and procedures for issuing the business license for driving school operations and the examination for the driving instructor certificate, as well as the curriculum of driving schools, shall be determined by a declaration (Prokas) of the Minister of Public Works and Transport.

In case of necessity, the Ministry of Interior has the right to inspect all driving schools.

#### Article 47.- New

Every vehicle, when put into operation on the road, must have a vehicle registration certificate and a license plate.

Every vehicle must be registered, issued a vehicle registration certificate, and equipped with a license plate. For trailers or semi-trailers with a total weight exceeding 550 (five hundred fifty) kilograms, they must also be registered, issued a registration certificate, and equipped with a license plate similar to vehicles.

The Ministry of Public Works and Transport must provide the statistics of registered vehicles of all types to the Ministry of Interior once a month.

The Ministry of Interior and the Ministry of National Defense must provide information on the number of vehicles, motor tractors, machinery vehicles, or machinery which are state property under their management to the Ministry of Public Works and Transport once every 3 (three) months, except for confidential information related to national security.

The registration, issuance of vehicle registration certificates, and installation of license plates shall be determined by a sub-decree (Anukret).

#### Article 48.- New

Every owner of a shop selling all types of vehicles has the obligation to complete registration procedures for vehicles

vehicles to apply for vehicle registration certificates and license plates completely before delivering them to buyers.

Buying and selling, gifting, and all other forms of transaction that lead to a transfer of ownership of a vehicle, must complete procedures to transfer vehicle ownership. The forms and procedures for vehicle ownership transfer shall be determined by a joint declaration (Prokas) of the Ministry of Public Works and Transport and the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

Owners of all types of vehicles have the obligation to complete procedures to remove from the vehicle registry any vehicles that are no longer in use.

The forms and procedures for vehicle registration, issuance of vehicle license plates, and removal from the vehicle registry shall be determined by a declaration (Prokas) of the Minister of Public Works and Transport.

#### Article 49.-

Every vehicle, trailer, and semi-trailer operating on the roads must have a vehicle technical inspection certificate issued by the Ministry of Public Works and Transport or by a company authorized by the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

The validity of the vehicle technical inspection certificate shall be determined as follows:

1- New family-type cars that have not been used must apply for the first vehicle technical inspection certificate, which is valid for 4 (four) years. Thereafter, they must undergo technical inspections every 2 (two) years.

2- Used family-type cars that have already been used must undergo technical inspections every 02 (two) years.

3- New commercial passenger-carrying vehicles, cargo-carrying vehicles, and new special equipment vehicles that have not been used must apply for the first vehicle technical inspection certificate, which is valid for 2 (two) years. Thereafter, they must undergo technical inspections every 1 (one) year.

4- Used commercial passenger-carrying vehicles, cargo-carrying vehicles, and used special equipment vehicles that have already been used must undergo technical inspections every 1 (one) year.

5- New trailers and new semi-trailers that have not been used must apply for a vehicle technical inspection certificate valid for 2 (two) years. Thereafter, they must undergo technical inspections every 1 (one) year.

6- Used trailers and used semi-trailers that have already been used must undergo technical inspections every 1 (one) year.

7- Motorized tricycles and motorcycles towing trailers must undergo technical inspections every 1 (one) year.

8- Every vehicle, trailer, and semi-trailer that has newly been modified locally must undergo a technical inspection before applying for registration, a registration certificate, and a license plate. Thereafter, they must undergo technical inspections every 1 (one) year.

The technical specifications of vehicles and the technical inspection of all types of vehicles shall be determined by a declaration (Prokas) of the Minister of Public Works and Transport.

#### Article 50.-

Every vehicle technical inspection center must have a business license for vehicle technical inspection operations issued by the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

The conditions and procedures for issuing the business license for vehicle technical inspection operations shall be determined by a declaration (Prokas) of the Minister of Public Works and Transport.

#### Article 51.-

Vehicle owners who are required to have compulsory insurance must fulfill their insurance obligations in accordance with the Law on Insurance and regulations related to insurance when the vehicles are operated on the road.

#### Article 52.-

Every vehicle repair garage and vehicle modification/assembly garage must have a business license issued by the Ministry of Public Works and Transport, the Ministry of Industry and Handicrafts, and in case of necessity, must be subject to inspection by the Ministry of Interior.

The management of repair garages and vehicle modification/assembly garages shall be determined by a sub-decree (Anukret).

#### Article 53.-

Legal entities operating cargo and passenger transport services domestically or across borders must have a business license issued by the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

The conditions and procedures for issuing business licenses to legal entities operating road transport services domestically or across borders shall be determined by a declaration (Prokas) of the Minister of Public Works and Transport.

#### Article 54.-

Every vehicle or trailer used for commercial cargo or passenger transport services must have a transport business license issued by the Ministry of Public Works and Transport.

The conditions and procedures for issuing transport business licenses for vehicles operating road transport services domestically or across borders shall be determined by a declaration (Prokas) of the Minister of Public Works and Transport.

#### Article 55.-

Every vehicle or trailer operating in commercial transport services must have:

A- A vehicle registration certificate or trailer registration certificate and license plate

B- A driving license corresponding to the vehicle type

C- A vehicle technical inspection certificate

D- A transport business license for the vehicle or trailer

E- An insurance certificate issued by an insurance company

F- Other permits issued by relevant competent authorities.

#### Article 56.-

The loading of cargo and passengers must be carried out as follows:

1- Cargo loading on a vehicle must be properly arranged to prevent:

- Causing danger to persons or damage to public or private property by dragging or falling on the road

- Obstructing the driver's vision or reducing the stability of the vehicle and its operation

- Causing noise, generating dust, or causing other avoidable disturbances

- Obstructing the headlights, turn signals, rear-view mirrors, and vehicle license plates.

2- All additional protective equipment such as nylon ropes, binding cables, and canvas tarpaulins used for safety or cargo protection must be properly covered and securely tied.

3- In all cases of loading long objects, the load must not extend beyond the front edge of the vehicle, and must not extend more than 3 (three) meters beyond the rear edge of the vehicle or drag on the ground.

4- If the load extends more than 1 (one) meter beyond the rear edge of the vehicle, during daytime it must be tied with a red cloth, and during nighttime it must be equipped with a red light reflector at the end of the load. The width of the load must not exceed the width of the vehicle, and the height must not exceed the vehicle roof, except for buses carrying a maximum of 20 (twenty) passengers, which may load cargo to a maximum height of 0.50 (zero point fifty) meters above the roof and must have a firmly fixed roof rack or barrier.

5- In case of carrying dangerous goods, they must be safely arranged or packaged, and the name of the type and dangerous goods labels must be written and affixed.

6- Passenger transport must have seats or have sturdy handrails or grips for standing, and must ensure good safety.

Passenger transport is prohibited as follows:

- Prohibited from carrying passengers exceeding the number of seats determined by the Ministry of Public Works and Transport

- Prohibited from carrying passengers on the cabin roof or vehicle roof

- Prohibited from clinging to the front, rear, or sides of the vehicle

- Prohibited from carrying passengers on trailers or semi-trailers

- Prohibited from carrying foul-smelling or toxic objects in passenger-carrying vehicles.

7- Loading techniques shall be determined by a declaration (Prokas) of the Minister of Public Works and Transport.

#### Article 57.-

The loading of cargo on a vehicle, trailer, or semi-trailer must not exceed the maximum total weight of the vehicle determined by the vehicle manufacturer and must not exceed the maximum axle load and maximum total weight of the vehicle permitted as stipulated in the Law on Roads.

When crossing bridges, all drivers of vehicles, vehicles towing trailers or semi-trailers must respect the maximum total weight limit specified on the sign placed in front of the bridge.

The dimensions of vehicles and vehicles towing trailers or semi-trailers shall be determined as follows:

- The maximum width must not exceed 2.50 (two point fifty) meters, except for machinery vehicles which must not exceed 3 (three) meters

- The maximum height must not exceed 4.20 (four point twenty) meters

- The maximum length of each vehicle must not exceed 12.20 (twelve point twenty) meters

- The maximum length of each vehicle with a semi-trailer must not exceed 16 (sixteen) meters

- The maximum length of each vehicle with a trailer must not exceed 18 (eighteen) meters

The maximum dimensions of vehicles and vehicles towing trailers or semi-trailers which are not specified in the above points must request a special permit from the Minister of Public Works and Transport.

Detailed technical standards regarding the dimensions and weights of vehicles shall be determined by a declaration (Prokas) of the Minister of Public Works and Transport.

#### Article 58.-

Payments for service fees for issuing driving licenses, registration certificates, license plates, technical inspection certificates, vehicle transport business licenses, fines from law enforcement, and other services shall be determined by a joint declaration (Prokas) of the Minister of Economy and Finance and the Minister of Public Works and Transport.

### Chapter 8

### Mechanisms for Implementing the Law on Road Traffic

#### Article 59.-

The National Road Safety Committee has the role to formulate policies on road traffic safety and lead the inspection of the implementation of the law and other legal instruments related to road traffic safety.

The National Road Safety Committee is led by the Minister of Interior as Chairman, the Minister of Public Works and Transport as Permanent Vice-Chairman, and members from relevant ministries and institutions.

The National Road Safety Committee has a General Secretariat as its chief of staff led by the Ministry of Public Works and Transport, and a number of sub-committees as assistants.

The establishment, organization, and functioning of the National Road Safety Committee shall be determined by a sub-decree (Anukret).

### Chapter 9

### Competence and Power of Officers Implementing the Law on Road Traffic

#### Article 60.-

Road traffic law enforcement officers include:

1- Officers from the Ministry of Public Works and Transport

2- Police or Gendarmerie units responsible for managing road traffic order and safety, who have the duty to:

- Direct traffic to ensure good order

- Monitor, patrol, and inspect violations of traffic regulations directly or through technical equipment

- Make reports on the traffic order situation, traffic offenses, and traffic accidents that occur.

In addition, the police units responsible for managing traffic order and safety have the additional duties to:

- Impose fines for offenses subject to transitional fines

- Mediate in case of traffic accidents as stipulated in Article 65 of this Law

- Inspect and record the course of events when a traffic accident occurs, in case

necessary, temporarily confiscate driving licenses and detain vehicles in accordance with Article 62, Article 66, and Article 67 of this Law.

- Prepare and send case files and evidence to the prosecution office attached to the court of first instance in cases determined by this Law.

#### Article 61.-

In detaining a vehicle for inspection, road traffic police must perform it while avoiding causing traffic congestion or traffic accidents.

#### Article 62.-

Road traffic police officers have the right to confiscate and temporarily keep a driving license for a period not exceeding 7 (seven) days and deduct points as stipulated in Article 43 of this Law for any of the following offenses:

1- Driving against the traffic direction, or driving in a traffic direction with a prohibitory sign.

2- Driving at a speed exceeding the speed limit by 40 (forty) kilometers per hour or more as determined in this Law.

3- Failing to respect the right of way.

4- Disobeying stop signs or red traffic lights or flashing red lights.

5- Driving a vehicle with steel chain tracks directly on asphalt roads.

Traffic police officers must not confiscate driving licenses and other documents of the vehicle in cases not stipulated in this Law.

#### Article 63.-

In the event of a traffic accident, depending on each case, detaining vehicles, arresting drivers for questioning, and making reports are within the competence of road traffic police officers at the scene. In cases where the driver is suspected of being under the influence of alcohol or drugs, judicial police officers in traffic or medical staff must inspect and issue a certificate confirming the alcohol or drug level to be attached to the traffic accident report for the prosecution office attached to the court of first instance.

#### Article 64.-

Road traffic offenses subject to imprisonment and criminal fines are within the jurisdiction of the court. In this case, competent road traffic police officers inspecting road traffic offenses must send the report on road traffic offenses to the prosecution office attached to the court of first instance.

#### Article 65.-

Traffic offenses subject to transitional fines are within the competence of road traffic police officers. Road traffic police officers may mediate in case of accidents causing only property damage. If mediation is unsuccessful, road traffic police officers must make a report recording fingerprints or signatures and draw a temporary sketch, and in case of necessity to ensure the protection of the parties' legal interests, road traffic police officers may temporarily keep the vehicles for no more than one week.

#### Article 66.-

Road traffic police officers must detain a vehicle from operating or impound the vehicle for a period not exceeding 3 (three) days in order to impose fines and correct technical specifications, depending on the case where the driver has committed a traffic offense as follows:

1- Vehicles where the driver is under the influence of alcohol with an alcohol concentration from 0.25 (zero point twenty-five) to 0.39 (zero point thirty-nine) milligrams per liter of breath or from 0.50 (zero point fifty) to 0.79 (zero point seventy-nine) grams per liter of blood, or under the influence of drugs.

2- Vehicles where the driver refuses to undergo alcohol or drug testing.

3- Vehicles with insufficient technical specifications that may cause traffic accidents.

4- Vehicles emitting excessive smoke or loud engine noise exceeding technical standards, or equipped with lighting systems violating standards that disturb other road users or residents living along the road.

5- Vehicles carrying cargo or passengers that obstruct the driver's vision or exceed the limits.

6- Vehicles parked on the roadway in urban areas for more than 24 hours.

7- Vehicles operated past the technical inspection deadline.

8- Vehicles driven over the speed limit by 40 (forty) kilometers per hour or more.

#### Article 67.-

Road traffic police officers have the right to tow vehicles that are parked in a way that obstructs or may cause danger to other vehicles, to be kept at the road traffic police unit, in cases where the vehicle owner refuses to accept the fine or is absent.

All towing of vehicles to be kept at the road traffic police unit must also be applied to vehicles involved in traffic accidents that cannot be resolved.

The vehicle owner must be responsible for paying towing and storage fees in addition to the fines.

Towing and storage fees shall be determined by a joint declaration (Prokas) of the Minister of Interior and the Minister of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 68.-

Competent road traffic police officers inspecting traffic offenses must inspect, investigate, and record offenses in accordance with the procedures of inspection, investigation, recording of offenses, and making traffic accident reports determined by a joint declaration (Prokas) of the Minister of Interior, the Minister of Justice, and the Minister of Public Works and Transport.

#### Article 69.-

Competent road traffic police officers imposing fines for traffic offenses must issue a fine receipt with a proper stub. The form of the fine receipt and the management of revenue received from fines shall be determined by a joint declaration (Prokas) of the Minister of Interior, the Minister of Economy and Finance, and the Minister of Public Works and Transport.

#### Article 70.-

Any person who is dissatisfied with the decision of a law enforcement officer as stipulated in Article 60 of this Law has the right to file a complaint to the head of the unit managing that officer. The head of the unit must make a decision on the complaint within a maximum of 15 (fifteen) days from the date of receiving the complaint. Any person who is dissatisfied with the decision of the head of the unit above has the right to file a lawsuit in court according to procedures.

### Chapter 10

### Penalty Provisions

#### Article 71.-

Offenses subject to transitional fines shall be determined by a sub-decree (Anukret).

#### Article 72.-

The driver must be criminally responsible for offenses they have committed while driving the vehicle.

The manager or owner of the vehicle must be civilly responsible in case their vehicle causes harm to others.

#### Article 73.-

Assault on the body, honor, or dignity of road traffic law enforcement officers

who is performing their duties, shall be punished according to the Criminal Code in force.

#### Article 74.-

Any road traffic police officer who, within the scope of their functions or on the occasion of performing their duties, confiscates driving licenses, license plates, vehicle registration certificates, or detains vehicles in violation of the law, shall be punished with imprisonment from 6 (six) days to 1 (one) month and a fine from 100,000 (one hundred thousand) Riels to 800,000 (eight hundred thousand) Riels.

In the event that a road traffic police officer causes damage or loss to any part of the detained vehicle, the unit to which the officer belongs shall be responsible for compensating for such damage or loss.

#### Article 75.- New

Any person who drives a vehicle without a driving license, or during a period when the driving license is confiscated, suspended, or invalidated, shall be subject to a transitional fine determined by a sub-decree (Anukret).

The above offense shall be punished with imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 6 (six) months and a fine from 800,000 (eight hundred thousand) Riels to 4,000,000 (four million) Riels, in the event that the offender has already been subject to a transitional fine once but continues to commit the offense again within 6 (six) months from the date of the previous transitional fine.

Both provisions of the second paragraph above shall not apply to drivers of motorcycles, motorcycles towing trailers, or motorized tricycles.

#### Article 76.-

Any person who drives a vehicle and refuses to stop the vehicle upon the order of a road traffic police officer for inspection of alcohol or drugs, shall be punished with imprisonment from 6 (six) days to 1 (one) month and a fine from 100,000 (one hundred thousand) Riels to 800,000 (eight hundred thousand) Riels.

The same penalty as in the first paragraph above shall be imposed on any driver who refuses to undergo vehicle weight inspection or testing for alcohol or drugs.

#### Article 77.- New

Any person who drives a vehicle while under the influence of alcohol with an alcohol concentration of 0.40 (zero point forty) milligrams per liter of breath or 0.80 (zero point eighty) grams per liter of blood or more, shall be subject to a transitional fine determined by a sub-decree (Anukret).

The above offense shall be punished with imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 6 (six) months and a fine from 800,000 (eight hundred thousand) Riels to 4,000,000 (four million) Riels, in the event that the offender has already been subject to a transitional fine once but continues to commit the offense again within 6 (six) months from the date of the previous transitional fine.

#### Article 78.-

Any person who operates a vehicle by using a driving license, vehicle registration certificate, vehicle license plate, or other permits knowing that they are forged documents, shall be punished in accordance with Article 630 (Use of forged public documents) of the Criminal Code.

#### Article 79.-

Any person who forges a driving license, vehicle registration certificate, vehicle license plate, or other permits issued by a public officer, shall be punished in accordance with Article 629 (Forgery of public documents) of the Criminal Code.

#### Article 80.-

Any person who blocks or uses any means to obstruct public traffic, shall be punished with imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 1 (one) year and a fine from 800,000 (eight hundred thousand) Riels to 8,000,000 (eight million) Riels.

#### Article 81.-

Any person who drives a vehicle and fails to obey the control signals of competent officers protecting convoys of delegations or high-ranking leaders, causing damage to convoy vehicles, shall be punished with imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 6 (six) months and a fine from 800,000 (eight hundred thousand) Riels to 4,000,000 (four million) Riels.

#### Article 82.- New

Any person who operates a vehicle without a vehicle registration certificate or without a vehicle license plate, shall be subject to a transitional fine determined by a sub-decree (Anukret).

The above offense shall be punished with imprisonment from 6 (six) days to 1 (one) month and a fine from 400,000 (four hundred thousand) Riels to 1,000,000 (one million) Riels, in the event that the offender has already been subject to a transitional fine once but continues to commit the offense again within 6 (six) months from the date of the previous transitional fine.

Both provisions of the second paragraph above shall not apply to drivers of motorcycles, motorcycles towing trailers, or motorized tricycles.

#### Article 83.-

Any person who drives with negligence, carelessness, lack of caution, or fails to fulfill obligations required by road traffic regulations, causing injury to another person, and resulting in the victim's inability to work for a duration equal to or exceeding 8 (eight) days from the date of the incident, shall be punished with imprisonment from 6 (six) months to 2 (two) years and a fine from 2,000,000 (two million) Riels to 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels, and may be subject to one or more additional penalties as stipulated in Article 53 (Types of additional penalties) of the Criminal Code.

The above offense shall be punished with imprisonment from 1 (one) year to 3 (three) years and a fine from 4,000,000 (four million) Riels to 15,000,000 (fifteen million) Riels, if the offense is committed in any of the following cases:

A- Driving without a driving license or with a driving license that does not correspond to the vehicle type.

B- Under the influence of alcohol with an alcohol concentration of 0.40 (zero point forty) milligrams per liter of breath or 0.80 (zero point eighty) grams per liter of blood or more.

C- Causing injury to multiple people.

D- Fleeing the scene of the incident in order to evade responsibility.

For offenses stipulated in this article, the following additional penalties may be declared:

1- Prohibition from practicing a profession, if the offense was committed in the practice of a profession or on the occasion of practicing this profession, permanently or for a maximum period of 5 (five) years.

2- Prohibition from driving a vehicle of any type for a maximum period of 5 (five) years.

3- Suspension of driving license for a maximum period of 5 (five) years.

4- Posting of the sentencing decision for a maximum period of 2 (two) months.

5- Publication of the sentencing decision in the press.

6- Broadcasting of the sentencing decision by all audiovisual telecommunication means for a maximum period of 8 (eight) days.

#### Article 84.-

Legal entities may be declared criminally responsible under the conditions stipulated in Article 42 (Criminal responsibility of legal entities) of the Criminal Code for offenses stipulated in Article 83 of this Law.

Legal entities shall be punished with a fine from 5,000,000 (five million) Riels to 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels, as well as one or more of the following additional penalties:

1- Dissolution according to the procedures determined by Article 170 (Dissolution and liquidation of legal entities) of the Criminal Code.

2- Placement under judicial supervision according to the procedures determined by Article 171 (Placement under judicial supervision) of the Criminal Code.

3- Prohibition from performing one or more activities according to the procedures determined by Article 172 (Prohibition from performing activities) of the Criminal Code.

4- Closure of the establishment used for committing the offense according to the procedures determined by Article 176 (Closure of establishment) of the Criminal Code.

5- Prohibition from conducting business in an establishment open to the public or used by the public according to the procedures determined by Article 177 (Prohibition from conducting business in an establishment) of the Criminal Code.

6- Posting of the sentencing decision according to the procedures determined by Article 180 (Posting of decision) of the Criminal Code.

7- Publication of the sentencing decision in the print media or broadcasting by all audiovisual telecommunication means according to the procedures determined by Article 181 (Publication of decision by telecommunication/audiovisual means) of the Criminal Code.

#### Article 85.-

Any person who drives with negligence, carelessness, lack of caution, or fails to fulfill obligations required by road traffic regulations, causing the death of another person, shall be punished with imprisonment from 1 (one) year to 3 (three) years and a fine from 4,000,000 (four million) Riels to 15,000,000 (fifteen million) Riels, and may be subject to one or more additional penalties as stipulated in Article 53 (Types of additional penalties) of the Criminal Code.

The above offense shall be punished with imprisonment from 2 (two) years to 5 (five) years and a fine from 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels to 25,000,000 (twenty-five million) Riels, if the offense is committed in any of the following cases:

A- Driving without a driving license or with a driving license that does not correspond to the vehicle type.

B- Under the influence of alcohol with an alcohol concentration of 0.40 (zero point forty) milligrams per liter of breath or 0.80 (zero point eighty) grams per liter of blood or more.

C- Causing the death of multiple people.

D- Fleeing the scene of the incident in order to evade responsibility.

For offenses stipulated in this article, the following additional penalties may be declared:

1- Prohibition from practicing a profession, if the offense was committed in the practice of a profession or on the occasion of practicing this profession, permanently or for a maximum period of 5 (five) years.

2- Prohibition from driving a vehicle of any type for a maximum period of 5 (five) years.

3- Suspension of driving license for a maximum period of 5 (five) years.

4- Posting of the sentencing decision for a maximum period of 2 (two) months.

5- Publication of the sentencing decision in the press.

6- Broadcasting of the sentencing decision by all audiovisual telecommunication means for a maximum period of 8 (eight) days.

#### Article 86.-

Legal entities may be declared criminally responsible under the conditions stipulated in Article 42 (Criminal responsibility of legal entities) of the Criminal Code for offenses stipulated in Article 85 of this Law.

Legal entities shall be punished with a fine from 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels to 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels, as well as one or more of the following additional penalties:

1- Dissolution according to the procedures determined by Article 170 (Dissolution and liquidation of legal entities) of the Criminal Code.

2- Placement under judicial supervision according to the procedures determined by Article 171 (Placement under judicial supervision) of the Criminal Code.

3- Prohibition from performing one or more activities according to the procedures determined by Article 172 (Prohibition from performing activities) of the Criminal Code.

4- Closure of the establishment used for committing the offense according to the procedures determined by Article 176 (Closure of establishment) of the Criminal Code.

5- Prohibition from conducting business in an establishment open to the public or used by the public according to the procedures determined by Article 177 (Prohibition from conducting business in an establishment) of the Criminal Code.

6- Posting of the sentencing decision according to the procedures determined by Article 180 (Posting of decision) of the Criminal Code.

7- Publication of the sentencing decision in the print media or broadcasting by all audiovisual telecommunication means according to the procedures determined by Article 181 (Publication of decision by telecommunication/audiovisual means) of the Criminal Code.

#### Article 87.-

Payment of the transitional fine shall extinguish the criminal action.

For offenses subject to transitional fines, and where the involved person refuses to pay the transitional fine within the determined period, the road traffic police officer must send the report and evidence to the prosecution office attached to the court of first instance.

### Chapter 11

### Transitional Provisions

#### Article 88.-

All legal instruments created to implement the Law on Road Traffic promulgated by Royal Decree No. NS/RKM/0207/007 dated February 08, 2007, shall remain in force until there are new provisions to amend or abrogate them, except for provisions that are contrary to the spirit of this Law.

#### Article 89.-

Holders of driving licenses that are compliant with the provisions of the Law on Road Traffic promulgated by Royal Decree No. NS/RKM/0207/007 dated February 08, 2007, may continue to use them until their expiration.

Holders of driving licenses that are not compliant with the provisions of the Law on Road Traffic promulgated by Royal Decree No. NS/RKM/0207/007 dated February 08, 2007, must complete the application procedures to exchange their driving licenses within 1 (one) year after this Law enters into force nationwide.

#### Article 90.- New

The obligation to wear seat belts for all passengers sitting in the rear seats of vehicles outside urban areas, and the provisions stipulated in the second paragraph of Article 7 as well as points 11 and 12 of the new Article 8 of this Law, shall be implemented within 5 (five) years after this Law enters into force nationwide.

#### Article 91.-

The provisions stipulated in Article 43 and Article 44, Chapter 7 of this Law, shall be implemented within 5 (five) years after this Law enters into force nationwide.

### Chapter 12

### Final Provisions

#### Article 92.-

The Law on Road Traffic promulgated by Royal Decree No. NS/RKM/0207/007 dated February 08, 2007, shall be abrogated and replaced by this Law.
