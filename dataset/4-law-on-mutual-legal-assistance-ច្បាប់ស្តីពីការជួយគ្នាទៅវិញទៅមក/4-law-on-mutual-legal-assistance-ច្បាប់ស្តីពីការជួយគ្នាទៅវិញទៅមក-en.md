### PROMULGATE

The Law on Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters, which was adopted by the National Assembly on June 05, 2020 at its 4th session of the 6th legislature, and which was reviewed in its entirety by the Senate on June 16, 2020 at its 4th plenary session of the 4th legislature, and its entire content is as follows:

# Law on Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters

### Chapter 1 General Provisions

#### Article 1 - Purpose

This Law determines mutual legal assistance in criminal matters between the Kingdom of Cambodia and foreign states regarding various procedures related to criminal offenses, as well as freezing, seizures, and confiscation of property, in order to strengthen and enhance international cooperation.

#### Article 2 - Scope

This Law shall apply to:

- Mutual legal assistance in criminal matters to be provided to all requesting states, even if such requesting state does not have an agreement with the Kingdom of Cambodia;

- Seeking mutual legal assistance in criminal matters from all requested states, even if such requested state does not have an agreement with the Kingdom of Cambodia.

This Law shall not apply to mutual legal assistance in criminal matters carried out in accordance with bilateral or multilateral treaties or conventions ratified by the Kingdom of Cambodia.

Procedures provided for in this Law shall apply to all cases of mutual legal assistance in criminal matters, including mutual legal assistance in criminal matters conducted in accordance with bilateral or multilateral treaties or conventions ratified by the Kingdom of Cambodia, except where there are contrary provisions specifically stated in such treaties or conventions or other laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

This Law also does not preclude making or receiving requests related to mutual legal assistance in criminal matters or cooperation between the Kingdom of Cambodia and any foreign state through any other lawful means.

#### Article 3 - Definitions

The technical terms used in this Law shall be defined as follows:

1- Legal assistance refers to mutual assistance in criminal matters, including:

- Collection of evidence or taking testimony of witnesses

- Search and seizure

- Provision of evidence before a court

- Provision of information controlled by the Kingdom of Cambodia

- Handling of service of court documents

- Transfer of detained persons for the purpose of giving testimony

- Identifying the location of any person

- Identifying the location of property

- Taking measures on requests related to freezing, seizure, or confiscation of property, and

- Seizure of evidentiary materials from financial institutions.

2- Investigation refers to research, investigation, and inquiry in criminal matters.

3- Proceeds of crime refers to any property derived or obtained directly or indirectly through the commission of a criminal offense.

4- Property refers to:

- All types of property, including movable or immovable property, tangible or intangible property, and legal documents or instruments evidencing title to or interest in such property

- Property including instruments and proceeds derived from money laundering, terrorist financing, and other serious offenses; and

- Property including substitute assets of a natural or legal person with a value equal to the property that is the subject of freezing, detention, and confiscation.

5- Internal procedures refers to procedures for implementation in the Kingdom of Cambodia regarding requests for mutual legal assistance in criminal matters.

6- Requested state refers to a foreign state that receives a request for legal assistance in criminal matters from the Kingdom of Cambodia.

7- Requesting state refers to a foreign state that seeks legal assistance in criminal matters from the Kingdom of Cambodia.

8- Criminal matters refer to:

- A criminal offense; or

- Determination of the proceeds of crime or instruments of crime, or terrorist property; or

- An enforceable confiscation order; or

- Freezing or seizure of proceeds of crime or instruments of crime, or terrorist property.

9- Instruments of crime refer to property used in the commission of a crime or intended to be used in the commission of a crime.

### Chapter 2 Central Authority and Implementing Authority

#### Article 4 - Institution in Charge as Central Authority

The Ministry of Justice of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be the Central Authority. The Minister of Justice may appoint officials as representatives of the Central Authority for communication with foreign states, implementing authorities, and relevant competent authorities.

#### Article 5 - Competence of Central Authority

The Central Authority shall have the following competences, among others:

1- Receive requests from requesting states and transmit such requests to the relevant competent implementing authority of the Kingdom of Cambodia;

2- Receive requests made by relevant competent authorities of the Kingdom of Cambodia and transmit such requests to the requested state;

3- Evaluate and determine the conditions of the request from the requesting state in accordance with the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

4- Evaluate and determine the conditions for transmitting requests from the relevant competent authorities of the Kingdom of Cambodia to requested states in accordance with the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia;

5- Monitor and expedite the implementation by the implementing authorities in providing legal assistance to requesting states;

6- Monitor and communicate with the Central Authority of the requested state to expedite the implementation of requests of the Kingdom of Cambodia;

7- Coordinate with all implementing authorities to promote efficiency in the implementation of requests in accordance with this Law and other relevant regulations;

8- If necessary, issue instructions or rules to ensure efficiency in the implementation of this Law.

#### Article 6 - Implementing Authority

The implementing authority for requests for assistance provided for in this Law shall be the Court of First Instance of the Capital/Province and the Prosecution Office attached to the Court of First Instance of the Capital/Province, depending on each case of request and as determined by the Central Authority.

In cases where the Court of First Instance of the Capital/Province receives a request from the Central Authority, the President of the Court shall be entrusted with competence under this Law to implement such request by exercising all powers entrusted to judges, by reference to the Code of Criminal Procedure and other laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

In cases where the Prosecution Office attached to the Court of First Instance of the Capital/Province receives a request from the Central Authority, the Prosecutor of the Prosecution Office shall be entrusted with competence under this Law to implement such request by exercising all powers entrusted to prosecutors, by reference to the Code of Criminal Procedure and other laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

Separately, the implementing authority for requests related to the provision of documents and information under the control of a state institution as provided for in Sub-Section 3 (Assistance Related to Provision of Documents and Information Under Control of State Institution), Section 2 (Particular Rules Related to Specific Formality of Assistance), Chapter 3 (Request for Assistance by Requesting State) of this Law, shall be the relevant competent state institution.

### Chapter 3 Request for Assistance by Requesting State

#### Section 1 General Rules

#### Article 7 - Transmission of Request to the Kingdom of Cambodia

The requesting state shall transmit the request to the Central Authority directly or through diplomatic channels.

#### Article 8 - Contents and Forms of Request to the Kingdom of Cambodia

A request shall include the following information:

1- Name of the authority conducting investigation, prosecution, inquiry, trial, or implementation of criminal procedures related to the request, such as details of the competent person to respond to requests related to the proceedings;

2- Description of the nature of the criminal case, including a summary of facts, name of the offense, and applicable penalties, as well as relevant legal texts; and

3- Description of the purpose of the request and the type of assistance sought.

The Central Authority may request the requesting state to provide additional relevant information and documents if it considers that the information and documents in the request are insufficient.

Requests with insufficient information shall not affect the validity of the request or automatically hinder the implementation of the request.

Requests and documents related to the request shall be in writing in Khmer and English.

#### Article 9 - Conditions for Provision of Assistance Based on Principle of Reciprocity

Legal assistance may be provided on the condition that the requesting state commits to providing legal assistance to the Kingdom of Cambodia in a similar manner when requested by the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 10 - Conditions for Refusal of Request

The Central Authority shall refuse a request from a requesting state in any of the following conditions:

1- Implementation of the request would affect the sovereignty, national security, public order, or other essential public interests of the Kingdom of Cambodia;

2- The request relates to investigation, prosecution, inquiry, or trial of any person for a political offense;

3- The request relates to investigation, prosecution, inquiry, or trial of any person for a military offense;

4- There are reasonable grounds to believe that the request is made for the purpose of investigation, prosecution, inquiry, or trial of any person on account of that person's race, religion, gender, ethnic origin, nationality, or political views;

5- There are reasonable grounds to believe that if the request is implemented, that person would face torture.

The Central Authority may refuse a request in any of the following conditions:

1- The request relates to investigation, prosecution, inquiry, or trial of any person for an act that is not considered a criminal offense under the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia, if such act occurred in the Kingdom of Cambodia. This provision shall not apply if the underlying facts presented by the requesting state are considered a criminal offense under the provisions of the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia, even if:

- The category of offense is different;

- The name of the offense, terminology usage, definition, or characterization of the offense is not the same;

- The constituent elements of the offense as provided for in the laws of the requesting state are different from those provided for in the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

2- Provision of assistance would impose an excessive burden on the resources of the Kingdom of Cambodia;

3- Provision of assistance would affect investigation or proceedings related to a criminal case in the Kingdom of Cambodia;

4- In all circumstances where it is shown that the request should not be granted.

5- There are reasonable grounds to believe that if the request is implemented, that person would face the death penalty, and the requesting state has not provided an assurance that the death penalty will not be imposed or, if imposed, will not be carried out;

6- The request relates to investigation, prosecution, inquiry, or trial of any person for an offense for which the person has been acquitted, pardoned, or has served the sentence in full in the Kingdom of Cambodia or in a foreign state;

7- The request relates to investigation, prosecution, inquiry, or trial of any person for an offense for which the statute of limitations has expired under the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

A request shall not be refused solely on the ground of:

1- Bank secrecy, financial institution secrecy, or reporting person secrecy as provided for in the Law on Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the Financing of Terrorism; or

2- The offense is considered to involve fiscal matters.

#### Article 11 - Review and Determination of Request

Upon receipt of a request from a requesting state, the Central Authority shall review and consider the request as well as all conditions for implementing the request.

If the request meets all conditions for implementation in accordance with this Law, the Central Authority shall proceed with the next step by transmitting the request to the implementing authority for implementation.

If the request does not meet the conditions provided for in this Law which makes the request unimplementable, the Central Authority shall notify the requesting state.

If the request cannot be implemented due to lack of information, the Central Authority shall request additional information from the requesting state. Even if additional information is received from the requesting state, but the request still cannot be implemented in accordance with this Law, the Central Authority shall notify the requesting state of the refusal of the request.

#### Article 12 - Suspension of Implementation of Request

If it is found that the implementation of the request would affect the process of investigation, prosecution, inquiry, trial, or court proceedings in the Kingdom of Cambodia, the Authority...

...the Central Authority may suspend the implementation of the request or implement it under specific conditions and notify the requesting state.

#### Article 13 - Implementation of Request by Relevant Competent Implementing Authority

Upon receipt of a request from the Central Authority, the relevant competent implementing authority shall implement the request. After completing the implementation, the relevant competent implementing authority shall transmit the results together with related documents and evidence to the Central Authority.

In cases where there is an obstacle to implementing a request, the relevant competent implementing authority shall report to the Central Authority the reasons for the failure to implement the request. In cases where the request cannot be implemented, the Central Authority shall notify the requesting state.

#### Article 14 - Authentication of Documents

If a treaty or convention on mutual legal assistance in criminal matters between the Kingdom of Cambodia and the requesting state requires authenticated documents, the Central Authority shall request the relevant competent implementing authority to authenticate such documents in accordance with the forms and procedures provided for in the treaty or convention or as determined by the Central Authority.

#### Article 15 - Transmission of Results of Implementation of Request to Requesting State

Upon receipt of the results of the implementation of the request from the relevant competent implementing authority, the Central Authority shall transmit the results together with documents and evidence, if any, to the requesting state, under the conditions agreed upon between the Kingdom of Cambodia and the requesting state.

#### Article 16 - Confidentiality

The Central Authority shall take all appropriate measures to maintain the confidentiality of the request for assistance, its content and supporting documents, the decision to grant assistance, and any actions taken in implementing the request, if so requested by the requesting state. If the case cannot be implemented without violating the confidentiality requirement, the Central Authority shall notify the requesting state of this issue to determine whether the request should be implemented.

The Central Authority may request the requesting state to maintain the confidentiality of information or evidence provided, or the source of such information or evidence. The Central Authority shall attach conditions regarding the use or disclosure of such information or evidence when transmitting the results of the implementation of the request to the requesting state.

#### Section 2 Particular Rules Related to Specific Formality of Assistance

#### Sub-Section 1 Assistance Related to Taking Statements and Evidence

#### Article 17 - Principles related to Taking Statements

Upon receipt of a request for taking statements from any person from the Central Authority, the relevant competent implementing authority shall locate such person and request him/her to give statements in accordance with the request of the requesting state.

If such person refuses to give statements, the implementing authority shall report to the Central Authority, which shall provide such information to the requesting state.

#### Article 18 - Procedures for Implementation of Request to Take Statements and Evidences

Upon receipt of a request from the requesting state for taking statements or collecting evidence based on Cambodian court procedures, the Central Authority shall transmit the request to the relevant competent implementing authority.

Specific procedures determined by the requesting state shall be implemented, unless such procedures are contrary to the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia. In cases where such procedures are contrary to the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia or no procedures are determined in the request, the legal procedures of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be implemented, unless otherwise agreed between the Kingdom of Cambodia and the requesting state.

After completing the implementation of the request, the implementing authority shall transmit the record of statements or evidence collected to the Central Authority for further action.

#### Sub-Section 2 Assistance Related to Searches and Seizures

#### Article 19 - Principles Related to Searches and Seizures

Upon receipt of a request from the requesting state for search or seizure of items for the benefit of collection of evidence, the Central Authority shall transmit such request to the relevant competent implementing authority for implementation in accordance with the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

The implementing authority may search and seize property that is an instrument of crime used for committing a crime or proceeds of crime. The implementing authority may also search and seize documents or information identifying the property that is an instrument of crime or proceeds of crime.

In cases where the request relates to information held by a bank or financial institution or other entity or person, the implementing authority is empowered by this Law to require such bank or financial institution or entity or person to provide the information, in accordance with the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 20 - Procedures for Implementation Related to Search and Seizure

The relevant competent implementing authority conducting search and seizure of items upon request shall prepare a record of search and seizure in accordance with the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia and transmit the record and seized items to the Central Authority for further action. Such record shall be in the form and manner in accordance with the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

Other provisions related to search and seizure as provided for in the Code of Criminal Procedure of the Kingdom of Cambodia shall also be implemented, even if the offense that led to the search or seizure did not occur in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Sub-Section 3 Assistance Related to Provision of Documents and Information Under Control of State Institution

#### Article 21 - Procedures for Implementation Related to Requests for Documents or Information Under Control of State Institution

Upon receipt of a request from the requesting state seeking documents or information under the control of any state institution of the Kingdom of Cambodia, the Central Authority shall transmit such request to the relevant state institution to implement the request of the requesting state. Such state institution shall provide the results of the implementation of the request to the Central Authority.

#### Article 22 - Conditions for Provision of Documents or Information Under Control of State Institution

If the documents or information requested by the requesting state, as provided for in Article 21 (Procedures for Implementation Related to Requests for Documents or Information Under Control of State Institution) above, are documents or information that cannot be disclosed, and the relevant competent state institution controlling such documents or information considers that such documents or information cannot be provided due to the confidentiality of such documents or information, or can be provided under specific conditions, the relevant institution shall notify the Central Authority of the reasons or conditions for providing the requested documents or information.

#### Article 23 - Authentication of Documents Under Control of State Institution

In providing documents upon request of the requesting state as provided for in this Sub-Section, the Central Authority shall request the relevant competent authority to authenticate the documents in accordance with the forms, procedures, and conditions requested by the requesting state as provided for in a treaty or convention on mutual legal assistance in criminal matters between the Kingdom of Cambodia and the requesting state or as determined by the Central Authority.

#### Sub-Section 4 Assistance Related to Service of Court Documents

#### Article 24 - Implementation of Request Related to Service of Court Documents

Upon receipt of a request from the requesting state for service of court documents, the Central Authority shall transmit the request to the relevant competent implementing authority, and the implementing authority shall implement the request and provide the results of the implementation back to the Central Authority. The relevant competent implementing authority shall use all lawful means to implement the request as soon as possible.

If the court documents to be served require any person to appear before an official or court in the requesting state, the relevant competent implementing authority shall serve such documents to that person within a reasonable time before the date of appearance in the requesting state.

The results of the service of court documents shall be reported in the forms and procedures determined...

...by the Central Authority, unless a treaty on mutual legal assistance in criminal matters between the Kingdom of Cambodia and the requesting state provides otherwise.

The Central Authority shall transmit to the requesting state the evidence of service of court documents. If the service of documents cannot be completed, the Central Authority shall notify the requesting state, stating the reasons for the failure to complete the service.

#### Article 25 - No Penalty for Failure to Comply with a Summons by Requesting State

If any person in the Kingdom of Cambodia is summoned as a witness in the requesting state, such person shall not be subject to any criminal or civil liability in the Kingdom of Cambodia for refusing or failing to comply with such summons, even if such refusal or failure is contrary to the provisions of the summons.

#### Sub-Section 5 Assistance Related to Transfer of Detained Person to Provide Testimony or Assist in Investigation in Requesting State

#### Article 26 - Conditions for Transfer of Detained Person to Provide Testimony or Assist in Investigation in Requesting State

Upon receipt of a request for temporary transfer of any person in detention in the Kingdom of Cambodia to provide testimony or assist in investigation in court proceedings as a witness in the requesting state, the Central Authority shall transmit the request to the relevant competent implementing authority for action in accordance with the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia, if the Central Authority considers that such transfer is necessary.

The transfer of a detained person may be carried out in compliance with the following conditions:

- The detained person voluntarily consents to the transfer to provide testimony or assist in the requested investigation; and

- The requesting state agrees to comply with specific conditions required by the Central Authority, including but not limited to costs related to the implementation of this assistance, as well as safety measures such as detention and ensuring the safety of the detained person.

...the transferred person shall not be subject to prosecution for any criminal offense in the requesting state and shall be returned.

#### Article 27 - Period of Detention of the Transferred Person

The period during which the transferred person is in the requesting state and the period of detention of the transferred person in the requesting state to provide testimony or assist in investigation shall be included as the period of detention of that person in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 28 - Transit of the Transferred Person

Upon receipt of a request from the requesting state to permit any person in detention by a foreign state to transit through the territory of the Kingdom of Cambodia to provide testimony or assist in investigation in the territory of the requesting state, the Central Authority shall notify the relevant competent prosecutor to coordinate the transit of such person, if the Central Authority considers that the requesting state has met the conditions to receive this assistance.

The person as provided for in paragraph 1 above shall transit through the Kingdom of Cambodia within the period permitted by the Central Authority and based on the agreed conditions with the requesting state. If the permitted period expires or the conditions are not implemented, such person shall be re-released or deported in accordance with the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia, unless there is a different agreement between the requesting state and the Central Authority of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Sub-Section 6 Assistance Related to Locating Persons

#### Article 29 - Locating Persons

Upon receipt of a request for assistance to locate any person who is believed to be in the Kingdom of Cambodia and whom the requesting state needs for the purpose of court proceedings in its territory, the Central Authority shall transmit the request to the relevant competent implementing authority to locate such person. The implementing authority shall report the results of the search for such person back to the Central Authority for further action.

#### Sub-Section 7 Assistance Related to Freezing, Seizure, and Confiscation of Property

#### Article 30 - Freezing or Seizure of Property Based on Non-Final Court Order of Requesting State

Upon receipt of a request for freezing or seizure of any property based on a non-final court order of the requesting state, the Central Authority shall transmit the request to the Court of First Instance of the Capital/Province having jurisdiction to freeze or seize such property. If several Courts of First Instance have jurisdiction over the case, the Central Authority may transmit the request to any of such Courts. The decision to freeze or seize the property shall be in the form of an order by the President of the Court of First Instance, with clear and proper legal grounds.

The Court of First Instance shall consider the request in paragraph 1 above as a preventive measure. The Court of First Instance shall issue an order to freeze or seize such property if:

1- Such property can be frozen or seized under the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia; and

2- There are reasonable grounds to believe that such property may be sold, transferred, concealed, or dealt with in a manner that would exempt such property from confiscation or make it useless.

The order of freezing or seizure of property by the Court of First Instance shall be notified through publication on the notice board of the Court of First Instance and shall be notified to relevant persons, if any, or in cases of necessity, published to the public through newspapers or audiovisual telecommunication means.

A third party or person having interest in the order of freezing or seizure as provided for in paragraph 2 above may file a protest to the Court of First Instance to vacate such order within 15 (fifteen) days from the date of notification of the order. If there is evidence proving that the property is not related to the request of the requesting state as provided for in paragraph 1 above, or the relevant person has not committed any act as charged in paragraph 1 above, the Court of First Instance shall vacate the order. This decision shall be final. The forms and procedures for protest under the Code of Criminal Procedure shall be implemented for the protest in this paragraph.

A third party or person having interest in the order of freezing or seizure as provided for in paragraph 2 above may appeal to the Appeal Court against the freezing or seizure order of the Court of First Instance within 1 (one) month from the date of expiry of the period for protest. The decision of the Appeal Court shall be final. An appeal may also be filed against the order rejecting the protest within 1 (one) month from the date of notification of the rejection order. The decision of the Appeal Court shall be final. The forms and procedures for appeal under the Code of Criminal Procedure shall be implemented for this case.

The decision rejecting the request for freezing or seizure of property by the Court of First Instance, or the decision vacating the order of freezing or seizure upon the request of the requesting state, shall not preclude the Central Authority from submitting a new request to the Court of First Instance to issue a new order for freezing or seizure if there is new evidence that could lead the Court of First Instance to issue such an order.

In cases where the Court of First Instance rejects the request of the requesting state, this order shall be final and shall be immediately reported to the Central Authority.

#### Article 31 - Confiscation of Property Based on Final Court Decision of Requesting State

Upon receipt of a request for confiscation of property based on a final court decision of the requesting state, the Central Authority shall transmit the request to the Court of First Instance having jurisdiction to decide on the confiscation of property. If several Courts of First Instance have jurisdiction over the case, the Central Authority may transmit the request to any of such Courts. The decision for confiscation of property shall be in the form of a judgment with legal and factual grounds. The Court shall make the decision by a single judge who is the President of the Court or by a trial panel chaired by the President of the Court and two other judges appointed by the President of the Court in complex cases.

Upon receipt of the request, the Court of First Instance shall organize a hearing for debate and examination of various evidences and hearing of testimony from all relevant parties. The provisions related to the composition and conduct of the hearing as provided for in the Code of Criminal Procedure and other relevant laws shall be implemented in this case.

In cases where there are sufficient grounds, the Court of First Instance may decide to confiscate property based on a final court decision of the requesting state if:

1- Such property can be confiscated under the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia; and

2- The court of the requesting state has jurisdiction to decide on this case.

In cases where the judgment of the court of the requesting state was made not in accordance with the fundamental principles of fair trial in criminal matters, such as the right to defense of the accused, or where the implementation of the judgment of the requesting state would seriously affect public order in the Kingdom of Cambodia, the Court shall refuse the above request.

#### Article 32 - Decision on Freezing, Seizure, or Confiscation of Property

The Court of First Instance has the right to decide on the freezing, seizure, or confiscation of property as provided for in this Sub-Section, even if the offense that led to the freezing, seizure, or confiscation did not occur in the Kingdom of Cambodia. The Court of First Instance may also decide to freeze, seize, or confiscate property even if the offender or the accused has died or is unknown or has fled, when the court of the requesting state has issued a decision that such property is proceeds of crime or instruments of crime to be confiscated. In cases where assistance is requested to freeze, seize, or confiscate the property of an offender or accused who has died or is unknown or has fled, the conditions related to the right to defense as provided for in Article 31 (Confiscation of Property Based on Final Court Decision of Requesting State) of this Law shall not be implemented.

#### Article 33 - Coordination of Seizure and Confiscation

The Central Authority may enter into an agreement with the requesting state for the purpose of coordinating seizure and confiscation, upon the request of the requesting state in accordance with this Law.

#### Article 34 - Management, Disposal, and Sharing of Confiscated Property

Property frozen, seized, or confiscated in accordance with this Law shall be managed and disposed of in accordance with the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

Property confiscated based on a final judgment of the Cambodian court in accordance with the provisions of this Sub-Section shall be under the management and disposal of the Central Authority.

Based on the agreement between the Central Authority and the requesting state and cases permitted by its law, the Central Authority may transfer all or part of the confiscated property to the requesting state, unless such transfer would affect public order or social morality in the Kingdom of Cambodia. In all cases, the Central Authority shall deduct from the confiscated property all expenses related to the activities of freezing, seizure, and confiscation until the transfer of such property to the requesting state. In cases of necessity, the Central Authority shall dispose of the confiscated property which is not in the form of cash, in accordance with the procedures in force.

Confiscated property that is not transferred to the requesting state shall be deposited into a fund for the use of the Central Authority in the work of mutual legal assistance in criminal matters with foreign states or other activities related to cooperation in criminal matters.

As for confiscated property that is dangerous to humans, property, or the environment, as well as property that cannot be legally possessed, the Central Authority may apply to the same court that issued the confiscation order to destroy it.

### Chapter 4 Request for Assistance by the Kingdom of Cambodia

#### Section 1 General Rules

#### Article 35 - Requests Made by Relevant Competent Authorities

Relevant competent authorities may request legal assistance from foreign states through the Central Authority. The Central Authority shall consider the appropriateness of the request by considering the detailed provisions, facts, and relevant documents, and shall notify the requesting competent authority of its decision. In cases of necessity, the Central Authority may seek a decision from the Royal Government before proceeding with further procedures.

#### Article 36 - Formality of Request for Assistance

Relevant competent authorities shall complete the request form for assistance which will be determined by the Central Authority. In cases of necessity, the Central Authority may request additional information to ensure that the request for assistance can be accepted by the requested state.

#### Article 37 - Conditions for Use of Information and Evidence

Requesting competent authorities shall not use or disclose information or evidence provided by the requested state for any purpose other than that stated in the request, without the prior consent of the requested state.

Requesting competent authorities shall take appropriate measures to maintain the confidentiality of information and evidence provided by the requested state, except for information and evidence necessary for the purpose stated in the request.

#### Article 38 - Admissibility of Evidence

Courts shall admit all evidence obtained through legal assistance that the Kingdom of Cambodia received from the requested state for consideration in accordance with the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Section 2 Particular Rules Related to Requests for Detained Persons or Other Persons to Provide Testimony or Assist in Investigation in the Kingdom of Cambodia

#### Article 39 - Conditions for Requesting Persons to Provide Testimony or Assist in Investigation

Relevant competent authorities may make a request to the requested state through the Central Authority to temporarily transfer a detained person or send another person in the requested state to provide testimony or assist in investigation in court proceedings of the Kingdom of Cambodia, under the following conditions:

1- A criminal case has commenced in the Kingdom of Cambodia; and

2- Such detained person or other person can provide evidence beneficial to the court proceedings and consents to provide testimony or assist in investigation in the court proceedings of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

Upon receipt of any detained person from the requested state to provide testimony or assist in investigation in court proceedings of the Kingdom of Cambodia, the detention of such person in the Kingdom of Cambodia shall be in accordance with the conditions agreed with the requested state or as determined by the Central Authority.

The receipt and detention of a detained person shall be in accordance with the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 40 - Rights of Persons Providing Testimony or Assisting in Investigation

Apart from the conditions agreed with the requested state, any person, whether a detained person or other person, who comes to the Kingdom of Cambodia for the purpose of providing testimony or assisting in investigation under this Law shall:

1- Not be prosecuted, detained, punished, or subject to any restriction of personal liberty in the Kingdom of Cambodia for any offense against the laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia that was charged or committed before departure from the requested state.

2- Not be required to provide evidence related to any other criminal offense in the Kingdom of Cambodia, other than the criminal offense stated in the request, unless such person consents.

3- Not be subject to any civil suit or other suits related to any act or omission that the person was alleged to have performed before departure from the requested state.

The rights as provided for in paragraph 1 above shall expire when the person has had an opportunity to leave the Kingdom of Cambodia within 15 days after notification by the relevant competent authority that the person's presence is no longer required, yet the person continues to stay in the Kingdom of Cambodia or returns voluntarily after having left the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 41 - Report on Completion of Providing Testimony or Assisting in Investigation

The relevant competent authority shall submit a report to the Central Authority upon completion of the providing of testimony or assisting in investigation by a detained person or other person.

#### Article 42 - Return of Detained Person

Upon receipt of the report from the relevant competent authority as provided for in Article 41 (Report on Completion of Providing Testimony or Assisting in Investigation) of this Law, the Central Authority shall coordinate with the relevant competent authority to immediately return the detained person to the requested state.

### Chapter 5 Expenses

#### Article 43 - Expenses

All expenses of the Central Authority related to the coordination of legal assistance to requesting states and requests for legal assistance from requested states shall be the burden of the Ministry of Justice. Expenses in the implementation of the request shall be the burden of the implementing authority, and expenses related to the request for assistance shall be the burden of the relevant competent authorities. In cases where the expenses for providing legal assistance are a heavy burden for the Kingdom of Cambodia, the Kingdom of Cambodia may request the requesting state to contribute to such expenses.

This Law is declared as urgent.
