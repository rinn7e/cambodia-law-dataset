# HEREBY PROMULGATE

The Law on the Management of Petroleum and Petroleum Products, which was adopted by the National Assembly on June 17, 2019, during the 2nd session of the 6th legislature, and reviewed in its entirety by the Senate regarding its form and legal aspects on June 25, 2019, during the 3rd plenary session of the 4th legislature, the entire text of which is as follows:

# Law on the Management of Petroleum and Petroleum Products

# Chapter 1

## General Provisions

### Article 1.-

This Law aims to promote the development of the petroleum sector in a sustainable and efficient manner to contribute to the economic and social development and the long-term benefit of the nation.

### Article 2.-

This Law has the objective of defining the competence and rules for the management of the petroleum sector.

### Article 3.-

This Law has the scope of application over all activities in the petroleum sector within the territorial integrity and sovereignty of the Kingdom of Cambodia, including the seabed and subsoil of the exclusive economic zone and the continental shelf, as well as other areas where the Kingdom of Cambodia has sovereign rights.

### Article 4.-

Key technical terms used in this Law shall have the meanings as defined in the Glossary in the Annex.

# Chapter 2

## Competence and Management

### Article 5.-

All petroleum resources are the property of the State.

### Article 6.-

The Ministry of Mines and Energy is the competent institution to manage and inspect all activities in the petroleum sector.

Physical or legal entities wishing to conduct activities in the petroleum sector must request permission from the Ministry of Mines and Energy.

The Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy may delegate functions and resources to sub-national levels for certain activities in the petroleum sector in accordance with the procedures in force.

### Article 7.-

The management of the petroleum sector shall be based on the principles of transparency, accountability, equity in the value chain, community inclusivity, security and safety, and environmental responsibility.

To implement this principle effectively, the management of the petroleum sector shall ensure:

- Maximum national benefits

- Best practices in upstream and downstream activities

- Supply of petroleum products with quality, quantity, and price that contribute to strengthening national energy security

- High environmental responsibility, reduction of environmental pollution, and environmental restoration

- Effectiveness of monitoring the implementation of management rules on safety and occupational health conditions

- Building, promoting, and enhancing national capacity in both technical and contractual aspects in the petroleum sector

- Creation of employment opportunities for citizens and local services

- Increasing national budget revenue and contributing to accelerating economic growth and competitiveness of the national industrial and commercial sectors, and

- Creating direct benefits for local communities and improving the living standards of citizens in petroleum operation areas.

# Chapter 3

## Management of Upstream Activities

### Article 8.-

Upstream activities include reconnaissance, exploration, development, and petroleum production.

### Article 9.-

All exploration, development, and petroleum production activities must have a petroleum agreement.

The Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy shall request prior permission from the Prime Minister to enter into a petroleum agreement in accordance with the laws and other legal instruments in force.

The conditions and procedures for selecting a party to the petroleum agreement shall be determined by sub-decree.

### Article 10.-

Physical or legal entities have the right to request to conduct reconnaissance. For any legal entity that has received permission to conduct reconnaissance in any area, that legal entity has the right to request to enter into a petroleum agreement in any petroleum block within that area, but this right to request is not an exclusive right.

All reconnaissance activities must have permission from the Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy.

The conditions and procedures for authorizing reconnaissance shall be determined by a proclamation of the Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy.

#### Article 11.-

Legal entities that may be permitted to enter into a petroleum agreement are as follows:

- Public enterprises established in accordance with the law and other legal instruments in force

- Capital companies duly registered in accordance with the Law on Commercial Rules and Register and the Law on Commercial Enterprises

- Foreign commercial companies conducting business operations in the Kingdom of Cambodia in accordance with the laws in force of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 12.-

Petroleum agreements in accordance with the provisions of this law shall determine the minimum key points as follows:

a- State revenue from petroleum.

b- State participation in shares.

c- Petroleum benefits sharing mechanism.

d- Petroleum costs and cost recovery.

e- Agreement area and relinquishment of surface area.

f- Minimum work obligations and expenditure budget.

g- Determination of transfer points related to the transfer of ownership of the petroleum production share.

h- Validity period of the agreement and conditions for termination and extension of validity.

i- Obligation to supply petroleum to meet domestic demand.

j- Obligations after completion of petroleum operations and environmental restoration.

k- Safety, security, and environmental obligations in petroleum operations.

l- Conditions for sale or transfer of shares.

m- Obligation to provide reports and data.

n- Petroleum development and production plan.

o- Obligation to provide training and institutional capacity building in the petroleum sector.

p- Obligation to use local workforce, goods, and services.

q- Obligation to participate in local development, promotion of welfare, and protection of the rights of the local community.

r- Dispute resolution.

#### Article 13.-

A petroleum agreement contractor shall not obtain more than 2 (two) petroleum blocks. Each petroleum block shall be subject to a separate petroleum agreement.

#### Article 14.-

The Ministry of Mines and Energy shall determine the division of petroleum blocks with specific coordinates based on geological characteristics.

Before opening any petroleum block to select a petroleum agreement contractor, the Ministry of Mines and Energy may arrange for a preliminary assessment study on socio-economic characteristics, risk levels, and environmental impacts.

#### Article 15.-

A petroleum agreement contractor shall obtain ownership rights over its share of petroleum production at the transfer point, after such petroleum resources are produced in accordance with the provisions of this law as well as other laws and regulations in force.

#### Article 16.-

An upstream petroleum agreement contractor wishing to conduct downstream activities shall register commercial business as another separate legal entity.

#### Article 17.-

A petroleum agreement shall have a validity period of not more than 30 (thirty) years, counting from the date of signing. The petroleum agreement contractor may request an extension of this validity period, but the total duration of the validity extension shall not exceed 15 (fifteen) years.

The validity period of a petroleum agreement shall be divided into two: the exploration period and the development and production period. The exploration period shall not exceed 7 (seven) years and may be requested for validity extension, in which the total duration of the validity extension shall not exceed 3 (three) years.

Conditions and procedures for the validity extension of the petroleum agreement shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 18.-

A petroleum agreement contractor has the right to request extension, modification of rights and benefits, pledge as security, transfer of rights or power of control, or creation of security interest in the petroleum agreement.

The conditions and procedures for requesting extension, modification of rights and benefits, pledge as security, transfer of rights or power of control, or creation of security interest in the petroleum agreement shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 19.-

A petroleum agreement may be suspended or terminated in the event that the petroleum agreement contractor fails to perform obligations in accordance with the petroleum agreement or the provisions of this law.

The conditions and procedures for the suspension and termination of the petroleum agreement shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 20.-

A petroleum agreement contractor shall have key obligations as follows:

a- Perform operations properly and effectively in accordance with technical, industrial, and international standards.

b- Protect the environment and society, conduct environmental and social impact assessments, prepare environmental management plans, and prepare plans for decommissioning and environmental restoration.

c- Protect occupational health and safety.

d- Protect public safety in and around the petroleum production operations area.

e- Provide education, training, and prioritize employment for Cambodian citizens.

f- Participate in local community development, promoting the well-being and livelihoods of local people around the production site area.

g- Maximize the use of local labor, goods, and services.

h- Provide written reports on petroleum operations to the Ministry of Mines and Energy.

i- Comply with the law and other legal instruments in force.

#### Article 21.-

A petroleum agreement contractor shall notify in writing the Ministry of Mines and Energy of the discovery of petroleum resources and/or the discovery of commercial petroleum resources, in order to request permission for petroleum development and production. The Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy shall request approval in principle from the Prime Minister before granting permission for petroleum development and production.

The conditions and procedures for notification of the discovery of petroleum resources and the permission to conduct development and production of petroleum shall be determined by Sub-decree.

#### Article 22.-

In case the discovered petroleum resources have only commercial potential, the petroleum agreement contractor shall submit an application to request conditional permission to conduct petroleum development and production from the Ministry of Mines and Energy. The Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy shall request approval in principle from the Prime Minister before granting conditional permission for petroleum development and production.

The conditions and procedures for granting authorization for petroleum development and production shall be determined by sub-decree.

**Article 23.- (មាត្រា ២៣.-)**

The petroleum agreement contractor who fails to fully implement development activities within a maximum period of 5 (five) years after the date of receiving the initial development and production authorization, that petroleum agreement contractor shall return the entire agreement area to the Ministry of Mines and Energy, and the petroleum agreement shall be terminated.

**Article 24.- (មាត្រា ២៤.-)**

The Ministry of Mines and Energy may require two or more petroleum agreement contractors to conduct joint operations in the development and production of one or more petroleum reservoirs located in different petroleum agreement areas in any of the following cases:

a- The development and production of a petroleum reservoir in a single petroleum agreement area cannot be commercial and requires joint development and production of the entire reservoir with other petroleum agreement parties to make it commercial; or

b- The joint development and production of the petroleum reservoir provides better and higher economic, social, and efficiency benefits than separate operations.

The joint development and production agreement shall be submitted to the Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy for review and approval in accordance with laws and other legal regulations in force.

**Article 25.- (មាត្រា ២៥.-)**

The petroleum agreement contractor has an obligation to return any portion of the agreement area in accordance with each phase of petroleum operations.

Apart from the mandatory obligations as stipulated in the first paragraph of this Article, the petroleum agreement contractor may request the Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy to return voluntarily any additional portion or the entire agreement area.

The conditions and procedures on the obligation to return the area shall be determined by sub-decree.

**Article 26.- (មាត្រា ២៦.-)**

Any petroleum industry activities that are necessary to be conducted in or across natural protected areas, cultural and historical areas, archaeological areas, and national heritage areas may only be conducted upon prior consent from the ministries or institutions managing those areas in accordance with laws and other legal regulations in force.

**Article 27.- (មាត្រា ២៧.-)**

The petroleum agreement contractor shall negotiate with the landowner or lawful land manager before conducting petroleum operations, and shall pay compensation for damage caused by its operations. In case no agreement is reached, the petroleum agreement contractor may request the Ministry of Mines and Energy to coordinate between the petroleum agreement contractor and the landowner or lawful land manager in accordance with laws and other legal regulations in force.

The conditions and procedures for paying compensation shall be determined by sub-decree.

**Article 28.- (មាត្រា ២៨.-)**

All petroleum data obtained from petroleum operations is the property of the State and shall be managed and administered by the Ministry of Mines and Energy.

The petroleum agreement contractor may use petroleum data relevant to the agreement area during the validity period of the petroleum agreement.

The petroleum agreement contractor shall submit all petroleum data from petroleum operations to the Ministry of Mines and Energy when the petroleum agreement is terminated or expires.

**Article 29.- (មាត្រា ២៩.-)**

The petroleum agreement contractor shall provide the Ministry of Mines and Energy with all information and technical reports related to petroleum operations.

**Article 30.- (មាត្រា ៣០.-)**

The Ministry of Mines and Energy may publicly disseminate information related to the environment and society relevant to petroleum operations.

**Article 31.- (មាត្រា ៣១.-)**

The Royal Government may require the petroleum agreement contractor to supply a portion of its petroleum production not exceeding 25 (twenty-five) percent to meet necessary domestic demand. In case of an emergency in domestic supply, the export of petroleum resources may be prohibited.

The administration and obligations of meeting domestic demand shall be determined by sub-decree.

**Article 32.- (មាត្រា ៣២.-)**

The petroleum agreement contractor conducting petroleum operations shall have insurance for the safety of petroleum operations, facilities and equipment used for petroleum operations, environmental damage, and other damages in accordance with laws and other legal regulations in force, and in accordance with the contract approved by the Ministry of Mines and Energy.

**Article 33.- (មាត្រា ៣៣.-)**

In case there are more than one petroleum agreement contractors in a petroleum agreement, each petroleum agreement contractor shall be jointly and severally liable.

### Chapter 4
### Management of Petroleum Revenue and State Dividends

**Article 34.- (មាត្រា ៣៤.-)**

The State has the right to participate in shares in petroleum operations. The State's share participation shall be decided by the Prime Minister.

**Article 35.- (មាត្រា ៣៥.-)**

The applicant or petroleum agreement contractor conducting licensing activities shall pay public service fees.

The forms and procedures for paying public service fees shall be determined by joint proclamation (inter-ministerial declaration) between the Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy and the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

**Article 36.- (មាត្រា ៣៦.-)**

The Royal Government shall withhold petroleum royalty before allowing the petroleum agreement contractor to make recovery of petroleum costs and allocation of petroleum production.

Petroleum production remaining after withholding royalty and recovery of petroleum costs shall be allocated between the Royal Government and the petroleum agreement contractor in accordance with the petroleum agreement.

The royalty rate, procedures, rules for valuation, measurement of quantity, and location for pricing shall be determined by sub-decree.

**Article 37.- (មាត្រា ៣៧.-)**

The petroleum agreement contractor shall be subject to tax obligations and the fiscal regime in accordance with laws and other legal regulations in force.

**Article 38.- (មាត្រា ៣៨.-)**

A petroleum account shall be established in accordance with laws and other legal regulations in force in order to manage revenue from the petroleum sector.

## Chapter 5
### Management of Downstream Activities

#### Article 39 .-

Downstream activities include processing, transportation, storage, and trade of petroleum and petroleum products, except for the export of petroleum which is implemented in accordance with the petroleum agreement.

#### Article 40 .-

Downstream activities shall be permitted to be implemented for:

1- Physical person, or

2- Any of the following legal entities:

- Public enterprises established in accordance with the laws and other legal instruments in force

- Partnership companies and capital companies properly registered in accordance with the Law on Commercial Rules and Register and the Law on Commercial Enterprises

- Foreign commercial companies conducting business operations in the Kingdom of Cambodia in accordance with the laws in force of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

#### Article 41 .-

A physical person or legal entity conducting downstream activities must have a license or permit from the Ministry of Mines and Energy in accordance with the laws and other legal instruments in force.

A physical person or legal entity may request to conduct more than one business activity in the downstream sector, but must have different licenses or permits.

The types of licenses and permits, insurance, conditions, and procedures for granting downstream licenses or permits shall be determined by sub-decree.

#### Article 42 .-

A physical person or legal entity conducting downstream activities must have a technical certification from the Ministry of Mines and Energy before starting the construction of infrastructure to serve downstream activities.

A physical person or legal entity that has obtained a downstream license or permit who has

the intention of conducting upstream activities shall register a separate legal entity for conducting upstream activities in accordance with the provisions of this law.

#### Article 44 .-

A physical person or legal entity applying for a safety technical certificate, license, or permit to conduct downstream activities must pay public service fees.

Formalities and procedures for public service fee payment shall be determined by an inter-ministerial prakas between the Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy and the Minister of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

#### Article 45 .-

A physical person or legal entity that has obtained a license or permit has the right to request renewal, modification of rights and benefits, pledge as collateral, transfer rights or control authority, or succeed the license or permit.

Conditions and procedures for requesting renewal, modification of rights and benefits, pledging as collateral, transferring rights or control authority, or succession shall be determined by a prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy.

#### Article 46 .-

A license or permit may be suspended or revoked in the event that the physical person or legal entity that has obtained the license or permit fails to fulfill the obligations in accordance with the conditions stipulated in the license or permit and the provisions of this law.

Conditions and procedures for suspension and revocation of a license or permit shall be determined by sub-decree.

#### Article 47 .-

For any downstream infrastructure project with an investment capital from 200,000,000,000 (two hundred billion) Riels or more, the Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy must organize negotiations with the participation of relevant competent ministries and institutions and the applicant to reach an investment agreement and request approval from the Prime Minister before deciding to grant the license.

#### Article 48 .-

The Ministry of Mines and Energy has the competence to formulate downstream technical regulations as follows:

- Quality standards for petroleum products

- Storage of petroleum and petroleum products

- Transportation of petroleum and petroleum products

- Safety and management of petroleum and petroleum products stations

- petroleum processing and petrochemicals, and

- other technical regulations.

Other technical regulations shall be determined by a prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy.

#### Article 49 .-

The Ministry of Mines and Energy shall formulate the national master plan on the transportation and distribution of petroleum products and natural gas in promoting and managing investment in the construction of transportation pipeline networks and distribution of petroleum products and natural gas. The Ministry of Mines and Energy shall lead and coordinate with relevant ministries, institutions, and/or competent authorities on the formulation of this master plan.

Permitting the private sector to invest in the construction of transportation pipeline networks and distribution of petroleum products and natural gas shall be limited only to a section of the entire system or to some commercial supply areas, and shall be based on technical conditions, security and safety, socio-economics, and competition principles.

#### Article 50 .-

A physical person or legal entity obtaining a license or permit shall have the following major obligations:

a- Conduct operations properly and efficiently in accordance with technical characteristics, best practices, and international standards.

b- Conduct operations properly and efficiently in accordance with all legal instruments and regulations related to the environment sector.

c- Protect occupational health and safety.

d- Protect public safety within and around the permitted area.

e- Utilize local labor, goods, and services to the maximum extent possible.

f- Implement in compliance with other laws and legal instruments in force.

# Chapter 6

## Inspection

### Article 51 .-

The Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy has the right to appoint petroleum inspection officials to monitor, investigate, and inspect the implementation of the law.

Petroleum inspection officials shall be qualified as judicial police as stipulated in this law in accordance with the provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure.

Formalities and procedures for granting qualification to petroleum inspection officials shall be determined by an inter-ministerial prakas between the Minister of the Ministry of Justice and the Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy.

### Article 52 .-

Petroleum inspection officials have the right to request cooperative assistance from relevant competent institutions to participate in cracking down on offenses as stipulated in this law.

### Article 53 .-

During law enforcement operations, petroleum inspection officials must wear uniforms and/or display identification badges, and carry a mission permit.

Uniforms and identification badges of petroleum inspection officials shall be determined by sub-decree.

### Article 54 .-

Petroleum inspection officials have the right to inspect all operations in the petroleum sector and take legal measures.

### Article 55 .-

Powers, roles, duties, and various implementation procedures of petroleum inspection officials shall be determined by sub-decree.

### Article 56 .-

Any person who is dissatisfied with any measure taken by a petroleum inspection official may file a complaint to the Ministry of Mines and Energy within 30 (thirty) working days, starting from the date of receiving notification of the decision.

The Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy shall decide on the complaint within 45 (forty-five) working days at the latest, starting from the date of receiving the complaint.

In case the person is dissatisfied with the decision of the Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy, that person has the right to file a complaint to the court according to procedures within 30 (thirty) days at the latest, starting from the date of receiving notification of the decision of the Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy.

# Chapter 7
## Penal Provisions

### Article 57 ._
Sanctions on offenses in the petroleum sector under this law include the revocation or suspension of rights granted under this law, reparation of damages, fines, confiscation of exhibits, and imprisonment.

The revocation or suspension of rights granted under this law, the requirement to make reparations for damages, and fines on offenses in the petroleum sector shall be under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Mines and Energy.

Procedures for fines and the revocation or suspension of rights as provided in this law shall be determined by a Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy.

Fines, fines imposed under a court judgment or verdict, or proceeds from the sale of exhibits confiscated by the court as state property shall be paid into the national budget.

The Royal Government may decide to reward officials who participate in cracking down on offenses in the petroleum sector.

Any civil servant who commits an offense in the petroleum sector shall be subject to administrative disciplinary sanctions in accordance with the laws in force, without prejudice to other criminal penalties.

Any person who is directly affected by a decision on sanctions of the Minister of Mines and Energy as provided in this law has the right to file a complaint to the court in accordance with procedures within a period of 30 (thirty) days, counting from the date of receiving notification of the decision.

The provisions of the Single Section (General Provisions) of Title 1 (General Provisions), Single Chapter (Physical Coercion) of Title 3 (Physical Coercion) of Book 8 of the Criminal Procedure Code shall be applicable in case the decision on fines has become final.

### Article 58 ._
Any physical person who conducts petroleum exploration, development, or production activities without a petroleum agreement, or whose petroleum agreement is suspended, shall be sentenced to imprisonment from 2 (two) years to 5 (five) years and fined in cash from 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels to 500,000,000 (five hundred million) Riels.

For any legal entity that conducts petroleum exploration, development, or production activities without a petroleum agreement, or whose petroleum agreement is suspended, shall be fined in cash by the Ministry of Mines and Energy from 4,000,000,000 (four billion) Riels to 50,000,000,000 (fifty billion) Riels.

### Article 59 ._
Any physical person who conducts initial aerial survey operations without permission from the Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy shall be fined in cash by the Ministry of Mines and Energy from 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels to 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels.

In case of continuing to commit the same offense, the offender shall be sentenced to imprisonment from 6 (six) months to 2 (two) years and fined in cash from 20,000,000 (twenty million) Riels to 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels.

For any legal entity that conducts initial aerial survey operations without permission from the Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy shall be fined in cash by the Ministry of Mines and Energy from 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels to 1,000,000,000 (one billion) Riels.

### Article 60 ._
Any contractor of a petroleum agreement who fails to fulfill the obligations as provided in point (a) or (b) of Article 20 of this law, and causes damage to the environment by negligence or intent, shall be fined in cash by the Ministry of Mines and Energy from 400,000,000 (four hundred million) Riels to 2,000,000,000 (two billion) Riels and shall rectify or terminate the petroleum agreement.

In case of continuing to commit the same offense, the offender shall be fined in cash by the Ministry of Mines and Energy double the amount.

### Article 61 ._
Any contractor of a petroleum agreement who fails to fulfill the obligations as provided in point (c) or (d) of Article 20 of this law, and causes permanent disability or death to others by negligence or intent, shall be fined in cash by the Ministry of Mines and Energy from 400,000,000 (four hundred million) Riels to 2,000,000,000 (two billion) Riels and shall rectify or terminate the petroleum agreement.

In case of continuing to commit the same offense, the offender shall be fined in cash by the Ministry of Mines and Energy double the amount.

### Article 62 ._
Falsification of financial reports in the petroleum sector shall be punished in accordance with the Penal Code of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

Any person who disseminates or shares petroleum data without permission from the Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy shall be sentenced to imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 1 (one) year and fined in cash from 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels to 1,000,000,000 (one billion) Riels.

Any person who submits or provides reports, data, or information in the petroleum sector with the intent to mislead or cause misunderstanding to the Ministry of Mines and Energy shall be punished by imprisonment from 1 (one) year to 3 (three) years and a fine from 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels to 1,000,000,000 (one billion) Riels.

### Article 63 ._
Any petroleum agreement contractor who is late in providing any or all petroleum data, information, and technical reports from petroleum operations to the Ministry of Mines and Energy within the timeframe specified in the petroleum agreement, or after receiving a written determination from the Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy within a maximum period of 90 (ninety) days, shall be fined in cash by the Ministry of Mines and Energy from 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels to 1,000,000,000 (one billion) Riels.

After having received a fine in accordance with the first paragraph above, if the petroleum agreement contractor still fails to provide petroleum data, information, and technical reports within a maximum period of 30 (thirty) days starting from the date determined by the Minister of the Ministry of Mines and Energy, [they] shall be punished by imprisonment from 1 (one) year to 3 (three) years and a fine from 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels to 150,000,000 (one hundred fifty million) Riels for physical persons, and a fine from 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels to 300,000,000 (three hundred million) Riels for legal entities. All related petroleum data, information, technical reports, and technical equipment shall be confiscated as state property.

In the event that the petroleum agreement contractor is punished in accordance with the second paragraph above, the right to use petroleum data related to the petroleum agreement area shall be revoked.

### Article 64 ._
Any person who conducts downstream activities without a license in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 41 of this Law shall be fined in cash by the Ministry of Mines and Energy from 1,000,000 (one million) Riels to 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels.

Any person who conducts downstream activities without a permit in accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 41 of this Law shall be fined in cash by the Ministry of Mines and Energy from 200,000 (two hundred thousand) Riels to 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels.

### Article 65 ._
Any person who conducts downstream activities without complying with the technical regulations issued

by the Ministry of Mines and Energy in accordance with Article 48 of this Law shall be fined in cash by the Ministry of Mines and Energy from 1,000,000 (one million) Riels to 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels.

### Article 66 ._
Any person who conducts downstream activities without a technical safety certificate from the Ministry of Mines and Energy as provided in Article 42 of this Law, prior to commencing the construction of infrastructure to serve downstream activities, shall be fined in cash as follows:

a- For infrastructure with a value of less than 800,000,000 (eight hundred million) Riels, shall be fined in cash by the Ministry of Mines and Energy in the amount of 4,000,000 (four million) Riels and have such construction activities suspended.

b- For infrastructure with a value starting from 800,000,000 (eight hundred million) Riels, shall be fined in cash by the Ministry of Mines and Energy in the amount of 10,000,000 (ten million) Riels and have such construction activities suspended.

In case of continued commission of the offense, the fine in cash imposed by the Ministry of Mines and Energy shall be doubled.

### Article 67 ._
Any person who acts to obstruct or cause disturbance to the inspection process of the petroleum inspection officials shall be punished by imprisonment from 1 (one) month to 1 (one) year and a fine from 1,000,000 (one million) Riels to 50,000,000 (fifty million) Riels. This act shall be punished by imprisonment from 2 (two) years to 5 (five) years and a fine from 100,000,000 (one hundred million) Riels to 250,000,000 (two hundred fifty million) Riels if committed by a public civil servant or a citizen holding a public mandate by election within the framework of their functions or on the occasion of performing their functions.

### Article 68 ._
The fines set forth in this Chapter shall not bar the implementation of the criminal law in force and are without prejudice to other compensations for damages.

# Chapter 8
## Transitory Provisions

### Article 69 .-
Obligations, responsibilities, rights, and benefits defined in petroleum agreements that are in force prior to the entry into force of this Law shall continue to be in force in accordance with this Law. In case of any provisions to the contrary with this Law, such petroleum agreements shall be amended in compliance with the provisions of this Law.

### Article 70 .-
Any physical person or legal entity who does not have a license or authorization as provided in the first paragraph of Article 41 of this Law shall complete the application procedures for a license or authorization within 1 (one) year after this Law enters into force.

# Chapter 9
## Final Provisions

### Article 71 .-
Any provisions that contradict this Law shall be abrogated.

### Article 72 .-
This Law is declared urgent.

# Annex to the Law on the Management of Petroleum and Petroleum Products
## Glossary

**Transportation** refers to the transport of petroleum and petroleum products by road, rail, and waterway, including main pipelines and pipeline systems.

**Export** refers to the activity of exporting petroleum and petroleum products.

**Processing** refers to lowering the temperature to liquefy gas, refining, purifying, separating, and blending to enhance the quality of petroleum, petroleum products, and petrochemicals, except for processing at petroleum production sites.

**Exploration** refers to geological, lithological, geophysical, geochemical, topographic, and geological survey studies, exploration drilling, appraisal drilling, and other operations utilizing various techniques for the purpose of exploring for petroleum resources.

**Transfer of Rights and Interests** refers to the transfer of rights, sale of shares, establishment of a joint venture, association or business partnership, or transfer of interest or any portion of interest, or entering into other business agreements related to the transfer of interest in a petroleum agreement, executed in accordance with relevant laws and regulations in force.

**Petroleum Agreement** refers to a production sharing contract or other forms of petroleum contract for the implementation of upstream activities, which yields favorable commercial benefits to the State and socio-economic benefits of the nation.

**Petrochemicals** refers to any products produced by chemical treatment of petroleum.

**Point of Delivery** refers to the point of transfer of ownership of petroleum products, which shall be specified in the petroleum agreement.

**Petroleum Reservoir** refers to an accumulation of petroleum in rock characterized by the configuration of each rock layer and/or the contact surface between petroleum and water in that layer.

**Installations** refers to equipment, plants, pipelines, and other materials for petroleum operations, excluding petroleum tankers or vessels carrying supply and support materials.

**Agreement Area** refers to a petroleum area for which a petroleum agreement has been entered into for conducting petroleum operations. The agreement area may vary according to each phase of relinquishment of the land area in accordance with the regulations and conditions in the petroleum agreement.

**Petroleum Data** refers to all data in raw, processed, or interpreted form related to

**Petroleum Data** includes, among others, geological, geophysical, geochemical data, surface and subsurface maps, samples, geological techniques, drilled wells, well tests, analysis results, pilot production, and technical data.

**Pricing Point** refers to the location agreed upon by the Royal Government and the petroleum agreement contractor as specified in the petroleum agreement.

**Petroleum Resources** refers to petroleum naturally occurring through geological processes and/or as a result of petroleum extraction and production within the territorial integrity and sovereignty of the Kingdom of Cambodia, including the seabed and subsoil of the exclusive economic zone and the continental shelf, as well as other areas where the Kingdom of Cambodia has sovereign rights.

**Reconnaissance** refers to the preliminary search to determine the geological potential of petroleum without the right to conduct development and production.

**Transfer of Power of Control** refers to the direct or indirect change of control of the petroleum agreement contractor or candidate or legal entity holding a license or permit, through merger, sale of shares, or transfer of other capital benefits, or the sale of any corporation, in a single transaction or a series of transactions related from one or more transferors to one or more transferees.

**Petroleum Operations** refers to all petroleum exploration, development, and production activities conducted within the petroleum agreement area in accordance with the petroleum agreement.

**Petroleum** refers to crude oil, natural gas, and other hydrocarbons, whether in liquid, slurry, or gaseous state in their natural condition, and all other substances that are components of those hydrocarbons.

**Petroleum Block** refers to a location with specific coordinates defined by the Ministry of Mines and Energy for granting the right to explore, develop, and produce petroleum.

**Production** refers to petroleum production, including the drilling and maintenance of production wells, injection, enhancement of recovery efficiency, processing, storage of petroleum for transportation, as well as all activities related to petroleum production within the petroleum field.

**Petroleum Products** refers to all products derived from petroleum through various processes.

**Commerce** refers to the supply, distribution, sale, export, and import of petroleum, petroleum products, including the filling of various gases into tanks or bottles.

**Commercial Discovery** refers to the discovery of petroleum resources that can be developed and produced.

**Petroleum Sector** refers to upstream and downstream activities and important related activities.

**Commercial Potential** refers to the discovery of petroleum resources that cannot be immediately developed and produced, but may become commercial when appropriate conditions are met.

**Stock (Storage)** refers to the storage of petroleum, petroleum products, and petroleum chemicals on land, underground and underwater, floating, and in underground caverns, with facilities for receiving, storing, and discharging.

**Petroleum Royalty** refers to the amount of petroleum collected by the State from the production volume of petroleum produced by the petroleum agreement contractor.

**Petroleum Costs** refers to the expenses incurred for petroleum operations within any agreement area of the petroleum agreement contractor in accordance with the petroleum agreement.

**Development** refers to the design, construction, installation of production facilities, drilling of production wells, and all activities related to development by the petroleum agreement contractor in accordance with the conditions set forth in the production permit.

**Petroleum Agreement Contractor** refers to a legal entity that has signed a petroleum agreement with the Royal Government of Cambodia.

**Natural Gas** refers to gaseous hydrocarbons produced from wells, such as wet gas, dry gas, and gas remaining from the extraction of liquid hydrocarbons.
